## Общие замечания экзаменаторов

This paper assessed topic 4, Materials, and topic 5, Waves and Particle Nature of Light. The paper provided candidates with the opportunity to demonstrate progression from GCSE and consolidate their knowledge at the end of the first year of the AL qualification.

Section A, a total of 58 marks, consisted of 8 multiple-choice questions and questions of differing style and length, drawing on a range of different concepts, from topics 4 and 5. Section B, a total of 22 marks, consisted of two synoptic questions drawing on content from the whole of the AS specification.

Practical skills and knowledge of aspects of each of the core practicals were assessed throughout. Application of practical skills was also assessed in other familiar or unfamiliar contexts.

Based on their performance on this paper, students should:

- Revise how the rate of incident photons determines the intensity of light.

- Use of suitable terminology when giving an explanation.

- Revise energy levels and emission/absorption spectra.

- The displacement of particles along a longitudinal wave, to identify a compression and rarefaction.
