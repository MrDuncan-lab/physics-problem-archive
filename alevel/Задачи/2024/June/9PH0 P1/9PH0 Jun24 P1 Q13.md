> Масса покоя антипротона в МэВ/c²; можно ли получить антипротон в столкновении двух протонов

Beams of antiprotons are often used in particle physics experiments.

**(a)** Show that the rest mass of an antiproton is about 900 MeV / c$^{2}$. **[4]**

**(b)** Antiprotons can be produced by accelerating and colliding two protons moving in opposite directions. A website suggests a possible outcome for a collision between these protons is described by the nuclear equation:

p + p → p + p + p + p

Deduce, by using conservation laws, whether it should be possible to produce an antiproton in this way. **[4]**
