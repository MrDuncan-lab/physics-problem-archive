> Сила реакции пола на человека в лифте, ускоряющемся вниз

A person of mass 70 kg is standing on the floor of a lift, as shown. The lift is accelerating downwards at 1.5 m s$^{-2}$.

![[9PH0-jun24-1-p5-f1.svg]]

Which of the following gives the normal reaction *R*, in N, acting on the person?

**A** *R* = 70 × 9.81  
**B** *R* = 70 × 1.5  
**C** *R* = (70 × 9.81) + (70 × 1.5)  
**D** *R* = (70 × 9.81) – (70 × 1.5)

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
