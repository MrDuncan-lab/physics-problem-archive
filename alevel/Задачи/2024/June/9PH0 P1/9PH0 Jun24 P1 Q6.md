> Сила Ампера на стороны квадратной рамки с током в магнитном поле

A square coil has sides of length *l* and carries a current *I*, as shown. The plane of the coil is parallel to a magnetic field of flux density *B*.

![[9PH0-jun24-1-p4-f1.svg]]

Which row of the table gives the magnetic force on the named side of the coil?

|  | **Named side** | **Magnetic force** |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | PQ | *BIl* to the left |
| **B** | QR | *BIl* to the right |
| **C** | RS | *BIl* upwards |
| **D** | SP | *BIl* downwards |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
