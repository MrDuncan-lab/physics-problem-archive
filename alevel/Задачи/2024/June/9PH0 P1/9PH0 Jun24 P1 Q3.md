> Работа против сил сопротивления у игрушечной машины на склоне по мощности мотора и приросту ПЭ

A toy car moves up a slope at a constant speed, as shown. The car is moved by a motor with a power output of 5.2 W. The car gains a gravitational potential energy of 0.40 J in a time of 1.1 s.

![[9PH0-jun24-1-p2-f1.png]]

Which of the following expressions gives the work done, in J, against resistive forces?

**A** 5.2 − (0.40 × 1.1)  
**B** (5.2 × 1.1) − 0.40  
**C** (5.2 ÷ 1.1) + 0.40  
**D** (5.2 × 1.1) + 0.40

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
