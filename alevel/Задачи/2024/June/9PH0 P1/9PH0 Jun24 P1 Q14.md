![[9PH0-jun24-1-p10-f1.svg]]

**(a)** A muon belongs to a family of particles called leptons.

State two features that all particles in the lepton family have in common. **[2]**

**(b)** Write a nuclear equation for the decay of the muon (μ) described above. **[2]**

**(c)** Describe the role of the magnetic field in a particle detector. **[3]**

**(d)** Explain how the diagram gives evidence that a particle or particles, other than the positron, were produced in this decay. **[4]**
