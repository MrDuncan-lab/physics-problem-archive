> Тележка на пружинах: как улучшить измерение периода и найти массу по графику T² от m

A student used springs to attach a trolley between two fixed supports, as shown.

![[9PH0-jun24-3-p10-f1.svg]]

When displaced horizontally, the trolley oscillated with simple harmonic motion.

To determine the time period *T* of oscillation of the trolley, the student displaced the trolley from its equilibrium position and released it. As she released the trolley, she started a stopwatch. She stopped the stopwatch when the trolley had returned to its starting point.

**(a)** Explain how the procedure used by the student to determine *T* could have been improved. **[6]**

**(b)** The mass of the trolley was *M*. The student added a small mass *m* to the trolley and determined the new value of *T*. She repeated the procedure for a range of values of *m*.

She plotted a graph of *T*$^{2}$ against *m*.

Explain how she could use her graph to determine a value for *M*. **[4]**

> | Пункт | Ответ (acceptable answers) | Указания (additional guidance) | Баллы |

> | (a) | • Time a number of (complete) oscillations calculate a mean time period **(1)**<br>• This reduces the effect of random error (dependent on MP1) **(1)**<br>• Allow oscillation to settle before starting to time. **(1)**<br>• Use a (fiducial) marker (so easier to see when trolley passes a point) **(1)**<br>• Use the mid-point of the oscillation (for timing), **(1)**<br>• Trolley is travelling fastest at mid-point, so effect of reaction time is reduced. **(1)** | Accept description of how to determine a mean – don’t need to see the word “mean”<br>Accept reaction time for random error, also accept reference to reducing percentage uncertainty<br>Accept equilibrium/undisplaced position<br>For MP6 allow use a datalogger to eliminate effect of reaction time [not dependent upon MP5] | 6 |
> | (b) | • Substitutes into equation for mass on spring to give<br>$T = 2\pi\sqrt{\dfrac{M + m}{k}}$ **(1)**<br>• $T^{2} = \frac{4\pi^{2}}{k}m + \frac{4\pi^{2}M}{k}$ **(1)**<br>• Identify gradient as $4\pi^{2}/k$ (and calculate a value for $k$) **(1)**<br>• Identify y-intercept as $4\pi^{2}M/k$ (and calculate a value for $M$) **(1)** |  | 4 |
