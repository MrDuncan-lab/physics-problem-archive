> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Прыгунки: безопасна ли скорость при амплитуде 22 см, резонансная частота для более тяжёлого ребёнка

A ‘jumperoo’ is a harness, suspended by a vertical spring, into which a baby can be placed, as shown.

![[9PH0-jun24-3-p22-f1.png]]

The jumperoo is adjusted so that the baby’s feet are a few centimetres above the floor. If the baby is then displaced downwards and released, he oscillates vertically.

**(a)** The graph shows how the acceleration *a* of the baby depends upon the displacement *x* of the baby from its equilibrium position.

![[9PH0-jun24-3-p22-f2.svg]]

For safety reasons, it is suggested that the maximum velocity of the baby should not exceed 0.5 m s$^{-1}$.

Assess whether it is safe for the baby to oscillate in the jumperoo with an amplitude of 22 cm. **[3]**

**(b)** The amplitude of the oscillations quickly decreases, so the baby has to push down on the floor to maintain the oscillations.

When the baby pushes at a particular frequency, the amplitude of oscillation increases to a maximum.

A baby of greater mass is placed in the jumperoo.

This baby pushes on the floor at a frequency that produces a maximum amplitude of oscillation.

Explain how this frequency compares with the frequency of pushing of the original baby.

A calculation is not necessary. **[3]**
