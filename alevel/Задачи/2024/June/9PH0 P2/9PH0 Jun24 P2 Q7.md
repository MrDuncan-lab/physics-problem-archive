> Порядок скоростей света в трёх средах по ходу преломлённого луча

The diagram shows a ray of light passing from medium X to medium Y to medium Z. The speed of light in medium X is *v*$_{\text{X}}$, the speed of light in medium Y is *v*$_{\text{Y}}$ and the speed of light in medium Z is *v*$_{\text{Z}}$ .

![[9PH0-jun24-2-p5-f1.svg]]

Which of the following shows the speeds in order of increasing magnitude?

**A** *v*$_{\text{Y}}$ *v*$_{\text{X}}$ *v*$_{\text{Z}}$  
**B** *v*$_{\text{Z}}$ *v*$_{\text{X}}$ *v*$_{\text{Y}}$  
**C** *v*$_{\text{X}}$ *v*$_{\text{Y}}$ *v*$_{\text{Z}}$  
**D** *v*$_{\text{Y}}$ *v*$_{\text{Z}}$ *v*$_{\text{X}}$

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**
