> Удлинение второй проволоки: вдвое длиннее, вдвое тоньше, сила вдвое больше

A wire of length *L* and diameter *d* is fixed at one end. A force *F* is applied to the wire causing an extension *e*.

A second wire, made of the same material, of length 2*L* and diameter $\dfrac{d}{2}$ , is fixed in the

same way. A force 2*F* is applied to this wire.

What is the extension of the second wire?

**A** $\dfrac{e}{4}$  
**B** *e*  
**C** 8*e*  
**D** 16*e*

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
