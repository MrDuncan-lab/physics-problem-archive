> Число альфа- и бета-распадов в цепочке от франция-221 до таллия-205

The isotope ${}^{221}_{87}\mathrm{Fr}$ undergoes a series of alpha and beta decays before forming ${}^{205}_{81}\mathrm{Tl}$.

Which row of the table shows the number of alpha and beta particles emitted during the decays?

|  | **Alpha** | **Beta** |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | 3 | 2 |
| **B** | 3 | 6 |
| **C** | 4 | 2 |
| **D** | 4 | 6 |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
