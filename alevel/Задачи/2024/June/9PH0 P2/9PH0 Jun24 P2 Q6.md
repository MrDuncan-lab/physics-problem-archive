Fission and fusion of nuclei may result in the release of energy.

Which row of the table gives the arrows representing energy release by fission and fusion?

|  | **Fission** | **Fusion** |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | x | w |
| **B** | x | y |
| **C** | z | w |
| **D** | z | y |
