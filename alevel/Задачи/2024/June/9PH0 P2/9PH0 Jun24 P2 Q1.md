> Последовательность положений Солнца на диаграмме Герцшпрунга — Рассела за его жизнь

A Hertzsprung–Russell diagram for stars in our galaxy is shown. V, W, X, Y and Z are positions on the diagram.

![[9PH0-jun24-2-p2-f1.png]]

Which of the following gives a sequence of positions of the Sun during its lifetime?

**A** V X Z  
**B** X W Y  
**C** X W Z  
**D** Z X V

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> |---|---|---|---|
