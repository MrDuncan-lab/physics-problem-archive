> Что увеличит угол дифракции электронов: длина волны де Бройля и ускоряющее напряжение

In an electron diffraction experiment, electrons are accelerated by a potential difference between a hot filament and an anode. The electron beam produced strikes a sample of crystalline material causing a diffraction pattern.

Which of the following would cause the angle of diffraction to increase?

**A** decreasing the distance between the filament and the anode  
**B** decreasing the potential difference  
**C** increasing the filament temperature  
**D** using a crystalline material with a larger lattice spacing

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
