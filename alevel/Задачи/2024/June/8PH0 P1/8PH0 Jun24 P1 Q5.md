> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выражение для скорости дрейфа электронов через сопротивление и напряжение

A copper wire has a cross-sectional area *A* and length *l*. There is a potential difference *V* across the wire. The resistance of the wire is *R*.

Copper has *n* conduction electrons per unit volume.

Which of the following expressions gives the drift velocity of the electrons along the wire?

**A** $\dfrac{RnAe}{V}$  
**B** $\dfrac{R}{VnAe}$  
**C** $\dfrac{V}{RnAe}$  
**D** $\dfrac{VnAe}{R}$

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
