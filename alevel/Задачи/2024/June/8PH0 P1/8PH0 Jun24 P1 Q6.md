> Скорость мяча у пола после скатывания со стола

A ball rolls along a table of height 0.83 m. The ball has a horizontal velocity of 1.72 m s$^{-1}$ as it falls off the edge of the table.

Which of the following is an expression for the speed of the ball, in m s$^{-1}$, as it hits the floor?
