> Оптическая сила двух рассеивающих линз

Two diverging lenses are combined, as shown.

![[8PH0-jun24-2-p3-f1.svg]]

The focal length of each lens is 0.2 m.

Which of the following is the total power of the combination?

**A** 5 D  
**B** –5 D  
**C** 10 D  
**D** –10 D

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
