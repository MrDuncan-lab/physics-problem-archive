> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Что не наблюдается при фотоэффекте

The photoelectric effect provides evidence for the particle nature of electromagnetic radiation.

Which of the following is **not** observed in the photoelectric effect?

**A** Photoelectrons are released instantly.  
**B** Photoelectrons are only released above a certain frequency of radiation.  
**C** The energy of the photoelectrons is determined by the intensity of the light.  
**D** The energy of the photons is proportional to the frequency of the radiation.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
