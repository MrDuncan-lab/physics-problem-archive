> Длина волны фотона при переходе электрона между соседними уровнями

An electron in an atom absorbs energy *E* and is excited from one energy level to the next. When the electron returns to its original energy level a photon is emitted.

Which of the following is an expression for the wavelength of the emitted photon?

**A** $\dfrac{hc}{E}$  
**B** $\dfrac{E}{hc}$  
**C** $\dfrac{Eh}{c}$  
**D** $\dfrac{c}{Eh}$

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**
