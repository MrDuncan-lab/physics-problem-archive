> Интенсивность света после поворота второго поляризатора на 90°

Unpolarised light passes through two polarising filters, as shown. The planes of polarisation of the two filters are parallel.

![[8PH0-jun24-2-p5-f1.svg]]

incident light

One filter is rotated so that the planes of polarisation of the two filters are perpendicular.

Which of the following describes the change to the intensity of the transmitted light?

**A** decreases by half  
**B** falls to zero  
**C** increases  
**D** unchanged

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
