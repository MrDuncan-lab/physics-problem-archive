> Относительная погрешность площади сечения проволоки по погрешности диаметра

A student measures the diameter of a steel wire in order to determine the cross-sectional area of the wire. The percentage uncertainty in the measurement of the diameter was 1.8%.

Which of the following is the percentage uncertainty in the value for the cross-sectional area?

**A** 1.8%  
**B** (1.8 + 1.8)%  
**C** (1.8 + 1.8 + 1.8)%  
**D** (1.8 × 1.8)%

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
