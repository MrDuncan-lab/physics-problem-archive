> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ (acceptable answers) | Указания (additional guidance) | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | (a) | • Use of $hf = \emptyset + \dfrac{1}{2}mv^{2}$ **(1)**<br>• $v = 6.3 \times 10^{6}\ \text{m s}^{-1}$ **(1)** | <u>Example of Calculation</u><br>$E_{k} = (6.63\times10^{-34}\ \text{J s} \times 2.8\times10^{16}\ \text{s}^{-1}) - 6.9\times10^{-19}\ \text{(J)} = 1.78712\times10^{-17}\ \text{J}$<br>$v = \sqrt{\dfrac{2 \times 1.78712\times10^{-17}\ \text{J}}{9.11\times10^{-31}\ \text{kg}}} = 6.26\times10^{6}\ \text{m s}^{-1}$ | 2 |
> | (b)(i) | • Use of $\lambda = \dfrac{h}{p}$ **(1)**<br>• recognise $\lambda = 2 \times$ diameter of atom **(1)**<br>• $1.7\times10^{-24}\ \text{kg m s}^{-1}$ **(1)** | <u>Example of calculation</u><br>$p = \dfrac{6.63\times10^{-34}\ \text{(J s)}}{2\times2.0\times10^{-10}\ \text{(m)}} = 1.67\times10^{-24}\ \text{kg m s}^{-1}$ | 3 |
> | (b)(ii) | • A discrete number of half wavelengths fit into the diameter of an atom **(1)**<br>• Reference to $E = \dfrac{hc}{\lambda}$ to link wavelength to discrete energy levels **(1)** | Accept reference to $E = \dfrac{hc}{\lambda}$ | 2 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)**
>
> Many completed this calculation successfully. A significant number having correctly selected the equation and made correct substitutions, then struggled with the algebraic manipulation.
>
> Q15(b)(i) and (ii)
>
> Quite a few didn’t know what to do here and tried a momentum calculation with p=mv using the mass of the electron and a velocity.
>
> The second part was challenging and very few were able to make the link between the diameter of the atom and the discrete number of half wavelengths, often discussing electrons jumping to energy levels. A few more students were able to make a statement regarding E=hf and score a mark although this was often by luck rather than fully understanding the question.
