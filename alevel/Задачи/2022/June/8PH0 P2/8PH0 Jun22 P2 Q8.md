> Какую характеристику волны нельзя найти по графику смещения от времени

A displacement-time graph is shown for a particle in a transverse wave.

![[8PH0-jun22-2-p5-f1.svg]]

Which property of the wave can **not** be determined directly from the displacement-time graph?

**A** amplitude  
**B** frequency  
**C** time period  
**D** wavelength

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
