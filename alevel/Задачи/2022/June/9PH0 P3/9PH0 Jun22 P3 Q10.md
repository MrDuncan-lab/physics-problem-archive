> Вязкость мёда по падению шарика: критика методики, средняя скорость, выбор сорта мёда

A student carried out an experiment to determine the viscosity of some honey. He filled a tall glass cylinder with honey as shown, and timed a ball bearing as it fell through the honey.

![[9PH0-jun22-3-p18-f1.png]]

**(a)** The student placed rubber bands near the top and bottom of the cylinder. He started a stopwatch when the ball bearing passed the first band and stopped the stopwatch when the ball bearing passed the second band. He repeated this several times to determine a mean time.

Criticise the student’s method. **[2]**

**(b)** The time *t* for the sphere to fall through a distance of 25.0 cm is shown in the table.

| ***t*** **/ s** |  |  |  |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6.40 | 6.35 | 6.36 | 6.38 |

**(i)** Show that the mean velocity *v* of the ball bearing is about 0.04 m s$^{-1}$. **[3]**

**(ii)** The student had three different types of honey available.

Viscosity *η* is given by the following expression

$\eta = \dfrac{2r^{2}g\left(\rho_{\text{B}} - \rho_{\text{H}}\right)}{9v}$

radius *r* of ball bearing = 5.50 × 10$^{-3}$ m

density of ball bearing *ρ*$_{\text{B}}$ = 7750 kg m$^{-3}$

density of honey *ρ*$_{\text{H}}$ = 1360 kg m$^{-3}$

| **Viscosity (at 20 °C) / Pa s** |  |  |
|---|---|---|
| Honey A | Honey B | Honey C |
| 10.6 | 12.5 | 13.6 |

Deduce which honey the student used. **[2]**

Many candidates did score MP1 and mentioned terminal velocity, but almost none then went on to explain this further so did not score MP2.

> Question 10 (b)(i)-(ii)

> (b)(i)
>

>
> (b)(ii)
>
> This question was also answered very well, although a minority of candidates did not give a conclusion, having calculated a correct value for the viscosity.
>
