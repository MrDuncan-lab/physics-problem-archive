> Снаряд застревает в бруске и поднимает его на 55 см: энергия, рассеянная при ударе

A projectile of mass 65 g is fired vertically upwards into a stationary wooden block of mass 2.400 kg, as shown.

![[9PH0-jun22-3-p12-f1.svg]]

**(a)** The projectile becomes embedded in the block. They both move vertically upwards through a vertical displacement of 55 cm before momentarily coming to rest.

Calculate the energy dissipated as the projectile hits the block. **[6]**

Energy dissipated =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

**(b)** Explain how the principle of conservation of energy applies to this collision. **[2]**
