Which of the following is the SI base unit for resistance?

**A** Ω  
**B** V A$^{-1}$  
**C** kg m$^{2}$ s$^{-3}$ A$^{-2}$  
**D** kg m$^{2}$ s$^{-1}$ C$^{-2}$

> [!answer]- Ответ
