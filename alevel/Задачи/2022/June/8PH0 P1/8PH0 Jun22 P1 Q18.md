> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объём цилиндра из проводящего пластилина по плотности и его сопротивление по удельному сопротивлению

A student was given a block of conducting putty of mass 43 g.

**(a)** **(i)** Show that the volume of the block of conducting putty was about 8 × 10$^{-6}$ m$^{3}$.

density of conducting putty = 5300 kg m$^{-3}$ **[2]**

**(ii)** The student formed the putty into a cylinder. Coins were pushed onto the ends of the cylinder as shown. The coins each had a crocodile clip attached and an ohmmeter was connected across the putty.

![[8PH0-jun22-1-p28-f1.png]]

Determine the resistance of the cylinder of putty.

diameter of cylinder = 12 mm resistivity of conducting putty = 4.0 × 10$^{-3}$ Ω m **[3]**

Resistance =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

**(b)** The student then made a cylinder that had a larger resistance.

Explain how the student used the putty to make a larger resistance. **[2]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ (acceptable answers) | Указания (additional guidance) | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | • Use of $\rho = \dfrac{m}{V}$ **(1)**<br>• $V = 8.1 \times 10^{-6}$ (m$^{3}$) **(1)** | <u>Example of Calculation.</u><br>$V = \dfrac{0.043\ \text{kg}}{5300\ \text{kgm}^{-3}}$<br>$V = 8.1 \times 10^{-6}\ \text{m}^{3}$ | 2 |
> | (a)(ii) | • Use of $A = \pi r^{2}$ and $V = Al$ **(1)**<br>• Use of $R = \dfrac{\rho l}{A}$ **(1)**<br>• *R* = 2.5 Ω (ecf from (a)(i)) **(1)** | Show that value gives 2.50 Ω<br><u>Example of Calculation.</u><br>$A = \pi(6 \times 10^{-3}\ \text{mm})^{2} = 1.13 \times 10^{-4}\ \text{m}^{2}$<br>$8.1 \times 10^{-6}\ \text{m}^{3} = (1.13 \times 10^{-4}\ \text{m}^{3})\, l$<br>$l = 0.0716\ \text{m}$<br>$R = \dfrac{(4.0 \times 10^{-3}\ \Omega\ \text{m})(0.0716\ \text{m})}{(1.13 \times 10^{-4}\ \text{m}^{3})}$<br>R = 2.54 Ω | 3 |
> | (b) | • Makes a cylinder of longer length<br>**Or**<br>Makes a cylinder of smaller radius/area **(1)**<br>• As $R = \dfrac{\rho l}{A}$, this change will increase the resistance **(1)**<br>[MP2 dependent on MP1] |  | 2 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)(i)**
>
> This density calculation was carried out well by most. Mistakes came in rearranging the equation incorrectly or in not converting mass to kg. ‘Show that’ questions require the answer to be given to an extra decimal place when compared to the value given in the question. Most candidates did this well.
>
> This a correct response with clear working.
>
> **(a)(ii)**
>
> Most students performed this calculation well. Students who didn’t, often failed to realise that the volume of a cylinder is cross sectional area x length, which made it impossible to find the length correctly. Students also used the diameter as the radius to find the area.
>
> The following student correctly calculated the answer.
>
> Whereas this student failed to use the volume of a cylinder to find the length, instead assuming that it was a cube and finding the cube root of the volume.
>
> **(b)**
>
> The increase of the length and the reduction of the cross-sectional area both increase the resistance here. Most students spotted at least one of these, scoring the first mark point, but few quoted the equation to prove the relationship for the second mark point.
>
> This student used the equation to prove the relationship.
