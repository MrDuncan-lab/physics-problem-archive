> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Связь скорости дрейфа носителей заряда с сопротивлением при постоянном напряжении

A conductor has a resistance *R*. A constant potential difference is applied across the conductor. The drift velocity of the charge carriers in the conductor is *v*.

Which of the following is the relationship between *R* and *v*?

**A** $v \propto R$  
**B** $v \propto \sqrt{R}$  
**C** $v \propto \dfrac{1}{R}$  
**D** $v \propto \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{R}}$

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
