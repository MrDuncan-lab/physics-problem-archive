> Натяжение троса при подъёме груза с ускорением

A cable is used to pull a mass of 2.5 kg vertically upwards with an acceleration of 4.3 m s$^{-2}$.

Which of the following expressions gives the tension in the cable in N?

**A** 2.5 × 4.3  
**B** (2.5 × 4.3) – (2.5 × 9.81)  
**C** (2.5 × 4.3) + (2.5 × 9.81)  
**D** 2.5 × 9.81

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
