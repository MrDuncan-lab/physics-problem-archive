> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ (acceptable answers) | Указания (additional guidance) | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|---|
> |  | • Use of $\dfrac{1}{R_{\text{P}}} = \dfrac{1}{R_{1}} + \dfrac{1}{R_{2}}$ for resistors in parallel **(1)**<br>• Use of $R_{\text{T}} = R_{1} + R_{2}$ for resistors in series **(1)**<br>• $R_{\text{T}} = 47\ \Omega$ **(1)** | <u>Example Calculation</u><br>$\dfrac{1}{R_{\text{P}}} = \dfrac{1}{30} + \dfrac{1}{40}$<br>$R_{\text{P}} = 17\ \Omega$<br>$R_{\text{T}} = 20+17+10 = 47\ \Omega$ | 3 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This 3 mark question required a knowledge of combining resistances. Most students were able to recall and use the equations for combining resistors correctly as shown by the student response below.
>
> The following student added the reciprocals of the correct resistors for the parallel part of the circuit but forgot to then find the reciprocal of that answer. This was a common error.
>
> Some students still left their answers as a fraction and therefore forfeited the final mark.
