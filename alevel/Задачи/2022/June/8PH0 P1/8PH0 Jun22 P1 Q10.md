> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Тормозной путь бегуна по площади под графиком скорости

A sprinter crossed the finish line of a race and then decelerated to rest. The graph shows how the velocity of the sprinter varied with time once the sprinter had crossed the finish line.

![[8PH0-jun22-1-p8-f1.svg]]

Determine the distance travelled by the sprinter whilst decelerating to rest.

Distance =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ m

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ (acceptable answers) | Указания (additional guidance) | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|---|
> |  | • Use of distance = area under graph **(1)**<br>• Use of scales on axes **(1)**<br>• Distance in range 6.0 m – 6.2 m **(1)**<br>MP3 dependent on MP1 | <u>Example of Calculation</u><br>Distance (2 cm square) = 2.0 ms$^{-1}$ × 0.20 s<br>= 0.40 m<br>Number of squares = 15<br>Distance = 15 × 0.40 m = 6.0 m | 3 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question required students to calculate the distance travelled for motion with a non-linear deceleration by considering the area under a velocity-time graph.
>
> Most candidates were aware that they needed to find the area under the graph, although not all methods yielded results within the range. A few students tried to apply equations of linear motion. Although this produced an answer within the range, this was not credited as these equations can only be used when acceleration is a constant value.
>
> The following student used the method of ‘counting squares’. The student was careful to add in partial squares resulting in an accurate estimate of the area and a distance within the given range.
>
> This student divided the area up into known shapes. In this case the answer fell within the range, but many students made errors using this method as they had to do many separate calculations.
