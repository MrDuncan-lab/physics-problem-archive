> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Как меняются фокусное расстояние и оптическая сила стеклянной линзы в воде

The focal length and power of a converging glass lens are determined for the lens in air. The lens is then immersed in water.

Which row in the table shows how the focal length and power of the lens change?

|  | **Focal length** | **Power of lens** |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | decreases | decreases |
| **B** | decreases | increases |
| **C** | increases | decreases |
| **D** | increases | increases |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
