> Выражение для критического угла на границе стекло–вода

Light travelling in glass of refractive index *n*$_{\text{g}}$ is incident at a boundary with water of refractive index *n*$_{\text{w}}$. The critical angle for the boundary is *C*.

Which of the following expressions is correct for this boundary?

**A** sin *C* = $\dfrac{1}{n}$

*g*

**B** sin *C* = $\dfrac{n}{n}^{\text{w}}$

*g*

**C** sin *C* = $\dfrac{n_{g}}{n}$

*w*

**D** sin *C* = $\dfrac{1}{n}$

$^{\text{w}}$

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
