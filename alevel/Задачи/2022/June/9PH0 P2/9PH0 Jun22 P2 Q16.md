> Постоянная Планка по графику зависимости E_k от частоты и определение металла по работе выхода

In an investigation of the photoelectric effect, electromagnetic radiation of frequency *f* was directed onto a metal plate. The maximum kinetic energy *E* of the photoelectrons emitted from the metal plate was determined. The procedure was repeated for a range of frequencies.

The graph shows how *E* depended upon *f*.

![[9PH0-jun22-2-p12-f1.svg]]

**(a)** Determine a value for the Planck constant, *h*, in J s. **[4]**

*h* =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ J s

**(b)** The table gives data for different metal surfaces.

| **Metal surface** | **Work function / eV** |
|---|---|
| Caesium | 2.0 |
| Calcium | 2.9 |
| Magnesium | 3.7 |

Deduce which metal was being used in the investigation. **[3]**
