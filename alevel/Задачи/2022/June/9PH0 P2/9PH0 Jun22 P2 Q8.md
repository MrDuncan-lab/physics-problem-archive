> Условия полного внутреннего отражения: угол падения и соотношение показателей преломления

Total internal reflection occurs when light is incident on the boundary between medium 1 and medium 2, as shown.

![[9PH0-jun22-2-p5-f1.svg]]

The refractive index of medium 1 is $n_{1}$ and the refractive index of medium 2 is $n_{2}$.

The critical angle for the boundary is *C*.

Which row of the table is correct?

|  |  |  |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | *θ* < *C* | $n_{1} > n_{2}$ |
| **B** | *θ* < *C* | $n_{2} > n_{1}$ |
| **C** | *θ* > *C* | $n_{1} < n_{2}$ |
| **D** | *θ* > *C* | $n_{2} < n_{1}$ |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
