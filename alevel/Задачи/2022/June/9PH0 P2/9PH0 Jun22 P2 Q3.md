> От чего зависит дифракция звуковых волн вокруг препятствий

Sound waves can diffract around obstacles as shown in the diagram.

![[9PH0-jun22-2-p3-f1.svg]]

The diffraction effect is

**A** greater for large amplitude sound waves.  
**B** greater for low frequency sound waves.  
**C** independent of the frequency of the sound waves.  
**D** independent of the speed of the sound waves.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> | Пункт | Ответ (acceptable answers) | Указания (additional guidance) | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|---|
> |  | **The only correct answer is B**<br>***A*** *is incorrect because amplitude does not affect the diffraction effect*<br>***C*** *is incorrect because frequency affects the wavelength and hence the diffraction effect*<br>***D*** *is incorrect because the speed of sound affects the wavelength and hence the diffraction effect* |  | 1 |
