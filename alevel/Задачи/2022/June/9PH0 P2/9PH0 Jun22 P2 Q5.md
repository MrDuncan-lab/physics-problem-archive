> Сравнение температуры и площади поверхности красного гиганта Бетельгейзе и Солнца

Betelgeuse is a red giant star.

The surface temperature of Betelgeuse is *T*$_{\text{B}}$ and the surface area of Betelgeuse is *A*$_{\text{B}}$. The surface temperature of the Sun is *T*$_{\text{S}}$ and the surface area of the Sun is *A*$_{\text{S}}$.

Which row in the table shows a correct comparison of the surface temperature and surface area of Betelgeuse with those of the Sun?

|  | ***T***$_{\text{B}}$ \> ***T***$_{\text{S}}$ | ***A***$_{\text{B}}$ \> ***A***$_{\text{S}}$ |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | false | false |
| **B** | false | true |
| **C** | true | false |
| **D** | true | true |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
