> Телескоп-рефрактор: расстояние между линзами и перевёрнутое изображение; толщина просветляющего покрытия

A simple astronomical refracting telescope consists of two converging lenses. Light from a star is brought to a focus by the objective lens and then viewed through an eyepiece lens as shown.

![[9PH0-jun22-2-p14-f1.svg]]

**(a)** **(i)** In the arrangement shown, the final image is formed at infinity.

Explain why the separation of the objective and eyepiece lenses is equal to the sum of their focal lengths. **[2]**

**(ii)** State why the final image is inverted. **[1]**

**(b)** Glass lenses used for optical instruments often have an anti-reflective coating. The coating is a thin layer of a transparent substance with refractive index *n*$_{\text{c}}$.

Light is reflected from the coating surface and from the lens surface as shown. The reflected light interferes destructively.

![[9PH0-jun22-2-p15-f1.svg]]

When a single-layer coating is used, the coating thickness is chosen to eliminate reflections for green light, which is in the middle of the visible spectrum.

**(i)** Calculate the minimum thickness *d* of the coating required for the reflection of green light to be eliminated.

frequency of green light = 6.00 × 10$^{14}$ Hz *n*$_{\text{c}}$ = 1.38 **[4]**

*d* =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

**(ii)** State why white light reflected from coated lenses is seen as purple. **[1]**
