All the strings are under tension.

The strings on one type of harp are made from nylon of density 1070 kg m$^{-3}$. One string has a diameter of 1.14 mm.

**(a)** **(i)** Show that the mass per unit length *μ* of the string is about 1.1 × 10$^{-3}$ kg m$^{-1}$. **[2]**

**(ii)** When the middle of the string is plucked, a note of frequency 440 Hz is produced.

Calculate the tension in the string.

**(b)** The graph shows how the Young modulus *E* of the nylon varies with temperature.

![[9PH0-jun22-2-p19-f1.svg]]

When the harp is played, the temperature of the string increases.

Explain how this temperature change would affect the frequency of the note produced when the string is plucked. **[3]**
