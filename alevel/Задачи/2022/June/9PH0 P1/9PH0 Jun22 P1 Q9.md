*Общее условие вопросов 9 и 10:*

**Questions 9 and 10 refer to the information below.**

Alpha particle scattering investigations were first carried out in the early part of the 20th century.

---

An alpha particle with initial kinetic energy 8.8 × 10$^{-13}$ J approaches a nucleus of a gold (${}^{197}_{79}\mathrm{Au}$) atom.

Which of the following is an equation for the closest distance *r*, in metres, between the alpha particle and the nucleus?

**A** *r* = $8.99 \times 10^{9} \left(\dfrac{2 \times 1.6 \times 10^{-19} \times 79 \times 1.6 \times 10^{-19}}{8.8 \times 10^{-13}}\right)$  
**B** *r* = $\dfrac{2 \times 1.6 \times 10^{-19} \times 197 \times 1.6 \times 10^{-19}}{8.99 \times 10^{9} \times 8.8 \times 10^{-13}}$  
**C** *r* = $8.99 \times 10^{9} \left(\dfrac{8.8 \times 10^{-13}}{4 \times 1.6 \times 10^{-19} \times 79 \times 1.6 \times 10^{-19}}\right)$  
**D** *r* = $8.99 \times 10^{9} \left(\dfrac{2 \times 79}{8.8 \times 10^{-13}}\right)$

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**
