> Какая частица имеет массу 1 u и заряд −1,6·10⁻¹⁹ Кл

A particle has a mass of 1 u and a charge of −1.6 × 10$^{-19}$ C.

Which of the following could be the particle?

**A** antiproton  
**B** electron  
**C** neutron  
**D** positron

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**
