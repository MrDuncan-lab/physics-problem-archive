> Выражение скорости нерелятивистской частицы через кинетическую энергию, импульс и массу

The velocity *v* of a non-relativistic particle can be expressed in terms of combinations of the following quantities: kinetic energy $E_{\text{k}}$, momentum *p* and mass *m*.

Which of the following expressions is correct?

**A** *v* = $\dfrac{p^{2}}{m}$

![[9PH0-jun22-1-p2-f1.svg]]

**C** *v* = $\dfrac{E_{\text{k}}}{2p}$  
**D** *v* = $\dfrac{2E_{\text{k}}}{pm}$

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
