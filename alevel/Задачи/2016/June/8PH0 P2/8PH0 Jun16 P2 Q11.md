> Пятно Араго: дифракция по Гюйгенсу, интерференция в центре тени, волновая модель, измерение диаметра

The diagram shows a coherent beam of light incident on a metal ball bearing.

A dark shadow is seen on a screen behind the ball bearing. There is a small spot of light in the centre of the shadow. This spot of light is known as the Arago spot.

![[8PH0-jun16-2-p8-f1.svg]]

**(a)** Use Huygens’ construction to explain the behaviour of light as it travels past the edge of the ball bearing. **[2]**

**(b)** Explain why a spot of light is produced at the centre of the shadow. **[3]**

**(c)** François Arago first demonstrated this experiment in 1818 for a group of eminent scientists, to show the behaviour of light.

State the model for the behaviour of light that this experiment demonstrated and explain why the scientific community accepted this model. **[3]**

**(d)** The ball bearing shown in the experimental set-up has a diameter of about 1 cm.

Describe how the diameter could be measured accurately. **[2]**
