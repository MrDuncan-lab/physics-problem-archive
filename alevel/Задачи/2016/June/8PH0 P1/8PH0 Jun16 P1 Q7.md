> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This vector question was answered well by most candidates. They were given the direction of one of the forces on the plane and told the direction that the plane should move, i.e. the resultant due east. They were therefore required to select the correct direction that the plane should point, i.e. the other contributing vector to the resultant direction. Candidates tend to find the construction of vector diagrams challenging, and as no actual diagram was required here, an element of common sense could be used to select the correct direction.
