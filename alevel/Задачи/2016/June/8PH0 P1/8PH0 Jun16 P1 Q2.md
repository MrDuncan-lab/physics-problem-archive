> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Изменение импульса мяча при упругом отскоке от стены

A tennis ball of mass 0.06kg moves towards a wall at a velocity of 5 m s$^{-1}$as shown.

![[8PH0-jun16-1-p2-f1.svg]]

The tennis ball hits the wall perpendicularly and rebounds at the same speed.

What is the change in momentum of the ball?

**A** 0.60kg m s$^{-1}$  
**B** 0.30kg m s$^{-1}$

**C −**0.30kg m s$^{-1}$

**D −**0.60kg m s$^{-1}$

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ (acceptable answers) | Указания (additional guidance) | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|---|
> |  | **D −**0.60 kg m s$^{-1}$ |  | 1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> A fair proportion of candidates chose the change in momentum of the ball without considering the direction, i.e. initial−final momentum, response A, 0.60 kg m s−1. A more significant number chose response C, the final momentum of the ball (−0.30 kg m s−1).
>
> Only those realising that the change in momentum was the final momentum – the initial momentum scored the mark.
