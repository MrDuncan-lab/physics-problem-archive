> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question examined specification point 42, understanding how the potential difference along a uniform current-carrying wire varies with the distance along it. Candidates were expected to appreciate that the relationship is one of direct proportion and identify the only graph with an increasing line of constant gradient. Therefore, although answered well by most E grade and above candidates, the incorrect responses of C and D were very surprising as this question was recall only.
