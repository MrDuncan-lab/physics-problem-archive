> Турист на склоне: равновесие сил и вес рюкзака по моментам

A hiker of weight 740 N walks up a hill carrying a large bag of weight *W*. The hiker stops for a moment in the position shown.

![[8PH0-jun19-1-p16-f13.svg]]

The normal force *R* of the ground on the hiker is the same at each foot. The frictional force between each foot and the ground is *F*. The hill is at an incline of 20° to the horizontal.

**(a)** An expression for the components of force perpendicular to the ground acting on the hiker is

$740\cos 20 + W\cos 20 - 2R = 0$

**(i)** Explain why this expression is correct. **[2]**

**(ii)** The diagram shows the lines of action of the forces acting on the hiker and backpack. Position O represents the middle of the back foot of the hiker.

![[8PH0-jun19-1-p17-f14.svg]]

Determine *W*. You should take moments about O. **[6]**

*W* = \_\_\_\_\_\_

**(b)** The hiker repacks his bag, placing the heavier items at the bottom of the bag.

Explain why this may cause *R* on the front foot to decrease. **[2]**
