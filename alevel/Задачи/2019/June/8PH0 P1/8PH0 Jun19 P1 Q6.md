> Какие величины откладывать, чтобы наклон графика был удельным сопротивлением

*Общее условие вопросов 6 и 7:*

**Questions 6 and 7 refer to the information below.**

An ohmmeter was used to measure the resistance *R* of a length *l* of nichrome wire as shown. This was then repeated for different values of *l*.

![[8PH0-jun19-1-p6-f5.svg]]

---

The diameter of the wire was measured and the cross-sectional area *A* of the wire calculated.

A graph was plotted. The gradient of the graph was equal to the resistivity of nichrome.

Which row of the table shows the quantities which were plotted on the *y*-axis and the *x*-axis?

|  | ***y*-axis** | ***x*-axis** |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | *l* | *RA* |
| **B** | *lA* | *R* |
| **C** | *R* | *Al* |
| **D** | *RA* | *l* |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
