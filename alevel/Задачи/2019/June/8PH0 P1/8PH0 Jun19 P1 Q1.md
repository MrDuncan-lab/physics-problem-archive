> Выражение для «потерянного напряжения» на внутреннем сопротивлении

A cell of e.m.f $\mathcal{E}$ and internal resistance *r* is connected across a fixed resistor of resistance *R*. There is a current *I* in the circuit and a potential difference *V* across the fixed resistor.

![[8PH0-jun19-1-p2-f1.svg]]

The term ‘lost volts’ refers to the difference between the e.m.f. and the terminal potential difference.

Which of the following is an expression for the lost volts?

**A** *I* (*R* + *r*)  
**B** *Ir*  
**C** *IR*  
**D** *I*(*R* − *r*)

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
