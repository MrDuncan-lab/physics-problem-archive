> Неопределённость длины проволоки по трём измерениям

*Общее условие вопросов 6 и 7:*

**Questions 6 and 7 refer to the information below.**

An ohmmeter was used to measure the resistance *R* of a length *l* of nichrome wire as shown. This was then repeated for different values of *l*.

![[8PH0-jun19-1-p6-f6.svg]]

---

A metre rule was used to measure *l*.

The following measurements were recorded.

| *l*$_{1}$ / cm | *l*$_{2}$ / cm | *l*$_{3}$ / cm |
|---|---|---|
| 89.1 | 89.6 | 89.4 |

Which of the following is the most appropriate uncertainty to use for the length of the piece of wire?

**A** ±0.1 cm  
**B** ±0.2 cm  
**C** ±0.3 cm  
**D** ±0.4 cm

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
