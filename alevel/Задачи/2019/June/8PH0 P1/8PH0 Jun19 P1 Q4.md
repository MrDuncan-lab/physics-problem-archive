> Зависимость дрейфовой скорости от приложенного напряжения

A potential difference *V* is applied across the ends of a conductor. The drift velocity of the charge carriers in the conductor is *v*.

Which of the following expressions gives the relationship between drift velocity and potential difference?

**A** *v* ∝ $\dfrac{1}{V}$  
**B** *v* ∝ $\dfrac{1}{V^{2}}$  
**C** *v* ∝ *V*  
**D** *v* ∝ *V*$^{2}$

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
