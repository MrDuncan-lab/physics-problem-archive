> Множитель перевода кинетической энергии из граммов и км/ч в джоули

A student measured the mass, in grams, of a small projectile, and its velocity in kilometres per hour.

To calculate the kinetic energy *E*$_{\text{k}}$ of the projectile he substituted these quantities into the equation *E*$_{\text{k}}$ = ½ *mv*$^{2}$.

Which of the following should the student multiply the answer by to obtain an answer in joules?

**A** $\dfrac{1000}{3600^{2}}$  
**B** $\left(\dfrac{1000}{3600}\right)^{2}$  
**C** $\dfrac{3600^{2}}{1000}$  
**D** $\left(\dfrac{3600}{1000}\right)^{2}$
