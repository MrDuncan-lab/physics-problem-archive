> Почему ракета взлетает: какая сила действует на ракету

A space rocket lifts off vertically.

![[9PH0-jun19-1-p3-f2.png]]

The rocket lifts off because

**A** the exhaust gases exert a force on the ground.
**B** the exhaust gases exert a force on the rocket.
**C** the ground exerts a force on the rocket.
**D** the rocket exerts a force on the ground.
