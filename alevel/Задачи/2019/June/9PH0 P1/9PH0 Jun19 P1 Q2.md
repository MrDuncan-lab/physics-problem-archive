> Момент веса валуна относительно опоры наклонного рычага

A person uses a pivoted lever to lift a boulder of weight *W* as shown.

![[9PH0-jun19-1-p2-f1.svg]]

The centre of gravity of the boulder is a distance *d* from the pivot. The angle of the lever to the horizontal is *θ*.

Which expression is equal to the moment of *W* about the pivot?

**A** *Wd*
**B** *Wd* cos *θ*
**C** *Wd* sin *θ*
**D** *Wd* tan *θ*

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
