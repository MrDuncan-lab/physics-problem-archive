> Изменение яркости ламп в цепи из двух элементов при замыкании ключа

A circuit consists of three identical 1.5 V bulbs connected to two 1.5 V cells.

![[9PH0-jun19-1-p3-f3.svg]]

The switch is closed.

Which row describes the change in brightness of bulb Y and bulb Z?

|  | **Y** | **Z** |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | brighter | brighter |
| **B** | brighter | dimmer |
| **C** | dimmer | brighter |
| **D** | dimmer | dimmer |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
