> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Угол поворота третьего поляроида, при котором интенсивность света минимальна

Three polarising filters X, Y and Z, are placed in front of a source of unpolarised light. The planes of polarisation of the filters are initially parallel.

Filter Y is rotated by 45° as shown.

![[9PH0-jun19-2-p5-f4.svg]]

Filter Z is then rotated clockwise and the intensity of light emerging from Z is measured.

Which angle of rotation of Z will result in the lowest intensity of light?

**A** 90°  
**B** 135°  
**C** 180°  
**D** 225°

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
