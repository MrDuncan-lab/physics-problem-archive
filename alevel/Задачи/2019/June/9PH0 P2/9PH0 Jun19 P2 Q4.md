> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Число альфа- и бета-распадов в цепочке от радия-226 до свинца-206

The radium isotope ${}^{226}_{88}\mathrm{Ra}$ is unstable and undergoes a series of $\alpha$ and $\beta^{-}$ emissions until a stable isotope of lead ${}^{206}_{82}\mathrm{Pb}$ is formed.

Which of the following nuclear equations correctly shows the number of $\alpha$ and $\beta^{-}$ emissions?

**A** ${}^{226}_{88}\mathrm{Ra} \rightarrow {}^{206}_{82}\mathrm{Pb} + 3\alpha + 8\beta^{-}$  
**B** ${}^{226}_{88}\mathrm{Ra} \rightarrow {}^{206}_{82}\mathrm{Pb} + 4\alpha + 5\beta^{-}$  
**C** ${}^{226}_{88}\mathrm{Ra} \rightarrow {}^{206}_{82}\mathrm{Pb} + 5\alpha + 4\beta^{-}$  
**D** ${}^{226}_{88}\mathrm{Ra} \rightarrow {}^{206}_{82}\mathrm{Pb} + 5\alpha + 6\beta^{-}$

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
