> Выталкивающая сила, действующая на тело в жидкости

An object of volume *V* made from a material of density $\rho_{1}$ is placed into a fluid of density $\rho_{2}$.

Which of the following gives the upthrust on the object?

**A** $\rho_{1} V g$  
**B** $\rho_{2} V g$  
**C** $(\rho_{2} - \rho_{1})\, V g$  
**D** $\dfrac{(\rho_{2} + \rho_{1})}{2}\, V g$

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ (acceptable answers) | Указания (additional guidance) | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|---|
> |  | B | The only correct answer is **B**: Upthrust is density of fluid × volume of object × *g*<br>A is not the correct answer because density of object has been used, so this is the gravitational force acting on the<br>object<br>C is not the correct answer because this is the resultant force<br>D is not the correct answer because this is the mean of the magnitude of the forces in A and B | 1 |
