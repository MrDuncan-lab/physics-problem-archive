> Расстояние до самолёта по времени возврата радиолокационного импульса

Radar is used to detect the position of an aircraft. Microwave pulses are emitted from the radar transducer. The pulses reflect from the aircraft and are received back at the same transducer. The time in seconds between emission and reception of the pulse is *t*.

The distance of the aircraft in metres from the transducer is given by

**A** 2 × 3.0 × 10$^{8}$ × *t*
**B** 2 × 340 × *t*
**C** $\dfrac{3.0 \times 10^{8} \times t}{2}$
**D** $\dfrac{340 \times t}{2}$

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
