Which of the following gives the work done, in joules, in extending the spring by 0.15 m from its original length?

**A** 0.15 × 2.5  
**B** 0.5 × 0.15 × 2.5  
**C** $\dfrac{2.5}{0.15}$  
**D** $\dfrac{0.15}{2.5}$
