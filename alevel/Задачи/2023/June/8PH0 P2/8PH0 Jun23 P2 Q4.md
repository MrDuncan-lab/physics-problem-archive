> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Относительная погрешность установившейся скорости по погрешностям пути и времени

A ball bearing falling freely through a liquid reaches terminal velocity. The terminal velocity is determined by measuring the time taken for the ball bearing to fall a measured distance.

The measured distance has a percentage uncertainty X.

The measured time has a percentage uncertainty Y.

Which of the following gives the percentage uncertainty in the value for the terminal velocity of the ball bearing?

**A** X + Y  
**B** XY  
**C** $\dfrac{X}{Y}$  
**D** X – Y

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**
