> Измерительные приборы для определения модуля Юнга

A student carries out an investigation to determine the Young modulus of a material.

Which of the following gives two measuring instruments the student should use?

**A** calipers and stopwatch  
**B** metre rule and micrometer  
**C** metre rule and stopwatch  
**D** micrometer and calipers

> [!answer]- Ответ
