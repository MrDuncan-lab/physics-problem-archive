> Пузырёк в пруду: выталкивающая сила и скорость по закону Стокса; энергия связи-пружины в молекуле CO

Carbon monoxide gas is produced in a pond by the decay of organic matter.

**(a)** A bubble of carbon monoxide rises at a steady speed through the still water of the pond. The weight of the bubble is negligible.

The free-body force diagram below shows the forces acting on the bubble.

![[8PH0-jun23-2-p18-f1.svg]]

diameter of bubble = 1.5 mm

**(i)** Show that the upthrust acting on the bubble is about 1.7 × 10$^{-5}$ N.

density of water = 997 kg m$^{-3}$ **[3]**

**(ii)** Calculate the steady speed at which the bubble rises.

viscosity of water = 0.0011 Pa s **[2]**

Speed =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

**(b)** A molecule of carbon monoxide consists of one atom of carbon bonded to one atom of oxygen.

The bond between the two atoms can be modelled as a spring as shown.

![[8PH0-jun23-2-p19-f1.svg]]

The spring has a stiffness constant *k*.

At equilibrium the distance *d* between the two atoms is 12 nm.

External forces caused *d* to increase to 18 nm.

Calculate the increase in the potential energy between the two atoms.

*k* = 1195 N m$^{-1}$ **[2]**

Increase in potential energy =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)**
>
> Over 40% of candidates scored full marks across both (i) and (ii).
>
> (a)(i) Upthrust is the weight of the displaced water. The equation for the volume of a sphere needs to be recalled and substituted into the density equation ρ= mV to determine the mass of the displaced water. The mass then needs to be converted into weight by multiplying by g. Some candidates recalled the equation for the volume of a sphere but then, having written r3, forgot to cube the radius in their calculation. Incorrect rounding to 1.72 was also commonly seen.
>
> (a)(ii) Use Stoke’s law F = 6πrηv to determine v. This was calculated successfully overall.
>
> **(b)**
>
> Nearly 50% of candidates failed to score on this question. The main loss of marks was failing to correctly substitute the Hookes’s law equation into the equation for the elastic strain energy.
