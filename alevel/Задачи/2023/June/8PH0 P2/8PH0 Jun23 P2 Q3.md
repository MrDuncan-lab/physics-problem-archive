> Частота сигнала на осциллографе по цене деления развёртки

A 2-beam oscilloscope is used to determine the speed of sound in air. The oscilloscope screen shown displays the signal used to produce the sound wave.

![[8PH0-jun23-2-p3-f1.png]]

(Source: © Dorling Kindersley/UIG/SCIENCE PHOTO LIBRARY)

The time-base is set to 50 µs / division.

Which of the following gives the frequency, in Hz, of the signal?

**A** $5 \times 50 \times 10^{-6}$  
**B** $2.5 \times 50 \times 10^{-6}$  
**C** $\dfrac{1}{5 \times 50 \times 10^{-6}}$  
**D** $\dfrac{1}{2.5 \times 50 \times 10^{-6}}$

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
