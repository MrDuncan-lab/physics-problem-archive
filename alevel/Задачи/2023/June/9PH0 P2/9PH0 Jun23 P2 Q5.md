> Соотношение веса, архимедовой силы и вязкого сопротивления для ускоряющейся капли дождя

A raindrop is falling through the air with an increasing velocity. The forces on the raindrop are weight *W*, upthrust *U* and viscous drag *F*.

Which of the following shows the relationship between these forces?

**A** *F* + *U* = *W*  
**B** *F* = *W* + *U*  
**C** *F* + *W* \< *U*  
**D** *F* + *U* \< *W*

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
