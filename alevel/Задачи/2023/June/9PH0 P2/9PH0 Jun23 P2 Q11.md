# Question 11 (4 marks)

**Pearson Edexcel A level Physics (9PH0)** · June 2023 · Paper 2 — Advanced Physics II · вопрос 11

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выше или ниже частота толстой струны виолончели при той же длине и натяжении

The photograph shows a stringed instrument called a cello being played with a bow.

![[9PH0-jun23-2-p9-f1.png]]

(Source: © Vadim Ponomarenko/Alamy Stock Photo)

A standing wave forms on a cello string when the bow moves across the string.

Deduce whether a thicker string will produce a note of higher or lower frequency compared with a thinner string.

Assume each string is the same length and at the same tension.

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ (acceptable answers) | Указания (additional guidance) | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|---|
> |  | • Thicker string has greater *μ* **(1)**<br>• Refers to $v = \sqrt{\dfrac{T}{\mu}}$, so *v* is smaller **(1)**<br>**Or** states *v* is proportional to $\sqrt{\dfrac{1}{\mu}}$, so *v* is smaller<br>• Refers to $v = f\lambda$ with *λ* constant **(1)**<br>• So thicker string has lower frequency **(1)**<br>Dependent on MP1and 2 or MP1 and 3 | Accept words or symbols, including ↑ and ↓<br>Could be answered using an equation of the form:<br>$f = \dfrac{1}{\lambda}\sqrt{\dfrac{T}{\mu}}$ gives MP2 | 4 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> While candidates were helped by knowing the effect of a thicker string on frequency from experience, the requirement in this question was to use the information provided regarding the length and tension to arrive at the answer by a process of logical deduction, which a majority of candidates achieved, being awarded at least three marks. Few candidates were unable to state that the mass per unit length was greater, although some omitted ‘per unit length’, and most candidates who mentioned that knew that this resulted in a lower wave speed, although not all quoted the relationship v = √(T/m) to justify this. Those candidates obtaining three marks arrived at the correct conclusion but did not include a reference to the constant wavelength.
>
> Some candidates confused T for tension with T for period and said v is constant, so if m increases, period increases and frequency decreases.
>
> 3 marks. This follows the required steps, only failing to state that the wavelength is the same.
>
> 4 marks. This includes all of the relevant physics but approaches it in terms of the thinner string. As there are only two possibilities, this answers the question.
