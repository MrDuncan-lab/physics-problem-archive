> Удлинение второй проволоки вдвое длиннее и вдвое большего сечения под той же силой

A wire Y of cross-sectional area *A* and length *l* is joined to a second wire Z of cross-sectional area 2*A* and length 2*l* as shown. Wire Z is fixed at one end and a force *F* is applied to the other end of wire Y.

![[9PH0-jun23-2-p4-f1.svg]]

The wires are made of the same material.

Wire Y extends by a distance *x*.

Which of the following is the extension of wire Z?

**A** 4*x*  
**B** 2*x*  
**C** *x*  
**D** $\dfrac{x}{2}$

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
