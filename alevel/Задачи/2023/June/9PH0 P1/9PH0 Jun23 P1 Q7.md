> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Что увеличивает подушка безопасности при резкой остановке: время торможения водителя

A car is fitted with an airbag which will inflate if the car stops very suddenly.

![[9PH0-jun23-1-p4-f1.png]]

(Source: © KAIROS, LATIN STOCK/SCIENCE PHOTO LIBRARY)

Which of the following is increased if the airbag inflates because the car suddenly stops?

**A** change in momentum of the driver  
**B** change in velocity of the driver  
**C** force on the driver  
**D** time that the driver takes to stop

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
