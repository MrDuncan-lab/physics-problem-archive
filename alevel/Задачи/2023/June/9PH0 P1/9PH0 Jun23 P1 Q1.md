> Число нейтронов и протонов в ядре кислорода-17

One isotope of oxygen is ${}^{17}_{8}\mathrm{O}$.

Which row of the table shows the number of neutrons and the number of protons in a nucleus of ${}^{17}_{8}\mathrm{O}$?

|  | **Number of neutrons** | **Number of protons** |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | 8 | 9 |
| **B** | 8 | 17 |
| **C** | 9 | 8 |
| **D** | 17 | 8 |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
