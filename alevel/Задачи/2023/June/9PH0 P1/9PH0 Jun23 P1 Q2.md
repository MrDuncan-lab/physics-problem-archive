> Классификация частицы из кварка и антикварка (u, анти-s)

A subatomic particle consists of the quark combination u s .

Which of the following is the classification for this particle?

**A** baryon  
**B** lepton  
**C** meson  
**D** nucleon

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
