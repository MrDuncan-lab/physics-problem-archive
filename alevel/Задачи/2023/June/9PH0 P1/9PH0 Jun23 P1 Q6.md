> КПД котла по передаваемой воде мощности и потерям

In a boiler, energy is transferred to water at a rate of 40 kW. The corresponding power loss from the boiler to the surroundings is 4.0 kW.

Which of the following is the efficiency of this boiler?

**A** 0.10  
**B** 0.11  
**C** 0.90  
**D** 0.91

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
