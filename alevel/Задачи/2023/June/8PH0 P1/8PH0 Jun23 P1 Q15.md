> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Пуля и брусок на подвесе: импульс пули, высота подъёма бруска, отскок пули от стального бруска

A bullet of mass 12 g moved at a speed of 450 m s$^{-1}$. The bullet hit a soft wooden block of mass 2.5 kg which was attached to a wire, as shown. The bullet became stuck in the wooden block which swung upwards.

![[8PH0-jun23-1-p18-f1.svg]]

**(a)** **(i)** Show that the momentum of the bullet is about 5 kg m s$^{-1}$. **[2]**

**(ii)** Determine the maximum change in vertical height of the wooden block. **[3]**

Maximum change in vertical height =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

**(b)** The soft wooden block was replaced with a hard steel block of the same mass.

An identical bullet was fired at the steel block with the same speed as before. The bullet rebounded from the steel block.

Explain why the steel block moved through a greater maximum vertical height than the wooden block. **[5]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ (acceptable answers) | Указания (additional guidance) | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | • Use of $p = mv$ **(1)**<br>• $p = 5.4\ (\text{kg m s}^{-1})$ **(1)** | <u>Example of calculation</u><br>$p = 0.012\ \text{kg} \times 450\ \text{m s}^{-1} = 5.4\ \text{kg m s}^{-1}$ | 2 |
> | (a)(ii) | • Use of principle of conservation of linear momentum **(1)**<br>• Use of $\frac{1}{2}mv^{2} = mgh$ **(1)**<br>• $h = 0.24\ \text{m}$ (ecf from (a)(i)) **(1)** | <u>Example of calculation</u><br>$5.4\ \text{kg m s}^{-1} = (2.5\ \text{kg} + 0.012\ \text{kg}) \times v$<br>$v = 2.1\ \text{m s}^{-1}$<br>$\frac{1}{2}mv^{2} = mgh$<br>$\frac{1}{2}(2.1\ \text{m s}^{-1})^{2} = 9.81\ \text{m s}^{-2} \times h$<br>$h = 0.24\ \text{m}$ | 3 |
> | (b) | An explanation that makes reference to the following points:<br>• The final momentum/velocity of the bullet is negative<br>**Or** The final momentum/velocity of the bullet is in the opposite direction **(1)**<br>• The change in momentum of the bullet is greater **(1)**<br>• The (change in) momentum of the steel block is greater (because momentum is conserved) **(1)**<br>• So steel block has greater initial velocity<br>**Or** steel block has greater initial $E_{\text{K}}$ **(1)**<br>• So gains greater $E_{\text{P}}$ (and moves through a greater maximum vertical height)<br>\[Dependent upon MP4\] **(1)** |  | 5 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(b)**
>
> Many candidates failed to consider momentum in this answer, concentrating instead on the tiny difference in mass between the wooden block with the bullet embedded, and the steel block without the bullet embedded, or on the fact that the steel block would have a smaller volume and therefore less air resistance (a negligible effect at these speeds). Those that did concentrate on momentum scored quite well, but failed to explain each step, making jumps for instance from the higher initial velocity of the steel block, straight to its increased maximum height, with no mention of gravitational potential energy.
>
> This candidate concentrated on momentum and did well at describing the intermediate steps to link one change to the other.
>
> This candidate was less successful. Whilst realising that momentum was important, the candidate missed several important steps in the explanation.
