> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сопротивление цилиндра из пластилина после раскатывания до двойной длины

A piece of conducting putty is shaped into a cylinder of uniform cross-sectional area, as shown. The length of the cylinder is *l*. The resistance between the two ends is 8.0 Ω.

![[8PH0-jun23-1-p3-f3.svg]]

The piece of putty is then rolled out until the length is 2*l*.

Which of the following is now the value of the resistance between the two ends?

**A** 2.0 Ω  
**B** 4.0 Ω  
**C** 16.0 Ω  
**D** 32.0 Ω

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
