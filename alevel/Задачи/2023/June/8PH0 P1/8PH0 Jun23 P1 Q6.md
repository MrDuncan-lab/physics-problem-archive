> Формула КПД мотора, тянущего тележку по склону

The diagram shows an electric motor pulling a truck of mass *m* along a slope. The truck moves through a vertical height *h* and a distance *l* along the slope, during a time *t*.

There is a potential difference *V* across the motor and a current *I* in the motor.

![[8PH0-jun23-1-p4-f1.svg]]

Which of the following expressions gives the efficiency of the motor?

**A** $\dfrac{VIt}{mgl}$  
**B** $\dfrac{VIt}{mgh}$  
**C** $\dfrac{mgl}{VIt}$  
**D** $\dfrac{mgh}{VIt}$

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
