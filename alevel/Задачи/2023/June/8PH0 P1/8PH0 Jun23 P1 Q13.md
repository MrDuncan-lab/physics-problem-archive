> Доска-мостик на камне и стене: сила реакции стены и условие опрокидывания

Some students used a plank to make a bridge to cross a stream. The plank rested on a rock and a wall as shown.

Assume the plank is uniform.

![[8PH0-jun23-1-p14-f1.svg]]

**(a)** **(i)** Show that the weight of the plank is about 250 N.

mass of plank = 25 kg **[1]**

**(ii)** Determine the force exerted by the wall on the plank. **[3]**

Force exerted by wall on plank =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

**(b)** A student of weight 550 N states that the plank will tip if she walks from the rock to the other end of the plank.

Justify the student’s statement. **[3]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ (acceptable answers) | Указания (additional guidance) | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | • Working leading to *W* = 245 (N) **(1)** | <u>Example of calculation</u><br>$W = 25.0\ \text{kg} \times 9.81\ \text{m s}^{-2} = 245\ \text{N}$ | 1 |
> | (a)(ii) | • Use of moment of force = *Fx* **(1)**<br>• Use of the principle of moments **(1)**<br>• Support force = 170 N (ecf from (a)(i)) **(1)** | <u>Example of calculation</u><br>$245\ \text{N} \times 2.5\ \text{m} = F \times 3.6\ \text{m}$<br>$F = 170\ \text{N}$<br>Show that value gives 174 N | 3 |
> | (b) | • When plank tips, support force at rock = 0 **(1)**<br>**Or** When plank tips, clockwise moment is greater than anticlockwise moment<br>• Use of principle of moments **(1)**<br>• If person is at the end, clockwise moment = 770 N, anticlockwise moment due to weight is 270 Nm \< 770 Nm so student is correct **(1)**<br>**Or** Starts to tip when person is 0.49 m to the right of the wall, 0.49 m \< 1.4 m so student is correct | <u>Example of calculation</u><br>$245\ \text{N} \times (2.5 - 1.4)\ \text{m} = 550\ \text{N} \times x$<br>$x = 0.49\ \text{m} < 1.4\ \text{m}$ so tips<br>**Or**<br>$245\ \text{N} \times (2.5 - 1.4)\ \text{m} = 270\ \text{Nm}$<br>$550\ \text{N} \times 1.4\ \text{m} = 770\ \text{Nm}$<br>$770\ \text{Nm} > 270\ \text{Nm}$ so it tips | 3 |
