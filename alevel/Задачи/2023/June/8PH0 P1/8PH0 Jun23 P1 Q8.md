> Максимальная высота подъёма мяча, брошенного вертикально вверх

A ball is thrown vertically upwards at a velocity of 6.0 m s$^{-1}$.

Which of the following gives the maximum height, in m, reached by the ball?

**A** $\dfrac{6.0^{2}}{2 \times 9.81}$  
**B** $\dfrac{6.0^{2}}{2 \times (-9.81)}$  
**C** $\dfrac{6.0}{2 \times 9.81}$  
**D** $\dfrac{6.0}{2 \times (-9.81)}$

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**
