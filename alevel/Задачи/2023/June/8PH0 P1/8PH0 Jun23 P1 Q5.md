> Пара сил по третьему закону Ньютона для автомобиля на дороге

A car is travelling at a constant speed in a straight line along a horizontal road.

Which row of the table gives a Newton’s third law pair of forces?

|  | **Force 1** | **Force 2** |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | normal force of car on road | friction between wheels and road |
| **B** | normal force of car on road | normal force of road on car |
| **C** | weight of car | normal force of car on road |
| **D** | weight of car | normal force of road on car |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> | Пункт | Ответ (acceptable answers) | Указания (additional guidance) | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|---|
> |  | **B**<br>Incorrect Answers:<br>A – normal force and friction are different types of forces and not in opposite directions<br>C – weight and normal force on the road, are in the same direction and are different types of force.<br>D – weight and normal force on the car are different types of force and are both on the same object. |  | 1 |
