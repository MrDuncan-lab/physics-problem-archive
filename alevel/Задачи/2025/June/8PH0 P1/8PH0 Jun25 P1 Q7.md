> Ток и скорость дрейфа в проводе сужающегося сечения

A wire of decreasing diameter is connected in a circuit. There is a current from X to Y in the wire, as shown.

![[8PH0-jun25-1-p5-f1.svg]]

Which row of the table gives the current and the drift velocity of charge carriers at Y compared to X?

|  | **Current** | **Drift velocity** |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | lower | higher |
| **B** | lower | lower |
| **C** | the same | higher |
| **D** | the same | lower |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
