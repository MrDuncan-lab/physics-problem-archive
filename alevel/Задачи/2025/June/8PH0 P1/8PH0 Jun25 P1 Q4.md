> Внутреннее сопротивление элемента по ЭДС и показанию вольтметра

A circuit consists of a cell of e.m.f. 1.50 V connected to a 100 Ω resistor. A voltmeter is connected across the 100 Ω resistor, as shown. The voltmeter reading is 1.45 V.

![[8PH0-jun25-1-p3-f1.svg]]

Which of the following gives the internal resistance, in Ω, of the cell?

**A** $\dfrac{(1.50 - 1.45)\times100}{1.45}$  
**B** $\dfrac{1.50\times100}{1.45}$  
**C** $\dfrac{(1.50 - 1.45)\times100}{1.50}$  
**D** $\dfrac{1.45\times100}{1.50}$

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**
