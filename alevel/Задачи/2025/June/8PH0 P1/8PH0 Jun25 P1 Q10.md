> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Работа при толкании коляски под углом и нужная сила при горизонтальном толкании

A person pushes a baby in a pram.

**(a)** The person pushes the pram with a constant force. The force is applied at an angle of 50° to the horizontal, as shown.

![[8PH0-jun25-1-p8-f1.svg]]

When a force of 35 N is applied, the pram moves at constant speed.

Calculate the work done to push the pram at constant speed for 500 m. **[3]**

Work done =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

**(b)** The pram is then moved along the same surface, at the same constant speed, by a different person. This person pushes the pram by applying a force horizontally, as shown.

![[8PH0-jun25-1-p9-f1.svg]]

Explain how applying a force horizontally changes the magnitude of the force needed to push the pram at the same constant speed. **[2]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ (acceptable answers) | Указания (additional guidance) | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | (a) | • Use of horizontal component of force **(1)**<br>• Use of $\Delta W = F\Delta s$ **(1)**<br>• $\Delta W = 1.1\times10^{4}\ \text{J}$ **(1)** | <u>Example of calculation</u><br>Horizontal component of force $= 35\ \text{N}\times\cos 50^{\circ} = 22\ \text{N}$<br>$\Delta W = 22\ \text{N}\times500\ \text{m} = 1.1\times10^{4}\ \text{J}$ | 3 |
> | (b) | An explanation that makes reference to the following points:<br>• Force is in the direction of motion<br>**Or** Force is parallel to the direction of motion<br>**Or** there is a greater proportion of the force in the direction of motion **(1)**<br>• So lower applied force<br>**Or** Force is now 22 N **(1)** |  | 2 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)**
>
> This question was based on the equation for work done and involved a force at an angle to the direction of the motion. Errors commonly seen with this method included using the wrong trigonometric function when calculating the component of the force.
>
> **(b)**
>
> This question involved explaining why when the angle of the force changed, its magnitude decreased. Although most candidates knew that the force would decrease, few could explain why, with most simply discussing the horizontal component of the force without linking this to the direction the pram was moving in.
