> Сопротивление резистора в последовательной цепи и работа мотора, включённого параллельно

Two resistors are connected in series in a circuit, as shown. The power supply has negligible internal resistance.

![[8PH0-jun25-1-p10-f1.svg]]

**(a)** Calculate the resistance *R* of the resistor when the voltmeter reads 5.5 V. **[3]**

*R* =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

**(b)** A student uses the circuit to power an electric motor. He connects the motor in parallel with the 45 Ω resistor.

The electric motor has a resistance of 45 Ω and a normal working potential difference of 5.5 V.

Deduce how the motor performs when connected to the circuit. You do not need to do a further calculation. **[3]**

> | (a) | • Use of $R = \dfrac{V}{I}$ for the 45 Ω resistor **(1)**<br>• Use of $R = \dfrac{V}{I}$ for the whole circuit<br>**Or**<br>Use of $R = \dfrac{V}{I}$ for the resistor R **(1)**<br>• $R_{\text{variable resistor}} = 80\ (\Omega)$ **(1)**<br>**OR**<br>• Applies the principles of a potential divider circuit **(1)**<br>• Use of $R_{\text{total}} = R_{\text{variable resistor}} + 45\ \Omega$<br>**Or**<br>Use of $15\ \text{V} = V_{\text{variable resistor}} + 5.5\ \text{V}$ **(1)**<br>• $R_{\text{variable resistor}} = 80\ (\Omega)$ **(1)** | <u>Example of calculation</u><br>$I = 5.5\ \text{V}/45\ \Omega = 0.12\ \text{A}$<br>$R_{\text{total}} = 15\ \text{V}/0.12\ \text{A} = 125\ \Omega$<br>$R_{\text{variable resistor}} = 125\ \Omega - 45\ \Omega = 80\ \Omega$<br>e.g. ratio of resistances equated to ratio of p.d.s | 3 |
> | (b) | EITHER<br>• Decreased resistance for parallel combination of motor with 45 Ω resistor **(1)**<br>• So lower (proportion of the total) potential difference across motor, **(1)**<br>• hence potential difference is too low and motor runs slowly **(1)**<br>OR<br>• higher total current in circuit/battery **(1)**<br>• potential difference across R increases, so potential difference across motor decreases **(1)**<br>• hence potential difference is too low and motor runs slowly **(1)** |  | 3 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)**
>
> This question was a standard calculation involving a potential divider circuit.
>
> Most candidates were successful with this, with a fairly even split of candidates who chose to use the potential divider principle and those who chose to calculate the current and use this with R = V/I. Those who chose to calculate current were slightly more successful with this.
>
> **(b)**
>
> This question involved understanding the changes to the circuit when a motor was added in parallel with one component. Most candidates found this difficult, despite several similar questions over recent years. A common error was to assume that the current through the battery did not change and that when adding the motor, the current then split between the components in parallel. Some candidates also believed that the potential difference over this part of the circuit would not be changed by the addition of the motor.
