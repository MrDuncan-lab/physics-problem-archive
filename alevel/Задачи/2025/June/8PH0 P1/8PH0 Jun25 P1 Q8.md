> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Скорость тележек после сцепления с пятью покоящимися

A shopping trolley moves at velocity *v*. The trolley collides and combines with a stack of 5 identical stationary trolleys.

Which of the following expressions gives the velocity of the combined stack of trolleys?

**A** 6*v*  
**B** 5*v*  
**C** $\dfrac{v}{5}$  
**D** $\dfrac{v}{6}$

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
