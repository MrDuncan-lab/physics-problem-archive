> Удлинение двух параллельно соединённых пружин

When a force *F* is applied to a spring, the spring extends by a distance *x*. The spring has a spring constant *k*.

A second identical spring is connected in parallel with the first spring. The same force *F* is applied to the arrangement, as shown.

![[8PH0-jun25-2-p4-f1.svg]]

What is the extension of this arrangement?

**A** 0.25*x*  
**B** 0.5*x*  
**C** *x*  
**D** 2*x*

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
