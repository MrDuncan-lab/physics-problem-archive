> Разность фаз точек волны на расстоянии 3λ/4

Two points on a wave are a distance $\dfrac{3}{4}$ *λ* apart.

Which of the following is the phase difference between the two points?

**A** $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$  
**B** $\dfrac{3\pi}{4}$  
**C** $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$  
**D** $\dfrac{3\pi}{2}$

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
