> Постоянная времени RC-цепи, если напряжение упало втрое за 2 мс

The initial potential difference (p.d.) across a capacitor is *V*$_{0}$. The capacitor is discharged through a resistor. The p.d. across the capacitor decreases to $\dfrac{V_{0}}{3}$ in 2 milliseconds.

Which of the following is equal to the time constant, in milliseconds, for this capacitor-resistor circuit?

**A** $\dfrac{\ln 3}{2}$  
**B** $\dfrac{2}{\ln 3}$  
**C** 3  
**D** $\dfrac{2}{3}$

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
