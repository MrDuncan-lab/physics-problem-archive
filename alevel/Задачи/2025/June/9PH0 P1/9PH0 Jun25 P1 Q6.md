![[9PH0-jun25-1-p4-f1.svg]]

The distance between the two plates was decreased.

Which row of the table is correct?

|  | **Direction of electric field** | **Change in electric field strength** |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | P to Q | decreased |
| **B** | Q to P | decreased |
| **C** | P to Q | increased |
| **D** | Q to P | increased |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
