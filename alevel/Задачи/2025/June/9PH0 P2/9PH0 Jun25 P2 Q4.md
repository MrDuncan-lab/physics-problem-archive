> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Как называется Вселенная, которая достигнет максимального размера и начнёт сжиматься

Scientists believe that our universe is currently expanding. One possible fate is for the universe to reach a maximum size and then start to contract.

Which of the following descriptions of a universe with this fate is correct?

**A** a closed universe  
**B** a critical universe  
**C** a flat universe  
**D** an open universe

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**
