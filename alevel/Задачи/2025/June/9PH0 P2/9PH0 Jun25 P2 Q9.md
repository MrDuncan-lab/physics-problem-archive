> Повороты двух поляризаторов, при которых интенсивность на детекторе минимальна

Light from a lamp passes through two polarising filters, P$_{1}$ and P$_{2}$, as shown. The light intensity is recorded by a detector.

The filters initially have their planes of polarisation parallel.

![[9PH0-jun25-2-p6-f1.svg]]

Both filters are rotated in the same direction.

Which row of the table gives the changes that result in the minimum intensity of light at the detector?

|  | **Rotation of P$_{1}$ / rad** | **Rotation of P$_{2}$ / rad** |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$ | $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$ |
| **B** | $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$ | $\pi$ |
| **C** | $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$ | $\pi$ |
| **D** | $\dfrac{\pi}{2}$ | $\dfrac{3\pi}{2}$ |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
