> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Верен ли угол между красным и фиолетовым лучами в призме по отношению скоростей света

The image from the cover of a music album is represented below.

![[9PH0-jun25-2-p9-f1.svg]]

White light enters the prism at an angle of incidence of 45°. The light disperses and splits into a spectrum of colours as it enters the prism.

In this image the angle of refraction for violet light at the first boundary is 22°. The angle between the red light and the violet light is 11°.

Evaluate whether the angle between the red light and the violet light is accurate.

$\dfrac{\text{speed of violet light in prism}}{\text{speed of red light in prism}}$ = 0.99

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ (acceptable answers) | Указания (additional guidance) | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|---|
> |  | • Use of $n_{1}\sin\theta_{1} = n_{2}\sin\theta_{2}$, with $n_{1} = 1$ and $\theta_{1} = 45$ **(1)**<br>• Use of $n = \dfrac{c}{v}$ **(1)**<br>• Ratio of speeds = 0.69<br>Or angle for red light = 22.2° **(1)**<br>• 0.69 is less than 0.99, so angle is not accurate.<br>**Or** 22.2° is less than 33 degrees, so angle is not accurate. **(1)**<br>**Or** Comparison of calculated value with value in question and consistent conclusion | <u>Example of calculation</u><br>$n = \dfrac{c}{v}$, so $\dfrac{n_{\text{red}}}{n_{\text{violet}}} = \dfrac{v_{\text{violet}}}{v_{\text{red}}} = 0.99$<br>$n_{\text{air}}\sin 45^{\circ} = n_{\text{violet}}\sin 22^{\circ}$<br>$n_{\text{air}}\sin 45^{\circ} = n_{\text{violet}}\sin(22^{\circ} + 11^{\circ})$<br>$n_{\text{red}}\sin 33^{\circ} = n_{violet}\sin 22^{\circ}$<br>$\dfrac{n_{\text{red}}}{n_{\text{violet}}} = \dfrac{\sin 22^{\circ}}{\sin 33^{\circ}} = 0.688$<br>For MP4 allow a conclusion based on candidate’s calculated value of refractive index ratio<br>[Alternative method:<br>Use sin 45° / sin 22° to calculate $n_{\text{violet}}$ 1.89<br>Using ratio of velocities = 0.99, $n_{\text{red}}$ 1.87<br>Then calculated angle for red = 22.2°<br>The red and violet lines would be very close together as the angular dispersion is negligible so diagram is incorrect"<br>If students use this method and round to 2 sf then they will just get the angle for violet 22° ie same as red] | 4 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Whilst some candidates found this question challenging, a significant number of responses scored all four marks. This was for correctly determining the ratio of the speeds and making a comparison between the calculated ratio and the given ratio, or for calculating the angle and comparing it to the angle from the diagram. In order to score the fourth mark a mathematical comparison and a conclusion was necessary.
>
> Here we see the use of Snell’s law to calculate the refractive index of violet light. The candidate then uses this value with n=c/v to determine the speed of the violet light. They then use the ratio of the speeds to calculate the speed of the red light. The refractive index of red light is then determined and the candidate goes on to use this correctly with Snell’s law to determine the correct angle. They then make a mathematical comparison and state that the angle isn’t accurate. This scored all four marks.
>
> When the command word is evaluate always include a mathematical comparison between the calculated value and the reference value from the question. This needs to be supported by a conclusion.
>
> This response scored the first 3 marks, as the ratio of the speeds have been correctly calculated. However, the fourth mark was not awarded as no mathematical comparison is provided. To score the final mark 0.69 is not equal to 0.99 was required.
>
> Here we see the use of Snell’s law to correctly determine the refractive index for the violet light. But no further working has been provided after this. Therefore, this response scores 1 mark.
