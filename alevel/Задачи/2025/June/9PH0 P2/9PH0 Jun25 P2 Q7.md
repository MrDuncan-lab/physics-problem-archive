> Изменение вязкости жидкости и предельной скорости шарика при нагревании

A small ball bearing falls slowly through a liquid at terminal velocity. The temperature of the liquid is increased.

Which row of the table shows the change in viscosity of the liquid and the change in terminal velocity of the ball bearing?

|  | **Viscosity of liquid** | **Terminal velocity of ball bearing** |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | increases | increases |
| **B** | increases | decreases |
| **C** | decreases | increases |
| **D** | decreases | decreases |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
