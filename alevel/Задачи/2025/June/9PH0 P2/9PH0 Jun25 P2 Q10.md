> Разность фаз двух бегущих волн по графику смещения от расстояния

The diagram shows how displacement varies with distance for two waves, X and Y, at a particular instant. The waves are travelling to the right.

![[9PH0-jun25-2-p7-f1.svg]]

Which of the following statements describes the phase relationship between the two waves?

**A** X leads Y by 90°  
**B** X leads Y by 180°  
**C** Y leads X by 90°  
**D** Y leads X by 180°

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
