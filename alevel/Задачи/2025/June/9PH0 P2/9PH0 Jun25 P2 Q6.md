> Кинетическая и потенциальная энергия тележки на пружинах в равновесии и в крайней точке

A trolley is attached to two springs and set into horizontal oscillation, as shown.

![[9PH0-jun25-2-p4-f1.svg]]

The kinetic energy of the trolley is *E*$_{\text{k}}$, and the potential energy of the trolley is *E*$_{\text{p}}$.

Which row of the table is correct?

|  | **Trolley at equilibrium position** | **Trolley at maximum displacement** |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | *E*$_{\text{k}}$ is a maximum | *E*$_{\text{p}}$ is minimum |
| **B** | *E*$_{\text{k}}$ is a maximum | *E*$_{\text{p}}$ is a maximum |
| **C** | *E*$_{\text{k}}$ is zero | *E*$_{\text{p}}$ is a maximum |
| **D** | *E*$_{\text{k}}$ is zero | *E*$_{\text{p}}$ is minimum |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
