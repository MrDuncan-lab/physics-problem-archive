> Отношение расстояний до двух одинаковых звёзд по отношению их интенсивностей

X and Y are identical stars. When viewed from Earth the intensity of star X is four times the intensity of star Y.

Which of the following explanations is possible?

**A** X is twice as far away as Y.  
**B** X is four times as far away as Y.  
**C** Y is twice as far away as X.  
**D** Y is four times as far away as X.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
