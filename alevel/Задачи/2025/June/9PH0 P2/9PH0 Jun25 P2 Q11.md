> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Скорость и направление движения галактики Треугольника по смещению спектральной линии

A spectral line with wavelength 655.88 nm is detected on Earth from the Triangulum Galaxy. The wavelength of this spectral line measured using a laboratory source is 656.28 nm.

Calculate the velocity of the Triangulum Galaxy relative to the Earth.

Magnitude of velocity =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

Direction =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

> | Пункт | Ответ (acceptable answers) | Указания (additional guidance) | Баллы |

> |  | • Use of $\dfrac{\Delta\lambda}{\lambda} \approx \dfrac{v}{c}$ **(1)**<br>• $v = (-)\ 1.8\times10^{5}\ \text{m s}^{-1}$ **(1)**<br>• Towards the Earth (dependent upon correct numerator in Doppler equation) **(1)** | <u>Example of calculation</u><br>$\dfrac{(655.88 - 656.28)\ \text{nm}}{656.28\ \text{nm}} \approx \dfrac{v}{3.0\times10^{8}\ \text{m s}^{-1}}$<br>$v = \dfrac{(-)0.4\ \text{nm}}{656.28\ \text{nm}}\times3.0\times10^{8}\ \text{m s}^{-1} = -1.83\times10^{5}\ \text{m s}^{-1}$ | 3 |
