> Почему машинка соскальзывает с трассы на повороте: трение и центростремительная сила

The photograph shows the Petronas Twin Towers in Kuala Lumpur, which are the world’s tallest twin buildings.

![[9PH0-jun25-2-p18-f1.png]]

(Source: © saiko3p/Shutterstock)

**(a)** Each tower is supported by 16 cylindrical concrete columns.

Calculate the stress at the base of each cylindrical concrete column.

mass of each tower = 3.0 × 10$^{8}$ kg diameter of each column = 2.4 m **[5]**

Stress at the base of each column =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

**(b)** Tall buildings sway in the wind. The design of the Petronas Towers permits safe movement of the towers.

**(i)** The towers were designed to permit a maximum horizontal displacement of 0.90 m at the top of the towers. The natural frequency of each tower is 0.17 Hz.

Calculate the maximum velocity at the top of one of the towers when the tower is oscillating at its natural frequency. **[3]**

Maximum velocity at the top of the tower =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

**(ii)** The twin towers were linked together to improve the response of the building to strong winds.

The graph below shows how the maximum displacement *x* varies with the frequency *f* for a single tower and for two linked towers.

![[9PH0-jun25-2-p20-f1.svg]]

Explain the behaviour of a single tower compared with the behaviour of the linked towers. **[3]**
