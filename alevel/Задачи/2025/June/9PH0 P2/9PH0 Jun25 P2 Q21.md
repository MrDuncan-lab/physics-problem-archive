All objects with a temperature above absolute zero emit a spectrum of electromagnetic radiation.

**(a)** State what is meant by absolute zero. **[1]**

**(b)** The graph shows how the intensity of radiation emitted by the Earth varied with wavelength in 2020.

![[9PH0-jun25-2-p21-f1.svg]]

Explain the differences between this graph and a graph representing the emission of radiation by the Earth in 2050. Assume that the average global temperature increases over time. **[4]**

**(c)** At the end of the 19th century, Wien investigated the radiation emitted by hot objects and formulated a law.

The table shows data for some stars.

| **Star** | ***λ***$_{\text{max}}$ **/ nm** | ***T*** **/ K** |
|---|---|---|
| Bellatrix | 132 | 21 500 |
| Rigel | 245 | 11 600 |
| Sirius | 287 | 9910 |

Deduce whether Wien’s law is confirmed by this data. **[3]**

**(d)** The Hertzsprung–Russell diagram below shows part of the main sequence of stars. The star Sirius A is marked on the diagram.

![[9PH0-jun25-2-p23-f1.svg]]

On a website it states that the radius of Sirius A is twice the radius of the Sun.

Deduce whether this statement is correct.

radius of the Sun = 6.96 × 10$^{8}$ m surface temperature of the Sun = 5800 K **[5]**
