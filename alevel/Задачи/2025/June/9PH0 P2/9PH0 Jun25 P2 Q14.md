> Как меняется давление воздуха при изотермическом сжатии поршнем: молекулярное объяснение

> [!info] Пункт со звёздочкой (*) оценивает и связность ответа: логичную структуру, связи между утверждениями и обоснованные выводы (indicative content + structure).

Air molecules are trapped in a container by a piston, as shown.

![[9PH0-jun25-2-p11-f1.png]]

The position of the piston is adjusted so that the volume available to the air molecules is reduced. The temperature of the air stays constant.

![[9PH0-jun25-2-p11-f2.png]]

Explain how the pressure exerted by the air changes. You should refer to the motion of the air molecules.

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question involved extended writing to discuss how changing the volume of gas will affect the pressure. Candidates found the question accessible with the vast majority being able to score at least 2 marks. There were some excellent responses which scored all 6 marks; however, these were not seen frequently.
>
> It is worth noting that in order for IC1, IC2 and IC5 to be awarded, reference needed to be made to collisions between the molecules and the walls of the container or piston. In the case of IC2, this could only be scored if the response referred to an increase in the rate or frequency of the collisions. IC3 required reference to the velocity of the molecules being constant or that the change in momentum per collision is constant. IC4 was awarded for rate of change of momentum increases. IC5 was for increase in force of collisions against the walls or piston, this was often awarded. IC6 was for P=F/A and pressure increases was also commonly awarded.
>
> This response scores IC1 in line 2 and IC3 in line 3. IC2 is awarded in line 4 as the candidate has been clear that the collisions are between the molecules and the walls of the container earlier in the response. IC4 is scored in line 4 and 5. IC5 and IC6 are awarded in lines 6 and 7 respectively. There is a logical sequence to the response, so this scores all 6 marks.
>
> Here IC2 is awarded in line 7 for the idea that the frequency of collisions has increased. IC5 is awarded in line 9 and IC6 is awarded in line 10 and 11. This response scores 3 marks.
