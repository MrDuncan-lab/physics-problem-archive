> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Как точно измерить плотность стекла предметных стёкол: приборы и предосторожности

A student carries out measurements to determine the density of glass. The student has 20 glass microscope slides available.

The approximate dimensions of one slide are shown.

![[9PH0-jun17-3-p3-f1.svg]]

*x* $\approx$ 75 mm  
*y* $\approx$ 1 mm  
*z* $\approx$ 25 mm

**(a)** The density is calculated using the equation

density = $\dfrac{\text{mass}}{\text{volume}}$

Describe how the student can determine an accurate value for the density of the glass. Your answer should include the measuring instruments required. **[4]**

**(b)** State one precaution that the student should take to ensure the measurements are accurate. **[1]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ (acceptable answers) | Указания (additional guidance) | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | (a) | • Use a micrometer to measure y and/or z **(1)**<br>• Use Vernier/digital calipers to measure x and/or z **(1)**<br>• Mass of slide(s) measured using (top pan) balance/scales **(1)**<br>• Repeat and determine mean for at least one measurement **(1)** | **(Part (a) and (b) to be marked holistically**<br>**MP1** accept <u>digital</u> calipers for a single slide<br>Accept Vernier calipers if it is clear that the thickness of a number of slides is being measured.<br>To award both MP1 & 2, *x, y* & *z* must all be referred to.<br>MP4 can be awarded for a reference to averaging any of the measurements. | 4 |
> | (b) | Check zero error on micrometer/callipers/balance<br>**Or** measure *x/y/z* of slide in different places<br>**Or** measure thickness/mass of multiple slides **(1)** | Accept ‘tare’ for zero error check on balance | 1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question was chosen as a straightforward experimental determination that should have been familiar to most candidates. The question tests candidates’ knowledge of the need for specific measuring instruments to be used, as well as their understanding of simple techniques to improve the accuracy of the density value obtained. The question was marked holistically, so that a precaution given in the answer to part (a) or a reference to an averaging technique in part (b) would be credited.
>
> Most candidates specified both the quantity being measured and the instrument to use.
>
> It was expected that candidates would select a micrometer screw gauge to measure y and Vernier calipers to measure x and z.
>
> Given that many candidates will have used digital instruments to make such measurements it was accepted that digital calipers could be an alternative to using a micrometer to measure y. A number of candidates described using a ruler to measure at least one of the lengths, although it was not thought that this was not the most suitable instrument to select.
>
> In the context of the question, using a scale to measure the mass was just about acceptable, although scales would be a more usual description. Many candidates referred to a balance, with some calling it a top pan balance or an electronic balance. All these descriptions were accepted.
>
> Some candidates scored MP4 and MP5 together by describing a length measurement being repeated in several places on the slides and then a mean value being calculated.
>
> Examiner Comments This response makes all the required points and so gains full marks.
>
> Examiner Tip Consider the resolution of all the measuring instruments available to allow the most suitable instrument to be selected.
>
> Examiner Comments This response includes an ambiguous statement about determining a mean value, and so MP4 cannot be awarded. All the other marking points are stated and so this response is worth 4 marks.
