> Зачем ультразвук при обследовании плода посылают импульсами

Prenatal ultrasound scans are performed to produce an image of a fetus.

![[8PH0-jun17-2-p5-f1.png]]

The ultrasound wave is transmitted in pulses so that

**A** exposure of the patient to ultrasound radiation is reduced.  
**B** smaller distances can be measured.  
**C** the reflected pulse can be detected before the next pulse is transmitted.  
**D** there is less diffraction.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
