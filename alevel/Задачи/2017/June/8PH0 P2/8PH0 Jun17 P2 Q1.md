> Работа по растяжению пружины по графику удлинения от массы груза

A spring is hung vertically and masses are added to the lower end. The graph shows how the extension Δ*x* of the spring varies with the mass *m* added.

![[8PH0-jun17-2-p2-f1.svg]]

The work done in extending the spring can be expressed as

**A** *mg*Δ*x*  
**B** $\dfrac{mg}{\Delta x}$  
**C** $\dfrac{1}{2}$ *mg*Δ*x*  
**D** $\dfrac{mg}{2\Delta x}$

![[8PH0-jun17-2-p2-f2.svg]]

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
