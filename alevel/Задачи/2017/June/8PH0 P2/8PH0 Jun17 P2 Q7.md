> Условие когерентности двух световых волн

For two waves of light to be coherent the waves must

**A** always have a phase difference equal to 0.  
**B** oscillate in the same plane.  
**C** have a similar amplitude.  
**D** originate from one source.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
