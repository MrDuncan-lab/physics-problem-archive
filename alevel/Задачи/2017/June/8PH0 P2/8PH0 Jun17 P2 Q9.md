> Длина волны де Бройля электрона по его скорости

In the 1920s Louis de Broglie proposed that an electron could behave as a wave.

Calculate the wavelength of an electron that is travelling at a speed of 2.2 × 10$^{7}$ ms$^{-1}$. **[3]**

Wavelength =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

> | Пункт | Ответ (acceptable answers) | Указания (additional guidance) | Баллы |

> |  | • Use of $p = mv$ using mass of electron **(1)**<br>• Use of $\lambda = \dfrac{h}{p}$ **(1)**<br>• $\lambda = 3.3\times10^{-11}\ \text{m}$ **(1)** | <u>Example of Calculation</u><br>$\lambda = \dfrac{6.63\times10^{-34}\ \text{J s}}{9.11\times10^{-31}\ \text{kg}\times2.2\times10^{7}\ \text{m s}^{-1}}$<br>$\lambda = 3.3\times10^{-11}\ \text{m}$ | 3 |
