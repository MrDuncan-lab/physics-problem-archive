> Мощность излучения на солнечном элементе вдвое большего радиуса

The rate at which energy is transferred to a solar cell of radius *r* is *P*.

In the same conditions, the rate at which energy is transferred to a solar cell of radius 2*r* is given by

**A** $\dfrac{P}{4}$  
**B** $\dfrac{P}{2}$  
**C** 2*P*  
**D** 4*P*

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
