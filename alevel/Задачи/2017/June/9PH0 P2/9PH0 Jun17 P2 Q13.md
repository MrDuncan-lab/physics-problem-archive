> Стандартные свечи и предел метода параллакса; расстояния по красному смещению и закону Хаббла

The distance to astronomical objects relatively close to the Sun is determined using trigonometric parallax. For objects beyond a certain distance standard candles are used.

**(a)** State what is meant by a standard candle. **[1]**

**(b)** Explain why trigonometric parallax is not used beyond a certain distance. **[2]**

**(c)** Describe how distances too large for the use of standard candles can be determined. **[3]**
