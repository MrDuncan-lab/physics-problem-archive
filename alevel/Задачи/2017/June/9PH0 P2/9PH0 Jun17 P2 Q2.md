> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Удлинение одной пружины по данным для двух параллельных пружин

Two identical springs are arranged side by side as shown.

![[9PH0-jun17-2-p2-f1.svg]]

When a force of 20 N is applied, an extension of 8 cm is obtained.

A force of 5 N is applied to one of the springs on its own.

Which of the following is the extension obtained?

**A** 2 cm  
**B** 4 cm  
**C** 8 cm  
**D** 16 cm

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ (acceptable answers) | Указания (additional guidance) | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|---|
> |  | **B – 4 cm**<br>Incorrect Answers:<br>Correct method:<br>1. force on one spring is 20 N ÷ 2 = 10 N,<br>2. spring constant *k* = 10 N ÷ 8 cm = 1.25 N cm$^{-1}$,<br>3. extension = 5 N ÷ 1.25 N cm$^{-1}$ = 4 cm<br>A – 2 cm, omits step 1<br>C – 8 cm, applies 10 N in step 3<br>D – 16 cm, applies 20 N in step 3 |  | 1 |
