> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Напряжение на одном из двух последовательных резисторов

A series circuit consists of two resistors with resistances $R_{1}$ and $R_{2}$ and a battery of potential difference *V*.

Which of the following gives the potential difference across the resistor with resistance $R_{2}$?

**A** $\dfrac{R_{1}}{R_{2}}V$  
**B** $\dfrac{R_{2}}{R_{1}}V$  
**C** $\dfrac{R_{1}}{R_{1} + R_{2}}V$  
**D** $\dfrac{R_{2}}{R_{1} + R_{2}}V$

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question was answered well and candidates could answer by applying their knowledge of potential dividers, perhaps even from memory, or by using Ohm’s law with the total resistance of resistors in series. Candidates consistently find using such a relationship difficult, partially due to the selection of the correct data to be used. This was seen as a more straightforward task by many, with a slip by some, in selecting response C incorrectly due to the incorrect resistance as the numerator of the equation.
