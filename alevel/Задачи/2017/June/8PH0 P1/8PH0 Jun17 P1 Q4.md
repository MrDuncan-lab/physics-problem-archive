> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Заряд, протекший через фен за 3 минуты при известном токе

A hair dryer is used for 3 minutes. The operating current is 6 A.

What charge flows in this time?

**A** 0.03 C  
**B** 2 C  
**C** 18 C  
**D** 1080 C

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ (acceptable answers) | Указания (additional guidance) | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|---|
> |  | **D 1080 C**<br>Incorrect Answers:<br>A – current divided by time, with the time in seconds<br>B – current divided by time, with the time in minutes<br>C – correct formula of current × time but the time is in minutes and not seconds |  | 1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> While nearly all candidates knew to use the equation I = nqvA, those who were working through the question a little too quickly missed the unit conversion, selecting 18 C rather than 1080 C.
