> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Какой шаг вывода сопротивления последовательной цепи следует из сохранения заряда

A student is deriving an equation for the total resistance of resistors in series.

She writes the following steps but does not justify them.

Step 1 *V = V*$_{1}$ *+ V*$_{2}$ Step 2 *but V = IR* Step 3 *so IR = I*$_{1}$*R*$_{1}$ *+ I*$_{2}$*R*$_{2}$ Step 4 *but I = I*$_{1}$ *= I*$_{2}$ Step 5 *Therefore R = R*$_{1}$ *+ R*$_{2}$

Which step is justified using conservation of charge?

**A** Step 1  
**B** Step 2  
**C** Step 3  
**D** Step 4

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Derivations of the total resistance equation are one difference between the new AS specification and the legacy AS specification. The bringing together of the conservation of charge and energy for resistors in series and parallel is viewed by many candidates as a method that should be learnt by rote rather than through understanding. This question gave the candidates the steps involved but required them to identify which step used the conservation of charge. Step 4 used the conservation of charge; however, many candidates incorrectly chose step 3 and fewer chose step 1, the stages that are justified using the conservation of energy.
