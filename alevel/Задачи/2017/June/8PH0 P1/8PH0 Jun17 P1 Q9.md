> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Почему вольтметр не показывает ЭДС; изменение ЭДС и внутреннего сопротивления разряжающегося элемента

A torch uses a 1.5 V dry cell. Over time, the light intensity produced by the torch decreases as the cell ‘goes flat’.

**(a)** Student A sets up the following circuit in an attempt to measure the e.m.f. of a cell.

![[8PH0-jun17-1-p6-f1.svg]]

Explain why the voltmeter reading will **not** be the e.m.f. of the cell. **[2]**

**(b)** Student B correctly determined the e.m.f. of an unused cell as 1.63 V and its internal resistance as 1.15 Ω.

He repeated this after the cell had been used for several weeks. When a voltmeter was connected directly across the used cell, the reading was 1.36 V. A bulb of resistance 5.92 Ω was then connected across the used cell and the reading fell to 0.84 V.

Student A suggests that the cell goes flat as it is used because the e.m.f. decreases. Student B suggests it is because the internal resistance increases.

Determine whether either student is correct about the changes in the cell as it goes flat. **[5]**
