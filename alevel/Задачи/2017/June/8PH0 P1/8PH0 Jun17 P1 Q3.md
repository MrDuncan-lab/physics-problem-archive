A small plastic bead is released below the surface of water and rises towards the surface.

The diagrams show the forces acting on the bead as it rises with constant velocity.

*U* = upthrust, *D* = drag, *W* = weight

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> A common error with the movement of bubbles or objects through a fluid is to not take note of the direction of motion and place the drag force in the wrong direction. In this question while most candidates placed the drag as correctly acting downwards, the relative lengths of the arrowed lines were also of importance. The question referred to the bubble moving at a constant velocity so the total length of the downwards arrows should equal the length of the up thrust arrow to give a resultant force of zero.
