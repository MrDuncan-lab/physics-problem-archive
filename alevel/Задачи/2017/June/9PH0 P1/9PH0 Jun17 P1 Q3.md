> Сопротивление между точками X и Y для двух одинаковых резисторов

The two resistors shown each have resistance *R*.

![[9PH0-jun17-1-p2-f1.svg]]

Which expression gives the correct resistance between point X and point Y?

**A** $\dfrac{R}{2}$  
**B** *R*  
**C** 2*R*  
**D** *R*$^{2}$

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ (acceptable answers) | Указания (additional guidance) | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|---|
> |  | 2<br>A uses the parallel resistors equation $\dfrac{1}{R}_{\text{T}}$ = $\dfrac{1}{R}$ + $\dfrac{1}{R}$ = *R*<br>B assumes resistors in parallel have the same total R as each individual R<br>C is the addition of both resistances as if they were in series<br>D is the product of both resistances | $\dfrac{R}{2}$ | 1 |
