> Показание вольтметра на фоторезисторе в делителе напряжения в темноте

A light-dependent resistor (LDR) and resistor are connected in series with a 6V battery as shown. A voltmeter measures the potential difference across the LDR.

![[9PH0-jun17-1-p4-f1.svg]]

In daylight the voltmeter reads 3.0V.

Which reading is most likely if the circuit is now in total darkness?

**A** a little above 0 V  
**B** a little below 3 V  
**C** a little above 3 V  
**D** a little below 6 V

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
