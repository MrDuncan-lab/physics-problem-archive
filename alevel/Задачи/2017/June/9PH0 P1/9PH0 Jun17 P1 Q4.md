> Работа при перемещении тела с пола на скамью высотой h

An object of mass *m* is moved from point A on the ground, to point B on a bench of height *h* as shown in the diagram.

![[9PH0-jun17-1-p3-f1.svg]]

Which of the following is a correct expression for the work done on the object?

**A** $\dfrac{mgh}{\text{sin} \theta}$  
**B** $\dfrac{mgh}{\text{cos} \theta}$  
**C** *mgh*  
**D** *mgh*sin*θ*

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
