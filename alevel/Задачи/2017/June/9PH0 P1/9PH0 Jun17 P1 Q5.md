> Энергия конденсатора при удвоении напряжения

A capacitor of capacitance *C* is charged to a potential difference *V* by a power supply. The energy stored on the charged capacitor is *W*.

What would be the energy stored if the potential difference were 2*V*?

**A** $\dfrac{W}{4}$  
**B** $\dfrac{W}{2}$  
**C** 2*W*  
**D** 4*W*

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
