**Pearson Edexcel A level Physics (9PH0)** · June 2017 · Paper 1 — Advanced Physics I · вопрос 12

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Высота броска и дальность полёта ядра по начальной скорости, углу и времени полёта

The shot put is an Olympic field event. The distance for the women’s world record shot put is in excess of twenty two metres.

The shot is a metal ball, which is thrown from a standing position so that it lands on the ground a horizontal distance *R* away from the thrower.

![[9PH0-jun17-1-p8-f1.svg]]

![[9PH0-jun17-1-p8-f2.svg]]

A shot is thrown from a height *h* above the ground with an initial velocity of 14.2 m s$^{-1}$ at an angle of 37.0° to the horizontal. The time it takes for the shot to reach the ground is 1.98 s.

**(a)** Show that the vertical component of the initial velocity is about 8.6 m s$^{-1}$. **[2]**

**(b)** Calculate the height *h* above the ground from which the shot was thrown. **[3]**

*h* =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

**(c)** Calculate the horizontal distance *R* for this throw. **[3]**

*R* =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ (acceptable answers) | Указания (additional guidance) | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | (a) | • Use of *v* cos*θ* where *θ* is angle between *v* and vertical or sin equivalent **(1)**<br>• Component = 8.55 (m s$^{-1}$) **(1)** | <u>Example of calculation</u><br>Component = 14.2 cos53°<br>Component = 8.546 m s$^{-1}$ | 2 |
> | (b) | • Use of $s = ut + \frac{1}{2}at^{2}$ (ecf value from (a)) **(1)**<br>• Using $a = -9.81\ \text{m s}^{-2}$ **(1)**<br>• *h* = 2.3 m above ground **(1)** | If show that value used h = 2.2 m<br><u>Example of calculation</u><br>$s = 8.55\ \text{m s}^{-1}\times1.98\ \text{s} + \frac{1}{2}\times(-9.81\ \text{m s}^{-2})\times1.98^{2}\ \text{s}^{2}$<br>*s* = 16.93 m - 19.23 m<br>*s* = -2.3 m | 3 |
> | (c) | • Horizontal component velocity = *v* cos37° **(1)**<br>• *R* = horizontal component x time **(1)**<br>• *R* = 22.5 m **(1)** | <u>Example of calculation</u><br>$R = 14.2\cos 37^{\circ}\times1.98$<br>*R* = 22.5 m | 3 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(c)**
>
> This part of the question required candidates to calculate the range - a horizontal distance - and this was emphasized in the question.
>
> This is the product of the horizontal component of velocity and the time of flight as there is no acceleration in this direction.
>
> Examiner Comments The most common mistake was to use SUVAT and assume there is an acceleration in the horizontal direction as this example shows.
>
> This gained 1 mark for a correct horizontal component of velocity.
>
> Examiner Tip In straightforward projectile questions, remember that the vertical acceleration is g, and the horizontal acceleration is 0.
