> Суммарный импульс тележек после встречного столкновения

A trolley, mass 0.50 kg, has a speed of 2.0 m s$^{-1}$. A second trolley, mass 1.0 kg, has a speed of 2.0 m s$^{-1}$. The two trolleys are travelling in opposite directions and collide.

Which of the following could be a correct value of total momentum, in kg m s$^{-1}$, after the collision?

**A** 0  
**B** 1.0  
**C** 2.0  
**D** 3.0

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
