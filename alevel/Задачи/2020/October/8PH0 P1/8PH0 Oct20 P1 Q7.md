> Эквивалентное сопротивление параллельных резисторов 2R и R

Part of an electric circuit consists of two resistors. One resistor has a resistance 2*R* and the other resistor has a resistance *R* as shown.

![[8PH0-oct20-1-p5-f1.svg]]

Which of the following is the equivalent resistance of this combination?

**A** $\dfrac{R}{3}$  
**B** $\dfrac{2 R}{3}$  
**C** $\dfrac{3 R}{2}$  
**D** 3*R*

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
