> Прирост потенциальной энергии тела, поднятого по склону

An object of mass *m* is moved from the bottom to the top of a slope. The vertical height of the slope is *y*. The horizontal distance between the bottom and top of the slope is *x*.

![[8PH0-oct20-1-p2-f1.svg]]

Which of the following gives the gain of gravitational potential energy of the object as it moves from the bottom to the top of the slope?

**A** *mgx*  
**B** *mgy*  
**C** *mg(x* + *y)*  
**D** *mg* √*(x*$^{2}$ + *y*$^{2}$)

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
