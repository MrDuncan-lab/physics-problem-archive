A student wants to investigate how the resistance *R* of a negative temperature coefficient thermistor varies with its temperature *T*.

**(a)** Write a set of instructions that the student could follow and include one safety precaution. **[6]**

**(b)** The following graph was plotted from the results of another student’s experiment for the same topic.

![[8PH0-oct20-1-p13-f1.svg]]

Give two modifications to the experimental procedure that could improve these results. **[2]**

**(c)** Explain, in terms of particle behaviour, why the resistance of the thermistor decreases as temperature increases. **[4]**
