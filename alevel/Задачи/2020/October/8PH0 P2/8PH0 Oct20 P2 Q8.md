In an experiment to determine the wavelength of light, three values for the wavelength are obtained and the mean value calculated.

| Wavelength / nm | 466 | 448 | 473 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mean wavelength / nm | 462 |  |  |

What is the uncertainty, in nm, in these results?

**B** 18  
**C** 14  
**D** 11
