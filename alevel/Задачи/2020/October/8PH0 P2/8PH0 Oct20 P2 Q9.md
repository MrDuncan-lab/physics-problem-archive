> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Как поляризационным фильтром показать плоскость поляризации света экрана ноутбука

The light emitted from a laptop screen is plane polarised.

Explain how the plane of polarisation of the emitted light can be demonstrated using a polarising filter. **[3]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ (acceptable answers) | Указания (additional guidance) | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|---|
> |  | • Rotate filter or laptop and brightness of the screen varies **(1)**<br>**Either**<br>• When screen goes dark plane of polarised light is perpendicular to the plane of polarisation of the filter **(1)**<br>• As polarised light from the screen is absorbed by the filter. **(1)**<br>**Or**<br>• When screen is brightest plane of polarised light is parallel to the plane of polarisation of the filter **(1)**<br>• As polarised light from the screen is transmitted by the filter. **(1)** |  | 3 |
