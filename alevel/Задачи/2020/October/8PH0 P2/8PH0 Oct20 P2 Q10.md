> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Модуль Юнга проволоки графическим методом: что откладывать и как найти по наклону

A student carries out an investigation to measure the Young modulus of the material of a wire. He clamps one end of the wire and passes the other end over a pulley as shown.

![[8PH0-oct20-2-p7-f1.svg]]

The student measures the length and diameter of the wire. He hangs masses from the free end of the wire and completes a table with values of mass and extension.

Describe how the data collected should be used to determine the Young modulus using a graphical method. Your answer should include a sketch of the expected graph. **[4]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ (acceptable answers) | Указания (additional guidance) | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|---|
> |  | • Calculate force = *mg* **(1)**<br>• calculate the cross-sectional area $A = \pi\dfrac{d^{2}}{4}$ **(1)**<br>• x- and y- variables to produce a suitable straight-line graph **(1)**<br>• correct use of the gradient from their graph to determine *E* **(1)** | Accept $A = \pi r^{2}$ **and** $r = \dfrac{d}{2}$<br>Accept stress and strain only if definition of each is given | 4 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question assessed the candidates knowledge of a core practical so prior knowledge was expected. The question asked how the data should be used but this was missed by some who described the apparatus and method instead. Candidates need to remember the equation for the area of a circle. The easiest way to achieve MP3 and 4 was to sketch a graph which was suggested in the question.
