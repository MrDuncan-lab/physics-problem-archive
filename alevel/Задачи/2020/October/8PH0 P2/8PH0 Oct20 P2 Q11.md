> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Свет как поперечная электромагнитная волна; дифракция в узкой щели по принципу Гюйгенса

Light can be modelled as a wave.

**(a)** Describe how light is transmitted as a transverse wave. **[2]**

**(b)** Diffraction provides evidence for the wave nature of light.

Use Huygens' construction to describe what happens to light waves after passing through a narrow gap. **[3]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ (acceptable answers) | Указания (additional guidance) | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | (a) | • Light is an electromagnetic wave<br>**Or** light is oscillations of electric and magnetic fields **(1)**<br>• Oscillations are perpendicular to the direction of energy transfer **(1)** |  | 2 |
> | (b) | • The wave spreads out (after passing through a gap) **(1)**<br>• Each point on the wave acts as a source of secondary wavelets **(1)**<br>• That interfere/superpose **(1)** |  | 3 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)**
>
> The description of transverse waves needs to be revised. Answers demonstrated little knowledge of a transverse wave as an electromagnetic wave consisting of oscillations of electric and magnetic fields.
>
> **(b)**
>
> Candidates knew that the wave spreads out but need to apply Huygens law clearly to explain why, using appropriate terminology for secondary wavelets.
