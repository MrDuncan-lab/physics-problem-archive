> Выражение для длины волны де Бройля электрона

An electron travels at a velocity *v*.

Which of the following is the correct expression for the de Broglie wavelength *λ* of the electron?

**A** *λ* = $\dfrac{3.00 \times 10^{8}}{9.11 \times 10^{-31} \times v}$  
**B** *λ* = $\dfrac{9.11 \times 10^{-31} \times v}{3.00 \times 10^{8}}$  
**C** *λ* = $\dfrac{6.63 \times 10^{-34}}{9.11 \times 10^{-31} \times v}$  
**D** *λ* = $\dfrac{9.11 \times 10^{-31} \times v}{6.63 \times 10^{-34}}$

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
