> Интенсивность излучения на круглой площадке радиуса r

A light source radiates a power *P* onto a surface, covering a circular area of radius *r*.

Which of the following is the correct expression for the intensity *I* of the radiation at the surface?

**A** *I* = $\dfrac{P}{\pi r^{2}}$  
**B** *I* = *Pπr*$^{2}$  
**C** *I* = $\dfrac{P}{2 \pi r}$  
**D** *I* = 2*Pπr*

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**
