> Светимость Альдебарана по закону Вина и закону Стефана–Больцмана

Analogue ammeters were used before digital meters became widely available. The analogue ammeter shown will measure a maximum current of 1.0 mA and has a resistance of 18 Ω.

![[9PH0-oct20-1-p8-f1.png]]

(Source: © David J. Green/Alamy Stock Photo)

The analogue ammeter can be adapted to measure a larger current by adding a resistor, known as a shunt, in parallel with the ammeter. The arrangement is shown below. The analogue ammeter is represented by the 18 Ω resistor.

![[9PH0-oct20-1-p8-f2.svg]]

The maximum current through the 18 Ω resistor remains as 1.0 mA.

**(a)** Show that the shunt would need to have a resistance of about 0.01 Ω to adapt this ammeter to read up to a maximum current of 2.0 A. **[3]**

**(b)** A shunt of this resistance was usually made from Manganin wire.

Calculate the length of Manganin wire of radius 0.95 mm required to make this shunt.

resistivity of Manganin = 4.55 × 10$^{-7}$ Ω m **[3]**

Length =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ (acceptable answers) | Указания (additional guidance) | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | (a) | • use of $V = IR$ for the ammeter **(1)**<br>• calculates current through the shunt **(1)**<br>• Shunt resistance = $9.0\times10^{-3}$ (Ω) (dependent on MP2) **(1)**<br>Alternative if using $1/R$ rule for parallel resistors<br>• use of $V = IR$ for the ammeter<br>• Calculates equivalent resistance of the circuit<br>• Uses $1/R$ rule to calculate shunt resistance = $9.0\times10^{-3}$ (Ω) | <u>Example of calculation</u>:<br>$V = 1.0\times10^{-3}\ \text{A}\times18\ \Omega = 0.018\ \text{V}$<br>$I = 2.0\ \text{A} - 0.001\ \text{A} = 1.999\ \text{A}$<br>$R = \dfrac{0.018\ \text{V}}{1.999\ \text{A}} = 9.0\times10^{-3}\ \Omega$<br>Alternative:<br>$V = 1.0\times10^{-3}\ \text{A}\times18\ \Omega = 0.018\ \text{V}$<br>$R_{T} = \dfrac{0.018\ \text{V}}{2\ \text{A}} = 0.009\ \Omega$<br>$\dfrac{1}{0.009} = \dfrac{1}{18} + \dfrac{1}{R_{S}}$<br>$R_{\text{S}} = 9.0\times10^{-3}\ \Omega$ | 3 |
> | (b) | • Use of $\pi r^{2}$ **(1)**<br>• Use of $R = \rho l/A$ **(1)**<br>• Length = 0.056 m **(1)** | <u>Example of calculation</u>:<br>Area $= \pi(0.95\times10^{-3}\ \text{m})^{2} = 2.8(4)\times10^{-6}\ \text{m}^{2}$<br>$l = \dfrac{9.0\times10^{-3}\ \Omega\times2.8(4)\times10^{-6}\ \text{m}^{2}}{4.55\times10^{-7}\ \Omega\ \text{m}} = 0.056\ \text{m}$<br>“Show that” value $l$ = 0.062 m | 3 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)**
>
> This question examined parallel circuits. The most common error was to assume the current in the shunt was 2 A rather than to subtract 1mA (the current through the 18 Ω resistor). These answers could still gain credit if there was a calculation of potential difference across the pair of resistors. Some responses were a jumble of numbers with little or no method or explanation.
>
> **(b)**
>
> This calculation, using resistivity, was well answered by almost all candidates.
