> Энергия упругой деформации пружины под грузом 2 Н

The intensity of light incident on a light dependent resistor (LDR) can vary both its electrical resistance *R* and the number of charge carriers per unit volume *n*. The light intensity on an LDR is increased.

Which row of the table describes the effect on *R* and *n*?

**R n**

| **A** | decreases | decreases |
|---|---|---|
| **B** | decreases | increases |
| **C** | increases | decreases |
| **D** | increases | increases |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
