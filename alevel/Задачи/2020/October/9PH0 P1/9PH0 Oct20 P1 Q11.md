# Question 11 (5 marks)

**Pearson Edexcel A level Physics (9PH0)** · October 2020 · Paper 1 — Advanced Physics I · вопрос 11  
Источник: [[_pdf/2020/9PH0_01_qp_oct20.pdf#page=7|условие, стр. 7]] · [[_pdf/2020/9PH0_01_ms_oct20.pdf#page=8|схема оценивания, стр. 8]] · [[_pdf/2020/9PH0_01_er_oct20.pdf#page=3|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Пороговая частота фотоэффекта, противоречие волновой теории, понятие фотона

A uniform paving slab is to be used as a garden step.

**(a)** State what is meant by the centre of gravity of an extended body.

. **[1]**

**(b)** The paving slab has a weight of 310 N and a length of 90 cm and will be supported at two points, P and Q, as shown. The distance between P and Q will be 75 cm.

![[9PH0-oct20-1-p7-f1.png]]

This might be unsafe because a person who places all their weight at the end of the slab might tip the slab.

A person of mass 70 kg stands at the end of the slab.

Deduce whether the slab will tip. **[4]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ (acceptable answers) | Указания (additional guidance) | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | (a) | • the point through which the weight (of a body) acts **(1)**<br>**Or** The point around which the mass is equally distributed<br>**Or** if supported at/below this point the body would be in equilibrium | alt. the point at which the entire mass can be assumed to be located. | 1 |
> | (b) | • take one moment around P **(1)**<br>• Use of $W = mg$ **(1)**<br>• Use of weight/mass of paving slab at centre point **(1)**<br>• It would tip because the moment of the weight of person is 103 N m / 10300 N cm and is larger than the moment of the weight of slab 93 N m / 9300 N cm **(1)** | either person or paving slab<br>e.g. distance to P = 30 cm<br><u>Example of calculation</u>:<br>Moment of weight of person $= 70\ \text{kg}\times9.81\ \text{N kg}^{-1}\times0.15\ \text{m} = 103\ \text{N m}$<br>Moment of weight of paving slab $= 310\ \text{N}\times0.30\ \text{m} = 93\ \text{N m}$ | 4 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)**
>
> This question was answered well by many candidates who generally understood that this is the point through which the weight acts. Some answers incorrectly made references to mass acting at the centre which is a special case or suggested mass acts through the point, inferring that mass is a vector
>
> **(b)**
>
> This question required candidates to take moments around P and recognising that the weight of the slab acts at its centre. A few candidates tried to take moments around Q. Some responses didn’t recognise that the weight of the slab would be at its centre and tried to distribute it either side of P. In general, many responses demonstrated a good grasp of moments.
