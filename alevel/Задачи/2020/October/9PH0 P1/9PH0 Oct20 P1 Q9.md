The blade of a lawnmower rotates at a speed of 50 revolutions per second.

Which of the following is the angular speed of the blade in rads s$^{-1}$?

**A** 7.96  
**B** 15.9  
**C** 157  
**D** 314

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
