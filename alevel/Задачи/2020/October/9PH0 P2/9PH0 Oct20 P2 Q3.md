> Яркость ламп в цепи до и после замыкания второго ключа

When a force *F* is applied to a spring with stiffness *k*, the elastic potential energy stored is *E*.

What is the elastic potential energy stored when a force 2*F* is applied to a spring with stiffness 2*k*?

**A** $\dfrac{E}{2}$  
**B** *E*  
**C** 2*E*  
**D** 8*E*

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
