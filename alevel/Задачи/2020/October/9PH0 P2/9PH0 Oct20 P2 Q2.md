Which line of the table correctly identifies the process that would increase stability for nuclei in the positions indicated by X and Y?

|  | **X** | **Y** |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | nuclear fission | nuclear fission |
| **B** | nuclear fission | nuclear fusion |

| **D** | nuclear fusion | nuclear fusion |
