The diagram shows a source of sound waves and an observer.

source observer

Which row of the table shows a situation which would result in a decrease in the frequency of sound observed?

|  | **Source** | **Observer** |
|---|---|---|
|  | moves to the right at 20 m s$^{-1}$ | moves to the left at 20 m s$^{-1}$ |
|  | moves to the right at 20 m s$^{-1}$ | moves to the right at 20 m s$^{-1}$ |
| **C** | moves to the right at 20 m s$^{-1}$ | stationary |
| **D** | stationary | moves to the right at 20 m s$^{-1}$ |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
