> Излучение неоновой лампы при разряде и определение металла по фотоэффекту

The acceleration of free fall at the surface of the Earth is 9.81 m s$^{-2}$. The mass of the Earth is *M* and the diameter of the Earth is *D*.

Which of the following gives the acceleration of free fall, in m s$^{-2}$, at the surface of a

planet with diameter $\dfrac{D}{2}$ and mass $\dfrac{M}{9}$ ?

**A** $\dfrac{9.81 \times 2}{9}$  
**B** $\dfrac{9.81 \times 4}{9}$  
**C** $\dfrac{9.81 \times 2}{3}$  
**D** $\dfrac{9.81 \times 9}{4}$

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ (acceptable answers) | Указания (additional guidance) | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|---|
> |  | The only correct answer is B because *mg* = *GMm*/*r*$^{2}$ so acceleration of free fall is proportional to mass / diameter$^{2}$ = *g*(*M*/9)/(*D*/2)$^{2}$ = $\dfrac{9.81\times4}{9}$ |  | 1 |
