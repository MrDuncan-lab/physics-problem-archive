> Напряжение на клеммах батареи с внутренним сопротивлением и яркость лампы при замыкании ключа

*Общее условие вопросов 3 и 4:*

**Questions 3 and 4 refer to the following information.**

A horizontal force *F* is applied to a horizontal spring, fixed at one end.

The stiffness of the spring is *k* and the elastic strain energy stored is *E*.

A second, identical spring is added and the same force is applied to the combination of springs, as shown.

![[9PH0-nov21-2-p3-f1.png]]

---

What is the stiffness of the combination of springs?

**A** $\dfrac{k}{2}$  
**B** *k*  
**C** 2*k*  
**D** 4*k*

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> | Пункт | Ответ (acceptable answers) | Указания (additional guidance) | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|---|
> |  | The only correct answer is A because the extension is doubled and the force for each spring is the same and *k* = *F/x* |  | 1 |
