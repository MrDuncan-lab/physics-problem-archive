> График кинетической энергии от смещения при гармонических колебаниях

A capacitor is connected to a power supply and charged to a potential difference *V*$_{0}$.

The graph shows how the potential difference *V* across the capacitor varies with the charge *Q* on the capacitor.

![[9PH0-nov21-1-p7-f1.svg]]

At a potential difference *V*$_{0}$ a small charge ∆*Q* is added to the capacitor. This results in a small increase in potential difference ∆*V* across the capacitor.

Which of the following gives the approximate increase in energy stored on the capacitor due to this extra charge?

**A** ∆*V* × ∆*Q*  
**B** $\dfrac{\Delta V \times \Delta Q}{2}$  
**C** *V*$_{0}$ × ∆*Q*  
**D** $\dfrac{V_{0} \times \Delta Q}{2}$

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
