> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Модуль Юнга металлического стержня по силе, площади сечения, длине и удлинению

The following measurements were made to determine the Young modulus of a metal bar.

original length of bar = 0.50 m  
area of cross section = 4.5 × 10$^{-4}$ m$^{2}$  
tensile force applied to bar = 36 000 N  
extension of bar = 2.0 × 10$^{-4}$ m

Which of the following gives the Young modulus of the metal?

**A** $\dfrac{36000 \times 0.50}{4.5 \times 10^{-4} \times 2.0 \times 10^{-4}}$  
**B** $\dfrac{4.5 \times 10^{-4} \times 2.0 \times 10^{-4}}{36000 \times 0.50}$  
**C** $\dfrac{36000 \times 2.0 \times 10^{-4}}{4.5 \times 10^{-4} \times 0.50}$  
**D** $\dfrac{4.5 \times 10^{-4} \times 0.50}{36000 \times 2.0 \times 10^{-4}}$

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**
