> Показатель преломления стекла по предельному углу 35°

*Общее условие вопросов 2 и 3:*

**Questions 2 and 3 refer to the following information.**

A ray of light, in air, is incident on the edge of a triangular glass prism as shown. The critical angle for a light ray meeting a glass to air boundary is 35°.

![[8PH0-nov21-2-p3-f1.svg]]

---

Which of the following gives the value of the refractive index of the glass?

**A** sin 35  
**B** $\dfrac{1}{\sin 35}$  
**C** $\sin^{-1}\left(\dfrac{1}{35}\right)$  
**D** $\dfrac{1}{\sin^{-1}\left(\dfrac{1}{35}\right)}$

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
