> Сила реакции пола при приземлении гимнастки; роль сгибания колен; диаграмма напряжение–деформация кости

Some sports place high stresses on the bones in the body, which can result in injury.

**(a)** A gymnast of mass 45 kg dismounts from a beam. Her centre of mass is displaced through 1.6 m vertically before her feet touch the ground. As she lands, the bones in the lower part of her legs experience a force from the ground. The time between hitting the ground and coming to rest is 0.90 s.

**(i)** Calculate the mean force from the ground on the gymnast. **[4]**

Mean force from the ground =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

**(ii)** Explain how bending both knees when landing helps the gymnast prevent

an injury. **[3]**

**(b)** The graph shows how stress varies with strain for normal bone and for unhealthy bone.

![[8PH0-nov21-2-p22-f1.svg]]

Describe how the graph shows that unhealthy bone under stress is more likely to break than normal bone. **[3]**
