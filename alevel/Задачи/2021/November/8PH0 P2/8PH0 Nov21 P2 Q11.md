> Длина волны поглощённого излучения по диаграмме уровней; испускание более длинных волн при переходах

An electron in its ground state absorbs electromagnetic radiation of wavelength *λ*. The energy level diagram represents the resulting energy transition of the electron.

![[8PH0-nov21-2-p9-f1.svg]]

**(a)** Calculate the wavelength of radiation absorbed by the electron. **[3]**

Wavelength =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

**(b)** The electron eventually returns to its ground state.

Explain, with reference to the energy level diagram, how this may result in the emission of radiation with a longer wavelength than *λ*. **[3]**
