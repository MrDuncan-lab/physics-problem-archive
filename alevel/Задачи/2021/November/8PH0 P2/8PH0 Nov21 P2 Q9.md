> Частота звука по осциллограмме и развёртке; вид сигнала при меньшей развёртке

In an investigation to determine the speed of sound in air, a student sets up an oscilloscope to display the waveform of a sound wave as shown.

![[8PH0-nov21-2-p7-f1.png]]

The timebase is set to 25 µs / division.

**(a)** Determine the frequency of the sound wave. **[2]**

Frequency =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

**(b)** The student sets the timebase on the oscilloscope to a lower value per division.

Describe any changes to the appearance of the waveform on the screen. **[1]**
