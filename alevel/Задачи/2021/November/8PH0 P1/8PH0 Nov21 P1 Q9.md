> Положение опоры однородного стержня в равновесии по правилу моментов

A uniform rigid rod AB of length 1.50 m has a weight *W* of 6.5 N. A force of 3.5 N applied at A balances the rod on a pivot as shown.

![[8PH0-nov21-1-p6-f1.svg]]

![[8PH0-nov21-1-p6-f2.svg]]

Calculate the distance of the pivot from A when the rod is in equilibrium. **[2]**

Distance of pivot from A =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ (acceptable answers) | Указания (additional guidance) | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|---|
> |  | • Use moment = *F x* **(1)**<br>• *x* = 0.49 m **(1)** | <u>Example of calculation</u><br>$6.5\ \text{N}\times(0.75\ \text{m} - x) = 3.5\ \text{N}\ x$<br>$4.875\ \text{Nm} - 6.5\ x = 3.5\ x$<br>$x = \dfrac{4.875\ \text{Nm}}{10\ \text{N}} = 0.488\ \text{m}$ | 2 |
