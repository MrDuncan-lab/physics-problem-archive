> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Что не растёт при нагреве медного провода током: колебания ионов, концентрация электронов, столкновения

A constant current maintained in a copper wire causes the temperature of the wire to increase.

Which of the following does **not** increase?

**A** amplitude of vibration of the lattice ions  
**B** number of conduction electrons per unit volume  
**C** rate of collision of conduction electrons with lattice ions  
**D** rate of energy transfer from conduction electrons to lattice ions

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
