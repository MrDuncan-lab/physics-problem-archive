> Напряжение на одном из двух последовательных резисторов (делитель напряжения)

Two resistors of resistance *R*$_{1}$ and *R*$_{2}$ are connected to a battery as shown. The terminal potential difference of the battery is *V*.

![[8PH0-nov21-1-p4-f2.svg]]

Which of the following gives the potential difference across the resistor of resistance *R*$_{1}$?

**A** $\dfrac{R_{1}}{R_{2}}\times V$  
**B** $\dfrac{R_{1}}{R_{1}+R_{2}}\times V$  
**C** $\dfrac{R_{2}}{R_{1}}\times V$  
**D** $\dfrac{R_{2}}{R_{1}+R_{2}}\times V$

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
