The resistivity of a metal is an important property of wire used in an electric circuit.

**(a)** A student carried out an experiment to determine the resistivity of a type of wire. He used a micrometer to measure the diameter *d* of the wire.

![[8PH0-nov21-1-p10-f1.png]]

(Source: © Viktor Chursin/Shutterstock)

He recorded the following values.

| ***d***$_{1}$ **/ mm** | ***d***$_{2}$ **/ mm** | ***d***$_{3}$ **/ mm** | ***d***$_{4}$ **/ mm** |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1.40 | 1.44 | 1.42 | 1.41 |

**(i)** Calculate the percentage uncertainty in the mean diameter of the wire. **[3]**

% uncertainty in mean diameter of wire =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

**(ii)** The student used an ohmmeter to measure the resistance *R* of a 1.65 m length of

the wire.

He looked up the resistivity values of some materials.

| **Material** | **Titanium** | **Constantan** | **Stainless Steel** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Resistivity / 10**$^{-7}$ **Ω m** | 4.2 | 4.7 | 6.9 |

Identify the material of the wire.

*R* = 0.72 Ω **[3]**

**(b)** Nichrome wire is often used in heating elements. Nichrome wire is used to make a coil for a 65 W mains powered heater. The nichrome wire has a resistance per metre of 87.5 Ω m$^{-1}$ .

Calculate the length of wire required.

potential difference across the coil = 230 V **[3]**

Length of wire required =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ (acceptable answers) | Указания (additional guidance) | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | • Calculation of mean **(1)**<br>• Use of half range **Or** maximum difference from the mean **(1)**<br>• % uncertainty = 1.4% **(1)** | <u>Example of calculation</u><br>$\bar{d} = \dfrac{(1.40+1.44+1.42+1.41)\ \text{mm}}{4} = 1.42\ \text{mm}$<br>$\%U = \dfrac{(1.44 - 1.40)\ \text{mm}/2}{1.42\ \text{mm}}\times100\% = 1.41\%$ | 3 |
> | (a)(ii) | • Use of $A = \pi r^{2}$ **(1)**<br>• Use of $R = \dfrac{\rho l}{A}$ **(1)**<br>• $\rho = 6.9\times10^{-7}\ (\Omega\ \text{m})$, so wire is made from stainless steel **(1)** | <u>Example of calculation</u><br>$A = \pi\left(\dfrac{1.42\times10^{-3}\ \text{m}}{2}\right)^{2} = 1.58\times10^{-6}\ \text{m}^{2}$<br>$\rho = \dfrac{0.72\ \Omega\times1.58\times10^{-6}\ \text{m}^{2}}{1.65\ \text{m}} = 6.9\times10^{-7}\ \Omega\ \text{m}$ | 3 |
> | (b) | • Use of $P = \dfrac{V^{2}}{R}$ **(1)**<br>• Use of resistance per unit length **(1)**<br>• $l = 9.3$ m **(1)** | <u>Example of calculation</u><br>$P = \dfrac{(230\ \text{V})^{2}}{65\ \text{W}} = 813.8\ \text{m}$<br>$l = \dfrac{813.8\ \text{m}}{87.5\ \Omega\ \text{m}^{-1}} = 9.30\ \text{m}$ | 3 |
