Two resistors are connected as shown.

![[8PH0-nov21-1-p7-f1.svg]]

**(a)** Show that the resistance of the combination is about 70 Ω. **[2]**

**(b)** This resistor combination is connected to a battery of e.m.f. *ε* and internal resistance *r*.

![[8PH0-nov21-1-p7-f2.svg]]

The switch is closed for 5 minutes.

Calculate the energy dissipated in the resistor combination.

*ε* = 9.0 V *r* = 2.5 Ω **[4]**

Energy dissipated in resistor combination =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

> | Пункт | Ответ (acceptable answers) | Указания (additional guidance) | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | (a) | • Use of $\dfrac{1}{R} + \dfrac{1}{R_{1}} = \dfrac{1}{R_{2}}$ **(1)**<br>• $R = 72\ (\Omega)$ **(1)** | <u>Example of calculation</u><br>$\dfrac{1}{R} = \dfrac{1}{120\ \Omega} + \dfrac{1}{180\ \Omega} = 0.0139\ \Omega^{-1}$<br>$\therefore R = \dfrac{1}{0.0139\ \Omega^{-1}} = 72.0\ \Omega$ | 2 |
> | (b) | • Use of $V = IR$ **(1)**<br>• Use of $P = I^{2}R$ **(1)**<br>• Use of $P = \dfrac{E}{t}$ **(1)**<br>• $E = 320$ J ECF from (a) [show that answer gives 323 J] **(1)**<br>**Or**<br>• Use of $V = IR$ to find *I* **(1)**<br>• Use of $V = IR$ to find terminal pd **(1)**<br>• Use of $W = VIt$ **(1)**<br>• $E = 320$ J ECF from (a) [show that answer gives 323 J] **(1)** | <u>Example of calculation</u><br>$I = \dfrac{9\ \text{V}}{(72+2.5)\ \Omega} = 0.121\ \text{A}$<br>$P = (0.121\ \text{A})^{2}\times72\ \Omega = 1.05\ \text{W}$<br>$E = 1.05\ \text{W}\times300\ \text{s} = 316\ \text{J}$ | 4 |
