> Скорость роста потенциальной энергии груза, равномерно поднимаемого мотором

A motor is used to lift an object as shown. The object is raised through a vertical height of 75 cm at a constant speed of 0.40 m s$^{-1}$.

![[8PH0-nov21-1-p5-f1.svg]]

Which of the following gives the rate of increase of potential energy of the object in watts?

**A** 0.25 × 9.81 × 0.40  
**B** 0.25 × 0.75  
**C** 0.25 × 9.81 × 0.75  
**D** 0.5 × 0.25 × (0.40)$^{2}$

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**
