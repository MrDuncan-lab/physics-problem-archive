> Скорость фигуриста после толчка партнёрши; объяснение по законам Ньютона; рост кинетической энергии

Two ice skaters are gliding across the horizontal ice surface at an ice rink.

![[8PH0-nov21-1-p12-f1.png]]

(Source: © ITAR-TASS News Agency/Alamy Stock Photo)

**(a)** Initially the skaters move together with a speed of 5.6 m s$^{-1}$.

The male skater pushes the female skater forwards. After being pushed, she has a forward speed of 7.5 m s$^{-1}$.

Calculate the speed of the male skater immediately after pushing the female skater forwards.

mass of male skater = 66 kg mass of female skater = 52 kg **[3]**

Speed of male skater =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

**(b)** Explain why the male skater experiences a change in his velocity when he pushes the female skater forwards. You should make reference to Newton’s laws of motion in your explanation. **[4]**

**(c)** When the male skater pushes the female skater forwards, the total kinetic energy of the skaters increases.

Explain why kinetic energy is not conserved in this interaction. **[2]**
