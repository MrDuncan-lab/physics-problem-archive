> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Ускорение по касательной и перемещение по площади под графиком v(t) парашютиста

A skydiver made a skydive from a plane.

![[8PH0-nov21-1-p8-f1.png]]

(Source: © Sky Antonio/Shutterstock)

The graph shows how the velocity *v* of the skydiver varied with time *t*, from the instant she left the plane to the instant just before the parachute opened.

![[8PH0-nov21-1-p8-f2.svg]]

**(a)** Determine the acceleration of the skydiver when *t* = 4.0 s. **[3]**

Acceleration of skydiver =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

**(b)** Determine an approximate value for the displacement of the skydiver over the first 16.0 s of the skydive. **[3]**

Displacement of skydiver =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ (acceptable answers) | Указания (additional guidance) | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | (a) | • Tangent drawn at t = 4.0 s **(1)**<br>• Gradient of tangent determined **(1)**<br>• a in the range 1.4 – 1.6 m s$^{-2}$ **(1)** | <u>Example of calculation</u><br>$a = \dfrac{(35.0-25.0)\ \text{m s}^{-1}}{(10.0-0.0)\ \text{s}} = 1.50\ \text{m s}^{-2}$ | 3 |
> | (b) | • Use of displacement = area under line **(1)**<br>• 1 square = 20 m<br>**Or** area divided into regular shapes **(1)**<br>• *s* in the range 410 – 430 m **(1)** | <u>Example of calculation</u><br>Number of squares = 42<br>1 square = 2 s × 5 m s$^{-1}$ = 10 m<br>s = 42 × 10 m = 420 m | 3 |
