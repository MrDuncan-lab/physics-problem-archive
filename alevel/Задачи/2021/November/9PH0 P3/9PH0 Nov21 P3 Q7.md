> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Поле между пластинами, ЭДС источника с большим внутренним сопротивлением, пробой воздуха

A student connected the output from a source of alternating potential difference (p.d.) to a series resistor combination.

She connected an oscilloscope across the 150 Ω resistor as shown.

![[9PH0-nov21-3-p14-f1.svg]]

**(a)** The trace obtained on the oscilloscope is shown below.

![[9PH0-nov21-3-p14-f2.svg]]

**(i)** Determine the peak p.d. across the 150 Ω resistor.

*y*-sensitivity of oscilloscope = 2.0 V per division **[2]**

Peak p.d. across 150 Ω resistor =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

**(ii)** Calculate the root mean square (r.m.s.) value of the current in the circuit. **[3]**

r.m.s. value of current =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

**(iii)** Calculate the power dissipated in the circuit. **[3]**

Power dissipated in circuit =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

**(b)** Another student suggested that a voltmeter would be more accurate than using an oscilloscope to determine the magnitude of the p.d.

Comment on this suggestion. **[3]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ (acceptable answers) | Указания (additional guidance) | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | • Use of $y$-sensitivity value **(1)**<br>• $V_{0} = 4.0\ \text{V}$ **(1)** | <u>Example of calculation:</u><br>$V_{0} = 2\times2.0\ \text{V} = 4.0\ \text{V}$ | 2 |
> | (a)(ii) | • Use of $I = \dfrac{V}{R}$ **(1)**<br>• Use of $I_{\text{rms}} = \dfrac{I_{0}}{\sqrt{2}}$<br>**Or** use of $V_{\text{rms}} = \dfrac{V_{0}}{\sqrt{2}}$ **(1)**<br>• $I_{\text{rms}} = 0.019\ \text{A}$ ECF from(i) **(1)** | <u>Example of calculation</u><br>$I_{0} = \dfrac{4.0\ \text{V}}{150\ \Omega} = 0.0267\ \text{A}$<br>$I_{\text{rms}} = \dfrac{0.0267\ \text{A}}{\sqrt{2}} = 0.0189\ \text{A}$ | 3 |
> | (a)(iii) | • Use of $R = R_{1} + R_{2}$ **(1)**<br>• Use of $P = I^{2}R$ (or other valid power equation) **(1)**<br>• $P = 0.096\ \text{W}$ ECF from(i) and (ii) **(1)** | <u>Example of calculation:</u><br>$R = 150\ \Omega + 120\ \Omega = 270\ \Omega$<br>$P = I^{2}R = (0.019\ \text{A})^{2}\times270\ \Omega = 0.0964\ \text{A}$ | 3 |
> | (b) | **MAX 3**<br>• Voltmeter must measure alternating p.d.s<br>**Or** voltmeter would indicate zero for a.c. **(1)**<br>• A.C. voltmeter would give an r.m.s. p.d. directly **(1)**<br>• Voltmeter may draw current and affect the circuit it was connected to<br>**Or** oscilloscope would have little effect on the circuit it was connected to **(1)**<br>• Accuracy would depend upon the calibration of the voltmeter **(1)**<br>• A (digital) voltmeter would give better resolution than measuring trace height on an oscilloscope. **(1)** | Accept “voltmeter reading would change too fast to measure” | 3 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)(i)**
>
> This was a straightforward question for most candidates, although some candidates went on to calculate an rms value.
>
> **(a)(ii)**
>
> There was confusion about which resistance to use here, and so many responses missed out on marking point 3 as their final answer was incorrect.
>
> **(a)(iii)**
>
> Candidates had often calculated the total circuit resistance in (i), so this was credited here. Those candidates who chose to use P = VI often used peak p.d. or the p.d. across only one resistor. There was also further confusion about resistance.
>
> **(b)**
>
> Candidates were most likely to score marking point 1. Other marking points were not often seen. Although responses included statements approaching the correct point, the statements were usually not quite correct, e.g., the voltmeter “couldn’t be calibrated”.
