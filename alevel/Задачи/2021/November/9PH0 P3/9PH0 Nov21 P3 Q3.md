> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Момент силы и положение опоры автобуса с золотом в равновесии на краю обрыва

Genuine crystal balls are made from clarified quartz rather than glass. A student was given a small crystal ball and wanted to know whether it was genuine.

**(a)** The mean diameter of the crystal ball was measured to be 5.06 cm and the mass of the crystal ball was measured to be 175 g.

Show that the density of the material of the crystal ball is about 2600 kg m$^{-3}$. **[3]**

**(b)** The student measured the diameter of the crystal ball using vernier calipers with a resolution of 0.01 cm. She measured the mass of the crystal ball using a balance with a resolution of 1 g.

The table gives the densities of clarified quartz and glass.

| **Material** | **Density / kg m**$^{-3}$ |
|---|---|
| Clarified quartz | 2650 |
| Glass | 2590 |

Determine whether the crystal ball was genuine. **[6]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ (acceptable answers) | Указания (additional guidance) | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | (a) | • Use of $V = \frac{4}{3}\pi r^{3}$ **(1)**<br>• Use of $\rho = \dfrac{m}{V}$ **(1)**<br>• $\rho = 2580\ (\text{kg m}^{-3})$ **(1)** | <u>Example of calculation</u><br>$V = \dfrac{4}{3}\pi\left(\dfrac{5.06\times10^{-2}\ \text{m}}{2}\right)^{3} = 6.78\times10^{-5}\ \text{m}^{3}$<br>$\rho = \dfrac{0.175\ \text{kg}}{6.78\times10^{-5}\ \text{m}^{3}} = 2580\ \text{kg m}^{-3}$ | 3 |
> | (b) | • Use of half resolution to calculate % uncertainty **(1)**<br>• % uncertainty in $V$ = 3 × % uncertainty in $r$ **(1)**<br>• % uncertainty in $\rho$ = (% uncertainty in $m$ + % uncertainty in $V$) **(1)**<br>• Use of % uncertainty to calculate upper value of density **(1)**<br>• Upper value of density 2596 ($\text{kg m}^{-3}$) **(1)**<br>[2616 ($\text{kg m}^{-3}$) if “show that” value used]<br>• Glass is in the range and Quartz isn’t, so it may not be genuine **(1)**<br>ECF from (a) | % uncertainty in $r = \dfrac{0.005\ \text{cm}}{5.06\ \text{cm}}\times100\ \% = 0.10\ \%$<br>% uncertainty in $m = \dfrac{0.5\ \text{g}}{175\ \text{g}}\times100\ \% = 0.29\ \%$<br>% uncertainty in $\rho$ = (3 × 0.1%) + 0.29% = 0.59%<br>Range $= \pm\dfrac{0.6}{100}\times2580\ \text{kg m}^{-3} = \pm15.5\ \text{kg m}^{-3}$<br>Density range = 2565 → 2596 $\text{kg m}^{-3}$<br>Allow use of half resolution in either $r$ or $m$ to calculate minimum $V$ and maximum $m$ and then calculate maximum $\rho$ for MP1 → MP4 | 6 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)**
>
> This was generally well answered, although some candidates were not sure how to calculate the volume of a sphere. Some candidates used the area of a circle, others the volume of a cylinder. Candidates should be aware that mathematical formulas are not included on the exam paper, and so they will need to memorise these.
>
> **(b)**
>
> Responses to this question were quite mixed. Generally, there was some understanding - but only a few candidates produced a fully correct solution.
>
> Some candidates used half resolution and then doubled it. Many candidates added the percentage uncertainties, but fewer candidates remembered to multiply by 3, (even those who had used the correct volume formula).
>
> Most responses included a conclusion, but these were sometimes poorly expressed.
