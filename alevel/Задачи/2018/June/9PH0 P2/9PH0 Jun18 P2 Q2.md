> Новое давление идеального газа при уменьшении объёма и увеличении температуры

A sample of an ideal gas has pressure *p*, volume *V* and absolute temperature *T*.

The volume of the gas is decreased to $\dfrac{2}{3}$ *V* and the temperature increased to $\dfrac{6}{5}$ *T*.

Which of the following is the new pressure of the gas?

**A** $\dfrac{5}{9}$ *p*  
**B** $\dfrac{4}{5}$ *p*  
**C** $\dfrac{5}{4}$ *p*  
**D** $\dfrac{9}{5}$ *p*

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
