> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Что увеличивает максимальную кинетическую энергию фотоэлектронов

When monochromatic light is incident on the surface of a metal, electrons are emitted by the photoelectric effect.

If other conditions are unchanged, the maximum kinetic energy of the electrons will be increased by

**A** increasing the frequency of the incident light.  
**B** increasing the intensity of the incident light.  
**C** using a metal with a higher threshold frequency.  
**D** using a metal with a higher work function.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**
