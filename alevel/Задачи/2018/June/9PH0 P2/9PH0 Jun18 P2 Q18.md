> Радий-226 в фосфогипсе: число ядер по активности, время спада активности и энергия альфа-распада

Phosphogypsum is a by-product in the manufacture of fertiliser. It is slightly radioactive because of the presence of radium-226, a radioisotope with a half-life of 1600 years.

It must be stored securely as long as the activity of the radium-226 it contains is greater than 0.4 Bq per gram of phosphogypsum.

**(a)** **(i)** In a sample of 1.0 g of phosphogypsum, the activity of radium-226 is 1.3 Bq.

Calculate the number of nuclei of radium-226 in this sample. **[3]**

Number of nuclei = \_\_\_\_\_\_

**(ii)** Calculate the time in years it would take before this sample reached the permitted level of decay rate. **[3]**

Time = \_\_\_\_\_\_ years

**(b)** Radium-226 decays to radon-222 by alpha emission.

Determine the energy released in MeV in the decay of a single nucleus of radium-226.

mass of radium-226 nucleus = 225.97713 u

mass of radon-222 nucleus = 221.97040 u

mass of α particle = 4.00151 u **[5]**

Energy released = \_\_\_\_\_\_ MeV

> | Пункт | Ответ (acceptable answers) | Указания (additional guidance) | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | • Use of $\lambda t_{\frac{1}{2}} = \ln 2$ **(1)**<br>• Use of $\mathrm{d}N/\mathrm{d}t = -\lambda N$ **(1)**<br>• $N = 9.5\times10^{10}$ **(1)** | $\lambda\times(1600\times365\times24\times60\times60)\ \text{s} = \ln 2$<br>$\lambda = 1.37\times10^{-11}\ \text{s}^{-1}$<br>$1.3\ \text{Bq} = 1.37\times10^{-11}\ \text{s}^{-1}\times N$<br>$N = 9.46\times10^{10}$ | 3 |
> | (a)(ii) | • Use of $A = A_{0}e^{-\lambda t}$ **(1)**<br>• Use of $\ln A = \ln A_{0} - \lambda t$ or equivalent **(1)**<br>• $t = 8.58\times10^{10}\ \text{s} = 2700$ years **(1)** | ecf *λ* calculated in (i)<br>$\ln 0.4 = \ln 1.3 - 1.37\times10^{-11}\ \text{s}^{-1}\times t$<br>$t = 8.58\times10^{10}\ \text{s} = 2721$ years | 3 |
> | (b) | • attempt to determine mass difference between radium and radon-plus-alpha **(1)**<br>• conversion to kg **(1)**<br>• Use of $\Delta E = c^{2}\Delta m$ **(1)**<br>• Use of $1.6\times10^{-19}$ factor **(1)**<br>• Answer = 4.87 (MeV) **(1)** | $\Delta m = 225.97713\ \text{u} - (221.97040\ \text{u} + 4.00151\ \text{u})$<br>$= 5.22\times10^{-3}\ \text{u} = 5.22\times10^{-3}\times1.66\times10^{-27}\ \text{kg}$<br>$= 8.67\times10^{-30}\ \text{kg}$<br>$\Delta E = c^{2}\Delta m = (3\times10^{8}\ \text{m s}^{-1})^{2}\times8.67\times10^{-30}\ \text{kg}$<br>$= 7.80\times10^{-13}\ \text{J}$<br>$\Delta E$ in MeV $= 7.80\times10^{-13}\ \text{J} \div 1.6\times10^{-19}\ \text{C}$<br>= 4.87 MeV | 5 |
