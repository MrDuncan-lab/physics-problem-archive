> Недостающий шаг вывода формулы модуля Юнга

The Young Modulus of a material can be expressed by the formula *E* = $\dfrac{Fx}{A\Delta x}$. The derivation of this formula is

*E* = $\dfrac{\sigma}{\varepsilon}$

So *E* = \_\_\_\_\_\_

And *E* = $\dfrac{Fx}{A\Delta x}$

Which of the following completes the second line of the derivation?

**A** $\dfrac{\;\dfrac{x}{\Delta x}\;}{\dfrac{F}{A}}$  
**B** $\dfrac{\;\dfrac{\Delta x}{x}\;}{\dfrac{F}{A}}$  
**C** $\dfrac{\;\dfrac{F}{A}\;}{\dfrac{\Delta x}{x}}$  
**D** $\dfrac{\;\dfrac{F}{A}\;}{\dfrac{x}{\Delta x}}$

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
