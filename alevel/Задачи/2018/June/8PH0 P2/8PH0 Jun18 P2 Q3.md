> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Верное утверждение о стоячей волне

Which of the following is a correct statement about a stationary wave?

**A** All points on the wave oscillate in phase.  
**B** A node is formed at a point of constructive interference.  
**C** Stationary waves can only be formed from transverse waves.  
**D** Two points $\dfrac{\lambda}{2}$ apart oscillate with the same amplitude.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
