> Эспандер: петля нагрузки и разгрузки на графике силы от удлинения и нагрев

> [!info] Пункт со звёздочкой (*) оценивает и связность ответа: логичную структуру, связи между утверждениями и обоснованные выводы (indicative content + structure).

A resistance band is a length of an elastic material that can be used for exercise. The user repeatedly applies an increasing tensile force (loading) and then releases the force (unloading).

![[8PH0-jun18-2-p6-f1.svg]]

The force-extension graph for the resistance band is shown.

![[8PH0-jun18-2-p6-f2.svg]]

The user finds that the band gets warm during use.

Describe, with reference to the graph, the behaviour of the resistance band when it is repeatedly loaded and unloaded. **[6]**
