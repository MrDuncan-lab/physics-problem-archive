> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Время между посланным и принятым ультразвуковым импульсом от косяка рыбы

Fishermen use ultrasound pulses to detect shoals of fish under the surface of the water. Some fish are at a depth of 90 m below the surface.

Which of the following is the expression for the time *t*, in seconds, between the transmitted and the received pulse?

speed of sound in water = 1500 m s$^{-1}$

**A** *t* = $\dfrac{90}{1500}$  
**B** *t* = $\dfrac{1500}{90}$  
**C** *t* = $\dfrac{90 \times 2}{1500}$  
**D** *t* = $\dfrac{1500}{90 \times 2}$

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
