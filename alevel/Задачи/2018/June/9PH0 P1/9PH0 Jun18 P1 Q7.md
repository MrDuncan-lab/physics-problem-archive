> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сила на электрон в однородном поле между пластинами в другой точке

A potential difference *V* is applied across two parallel plates. An electron midway between

the two plates at point X experiences an electric force *F*.

![[9PH0-jun18-1-p4-f1.svg]]

The electron moves to point Y which is halfway between point X and the left-hand plate.

Which of the following is the electric force experienced by the electron at Y?

**A** 2*F*  
**B** *F*  
**C** $\dfrac{F}{2}$  
**D** $\dfrac{F}{4}$

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
