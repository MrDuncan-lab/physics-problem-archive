A cell is connected across a resistor. After a while the internal resistance of the cell

increases.

Which row of the table correctly shows the change in the current in the circuit and the change in the terminal potential difference across the cell?

|  | **Current** | **Terminal potential difference** |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | decreases | decreases |
| **B** | decreases | increases |
| **C** | increases | decreases |
| **D** | increases | increases |
