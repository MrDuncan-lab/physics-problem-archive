> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The range cannot increase for a projectile launched at the same speed at a greater angle, if the original angle is greater than 45°. Distractors A and C are for projectiles launched at a greater angle, at greater speed. For the same launch speed, a greater angle can only reduce the range.
