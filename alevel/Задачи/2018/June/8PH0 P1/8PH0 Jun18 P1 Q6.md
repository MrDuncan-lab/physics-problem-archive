> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Работа тормозов при остановке машины

A car of mass 1.2 × 10$^{3}$ kg is travelling at a speed of 18 m s$^{-1}$. The driver applies the brakes and the car comes to rest.

What is the work done by the brakes in stopping the car?

**A** 11 kJ  
**B** 22 kJ  
**C** 190 kJ  
**D** 390 kJ

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ (acceptable answers) | Указания (additional guidance) | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|---|
> |  | C **190 kJ**<br>Incorrect Answers:<br>A – The velocity was not squared when using the formula *E*$_{\text{k}}$ = ½ *mv*$^{2}$e.g. ½ (1.2 × 10$^{3}$)(18) = 11 kJ<br>B – The velocity was not squared and the ½ was omitted when using the formula *E*$_{\text{k}}$ = ½ *mv*$^{2}$e.g. (1.2 × 10$^{3}$)(18) = 22 kJ<br>D – The ½ was omitted when using the formula *E*$_{\text{k}}$ = ½ *mv*$^{2}$e.g. (1.2 × 10$^{3}$)(18)$^{2}$ = 390 kJ |  | 1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Another multiple response answered well, the majority of errors occurred when learners saw the term ‘work done’ and assumed that they have been given the force and the distance over which the force was applied and just multiplied together the two quantities given. This was a very straightforward question, only requiring use of the formula for kinetic energy and would have only been answered incorrectly due to the speed at which the learner read through the question.
