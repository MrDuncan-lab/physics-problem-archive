# 4. Transparent film (6 pts)

**NbPhO 2004** · Международная олимпиада · теория · 10–12 класс · задача 4  
Источник: [[_pdf/NbPhO/nbpho-2004.pdf#page=1|условие, стр. 1]] · [[_pdf/NbPhO/nbpho-2004.pdf#page=3|решение, стр. 3]]

Темы: [[6.5 Волновая оптика]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить толщину прозрачной плёнки по спектру пропускания.

A thick glass plate is coated by a thin transparent film. The transmission spectrum of the system is depicted in graph (light falls normal to the plate). The refractive index of the film $n\approx1.3$. What is the thickness of the film $d$?

![[nbpho-2004-p1-c7506.png]]

> [!answer]- Ответ
> $d\approx50\,\mu\mathrm m$.

> [!solution]- Решение
> # 4. Transparent film (6 pts)
>
> The short-wavelength oscillations on the graph are due to the diffraction on the film, therefore the local maximum condition is $2dn=\lambda N=cN/\nu$. So, $2dn\nu=cN$ and $2dn(\nu+\delta)\nu=c(N+1)$, hence $2dn\delta\nu=c$ and $d=c/2n\delta\nu$. In order to measure the distance between two maxima more precisely, we take a longer frequency interval, e.g. $\Delta\nu=80\,\mathrm{THz}$ and count the number of maxima between them, $m\approx34$. Consequently, $\delta\nu=\Delta\nu/m\approx2.35\,\mathrm{THz}$, and
> $d\approx50\,\mu\mathrm m$.
