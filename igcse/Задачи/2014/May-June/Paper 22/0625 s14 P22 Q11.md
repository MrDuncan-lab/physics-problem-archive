# Question 11 (4 marks)

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Нуклонный состав и электроны нейтрального атома натрия.

One nuclide of sodium is represented in nuclide notation as$^{23}_{11}$Na.

For one neutral atom of$^{23}_{11}$Na, state

**(a)** its nucleon number,  \_\_\_\_\_\_

**(b)** its proton number,  \_\_\_\_\_\_

**(c)** the number of neutrons,  \_\_\_\_\_\_

**(d)** the number of electrons \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[4]**

**Question 12 is on the next page.**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | 23 | B1 |
> | (b) | 11 | B1 |
> | (c) | 12 | B1 |
> | (d) | 11 no e.c.f. from (b) | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** The majority of candidates answered correctly.
>
> **(b)** Almost all candidates scoring part **(a)** went on to score part **(b)**.
>
> **(c)** Many correct answers were seen, but a significant number of candidates thought that the number of protons was the same as the number of neutrons.
>
> **(d)** Many correct answers were seen, but a significant number of candidates thought that the number of electrons was either equal to the nucleon number or the difference between the nucleon and proton numbers.
