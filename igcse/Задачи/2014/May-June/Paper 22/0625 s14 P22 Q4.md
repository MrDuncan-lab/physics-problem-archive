> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Тепловое расширение железного обода при креплении колеса.

A metal tyre for a wooden wheel is made from an iron ring. This is shown in Fig. 4.1.

![[0625-s14-22-p6-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 4.1**

The iron ring is made slightly too small to fit on the wheel.

**(a)** Explain why heating the iron ring will help it fit on the wheel. **[1]**

**(b)** Explain your answer to **(a)** in terms of the particles in the iron ring. **[2]**

**(c)** Explain what will happen to the iron ring after it has been fitted on the wheel and left to cool. **[1]**

**(d)** Suggest what happens to the joints in the wooden wheel after fitting the iron ring. **[1]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | idea of expansion / gets bigger | B1 |
> | (b) | particles have more energy / vibrate faster | B1 |
> |  | ignore move quickly or move faster<br>particles move apart / space between particles increases<br>NOT particles expand | B1 |
> | (c) | contracts / gets smaller / shrinks<br>IGNORE fits tightly | B1 |
> | (d) | idea of being pushed together<br>accept move / stick together / compressed<br>accept pulled tight/together | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Many candidates were able to explain that heating the ring would cause it to expand. The most common error was to state that it would melt.
>
> **(b)** Many candidates gained some credit for the idea that the particles in the ring would have more energy or vibrate faster. More able candidates gained full credit by going on to explain that the particles would move further apart. Weaker candidates incorrectly stated that the particles would expand.
>
> **(c)** Almost all of those candidates who scored the credit for part **(a)** went on to gain credit here also by explaining that the iron ring would contract when left to cool.
>
> **(d)** Many candidates recognised that the joints in the wheel would be pushed together by the iron ring contracting.
