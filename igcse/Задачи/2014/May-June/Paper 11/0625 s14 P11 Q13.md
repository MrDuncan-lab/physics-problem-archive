> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Уменьшение средней энергии и температуры жидкости при испарении.

During evaporation, molecules escape rapidly from the surface of a liquid.

What happens to the average energy of the molecules of the remaining liquid and what happens to the temperature of the remaining liquid?

|  | average energy of remaining molecules | temperature of remaining liquid |
|---|---|---|
| A | decreases | decreases |
| B | decreases | increases |
| C | stays the same | decreases |
| D | stays the same | increases |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question tested understanding of the process of evaporation, and it was generally well answered. However, the popularity of distractor C showed that, although there was awareness that the remaining liquid would cool, the link between this and the lowering of the average energy of its molecules was less widely known.
