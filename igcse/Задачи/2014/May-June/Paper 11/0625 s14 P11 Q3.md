> Площадь под графиком скорости автомобиля как пройденный путь.

The graph shows how the speed of a car changes with time.

![[0625-s14-11-p3-f1.svg]]

Which calculation gives the distance travelled by the car in 24 seconds?

**A**  $\dfrac{14}{24}$ m

   

**B**  $\dfrac{24}{14}$ m

   

 

**C**  $\dfrac{24 \times 14}{2}$  m

 

**D** (24 ×14)m

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question involved calculating distance travelled from the area under a speed/time graph. A significant number of candidates forgot to halve the product of final speed and time.
