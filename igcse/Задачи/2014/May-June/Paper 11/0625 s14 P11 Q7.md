> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Ось графика изменения длины упругого шнура при нагрузке.

A student adds weights to an elastic cord. He measures the length of the cord for each weight.

He then plots a graph from the results, as shown.

![[0625-s14-11-p5-f1.svg]]

What has he plotted on the vertical axis?

**A** measured length  
**B** original length  
**C** (measured length + original length)  
**D** (measured length – original length)

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The topic here was extension/load graphs. Many candidates did not realise the significance of the graph starting at the origin, meaning that the vertical axis represented extension rather than measured length.
