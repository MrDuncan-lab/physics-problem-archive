> Выбор мерного цилиндра и отсчёт объёма по мениску.

A student wishes to measure accurately the volume of approximately 40 cm$^{3}$ of water. She has two measuring cylinders, a larger one that can hold 100 cm$^{3}$, and a smaller one that can hold 50 cm$^{3}$. The water forms a meniscus where it touches the glass.

![[0625-s14-11-p2-f1.svg]]

Which cylinder should the student use and which water level should she use to ensure an accurate result?

|  | cylinder | water level |
|---|---|---|
| A | larger one | bottom of meniscus |
| B | larger one | top of meniscus |
| C | smaller one | bottom of meniscus |
| D | smaller one | top of meniscus |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
