Источник: [[_pdf/2014/0625_s14_qp_11.pdf#page=10|условие, стр. 10]] · [[_pdf/2014/0625_s14_ms_11.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2014/0625_s14_er.pdf#page=1|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

Темы: [[3.3 Electromagnetic spectrum]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Порядок электромагнитных волн по частоте.

Which list shows electromagnetic waves in order of increasing frequency?

**A** visible light, X-rays, γ-rays  
**B** visible light, γ-rays, X-rays  
**C** X-rays, γ-rays, visible light  
**D** γ-rays, X-rays, visible light

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Most candidates knew the correct order for the electromagnetic spectrum, although a significant proportion chose D, which showed the electromagnetic radiations in order of *decreasing* frequency.
