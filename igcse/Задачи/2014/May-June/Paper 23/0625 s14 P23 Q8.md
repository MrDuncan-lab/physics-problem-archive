> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Построение изображения с помощью главных лучей линзы.

Fig. 8.1 shows a lens, to the left of which is a vertical object AX with one end on the principal axis.

![[0625-s14-23-p12-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 8.1**

**(a)** Distance PF is the focal length of the lens.

State the name given to the point F \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(b)** **(i)** On Fig. 8.1, draw a ray from the top of the object X, which is parallel to the principal axis. Continue this ray at least 6 cm after the lens, to show its path through the air on the other side of the lens. **[2]**

**(ii)** On Fig. 8.1, draw another ray that will enable you to locate the image of X. Label this image with the letter Y. **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)(ii)** OR other principal focus correctly positioned and ray drawn through this and emerging from lens parallel to principal axis image (marked Y) correctly positioned condone inverted or indicated where rays cross

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | principal focus<br>condone focus / focal point | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | ray shown parallel to principal axis<br>AND<br>ray emerges to pass through F | B1 |
> |  | refraction shown at centre line or at each surface | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | ray from X to P continues straight on | M1 |
> |  | OR other principal focus correctly positioned and ray drawn<br>through this and emerging from lens parallel to principal axis<br>image (marked Y) correctly positioned<br>condone inverted or indicated where rays cross | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Most candidates gave the correct response.
>
> **(b)** There were many good examples of ray diagrams, clearly showing refraction at the centre or at both surfaces of the lens. However, many candidates would benefit from further practise in drawing ray diagrams to improve their accuracy.
