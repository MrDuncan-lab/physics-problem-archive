> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** The more able candidates recognised that this arrangement would produce a spectrum on the screen.
>
> **(b)** Very few candidates ticked only one box, which is a significant improvement on a similar question from last year. The most common mistake was to tick the box for diffraction.
>
> **(c)** A large number of candidates did not use a ruler to continue the rays to the screen, and whilst many gained some credit only the most able could more fully describe what would be seen on the screen.
