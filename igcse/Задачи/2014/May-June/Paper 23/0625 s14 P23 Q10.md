> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Заряжение палочки и взаимодействие положительных и отрицательных шаров.

A positively charged plastic ball is suspended on an insulating nylon thread, as shown in Fig. 10.1.

![[0625-s14-23-p14-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 10.1**

A negatively charged plastic rod is brought towards the positively charged ball.

**(a)** **(i)** Describe how the rod might have been given its charge. **[2]**

**(ii)** Describe what is **seen** happening to the ball as the rod is brought near to it. **[2]**

**(iii)** State the reason for what you have described in **(a)(ii)**. **[1]**

**(b)** The negatively charged rod is removed.

An identical plastic ball is suspended on another nylon thread of equal length, so that the balls are a short distance from each other. The second ball is also given a positive charge.

In the space below, sketch how the two balls and their threads will hang. **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)(i)** on / with (dry) cloth / insulator
>
> **(a)(ii)** to the right / to(wards) / by the rod / closer to (the rod) ignore sticks to, accept attracts / attracted for both marks
>
> **(b)** straight threads OR equal angles to vertical

> | (b) | threads further apart at bottom than top | M1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** Many candidates gave a correct description for charging the rod.
>
> **(ii)** The majority of candidates correctly described the ball moving towards the rod as the rod was brought near to the ball.
>
> **(iii)** Most candidates knew that unlike charges attract each other.
>
> **(b)** Most candidates gave a correct sketch showing the balls repelling each other, but a significant number thought that they would attract, or did not draw a diagram.
