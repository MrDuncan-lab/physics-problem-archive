> Измерение удлинения пружины и ожидаемый график силы.

A spring is attached to the underside of the edge of a bench. A metre rule is fixed next to it, as shown in Fig. 2.1.

![[0625-s14-23-p4-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 2.1 Fig. 2.2**

When there is no load on the spring, the marker pin is at X.

When a load of weight *L* is hanging on the spring, the marker pin is at Y, as shown in Fig. 2.2.

**(a)** Describe how you would determine the value of the extension of the spring caused by the load. **[3]**

**(b)** On Fig. 2.3, sketch the graph you would expect to be able to obtain as the spring is gradually stretched from X to Y.
