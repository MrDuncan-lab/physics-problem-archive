> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Газовое давление по молекулярной модели и расчёт закона Бойля.

Fig. 6.1 shows a quantity of gas in a cylinder fitted with a piston P.

![[0625-s14-31-p7-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 6.1**

**(a)** Describe the motion of the molecules of the gas. **[3]**

**(b)** The piston is now slowly pushed down to decrease the volume of the gas. The temperature of the gas does not change.

**(i)** State and explain, in terms of molecules, what happens to the pressure of the gas. **[2]**

**(ii)** Before pushing the piston down, the pressure of the gas was 1.0 × 10$^{5}$ Pa. Pushing the piston down reduces the volume of the gas from 500 cm$^{3}$ to 240 cm$^{3}$.

Calculate the final pressure of the gas.

pressure =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)(i)** <u>more frequent collisions between molecules and walls</u> <u>OR molecules collide with walls more often</u> / at greater rate
>
> **(b)(ii)** 2.1 × 10$^{5}$ Pa to 2 or more sig. figs

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | (molecules) move in random directions / randomly / with constant random motion / zig-<br>zag motion / in all directions | B1 |
> |  | (molecules) have random speeds OR a range of speeds OR move (very) fast / at<br>(very) high speed | B1 |
> |  | any 1 from:<br>(molecules) collide with each other<br>(molecules) move in straight lines between collisions<br>(molecules) change direction in collisions<br>(molecules) collide with walls (of cylinder) | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | pressure increases | M1 |
> |  | <u>more frequent collisions between molecules and walls</u><br><u>OR molecules collide with walls more often</u> / at greater rate | A1 |
> | (b)(ii) | pV = constant<br>OR p$_{1}$V$_{1}$ = p$_{2}$V$_{2}$ in any form<br>OR 1.0 × 10$^{5}$ × 500 = p$_{2}$ × 240 | C1 |
> |  | 2.1 × 10$^{5}$ Pa to 2 or more sig. figs | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Most candidates referred to the random motion of the molecules and their collisions either with each other or the walls of the cylinder or the piston, but fewer candidates mentioned the fast speed of the molecules. A number of candidates included some details that were only required in **(b)(i)**, suggesting that they had read the question with insufficient care or were recalling an answer from a different question. Depending on the precise wording, most were able to gain some credit.
>
> **(b) (i)** Almost all of the candidates were awarded some credit for stating that the pressure increases. The explanation required some reference to the increase in frequency of the collisions with the walls, not just more collisions.
>
> **(ii)** A good majority of the candidates realised that Boyle’s law was involved and stated the relevant formula. Almost all then worked out the final pressure correctly.
