> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Вторичное напряжение по числу витков трансформатора.

A transformer is a device for changing voltages.

Fig. 10.1 shows a transformer that has a primary coil with 1000 turns and a secondary coil with 50 turns.

![[0625-s14-21-p15-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 10.1**

**(a)** State the material from which the core of the transformer is made. **[1]**

**(b)** The primary coil is connected to a 240 V alternating current supply.

Calculate the voltage across the secondary coil.

secondary coil voltage =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ V **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** 12 (V)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | iron | B1 |
> | (b) | V$_{1}$ / V$_{2}$ = N$_{1}$ / N$_{2}$ in words, symbols or numbers | C1 |
> |  | correct substitution | C1 |
> |  | 12 (V) | A1 |
