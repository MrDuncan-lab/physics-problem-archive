> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Уменьшение давления на тонкий лёд увеличением площади опоры.

A young boy, skating on a frozen pond, has fallen through some thin ice about 10 m from the shore of the pond. Fig. 4.1 shows the situation.

![[0625-s14-21-p7-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 4.1**

A man, standing near the pond, hears the boy’s shouts for help.

The man weighs more than the boy.

**(a)** Why would it be unsafe for the man to walk on the ice to rescue the boy? **[1]**

**(b)** Suggest and explain what the man could do to cross the ice to reach the boy safely. **[4]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** any three from: larger (surface) area load / weight / force more spread out less pressure use of P = F / A

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | greater pressure from man OR man will fall through ice OR ice will break / crack | B1 |
> | (b) | idea of increasing area OR spreading load | M1 |
> |  | any three from:<br>larger (surface) area<br>load / weight / force more spread out<br>less pressure<br>use of P = F / A | A3 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** A correct response was usually stated.
>
> **(b)** Many candidates scored three of the available marking points, identifying the use of planks or a ladder to increase surface area and reduce pressure. Few candidates made use of pressure = force per unit area or the idea of spreading the load. In a small number of cases, candidates gave an incorrect response by referring to the use of skates.
