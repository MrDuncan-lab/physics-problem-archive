> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Наблюдение броуновского движения дыма и объяснение ударов молекул.

The apparatus in Fig. 3.1 is being used to view the movement of some smoke particles trapped in a box.

![[0625-s14-21-p6-f1.svg]]

![[0625-s14-21-p6-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 3.1**

**(a)** Describe what is seen when the smoke is viewed through the microscope. **[2]**

**(b)** In the space below, sketch how one smoke particle might move during a short interval of time. **[2]**

**(c)** What causes the smoke particles to move? **[2]**

**(d)** What name is used for this motion of the smoke particles? Complete the sentence.

The motion of the smoke particles is known as  \_\_\_\_\_\_ motion. **[1]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | bright specks OR spots / dots OR flashes of light | B1 |
> |  | moving randomly OR jerky movements OR zig zag / jiggling | B1 |
> | (b) | line representing a smoke particle moving with a change of direction | B1 |
> |  | line is straight with at least 2 changes of direction | B1 |
> | (c) | collisions / bombardment | B1 |
> |  | <u>(with) air atoms</u> / molecules / particles | B1 |
> | (d) | <u>Brownian</u> | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** This question was well answered by those candidates that had had experience of observing Brownian motion.
>
> **(b)** Fewer candidates than expected were able to represent the random movement of a smoke particle and gain full credit. A common incorrect response was to draw a number of atoms representing a gas rather than on smoke particle.
>
> **(c)** A good number of candidates gained full credit for indicating collisions between air molecules and smoke particles.
>
> **(d)** There were many good answers from middle and higher ability candidates although in some misspelling prevented credit from being given.
