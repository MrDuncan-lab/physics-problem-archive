> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Протоны нейтроны и электроны изотопов хлора.

One nuclide of chlorine has 35 nucleons and the other nuclide of chlorine has 37 nucleons. The proton number of chlorine is 17.

**(a)** How many protons are there in a neutral atom of chlorine-35?  \_\_\_\_\_\_

**(b)** How many neutrons are there in a neutral atom of chlorine-37?  \_\_\_\_\_\_

**(c)** How many electrons are there in a neutral atom of chlorine-37?  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | 17 | B1 |
> | (b) | 20 | B1 |
> | (c) | 17 | B1 |
