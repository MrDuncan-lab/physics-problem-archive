> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Свойства ламп соединённых параллельно.

Which statement is not correct for lamps connected in parallel?

**A** They can be switched on and off separately.  
**B** They will remain bright if another lamp is connected in parallel.  
**C** They share the supply voltage equally between them.  
**D** They still operate if one lamp is removed.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Many candidates coped well with this question on lamps in parallel, although a popular distractor was option B, corresponding to the common misconception that adding lamps to a parallel circuit will produce a significant reduction in brightness. Candidates need to take very careful note of the word ‘**not**’ when it occurs in a question.
