> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Разность температур реперных точек термометра.

A liquid-in-glass thermometer is marked with a scale in °C.

![[0625-s14-12-p7-f3.svg]]

What is the temperature difference between the two fixed points for this thermometer?

**A** 40°C  
**B** 50°C  
**C** 100°C  
**D** 120°C

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The concept of thermometer fixed points was not well known, and the answers frequently followed the misconception that these were the highest and lowest temperatures shown on the thermometer scale, leading to the incorrect choice of option D.
