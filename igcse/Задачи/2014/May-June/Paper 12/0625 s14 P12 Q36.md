> Расчёт напряжения по числу витков обмоток трансформатора.

A transformer has 100 turns on its primary coil and 25 turns on its secondary coil. The primary

coil is connected to a 12 V a.c. supply.

![[0625-s14-12-p17-f2.svg]]

What is the voltage induced across the secondary coil?

**A** 3.0 V  
**B** 4.0 V  
**C** 48 V  
**D** 300 V

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**
