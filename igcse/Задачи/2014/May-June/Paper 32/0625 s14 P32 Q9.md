# Question 9 (6 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2014 · Paper 3 — Extended theory, вариант 2 · вопрос 9  
Источник: [[_pdf/2014/0625_s14_qp_32.pdf#page=16|условие, стр. 16]] · [[_pdf/2014/0625_s14_ms_32.pdf#page=7|схема оценивания, стр. 7]] · [[_pdf/2014/0625_s14_er.pdf#page=23|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 s14 P32]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Электризация металлического блока методом электростатической индукции.

Fig. 9.1 shows a positively charged plastic rod, a metal block resting on an insulator, and a wire connected to earth.

![[0625-s14-32-p16-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 9.1**

**(a)** On Fig. 9.1, draw the charge distribution in the metal block. **[2]**

**(b)** The earth wire is held against the metal block, as shown in Fig. 9.2.

![[0625-s14-32-p16-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 9.2**

On Fig. 9.2, draw the new charge distribution. **[1]**

**(c)** The charged rod and the earth wire are removed and the metal block is left charged.

State the order in which the rod and the wire were removed. Explain your answer. **[2]**

**(d)** Name this charging process. **[1]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** roughly same no of positives as negatives
>
> **(c)** charges kept in block OR so no charge can flow to or from block NOT any mention of positive charges moving accept reverse argument

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | more negatives in top half than bottom half | M1 |
> |  | roughly same no of positives as negatives | A1 |
> | (b) | clearly more negatives than positives, anywhere in / on block | B1 |
> | (c) | wire removed first | M1 |
> |  | charges kept in block OR so no charge can flow to or from block<br>NOT any mention of positive charges moving<br>accept reverse argument | A1 |
> | (d) | (charging by) induction NOT e.m. induction OR earthing | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> (a)(b) The majority of candidates gave good answers to these part questions and gained full credit.
>
> (c) Most candidates were aware that the wire should be removed first but many of the explanations were very vague. Many of the incorrect answers involved the movement of positive charges.
>
> (d) Many candidates used the word induction but some could not be given credit as they stated some inappropriate type of induction, especially electromagnetic induction.
