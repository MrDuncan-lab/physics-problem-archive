> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Скорость и ускорение по наклонам графика расстояния.

Fig. 1.1 shows a distance-time graph for a moving object.

![[0625-s14-32-p2-t1.svg]]

0 15 40

time / s

**Fig. 1.1**

**(a)** Describe the speed of the object between points

**(i)** A and B,

**(ii)** B and C. **[2]**

**(b)** State whether the acceleration of the object is zero, negative or positive, as shown on the graph between points

**(i)** A and B,

**(ii)** B and C. **[2]**

**(c)** Calculate the average speed of the object during the 40 seconds.

speed =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(c)** (av. vel = 50 / 40 =) 1.3 m / s or 1.25 m / s

> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |

> | (a)(ii) | constant / speed 0.8 m / s | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | negative | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | zero | B1 |
> | (c) | uses v = d / t in any form or d / t | C1 |
> |  | (av. vel = 50 / 40 =) 1.3 m / s or 1.25 m / s | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> (a)(b) A large number of candidates answered these part questions as if Fig. 1.1 was a speed-time graph not the less familiar distance-time graph. In (a)(ii), credit was given for calculating the speed to be 0.8 m / s instead of the expected description. In (a)(i) many candidates gave a numerical answer of 2.0 m / s. As the speed was varying, credit could only be given to those candidates who specified that this was the average speed.
>
> (c) The vast majority of candidates gave the correct answer.
