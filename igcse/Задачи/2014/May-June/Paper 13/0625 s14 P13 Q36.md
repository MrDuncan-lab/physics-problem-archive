> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Пропорциональность напряжений обмоток трансформатора.

An input voltage of 10 V is supplied to the primary coil of a transformer. An output voltage of 40 V

is produced across the secondary coil.

The 10 V supply at the primary coil is now replaced with a 40 V supply.

What is the new output voltage across the secondary coil?

**A** 10 V  
**B** 40 V  
**C** 70 V  
**D** 160 V

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
