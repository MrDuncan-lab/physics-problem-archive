> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Размагничивание магнита переменным током в катушке.

A steel magnet is placed inside a coil of wire. There is a large alternating current in the coil. The

magnet is slowly moved out of the coil to position P.

![[0625-s14-13-p15-f2.svg]]

P

How has the steel changed, if at all, when it reaches position P?

**A** It has become a stronger magnet.  
**B** It has become demagnetised.  
**C** The poles have changed ends.  
**D** There has been no change.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Although the majority of candidates answered this question correctly, a significant minority seemed unaware of this standard method of demagnetisation.
