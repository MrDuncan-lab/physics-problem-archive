> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определение периода полураспада по таблице скоростей счёта.

The table shows the results of an experiment to find the half-life of a radioactive substance.

| time / s | count rate from substance counts / second |
|---|---|
| 0 | 150 |
| 60 | 120 |
| 120 | 95 |
| 180 | 75 |
| 240 | 60 |

What is the half-life of the substance?

**A** 60 seconds  
**B** 120 seconds  
**C** 180 seconds  
**D** 240 seconds

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
