# Question 8 (9 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2014 · Paper 3 — Extended theory, вариант 3 · вопрос 8

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> ЭДС батареи заряд и сопротивление участков цепи.

A student sets up a circuit containing three identical cells. Each cell has an e.m.f. (electromotive force) of 2.0 V.

Fig. 8.1 shows the cells in series with a length of uniform metal wire connected between two terminals K and L, an ammeter and a resistor X.

![[0625-w14-33-p14-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 8.1**

**(a)** State the total e.m.f. of the three cells in series.

total e.m.f. =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(b)** The ammeter reading is 0.25 A.

**(i)** State the name of the unit in which electric charge is measured. **[1]**

**(ii)** Calculate the charge that flows through the circuit in twelve minutes.

charge =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(iii)** The metal wire has a resistance of 16 Ω.

Calculate the resistance of resistor X.

resistance =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(c)** The student removes the 16 Ω wire from the circuit and cuts it into two equal lengths.

He then connects the two lengths in parallel between K and L, as shown in Fig. 8.2.

![[0625-w14-33-p15-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 8.2**

Calculate the resistance of the two lengths of wire in parallel.

resistance =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)(ii)** 180 (C)
>
> **(b)(iii)** 8.0 Ω
>
> **(c)** 4.0 Ω

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | 6.0 V | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | coulomb (IGNORE C) | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | (Q =) I t<br>OR 0.25 × 12 × 60 OR 0.25 × 720 OR 0.25 × 12 OR 3.0 OR 0.25 × 60 OR 15 | C1 |
> |  | 180 (C) | A1 |
> | (b)(iii) | (R =) V / I or 6.0 / 0.25 or 24.0 e.c.f. from (a)<br>OR<br>(V =) IR OR 0.25 × 16 OR 4.0 e.c.f. from (a) | C1 |
> |  | 8.0 Ω | A1 |
> | (c) | R ∝ l OR 8.0 OR 16 / 2 | C1 |
> |  | R$_{1}$R$_{2}$/ (R$_{1}$ + R$_{2}$) OR 1 / R = 1 / R$_{1}$ + 1 / R$_{2}$ OR 64 / 16 OR 1 / R = 1 / 8 + 1 / 8 | C1 |
> |  | 4.0 Ω | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** This was almost invariably correct.
>
> **(b) (i)** This piece of direct, factual recall was very commonly incorrect, with answers ranging from *amperes* and *volts* to *joules* and *watts*. Oddly, this included many candidates who correctly used the unit *coulomb* or more commonly the symbol C in **(b)(ii)**.
>
> **(ii)** This part was often well answered with many candidates giving the correct value with the correct unit. The correct equation *I* = *Q*/*t* was rearranged to give *Q* = *I*/*t* by a fair number of candidates, so producing a wrong value.
>
> **(iii)** This calculation was very well performed and most candidates obtained full credit.
>
> **(c)** This quite tricky calculation was well performed and candidates were clearly familiar with what was needed.
