> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Магнитное поле соленоида и взаимная индукция катушек.

A circuit contains a battery, a variable resistor and a solenoid. Fig. 9.1 shows the magnetic field pattern produced by the current in the solenoid.

![[0625-w14-33-p16-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 9.1**

**(a)** **(i)** State how the magnetic field pattern indicates regions where the magnetic field is stronger. **[1]**

**(ii)** State what happens to the magnetic field when the current in the circuit is reversed. **[1]**

**(b)** A second solenoid is placed next to the first solenoid.

Fig. 9.2 shows the second solenoid connected to a very sensitive ammeter.

![[0625-w14-33-p17-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 9.2**

**(i)** The variable resistor is adjusted so that its resistance changes quickly.

State and explain what is seen to happen in the circuit of the second solenoid. **[3]**

**(ii)** The variable resistor is adjusted much more slowly than in **(i)**.

State and explain the difference in what is seen to happen in the circuit of the second solenoid. **[2]**

> | (a)(ii) | (magnetic field (lines) direction reversed | B1 |

> | (b)(ii) | deflection / reading on ammeter smaller OR lasts longer | B1 |
