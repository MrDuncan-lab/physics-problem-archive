> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Построение лучей при отражении света плоским зеркалом.

The IGCSE class is investigating the reflection of light by a plane mirror. Fig. 1.1 shows a student’s ray-trace sheet.

![[0625-w14-61-p2-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 1.1**

**(a)** On Fig. 1.1, draw a normal to the centre of the mirror. **[1]**

**(b)** On Fig. 1.1, draw an incident ray at 30 ° to the normal and to the left of the normal. **[1]**

**(c)** Fig. 1.2 shows a diagram of a ray box.

![[0625-w14-61-p2-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 1.2**

On Fig. 1.1, draw the ray box in a suitable position to produce the incident ray that you have drawn. **[1]**

**(d)** On Fig. 1.1, draw a reflected ray in the position you would expect it to be using the incident ray that you have drawn. **[1]**

**(e)** State two precautions that you could take in this experiment to obtain reliable results.

1 \_\_\_\_\_\_

2 \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | normal at 90°, straight, at centre | 1 |
> | (b) | incident ray at 30° on left of normal, straight | 1 |
> | (c) | ray box near beginning of incident ray and pointing along it | 1 |
> | (d) | reflected ray at angle of reflection approximately 30° | 1 |
> | (e) | any two from:<br>darkened room / brighter ray box owtte<br>mark rays at centre / edge of beam<br>use sharp pencil<br>thin ray / small slit in ray box<br>perpendicular viewing of protractor | 2 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> (a) – (d) Most candidates drew neat and accurate normals and rays. However, a significant number of
>
> candidates were unable to correctly position the ray box.
>
> (e) Successful candidates made relevant suggestions from their experience. Others made vague suggestions that only amounted to writing that they would follow the instructions carefully. This did not gain the available credit. The question asked for two precautions. There are more than two
>
> possible correct answers but candidates should be warned against offering more than the number requested as they run the risk of effectively asking the Examiner to choose the correct answers from a list – it is the candidates’ task to select two precautions and marks are awarded accordingly. Some candidates appeared to be relying on answers they had learned from past papers which often resulted in irrelevant responses such as ‘place the pins far apart’.
