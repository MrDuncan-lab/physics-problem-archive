> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сила тока в параллельных ветвях после замыкания ключа.

The diagram shows a circuit with a 3.0 Ω resistor and a 2.0 Ω resistor connected in parallel.

![[0625-w14-11-p13-f3.svg]]

The switch is open, and the ammeter reads 2.0 A.

The switch is now closed and the ammeter reads the total current in both resistors.

What is the ammeter reading with the switch closed?

**A** 1.2 A  
**B** 3.0 A  
**C** 4.0 A  
**D** 5.0 A

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question involved a parallel circuit, with one branch containing a switch that was initially open. A common error was to think that closing the switch increased the total circuit resistance to 5.0 Ω, leading to a choice of option **A**.
