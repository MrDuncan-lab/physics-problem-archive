> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Вычисление объёма стального тела по плотности и массе.

A steel object has **5 kg** stamped on its side, as shown in Fig. 2.1.

![[0625-w14-21-p4-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 2.1**

**(a)** Express 5.0 kg in grams. 5.0 kg =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ g **[1]**

**(b)** The density of the steel is 7.81 g / cm$^{3}$.

Calculate the volume of the object.

volume =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm$^{3}$ **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** 640 (cm$^{3}$), accept 6.4 × 10$^{\text{–4}}$ if clearly stated in m$^{3}$

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | 5000 (g) | B1 |
> | (b) | density = mass / volume in any form OR (volume =) mass / density | C1 |
> |  | 5000 / 7.81 OR 5 / 7.81 OR 0.64, ecf from (a) | C1 |
> |  | 640 (cm$^{3}$), accept 6.4 × 10$^{\text{–4}}$ if clearly stated in m$^{3}$ | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> (a) Many candidates answered correctly, but some divided by 1000 or multiplied by 100.
>
> (b) A significant number of candidates either did not remember or were unable to correctly transpose the equation for density.
