# Question 3 (5 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2014 · Paper 2 — Core theory, вариант 1 · вопрос 3  
Источник: [[_pdf/2014/0625_w14_qp_21.pdf#page=5|условие, стр. 5]] · [[_pdf/2014/0625_w14_ms_21.pdf#page=4|схема оценивания, стр. 4]] · [[_pdf/2014/0625_w14_er.pdf#page=7|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w14 P21]]  
Темы: [[1.7.2 Work]], [[1.7.4 Power]], [[1.7.1 Energy]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Работа мощность и причины потерь энергии машины.

**(a)** Use words from the list below to complete the sentences about work and energy.

**initial acceleration**

**distance moved**

**force exerted**

**potential energy**

**time taken**

An object is dragged across a rough surface. In order to find the work done on the object, it is

necessary to know the  \_\_\_\_\_\_ and the  \_\_\_\_\_\_

To calculate the power, it is also necessary to know the  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

**(b)** A machine working in a factory actually uses more energy than is needed to do the task it is involved in.

Suggest why this is so. **[2]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | force (exerted), distance (moved), either order | B1+B1 |
> |  | time (taken) | B1 |
> | (b) | energy lost / wasted / transferred (to surroundings) OR inefficiency | B1 |
> |  | suitable cause for energy lost e.g. friction, heat, sound, moving parts | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> (a) Most candidates scored partial credit for this question. The most common mistake was having distance and time in the first sentence.
>
> (b) Many candidates gave responses that did not properly address what was being asked in the question. Only the higher achieving candidates gave suggestions about energy being transferred as thermal energy to the surroundings.
