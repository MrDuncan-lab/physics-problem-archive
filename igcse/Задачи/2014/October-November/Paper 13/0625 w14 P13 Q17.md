> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнение способов теплопередачи проводимостью и излучением.

Two methods by which thermal energy can be transferred are conduction and radiation.

Which statement is correct?

**A** Conduction involves density changes in fluids.  
**B** Conduction only occurs in solids.  
**C** Radiation cannot occur in a vacuum.  
**D** Radiation involves electromagnetic waves.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> A significant number of candidates thought that thermal conduction only occurred in solids, neglecting substances such as mercury, or indeed any other molten metals.
