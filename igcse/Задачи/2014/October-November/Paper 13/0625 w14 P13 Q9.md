> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Превращение энергии аккумулятора в велосипедной лампе.

When a bicycle lamp is switched on, what is the useful energy change within the battery?

**A** chemical energy to electrical energy  
**B** electrical energy to chemical energy  
**C** electrical energy to light energy  
**D** light energy to chemical energy

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This was generally a well answered question; however some of the lower-achieving candidates chose option **C**, possibly having not taken account of the emboldened ‘within the battery’. Candidates should always pay particular attention to any text written in **bold**.
