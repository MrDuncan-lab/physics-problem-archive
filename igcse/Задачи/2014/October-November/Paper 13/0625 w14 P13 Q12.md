> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Изменение разности уровней жидкости в манометре.

A manometer is used to measure the pressure of the air in a container.

![[0625-w14-13-p6-f2.svg]]

Which change would give a bigger value of height h ?

**A** using a less dense liquid  
**B** using a more dense liquid  
**C** using a narrower tube  
**D** using a wider tube

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question was not answered correctly by a sizeable proportion of candidates, with many thinking that a narrower tube would cause *h* to increase.
