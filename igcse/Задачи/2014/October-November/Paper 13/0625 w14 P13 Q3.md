> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Средняя скорость самолёта без учёта промежуточной стоянки.

An aeroplane flies from town X to town Z, stopping for 1 hour at town Y to pick up more passengers. The distances between the towns are shown in the diagram.

![[0625-w14-13-p3-f1.svg]]

The total time taken between leaving X and arriving at Z is 3 hours.

What is the average speed of the aeroplane in the air?

**A** $\dfrac{500}{4}$ km / h  
**B** $\dfrac{500}{3}$ km / h  
**C** $\dfrac{500}{2}$ km / h  
**D** $\dfrac{500}{1}$ km / h

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question concerned average speed, and was generally well answered. Those candidates who wrongly chose option **B** may not have taken account of ‘in the air’ in the question, despite this phrase being printed in bold.
