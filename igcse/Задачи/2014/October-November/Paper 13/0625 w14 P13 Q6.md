> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Плотность жидкости по массе и объёму.

Diagram 1 shows an empty measuring cylinder on a balance.

Diagram 2 shows the same measuring cylinder on the balance, but it now contains a liquid.

![[0625-w14-13-p4-f1.svg]]

diagram 1 diagram 2

What is the density of the liquid?

**A** 0.2 g / cm$^{3}$  
**B** 0.5 g / cm$^{3}$  
**C** 2.0 g / cm$^{3}$  
**D** 5.0 g / cm$^{3}$

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Although the vast majority of responses to this question on density were correct, some of the lower-achieving candidates did not subtract the mass of the empty measuring cylinder, therefore opting for **D**.
