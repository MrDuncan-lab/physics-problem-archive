> Определение слышимого диапазона частот звука.

Three vibrating objects P, Q and R produce waves in the air of different frequencies as shown.

| object | frequency / Hz |
|---|---|
| P | 25 |
| Q | 1000 |
| R | 15 000 |

Which of these waves can be heard by a human ear?

**A** P, Q and R  
**B** P and Q only  
**C** P and R only  
**D** Q and R only

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**
