> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Наведение тока движущимся магнитным стержнем.

A solenoid is connected to a very sensitive ammeter. A rod is inserted into one end of the

solenoid. The ammeter shows that there is a small electric current in the circuit while the rod is moving.

![[0625-w14-13-p16-f2.svg]]

Which rod is being inserted?

**A** a heated copper rod  
**B** a magnetised steel rod  
**C** an uncharged nylon rod  
**D** a radioactive uranium rod

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
