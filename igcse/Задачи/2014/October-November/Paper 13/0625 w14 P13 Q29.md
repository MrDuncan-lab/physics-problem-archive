> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Напряжение на резисторе в последовательной цепи.

A 30 Ω resistor is connected in series with another resistor and a 6.0 V battery. The current in the

circuit is 0.12 A. A voltmeter is connected across the other resistor.

![[0625-w14-13-p14-t2.svg]]

What is the reading on the voltmeter?

**A** 2.4 V  
**B** 3.6 V  
**C** 6.0 V  
**D** 9.6 V

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The most common mistake was to calculate the p.d. across the 30 Ω resistor and giving this as the answer, rather than subtracting it from 6.0 V.
