> Связь громкости и высоты звука с амплитудой и частотой.

A fire alarm is not loud enough and the pitch is too low. An engineer adjusts the alarm so that it

produces a louder note of a higher pitch.

What effect does this have on the amplitude and on the frequency of the sound waves that the alarm produces?

|  | amplitude | frequency |
|---|---|---|
| A | larger | larger |
| B | larger | smaller |
| C | smaller | larger |
| D | smaller | smaller |
