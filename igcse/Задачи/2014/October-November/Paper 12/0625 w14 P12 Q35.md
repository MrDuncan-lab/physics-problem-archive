> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Положение предмета для получения увеличенного действительного изображения.

An object is placed in front of a converging lens. The lens has a focal length f.

The lens produces a real, enlarged image of the object.

In which labelled position is the object placed?

![[0625-w14-12-p15-f1.svg]]

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question concerned the image produced by a converging lens and was less well answered than many others. All options were popular, suggesting that candidates may have been guessing; many of the lower achieving candidates opted for **C**, showing the object placed at a focus of the lens.
