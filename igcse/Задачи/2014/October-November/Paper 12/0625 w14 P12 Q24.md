> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Подбор резисторов делителя для заданного выходного напряжения.

An engineer uses the potential divider shown in the diagram. He needs the output voltage to be

one tenth ( $\dfrac{1}{10}$ ) of the input voltage.

![[0625-w14-12-p11-f3.svg]]

Which pair of values could he use for the two resistors X and Y?

|  | X / kΩ | Y / kΩ |
|---|---|---|
| A | 1.0 | 9.0 |
| B | 1.0 | 10.0 |
| C | 9.0 | 1.0 |
| D | 10.0 | 1.0 |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The potential divider caused problems for a significant proportion of the candidates, with almost as many choosing **D** as **C**. This suggests uncertainty over the correct application of the concept of a ratio of 1 to 10.
