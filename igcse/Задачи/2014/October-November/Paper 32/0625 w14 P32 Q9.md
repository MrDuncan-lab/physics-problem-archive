> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Принцип трансформатора и расчёт входного тока.

A transformer is used to reduce the voltage of a supply from 120 V a.c. to 12 V a.c.

**(a)** Explain how a transformer works. Your answer should include an explanation of why a transformer would not work with a d.c. supply voltage. **[3]**

**(b)** The output current is 1.2 A.

**(i)** Calculate the input current.

input current =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(ii)** State an assumption you made in your calculation for **(b)(i)**. **[1]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)(i)** (I$_{2}$ = 1.2 × 12/120 =) 0.12 A

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | changing (magnetic) flux | B1 |
> |  | <u>induces e.m.f. in secondary IGNORE induces current</u> | B1 |
> |  | no change of flux with constant supply voltage / d.c. | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | I$_{1}$V$_{1}$= I$_{2}$V$_{2}$ in any form OR I$_{2}$V$_{2}$/V$_{1}$ | C1 |
> |  | (I$_{2}$ = 1.2 × 12/120 =) 0.12 A | A1 |
> | (b)(ii) | transformer 100% efficient OR has no (heat/energy) losses OR output<br>power = input power | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** A significant number of candidates wrote about step-up or step-down transformers without explaining how a transformer works, or attempted an explanation with a vague account using some of the terms involved in the operation of a transformer. Those lower achieving candidates who did attempt an explanation, often did not indicate clearly that there is a changing flux in the transformer or stated that the changing flux induced a current rather than an e.m.f. in the secondary coil. A significant number of candidates did not discuss at all the situation with a d.c. supply; presumably they had not read the question carefully.
>
> **(b) (i)** Many candidates attempted unsuccessfully to use the turns ratio to calculate current in the secondary coil. Only the higher achieving candidates were able to equate power input and power output.
>
> **(ii)** Only a minority of candidates stated that the transformer should be 100% efficient or have no losses.
