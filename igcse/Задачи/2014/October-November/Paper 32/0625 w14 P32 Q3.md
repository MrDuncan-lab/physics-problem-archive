> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> График скорости взлёта и падения ракеты с парашютом.

Fig. 3.1 shows the speed- time graph of a firework rocket as it rises and then falls to the ground.

![[0625-w14-32-p5-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 3.1**

The rocket runs out of fuel at A. It reaches its maximum height at B. At E it returns to the ground.

**(a)** **(i)** State the gradient of the graph at B. gradient =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(ii)** State why the gradient has this value at B. **[1]**

**(b)** State and explain the relationship between the shaded areas above and below the time axis. **[3]**

**(c)** Another rocket, of the same size and mass, opens a parachute at point B.

On Fig. 3.1, sketch a possible graph of its speed from B until it reaches the ground. **[3]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | 10 m / s$^{2}$ignore sign | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | (same as) acceleration (of rocket at B) OR gravitational acceleration | B1 |
> | (b) | same area | B1 |
> |  | area represents distance travelled | B1 |
> |  | distance up = distance down<br>OR overall displacement = 0<br>OR area above = distance up AND area below = distance below | B1 |
> | (c) | any three from:<br>• all of graph below x-axis after B<br>• final section horizontal and above CD AND gradient always \< 0<br>• continuous graph from B until time \> at DE<br>• new area not clearly different from old | B3 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Very few candidates answered this part correctly. Even the majority of the higher achieving candidates did not appreciate the meaning of the gradient of a graph, often quoting 0, which was the value of speed at B.
>
> **(b)** Many candidates gained full credit. A common omission was not stating that the shaded areas were equal. Many of the lower-achieving candidates gave descriptions of the motion of the rocket as relating to the lines on the speed-time graph rather than considering the areas between the lines and the axes.
>
> **(c)** This part was better answered and many candidates gained part or full credit. The most frequent shortcoming was not showing that the rocket with a parachute would take more time to descend.
