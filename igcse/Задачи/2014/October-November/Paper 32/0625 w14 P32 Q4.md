> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Кинетическая энергия ветра мощность турбины и плотность воздуха.

Fig. 4.1 shows a small wind-turbine used to generate electricity.

![[0625-w14-32-p6-f1.png]]

**Fig. 4.1**

The wind-turbine drives an electric generator.

The wind blows with a velocity of 7.0 m / s at right angles to the plane of the turbine. The mass of air passing per second through the turbine is 6.7 kg.

**(a)** **(i)** Calculate the kinetic energy of the air blown through the turbine per second.

kinetic energy =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(ii)** Only 8% of this energy is converted to electrical energy.

Calculate the power output of the electric generator.

power output =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(b)** The volume of air passing through the turbine each second is 5.6 m$^{3}$ (flow rate is 5.6 m$^{3}$ / s).

Calculate the density of the air.

density of air =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(c)** The turbine turns a generator.

Describe the essential action within the generator that produces electricity. **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)(i)** (KE = 24.5 × 6.7 =) 164 J OR 160 J
>
> **(a)(ii)** 0.08 × candidate’s (a)(i) correctly evaluated
>
> **(b)** ( ρ = 6.72÷5.6 =) 1.2 kg / m$^{3}$

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | KE = ½ mv$^{2}$ in any form OR ½ mv$^{2}$ | C1 |
> |  | (KE = 24.5 × 6.7 =) 164 J OR 160 J | A1 |
> | (a)(ii) | efficiency = output (power) ÷ input (power)<br>OR <u>useful power ÷</u> input (power) | C1 |
> |  | 0.08 × candidate’s (a)(i) correctly evaluated | A1 |
> | (b) | use of ρ = m÷V in any form OR m÷V | C1 |
> |  | ( ρ = 6.72÷5.6 =) 1.2 kg / m$^{3}$ | A1 |
> | (c) | <u>rotation/movement of wire/coil</u> OR <u>rotation/movement of magnet</u> | B1 |
> |  | <u>consistent with above mark: in magnetic field / between magnetic poles /</u><br>cutting magnetic field OR in coil / near wire | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** The vast majority of candidates gained full credit. Some candidates correctly quoted the kinetic energy equation but failed to square *v* in their calculation.
>
> **(ii)** Most candidates gained full credit, but an appreciable number gave the unit of power as joules and there was sometimes confusion in applying the efficiency equation.
>
> **(b)** Most candidates correctly evaluated the density of the air.
>
> **(c)** There were many excellent answers giving clear descriptions, which scored full credit. Many candidates, however, gave an answer in terms of energy transfers, which did not address the question.
