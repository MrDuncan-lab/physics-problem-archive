> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выпрямитель телефона и расчёт мощности зарядного устройства.

A charger for a cellphone (mobile phone) is marked:

input: a.c. 240 V, 50 Hz, 80 mA. output: d.c. 5.3 V, 500 mA.

**(a)** State

**(i)** the component in the charger that converts a.c. to d.c.,

**(ii)** the quantity that has the value 50 Hz. **[2]**

**(b)** Calculate

**(i)** the output power of the charger,

output power =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(ii)** the energy transferred in the output circuit when the cellphone is charged for 1.5 hours.

energy =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(c)** In the following list, underline the quantity that is stored in the battery of the cellphone.

voltage current power energy **[1]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)(i)** 2.6 W OR 2600 mW
>
> **(b)(ii)** 14000 J

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | rectifier / diode | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | frequency (of A.C. supply) | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | (P =) IV OR 0.5 × 5.3 OR 500 × 5.3 | C1 |
> |  | 2.6 W OR 2600 mW | A1 |
> | (b)(ii) | (E =) Pt OR IVt OR 2.65 × 1.5 × 3600 OR 0.5 × 5.3 × 1.5 × 3600 | C1 |
> |  | 14000 J | A1 |
> | (c) | energy only underlined | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i) (ii)** Few candidates could identify a rectifier or diode for **(i)**. Rather more could give the answer ‘frequency’ for **(ii)**.
>
> **(b) (i) (ii)** The correct formulae for both parts were usually quoted and appropriate numbers substituted. Because of the use of mA in the in the question, many candidates lost credit for incorrect units in one or other part.
>
> **(c)** Each of the given choice of quantities was underlined with approximately equal frequency, suggesting that many candidates were guessing at the answer.
