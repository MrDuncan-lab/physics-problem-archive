**Turn over for Question 2**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(c)(i)** = 7.0 m / s$^{2}$

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)(b)** Many candidates identified all six graphs correctly. Where a single error was made, it was usually in identifying the graph F as representing stationary motion rather than constant speed.
>
> **(c) (i)** Errors in recalling the required formula and performing the calculation were rare. However, the unit was frequently stated as m / s rather than m / s$^{2}$.
>
> **(ii)** In **1**, many candidates had not appreciated that at the point when the maximum speed had been reached the acceleration must have ceased. The answer was consequently stated by many candidates as 10 m / s$^{2}$ rather than zero. In **2**, the answer needed to express in some way that there was no resultant force. Some candidates, who had correctly stated the acceleration as zero, did not convey this idea correctly.
