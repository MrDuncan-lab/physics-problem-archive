> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** A correct response was usually given.
>
> **(b)** This question and the question following were not well answered. In many cases candidates had not appreciated that for evaporation to take place water molecules escape from the surface after having gained sufficient energy. Common errors included stating the process of evaporation rather than describing it. Other vague responses in terms of kinetic energy and convection, and incorrect responses in terms of expansion of molecules, were also seen.
>
> **(c)** Only the highest scoring candidates obtained any credit for their responses to this question. Many candidates repeated incorrect responses given in part **(b)**.
