> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Возобновляемая энергия и работа гидроэлектростанции.

**(a)** State two sources of energy that are renewable.

1 \_\_\_\_\_\_

2 \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(b)** State two sources of energy that will eventually run out or become uneconomic to extract.

1 \_\_\_\_\_\_

2 \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(c)** In a hydroelectric power station, water is stored high up behind a dam.

**(i)** State the type of energy stored by the water. **[1]**

**(ii)** Explain what happens to the water in order to generate electricity. **[2]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | any two from:<br>waves<br>wind<br>hydro(electric)<br>tidal<br>geothermal<br>solar<br>biofuel / biomass<br>wood | B2 |
> | (b) | any two from:<br>coal<br>oil OR any stated refined fuel<br>(natural) gas<br>nuclear | B2 |
> | (c)(i) | gravitational potential (energy) | B1 |
> | (c)(ii) | water falls / drops OR GPE transferred to KE | B1 |
> |  | spins / turns / moves turbine /generator | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Correct responses were usually stated.
>
> **(b)** There were many correct responses. Some candidates gained only partial credit since one of their answers replicated their other acceptable response, for example, fossil fuels being given alongside coal, gas or oil.
>
> **(c) (i)** There were many good answers from middle and higher scoring candidates. Incorrect responses included either kinetic, tidal or wave energy.
>
> **(ii)** Many candidates scored credit for at least one of the available marking points.
