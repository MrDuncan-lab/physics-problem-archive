> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Продольные звуковые волны и электромагнитный спектр.

**(a)** In the space below, draw a diagram to represent a sound wave.

On your diagram, mark and label

**(i)** **two** consecutive compressions and **two** consecutive rarefactions,

**(ii)** the wavelength of the wave. **[3]**

**(b)** Fig. 7.1 shows part of the electromagnetic spectrum.

![[0625-w07-3-p9-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 7.1**

**(i)** On Fig. 7.1, label the positions of γ-rays, visible light waves and radio waves. **[1]**

**(ii)** State which of the three types of wave in **(i)** has the lowest frequency. **[1]**

**(iii)** State the approximate value of the speed in air of radio waves. **[1]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | diagram showing compressions and rarefactions<br>(could be either spaced vertical lines or dots, or coil or sine wave) | B1 |
> |  | 2C’s and 2R’s in approx correct place | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | wavelength correctly marked, by eye | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | all 3 in correct positions | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | radio (waves) | B1 |
> | (b)(iii) | 3 × 10$^{8}$ m/s | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Diagrams of longitudinal waves are particularly difficult to draw and many candidates are to be complimented on the quality of their attempts. Generally the best attempts were a series of lines or dots with varying spacing. Some “sine” wave graphs were regarded as valid answers, but often these were difficult to interpret, especially as many did not have their axes labelled.
>
> **(b)** All three parts of this were well answered, although the speed of radio waves was not so frequently known.
