> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Схема логических элементов И и НЕ для сигнализации о микроклимате.

**(a)** Fig. 10.1 shows an AND gate with two inputs A and B and one output.

![[0625-w07-3-p13-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 10.1**

State the output when

**(i)** A is high and B is low, **[1]**

**(ii)** both A and B are low. **[1]**

**(b)** An electrical thermometer in a greenhouse gives a low output if the temperature is too low.

A humidity sensor in the same greenhouse gives a high output if the humidity in the greenhouse is too high.

An alarm sounds when both the temperature is too low and the humidity is too high.

**(i)** Complete the diagram below to show how a NOT gate and an AND gate may be used to provide the required output to the alarm. **[2]**

![[0625-w07-3-p13-f2.svg]]

**(ii)** On your diagram, use either ‘high’ or ‘low’ to indicate the level of the inputs and outputs of both gates when the alarm sounds. **[2]**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Logic gates were not very well understood by candidates sitting this paper. Having said that, there were a small number of excellent answers from candidates who had been well taught. This topic requires a different kind of knowledge from most other parts of the syllabus, and teachers would help their students by making sure that adequate time is spent on it. There will be questions on this topic from time to time, as with all other topics.
