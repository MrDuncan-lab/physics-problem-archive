> Изменение уровня ртути и давления в барометре при росте атмосферного давления.

The diagram shows a simple mercury barometer, used to measure atmospheric pressure.

![[0625-w07-1-p7-f1.svg]]

Atmospheric pressure increases.

What happens to the level L and to the pressure at P?

|  | level L | pressure at P |
|---|---|---|
| A | falls | increases |
| B | falls | stays the same |
| C | rises | increases |
| D | rises | stays the same |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
