> Выбор приборов для измерения плотности жидкости и правильного твёрдого тела.

A student is trying to find the density of water and of a large, regularly-shaped solid.

Which apparatus is needed to find the density of both?

**A** balance, clock, ruler  
**B** balance, measuring cylinder, ruler  
**C** balance, measuring cylinder, string  
**D** clock, ruler, string

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
