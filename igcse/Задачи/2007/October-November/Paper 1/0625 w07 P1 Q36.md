> Работа контактных колец и щёток генератора переменного тока.

Which parts of an a.c. generator slide past each other when the generator is working?

**A** brushes and coil  
**B** coil and magnets  
**C** magnets and slip rings  
**D** slip rings and brushes

> [!answer]- Ответ
