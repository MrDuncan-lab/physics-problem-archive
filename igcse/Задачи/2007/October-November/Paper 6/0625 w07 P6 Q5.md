*V* =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

$\textit{density} = \dfrac{m}{V}$,

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Most candidates recorded the values of *V*$_{1}$ and *V*$_{2}$ correctly and went on to a successful calculation of *V* followed by correctly calculating the density.
>
> **(b)** In the table headings the units were often correct but, a significant number of candidates left the *V*$_{2}$ and *V*$_{1}$ headings blank.
>
> **(c)** Relatively few candidates realised that since a brief explanation was required and only 1 mark was available that they should have made the point that the same method would be appropriate provided a known mass of sand was used. Any mention of counting sand grains did not gain the mark.
