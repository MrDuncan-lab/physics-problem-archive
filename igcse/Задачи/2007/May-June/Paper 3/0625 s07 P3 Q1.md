> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Центростремительная сила и скорость движения модели автомобиля по кругу.

Fig. 1.1 shows a model car moving clockwise around a horizontal circular track.

![[0625-s07-3-p2-f1.svg]]

circular

track

**Fig. 1.1**

**(a)** A force acts on the car to keep it moving in a circle.

**(i)** Draw an arrow on Fig. 1.1 to show the direction of this force. **[1]**

**(ii)** The speed of the car increases. State what happens to the magnitude of this force. **[1]**

**(b)** **(i)** The car travels too quickly and leaves the track at P. On Fig. 1.1, draw an arrow to show the direction of travel after it has left the track. **[1]**

**(ii)** In terms of the forces acting on the car, suggest why it left the track at P. **[2]**

**(c)** The car, starting from rest, completes one lap of the track in 10 s. Its motion is shown graphically in Fig. 1.2.

![[0625-s07-3-p3-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 1.2**

**(i)** Describe the motion between 3.0 s and 10.0 s after the car has started. **[1]**

**(ii)** Use Fig. 1.2 to calculate the circumference of the track.

circumference =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(iii)** Calculate the increase in speed per second during the time 0 to 3.0 s.

increase in speed per second =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(c)(ii)** 212.5 cm any no s.f. ğ 2
>
> **(c)(iii)** 8.33 cm/s any no s.f. ğ 2 OR 8⅓ cm/s accept cm/s$^{2}$

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Most candidates knew about centripetal force and that it would increase when the car speeds up.
>
> **(b)** It was pleasing that most candidates attempted some form of tangential arrow for part **(i)**, even if some were too careless to draw it sufficiently accurately. However, answers to part **(ii)** were mostly of the form “the centripetal force was not big enough”. What was required was an understanding of the role of friction between the tyres and the track.
>
> **(c)** Most candidates of any ability were able to score well on this part of the question. In part **(i)**, “constant motion” was not regarded as satisfactory, but in order not to penalise too many candidates “constant velocity” was awarded the mark, even though it was the speed, not the velocity, which remained constant, as the track was circular
>
