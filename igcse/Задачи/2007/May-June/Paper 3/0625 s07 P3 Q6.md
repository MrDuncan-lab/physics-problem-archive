**Fig. 6.1**

**(a)** The ray FE is partly reflected and partly refracted at E.

**(i)** On Fig. 6.1, draw in the approximate path of the refracted ray, within and beyond the block. Label the ray *refracted ray*. **[1]**

**(ii)** On Fig. 6.1, draw in the path of the reflected ray. Label the ray *reflected ray*. **[1]**

**(b)** A second ray, almost parallel to AE, strikes the block at E and is partly refracted at an angle of refraction of 43°.

**(i)** State an approximate value for the angle of incidence at E. **[1]**

**(ii)** State an approximate value for the critical angle for the light in the glass block. **[1]**

**(iii)** Calculate an approximate value for the refractive index of the glass of the block.

refractive index =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(c)** The speed of the light along ray FE is 3.0 x 10$^{8}$ m/s. Calculate the speed of the refracted light in the glass block.

speed =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)(iii)** 1.466 or 1.47 or 1.5 c.a.o. any no s.f. ğ 2
>
> **(c)** speed in glass = 2(.0) × 10$^{8}$ m/s e.c.f. any no s.f. ğ 2

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** The majority of candidates could draw in the two rays with acceptable accuracy.
>
> **(b)** The critical angle part of this question was approached in a slightly unusual way, and this seemed to confuse many candidates. The second ray being “almost parallel to AE” should have indicated to the candidates that this was the critical ray. For some, it did, but for others it did not. The marking scheme made allowance for both.
>
> **(c)** Errors carried forward from (b) were not penalised further in this part. Most had some idea of n = speed in air/speed in glass.
>
