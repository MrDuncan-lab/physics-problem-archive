> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Экспериментальное измерение скорости звука на школьном дворе.

Two students are asked to determine the speed of sound in air on the school playing fields.

**(a)** List the apparatus they need. **[1]**

**(b)** List the readings that the students need to take. **[1]**

**(c)** State how the speed of sound is calculated from the readings. **[1]**

**(d)** State one precaution that could be taken to improve the accuracy of the value obtained. **[1]**

**(e)** The table gives some speeds.

![[0625-s07-3-p10-f1.svg]]

Place a tick in the table to show the speed which is closest to

**(i)** the speed of sound in air,

**(ii)** the speed of sound in water. **[2]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | source of sound (e.g. gun/hooter), tape (100 m), stopwatch<br>NOT clock, metre rule (unless lab method) | B1 |
> | (b) | <u>distance and time between “flash and bang” (must be clear)</u> | B1 |
> | (c) | distance/time OR d/t OR 2d/t | B1 |
> | (d) | further apart/more accurate timer/repeat/any other | B1 |
> | (e) | speed of sound in air, tick 100 | B1 |
> |  | speed of sound in water, tick 1000 | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This was another question in the style of **Question 5**, and again the same weaknesses were displayed, namely imprecise descriptions of what had to be measured and how. The most common omission was the description of how the person doing the timing knew when the sound had been made. “Measure the time between making the sound and hearing it” was not adequate for (b); instead something like “the time between the flash and the bang” would have been satisfactory. In part **(e)**, many were able to choose appropriate speed for sound in air and in water, which was good because the answer for speed in air, in particular, did not look correct at first.
