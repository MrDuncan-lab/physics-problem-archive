> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The details in the table were usually correctly identified, so that many candidates scored all four marks. However, much more difficulty was found in explaining what happens during evaporation. It would be well to point out that in answers such as these it is not usually acceptable to use in the answer the term used in the question. Most answers, which showed any understanding, were along the lines of “The molecules near the surface gain energy to evaporate.” The basic fact needed in the answer is that evaporation is molecules breaking free, and then some further elaboration.
