# Question 3 (7 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2007 · Paper 2 — Core theory · вопрос 3  
Источник: [[_pdf/2007/0625_s07_qp_2.pdf#page=6|условие, стр. 6–7]] · [[_pdf/2007/0625_s07_ms_2.pdf#page=3|схема оценивания, стр. 3]] · [[_pdf/2007/0625_s07_er.pdf#page=3|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Момент силы для открывания картона и устойчивость коробки спичек.

A piece of stiff cardboard is stuck to a plank of wood by means of two sticky-tape “hinges”. This is shown in Fig. 3.1.

![[0625-s07-2-p6-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 3.1**

**(a)** The cardboard is lifted as shown, using a force applied either at A or B or C.

**(i)** On Fig. 3.1, draw the force in the position where its value will be as small as possible. **[2]**

**(ii)** Explain why the position you have chosen in **(a)(i)** results in the smallest force. **[1]**

**(b)** Initially, the cardboard is flat on the plank of wood. A box of matches is placed on it. The cardboard is then slowly raised at the left hand edge, as shown in Fig. 3.2.

![[0625-s07-2-p6-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 3.2**

State the condition for the box of matches to fall over. **[2]**

**(c)** The box of matches is opened, as shown in Fig. 3.3. The procedure in **(b)** is repeated.

![[0625-s07-2-p7-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 3.3**

**(i)** Complete the sentence below, using either the words “greater than” or “the same as” or “less than”.

In Fig. 3.3, the angle through which the cardboard can be lifted before

the box of matches falls is  \_\_\_\_\_\_  the angle

before the box of matches falls in Fig. 3.2. **[1]**

**(ii)** Give a reason for your answer to **(c)(i)**. **[1]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)(i)** upward vertical force OR upward force at rt. angles to card C
>
> **(b)** when vertical through C of M lies outside base (idea of) C

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | indication of force at A F | M1 |
> |  | upward vertical force OR upward force at rt. angles to card C | A1 |
> | (a)(ii) | largest distance from hinge F | B1 |
> | (b) | when C of M lies outside base (idea of) F | C1 |
> |  | when vertical through C of M lies outside base (idea of) C | A1 |
> | (c)(i) | less than F | B1 |
> | (c)(ii) | idea of C of M of box raised OR matchbox less stable C<br>NOT matchbox is taller | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The whole of this question was poorly done. Very few candidates have any understanding of moments or stability. Even in part **(a)** there was only a handful who knew that the least force would be applied at A, and very few of these showed the force in an upward direction.
