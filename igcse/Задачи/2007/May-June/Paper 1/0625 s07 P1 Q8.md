> Изменение движения и формы мяча под действием силы.

A force acts on a moving rubber ball.

How many of the following changes could happen to the ball because of the force?

- a change in direction
- a change in shape
- a change in mass
- a change in speed

**A** 1  
**B** 2  
**C** 3  
**D** 4

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
