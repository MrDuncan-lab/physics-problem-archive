> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравниваются измерения объёма глины по размерам и вытесненной воде, оцениваются погрешности и способы их уменьшения.

A student determines the volume of a block of modelling clay by two methods.

**Method 1**

He measures the block of modelling clay shown full size in Fig. 1.1.

![[0625-w25-63-p2-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 1.1**

**(a)** **(i)** Measure the length l, width w and height h of the block of modelling clay on Fig. 1.1.

Record your values in centimetres to the nearest millimetre.

length l =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm

width w =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm

height h =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm **[2]**

**(ii)** Calculate a value V$_{\text{A}}$ for the volume of the block. Use your measurements from (a)(i) and the equation:

V$_{\text{A}}$= l × w × h.

V$_{\text{A}}$=  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm$^{3}$ **[1]**

**(b)** Suggest why V$_{\text{A}}$ is only an approximate value for the volume of the block.

Describe how the accuracy of V$_{\text{A}}$ can be improved.

suggestion  \_\_\_\_\_\_

improvement  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(c)** Another student measures the dimensions of a block of clay which is much smaller than the block in (a). Suggest why these measurements may not be as accurate as yours, even if they are done carefully. **[1]**

**Method 2**

**(d)** **(i)** The student pours water into a measuring cylinder.

Record the volume V$_{1}$ of the water in the measuring cylinder shown in Fig. 1.2.

V$_{1}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm$^{3}$ **[1]**

![[0625-w25-63-p3-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 1.2**

**(ii)** Describe briefly how a measuring cylinder is read to obtain an accurate value for the volume of water. **[1]**

**(e)** The student lowers the modelling clay into the water using a loop of thread, as shown in Fig. 1.3.

![[0625-w25-63-p4-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 1.3**

Record the new reading V$_{2}$ shown on the measuring cylinder scale in Fig.1.3.

V$_{2}$=  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm$^{3}$

Calculate another value V$_{\text{B}}$ for the volume of the block.

Use your measurements from (d)(i) and (e) and the equation: V$_{\text{B}}$= (V$_{2}$ – V$_{1}$).

V$_{\text{B}}$=  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm$^{3}$ **[1]**

**(f)** Suggest **one** possible source of inaccuracy in Method 2 and suggest **one** improvement to reduce its effect.

source of inaccuracy  \_\_\_\_\_\_

improvement  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**
