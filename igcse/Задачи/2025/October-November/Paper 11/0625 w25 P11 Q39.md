> Распознавание Солнца как звезды среднего размера.

Which statement about the Sun is correct?

**A** The Sun consists of mainly carbon and nitrogen.  
**B** The Sun is the largest planet in the Solar System.  
**C** The Sun is a medium-sized star.  
**D** The Sun radiates most of its energy in the gamma-ray region of the electromagnetic

spectrum.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
