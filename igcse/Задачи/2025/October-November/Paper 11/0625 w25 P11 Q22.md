> Определение ультрафиолетового излучения, используемого для обнаружения защитной маркировки.

Valuable objects are often marked with a special ink. In visible light, the ink **cannot** be seen.

The ink can be seen when exposed to the right type of electromagnetic waves.

Which type of electromagnetic waves are used for this type of security marking?

**A** infrared  
**B** microwaves  
**C** ultraviolet  
**D** X-rays

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
