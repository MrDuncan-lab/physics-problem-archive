> Выбор формулы сопротивления через напряжение и силу тока.

Which expression is used to calculate electrical resistance?

**A** charge ÷ potential difference  
**B** current ÷ potential difference  
**C** potential difference ÷ charge  
**D** potential difference ÷ current

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
