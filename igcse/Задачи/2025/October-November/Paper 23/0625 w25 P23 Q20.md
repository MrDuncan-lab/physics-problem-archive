> Расчёт угла преломления по скоростям света и углу падения.

The speed of light in a vacuum is 3.0 × 10$^{8}$ m / s.

The speed of light in glass is 2.2 × 10$^{8}$ m / s.

A light ray in a vacuum enters glass with an angle of incidence of 40°.

What is the angle of refraction within the glass?

**A** 28°  
**B** 29°  
**C** 55°  
**D** 61°

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**
