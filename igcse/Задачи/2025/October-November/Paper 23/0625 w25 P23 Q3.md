> Описание изменений скорости и ускорения падающего тела при достижении предельной скорости.

An object is falling in a uniform gravitational field.

After a certain time, it reaches terminal velocity.

Which row is correct?

|  | velocity of object | acceleration of object |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | increases then decreases | positive then negative |
| **B** | increases then decreases | positive then zero |
| **C** | increases then constant | positive then negative |
| **D** | increases then constant | positive then zero |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
