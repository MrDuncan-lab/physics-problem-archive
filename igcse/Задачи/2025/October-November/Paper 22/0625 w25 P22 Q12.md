> Сопоставление относительного теплового расширения веществ с их частичным строением.

The diagram represents the particle arrangements in samples of three different substances, P, Q

and R.

![[0625-w25-22-p6-f1.svg]]

The samples have equal volumes and the same temperature.

The samples are heated. The temperature increase of each sample is the same and **no** changes of state take place.

Which row shows possible percentage increases in volume for the three samples?

|  | sample of substance P / % | sample of substance Q / % | sample of substance R / % |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | 0.066 | 1.9 | 7.2 |
| **B** | 0.066 | 7.2 | 1.9 |
| **C** | 1.9 | 0.066 | 7.2 |
| **D** | 1.9 | 7.2 | 0.066 |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
