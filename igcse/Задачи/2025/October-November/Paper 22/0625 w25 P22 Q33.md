> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определение обратного отклонения альфа-частицы при приближении к центру ядра золота.

The arrow shows the path of an α-particle travelling towards the centre of a gold nucleus.

![[0625-w25-22-p12-f3.svg]]

Which description of the path of the α-particle after striking the gold nucleus is correct?

**A** The α-particle is deflected through a very small angle towards the top of the page.  
**B** The α-particle is deflected back along its initial path.  
**C** The α-particle carries on in a straight line with **no** deflection.  
**D** The α-particle is captured by the nucleus and goes into orbit around it.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
