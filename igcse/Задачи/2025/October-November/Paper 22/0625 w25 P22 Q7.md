> Описание изменений потенциальной, кинетической и упругой энергии при движении груза на пружине.

A mass is suspended from a vertical spring. The mass is pulled downwards causing the spring to extend further. The mass is held in this position and then released.

Which energy changes occur during the initial movement of the mass when the mass is released?

|  | gravitational potential energy store | kinetic energy store | elastic potential energy store |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | decreases | decreases | increases |
| **B** | decreases | increases | increases |
| **C** | increases | decreases | decreases |
| **D** | increases | increases | decreases |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
