> Определение разрезного коллектора как детали, меняющей направление тока в катушке двигателя.

The diagrams show the coil in a d.c. electric motor in two positions. As the coil rotates through

180°, the direction of the current in the coil reverses from PQRS to SRQP.

![[0625-w25-22-p12-f2.svg]]

Which part of the motor is responsible for reversing the current?

**A** the brushes  
**B** the coil  
**C** the magnets  
**D** the split-ring commutator

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
