> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определение расстояния и скорости зеркального изображения падающей капли.

A water droplet is falling towards a horizontal plane mirror.

At one moment, the droplet is a distance of 1.5 m above the mirror and falling at a speed of 2.0 m / s.

What is the distance between the droplet and its image and what is the speed of the image at this moment?

|  | distance between water droplet and its image / m | speed of image m/s |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | 1.5 | 2.0 |
| **B** | 1.5 | 4.0 |
| **C** | 3.0 | 2.0 |
| **D** | 3.0 | 4.0 |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
