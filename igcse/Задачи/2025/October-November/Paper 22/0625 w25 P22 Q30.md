> Выбор скорости движения провода и силы магнита для максимальной индуцированной ЭДС.

A length of wire moves across the space between the poles of a magnet. A voltmeter measures

the electromotive force (e.m.f.) generated between the two ends of the wire.

What gives the highest reading on the voltmeter?

|  | speed of wire | strength of magnet |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | fast | weak |
| **B** | fast | strong |
| **C** | slow | weak |
| **D** | slow | strong |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
