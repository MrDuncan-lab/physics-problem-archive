**(c)** Curium-242 is a radioactive isotope. It emits alpha particles. It has a half-life of 160 days.

After 480 days, the mass of curium-242 in a sample is 2.4 × 10$^{\text{–3}}$ g.

Calculate the initial mass of curium-242 in the sample.

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(c)** 1.9 × 10$^{\text{–2}}$ g **OR** 1.9 × 10$^{\text{–5}}$ kg
