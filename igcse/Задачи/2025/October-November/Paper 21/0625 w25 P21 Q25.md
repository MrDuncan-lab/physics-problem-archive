> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Расчёт времени зарядки аккумулятора по заряду и силе тока.

A fully charged battery stores a charge of 18 000 C. The battery is charged with a current of

830 mA.

How long does it take to fully charge a completely discharged battery?

**A** about 10 minutes  
**B** about 3.5 hours  
**C** about 4 hours  
**D** about 6 hours

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
