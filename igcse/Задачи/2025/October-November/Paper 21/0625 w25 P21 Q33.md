> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Расчёт числа нейтронов, испускаемых при делении ядра плутония.

$^{239}_{94}$Pu is an isotope of plutonium.

The nucleus can absorb a neutron and then split to form two other nuclei.

Two nuclei that form are gold,$^{207}_{79}$ Au, and phosphorus, $_{15}^{31}$P.

In this reaction, neutrons are also produced.

What is the number of neutrons produced when the nucleus splits?

**A** 1  
**B** 2  
**C** 4  
**D** 5

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
