> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определение преимущества независимой работы параллельно подключённых ламп.

Lamps can be connected in series or in parallel to a power supply.

What is an advantage of connecting the lamps in parallel rather than in series?

**A** All of the lamps can be switched on and off using the same switch.  
**B** Fewer connecting leads are needed to make the circuit.  
**C** When one lamp stops working, the others may continue to work.  
**D** Less power is transferred from the supply.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
