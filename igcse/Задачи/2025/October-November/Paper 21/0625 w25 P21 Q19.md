> Расчёт скорости света в жидкости по геометрии преломления.

A beam of light passes through a vacuum and then enters a transparent liquid. The diagram

shows the path it takes.

![[0625-w25-21-p8-f1.svg]]

The light travels through the vacuum at a speed of 3.0 × 10$^{8}$ m / s.

What is the speed of light in the liquid?

**A** 1.9 × 10$^{8}$ m / s  
**B** 2.0 × 10$^{8}$ m / s  
**C** 4.6 × 10$^{8}$ m / s  
**D** 4.8 × 10$^{8}$ m / s

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
