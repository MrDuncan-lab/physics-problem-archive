> Выбор изменения, уменьшающего вращающее действие магнитного поля на катушку с током.

A current-carrying coil in the magnetic field between the poles of two magnets experiences a

turning effect.

Which change would decrease the turning effect?

**A** decreasing the distance between the magnets  
**B** increasing the current in the coil  
**C** using stronger magnets  
**D** decreasing the number of turns on the coil

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
