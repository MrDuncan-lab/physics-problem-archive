> Выбор высокого напряжения и толстых кабелей для уменьшения потерь при передаче электроэнергии.

Cables transmit electrical power from the output of the transformer at a power station to the input

of another transformer at a substation.

![[0625-w25-21-p12-f1.svg]]

The power at the output of the transformer at the power station is 400 MW.

Which situation delivers the most power to the input of the transformer at the substation?

|  | potential difference at power station transformer output / kV | diameter of cables |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | 200 | large |
| **B** | 200 | small |
| **C** | 400 | large |
| **D** | 400 | small |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
