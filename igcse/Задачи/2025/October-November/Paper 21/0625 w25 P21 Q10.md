> Определение уменьшения скорости частиц и давления газа при охлаждении закрытой колбы.

A flask with a tight-fitting stopper contains a gas. The gas and flask are initially at room

temperature.

![[0625-w25-21-p5-f1.svg]]

The gas and flask are cooled.

Which row describes the average speed of the gas particles and the pressure of the gas as the temperature decreases?

|  | average speed of the gas particles | pressure of the gas |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | decreases | decreases |
| **B** | decreases | stays the same |
| **C** | increases | increases |
| **D** | increases | stays the same |
