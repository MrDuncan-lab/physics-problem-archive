Ultraviolet radiation and infrared radiation are both parts of the electromagnetic spectrum.

However, they differ in wavelength and frequency.

Which row describes how they differ?

|  | ultraviolet | infrared |
|---|---|---|
| **A** **B** **C** **D** | longer wavelength than infrared longer wavelength than infrared shorter wavelength than infrared shorter wavelength than infrared | higher frequency than ultraviolet lower frequency than ultraviolet higher frequency than ultraviolet lower frequency than ultraviolet |
