# Question 7 (8 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2025 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 7  
Источник: [[_pdf/2025/0625_w25_qp_32.pdf#page=11|условие, стр. 11–12]] · [[_pdf/2025/0625_w25_ms_32.pdf#page=13|схема оценивания, стр. 13]]

Темы: [[4.3.2 Series and parallel circuits]], [[4.2.4 Resistance]], [[4.2.5 Electrical energy and electrical power]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определяется напряжение батареи, рассчитываются сопротивление и энергия лампы, выбирается подключение второй лампы для увеличения тока.

Fig. 7.1 shows a lamp connected in series with four 1.5 V cells and an ammeter. A voltmeter is connected across the lamp.

![[0625-w25-32-p11-f1.svg]]

Fig. 7.1

**(a)** Determine the potential difference across the four cells.

potential difference =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ V **[1]**

**(b)** **(i)** The reading on the ammeter is 0.64 A.

The reading on the voltmeter is 5.8 V. Calculate the resistance of the lamp.

resistance of the lamp =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ Ω **[3]**

**(ii)** Calculate the energy transferred by the lamp in 140 s.

energy transferred =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ J **[3]**

**(c)** On Fig. 7.1, draw a second lamp so that there is a bigger current in the ammeter when the second lamp is connected. **[1]**

\* 0000800000012 \* DFD

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)(i)** 9.1 (Ω)
>
> **(b)(ii)** 520 (J)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | 6(.0) (V) | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | 9.1 (Ω) | A3 |
> |  | 5.8 ÷ 0.64 | C2 |
> |  | (resistance =) potential difference ÷ current | C1 |
> | (b)(ii) | 520 (J) | A3 |
> |  | 5.8 × 0.64 × 140 **or** *3.712* × 140 **or** 5.8 × *89.6* **or** 0.64 × *812* | C2 |
> |  | (energy transferred =) potential difference × current × time | C1 |
> | (c) | lamp in parallel with original lamp | B1 |
