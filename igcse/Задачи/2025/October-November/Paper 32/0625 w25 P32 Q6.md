# Question 6 (6 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2025 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 6  
Источник: [[_pdf/2025/0625_w25_qp_32.pdf#page=10|условие, стр. 10–11]] · [[_pdf/2025/0625_w25_ms_32.pdf#page=13|схема оценивания, стр. 13]]

Темы: [[3.2.3 Thin lenses]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Обозначаются элементы собирающей линзы и строятся лучи для увеличенного перевёрнутого действительного изображения.

Fig. 6.1 shows an object near a converging lens. The diagram also shows the image of the object that is produced by the lens.

![[0625-w25-32-p10-f1.svg]]

Fig. 6.1

**(a)** State the name of the line labelled Y. **[1]**

**(b)** State the name of the points that are labelled F. **[1]**

**(c)** The tip of the object is point O. OA shows the path of a ray from the object as it travels to the lens. On Fig. 6.1, draw the path of this ray as it leaves the lens and travels to the image. **[1]**

**(d)** On Fig. 6.1, locate the tip of the image. Draw the path of another ray from point O, through the lens and to the tip of the image. **[2]**

**(e)** The image in Fig. 6.1 is enlarged and inverted. State one more characteristic of the image. **[1]**

\* 0000800000011 \* DFD

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(d)** **or** • ray from top of object through F to lens • horizontal paraxial ray from lens to top of image

> | (a) | <u>principal axis</u> | B1 |
> | (b) | principal focus **or** principal focii **or** focal point (s) | B1 |
> | (c) | ray continued through F to top of image | B1 |
> | (d) | **one** of the following:<br>• ray from top of object to centre of lens<br>• ray continued to top of image | M1 |
> |  | **or**<br>• ray from top of object through F to lens<br>• horizontal paraxial ray from lens to top of image | A1 |
> | (e) | real | B1 |
