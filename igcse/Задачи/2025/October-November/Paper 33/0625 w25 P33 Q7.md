# Question 7 (10 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2025 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 3 · вопрос 7

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбирается число элементов батареи, рисуется схема фонарика и рассчитываются сопротивление и мощность лампы.

**(a)** A student is making a torch.

**(i)** The torch needs a potential difference (p.d) of 6.0 V. The electromotive force (e.m.f.) of each cell for the torch is 1.5 V.

State the number of cells that the torch needs.

number of cells =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(ii)** The student has the following components for the electrical part of the torch:

a 6.0 V lamp a switch several 1.5 V cells connecting wire

Draw a circuit diagram to show how the components are connected in series to make the

torch. Use standard circuit symbols. **[3]**

**(b)** The p.d. across the lamp is 6.0 V.

The current in the lamp is 0.25 A

**(i)** Calculate the resistance of the lamp.

resistance =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ Ω **[3]**

**(ii)** Calculate the power of the lamp.

power =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ W **[3]**

\* 0000800000013 \* DFD

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)(ii)** **three** (cell (s) / battery, lamp and switch) circuit symbols correct
>
> **(b)(i)** 24 (Ω)
>
> **(b)(ii)** 1.5 (W)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | 4 (cells) | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | **three** (cell (s) / battery, lamp and switch) circuit symbols correct | A2 |
> |  | **one or two** (from cell(s) / battery, lamp and switch) circuit symbols correct | C1 |
> |  | a complete circuit with cell (s), lamp and switch in series | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | 24 (Ω) | A3 |
> |  | 6(.0) ÷ 0.25 | C2 |
> |  | (resistance =) potential difference ÷ current | C1 |
> | (b)(ii) | 1.5 (W) | A3 |
> |  | 6(.0) × 0.25 | C2 |
> |  | (power =) potential difference × current | C1 |
