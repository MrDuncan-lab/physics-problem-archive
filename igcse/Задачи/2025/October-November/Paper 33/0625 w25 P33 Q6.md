> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определяются ось и фокусное расстояние собирающей линзы, строится перевёрнутое действительное изображение.

Fig. 6.1 shows the path of a ray of visible light travelling from the top of an object and passing through a converging lens.

![[0625-w25-33-p11-f1.svg]]

Fig. 6.1

**(a)** State what the line XY represents. **[1]**

**(b)** Determine the focal length of the converging lens.

focal length =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm **[1]**

**(c)** On Fig. 6.1, draw another ray from the object. Continue the path of the ray through the lens until it crosses the path of the ray shown in Fig. 6.1. **[2]**

**(d)** On Fig. 6.1, draw an arrow to represent the image. Label it ‘image’. **[2]**

\* 0000800000012 \* DFD

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(c)** **or** ray from top of object through principal focus on LHS of lens to the centre of the lens ray continued as a straight paraxial line to cross drawn ray
>
> **(d)** <u>upside down (vertical) arrow (labelled image) from principal axis to point where rays cross</u>
