> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитываются момент силы рычага, работа подъёма камня и мощность грузовика.

**(a)** A builder uses a metal bar to raise one end of the rock.

![[0625-w25-33-p4-f1.svg]]

Fig. 2.1 (not to scale)

Calculate the moment of the 320 N force about the pivot.

moment =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ Nm **[3]**

**(b)** The builder lifts another rock using a truck as shown in Fig. 2.2.

![[0625-w25-33-p4-f2.svg]]

Fig. 2.2 (not to scale)

The truck lifts the rock through a vertical height of 0.60 m.

The weight of the rock is 4800 N.

Calculate the work done in lifting the rock.

work done  \_\_\_\_\_\_ J **[3]**

\* 0000800000005 \* DFD

**(c)** The work done by the truck in lifting a different rock is 5800 J.

The truck lifts the rock in a time of 7.4 s.

Calculate the power of the truck in lifting the rock. Include the unit.

power =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ unit  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[4]**

\* 0000800000006 \* DFD

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** 380 (Nm)
>
> **(b)** 2900 (J)
>
> **(c)** 780 (W)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | 380 (Nm) | A3 |
> |  | 1.2 × 320 | C2 |
> |  | (clockwise moment =) force × (perpendicular) distance | C1 |
> | (b) | 2900 (J) | A3 |
> |  | 4800 × 0.6 | C2 |
> |  | (work done =) force × distance | C1 |
> | (c) | 780 (W) | A3 |
> |  | 5800 / 7.4 | C2 |
> |  | (power =) work(done) / time **or** energy / time | C1 |
> |  | W or watts | B1 |
