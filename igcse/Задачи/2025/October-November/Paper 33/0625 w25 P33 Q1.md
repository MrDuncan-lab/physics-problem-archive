Fig. 1.1

**(a)** Fig. 1.2 shows the speed-time graph for the stone.

![[0625-w25-33-p2-f2.svg]]

Fig. 1.2

**(i)** Determine the speed of the stone at time = 2.0 s.

speed =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ m / s **[1]**

**(ii)** Describe the motion of the stone during section AB of the graph in Fig. 1.2. **[1]**

**(iii)** Calculate the distance travelled by the stone between time = 0 and time = 3.4 s.

distance =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ m **[3]**

\* 0000800000003 \* DFD

**(b)** The weight of the stone is 0.12 N.

Calculate the mass of the stone.

mass =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ kg **[3]**

\* 0000800000004 \* DFD

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)(iii)** 56 (m)
>
> **(b)** 0.012 (kg)
