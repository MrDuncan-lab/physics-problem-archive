**(c)** A device on the surface of Mars sends a radio wave to the Earth. The distance from Mars to the Earth is 1.3 × 10$^{11}$ m. The speed of the radio wave is 3.0 × 10$^{8}$ m / s.

Calculate the time taken for the radio wave to travel from Mars to the Earth.

time taken =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ s **[3]**
