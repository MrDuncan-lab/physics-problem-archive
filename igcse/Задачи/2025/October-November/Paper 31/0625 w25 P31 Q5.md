**(a)** Describe the arrangement, separation and motion of the metal particles. **[3]**

**(b)** The cylinder is filled with a gas. Describe how particles of the gas exert a pressure on the inside surface of the metal cylinder. **[3]**

**(c)** The weight of the metal cylinder is 420 N. The area of the metal cylinder in contact with the ground is 300 cm$^{2}$.

Calculate the pressure on the ground due to the metal cylinder.

pressure =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ N / cm$^{2}$ **[3]**

\* 0000800000010 \* DFD

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(c)** (*P* = ) 1.4 (N/cm$^{2}$)
