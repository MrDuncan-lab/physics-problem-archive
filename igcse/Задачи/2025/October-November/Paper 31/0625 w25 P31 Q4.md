> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитываются работа подъёма и мощность человека, определяются изменения энергетических запасов при упражнении.

Fig. 4.1 shows a student doing some repetitive ‘step-up’ exercises. In each ‘step-up’, the student steps up from the floor onto a box and then back down to the floor.

![[0625-w25-31-p8-f1.svg]]

Fig. 4.1

**(a)** **(i)** The height of the box is 0.40 m. The weight of the student is 600 N.

Calculate the work done by the student in rising 0.40 m.

work done =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ J **[3]**

**(ii)** The chemical energy store in the student’s body decreases as she does

‘step-up’ exercises.

State the energy stores that increase as a result of energy transfers from the student’s

chemical energy store. **[2]**

**(b)** Another student transfers 3600 J of energy in a time of 30 s.

Calculate the student’s power when transferring this energy.

student’s power =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ W **[3]**

\* 0000800000009 \* DFD

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)(i)** 240 (J)
>
> **(b)** 120 (W)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | 240 (J) | A3 |
> |  | (work done = ) 600 × 0.4(0) | (C2) |
> |  | (work done = ) force × distance (moved in the direction of the force) | (C1) |
> | (a)(ii) | any **TWO** from:<br>(energy is transferred to) kinetic energy (store)<br>gravitational potential energy (store)<br>thermal/internal energy (store) | B2 |
> | (b) | 120 (W) | A3 |
> |  | (energy transferred = ) 3600 ÷ 30 | (C2) |
> |  | (power = ) energy transferred ÷ time (taken to do work) | (C1) |
