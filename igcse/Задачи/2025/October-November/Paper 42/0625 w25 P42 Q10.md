distance =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ m **[3]**

**(c)** The Whirlpool galaxy is 2.3 × 10$^{7}$ light-years from the Earth. The current value of the Hubble constant is 2.2 × 10$^{\text{–18}}$ per second.

Calculate the speed at which the Whirlpool galaxy is moving away from the Earth.

speed =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**
