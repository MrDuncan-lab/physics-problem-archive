The solar panels produce electricity and give a maximum power output of 3.5 kW. The efficiency of the solar panels is 16%.

**(a)** State and explain one advantage of using black solar panels. **[2]**

**(b)** Calculate the power received by the solar panels from the Sun.

power =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

**(c)** The solar panels produce direct current (d.c.) and household appliances use alternating current (a.c.).

State the difference between alternating current and direct current. **[1]**

**(d)** Suggest one advantage of storing energy in the large rechargeable battery. **[1]**

**(e)** Calculate the charge that flows into the battery when there is a current of 4.0 A for 2.0 hours.

charge =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

\* 0000800000008 \* DFD

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** 22 kW **OR** 22 000 W
>
> **(e)** 29 000 C
