**(a)** A truck moves with a constant acceleration. The speed of the truck increases from 13 m / s to 22 m / s. The time taken for this increase in speed is 4.5 s. The mass of the truck is 3700 kg.

**(i)** Calculate the resultant force acting on the truck.
