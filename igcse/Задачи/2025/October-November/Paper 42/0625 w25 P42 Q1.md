**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2025 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 2 · вопрос 1

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Различаются скалярные и векторные величины, по высоте подъёма определяются начальная скорость и импульс теннисного мяча.

**(a)** **(i)** State the difference between a scalar quantity and a vector quantity. **[1]**

**(ii)** In the list below, draw a line under each of the quantities that is a scalar quantity.

acceleration mass momentum electric field strength

energy temperature **[2]**

**(b)** A tennis player throws a tennis ball into the air. The mass of the tennis ball is 5.8 × 10$^{\text{–2}}$ kg. The tennis ball leaves the tennis player’s hand and reaches a maximum height of 5.0 m above the point of release.

**(i)** Show that the initial velocity of the tennis ball is 9.9 m / s upwards. **[3]**

**(ii)** Calculate the momentum of the tennis ball as it leaves the tennis player’s hand.

momentum =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

\* 0000800000004 \* DFD

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)(ii)** 0.57 kg m / s (upwards) **OR** 0.57 Ns (upwards)
