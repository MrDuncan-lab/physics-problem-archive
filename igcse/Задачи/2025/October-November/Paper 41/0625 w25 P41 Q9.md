**(a)** Describe the structure of an atom of helium-4,$_{2}^{4}$He. **[3]**

**(b)** The Sun is a medium-sized star powered by nuclear fusion reactions which release energy.

**(i)** State what happens during nuclear fusion reactions which form helium. **[2]**

**(ii)** State two regions of the electromagnetic spectrum by which the Sun radiates most of its energy. **[2]**

**(c)** Describe what happens to a star when most of the fuel in its centre has been converted to helium. **[2]**
