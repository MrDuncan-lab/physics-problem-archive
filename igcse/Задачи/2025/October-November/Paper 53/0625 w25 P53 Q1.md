> Объём глины определяется по размерам и вытесненной воде, сравниваются источники погрешности и способы точного измерения.

In this experiment, you will determine the volume of a block of modelling clay by two methods. Do not change the shape of the block of modelling clay or remove the thread from it.

h =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm **[1]**

V$_{\text{A}}$ = l × w × h

V$_{\text{A}}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm$^{3}$ **[1]**

Describe how the accuracy of these measurements can be improved. You are not required to carry out this improved procedure.
