> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> По равновесию линейки определяется вес предмета, сравниваются результаты и предлагаются способы точного размещения грузов и балансировки.

In this experiment, you will investigate the balancing of a metre ruler.

Refer to Fig. 1.1.

![[0625-w25-51-p2-f1.svg]]

Fig. 1.1

- Place the metre ruler on the pivot at the 50.0 cm mark.
- Place the object Q with its centre on the metre ruler at the 90.0 cm mark.

Determine the distance y from the 50.0 cm mark to the centre of the object Q.

y =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm **[2]**

- Place the 2.0 N load on the metre ruler.
- Adjust the position of the load so that the metre ruler is as near as possible to being balanced.

**(i)** Determine the distance x from the centre of the load to the 50.0 cm mark.

x =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm **[1]**

**(ii)** Calculate the weight W of object Q, using the equation W = $\dfrac{x}{y}$ × 2.0 N.

Give your answer to a suitable number of significant figures for this experiment.

W =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ N **[2]**

**(c)** Remove the 2.0 N load from the metre ruler. Do not change the position of object Q.

Repeat the steps above, using the 3.0 N load and the equation W = $\dfrac{x}{y}$ × 3.0 N.

y =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm

x =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm

W =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ N **[2]**

**(d)** State and explain whether your two values of W are equal within the limits of experimental accuracy. Refer to the values of W in your answer.

statement  \_\_\_\_\_\_

explanation  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(e)** Explain how you ensure that the centre of object Q is directly over the 90.0 cm mark of the metre ruler. You may draw a diagram. **[1]**

**(f)** It is difficult to find the position of the load to obtain the exact balance of the metre ruler.

Explain how you try to overcome this difficulty. **[1]**
