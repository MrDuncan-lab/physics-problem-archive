> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнение углов падения и преломления при переходе света из воздуха в стекло.

A ray of light passes from air into a block of glass. The critical angle is 42°.

Which statement is correct?

**A** The angle of incidence is the angle between the ray of light and the surface of the glass

block.

**B** The angle of incidence is always bigger than 42°.  
**C** The angle of refraction **cannot** be less than 42°.  
**D** The angle of refraction is smaller than the angle of incidence.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
