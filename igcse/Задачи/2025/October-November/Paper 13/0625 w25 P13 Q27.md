> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Распознавание верных утверждений о потоке зарядов, измерении тока и переменном токе.

A student writes three statements about electric current.

1 Electric current is a flow of charge.

2 Electric current in a circuit is measured using a voltmeter.

3 The direction of an alternating current (a.c.) continually changes.

Which statements are correct?

**A** 1, 2 and 3  
**B** 1 and 2 only  
**C** 1 and 3 only  
**D** 2 and 3 only

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
