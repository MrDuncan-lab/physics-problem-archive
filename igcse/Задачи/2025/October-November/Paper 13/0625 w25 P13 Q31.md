> Расчёт энергии, переданной резистором за заданное время.

There is a current of 2.0 A in a resistor for a time of 30 s. The potential difference (p.d.) across the

resistor is 12 V.

How much energy is transferred in the resistor?

**A** 1.25 J  
**B** 5.0 J  
**C** 180 J  
**D** 720 J

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
