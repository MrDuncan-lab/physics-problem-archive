> Определение изменения длины волны космического микроволнового фонового излучения при расширении Вселенной.

Which statement about the cosmic microwave background radiation (CMBR) is correct?

**A** CMBR now has a frequency higher than that of infrared radiation.  
**B** CMBR has always had exactly the same frequency.  
**C** CMBR had a shorter wavelength in the past.  
**D** CMBR was produced a long time after the Universe was formed.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
