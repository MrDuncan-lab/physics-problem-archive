> Сравнение периодов вращения Земли, её обращения вокруг Солнца и обращения Луны.

A student defines three periods of time:

- the time *T*$_{\text{S}}$ taken for the Earth to orbit the Sun
- the time *T*$_{\text{R}}$ taken for the Earth to rotate once on its axis
- the time *T*$_{\text{M}}$ taken for the Moon to orbit the Earth.

Which row shows the shortest period of time and the longest period of time?

|  | shortest period of time | longest period of time |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | *T*$_{\text{S}}$ | *T*$_{\text{R}}$ |
| **B** | *T*$_{\text{R}}$ | *T*$_{\text{S}}$ |
| **C** | *T*$_{\text{R}}$ | *T*$_{\text{M}}$ |
| **D** | *T*$_{\text{M}}$ | *T*$_{\text{S}}$ |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
