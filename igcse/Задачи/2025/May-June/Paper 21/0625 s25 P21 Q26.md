> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определение зависимости сопротивления медного провода от квадрата его диаметра.

Copper wire is available in fixed lengths but in various diameters *d*. Each wire has a different

resistance *R*.

Which relationship between *R* and *d* is correct?

**A** *R* is directly proportional to *d*.  
**B** *R* is directly proportional to *d*$^{2}$.  
**C** *R* is inversely proportional to *d*.  
**D** *R* is inversely proportional to *d*$^{2}$.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
