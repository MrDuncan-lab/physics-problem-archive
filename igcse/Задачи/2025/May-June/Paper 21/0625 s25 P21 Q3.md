> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнение объёмов и весов металлических блоков одинаковых размеров по показаниям динамометров.

The diagrams show two metal blocks, X and Y, suspended from identical force meters. X and Y have identical dimensions.

![[0625-s25-21-p3-f1.svg]]

Which statement about X and Y is correct?

**A** They have different volumes and different weights.  
**B** They have different volumes and equal weights.  
**C** They have equal volumes and equal weights.  
**D** They have equal volumes and different weights.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
