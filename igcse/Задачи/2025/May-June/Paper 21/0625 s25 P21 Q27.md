> Расчёт изменения напряжения на резисторе после замыкания переключателя в цепи.

The diagram shows three 20 Ω resistors, R$_{1}$, R$_{2}$ and R$_{3}$, and a switch S connected to a d.c. power

supply.

![[0625-s25-21-p11-f1.svg]]

Switch S is open.

The potential difference (p.d.) across R$_{3}$ is 9.0 V.

What is the p.d. across R$_{3}$ when switch S is closed?

**A** 3.0 V  
**B** 6.0 V  
**C** 12 V  
**D** 27 V

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
