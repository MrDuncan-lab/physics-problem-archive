> Объяснение меньшего погружения широких колёс в грунт уменьшением давления.

A farmer has two carts. The carts have the same weight, but one has four narrow wheels and the other has four wide wheels.

![[0625-s25-21-p5-f1.svg]]

Which cart wheels sink less into soft ground and what is the reason?

|  | cart wheels | reason |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | narrow | greater pressure on the ground |
| **B** | narrow | less pressure on the ground |
| **C** | wide | greater pressure on the ground |
| **D** | wide | less pressure on the ground |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
