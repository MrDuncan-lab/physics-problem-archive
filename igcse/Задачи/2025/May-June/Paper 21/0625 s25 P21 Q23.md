> Сравнение скорости и длины звуковой волны при переходе из воздуха в сталь.

A sound wave travels through the air and strikes a solid block of steel.

Some of the sound is reflected from the steel, and some is transmitted through the steel.

How does the speed and the wavelength of the sound in steel compare to the speed and the wavelength of the sound in air?

|  | speed in steel | wavelength in steel |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | less than in air | less than in air |
| **B** | less than in air | greater than in air |
| **C** | greater than in air | less than in air |
| **D** | greater than in air | greater than in air |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
