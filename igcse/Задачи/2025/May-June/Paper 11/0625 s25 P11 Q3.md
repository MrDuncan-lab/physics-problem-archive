> Сравнение средних скоростей спортсмена на трёх дистанциях.

The table shows a student’s times in three races.

| race | distance / m | time / s |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 100 | 12 |
| 2 | 150 | 17 |
| 3 | 200 | 25 |

What is the order of the student’s average speeds in each race, from lowest to highest?

**A** 1 → 2 → 3  
**B** 1 → 3 → 2  
**C** 2 → 1 → 3  
**D** 3 → 1 → 2

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
