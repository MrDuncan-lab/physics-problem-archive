> Выбор цвета и фактуры поверхности с наибольшей излучательной способностью.

A student has four metal containers filled with the same volume of water at a temperature of

80 °C.

The metal containers are put in a room at a temperature of 20 °C.

The metal containers are identical except for the texture and colour of their surfaces.

Which surface will emit energy at the greatest rate?

**A** dull black  
**B** shiny black  
**C** dull white  
**D** shiny white

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**
