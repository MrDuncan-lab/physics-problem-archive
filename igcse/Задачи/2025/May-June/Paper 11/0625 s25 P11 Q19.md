Which row correctly describes the vibrations of a transverse wave and also gives a correct

example of a transverse wave?

|  | description of vibrations | example of a transverse wave |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | at right angles to the wave direction | sound |
| **B** | at right angles to the wave direction | water wave |
| **C** | parallel to the wave direction | sound |
| **D** | parallel to the wave direction | water wave |
