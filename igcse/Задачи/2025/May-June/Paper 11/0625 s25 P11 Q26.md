> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определение знаков зарядов стержней по их притяжению и отталкиванию.

Two charged rods, X and Y, are brought, in turn, near to a negatively charged rod.

X is attracted by the negatively charged rod, and Y is repelled.

What are the charges on X and Y?

|  | charge on X | charge on Y |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | negative | negative |
| **B** | negative | positive |
| **C** | positive | negative |
| **D** | positive | positive |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
