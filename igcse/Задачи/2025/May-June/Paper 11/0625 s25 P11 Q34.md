> Расчёт доли фонового излучения, приходящейся на неуказанные источники.

The table shows the contribution to background radiation from the four most common sources.

| source | percentage of total background radiation / % |
|---|---|
| radon gas (in the air) | 48 |
| rocks and buildings | 13 |
| food and drink | 10 |
| cosmic rays | 11 |

Which percentage of background radiation comes from all other sources?

**A** 14%  
**B** 18%  
**C** 21%  
**D** 82%

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
