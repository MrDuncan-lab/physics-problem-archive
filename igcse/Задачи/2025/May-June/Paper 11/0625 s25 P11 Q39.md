> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определение красного смещения как увеличения длины волны излучения галактик.

Electromagnetic radiation from distant galaxies is redshifted when observed from the Earth.

This is evidence that supports the Big Bang Theory.

Which statement is correct?

**A** Light from galaxies that are moving towards the Earth is redshifted.  
**B** Redshift is an increase in the wavelength of electromagnetic radiation.  
**C** The Big Bang Theory says that the Solar System is expanding out into the Universe.  
**D** The Big Bang Theory says that, billions of years ago, the Universe expanded to its current

size in a few seconds.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
