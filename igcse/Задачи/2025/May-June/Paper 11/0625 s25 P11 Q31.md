> Расчёт суммарных ЭДС элементов и сопротивления последовательно соединённых резисторов.

A teacher makes a circuit using three 1.5 V cells and two 20 Ω resistors connected in series.

![[0625-s25-11-p12-f1.svg]]

Which row gives the combined electromotive force (e.m.f.) of the cells and the combined resistance of the resistors?

|  | combined e.m.f. / V | combined resistance / Ω |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | 1.5 | 10 |
| **B** | 1.5 | 40 |
| **C** | 4.5 | 10 |
| **D** | 4.5 | 40 |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
