> Сопоставление периодов вращения Земли вокруг оси и обращения вокруг Солнца.

What is the time taken for the Earth to spin once on its axis and the time taken for the Earth to

orbit the Sun?

|  | spin on its axis | orbit the Sun |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | 24 hours | 1 month |
| **B** | 24 hours | 365 days |
| **C** | 1 month | 1 month |
| **D** | 1 month | 365 days |
