A student compares alpha emissions, beta emissions and gamma emissions.

Which statement is correct?

**A** Alpha emissions are the most ionising.  
**B** Beta emissions are the most penetrating.  
**C** Beta emissions are part of the electromagnetic spectrum.  
**D** Gamma emissions are streams of charged particles.
