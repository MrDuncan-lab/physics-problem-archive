**(a)** **(i)** State what is meant by an electric field. **[1]**

**(ii)** Fig. 8.1 shows a negative point charge.

![[0625-s25-43-p11-f1.svg]]

Fig. 8.1

On Fig. 8.1, draw four field lines to show the pattern and the direction of the electric field

due to the negative point charge. **[2]**

**(b)** The potential difference (p.d.) of a lightning strike is 2.9 × 10$^{8}$ V. The energy transferred by the

lightning strike is 4.5 × 10$^{5}$ MJ.

Calculate the charge that flows.

charge =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

**(c)** The current in a lamp is 2.0 A. The lamp is switched on for a time of 120 s.

Calculate the charge that flows.

charge =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

\* 0000800000012 \* DFD

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** 1600 C
>
> **(c)** 240 C
