> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определение скорости, пути и замедления тормозящего автомобиля по графику движения.

A car travels at a speed of 20 m / s. The driver applies the brakes when he sees a red traffic light.

Fig. 1.1 shows the speed–time graph for the car.

![[0625-s25-43-p3-f1.svg]]

Fig. 1.1

**(a)** Determine the speed of the car at time = 2.0 s.

speed of the car =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(b)** Calculate the distance travelled by the car between time = 0 and time = 4.0 s.

distance travelled =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

**(c)** Calculate the deceleration of the car between time = 0.5 s and time = 4.0 s.

deceleration =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

\* 0000800000004 \* DFD

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** 45 m
>
> **(c)** 5.7 m / s$^{2}$

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | 11.5 m / s | B1 |
> | (b) | 45 m | A3 |
> |  | (distance =) area under graph **OR** ½ *bh* (+ *bh*) | C1 |
> |  | {0.5 × 20} + ½ {20 × 3.5} **OR** {10 + 35} **OR** \[{0.5 + 4} ÷ 2\] × 20 | C1 |
> | (c) | 5.7 m / s$^{2}$ | A3 |
> |  | (deceleration =) (Δ)*v* ÷ Δ*t* **OR** deceleration = gradient | C1 |
> |  | (0 –) 20 ÷ {4(.0) - 0.5} **OR** 20 ÷ 3.5 | C1 |
