> Расчёт импульса и скорости космического аппарата после разделения и массы капсулы по равновесию сил.

Fig. 2.1 shows a space vehicle which consists of a capsule and a nose cone.

The space vehicle is moving at a velocity of 7800 m / s. The mass of the space vehicle is 840 kg.

![[0625-s25-43-p4-f1.svg]]

Fig. 2.1

**(a)** Show that the momentum of the space vehicle is approximately 6.55 × 10$^{6}$ kg m / s. **[1]**

**(b)** The capsule ejects the nose cone, as shown in Fig. 2.2.

![[0625-s25-43-p4-f2.svg]]

mass = 720 kg

Fig. 2.2 (not to scale)

Determine the velocity v of the capsule after the nose cone is ejected. Give your answer to 3 significant figures.

velocity v of the capsule =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

\* 0000800000005 \* DFD

**(c)** A different space capsule returns to Earth. Fig. 2.3 shows this capsule just before it lands in the sea. The capsule travels at terminal velocity.

![[0625-s25-43-p5-f1.svg]]

Fig. 2.3

The upward vertical force acting on the capsule is 120 kN.

Calculate the mass of the capsule.

mass of the capsule =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

\* 0000800000006 \* DFD

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** 7790 m / s
>
> **(c)** 12 000 kg
