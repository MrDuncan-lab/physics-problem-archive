> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определение светового года, расстояния до Солнца, постоянной Хаббла и смысла красного смещения.

**(a)** Define a light-year in words. **[1]**

**(b)** It takes light 490 s to travel from the Sun to the Earth.

Calculate the distance from the Sun to the Earth.

distance =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(c)** Fig. 10.1 is a scatter graph showing how the speed of galaxies moving away from the Earth varies with their distances from the Earth.

![[0625-s25-43-p14-f1.svg]]

distance of galaxy from Earth / 10$^{20}$ km

Fig. 10.1

A scientist draws a best-fit line on the scatter graph.

Use this best-fit line to determine a value for the Hubble constant.

Hubble constant =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

\* 0000800000015 \* DFD

**(d)** The speed of a receding galaxy can be estimated using redshift.

Describe what is meant by redshift. **[2]**

\* 0000800000016 \* DFD

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** 1.5 × 10$^{11}$ m
>
> **(c)** 2.5 × 10$^{\text{–18}}$ per second

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | distance travelled (in the vacuum of space) by light in one year | B1 |
> | (b) | 1.5 × 10$^{11}$ m | A2 |
> |  | 9.5 × 10$^{15}$(m) **OR** (*c* =) 3(.0) × 10$^{8}$(m / s) **OR** 3(.0) × 10$^{\text{N}}$ × 490 | C1 |
> | (c) | 2.5 × 10$^{\text{–18}}$ per second | A3 |
> |  | (Hubble’s constant =) gradient **OR** (*H*0 =) *v* ÷ *d* | C1 |

> | (d) | electromagnetic radiation / light from (distant) galaxies | B1 |
> |  | (observed) increase in wavelength (compared to wavelength measured on the Earth) | B1 |
