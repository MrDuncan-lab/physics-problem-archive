> Выбор поверхности с наименьшей скоростью теплового излучения по цвету, фактуре и площади.

Four different surfaces are at the same high temperature.

Which surface emits thermal radiation at the slowest rate?

|  | colour of surface | texture of surface | surface area / cm$^{2}$ |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | black | dull | 10 |
| **B** | black | dull | 100 |
| **C** | white | shiny | 10 |
| **D** | white | shiny | 100 |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
