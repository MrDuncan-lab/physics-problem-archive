> Определение равенства скоростей получения и потери энергии при постоянной температуре тела.

An object has a constant temperature.

Which statement is correct?

**A** There is **no** energy transfer to or from the object.  
**B** The rate at which the object receives energy is the same as the rate at which it transfers

energy away.

**C** The rate at which the object receives energy is smaller than the rate at which it transfers

energy away.

**D** The rate at which the object receives energy is greater than the rate at which it transfers

energy away.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
