What is the electromotive force (e.m.f.) of a cell?

**A** the charge that passes through the cell per unit time  
**B** the energy supplied per unit charge as charge passes through the cell  
**C** the total charge flowing through the cell  
**D** the total energy stored in the cell

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
