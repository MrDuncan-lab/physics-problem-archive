What is the speed of radio waves in a vacuum and what is the speed of gamma rays in a

vacuum?

|  | speed of radio waves | speed of gamma rays |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | less than 3.0 × 10$^{8}$ m / s | less than 3.0 × 10$^{8}$ m / s |
| **B** | less than 3.0 × 10$^{8}$ m / s | more than 3.0 × 10$^{8}$ m / s |
| **C** | 3.0 × 10$^{8}$ m / s | 3.0 × 10$^{8}$ m / s |
| **D** | more than 3.0 × 10$^{8}$ m / s | more than 3.0 × 10$^{8}$ m / s |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
