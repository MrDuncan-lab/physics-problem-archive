> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнение дифракции волн разной длины на остром крае препятствия.

A narrow beam of light consists of two waves of different wavelengths.

The beam of light hits a sharp edge.

Which effect will be seen beyond the sharp edge?

**A** Both waves continue along the same straight line as before.  
**B** Both waves bend sharply and continue along a new straight line.  
**C** The wave of longer wavelength bends round the sharp edge more than the wave of shorter

wavelength.

**D** The wave of shorter wavelength bends round the sharp edge more than the wave of longer

wavelength.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
