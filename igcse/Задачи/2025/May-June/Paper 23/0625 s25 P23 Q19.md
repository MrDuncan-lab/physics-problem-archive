> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определение размера зеркального изображения и расстояния между предметом и изображением.

An object is placed 30 cm in front of a plane mirror.

Which statement describes the image of the object?

**A** The image is the same size as the object and 30 cm from the object.  
**B** The image is the same size as the object and 60 cm from the object.  
**C** The image is smaller than the object and 30 cm from the object.  
**D** The image is smaller than the object and 60 cm from the object.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
