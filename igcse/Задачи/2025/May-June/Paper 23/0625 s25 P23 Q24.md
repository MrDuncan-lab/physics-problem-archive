> Определение направления и плотности силовых линий слабого магнита.

Which row describes the magnetic field around a weak magnet?

|  | direction of field | spacing of field lines |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | north pole to south pole | close together |
| **B** | north pole to south pole | far apart |
| **C** | south pole to north pole | close together |
| **D** | south pole to north pole | far apart |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
