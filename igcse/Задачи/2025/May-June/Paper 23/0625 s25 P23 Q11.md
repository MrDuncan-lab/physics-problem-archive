> Определение повышения температуры и давления газа при увеличении энергии движения его частиц.

The particles of a gas in a container of fixed volume gain energy in the kinetic store.

Which effect does this have on the gas?

**A** Both the pressure and the temperature of the gas increase.  
**B** Only the temperature of the gas increases.  
**C** Neither the pressure nor the temperature of the gas increases.  
**D** Only the pressure of the gas increases.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**
