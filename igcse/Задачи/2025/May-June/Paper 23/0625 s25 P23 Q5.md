> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определение порядка несмешивающихся жидкостей и уровней их границ по массам и объёмам.

Samples of three different liquids have equal masses.

The volumes of the samples are 190 cm$^{3}$, 200 cm$^{3}$ and 235 cm$^{3}$.

The liquids are poured into a large measuring cylinder and the liquids form three separate layers because they do **not** mix.

What are the readings on the measuring cylinder at the two boundaries between the samples?

**A** 190 cm$^{3}$ and 390 cm$^{3}$  
**B** 190 cm$^{3}$ and 425 cm$^{3}$  
**C** 235 cm$^{3}$ and 425 cm$^{3}$  
**D** 235 cm$^{3}$ and 435 cm$^{3}$

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**
