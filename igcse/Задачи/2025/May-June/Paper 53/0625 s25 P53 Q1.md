In this experiment, you will investigate a suspended metre ruler and determine the weight of the metre ruler.

Refer to Fig. 1.1. Do not change the position of the loops of thread attached to the force meters.

![[0625-s25-53-p2-f1.svg]]

Fig. 1.1

**(a)** **(i)** Move the 1.50 N load to a distance d = 40.0 cm from the 0.0 cm end of the ruler.

Raise or lower one clamp slightly, if necessary, so that the metre ruler is as near to

horizontal as possible.

Read the value F$_{\text{X}}$ on force meter X and the value F$_{\text{Y}}$ on force meter Y.

F$_{\text{X}}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ N

F$_{\text{Y}}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ N

Calculate a value W$_{\text{X}}$ using the equation W$_{\text{X}}$ = F$_{\text{X}}$ + F$_{\text{Y}}$ – k, where k = 1.50 N.

W$_{\text{X}}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ N **[1]**

**(ii)** Briefly describe how to check that the ruler is horizontal before taking the reading.

You may draw a diagram. **[1]**
