> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определение центра тяжести линейки и расчёт её массы по равновесию и плотности древесины.

Fig. 2.1 shows a balanced, uniform metre ruler made of wood.

![[0625-s25-42-p4-f1.svg]]

Fig. 2.1

The width of the metre ruler is 2.6 × 10$^{\text{–2}}$ m and the thickness of the ruler is 6.0 × 10$^{\text{–3}}$ m.

**(a)** Define the ‘moment’ of a force in words. **[1]**

**(b)** On Fig. 2.1, mark the position of the centre of gravity of the metre ruler with a point labelled X.

Label the distance of X from the 0 cm end of the ruler. **[1]**

**(c)** **(i)** Show that the mass of the metre ruler is 0.081 kg. **[3]**

**(ii)** Calculate the density of the wood of the metre ruler.

density =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

\* 0000800000006 \* DFD

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(c)(ii)** 520 kg / m$^{3}$

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | (moment =) force × <u>perpendicular distance (from the pivot)</u> | B1 |
> | (b) | X **AND** 50 cm labelled to the right of the pivot **AND** left of 80 cm | B1 |
> | (c)(i) | sum of clockwise moments = sum of anticlockwise moments | B1 |
> |  | {*mg* × 8} + {0.12 × 38} = {0.34 × 32} | B1 |
> |  | any **one** from:<br>• *mg* = \[{0.34 × 32} – {0.12 × 38}\] ÷ 8<br>• *mg* = {10.88 – 4.56} ÷ 8<br>• *mg* = 6.32 ÷ 8<br>• *mg* = 0.79<br>• (mass =) 0.79 ÷ 9.8 | B1 |
> | (c)(ii) | 520 kg / m$^{3}$ | A2 |
> |  | (density =) mass ÷ volume **OR** 0.081 ÷ {1(.0) × 2.6 × 10$^{\text{–2}}$× 6(.0) × 10$^{\text{–3}}$} | C1 |
