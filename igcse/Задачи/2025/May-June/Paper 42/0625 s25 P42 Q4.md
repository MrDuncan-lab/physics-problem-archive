> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объяснение охлаждения разных поверхностей, теплового равновесия и различной теплопроводности металла и древесины.

**(a)** Fig. 4.1 shows two metal containers, A and B.

![[0625-s25-42-p8-f1.svg]]

Fig. 4.1

Container A has a dull black outer surface and container B has a shiny white outer surface. A and B both contain very hot water at time = 0. The temperature of the water in each container is recorded every minute.

**(i)** State which container has the larger temperature change over the first 5 minutes. Explain your answer. **[2]**

**(ii)** After 30 minutes, the temperature of the water in container A remains constant.

State, in terms of energy transfers, why the temperature remains constant. **[1]**

**(iii)** The experiment is repeated without the lids on the containers.

State the name of the additional method of energy transfer that occurs. **[1]**

\* 0000800000009 \* DFD

**(b)** Fig. 4.2 shows a metal pan with a wooden handle.

![[0625-s25-42-p9-f1.svg]]

Fig. 4.2

Explain why there is a much greater rate of transfer of energy through the metal than through the wood. **[3]**

\* 0000800000010 \* DFD

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)(i)** container A or container with (dull) black (surface) **AND** (dull) black (surfaces) are better emitters of infrared / radiation (than shiny white surfaces)
