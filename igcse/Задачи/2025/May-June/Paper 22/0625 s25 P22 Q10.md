> Распознавание жидкости и газа по способности принимать форму сосуда.

A sample of a substance has a shape that is determined by its container.

What are the possible states of this sample?

**A** gas, liquid and solid  
**B** gas and liquid only  
**C** gas and solid only  
**D** solid and liquid only

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
