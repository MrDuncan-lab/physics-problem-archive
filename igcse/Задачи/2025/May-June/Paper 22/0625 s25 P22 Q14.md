> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбор формулы удельной теплоёмкости по мощности нагревателя, времени, массе и росту температуры.

The diagram shows the equipment used in an experiment to determine the specific heat capacity

of a metal.

In the experiment, a heater is switched on for *t* s and the increase in temperature Δθ of a block of the metal is measured in °C.

![[0625-s25-22-p7-f1.svg]]

Which equation gives the specific heat capacity of the metal?

**A** *c* = $\dfrac{P}{tm \Delta \theta}$  
**B** *c* = $\dfrac{P t}{m \Delta \theta}$  
**C** *c* = $\dfrac{tm \Delta \theta}{P}$  
**D** *c* = $\dfrac{m \Delta \theta}{P t}$

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
