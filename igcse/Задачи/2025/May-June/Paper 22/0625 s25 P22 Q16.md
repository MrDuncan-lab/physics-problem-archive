> Сравнение мощности теплового излучения сфер разного диаметра и цвета.

Three solid copper spheres, X, Y and Z, are heated to the same temperature.

| sphere | diameter | colour of surface |
|---|---|---|
| X | 2*d* | black |
| Y | *d* | white |
| Z | 2*d* | white |

Which row gives the order for the rate of emission of radiation?

|  | highest rate of emission | second highest rate of emission | lowest rate of emission |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | X | Y | Z |
| **B** | X | Z | Y |
| **C** | Z | X | Y |
| **D** | Z | Y | X |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
