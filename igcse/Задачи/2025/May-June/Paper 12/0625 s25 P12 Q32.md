> Определение типа трансформатора и числа первичных витков по изменению напряжения.

The transformer in a laptop power supply is used to change mains voltage from 240 V to 20 V.

The transformer has 600 turns on the secondary coil.

Which type of transformer is it and how many turns does the transformer have on the primary coil?

|  | transformer type | number of turns on primary coil |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | step-down | 50 |
| **B** | step-down | 7200 |
| **C** | step-up | 50 |
| **D** | step-up | 7200 |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
