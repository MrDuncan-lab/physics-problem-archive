> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определение равнодействующей сил при движении автомобиля с постоянной скоростью по прямому склону.

A car travels at constant speed along a straight road that has a slight downwards slope.

Which statement is correct?

**A** There is a resultant force on the car down the slope.  
**B** There is a resultant force on the car up the slope.  
**C** There is a resultant force on the car vertically downwards.  
**D** There is no resultant force on the car.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
