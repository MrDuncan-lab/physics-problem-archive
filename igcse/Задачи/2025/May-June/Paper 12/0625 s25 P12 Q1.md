> Определение периода маятника по времени двадцати колебаний.

A pendulum starts swinging and its motion is timed. The time measured for 20 complete swings is 30 s.

What is the time for **one** complete swing of the pendulum?

**A** 0.67 s  
**B** 0.75 s  
**C** 1.5 s  
**D** 3.0 s

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
