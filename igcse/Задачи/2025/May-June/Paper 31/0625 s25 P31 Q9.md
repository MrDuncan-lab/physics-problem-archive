> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Расчёт тока, стоимости работы роутера и числа витков трансформатора с объяснением действия предохранителя.

Fig. 9.1 shows a router. The router emits Wi-Fi signals.

![[0625-s25-31-p13-f1.svg]]

Fig. 9.1

**(a)** The power input to the router circuit is 9.0 W. The potential difference across the router circuit

is 12 V.

Calculate the current in the router circuit.

current in router circuit =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ A **[3]**

**(b)** The energy used by the router each hour is 0.0090 kW h. One unit (kW h) of energy costs 50 cents.

Calculate the cost of using the router for 24 hours.

cost for 24 hours =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cents **[3]**

**(c)** The router uses a transformer. The number of turns N$_{\text{p}}$ on the primary coil of the transformer is 3600. The primary voltage V$_{\text{p}}$ to the transformer is 240 V. The secondary voltage V$_{\text{s}}$ of the transformer is 12 V.

Calculate the number of turns N$_{\text{s}}$ on the secondary coil of the transformer.

number of turns on secondary coil =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

**(d)** The mains plug for the router includes a fuse that protects the router.

Explain how a fuse works. **[3]**

\* 0000800000014 \* DFD

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** (current =) 0.75 (A)
>
> **(b)** 11 (cents)
>
> **(c)** (*N*s =) 180
