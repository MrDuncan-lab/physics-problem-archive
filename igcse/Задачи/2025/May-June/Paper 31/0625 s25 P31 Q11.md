> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Описание гравитации Солнечной системы, светового года и свидетельств расширения Вселенной.

**(a)** The Solar System includes the Sun and eight planets.

**(i)** State the name of the force that keeps the planets in orbit around the Sun. **[1]**

**(ii)** State the name of the galaxy that includes the Solar System. **[1]**

**(b)** State the meaning of the term light-year. **[1]**

**(c)** Describe the evidence for an expanding Universe. **[3]**

> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | gravitational (force / attraction) | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | <u>Milky Way</u> | B1 |
> | (b) | <u>distance travelled (in the vacuum of space) by light in one year</u> | B1 |
