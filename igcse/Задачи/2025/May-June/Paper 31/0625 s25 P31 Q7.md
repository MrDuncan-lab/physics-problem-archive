**(ii)** The speed of yellow light is 3.0 × 10$^{8}$m / s. The wavelength of yellow light is 5.8 × 10$^{\text{–7}}$ m.

Calculate the frequency of yellow light.

frequency =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ Hz **[3]**

\* 0000800000012 \* DFD

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)(ii)** (frequency =) 2(.0) (Hz)
>
> **(b)(ii)** 5.2 × 10$^{14}$ (Hz)
