> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнение ускорения по графику скорости, описание движения и расчёт пути и средней скорости.

Fig. 1.1 shows the speed–time graph for a car travelling along a flat, straight road. The graph has four sections, labelled JK, KL, LM and MN.

![[0625-s25-31-p2-f1.svg]]

Fig. 1.1

**(a)** Complete the following sentence.

The car has the largest acceleration in section  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(b)** Calculate the distance travelled by the car between time = 20 s and time = 35 s.

distance travelled =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ m **[3]**

**(c)** Describe the motion of the car during:

(i) section LM **[1]**

(ii) section MN. **[1]**

\* 0000800000003 \* DFD

**(d)** Another car travels a distance of 500 m in a time of 20 s.

Calculate the average speed of this car.

average speed =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ m / s **[3]**

\* 0000800000004 \* DFD

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** (distance travelled =) 150 (m)
>
> **(d)** (average speed =) 25 (m / s)
