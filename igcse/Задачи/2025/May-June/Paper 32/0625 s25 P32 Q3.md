> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определение веса блока по равновесию рычага и расчёт давления другого блока на землю.

**(a)** A student determines the weight W of a metal block by using a 1.5 N load and a uniform metre ruler. She places the centre of the uniform metre ruler on a pivot.

![[0625-s25-32-p5-f1.svg]]

Fig. 3.1 (not to scale)

She moves the metal block and the 1.5 N load until the uniform metre ruler balances horizontally as shown in Fig. 3.1.

Calculate the weight W of the metal block. Use the principle of moments in your answer.

weight W =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ N **[4]**

**(b)** A different metal block is lying on the ground, as shown in Fig. 3.2.

![[0625-s25-32-p5-f2.svg]]

Fig. 3.2 (not to scale)

The weight of the metal block is 890 N.

Calculate the pressure on the ground caused by the block in the position shown in Fig. 3.2.

pressure =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ N / m$^{2}$ **[3]**

\* 0000800000006 \* DFD

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** 3.1 (N)
>
> **(b)** 9200 (N / m$^{2}$)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | 3.1 (N) | A4 |
> |  | 1.5 × 0.44 = *W* × 0.21 **OR** (*W* =) (1.5 × 0.44) ÷ 0.21<br>**OR**<br>(*W* =) 0.66 ÷ 0.21 | C3 |
> |  | (anticlockwise moment =) 1.5 × 0.44 **OR** 0.66 seen | C1 |
> |  | (sum of) anticlockwise moment = (sum of) clockwise moment | C1 |
> | (b) | 9200 (N / m$^{2}$) | A3 |
> |  | 890 ÷ (0.54 × 0.18) **OR** 890 ÷ 0.0972 | C2 |
> |  | (pressure =) force ÷ area | C1 |
