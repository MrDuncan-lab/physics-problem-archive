> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Расчёт массы и работы подъёма молота и описание энергетических превращений при забивании стержня.

Some buildings are built on large, strong metal rods that are pushed deep into the ground. A machine drops a heavy hammer onto each metal rod to push it into the ground, as shown in Fig. 2.1.

Fig. 2.1 (not to scale)

The weight of the heavy hammer is 25 000 N.

**(a)** Calculate the mass of the heavy hammer.

mass =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ kg **[3]**

**(b)** The machine lifts the heavy hammer through 0.72 m vertically.

Calculate the work done by the machine in lifting the heavy hammer. Include the unit.

work done =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ unit  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[4]**

**(c)** The heavy hammer falls onto the metal rod and pushes it into the ground.

Describe the energy transfers from the heavy hammer to the metal rod.

Your answer should refer to energy stores as well as transfers between energy stores. **[2]**

\* 0000800000005 \* DFD

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** 2600 (kg)
>
> **(b)** 18 000

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)

> | (a) | 2600 (kg) | A3 |

> |  | (W =) force × distance (moved in direction of force) | C1 |
