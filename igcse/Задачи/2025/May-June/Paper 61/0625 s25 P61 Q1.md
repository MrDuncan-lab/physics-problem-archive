A student determines the density of a ball.

**(a)** He places the ball between two wooden blocks, as seen from above in Fig. 1.1.

He takes two measurements, d$_{1}$ and d$_{2}$.

![[0625-s25-61-p2-f1.svg]]

Fig. 1.1

**(i)** On Fig. 1.1, measure the lengths d$_{1}$ and d$_{2}$.

d$_{1}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm

d$_{2}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm **[1]**

**(ii)** Using your measurements, calculate the diameter d of the ball. Show your working.

d =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm **[1]**

**(iii)** Explain why this method is used to measure the diameter of the ball. **[2]**

**(b)** Calculate the volume V of the ball using the equation V = 0.52d$^{3}$.

Include the unit.

V =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

\* 0000800000003 \* DFD

**(c)** The student measures the mass m$_{\text{D}}$ of a dish.

102.5

m$_{\text{D}}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ g

He places the ball in the dish and measures the combined mass m$_{\text{C}}$ of the dish and the ball.

Fig. 1.2 shows the dish and the ball on a balance.

Record the reading shown on the balance.

![[0625-s25-61-p3-f1.svg]]

Fig. 1.2

m$_{\text{C}}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ g

Calculate the mass m$_{\text{B}}$ of the ball. Show your working.

m$_{\text{B}}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ g **[2]**

**(d)** Calculate the density ρ of the ball using the equation:

ρ = $\dfrac{m_{B}}{V}$ .

Give your answer to a suitable number of significant figures for this experiment. Include the unit.

ρ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

\* 0000800000004 \* DFD
