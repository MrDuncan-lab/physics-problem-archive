> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Измерение диаметра и массы шара для расчёта плотности с подходящей точностью.

In this experiment, you will determine the density of a ball.

**(a)** Place the ball between the two blocks as seen from above in Fig. 1.1.

![[0625-s25-51-p2-f1.svg]]

Fig. 1.1

**(i)** Describe how to use the apparatus in Fig. 1.1 to take two measurements to determine the diameter d of the ball. You may draw on Fig. 1.1 to help your description. **[2]**

**(ii)** Using the blocks as shown in Fig. 1.1, take two measurements to determine the diameter d of the ball.

measurement 1 =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm

measurement 2 =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm **[1]**

**(iii)** Use your measurements to calculate a value for the diameter d of the ball. Show your working.

d =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm **[1]**

**(b)** Calculate the volume V of the ball using the equation V = 0.52d$^{3}$.

Include the unit.

V =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(c)** **(i)** Use the balance to measure the mass m$_{\text{D}}$ of the dish provided.

m$_{\text{D}}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ g **[1]**

**(ii)** Place the ball in the dish and record the combined mass m$_{\text{C}}$ of the dish and the ball.

m$_{\text{C}}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ g **[1]**

**(iii)** Calculate the mass m$_{\text{B}}$ of the ball. Show your working.

m$_{\text{B}}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ g **[1]**

**(d)** Calculate the density ρ of the ball using the equation:

ρ = $\dfrac{m_{B}}{V}$ .

Give your answer to a suitable number of significant figures for this experiment.

ρ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ g / cm$^{3}$ **[2]**
