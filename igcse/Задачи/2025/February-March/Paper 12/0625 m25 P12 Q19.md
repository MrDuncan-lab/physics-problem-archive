> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определение изменения агрегатного состояния по графику изменения плотности вещества.

The graph shows what happens to the density of a substance as its temperature changes over

time.

![[0625-m25-12-p8-f1.svg]]

Which term describes the change of state taking place at time *t*$_{2}$?

**A** melting  
**B** freezing  
**C** condensation  
**D** boiling

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Many candidates struggled with this question where they had to identify which change of state was taking place on a density-time graph. Only the strongest candidates correctly identified that the substance was changing from a liquid to a gas. The most common incorrect answer was option **A**.
