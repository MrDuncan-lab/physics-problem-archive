> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Описание измерения плотности жидкости и расчёт массы металлического блока.

**(a)** A student determines the density of a liquid, by using the equipment shown in Fig. 2.1.

![[0625-m25-32-p4-f1.svg]]

Fig. 2.1

Describe how the student uses the equipment to determine the density of the liquid. **[3]**

**(b)** Fig. 2.2 shows a metal block.

![[0625-m25-32-p4-f2.svg]]

Fig. 2.2

The density of the metal is 8.0 g / cm$^{3}$ .

Calculate the mass of the metal block.

mass =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ g **[4]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** (mass =) 96 (g)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | Any **two** from:<br>idea of using measuring cylinder to measure (liquid) volume<br>measure mass of cylinder / beaker empty / with liquid<br>measure (any) mass with balance | B2 |
> |  | Any **one** from:<br>idea of subtracting mass of empty beaker<br>use of density = mass ÷ volume | B1 |
> | (b) | (mass =) 96 (g) | A4 |
> |  | (mass =) 8(.0) × 12 | (C3) |
> |  | (mass =) density × volume | (C1) |
> |  | (volume of block =) 2 × 2 × 3 OR 12 (cm$^{3}$) | (C1) |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** There were many excellent answers here and most candidates scored full credit. Answers not scoring full credit usually lacked detail and precision, e.g., failing to state that the balance is used to find the mass of the empty measuring cylinder.
>
> **(b)** The majority of candidates gained full credit for this calculation with an answer of 96 g. The most common errors included calculating an incorrect volume for the metal block. Many candidates thought it was 2 + 2 + 3 = 7. Other candidates used an incorrect rearrangement of the equation density = mass ÷ volume, e.g., mass = volume ÷ density.
