> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Описание частиц атома, заряда альфа-частицы и проникающей способности ядерных излучений.

**(a)** Table 11.1 gives information about the particles in an atom.

Table 11.1

| name of particle | relative charge | location in the atom |
|---|---|---|
| proton | +1 | in the nucleus |
| neutron |  |  |
| electron |  | orbiting the nucleus |

Complete the table by writing the correct information in the three empty spaces. **[3]**

**(b)** State the relative charge on an alpha particle. **[1]**

**(c)** Compare the penetrating abilities of alpha particles, beta particles and gamma rays. **[2]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | name of particle relative charge location in the atom<br>proton +1 in the nucleus<br>neutron 0 OR zero in the nucleus<br>electron –1 OR minus 1 orbiting the nucleus<br>**B1 B1 B1** | B3 |
> | (b) | +2 OR plus two | B1 |
> | (c) | any **two** from:<br>gamma (rays) most / more penetrating<br>alpha (particles) least / less penetrating<br>beta are between alpha and gamma | B2 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Candidates’ understanding of atomic structure and nomenclature was often good. Most candidates gained at least partial credit for this question. The most common error was to transpose the charges for a neutron and an electron.
>
> **(b)** Candidates found this question quite challenging with many failing to identify +2 as the charge on an alpha particle. The most common error was to simply say it was positive, i.e. not giving the relative size of the charge.
>
> **(c)** The majority of candidates gave clear comparisons of the relative penetrating abilities of alpha, beta and gamma radiations. However, some were very confused and sometimes contradictory. Candidates would have benefited from opportunities to write comparison type responses. A common error was to confuse the abilities of alpha particles and gamma rays.
