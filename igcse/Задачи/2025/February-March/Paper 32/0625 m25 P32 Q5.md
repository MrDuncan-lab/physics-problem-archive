> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определение энергетических превращений при подъёме груза и расчёт работы и мощности электродвигателя.

An electric motor lifts a load, as shown in Fig. 5.1.

![[0625-m25-32-p7-f1.svg]]

Fig. 5.1

**(a)** After lifting the load, the motor is switched off. Fig. 5.2 represents the transfer of energy from

the battery to the load in Fig. 5.1.

In Fig. 5.2, add the names of the energy stores.

![[0625-m25-32-p7-f2.svg]]

energy store in the battery energy store in the load

Fig. 5.2 **[2]**

**(b)** The weight of the load is 8.0 N. The motor lifts the load through a vertical distance of 0.60 m.

Calculate the work done on the load.

work done on load =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ J **[3]**

**(c)** The current in the motor is 0.40 A. The potential difference across the motor is 6.0 V.

Calculate the power of the electric motor.

power of electric motor =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ W **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** 4.8 (J)
>
> **(c)** 2.4 (W)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | chemical (energy) | B1 |
> |  | gravitational potential (energy) | B1 |
> | (b) | 4.8 (J) | A3 |
> |  | (work done =) 8(.0) × 0.6 | (C2) |
> |  | (work done =) force × distance (moved in direction of force) | (C1) |
> | (c) | 2.4 (W) | A3 |
> |  | (power input =) 0.4(0) × 6(.0) | (C2) |
> |  | (power input =) *I* × *V* | (C1) |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Candidates found this item challenging. Many gained partial credit for chemical energy but others gave an incorrect answer of electrical or kinetic energy. Fewer candidates gained further credit with the most common error in the second part of the question being thermal energy and many candidates stated kinetic or electrical.
>
> **(b)** Many candidates correctly determined the work done as 4.8 J. The most common error was to divide the weight by the distance to give 13.3.
>
> **(c)** Many candidates had learnt the different arrangements of the energy and power equations. The most common error was to confuse *P = I* × *V* with *R = V* / *I* and so some candidates used *P = V* / *I* to give an answer of 15.
