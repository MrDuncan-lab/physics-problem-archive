# Question 3 (7 marks)

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнение устойчивости тел и расчёт давления на опору и момента силы.

Fig. 3.1 shows a cross-section through the centre of a solid cone and a cross-section through the centre of a solid cuboid. The cone and the cuboid are placed on the ground as shown in Fig. 3.1. The solid cone is more stable than the solid cuboid. The symbol × indicates the position of the centre of gravity of each object.

![[0625-m25-32-p5-f1.svg]]

Fig. 3.1

**(a)** Explain why the solid cone in Fig. 3.1 is more stable than the solid cuboid. **[1]**

**(b)** The weight of the solid cuboid is 48 N. The area of the solid cuboid in contact with the ground is 32 cm$^{2}$.

Calculate the pressure exerted by the solid cuboid on the ground.

pressure on ground =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ N / cm$^{2}$ **[3]**

**(c)** A horizontal force of 15 N tilts the solid cone, as shown in Fig. 3.2.

![[0625-m25-32-p5-f2.svg]]

Fig. 3.2

Calculate the moment of the 15 N force about the pivot.

moment =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ N cm **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** (*P* =) 1.5 (N / cm$^{2}$)
>
> **(c)** (moment =) 270 (Ncm)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | (has) lower centre of gravity owtte | B1 |
> | (b) | (*P* =) 1.5 (N / cm$^{2}$) | A3 |
> |  | (*P* =) 48 ÷ 32 | (C2) |
> |  | (*P* =) *F* ÷ *A* | (C1) |
> | (c) | (moment =) 270 (Ncm) | A3 |
> |  | (moment =) 15 × 18 | (C2) |
> |  | moment = force × (perp.) distance (from pivot) | (C1) |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Many candidates found this item difficult. A very common error was to state that the cone had a greater surface area.
>
> **(b)** The vast majority of candidates correctly evaluated the pressure as 1.5 N / cm$^{2}$. The most common error was to multiply the force and the area to give 1536.
>
> **(c)** The majority of candidates were able to correctly determine the moment of the force as 270 N cm. The most common error was to divide the force by the distance to give 0.83.
