> Сравнение теплопроводности металлической и пластмассовой ложек по таянию льда.

An experiment is done to compare the rate of conduction of thermal energy in metal and plastic spoons.

An ice cube is placed on one end of each spoon and the other end is heated gently.

![[0625-m25-22-p5-f1.svg]]

Which row has the correct prediction and correct explanation?

|  | prediction | explanation |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | The ice cube would melt more quickly on the metal spoon. | Metal is a good conductor. |
| **B** | The ice cube would melt more quickly on the metal spoon. | Plastic is a good conductor. |
| **C** | The ice cube would melt more quickly on the plastic spoon. | Metal is a poor conductor. |
| **D** | The ice cube would melt more quickly on the plastic spoon. | Plastic is a poor conductor. |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**
