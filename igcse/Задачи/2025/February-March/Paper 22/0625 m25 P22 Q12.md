> Выбор невозможного значения КПД, превышающего сто процентов.

Four students determine the efficiency of four different electrical devices. The teacher knows that

one student has made a mistake in the calculation.

Which value for efficiency is **not** correct?

**A** 12%  
**B** 36%  
**C** 78%  
**D** 102%

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
