# Question 30 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · February/March 2025 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 2 · вопрос 30  
Источник: [[_pdf/2025/0625_m25_qp_22.pdf#page=12|условие, стр. 12]] · [[_pdf/2025/0625_m25_ms_22.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2025/0625_m25_er.pdf#page=3|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определение направления движения проводника по направлению индуцированного тока и магнитного поля.

A wire is moved in the region between the poles of a magnet.

![[0625-m25-22-p12-f1.svg]]

A current is induced in the wire.

In which direction is the wire moved?

**A** horizontally from the N pole to the S pole  
**B** horizontally from the S pole to the N pole  
**C** perpendicularly into the plane of the paper  
**D** perpendicularly out of the plane of the paper

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Most stronger candidates were able to use the right-hand rule to correctly determine the direction in which the wire was moved. Although most weaker candidates were able to work out that the movement of the wire was perpendicular to the paper, it appears that they used the left-hand rule instead and therefore chose option **D**.
