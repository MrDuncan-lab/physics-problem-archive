> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Расчёт начального объёма газа при изотермическом изменении давления.

A fixed mass of gas is contained within a sealed syringe at a pressure of 100 kPa. The plunger is slowly moved until the pressure is 80 kPa. The final volume of the gas is 50 cm$^{3}$. The temperature of the gas does **not** change.

What is the volume of the gas before the plunger is moved?

**A** 0.25 cm$^{3}$  
**B** 40 cm$^{3}$  
**C** 63 cm$^{3}$  
**D** 160 cm$^{3}$

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> In this question, candidates had to use the relationship *pV* = constant. Most candidates were confident in using this relationship correctly. The most common incorrect option chosen by weaker candidates was option **C**. This indicated that they had misread the information in the question and used the value given for the final volume of the gas as the initial volume instead, and then proceeded to calculate a new value for the final volume of the gas. Candidates could benefit from underlining or highlighting numerical data in questions and writing a clear list of each quantity before substituting into equations.
