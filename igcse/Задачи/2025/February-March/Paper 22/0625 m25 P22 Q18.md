> Определение влияния площади поверхности и температуры на скорость теплового излучения.

A hot object emits thermal radiation.

Which row describes two changes that both reduce the rate of emission of radiation?

|  | surface area | surface temperature |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | increased | increased |
| **B** | increased | reduced |
| **C** | reduced | increased |
| **D** | reduced | reduced |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
