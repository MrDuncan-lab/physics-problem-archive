# Question 8 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · February/March 2025 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 2 · вопрос 8  
Источник: [[_pdf/2025/0625_m25_qp_22.pdf#page=4|условие, стр. 4]] · [[_pdf/2025/0625_m25_ms_22.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2025/0625_m25_er.pdf#page=3|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определение конечного импульса отбитого мяча по силе и длительности удара.

A tennis ball with momentum 2.3 kg m / s is struck by a tennis racket.

The racket causes the direction of motion of the ball to reverse.

The racket exerts an average force of 1500 N during the impact with the ball. The racket and ball are in contact with each other for a time of 0.0024 s.

What is the magnitude of the momentum of the ball as it leaves the tennis racket?

**A** 1.3 kg m / s  
**B** 3.6 kg m / s  
**C** 5.9 kg m / s  
**D** 8.6 kg m / s

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> In this question, candidates had to use the equation *F*Δ*t* = Δ*mv* to calculate the magnitude of the momentum of the ball as it left the tennis racket. Most candidates found this calculation challenging, with only some stronger candidates answering correctly. The most common incorrect answer for weaker candidates was option **B**, indicating that they only calculated the change in momentum. Some other candidates chose option **C**, as they did not consider that the direction of the ball reversed after hitting the racket.
