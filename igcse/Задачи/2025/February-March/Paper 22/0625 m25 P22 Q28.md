> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Расчёт изменения эквивалентного сопротивления цепи после удаления одного резистора.

Four identical resistors are connected to a battery as shown. Each resistor has a value of 120 Ω.

![[0625-m25-22-p10-f1.svg]]

Resistor R$_{4}$ is removed.

Which effect does removing this resistor have on the effective resistance of the circuit?

**A** The effective resistance does **not** change.  
**B** The effective resistance decreases by 20 Ω.  
**C** The effective resistance increases by 20 Ω.  
**D** The effective resistance decreases by 120 Ω.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
