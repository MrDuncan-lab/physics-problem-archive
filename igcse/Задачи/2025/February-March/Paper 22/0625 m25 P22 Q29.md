> Определение показаний амперметра и вольтметра по токам и напряжению в цепи резисторов.

The resistors in the circuit shown are identical.

The reading on voltmeter V$_{2}$ is 1.5 V.

The reading on ammeter A$_{2}$ is 0.50 A.

The reading on ammeter A$_{3}$ is 0.25 A.

![[0625-m25-22-p11-f1.svg]]

What are the readings on ammeter A$_{1}$ and voltmeter V$_{1}$?

|  | ammeter A$_{1}$ / A | voltmeter V$_{1}$ / V |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | 0.25 | 1.5 |
| **B** | 0.25 | 3.0 |
| **C** | 0.75 | 1.5 |
| **D** | 0.75 | 3.0 |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
