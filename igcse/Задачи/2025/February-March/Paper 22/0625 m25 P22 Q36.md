> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбор меры, которая не гарантирует уменьшение облучения при работе с радиоактивными источниками.

Which precaution does **not** always reduce a scientist’s exposure when working with sources of

ionising radiation?

**A** ensuring the scientist only works with sources of radiation that have long half-lives  
**B** increasing the distance between the scientist and the source of radiation  
**C** limiting the time for which the scientist handles the source of radiation  
**D** placing a lead shield between the scientist and the source of radiation

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**
