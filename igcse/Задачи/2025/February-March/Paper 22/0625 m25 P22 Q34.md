> Определение атомного и массового чисел после последовательных альфа- и бета-распадов.

A uranium nucleus $^{238}_{92}$U decays to form thorium by emitting an α-particle. Thorium decays to protactinium by emitting a β-particle.

Which row gives the atomic number and mass number of this isotope of protactinium?

|  | atomic number | mass number |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | 89 | 234 |
| **B** | 90 | 235 |
| **C** | 91 | 234 |
| **D** | 95 | 235 |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
