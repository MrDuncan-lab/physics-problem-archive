> Сопоставление угла поворота катушки генератора с участком графика индуцированной ЭДС.

An a.c. generator is set up so that its coil of wire is perpendicular to the magnetic field. The coil

of wire is rotated slowly to produce an alternating electromotive force (e.m.f.). A graph of the induced e.m.f. against time is plotted as the coil rotates.

![[0625-m25-22-p13-f1.svg]]

Which section of the graph represents a rotation of three-quarters of a turn?

**A** OP  
**B** OQ  
**C** OR  
**D** OS

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
