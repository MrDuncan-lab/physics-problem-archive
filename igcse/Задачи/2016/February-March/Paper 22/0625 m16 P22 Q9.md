> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Связь солнечной энергии с разными природными источниками энергии.

The table gives four energy sources and states whether the energy of the source is derived from the Sun.

Which row is correct?

|  | source of energy | derived from the Sun |
|---|---|---|
| A | geothermal | yes |
| B | oil | no |
| C | water held behind a dam | yes |
| D | wind | no |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Candidates who performed strongly overall often chose option B as correct, incorrectly indicating that oil is not derived from the Sun. Candidates need to be clear about which energy resources are derived from the Sun.
