> Выбор микрометра для точного измерения диаметра тонкой проволоки.

The diameter of a copper wire is thought to be approximately 0.3 mm.

Which instrument should be used to obtain a more accurate measurement of the diameter of the wire?

**A** measuring tape  
**B** metre rule  
**C** micrometer  
**D** ruler

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
