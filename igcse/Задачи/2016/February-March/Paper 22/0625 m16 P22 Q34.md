> Опасность поражения током от неизолированного фазного провода.

After some building work in a house, a bare (uninsulated) live wire is left protruding from a wall.

What is the greatest hazard?

**A** a fire  
**B** a fuse blows  
**C** an electric shock  
**D** no current flows

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
