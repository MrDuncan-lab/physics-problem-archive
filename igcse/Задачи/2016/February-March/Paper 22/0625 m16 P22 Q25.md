> Сравнение скорости звука в газах, жидкостях и твёрдых телах.

The speed of sound in air is 340 m / s.

Which row gives typical values for the speed of sound in a liquid and in a solid?

|  | speed of sound in a liquid / (m / s) | speed of sound in a solid / (m / s) |
|---|---|---|
| A | 250 | 180 |
| B | 250 | 5000 |
| C | 1500 | 180 |
| D | 1500 | 5000 |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
