> Единица измерения длины волны.

Which is a unit of wavelength?

**A** hertz  
**B** metre  
**C** metre per second  
**D** second

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Candidates need to be familiar with the unit of wavelength; many incorrectly chose hertz or metre per second.
