> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Ускорение свободно падающего мяча и увеличение скорости.

A ball is dropped from a table-top. Air resistance may be ignored.

![[0625-m16-12-p2-f2.svg]]

Which row describes the velocity and the acceleration of the ball at point X?

|  | acceleration | velocity |
|---|---|---|
| A | constant | constant |
| B | constant | increasing |
| C | increasing | constant |
| D | increasing | increasing |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Some candidates believed that an object falling without significant air resistance experiences increasing acceleration but has a constant velocity. Candidates need to be clear about the meaning of these two terms.
