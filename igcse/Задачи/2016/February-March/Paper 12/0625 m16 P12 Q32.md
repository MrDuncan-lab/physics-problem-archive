> Эквивалентное сопротивление двух параллельных резисторов.

The diagram shows a 40 Ω resistor and a 60 Ω resistor connected in parallel.

![[0625-m16-12-p15-f1.svg]]

What is the total resistance between points P and Q?

**A** less than 40 Ω  
**B** 50 Ω  
**C** between 60 Ω and 100 Ω  
**D** 100 Ω

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Option D was chosen by many of the candidates. Candidates must ensure that they understand the difference between series and parallel connections of resistors.
