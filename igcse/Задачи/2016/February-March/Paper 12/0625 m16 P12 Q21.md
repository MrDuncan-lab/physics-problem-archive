> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Постоянство скорости и длины волны при прохождении сквозь щель.

A water wave in a shallow tank passes through a gap in a barrier.

![[0625-m16-12-p10-f2.svg]]

What happens to the speed and what happens to the wavelength of the wave as it passes through the gap?

|  | speed | wavelength |
|---|---|---|
| A | decreases | decreases |
| B | decreases | remains constant |
| C | remains constant | decreases |
| D | remains constant | remains constant |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Candidates found this question challenging. Candidates should know that neither the speed nor the wavelength of the waves changes when diffraction occurs.
