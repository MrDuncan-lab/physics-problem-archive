# Question 2 (5 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · February/March 2016 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 2 · вопрос 2

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Импульс молотка, переданный гвоздю, и средняя сила удара.

Fig. 2.1 shows a hammer being used to drive a nail into a piece of wood.

![[0625-m16-42-p4-f1.png]]

**Fig. 2.1**

The mass of the hammer head is 0.15 kg. The speed of the hammer head when it hits the nail is 8.0 m / s. The time for which the hammer head is in contact with the nail is 0.0015 s.

The hammer head stops after hitting the nail.

**(a)** Calculate the change in momentum of the hammer head.

change in momentum =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(b)** State the impulse given to the nail.

impulse =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(c)** Calculate the average force between the hammer and the nail.

average force =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** 1.2 N s or kg m / s
>
> **(c)** 800 N

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | mv – mu OR m(v – u) OR mv OR 0.15 × 8.0 | C1 |
> |  | 1.2 N s or kg m / s | A1 |
> | (b) | 1.2 N s or kg m / s | B1 |
> | (c) | F = (mv – mu) / t OR F = mv / t OR impulse / t OR 1.2 / 0.0015 | C1 |
> |  | 800 N | A1 |
> |  | OR<br>(F =) ma OR m\[(v – u) / t\] OR 0.15 × 8 / 0.0015 | (C1) |
> |  | 800 N | (A1) |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> (a) The numerical value of the change in momentum was calculated correctly in most cases. Mistakes in stating the unit were fairly common however, with kg/m/s often seen.
>
> (b) Many candidates seemed unaware that impulse is change of momentum, so that unnecessary calculations that produced the wrong value of the impulse were common.
>
> (c) Some calculated the deceleration of the hammer head starting with F = ma. Others worked out the force using change of momentum divided by time. Many correct answers were seen.
