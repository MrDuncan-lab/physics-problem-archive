> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Закон Гука и превращение кинетической энергии поезда в энергию пружины.

**(a)** **(i)** On Fig. 3.1, draw a graph of extension against load for a spring which obeys Hooke’s law. **[1]**

![[0625-m16-42-p5-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 3.1**

**(ii)** State the word used to describe the energy stored in a spring that has been stretched or compressed. **[1]**

**(b)** Fig. 3.2 shows a model train, travelling at speed *v,* approaching a buffer.

![[0625-m16-42-p5-f2.png]]

**Fig. 3.2**

The train, of mass 2.5 kg, is stopped by compressing a spring in the buffer. After the train has stopped, the energy stored in the spring is 0.48 J.

Calculate the initial speed *v* of the train.

*v* =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[4]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** v = 0.62 m / s

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | Straight line through origin | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | Strain (energy) OR elastic (energy) | B1 |
> | (b) | Use of 1 / 2mv$^{2}$ | C1 |
> |  | 0.5 × 2.5 × v$^{2}$ = 0.48 | C1 |
> |  | v$^{2}$ = 0.48 / (0.5 × 2.5) OR v$^{2}$ = 0.384 | C1 |
> |  | v = 0.62 m / s | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> (a) (i) A straight line from the origin was required. Some drew a curve from the origin and others added a curve from the end of the straight line, no credit was awarded here.
>
> (ii) Strain energy and elastic energy were both acceptable, and few wrong alternatives were given.
>
> (b) A high number of correct values for the speed were calculated. A few omitted the unit.
