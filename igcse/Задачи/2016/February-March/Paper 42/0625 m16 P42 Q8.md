# Question 8 (8 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · February/March 2016 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 2 · вопрос 8  
Источник: [[_pdf/2016/0625_m16_qp_42.pdf#page=12|условие, стр. 12–13]] · [[_pdf/2016/0625_m16_ms_42.pdf#page=6|схема оценивания, стр. 6]] · [[_pdf/2016/0625_m16_er.pdf#page=8|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Мощность параллельных ламп, допустимый ток предохранителя и сопротивление нити.

**(a)** Fig. 8.1 shows 3 lamps and a fuse connected to a power supply.

![[0625-m16-42-p12-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 8.1**

The e.m.f. of the supply is 220 V. Each lamp is labelled 220 V, 40 W. The rating of the fuse is 2.0 A.

Calculate

**(i)** the current in each lamp,

current =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(ii)** the current in the fuse,

current =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(iii)** the total number of lamps, all in parallel, that could be connected without blowing the fuse.

number =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(b)** After a very long period of use, the wire filament of one of the lamps becomes thinner.

**(i)** Underline the effect of this change on the resistance of the filament.

resistance increases resistance remains the same resistance decreases **[1]**

**(ii)** State and explain the effect of this change on the power of the lamp. **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)(i)** 0.18 A
>
> **(a)(iii)** 10 lamps OR 11 lamps

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | P =IV OR 40 = 220 × I OR (I =) P / V OR 40 / 220 | C1 |
> |  | 0.18 A | A1 |
> | (a)(ii) | \[3 × 0.18(2)\] = 0.54 A OR 0.55 A | B1 |
> | (a)(iii) | 2 / 0.182 = 10.99 OR 2 / 0.18 = 11.1 | C1 |
> |  | 10 lamps OR 11 lamps | A1 |
> | (b)(i) | <u>Resistance increases</u> | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | Power (of lamp) decreases | B1 |
> |  | P = IV <u>and current in lamp decreases. OR P = V</u>$^{2}$ / R | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> (a) (i) A large majority of candidates used the formula P = IV correctly.
>
> (ii) Most of these candidates also correctly trebled the answer in (i).
>
> (iii) According to the number of significant figures used, the answers 11 lamps or 10 lamps were acceptable.
>
> (b) (i) Most candidates recalled that decreasing the thickness of a lamp filament increases its resistance.
>
> (ii) The correct connection between the increase in resistance and a decrease in power was usually made, with an error in (i) allowed for. For the explanation, a reference to either P = IV or P = V$^{2}$ / R was essential.
