> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Изменение объёма воздушного шара при нагревании газа солнцем.

Fig. 4.1 shows a balloon near a window on a warm sunny day.

![[0625-m16-32-p8-f1.png]]

**Fig. 4.1**

Fig. 4.2 shows how the volume of the balloon changes throughout the day.

![[0625-m16-32-p8-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 4.2**

**(a)** Describe how the volume changes throughout the day. **[1]**

**(b)** Explain, in terms of the gas molecules inside the balloon, why the volume changes in this way between 10:00 and 14:00. **[3]**

\[To tal: 4\]

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | volume of balloon increases (until 14:00) then decreases again | B1 |
> | (b) | any three from:<br>• temperature (in room / balloon) increases<br>• gas molecules move faster / have more energy OR collisions more energetic<br>when heated<br>• more frequent / harder collisions<br>• collisions result in greater force on balloon (surface) / gas pressure increases | B3 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> (a) Most candidates were awarded credit. The most common mistake was to only give a description for part of the day.
>
> (b) The strongest candidates scored full credit. Weaker candidates did not link the ideas of temperature increase, increased molecular motion and more frequent or harder collisions with the wall of the balloon.
