> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Равновесие качелей при изменении плеча силы тяжести пассажира.

Fig. 3.1 shows a see-saw. The see-saw is horizontal when not in use.

![[0625-m16-32-p6-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 3.1**

A small child sits on one seat of the see-saw. This creates a turning effect about point P.

**(a)** Which of these words means the turning effect of a force? Tick one box.

![[0625-m16-32-p6-f2.svg]]

**[1]**

**(b)** State the scientific name for point P. **[1]**

**(c)** A much heavier boy sits on the other end of the see-saw, as shown in Fig. 3.2.

![[0625-m16-32-p7-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 3.2**

The heavier boy moves slowly along the see-saw from end B until he reaches point P.

Describe and explain what happens to the see-saw. **[4]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | middle box ticked – moment | B1 |
> | (b) | pivot / fulcrum | B1 |
> | (c) | any four from:<br>• (heavier) boy has greater force / weight / moment<br>• when (heavier) boy lifts feet initially tips clockwise<br>• as boy moves his (clockwise) moment (about P) becomes less<br>• as distance (of boy’s weight) from the pivot decreases end B moves upward<br>• see-saw level o.w.t.t.e (when) turning forces balanced / moments equal<br>• then end A tips down as anticlockwise moment is greater | B4 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> (a),(b) The majority of candidates scored full credit.
>
> (c) Only the strongest candidates gained the majority of the available credit. Most candidates merely described what would happen as the boy moves, and failed to give any explanation in terms of moments.
