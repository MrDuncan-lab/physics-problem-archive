> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определение полюсов магнитов и магнитного поля катушки и прямого тока.

A student uses a bar magnet to distinguish between an unlabelled magnet and an iron bar.

**(a)** Describe how the student identifies which is the magnet and which is the iron bar. **[2]**

**(b)** The student suspends the iron bar near to a coil, as shown in Fig. 10.1. The iron bar is free to move.

> | (a) | (bar) magnet is brought close to both ends (of one of unknown bars) | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> (a) Stronger candidates gained full credit, but weaker candidates did not state that similar poles of magnets would repel.
>
> (b) The majority of candidates scored most of the credit available for this question. Some candidates misread the question and tried to explain that nothing would happen when the switch was open.
>
> (c) There were many examples of carefully drawn field patterns, including the correct direction of the magnetic field.
