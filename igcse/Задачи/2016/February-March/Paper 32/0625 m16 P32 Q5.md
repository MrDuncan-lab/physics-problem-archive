> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Работа волновой электростанции и преимущества возобновляемых источников.

Fig. 5.1 shows a wave-powered generator. It generates electricity from the movement of sea waves.

![[0625-m16-32-p10-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 5.1**

**(a)** The sentences below describe how the wave-powered generator works.

A Air is pushed through the turbine, making it spin. B Water rises and falls in the chamber. C The turbine turns a generator. D The generator produces electrical energy. E Waves travel towards the chamber.

Write letters in the boxes below to arrange the sentences in the correct order. The first one is done for you.

![[0625-m16-32-p10-f2.svg]]

**[3]**

**(b)** More electricity needs to be generated from renewable sources instead of from burning fossil fuels.

State **three** benefits of generating electricity from renewable sources rather than from fossil fuels. **[3]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | correct order: E B A C D<br>1 mark for B immediately before A<br>1 mark for C immediately before D<br>3 marks for all correct i.e. B, A, C then D | B3 |
> | (b) | any three from:<br>• conserve non-renewable reserves<br>• less atmospheric pollution / acid rain<br>• reduces greenhouse gases / global warming<br>• (renewable) energy source will not run out<br>• reduces dependence on fossil fuels (from other countries) | B3 |

>
> (b) Candidates found this a challenging question. The majority of candidates only gave one or two benefits of renewable energy sources, and weaker candidates merely listed other examples of renewable energy sources.
