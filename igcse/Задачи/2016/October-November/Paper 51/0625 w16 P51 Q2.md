> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Исследовать смешивание горячей и холодной воды, уменьшить теплопотери и оценить влияние теплоизоляции на температуру смеси.

In this experiment, you will investigate the cooling of water.

- Pour 100 cm$^{3}$ of the hot water provided into beaker **A**.
- Measure the temperature θ$_{\text{H}}$ of the water in beaker **A**.

θ$_{\text{H}}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

- Pour 100 cm$^{3}$ of the cold water provided into beaker **B**.
- Measure the temperature θ$_{\text{C}}$ of the water in beaker **B**.

θ$_{\text{C}}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

- Calculate the average temperature θ$_{\text{AV}}$ using the equation θ$_{\text{AV}}$ = $\dfrac{\theta _{H} + \theta _{C}}{2}$ .

θ$_{\text{AV}}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

**(b)** Add the water from beaker **B** to the hot water in beaker **A**. Stir briefly.

Measure the temperature θ$_{\text{M}}$ of the mixture.

θ$_{\text{M}}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(c)** State one precaution that you took to ensure that the temperature readings are as reliable as possible. **[1]**

**(d)** Empty both beakers.

You are provided with

- a lid, with a hole for the thermometer,
- some insulating material,
- two elastic bands.

**(i)** In the space below, draw a labelled diagram to show how you will use these items to reduce the loss of thermal energy when the procedure is repeated. **[2]**

**(ii)** Using the improvements shown in your diagram, repeat the procedure in parts **(a)** and

**(b)** .

θ$_{\text{H}}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

θ$_{\text{C}}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

θ$_{\text{AV}}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

θ$_{\text{M}}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(iii)** Comment on whether the improvements made to the apparatus have significantly changed the value of the temperature θ$_{\text{M}}$. Use your results to justify your answer. **[1]**

**(iv)** Suggest two conditions that should be kept constant for all parts of this experiment.

> **(a)** Most candidates recorded realistic temperatures of hot and cold water and calculated the average correctly. Most specified the correct unit for temperature.

>
