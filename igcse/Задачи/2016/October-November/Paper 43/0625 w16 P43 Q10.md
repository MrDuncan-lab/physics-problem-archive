# Question 10 (8 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2016 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 3 · вопрос 10  
Источник: [[_pdf/2016/0625_w16_qp_43.pdf#page=14|условие, стр. 14–15]] · [[_pdf/2016/0625_w16_ms_43.pdf#page=10|схема оценивания, стр. 10]] · [[_pdf/2016/0625_w16_er.pdf#page=32|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w16 P43]]  
Темы: [[4.5.4 Force on a current-carrying conductor]], [[4.5.1 Electromagnetic induction]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Описать движение проводника с током в магнитном поле и показания гальванометра при перемещении проводника.

A wire AB hangs loosely between the N pole and the S pole of a strong magnet.

Fig. 10.1 shows the arrangement.

![[0625-w16-43-p14-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 10.1**

**(a)** The wire is connected to a power supply.

**(i)** When the power supply is switched on, there is a very large direct current (d.c.) in the wire in the direction from B to A.

Describe what happens to the wire as the power supply is switched on. **[2]**

**(ii)** The power supply is adjusted and there is now a large alternating current (a.c.) in the wire.

Describe what happens to the wire. **[1]**

**(b)** The power supply is removed from the circuit and a galvanometer is connected in its place.

**(i)** The wire is moved quickly to the right, between the poles of the magnet.

Explain why the galvanometer registers a current. **[2]**

**(ii)** State what is observed on the galvanometer when the wire is moved to the left at the same speed as in **(b)(i)**. **[2]**

**(iii)** State what is observed on the galvanometer when the wire is moved to the right at a much lower speed than in **(b)(i)**. **[1]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)(i)** moves to right **or** bows out to right

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | experiences force to left or right **or** moves to left or right | C1 |
> |  | moves to right **or** bows out to right | A1 |
> | (a)(ii) | vibrates (to left and right) **or** <u>moves left and right repeatedly</u> | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | (magnetic) field (lines) cut | B1 |
> |  | e.m.f. induced | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | opposite deflection **or** current reversed | B1 |
> |  | same size deflection / current | B1 |
> | (b)(iii) | small(er) deflection / current (in same direction) | B1 |
> |  | **Cambridge IGCSE – October/November 2016** <u>**0625 43**</u> |  |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)(i)** More candidates suggested that the wire would move in the direction of the magnetic field than at right angles to it.
>
> **(ii)** Only a minority of candidates stated that a vibration would be produced.
>
> **(b)(i)** Only the strongest candidates answered this correctly. Very few answers made any reference to induction or to the electromotive force (e.m.f.) induced.
>
> **(ii)** Many candidates stated that the deflection would be through a smaller angle or that the reading would be reduced. A much smaller number indicated that the direction of the deflection would be unchanged. Some candidates incorrectly stated that the deflection would also be slower.
>
> **(iii)** This was generally well answered.
