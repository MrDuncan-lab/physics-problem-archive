**(a)** Fig. 6.1 shows a ray of light striking a plane mirror.

![[0625-w16-32-p9-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 6.1**

State the name given to angle *x*. **[1]**

**(b)** Fig. 6.2 shows an **incorrect** ray diagram for an image of object O formed by a plane mirror.

![[0625-w16-32-p9-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 6.2**

**(i)** On Fig. 6.2, clearly mark the **correct** position of the image. Label this I. **[1]**

**(ii)** Explain why the ray diagram shown in Fig. 6.2 is incorrect. **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)(ii)** angle of incidence ≠ angle of reflection owtte

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Many correct responses were seen. Common errors included reflected ray and refraction.
>
> **(b)** Stronger candidates answered both parts of this question well.
>
> **(c)** This question proved challenging for many candidates. Few candidates gave responses in terms of the angle of incidence not being equal to the angle of reflection. Those that gained partial credit often referred to the image not being equal distance to the mirror as the object.
