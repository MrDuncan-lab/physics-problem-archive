> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Very few candidates gained full credit. Many candidates did not follow the instructions in the question, and gave charges that were not in terms of *e*. Many candidates obtained credit for the correct charge on the proton and beta particle. Common mistakes were to give the charge on an alpha particle as +*e* rather than +2*e* and to state that the neutron and gamma ray were charged.
>
> **(b) (i)** Only the most able candidates gained credit on this question.
>
> **(ii)** Most candidates gained credit here, clearly demonstrating an understanding that alpha and beta particles carry opposite charges.
>
> **(iii)** Most candidates answered this correctly recognising that gamma rays are uncharged.
