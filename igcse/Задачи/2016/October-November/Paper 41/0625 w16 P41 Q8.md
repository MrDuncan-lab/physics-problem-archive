> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать ЭДС последовательно соединённых элементов и энергию, выделенную резистором, описать преобразования энергии.

A battery is made up of 8 cells in series. Each cell has an e.m.f. of 1.5 V.

The battery is connected to one 8.0 Ω resistor for 40 minutes.

**(a)** Calculate the e.m.f. of the battery.

e.m.f. =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(b)** Calculate the energy transferred from the battery in 40 minutes.

energy =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[4]**

**(c)** Describe the energy changes that take place during the 40 minutes. **[2]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | 12 V | B1 |
> | (b) | (I = ) V/R<br>12 / 8 OR 1.5 (A)<br>(W =) IVt OR 1.5 × 12 × 40 (× 60)<br>OR<br>(W =) I$^{2}$Rt OR 1.5$^{2}$ × 8 × 40 (× 60)<br>OR<br>W = V$^{2}$t / R OR 12$^{2}$ × 40 (× 60) / 8<br>43 000 J | C1 C1 C1 A1 |
> | (c) | Chemical (energy) to electrical (energy) (in battery) | B1 |
> |  | Electrical (energy) to thermal / heat (energy) (in resistor) | B1 |
> |  | **Cambridge IGCSE – October/November 2016** <u>**0625 41**</u> |  |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Most candidates gave the correct value of 12 V.
>
> **(b)** The common approach was to begin with a calculation of the current but some candidates used the correct value subsequently without showing the calculation. Some wrong formulae for calculating the energy transferred were written down. Amongst those quoting the correct formula a common error was to fail to convert 40 minutes to seconds.
>
> **(c)** The conversion of chemical energy to electrical energy was given by most candidates but the conversion of electrical to thermal or heat was much less frequently referred to.
