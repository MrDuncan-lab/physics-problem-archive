# Question 5 (7 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2016 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 1 · вопрос 5  
Источник: [[_pdf/2016/0625_w16_qp_31.pdf#page=8|условие, стр. 8–9]] · [[_pdf/2016/0625_w16_ms_31.pdf#page=8|схема оценивания, стр. 8]] · [[_pdf/2016/0625_w16_er.pdf#page=13|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w16 P31]]  
Темы: [[3.2.2 Refraction of light]], [[3.2.1 Reflection of light]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Построить ход лучей при полном внутреннем отражении в стекле и отражении автомобильных фар в плоском окне.

**(a)** Fig. 5.1 shows a ray of red light passing through a semi-circular glass block.

![[0625-w16-31-p8-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 5.1**

**(i)** The ray of light changes direction as it travels into the block.

State the name that is given to this change of direction. **[1]**

**(ii)** Fig. 5.2 shows another ray of red light travelling into the semi-circular glass block. It meets the curved surface at 90°.

Inside the block, the ray meets the flat surface of the block at an angle greater than the critical angle.

![[0625-w16-31-p8-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 5.2**

On Fig. 5.2, complete the path of the ray of red light. **[2]**

**(b)** Fig. 5.3 shows the view from above of a car approaching an observer, marked with a cross (×).

![[0625-w16-31-p9-f1.png]]

**Fig. 5.3**

**(i)** The observer sees the car’s headlights reflected in one of the shop windows. The car’s headlights are labelled.

In which shop window does the observer see the reflection? Show your answer by drawing, on Fig. 5.3, the path of a ray of light from a headlight to the observer. Use a ruler. **[1]**

**(ii)** State the law that you used to answer **(b)(i)**. **[1]**

**(iii)** Add labels to Fig. 5.3 to show how the law stated in **(b)(ii)** applies. **[2]**
