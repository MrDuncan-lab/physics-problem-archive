# Question 9 (7 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2016 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 1 · вопрос 9  
Источник: [[_pdf/2016/0625_w16_qp_31.pdf#page=13|условие, стр. 13]] · [[_pdf/2016/0625_w16_ms_31.pdf#page=11|схема оценивания, стр. 11]] · [[_pdf/2016/0625_w16_er.pdf#page=13|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w16 P31]]  
Темы: [[4.5.6 The transformer]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Указать материал сердечника трансформатора, рассчитать выходное напряжение и объяснить преимущества передачи электроэнергии при высоком напряжении.

The charger for a mobile phone contains a transformer. Fig. 9.1 shows a simple transformer.

![[0625-w16-31-p13-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 9.1**

**(a)** State the name of the material used in the core. **[1]**

**(b)** **(i)** The transformer has 36 000 turns on the primary coil and 900 turns on the secondary coil. The input voltage is 240 V.

Calculate the output voltage.

output voltage =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ V **[3]**

**(ii)** State whether this transformer is *step-up* or *step-down*. Give a reason for your answer. **[1]**

**(c)** Transformers can produce high voltages for transmitting electricity from power stations to towns.

Describe the advantages of transmitting electricity at a high voltage. **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)(i)** 6.0 (V)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | (laminated) iron | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | (V$_{\text{p}}$ / V$_{\text{s}}$ ) = (N$_{\text{p}}$ / N$_{\text{s}}$ ) in any form | C1 |
> |  | 240 / V$_{\text{s}}$ = 36 000 / 900 or V$_{\text{s}}$ = 240 / 40 | C1 |
> |  | 6.0 (V) | A1 |
> | (b)(ii) | step-down (transformer because) there are fewer turns on secondary<br>(compared to primary coil) OR the output voltage is smaller than the<br>input voltage | B1 |
> | (c) | Any 2 from:<br>less energy or power wasted or less heating or more efficient<br>accept lower current<br>can use thinner (transmission) wires or cables<br>fewer power stations needed<br>(so) lower cost for cable and supporting pylons<br>transmit (electricity over) longer distances (without drop in power)<br><u>**Question**</u> **Expected answer** | B2 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** The majority of candidates answered well. Weaker candidates gave materials such as copper and steel.
>
> **(b) (i)** Many candidates correctly calculated the output voltage of the transformer. In weaker answers, numbers were given in some form of ratio, which could not be given any credit.
>
> **(ii)** Only more able candidates linked the step down transformer to either the number of turns on the coils or the change in voltage from one coil to another.
>
> **(c)** While there were a number of strong answers, the vast majority of candidates failed to clearly describe the advantages of transmitting electricity at a high voltage.
