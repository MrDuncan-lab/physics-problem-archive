# Question 3 (6 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2016 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 1 · вопрос 3  
Источник: [[_pdf/2016/0625_w16_qp_31.pdf#page=6|условие, стр. 6]] · [[_pdf/2016/0625_w16_ms_31.pdf#page=6|схема оценивания, стр. 6]] · [[_pdf/2016/0625_w16_er.pdf#page=13|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбрать положение наименьшей поднимающей силы и рассчитать момент веса шлагбаума и вес уравновешивающего противовеса.

Fig. 3.1 shows a barrier pivoted near one end. The barrier is raised to allow cars to pass.

![[0625-w16-31-p6-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 3.1**

**(a)** A force is used to raise the barrier off the support.

On Fig. 3.1, draw an arrow to show the position and direction of the smallest force that can be used to raise the barrier. **[2]**

**(b)** The barrier has a weight of 200 N. This acts at a distance of 2.0 m from the pivot, as shown in Fig. 3.1.

Calculate the moment of the weight of the barrier about the pivot.

moment =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ N m **[2]**

**(c)** To reduce the force needed to raise the barrier, a counterweight is added, as shown in Fig. 3.2.

![[0625-w16-31-p6-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 3.2** (not to scale)

The weight *W* of the counterweight acts at a distance of 0.5 m from the pivot. The barrier is in equilibrium, without the support.

Calculate the weight *W* of the counterweight.

weight =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ N **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** 400 (Nm)
>
> **(c)** **OR** 400 = W × 0.50 **OR** 400 / 0.50 800 (N) <u>**Question**</u> **Expected answer**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | arrow drawn vertically upwards (by eye) | B1 |
> |  | arrow positioned beyond RH support | B1 |
> | (b) | moment = force × (perp.) distance from pivot or 200 × 2.0 | C1 |
> |  | 400 (Nm) | A1 |
> | (c) | sum of Clockwise moments = sum of Anticlockwise moments | C1 |
> |  | **OR** 400 = W × 0.50 **OR** 400 / 0.50<br>800 (N)<br><u>**Question**</u> **Expected answer** | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Only the stronger candidates answered this question fully correctly and recognised that the force must be applied further away from the pivot than the support for the barrier.
>
> **(b)** Many candidates answered this question well but a large number thought that moment = force ÷ distance from pivot.
>
> **(c)** Many candidates found this question challenging. In many answers there was confusion of forces and distances in attempts at principle of moments calculations.
