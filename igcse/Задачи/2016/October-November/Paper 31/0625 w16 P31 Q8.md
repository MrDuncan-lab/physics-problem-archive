# Question 8 (7 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2016 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 1 · вопрос 8  
Источник: [[_pdf/2016/0625_w16_qp_31.pdf#page=12|условие, стр. 12]] · [[_pdf/2016/0625_w16_ms_31.pdf#page=10|схема оценивания, стр. 10]] · [[_pdf/2016/0625_w16_er.pdf#page=13|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w16 P31]]  
Темы: [[4.3.1 Circuit diagrams and circuit components]], [[4.2.4 Resistance]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Дополнить обозначения измерительных приборов, рассчитать сопротивление проволоки и объяснить изменение тока при уменьшении её толщины.

A student measures the resistance of a sample of wire.

She plans to use the circuit shown in Fig. 8.1.

![[0625-w16-31-p12-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 8.1**

Two circuit symbols are incomplete.

**(a)** Complete the symbols for the two meters on Fig. 8.1. **[2]**

**(b)** The current in the wire is 0.20 A. The potential difference across the wire is 6.0 V.

Calculate the resistance of the wire.

resistance =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ Ω **[3]**

**(c)** The student tests a thinner wire. It is the same length as the wire in **(b)** and is made of the same material. The potential difference across the wire is 6.0 V.

Explain how the current in this thinner wire compares with that in the first wire. **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** 30 (Ω)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | A in circle in series with wire | B1 |
> |  | V in circle in parallel with wire | B1 |
> | (b) | V = I R **OR** (R =) V / I | C1 |
> |  | 6.0 / 0.2 | C1 |
> |  | 30 (Ω) | A1 |
> | (c) | current is smaller (in 2nd wire) | B1 |
> |  | (as) resistance is greater (in 2nd wire)<br><u>**Question**</u> **Expected answer** | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** A number of candidates failed to put an A or a V in either meter.
>
> **(b)** The majority of candidates correctly calculated the resistance of the wire. Weaker candidates used an incorrect transformation of V = IR.
>
> **(c)** Only the strongest candidates were able to link a decrease in current to the increased resistance of the thinner wire.
