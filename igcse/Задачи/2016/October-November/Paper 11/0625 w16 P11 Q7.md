> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить удлинение пружины при заданной нагрузке по таблице её длины.

A student investigates a steel spring. He measures the length of the spring, then he hangs different weights from the spring. He measures the length of the spring for each different weight.

The table shows his results.

| weight / N | length of spring / mm |
|---|---|
| 0.0 | 108 |
| 2.0 | 116 |
| 4.0 | 124 |
| 6.0 | 132 |

A weight of 3.0 N is hung from the spring.

What is the extension of the spring?

**A** 4 mm  
**B** 12 mm  
**C** 40 mm  
**D** 120 mm

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Many candidates failed to read this question carefully and chose option **D**, giving the spring’s length rather than its extension.
