> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнить преломление красного и фиолетового света на обеих гранях призмы.

When white light passes through a glass prism, it disperses as shown in the diagram.

![[0625-w16-11-p10-f3.svg]]

white light

Which row compares the refraction of violet light with the refraction of red light at the faces 1 and 2 of the prism?

|  | face 1 | face 2 |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | violet refracts less | violet refracts less |
| **B** | violet refracts less | violet refracts more |
| **C** | violet refracts more | violet refracts less |
| **D** | violet refracts more | violet refracts more |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Despite the labelled diagram, many candidates believed that violet light refracted less at face 1 and more at face 2 (Option **B**).
