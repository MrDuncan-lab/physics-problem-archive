> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить способ передачи тепла, потери которого уменьшает алюминиевая фольга на стенах помещения.

Aluminium foil is attached to the walls of a warm room in a cold country. This reduces the rate of

thermal energy loss from inside the warm room.

Which method or methods of thermal energy transfer are reduced by the foil?

**A** conduction and convection only  
**B** conduction only  
**C** convection and radiation only  
**D** radiation only

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> All options were equally popular on this question on transfer of thermal energy suggesting a lack of knowledge of this topic.
