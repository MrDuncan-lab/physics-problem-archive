> Определить, какой запас энергии увеличивается при зарядке аккумулятора телефона.

What is the main energy change taking place in the battery of a mobile phone (cell phone) as it is being charged but not used?

**A** chemical energy increases  
**B** elastic energy increases  
**C** gravitational potential energy increases  
**D** kinetic energy increases
