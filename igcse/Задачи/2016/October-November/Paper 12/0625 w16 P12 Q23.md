> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Найти ошибочную схему прохождения света через стеклянный блок с учётом заданного критического угла.

A student draws four diagrams to represent light passing from air through a glass block and then

back into the air.

The critical angle for the glass is 42°.

In which diagram is the path of the light through the glass block **not** correct?

![[0625-w16-12-q23-options.svg]]

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> A large proportion of candidates did not believe that a ray of light could pass through a rectangular glass block without being deviated, and therefore chose diagram **B** as the incorrect one.
