> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить направление отклонения альфа-частицы в однородном магнитном поле.

An α-particle enters a uniform magnetic field directed out of the page.

![[0625-w16-23-p17-f1.svg]]

In which direction is the α-particle deflected by the field?

**A** into the page  
**B** out of the page  
**C** to the left  
**D** to the right

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Although most candidates knew that α-particles would be deflected towards the left or right, many of them either misused or misunderstood the left-hand rule.
