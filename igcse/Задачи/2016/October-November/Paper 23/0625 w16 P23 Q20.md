> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объяснить различие огибания препятствия звуком и светом через различие длин волн.

A man is talking at the side of a house. He can be heard by a woman at the front of the house

even though she cannot see him.

![[0625-w16-23-p9-f2.svg]]

What is the explanation for this?

**A** Sound waves are longitudinal and light waves are transverse.  
**B** Sound waves are transverse and light waves are longitudinal.  
**C** The sound waves have a long wavelength and the light waves have short wavelength.  
**D** The sound waves have a short wavelength and the light waves have long wavelength.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> More than half the candidates incorrectly chose option **A**. This was a true statement about the nature of sound and light waves, but did not explain the effect described. Candidates should be warned that a statement, although true, might not be the correct answer to a question, and they should read carefully what is required from the question.
