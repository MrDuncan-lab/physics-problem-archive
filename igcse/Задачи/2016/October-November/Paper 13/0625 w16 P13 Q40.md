> Определить период полураспада и пропущенное значение скорости испускания частиц по таблице.

A radioactive material emits α-particles.

The table shows the rate of emission of α-particles from the material at different times.

| time | rate of emission |
|---|---|
| / hours | / particles per minute |
| 0.0 4.0 8.0 | 160 40 |

What is the half-life of the material and what is the missing value at 8.0 hours?

|  | half-life / hours | rate of emission at 8.0 hours / particles per minute |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | 2.0 | 0 |
| **B** | 2.0 | 10 |
| **C** | 4.0 | 0 |
| **D** | 4.0 | 10 |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
