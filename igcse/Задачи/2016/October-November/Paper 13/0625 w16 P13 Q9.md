# Question 9 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2016 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 3 · вопрос 9  
Источник: [[_pdf/2016/0625_w16_qp_13.pdf#page=6|условие, стр. 6]] · [[_pdf/2016/0625_w16_ms_13.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2016/0625_w16_er.pdf#page=5|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбрать энергетический баланс для шарика, движущегося по шероховатому желобу с потерями на трение.

A ball is released from point X on a rough semicircular track. It comes to rest for an instant at point Y. The gravitational potential energy of the ball at point X is *E*$_{\text{X}}$. The gravitational potential energy of the ball at point Y is *E*$_{\text{Y}}$.

![[0625-w16-13-p6-f1.svg]]

Which equation relates the energy of the ball at point X to its energy at point Y?

**A** *E*$_{\text{X}}$ = *E*$_{\text{Y}}$ + work done against friction  
**B** *E*$_{\text{X}}$ = *E*$_{\text{Y}}$ – work done against friction  
**C** *E*$_{\text{X}}$ + *E*$_{\text{Y}}$ = work done against friction  
**D** *E*$_{\text{X}}$ + *E*$_{\text{Y}}$ + work done against friction = 0

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Stronger candidates often performed well in this question dealing with conservation of energy, although weaker ones tended to choose option **C**, believing that work done against friction was equal to the sum of the gravitational potential energies at **X** and **Y**.
