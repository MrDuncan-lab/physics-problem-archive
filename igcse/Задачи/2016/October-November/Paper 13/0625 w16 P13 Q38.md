> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбрать обязательное общее свойство изотопов одного химического элемента.

Below are four statements about different isotopes of a certain element.

Which statement **must** be true?

**A** They have a different number of electrons.  
**B** They have the same number of neutrons.  
**C** They have the same number of nucleons.  
**D** They have the same number of protons.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
