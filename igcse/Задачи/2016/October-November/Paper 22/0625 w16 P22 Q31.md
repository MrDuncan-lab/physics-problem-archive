Источник: [[_pdf/2016/0625_w16_qp_22.pdf#page=13|условие, стр. 13]] · [[_pdf/2016/0625_w16_ms_22.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2016/0625_w16_er.pdf#page=9|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить сопротивление медной проволоки при увеличении её длины и диаметра.

A copper wire has a resistance of 2.0 Ω.

A second copper wire is twice as long as the first wire, and its diameter is twice the diameter of the first wire.

What is the resistance of the second wire?

**A** 1.0 Ω  
**B** 2.0 Ω  
**C** 8.0 Ω  
**D** 16.0 Ω

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> A very large proportion of candidates, even the most able, failed to appreciate that doubling the diameter of the wire (rather than its cross-sectional area) would not have an equal but opposite effect to doubling its length, and they therefore chose option **B**.
