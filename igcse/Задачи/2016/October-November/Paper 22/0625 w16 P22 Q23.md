> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбрать формулы показателя преломления через скорости света и углы падения и преломления.

The diagram shows light passing from air into glass. Two angles *p* and *q* are marked.

![[0625-w16-22-p11-f1.svg]]

Which pair of equations can both be used to calculate the refractive index *n* of the glass?

**A** *n* = $\dfrac{\text{speed} \text{of} \text{light} \text{in} \text{air}}{\text{speed} \text{of} \text{light} \text{in} \text{glass}}$ , *n* = $\dfrac{\text{sin} p}{\text{sin} q}$  
**B** *n* = $\dfrac{\text{speed} \text{of} \text{light} \text{in} \text{glass}}{\text{speed} \text{of} \text{light} \text{in} \text{air}}$ , *n* = $\dfrac{\text{sin} p}{\text{sin} q}$  
**C** *n* = $\dfrac{\text{speed} \text{of} \text{light} \text{in} \text{air}}{\text{speed} \text{of} \text{light} \text{in} \text{glass}}$ , *n* = $\dfrac{\text{sin} q}{\text{sin} p}$  
**D** *n* = $\dfrac{\text{speed} \text{of} \text{light} \text{in} \text{glass}}{\text{speed} \text{of} \text{light} \text{in} \text{air}}$ , *n* = $\dfrac{\text{sin} q}{\text{sin} p}$

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**
