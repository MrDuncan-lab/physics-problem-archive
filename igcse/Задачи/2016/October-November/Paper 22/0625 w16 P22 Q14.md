> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить давление газа после уменьшения объёма при постоянной температуре.

The diagram shows a gas that is trapped in a cylinder by a piston. The volume of the gas is

120 cm$^{3}$ and the pressure of the gas is *P*.

![[0625-w16-22-p7-f1.svg]]

The piston is moved slowly to the left so that the volume of the gas is reduced to 30 cm$^{3}$. The temperature of the gas does not change.

What is the new pressure of the trapped gas?

**A** $\dfrac{P}{4}$  
**B** $\dfrac{P}{2}$  
**C** *P*  
**D** 4*P*

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
