> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнить мощность инфракрасного излучения тел с разными размерами и температурами.

The diagrams show four spherical objects of the same colour and same type of surface. Two of

the objects are small and two are large. Two of the objects are at the same high temperature and two are at the same low temperature.

Which object emits infra-red radiation at the greatest rate?
