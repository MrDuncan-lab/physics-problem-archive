# Question 22 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2016 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 2 · вопрос 22  
Источник: [[_pdf/2016/0625_w16_qp_22.pdf#page=10|условие, стр. 10]] · [[_pdf/2016/0625_w16_ms_22.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2016/0625_w16_er.pdf#page=9|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w16 P22]]  
Темы: [[3.2.1 Reflection of light]], [[3.2.3 Thin lenses]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнить типы изображений, создаваемых плоским зеркалом и увеличительным стеклом.

An image is formed by a plane mirror. A second image is formed by a lens used as a magnifying

glass.

Which row states the nature of each of these images?

|  | plane mirror | magnifying glass |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | real | real |
| **B** | real | virtual |
| **C** | virtual | real |
| **D** | virtual | virtual |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Weaker candidates were often not confident enough in their knowledge of the nature of images to choose the correct option. The popularity of options **B** and **C** suggested that many thought the descriptions must be different for the two images.
