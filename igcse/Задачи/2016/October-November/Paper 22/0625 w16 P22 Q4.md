> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить свойство тела, характеризующее инертность, и поле, вызывающее его вес.

An object tends to keep moving with the same speed and in the same direction due to a certain property.

The object also has weight due to the action of a field.

What is the name of the property, and what is the name of the field?

|  | property | field |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | mass | electric |
| **B** | mass | gravitational |
| **C** | volume | electric |
| **D** | volume | gravitational |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
