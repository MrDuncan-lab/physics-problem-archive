> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить удлинение пружины по графику её длины в зависимости от нагрузки.

The diagrams show a spring and a graph of the length of the spring against the load applied to it.

![[0625-w16-22-p5-f1.svg]]

What is the extension of the spring when a load of 40 N is applied to it?

**A** 1.5 cm  
**B** 2.5 cm  
**C** 4.0 cm  
**D** 6.5 cm

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> A significant number of candidates failed to read this question carefully and chose option **C**, giving the spring’s length rather than its extension.
