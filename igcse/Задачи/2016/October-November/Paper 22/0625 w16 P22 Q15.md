# Question 15 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2016 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 2 · вопрос 15  
Источник: [[_pdf/2016/0625_w16_qp_22.pdf#page=7|условие, стр. 7]] · [[_pdf/2016/0625_w16_ms_22.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2016/0625_w16_er.pdf#page=9|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

Темы: [[2.2.3 Melting, boiling and evaporation]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить, меняется ли температура льда при плавлении и воды при кипении.

A piece of melting ice at 0 °C and a beaker of boiling water are both in a laboratory. The laboratory is at 20 °C.

![[0625-w16-22-p7-f2.svg]]

What is happening to the temperature of the melting ice and what is happening to the temperature of the boiling water?

|  | temperature of melting ice | temperature of boiling water |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | constant | constant |
| **B** | constant | increasing |
| **C** | increasing | constant |
| **D** | increasing | increasing |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Although stronger candidates answered this question correctly, Option **C** was a very popular choice suggesting a belief that the temperature of ice increases as it melts.
