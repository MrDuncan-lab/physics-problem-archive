> Выбрать изолирующие нити, определить взаимодействие заряженных шариков и объяснить получение положительного заряда.

Fig. 8.1 shows two uncharged (neutral) plastic spheres. Each sphere is suspended by an insulating thread.

![[0625-w16-33-p13-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 8.1**

**(a)** Suggest a material for the insulating threads. **[1]**

**(b)** The spheres can be given a charge.

Three different experiments are carried out using the arrangements shown in Fig. 8.2.

![[0625-w16-33-p13-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 8.2**

For each experiment, describe the force, if any, between the spheres.

experiment 1  \_\_\_\_\_\_

experiment 2  \_\_\_\_\_\_

experiment 3  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

**(c)** State and explain how one of the plastic spheres can be given a positive charge. **[2]**
