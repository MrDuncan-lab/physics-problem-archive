> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнить задержку грома после вспышки молнии для наблюдателей на разных расстояниях от места удара.

Two people watch a storm cloud above a tree. Person A is watching from her house and person B is watching from a distant hill.

Lightning creates a bright flash and loud thunder at the same time. The lightning strikes the tree not far from person A’s house, as shown in Fig. 7.1.

![[0625-w16-33-p12-f1.png]]

**Fig. 7.1**

For each person, describe the time interval between seeing the lightning and hearing the thunder and explain your answers.

person A description of time interval  \_\_\_\_\_\_

person B description of time interval  \_\_\_\_\_\_

explanation  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[4]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> |  | Person A : lightning seen and thunder heard at (almost) same time<br>Person B : lightning seen first OR thunder heard later/after flash of lightning<br>Explanation:<br>light travels faster than sound **OR** reverse argument<br>sound has further to travel to B so time delay is greater or similar argument **OR** distances for A are so<br>short that no observable difference in time. | B1 B1 B1 B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Stronger candidates gave clear, correct responses and gained full credit. However, there were many vague and incorrect responses. A significant number of these did not refer to the time interval between the lightning and the thunder. Misconceptions included sound being heard more quickly or louder by the person on the hill as they were closer to the clouds and the lightning being brighter because the person in the house was closer.
