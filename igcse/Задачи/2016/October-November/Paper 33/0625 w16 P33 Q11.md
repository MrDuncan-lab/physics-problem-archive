# Question 11 (5 marks)

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить типы трансформаторов в электросети, рассчитать выходное напряжение и назвать преимущества высоковольтной передачи.

Fig. 11.1 represents part of an electricity transmission system.

![[0625-w16-33-p17-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 11.1**

**(a)** Transformers can be *step-up* or *step-down*.

State the type of transformer shown at X and the type of transformer shown at Y.

X  \_\_\_\_\_\_

Y  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(b)** A transformer has a primary coil of 24 000 turns, a secondary coil of 2000 turns and an input voltage of 132 000 V.

Calculate the output voltage.

output voltage =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ V **[2]**

**(c)** State **two** advantages of transmitting electricity at high voltages.

1 \_\_\_\_\_\_

2 \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** 11 000 (V)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | X = step up **AND** Y = step down | B1 |
> | (b) | V$_{\text{p}}$/V$_{\text{s}}$ = N$_{\text{p}}$/N$_{\text{s}}$ **OR** V$_{\text{s}}$ = 132 000 /(24 000/2000) **OR** turns ratio, 12 calculated | C1 |
> |  | 11 000 (V) | A1 |
> | (c) | any two from:<br>less heating **OR** less energy **OR** power wasted **OR** more efficient<br>thinner wires **OR** cables<br>fewer power stations<br>lower current in cables<br>transmit longer distances (without drop in power) | B2 |
> |  | **Cambridge IGCSE – October/November 2016** <u>**0625 33**</u> |  |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** This was well answered by most candidates.
>
> **(b)** A high proportion of candidates obtained the correct value.
>
> **(c)** Many candidates obtained at least partial credit for this question. There were many vague responses, for example, “cheaper” that could not be credited. A common misconception was that higher voltages allowed electricity to be transmitted faster.
