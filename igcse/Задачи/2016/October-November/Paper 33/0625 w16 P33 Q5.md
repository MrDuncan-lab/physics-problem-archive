# Question 5 (5 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2016 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 3 · вопрос 5  
Источник: [[_pdf/2016/0625_w16_qp_33.pdf#page=10|условие, стр. 10]] · [[_pdf/2016/0625_w16_ms_33.pdf#page=5|схема оценивания, стр. 5]] · [[_pdf/2016/0625_w16_er.pdf#page=20|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объяснить возникновение бриза у тёплого озера и теплоизоляцию куртки через конвекцию и теплопроводность.

A fisherman notices that the water in a lake is warm and that there is a cool breeze from the land, as shown in Fig. 5.1.

![[0625-w16-33-p10-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 5.1**

**(a)** The fisherman tries to explain why there is a cool breeze from the land. The statements below describe his ideas.

Put a tick (✓) in the box alongside each correct statement.

![[0625-w16-33-p10-f2.svg]]

Air above the surface of the warm lake becomes less dense.

Air is a good thermal conductor.

Denser water rises to the surface of the lake.

The cool breeze occurs as a result of convection.

Warmer air rises.

Water is a good thermal conductor. **[3]**

**(b)** As the night gets colder, the fisherman puts on a jacket to keep warm.

Explain how the jacket keeps the fisherman warm. **[2]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | air above water becomes less dense | B1 |
> |  | cool breeze occurs as a result of convection | B1 |
> |  | warm air rises | B1 |
> | (b) | (jacket) traps air | B1 |
> |  | air is an insulator **OR** prevents convection | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Many candidates gained partial credit here with only the better prepared candidates answering fully correctly.
>
> **(b)** This question was challenging for almost all candidates. A small number of candidates gave rather vague responses about insulation but there were very few that explained that trapped air acted as the insulator.
