# Question 6 (5 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2016 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 3 · вопрос 6

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Показать направление отражённого луча, обозначить углы и описать свойства изображения свечи в плоском зеркале.

A candle is placed in front of a vertical mirror. Fig. 6.1 shows a ray of light reflected from the mirror.

![[0625-w16-33-p11-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 6.1**

**(a)** **(i)** On Fig. 6.1, draw an arrow on the ray to show the direction of travel of the ray of light. **[1]**

**(ii)** On Fig. 6.1, label the angle of incidence *i* and the angle of reflection *r*. **[1]**

**(iii)** Tick (✓) **two** boxes to describe the image of the candle in the mirror.

![[0625-w16-33-p11-f2.svg]]

closer to the mirror than the candle

the same distance from the mirror as the candle

on the surface of the mirror

smaller than the candle

the same size as the candle **[2]**

**(b)** Other rays of light also strike the mirror and are reflected.

State the rule that always links the angle of incidence and the angle of reflection. **[1]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | arrow on incident ray pointing towards mirror **OR** arrow on reflected ray pointing away from mirror | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | *i* **AND** *r* both correctly labelled | B1 |
> | (a)(iii) | same distance from mirror as candle | B1 |
> |  | same size as the candle | B1 |
> | (b) | angle of incidence = angle of reflection | B1 |
