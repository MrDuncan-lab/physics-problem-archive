> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Прочитать график скорости, описать движение, определить пройденное расстояние и среднюю скорость за весь промежуток.

Fig. 1.1 shows how the speed of an object varies during a period of 30 s.

![[0625-y16-3-p2-f1.svg]]

Fig. 1.1

**(a)** State the speed of the object

**(i)** at the start, time = 0 s,

speed = m / s

**(ii)** at the end, time = 30 s.

speed = m / s **[2]**

**(b)** Describe what, if anything, is happening to the speed during the period 10 s to 25 s. **[1]**

**(c)** Determine the distance travelled in the last 5 s.

distance = m **[3]**

**(d)** The total distance travelled during the 30 s is 750 m.

Calculate the average speed of the object during the 30 s.

average speed = m / s **[3]**
