**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · Specimen 2016 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core) · вопрос 6  
Источник: [[_pdf/2016/0625_y16_qp_3.pdf#page=10|условие, стр. 10]] · [[_pdf/2016/0625_y16_ms_3.pdf#page=4|схема оценивания, стр. 4]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Оценить экологические преимущества и практические условия установки ветрогенератора на предприятии.

The owner of a small factory suggests installing a wind turbine to generate some of the electricity needed by the factory.

**(a)** Give one environmental reason for using a wind turbine. **[1]**

**(b)** Discuss three of the factors that the owner will need to consider when deciding whether to install a wind turbine. **[4]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | less pollution / reduced carbon (dioxide) emissions (compared to fossil fuels) OR other<br><u>environmental</u> reason | 1 |
> | (b) | any three from:<br>output expected from wind turbine<br>energy use by factory<br>wind is intermittent<br><u>whether location has suitable amount of wind</u><br>cost / time to recoup cost of turbine<br>whether location / noise will cause nuisance to neighbours | max 3 |
> |  | valid discussion of at least one factor from list above, linking it to the decision | 1 |
