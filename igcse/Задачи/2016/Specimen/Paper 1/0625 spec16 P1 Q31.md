A circuit is set up to determine the resistance of a resistor R. The meter readings are 2.0 A and 3.0 V.

![[0625-y16-1-p15-f1.svg]]

What is the resistance of the resistor R?

**C** 5.0 Ω  
**D** 6.0 Ω
