> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить массу неизвестного груза по результатам балансирования линейки и наклону графика.

A student is determining the mass of a load using a balancing method.

Fig. 1.1 shows the apparatus.

![[0625-y16-6-p2-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 1.1**

The load **X** has been taped to the metre rule so that its centre is exactly over the 90.0 cm mark. It is not moved during the experiment.

A mass *m* of 40 g is placed on the rule and its position adjusted so that the rule is as near as possible to being balanced with the 50.0 cm mark exactly over the pivot. Fig. 1.2(a) shows part of the rule when it is balanced.

The procedure is repeated for a range of masses. Fig. 1.2(b) – (e) shows the rule when balanced for values of *m* of 50 g, 60 g, 70 g and 80 g.

![[0625-y16-6-p2-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 1.2**

**(a)** **(i)** Use Fig. 1.2 to determine *d*, the distance between the mass and the pivot at balance, for each value of *m*. Record your results in Table 1.1. **[3]**

**Table 1.1**

![[0625-y16-6-p3-f1.svg]]

**(ii)** For each value of *d*, calculate 1 / *d* and record it in the table. **[1]**

**(b)** Describe one diffi culty the student might have when carrying out this experiment, and how he might overcome this diffi culty. **[2]**

![[0625-y16-6-p3-f2.svg]]

**(c)** Plot a graph of *m* / g (*y*-axis) against (*x*-axis).

![[0625-y16-6-p3-f3.svg]]

**[4]**

**(d)** Determine the gradient *G* of the graph. Show clearly on the graph how you obtained the necessary information.

*G* =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(e)** Determine the mass µ, in grams, of the load **X**. Use the equation µ = $\dfrac{G}{40.0}$ .

µ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ g **[1]**
