> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить индуцированные полюса катушки при приближении и удалении магнита по правилу Ленца.

A solenoid is connected in series with a sensitive ammeter. The N pole of a magnet is placed

next to one end of the solenoid, marked X.

![[0625-y16-2-p19-f1.svg]]

First, the N pole of the magnet is pushed towards X, then the magnet is pulled away from X. During both stages the ammeter deflects.

Which type of magnetic pole is induced at X during these two stages?

|  | as N pole moves towards X | as N pole moves away from X |
|---|---|---|
| A | N pole | N pole |
| B | N pole | S pole |
| C | S pole | N pole |
| D | S pole | S pole |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
