# Question 12 (8 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2016 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 3 · вопрос 12  
Источник: [[_pdf/2016/0625_s16_qp_33.pdf#page=18|условие, стр. 18–19]] · [[_pdf/2016/0625_s16_ms_33.pdf#page=10|схема оценивания, стр. 10]] · [[_pdf/2016/0625_s16_er.pdf#page=22|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 s16 P33]]  
Темы: [[5.2.2 The three types of nuclear emission]], [[5.2.4 Half-life]], [[5.2.5 Safety precautions]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Описать бета-частицу, определить период полураспада и активность цезия, объяснить опасность длительного радиоактивного загрязнения.

**(a)** A radioactive nucleus decays by the emission of a β-particle.

State what a β-particle is and give its charge. **[2]**

**(b)** The graph in Fig. 12.1 shows how the count rate from a sample of a radioactive substance varies with time.

![[0625-s16-33-p18-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 12.1**

Use the graph to find the half-life. Show your working on the graph.

half-life =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ days **[2]**

**(c)** Following an accident, the soil around a nuclear power station is contaminated by caesium-137, which is radioactive.

A sample of this soil containing caesium-137 has a count rate of 180 counts / min. Caesium-137 has a half-life of 30 years and decays by β-emission.

**(i)** Calculate the count rate from the caesium-137 in the sample after 60 years.

count rate =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ counts / min **[2]**

**(ii)** Suggest why people do not want to live near the power station, even after it has closed. **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** 8 (days)
>
> **(c)(i)** 45 (counts / min)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | (fast moving) electron | B1 |
> |  | negative (charge) | B1 |
> | (b) | line from count rate of 2000 | C1 |
> |  | 8 (days) | A1 |
> | (c)(i) | 180 ÷ 4 | C1 |
> |  | 45 (counts / min) | A1 |
> | (c)(ii) | any two from:<br>radiation mutates DNA / damages (living) cells<br>radioactive material still present (in soil / reactor core / after many years)<br>negative public perception of nuclear power<br>radioactive waste on site contains isotopes with long half-lives | B2 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> (a) The majority of candidates correctly stated that a beta particle was an electron, and that its charge was negative or minus 1. The most common mistake was to give a description of a helium nucleus.
>
> (b) The majority of candidates correctly determined the half-life as 8 days.
>
> (c) (i) Many candidates correctly calculated the count rate as 45 counts/min. Weaker candidates often gained partial credit by correctly stating that 60 years was two half-lives.
>
> (ii) Many candidates gave valid suggestions.
