# Question 10 (4 marks)

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать выходное напряжение зарядного устройства по числу витков и определить тип трансформатора.

Fig. 10.1 shows the charger for a laptop computer.

![[0625-s16-32-p16-f1.png]]

**Fig. 10.1**

The charger contains a transformer.

**(a)** The voltage across the primary coil of the transformer is 230 V.

The primary coil has 4995 turns and the secondary coil has 555 turns.

Calculate the output voltage of the transformer.

output voltage =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ V **[3]**

**(b)** State the term used to describe this type of transformer. **[1]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** 25.6 (V)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | Vp ÷ Vs = Np ÷ Ns in any form | C1 |
> |  | 230 / V s = 4995 ÷ 555 or Vs = 230 ÷ 9 Vs = (555 ÷ 4995) × 230 | C1 |
> |  | 25.6 (V) | A1 |
> | (b) | step-down (transformer)<br><u>32</u><br><u>Question</u> Expected answer <u>Mark</u> | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> (a) The majority of candidates were able to use the transformer equation to correctly determine the output voltage. In this question more candidates set out their working and some could be credited for this even when their final answer was incorrect.
>
> (b) Many candidates recognised that this was a step-down transformer.
