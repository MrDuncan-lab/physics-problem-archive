> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объяснить сохранение температуры кофе за счёт теплоизоляции кружки и плавления либо затвердевания материала стенок.

Researchers have found that the best temperature for drinking coffee is 60 °C.

A designer has developed a new type of cup for keeping coffee at 60 °C. The cup is shown in Fig. 8.1.

![[0625-s16-32-p12-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 8.1**

Material Z has a melting point of 60 °C. At room temperature, material Z is solid.

Coffee, at a temperature of 90 °C, is poured into the cup. The coffee cools rapidly to 60 °C.

**(a)** State what happens to material Z when the hot coffee is poured into the cup. **[1]**

**(b)** Explain how the features of the cup enable the coffee to be kept at 60 °C for a long time.

plastic lid  \_\_\_\_\_\_

vacuum  \_\_\_\_\_\_

shiny outer steel wall  \_\_\_\_\_\_

material Z  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[5]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | (material Z) melts | B1 |
> | (b) | any five from:<br>(plastic lid) is a good insulator<br>(plastic lid) reduces ( heat loss by) convection<br>(plastic lid) reduces ( heat loss by) evaporation<br>(vacuum) reduces / prevents (heat loss by) convection<br>(vacuum) reduces / prevents (heat loss by) conduction<br>shiny / silver surface is a poor radiator / bad emitter (of thermal energy)<br>at 60 °C material Z solidifies<br>material Z maintains a constant temperature (60 °C) during solidification / internal energy is given out<br><u>32</u><br><u>Question</u> Expected answer <u>Mark</u> | B5 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> (a) Most candidates recognised that the material Z would melt when the hot coffee was poured into the cup.
>
> (b) Weaker candidates struggled with this question, giving vague statements about keeping heat in or stopping heat moving.
