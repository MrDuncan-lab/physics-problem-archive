> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Найти путь велосипедиста по графику скорости, определить действующие силы и рассчитать давление обуви на педаль.

Fig. 2.1 shows the speed-time graph for a student cycling along a straight, flat road.

![[0625-s16-32-p4-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 2.1**

**(a)** Calculate the distance he travels in the first 10 s.

distance =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ m **[3]**

**(b)** Fig. 2.2 shows three pairs of forces A, B and C.

![[0625-s16-32-p5-f1.svg]]

![[0625-s16-32-p5-f2.svg]]

![[0625-s16-32-p5-f3.svg]]

**Fig. 2.2**

Identify which pair of forces, A, B or C, acts on the cyclist between 11 s and 16 s. Explain your choice.

pair of forces  \_\_\_\_\_\_

explanation  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

**(c)** The cyclist pushes on one pedal with a force of 120 N. The area of his shoe in contact with the pedal is 16 cm$^{2}$.

Calculate the pressure on the pedal. Include the unit.

pressure =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[4]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** 44 (m)
>
> **(c)** 7.5

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | (distance) = area under (speed-time) graph in words, numbers or symbols OR distance = speed × time | C1 |
> |  | 4.4 × 10 | C1 |
> |  | 44 (m) | A1 |
> | (b) | C | B1 |
> |  | (cyclist is) accelerating | B1 |
> |  | (so) forward force must be greater than backward force OR there is a resultant (forward) force | B1 |
> | (c) | *P* = *F* ÷ *A* | C1 |
> |  | 120 ÷ 16 | C1 |
> |  | 7.5 | A1 |
> |  | N/$\text{cm}^{2}$ | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> (a) Most candidates correctly determined the area under the graph to give the distance travelled by the student. Common errors were misreads of the speed, with 4.2 m/s very frequently seen. Weaker candidates inverted the equation and divided the speed by 10 instead of multiplying.
>
> (b) Many candidates correctly selected the forces in diagram C and gave clear explanations to gain full credit. Weaker candidates tended to give only partial explanations.
>
> (c) Most candidates correctly determined the pressure and gave the correct unit. Many candidates increased the complexity of the calculation by converting the area into metres in order to give their answer in pascals. There was no need to do this as N/cm$^{2}$ was an acceptable unit. Weaker candidates tended to invert the formula and consequently multiplied force by area.
