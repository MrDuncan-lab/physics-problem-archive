# Question 3 (5 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2016 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 3  
Источник: [[_pdf/2016/0625_s16_qp_32.pdf#page=6|условие, стр. 6]] · [[_pdf/2016/0625_s16_ms_32.pdf#page=5|схема оценивания, стр. 5]] · [[_pdf/2016/0625_s16_er.pdf#page=18|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать момент силы и вес мальчика по условию равновесия качелей.

Fig. 3.1 shows a girl and a boy on a see-saw.

![[0625-s16-32-p6-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 3.1**

The see-saw pivots on the log.

**(a)** Calculate the girl’s moment about the pivot.

girl’s moment =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ N m **[2]**

**(b)** The see-saw is balanced horizontally.

Calculate the weight *W* of the boy.

weight of boy =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ N **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** 720 (N m)
>
> **(b)** 450 (N) <u>Question</u> Expected answer <u>Mark</u>

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | force × distance (from pivot) OR 300 × 2.4 | C1 |
> |  | 720 (N m) | A1 |
> | (b) | sum of clockwise moment = sum of anticlockwise moment | C1 |
> |  | 720 = W × 1.6 OR 720 ÷ 1.6 | C1 |
> |  | 450 (N)<br><u>Question</u> Expected answer <u>Mark</u> | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> (a) Most candidates correctly gave 720 Nm as the value for the girl’s moment about the pivot. Common errors were to divide by 2.4 metres or to multiply by the wrong distance.
>
> (b) Many candidates correctly determined the weight of the boy as 450 N. However, very few candidates set out their working as a moments calculation, and so could gain no credit for any correct workings. Candidates should be encouraged to show their working for all stages of calculations.
