> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Средняя скорость поезда по пройденному расстоянию и времени.

A train begins a journey from a station and travels 60 km in a time of 20 minutes.

What is the average speed of the train?

**A** 3.0 m / s  
**B** 5.0 m / s  
**C** 50 m / s  
**D** 60 m / s

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> In this question on average speed, a very large proportion of candidates chose option A. Students should be reminded always to check units in calculations.
