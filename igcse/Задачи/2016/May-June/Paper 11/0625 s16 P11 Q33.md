> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Изменение напряжения фоторезистора в автоматическом освещении.

The diagram shows part of a circuit used to switch street lamps on and off automatically.

![[0625-s16-11-p14-f2.svg]]

In the evening it gets dark.

Which row shows the effect on the resistance of the light-dependent resistor (LDR) and on the potential difference (p.d.) across it?

|  | resistance of LDR | p.d. across LDR |
|---|---|---|
| A | decreases | decreases |
| B | decreases | increases |
| C | increases | decreases |
| D | increases | increases |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question involving an LDR was not well answered by even the stronger candidates with many believing that the LDR’s resistance would decrease as the light level fell.
