> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определение электрических величин и их единиц по подключённым приборам.

The circuit shown includes two meters X and Y, connected correctly.

![[0625-s16-11-p12-f1.svg]]

Which row gives the unit of the quantity measured by X and the unit of the quantity measured by Y?

|  | meter X | meter Y |
|---|---|---|
| A | ampere | ampere |
| B | ampere | volt |
| C | volt | ampere |
| D | volt | volt |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Only one in four identified the volt as the correct unit for both meters. Students must be careful to read the answers in both of the columns before making their choice.
