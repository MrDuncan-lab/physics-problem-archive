> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объяснить индукцию в движущемся проводнике и построить график ЭДС вращающейся катушки.

The two ends of a metal rod are connected by copper wire to a very sensitive ammeter. The rod is placed horizontally in the horizontal magnetic field between the N-pole and S-pole of a permanent magnet, as shown in Fig. 9.1.

![[0625-s16-43-p18-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 9.1**

**(a)** The rod is moved upwards at constant speed in the direction shown by the arrow in Fig. 9.1.

An e.m.f. is induced in the rod.

**(i)** State **two** factors that determine the size of the induced e.m.f.

1 \_\_\_\_\_\_

2 \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(ii)** The size of the current in the ammeter depends on the size of the induced e.m.f.

State **one** other factor that affects the size of the current. **[1]**

**(iii)** The direction of motion of the rod is now changed . It moves at a constant speed towards the N-pole, as shown in Fig. 9.2.

![[0625-s16-43-p19-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 9.2**

State and explain what happens to the induced e.m.f. **[2]**

**(b)** Fig. 9.3 shows a rectangular coil of wire placed in the same horizontal magnetic field.

![[0625-s16-43-p20-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 9.3**

The coil rotates in the direction shown by the arrow in Fig. 9.3. An e.m.f. is induced in the coil.

**(i)** On Fig. 9.4, sketch a graph to show how the e.m.f. varies with time for **two** complete revolutions of the coil.

![[0625-s16-43-p20-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 9.4** **[2]**

**(ii)** On your sketch in Fig. 9.4, mark a letter T to indicate a time when the coil is horizontal, as shown in Fig. 9.3. **[1]**

![[0625-s16-43-p21-f1.svg]]

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> (a) (i) Many candidates offered one or two reasons and these were very usually correct.
>
> (ii) Although some candidates gave a correct factor, many others suggested an answer that influenced the current by influencing the e.m.f. That this part was concerned with the current was not always noticed.
>
> (b) (i) Most candidates aimed at a sinusoidal curve of some description but many of these drew curves that were not drawn with sufficient care and full credit could often not be awarded.
>
> (ii) Many candidates drew a T at one of the peaks but there were other answers which were not correct.
>
> (iii) Many candidates found this quite challenging but stronger candidates were often able to state what happened and explain why.
