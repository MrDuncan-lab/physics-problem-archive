# Question 5 (7 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2016 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 3 · вопрос 5  
Источник: [[_pdf/2016/0625_s16_qp_43.pdf#page=10|условие, стр. 10–11]] · [[_pdf/2016/0625_s16_ms_43.pdf#page=6|схема оценивания, стр. 6]] · [[_pdf/2016/0625_s16_er.pdf#page=34|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 s16 P43]]  
Темы: [[3.4 Sound]], [[3.1 General properties of waves]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Охарактеризовать ультразвук и виды волн, определить толщину кости по времени прохождения отражённого ультразвукового импульса.

**(a)** Radio waves, ultrasound and visible light are all waves.

**(i)** State what is meant by *ultrasound* and suggest a value for the minimum possible frequency of ultrasound waves. **[2]**

**(ii)** State which of these waves are

- electromagnetic,
- longitudinal. **[2]**

**(b)** Ultrasound passes through both human flesh and bone.

Fig. 5.1 shows an ultrasound source sending an ultrasound wave into human flesh.

![[0625-s16-43-p11-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 5.1**

The ultrasound wave travels through the flesh to point X and then through the thickness of the bone to point Y. At Y, some ultrasound is reflected and returns to X.

The total time taken for ultrasound to travel in the bone from X to Y and back to X is 9.0 × 10$^{\text{–6}}$ s. The speed of ultrasound in bone is 4100m/s.

Calculate the thickness *t* of the bone.

thickness *t* =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** 0.018(45) m

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | (a sound wave with a) frequency above the frequency audible by humans or inaudible (to humans) | B1 |
> |  | 20 000 Hz | B1 |
> |  | visible light and radio | B1 |
> |  | ultrasound | B1 |
> | (b) | (d = )vt ÷ 2 or (d = )vt or 0.0369 (m) | C1 |
> |  | (d = )4100 × 9.0 × 10 – 6 ÷ 2 | C1 |
> |  | 0.018(45) m | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> (a) (i) This part proved challenging for many candidates. The strongest answers mentioned the fact that ultrasound is inaudible to humans. Although the range of audible sound frequencies needs to be known, only a minority of candidates adapted this knowledge to deduce a minimum possible frequency of ultrasound waves.
>
> (ii) This part revealed several misunderstandings although many candidates did produce strong answers.
>
> (b) Most candidates approached this part in the correct way by using the equation x = vt in some form. A very common mistake was to ignore the factor of 2 that is needed because the time given in the question is for the journey from X to Y and back.
