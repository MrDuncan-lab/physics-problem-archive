# Question 6 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2016 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 3 · вопрос 6

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Найти вес нагруженной доски по силе подъёма её конца, используя равновесие моментов.

A long plank XY lies on the ground. A load of 120 N is placed on it, at a distance of 0.50 m from end X, as shown.

End Y is lifted off the ground. The upward force needed to do this is 65 N.

![[0625-s16-23-p4-f1.svg]]

In the diagram, W is the weight of the plank, acting at its mid-point.

What is the value of W ?

**A** 35 N  
**B** 47 N  
**C** 100 N  
**D** 133 N

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question was answered correctly by stronger students, with option B being slightly more popular than the correct answer C. This incorrect value is produced by multiplying each force by the distance shown immediately to its left on the diagram; students choosing this did not appreciate that the total distance to the pivot should be used in each case.
