> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Указать провод для установки предохранителя и объяснить его действие при перегрузке.

A domestic circuit includes a 30 A fuse. This protects the wiring if there is too much current in the

circuit.

In which wire is the 30 A fuse positioned, and what does it do when it operates?

|  | position | operation |
|---|---|---|
| A | live wire | disconnects the circuit |
| B | live wire | reduces the current to 30 A |
| C | neutral wire | disconnects the circuit |
| D | neutral wire | reduces the current to 30 A |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Here a very common mistake was to think that a fuse would reduce the current to its rated value.
