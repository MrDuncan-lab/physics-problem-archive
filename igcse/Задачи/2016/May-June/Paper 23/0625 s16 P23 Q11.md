> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать полезную мощность человека при подъёме трёх ящиков на полку.

Three boxes each weigh 100 N. A man lifts all the boxes together from the ground on to a shelf

that is 1.5 m above the ground. The man takes 2.0 s to do this.

![[0625-s16-23-p5-f2.png]]

How much useful power does the man produce to lift the boxes?

**A** 75 W  
**B** 225 W  
**C** 300 W  
**D** 900 W

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Students should be advised to read questions carefully; many failed to take into account here that there were three boxes.
