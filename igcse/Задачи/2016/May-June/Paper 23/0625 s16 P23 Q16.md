> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбрать изменение конструкции жидкостного термометра, повышающее его чувствительность.

The diagram shows a liquid-in-glass thermometer.

![[0625-s16-23-p7-f2.svg]]

How can the thermometer be made more sensitive?

**A** increase the internal diameter of the tube containing the liquid thread  
**B** increase the internal volume of the glass bulb and the volume of the liquid  
**C** increase the length of the tube and stem  
**D** increase the thickness of the glass in the glass bulb

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> A common error, even for stronger candidates, was to think that increasing the length of the tube and stem would increase sensitivity.
