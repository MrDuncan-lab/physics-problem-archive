> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить изменения частоты и длины водяной волны при переходе в область меньшей скорости распространения.

A water wave passes into a region where the wave travels more slowly.

As it passes into the slow region, what happens to the frequency and what happens to the wavelength of the wave?

|  | frequency | wavelength |
|---|---|---|
| A | decreases | remains the same |
| B | increases | remains the same |
| C | remains the same | decreases |
| D | remains the same | increases |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Many candidates thought that the frequency of the wave, rather than its wavelength, would decrease when refraction occurred.
