# Question 40 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2016 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 3 · вопрос 40  
Источник: [[_pdf/2016/0625_s16_qp_23.pdf#page=17|условие, стр. 17]] · [[_pdf/2016/0625_s16_ms_23.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2016/0625_s16_er.pdf#page=12|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

Темы: [[5.2.4 Half-life]], [[5.2.1 Detection of radioactivity]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать показание детектора после двух периодов полураспада с учётом фонового излучения.

The reading on a detector placed near a radioactive material is 536 counts per second.

The background count rate is 44 counts per second.

The half-life of the radioactive material is 34 hours.

What is the reading on the detector after 68 hours?

**A** 44 counts per second  
**B** 123 counts per second  
**C** 134 counts per second  
**D** 167 counts per second

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question proved challenging with only the strongest candidates answering correctly. Many candidates either forgot to add on background radiation at the end of their calculation, or ignored it completely.
