> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить изменения средней скорости молекул воздуха и расстояния между ними при нагревании герметичной бутылки.

A sealed bottle of constant volume contains air.

The air in the bottle is heated by the Sun.

What is the effect on the average speed of the air molecules in the bottle, and the average distance between them?

![[0625-s16-23-p7-f1.svg]]

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Almost all candidates knew that the average speed of the molecules would increase, but many of them also believed that the average separation would increase, even though the volume of the container was constant.
