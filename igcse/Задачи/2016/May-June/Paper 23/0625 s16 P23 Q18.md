> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнить поглощение и испускание инфракрасного излучения чёрной и белой поверхностями.

Which row shows the surface that is the better absorber and the surface that is the better emitter

of infra-red radiation?

|  | better absorber | better emitter |
|---|---|---|
| A | black surface | black surface |
| B | black surface | white surface |
| C | white surface | black surface |
| D | white surface | white surface |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> A very high proportion of candidates were aware that a black surface was the better absorber, but many of these also believed that a white surface was the better emitter.
