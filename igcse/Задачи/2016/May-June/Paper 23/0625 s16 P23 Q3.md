> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить характер ускорения свободно падающего тела при отсутствии сопротивления воздуха.

A heavy object is released near the surface of the Earth and falls freely. Air resistance can be ignored.

Which statement about the acceleration of the object due to gravity is correct?

**A** The acceleration depends on the mass of the object.  
**B** The acceleration depends on the volume of the object.  
**C** The acceleration is constant.  
**D** The acceleration is initially zero and increases as the object falls.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Weaker candidates often chose option D here, believing that the acceleration of the stone, rather than its speed, would increase from zero as it fell.
