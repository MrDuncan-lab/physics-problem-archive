> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определение периода полураспада по таблице счёта излучений.

A reading is taken every 10 minutes of the number of emissions per second from a radioactive

source. The table shows the readings.

| time / min | number of emissions per second |
|---|---|
| 0 | 800 |
| 10 | 560 |
| 20 | 400 |
| 30 | 280 |
| 40 | 200 |
| 50 | 140 |
| 60 | 100 |

What is the half-life of the source?

**A** 10 min  
**B** 20 min  
**C** 40 min  
**D** 60 min

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
