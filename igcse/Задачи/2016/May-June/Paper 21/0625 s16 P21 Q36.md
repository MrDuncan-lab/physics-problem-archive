The diagram shows a shaded area where the direction of a magnetic field is into the page.

![[0625-s16-21-p16-f2.svg]]

In which direction is the beam of β-particles deflected as they enter the magnetic field?

**B** out of the page  
**C** down the page  
**D** up the page
