> Выбор проникающего излучения для контроля уровня муки.

The arrangement shown is used to check whether the flour inside a cardboard packet is above a

certain level. If it is above this level, the flour absorbs the radiation from the source so that it doesn’t reach the detector.

![[0625-s16-21-p17-f1.svg]]

Which type of radiation is suitable to use?

**A** α-particles only  
**B** β-particles only  
**C** either α-particles or β-particles  
**D** γ-rays only

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
