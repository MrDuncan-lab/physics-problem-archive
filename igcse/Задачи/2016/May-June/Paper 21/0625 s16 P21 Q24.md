> Сравнение давления воздуха в сгущениях и разрежениях звуковой волны.

A sound wave travels through air as a series of compressions and rarefactions.

Which row correctly compares the air pressure in a compression and the air pressure in a rarefaction to the air pressure nearby where there is no sound wave?

|  | air pressure in a compression | air pressure in a rarefaction |
|---|---|---|
| A | higher | higher |
| B | higher | lower |
| C | lower | higher |
| D | lower | lower |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
