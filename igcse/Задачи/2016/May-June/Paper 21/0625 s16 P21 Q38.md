> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Правильная запись ядерного уравнения альфа-распада америция.

A nucleus of americium ${}^{243}_{95}\text{Am}$ emits an α-particle to form a nucleus of neptunium (Np).

Which equation represents this decay?

**A** ${}^{243}_{95}\text{Am} \rightarrow {}^{247}_{97}\text{Np} + {}^{4}_{2}\alpha$

**B** ${}^{243}_{95}\text{Am} \rightarrow {}^{243}_{96}\text{Np} + {}^{0}_{-1}\alpha$

**C** ${}^{243}_{95}\text{Am} \rightarrow {}^{243}_{94}\text{Np} + {}^{0}_{-1}\alpha$

**D** ${}^{243}_{95}\text{Am} \rightarrow {}^{239}_{93}\text{Np} + {}^{4}_{2}\alpha$

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
