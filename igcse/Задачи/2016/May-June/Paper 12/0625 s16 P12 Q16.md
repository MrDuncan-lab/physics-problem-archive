Источник: [[_pdf/2016/0625_s16_qp_12.pdf#page=7|условие, стр. 7]] · [[_pdf/2016/0625_s16_ms_12.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2016/0625_s16_er.pdf#page=3|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Поглощение теплоты при плавлении льда при постоянной температуре.

Which statement describes what happens as ice at 0 °C starts to melt to become water?

**A** Energy is absorbed and the temperature remains constant.  
**B** Energy is absorbed and the temperature rises.  
**C** Energy is released and the temperature remains constant.  
**D** Energy is released and the temperature rises.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Very many candidates were unclear about the process of melting and its lack of effect on temperature, with options B and D being common choices.
