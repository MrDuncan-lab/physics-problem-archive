> Влияние площади опоры балки на давление при неизменном весе.

A T-shaped girder is placed on the ground in position X. It is then turned over to position Y.

![[0625-s16-12-p6-f1.svg]]

What happens to the force on the ground and what happens to the pressure on the ground?

|  | force | pressure |
|---|---|---|
| A | increases | increases |
| B | increases | remains the same |
| C | remains the same | increases |
| D | remains the same | remains the same |
