> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Ускорение и скорость камня при свободном падении.

A large stone is dropped from a bridge into a river. Air resistance can be ignored.

Which row describes the acceleration and the speed of the stone as it falls?

|  | acceleration of the stone | speed of the stone |
|---|---|---|
| A | constant | constant |
| B | constant | increasing |
| C | increasing | constant |
| D | increasing | increasing |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Confusion between the concepts of speed and acceleration led many candidates to select option D.
