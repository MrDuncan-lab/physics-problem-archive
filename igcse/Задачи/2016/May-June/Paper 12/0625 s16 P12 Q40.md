> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определение активности радиоизотопа по кривой радиоактивного распада.

The graph shows how the decay rate of a radioactive source changes with time.

![[0625-s16-12-p16-f1.svg]]

What will be the activity at 8 days?

**A** 0 decays / s  
**B** 125 decays / s  
**C** 250 decays / s  
**D** 500 decays / s

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Weaker candidates often judged the likely continuation of the decay curve by eye and deduced that the activity would reduce to either 125 decays / s or to zero at 8 days.
