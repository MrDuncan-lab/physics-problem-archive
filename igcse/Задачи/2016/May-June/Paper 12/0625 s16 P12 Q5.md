> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Направление постоянной силы тяжести после броска камня вверх.

A boy throws a stone. The stone leaves the boy’s hand and moves vertically upwards. Air resistance can be ignored.

How should the force on the stone be described just after the stone leaves the boy’s hand?

**A** downwards and constant  
**B** downwards and increasing  
**C** upwards and constant  
**D** upwards and decreasing

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Few responses were correct here, with the popularity of option D suggesting that many had either failed to read the question carefully or believed that there must be an upward force on any object that is moving upwards.
