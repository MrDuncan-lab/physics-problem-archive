> Рост высоты ртутного столба при увеличении давления атмосферы.

The diagram shows a simple mercury barometer.

![[0625-s16-12-p6-f2.svg]]

The atmospheric pressure increases.

Which distance increases?

**A** VW  
**B** WY  
**C** XY  
**D** XZ

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question involved a barometer and considerably more candidates thought that the space above the column of mercury would increase and so chose option A.
