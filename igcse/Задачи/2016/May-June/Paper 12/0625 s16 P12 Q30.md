> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Увеличение электрического сопротивления при изменении длины и диаметра провода.

A wire has a certain electrical resistance.

The diameter and length of the wire may be changed.

Which pair of changes must cause the resistance of the wire to increase?

|  | change of diameter | change of length |
|---|---|---|
| A | decrease | decrease |
| B | decrease | increase |
| C | increase | decrease |
| D | increase | increase |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
