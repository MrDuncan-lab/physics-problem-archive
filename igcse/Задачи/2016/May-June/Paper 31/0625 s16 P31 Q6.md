# Question 6 (5 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2016 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 1 · вопрос 6

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Изобразить броуновское движение частицы дыма, объяснить его причину и влияние нагревания воздуха.

Fig. 6.1 shows an experiment to observe the motion of smoke particles in air.

![[0625-s16-31-p8-f1.png]]

**Fig. 6.1 Fig. 6.2**

**(a)** **(i)** Fig. 6.2 shows the view through the microscope of one smoke particle, labelled P.

On Fig. 6.2, draw 3 lines to show the movement of this particle. **[2]**

**(ii)** Explain what causes the smoke particle to move. **[2]**

**(b)** The air containing the smoke particles becomes warmer.

Suggest how this changes the movement of the smoke particles. **[1]**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> (a) (i) This question was not well answered. A very common incorrect response was to draw three straight lines drawn out from point P.
>
> (ii) Many candidates gained some credit, normally for the idea of collisions. However, there were many vague responses in terms of particles and molecules without specifying that these particles were air particles.
>
> (b) There were many correct responses to this question.
