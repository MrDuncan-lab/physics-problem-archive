# Question 12 (5 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2016 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 1 · вопрос 12  
Источник: [[_pdf/2016/0625_s16_qp_31.pdf#page=18|условие, стр. 18]] · [[_pdf/2016/0625_s16_ms_31.pdf#page=10|схема оценивания, стр. 10]] · [[_pdf/2016/0625_s16_er.pdf#page=15|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 s16 P31]]  
Темы: [[5.2.2 The three types of nuclear emission]], [[5.2.5 Safety precautions]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Различить источники альфа- и бета-излучения, сравнить альфа- и гамма-излучение и указать биологическое действие радиации.

Two radioactive sources are used by a teacher. One source emits only alpha particles and the other source emits only beta particles.

**(a)** Suggest how the sources can be identified. **[2]**

**(b)** The teacher also has a source that emits gamma rays.

State **two** ways in which gamma rays are different from alpha particles.

1 \_\_\_\_\_\_

2 \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(c)** State an effect of ionising radiation on living things. **[1]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | idea of paper between source and detector OR measuring range (in air) OR pass through an electric or magnetic<br>field<br>alpha stopped by paper OR larger range in air for beta OR identify deflection when in field | B1 B1 |
> | (b) | any two from:<br>gamma travel at the speed of light<br>gamma rays have no charge<br>gamma rays have no mass<br>gamma is a wave OR part of the electromagnetic spectrum<br>gamma less ionising<br>greater penetration<br>not deflected by electric or magnetic fields | B2 |
> | (c) | damages cells / tissues / DNA OR causes (cell) mutations OR <u>radiation sickness</u> | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> (a) Very few candidates gained full credit. Most answers, however, gained credit for the idea of using a sheet of paper to stop alpha radiation.
>
> (b) This question was generally well answered by stronger candidates who recognised that gamma was less ionising and had greater penetration.
>
> (c) There were many correct responses in terms of mutations of cells. There were also a number of vague responses in terms of causing harm, illness or death.
