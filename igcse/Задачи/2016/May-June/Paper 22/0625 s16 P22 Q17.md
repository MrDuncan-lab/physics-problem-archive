> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Влияние площади поверхности и потока воздуха на испарение.

A beaker of liquid is left on a laboratory bench. There is an electric fan in the laboratory causing a

draught over the liquid.

The liquid evaporates.

Which row shows two changes that will both cause the liquid to evaporate more quickly?

|  | change to surface area of the liquid | change to speed of fan |
|---|---|---|
| A | decrease | decrease |
| B | decrease | increase |
| C | increase | decrease |
| D | increase | increase |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
