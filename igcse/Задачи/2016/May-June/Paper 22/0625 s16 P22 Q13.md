> Изменение высоты ртутного столба при росте давления.

The diagram shows a simple mercury barometer.

![[0625-s16-22-p6-f1.svg]]

The atmospheric pressure increases.

Which distance increases?

**A** VW  
**B** WY  
**C** XY  
**D** XZ

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question concerned a barometer, and a significant proportion of candidates believed that the space above the column of mercury would increase in length and chose option A.
