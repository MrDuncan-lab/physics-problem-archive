> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Вычисление силы сопротивления автомобиля по массе и ускорению.

The engine of a car produces a driving force of 5000 N on the car. Resistive forces R also act on the car, as shown.

![[0625-s16-22-p4-f3.png]]

The car has a mass of 800 kg and an acceleration of 1.0 m / s$^{2}$.

What is the value of R?

**A** 800 N  
**B** 4200 N  
**C** 5800 N  
**D** 8000 N

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> A number of candidates opted for A, which is the value of the resultant force, rather than calculating the value of R.
