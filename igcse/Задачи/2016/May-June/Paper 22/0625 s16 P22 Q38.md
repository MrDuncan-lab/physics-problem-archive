$^{35}_{17}$ X$^{37}_{17}$ X$^{38}_{18}$ X$_{35}^{81}$ X$_{37}^{81}$ X

nuclide 1 nuclide 2 nuclide 3 nuclide 4 nuclide 5

**B** nuclide 2 and nuclide 3  
**C** nuclide 2 and nuclide 5  
**D** nuclide 4 and nuclide 5
