> Сравнение поглощения и излучения инфракрасной энергии чёрной и белой поверхности.

A wooden beam is painted part black and part white. The beam absorbs infra-red radiation from

the Sun during the day, and loses infra-red radiation to the surroundings at night.

![[0625-s16-22-p8-f1.svg]]

Which part of the beam heats up more quickly during the day, and which part cools down more quickly at night?

|  | part heating up more quickly | part cooling down more quickly |
|---|---|---|
| A | black | black |
| B | black | white |
| C | white | black |
| D | white | white |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**
