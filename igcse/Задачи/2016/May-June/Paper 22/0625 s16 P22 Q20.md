> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определение массы растаявшего льда по охлаждению воды и удельной теплоте плавления.

5.0 g of water at 25 °C is dropped onto a large block of ice at 0 °C. The water cools to 0 °C and

some of the ice melts.

Assume that all the energy lost by the water is gained by the ice.

What is the mass of ice that melts?

The specific heat capacity of water is 4.2 J / (g °C).

The specific latent heat of fusion of ice is 340 J / g.

**A** 0.062 g  
**B** 0.087 g  
**C** 1.5 g  
**D** 10 g

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
