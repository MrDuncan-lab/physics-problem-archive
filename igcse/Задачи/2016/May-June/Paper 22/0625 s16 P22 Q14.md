Источник: [[_pdf/2016/0625_s16_qp_22.pdf#page=6|условие, стр. 6]] · [[_pdf/2016/0625_s16_ms_22.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2016/0625_s16_er.pdf#page=10|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Поглощение теплоты при плавлении льда при постоянной температуре.

Which statement describes what happens as ice at 0 °C starts to melt to become water?

**A** Energy is absorbed and the temperature remains constant.  
**B** Energy is absorbed and the temperature rises.  
**C** Energy is released and the temperature remains constant.  
**D** Energy is released and the temperature rises.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> A large number of candidates chose option B for this question on change of state.
