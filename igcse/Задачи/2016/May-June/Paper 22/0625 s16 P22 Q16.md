# Question 16 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2016 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 2 · вопрос 16  
Источник: [[_pdf/2016/0625_s16_qp_22.pdf#page=7|условие, стр. 7]] · [[_pdf/2016/0625_s16_ms_22.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2016/0625_s16_er.pdf#page=10|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнение скорости и расстояний между частицами жидкости и газа.

A liquid is at a temperature below its boiling point.

The liquid is then heated so that it becomes a gas at a temperature above its boiling point.

Which row correctly compares the liquid with the gas?

|  | average distance between the particles | average speed of the particles |
|---|---|---|
| A | greater in the liquid | greater in the liquid |
| B | greater in the liquid | smaller in the liquid |
| C | smaller in the liquid | greater in the liquid |
| D | smaller in the liquid | smaller in the liquid |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Virtually all candidates were aware that the black section would show the faster temperature increase, but many of these also thought that the white section would show the faster decrease, and chose option B.
