> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Изменение средней скорости и расстояний молекул нагреваемого газа.

A sealed bottle of constant volume contains air.

The air in the bottle is heated by the Sun.

What is the effect on the average speed of the air molecules in the bottle, and the average distance between them?

|  | average speed of air molecules | average distance between air molecules |
|---|---|---|
| A | decreases | decreases |
| B | decreases | stays the same |
| C | increases | increases |
| D | increases | stays the same |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> A very large proportion of able candidates knew that the average speed of the molecules would increase, but also thought that their average separation would increase, even though the volume of the container was constant.
