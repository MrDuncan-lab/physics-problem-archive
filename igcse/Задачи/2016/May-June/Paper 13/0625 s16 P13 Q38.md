> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Обязательное совпадение протонных чисел у изотопов.

Below are four statements about isotopes of a certain element.

Which statement about the isotopes must be correct?

**A** They are radioactive.  
**B** They are unstable.  
**C** They have the same number of neutrons.  
**D** They have the same number of protons.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> A significant proportion of candidates of all abilities thought that isotopes must be radioactive/unstable.
