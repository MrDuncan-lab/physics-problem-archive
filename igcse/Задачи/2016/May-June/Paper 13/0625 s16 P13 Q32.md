> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнение токов в ветвях цепи с одинаковыми резисторами.

The diagram shows a cell connected to two 6.0 Ω resistors. Three currents, I$_{1}$, I$_{2}$ and I$_{3}$, are

labelled.

![[0625-s16-13-p14-f1.svg]]

Which row correctly compares I$_{2}$ and I$_{3}$ with I$_{1}$?

|  | I$_{2}$ | I$_{3}$ |
|---|---|---|
| A | same as I$_{1}$ | same as I$_{1}$ |
| B | same as I$_{1}$ | smaller than I$_{1}$ |
| C | smaller than I$_{1}$ | same as I$_{1}$ |
| D | smaller than I$_{1}$ | smaller than I$_{1}$ |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The most common error here was to confuse series and parallel circuits, leading to a choice of option A.
