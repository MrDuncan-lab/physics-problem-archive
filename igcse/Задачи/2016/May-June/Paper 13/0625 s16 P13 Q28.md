Источник: [[_pdf/2016/0625_s16_qp_13.pdf#page=12|условие, стр. 12]] · [[_pdf/2016/0625_s16_ms_13.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2016/0625_s16_er.pdf#page=5|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Материал для изготовления постоянного магнита.

A student wishes to make a permanent magnet. She has an iron rod and a steel rod.

Which rod should she use to make the permanent magnet, and is this rod a hard magnetic material or a soft magnetic material?

|  | rod | type of magnetic material |
|---|---|---|
| A | iron | hard |
| B | iron | soft |
| C | steel | hard |
| D | steel | soft |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Although many candidates knew whether iron or steel were hard or soft magnetic materials, weaker candidates did not show an understanding of what this actually means, and that steel would be needed for a permanent magnet.
