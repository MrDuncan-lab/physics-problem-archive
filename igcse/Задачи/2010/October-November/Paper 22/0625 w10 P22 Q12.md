> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Обозначение бета-частицы и ядерная реакция бета-распада.

**(a)** A β-particle may be represented by the symbol $_{\text{–1}}^{0}$**e**.

**(i)** What does the **e** indicate about a β-particle?

**(ii)** What does the **0** indicate about a β-particle?

**(iii)** What does the **–1** indicate about a β-particle? **[4]**

**(b)** The nuclide$^{250}_{97}$Bk decays by emitting a β-particle.

Complete the nuclear equation for this decay by writing appropriate numbers in the boxes.
