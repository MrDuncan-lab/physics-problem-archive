# Question 8 (5 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2010 · Paper 2 — Core theory, вариант 2 · вопрос 8  
Источник: [[_pdf/2010/0625_w10_qp_22.pdf#page=11|условие, стр. 11]] · [[_pdf/2010/0625_w10_ms_22.pdf#page=5|схема оценивания, стр. 5]] · [[_pdf/2010/0625_w10_er.pdf#page=10|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Амплитуда колебаний струны и образование звука.

A stretched string is vibrating between two fixed ends. Fig. 8.1 shows how the string is vibrating.

![[0625-w10-22-p11-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 8.1**

**(a)** State the name of

**(i)** distance *a*,  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(ii)** distance *b* \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(b)** The string is causing a sound to be transmitted through the air.

**(i)** Describe how the string causes the sound. **[2]**

**(ii)** State what happens to the sound as the distance *a* decreases. **[1]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)(i)** backwards & forwards OR up & down OR compressions & rarefactions

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | amplitude | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | wavelength | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | string moves air | M1 |
> |  | backwards & forwards OR up & down<br>OR compressions & rarefactions | A1 |
> | (b)(ii) | gets quieter/softer/less loud | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Most candidates could identify distance *b*, but far fewer knew that distance *a* was the amplitude of the wave. A common incorrect answer for this was “peak” or “crest”.
>
> **(b)** Very few could describe how the string causes the sound. Some answered that the air vibrating caused the string to vibrate. Similarly, in part **(ii)**, only a minority recognised that the loudness would decrease. Quite a few responded that the effect would be a change of pitch or frequency.
