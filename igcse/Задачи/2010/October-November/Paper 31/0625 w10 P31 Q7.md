# Question 7 (6 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2010 · Paper 3 — Extended theory, вариант 1 · вопрос 7  
Источник: [[_pdf/2010/0625_w10_qp_31.pdf#page=11|условие, стр. 11]] · [[_pdf/2010/0625_w10_ms_31.pdf#page=6|схема оценивания, стр. 6]] · [[_pdf/2010/0625_w10_er.pdf#page=16|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

Темы: [[3.3 Electromagnetic spectrum]], [[3.1 General properties of waves]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Электромагнитный спектр и длина волны телепередачи.

**(a)** The following list contains the names of types of energy transfer by means of waves.

γ-rays, infra-red, radio/TV/microwaves, sound, visible light, X-rays

**(i)** Which one of these is **not** a type of electromagnetic wave? **[1]**

**(ii)** State the nature of the wave you have named in **(a)(i)**. **[1]**

**(iii)** The remaining names in the list are all regions of the electromagnetic spectrum, but one region is missing.

Name the missing region. **[1]**

**(b)** A television station emits waves with a frequency of 2.5 × 10$^{8}$ Hz. Electromagnetic waves travel at a speed of 3.0 × 10$^{8}$ m / s.

Calculate the wavelength of the waves emitted by this television station. State the equation you use.

wavelength =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** 1.2 m

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | sound | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | particle OR mechanical OR compression OR longitudinal<br>OR matter wave | B1 |
> | (a)(iii) | ultra violet/uv | B1 |
> | (b) | v = fλ OR λ = v/f | B1 |
> |  | 3.0 × 10$^{8}$/2.5 × 10$^{8}$ OR 3.0 x 10$^{8}$ = 2.5 × 10$^{8}$ λ | C1 |
> |  | 1.2 m | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** This part of the question required recall of Core syllabus material. There were numerous examples of candidates who gained up to a quarter of the marks on the whole paper but gained only one or none of the 3 marks here. In **(iii)**, the missing region was sometimes identified as α or β radiation, or even water waves.
>
> **(b)** Candidates who had been unsuccessful in **(a)** tended to make mistakes here also, usually through failure to state the correct equation. The infrequent error by better candidates was to omit the unit.
>
> \[1.2 m\]
