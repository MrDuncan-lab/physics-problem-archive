> Сравнение электромагнитного и ядерного излучения.

Here is a list of different types of radiation.

alpha (α), beta (β), gamma (γ), infra-red, radio, ultra-violet, visible, X-rays

**(a)** Underline all those radiations in the list which are **not** electromagnetic radiations. **[2]**

**(b)** Which radiation is the most penetrating?  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(c)** Which radiation has the longest wavelength?  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(d)** Which radiation consists of particles that are the same as$^{4}$He nuclei?  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**
