> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The question demonstrated that many candidates find the concepts of thermal energy transfer challenging, particularly when asked to apply their knowledge to an unfamiliar situation.
>
> **(a)** Convection was a more popular answer than conduction, even when convection was also given as the answer to the next part.
>
> **(b)(c)** Answers to these question parts were often insufficiently clear, with candidates writing about something going up and/or down, but rarely being worth any marks.
