> Измерение размеров бруска и вычисление его объёма и плотности.

**(a)** **(i)** Figs. 1.1 and 1.2 show the dimensions of a rectangular block being measured using a ruler. They are not shown full size.

Use the scales shown to find the length and the width of the block, giving your answers in cm.

width of block  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm **[1]**

**(ii)** When the block was made, it was cut from a piece of metal 2.0 cm thick.

Calculate the volume of the block.

volume =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm$^{3}$ **[2]**

**(b)** **Another** block has a volume of 20 cm$^{3}$.

Fig. 1.3 shows the reading when the block is placed on a balance.

![[0625-w10-21-p3-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 1.3**

Find the density of this block.

density =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[4]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)(ii)** 60 (cm$^{3}$) ecf
>
> **(b)** 2.65 OR 2650

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** The question clearly stated, “....giving your answers in cm.”, and the answer lines had “cm” printed on them. Nevertheless, a large proportion still gave their answers in mm, and lost marks. Some attempted to convert to m. The calculation of volume was usually correctly done, although some candidates omitted to involve the thickness. Any error, carried over from the previous part, was not penalised further.
>
> \[6 cm, 5 cm, 60 cm$^{3}$\]
>
> **(b)** A few candidates multiplied mass and volume, but most knew how to find the density of the block. A small number of candidates used the volume from part **(i)**.
>
> \[2.65 g/cm$^{3}$\]
