What, if anything, happens to it? **[1]**

**(iii)** A small piece of positively charged plastic is placed at B.

What, if anything, happens to it? **[1]**

**Fig. 9.2**

**(i)** What must be done to magnetise the core? **[1]**

**(iii)** State one advantage of an electromagnet, compared with a magnet such as that in **(a)**. **[1]**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** The poles of the magnet were usually correctly marked, but very few could state what happens to the unmagnetised iron or the charged plastic. It was common for the candidate to think that nothing would happen to the iron because it was unmagnetised, and that the charged plastic would be attracted.
>
> **(b)** Whilst many candidates knew that a current should be passed to magnetise the core, they did not make it clear that the current should be in the coil (it is a common misconception that the current is passed through the core). It should be noted that steel is not suitable for the core.
