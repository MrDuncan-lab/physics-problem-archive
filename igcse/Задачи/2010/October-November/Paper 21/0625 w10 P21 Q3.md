> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Very few candidates could name three energy resources involving water. Many simply quoted three types of energy such as PE, KE and heat.
>
> **(b)** Candidates who showed a poor understanding of what is meant by an energy *resource* in **(a)** struggled with this part also. Interestingly, though, quite a few still ended up trying to describe hydroelectric power, and thereby usually earned at least one mark. Candidates describing hydroelectric power rarely mentioned the significance of the reservoir being at high level, and most thought the turbine and the generator are the same thing.
