# Question 4 (5 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2010 · Paper 2 — Core theory, вариант 1 · вопрос 4  
Источник: [[_pdf/2010/0625_w10_qp_21.pdf#page=5|условие, стр. 5]] · [[_pdf/2010/0625_w10_ms_21.pdf#page=3|схема оценивания, стр. 3]] · [[_pdf/2010/0625_w10_er.pdf#page=7|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Фокусное расстояние и ход параллельных лучей через собирающую линзу.

Fig. 4.1 shows four parallel rays of light reaching a thin converging lens. Point F is a principal focus of the lens.

![[0625-w10-21-p5-f1.svg]]

F

**Fig. 4.1**

**(a)** What name do we give to the distance PF?  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(b)** On Fig. 4.1, carefully draw the paths of the rays through the lens and into the air as far as the broken line. **[2]**

**(c)** A flat white screen is placed at F, parallel to the broken line.

Describe what is seen on the screen. **[1]**

**(d)** The screen is moved so that it is along the broken line.

Describe what is now seen on the screen. **[1]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** appropriate refraction at both lens surfaces OR all rays bent at lens mid-line

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | focal length OR focal distance | B1 |
> | (b) | 4 rays all passing through F | M1 |
> |  | appropriate refraction at both lens surfaces<br>OR all rays bent at lens mid-line | A1 |
> | (c) | <u>focused image OR sharp image OR dot</u> | B1 |
> | (d) | 4 dots OR out-of-focus/blurred/fuzzy image | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (b)** Candidates frequently find ray optics challenging. Very few identified PF as the focal length, and whilst most showed the rays converging on F, the majority showed the refraction occurring at the first surface only. For the second of the two marks it was expected that either the refraction be shown at the mid-line of the mirror (following the usual ray-drawing convention) or that refractions be shown at both surfaces. The standard of care taken over the drawing of the four rays was poor, with the rays often missing F by a substantial amount.
>
> **(c) (d)** Most candidates had little idea what to expect on the screen in either case.
