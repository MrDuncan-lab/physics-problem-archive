> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Средняя скорость велосипедиста и работа против трения.

A boy cycles a distance of 960 m from home to school in 8.0 minutes.

**(a)** Calculate his average speed for the journey.

average speed =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[4]**

**(b)** The journey is all along a horizontal road. At the end of the journey the boy is tired because of the work he has done.

Against which force has this work been done? **[1]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** 120 OR 2

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | distance/time in any form | C1 |
> |  | 960/8 OR 960/(8 × 60) | C1 |
> |  | 120 OR 2 | A1 |
> |  | m/min OR m/s must correspond with value | B1 |
> | (b) | friction or air resistance or force accelerating/decelerating legs | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** The calculation of the average speed was well done, with some even going to the trouble of converting from m/min to m/s, or even km/hr.
>
> \[120 m/min\]
>
> **(b)** There was some confusion about the force against which the work was done, but a majority realised it was some kind of friction.
