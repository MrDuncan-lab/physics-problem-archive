> Определение глубины моря по задержке эхосигнала.

A ship sends a pulse of sound vertically downwards to the sea bed. An echo is heard

0.4 seconds later.

If the speed of sound in the water is 1200 m / s, how deep is the water below the ship?

**A** 240 m  
**B** 480 m  
**C** 1500 m  
**D** 3000 m

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**
