> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Превращения механической энергии во время колебаний маятника.

A simple pendulum starts with its bob at position X, shown in Fig. 4.1. The bob is pulled aside to Y and then released. It swings from Y to Z and back to Y.

![[0625-w10-23-p7-f1.svg]]

![[0625-w10-23-p7-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 4.1**

Write suitable words in the gaps in the following sentences. Ignore air resistance.

In order to move the bob from X to Y,  \_\_\_\_\_\_ has to be done on it and its

\_\_\_\_\_\_ energy increases because it is raised further from the

ground. As it moves towards X, some of this energy is converted into

\_\_\_\_\_\_ energy. Throughout the swing from Y to Z and back to Y, the total

energy is  \_\_\_\_\_\_ Energy is measured in units called  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[5]**
