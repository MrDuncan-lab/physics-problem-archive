# Question 3 (8 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2010 · Paper 3 — Extended theory, вариант 2 · вопрос 3  
Источник: [[_pdf/2010/0625_w10_qp_32.pdf#page=8|условие, стр. 8–9]] · [[_pdf/2010/0625_w10_ms_32.pdf#page=5|схема оценивания, стр. 5]] · [[_pdf/2010/0625_w10_er.pdf#page=20|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w10 P32]]  
Темы: [[1.5.2 Turning effect of forces]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Условие равновесия метровой линейки и моменты сил.

**(a)** A uniform metre rule is pivoted at its centre, which is also the position of its centre of mass.

Three loads, 2.0 N, *F* and 3.0 N are positioned on the rule at the 20 cm, 30 cm and 90 cm marks respectively, as shown in Fig. 3.1.

![[0625-w10-32-p8-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 3.1**

**(i)** Calculate the moment of the 3.0 N load about the pivot.

moment =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(ii)** Calculate the moment of the 2.0 N load about the pivot.

moment =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(iii)** The force *F* maintains the metre rule in equilibrium on the pivot.

Calculate the value of *F*.

*F* =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

**(b)** The weight of the metre rule is 1.2 N and can be considered to act at the 50 cm mark.

All the weights in **(a)** are removed. The pivot is positioned under the 30 cm mark and the 2.0 N load is placed on the rule as shown in Fig. 3.2.

![[0625-w10-32-p9-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 3.2**

The position of the 2.0 N load is adjusted until the metre rule is again in equilibrium.

Determine the position of the 2.0 N load.

2.0 N load is at the  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm mark **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)(iii)** 3.0 N OR 3 N e.c.f.
>
> **(b)** 18 c.a.o. OR special case (30 – his 12) correctly evaluated B1

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | 120 Ncm OR 1.2 Nm | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | 60 Ncm OR 0.6 Nm | B1 |
> | (a)(iii) | idea of CW moments = ACW moments | C1 |
> |  | 60 + 20F = 120 OR 0.6 + 0.2F = 1.2 e.c.f. | C1 |
> |  | 3.0 N OR 3 N e.c.f. | A1 |
> | (b) | 1.2 × 20 = 2.0 × d OR 1.2 × 0.2 = 2.0 × d | C1 |
> |  | (d =) 12 OR 0.12 | C1 |
> |  | 18 c.a.o. OR special case (30 – his 12) correctly evaluated B1 | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)(i) and (ii)** Most candidates had the right idea about moments but there were very many unit errors,
>
> especially a confusion between metres and centimetres, and incorrect distances from the pivot.
>
> **(iii) and (b)** Nearly all candidates showed a knowledge of the law of moments and most went on to
>
> calculate correctly the required force and position. The most common error in both parts was in determining the correct distance from the pivot.
