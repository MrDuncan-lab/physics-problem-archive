Источник: [[_pdf/2010/0625_w10_qp_32.pdf#page=13|условие, стр. 13]] · [[_pdf/2010/0625_w10_ms_32.pdf#page=6|схема оценивания, стр. 6]] · [[_pdf/2010/0625_w10_er.pdf#page=20|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Аналоговый и цифровой сигналы и логический элемент И.

**(a)** Explain what is meant by the terms *analogue* and *digital*, as applied to electronic circuits.

analogue  \_\_\_\_\_\_

digital  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(b)** Describe, if necessary using a diagram, the function of an AND gate in digital electronics. **[2]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | analogue <u>any reading possible/idea of continuous variation</u><br>of value of quantity | B1 |
> |  | digital idea of two states only | B1 |
> | (b) | if both inputs are 1/high, the output is 1/high | B1 |
> |  | only added to previous line<br>OR if either or both inputs are 0/low, then output is 0/low<br>(accept both answers in form of a truth table) | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Most answers showed an awareness of the meaning of digital, even if the mark was sometimes lost by poor wording. Very few answers showed any sort of correct explanation of analogue.
>
> **(b)** Candidates almost always correctly identified the function of an AND gate and generally answered the question well, normally in the form of a truth table.
