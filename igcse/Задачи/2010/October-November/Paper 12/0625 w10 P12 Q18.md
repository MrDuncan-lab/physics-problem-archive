> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнение теплопроводности железа и дерева.

A piece of wood has some iron nails pushed through it. One side of the wood is covered with heat

sensitive paper which turns from pink to blue when heated. The wood is heated as shown for a few minutes and blue dots appear on the heat sensitive paper where it touches the nails.

![[0625-w10-12-p8-f1.svg]]

This experiment shows that, compared to wood, iron is a good

**A** absorber of heat.  
**B** conductor of heat.  
**C** convector of heat.  
**D** emitter of heat.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
