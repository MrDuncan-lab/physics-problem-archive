> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** The majority of candidates correctly named this process.
>
> **(ii)** This question generated both convincing and less convincing answers. Most candidates had some idea about the molecules colliding and that this leads to further collisions. The better and higher- scoring answers made it clear that there was increased vibration at the end where the temperature was raised and that it was energy that was being passed on in the collisions. In a metal, the electrons are responsible for transferring most of the energy. Unfortunately, some candidates who wrote about electrons suggested that the electrons had fixed positions.
>
> **(b)** This part was very frequently correctly answered.
>
> **(c)** This testing part of the question was worth four marks; many of the answers offered here did not go into sufficient detail. The question presents an experiment that compares two ostensibly similar arrangements and so the best answers compared the two cases and highlighted the differences that caused the different behaviours. Some candidates who were unclear as to the true explanation referred to the flame being conducted through the iron gauze.
