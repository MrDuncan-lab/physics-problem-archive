# Question 5 (9 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2010 · Paper 3 — Extended theory, вариант 3 · вопрос 5  
Источник: [[_pdf/2010/0625_w10_qp_33.pdf#page=10|условие, стр. 10]] · [[_pdf/2010/0625_w10_ms_33.pdf#page=4|схема оценивания, стр. 4]] · [[_pdf/2010/0625_w10_er.pdf#page=24|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

Темы: [[2.2.2 Specific heat capacity]], [[2.3.3 Radiation]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Поглощение солнечного излучения и нагрев металлической палубы.

**(a)** Define *specific heat capacity*. **[2]**

**(b)** Solar energy is striking the steel deck of a ship.

**(i)** Describe how the colour of the deck affects the absorption of the solar energy. **[1]**

**(ii)** The solar energy strikes the deck at the rate of 1400 W on every square metre.

The steel plate of the deck is 0.010 m thick.

Steel has a density of 7800 kg / m$^{3}$ and a specific heat capacity of 450 J / (kg °C).

13% of the solar energy striking the deck is absorbed and the rest is reflected.

Using these figures, calculate

**1.** how many joules of solar energy are absorbed by 1.0 m$^{2}$ of the deck in 1.0 s,

number of joules =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**2.** the mass of 1.0 m$^{2}$ of deck,

mass =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**3.** the rate of rise in temperature of the deck, stating the equation you use.

rate of rise =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ °C / s **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** of 1 kg/g/unit mass through 1°C/1K/unit temperature
>
> **(b)(ii)** 78 kg
>
> **(b)(ii)** 0.00519 °C/s OR 5.19 × 10$^{-3}$°C/s (e.c.f. from 1,2)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | heat/energy to raise/change temperature | M1 |
> |  | of 1 kg/g/unit mass through 1°C/1K/unit temperature | A1 |
> | (b)(i) | darker colours absorb more OR lighter/shiny colours absorb less | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | 1. 182 | B1 |
> |  | 2. (mass of 1m$^{2}$ =) volume × density OR D = M/V OR (1 ×) 0.01 × 7800 | C1 |

> |  | 3. Q = mcθ | B1 |
> |  | 182 = 78 × 450 × θ (e.c.f. from 1,2) | C1 |
> |  | 0.00519 °C/s OR 5.19 × 10$^{-3}$°C/s (e.c.f. from 1,2) | A1 |

> **(a)** This was quite often correctly defined, although some candidates did not refer to a 1 °C or 1 K rise in temperature. There were some who defined the *specific latent heat*.

Answers such as *surfaces of different colours can affect the amount of energy absorbed* or *different coloured* *surfaces absorb solar energy to differing extents* were insufficient to score the mark.
