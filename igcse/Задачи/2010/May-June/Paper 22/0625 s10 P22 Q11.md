> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** A good number knew what thermionic emission is, even if some were uncertain about what was emitted. Some thought it is heat released when particles bombard.
>
> **(b)** The nature of cathode rays was known by many. Probably the most common incorrect answer was alpha particles. Hardly any were able to show the path of the cathode rays correctly on the diagram. Some seemed to have the right idea, but drew lines so carelessly that they were not worth any marks. It is important to note that the path is curved from the instant the cathode rays enter the electric field, and straight from the instant they leave the field.
