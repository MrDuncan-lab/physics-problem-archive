**(b)** Fig. 2.2 shows the space probe just after the rocket motors are fired.

![[0625-s10-22-p3-f4.svg]]

**Fig. 2.2**

State what effect this has on the space probe. **[2]**

**(c)** Later into its mission, the space probe is brought back into the Earth’s atmosphere again, with no rockets working.

Suggest two effects that the atmosphere has on the space probe.

1 \_\_\_\_\_\_

2 \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** in same direction/opposite direction to exhaust gases

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** A good proportion realised that a force would not be necessary.
>
> **(b)** Many realised that the firing of the rocket motors would “make it go faster” (very few referred to acceleration), but most failed to score the second mark for indicating the direction of the acceleration.
>
> **(c)** Answers to this part were usually vague and meaningless.
