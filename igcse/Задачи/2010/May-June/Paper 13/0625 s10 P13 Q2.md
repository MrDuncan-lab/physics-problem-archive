> Средняя скорость улитки по перемещению вдоль линейки.

A snail moves along a ruler. It takes 20 s to move from Q to R.

![[0625-s10-13-p2-f2.png]]

What is its average speed from Q to R?

**A** $\dfrac{12}{20}$ cm / s  
**B** $\dfrac{12 - 2}{20}$ cm / s  
**C** $\dfrac{20}{12}$ cm / s  
**D** $\dfrac{20}{12 - 2}$ cm / s
