> Показания жидкостного манометра при смене плотности жидкости.

The pressure of a gas is measured using a manometer as shown in the diagram.

![[0625-s10-12-p7-f2.svg]]

The mercury in the manometer is replaced with a liquid which is less dense.

How does the value of h change?

**A** It becomes zero.  
**B** It decreases, but not to zero.  
**C** It stays the same.  
**D** It increases.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
