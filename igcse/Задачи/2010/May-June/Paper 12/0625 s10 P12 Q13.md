> Значения нижней и верхней фиксированных точек термометра.

A thermometer has a scale which starts at –10 °C and ends at 110 °C.

![[0625-s10-12-p8-f1.svg]]

What is the value of the lower fixed point and of the upper fixed point of the scale?

|  | lower fixed point / °C | upper fixed point / °C |
|---|---|---|
| A | –10 | 100 |
| B | –10 | 110 |
| C | 0 | 100 |
| D | 0 | 110 |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
