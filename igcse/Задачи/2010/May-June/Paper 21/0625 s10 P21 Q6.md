**(b)** **(i)** State the meaning of the term *thermal capacity*. **[2]**

**(ii)** Another airport kiosk serves coffee in pottery mugs. The mugs all have the same internal dimensions but some have a high thermal capacity and some have a low thermal capacity.

When hot drinks are poured into the mugs, the temperature of the drink always drops because of the thermal energy absorbed by the mug.

State which mug, high thermal capacity or low thermal capacity, causes the least fall in temperature of the hot drink, and explain why.

mug  \_\_\_\_\_\_

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)(ii)** less heat needed to raise temperature OR absorbs less heat
