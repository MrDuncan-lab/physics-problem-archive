> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Удлинение пружины и определение коэффициента жёсткости.

A helical spring is hung from the edge of a bench top, as shown in Fig. 2.1.

![[0625-s10-21-p3-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 2.1**

Before the load is hung on the spring, the pin points to the 29.8 cm mark on the metre rule.

When a load of 5.5 N is hung on the spring, the pin points to 62.8 cm.

**(a)** Calculate the extension of the spring.

extension =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm **[2]**

**(b)** The law relating extension to load is given by the equation

load = constant × extension.

**(i)** Calculate the numerical value of the constant.

constant =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(ii)** Suggest a suitable unit for the constant. **[1]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** 33.0 (cm) OR 33 (cm)
>
> **(b)(i)** 0.166 recurring e.c.f. ignore units accept 1/6 or 0.16 or 0.166 or 0.167 or 0.17 or 0.2 NOT 0.20
