# Question 3 (6 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2010 · Paper 2 — Core theory, вариант 1 · вопрос 3  
Источник: [[_pdf/2010/0625_s10_qp_21.pdf#page=4|условие, стр. 4–5]] · [[_pdf/2010/0625_s10_ms_21.pdf#page=3|схема оценивания, стр. 3]] · [[_pdf/2010/0625_s10_er.pdf#page=5|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Баланс энергии и работа мотора при подъёме груза.

**(a)** Fig. 3.1 represents the energy into and out of a machine.

![[0625-s10-21-p4-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 3.1**

Write down the equation linking *I*, *U* and *W*. **[1]**

**(b)** An electric motor and a pulley in a warehouse are being used to lift a packing case of goods from the ground up to a higher level. This is shown in Fig. 3.2.

![[0625-s10-21-p4-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 3.2**

The packing case of goods, the chains and the pallet together weigh 850 N.

**(i)** State the value of the tension force in the cable when the load is being lifted at a steady speed.

tension force =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ N **[1]**

**(ii)** When the load is just leaving the floor, why is the force larger than your answer to

**(b)** **(i)** ? **[1]**

**(iii)** The warehouse manager wishes to calculate the useful work done when the load is lifted from the ground to the higher level. Which quantity, other than the weight, does he need to measure? **[1]**

**(iv)** Which further quantity does the manager need to know, in order to calculate the power required to lift the load? **[1]**

**(c)** How does the electrical energy supplied to the electric motor compare with the increase in energy of the load? Answer by completing the sentence below.

The electrical energy supplied to the motor is  \_\_\_\_\_\_  the

increase in energy of the load. **[1]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | I = U + W accept words or mixture of words/symbols | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | 850 (N) | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | force needed to accelerate load/get it started<br>OR if forces equal, then no movement | B1 |
> | (b)(iii) | height OR distance (use ✓ + = 0 for extras) | B1 |
> | (b)(iv) | time (use ✓ + = 0 for extras) | B1 |
> | (c) | greater than OR \> OR stronger accept “double” etc | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Not all could answer this correctly, with answers like *U* = *I* + *W* and *W* = *U / I* being common.
>
> 5 © UCLES 2010
>
> **(b)** A good proportion knew the value of the tension force when moving steadily, but very few linked the increased value at the start with the initial acceleration. A lot thought it was because “gravity is bigger on the ground”. Candidates seemed to be familiar with the quantities needed to calculate work and power, but frequently muddled them up in **(iii)** and **(iv)**.
>
> **(c)** Likewise there was confusion about the answer to **(c)**, where a lot thought the energy supplied to the motor would be the same or even less than the increase in energy of the load.
