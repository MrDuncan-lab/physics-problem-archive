> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** The majority of candidates suggested that monochromatic means “one colour”, and this was accepted on this occasion. A better definition is “one frequency”, as, for example, the colour red spans a range of frequencies and wavelengths. The syllabus states that candidates should be able to use the term monochromatic so teachers are advised that “one colour” may not be accepted in future. Weaker candidates suggested that monochromatic light has the same colour or made statements about black and white.
>

>
> **(b), (c)** Answers were generally correct.
>
> **(d)** The formula for refractive index was well known. Some candidates appeared to obtain the value 1.4585246 on their calculator and wrote down 1.45. Candidates should be encouraged to round their answers correctly to the number of significant figures that they choose.
>
> **(e)** Many candidates suggested that the angle of incidence was larger than the critical angle but did not mention why refraction occurs at C, rather than total internal reflection.
>
> **(f)** The diagrams in this part were encouraging. When a candidate recognised that there was no deviation at B they often obtained full marks for bending the ray correctly at the second surface and leaving it parallel to the incident ray. Weaker candidates bent the ray in the wrong direction at the second face.
