> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Almost all candidates earned at least one mark in **(a)** for recognising that the gamma radiation is undeflected. A large percentage of candidates wrongly suggested that the alpha particles were deflected to the right, with a larger deflection than the deflection of the beta particles. It was disappointing that so few candidates realised or could apply correct understanding of the relative direction of magnetic field, force and movement of charged particles. Too often statements that the magnetic field is “from N to S” or “left to right” were unhelpful or failed to apply any knowledge to the actual situation. Ambiguous answers such as “top to bottom” were also frequently seen but failed to score.
