> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Изменение осциллограммы при увеличении частоты переменного напряжения.

A student is using a cathode-ray oscilloscope to display the waveform of an alternating current supply. The arrangement is shown in Fig. 10.1.

![[0625-s10-33-p18-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 10.1**

When switch S is closed, the trace seen on the screen is as shown in Fig. 10.2. To get this trace, the settings of the oscilloscope controls are

volts / cm: 5 V / cm

time-base: 10 ms / cm

![[0625-s10-33-p18-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 10.2**

**(a)** On Fig. 10.2, carefully draw what is seen on the screen when the frequency of the supply is increased to 1.5 times its previous value. **[3]**

**(b)** What change, if any, must be made to the oscilloscope volts / cm and time-base controls in order to reduce the peak-to-peak height of the trace to half that shown in Fig. 10.2?

volts / cm setting  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

time-base setting  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** 3 waves drawn, with first 4 half-wavelengths having 2.0 (±0.2)cm interval

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | waves clearly more bunched<br>condone poor accuracy / shape or waves not filling screen | C1 |
> |  | 3 waves drawn, with first 4 half-wavelengths having 2.0 (±0.2)cm interval | A1 |
> |  | all waves drawn same amplitude (±0.2)cm as original AND<br>at least 1 peak and 1 trough drawn | B1 |
> | (b) | volts/cm: increased / any value \> 5 (V / cm) | B1 |
> |  | factor of 2, increase or decrease / 10 (V / cm) / 2.5 (V / cm) | B1 |
> |  | N.B. 10 (V / cm) scores B1, B1<br>time base: no change / 10 ms / cm | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This produced a great variety of responses and it was evident that some candidates were not very familiar with the cathode-ray oscilloscope.
>
> **(a)** There was an encouraging number of very good traces drawn, but obtaining exactly the correct trace when the frequency was increased to 1.5 times the previous value was difficult for some candidates, particularly if they did not take care in drawing the trace exactly. The amplitude, however, was often drawn correctly by weaker candidates.
>
> **(b)** Relatively few candidates suggested that the volts/cm setting became 10 V/m or doubled. Often it was suggested that the setting should be halved and some candidates gave fairly random answers both of which revealed a lack of understanding of the controls and the workings of an oscilloscope.
