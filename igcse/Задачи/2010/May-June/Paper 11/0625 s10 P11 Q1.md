> Определение объёма неправильного камня вытеснением воды.

A scientist needs to determine the volume of a small, irregularly shaped rock sample. Only a rule and a measuring cylinder, partially filled with water, are available.

![[0625-s10-11-p2-f1.svg]]

![[0625-s10-11-p2-t1.svg]]

To determine the volume, which apparatus should the scientist use?

**A** both the measuring cylinder and the rule  
**B** neither the measuring cylinder nor the rule  
**C** the measuring cylinder only  
**D** the rule only

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
