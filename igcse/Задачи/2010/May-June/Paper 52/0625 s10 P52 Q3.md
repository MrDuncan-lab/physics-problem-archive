> Измерение сопротивлений отдельных ламп и их параллельного соединения.

In this experiment, you are to determine the resistances of lamps in different circuit arrangements.

Carry out the following instructions, referring to Fig. 3.1.

The circuit shown in Fig. 3.1 has been set up for you. This is Circuit 1.

![[0625-s10-52-p6-f1.svg]]

Circuit 1

**Fig. 3.1**

**(a)** Switch on. Measure and record in Table 3.1 the current *I* in the circuit and the p.d. *V* across lamp **P**. Switch off.

**(b)** Calculate the resistance *R* of lamp **P** using the equation *R* = $\dfrac{V}{I}$ .

Record this value of *R* in the table.

**(c)** Replace lamp **P** with lamp **Q**. This is Circuit 2 (not shown). Repeat steps **(a)** and **(b)**.

**(d)** Return lamp **P** to the circuit so that lamps **P** and **Q** are in parallel with each other. This is Circuit 3. Repeat steps **(a)** and **(b)**.
