> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Изотопы элемента и изменение ядра при бета-распаде.

A certain element is known to exist as two different isotopes.

**(a)** State one thing that is the same for atoms of both isotopes. **[1]**

**(b)** State one thing that is different between atoms of these two isotopes. **[1]**

**(c)** An atom of one of these isotopes is unstable and decays into a different element by emitting a -particle.

**(i)** State one thing about the atom that remains the same during this decay. **[1]**

**(ii)** State one thing about the atom that changes as a result of this decay. **[1]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | proton number OR atomic number OR (number of) protons / electrons<br>OR position in periodic table OR chemical properties | B1 |
> | (b) | mass (number) OR nucleon number OR (number of) neutrons / nucleons<br><u>OR (number of) protons plus (number of) neutrons</u> | B1 |
> | (c)(i) | mass (number) OR nucleon number OR (number of) nucleons<br><u>OR (number of) protons plus (number of) neutrons</u> | B1 |
> | (c)(ii) | proton number OR atomic number OR (number of) neutrons<br>OR (number of) protons / neutrons / electrons<br>OR position in periodic table OR chemical properties<br>OR a neutron changes into a proton | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question was about atoms rather than nuclei, so that answers referring to electrons as well as protons, neutrons, nucleons, atomic number and mass number were acceptable.
>
> Answers to **(a)** and **(b)** suggested that most candidates have good recall of the similarity and difference in the numbers of particles in atoms of two isotopes.
>
> In **(c)** the recall of the changes in an atom as the result of the emission of a β-particle was not as sound, and fewer marks were gained. Some of those who gained both marks first wrote down an equation representing such a decay.
