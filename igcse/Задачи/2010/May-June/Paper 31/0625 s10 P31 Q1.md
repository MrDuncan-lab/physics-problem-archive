> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Путь и ускорение автомобиля по графику скорости.

Fig. 1.1 shows the speed/time graph for a car travelling along a straight road.

The graph shows how the speed of the car changes as the car passes through a small town.

![[0625-s10-31-p2-f1.svg]]

time / s

**Fig. 1.1**

**(a)** Describe what happens to the speed of the car

**(i)** between A and B,  \_\_\_\_\_\_

**(ii)** between B and C,  \_\_\_\_\_\_

**(iii)** between C and D \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(b)** Calculate the distance between the start of the town and the end of the town.

distance =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

**(c)** Calculate the acceleration of the car between C and D.

acceleration =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

**(d)** State how the graph shows that the deceleration of the car has the same numerical value as its acceleration. **[1]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** 312 m
>
> **(c)** 1.5 m/s$^{2}$
