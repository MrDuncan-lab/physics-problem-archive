# Question 7 (5 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2010 · Paper 3 — Extended theory, вариант 2 · вопрос 7  
Источник: [[_pdf/2010/0625_s10_qp_32.pdf#page=13|условие, стр. 13]] · [[_pdf/2010/0625_s10_ms_32.pdf#page=6|схема оценивания, стр. 6]] · [[_pdf/2010/0625_s10_er.pdf#page=17|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Различие скорости звука вдоль стального рельса и в воздухе.

A disused railway line has a length of 300 m. A man puts his ear against one end of the rail and another man hits the other end with a metal hammer, as shown in Fig. 7.1.

![[0625-s10-32-p13-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 7.1**

**(a)** **(i)** State an approximate value for the speed of sound in air. **[1]**

**(ii)** Sound travels at 5000 m / s in steel.

Calculate the time it takes for the sound to travel along the rail.

time taken =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(b)** The man with his ear to the railway line actually hears two sounds from the hammer, separated by a short interval.

Explain why he hears two sounds. **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)(ii)** 0.06 s

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | approximately 330 m/s<br>(correct order of magnitude) | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | 300 / 5000 OR t = d/v NOT t = 2d/v | C1 |
> |  | 0.06 s | A1 |
> | (b) | <u>sound through air and sound through steel NOT echo</u> | B1 |
> |  | speeds in air and steel are different NOT if faster in air<br>accept sound in steel/rail heard first | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Values for the order of magnitude of the speed of sound in air were generally known although a significant number of candidates gave the speed of light. It was disappointing to find a number of candidates who knew the formula for speed, distance and time in part **(ii)** but were unable to transpose the equation to obtain the correct time.
>
> **(b)** The majority of candidates suggested that there was an echo rather than that sound travels at different speeds in the air and the steel. Good candidates were able to suggest that the sound arrives first along the rail or that the speed of sound in steel is larger than in air.
