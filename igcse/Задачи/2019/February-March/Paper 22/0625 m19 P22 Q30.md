**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · February/March 2019 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 2 · вопрос 30  
Источник: [[_pdf/2019/0625_m19_qp_22.pdf#page=11|условие, стр. 11]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_m19_ms_22.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_m19_er.pdf#page=3|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 m19 P22]]  
Темы: [[4.2.3 Electromotive force and potential difference]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Распознать ЭДС по определению через энергию источника при перемещении заряда по полной цепи.

Which electrical quantity is defined in terms of the energy supplied in driving charge round a

complete circuit?

**A** current  
**B** electromotive force  
**C** potential difference  
**D** power

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Many candidates were able to answer the question correctly but other candidates clearly did not understand the term ‘electromotive force’. The most common error was to confuse e.m.f. with potential difference. However, almost the same proportion confused it with current.
