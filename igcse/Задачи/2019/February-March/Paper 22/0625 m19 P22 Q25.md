What is the speed of X-rays in a vacuum and in air?

|  | in a vacuum | in air |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | 3.0 × 10$^{6}$ m / s | 2.0 × 10$^{6}$ m / s |
| **B** | 3.0 × 10$^{6}$ m / s | 3.0 × 10$^{6}$ m / s |
| **C** | 3.0 × 10$^{8}$ m / s | 2.0 × 10$^{8}$ m / s |
| **D** | 3.0 × 10$^{8}$ m / s | 3.0 × 10$^{8}$ m / s |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
