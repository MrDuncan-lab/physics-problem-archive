# Question 9 (1 mark)

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить наличие результирующей силы при равномерном движении по окружности среди разных видов движения.

Which moving body has a resultant force acting on it?

**A** a diver rising vertically through water at constant speed  
**B** an aircraft circling an airport at constant speed  
**C** a train going up a straight incline at constant speed  
**D** a parachutist descending vertically at terminal velocity

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The physics of circular motion was challenging for many candidates. Only stronger candidates recognised that an object travelling at a constant speed in a circle is not in equilibrium and has a resultant force towards the centre of the circle acting on it. The most common incorrect answer was **D**, and candidates did not recognise that the downward force of gravity is equal to the upward forces of friction and buoyancy.
