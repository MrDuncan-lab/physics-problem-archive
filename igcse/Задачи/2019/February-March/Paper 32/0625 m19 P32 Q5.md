# Question 5 (6 marks)

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Описать преобразование энергии солнечной электростанцией, её преимущества и недостатки и пользу поворота панелей.

Fig. 5.1 shows part of a solar farm. The solar panels tilt and rotate.

![[0625-m19-32-p9-f1.png]]

**Fig. 5.1**

**(a)** The solar farm converts energy from a source into a different, useful form of energy.

State the energy source and the useful form of energy.

source  \_\_\_\_\_\_

useful form of energy  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(b)** Solar farms have advantages and disadvantages.

**(i)** State **two** advantages of a solar farm.

1 \_\_\_\_\_\_

2 \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(ii)** State **one** disadvantage of a solar farm. **[1]**

**(c)** Suggest why it is useful that the panels can tilt and rotate. **[1]**

> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | sun(light) | B1 |
> |  | electrical | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | Any two from:<br>uses a renewable source of energy<br>no cost for source of energy<br>no polluting / greenhouse gases OR no carbon dioxide produced<br>easy to erect and dismantle<br>conserves fossil fuels | B2 |
> | (b)(ii) | Any ONE from:<br>does not work at night<br>need large area of land (for sufficient output) | B1 |
> | (c) | idea that (panel can) follow the sun as it moves across the sky OR will absorb more energy OR transfer energy / work more<br>efficiently | B1 |

>
