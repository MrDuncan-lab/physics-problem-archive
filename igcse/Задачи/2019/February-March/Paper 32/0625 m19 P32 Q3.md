# Question 3 (7 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · February/March 2019 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 3  
Источник: [[_pdf/2019/0625_m19_qp_32.pdf#page=5|условие, стр. 5–6]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_m19_ms_32.pdf#page=4|схема оценивания, стр. 4]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_m19_er.pdf#page=5|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 m19 P32]]  
Темы: [[1.5.1 Effects of forces]], [[1.7.1 Energy]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить силы на подвешенном грузе и равнодействующую, объяснить затухание колебаний сохранением и рассеянием энергии.

A load is attached to a spring, as shown in Fig. 3.1. Two arrows indicate the vertical forces acting on the load. The spring and the load are stationary.

![[0625-m19-32-p5-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 3.1**

**(a)** **(i)** State the name of the force acting vertically downwards. **[1]**

**(ii)** The vertical force that acts upwards is 4.0 N.

State the value of the force acting vertically downwards.

force =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ N **[1]**

**(b)** The load is pulled downwards and then released. The load moves up and down.

Fig. 3.2 represents the vertical forces acting on the load at some time after it is released.

![[0625-m19-32-p5-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 3.2**

Calculate the resultant force on the load and state its direction.

resultant force =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ N

direction =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(c)** **(i)** State the principle of conservation of energy. **[1]**

**(ii)** Eventually the load stops moving up and down.

Describe and explain why the load stops moving. Use your ideas about conservation of energy. **[2]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | gravity **OR** weight | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | 4.0 (N) | B1 |
> | (b) | 4.8 (N) | B1 |
> |  | Up(wards) | B1 |
> | (c)(i) | energy cannot be created or destroyed (but can be transformed) | B1 |
> | (c)(ii) | PE / KE / elastic energy of load / spring decreases / is transformed | B1 |
> |  | Any one from:<br>to thermal energy<br>(which is) dissipated (to surroundings) | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** Most candidates answered this question well. A common incorrect response was resultant force, or simply downwards force.
>
> **(ii)** The majority of candidates answered this correctly. A common error was to multiply the force by 10
>
> to give 40 N.
>
> **(b)** Most candidates correctly calculated the resultant force. Weaker candidates added the forces instead of subtracting.
>
> **(c) (i)** Many candidates correctly stated the principle of conservation of energy, but a significant number of candidates were unable to recall this principle.
>
> **(ii)** Only the strongest candidates answered this fully correctly. A significant number of candidates
>
> attempted to answer the question by describing the forces acting on the load.
