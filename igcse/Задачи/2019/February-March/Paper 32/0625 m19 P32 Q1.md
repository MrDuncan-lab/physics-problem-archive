# Question 1 (6 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · February/March 2019 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 1  
Источник: [[_pdf/2019/0625_m19_qp_32.pdf#page=3|условие, стр. 3]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_m19_ms_32.pdf#page=4|схема оценивания, стр. 4]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_m19_er.pdf#page=5|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 m19 P32]]  
Темы: [[1.4 Density]], [[1.3 Mass and weight]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить общую плотность грузов одного материала, перевести массу в граммы и вычислить суммарный вес.

Fig. 1.1 shows a set of masses made from the same material.

![[0625-m19-32-p3-f1.png]]

**Fig. 1.1**

**(a)** Identify the quantity that is the same for all the masses.

![[0625-m19-32-p3-f2.svg]]

**[1]**

**(b)** The largest mass is 2.5 kg.

State the number of grams in 2.5 kg.

2.5 kg =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ g **[1]**

**(c)** The three largest masses are 2.5 kg, 1.0 kg and 0.5 kg.

Calculate the combined **weight** of these three masses. Include the unit.

weight =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[4]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(c)** 40

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | top box ticked: density | B1 |
> | (b) | 2500 (g) | B1 |
> | (c) | W = mg in any form | C1 |
> |  | (2.5 + 1.0 + 0.5) = 4 | C1 |
> |  | 40 | A1 |
> |  | N or newtons | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** The vast majority of candidates answered this correctly. Weaker candidates were equally distracted by the options of volume and weight.
>
> **(b)** Most candidates answered this question correctly. The most common error was dividing by 1000 instead of multiplying by 1000.
>
> **(c)** The majority of candidates gained at least partial credit. A common error was to omit or to give the wrong unit. Weaker candidates simply gave the combined mass.
