> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Распознать невозможность намагничивания медного стержня среди способов получения магнитов.

Which action will **not** magnetise a rod?

**A** placing a copper rod inside a coil carrying a direct current  
**B** stroking a steel rod with a permanent magnet  
**C** hammering a steel rod aligned with the Earth’s magnetic field  
**D** placing a soft-iron rod close to a permanent magnet

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> It was clear that very few candidates had seen the experiment in which a steel rod (which has been carefully demagnetised) is placed parallel to the Earth’s magnetic field and is hammered. Before hammering, the rod will not attract small pieces of demagnetised iron (e.g. pins), but afterwards it will pick them up.
