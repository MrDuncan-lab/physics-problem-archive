> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить намагниченные стержни по результатам притяжения и отталкивания их концов.

The ends of three metal rods are tested by holding end Q of rod 1 close to the others in turn.

![[0625-m19-12-p11-f1.svg]]

rod 1 rod 2 rod 3

The results are as follows.

End Q attracts end R.

End Q attracts end S.

End Q attracts end T.

End Q repels end U.

Which of the metal rods is a magnet?

**A** rod 1 only  
**B** rod 1 and rod 2  
**C** rod 1 and rod 3  
**D** rod 3 only

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Most candidates recognised that that repulsion meant that a rod is magnetised and many of those who recognised this realised that both rods must be magnetised for this to happen.
