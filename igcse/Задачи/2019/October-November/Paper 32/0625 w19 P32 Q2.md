# Question 2 (9 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2019 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 2

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Описывают точное измерение объёма металлического тела вытеснением воды и рассчитывают его плотность.

**(a)** A student has an irregularly shaped piece of metal, a beaker of water and a measuring cylinder, as shown in Fig. 2.1.

![[0625-w19-32-p4-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 2.1**

Describe how the student can accurately determine the volume of the piece of metal using the equipment provided. **[4]**

**(b)** The student measures the mass of the piece of metal. Its mass is 146 g.

**(i)** State the name of the instrument used to measure the mass. **[1]**

**(ii)** The volume of the piece of metal is 20 cm$^{3}$. Calculate the density of the metal. State the unit.

density =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[4]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)(ii)** 7.3

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | Any **four** from:<br>pour some water into measuring cylinder<br>record volume / reading of water (in measuring cylinder)<br>place metal in water (in cylinder and completely submerge)<br>record volume of water and metal (in cylinder)<br>subtract starting volume from final volume (to give volume of metal) | B4 |
> | (b)(i) | balance | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | density = mass ÷ volume | C1 |
> |  | 146 ÷ 20 | C1 |
> |  | 7.3 | A1 |
> |  | g/cm$^{3}$ | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** The majority of candidates described the correct steps to determine the volume of the piece of metal.
>
> **(b) (i)** Most candidates correctly gave mass balance or just balance. Some candidates gave weighing scales which was not credited.
>
> **(ii)** Most candidates used the formula for density and calculated the correct answer with the correct unit.
