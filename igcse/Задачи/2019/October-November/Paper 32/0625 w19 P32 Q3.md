# Question 3 (7 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2019 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 3  
Источник: [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_qp_32.pdf#page=5|условие, стр. 5–6]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_ms_32.pdf#page=7|схема оценивания, стр. 7]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_er.pdf#page=22|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Находят результирующую силу на пловца, объясняют движение и вычисляют момент веса на трамплине.

**(a)** Fig. 3.1 shows the horizontal forces acting on a swimmer.

![[0625-w19-32-p5-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 3.1**

**(i)** Calculate the size and direction of the resultant horizontal force on the swimmer.

size of resultant horizontal force =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ N

direction of resultant horizontal force =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(ii)** State the name of the 110 N force on the swimmer. **[1]**

**(iii)** Fig. 3.2 shows the horizontal forces acting on the swimmer as he moves forwards a

![[0625-w19-32-p5-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 3.2**

Describe and explain the motion of the swimmer. **[2]**

**(b)** Another swimmer weighs 700 N. He stands on a diving board, as shown in Fig. 3.3.

![[0625-w19-32-p6-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 3.3**

Calculate the moment of the swimmer’s weight about point P.

moment =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ N m **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** 2450 (Nm)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | 10 (N) AND forwards/to the right | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | friction (between swimmer and water) | B1 |
> | (a)(iii) | (now) moving at steady/constant speed | B1 |
> |  | forces (now)balanced / in equilibrium<br>**OR** forward force = backward force OR no resultant force | B1 |
> | (b) | moment = force × (perp.) distance (from pivot) | C1 |
> |  | 700 × 3.5 | C1 |
> |  | 2450 (Nm) | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** Most candidates answered correctly with 10 N, forwards / to the right.
>
> **(ii)** Most stronger candidates correctly identified the force as water resistance, drag or friction. Others referred incorrectly to backward or reaction force.
>
> **(iii)** Many candidates correctly identified that there was no resultant force. The strongest candidates described the motion correctly as constant speed. Weaker candidates stated that the swimmer was slowing down or stationary.
>
> **(b)** The majority of candidates correctly calculated the moment.
