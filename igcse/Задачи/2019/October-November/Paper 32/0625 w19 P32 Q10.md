A circuit is made from two lamps, a cell and a switch, as shown in Fig. 10.1.

![[0625-w19-32-p15-f1.png]]

**Fig. 10.1**

**(a)** **(i)** Draw the circuit symbol for a cell. **[1]**

**(ii)** State the term used for the arrangement of lamps in the circuit in Fig. 10.1. **[1]**

**(iii)** The switch is closed and the lamps light.

State the name of the charged particles that are flowing through the wires **[1]**

**(b)** Fig. 10.2 represents a different type of circuit.

![[0625-w19-32-p16-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 10.2**

**(i)** Compare Fig. 10.1 and Fig. 10.2. State **two** advantages of the type of circuit shown in Fig. 10.2 with the type of circuit shown in Fig. 10.1.

1 \_\_\_\_\_\_

2 \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(ii)** The potential difference across the power source in Fig. 10.2 is 3.0 V. The combined resistance of the two lamps is 12 Ω. Calculate the size of the current in the circuit.

current =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ A **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)(ii)** 0.25 (A)

>
> **(ii)** Most candidates correctly identified this as a series circuit.
>
> **(iii)** Only stronger candidates identified the charged particles flowing through wire as electrons.
