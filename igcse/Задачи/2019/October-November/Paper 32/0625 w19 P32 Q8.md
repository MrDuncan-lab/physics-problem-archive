> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Строят лучи собирающей линзы и описывают изменение изображения при удалении предмета.

Fig. 8.1 is a partially completed ray diagram.

![[0625-w19-32-p13-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 8.1**

The object is at O and its image is at I.

**(a)** Which distance is the focal length of the lens? Tick **one** box.

C to F$_{1}$

O to C

F$_{2}$ to I

O to I **[1]**

**(b)** On Fig. 8.1, extend the **two** rays from the arrowhead on the object until both reach the position of the image. **[3]**

**(c)** The object is moved a small distance **away** from the lens. State the effect, if any, this has on the position and size of the image.

position  \_\_\_\_\_\_

size  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | top box ticked C to F$_{1}$ | B1 |
> | (b) | Diagonal ray <u>through F1 to lens then parallel to optical axis to I.</u> | B1 |
> |  | ray parallel to principal axis to lens then refracted through F$_{2}$ to I | B1 |
> |  | both rays meet at arrowhead of image | B1 |
> | (c) | (image) closer (to lens / F$_{2}$) owtte | B1 |
> |  | (image) smaller | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Many candidates were able to correctly identify the focal length as *C* to *F*1.
>
> **(b)** Stronger candidates were able to extend the rays to show how the image was formed. Candidates should be careful to follow the instructions and extend the rays provided and not draw their own rays.
>
> **(c)** Very few candidates correctly stated that the image moved closer to the lens or *F*2. Many thought that it moved away. More candidates correctly stated that the image would be smaller.
