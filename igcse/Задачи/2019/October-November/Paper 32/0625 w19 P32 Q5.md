> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбирают невозобновляемые источники энергии и объясняют работу геотермальной электростанции.

**(a)** Energy sources used to generate electricity are shown in the box.

![[0625-w19-32-p9-f1.svg]]

Which energy sources are non-renewable?

Draw a ring around **each** energy source that is non-renewable. **[1]**

**(b)** The diagram shows a geothermal power station.

![[0625-w19-32-p9-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 5.1**

Describe how the geothermal power station generates electricity. **[4]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | gas AND oil both circled | B1 |
> | (b) | water is heated / **changed to steam** as it passes through (fractures in) rocks | B1 |
> |  | steam turns a turbine | B1 |
> |  | the turbine drives a generator | B1 |
> |  | <u>generator produces electricity</u> | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Many candidates were able to identify the two non-renewable energy sources as gas and oil. Weaker candidates only identified one or identified renewable energy sources.
>
> **(b)** Only the strongest candidates were able to describe how the geothermal power station generated electricity. Some candidates omitted the generator from the sequence. Many weaker candidates just described what was shown in the diagram.
