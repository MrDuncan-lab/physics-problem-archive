# Question 12 (6 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2019 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 12  
Источник: [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_qp_32.pdf#page=18|условие, стр. 18–19]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_ms_32.pdf#page=11|схема оценивания, стр. 11]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_er.pdf#page=22|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

Темы: [[5.1.2 The nucleus]], [[5.2.4 Half-life]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определяют смысл чисел в обозначении альфа-частицы и находят период полураспада по графику.

A radioactive substance decays by emitting an α-particle.

**(a)** The nuclide notation for an α-particle is

α

4 2

**(i)** State the term given to the number 4, written in the nuclide notation. **[1]**

**(ii)** State the term given to the number 2, written in the nuclide notation. **[1]**

**(b)** Fig. 12.1 shows the decay curve for a radioactive material.

![[0625-w19-32-p18-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 12.1**

**(i)** Use information from the graph in Fig. 12.1 to determine the half-life of the material. Clearly show how you used the graph to obtain your answer.

half-life =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ minutes **[3]**

**(ii)** Another radioactive material with the same half-life has an initial count rate of 600 counts / min. On Fig. 12.1 sketch the decay curve for this material. **[1]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | nucleon number OR mass number | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | proton number OR atomic number | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | selected count rate halved | B1 |
> |  | two pairs of co-ordinates clearly indicated | B1 |
> |  | (half-life =) 4 (minutes) | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | shallower curve drawn | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** Stronger candidates were able to correctly identify 4 as the nucleon number or mass number.
>
> **(ii)** The strongest candidates were able to correctly identify 2 as the proton number or atomic number.
>
> **(b) (i)** Most of the stronger candidates correctly calculated the half-life as 4 minutes.
>
> **(ii)** Most of these candidates went on to correctly draw a shallower curve.
