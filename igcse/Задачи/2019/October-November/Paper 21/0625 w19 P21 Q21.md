# Question 21 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2019 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 1 · вопрос 21  
Источник: [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_qp_21.pdf#page=7|условие, стр. 7]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_ms_21.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_er.pdf#page=10|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

Темы: [[3.1 General properties of waves]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объясняют приём радиосигнала за холмом дифракцией и определяют влияние длины волны.

A large hill blocks the direct path between a transmitter of radio waves and a receiver, as shown.

![[0625-w19-21-p7-f1.svg]]

The receiver picks up the signal from the transmitter even though the radio waves do not travel through the hill.

Which row is correct?

|  | A possible way for this to happen is | A stronger signal is received using |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | diffraction round the hill. | longer wavelengths. |
| **B** | diffraction round the hill. | shorter wavelengths. |
| **C** | refraction round the hill. | longer wavelengths. |
| **D** | refraction round the hill. | shorter wavelengths. |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The most common error on this question was to think that shorter wavelength waves diffract more than longer wavelength waves.
