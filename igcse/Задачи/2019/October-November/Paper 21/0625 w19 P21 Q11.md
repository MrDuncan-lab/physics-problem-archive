> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Вычисляют работу подъёма строительных плиток на крышу по их массе и высоте.

A man carries 20 tiles from the ground to the roof of a house. Each tile has a mass of 1.2 kg. The roof of the house is 15 m above the ground.

How much work does the man do against gravity on the tiles in carrying them to the roof?

**A** 36 J  
**B** 180 J  
**C** 360 J  
**D** 3600 J

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> A common error here was to not recognise that the force against which work is being done is equal to the mass of the bricks multiplied by the Earth’s gravitational field.
