Темы: [[4.2.1 Electric charge]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объясняют положительный заряд стержня потерей электронов при трении.

A plastic rod is rubbed with a dry cloth. The rod becomes positively charged.

Why has the rod become positively charged?

**A** It has gained electrons.  
**B** It has gained neutrons.  
**C** It has lost electrons.  
**D** It has lost neutrons.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The vast majority of candidates recognised that the positive charge on a rod is due to the loss of electrons.
