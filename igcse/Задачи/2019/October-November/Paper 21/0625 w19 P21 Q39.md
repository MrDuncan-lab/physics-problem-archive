> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Находят направление отклонения бета-частиц в магнитном поле.

The diagram shows β-particles being directed between the poles of a magnet.

![[0625-w19-21-p15-f1.svg]]

![[0625-w19-21-p15-f2.svg]]

![[0625-w19-21-p15-f3.svg]]

In which direction will the particles be deflected?

**A** into the page  
**B** out of the page  
**C** towards the bottom of the page  
**D** towards the top of the page

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The majority of candidates incorrectly thought that the β-particles are deflected towards one or other of the two magnetic poles. At this level candidates should understand this is an example of the motor effect and that the deflection is at right angles to both the magnetic field and the velocity of the particles.
