> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнивают два момента сил, вызывающих вращение линейки вокруг опоры.

Two forces *P* and *Q* act on a metre rule as shown. The metre rule is pivoted at one end. The rule starts to rotate in a clockwise direction.

![[0625-w19-21-p3-f2.svg]]

metre rule

Which statement is correct?

**A** *P* equals *Q*  
**B** *P* is less than *Q*  
**C** (*P* × *a*) is equal to (*Q* × *b*)  
**D** (*P* × *a*) is greater than (*Q* × (*a* + *b*))

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
