# Question 9 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2019 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 1 · вопрос 9  
Источник: [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_qp_21.pdf#page=4|условие, стр. 4]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_ms_21.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_er.pdf#page=10|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w19 P21]]  
Темы: [[1.6 Momentum]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Применяют сохранение импульса к неупругому столкновению тел, движущихся навстречу.

Two objects X and Y move directly towards each other. The objects have the same mass.

Object X has a velocity of 5.0 m / s to the right. Object Y has a velocity of 3.0 m / s to the left.

![[0625-w19-21-p4-f1.svg]]

Object X and object Y collide and stick together.

What is their velocity after colliding?

**A** 1.0 m / s to the left  
**B** 1.0 m / s to the right  
**C** 4.0 m / s to the left  
**D** 4.0 m / s to the right

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Most candidates were able to work successfully through this problem, but there were a significant number of candidates who did not recognise that if the momentum of object X is +5.0 *m*, the momentum of object Y would be –3.0 *m* (where *m* is the mass of the objects).
