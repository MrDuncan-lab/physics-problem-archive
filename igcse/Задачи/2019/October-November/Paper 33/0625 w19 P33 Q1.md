**(b)** A device has a light-emitting diode (LED) that flashes briefly at regular intervals.

Describe how to determine accurately the average time for each interval, using a stopwatch. **[4]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** 144.4(0) (s)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | 226.50 – 82.10 **OR** 3:46.5(0) – 1:22.1(0) **OR** <u>2 min 24.4 (s)</u> | C1 |
> |  | 144.4(0) (s) | A1 |
> | (b) | start stopwatch as LED lights owtte<br>count large number of flashes i.e. \> = 10<br>stop stopwatch on nth lighting of LED AND n \> = 1<br>divide time on stopwatch by n | B4 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Many correct answers were seen here. The most common errors were either omitting the hundredths of a second in the calculation or not converting to seconds.
>
> **(b)** A considerable number of candidates correctly indicated that the stopwatch should be started when the LED flashed. Many did not fully understand that the period between two flashes was too brief to measure with accuracy. The time interval for a large number of flashes needed to be found.
