> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Распознают термистор, читают график сопротивления и рассчитывают ток при заданном напряжении.

Fig. 10.1 shows the symbol for an electrical component.

![[0625-w19-33-p13-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 10.1**

**(a)** State the name of the component shown in Fig. 10.1. **[1]**

**(b)** The resistance of the component shown in Fig. 10.1 varies with temperature. Fig. 10.2 shows a graph of resistance against temperature for the component.

![[0625-w19-33-p13-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 10.2**

**(i)** Use Fig. 10.2 to determine the resistance of the component at a temperature of 10 °C.

\_\_\_\_\_\_ Ω **[1]**

**(ii)** At another temperature, the resistance of the component is 800 Ω. Calculate the current in the component when it is connected to a 12.0 V supply.

current =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ A **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)(ii)** 0.015 (A)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | <u>thermistor</u> | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | 2000 (Ω) | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | (I =) V ÷ R **OR** V = I × R in any form | C1 |
> |  | 12 ÷ 800 | C1 |
> |  | 0.015 (A) | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** This part was answered well but variable resistor or just resistor were common errors.
>
> **(b) (i)** The majority of candidates gave the correct answer.
>
> **(ii)** Many correct answers were seen for this calculation and these often showed both the correct equation and full working. Weaker candidates were unable to rearrange the *V = I × R* equation and used the incorrect *I = V × R*. (0.015 A)
