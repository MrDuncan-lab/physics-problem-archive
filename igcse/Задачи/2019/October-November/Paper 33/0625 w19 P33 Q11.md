> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** Many of the answers seen merely stated that d.c. was the abbreviation for direct current instead of explaining its meaning.
>
> **(ii)** Few correct answers were seen. The two cells were often drawn in parallel and candidates did not seem to know the symbol for a switch.
>
> **(b) (i)** Very few correct answers were seen and transformer was a popular incorrect answer.
>
> **(ii)** A number of candidates gained partial credit here, usually *for “*increasing the current”. A common error was to suggest using bigger magnets instead of stronger or more powerful magnets.
