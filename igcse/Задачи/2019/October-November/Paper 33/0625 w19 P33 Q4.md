> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** A common incorrect answer was kinetic energy. The bag is moving, so it does have kinetic energy; however, it does not gain kinetic energy because ‘*it is lifted at constant speed.’*
>
> **(ii)** This part was answered well by most candidates.
>
> **(iii)** Many candidates realised that the height raised was required but there was a lack of knowledge that the time was also needed.
>
> **(b)** The majority of candidates gained full credit on this part.
