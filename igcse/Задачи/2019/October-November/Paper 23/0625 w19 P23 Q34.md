> Определяют направление движения проводника для получения заданного направления индукционного тока.

A wire XY is connected to a resistor R. The wire is moved in the magnetic field between two magnetic poles.

In which direction must the wire be moved so that the induced current is in the direction shown?

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Only stronger candidates answered this question correctly. Although most candidates recognised that the wire needed to be moved in the vertical direction, most chose the incorrect sense (upwards rather than downwards).
