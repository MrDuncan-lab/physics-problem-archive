A ship sails due North at a speed of 20 m / s. A current in the water begins to move from East to West. The speed of this current is 20 m / s.

What is the magnitude of the resultant velocity of the ship?

**A** 0 m / s  
**B** 20 m / s  
**C** 28 m / s  
**D** 40 m / s

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
