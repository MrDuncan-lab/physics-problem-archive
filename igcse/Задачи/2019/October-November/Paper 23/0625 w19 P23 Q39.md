> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Делают вывод о малом размере атомного ядра из опыта рассеяния альфа-частиц.

A thin metal foil is placed in a vacuum. α-particles are fired at the foil and most go straight through. A very small proportion of the α-particles are deflected through large angles.

What does this provide evidence for?

**A** α-particles are very small.  
**B** There are negative electrons in each atom.  
**C** There is a tiny nucleus in each atom.  
**D** There are neutrons in each atom.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
