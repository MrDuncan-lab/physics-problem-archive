# Question 14 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2019 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 3 · вопрос 14  
Источник: [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_qp_23.pdf#page=5|условие, стр. 5]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_ms_23.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_er.pdf#page=15|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w19 P23]]  
Темы: [[1.8 Pressure]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнивают давление на острие кнопки и её широкой поверхности.

A drawing pin (thumb tack) has a sharp point at one end and a flat surface at the other end.

![[0625-w19-23-p5-f1.png]]

The pin is pushed into a wooden board.

How do the pressure and the force at the sharp point compare with the pressure and the force on the flat surface?

|  | force at the sharp point | pressure at the sharp point |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | greater than on the flat surface | greater than on the flat surface |
| **B** | greater than on the flat surface | less than on the flat surface |
| **C** | the same as on the flat surface | greater than on the flat surface |
| **D** | the same as on the flat surface | less than on the flat surface |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The majority of candidates showed good understanding of pressure and force. However, there were others who thought that the force at the point of the pin was greater than the force on the flat surface.
