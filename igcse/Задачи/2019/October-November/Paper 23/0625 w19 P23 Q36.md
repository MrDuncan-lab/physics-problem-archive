An a.c. generator contains a coil that rotates at a rate of 4500 revolutions per minute.

What is the frequency of the alternating current?

**A** 1.25 Hz  
**B** 75 Hz  
**C** 150 Hz  
**D** 4500 Hz

> [!answer]- Ответ
