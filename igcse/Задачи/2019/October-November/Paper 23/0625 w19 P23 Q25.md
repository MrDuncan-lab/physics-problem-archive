> Выбирают относительные скорости звука во льду, воде и паре.

Which row gives a possible set of values for the speed of sound in ice, in water and in steam?

|  | speed of sound in ice m/s | speed of sound in water m/s | speed of sound in steam m/s |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | 500 | 1500 | 4000 |
| **B** | 1500 | 4000 | 500 |
| **C** | 4000 | 500 | 1500 |
| **D** | 4000 | 1500 | 500 |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
