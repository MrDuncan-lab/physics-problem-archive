# Question 30 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2019 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 3 · вопрос 30  
Источник: [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_qp_23.pdf#page=11|условие, стр. 11]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_ms_23.pdf|схема оценивания]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбирают соотношение токов и напряжений для трёх параллельных резисторов.

Resistors of resistance 1.0 Ω, 2.0 Ω and 3.0 Ω are connected in parallel across the terminals of

a cell.

Which statement is correct?

**A** The currents in the resistors are equal.  
**B** The sum of the currents in the three resistors is equal to the current in the cell.  
**C** The sum of the potential differences (p.d.’s) across the resistors is equal to the electromotive

force (e.m.f.) of the cell.

**D** The potential difference across the 3.0 Ω resistor is greater than the potential difference

across the other two resistors.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
