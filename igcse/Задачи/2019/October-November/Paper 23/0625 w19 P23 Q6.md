# Question 6 (1 mark)

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определяют, какие стеклянные сферы утонут, сравнивая плотности сфер и жидкости.

Four hollow glass spheres P, Q, R and S each have a mass of 72 g.

Their volumes are given in the table.

|  | volume / cm$^{3}$ |
|---|---|
| P | 55 |
| Q | 65 |
| R | 75 |
| S | 85 |

Which spheres sink in a liquid of density 0.9 g / cm$^{3}$?

**A** P, Q and R  
**B** Q, R and S  
**C** R and S only  
**D** S only

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Although in general the question was successfully completed by most candidates, there were a significant number who thought that the objects with the smaller masses would sink. In order for the spheres to sink, their density must be greater than the density of the liquid (0.90 g/cm$^{3}$). Simple calculations of
>
> mass
>
> density= volume for each sphere lead to the answer.
