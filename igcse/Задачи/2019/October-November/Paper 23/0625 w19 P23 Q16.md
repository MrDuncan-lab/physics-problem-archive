# Question 16 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2019 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 3 · вопрос 16  
Источник: [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_qp_23.pdf#page=5|условие, стр. 5]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_ms_23.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_er.pdf#page=15|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

Темы: [[2.1.3 Gases and the absolute scale of temperature]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитывают изменение давления газа при изотермическом уменьшении объёма.

A fixed mass of gas has a volume of 25 cm$^{3}$. The pressure of the gas is 100 kPa.

The volume of the gas is slowly decreased by 15 cm$^{3}$ at constant temperature.

What is the change in pressure of the gas?

**A** 67 kPa  
**B** 150 kPa  
**C** 170 kPa  
**D** 250 kPa

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question proved challenging, partly due to many candidates not reading it carefully enough. It asked for the **change** in pressure (= (250 – 100)kPa) not the new pressure (250kPa). This emphasises the need for care when reading the question. Also, a lot of candidates took the new volume to be 15 cm$^{3}$ rather than 10 cm$^{3}$.
