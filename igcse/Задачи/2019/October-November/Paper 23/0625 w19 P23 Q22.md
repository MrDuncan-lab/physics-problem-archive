# Question 22 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2019 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 3 · вопрос 22  
Источник: [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_qp_23.pdf#page=8|условие, стр. 8]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_ms_23.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_er.pdf#page=15|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

Темы: [[3.2.1 Reflection of light]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определяют движение изображения при удалении плоского зеркала от наблюдателя.

A person stands 1.0 m in front of a plane mirror. The mirror is moved away from the person at a speed of 1.0 m / s.

Which statement is correct?

**A** The image moves away from the person at a speed of 1.0 m / s.  
**B** The image moves away from the person at a speed of 2.0 m / s.  
**C** The image moves towards the person at a speed of 1.0 m / s.  
**D** The image moves towards the person at a speed of 2.0 m / s.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question was not always answered correctly, mainly because many candidates were under the misconception that shorter wavelength waves diffract more than longer wavelengths.
