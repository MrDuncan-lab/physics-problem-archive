> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбирают температуры фиксированных точек для калибровки термометра по шкале Цельсия.

A liquid-in-glass thermometer has a range from –10 °C to 110 °C.

What are the fixed point temperatures used when calibrating this thermometer in °C?

**A** –10 °C and 0 °C  
**B** –10 °C and 110 °C  
**C** 0 °C and 100 °C  
**D** 0 °C and 110 °C

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The question was answered well. The most frequent mistake was to take the two extreme values marked on the thermometer. These points are marked on the thermometer by extrapolating from the two fixed points (the melting point of pure ice and the boiling point of pure water at 760 mmHg), 0°C and 100°C respectively.
