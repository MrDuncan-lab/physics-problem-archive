# Question 16 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2019 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 3 · вопрос 16  
Источник: [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_qp_13.pdf#page=6|условие, стр. 6]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_ms_13.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_er.pdf#page=7|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w19 P13]]  
Темы: [[2.2.1 Thermal expansion of solids, liquids and gases]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объясняют движение жидкости в термометре сравнением расширения жидкостей и твёрдых тел.

The diagram shows a liquid-in-glass thermometer.

![[0625-w19-13-p6-f3.svg]]

When the temperature of the thermometer rises, the changes produced cause the liquid thread to move to the right.

Why does this happen when the temperature of the thermometer rises?

**A** Gases contract and liquids expand.  
**B** Gases contract and solids expand.  
**C** Liquids expand more than gases.  
**D** Liquids expand more than solids.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Few candidates understood the action of a liquid-in-glass thermometer. It relies on the expansion of the liquid. The glass container also expands but importantly, significantly less than the liquid.
