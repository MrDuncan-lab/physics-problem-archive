> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определяют, какие полые стеклянные шары утонут, сравнивая их плотности с плотностью жидкости.

Four hollow glass spheres P, Q, R and S each have a mass of 72 g.

Their volumes are given in the table.

|  | volume / cm$^{3}$ |
|---|---|
| P | 55 |
| Q | 65 |
| R | 75 |
| S | 85 |

Which spheres sink in a liquid of density 0.9 g / cm$^{3}$?

**A** P, Q and R  
**B** Q, R and S  
**C** R and S only  
**D** S only

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question was challenging for many candidates. In order for the spheres to sink, their density must be
>
> mass
>
> greater than the density of the liquid (0.90 g/cm$^{3}$). Simple calculations of density= volume for each sphere
>
> leads to the answer.
