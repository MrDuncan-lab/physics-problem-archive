# Question 7 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2019 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 3 · вопрос 7  
Источник: [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_qp_13.pdf#page=3|условие, стр. 3]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_ms_13.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_er.pdf#page=7|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w19 P13]]  
Темы: [[1.5.2 Turning effect of forces]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитывают натяжение нити из равновесия двух моментов сил на линейке.

The diagram shows a uniform metre rule. The rule is pivoted at its mid-point. A downward force of 4.0 N acts on the rule at the 5 cm mark. The rule is held by a string at the 30 cm mark. The rule is in equilibrium.

![[0625-w19-13-p3-f1.svg]]

What is the upward force that the string exerts on the rule?

**A** 0.67 N  
**B** 4.0 N  
**C** 6.0 N  
**D** 9.0 N

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The majority of candidates did not recognise this as a moments problem and assumed that the upward force at the string would be equal to the downward force at the 5 cm mark. Only stronger candidates answered correctly.
