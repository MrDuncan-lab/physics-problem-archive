# Question 25 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2019 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 3 · вопрос 25  
Источник: [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_qp_13.pdf#page=9|условие, стр. 9]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_ms_13.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_er.pdf#page=7|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w19 P13]]  
Темы: [[3.3 Electromagnetic spectrum]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбирают величину, возрастающую от гамма-излучения к микроволнам.

The diagram shows the electromagnetic spectrum.

![[0625-w19-13-p9-f2.svg]]

γ-rays ultraviolet visible light infrared microwaves

![[0625-w19-13-p9-f3.svg]]

increasing  \_\_\_\_\_\_

A word is missing from the label below the spectrum.

Which word is missing?

**A** amplitude  
**B** frequency  
**C** speed  
**D** wavelength

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Many candidates recognised that the wavelength of the radiations increases going from γ-radiation towards radio waves but a significant number thought it was the frequency that increased.
