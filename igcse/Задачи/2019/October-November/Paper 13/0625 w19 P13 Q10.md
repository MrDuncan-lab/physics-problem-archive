> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Распознают ситуации, в которых сила совершает механическую работу.

Three situations are listed.

1 someone blowing air into a party balloon

2 a crane lifting a block of concrete

3 a pile of books at rest on a shelf

In which situations is work being done?

**A** 1 only  
**B** 1 and 2 only  
**C** 2 and 3 only  
**D** 1, 2 and 3

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The question was answered well. Most candidates recognised that when a balloon is being blown up, work is done. The work is actually done in both stretching the fabric of the balloon and in compressing the air inside the balloon.
