# Question 12 (5 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2019 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 1 · вопрос 12  
Источник: [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_qp_31.pdf#page=19|условие, стр. 19–20]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_ms_31.pdf#page=11|схема оценивания, стр. 11]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_er.pdf#page=18|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w19 P31]]  
Темы: [[5.2.4 Half-life]], [[5.2.2 The three types of nuclear emission]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определяют период полураспада по таблице и характеризуют природу, ионизацию и проникновение альфа-частиц.

A teacher carries out two experiments at the same time.

**(a)** In the first experiment the count rate for a sample of a radioactive isotope is measured every 30 seconds for 6 minutes.

The results are shown in Table 12.1.

**Table 12.1**

| time / minutes | count rate counts / second |
|---|---|
| 0.0 | 1246 |
| 0.5 | 1036 |
| 1.0 | 941 |
| 1.5 | 810 |
| 2.0 | 686 |
| 2.5 | 621 |
| 3.0 | 550 |
| 3.5 | 468 |
| 4.0 | 421 |
| 4.5 | 368 |
| 5.0 | 318 |
| 5.5 | 280 |
| 6.0 | 242 |

Estimate the half-life of the radioactive isotope. Use the information in the table.

half-life =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ minutes **[1]**

**(b)** In the second experiment the teacher repeats the procedure with another sample of the same radioactive isotope. The mass of the second sample is greater than that of the first sample.

Suggest a value for the count rate for this sample at the start of the experiment.

count rate =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ counts / second **[1]**

**(c)** One type of particle emitted during radioactive decay is an α-particle (alpha particle).

Describe:

**(i)** the nature of an α-particle **[1]**

**(ii)** the ionising ability of an α-particle **[1]**

**(iii)** the penetrating ability of an α-particle. **[1]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | 2.5 (minutes) | B1 |
> | (b) | any answer above 1246 (counts/s), e.g. 1247 | B1 |
> | (c) | 1. helium nucleus OR 2 protons **AND** 2 neutrons | B1 |
> |  | 2. strongly (ionising) | B1 |
> |  | 3. weakly (penetrating) | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Many candidates gained credit for identifying 2.5 minutes as the half-life. However, the majority of candidates thought that half way down the column of times, i.e. 3 minutes, was the half-life.
>
> **(b)** Many candidates gained credit for identifying a count rate higher than the starting count rate of the first sample.
>
> **(c) (i)** Only the strongest candidates answered correctly, usually by using the nuclide notation for a helium nucleus.
>
> **(ii)** Many candidates gained credit for identifying alpha as being strongly ionising.
>
> **(iii)** Many candidates gained credit for identifying alpha as being weakly penetrating. However, many incorrectly stated that alpha particles would penetrate paper.
