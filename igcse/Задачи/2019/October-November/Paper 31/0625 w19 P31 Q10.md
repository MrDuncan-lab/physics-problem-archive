# Question 10 (9 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2019 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 1 · вопрос 10  
Источник: [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_qp_31.pdf#page=17|условие, стр. 17]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_ms_31.pdf#page=10|схема оценивания, стр. 10]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_er.pdf#page=18|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w19 P31]]  
Темы: [[4.2.4 Resistance]], [[4.3.1 Circuit diagrams and circuit components]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Дополняют измерительную цепь лампы, рассчитывают сопротивление и описывают применение переменного резистора.

A teacher is investigating the resistance of a lamp.

Fig. 10.1 shows part of the circuit she uses. The circuit is incomplete.

![[0625-w19-31-p17-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 10.1**

**(a)** **(i)** To determine the resistance of the lamp, the teacher adds two meters to her circuit.

On Fig. 10.1, draw circuit symbols to show each meter correctly connected in the circuit. **[3]**

**(ii)** When the current in the lamp is 0.25 A, the potential difference (p.d.) across the lamp is 4.5 V. Calculate the resistance of the lamp.

resistance =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ Ω **[3]**

**(b)** **(i)** State the name of component X. **[1]**

**(ii)** Describe and explain how the teacher uses component X to investigate the resistance of the lamp. **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)(ii)** 18 ( Ω)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | correct symbol for ammeter | B1 |
> |  | correct symbol for voltmeter | B1 |
> |  | ammeter in series and voltmeter in parallel with lamp | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | (R =) V ÷ I OR *V =* I× *R* in any form | C1 |
> |  | (R = ) 4.5 ÷ 0.25 | C1 |
> |  | 18 ( Ω) | A1 |
> | (b)(i) | <u>variable resistor</u> | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | (sliding contact moved to) change resistance (in circuit) | B1 |
> |  | (and so) change current (in lamp) or p.d. (across lamp) | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** Most candidates gave the correct symbols, but considerably fewer had them correctly connected.
>
> **(ii)** The majority of candidates correctly calculated the resistance of the lamp as 18 ohms. Weaker candidates used an incorrect transformation of *V* = *IR*.
>
> **(b) (i)** Most candidates clearly identified the variable resistor. The most common error was to state that component X was a resistor.
>
> **(ii)** Only the strongest candidates were able to link the use of a variable resistor to a change in the circuit resistance giving an ability to control the current in the circuit.
