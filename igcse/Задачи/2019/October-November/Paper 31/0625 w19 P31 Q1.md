# Question 1 (5 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2019 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 1 · вопрос 1  
Источник: [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_qp_31.pdf#page=2|условие, стр. 2]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_ms_31.pdf#page=6|схема оценивания, стр. 6]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_er.pdf#page=18|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w19 P31]]  
Темы: [[1.4 Density]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитывают массу воды в бочке и определяют возможность плавания полной бочки в морской воде.

Fig. 1.1 shows a plastic water barrel. The barrel is full of water.

![[0625-w19-31-p2-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 1.1**

**(a)** The water barrel contains 0.050 m$^{3}$ of pure water. The density of pure water is 1000 kg / m$^{3}$.

Calculate the mass of pure water in the barrel.

mass of water =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ kg **[3]**

**(b)** The density of sea water is 1030 kg / m$^{3}$. The density of the plastic is 1000 kg / m$^{3}$. Use this information and the information in **(a)** to state and explain whether the full barrel will float in sea water.

statement  \_\_\_\_\_\_

explanation  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** 50 (kg)
>
> **(b)** density of plastic OR barrel AND (pure) water is less than sea water

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | density = mass ÷ volume in any form OR (mass =) density × volume | C1 |
> |  | mass = 1000 × 0.05 | C1 |
> |  | 50 (kg) | A1 |
> | (b) | Floats OR does not sink | M0 |
> |  | density of full barrel OR its density OR density of plastic OR density of barrel OR density of (pure) water is less than<br>sea water | C1 |
> |  | density of plastic OR barrel AND (pure) water is less than sea water | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** This item was challenging for many candidates, with a significant number using an incorrect form of ρ = *m/V*. This was often compounded by a lack of working meaning that candidates failed to gain credit for the correct equation. Candidates should be encouraged to write down a correct form of the equation and then show its re-arrangement.
>
> **(b)** The majority of candidates gained partial credit, often as a result of only identifying the plastic as being less dense than seawater.
