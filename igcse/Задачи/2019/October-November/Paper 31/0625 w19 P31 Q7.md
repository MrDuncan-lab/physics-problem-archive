# Question 7 (6 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2019 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 1 · вопрос 7  
Источник: [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_qp_31.pdf#page=12|условие, стр. 12]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_ms_31.pdf#page=9|схема оценивания, стр. 9]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_er.pdf#page=18|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w19 P31]]  
Темы: [[3.2.3 Thin lenses]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Строят изображение собирающей линзой, определяют фокусное расстояние и свойства изображения.

An object, OX, is placed in front of a converging lens.

Fig. 7.1 shows a ray of light from the object passing through the lens.

![[0625-w19-31-p12-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 7.1**

**(a)** **(i)** The lens forms an image of object OX.

On Fig. 7.1, draw another ray from X to locate the position of the image. **[1]**

**(ii)** On Fig. 7.1, draw an arrow to represent the image of OX and label it I. **[1]**

**(iii)** On Fig. 7.1, mark a principal focus for the lens and label it F. **[1]**

**(iv)** On Fig. 7.1, measure and record the focal length of the lens.

focal length =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm **[1]**

**(b)** Describe the image I.

Choose words from the list. Tick (✓) **two** boxes.

![[0625-w19-31-p12-f2.svg]]

enlarged

diminished

same size

inverted

upright **[2]**

> | (a)(i) | ray from X through centre of lens | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | image drawn from axis to point where rays cross and labelled I | B1 |
> | (a)(iii) | point labelled F where ray crosses principal axis | B1 |
> | (a)(iv) | 2.7 (cm) ± 0.2 cm | B1 |
> | (b) | diminished 2nd box ticked | B1 |
> |  | inverted 4th box ticked | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** Very few candidates were able to draw a ray with sufficient accuracy to gain credit. Centres should encourage candidates to use a ruler in all ray diagrams and to practise drawing ray diagrams to show the formation of an image by a thin converging lens.
>
> **(ii)** Very few candidates were able to draw an image with sufficient accuracy to gain credit.
>
> **(iii)** Very few candidates were able to correctly identify the position of a principal focus.
>
> **(iv)** Very few candidates were able to correctly measure the focal length of the lens.
>
> **(b)** With error carried forward from the diagram, the majority of candidates gained at least partial credit for this question.
