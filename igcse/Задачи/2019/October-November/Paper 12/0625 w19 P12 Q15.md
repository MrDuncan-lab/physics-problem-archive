> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Связывают повышение давления воздуха в шинах с ростом температуры и скорости молекул.

A driver of a car measures the pressure of the air in the tyres. He measures the pressure again

after a long journey. The pressure reading has increased.

Which row states what has happened to the speed of the air molecules and the temperature of the air in the tyres?

|  | speed of molecules | temperature |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | decreases | decreases |
| **B** | decreases | increases |
| **C** | increases | decreases |
| **D** | increases | increases |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
