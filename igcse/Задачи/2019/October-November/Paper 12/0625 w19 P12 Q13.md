> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определяют величину, необходимую вместе с весом для расчёта давления блока на стол.

A metal block of weight *W* rests on a table. In order to calculate the pressure that the block exerts on the table, one other quantity must be known.

What is the other quantity?

**A** the area of contact between the block and the table  
**B** the density of the block  
**C** the mass of the block  
**D** the volume of the block

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**
