> Вычисляют скорость звука по задержке выстрела и расстоянию до источника.

A man hears a starting pistol fire 1.5 seconds after he sees a puff of smoke from the pistol. The

sound and the smoke are made at the same time. The starting pistol is 450 metres away from the man.

What is the speed of sound calculated from this observation?

**A** 150 m / s  
**B** 300 m / s  
**C** 330 m / s  
**D** 625 m / s

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
