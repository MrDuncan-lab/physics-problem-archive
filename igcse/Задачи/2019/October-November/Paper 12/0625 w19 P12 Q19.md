# Question 19 (1 mark)

Источник: [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_qp_12.pdf#page=7|условие, стр. 7]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_ms_12.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_er.pdf#page=4|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Распознают конвекцию по подъёму жидкости после уменьшения её плотности.

Which method of thermal transfer occurs when the density of some of a liquid decreases and the liquid moves upwards?

**A** conduction  
**B** convection  
**C** evaporation  
**D** radiation

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question proved challenging and candidates showed little understanding of the transfer of thermal energy by convection. The hot water rises because as it is heated and as it gets warmer, it expands and becomes less dense. This sets up a circulation of the water (or convection current) as more water is heated.
