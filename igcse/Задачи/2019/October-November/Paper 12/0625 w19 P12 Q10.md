> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбирают единицу измерения тепловой энергии.

What is the unit of thermal energy?

**A** °C  
**B** N  
**C** J  
**D** W

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Although a small majority of candidates got this correct, many candidates thought that thermal energy is measured in °C. It is imperative that candidates recognise the difference between thermal energy and temperature.
