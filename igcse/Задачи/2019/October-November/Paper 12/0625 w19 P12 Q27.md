# Question 27 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2019 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 27  
Источник: [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_qp_12.pdf#page=10|условие, стр. 10]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_ms_12.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_er.pdf#page=4|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объясняют притяжение ненамагниченного металлического предмета магнитной индукцией.

A bar magnet is slowly moved towards an unmagnetised metal object X.

![[0625-w19-12-p10-f3.svg]]

![[0625-w19-12-p10-t1.svg]]

When it is a few centimetres away, the object begins to slide towards the magnet.

Why does this happen?

**A** X is a non-magnetic material and magnetism is induced in it.  
**B** X is a non-magnetic material and magnetism is not induced in it.  
**C** X is a magnetic material and magnetism is induced in it.  
**D** X is a magnetic material and magnetism is not induced in it.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Many candidates were confused by the diagram and needed to understand that the line of magnetic flux (or field) shows the direction of the field at that place. The compass needle will point along that line with the North Pole pointing in the direction of the field.
