**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2019 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 21  
Источник: [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_qp_12.pdf#page=8|условие, стр. 8]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_ms_12.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_er.pdf#page=4|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбирают направление колебаний и пример поперечной волны.

Which row correctly describes the vibrations of a transverse wave and also gives a correct

example of a transverse wave?

|  | description of vibration | example of a transverse wave |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | right-angles to the wave direction | sound |
| **B** | right-angles to the wave direction | water wave |
| **C** | parallel to the wave direction | sound |
| **D** | parallel to the wave direction | water wave |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Only stronger candidates recognised the direction of vibrations in transverse wave and were able to identify a transverse wave.
