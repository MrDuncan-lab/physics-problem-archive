# Question 28 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2019 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 28  
Источник: [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_qp_12.pdf#page=11|условие, стр. 11]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_ms_12.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_er.pdf#page=4|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

Темы: [[4.1 Simple phenomena of magnetism]], [[4.5.3 Magnetic effect of a current]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определяют взаимодействие железных стержней в катушке до и после обращения полярности батареи.

Two soft-iron rods are placed end-to-end inside a coil. The coil is connected to a battery.

![[0625-w19-12-p11-f1.svg]]

The connections from the battery to the coil are now reversed.

What happens to the soft-iron rods in each case?

|  | battery connections as shown | battery connections reversed |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | rods attract | rods attract |
| **B** | rods attract | rods repel |
| **C** | rods repel | rods attract |
| **D** | rods repel | rods repel |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Only stronger candidates recognised that the two magnets in the coil would be magnetised in the same sense. Thus, adjacent poles of the two magnets would be of opposite polarity and they would attract each other regardless of the current direction.
