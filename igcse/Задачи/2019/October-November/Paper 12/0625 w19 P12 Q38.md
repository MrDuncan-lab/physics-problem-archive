> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определяют нуклонное число хлора по обозначению нуклида.

A nuclide of chlorine has the symbol shown.

${}^{35}_{17}\text{Cl}$

What is the nucleon number of this nuclide of chlorine?

**A** 17  
**B** 18  
**C** 35  
**D** 52

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> Stronger candidates showed a knowledge and understanding of the meaning of proton number and nucleon number and were able to solve this problem.
