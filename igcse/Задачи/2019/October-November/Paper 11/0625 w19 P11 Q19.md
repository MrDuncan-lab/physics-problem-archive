# Question 19 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2019 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 1 · вопрос 19  
Источник: [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_qp_11.pdf#page=7|условие, стр. 7]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_ms_11.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_er.pdf#page=1|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w19 P11]]  
Темы: [[2.3.1 Conduction]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объясняют сохранение холодной воды внизу пробирки низкой теплопроводностью воды и стекла.

A glass test-tube containing water is heated at the top. The water at the top boils, but the water at

the bottom remains cold.

![[0625-w19-11-p7-f1.svg]]

Which row explains why the water at the bottom of the test-tube remains cold?

|  | glass | water |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | good thermal conductor | good thermal conductor |
| **B** | good thermal conductor | poor thermal conductor |
| **C** | poor thermal conductor | good thermal conductor |
| **D** | poor thermal conductor | poor thermal conductor |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Only stronger candidates recognised that if the water at the bottom of the container remained cool, then neither the water nor the material, from which the container is made, can be a good conductor.
