> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнивают яркость одинаковых ламп в эквивалентных соединениях.

The circuit shows two lamps connected to a d.c. supply.

![[0625-w19-11-p13-f1.svg]]

The same lamps and power supply are arranged in different ways, as shown.

![[0625-w19-11-p13-f2.svg]]

In which circuits will the lamps be the same brightness as in the original circuit?

**A** Q only  
**B** P and Q only  
**C** P and R only  
**D** P, Q and R

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Few candidates were able to trace a circuit and recognise circuits which are, in practise, identical.
