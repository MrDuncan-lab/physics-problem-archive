# Question 25 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2019 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 1 · вопрос 25  
Источник: [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_qp_11.pdf#page=10|условие, стр. 10]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_ms_11.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_er.pdf#page=1|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w19 P11]]  
Темы: [[3.3 Electromagnetic spectrum]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определяют величину, возрастающую вдоль указанного порядка электромагнитного спектра.

The diagram shows the electromagnetic spectrum.

![[0625-w19-11-p10-f1.svg]]

γ-rays ultraviolet visible light infrared microwaves

![[0625-w19-11-p10-f2.svg]]

increasing  \_\_\_\_\_\_

A word is missing from the label below the spectrum.

Which word is missing?

**A** amplitude  
**B** frequency  
**C** speed  
**D** wavelength

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Although many candidates recognised that the wavelength of the radiations increases going from γ-radiation towards radio waves, a significant number thought it was the frequency that increased.
