> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определяют пройденное расстояние по графику скорости.

The graph shows how the speed of an object changes with time.

![[0625-w19-11-p2-f1.svg]]

How far does the object travel in 10 seconds?

**A** 8 m  
**B** 10 m  
**C** 40 m  
**D** 80 m

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The question was reasonably well answered but a significant number of candidates multiplied the maximum speed by the time taken, rather than using the average speed.
