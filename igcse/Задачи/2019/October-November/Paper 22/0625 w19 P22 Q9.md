> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитывают импульс силы удара и среднее ускорение мяча.

A ball is at rest on the ground. A boy kicks the ball. The boy’s boot is in contact with the ball for 0.040 s.

The average force on the ball is 200 N. The ball leaves the boy’s boot with a speed of 20 m / s.

Which row gives the impulse of the boot on the ball and the average acceleration of the ball?

|  | impulse on ball N s | average acceleration of ball m / s$^{2}$ |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | 8 | 0.8 |
| **B** | 8 | 500 |
| **C** | 5000 | 0.8 |
| **D** | 5000 | 500 |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
