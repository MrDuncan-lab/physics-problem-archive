> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Находят массу и вес ящика по силе его равномерного подъёма.

A box is placed on the ground. An upward force of 15 N is needed to lift the box at constant speed.

Which row correctly describes the box?

|  | mass of the box | weight of the box |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | 1.5 kg | 15 N |
| **B** | 15 N | 1.5 kg |
| **C** | 15 N | 150 kg |
| **D** | 150 kg | 15 N |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> Candidates showed an excellent understanding of the difference between mass and weight.
