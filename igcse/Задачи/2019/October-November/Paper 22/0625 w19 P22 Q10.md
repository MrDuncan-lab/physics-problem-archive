# Question 10 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2019 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 2 · вопрос 10  
Источник: [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_qp_22.pdf#page=5|условие, стр. 5]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_ms_22.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_er.pdf#page=12|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w19 P22]]  
Темы: [[1.6 Momentum]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Находят скорость слипшихся тел после столкновения по закону сохранения импульса.

An object P of mass 80 g collides with another object Q of mass 40 g.

After the collision, P and Q stick together and then travel on together.

Before the collision, P is travelling at a speed of 6.0 m / s and Q is at rest.

What is the speed of P and Q after the collision?

**A** 2.0 m / s  
**B** 3.0 m / s  
**C** 4.0 m / s  
**D** 6.0 m / s

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Although the question was answered well by most candidates, a number seemed to take the masses of the two objects to be equal resulting in a final speed of 3.0 m/s.
