# Question 39 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2019 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 2 · вопрос 39  
Источник: [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_qp_22.pdf#page=13|условие, стр. 13]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_ms_22.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_er.pdf#page=12|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w19 P22]]  
Темы: [[5.2.4 Half-life]], [[5.2.1 Detection of radioactivity]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитывают интенсивность источника после периода полураспада с поправкой на фон.

A radioactive isotope has a half-life of 8 days.

A detector close to a sample of this isotope gives a count rate of 200 counts per minute. Without the source, the background count is 20 counts per minute.

What is the count rate due to the source after 8 days?

**A** 80 counts per minute  
**B** 90 counts per minute  
**C** 100 counts per minute  
**D** 110 counts per minute

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question proved challenging as many candidates did not read it with sufficient care. The question clearly asked for the count rate due to the source after eight days, but most candidates gave the rate due to the source plus that due to the background count rate.
