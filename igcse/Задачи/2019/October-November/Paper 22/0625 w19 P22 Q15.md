> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитывают давление столба ртути по его высоте и плотности.

The density of mercury is 13 600 kg / m$^{3}$.

What is the pressure at the bottom of a column of mercury that has a height of 75.0 cm?

**A** 1.02 × 10$^{4}$ Pa  
**B** 1.02 × 10$^{5}$ Pa  
**C** 1.02 × 10$^{6}$ Pa  
**D** 1.02 × 10$^{7}$ Pa

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Although the majority of candidates correctly calculated the pressure at the bottom of the mercury column, there were some who forgot to multiply by the *g.*
