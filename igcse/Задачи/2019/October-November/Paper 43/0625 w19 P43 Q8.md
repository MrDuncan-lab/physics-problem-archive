# Question 8 (4 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2019 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 3 · вопрос 8  
Источник: [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_qp_43.pdf#page=10|условие, стр. 10]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_ms_43.pdf#page=8|схема оценивания, стр. 8]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_er.pdf#page=36|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

Темы: [[4.2.1 Electric charge]], [[4.2.2 Electric current]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Изображают поле отрицательно заряженной сферы и рассчитывают заряд по току и времени.

**(a)** Fig. 8.1 shows a negatively charged conducting sphere.

![[0625-w19-43-p10-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 8.1**

On Fig. 8.1, draw the electric field pattern around the sphere. **[2]**

**(b)** The current in an electrical device is 0.21 A.

Calculate the charge that flows during a 75 s period of time.

charge =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** 16 C

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | radial lines from sphere | B1 |
> |  | arrows pointing towards sphere | B1 |
> | (b) | *Q* = *I*t, in any form OR 0.21 × 75 | C1 |
> |  | 16 C | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Most candidates realised that the pattern was one of radial lines. Careless drawing resulted in some candidates not showing the lines as radial or spacing them unevenly. Some candidates drew arrows in the wrong direction.
>
> **(b)** This question was generally well answered with most candidates being able to identify the correct equation to use. Some candidates had the terms transposed. This was the question with the most unit errors.
