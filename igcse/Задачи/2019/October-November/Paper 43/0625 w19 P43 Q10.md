# Question 10 (6 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2019 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 3 · вопрос 10  
Источник: [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_qp_43.pdf#page=12|условие, стр. 12–13]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_ms_43.pdf#page=10|схема оценивания, стр. 10]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_er.pdf#page=36|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w19 P43]]  
Темы: [[4.5.1 Electromagnetic induction]], [[4.4 Electrical safety]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объясняют индукционную индикацию движения двери и преимущества автоматического выключателя для электрической газонокосилки.

**(a)** A magnet and a coil are attached separately to a door and a door frame as shown in Fig. 10.1.

The purpose of the arrangement is to activate a circuit connected to an LED indicator when the door is opening or closing. This will provide a visual indication that the door is being used.

![[0625-w19-43-p12-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 10.1**

Initially, the door is closed and then it is opened.

**(i)** Explain why the indicator comes on and then goes off when the door is opened. **[2]**

**(ii)** The door shuts. The indicator comes on more brightly but for a shorter time than it did in **(i)**. Suggest and explain why this happens. **[2]**

**(b)** A circuit breaker is recommended for use with an electric lawnmower.

State **two** reasons for this recommendation.

reason 1  \_\_\_\_\_\_

reason 2  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | movement of magnet relative to coil OR<br>induces emf / pd / current (across / in LED) | B1 |
> |  | light goes off when magnet no longer directly below coil | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | door closes more quickly than it was opened so higher current in LED | B1 |
> |  | door / magnet moving for shorter length of time | B1 |
> | (b) | Any two from:<br>• quick response<br>• protects against electric shock<br>• protects against overheating<br>• (easily) re-settable<br>• avoids damage to lawnmower | B2 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** Many stronger candidates correctly stated that due to the motion of the magnet an emf and hence a current was induced in the coil. Few went on to explain why the indicator went off again.
>
> **(ii)** This question was challenging for many candidates. Few candidates identified that the current in the indicator was higher because of the faster movement of the door and even fewer correctly stated that the door or the magnet was moving for a shorter length of time.
>
> **(b)** Stronger candidates referred to the protection against electric shock or overheating or to the fact that (unlike fuses) circuit breakers could be easily re-set. Some answers were too vague and did not refer to protection or suggested that the circuit breaker made it easy to switch the lawnmower on and off.
