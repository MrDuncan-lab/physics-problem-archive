# Question 4 (6 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2019 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 3 · вопрос 4  
Источник: [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_qp_43.pdf#page=6|условие, стр. 6]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_ms_43.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_w19_er.pdf#page=36|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объясняют форму и объём твёрдых тел, жидкостей и газов расположением молекул и межмолекулярными силами.

Solids have a fixed shape. Liquids adapt to the shape of their container. Gases fill their container.

Explain in terms of forces between molecules and arrangement of molecules, why solids, liquids and gases have these properties.

Solids  \_\_\_\_\_\_

Liquids  \_\_\_\_\_\_

Gases  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[6]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> |  | Solids – molecules in lattice arrangement | B1 |
> |  | solids – strong forces between molecules | B1 |
> |  | liquids – molecules not fixed in place OR molecules have an irregular arrangement OR molecules (slightly) further apart (on<br>average) than in solids OR spaces between the molecules | B1 |
> |  | liquids – (average) forces too weak to keep molecules in a definite pattern OR forces just enough to hold molecules in the<br>bulk of the liquid | B1 |
> |  | gases – molecules far apart | B1 |
> |  | gases – weak / no forces between molecules (except during collisions) | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Candidates who noted carefully that the question asked for explanations in terms of arrangement of molecules and the forces between them answered this question well. The most common omissions were a lack of reference to the lattice arrangement of solid molecules and to the separation of gas molecules. Weaker answers made no reference to the forces between molecules.
