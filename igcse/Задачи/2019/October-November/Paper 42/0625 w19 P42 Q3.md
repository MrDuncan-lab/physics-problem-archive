# Question 3 (6 marks)

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объясняют давление газа изменением импульса молекул и рассчитывают давление перед изотермическим сжатием.

Fig. 3.1 shows a gas contained in a cylinder enclosed by a piston.

![[0625-w19-42-p5-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 3.1**

**(a)** Describe, in terms of momentum of the molecules, how a pressure is exerted on the walls of the cylinder. **[3]**

**(b)** The piston is pushed into the cylinder. The volume decreases from 820 cm$^{3}$to 330 cm$^{3}$. The pressure gauge measures the pressure after compression as 20 000 Pa. The temperature remains constant.

Calculate the value of the pressure before the gas was compressed.

pressure =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** (p$_{1}$ =) 8000 Pa

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | <u>they / molecules collide with walls</u> | B1 |
> |  | <u>change of momentum causes force (to be exerted on walls)</u> | B1 |
> |  | pressure = force / area (so pressure is exerted on walls) | B1 |
> | (b) | pV = constant or p$_{1}$ V$_{1}$ = p$_{2}$V$_{2.}$in any form | C1 |
> |  | p$_{1}$ × 820 = 20 000 × 330 OR<br>(p$_{1}$ =) 20 000 × 330 / 820 | C1 |
> |  | (p$_{1}$ =) 8000 Pa | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** There were a number of clear and accurate responses, but the vast majority lacked precision and detail. Most candidates only scored partial credit for this question. Many answers were too vague in terms of particles colliding and did not state that they were colliding with the walls. Momentum change was often mentioned, but not linked to force.
>
> **(b)** Of those who knew the correct equation, most gained full credit. Some weaker candidates attempted to use the equation *p* = *F / A*. Others made an incorrect transformation of the equation or attempted a cross multiplication that was usually incorrect.
