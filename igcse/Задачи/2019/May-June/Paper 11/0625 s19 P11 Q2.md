> Выбрать формулу средней скорости бегуньи за всю дистанцию.

A long-distance runner wishes to calculate her average speed for a race.

Which calculation should she use?

**A** average speed = $\dfrac{\text{total} \text{distance}}{\text{total} \text{time}}$  
**B** average speed = total distance × total time  
**C** average speed = $\dfrac{\text{total} \text{time}}{\text{total} \text{distance}}$  
**D** average speed = total distance + total time

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**
