# Question 3 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2019 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 1 · вопрос 3  
Источник: [[_pdf/2019/0625_s19_qp_11.pdf#page=3|условие, стр. 3]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_s19_ms_11.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_s19_er.pdf#page=1|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

Темы: [[1.2 Motion]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнить скорость падающего шарика и время прохождения равных участков пути.

A heavy metal ball falls vertically downwards through air past four equally spaced levels J, K, L and M.

![[0625-s19-11-p3-f1.png]]

The times taken to fall from one level to the next are measured.

Where is the speed of the ball greatest and which time is shortest?

|  | speed is greatest between | time is shortest between |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | J and K | J and K |
| **B** | J and K | L and M |
| **C** | L and M | J and K |
| **D** | L and M | L and M |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Most candidates recognised that the falling ball would have its greatest speed in the lowest section of its flight. However, not all of these candidates could relate this to the fact that this section would also be covered in the shortest time.
