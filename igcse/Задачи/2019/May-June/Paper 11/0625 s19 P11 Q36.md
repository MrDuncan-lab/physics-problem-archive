# Question 36 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2019 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 1 · вопрос 36  
Источник: [[_pdf/2019/0625_s19_qp_11.pdf#page=17|условие, стр. 17]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_s19_ms_11.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_s19_er.pdf#page=1|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 s19 P11]]  
Темы: [[4.5.1 Electromagnetic induction]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить возможную величину и знак индуцированного напряжения при изменении направления и скорости движения магнита.

A bar magnet is held near a coil of wire. The coil is connected to a sensitive voltmeter.

![[0625-s19-11-p17-f1.svg]]

The N-pole of the magnet is moved quickly towards the coil. The voltmeter shows a reading of +10 mV.

The N-pole of the magnet is then moved slowly away from the same end of the coil. The reading on the voltmeter is observed.

Which voltmeter reading is possible?

**A** –15 mV  
**B** –5 mV  
**C** 0 mV  
**D** +5 mV

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Electromagnetic induction is conceptually difficult, and although most candidates rejected option **A**, there was clearly a lot of guessing when choosing between the other responses, with a small majority incorrectly believing that an e.m.f. is not induced when the magnet is moved away from the coil.
