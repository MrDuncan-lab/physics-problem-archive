# Question 23 (1 mark)

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить одновременность прекращения разных видов солнечного излучения при затмении.

An eclipse of the Sun happens when the Moon comes between the Earth and the Sun.

Which statement is correct?

**A** Infra-red radiation from the Sun disappears before visible light and ultra-violet radiation.  
**B** Ultra-violet radiation from the Sun disappears before visible light and infra-red radiation.  
**C** Visible light from the Sun disappears before ultra-violet radiation and infra-red radiation.  
**D** Infra-red radiation, ultra-violet radiation and visible light from the Sun all disappear at the

same moment.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Only the strongest candidates answered this question correctly. **The statistics suggest that candidates** **did not link this with the fact that all electromagnetic radiation travels at the same speed in a vacuum** **and resorted to guesswork.**
