> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбрать формулу расчёта жёсткости пружины по нагрузке и удлинению.

An experiment is carried out to determine the spring constant for a spring that obeys Hooke’s law.

A load is hung from the spring and the extension of the spring is measured.

Which calculation is used to calculate the spring constant?

**A** $\dfrac{\text{extension}}{\text{mass} \text{of} \text{the} \text{load}}$  
**B** $\dfrac{\text{extension}}{\text{weight} \text{of} \text{the} \text{load}}$  
**C** $\dfrac{\text{mas} s \text{of} \text{the} \text{load}}{\text{extension}}$  
**D** $\dfrac{\text{weight} \text{of} \text{the} \text{load}}{\text{extension}}$

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
