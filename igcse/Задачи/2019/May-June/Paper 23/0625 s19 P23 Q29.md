# Question 29 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2019 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 3 · вопрос 29  
Источник: [[_pdf/2019/0625_s19_qp_23.pdf#page=12|условие, стр. 12]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_s19_ms_23.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_s19_er.pdf#page=13|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 s19 P23]]  
Темы: [[4.2.4 Resistance]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбрать размеры двух проволок одинакового материала, обеспечивающие одинаковое сопротивление.

Two wires X and Y are made from the same metal and have the same resistance.

Which row identifies a possible pair of values for X and for Y?

|  | length of X / cm | diameter of X / mm | length of Y / cm | diameter of Y / mm |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | 50 | 0.40 | 200 | 0.10 |
| **B** | 50 | 0.40 | 200 | 0.20 |
| **C** | 50 | 0.40 | 200 | 0.80 |
| **D** | 50 | 0.40 | 200 | 1.60 |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Only the strongest candidates answered this question correctly. The vast majority chose either option **A** or option **D**. In all responses the length was quadrupled, this would have the effect of requiring the cross sectional area to be increased by a factor of 4, thus the diameter be increased by a factor of 2. In **A** the diameter is reduced, thereby further increasing the resistance of the wire by a factor of 16. In **D** the diameter is increased but by a factor of 4, thus the area is increased by a factor of 16.
