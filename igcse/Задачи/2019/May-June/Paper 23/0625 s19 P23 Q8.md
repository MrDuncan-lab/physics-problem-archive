# Question 8 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2019 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 3 · вопрос 8  
Источник: [[_pdf/2019/0625_s19_qp_23.pdf#page=5|условие, стр. 5]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_s19_ms_23.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_s19_er.pdf#page=13|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 s19 P23]]  
Темы: [[1.5.2 Turning effect of forces]], [[1.5.1 Effects of forces]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить натяжение и положение второй нити, поддерживающей балку в равновесии.

A beam of weight 6.0 N is suspended from two strings P and Q.

String P is 30 cm from the left-hand end of the beam, as shown. String Q is not shown.

![[0625-s19-23-p5-f1.svg]]

The tension in string P is 2.0 N.

What is the tension in string Q and where is it attached so that the beam is in equilibrium?

**A** 4.0 N at 10.0 cm from the left-hand end  
**B** 4.0 N at 15.0 cm from the left-hand end  
**C** 6.0 N at 10.0 cm from the left-hand end  
**D** 8.0 N at 7.5 cm from the left-hand end

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question showed both common misunderstanding of equilibrium and of the use of the principle of moments. A number of candidates failed to recognise that the total upward force must equal the total downward force. Amongst those who did recognise this, a significant number failed to complete the moments problem successfully.
