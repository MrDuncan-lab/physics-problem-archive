> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать ток батареи в цепи с комбинацией одинаковых резисторов.

A 12 V battery is connected to a combination of 2.0 Ω resistors as shown.

![[0625-s19-23-p13-f2.svg]]

What is the current in the battery?

**A** 1.5 A  
**B** 2.0 A  
**C** 4.0 A  
**D** 6.0 A

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Only the strongest candidates answered this question correctly. Many candidates saw the variable resistor and immediately assumed that this was the correct response without considering that the potential difference across it was equal to the potential difference across the cell at all times and therefore did not change.
