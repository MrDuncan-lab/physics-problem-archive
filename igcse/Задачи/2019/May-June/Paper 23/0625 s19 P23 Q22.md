A beam of light passes through a vacuum and then enters a liquid. The diagram shows the path it

takes.

![[0625-s19-23-p9-f1.svg]]

The light travels through the vacuum at a speed of 3.0 × 10$^{8}$ m / s.

**B** 2.0 × 10$^{8}$ m / s  
**C** 4.6 × 10$^{8}$ m / s  
**D** 4.8 × 10$^{8}$ m / s
