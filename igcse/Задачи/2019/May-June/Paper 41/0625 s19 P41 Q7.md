# Question 7 (10 marks)

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Анализируют цепь с термистором, рассчитывают общее сопротивление и ток, объясняют влияние температуры.

Fig. 7.1 shows a circuit diagram that includes component X.

![[0625-s19-41-p14-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 7.1**

**(a)** State the name of component X. **[1]**

**(b)** The electromotive force (e.m.f.) of the battery is *E*. The switch is closed.

The potential difference (p.d.) across the 30 Ω resistor is *V*$_{30}$. The p.d. across the 20 Ω resistor is *V*$_{20}$. The p.d. across component X is *V*$_{\text{X}}$.

State an equation that relates *V*$_{\text{X}}$ to:

**(i)** *V*$_{30}$ **[1]**

**(ii)** *E* and *V*$_{20}$. **[1]**

**(c)** The e.m.f. of the battery is 6.0 V and the resistance of component X is 15 Ω.

Calculate:

**(i)** the total resistance of the circuit

resistance =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

**(ii)** the ammeter reading.

reading =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(d)** The temperature of component X increases.

State and explain what happens to the ammeter reading. **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(c)(i)** 30 Ω
>
> **(c)(ii)** 0.20 A

> | (c)(i) | 1/*R*$_{1}$ + 1/*R*$_{2}$ = 1/*R*$_{\text{tot}}$ **OR** (*R*$_{\text{tot}}$ =) *R*$_{1}$ *R*$_{2}$ / (*R*$_{1}$ + *R*$_{2}$) **OR** 1/15 + 1/30 = 1/*R*$_{\text{tot}}$<br>**OR** (15 × 30) / (15 + 30) | C1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Component X was correctly identified very commonly but a few candidates gave the answer “variable resistor”.
>
> **(b)** In these two parts, equations were asked for and so answers that were not equations were unlikely to gain any credit. A frequent answer to **(ii)** was the incorrect *E* = *V*X + *V*30 + *V*20.
>
> **(c)** There were many good answers to these two parts. In **(i)**, almost all candidates used the equation for resistors in parallel correctly and only occasionally was the final reciprocal ignored.
>
> **(d)** Most candidates knew what was happening but some stated that the resistance of the thermistor increased as the temperature increased. Where full credit was not awarded, usually the effect on the resistance of the thermistor was stated, but how this affected the resistance of the complete circuit and hence the current and the ammeter reading was not.
