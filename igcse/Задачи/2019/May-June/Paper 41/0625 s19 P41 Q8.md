> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объясняют работу генератора переменного тока, положение максимального напряжения и увеличение усилия при подключении лампы.

A student turns the handle of an alternating current (a.c.) generator and the coil rotates.

Fig. 8.1 represents the structure of the a.c. generator.

![[0625-s19-41-p16-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 8.1**

**(a)** There is an alternating voltage output between the two terminals.

**(i)** Explain why rotating the coil produces an output voltage. **[3]**

**(ii)** State the position of the rotating coil when the alternating output voltage is at a maximum value and explain why the maximum output occurs at this position. **[2]**

**(b)** A lamp and an open switch are connected in series to the output terminals of the a.c. generator.

The switch is closed and the lamp lights up. The student has to apply a greater force on the handle.

Explain why a greater force is needed to keep the lamp lit. **[3]**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** There were many good answers here with full credit often awarded.
>
> **(ii)** This was less well answered and some answers described the position of the coil in ways that were not clear. Few candidates referred to the rate of cutting magnetic flux and simply stated that more flux was cut where the output voltage is a maximum.
