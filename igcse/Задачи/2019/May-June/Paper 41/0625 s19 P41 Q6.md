# Question 6 (9 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2019 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 1 · вопрос 6  
Источник: [[_pdf/2019/0625_s19_qp_41.pdf#page=12|условие, стр. 12–13]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_s19_ms_41.pdf#page=9|схема оценивания, стр. 9]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_s19_er.pdf#page=27|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

Темы: [[3.2.2 Refraction of light]], [[3.1 General properties of waves]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объясняют преломление через волновые фронты, рассчитывают угол преломления и длину световой волны в веществе.

Green light of frequency 5.7 × 10$^{14}$ Hz is travelling in air at a speed of 3.0 × 10$^{8}$ m / s. The light is incident on the surface of a transparent solid.

Fig. 6.1 shows the wavefronts and the direction of travel of the light in the air.

![[0625-s19-41-p12-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 6.1**

The light travels more slowly in the transparent solid.

**(a)** Explain, in terms of the wavefronts, why the light changes direction as it enters the solid. You may draw on Fig. 6.1 as part of your answer. **[3]**

**(b)** The refractive index of the transparent solid is 1.3.

**(i)** The light is incident on the surface of the solid at an angle of incidence of 67°.

Calculate the angle of refraction of the light in the solid.

angle of refraction =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(ii)** Determine the wavelength of the green light in the transparent solid.

wavelength =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[4]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)(i)** 45°
>
> **(b)(ii)** 4.0 × 10$^{\text{–7}}$ m
>
> **(b)(ii)** 4.0 × 10$^{\text{–7}}$ m
