# Question 28 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2019 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 3 · вопрос 28  
Источник: [[_pdf/2019/0625_s19_qp_13.pdf#page=14|условие, стр. 14]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_s19_ms_13.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_s19_er.pdf#page=6|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить материал сердечника электромагнита и влияние увеличения тока на притяжение гвоздей.

A metal bar is placed inside a current-carrying coil, as shown in diagram 1.

There is a small current in the coil. The bar holds a few nails, as shown in diagram 2.

![[0625-s19-13-p14-f1.svg]]

When there is no current in the coil, the nails drop off.

Which row is correct?

|  | metal from which the bar is made | effect of a larger current in the coil |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | soft iron | it makes no difference |
| **B** | soft iron | the bar holds more nails |
| **C** | steel | it makes no difference |
| **D** | steel | the bar holds more nails |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Many candidates showed a good understanding of the meaning of electromotive force, but a considerable number thought that e.m.f. was the rate of energy transfer per unit charge, rather than just the energy per unit charge.
