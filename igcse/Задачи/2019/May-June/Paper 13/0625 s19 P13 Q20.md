**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2019 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 3 · вопрос 20  
Источник: [[_pdf/2019/0625_s19_qp_13.pdf#page=11|условие, стр. 11]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_s19_ms_13.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_s19_er.pdf#page=6|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить плотность и направление движения воздуха, нагретого горячим чайником.

The metal surface of a kettle is hot.

What happens to the cool air outside the kettle when it comes into contact with the hot kettle?

**A** The density of the air decreases and the air falls.  
**B** The density of the air decreases and the air rises.  
**C** The density of the air increases and the air falls.  
**D** The density of the air increases and the air rises.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question was challenging for many candidates. This was possibly because it asked for the least diffraction, whereas candidates tend to think in terms of the most diffraction.
