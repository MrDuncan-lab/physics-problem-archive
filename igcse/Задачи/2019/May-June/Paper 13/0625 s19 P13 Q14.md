> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбрать верное утверждение о форме и сжимаемости твёрдого тела.

Which statement is correct?

**A** A solid can flow.  
**B** A solid can be compressed easily.  
**C** A solid has a fixed shape.  
**D** A solid takes the shape of its container.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This was answered well by almost all candidates.
>
> Candidates showed a good understanding of the collision process between gas molecules and larger particles, such as smoke particles.
