> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить положения выключателей, при которых звонок работает, а лампа не горит.

The diagram shows a circuit including a lamp, an electric bell and three switches S$_{1}$, S$_{2}$ and S$_{3}$.

The lamp and bell are not faulty.

![[0625-s19-13-p16-f1.svg]]

The bell is ringing but the lamp is not lit.

Which switches are closed?

**A** S$_{1}$ only  
**B** S$_{1}$ and S$_{2}$ only  
**C** S$_{1}$ and S$_{3}$ only  
**D** S$_{1}$, S$_{2}$ and S$_{3}$

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Most candidates correctly identified the logic gate as an and-gate.
