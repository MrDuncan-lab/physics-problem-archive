> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить направление колебаний поплавка при распространении поперечной волны.

The diagram shows a cork with a weight attached so that the cork floats upright in water.

![[0625-s19-13-p12-f1.svg]]

Transverse waves travel across the water from X to Y.

In which direction do the waves make the cork move?

**A** → ← right and left  
**B** ↑↓ up and down  
**C** → only to the right  
**D** ← only to the left

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Only the strongest candidates answered this question correctly. Many other candidates thought that the cork would move back and forth in the horizontal plane.
