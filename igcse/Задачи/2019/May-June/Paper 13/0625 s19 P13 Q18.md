> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить характер изменения температуры чистого вещества во время плавления.

A student investigates the melting point of a pure substance. She heats the substance slowly and takes readings of its temperature as the substance starts to melt and when it finishes melting.

Which statement is correct?

**A** The temperature decreases slightly as the substance melts.  
**B** The temperature fluctuates as the substance melts.  
**C** The temperature increases as the substance melts.  
**D** The temperature stays the same as the substance melts.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Few candidates answered this question correctly and many thought that the temperature would increase as the substance melts.
