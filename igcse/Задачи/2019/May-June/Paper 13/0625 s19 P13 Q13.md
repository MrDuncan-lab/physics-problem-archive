> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить изменение, увеличивающее давление жидкости на камень.

The diagram shows a stone suspended on a string under the surface of a liquid. The stone

experiences a pressure caused by the liquid.

![[0625-s19-13-p8-f2.png]]

What would increase the pressure on the stone?

**A** decreasing the surface area of the stone  
**B** increasing the mass of the stone  
**C** lowering the stone deeper into the liquid  
**D** using a liquid with a lower density

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question was well answered and most candidates understood that the pressure under the surface of a liquid increases with increasing depth.
