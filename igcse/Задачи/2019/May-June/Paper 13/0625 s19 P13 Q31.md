> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить способ передачи химической энергии батареи и её конечное превращение в резисторе.

A battery stores chemical potential energy. The battery is connected to a resistor.

As the battery runs down, what happens to its chemical energy?

|  | The energy is transferred by | and ends up as |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | electrical working | internal energy in the resistor |
| **B** | electrical working | potential energy in the resistor |
| **C** | mechanical working | internal energy in the resistor |
| **D** | mechanical working | potential energy in the resistor |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Although some candidates recognised that the energy from the cell was transferred to the resistor as internal energy by electrical working, there were many who had little idea of the process and the result.
>
> Many candidates saw a variable resistor and thought that this must be the answer without investigating how the p.d. would change or not change.
