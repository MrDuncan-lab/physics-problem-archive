# Question 33 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2019 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 3 · вопрос 33  
Источник: [[_pdf/2019/0625_s19_qp_13.pdf#page=17|условие, стр. 17]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_s19_ms_13.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_s19_er.pdf#page=6|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнить работу ламп при последовательном и параллельном соединении.

A student is designing a lighting circuit for a dolls’ house. He sets up two different circuits.

Each circuit contains a 12 V power supply and three identical lamps.

Each lamp is designed to operate at normal brightness when connected individually to a 12 V supply.

![[0625-s19-13-p17-f1.svg]]

Which statement is correct?

**A** In circuit 1, each of the lamps is at normal brightness.  
**B** In circuit 1, if one lamp fails, the other lamps remain lit.  
**C** In circuit 2, if one lamp fails, the other lamps remain lit.  
**D** In circuit 2, the current from the power supply is less than in circuit 1.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
