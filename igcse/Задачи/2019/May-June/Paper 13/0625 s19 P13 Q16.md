> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Проверить утверждения о фиксированных точках и построении шкалы Цельсия.

Here are three statements about a liquid-in-glass thermometer with a Celsius scale.

1 The lower fixed point is the temperature at which pure water freezes.

2 The upper fixed point is the temperature at which pure water boils.

3 A scale is made by dividing the distance between the fixed points into equal

divisions.

Which statements are correct?

**A** 1 and 2 only  
**B** 2 and 3 only  
**C** 1 and 3 only  
**D** 1, 2 and 3

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This was a challenging calculation, and although the majority of candidates had some idea how to carry it out, a common error was to assume that all the ice had melted, despite being told in the question that 18 g remained.
