Источник: [[_pdf/2019/0625_s19_qp_13.pdf#page=7|условие, стр. 7]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_s19_ms_13.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_s19_er.pdf#page=6|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Связать работу при подъёме тела с изменением его энергии.

A body is lifted against gravity.

Which equation shows the work done on the body?

**A** work done = change in distance moved by the body  
**B** work done = change in energy of the body  
**C** work done = change in force on the body  
**D** work done = change in power of the body

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Candidates were familiar with the idea that work is force × distance. They were less familiar with the idea that when work is done on an object, the object gains energy of some form. This lack of familiarity meant candidates searched for a response that included the idea that the object moved or the force changed.
