# Question 11 (5 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2019 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 2 · вопрос 11  
Источник: [[_pdf/2019/0625_s19_qp_42.pdf#page=15|условие, стр. 15]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_s19_ms_42.pdf#page=10|схема оценивания, стр. 10]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_s19_er.pdf#page=30|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 s19 P42]]  
Темы: [[5.2.3 Radioactive decay]], [[5.2.4 Half-life]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Записывают уравнение альфа-распада радона и определяют возраст кости по содержанию углерода-14.

**(a)** A radon-222 nucleus contains 86 protons and 136 neutrons. It decays by emitting an α-particle and becomes a nucleus of an isotope of polonium. The symbol for radon is Rn and the symbol for polonium is Po.

Write down the nuclide equation for this decay. **[3]**

**(b)** Carbon-14 is radioactive with a half-life of 5700 years. An animal bone is dug up in an archaeological excavation. The quantity of carbon-14 in the bone is 25% of what it was when the bone was buried.

Calculate the time that has elapsed since it was buried.

time =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ years **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** 11 000 (years)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | $^{222}_{86}$Rn on L side of equation | B1 |
> |  | $^{218}_{84}$Po on R side of equation | B1 |
> |  | $^{4}_{2}$α on R side of equation | B1 |
> | (b) | mention of 2 half-lives OR mention or use of two halvings of 100%<br>NOT 5700 ÷ 2 OR 14 ÷ 2 | C1 |
> |  | 11 000 (years) | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** This was well answered by stronger candidates who gained full credit. Common errors by weaker candidates were to use neutron numbers in place of nucleon numbers or to get the numbers the wrong way up.
>
> **(b)** Most candidates realised that there were 2 half-lives and calculated the correct final answer.
