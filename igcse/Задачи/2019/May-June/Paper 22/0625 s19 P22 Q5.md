A metal has a density of 8.0 g / cm$^{3}$. A solid cube of mass 1.0 kg is made from this metal.

How long is each side of the cube?

**A** 0.50 cm  
**B** 2.0 cm  
**C** 5.0 cm  
**D** 42 cm

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
