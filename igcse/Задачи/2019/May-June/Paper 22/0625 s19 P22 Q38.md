> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбрать экспериментальное свидетельство ядерного строения атома.

Which observation provides evidence for the nuclear atom?

**A** attraction of opposite charges  
**B** emission of γ-rays during the decay of a radioactive nuclide  
**C** scattering of α-particles by thin metal foils  
**D** scattering of γ-rays by a thin metal foil

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Many candidates recognised that α-particle scattering led to the adoption of the nuclear model of the atom. However, other candidates incorrectly thought it was the emission of γ-rays during radioactive decay was the evidence.
