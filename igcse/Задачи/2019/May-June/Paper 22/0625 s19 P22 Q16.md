> Рассчитать удельную теплоту плавления льда из результатов смешивания воды и льда.

100 g of water at 25 °C is poured into an insulated cup. 50 g of ice at 0 °C is added to the water.

The water is stirred until the temperature of the water has fallen to 0 °C.

18 g of ice remains unmelted.

The specific heat capacity of water is 4.2 J / g °C.

Which value does this experiment give for the specific latent heat of fusion of ice?

**A** 210 J / g  
**B** 330 J / g  
**C** 580 J / g  
**D** 770 J / g

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
