# Question 32 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2019 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 2 · вопрос 32  
Источник: [[_pdf/2019/0625_s19_qp_22.pdf#page=12|условие, стр. 12]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_s19_ms_22.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_s19_er.pdf#page=11|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

Темы: [[4.3.3 Action and use of circuit components]], [[4.3.1 Circuit diagrams and circuit components]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбрать расположение термистора и резистора в делителе с возрастающим при нагревании выходным напряжением.

Components X and Y can be inserted to complete the circuit below. The completed circuit is a

potential divider in which the potential difference across component Y increases when the temperature increases.

![[0625-s19-22-p12-f2.svg]]

Which row shows the components X and Y?

|  | X | Y |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | light-dependent resistor | resistor |
| **B** | resistor | light-dependent resistor |
| **C** | resistor | thermistor |
| **D** | thermistor | resistor |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Almost all candidates recognised that a thermistor was required in order for the potential difference across Y to change. However, having established this important fact, almost **as many candidates chose the** **incorrect option** C**, as the correct answer,** D.
