> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнить металлы по плотности, рассчитать объём блока и описать измерение объёма тела неправильной формы.

Fig. 1.1 shows three metal blocks. Each block has the same mass.

![[0625-s19-33-p2-f1.png]]

**Fig. 1.1**

The volumes of the blocks are different.

Each block is made of a different metal. The table gives the density of each metal.

| **name of metal** | **density (g / cm**$^{3}$) |
|---|---|
| aluminium | 2.83 |
| iron | 6.95 |
| lead | 11.3 |

**(a)** Use the data from the table to identify the metal used to make each block.

A  \_\_\_\_\_\_

B  \_\_\_\_\_\_

C  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(b)** Another metal block is made of brass. Its mass is 200 g.

The density of brass is 8.4 g / cm$^{3}$.

Calculate the volume of the brass block.

volume =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm$^{3}$ **[3]**

**(c)** Describe a method for determining the volume of a small, dense, irregularly-shaped object.

You may draw a labelled diagram. **[4]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** 24 (cm$^{3}$)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | (A =) lead, (B=) iron, (C=) aluminium | B1 |
> | (b) | density = mass ÷ volume in any form (V =) M/d | C1 |
> |  | 200 ÷ 8.4 | C1 |
> |  | 24 (cm$^{3}$) | A1 |
> | (c) | add water to measuring cylinder/note the volume of water added | B1 |
> |  | lower/immerse metal object into water | B1 |
> |  | note new volume of water owtte | B1 |
> |  | subtract new volume from initial volume/determine difference in volumes | B1 |
