# Question 10 (6 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2019 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 3 · вопрос 10  
Источник: [[_pdf/2019/0625_s19_qp_33.pdf#page=14|условие, стр. 14]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_s19_ms_33.pdf#page=8|схема оценивания, стр. 8]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_s19_er.pdf#page=23|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 s19 P33]]  
Темы: [[4.4 Electrical safety]], [[4.5.6 The transformer]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объясняют защитное действие заземления и предохранителя, рассчитывают число витков вторичной обмотки трансформатора.

Fig. 10.1 shows a desktop computer. The computer is connected to a mains supply by a plug containing a fuse.

![[0625-s19-33-p14-f1.png]]

**Fig. 10.1**

**(a)** The computer has a metal case. A fault occurs and a live wire touches the metal case.

Explain how an earth wire and the fuse in the plug protect the user. **[3]**

**(b)** The computer contains a transformer. The input voltage to the transformer is 240 V and the output voltage is 12.0 V. The input coil of the transformer has 3000 turns.

Calculate the number of turns on the output coil.

number of turns =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** 150 (turns)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | any 3 from:<br>earth wire is connected to metal case<br>earth wire has low resistance<br>large current in earth wire<br>fuse in live wire<br>fuse (heats up and) melts<br>this disconnects case/computer/circuit from supply ( and so protects user) | B3 |
> | (b) | (Vp / Vs ) = (Np / Ns ) in any form | C1 |
> |  | 240 / 12 = 3000 / Ns OR Ns = 3000 × (12/240) OR Ns = 3000 / 20 | C1 |
> |  | 150 (turns) | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Candidates did not seem to fully understand the purpose of the earth wire in protecting the user. A greater understanding regarding the role of the fuse was seen. Many candidates struggled to express themselves adequately.
>
> **(b)** The majority of candidates gave the correct answer, often without any working. The most common mistakes were power of 10 errors in the calculation.
