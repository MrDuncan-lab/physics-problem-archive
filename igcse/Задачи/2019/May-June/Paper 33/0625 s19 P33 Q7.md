# Question 7 (5 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2019 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 3 · вопрос 7  
Источник: [[_pdf/2019/0625_s19_qp_33.pdf#page=10|условие, стр. 10–11]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_s19_ms_33.pdf#page=7|схема оценивания, стр. 7]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_s19_er.pdf#page=23|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объясняют полное внутреннее отражение, называют цвета спектра и применение электромагнитного излучения.

Fig. 7.1 shows a ray of red light being reflected at the flat surface of a glass block.

![[0625-s19-33-p10-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 7.1**

**(a)** Explain why the ray of red light is totally internally reflected by the surface of the glass block. **[1]**

**(b)** A ray of white light passes through a prism and produces a spectrum of colours on a screen, as shown in Fig. 7.2.

![[0625-s19-33-p10-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 7.2**

**(i)** State the name of the process of separating white light into a spectrum. **[1]**

**(ii)** Write the names of the seven colours that appear on the screen between A and B.

colour at A  \_\_\_\_\_\_

colour at B  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(c)** Visible light is one part of the electromagnetic spectrum.

State the name of **one** other part of the electromagnetic spectrum and describe a use of this type of radiation.

name of radiation  \_\_\_\_\_\_

use of radiation  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(c)** correct use of named part of em spectrum

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | Any one from:<br>angle of incidence is greater than the critical angle<br>light is travelling from a(n optically) more dense medium to(wards an optically) less dense medium (at a large angle) | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | dispersion | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | From A to B: red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo, violet | B1 |
> | (c) | correct name for any part of em spectrum other than visible light | M1 |
> |  | correct use of named part of em spectrum | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Very few candidates understood total internal reflection. The term “critical angle” was seldom seen. Most answers vaguely stated that the glass block reflects the light.
>
> **(b) (i)** The correct answer of dispersion was seen in stronger responses. There were a notable number of candidates who did not answer this question.
>
> **(ii)** Only the strongest candidates answered this question correctly. Blue was often placed between red and orange.
>
> **(c)** This was well answered by the majority of candidates. The only frequently seen error was giving MRI scanners as a use for X-rays.
