# Question 2 (8 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2019 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 3 · вопрос 2

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> По графику пути находят расстояние, время, скорость пешехода и продолжительность остановки.

Fig. 2.1 shows a distance-time graph for a man walking from home to a café. At the café the man stops for a drink. On the return journey from the café, the man stops to rest.

![[0625-s19-33-p4-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 2.1**

**(a)** Using Fig. 2.1, determine

**(i)** the distance from the man’s home to the café.

distance =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ km **[1]**

**(ii)** the time taken to walk to the café.

time =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ hours **[1]**

**(iii)** the speed, in km / hour, of the man as he walks to the café.

speed =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ km / hour **[3]**

**(b)** On the return journey from the café, the man stopped to rest.

**(i)** The man left home at 13:00.

Determine the time when the man began his rest.

time when rest began  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(ii)** For how long did the man rest on the return journey? State the time in minutes.

time =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ minutes **[1]**

**(iii)** Describe, in words, how the graph in Fig. 2.1 shows that the man travelled at a slower speed on the return journey after resting. **[1]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)(iii)** 6.7 (km/h) accept 6.67 (km/h)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | 10 (km) | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | 1.5 (hours) | B1 |
> | (a)(iii) | speed = distance ÷ time in any form | C1 |
> |  | 10 ÷ 1.5 | C1 |
> |  | 6.7 (km/h) accept 6.67 (km/h) | A1 |
> | (b)(i) | 4:30 (pm) **OR** 16:30 | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | 30 (minutes) | B1 |
> | (b)(iii) | smaller gradient **OR** less steep slope owtte | B1 |
