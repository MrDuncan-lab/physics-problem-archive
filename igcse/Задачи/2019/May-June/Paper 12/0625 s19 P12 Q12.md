A student is testing four different electric motors. He measures the time it takes for a motor to lift

either a heavy load or a light load through a height of 1 metre.

![[0625-s19-12-p8-f1.svg]]

![[0625-s19-12-p8-f2.svg]]

The table shows his results.

Which motor produces the most power?

|  | load | time taken / s |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | heavy | 12 |
| **B** | heavy | 16 |
| **C** | light | 12 |
| **D** | light | 16 |
