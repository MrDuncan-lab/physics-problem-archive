# Question 26 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2019 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 26  
Источник: [[_pdf/2019/0625_s19_qp_12.pdf#page=14|условие, стр. 14]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_s19_ms_12.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_s19_er.pdf#page=4|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 s19 P12]]  
Темы: [[3.4 Sound]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать глубину нахождения рыб по времени возвращения звукового сигнала.

A pulse of sound is produced at the bottom of a boat. The sound travels through the water and is

reflected from a shoal of fish. The sound reaches the boat again after 1.2 s. The speed of sound in the water is 1500 m / s.

![[0625-s19-12-p14-f1.svg]]

How far below the bottom of the boat is the shoal of fish?

**A** 450 m  
**B** 900 m  
**C** 1800 m  
**D** 3600 m

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Only the strongest candidates recognised that when the echo sounds, the pulse of sound has to travel down to the fish and then travel back up to the sensor on the boat.
