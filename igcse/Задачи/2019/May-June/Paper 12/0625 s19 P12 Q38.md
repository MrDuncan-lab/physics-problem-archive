> Определить знаки зарядов атомного ядра и окружающих его электронов.

A simple model of the atom consists of small particles orbiting a central nucleus.

Which row is correct?

|  | charge on nucleus | charge on orbiting particles |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | negative | negative |
| **B** | negative | positive |
| **C** | positive | negative |
| **D** | positive | positive |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
