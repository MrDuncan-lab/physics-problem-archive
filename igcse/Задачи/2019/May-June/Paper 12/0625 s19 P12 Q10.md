Источник: [[_pdf/2019/0625_s19_qp_12.pdf#page=7|условие, стр. 7]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_s19_ms_12.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_s19_er.pdf#page=4|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбрать верное описание машины с высоким коэффициентом полезного действия.

A machine is very efficient.

What does this mean?

**A** It produces a large amount of power.  
**B** It uses very little energy.  
**C** It wastes very little energy.  
**D** It works very quickly.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Only stronger candidates understood the term “efficiency” and although the correct response was given by a number of candidates, the other three options were equally attractive suggesting that this was not an area that candidates were confident in.
