> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбрать свойства теплоаккумулятора для запасания наибольшей тепловой энергии.

A night storage heater contains a large block of material that is heated electrically during the

night. During the day the block cools down, releasing thermal energy into the room.

![[0625-s19-12-p10-f2.svg]]

Which thermal capacity and which night-time temperature increase will cause the most energy to be stored by the block?

![[0625-s19-12-p10-f3.svg]]

|  | thermal capacity of block | night-time temperature increase |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | large | large |
| **B** | large | small |
| **C** | small | large |
| **D** | small | small |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question proved challenging with many candidates clearly not understanding the meaning of the term “thermal capacity”. Although almost all candidates eliminated option **D**, the other three options were chosen by almost equal numbers of candidates, indicating that many candidates guessed.
