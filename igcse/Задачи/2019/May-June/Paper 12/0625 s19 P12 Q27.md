> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объяснить начало отсчёта времени по дыму стартового пистолета различием скоростей света и звука.

An observer stands at the finish line of a 100 m race. He wants to time the winner’s run. He starts

his stop-watch as soon as he sees the smoke from the starting gun instead of when he hears the bang.

What is the reason for doing this?

**A** Light travels much faster than sound.  
**B** There is a risk he might respond to an echo from a wall.  
**C** Humans react slower to sound than to light.  
**D** Humans react more quickly to sound than to light.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**
