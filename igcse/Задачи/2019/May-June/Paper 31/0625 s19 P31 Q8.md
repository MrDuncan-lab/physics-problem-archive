> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** Most candidates answered this question correctly. Weaker candidates chose protons instead of electrons.
>
> **(ii)** The majority of candidates gave a correct answer of ”negative” but a significant number thought
>
> there was a positive charge on the rod.
>
> **(iii)** Most candidates gained credit by explaining that like charges repel (one another), but many incorrectly stated that like poles repel (one another).
>
> **(b) (i) (ii)** The vast majority of candidates gave a correct answer of copper to **(i)** and this was usually
>
> followed up with a sensible reason in **(ii)** for using copper for an earth connection.
