# Question 2 (5 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2019 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 1 · вопрос 2  
Источник: [[_pdf/2019/0625_s19_qp_31.pdf#page=3|условие, стр. 3]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_s19_ms_31.pdf#page=4|схема оценивания, стр. 4]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_s19_er.pdf#page=16|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 s19 P31]]  
Темы: [[1.5.2 Turning effect of forces]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать силу рычага при подъёме бревна и объяснить, как уменьшить необходимое усилие.

Fig. 2.1 shows a man pushing down on a lever to lift one end of a heavy log.

![[0625-s19-31-p3-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 2.1**

**(a)** State the term used to describe the turning force exerted by the man. **[1]**

**(b)** **(i)** Fig. 2.2 shows the forces acting as the man starts to lift the heavy log.

![[0625-s19-31-p3-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 2.2**

Calculate the force *F*, exerted by the lever on the heavy log.

force *F* =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ N **[3]**

**(ii)** Describe how the man can use a smaller force to lift the heavy log. **[1]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)(i)** 1600 (N)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | <u>moment</u> | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | (sum of) clockwise moment(s) = (sum of) anticlockwise moment(s) | C1 |
> |  | 1.2 × 400 = 0.3 × F | C1 |
> |  | 1600 (N) | A1 |
> | (b)(ii) | use a longer lever **OR** pivot closer to log / force F | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** The majority of candidates answered this question correctly. Most knew “moment” as the correct term for a turning force and “torque” was also seen a few times. “Work” or “work done” was probably the most common incorrect answer, although “momentum” was also given by some candidates.
>
> **(b) (i)** This question proved challenging for many candidates. Candidates should be encouraged to practise working out calculations using the principle of moments. The majority of candidates worked out the moment of the 400 N force and then stopped. Another common error was the addition of the two distances on either side of the pivot in the figure.
>
> A significant number of candidates failed to show any working, and for many this resulted in no credit awarded for the question. Candidates should be encouraged to state the equation they are using, and then to show substitution of values into the equation.
>
> **(ii)** Most candidates answered this question correctly. However, a significant number were not precise enough in their answer. “Make the distance longer” was the most common answer but had little meaning unless the distance was specified. Some candidates thought that making the distance between the pivot and the log longer would produce the required result and a small number thought that it was necessary to apply the force nearer the pivot.
