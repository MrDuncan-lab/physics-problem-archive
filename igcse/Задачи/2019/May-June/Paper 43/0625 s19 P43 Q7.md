# Question 7 (4 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2019 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 3 · вопрос 7

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определяют угол падения и рассчитывают угол преломления по отношению скоростей света.

Fig. 7.1 shows light approaching a boundary between two materials at speed *v*. The speed of the light after crossing the boundary is 1.3*v*.

![[0625-s19-43-p13-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 7.1**

**(a)** Determine the angle of incidence.

angle of incidence =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(b)** Calculate the angle of refraction.

angle of refraction =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** (sin *r* = 1.3 × sin 40°) (r =) 57°

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | 40° | B1 |
> | (b) | n = 1.3 OR seen in calculation | C1 |
> |  | sin *i* / sin *r* = *n* in any form OR sin 40 / sin*r* = *n* sin *i* / sin *r* = 1 / *n* | C1 |
> |  | (sin *r* = 1.3 × sin 40°) (r =) 57° | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Weaker candidates just gave the value for the angle of incidence as the value for the angle shown in the diagram.
>
> **(b)** Many candidates realised that they needed to use a value of 1.3 when calculating the angle of refraction. Some candidates were correctly able to state that n = sini/sinr. However, they were often then unable to make the correct substitutions to get the correct answer. They needed to realise that the light was travelling from a more dense medium to a less dense medium in order to have the equation the correct way up.
