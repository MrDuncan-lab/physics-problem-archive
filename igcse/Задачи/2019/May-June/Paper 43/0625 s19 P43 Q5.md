# Question 5 (6 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2019 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 3 · вопрос 5

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объясняют, как конструкция термоса ограничивает теплопроводность, конвекцию и излучение, и выбирают материал пробки.

Fig. 5.1 shows a cross-section of the inside of a vacuum flask containing a cold liquid. The walls of the vacuum flask are made of glass.

![[0625-s19-43-p9-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 5.1**

**(a)** The vacuum flask is being used to keep a liquid cool on a hot day.

Explain how the labelled features of the vacuum flask keep the liquid cool by reducing thermal energy transfer. Include the names of the processes involved. **[5]**

**(b)** Suggest a suitable material for the stopper. **[1]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | <u>any mention of radiation/infra-red radiation wrt silvered surfaces</u> | B1 |
> |  | silvered surfaces are poor emitters / poor absorbers / (good) reflectors | B1 |
> |  | glass is a poor conductor OR glass reduces thermal energy / heat gain by conduction | B1 |
> |  | vacuum prevents thermal energy / heat gain by conduction OR convection | B1 |
> |  | stopper reduces thermal energy / heat gain by convection | B1 |
> | (b) | any suitable insulator e.g. cork, plastic, rubber | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Stronger answers clearly referred to each part of the vacuum flask in turn and correctly stated how each of them prevented or reduced thermal energy transfer, including the names of the processes of energy transfer. Weaker answers referred in generalised terms to reduction of conduction, convection or radiation without specifying clearly which of the labelled features reduced thermal energy transfer by this method. Stronger answers referred to radiation when stating that the silvered surfaces were good reflectors or poor absorbers. The use of correct physics terminology is important in descriptive answers to questions of this type.
>
> **(b)** The vast majority of candidates could name a suitable insulator.
