> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Дополнить параллельную цепь ламп измерительными приборами и объяснить последствия перегорания одной лампы.

Fig. 10.1 shows an incomplete circuit diagram for two identical lamps arranged in parallel. The circuit contains an ammeter and a voltmeter.

![[0625-s19-32-p14-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 10.1**

**(a)** On Fig. 10.1, complete the symbols for two lamps, an ammeter and a voltmeter positioned correctly. **[5]**

**(b)** One of the lamps breaks.

State the effect, if any, this has on the brightness of the other lamp. Explain your answer.

effect  \_\_\_\_\_\_

explanation  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Most candidates knew the circuit symbols and were able to place them correctly in the circuit.
>
> **(b)** Few candidates understood that the brightness of the remaining lamp in a parallel circuit would stay the same. Most thought that the remaining lamp would get brighter. Very few were able to explain that the current through the remaining lamp would remain the same.
