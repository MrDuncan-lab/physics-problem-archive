> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объяснить расширение нагретого воздуха в шарике и рассчитать давление на ручку насоса.

A student places a balloon filled with air next to a window, as shown in Fig. 4.1. The Sun warms the air in the balloon.

![[0625-s19-32-p6-f1.png]]

**Fig. 4.1**

**(a)** **(i)** Suggest what happens to the balloon as the air in it becomes hotter than the surroundings. **[1]**

**(ii)** Use ideas about molecules to explain your answer to **(a)(i)**. **[3]**

**(b)** The student uses a pump to inflate another balloon.

Fig. 4.2 shows the student inflating a balloon.

![[0625-s19-32-p6-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 4.2**

The student applies a force of 30 N to the pump handle. The force acts on an area of 12 cm$^{2}$.

Calculate the pressure on the pump handle. Include the unit.

pressure =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[4]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** 2.5

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | expand or increase in size/volume<br>increase in pressure<br>decrease in density | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | any 3 from:<br>density (of air) is less<br>molecules move faster/have more (kinetic) energy<br>more collisions ( per second)<br>collisions with surface OR balloon (owtte)<br>more force (in collisions)<br>molecules move (further) apart | B3 |
> | (b) | P = F/A in any form | C1 |
> |  | 30 ÷ 12 | C1 |
> |  | 2.5 | A1 |
> |  | N/cm$^{2}$ | B1 |

> **(ii)** Weaker candidates indicated that molecules would move faster. Stronger candidates went on to say
>

>
