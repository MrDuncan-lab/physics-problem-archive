# Question 6 (6 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2019 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 6  
Источник: [[_pdf/2019/0625_s19_qp_32.pdf#page=9|условие, стр. 9]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_s19_ms_32.pdf#page=6|схема оценивания, стр. 6]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_s19_er.pdf#page=20|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Применить закон отражения и описать фокус и действительное изображение собирающей линзы.

Fig. 6.1 shows a ray of light that is reflected by a mirror.

![[0625-s19-32-p9-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 6.1**

**(a)** **(i)** State the name of line X shown on Fig. 6.1. **[1]**

**(ii)** State the name of angle Y shown on Fig. 6.1. **[1]**

**(iii)** A student moves the ray of light and doubles the size of angle Y. State the effect on angle Z. **[1]**

**(b)** Fig. 6.2 shows a converging lens used to form an image I of an object O.

![[0625-s19-32-p9-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 6.2**

**(i)** State the name of the points labelled F on Fig. 6.2. **[1]**

**(ii)** Describe the nature of the image I. **[2]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | normal | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | (angle of) incidence | B1 |
> | (a)(iii) | double(s) | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | principal focus | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | inverted<br>diminished | B2 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** Most candidates were able to identify line **X** as the normal.
>
> **(ii)** Most candidates correctly identified angle **Y** as the angle of incidence.
>
> **(iii)** Most candidates correctly stated that angle **Z** would also double. Some candidates stated that **Z** would increase but this was not enough for credit.
>
> **(b) (i)** Many candidates correctly identified point **F** as the focal point but others named a length or angle.
>
> **(ii)** Many candidates correctly described the image as inverted, diminished and sometimes real. Others struggled to use the correct terminology resulting in confusing descriptions of the image.
