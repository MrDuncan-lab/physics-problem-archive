# Question 1 (7 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2019 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 1  
Источник: [[_pdf/2019/0625_s19_qp_32.pdf#page=2|условие, стр. 2]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_s19_ms_32.pdf#page=4|схема оценивания, стр. 4]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_s19_er.pdf#page=20|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 s19 P32]]  
Темы: [[1.2 Motion]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Описать движение модели автомобиля и определить путь по графику скорости.

A student moves a model car along a bench.

Fig. 1.1 is the speed-time graph for the motion of the model car.

![[0625-s19-32-p2-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 1.1**

**(a)** Describe the motion of the car in each of the sections A, B, C and D.

A  \_\_\_\_\_\_

B  \_\_\_\_\_\_

C  \_\_\_\_\_\_

D  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[4]**

**(b)** Determine the distance moved by the model car in the first five seconds.

distance =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ m **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** 8.75 (m)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | A accelerating (uniformly) / speeding up | B1 |
> |  | B steady/constant/uniform speed | B1 |
> |  | C deceleration (non-uniform) / slowing down | B1 |
> |  | D at rest / stopped/stationary / not moving | B1 |
> | (b) | distance = area under graph OR area = ½ × base × height | C1 |
> |  | 0.5 × 3.5 × 5 | C1 |
> |  | 8.75 (m) | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** The majority of candidates correctly identified the sections of the graph. Weaker candidates gave insufficient information such as stating “constant” when “constant speed” was required.
>
> **(b)** The majority of candidates attempted to use the correct equation. Stronger candidates went on to use average speed or the area under the graph.
