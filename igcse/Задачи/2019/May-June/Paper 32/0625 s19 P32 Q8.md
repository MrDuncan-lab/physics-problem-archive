# Question 8 (5 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2019 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 8  
Источник: [[_pdf/2019/0625_s19_qp_32.pdf#page=11|условие, стр. 11–12]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_s19_ms_32.pdf#page=7|схема оценивания, стр. 7]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_s19_er.pdf#page=20|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 s19 P32]]  
Темы: [[4.1 Simple phenomena of magnetism]], [[4.5.3 Magnetic effect of a current]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить магнитные полюса, объяснить притяжение железа и дополнить схему электромагнита.

**(a)** Fig. 8.1 shows the magnetic field pattern around a bar magnet.

![[0625-s19-32-p11-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 8.1**

**(i)** On Fig. 8.1, mark the North and South poles of the magnet. Use the letter N for the North pole and S for the South pole. **[1]**

**(ii)** A small bar of unmagnetised iron is placed next to a bar magnet, as shown in Fig. 8.2.

![[0625-s19-32-p11-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 8.2**

The iron bar moves towards the magnet.

Explain why the iron bar moves. **[2]**

**(b)** Fig. 8.3 shows a coil of wire wrapped around an iron core. A student uses these to make an electromagnet.

![[0625-s19-32-p12-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 8.3**

**(i)** Complete the diagram in Fig. 8.3 to show how it could be used to make an electromagnet. **[1]**

**(ii)** State **one** advantage of an electromagnet compared to a permanent magnet. **[1]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | poles correctly labelled<br>S N | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | Any two from<br>iron bar becomes induced magnet<br>with S pole nearest to (N pole of) magnet<br>opposite poles attract | B2 |
> | (b)(i) | ends of coil connected to power supply OR battery OR cell | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | can be switched on/off OR magnetised/demagnetised (easily) | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** Most candidates correctly placed the N pole on the right and the S pole on the left of the magnet. A few incorrectly placed the poles away from the magnet in the magnetic field.
>
> **(ii)** Very few candidates were able to explain that the magnet induced a N pole in the iron bar next to the S pole of the magnet.
>
> **(b) (i)** Stronger candidates were able to draw an appropriate symbol for a power supply and make the correct connections to the ends of the coil.
>
> **(ii)** Stronger candidates correctly stated that an advantage of an electromagnet was that it can be switched on/off easily.
