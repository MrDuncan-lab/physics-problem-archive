# Question 9 (5 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2019 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 9  
Источник: [[_pdf/2019/0625_s19_qp_32.pdf#page=13|условие, стр. 13]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_s19_ms_32.pdf#page=8|схема оценивания, стр. 8]] · [[_pdf/2019/0625_s19_er.pdf#page=20|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 s19 P32]]  
Темы: [[4.2.1 Electric charge]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объяснить положительное заряжение пластиковой линейки и определить заряд отталкиваемого шарика.

Fig. 9.1 shows a plastic ruler.

![[0625-s19-32-p13-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 9.1**

**(a)** Suggest and explain how a student could give a positive charge to a plastic ruler. **[3]**

**(b)** A plastic ruler is given a positive charge. A sphere hangs from an insulating thread.

A student holds the ruler near the sphere, as shown in Fig. 9.2. The ruler repels the sphere.

![[0625-s19-32-p13-f2.svg]]

![[0625-s19-32-p13-f3.svg]]

**Fig. 9.2**

**(i)** State what charge, if any, the sphere carries. **[1]**

**(ii)** Explain your answer to **(b)(i)**. **[1]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | (rule) rubbed with a cloth owtte | B1 |
> |  | electrons or negative charges move | B1 |
> |  | on to the cloth OR from / off the ruler | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | positive | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | same charges repel | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Most candidates correctly described rubbing the ruler with a cloth but few went on to describe how electrons were moved from the ruler to the cloth.
>
> **(b) (i)** Most candidates correctly identified the charge on the rod as positive.
>
> **(ii)** Most candidates correctly stated that same charges repel. A few were confused with magnetism and talked about poles. Some candidates stated “the ruler repels the sphere” but this was stated in the question and so could not be credited.
