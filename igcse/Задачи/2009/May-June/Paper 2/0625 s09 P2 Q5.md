> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Устройство жидкостного термометра и точки калибровки.

Fig. 5.1 shows a typical laboratory thermometer.

![[0625-s09-2-p7-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 5.1**

**(a)** Explain why the thermometer has

**(i)** thin walls on its bulb, **[1]**

**(ii)** thick walls on its stem, **[1]**

**(iii)** a narrow capillary tube along which the liquid expands. **[1]**

**(b)** Suggest a liquid which the thermometer might contain. **[1]**

**(c)** Such a thermometer is calibrated at the ice point and the steam point. The scale is put on between these two marks.

State the values of

**(i)** the ice point,  \_\_\_\_\_\_

**(ii)** the steam point \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | rapid/rapid heat transfer/gain OR rapid reading/response<br>NOT sensitivity/temperature transfer | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | strength OR reduce chance of breaking OR to magnify the thread<br>ignore any mention of safety | B1 |
> | (a)(iii) | sensitivity or equiv. (e.g. idea of large movement of thread) | B1 |
> | (b) | mercury OR alcohol | B1 |
> | (c) | <u>0 and 100</u> | B1 |
> |  | °C on at least 1 temperature | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** This was badly answered by virtually all candidates. The reasons for the various features of the thermometer simply were not known.
>
> **(b)** This part was usually answered correctly.
>
> **(c)** The question was deliberately set in terms of the ice and steam points, in order to steer candidates away from the mistaken linking of -10°C and 110°C with the upper and lower fixed points. For large numbers of candidates, the ploy failed, as -10°C and 110°C appeared with disappointing regularity as answers.
