> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Most obtained the correct answer, but there were some who added the two forces, or even divided them.
>
> **(b)** Again, this was mostly correctly answered. Interestingly, a small minority ticked several boxes. If they had stopped to think, they should have realised that there could only be one correct answer, and to tick more than one answer **must** involve getting the answer wrong.
>
