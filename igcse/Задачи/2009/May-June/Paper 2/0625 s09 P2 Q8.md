> Намагничивание и электризация стержней, взаимодействие полюсов и зарядов.

**(a)** Four rods are shown in Fig. 8.1.

![[0625-s09-2-p12-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 8.1**

State which of these could be held in the hand at one end and be

**(i)** magnetised by stroking it with a magnet, **[1]**

**(ii)** charged by stroking it with a dry cloth. **[1]**

**(b)** Magnets A and B in Fig. 8.2 are repelling each other.

![[0625-s09-2-p12-f2.svg]]

magnet A magnet B

**Fig. 8.2**

The north pole has been labelled on magnet A.

On Fig. 8.2, label the other three poles. **[1]**

**(c)** Charged rods C and D in Fig. 8.3 are attracting each other.

![[0625-s09-2-p13-f1.svg]]

rod C rod D

**Fig. 8.3**

On Fig. 8.3, show the charge on rod D. **[1]**

**(d)** Fig. 8.4 shows a plotting compass with its needle pointing north.

![[0625-s09-2-p13-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 8.4**

A brass rod is positioned in an east-west direction. A plotting compass is put at each end of the brass rod, as shown in Fig. 8.5.

![[0625-s09-2-p13-f3.svg]]

**Fig. 8.5**

On Fig. 8.5, mark the position of the pointer on each of the two plotting compasses. **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(d)** both needles pointing N, by eye
