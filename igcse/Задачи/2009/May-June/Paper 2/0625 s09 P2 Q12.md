> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question revealed the poor understanding that candidates have of the characteristics of these radiations. About the only characteristic that was known by most was that beta is negative and gamma has no charge. According to most, gamma has no nature! Answers to the line about penetrating ability were usually so vaguely worded that they were worth no marks.
