> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбор номинального тока предохранителя электрической лампы.

The current in a lamp at full brightness is 0.25 A. The flexible cable to the lamp is designed for

currents up to 5.0 A, so it can safely carry the 0.25 A taken by the lamp.

Which fuse should be inserted in the plug at the other end of the flexible cable?

**A** 0.2 A  
**B** 1.0 A  
**C** 5.0 A  
**D** 10.0 A

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
