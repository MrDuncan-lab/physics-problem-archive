> Изменение числа нейтронов при альфа-распаде ядра.

A radioactive nucleus contains 138 neutrons. The nucleus emits an α-particle.

How many neutrons are in the nucleus after it has emitted the α-particle?

**A** 134  
**B** 136  
**C** 138  
**D** 139

> [!answer]- Ответ
