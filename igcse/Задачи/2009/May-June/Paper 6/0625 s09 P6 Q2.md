The IGCSE class is investigating the cooling of thermometer bulbs under different conditions.

The students are provided with two thermometers **A** and **B**. Thermometer **B** has cotton wool wrapped around the bulb. Fig. 2.1 shows thermometer **A**.

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** and **(b)** Only a few candidates failed to insert the correct temperature and units into the table.
>
> **(c)** Most candidates saw that thermometer **A** cooled more quickly but too many tried to offer a theoretical explanation and did not follow the instruction 'Justify your answer *by reference to the* *readings*.'

>
> **(d)** Many candidates did not read the question sufficiently carefully. The answers had to relate to this particular experiment so mention of the size of beaker, volume of water, etc. did not gain a mark. It seems that many candidates used answers that might have been appropriate to a similar set-up in a previous examination. Training in examination technique using past papers is very valuable but candidates must understand the work so that they can apply their knowledge to each new situation not merely repeat something they have learned.
