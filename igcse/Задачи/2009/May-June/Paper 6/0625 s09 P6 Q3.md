> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Most candidates calculated the values correctly although some lost a mark due to 'rounding' errors.
>
> **(b)** Graph work was often good. Scales and plots were generally well done but the line judgement lost most marks with candidates drawing a straight line that went through some points whilst ignoring others.
>
> **(c)** Only the better candidates made the point that the line must be straight *and* pass through the origin.
>
> **(d)** Many candidates were successful in reading the value from the graph although some were careless and placed the line at 0.800 m rather than 0.750 m.
>
> **(e)** Too many candidates suggested ideas that indicated they had little practical experience of this type of experiment. For example insulating the wire or placing it in cold water.
