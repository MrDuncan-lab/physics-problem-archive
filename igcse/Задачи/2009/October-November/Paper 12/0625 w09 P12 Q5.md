> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Плотность жидкости по размерам сосуда и разности масс.

The diagrams show a rectangular box with inside measurements of 5 cm × 6 cm × 4 cm.

![[0625-w09-12-p3-f1.svg]]

mass = 40 g total mass = 220 g

The box has a mass of 40 g when empty. When filled with a liquid, it has a total mass of 220 g.

What is the density of the liquid?

**A** $\dfrac{220}{(5 \times 6 \times 4 )}$ g / cm$^{3}$  
**B** $\dfrac{( 220 - 40 )}{(5 \times 6 \times 4 )}$ g / cm$^{3}$  
**C** $\dfrac{(5 \times 6 \times 4 )}{220}$ g / cm$^{3}$  
**D** $\dfrac{(5 \times 6 \times 4)}{(220 - 40)}$ g / cm$^{3}$

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
