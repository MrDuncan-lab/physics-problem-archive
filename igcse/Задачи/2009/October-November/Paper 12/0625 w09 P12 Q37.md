> Число витков трансформатора для понижения напряжения.

A transformer is to be used to provide a 10 V output from a 100 V supply.

![[0625-w09-12-p14-f4.svg]]

What are suitable numbers of turns for the primary coil and for the secondary coil?

|  | number of turns on the primary coil | number of turns on the secondary coil |
|---|---|---|
| A | 100 | 1000 |
| B | 200 | 110 |
| C | 400 | 490 |
| D | 800 | 80 |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
