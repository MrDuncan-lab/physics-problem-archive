> Инфракрасное электромагнитное излучение от огня.

Which waves carry most heat to her hands and are these waves electromagnetic?

|  | waves | electromagnetic |
|---|---|---|
| A | infra-red | no |
| B | infra-red | yes |
| C | visible light | no |
| D | visible light | yes |
