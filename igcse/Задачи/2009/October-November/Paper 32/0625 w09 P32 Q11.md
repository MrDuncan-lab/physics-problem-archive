> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Терморезистор и логические элементы в автоматическом включении нагревателя.

Fig. 11.1 is an electronic circuit controlling an electric heater.

![[0625-w09-32-p16-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 11.1**

The relay contacts close when there is a current in the relay coil.

**(a)** State the name of the logic gate in the circuit. **[1]**

**(b)** **(i)** State the name of component P. **[1]**

**(ii)** State what happens to P when its temperature falls. **[1]**

**(c)** For the relay to operate, the output of the gate must be high (logic 1).

**(i)** What must be the input of the gate for the relay to operate? **[1]**

**(ii)** State what the resistance of P must be, compared with the resistance of Q, in order to give this input to the gate. **[1]**

**(iii)** Under what conditions will P have this resistance? **[1]**

**(d)** Suggest why component Q is a variable resistor, rather than one with a fixed value. **[1]**

**(e)** Suggest a practical use for this circuit. **[1]**

> | (c)(iii) | low temperature e.c.f. from (c) (ii) | B1 |
