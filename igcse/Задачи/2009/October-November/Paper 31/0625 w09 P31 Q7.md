> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Устройство термопары и её преимущества для измерения температуры.

Three wires and a meter are used to construct a thermocouple for measuring the surface temperature of a pipe carrying hot liquid, as shown in Fig. 7.1.

![[0625-w09-31-p10-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 7.1**

**(a)** Copper wire and constantan wire are used in the construction of the thermocouple.

State which metal might be used for

wire 1  \_\_\_\_\_\_

wire 2  \_\_\_\_\_\_

wire 3  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(b)** State what type of meter is used. **[1]**

**(c)** State one particular advantage of thermocouples for measuring temperature. **[1]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | EITHER OR<br>copper constantan<br>copper constantan<br>constantan copper | B1 |
> | (b) | <u>galvanometer OR millivoltmeter OR milliammeter OR digital ammeter</u><br><u>OR digital voltmeter</u> | B1 |
> | (c) | rapid response )<br>small area )<br>can measure high / low temperatures )<br>small thermal capacity (idea of) ) any 1<br>remote reading )<br>large range )<br>data logging / continuous monitoring possible )<br>takes temperature of a surface )<br>N.B. (very) sensitive not accepted | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** There were many clear cases of misreading or misunderstanding of the question. Many candidates introduced one or more wires made of different materials than the ones specified. Many of those who named the allowed materials frequently did not appreciate the need to have different materials meeting at a junction.
>
> **(b)** Many missed the point that the meter had to be one capable of measuring quantities of the order of mV or mA.
>
> **(c)** A sizeable proportion of candidates gave ‘sensitive’, which is not acceptable as a particular advantage.
