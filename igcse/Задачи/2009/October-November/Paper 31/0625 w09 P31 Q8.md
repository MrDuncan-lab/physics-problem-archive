# Question 8 (4 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2009 · Paper 3 — Extended theory, вариант 1 · вопрос 8  
Источник: [[_pdf/2009/0625_w09_qp_31.pdf#page=11|условие, стр. 11]] · [[_pdf/2009/0625_w09_ms_31.pdf#page=6|схема оценивания, стр. 6]] · [[_pdf/2009/0625_w09_er.pdf#page=8|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Построение мнимого изображения собирающей линзой.

Fig. 8.1 shows a thin converging lens. The two principal foci are shown.

![[0625-w09-31-p11-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 8.1**

A vertical object, 2 cm tall, is to be positioned to the left of the lens, with one end on the principal axis.

On Fig. 8.1,

**(a)** draw the object in a position which will produce a virtual image, labelling the object with the letter O, **[1]**

**(b)** draw two rays showing how the virtual image is formed, **[2]**

**(c)** draw in the image, labelling it with the letter I. **[1]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | 2 cm (by eye) vertical object somewhere between F$_{2}$ and lens<br>(condone no O, if clear) | B1 |
> | (b) | any two standard rays correctly drawn (no extrapolation needed) | B1 |
> |  | <u>correct rays extrapolated back to intersect</u> | B1 |
> |  | virtual image drawn at candidate’s intersection of extrapolated rays<br>(condone no I, if clear) | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> On the whole this question was successfully answered. Most candidates who placed the object in the correct position went on to complete the ray diagram successfully. The few who placed the image on the wrong side of the principal focus could only gain a mark for two correct rays.
