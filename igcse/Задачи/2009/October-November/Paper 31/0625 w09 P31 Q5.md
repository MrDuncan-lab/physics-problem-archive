> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Потенциальная энергия и полезная мощность водяного насоса.

A farmer uses an electric pump to raise water from a river in order to fill the irrigation channels that keep the soil in his fields moist.

![[0625-w09-31-p7-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 5.1**

Every minute, the pump raises 12 kg of water through a vertical height of 3 m.

**(a)** Calculate the increase in the gravitational potential energy of 12 kg of water when it is raised 3 m.

increase in gravitational potential energy =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

**(b)** Calculate the useful power output of the pump as it raises the water.

power =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** 360 J g = 9.8 gives 352.8 J (minimum 2 s.f.) g = 9.81 gives 353.16 J (minimum 2 s.f.)
>
> **(b)** 6 W 352.8 J gives 5.88 W 353.16 J gives 5.886 W (minimum 2 s.f.)
