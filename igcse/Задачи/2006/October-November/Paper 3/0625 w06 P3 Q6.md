> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Преломление и полное внутреннее отражение света в двух призмах.

Fig. 6.1 shows a ray of light, from the top of an object PQ, passing through two glass prisms.

![[0625-w06-3-p9-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 6.1**

**(a)** Complete the path through the two prisms of the ray shown leaving Q. **[1]**

**(b)** A person looking into the lower prism, at the position indicated by the eye symbol, sees an image of PQ. State the properties of this image. **[2]**

**(c)** Explain why there is no change in direction of the ray from P at points A, C, D and F. **[1]**

**(d)** The speed of light as it travels from P to A is 3 × 10$^{8}$ m/s and the refractive index of the prism glass is 1.5. Calculate the speed of light in the prism.

speed =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(e)** Explain why the ray AB reflects through 90° at B and does not pass out of the prism at B. **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(d)** Vg = 2 x 10$^{8}$ m/s

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | completed path | B1 |
> | (b) | any two correct, -1 each incorrect<br>virtual, inverted, same size as object | B2 |
> | (c) | angle of incidence zero/at right angles/along normal | B1 |
> | (d) | 1.5 = Va/Vg = 3x 10$^{8}$/Vg | C1 |
> |  | Vg = 2 x 10$^{8}$ m/s | A1 |
> | (e) | angle of incidence = 45°, so angle of reflection = 45°, so ray turns through 90°<br>OR angle i\> angle c | B1 |
> |  | so totally internally reflects | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** The diagram was completed well with few errors.
>
> **(b)** This section caused more difficulties with many candidates opting for lateral inversion, however there are two reflections, therefore it is ‘laterally re-inverted’.
>
> **(c)** Again the explanations showed a good understanding that the ray entering perpendicular to the surface (or with an angle of incidence of 90°) would pass straight through that surface.
>
> **(d)** The calculation was done well, although a surprising number of candidates did not include the unit.
>
> speed = 2 × 10$^{8}$m/s
>
> **(e)** Most candidates described total internal reflection and backed this up by explaining the angle of incidence at **B** is less than the critical angle. Only a few tackled the idea that the angle of incidence is 45°, so the angle of reflection is 45° and the deflection is 90°.
