**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2006 · Paper 2 — Core theory · вопрос 3

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Анализ движения трактора и пути по графику скорости.

Fig. 3.1 shows the speed/time graph for a journey travelled by a tractor.

![[0625-w06-2-p4-t1.svg]]

0 50 100 170 200

time / s

**Fig. 3.1**

**(a)** Use the graph to describe the motion of the tractor during each of the marked sections.

OP  \_\_\_\_\_\_

PQ  \_\_\_\_\_\_

QR  \_\_\_\_\_\_

RS  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[4]**

**(b)** Which two points on the graph show when the tractor is stationary?

point  \_\_\_\_\_\_ and point  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(c)** State the greatest speed reached by the tractor.

greatest speed =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ m/s **[1]**

**(d)** For how long was the tractor travelling at constant speed?

at constant speed for  \_\_\_\_\_\_ s **[1]**

**(e)** State how the graph may be used to find the total distance travelled during the 200 s journey. Do **not** attempt a calculation. **[1]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | OP accelerating | B1 |
> |  | PQ accelerating | B1 |
> |  | QR constant speed | B1 |
> |  | RS slowing down (however expressed) | B1 |
> | (b) | <u>O and S (both)</u> | B1 |
> | (c) | 6 (m/s) | B1 |
> | (d) | 70 (s) | B1 |
> | (e) | find area OPQRS (however expressed) | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** As might be expected, some candidates interpreted the graph as if it were a distance/time graph. However, the majority coped with it correctly and well. A few interpreted QR as “stationary” and QR as “going in reverse”, but generally the answers were pleasing.
>
> **(b) – (d)** were all answered well.
