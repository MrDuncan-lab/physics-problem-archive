> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Изменение длины или сечения проволоки для увеличения сопротивления.

A student uses a length of wire as a resistor. He discovers that the resistance of the wire is too

small.

To be certain of making a resistor of higher value, he should use a piece of wire that is

**A** longer and thicker.  
**B** longer and thinner.  
**C** shorter and thicker.  
**D** shorter and thinner.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
