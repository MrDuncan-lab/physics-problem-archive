> Определение полураспада по таблице счётных скоростей.

The count rates of four radioactive sources were measured at the same time on three

consecutive days.

Which source has a half-life of two days?

|  | Monday | Tuesday | Wednesday |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | 100 | 50 | 25 |
| B | 200 | 140 | 100 |
| C | 300 | 300 | 300 |
| D | 400 | 200 | 100 |
