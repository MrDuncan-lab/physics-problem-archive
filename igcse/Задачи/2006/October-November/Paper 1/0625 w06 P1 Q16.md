A thermometer with no scale is taped to a ruler as shown. When placed in steam, the mercury level rises to 22 cm. When placed in pure melting ice, the mercury level falls to 2 cm.

![[0625-w06-1-p9-f1.svg]]

![[0625-w06-1-p9-t2.svg]]

**B** 8 °C  
**C** 30 °C  
**D** 40 °C
