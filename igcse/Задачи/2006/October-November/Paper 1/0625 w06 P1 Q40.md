> Сравнение протонного числа трёх изотопов водорода.

There are three nuclides of hydrogen.

| nuclide 1 | nuclide 2 | nuclide 3 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2 | 3 |
| H 1 | H 1 | H 1 |
| H 1 | H 1 | H 1 |

Which of these nuclides have the same number of protons in their nuclei?

**A** 1 and 2 only  
**B** 2 and 3 only  
**C** all of them  
**D** none of them

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
