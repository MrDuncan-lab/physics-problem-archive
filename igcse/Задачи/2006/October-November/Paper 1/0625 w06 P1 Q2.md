> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определение тормозного пути по графику движения велосипедиста.

A cyclist is riding along a road when an animal runs in front of him. The graph shows the cyclist’s motion. He sees the animal at P, starts to brake at Q and stops at R.

![[0625-w06-1-p2-f2.svg]]

What is used to find the distance travelled after he applies the brakes?

**A** the area under line PQ  
**B** the area under line QR  
**C** the gradient of line PQ  
**D** the gradient of line QR

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
