> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Постоянство температуры во время плавления и кипения.

A block of ice is heated until it has all melted. The water that is produced is then heated until it

boils.

Which line in the table states what happens to the temperature of the ice while it is melting, and to the temperature of the water while it is boiling?

|  | temperature of ice while it is melting | temperature of water while it is boiling |
|---|---|---|
| A | increases | increases |
| B | increases | stays the same |
| C | stays the same | increases |
| D | stays the same | stays the same |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
