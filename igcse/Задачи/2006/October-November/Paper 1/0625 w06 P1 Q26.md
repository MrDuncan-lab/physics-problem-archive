> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определение намагниченных стержней по отталкиванию полюсов.

The ends of three metal rods are tested by holding end Q of rod 1 close to the others in turn.

![[0625-w06-1-p14-f1.svg]]

rod 1 rod 2 rod 3

The results are as follows.

End Q: attracts end R,

attracts end S,

attracts end T,

repels end U.

Which of the metal rods is a magnet?

**A** rod 1 only  
**B** rod 1 and rod 2 only  
**C** rod 1 and rod 3 only  
**D** rod 3 only

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
