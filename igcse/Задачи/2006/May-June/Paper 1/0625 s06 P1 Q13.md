> Случайное броуновское движение микрочастиц дыма.

Brownian motion is seen by looking at smoke particles through a microscope.

How do the smoke particles move in Brownian motion?

**A** all in the same direction  
**B** at random  
**C** in circles  
**D** vibrating about fixed points

> [!answer]- Ответ
