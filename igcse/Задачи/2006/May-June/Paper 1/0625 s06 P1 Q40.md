> Число протонов, электронов и нейтронов лития.

The nucleus of a neutral atom of lithium is represented by $^{7}_{3}$ Li.

How many protons, electrons and neutrons does the atom contain?

|  | protons | electrons | neutrons |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | 7 | 7 | 3 |
| B | 3 | 7 | 3 |
| C | 3 | 4 | 4 |
| D | 3 | 3 | 4 |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
