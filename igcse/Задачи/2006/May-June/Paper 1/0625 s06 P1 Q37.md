> Заряд и температурные условия анода электронной трубки.

A cathode-ray tube has an anode and an earthed cathode.

Which line in the table shows the charge and the temperature of the anode?

|  | anode charge | anode temperature |
|---|---|---|
| A | negative | cool |
| B | negative | hot |
| C | positive | cool |
| D | positive | hot |
