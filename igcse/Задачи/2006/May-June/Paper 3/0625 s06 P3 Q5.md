**(c)** A toilet flush is operated by the compression of air. The air inside the flush has a pressure of 1.0 × 10$^{5}$ Pa and a volume of 150 cm$^{3}$. When the flush is operated the volume is reduced to 50 cm$^{3}$. The temperature of the air remains constant during this process. Calculate the new pressure of the air inside the flush.

pressure =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(c)** p$_{2}$ = 3 x 10$^{5}$ (Pa)
