> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Измерение размеров прямоугольного блока и единиц объёма.

Fig. 2.1 is a full-size diagram of a rectangular block.

![[0625-s06-2-p4-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 2.1**

**(a)** Use your rule to measure the lengths of the three sides AB, BC and CD. Write your values below, in cm, to 2 significant figures.

length of AB =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm

length of BC =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm

length of CD =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm **[2]**

**(b)** Write down the equation you would use to calculate the volume of the block. Do **not** attempt a calculation. **[1]**

**(c)** If you used your values from **(a)**, what would be the unit for the volume of the block?

unit of volume =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | 6.0 ± 0.1 )<br>2.4 ± 0.1 ) -1 each error or omission 2F<br>3.1 ± 0.1 ) | B2 |
> | (b) | AB x BC x CD OR l x b x h OR his figures shown multiplied | B1 |
> | (c) | cm$^{3}$ OR cu.cm OR cubic cm | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Most candidates could cope with this very well. There was a handful who could not measure correctly, and some who insisted on attempting the calculation, but mostly good marks were scored.
