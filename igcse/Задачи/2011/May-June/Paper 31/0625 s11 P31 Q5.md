**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2011 · Paper 3 — Extended theory, вариант 1 · вопрос 5

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Безопасность заземлённого нагревателя и схема его элементов.

The manufacturer’s label on an electric heater is as shown in Fig. 5.1.

![[0625-s11-31-p10-f1.svg]]

**Suitable for use on 110 V, 60 Hz supply**

**This appliance must be earthed when in use**

**Fig. 5.1**

**(a)** State what electrical quantity is represented by

**(i)** 110 V,  \_\_\_\_\_\_

**(ii)** 60 Hz,  \_\_\_\_\_\_

**(iii)** 1 kW \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(b)** **(i)** Which part of the electric heater must be earthed? **[1]**

**(ii)** Explain what the hazard might be if the heater is not earthed. **[2]**

**(c)** The heater has two 110 V heating elements, with two switches, so that either one or both elements may be switched on.

In the space below, draw a circuit diagram showing how the heating elements and switches are connected to the mains supply.

![[0625-s11-31-p10-f2.svg]]

for each heating element. **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(c)** <u>one switch controlling both heaters and one switch controlling one heater</u> OR one switch in series with each element

> | (b)(ii) | electric shock/electrocution/death by electricity o.w.t.t.e. ignore anything else | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** This was generally well answered but weaker candidates often gave the unit instead of the quantity.
>
> **(b)** Answers here were often weak in that the part of the heater quoted to be earthed was sometimes vague, such as “the metal part”, or was not inside the heater itself, such as “the plug”. Surprisingly many stated that the live wire should be earthed. Many candidates stated that electric shock is the hazard reduced by proper earthing but then did not explain that this was caused when the live wire touches the case. Weaker candidates confused the action of earthing with the action of a fuse.
>
> **(c)** Circuit diagrams were generally correct although a significant number of candidates produced circuits containing a shorting wire or omitted a power source.
