The ball takes 1.2 s to travel from player A to player B.

**(i)** Calculate the average speed of the ball between A and B.

average speed =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(ii)** Player B kicks the ball to player C. It travels with the same average speed. Calculate the time taken for the ball to travel from B to C.

time =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(iii)** Suggest why the speed of the ball might change during its motion from A to B. **[1]**

**(iv)** Discuss whether the average velocities, from A to B and from B to C, are the same. **[1]**

> **(b)(i)** = 15 m / s
>
> **(b)(ii)** = 1.4 s
