# Question 9 (5 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2011 · Paper 3 — Extended theory, вариант 3 · вопрос 9  
Источник: [[_pdf/2011/0625_s11_qp_33.pdf#page=14|условие, стр. 14]] · [[_pdf/2011/0625_s11_ms_33.pdf#page=6|схема оценивания, стр. 6]] · [[_pdf/2011/0625_s11_er.pdf#page=22|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 s11 P33]]  
Темы: [[4.5.4 Force on a current-carrying conductor]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Магнитная сила на проводник и движущуюся заряженную частицу.

**(a)** Fig. 9.1 shows a wire, held between the poles of a magnet, carrying a current in the direction of the arrow.

![[0625-s11-33-p14-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 9.1**

**(i)** On Fig. 9.1, draw an arrow, labelled *F*, to show the direction of the force acting on the wire. **[1]**

**(ii)** Explain why the force *F* acts on the wire. **[1]**

**(iii)** The directions of the current and the magnetic field are both reversed. State the effect on the force *F*. **[1]**

**(b)** Fig. 9.2 shows a negatively charged particle travelling, in a vacuum, into a region where a magnetic field acts. The magnetic field, shown by the crosses, is acting **into** the paper.

![[0625-s11-33-p14-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 9.2**

**(i)** Draw an arrow, labelled *F*, to show the direction of the force on the particle at point P where it enters the field.

**(ii)** Describe the path of the particle as it continues to move through the magnetic field. **[2]**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)**
>
> **(i)** The rule was applied correctly in the majority of cases.
>
> **(ii)** The question effectively told candidates that a current in a wire placed in a magnetic field experiences a force, so an answer repeating this idea was not acceptable, nor was an answer stating that Fleming’s left-hand rule applies. A more fundamental reason was required, such as expressing the idea that the current produces a magnetic field, which interacts with that of the magnet to cause the force.
>
> **(iii)** Only answers suggesting reversal of the direction in **(i)** were accepted.
>
> **(b)**
>
> **(i)** Many candidates drew a curved line with an arrow, suggesting that the force acting was changing direction. These answers were rejected. Only an arrow at **P** pointing downwards, suggesting proper understanding of the rule in this case, was awarded credit.
>
> **(ii)** Credit was given for a suggestion that the particle followed a curved path, irrespective of the previous answer.
