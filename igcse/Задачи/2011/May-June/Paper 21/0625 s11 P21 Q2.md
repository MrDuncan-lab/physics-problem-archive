**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2011 · Paper 2 — Core theory, вариант 1 · вопрос 2

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Молекулярное объяснение давления и газовые законы.

A fixed amount of dry gas is exerting a pressure on its container.

**(a)** In terms of molecules, explain what causes the pressure. **[3]**

**(b)** One of the sketch graphs in Fig. 2.1 shows how the pressure of the gas varies with volume at constant temperature, and the other shows how the pressure varies with temperature at constant volume.

The pressure axis has been labelled in each case.

![[0625-s11-21-p4-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 2.1**

**(i)** On the appropriate graphs, label one horizontal axis “ volume / m$^{3}$ ” and the other horizontal axis “ temperature / °C ”.

**(ii)** On one of the graphs, mark, with the letter X, the pressure of the gas at the ice point. **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** molecules colliding with walls
>
> **(b)(ii)** X on LH graph at intersection of line and vertical axis

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | molecules/particles/atoms moving (accept vibrating/oscillating) | C1 |
> |  | molecules colliding (accept with each other) | C1 |
> |  | molecules colliding with walls | A1 |
> | (b)(i) | LH graph – temperature/T/θ / °C/K on horizontal axis<br>RH graph – volume/V / m$^{3}$/cm$^{3}$ on horizontal axis | M1 |
> | (b)(ii) | X on LH graph at intersection of line and vertical axis | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** There was a good understanding of pressure shown in most answers. Nearly all answered in terms of collisions, although a few stated that the molecules collided with each other to create pressure.
>
> **(b)** Candidates who labelled the axes correctly did not always mark X correctly, but there was a good proportion who scored gained full credit. There was no requirement that candidates should recognise the shapes of these graphs, merely to know that pressure increases as temperature increases and decreases as volume increases, and to spot which graph showed which trend.
