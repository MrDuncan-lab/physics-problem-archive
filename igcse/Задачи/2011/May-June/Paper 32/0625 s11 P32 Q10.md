# Question 10 (6 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2011 · Paper 3 — Extended theory, вариант 2 · вопрос 10  
Источник: [[_pdf/2011/0625_s11_qp_32.pdf#page=15|условие, стр. 15]] · [[_pdf/2011/0625_s11_ms_32.pdf#page=6|схема оценивания, стр. 6]] · [[_pdf/2011/0625_s11_er.pdf#page=17|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 s11 P32]]  
Темы: [[X.1 Logic gates and digital electronics]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Логический элемент ИЛИ в автомобильной сигнализации.

**(a)** In the space below, draw the symbol for an OR gate. **[1]**

**(b)** Describe the action of an OR gate in terms of its inputs and outputs. **[2]**

**(c)** A car manufacturer wishes to install an alarm system in a 2-door car to inform the driver if either door is not properly closed. An OR gate is to be used in the construction of this system. Describe suitable input and output arrangements for the gate. **[3]**

**Question 11 is on the next page.**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | correct symbol for OR gate | B1 |
> | (b) | output is low / zero / off if both inputs are low / zero / off | B1 |
> |  | output is high / one / on if one input is high / one / on<br>BUT this mark is not scored if candidate puts output low when both inputs high | B1 |
> | (c) | <u>switches in doors are on if doors are open or vice versa</u> | B1 |
> |  | (switches in) doors provide inputs (to gate) | B1 |
> |  | output (of gate) is connected to buzzer / warning light / alarm | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Most candidates drew an acceptable symbol for the OR gate. Those with a straight line at the input end or showing a triangular shape for the gate were rejected.
>
> **(b)** A large majority conveyed the correct information, with many completing a truth table to do so.
>
> **(c)** It was not intended that candidates should effectively repeat the answer to **(b)**. Candidates needed to describe in some way the necessary features built into the car to provide the required signal. A good answer referred to the following: switches or sensors of some kind to respond to the opening or closing of the doors, connections from these switches to provide inputs to the gate, the output of the gate connected to some kind of alarm and giving a signal to the driver. Complete answers were rare.
