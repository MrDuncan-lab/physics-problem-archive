# Question 3 (9 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2011 · Paper 3 — Extended theory, вариант 2 · вопрос 3  
Источник: [[_pdf/2011/0625_s11_qp_32.pdf#page=5|условие, стр. 5]] · [[_pdf/2011/0625_s11_ms_32.pdf#page=3|схема оценивания, стр. 3]] · [[_pdf/2011/0625_s11_er.pdf#page=17|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 s11 P32]]  
Темы: [[1.5.2 Turning effect of forces]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Равновесие рычага при подъёме бетонной плиты.

**(a)** Complete the following statement:

The moment of a force about a point is  \_\_\_\_\_\_

multiplied by  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(b)** Fig. 3.1 shows a uniform iron bar B of weight 30 N and length 1.40 m. The bar is being used to lift one edge of a concrete slab S. A stone, placed 0.20 m from one end of B, acts as a pivot. A force of 40 N pushing down at the other end of B is just enough to lift the slab and hold it as shown.

![[0625-s11-32-p5-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 3.1**

**(i)** On Fig. 3.1, draw an arrow to show the weight of bar B acting from its centre of mass. **[1]**

**(ii)** State the distance *d* of the centre of mass of bar B from the pivot.

*d* =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(iii)** Calculate the total clockwise moment, about the pivot, of the forces acting on bar B.

total clockwise moment =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

**(iv)** Calculate the downward force which the slab S exerts on the end of bar B.

force =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(v)** Suggest a change to the arrangement in Fig. 3.1 that would reduce the force required to lift the slab. **[1]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)(iii)** = 63 N m
>
> **(b)(iv)** F = 63/0.2 = 315 N

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | force AND<br><u>perpendicular distance (of force) from the point.</u> | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | downward arrow at centre of bar | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | 0.5(0) m / 50 cm | B1 |
> | (b)(iii) | 40 × 1.2 OR 48 seen anywhere | C1 |
> |  | (+) 30 × 0.5 0R 15 seen anywhere | C1 |
> |  | = 63 N m | A1 |
> | (b)(iv) | F × 0.2 = 63 | C1 |
> |  | F = 63/0.2 = 315 N | A1 |
> | (b)(v) | make bar / B longer<br>OR move pivot / stone to the left<br>OR increase distance between force and pivot (by moving pivot to left)<br>OR increase mass of the bar / B | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** A large majority did not include all the necessary detail. ‘Force multiplied by perpendicular distance from the point’ was required. The words ‘perpendicular’ and ‘from the point’ were usually missed.
>
> **(b)**
>
> **(i)** A downward pointing arrow at the centre of the bar was usually drawn, but surprisingly many placed the arrow at the pivot or the right-hand end of the bar. Some showed the arrow in the correct place but pointing upwards.
>
> **(ii)** Many did not use the given dimensions correctly to calculate the distance.
>
> **(iii)** The fact that the question used the words ‘total’ and ‘forces’ should have suggested to candidates that more than one moment was involved. However, the great majority did not include the moment due to the weight of the bar.
>
> **(iv)** With the benefit of any error from **(iii)** carried forward, full credit was usually awarded.
>
> **(v)** Either by analysing the previous numerical work, or by knowing how a crowbar works, many candidates gave the correct answer.
