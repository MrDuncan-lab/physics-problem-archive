# Question 7 (7 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2011 · Paper 3 — Extended theory, вариант 2 · вопрос 7  
Источник: [[_pdf/2011/0625_s11_qp_32.pdf#page=12|условие, стр. 12]] · [[_pdf/2011/0625_s11_ms_32.pdf#page=5|схема оценивания, стр. 5]] · [[_pdf/2011/0625_s11_er.pdf#page=17|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Построение изображения предмета собирающей линзой.

**(a)** What is meant by the *focal length* of a converging lens? **[1]**

**(b)** An object is placed in front of a converging lens. A real image is formed, as shown in Fig. 7.1. The converging lens is not shown.

![[0625-s11-32-p12-t1.svg]]

object

**Fig. 7.1**

**(i)** Explain what is meant by a *real image*. **[1]**

**(ii)** Rays of light from point A on the object form point B on the image.

On Fig. 6.1, draw

**1.** a ray to find the position of the converging lens, showing the lens as a vertical straight

line in this position,

**2.** a ray to find the position of a principal focus of the lens, marking this position **F**,

**3.** a third possible ray from A to B. **[3]**

**(iii)** The distance between the object and the lens is increased. State any changes which take place in

**1.** the distance of the image from the lens,

**2.** the size of the image. **[2]**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** The fact that the focal length is the distance between a principal focus or focal point and the centre of the lens, or simply the lens, is not well known. Many took no note of the word ‘length’ and wrote about a point instead.
>
> **(b)**
>
> **(i)** Many misconceptions were apparent in the candidates’ ideas about a real image. Acceptable answers referred to an image which can be formed on a screen or other surface or an image formed by rays of light meeting. The answer ‘not virtual’, often quoted, was not accepted.
>
> 19 © 2011
>
> **(ii)** The rays drawn had to start at **A** and finish, or pass through, **B**. Most candidates were correct in drawing ray 1 as a straight line from **A** to **B**, but many of these did not place the lens line at the intersection of this the ray and the principal axis. Some of those who drew ray 2 correctly did not show **F**, the principal focus, at the appropriate point. Ray 3 posed a problem for many, with some not showing it at all; any ray starting at **A**, bending at the lens and passing through **B** would suffice.
>
> **(iii)** There were three choices for each answer: smaller, larger or the same. Those who gained full credit probably worked out their answers by applying knowledge of ray diagrams.
