> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Схема измерения напряжения и сопротивления резистора.

The components in Fig. 8.1 are connected in a circuit.

![[0625-s11-22-p10-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 8.1**

**(a)** Complete the following sentence.

The components in the circuit of Fig. 8.1 are connected in  \_\_\_\_\_\_ with each

other. **[1]**

**(b)** On Fig. 8.1, draw

**(i)** an arrow to show the direction of the conventional current in the circuit,

**(ii)** a voltmeter connected to measure the potential difference across R. **[2]**

**(c)** **(i)** State the name of the component represented by this symbol:

![[0625-s11-22-p10-f2.svg]]

**(ii)** What is the purpose of this component in the circuit? **[2]**

**(d)** The top ammeter reads 1.5 A. The voltmeter reads 6.0 V.

**(i)** State the reading of the bottom ammeter \_\_\_\_\_\_ A **[1]**

**(ii)** Calculate the resistance of R.

resistance =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[4]**

**(e)** A piece of low resistance wire is carelessly allowed to connect P and Q.

State which component could be damaged when this happens. **[1]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(c)(ii)** change resistance/current
>
> **(d)(ii)** 4 e.c.f. (i)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | series | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | anticlockwise current clearly indicated | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | voltmeter connected across R only | B1 |
> | (c)(i) | <u>rheostat OR variable resistor</u> | M1 |
> | (c)(ii) | change resistance/current | A1 |
> | (d)(i) | 1.5 (A) | B1 |
> | (d)(ii) | R = V/I in any form | C1 |
> |  | 6/1.5 e.c.f. (i) | C1 |
> |  | 4 e.c.f. (i) | A1 |
> |  | Ω OR ohm(s) | B1 |
> | (e) | battery OR cell | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> All parts of this question were pleasingly well answered, with the exception of **(e)**, where very few realised that it would be the battery that would be damaged. The circuit symbol in **(c)** was sometimes identified as a thermistor, a switch, a fuse or simply as a resistor, but a good number made a correct identification.
