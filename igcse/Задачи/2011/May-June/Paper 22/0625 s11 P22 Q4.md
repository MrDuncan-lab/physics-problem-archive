# Question 4 (11 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2011 · Paper 2 — Core theory, вариант 2 · вопрос 4  
Источник: [[_pdf/2011/0625_s11_qp_22.pdf#page=4|условие, стр. 4–5]] · [[_pdf/2011/0625_s11_ms_22.pdf#page=3|схема оценивания, стр. 3]] · [[_pdf/2011/0625_s11_er.pdf#page=9|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Анализ графика скорости и вычисление пройденного пути.

Fig. 4.1 shows how the speed of an object varies during a period of 30 s.

![[0625-s11-22-p4-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 4.1**

**(a)** State the speed of the object

**(i)** at the start of the 30 s,

speed =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ m / s

**(ii)** at the end of the 30 s.

speed =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ m / s **[2]**

**(b)** Describe what is happening to the speed during the period

**(i)** 0 s – 10 s,  \_\_\_\_\_\_

**(ii)** 10 s – 25 s,  \_\_\_\_\_\_

**(iii)** 25 s – 30 s \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

**(c)** Determine the distance travelled in the last 5 s.

distance =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ m **[3]**

**(d)** The total distance travelled in the 30 s is 750 m.

Calculate the average speed of the object during the 30 s.

average speed =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ m / s **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(c)** 75 (m)
>
> **(d)** 25 (m / s)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | 15 (m / s) | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | 0 (m / s) | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | increasing OR accelerating | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | constant OR nothing | B1 |
> | (b)(iii) | decreasing OR decelerating (however expressed) | B1 |
> | (c) | area of triangle OR area under graph OR appropriate equation of motion | C1 |
> |  | ½ × 30 × 5 | C1 |
> |  | 75 (m) | A1 |
> | (d) | speed = distance/time in any form, letters, words, numbers | C1 |
> |  | 750/30 | C1 |
> |  | 25 (m / s) | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Nearly all candidates scored well on this question. The information deduced from the graph for **(a)** and **(b)** was usually correct. Very few did not gain full credit for **(d)**, but only a handful knew how to find the distance needed in **(c)**. There was virtually no evidence of the formula for the area of a triangle, or even a mention that the area under the graph would give the distance. There are very few things that, at this level, candidates can be asked to find from a speed-time graph, and this is highly likely to be asked again in the future.
