# Question 5 (8 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2011 · Paper 2 — Core theory, вариант 2 · вопрос 5

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Изображение в плоском зеркале и отражение волн от стены.

**(a)** Fig. 5.1 shows a girl looking at her reflection in a mirror on the wall. The reflecting surface of the mirror is the surface in contact with the wall.

![[0625-s11-22-p6-f1.png]]

**Fig. 5.1**

On Fig. 5.1,

**(i)** put a small X where the image of the girl’s eye is positioned, **[2]**

**(ii)** carefully draw lines to find the lowest part of her body that she can see reflected in the mirror. Mark clearly the portion of her body that she cannot see. **[3]**

**(b)** A helicopter is hovering over a harbour. The pilot can see the waves arriving from out at sea. The waves hit the harbour wall at an angle, as shown in Fig. 5.2.

![[0625-s11-22-p7-f1.png]]

**Fig. 5.2**

The waves are reflected when they hit the harbour wall.

Carefully complete Fig. 5.2 to show the reflected parts of the first two waves to hit the wall. **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)(ii)** line at same angle as above (by eye) drawn from mirror to girl

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Candidates struggled with this question. The vast majority incorrectly showed the image on the mirror. Some of the more able candidates drew appropriate rays from the eye to the bottom of the mirror and then reflected down to the girl. A few of these then did not indicate what part could not be seen. Most candidates unfortunately indicated that the region below the bottom edge of the mirror was what could not be seen.
>
> **(b)** Very few candidates gained full credit here. A good proportion showed waves reflected at the correct angle, even if the spacing was not correct. It was almost universal that the reflected waves were not actually continuations of the first two waves - they did not start where the incoming portions met the wall.
