> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Движение автомобиля и путь по графику скорости.

Fig. 1.1 shows the route OPQR taken by a car.

![[0625-w11-23-p2-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 1.1**

Fig. 1.2 shows the speed-time graph for the car journey. The car starts from rest at O.

![[0625-w11-23-p2-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 1.2**

**(a)** State the value of the distance represented by the shaded area. **[1]**

**(b)** State what the car was doing during the interval

**(i)** OP,  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(ii)** PQ,  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(iii)** QR \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(c)** Is the average speed during the journey the same as, less than or more than the maximum speed shown on the graph? **[1]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | 25km | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | accelerating OR increasing speed | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | steady/constant speed | B1 |
> | (b)(iii) | decelerating OR retarding OR slowing down | B1 |
> | (c) | less than | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> (a) Most candidates realised that the shaded area represented the 25 km shown on Fig. 1.1.
>
> (b) A few got muddled, but the majority could interpret the graph.
>
> (c) This was correctly answered by most.
