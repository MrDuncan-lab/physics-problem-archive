# Question 9 (5 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2011 · Paper 2 — Core theory, вариант 3 · вопрос 9  
Источник: [[_pdf/2011/0625_w11_qp_23.pdf#page=15|условие, стр. 15]] · [[_pdf/2011/0625_w11_ms_23.pdf#page=6|схема оценивания, стр. 6]] · [[_pdf/2011/0625_w11_er.pdf#page=13|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w11 P23]]  
Темы: [[4.1 Simple phenomena of magnetism]], [[4.2.1 Electric charge]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Признаки магнитного и электростатического взаимодействия.

**(a)** A girl has two metal bars, 1 and 2.

**(i)** When the girl holds one end of bar 1 near a magnet, she finds that it attracts both the N pole and the S pole of the magnet.

What does this tell you about bar 1? **[2]**

**(ii)** When the girl holds one end of bar 2 near the magnet, it attracts the N pole of the magnet, but repels the S pole.

What does this tell you about bar 2? **[1]**

**(b)** In another experiment, the girl uses two table-tennis balls A and B. Each is coated with a thin layer of conducting material. Ball A is mounted on an insulating stand and ball B is suspended from a thin nylon thread. The arrangement is shown in Fig. 9.1.

![[0625-w11-23-p15-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 9.1**

Ball A is given a negative charge, and is slowly moved towards ball B until it touches.

Predict what is seen to happen. **[2]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | iron OR ferromagnetic | B1 |
> |  | unmagnetised (before being brought near magnet) NOT non-magnetic | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | magnet | B1 |
> | (b) | attracts (at first) NOT goes towards | B1 |
> |  | repels after touching OR angle of thread increases as XY decreases | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> (a) Candidates’ application of their knowledge of magnetism to this problem needed more thought. Few seemed to realise the significance of whether something attracts or repels.
>
> (b) Most recognised that the balls would attract, but few tackled what would happen after they touched.
