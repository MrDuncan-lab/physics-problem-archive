# Question 5 (6 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2011 · Paper 2 — Core theory, вариант 3 · вопрос 5

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определение плотности воздуха по массе откачанного газа.

A flask with a tap has a volume of 200 cm$^{3}$.

When full of air, the flask has a mass of 30.98 g.

The flask is connected to a vacuum pump, the air is pumped out and then the tap is closed.

The flask now has a mass of 30.72 g.

Calculate

**(a)** the mass of the air in the flask before connecting to the vacuum pump,

mass =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ g **[2]**

**(b)** the density of the air in the flask.

density =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[4]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** 0.26 (g)
>
> **(b)** 0.0013 e.c.f. from (a)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | 30.98 – 30.72 | C1 |
> |  | 0.26 (g) | A1 |
> | (b) | D = M/V in any form | C1 |
> |  | candidate's 0.26/200 | C1 |
> |  | 0.0013 e.c.f. from (a) | A1 |
> |  | g/cm$^{3}$ | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> (a) Some tried to use density=mass/volume to find the mass, instead of simply subtracting, but most knew what to do.
>
> (b) Density was well understood and the calculation was usually clearly performed. If a candidate used a value of mass that had been incorrectly calculated in (a), no further penalty was applied provided no further mistake was made. This is a general principle in IGCSE Physics papers, to avoid penalising a candidate more than once for the same mistake. The printed answer line did not contain the unit for the answer. Teachers could explain that this usually indicates that the candidates have to supply the unit themselves (unless the quantity has no unit).
