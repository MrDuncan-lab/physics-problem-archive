> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> (a) There were some candidates who seemed to have a confident grasp of the topic, but there were others who appeared to be guessing.
>
> (b) Most knew that the instrument would be a voltmeter, and could show it in the correct position, using the standard symbol. There was a minority who showed it connected in series somewhere.
>
> (c) The wiring on the diagram was completed correctly by most candidates, although some added an extra wire across the bottom, incorrectly creating a short circuit. The calculation was correctly done by most candidates, including stating the correct unit, but candidates were far less sure about the total resistance of the electric fire.
