# Question 1 (5 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2011 · Paper 3 — Extended theory, вариант 3 · вопрос 1  
Источник: [[_pdf/2011/0625_w11_qp_33.pdf#page=3|условие, стр. 3]] · [[_pdf/2011/0625_w11_ms_33.pdf#page=4|схема оценивания, стр. 4]] · [[_pdf/2011/0625_w11_er.pdf#page=25|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

Темы: [[1.3 Mass and weight]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Масса и вес астронавта на Земле и Луне.

An astronaut has a mass of 65 kg on Earth, where the gravitational field strength is 10 N / kg.

**(a)** Calculate the astronaut’s weight on Earth.

weight on Earth =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(b)** Complete the following sentence.

The astronaut’s weight on Earth is the  \_\_\_\_\_\_ force

between the astronaut and  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(c)** The astronaut undertakes a Moon landing. On the Moon the gravitational field strength is 1.6 N / kg.

**(i)** State the astronaut’s mass on the Moon.

mass =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

**(ii)** Calculate the weight of the astronaut on the Moon.

weight on Moon =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** 650 N

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | mg in any form | C1 |
> |  | 650 N | A1 |
> | (b) | <u>gravitational / attractive and the Earth</u> | B1 |
> | (c)(i) | 65 kg | B1 |
> | (c)(ii) | 104 OR 100 N ecf (i) | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** This was almost invariably correctly answered by the candidates with just an occasional *kilogram* as the unit for weight or some other similar confusion.
>
> **(b)** Many candidates did not realise what was expected on the second line and wrote a variety of other answers; *gravitational field* was one example.
>
> **(c)** This was, again, very commonly correct with just the occasional confusion between mass and weight.
