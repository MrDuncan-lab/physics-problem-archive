# Question 10 (7 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2011 · Paper 2 — Core theory, вариант 1 · вопрос 10  
Источник: [[_pdf/2011/0625_w11_qp_21.pdf#page=13|условие, стр. 13]] · [[_pdf/2011/0625_w11_ms_21.pdf#page=6|схема оценивания, стр. 6]] · [[_pdf/2011/0625_w11_er.pdf#page=7|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Электрическое поле, притяжение разноимённых зарядов и равновесие шаров.

**(a)** Complete the following statement about an electric field.

An electric field is a region in which  \_\_\_\_\_\_

experiences a  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(b)** Fig. 10.1 shows two identical light uncharged balls suspended on thin nylon threads.

![[0625-w11-21-p13-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 10.1**

Ball A is given a positive charge and ball B is given a negative charge.

In the space below, draw a diagram showing the positions that the balls and threads will take, now that the balls are charged. **[2]**

**(c)** On ball B in your diagram, use labelled arrows to show the directions of

**(i)** the electrostatic force on the ball (label it E),

**(ii)** the weight of the ball (label it W),

**(iii)** the tension force of the thread on the ball (label it T). **[2]**

**(d)** Ball B is in equilibrium. State the value of the resultant of forces E, W and T.

resultant =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** threads straight and equal angle (by eye) to vertical

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | (electric) charge OR charged body | B1 |
> |  | force | B1 |
> | (b) | A and B closer together allow touching | M1 |
> |  | threads straight and equal angle (by eye) to vertical | A1 |
> | (c) | E horizontal to left<br>W vertically down all 3 marked on his diagram –1 e.e.o.o.<br>T up thread | B2 |
> | (d) | zero or 0 or nothing | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> (a) It was rare to find a candidate who knew what an electric field was.
>
> (b) Almost all knew that A and B would be closer together. Unfortunately, once again, careless drawing often meant that credit could not be awarded; the threads needed to be straight and at equal angles to the vertical.
>
> (c) It was pleasing to see how many gained at least partial credit for the forces on B. The question only required the directions of the forces, so there was no penalty if the forces did not start from B. Forces E and W were frequently marked correctly, but T was often shown coming down from the support.
