# Question 12 (8 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2011 · Paper 2 — Core theory, вариант 1 · вопрос 12

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Построение графика распада и вычисление периода полураспада.

The count-rate from a sample of radioactive material is investigated by a team of scientists in a laboratory.

**(a)** With the radioactive sample in place, the count-rate from the sample is determined every 7 days, with the results shown below. The background count-rate has already been subtracted.

| time / days | 0 | 7 | 14 | 21 | 28 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| count-rate counts / s | 1000 | 550 | 300 | 160 | 90 |

On Fig. 12.1, plot a graph of count-rate from the sample against time. **[3]**

![[0625-w11-21-p15-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 12.1**

**(b)** **(i)** From your graph, find the time at which the count-rate is

**1.** 800 counts / s,  \_\_\_\_\_\_ days

**2.** 200 counts / s \_\_\_\_\_\_ days **[2]**

**(ii)** From the figures in **(b)(i)**, find the value of the half-life of the substance.

half-life =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ days **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)(ii)** 7.5–8.6 (days) e.c.f.

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | points correctly plotted (±½ small square) –1 e.e.o.o. | B2 |
> |  | smooth curve through candidate’s points (by eye) | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | 1. in range 2.2–3.0 | B1 |
> |  | 2. in range 18.0–19.0 | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | 2 half-lives | C1 |
> |  | (candidate’s 2 – candidate’s 1)/2 | C1 |
> |  | 7.5–8.6 (days) e.c.f. | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> (a) The graph was usually well plotted, and a reasonable curve drawn though the points. The most common point to be mis-plotted was the final one.
>
> (b) Candidates were usually able to take the readings from the graph to an acceptable level of accuracy, but the translation of them into the half-life of the material proved too challenging for many.
