> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> (a) The use of a plumb-line was only familiar to a small number, and some of these resorted to describing it, rather than naming it (this was marked correct). Appropriate alternatives would have been awarded credit, but by far the most popular answer was metre rule, which was not credited. A pleasing number knew how to find the position of the centre of mass of the triangular card. Even those who received no credit seemed to know it was something to do with drawing lines from A, B and C.
>
