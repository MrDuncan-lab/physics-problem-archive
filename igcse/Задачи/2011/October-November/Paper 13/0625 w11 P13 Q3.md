> Выбор метода измерения скорости звука по эху.

A student wishes to measure the speed of sound in air. She plans to measure the time between making a sound and hearing the echo from a cliff.

![[0625-w11-13-p3-f1.svg]]

She will use the equation: speed = $\dfrac{\text{distance}}{\text{time}}$ .

Which type of sound should she make and which distance should she use in her calculation?

|  | type of sound | distance to use |
|---|---|---|
| A B C D | continuous sound continuous sound short, sharp sound short, sharp sound | distance to cliff 2 distance to cliff × 2 distance to cliff 2 distance to cliff × 2 |
