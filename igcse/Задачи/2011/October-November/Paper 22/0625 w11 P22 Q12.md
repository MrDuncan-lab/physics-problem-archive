> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Ядерная реакция распада урана и значения массового и зарядового чисел.

Fig. 12.1 represents the radioactive decay of a$^{238}_{92}$U nucleus.

![[0625-w11-22-p16-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 12.1**

**(a)** In the space below, write the nuclear equation for this decay, including the numerical values of *A* and *Z*. **[3]**

**(b)** What does the letter *A* stand for? **[1]**

**(c)** What does the letter *Z* stand for? **[1]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | correct form of equation<br>i.e. $^{238}_{92}$ U →$^{\text{A}}_{\text{Z}}$ Th + $^{4}_{2}$ He | B1 |
> |  | A clearly 234 | B1 |
> |  | Z clearly 90 | B1 |
> | (b) | mass number OR nucleon number OR no. of nucleons OR no. of protons<br>+ neutrons | B1 |
> | (c) | atomic number OR proton number OR no. of protons/positive charges<br>IGNORE no. of electrons | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The whole of this question was pleasingly well done by the vast majority of candidates. There was a bit of confusion between mass number and atomic number (where candidates used that terminology), but otherwise most candidates are to be congratulated on having grasped this tricky concept.
