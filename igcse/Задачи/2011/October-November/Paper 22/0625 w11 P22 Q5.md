> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Термисторный электрический термометр и его калибровка.

The circuit in Fig. 5.1 is connected, and the ammeter reading is noted as the water is heated.

![[0625-w11-22-p6-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 5.1**

It is found that the ammeter reading increases as the temperature rises.

**(a)** **(i)** State what happens to the resistance of component X as the temperature rises. **[1]**

**(ii)** Suggest what component X might be. **[1]**

**(b)** This circuit is to be used as a thermometer.

**(i)** What must be done to calibrate it

**1.** at 0°C, **[2]**

**2.** at 100 °C? **[3]**

**(ii)** The resistance of X does not vary linearly with temperature between 0 °C and 100 °C.

How will this affect the use of this circuit as a thermometer? **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)(i)** pure OR melting
>
> **(b)(i)** pure OR standard pressure
>
> **(b)(ii)** unless more calibration points (between 0°C and 100°C)/other logic

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | decreases | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | thermistor | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | 1. put X in ice | M1 |
> |  | pure OR melting | A1 |
> |  | 2. put X in steam/boiling water | M1 |
> |  | pure OR standard pressure | A1 |
> |  | record ammeter reading mentioned somewhere in (b)(i) | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | idea of not very accurate/ not linear | M1 |
> |  | unless more calibration points (between 0°C and 100°C)/other logic | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> (a) Only a small proportion were able to work out that the resistance of X decreases, and even fewer could relate that to a thermistor.
>
> (b) The whole idea of calibrating X (rather than a liquid-in-glass thermometer) as a thermometer
>
> seemed to throw most candidates. A good proportion realised that the resulting thermometer would not be very accurate if resistance did not vary linearly with temperature.
