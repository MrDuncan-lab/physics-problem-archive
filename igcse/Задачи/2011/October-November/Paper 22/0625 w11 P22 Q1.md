> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Плотность оливкового масла и её изменение при нагревании.

**(a)** An empty glass beaker has a mass of 210 g.

When 200 cm$^{3}$ of olive oil is poured into the beaker, the total mass is 394 g.

Calculate the density of the olive oil.

density =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[4]**

**(b)** When the beaker and olive oil are heated, the olive oil expands.

What happens to the density of the olive oil? **[1]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** 0.92

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | D = M/V in any form, letters, words, numbers | C1 |
> |  | 394 – 210 OR 184 | C1 |
> |  | 0.92 | A1 |
> |  | g/cm$^{3}$<br>(accept correct conversion to kg/m$^{3}$, with unit) | B1 |
> | (b) | decreases | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> (a) Most candidates knew how to calculate density from mass/volume, but a sizeable minority forgot to do the necessary subtraction to find the correct mass.
>
> (b) A large proportion thought incorrectly that the density of olive oil would increase when it was heated.
