> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Взаимодействие заряженных шаров и притяжение воды к пластику.

**(a)** Two charged metal spheres are placed next to each other.

![[0625-w11-22-p11-f1.svg]]

+ indicates that the sphere is positively charged.

![[0625-w11-22-p11-f2.svg]]

– indicates that the sphere is negatively charged.

In the box alongside each pair of spheres, write “attract” or “repel” or “no effect” to describe the effect the spheres have on each other.

![[0625-w11-22-p11-f3.svg]]

![[0625-w11-22-p11-f4.svg]]

![[0625-w11-22-p11-f5.svg]]

**[3]**

**(b)** Water is flowing in a very narrow stream from a water tap (faucet). A negatively-charged plastic strip is held close to the stream of water, as shown in Fig. 8.1.

![[0625-w11-22-p11-f6.svg]]

**Fig. 8.1**

The stream of water moves towards the plastic strip.

In terms of the water at the point labelled X, suggest why this happens. **[3]**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> (a) This was well answered by most candidates.
>
> (b) Many candidates gained full credit on this part, having worked out that the water at X was positively charged. However, the means by which this charge was acquired, when described, was often most imaginative. It was decided not to penalise this, if the candidate knew about the positive charge. It still remains, though, that most candidates probably don’t really understand what is happening at X, and teachers should take note of this.
