> Измерение температуры после смешивания горячей и холодной воды.

In this experiment, you will investigate temperature changes when hot water and cold water are mixed.

You are provided with a supply of hot water and a supply of cold (room temperature) water.

**(a)** **(i)** Pour 100 cm$^{3}$ of cold water into the beaker labelled **A**.

Measure and record the temperature θ$_{\text{c}}$ of the water in beaker **A**.

θ$_{\text{c}}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

**(ii)** Measure and record the temperature θ$_{\text{h}}$of the hot water supplied.

θ$_{\text{h}}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

**(iii)** Add 100 cm$^{3}$ of the hot water to the water in beaker **A**.

Measure and record the temperature θ$_{\text{m}}$of the mixture of hot and cold water.

θ$_{\text{m}}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

**(iv)** State two precautions that you took to ensure the reliability of your value of the temperature θ$_{\text{m}}$.

1 \_\_\_\_\_\_

2 \_\_\_\_\_\_

**(v)** Calculate θ$_{\text{av}}$, the average of θ$_{\text{c}}$and θ$_{\text{h}}$.

average θ$_{\text{av}}$=  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[4]**

**(b)** **(i)** Empty the water from beaker **A**.

**(ii)** Repeat the steps **(a)(i)**, **(ii)**, **(iii)** and **(v)** using 130 cm$^{3}$ of cold water and 130 cm$^{3}$ of hot water.

θ$_{\text{c}}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

θ$_{\text{h}}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

θ$_{\text{m}}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

average θ$_{\text{av}}$=  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(c)** A student suggests that the temperature of the mixture θ$_{\text{m}}$should be the average of θ$_{\text{c}}$ and θ$_{\text{h}}$.

State whether your experimental results support this suggestion and justify your statement by reference to your results.

statement  \_\_\_\_\_\_

justification  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(d)** Suggest a practical reason in this experiment for the temperature of the mixture θ$_{\text{m}}$ being different from the average value θ$_{\text{av}}$, even when the precautions you have stated in **(a)(iv)** have been taken. **[1]**

**(e)** Suggest a modification to the experiment which should reduce the difference between θ$_{\text{m}}$and θ$_{\text{av}}$. **[1]**
