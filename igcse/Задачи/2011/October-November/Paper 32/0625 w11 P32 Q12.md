> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Протоны, нейтроны и изотопы стронция.

The most abundant stable isotope of strontium is strontium-88. Its nucleon number is 88 and its proton number is 38. In nuclide notation it is written$^{\text{x}}_{\text{y}}$Sr.

**(a)** Write down

**(i)** the values of x and y for strontium-88, x =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

y =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

**(ii)** the number of neutrons in a nucleus of strontium-88,  \_\_\_\_\_\_

**(iii)** the number of electrons in a neutral atom of strontium-88 \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

**(b)** Strontium-90 is a radioactive isotope produced by nuclear reactions. State how the structure of this isotope differs from that of strontium-88. **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** (strontium-90 has) 52 neutrons / 90 nucleons OR 2 more neutrons / nucleons

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | x = 88<br>AND y = 38 | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | 50 | B1 |
> | (a)(iii) | 38 | B1 |
> | (b) | <u>different numbers of neutrons / nucleons NOT different no of protons / electrons</u> | C1 |
> |  | (strontium-90 has) 52 neutrons / 90 nucleons OR 2 more neutrons / nucleons | A1 |
