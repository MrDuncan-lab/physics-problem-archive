> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Светозависимый резистор и логическая сигнализация освещения фотолаборатории.

The circuit shown in Fig. 10.1 was designed by an electronics student to provide a warning, by sounding the buzzer, that there is light in a photographic darkroom at times when it is in use.

![[0625-w11-32-p14-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 10.1**

When the darkroom is in use the switch is moved to position P. When it is not in use, the switch is in position Q. Depending upon the light level, the resistance of the light dependent resistor varies between much higher and much lower than the resistance of resistor R.

**(a)** State what happens to the resistance of the light dependent resistor when the light level changes from dark to light. **[1]**

**(b)** Write down whether the voltage level is high (logic 1) or low (logic 0) at the various points in the circuit in the following situations:

**(i)** at A light in the darkroom,  \_\_\_\_\_\_

dark in the darkroom,  \_\_\_\_\_\_

**(ii)** at B switch in position P,  \_\_\_\_\_\_

switch in position Q \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(c)** The output C of logic gate X is only high (logic 1) when both inputs A and B are high (logic 1). State which type of gate is logic gate X. **[1]**

**(d)** State the name of component Y. **[1]**

**(e)** Explain whether or not the student’s circuit achieves the aim of providing a warning that there is light in the darkroom when it is in use. **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(e)** yes / it would work
