> Температура смеси равных объёмов воды разных температур.

The IGCSE class is investigating temperature changes when cold water and hot water are mixed.

**(a)** A student records the temperature θ$_{\text{c}}$ of 100 cm$^{3}$ of cold water and the temperature θ$_{\text{h}}$ of 100 cm$^{3}$ of hot water.

![[0625-w11-61-p5-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 2.1**

Write down the temperature θ$_{\text{c}}$shown on the thermometer in Fig. 2.1.

θ$_{\text{c}}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(b)** The hot water is at a temperature θ$_{\text{h}}$= 86 °C.

Calculate θ$_{\text{av}}$, the average of θ$_{\text{c}}$and θ$_{\text{h}}$.

average θ$_{\text{av}}$=  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(c)** The student adds 100 cm$^{3}$ of the hot water to the cold water. She records the temperature θ$_{\text{m}}$ of the mixture of hot and cold water, θ$_{\text{m}}$ = 48 °C.

State two precautions (other than repeating the experiment) that the student could take to ensure the reliability of her value of the temperature θ$_{\text{m}}$.

1 \_\_\_\_\_\_

2 \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(d)** Suggest a practical reason in this experiment for the temperature of the mixture θ$_{\text{m}}$being different from the average value θ$_{\text{av}}$, even when the student has taken the precautions you suggested in **(c)**. **[1]**

**(e)** Suggest a modification to the experiment which should reduce the difference between θ$_{\text{m}}$ and θ$_{\text{av}}$. **[1]**

**(f)** The student decides to repeat the experiment to check the readings. Suggest one possible variable that she should keep constant. **[1]**
