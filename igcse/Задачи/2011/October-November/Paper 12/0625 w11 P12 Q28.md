> Изменение яркости ламп при изменении делителя напряжения.

The diagram shows a potential divider circuit with two identical lamps L$_{1}$ and L$_{2}$.

![[0625-w11-12-p15-f1.svg]]

The contact K is halfway between X and Y and the lamps are equally bright.

What will happen to the brightness of the lamps when contact K is moved a short distance towards X?

|  | lamp L$_{1}$ | lamp L$_{2}$ |
|---|---|---|
| A | brighter | brighter |
| B | brighter | dimmer |
| C | dimmer | brighter |
| D | dimmer | dimmer |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
