> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Движение молекул, давление газа и изменение состояния при охлаждении.

Fig. 5.1 shows a way of indicating the positions and direction of movement of some molecules in a gas at one instant.

![[0625-w05-3-p6-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 5.1**

**(a)** **(i)** Describe the movement of the molecules. **[1]**

**(ii)** Explain how the molecules exert a pressure on the container walls. **[1]**

**(b)** When the gas in the cylinder is heated, it pushes the piston further out of the cylinder.

State what happens to

**(i)** the average spacing of the molecules, **[1]**

**(ii)** the average speed of the molecules. **[1]**

**(c)** The gas shown in Fig. 5.1 is changed into a liquid and then into a solid by cooling.

Compare the gaseous and solid states in terms of

**(i)** the movement of the molecules, **[1]**

**(ii)** the average separation of the molecules. **[1]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | random | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | hit and rebound | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | increase or further apart | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | increase or move faster | B1 |
> | (c)(i) | random, fast in gas to vibration in solid | B1 |
> | (c)(ii) | long way apart in gas to very close or touching | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> (a)(i) Almost all gave random motion as a correct answer.
>
> (ii) Almost all understood that pressure was due to molecular collisions on the wall.
>
> (b)(i) Only a few incorrect answers.
>
> (ii) Very few incorrect answers.
>
> (c)(i) Quite a number of candidates misread the question as comparing gases and liquids, otherwise very well answered by the majority.
>
> (ii) Apart from the same misreading as in (i) this was also well answered by the majority.
