> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Электрическое поле между пластинами, ток и выделенная энергия.

Fig. 8.1 shows a high-voltage supply connected across two metal plates.

![[0625-w05-3-p10-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 8.1**

When the supply is switched on, an electric field is present between the plates.

**(a)** Explain what is meant by an *electric field* . **[2]**

**(b)** On Fig. 8.1, draw the electric field lines between the plates and indicate their direction by arrows. **[2]**

**(c)** The metal plates are now joined by a high-resistance wire. A charge of 0.060 C passes along the wire in 30 s. Calculate the reading on the ammeter.

ammeter reading =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(d)** The potential difference of the supply is re-set to 1500 V and the ammeter reading changes to 0.0080 A. Calculate the energy supplied in 10 s. Show your working.

energy =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(c)** I = 0.002 A or 2 mA
>
> **(d)** = 120 J

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | force is produced | B1 |
> |  | on any charge placed in the field | B1 |
> | (b) | at least 3 parallel, straight lines plate to plate, ignore end | B1 |
> |  | effect<br>at least one correct arrow, none wrong | B1 |
> | (c) | q = It or 0.06 = I x 30 | C1 |
> |  | I = 0.002 A or 2 mA | A1 |
> | (d) | E = Vit | C1 |
> |  | = 1500 x 0.008 x 10 | C1 |
> |  | = 120 J | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> (a) Few candidates gave a correct explanation in terms of an area where there was a force on a charge.
>
> (b) Fully correct answers were in the minority though many managed one mark for the direction. A significant number of candidates made no attempt whilst others had curved lines in the centre of the plates.
>
> (c) The majority of candidates scored both marks. The main errors were incorrect transposition of the formula, arithmetic errors and failing to give a correct unit.
>
> (d) Mostly correct with a correct unit.
>
> Answers: (c) 0.002 A; (d) 120 J.
