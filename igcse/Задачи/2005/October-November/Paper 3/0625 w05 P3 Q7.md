# Question 7 (8 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2005 · Paper 3 — Extended theory · вопрос 7  
Источник: [[_pdf/2005/0625_w05_qp_3.pdf#page=8|условие, стр. 8–9]] · [[_pdf/2005/0625_w05_ms_3.pdf#page=3|схема оценивания, стр. 3]] · [[_pdf/2005/0625_w05_er.pdf#page=1|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

Темы: [[3.2.1 Reflection of light]], [[3.2.3 Thin lenses]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Изображения в плоском зеркале и собирающей линзе.

**(a)** Fig. 7.1 shows two rays of light from a point O on an object. These rays are incident on a plane mirror.

![[0625-w05-3-p8-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 7.1**

**(i)** On Fig. 7.1, continue the paths of the two rays after they reach the mirror. Hence locate the image of the object O. Label the image I. **[2]**

**(ii)** Describe the nature of the image I. **[2]**

**(b)** Fig. 7.2 is drawn to scale. It shows an object PQ and a convex lens.

![[0625-w05-3-p8-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 7.2**

**(i)** On Fig. 7.2, draw two rays from the top of the object P that pass through the lens. Use these rays to locate the top of the image. Label this point T. **[3]**

**(ii)** On Fig. 7.2, draw an eye symbol to show the position from which the image T should be viewed. **[1]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | two approximately correct reflections | B1 |
> |  | evidence of projecting back to image or use of equal<br>distance from the mirror, object and image | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | virtual | B1 |
> |  | any one of upright, same size, same distance from mirror | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | ray 1 correct | B1 |
> |  | ray 2 correct | B1 |
> |  | image correctly located | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | eye symbol to right of lens | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> (a)(i) Less than half the candidates scored both marks. Large numbers continued the rays behind the mirror converging to the image point.
>
> (ii) Generally well answered but a number of wrong answers such as real and inverted were common.
>
> (b)(i) Well done by most candidates even when (a)(i) was wrong. However, there were the usual random wrong rays generally ending in an “image” to the right of the lens.
>
> (ii) If the image was in the correct place the eye was usually correctly to the right of the lens. A carry forward error for an image to the right of the lens was not allowed.
