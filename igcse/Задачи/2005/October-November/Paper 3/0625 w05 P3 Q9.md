# Question 9 (6 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2005 · Paper 3 — Extended theory · вопрос 9  
Источник: [[_pdf/2005/0625_w05_qp_3.pdf#page=11|условие, стр. 11]] · [[_pdf/2005/0625_w05_ms_3.pdf#page=4|схема оценивания, стр. 4]] · [[_pdf/2005/0625_w05_er.pdf#page=1|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Логические элементы НЕ-ИЛИ и НЕ-И в цифровой схеме.

**(a)** In the space provided, draw the symbol for a NOR gate. Label the inputs and the output. **[2]**

**(b)** State whether the output of a NOR gate will be high (ON) or low (OFF) when

**(i)** one input is high and one input is low,

**(ii)** both inputs are high. **[1]**

**(c)** Fig. 9.1 shows a digital circuit made from three NOT gates and one NAND gate.

![[0625-w05-3-p11-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 9.1**

**(i)** Write HIGH or LOW in each of the boxes on Fig. 9.1. **[2]**

**(ii)** State the effect on the output of changing both of the inputs. **[1]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | correct symbol | B1 |
> |  | correct labels | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | **(b)(i)** low, OFF or 0<br>low, OFF or 0 need both correct | B1 |
> | (c)(i) | need 4 boxes correct for 2 marks, -1 for e.e.o.e. | B2 |
> | (c)(ii) | no change | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> (a) At least half drew the correct shape and added 2 correct inputs and 1 correct output. Considerable numbers drew the shape of the AND gate, others drew only one input or failed to label the inputs and output.
>
> (b)(i)(ii) As expected many had one input high and one input low. The majority understood this well and so scored the mark.
>
> (c)(i) Some candidates did not know the action of the NOR gate or the NAND gate and so scored no marks. The majority had no difficulty in scoring both marks.
>
> (ii) Mostly correct; some also gave excellent unnecessary explanations.
