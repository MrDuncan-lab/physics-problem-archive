# Question 2 (6 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2005 · Paper 3 — Extended theory · вопрос 2  
Источник: [[_pdf/2005/0625_w05_qp_3.pdf#page=3|условие, стр. 3]] · [[_pdf/2005/0625_w05_ms_3.pdf#page=2|схема оценивания, стр. 2]] · [[_pdf/2005/0625_w05_er.pdf#page=1|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Условия равновесия метровой линейки и реакция опоры.

Fig. 2.1 shows apparatus for investigating moments of forces.

![[0625-w05-3-p3-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 2.1**

The uniform metre rule shown in Fig. 2.1 is in equilibrium.

**(a)** Write down two conditions for the metre rule to be in equilibrium.

condition 1  \_\_\_\_\_\_

condition 2  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(b)** Show that the value of the reading on the spring balance is 8.0 N. **[2]**

**(c)** The weight of the uniform metre rule is 1.5 N.

Calculate the force exerted by the pivot on the metre rule.

magnitude of force =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

direction of force  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** spring balance reading = 8.0 N

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | upwards force = downwards force or no resultant force | B1 |
> |  | opposing moments equal or A.C.M. = C. M. | B1 |
> | (b) | 30 x spring balance reading = 40 x 6.0 or equivalent | C1 |
> |  | spring balance reading = 8.0 N | A1 |
> | (c) | 0.5 N | B1 |
> |  | downwards | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> (a) Very poorly answered with forces and moments regularly confused. Clear statements of the equality of opposing moments and opposing forces were rare.
>
> (b) Generally well done. An approach based on moments was usually successful.
>
> (c) Very few candidates scored any marks on this difficult part of the question. Most just reversed the weight of the ruler and wrote 1.5 N upwards. Those who took moments about the 20 cm point on the ruler had little difficulty in getting the correct answer.
>
> Answer: (c) 0.5 N downwards.
