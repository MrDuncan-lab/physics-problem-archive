> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This was a question where candidates’ answers (or lack of them) suggested that they simply had not come across the reed relay/switch. Part (a) only required candidates to apply knowledge from elsewhere in the syllabus (currents in coils and magnetising of the reeds), but most could not do this. It is accepted that part (b) required careful thought, but few, even of the better candidates, were successful in doing this.
