> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выявление постоянного магнита по отталкиванию полюсов.

Two rods X and Y look the same.

![[0625-w05-1-p12-f1.svg]]

The N pole of a magnet is brought close, in turn, to each end of both rods. The results of these four actions are shown in the table.

| end tested | result |
|---|---|
| P | attraction |
| Q | attraction |
| R | attraction |
| S | repulsion |

Which of the rods is a permanent magnet?

**A** neither of the rods  
**B** both of the rods  
**C** rod X only  
**D** rod Y only

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
