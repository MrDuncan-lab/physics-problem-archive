Источник: [[_pdf/2005/0625_w05_qp_1.pdf#page=11|условие, стр. 11]] · [[_pdf/2005/0625_w05_ms_1.pdf|схема оценивания]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Свойства действительного изображения свечи на экране.

A thin converging lens is used to produce, on a screen, a focused image of a candle.

![[0625-w05-1-p11-f1.png]]

The screen and the lens are moved back and forth and various focused images are produced on the screen.

Which statement is always true?

**A** The image is at the principal focus (focal point) of the lens.  
**B** The image is bigger than the object.  
**C** The image is closer to the lens than the object is.  
**D** The image is inverted.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
