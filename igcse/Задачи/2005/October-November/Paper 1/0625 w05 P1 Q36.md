> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Периодическое отклонение амперметра от движущегося магнита.

A magnet is suspended from a spring so that it can move freely inside a coil which is connected

to a sensitive centre-zero ammeter.

![[0625-w05-1-p16-f1.svg]]

What does the ammeter show when the magnet vibrates slowly up and down?

**A** a reading constantly changing from left to right and right to left  
**B** a steady reading to the left  
**C** a steady reading to the right  
**D** a steady zero reading

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**
