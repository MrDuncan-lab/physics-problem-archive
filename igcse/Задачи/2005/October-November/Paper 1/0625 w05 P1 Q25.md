> Расстояние до скалы по времени звукового эха.

When the horn on a ship is sounded, the passengers hear an echo from a cliff after 4.0 s.

If the speed of sound is 340 m / s, how far away is the cliff?

**A** 170 m  
**B** 340 m  
**C** 680 m  
**D** 1360 m

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
