> Поглощение бета-излучения толстым алюминием.

A radioactive source emits radiation that can pass through a sheet of paper but not through thick

aluminium.

![[0625-w05-1-p17-f1.svg]]

What does this show about the radiation?

**A** It is α-particles.  
**B** It is β-particles.  
**C** It is γ-rays.  
**D** It is a mixture of α-particles and γ-rays.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
