> Число нейтронов и протонов после бета-распада.

An unstable nucleus has 145 neutrons and 92 protons. It emits a β-particle.

How many neutrons and protons does the nucleus have after emitting the β-particle?

|  | neutrons | protons |
|---|---|---|
| A | 144 | 92 |
| B | 144 | 93 |
| C | 145 | 91 |
| D | 145 | 93 |

> [!answer]- Ответ
