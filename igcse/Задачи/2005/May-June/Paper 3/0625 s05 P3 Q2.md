# Question 2 (6 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2005 · Paper 3 — Extended theory · вопрос 2  
Источник: [[_pdf/2005/0625_s05_qp_3.pdf#page=4|условие, стр. 4]] · [[_pdf/2005/0625_s05_ms_3.pdf#page=4|схема оценивания, стр. 4]] · [[_pdf/2005/0625_s05_er.pdf#page=1|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Точное измерение периода маятника и изменение потенциальной энергии.

Fig. 2.1 shows a simple pendulum that swings backwards and forwards between P and Q.

![[0625-s05-3-p4-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 2.1**

**(a)** The time taken for the pendulum to swing from P to Q is approximately 0.5 s.

Describe how you would determine this time as accurately as possible. **[2]**

**(b)** **(i)** State the two vertical forces acting on the pendulum bob when it is at position R.

1 \_\_\_\_\_\_

2 \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(ii)** The pendulum bob moves along the arc of a circle. State the direction of the resultant of the two forces in **(i)**. **[1]**

**(c)** The mass of the bob is 0.2 kg. During the swing it moves so that P is 0.05 m higher than R.

Calculate the increase in potential energy of the pendulum bob between R and P.

potential energy =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** time divided by \[2 x number of swings\]
>
> **(c)** = 0.1 J

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | time a number of swings (if number stated, \>5) | M1 |
> |  | time divided by \[2 x number of swings\] | A1 |
> | (b)(i) | weight of gravity and tension | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | force towards centre of circular motion or towards support point | B1 |
> | (c) | p.e. = mgh or 0.2 x 10 x 0.05 | C1 |
> |  | = 0.1 J | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> (a) Far too many tried to measure the time for one swing or worse the time from P to Q, in spite of the swing time being given as approximately 0.5 s and the question asking for an accurate measurement. Only a small minority realised that the time from P to Q was half of the periodic time and divided accordingly.
>
> (b)(i) Wrong answers included upthrust and air resistance.
>
> (ii) The majority of candidates realised that this was the centripetal force and gave the correct direction, but quite a number said there was no reaction and others said “towards the centre” which could have been the point R and was therefore not allowed.
>
> (c) At least half of the entry scored both marks for a correct numerical answer and a correct unit. The most common error was to multiply 0.2 by 0.05, omitting the 10. Various wrong units were seen, the most common being newtons.
>
> Answer: (c) 0.1 J.
