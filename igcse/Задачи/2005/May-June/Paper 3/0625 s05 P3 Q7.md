> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Спектр электромагнитных волн и частота гамма-лучей.

Fig. 7.1 shows the parts of the electromagnetic spectrum.

![[0625-s05-3-p10-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 7.1**

**(a)** Name one type of radiation that has

**(i)** a higher frequency than ultra-violet, **[1]**

**(ii)** a longer wavelength than visible light. **[1]**

**(b)** Some γ-rays emitted from a radioactive source have a speed in air of 3.0 x 10$^{8}$ m/s and a wavelength of 1.0 x 10$^{\text{–12}}$ m.

Calculate the frequency of the γ-rays.

frequency =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(c)** State the approximate speed of infra-red waves in air. **[1]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** = 3 x 10$^{20}$ Hz

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | x-rays or gamma rays | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | infra red or radio | B1 |
> | (b) | f = v/ λ or 3 x 10$^{8}$/ 1 x 10$^{-12}$ | C1 |
> |  | = 3 x 10$^{20}$ Hz | A1 |
> | (c) | 3 x 10$^{8}$ m/s | 1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> (a)(i)(ii) In both parts the majority gave the correct answer. The expected wrong answer by reversing the answers to the parts was frequently seen.
>
> (b) Most candidates did well in dealing with powers of 10, and in transposing the formula. Quite a number gave a wrong unit.
>
> (c) The majority understood that the value required was the same as that for the speed of light.
>
> Answer: (b) 3 x 10$^{20}$ Hz.
