> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Изменение положения железных стержней при включении электромагнитов.

Two circuits are set up as shown. The iron rods are placed close together, and are free to move.

![[0625-s05-1-p16-f2.svg]]

What happens to the size of the gap at X when switch S is closed?

**A** It decreases.  
**B** It decreases then increases.  
**C** It increases.  
**D** It does not change.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> C was chosen by most who were incorrect, failing to appreciate that, although identical, the coils had opposite ends facing each other so would attract.
