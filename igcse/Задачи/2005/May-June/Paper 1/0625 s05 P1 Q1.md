Источник: [[_pdf/2005/0625_s05_qp_1.pdf#page=2|условие, стр. 2]] · [[_pdf/2005/0625_s05_ms_1.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2005/0625_s05_er.pdf#page=1|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Измерения размеров плитки для расчёта её площади.

A decorator wishes to calculate the area of a bathroom tile so that he can estimate the amount of adhesive that he needs to buy.

What must he use?

**A** a measuring cylinder only  
**B** a ruler only  
**C** a measuring cylinder and a clock only  
**D** a measuring cylinder and a ruler only

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The simple question was answered correctly by 89% of candidates, although 9% opted for D, perhaps thinking of measuring the volume of adhesive as well as the area of the room.
