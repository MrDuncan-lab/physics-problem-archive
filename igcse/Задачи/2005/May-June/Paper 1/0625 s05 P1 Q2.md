> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнение ускорений падающих шаров разной массы.

The three balls shown are dropped from a bench.

![[0625-s05-1-p2-f1.svg]]

Which balls have the same acceleration?

**A** aluminium and lead only  
**B** aluminium and wood only  
**C** lead and wood only  
**D** aluminium, lead and wood

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> In this question distractor A was popular, possibly showing a perceived link between density and acceleration (the short distance implied by mention of the bench made air resistance negligible).
