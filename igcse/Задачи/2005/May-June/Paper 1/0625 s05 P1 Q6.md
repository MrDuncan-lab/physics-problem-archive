> Вычисление плотности жидкости с учётом массы мензурки.

The masses of a measuring cylinder before and after pouring some liquid into it are shown in the diagram.

![[0625-s05-1-p3-f2.svg]]

What is the density of the liquid?

**A** $\dfrac{100}{120}$ g / cm$^{3}$  
**B** $\dfrac{100}{140}$ g / cm$^{3}$  
**C** $\dfrac{180}{120}$ g / cm$^{3}$  
**D** $\dfrac{180}{140}$ g / cm$^{3}$

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> D was popular, with candidates failing to subtract the cylinder’s mass.
