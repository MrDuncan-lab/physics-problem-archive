> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Путь разгоняющегося автомобиля по графику скорости.

A car accelerates from traffic lights. The graph shows how the car’s speed changes with time.

![[0625-s05-1-p2-f2.svg]]

How far does the car travel before it reaches a steady speed?

**A** 10 m  
**B** 20 m  
**C** 100 m  
**D** 200 m

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This caused almost a third to opt for D, failing to halve the distance.
