> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Вычисление ширины ущелья по времени возвращения эха.

To estimate the width of a valley, a climber starts a stopwatch as he shouts. He hears an echo

from the opposite side of the valley after 1.0 s.

![[0625-s05-1-p11-f1.svg]]

The sound travels at 340 m / s.

What is the width of the valley?

**A** 85 m  
**B** 170 m  
**C** 340 m  
**D** 680 m

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> A similar proportion to Question 24 made the classic mistake of overlooking the return time of the sound in this question.
