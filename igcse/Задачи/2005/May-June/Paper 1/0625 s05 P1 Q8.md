> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Масса груза по пропорциональности удлинения пружины.

Objects with different masses are hung on a 10 cm spring. The diagram shows how much the spring stretches.

![[0625-s05-1-p4-f2.svg]]

The extension of the spring is directly proportional to the mass hung on it.

What is the mass of object M?

**A** 110 g  
**B** 150 g  
**C** 200 g  
**D** 300 g

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Over a third believed B correct, failing to consider the original spring length.
