> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Распределение тока по трём амперметрам.

The diagram shows a circuit with three ammeters, X, Y and Z.

![[0625-s05-1-p14-f1.svg]]

Which set of readings is possible?

|  | X | Y | Z |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | 2 A | 3 A | 5 A |
| B | 3 A | 2 A | 5 A |
| C | 3 A | 3 A | 3 A |
| D | 5 A | 2 A | 3 A |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This deductive question showed nearly a fifth of candidates falling back on ‘the current is the same everywhere’ and choosing C.
