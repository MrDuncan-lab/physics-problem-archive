> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определение направления и величины равнодействующей сил.

A girl and a boy are pulling in opposite directions on a rope. The forces acting on the rope are shown in the diagram.

![[0625-s05-1-p4-f1.svg]]

Which single force has the same effect as the two forces shown?

**A** 50 N acting towards the girl  
**B** 350 N acting towards the girl  
**C** 50 N acting towards the boy  
**D** 350 N acting towards the boy

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This led a fair proportion to choose C, possibly looking for the force needed to balance the two shown.
