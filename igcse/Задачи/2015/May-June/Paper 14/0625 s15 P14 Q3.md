> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Расстояние до стены по времени задержки эха.

A tennis player hits a ball hard and 0.40 s later hears the echo from a wall.

![[0625-s15-14-p3-f1.svg]]

The speed of sound in air is 330 m / s.

How far away is the player from the wall?

**A** 66 m  
**B** 132 m  
**C** 264 m  
**D** 825 m

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> In this question concerning an echo, a large proportion of candidates made the mistake of not halving the distance calculated from the given speed and time.
