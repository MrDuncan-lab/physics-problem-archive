> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Единицы электродвижущей силы и разности потенциалов.

Which row gives the unit for electromotive force (e.m.f.) and the unit for potential difference

(p.d.)?

|  | electromotive force | potential difference |
|---|---|---|
| A | newton | joule |
| B | newton | volt |
| C | volt | joule |
| D | volt | volt |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The popularity of option B suggests that many candidates were unfamiliar with the term ‘electromotive force’.
