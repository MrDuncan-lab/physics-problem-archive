> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Описание движения тела по графику расстояния от времени.

The diagram shows the distance-time graph of an object.

![[0625-s15-14-p2-f2.svg]]

Which statement describes the object?

**A** It is accelerating.  
**B** It is moving at a constant speed.  
**C** It is slowing down.  
**D** It is stationary.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Many of the weaker candidates did not notice that the graph was distance-time, not speed-time, therefore believing that the object was accelerating.
