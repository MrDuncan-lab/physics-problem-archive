> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Время падения и изменение скорости по графику расстояния.

Fig. 1.1 shows a distance-time graph for a falling object.

![[0625-s15-22-p2-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 1.1**

**(a)** Use this graph to find the time it takes the object to fall from 0.60 m to 1.60 m.

time =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ s **[2]**

**(b)** State and explain what the graph shows about the motion of the falling object. **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** 0.28 (s)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | 0.38 and 0.66 seen<br>OR correct vertical lines/marks on axes ± ½ square | C1 |
> |  | 0.28 (s) | A1 |
> | (b) | speed is changing/increasing OR it is accelerating | B1 |
> |  | OR accelerating at first (when curved line) then steady speed (when line is<br>straight)<br>(because) graph is a curve OR gradient is changing<br>OR different distances travelled in equal time intervals<br>OR accept ‘due to force of gravity’ | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> (a) The majority of candidates gave good responses, but a significant number lost a mark through poor accuracy in determining times from the graph.
>
> (b) The more able candidates explained the motion for a distance-time graph. However, most described a velocity-time graph, and some did not respond.
