> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определение центра масс картона с помощью отвеса.

Fig. 2.1 shows an irregularly shaped piece of card.

![[0625-s15-22-p3-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 2.1**

A student is asked to find the centre of mass of the card. The student is provided with a clamp and stand, a small mass attached to a thin string and a long pin.

**(a)** Describe the procedure for finding the centre of mass of the card. You may draw a diagram. **[3]**

**(b)** What simple test can be carried out to confirm that the centre of mass has been found? **[1]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | Any three from: max.<br>• string with mass on pin in front of card<br>• hang card on pin from a hole<br>• make sure card can swing freely<br>• (place plumb line on pin) and mark line/position on card<br>• repeat using at least one more hole<br>• where lines cross is centre of mass | B3 |
> | (b) | card will balance at that point<br>OR repeat using third hole<br>accept place pivot beneath centre of mass | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> (a) The majority of candidates had either seen or carried out this experiment, but explaining the procedure adequately proved challenging. There was a lack of precision and detail in many cases.
>
> (b) Many candidates were able to outline a simple test to confirm the position of the centre of mass; some responses were inadequately expressed.
