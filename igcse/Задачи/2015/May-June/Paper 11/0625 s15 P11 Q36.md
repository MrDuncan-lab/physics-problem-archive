> Входное напряжение трансформатора по числам витков.

A transformer has 1200 turns on its primary coil and 400 turns on its secondary coil. An output

voltage of 90 V is induced across the secondary coil.

![[0625-s15-11-p17-f1.svg]]

What is the input voltage of the transformer?

**A** 30 V  
**B** 90 V  
**C** 270 V  
**D** 1200 V

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
