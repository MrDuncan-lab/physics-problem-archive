> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Масса и вес поролона после сжатия.

Diagram 1 shows a piece of foam rubber that contains many pockets of air. Diagram 2 shows the same piece of foam rubber after it has been compressed so that its volume decreases.

![[0625-s15-11-p3-f2.svg]]

What happens to the mass and to the weight of the foam rubber when it is compressed?

|  | mass | weight |
|---|---|---|
| A | increases | increases |
| B | increases | no change |
| C | no change | increases |
| D | no change | no change |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Although a large proportion of candidates of all abilities coped well with this question, a common error for the less able was to confuse weight with density, thus arriving at option C.
