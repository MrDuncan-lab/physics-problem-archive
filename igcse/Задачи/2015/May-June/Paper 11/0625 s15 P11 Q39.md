> Вычитание фонового счёта из активности источника.

In a laboratory, a detector of ionising radiation records an average background count rate of

8 counts per second.

![[0625-s15-11-p18-f1.svg]]

A radioactive source is now placed close to the detector. The count rate on the detector rises to 200 counts per second.

![[0625-s15-11-p18-f2.svg]]

What is the count rate due to radiation from the radioactive source?

**A** 25 counts / s  
**B** 192 counts / s  
**C** 200 counts / s  
**D** 208 counts / s

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
