> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определение плотности металла для установления его вида.

**(a)** A student is asked to find the density of a small block of metal, as shown in Fig. 2.1.

![[0625-s15-23-p4-f1.png]]

**Fig. 2.1**

Use words from the list below to complete the following sentences.

balance beaker measuring cylinder protractor rule thermometer

**(i)** The student should use a  \_\_\_\_\_\_ to measure the length, width and

height of the metal block.

**(ii)** The student should use a  \_\_\_\_\_\_ to find the mass of the metal

block. **[2]**

**(b)** A jeweller has a small block of metal. The block has a mass of 15.2 g and a volume of 1.36 cm$^{3}$.

**(i)** Calculate the density of the metal.

density =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ g / cm$^{3}$ **[3]**

**(ii)** The jeweller looks up the values for the density of various metals and produces a table, part of which is shown below.

| metal | density g / cm$^{3}$ |
|---|---|
| gold | 19.3 |
| lead | 11.3 |
| platinum | 21.5 |
| silver | 10.5 |

Using your answer to **(b)(i)** and the information in the table, state which metal the block is most likely to be made from. **[1]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)(i)** rule(r)
>
> **(a)(ii)** balance
>
> **(b)(i)** 11.2 (g / cm$^{3}$) accept 11

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | rule(r) | A1 |
> | (a)(ii) | balance | A1 |
> | (b)(i) | (density =) mass ÷ volume in any form: symbols, words, numbers | C1 |
> |  | 15.2 ÷ 1.36 | C1 |
> |  | 11.2 (g / cm$^{3}$) accept 11 | A1 |
> | (b)(ii) | lead, ecf from (b) (i) | B1 |
