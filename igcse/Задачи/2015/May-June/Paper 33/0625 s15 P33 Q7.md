# Question 7 (8 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2015 · Paper 3 — Extended theory, вариант 3 · вопрос 7  
Источник: [[_pdf/2015/0625_s15_qp_33.pdf#page=12|условие, стр. 12]] · [[_pdf/2015/0625_s15_ms_33.pdf#page=6|схема оценивания, стр. 6]] · [[_pdf/2015/0625_s15_er.pdf#page=28|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 s15 P33]]  
Темы: [[3.4 Sound]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Эхолокация летучей мыши и частота ультразвука.

**(a)** A sound wave in air consists of alternate compressions and rarefactions along its path.

**(i)** Explain how a compression differs from a rarefaction. **[1]**

**(ii)** Explain, in terms of compressions, what is meant by

**1.** the wavelength of the sound, **[1]**

**2.** the frequency of the sound. **[1]**

**(b)** At night, bats emit pulses of sound to detect obstacles and prey. The speed of sound in air is 340 m / s.

**(i)** A bat emits a pulse of sound of wavelength 0.0085 m.

Calculate the frequency of the sound.

frequency =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(ii)** State why this sound cannot be heard by human beings. **[1]**

**(iii)** The pulse of sound hits a stationary object and is reflected back to the bat. The pulse is received by the bat 0.12 s after it was emitted.

Calculate the distance travelled by the pulse of sound during this time.

distance =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)(i)** 40 000 Hz OR 40 kHz
>
> **(b)(iii)** 41 m or 40.8 m

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | (compression is a) region of higher pressure<br>OR region where air layers/particles/molecules are closer | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | 1. distance between (two successive / adjacent) compressions | B1 |
> |  | 2. number of compressions (passing a point) per second / unit time<br>OR number of compressions emitted per second/unit time | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | (f = ) v / λ OR 340 / 0.0085 | C1 |
> |  | 40 000 Hz OR 40 kHz | A1 |
> | (b)(ii) | frequency/pitch is above the upper threshold for human hearing / 20 kHz<br>OR it is ultrasound | B1 |
> | (b)(iii) | (d =) vt in any form: words, symbols, numbers | C1 |
> |  | 41 m or 40.8 m | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> (a) (i) There were many acceptable explanations given but some did not answer in a way that revealed the difference asked for.
>
> (ii) In both parts of this section, there were some good answers. Candidates did not always give their explanations in terms of compressions.
>
> (b) (i) This part was in the main correctly answered. The most common error was to multiply the wavelength of the sound by its speed.
>
> (ii) Most candidates realised what was required here and were able to state why the sound could not be heard.
>
> (iii) The correct calculation was commonly performed and full credit was commonly awarded. The most frequently made error involved the introduction of a factor of two either into the formula or into the final answer.
