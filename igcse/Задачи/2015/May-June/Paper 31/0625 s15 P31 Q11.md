# Question 11 (7 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2015 · Paper 3 — Extended theory, вариант 1 · вопрос 11  
Источник: [[_pdf/2015/0625_s15_qp_31.pdf#page=20|условие, стр. 20]] · [[_pdf/2015/0625_s15_ms_31.pdf#page=7|схема оценивания, стр. 7]] · [[_pdf/2015/0625_s15_er.pdf#page=19|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Отклонение радиоактивных частиц в полях и ядерное уравнение.

**(a)** State the nature of γ-rays. **[1]**

**(b)** A beam of α-particles and β-particles passes, in a vacuum, between the poles of a strong magnet.

Compare the deflections of the paths of the two types of particle. **[2]**

**(c)** A beam of β-particles passes, in a vacuum, through the electric field between a pair of oppositely charged metal plates.

Describe the path of the particles. **[2]**

**(d)** The nuclear equation shows the decay of an isotope of polonium.

$^{\text{A}}_{\text{Z}}$ Po $^{206}_{82}$ Pb +$^{4}_{2}$ X

**(i)** State the nature of X. **[1]**

**(ii)** Calculate the values of A and Z.

A =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ Z =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | electromagnetic (waves / radiation / rays / spectrum)<br>OR (high energy) photons | B1 |
> | (b) | α and β deflected in opposite directions | B1 |
> |  | any 1 from:<br>• β deflected more (than α)<br>• deflections perpendicular to field direction and to paths of particle<br>• paths (of particles) are curves / circular / arcs | B1 |
> | (c) | curved path | B1 |
> |  | (deflected/attracted) towards positively charged plate<br>OR in opposite direction to field | B1 |
> | (d)(i) | α<u>-particle OR helium nucleus OR 2 protons + 2 neutrons</u> | B1 |
> | (d)(ii) | A = 210 Z = 84 | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> (a) Many answers described ‘properties’ rather than giving the ‘nature’.
>
> (b) Candidates needed to convey two ideas, first for suggesting opposite deflections of the two types of particle, and the second that β-particles underwent greater deflection than α-particles. Many succeeded in both respects, but some suggested deflections towards positive and negative poles of the magnet.
>
> (c) Better attempts were in general made here than with (b). References to α-particles were sometimes made, but ignored.
>
> (d) (i) Credit was given for references to α-particle or helium nucleus. Helium atom or simply helium were not credited.
>
> (ii) Most candidates could write down the correct number.
