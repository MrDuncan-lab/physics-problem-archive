> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Изменение числа протонов и нейтронов после альфа распада.

The nucleus of an americium atom contains 146 neutrons and 95 protons. It decays by emitting

an α-particle.

How many neutrons and how many protons remain in the nucleus when this form of americium decays?

|  | number of neutrons remaining | number of protons remaining |
|---|---|---|
| A | 142 | 93 |
| B | 142 | 95 |
| C | 144 | 93 |
| D | 144 | 95 |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> A common mistake here was to confuse the loss of four nucleons when an α-particle is emitted with the loss of two neutrons. This led candidates to choose option A in relatively large numbers.
