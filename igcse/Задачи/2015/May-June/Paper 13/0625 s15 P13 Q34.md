> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сила взаимодействия магнитного стержня и катушки с током.

A student investigates the force on a bar magnet placed near a current-carrying coil. She carries

out three different experiments.

![[0625-s15-13-p15-f2.svg]]

In experiment 1, the magnet is attracted to the coil.

Which row shows what happens in the other two experiments?

|  | experiment 2 | experiment 3 |
|---|---|---|
| A | magnet attracted | magnet attracted |
| B | magnet attracted | magnet repelled |
| C | magnet repelled | magnet attracted |
| D | magnet repelled | magnet repelled |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The popularity of option B with less able candidates suggests that they did not notice that the cell in experiments 2 and 3 had been turned round compared with experiment 1.
