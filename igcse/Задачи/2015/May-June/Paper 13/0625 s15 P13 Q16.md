> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Высокая скорость переноса тепла излучением.

One method of heat transfer involves the energy travelling at a much greater speed than in other

methods.

What is the name of this method?

**A** conduction  
**B** convection  
**C** evaporation  
**D** radiation

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
