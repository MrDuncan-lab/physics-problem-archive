> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Средняя скорость автомобиля по пути и времени поездки.

A car travels 100 km. The journey takes two hours. The highest speed of the car is 80 km / h, and the lowest speed is 40 km / h.

What is the average speed for the journey?

**A** 40 km / h  
**B** 50 km / h  
**C** 60 km / h  
**D** 120 km / h

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question involved calculating average speed, and many weaker candidates used the average value of the maximum and minimum speed to obtain option C rather than dividing the total distance by the total time.
