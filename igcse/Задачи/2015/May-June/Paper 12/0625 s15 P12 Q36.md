> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Вычисление числа витков повышающего трансформатора.

A transformer has 1000 turns on its primary coil. An input voltage of 12 V is applied to the primary

coil, and an output voltage of 120 V is induced across the secondary coil.

![[0625-s15-12-p18-f2.svg]]

How many turns are on the secondary coil of the transformer?

**A** 100  
**B** 120  
**C** 1000  
**D** 10 000

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
