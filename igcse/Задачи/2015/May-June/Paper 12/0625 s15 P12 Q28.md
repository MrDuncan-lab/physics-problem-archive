> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Напряжение на резисторе в электрической цепи.

The circuit diagram shows a 4.0 Ω resistor and an 8.0 Ω resistor connected to a 6.0 V battery.

![[0625-s15-12-p14-f1.svg]]

What is the potential difference (p.d.) across the 4.0 Ω resistor?

**A** 0.5 V  
**B** 2.0 V  
**C** 4.0 V  
**D** 6.0 V

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Many of the less able candidates believed that the p.d. across the 4.0 Ω resistor was 2.0 V. It seems likely that they were confusing series and parallel circuits and so thought that the 6.0 V would be ‘shared’ between the resistors.
