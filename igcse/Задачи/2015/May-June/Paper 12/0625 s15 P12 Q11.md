> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Положение ртути в барометре при снижении атмосферного давления.

The diagram shows a simple mercury barometer, used to measure atmospheric pressure.

![[0625-s15-12-p6-f1.svg]]

Atmospheric pressure decreases.

Which row states what happens to the pressure at point P and what happens to the level L?

|  | pressure at P | level L |
|---|---|---|
| A | decreases | falls |
| B | decreases | rises |
| C | stays the same | falls |
| D | stays the same | rises |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question on the barometer showed that the great majority of candidates knew that the level would fall, but more than half of these also believed that the pressure inside the tube would fall; the concept of the (Torricellian) vacuum was unfamiliar.
