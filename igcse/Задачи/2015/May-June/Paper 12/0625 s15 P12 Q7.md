> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Расчёт неизвестного груза по закону Гука.

Different weights are hung from a spring. The diagram shows the original length of the spring, and the lengths when different weights are added.

![[0625-s15-12-p4-f1.svg]]

The extension of the spring is directly proportional to the weight hung from it.

What is the weight of W?

**A** 30 N  
**B** 35 N  
**C** 40 N  
**D** 45 N

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Able candidates coped easily with this question on extension of a spring, but almost as many weaker ones opted for A as chose the correct response.
