> Амплитуда звуковых волн при увеличении громкости.

A quiet sound is produced by a loudspeaker. The loudness of the sound is increased.

Which property of the sound wave is increased?

**A** amplitude  
**B** frequency  
**C** speed  
**D** wavelength
