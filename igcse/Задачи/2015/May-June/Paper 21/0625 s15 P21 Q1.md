> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Измерение толщины монеты по стопке с учётом погрешности.

A student has a stack of 20 identical coins.

Fig. 1.1 shows the student measuring the height of the stack using a ruler.

![[0625-s15-21-p2-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 1.1**

**(a)** With his eye at the position shown, the student’s measurement of the height of the stack is 6.8 cm.

Suggest two reasons why the student’s measurement is inaccurate.

1 \_\_\_\_\_\_

2 \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(b)** Another student correctly determines the height of the stack as 7.7 cm.

Calculate the average thickness of one coin.

thickness =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm **[2]**

**(c)** The mass of a single coin is 12 g.

State this mass in kg.

mass =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ kg **[1]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** 0.385 (cm) OR 0.39 (cm)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | any two from:<br>• gap between ruler and stack<br>• eye not perpendicular/ level with top of stack<br>• zero error of ruler | B2 |
> | (b) | 7.7 ÷ 20 | C1 |
> |  | 0.385 (cm) OR 0.39 (cm) | A1 |
> | (c) | 0.012 (kg) c.a.o. | B1 |
