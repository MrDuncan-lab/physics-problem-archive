> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Превращения энергии батарейки работающих часов.

Fig. 4.1 shows a battery-operated alarm clock.

![[0625-s15-21-p9-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 4.1**

Use words from the box to complete the sentences.

chemical electrical kinetic light sound thermal

The battery stores  \_\_\_\_\_\_ energy. When the battery is first connected, electrical

energy is transferred to  \_\_\_\_\_\_ energy of the clock’s hands. Some of the electrical

energy is transferred to the surroundings as  \_\_\_\_\_\_ energy. When the alarm bell

rings, electrical energy is transferred to  \_\_\_\_\_\_ energy. **[4]**
