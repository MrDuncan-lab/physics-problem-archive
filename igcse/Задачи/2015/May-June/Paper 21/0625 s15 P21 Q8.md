> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Преломление и полное внутреннее отражение в отражателе.

**(a)** A ray of red light passes through a glass block, as shown in Fig. 8.1.

![[0625-s15-21-p13-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 8.1**

**(i)** On Fig. 8.1, clearly label the angle of refraction. **[1]**

**(ii)** State the name given to the dashed line drawn at 90° to the boundary. **[1]**

**(b)** A roadside reflector is made of plastic. It reflects the light from car headlamps.

Fig. 8.2 shows part of the path of a ray of light through the reflector.

![[0625-s15-21-p13-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 8.2**

The critical angle for the plastic is less than 45°.

**(i)** On Fig. 8.2, complete the path of the ray of light. **[1]**

**(ii)** State the term used to describe this type of reflection. **[1]**

**(iii)** Explain why the ray turns though 90° at A. **[1]**
