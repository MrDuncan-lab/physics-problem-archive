# Question 7 (5 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2015 · Paper 2 — Core theory, вариант 3 · вопрос 7

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Изображение магнитных силовых линий и определение их направления компасом.

Fig. 7.1 shows an experiment to identify the pattern and direction of field lines around a bar magnet.

![[0625-w15-23-p12-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 7.1**

The bar magnet is placed on a sheet of paper. A plotting compass is placed in each of the four positions labelled A, B, C and D.

The plotting compass is a small pivoted magnet, as shown in Fig. 7.2.

![[0625-w15-23-p12-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 7.2**

**(a)** In each of positions A, B, C and D on Fig. 7.1, carefully draw an arrow showing the position of the pointer. Ignore the magnetic field due to the surroundings. **[3]**

**(b)** On Fig. 7.1, carefully draw two complete magnetic field lines, one through position B and the other through position D. The lines you draw should start and finish on the bar magnet. **[1]**

**(c)** State the material from which a permanent magnet is made. **[1]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | A: arrow pointing left / away from N | B1 |
> |  | C: arrow pointing left / same as A | B1 |
> |  | B and D: arrow pointing right | B1 |
> | (b) | correct field lines carefully drawn through B and D | B1 |
> | (c) | steel | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> (a) The majority of candidates gained some credit for this question. The most common mistake was to have the arrows in B and D pointing toward the centre of the magnet rather than to the S-pole.
>
> (b) This question was understood and answered correctly by most candidates.
>
> (c) While a majority of candidates answered corrected, the most common incorrect answer given was iron.
