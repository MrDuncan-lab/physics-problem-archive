# Question 10 (7 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2015 · Paper 2 — Core theory, вариант 3 · вопрос 10

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Фоторезистор фотоаппарата и построение изображения собирающей линзой.

A camera has a circuit containing a light-dependent resistor (LDR). Fig. 10.1 shows part of this circuit.

![[0625-w15-23-p18-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 10.1**

**(a)** Describe what happens to the resistance of the LDR and the current in the LDR when a bright light is shone on the LDR. **[2]**

**(b)** A camera lens is used to produce an image of an object OX. The arrangement is shown in Fig. 10.2.

![[0625-w15-23-p18-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 10.2**

The principal focuses of the lens are labelled F.

On Fig. 10.2,

**(i)** draw a ray from the top of the object, parallel to the principal axis and continuing through and beyond the lens, **[2]**

**(ii)** draw in another ray to locate the position of the image of OX, **[2]**

**(iii)** carefully draw and label the image obtained. **[1]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** current increases
>
> **(b)(i)** ray continued correctly through F
>
> **(b)(iii)** image at intersection drawn and labelled correctly

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | resistance of LDR decreases | M1 |
> |  | current increases | A1 |
> | (b)(i) | ray from object parallel to axis | M1 |
> |  | ray continued correctly through F | A1 |
> | (b)(ii) | second ray correctly drawn through centre of lens OR through F | B1 |
> |  | intersection of two or more rays | B1 |
> | (b)(iii) | image at intersection drawn and labelled correctly | A1 |
