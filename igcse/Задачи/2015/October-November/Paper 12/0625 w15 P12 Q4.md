> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Различие массы и веса и единиц их измерения.

Which statement about mass or weight is correct?

**A** Mass is a force.  
**B** Mass is measured in newtons.  
**C** Weight is a force.  
**D** Weight is measured in kilograms.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Many weaker candidates confused mass with weight, believing that mass would be less on the Moon than on Earth.
