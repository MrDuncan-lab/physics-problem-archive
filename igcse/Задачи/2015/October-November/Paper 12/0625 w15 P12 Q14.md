# Question 14 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2015 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice, вариант 2 · вопрос 14  
Источник: [[_pdf/2015/0625_w15_qp_12.pdf#page=6|условие, стр. 6]] · [[_pdf/2015/0625_w15_ms_12.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2015/0625_w15_er.pdf#page=3|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w15 P12]]  
Темы: [[2.2.1 Thermal expansion of solids, liquids and gases]], [[1.4 Density]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Уменьшение плотности нагреваемого металлического диска.

A circular metal disc is heated.

Which quantity decreases?

**A** its density  
**B** its diameter  
**C** its thickness  
**D** its volume

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The topic for this question was thermal expansion, and almost half the responses were incorrect. Candidates were required to link the increased volume of the heated disc to decreased density, and this caused difficulty for many.
