> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определение изменения скорости и наличия ускорения по таблице спидометра.

The table shows the readings on a car speedometer at 5 second intervals.

| time / s | speed km/h |
|---|---|
| 0 | 0 |
| 5 | 30 |
| 10 | 50 |
| 15 | 60 |
| 20 | 65 |

Which row describes the speed and the acceleration of the car?

|  | speed | acceleration |
|---|---|---|
| A | decreasing | zero |
| B | decreasing | not zero |
| C | increasing | zero |
| D | increasing | not zero |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
