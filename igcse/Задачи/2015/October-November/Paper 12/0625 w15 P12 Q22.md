> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Различение электромагнитных и звуковых волн.

Which group contains only electromagnetic waves?

**A** light waves, radio waves, γ-rays  
**B** light waves, radio waves, sound waves  
**C** light waves, sound waves, γ-rays  
**D** radio waves, sound waves, γ-rays

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> In this question on refraction a large number of candidates chose B, being unaware that some light is reflected internally even if the angle of incidence in the glass is less than the critical angle; the syllabus refers to “internal and total internal reflection”.
