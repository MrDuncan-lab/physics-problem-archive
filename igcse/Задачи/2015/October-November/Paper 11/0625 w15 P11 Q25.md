> Определение ферромагнитных и немагнитных металлов.

Which row states whether each metal is ferrous or non-ferrous?

|  | ferrous | non-ferrous |
|---|---|---|
| A | aluminium | copper |
| B | copper | iron |
| C | iron | steel |
| D | steel | aluminium |
