> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определение глубины моря по времени прохождения звукового эха.

A pulse of sound is produced at the bottom of a boat. The sound travels through the water and is

reflected from the sea bed. The sound reaches the boat again after 1.2 s. The speed of sound in the water is 1500 m / s.

![[0625-w15-13-p9-f2.svg]]

How far below the bottom of the boat is the sea bed?

**A** 450 m  
**B** 900 m  
**C** 1800 m  
**D** 3600 m

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Weaker candidates made the mistake of forgetting to divide the distance travelled by the sound by two to find the depth of the sea.
