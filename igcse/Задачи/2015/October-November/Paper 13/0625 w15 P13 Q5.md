> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Вычисление плотности металла по массе и вытесненному объёму.

The mass of a piece of metal is 1200 g.

A measuring cylinder contains 150 cm$^{3}$ of water.

The piece of metal is put into the measuring cylinder. The water level rises to 250 cm$^{3}$ and covers the metal.

What is the density of the metal?

**A** 3.0 g / cm$^{3}$  
**B** 4.8 g / cm$^{3}$  
**C** 8.0 g / cm$^{3}$  
**D** 12.0 g / cm$^{3}$

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question was well answered, but the most common mistake was to divide the mass of the metal by the original volume of water in the measuring cylinder, leading to option C as the answer.
