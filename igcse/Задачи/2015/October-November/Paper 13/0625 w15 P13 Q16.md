> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Передача теплоты от потолочного обогревателя излучением.

A heating engineer fits a heater to the ceiling of an office so that workers in the office are kept

warm.

How does thermal energy reach the workers below the heater?

**A** conduction and convection  
**B** convection and radiation  
**C** convection only  
**D** radiation only

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Only 40% of candidates answered this correctly, apparently being unfamiliar with the process of convection and/or the fact that air is an insulator; all the incorrect options were popular.
