**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2015 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice, вариант 3 · вопрос 22

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Условия полного внутреннего отражения на границе сред.

The diagram shows a ray of light travelling in a substance P. The ray reaches a boundary with a

substance Q. Total internal reflection occurs at the boundary.

![[0625-w15-13-p9-f1.svg]]

Which row contains correct statements about angle X and about the optical density of substance Q?

|  | angle X | substance Q |
|---|---|---|
| A | smaller than the critical angle | less dense than substance P |
| B | smaller than the critical angle | more dense than substance P |
| C | greater than the critical angle | less dense than substance P |
| D | greater than the critical angle | more dense than substance P |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Here more than half the candidates were confused over the meaning of critical angle and the fact that total internal reflection only occurs when light travelling in a more dense substance meets the boundary with a less dense one.
