> Изменение показаний вольтметров при регулировке делителя напряжения.

The diagram shows a potential divider circuit.

![[0625-w15-13-p13-t1.svg]]

The resistance of the variable resistor is increased.

Which row shows what happens to the readings on voltmeter P and on voltmeter Q?

|  | reading on voltmeter P | reading on voltmeter Q |
|---|---|---|
| A | decreases | decreases |
| B | decreases | increases |
| C | increases | decreases |
| D | increases | increases |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Potential divider questions are often more challenging for weaker candidates. In this case, just over a half of responses were correct, with option B being the most popular incorrect choice.
