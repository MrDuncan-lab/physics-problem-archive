**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2015 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice, вариант 3 · вопрос 14  
Источник: [[_pdf/2015/0625_w15_qp_13.pdf#page=6|условие, стр. 6]] · [[_pdf/2015/0625_w15_ms_13.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2015/0625_w15_er.pdf#page=5|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

Темы: [[2.2.1 Thermal expansion of solids, liquids and gases]], [[1.4 Density]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Изменение плотности металлического диска при нагревании.

A circular metal disc is heated.

Which quantity decreases?

**A** its density  
**B** its diameter  
**C** its thickness  
**D** its volume

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The topic for this question was thermal expansion, and slightly more than a quarter of the responses were incorrect. Candidates were required to link the increased volume of the heated disc to decreased density, and this caused difficulty for some.
