# Question 8 (6 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2015 · Paper 3 — Extended theory, вариант 1 · вопрос 8

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Расчёт заряда, сопротивления и мощности электрических часов.

A digital watch is powered by a 1.3 V cell. The cell supplies a current of 4.1 × 10$^{\text{–5}}$ A (0.000041 A) for 1.6 × 10$^{7}$ s.

Calculate

**(a)** the charge that passes through the cell in this time,

charge =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(b)** the resistance of the electrical circuit in the watch,

resistance =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(c)** the output power of the cell.

power =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** = 660 C
>
> **(b)** = 32 000 Ω OR 32 kΩ
>
> **(c)** 1st and 3rd methods: 5.3 × 10$^{\text{–5}}$ W / 0.000053 W 2nd and 4th methods: 5.4 × 10$^{\text{–5}}$ W / 0.000054 W
