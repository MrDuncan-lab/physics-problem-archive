> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> (a) This question required the recall of learned material. About two-thirds could identify the OR gate in
>
> (i) and correctly label an input and the output in (ii). Fewer, about a half, could draw a NAND gate
>
> in (iii).
>
> (b) The needle in (i) should have shown no deflection. This was often shown incorrectly.
