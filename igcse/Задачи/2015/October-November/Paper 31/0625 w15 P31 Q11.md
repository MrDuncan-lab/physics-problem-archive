# Question 11 (7 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2015 · Paper 3 — Extended theory, вариант 1 · вопрос 11

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Изотопы, альфа- и бета-распады висмута и период полураспада.

**(a)** State, in terms of the particles in each nucleus, how the nuclei of two isotopes of the same element are different. **[1]**

**(b)** Fig. 11.1 shows a graph of nucleon number against proton number. The nucleus ${}^{212}_{83}\text{Bi}$ is plotted on the graph at the cross marked P.

![[0625-w15-31-p16-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 11.1**

**(i)** On Fig. 11.1,

**1.** plot a cross labelled Q for the nucleus formed when the ${}^{212}_{83}\text{Bi}$ nucleus emits an α-particle,

**2.** plot a cross labelled R for the nucleus formed when the ${}^{212}_{83}\text{Bi}$ nucleus emits a β-particle. **[4]**

**(ii)** The half-life for the decay of ${}^{212}_{83}\text{Bi}$ is 60 minutes.

A sample of ${}^{212}_{83}\text{Bi}$ is placed at a fixed distance from a detector. The initial measurement of the count rate from the sample of ${}^{212}_{83}\text{Bi}$ is 2400 counts per minute.

Calculate the count rate from the sample 5.0 hours later.

count-rate =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)(ii)** 75 (counts) / min OR 1.25 (counts) / s OR 4500 (counts) / hr

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | different number of neutrons (in the nucleus) OR different neutron number | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | 1 letter Q at nucleon number = 208 | B1 |
> |  | proton number = 81 | B1 |
> |  | 2 letter R at nucleon number = 212 | B1 |
> |  | proton number = 84 | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | evidence of dividing original number by 2 | C1 |
> |  | 75 (counts) / min OR 1.25 (counts) / s OR 4500 (counts) / hr | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> (a) Many candidates correctly suggested that the number of neutrons would be different in the two isotopes. Many suggested nucleons instead of neutrons and could not be credited. Only a few candidates suggested protons.
>
> (b) (i) Most candidates had clearly understood the method of showing proton and nucleon numbers of nuclei in a grid. Many candidates gained full credit for this question.
>
> (b) (ii) This question proved challenging with fewer than half of the candidates calculating the count rate successfully.
