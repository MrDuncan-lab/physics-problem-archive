# Question 6 (8 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2015 · Paper 3 — Extended theory, вариант 1 · вопрос 6  
Источник: [[_pdf/2015/0625_w15_qp_31.pdf#page=9|условие, стр. 9]] · [[_pdf/2015/0625_w15_ms_31.pdf#page=6|схема оценивания, стр. 6]] · [[_pdf/2015/0625_w15_er.pdf#page=19|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w15 P31]]  
Темы: [[3.4 Sound]], [[3.1 General properties of waves]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Расчёт длины звуковой волны и её дифракция в щели.

A sound wave, travelling in air, approaches a solid barrier with a gap in the middle. Fig. 6.1 represents the compressions and rarefactions of the sound wave. The compressions are labelled A, B and C.

![[0625-w15-31-p9-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 6.1**

**(a)** State how a *compression* differs from a *rarefaction*. **[1]**

**(b)** The speed of sound in air is 340 m / s. The frequency of the sound is 850 Hz.

For this wave, determine

**(i)** the wavelength,

wavelength =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(ii)** the time that elapses before compression A reaches the barrier.

time =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(c)** On Fig. 6.1, draw the shape and positions of compressions B and C as compression A reaches the barrier. **[2]**

**(d)** Sound waves can also travel in water.

State how the speed of sound in water compares with the speed of sound in air. **[1]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)(i)** = 0.40 m
>
> **(b)(ii)** time (to reach barrier) = 1 / 340 = 2.9 × 10$^{\text{–3}}$ s OR 2.9 ms

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | (in compressions) pressure higher OR molecules / atoms / particles close(r) together / (more)<br>tightly packed | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | v = fλ in any form OR (λ =) v / f OR 340 / 850 | C1 |
> |  | = 0.40 m | A1 |
> | (b)(ii) | distance (of compression A from barrier) = 2.5 × 0.40 OR 1.0 m | C1 |
> |  | time (to reach barrier) = 1 / 340 = 2.9 × 10$^{\text{–3}}$ s OR 2.9 ms | A1 |
> |  | OR T (= 1 / f) = 1 / 850 OR 0.4 / 340 OR 1.2 × 10$^{\text{–3}}$ | (C1) |
> |  | (moves 2.5 wavelengths:) time = 2.5 / 850 = 2.9 × 10$^{\text{–3}}$ s OR 2.9 ms | (A1) |
> | (c) | <u>two circular arcs centred on mid-point of gap in barrier by eye</u> | B1 |
> |  | along centre line, arcs separated by the same distance as adjacent compressions<br>approaching barrier | B1 |
> | (d) | (speed in water) greater OR numerical value greater than 340 m / s | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> (a) Although many correct comparisons of compressions and rarefactions were seen, it was not uncommon to see the spacing of waves referred to rather than the spacing of particles or molecules.
>
> (b) (i) As in some previous examples, mistakes were frequently seen in the change from the formula v = f λ to the one needed, λ = v / f. The unit of the wavelength was sometimes written as λ.
>
> (ii) There was some success in calculating the time for compression A to advance by a distance of one wavelength, but it was rare to see this time multiplied by 2.5, the number of wavelengths it needed to advance to reach the barrier.
>
> (c) In general, candidates had understood the basic idea of what happens to the wave. Better answers used compasses to draw the arc.
>
> (d) This was answered correctly by the majority of candidates.
