# Question 1 (6 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2015 · Paper 3 — Extended theory, вариант 1 · вопрос 1  
Источник: [[_pdf/2015/0625_w15_qp_31.pdf#page=2|условие, стр. 2–3]] · [[_pdf/2015/0625_w15_ms_31.pdf#page=4|схема оценивания, стр. 4]] · [[_pdf/2015/0625_w15_er.pdf#page=19|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Ускорение реактивных саней по графику скорости при изменении массы.

Fig. 1.1 shows a rocket-powered sled travelling along a straight track. The sled is used to test components before they are sent into space.

![[0625-w15-31-p2-f1.png]]

**Fig. 1.1**

Fig. 1.2 is the speed-time graph for the sled from time *t* = 0 s.

![[0625-w15-31-p2-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 1.2**

**(a)** On Fig. 1.2, mark a point labelled P to indicate a time when the acceleration of the sled is not constant. **[1]**

**(b)** **(i)** Calculate the acceleration of the sled at *t* = 1.0 s.

acceleration =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(ii)** Determine the distance travelled by the sled between *t* = 1.0 s and *t* = 2.0 s.

distance =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(c)** The resultant force acting on the sled remains constant during the test.

Suggest why the acceleration of the sled is not constant. **[1]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)(i)** 120 m / s$^{2}$
>
> **(b)(ii)** 180 m

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | point marked P (on line or time axis) at t ≥ 2.0 s | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | attempt at gradient OR (a =) ∆v / t OR (v – u) / t OR 240 (–0) / 2.0<br>OR division of correct points on graph | C1 |
> |  | 120 m / s$^{2}$ | A1 |
> | (b)(ii) | suggestion of area (under graph) in words or formula or numbers<br>OR 0.5 (120 + 240) × 1.0 OR \[(120 × 1.0) + (0.5 × 120 × 1.0)\] | C1 |
> |  | 180 m | A1 |
> | (c) | mass of sled changes / decreases OR fuel used up | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> (a) A number of candidates failed to mark a point on the graph. The vast majority of those who did mark a point, placed it in a correct position.
>
> (b) (i) Many correct solutions were seen. Some candidates failed to read the y-axis scale correctly. A wrong unit, usually m / s, was common.
>
> (ii) The area under the relevant part of the graph was required. Some candidates suggested this, or wrote down a formula for the area of a triangle or trapezium, and could gain partial credit. Many went on to calculate the correct distance.
>
> (c) Correct answers were extremely rare. The information that the resultant force on the sled was constant had not been noted or its significance not recognised. Many irrelevant answers, some suggesting a change in the resultant force, were seen.
