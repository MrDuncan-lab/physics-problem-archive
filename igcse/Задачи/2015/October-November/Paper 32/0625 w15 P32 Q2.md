> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Измерение объёма плавающего куска дерева с грузом и мерным цилиндром.

A student wishes to find the volume of a piece of wood of irregular shape. Her experiment requires the use of a small brass object of mass 200 g.

**(a)** Calculate the volume of the brass object. The density of brass is 8.4 g / cm$^{3}$.

volume =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(b)** To find the volume of the piece of wood, the student has a measuring cylinder, a supply of water and the brass object in **(a)**. The piece of wood and the brass object are small enough to be placed in the measuring cylinder.

**(i)** The piece of wood does not sink in water.

Suggest why. **[1]**

**(ii)** Describe what the student does to find the volume of the piece of wood, stating the measurements that she makes and any calculations required. **[4]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** 24 cm$^{3}$

> |  | reads volume (V$_{2}$) from previous line and determines volume<br>of (wood + brass) (V$_{2}$– V$_{1}$) | B1 |
