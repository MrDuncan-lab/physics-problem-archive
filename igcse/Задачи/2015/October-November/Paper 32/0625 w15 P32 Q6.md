> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Установка для наблюдения броуновского движения и объяснение его причин.

**(a)** In the space below, draw a simple labelled diagram of the apparatus used to demonstrate Brownian motion. **[2]**

**(b)** State what is observed. **[2]**

**(c)** Explain what is observed in terms of molecules. **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** microscope AND, if smoke used, illumination

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | <u>suitable particles and fluid, and labelled, in suitable container</u><br>e.g. pollen and water (surface), smoke in air | M1 |
> |  | microscope AND, if smoke used, illumination | A1 |
> | (b) | movement of particles NOT atoms or molecules | B1 |
> |  | reasonable description of movement<br>OR any mention / clear description of movement in different directions<br>accept if diagram drawn | B1 |
> | (c) | collisions between molecules and particles | B1 |
> |  | random movement of molecules OR causes (random) motion of particles | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> (a) There were some clear labelled diagrams, but the majority of candidates either failed to label the particles or to include a microscope. A significant minority of candidates thought the question was about diffusion, the differences between solids, liquids and gases or how a gas exerts a pressure.
>
> (b), (c) Some candidates struggled to record their answer coherently for this question. In this topic the use
>
> of the appropriate terminology is essential to convey the ideas of Physics correctly. Candidates should learn the distinction between particles and molecules in explaining Brownian motion.
