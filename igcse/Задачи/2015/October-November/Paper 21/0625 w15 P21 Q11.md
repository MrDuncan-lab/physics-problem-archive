# Question 11 (6 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2015 · Paper 2 — Core theory, вариант 1 · вопрос 11  
Источник: [[_pdf/2015/0625_w15_qp_21.pdf#page=19|условие, стр. 19]] · [[_pdf/2015/0625_w15_ms_21.pdf#page=7|схема оценивания, стр. 7]] · [[_pdf/2015/0625_w15_er.pdf#page=7|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определение испускаемой частицы и ядерных чисел в альфа-распаде америция.

Three types of radioactive decay are by the emission of

α - radiation, β - radiation, γ - radiation.

**(a)** State which of the three types of emission has the greatest speed. **[1]**

**(b)** A nucleus of americium-241 decays to become a nucleus of neptunium-237 by the emission of one particle.

The equation below describes the change. The symbol$^{\text{A}}_{\text{Z}}$ X represents the particle emitted.

$^{241}_{95}$Am $^{237}_{93}$ Np +$^{\text{A}}_{\text{Z}}$ X

**(i)** State the name given to each of the numbers *A* and *Z*.

*A* is the  \_\_\_\_\_\_ number.

*Z* is the  \_\_\_\_\_\_ number. **[2]**

**(ii)** Determine the values of *A* and *Z*.

*A* =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

*Z* =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(iii)** State the name of the particle emitted. **[1]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | Gamma / γ | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | nucleon (accept mass) | B1 |
> |  | proton (accept atomic) | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | A = 4 | B1 |
> |  | Z = 2 | B1 |
> | (b)(iii) | alpha / α (particle) | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> (a) Most candidates completed this correctly. The most common error was to select alpha radiation as having the greatest speed.
>
> (b) (i) The majority of candidates gained some credit here.
>
> (ii) The vast majority of candidates gained full credit for this question.
>
> (iii) Many candidates correctly identified the emitted particle as an alpha particle. A common mistake by weaker candidates was to state that it was an electron.
