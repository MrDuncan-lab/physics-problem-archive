> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Расчёт скорости звука по задержке эха пушечного выстрела.

A battle re-enactment enables observers to see and hear an old cannon being fired.

Fig. 7.1 shows the battle site and the distant cliffs.

![[0625-w15-22-p10-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 7.1** (not to scale)

The cannon is fired. Observers see the smoke and then hear the bang.

**(a)** **(i)** Explain why there is a short delay between seeing the smoke and hearing the bang of the cannon. **[2]**

**(ii)** An observer notices that, after the cannon is fired, she hears a loud bang and then a quieter bang a short time later.

Tick one box to identify the reason for the second bang.

diffraction of the sound in the valley

dispersion of the sound in the valley

reflection of the sound from the cliff

refraction of the sound from the cliff **[1]**

**(b)** Another observer is standing 500 m away from the cannon. He uses a stopwatch to measure the time delay between seeing the smoke and hearing the first bang. His timings are shown in the table.

| measurement | time delay / s |
|---|---|
| 1 | 1.9 |
| 2 | 1.5 |
| 3 | 1.3 |
| 4 | 1.4 |
| 5 | 1.7 |

Use the measurements in the table to calculate an accurate value for the speed of the sound produced by the cannon.

speed of sound =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ m / s **[4]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** 321 OR e.c.f.

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)

> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | sound travels slowly | B1 |
> |  | sound travels slower than light or reverse argument | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | reflection of the sound from the cliff | B1 |
> | (b) | evidence of average found / 1.56 OR 1.6 | B1 |
> |  | speed = distance / time in any form: words, symbols, numbers | C1 |
> |  | 500 / 1.6 OR 500 / candidate’s time | C1 |
> |  | 321 OR e.c.f. | A1 |
