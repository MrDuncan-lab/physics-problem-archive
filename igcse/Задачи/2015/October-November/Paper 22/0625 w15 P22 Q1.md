> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Ошибки измерения длины и определение толщины тонкой проволоки линейкой.

A student uses a rule to measure a thin piece of wire as shown in Fig. 1.1.

![[0625-w15-22-p3-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 1.1**

The student records the length of the wire as 12.8 cm.

**(a)** State two errors in the student’s measurement of the length of wire.

1 \_\_\_\_\_\_

2 \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(b)** The student is asked to measure the thickness of the wire using the same ruler. The student does this by bending a short length of the wire and measuring distance *x* as shown in Fig. 1.2.

![[0625-w15-22-p3-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 1.2**

**(i)** Use the ruler in Fig. 1.2 to determine the distance *x*.

distance *x* =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm **[1]**

**(ii)** Calculate the average thickness of the wire, **in mm**. Give your answer correct to two significant figures.

average thickness of wire =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ mm **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)(ii)** 0.81

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |

> | (b)(i) | 0.65 (cm) | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | candidate’s (b)(i) / 8 | C1 |
> |  | 0.8125 OR 0.813 OR e.c.f. | C1 |
> |  | 0.81 | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> (a) This question was answered well by most candidates.
>
> (b) (i) This question was answered well by most candidates. Values ranging between 0.60 and 0.75 were seen.
>
> (ii) Surprisingly, this was not a well-answered question. A common incorrect response was for candidates to multiply the answer in (b)(i) by 10 or to convert cm into mm by multiplying by 100. A number of candidates responded with an incorrect value without showing their working and could not be given any credit. Weaker candidates also gave responses with more than two significant figures.
