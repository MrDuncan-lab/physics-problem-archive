# Question 9 (5 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · February/March 2015 · Paper 2 — Core theory, вариант 2 · вопрос 9  
Источник: [[_pdf/2015/0625_m15_qp_22.pdf#page=14|условие, стр. 14]] · [[_pdf/2015/0625_m15_ms_22.pdf#page=6|схема оценивания, стр. 6]] · [[_pdf/2015/0625_m15_er.pdf#page=3|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Действие электрического двигателя и увеличение скорости вращения.

Fig. 9.1 shows a simple motor.

![[0625-m15-22-p14-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 9.1**

**(a)** The switch is closed.

Explain why the coil starts to rotate. **[4]**

**(b)** Suggest **one** way of making the coil rotate faster. **[1]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | any four from:<br>reference to magnetic field<br>wire cuts (magnetic) field (lines)<br>current perpendicular to field (lines)<br>force(s) on (sides of) coil OR turning effect<br>current in opposite directions (in two long wires)<br>force/turning effect opposite direction on two (long) sides<br>force perpendicular to current / force perpendicular to field | B4 |
> | (b) | increase number of turns OR increase current OR increase strength of magnet | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> (a) This question was not well answered. There were very many vague responses that described components of the motor without mentioning how the motor was able to turn.
>
> (b) Many middle and higher scoring candidates gave creditworthy responses. However, the vague responses such as “use larger magnets” that were often given by candidates were not given any credit.
