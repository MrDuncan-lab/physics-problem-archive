> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определение удлинения пружины по нагрузке и графику.

Fig. 2.1 shows a spring before and after it is stretched by a small load.

![[0625-m15-22-p3-f1.svg]]

![[0625-m15-22-p3-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 2.1**

**(a)** On Fig. 2.1,

**(i)** mark the extension caused by the load,

**(ii)** draw the apparatus needed to determine the extension. **[2]**

**(b)** Fig. 2.2 shows a sketch graph of the length of the spring, plotted against the weight of the load.

![[0625-m15-22-p3-f3.svg]]

**Fig. 2.2**

On Fig. 2.2, mark clearly the unstretched length of the spring. **[1]**

**(c)** State the unit in which the weight of the load is measured. **[1]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | extension clearly marked on Fig. 2.1 | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | (vertical) rule drawn on Fig. 2.1 | B1 |
> | (b) | (distance to) intercept on length axis clearly indicated | B1 |
> | (c) | newton OR N | B1 |
