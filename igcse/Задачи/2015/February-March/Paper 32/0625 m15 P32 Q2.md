> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Равнодействующая сил при старте ракеты и ускорение.

The rocket shown in Fig. 2.1 is about to be launched.

![[0625-m15-32-p3-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 2.1**

The total mass of the rocket and its full load of fuel is 2.8 × 10$^{6}$ kg. The constant force provided by the rocket’s motors is 3.2 × 10$^{7}$ N.

**(a)** Calculate

**(i)** the total weight of the rocket and the fuel,

weight =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(ii)** the resultant force acting on the rocket,

resultant force =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(iii)** the vertical acceleration of the rocket immediately after lift-off.

acceleration =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(b)** Suggest why the acceleration of the rocket increases as it rises above the Earth’s surface. **[1]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)(ii)** 4.0 × 10$^{6}$ OR 0.4 × 10$^{7}$ N
>
> **(a)(iii)** 1.4 m / s$^{2}$

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | (W = mg = 2.8 × 10$^{6}$ × 10 =) 2.8 × 10$^{7}$ N | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | 3.2 × 10$^{7}$ – 2.8 × 10$^{7}$ | C1 |
> |  | 4.0 × 10$^{6}$ OR 0.4 × 10$^{7}$ N | A1 |
> | (a)(iii) | F = ma in any form OR (a =) F÷m OR 4.0 × 10$^{6}$÷(2.8 × 10$^{6}$) | C1 |
> |  | 1.4 m / s$^{2}$ | A1 |
> | (b) | Mass of rocket decreases (as fuel is used up)<br>OR<br>Value of g/gravitational force on rocket decreases as rocket rises<br>OR<br>Air resistance decreases | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> (a) (i) Most candidates correctly multiplied the given mass by 10. The available credit was forfeited, however, by those who gave the weight in kg.
>
> (ii) There were many correct calculations. The most common source of error was to subtract the mass rather than the weight from the given upward force acting on the rocket.
>
> (iii) With any error from (a)(ii) carried forward and not again penalised, most candidates applied the formula F = ma correctly.
>
> (b) Most candidates could identify one of the possible reasons for the increased acceleration of the rocket.
