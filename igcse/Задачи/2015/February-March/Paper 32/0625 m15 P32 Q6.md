# Question 6 (8 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · February/March 2015 · Paper 3 — Extended theory, вариант 2 · вопрос 6  
Источник: [[_pdf/2015/0625_m15_qp_32.pdf#page=8|условие, стр. 8–9]] · [[_pdf/2015/0625_m15_ms_32.pdf#page=5|схема оценивания, стр. 5]] · [[_pdf/2015/0625_m15_er.pdf#page=7|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 m15 P32]]  
Темы: [[3.2.2 Refraction of light]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Расчёт угла преломления и скорости света в стекле.

Fig. 6.1 shows a glass block ABCD surrounded by air. A ray of red light, PQ, is incident on face CD of the block.

![[0625-m15-32-p8-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 6.1**

**(a)** On Fig. 6.1,

**(i)** draw the normal at Q and the refracted ray inside the block so that it meets face AB,

**(ii)** draw the ray emerging from face AB of the block and the normal where the ray emerges,

**(iii)** between the rays and the normals you have drawn, label two equal angles X. **[3]**

**(b)** The angle of incidence of another red ray is 65°. The refractive index of the glass of block ABCD for red light is 1.62.

**(i)** Calculate the angle of refraction in the glass for this ray.

angle =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(ii)** The speed of light in air is 3.0 × 10$^{8}$ m / s.

Calculate the speed of the red light in the glass.

speed =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(c)** For the same angle of incidence, the angle of refraction of red light in glass is greater than the angle of refraction of violet light.

State the term which describes the separation of red and violet refracted rays in glass. **[1]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)(i)** OR sin r = sin 65 ÷ 1.62 r = 34°
>
> **(b)(ii)** OR 1.62 = 3.0 × 10$^{8}$ ÷ speed in glass in any form (speed in glass = 3.0 × 10$^{8}$ ÷ 1.62) = 1.8 OR 1.9 × 10$^{8}$ m / s

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | Normal at Q drawn AND refracted ray drawn with r less than i | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | Emerging ray drawn parallel to PQ AND normal drawn | B1 |
> | (a)(iii) | Two equal angles, marked X, between rays and normal | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | n = sin i ÷ sin r in any form OR 1.62 = sin 65 ÷ sin r in any form | C1 |
> |  | OR sin r = sin 65 ÷ 1.62<br>r = 34° | A1 |
> | (b)(ii) | n = speed (of light) in air ÷ speed (of light) in glass in any form | C1 |
> |  | OR 1.62 = 3.0 × 10$^{8}$ ÷ speed in glass in any form<br>(speed in glass = 3.0 × 10$^{8}$ ÷ 1.62) = 1.8 OR 1.9 × 10$^{8}$ m / s | A1 |
> | (c) | Dispersion | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> (a) Mistakes in dealing with the path of the ray through the glass block were infrequent. Most errors, if made, concerned the labelling of two correct equal angles.
>
> (b) In both (i) and (ii), the majority of candidates could quote the correct formulae and could usually use it to calculate the correct answers.
>
> (c) The correct term ‘dispersion’ was quoted by many of the candidates. It was apparent that the low achieving candidates were as likely to know the correct term as the stronger ones.
