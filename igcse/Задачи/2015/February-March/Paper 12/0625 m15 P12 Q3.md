> Средняя скорость автомобиля на маршруте из двух участков.

A car travels 6.0 km along a main road in 6.0 minutes. It then travels 2.0 km along a minor road in 6.0 minutes.

minor road

![[0625-m15-12-p3-f1.svg]]

Which calculation of average speed for the whole journey is correct?

**A** 8.0 ÷ 12.0 = 0.67 km / minute  
**B** 12.0 ÷ 8.0 = 1.5 km / minute  
**C** 8.0 + 12.0 = 20 km / minute  
**D** 8.0 × 12.0 = 96 km / minute

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**
