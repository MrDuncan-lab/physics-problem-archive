> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Скорость звука по времени эха от морского дна.

A pulse of sound is produced at the bottom of a boat. The sound travels through the water and is

reflected from the sea-bed. The sound reaches the boat again after 1.3 s. The sea-bed is 1000 m below the boat.

![[0625-m15-12-p11-f1.svg]]

Using this information, what is the speed of sound in the water?

**A** 769 m / s  
**B** 1300 m / s  
**C** 1538 m / s  
**D** 2600 m / s

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> As is very often the case in questions on reflection of sound, many of the lower achieving candidates did not double the distance to 2000 m when calculating the speed.
