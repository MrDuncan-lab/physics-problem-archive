# Question 14 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · February/March 2015 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice, вариант 2 · вопрос 14  
Источник: [[_pdf/2015/0625_m15_qp_12.pdf#page=7|условие, стр. 7]] · [[_pdf/2015/0625_m15_ms_12.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2015/0625_m15_er.pdf#page=1|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

Темы: [[2.2.3 Melting, boiling and evaporation]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Постоянство температуры при конденсации и замерзании.

When steam condenses it becomes liquid water. When liquid water solidifies it becomes ice.

What happens to the temperature of steam while it is condensing, and what happens to the temperature of water while it is solidifying?

|  | temperature of steam while it is condensing | temperature of water while it is solidifying |
|---|---|---|
| A | decreases | decreases |
| B | decreases | stays the same |
| C | stays the same | decreases |
| D | stays the same | stays the same |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> A very large proportion of the weaker candidates, and many of the more able too, selected an answer which suggested that as condensation and solidification takes place the temperature falls.
