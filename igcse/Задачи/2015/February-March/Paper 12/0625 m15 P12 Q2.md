> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Описание движения по графику поезда.

The graph represents the motion of a train travelling between two stations.

![[0625-m15-12-p2-f2.svg]]

Which statement about the train is correct?

**A** Its acceleration takes a longer time than its deceleration.  
**B** It travels at constant speed for less than half of its journey time.  
**C** It travels 2000 m in the first 100 s.  
**D** It travels 10 000 m at constant speed.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question tested understanding of a speed-time graph, and a significant proportion of candidates opted for C, not appreciating that the area under the graph in the first 100 s of the journey was only half of (20 × 100) m.
