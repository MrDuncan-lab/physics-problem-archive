> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнение средней мощности людей при подъёме на стену.

Four people of equal weight on a beach use different routes to get to the top of a sea wall.

![[0625-m15-12-p5-f1.png]]

Which person produces the greatest average power?

| person | route | time taken / s |
|---|---|---|
| A | runs across the beach, then climbs the ladder | 8 |
| B | walks across the beach, then climbs the ladder | 16 |
| C | runs up the slipway | 5 |
| D | walks up the slipway | 10 |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Although this question on work and power was generally well answered, a considerable number of candidates opted for A. As all four people did the same amount of work, all that was needed was to look for the shortest time taken.
