> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Масса воды в прямоугольном резервуаре по объёму и плотности.

The tank in Fig. 2.1 measures 2.0m × 1.5m × 1.0m. It is full of water.

![[0625-w08-2-p3-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 2.1**

**(a)** Calculate the volume of the tank.

volume =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

**(b)** Water has a density of 1000kg/m$^{3}$. Calculate the mass of water in the tank.

mass =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** 3 (m$^{3}$)
>
> **(b)** 3000 (kg) e.c.f.

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | L × B × H | C1 |
> |  | 2 × 1.5 × 1 | C1 |
> |  | 3 (m$^{3}$) | A1 |
> | (b) | M = V × D | C1 |
> |  | 3000 (kg) e.c.f. | A1 |
> |  | correct units in both (a) and (b) | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** A fairly simple question for most candidates. A few could not do the multiplication correctly, and a few gave the answer in cm$^{3}$. \[3 m$^{3}$\]
>
> **(b)** Some candidates had the density equation the wrong way round, and some gave the units for density rather than mass. However, the question was usually well answered. \[3000 kg\]
