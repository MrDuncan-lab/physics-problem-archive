> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Answers to this question were very pleasing, with lots of candidates scoring full marks, and lots more losing only one mark. Those candidates who put the wrong polarity for N and S in **(a)** and **(b)** were allowed “error carried forward” for the ticks in **part (ii)** of each. The most common mistake was in **(d)**, where some candidates thought that the rod and magnet would be attracted.
