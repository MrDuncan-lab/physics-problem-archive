# Question 7 (7 marks)

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Построение отражённых лучей в системе двух перпендикулярных зеркал.

This question requires the use of a ruler or other straight edge. Fig. 7.1 shows a ray of light striking a plane mirror.

![[0625-w08-2-p9-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 7.1**

**(a)** On Fig. 7.1,

**(i)** draw the normal to the mirror at A, **[1]**

**(ii)** draw the path of the reflected ray, **[1]**

**(iii)** mark and label the angle of incidence, *i*, **[1]**

**(iv)** mark and label the angle of reflection, *r*. **[1]**

**(b)** State the equation that links *i* and *r*. **[1]**

**(c)** On Fig. 7.1

**(i)** draw another mirror which is perpendicular to the one in the diagram and 2 cm to the right of A, **[1]**

**(ii)** draw the path of the ray of light after reflection from this second mirror. **[1]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | normal correct, by eye | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | reflected ray in correct direction, by eye (condone poor normal) | B1 |
> | (a)(iii) | i correctly labelled (condone poor normal) | B1 |
> | (a)(iv) | r correctly labelled (condone poor normal) | B1 |
> | (b) | i = r | B1 |
> | (c)(i) | second mirror correct, by eye | B1 |
> | (c)(ii) | ray parallel to original ray | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Candidates often scored well on this part. Common faults/criticisms were (i) careless drawing, including failure to use a straight edge, (ii) not drawing in the normal, as instructed, (iii) drawing the incident and reflected rays at right angles to each other, (iv) incorrectly labelling i and r, including showing the deviation as i, and showing i and r as angles with the surface.
>
> **(b)** All that was required here was i = r. Some insisted on writing it out in words but were awarded the mark nevertheless. There were also a considerable number of candidates that introduced sin i and sin r, which lost them the mark.
>
> **(c)** Candidates which followed the instructions usually scored both marks provided their drawing was clear. However, a lot of candidates put the second mirror above the existing one, and parallel with it.
