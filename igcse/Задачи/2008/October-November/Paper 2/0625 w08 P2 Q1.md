> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определение длительности рыбалки по наручным часам и числу рейсов парома.

A boy is fishing in a river. Nearby, a ferry-boat is taking passengers backwards and forwards across the river.

![[0625-w08-2-p2-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 1.1 Fig. 1.2**

When he starts fishing one morning, the boy’s wristwatch is as shown in Fig. 1.1. When he finishes his morning’s fishing, the wristwatch is as shown in Fig. 1.2.

**(a)** For how many minutes was the boy fishing?

number of minutes =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

**(b)** It takes the ferry-boat 20 minutes to load up passengers, take them across the river, load up with passengers at the other side, and return, to start all over again. Calculate how many journeys, across the river and back, the ferry made whilst the boy was fishing.

number of journeys =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** 200 mins
>
> **(b)** 10 OR 20 e.c.f.
