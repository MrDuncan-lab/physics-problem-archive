> Случайное движение броуновских частиц в жидкостях или газах.

Viewed through a microscope, very small particles can be seen moving with Brownian motion.

Which line in the table is correct?

|  | type of motion of particles | particles are suspended in |
|---|---|---|
| A | vibration | a liquid or a gas |
| B | vibration | a solid, a liquid or a gas |
| C | random | a liquid or a gas |
| D | random | a solid, a liquid or a gas |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
