> Теплопотери горячего напитка при открытой крышке.

A cup with a lid contains a hot drink.

![[0625-w08-1-p11-f1.svg]]

When the lid is removed, the rate of heat loss from the drink increases.

What causes this?

**A** convection only  
**B** evaporation only  
**C** both convection and evaporation  
**D** neither convection nor evaporation

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
