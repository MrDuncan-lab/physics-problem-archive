Fig. 7.1 shows a scale drawing of plane waves approaching a gap in a barrier.

![[0625-w08-3-p12-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 7.1**

**(a)** On Fig. 7.1, draw in the pattern of the waves after they have passed the gap. **[3]**

**(b)** The waves approaching the barrier have a wavelength of 2.5 cm and a speed of 20 cm/s. Calculate the frequency of the waves.

frequency =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(c)** State the frequency of the diffracted waves. **[1]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** 8 Hz or 8 s$^{\text{–1}}$ or 8 waves/second
