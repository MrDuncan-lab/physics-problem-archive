> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Давление воды на глубине и сила на крышку подводной лодки.

**(a)** A submarine descends to a depth of 70 m below the surface of water.

The density of the water is 1050 kg/m$^{3}$. Atmospheric pressure is 1.0 × 10$^{5}$ Pa.

Calculate

**(i)** the increase in pressure as it descends from the surface to a depth of 70 m,

increase in pressure =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(ii)** the total pressure on the submarine at a depth of 70 m.

total pressure =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(b)** On another dive, the submarine experiences a total pressure of 6.5 × 10$^{5}$ Pa. A hatch cover on the submarine has an area of 2.5 m$^{2}$.

Calculate the force on the outside of the cover.

force =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(c)** The submarine undergoes tests in fresh water of density 1000 kg/m$^{3}$.

Explain why the pressure on the submarine is less at the same depth. **[1]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)(i)** 735 000 Pa or 7.35 × 10$^{5}$Pa accept N/m$^{2}$ for Pa
>
> **(b)** 1.625 × 10$^{6}$N
