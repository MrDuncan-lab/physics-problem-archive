> Работа фоторезистора и транзисторного включения лампы в темноте.

Fig. 10.1 shows a circuit for a warning lamp that comes on when the external light intensity falls below a pre-set level.

![[0625-w08-3-p16-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 10.1**

**(a)** On Fig. 10.1, label

**(i)** with the letter X the component that detects the change in external light intensity,

**(ii)** with the letter Y the lamp,

**(iii)** with the letter Z the component that switches the lamp on and off. **[3]**

**(b)** Describe how the circuit works as the external light intensity decreases and the lamp comes on. **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** LDR gets larger share of the voltage OR voltage across LDR gets bigger
>
> **(b)** transistor switches/turns lamp on
