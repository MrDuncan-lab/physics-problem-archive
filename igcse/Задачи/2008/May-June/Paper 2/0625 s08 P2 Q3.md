# Question 3 (7 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2008 · Paper 2 — Core theory · вопрос 3  
Источник: [[_pdf/2008/0625_s08_qp_2.pdf#page=4|условие, стр. 4–5]] · [[_pdf/2008/0625_s08_ms_2.pdf#page=3|схема оценивания, стр. 3]] · [[_pdf/2008/0625_s08_er.pdf#page=3|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Равновесие рычага при действии нескольких сил и оценка массы.

A beam is pivoted at its centre. Three forces, *F*$_{1}$, *F*$_{2}$ and *F*$_{3}$, act on the beam as shown in Fig. 3.1.

![[0625-s08-2-p4-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 3.1**

**(a)** Which of the forces exert(s)

a clockwise moment,  \_\_\_\_\_\_

an anticlockwise moment?  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

**(b)** When the beam is released, the right-hand side of the beam starts to go down. Which of the three distances, *a*, *b* or *c*, should be decreased in order to balance the beam? Explain your answer.

Which distance?  \_\_\_\_\_\_

Explanation **[3]**

**(c)** Fig. 3.2 represents a simple beam-balance with the pivot accurately at its centre.

![[0625-s08-2-p4-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 3.2**

The person using the beam-balance puts the object to be weighed in the left-hand pan. He has a selection of standard masses to put in the right-hand pan, but he finds he cannot exactly balance the beam.

His best attempts are

| **masses used** | **effect** |
|---|---|
| 10 g, 10 g, 5 g, 2 g, 2 g | beam tips down slightly on the left-hand side |
| 20 g, 10 g | beam tips down slightly on the right-hand side |

Estimate the mass of the object.

mass =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ g **[1]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** clockwise moment (accept moment on RH side) was too big
>
> **(b)** reduce moment by reducing distance note: moment must be mentioned in both of the last 2 marks; accept turning effect, torque and leverage as alternatives to moment

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | clockwise: F$_{3}$ | B1 |
> |  | anticlockwise: F$_{1}$ | B1 |
> |  | F$_{2}$ | B1 |
> | (b) | c | M1 |
> |  | clockwise moment (accept moment on RH side) was too big | A1 |
> |  | reduce moment by reducing distance<br>note: moment must be mentioned in both of the last 2 marks; accept turning effect,<br>torque and leverage as alternatives to moment | A1 |
> | (c) | any value bigger than 29 g and less than 30 g, but NOT 29 g or 30 g | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** This was correctly answered by most, although occasionally only one force was offered for the anticlockwise moment.
>
> **(b)** Most could spot that distance *c* should be decreased, but few could give anything like a meaningful explanation. It really required an answer in terms of moments, but most wrote about forces balancing, or stated that changing *c* would change the force.
>
> **(c)** This was a slightly different approach from the usual balance questions, but many coped with it well. The most popular acceptable answer was 29.5 g.
