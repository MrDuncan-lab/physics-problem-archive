> Превращения кинетической и потенциальной энергии брошенного камня.

A stone is thrown from the edge of a cliff. Its path is shown in the diagram.

![[0625-s08-1-p5-f2.svg]]

In which position does the stone have its greatest kinetic energy and in which position does it have its least gravitational energy?

|  | greatest kinetic energy | least gravitational energy |
|---|---|---|
| A | 1 | 2 |
| B | 2 | 3 |
| C | 3 | 1 |
| D | 3 | 3 |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
