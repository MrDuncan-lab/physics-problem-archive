> Фокусные расстояния двух линз при параллельных промежуточных лучах.

Two thin converging lenses X and Y are used as shown to give a focused image of an illuminated

slit. The rays shown are parallel between X and Y.

![[0625-s08-1-p11-f2.svg]]

What are the correct values for the focal lengths of X and of Y?

|  | focal length of X / cm | focal length of Y / cm |
|---|---|---|
| A | 50 | 35 |
| B | 30 | 20 |
| C | 30 | 15 |
| D | 20 | 20 |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
