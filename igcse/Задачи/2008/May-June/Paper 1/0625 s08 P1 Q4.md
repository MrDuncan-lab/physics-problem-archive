> Постоянство массы и уменьшение веса космонавта на орбите.

The force of gravity acting on an astronaut in an orbiting spacecraft is less than when she is on the Earth’s surface.

Compared with being on the Earth’s surface, how do her mass and weight change when she goes into orbit?

|  | mass in orbit | weight in orbit |
|---|---|---|
| A | decreases | decreases |
| B | decreases | unchanged |
| C | unchanged | decreases |
| D | unchanged | unchanged |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
