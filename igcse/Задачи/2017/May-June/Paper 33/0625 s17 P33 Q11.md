> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Нарисовать направление магнитного поля катушки и назвать электромагнит и его применение.

Fig. 11.1 shows a coil connected in series with a d.c. supply.

![[0625-s17-33-p14-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 11.1**

**(a)** Fig. 11.2 shows a plan view of the coil. The arrows represent the current direction at the **top** of the coil.

![[0625-s17-33-p14-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 11.2**

On Fig. 11.2, draw lines to show the magnetic field due to the current in the coil.

- Draw **two** field lines above the line AB and **two** lines below it.
- Add arrows to show the direction of the magnetic field. \[3\] **[3]**

**(b)** **(i)** An iron rod is placed inside the coil, as shown in Fig. 11.3.

![[0625-s17-33-p15-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 11.3**

State the name given to the combination of iron rod and coil when used in this way. **[1]**

**(ii)** The arrangement shown in Fig. 11.3 is adapted to a number of commercial uses.

Suggest **one** of these uses. **[1]**

> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | Any two from:<br>two curves/loops drawn from one end of coil to the other above line AB<br>two curves/loops drawn from one end of coil to the other below line AB<br>field pattern symmetrical by eye above and below line XY<br>straight lines by eye within coil (accept some lines leaving side of coil near ends) | B2 |
> |  | arrow from B to A | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | electromagnet | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | Scrap yards/relay/motor/generator/(security) doors/(electric) bells | B1 |
