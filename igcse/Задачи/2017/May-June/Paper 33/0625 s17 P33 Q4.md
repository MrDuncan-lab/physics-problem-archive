# Question 4 (7 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2017 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 3 · вопрос 4

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать силу давления шины на дорогу и объяснить давление воздуха столкновениями молекул.

Fig. 4.1 shows a car tyre in contact with the road.

![[0625-s17-33-p6-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 4.1**

The area of tyre in contact with the road is 0.015 m$^{2}$. The tyre exerts a pressure on the road of 240 kN / m$^{2}$.

**(a)** Calculate the force on the road from the tyre.

force =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ N **[4]**

**(b)** The tyre is filled with air at high pressure.

Use ideas about molecules to explain how this air exerts a pressure on the inside of the tyre. **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** 3600 (N)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | convert kN / m$^{2}$ to N / m$^{2}$ or 240 × 1000 | B1 |
> |  | pressure = force ÷ area | C1 |
> |  | transformation force = pressure × area | C1 |
> |  | 3600 (N) | A1 |
> | (b) | any 3 from:<br>molecules move about (randomly)<br>collisions impacts<br>with walls/surfaces (of tyre)<br>idea of force produced (by bombarding molecules)<br>idea of pressure as force on an area | B3 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Many candidates correctly calculated the force on the road from the tyre. The most common error was to use force = pressure ÷ area.
>
> **(b)** Many candidates scored marks by referring to collisions between molecules, but only the most able referred to collisions with the walls of the tyre.
