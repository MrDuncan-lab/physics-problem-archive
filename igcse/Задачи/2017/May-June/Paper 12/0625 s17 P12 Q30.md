> Выбрать изменение, увеличивающее ток в последовательной цепи с термистором.

A thermistor is connected in series with a sensitive ammeter and a battery.

![[0625-s17-12-p16-f2.svg]]

Which change will give a larger ammeter reading?

**A** adding another thermistor in series  
**B** cooling the thermistor  
**C** heating the thermistor  
**D** reducing the number of cells in the battery

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
