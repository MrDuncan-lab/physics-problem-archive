> Определить конечную температуру воды со льдом после длительного пребывания в комнате.

A glass of water is taken out of a refrigerator. Several ice cubes are put into the glass of water.

The glass is then left in the room for several hours.

The graph shows how the temperature of the water in the glass varies from the time it is taken out of the refrigerator.

![[0625-s17-12-p11-f2.svg]]

What does the temperature marked X represent?

**A** boiling point of water  
**B** melting point of ice  
**C** room temperature  
**D** temperature inside refrigerator

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
