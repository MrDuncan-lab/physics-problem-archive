# Question 7 (1 mark)

Источник: [[_pdf/2017/0625_s17_qp_12.pdf#page=4|условие, стр. 4]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_s17_ms_12.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_s17_er.pdf#page=3|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить изменение скорости автомобиля по соотношению сил тяги и сопротивления.

A car travels forwards along a straight horizontal road. Only the horizontal forces acting on it are shown.

![[0625-s17-12-p4-f2.png]]

The length of each arrow represents the size of each force.

How do these forces affect the motion of the car?

**A** The car moves at constant speed.  
**B** The car moves backwards.  
**C** The car slows down.  
**D** The car’s forward speed increases.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Some candidates did not read the question carefully. The backward arrow was clearly longer than the forward arrow meaning that the car must be slowing down.
