# Question 12 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2017 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 12  
Источник: [[_pdf/2017/0625_s17_qp_12.pdf#page=8|условие, стр. 8]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_s17_ms_12.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_s17_er.pdf#page=3|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить избыточное давление газа по изменению уровней ртути в двух коленах манометра.

In diagram 1 a manometer containing mercury (Hg) is connected to a gas tap. The tap is turned

off and the mercury is at the same level on both sides.

![[0625-s17-12-p8-f1.svg]]

When the gas supply is turned on, the mercury in the tube connected to the supply falls by 40mm and the mercury in the tube open to the atmosphere rises by 40 mm, as shown in diagram 2.

![[0625-s17-12-p8-f2.svg]]

What is the pressure of the gas in the gas supply?

**A** 40 mm Hg above atmospheric pressure  
**B** 40 mm Hg below atmospheric pressure  
**C** 80 mm Hg above atmospheric pressure  
**D** 80 mm Hg below atmospheric pressure

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The question was not done well. Nearly eighty per cent of candidates failed to recognise that if one column falls by 40 mm and the other column rises by 40 mm then the difference in heights would be 80 mm.
