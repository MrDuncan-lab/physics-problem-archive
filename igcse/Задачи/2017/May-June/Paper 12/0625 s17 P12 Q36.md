> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить изменение вращающего момента катушки при увеличении числа витков и неизменном токе.

A coil of four loops of wire is placed in a magnetic field. When there is a current, the coil

experiences a turning effect.

Some extra loops of wire are wound on the coil but the current is unchanged.

How does this affect the turning effect?

**A** It is unchanged.  
**B** Its direction changes.  
**C** It decreases.  
**D** It increases.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
