# Question 4 (4 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2017 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 3 · вопрос 4

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать повышение давления тормозной жидкости по силе на педали, плечам рычага и площади поршня.

In the braking system of a car, the brake pedal rotates about a pivot when the pedal is pressed. Fig. 4.1 shows part of the braking system.

![[0625-s17-43-p6-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 4.1** (not to scale)

The driver exerts a force of 200 N on the pedal at a distance 22 cm from the pivot. As the pedal rotates about the pivot, a force is exerted on the piston and the pressure of the oil increases.

The area of the piston in the cylinder is 5.0 × 10$^{-4}$ m$^{2}$ (0.00050 m$^{2}$).

Calculate the increase in the pressure of the oil.

increase in pressure =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[4]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> 1.1 × 10$^{6}$ Pa

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> |  | $F_{1}d_{1} = F_{2}d_{2}$ OR ($F_{2}$ = ) $\dfrac{F_{1}d_{1}}{d_{2}}$<br>OR 200 × 22 ÷ 8.0 | C1 |
> |  | 550 (N) **or** 200 × 22 ÷ 8.0 | C1 |
> |  | (*p* = ) $\dfrac{F}{A}$ OR 550 ÷ 0.00050<br>OR 200 × 22 ÷ (8.0 × 0.00050) | C1 |
> |  | 1.1 × 10$^{6}$ Pa | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> There were four marks allocated to this question and this, along with the size of the answer space on the paper, might have suggested that this question was not completely straightforward. Many candidates simply ignored the effect of the lever and calculated the pressure using the force of 200 N. Of those candidates who did attempt to apply the principle of moments first, there was some uncertainty concerning the distances to be used. A perpendicular distance of 14 cm (i.e. 22 – 8.0) was not infrequently used in the calculation.
