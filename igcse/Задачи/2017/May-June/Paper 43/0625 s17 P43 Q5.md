# Question 5 (6 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2017 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 3 · вопрос 5  
Источник: [[_pdf/2017/0625_s17_qp_43.pdf#page=7|условие, стр. 7–8]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_s17_ms_43.pdf#page=5|схема оценивания, стр. 5]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_s17_er.pdf#page=33|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнить нагрев белой и чёрной одежды и объяснить влияние погоды на испарение лужи.

A footballer and a referee are discussing a puddle of water that has formed on the pitch. The footballer wears a white shirt whilst the referee wears a black shirt which, apart from its colour, is identical.

Fig. 5.1 shows the two men looking at the puddle.

![[0625-s17-43-p7-f1.png]]

**Fig. 5.1**

The weather is bright and sunny.

**(a)** State and explain how the temperature of the white shirt differs from the temperature of the black shirt. **[2]**

**(b)** The volume of water in the puddle is slowly decreasing.

**(i)** Describe how **two** changes in the weather conditions could affect the rate at which the puddle dries.

change 1  \_\_\_\_\_\_

effect  \_\_\_\_\_\_

change 2  \_\_\_\_\_\_

effect  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(ii)** Explain, in terms of the water molecules, what happens as the puddle dries. **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** white poor absorber/good reflector of <u>(IR)radiation/heat/thermal energy OR v.v. for</u> black

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | white kit cooler OR black kit warmer | M1 |
> |  | white poor absorber/good reflector of <u>(IR)radiation/heat/thermal energy OR v.v. for</u> black | A1 |
> | (b)(i) | any **two** pairs from:<br>more/less wind; dries quicker/slower temperature increases/decreases/sunnier/cloudier; dries quicker/slower<br>stops/starts raining; dries quicker/slower<br>less/more humid; dries quicker/slower | B2 |
> | (b)(ii) | molecules with most (kinetic) energy (escape) OR water cools | B1 |
> |  | escape liquid/break intermolecular bonds / molecules enter air / evaporate / become vapour | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** This section of the question was normally well answered with many candidates realising that the white shirt would be cooler than the black one. The simple statement that a white surface is a poor absorber was not credited but candidates found many different ways of suggesting that it is a poor absorber of infra-red radiation. A relatively common explanation was the statement that a white surface is a poor emitter and a poor absorber of infra-red radiation. This was not credited as in this situation, it is the absorption property of the surfaces that are significant.
>
> **(b) (i)** There were many good answers here, in which the effect of two changes in the weather were described. One source of error was to be not sufficiently specific when describing the effect. The question asks for the effect on the rate at which the puddle dries and so an answer such as *An* *increase in temperature causes more evaporation* does not quite answer the question asked. The candidates who referred to an increase (or decrease) in the humidity of the surrounding air, were divided as to the effect on the rate at which the puddle dries.
>
> **(ii)** This part was well answered; many candidates referred to the molecules needing energy in order for them to break the intermolecular bonds in the liquid.
