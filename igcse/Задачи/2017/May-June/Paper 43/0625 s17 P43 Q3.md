# Question 3 (4 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2017 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 3 · вопрос 3  
Источник: [[_pdf/2017/0625_s17_qp_43.pdf#page=5|условие, стр. 5]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_s17_ms_43.pdf#page=4|схема оценивания, стр. 4]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_s17_er.pdf#page=33|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 s17 P43]]  
Темы: [[1.4 Density]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать плотность деревянного блока и предсказать его плавание в жидкости.

A block of wood has a volume of 210 cm$^{3}$ and a mass of 180 g.

**(a)** Calculate the density of the block of wood.

density =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(b)** The block is held just above the surface of a liquid of density 0.88 g / cm$^{3}$.

Predict and explain what happens when the block is released. **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** 0.86 g / cm$^{3}$

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | (ρ = ) $\dfrac{m}{V}$ OR 180 ÷ 210 OR 0.18 ÷ 210 | C1 |
> |  | 0.86 g / cm$^{3}$ | A1 |
> | (b) | floats OR words to the same effect | B1 |
> |  | density of wood is less than density of liquid | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** This part of the question was very frequently correctly answered with only a small minority of candidates not achieving full credit. There were, however, a small number of candidates who calculated the reciprocal of the density or who supplied an incorrect unit.
>
> **(b)** There were a very large number of good answers here although a small number stated that the block would float because its density was greater than that of the liquid. The liquid was occasionally referred to as *water*.
