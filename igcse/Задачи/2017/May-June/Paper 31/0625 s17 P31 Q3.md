# Question 3 (8 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2017 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 1 · вопрос 3

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать вес шины и момент нагрузки на ветку, предложив изменение положения для более тяжёлого ребёнка.

Fig. 3.1 shows a tyre hanging from the branch of a tree.

![[0625-s17-31-p5-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 3.1**

**(a)** The mass of the tyre is 15 kg.

Calculate its weight.

weight of tyre =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ N **[2]**

**(b)** The weight of the tyre exerts a moment on the branch, about point P where the branch joins the tree.

**(i)** Explain what is meant by the term *moment*. **[1]**

**(ii)** A child sits on the tyre. The weight of the child and tyre together is 425 N. Calculate the moment of this force about point P. Use information given in Fig. 3.1. Include the unit.

moment =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[4]**

**(iii)** A heavier child wants to sit on the tyre. Describe how the tyre position should be adjusted so that the moment is the same as in **(b)(ii)**. **[1]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** 150 (N)
>
> **(b)(ii)** 1062.5 **OR** 1063

> |  | 1062.5 **OR** 1063 | A1 |
> |  | N m | B1 |
