# Question 9 (7 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2017 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 1 · вопрос 9  
Источник: [[_pdf/2017/0625_s17_qp_31.pdf#page=12|условие, стр. 12]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_s17_ms_31.pdf#page=9|схема оценивания, стр. 9]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_s17_er.pdf#page=13|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 s17 P31]]  
Темы: [[3.4 Sound]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объяснить запаздывание звука и эхо, сравнить амплитуды и рассчитать скорость звука.

Fig. 9.1 shows the position of a man working in a rock quarry. A single explosion is used to break part of one rock face.

![[0625-s17-31-p12-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 9.1**

**(a)** Explain why the man sees the flash of the explosion before he hears the bang. **[1]**

**(b)** The man hears a second bang shortly after the first bang.

**(i)** State the name given to this second bang. **[1]**

**(ii)** State how the second bang compares with the first bang in terms of its amplitude and speed.

amplitude  \_\_\_\_\_\_

speed  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(c)** The man stands 170 m from the back rock face. The time between hearing the first bang and hearing the second bang is 1.0 s.

Use the information in Fig. 9.1 to determine the speed of sound in the quarry.

speed of sound =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ m / s **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(c)** 340 (m / s)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | light travels faster than sound or converse argument | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | <u>echo</u> | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | amplitude – smaller | B1 |
> |  | speed – the same | B1 |
> | (c) | speed = distance ÷ time | C1 |
> |  | 170 + 170 **OR** 340 ÷ 1 | C1 |
> |  | 340 (m / s) | A1 |
