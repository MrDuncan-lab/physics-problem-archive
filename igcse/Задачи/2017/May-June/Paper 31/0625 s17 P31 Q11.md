# Question 11 (7 marks)

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Описать превращение электрической энергии и подключение приборов для измерения сопротивления проволоки.

Fig. 11.1 shows a power supply in series with a resistance wire and a switch.

![[0625-s17-31-p14-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 11.1**

**(a)** When the switch is closed, energy is transferred from the power supply. Explain what happens to this energy. **[2]**

**(b)** A student wants to determine the resistance of the wire. He adds components to the circuit shown in Fig. 11.1.

**(i)** He measures the current in the circuit. State the name of the component that he uses. **[1]**

**(ii)** The student measures the potential difference (p.d.) across the resistance wire. On Fig. 11.1, draw the correct symbol for the component he uses and show how he connects it. **[2]**

**(iii)** Fig. 11.2 shows the symbol for another component that the student adds to the circuit.

![[0625-s17-31-p14-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 11.2**

State the name and function of this component.

name  \_\_\_\_\_\_

function  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

> | (a) | thermal | B1 |
> |  | lost to surroundings/air owtte | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | <u>ammeter</u> | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | correct symbol for voltmeter | B1 |
> |  | connected in parallel with the resistance wire | B1 |

> |  | varies/changes current/resistance/voltage (in resistance wire) | B1 |
