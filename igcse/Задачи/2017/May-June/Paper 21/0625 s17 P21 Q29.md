> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать изменение сопротивления проводящего цилиндра при изменении диаметра и сохранении объёма.

A cylinder of conducting putty has length *l*, diameter *d* and resistance *R*. The putty is now

moulded into a cylinder of diameter 2*d* that has the same volume.

![[0625-s17-21-p14-f1.svg]]

By which factor does the resistance of the putty cylinder decrease?

**A** 2  
**B** 4  
**C** 8  
**D** 16

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The question was very demanding. Candidates did not recognise that doubling the diameter increases the cross sectional area, hence decrease the resistance by a factor of 4. This increase in area will cause the length, to also decrease by a factor of 4, giving an overall decrease in resistance of 4 × 4 =16.
