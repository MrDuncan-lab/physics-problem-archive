> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Найти равнодействующую при действии бокового ветра на мяч, падающий с предельной скоростью.

A ball of weight 1.2 N drops through the air at terminal velocity.

A sudden gust of wind exerts a horizontal force of 0.5 N on the ball from the left.

Which diagram shows the resultant force on the ball while the wind is blowing?

![[0625-s17-21-q7-options.svg]]

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The question really tested candidates’ depth of understanding of dynamics. The ball was described as falling at its terminal velocity, thus it had zero resultant force acting in a vertical direction. Consequently the resultant force acting on the ball when the wind blew was this horizontal force. Very few candidates showed this depth of understanding.
