# Question 6 (6 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2017 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 1 · вопрос 6  
Источник: [[_pdf/2017/0625_s17_qp_41.pdf#page=9|условие, стр. 9]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_s17_ms_41.pdf#page=6|схема оценивания, стр. 6]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_s17_er.pdf#page=23|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 s17 P41]]  
Темы: [[3.2.2 Refraction of light]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Построить лучи при преломлении и полном внутреннем отражении, рассчитать показатель преломления по критическому углу.

**(a)** A ray of light in glass is incident on a boundary with air.

State what happens to the ray when the angle of incidence of the ray is

**(i)** less than the critical angle of the glass, **[1]**

**(ii)** greater than the critical angle of the glass. **[1]**

**(b)** Fig. 6.1 shows a ray of light incident on a glass block at A. The critical angle of the glass is 41°.

![[0625-s17-41-p9-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 6.1** (not to scale)

**(i)** On Fig. 6.1, without calculation, continue the ray from point B until it leaves the glass block. **[2]**

**(ii)** Calculate the refractive index of the glass.

refractive index =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)(ii)** 1.5

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | (Ray passes into the air and) refracts / changes direction / bends | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | Total internal reflection (takes place) | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | Total internal reflection at B with angle of incidence equal to angle of reflection (by eye) | B1 |
> |  | Refraction into air at right-hand face with angle of refraction greater than angle of incidence | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | (n =) 1/sin c OR 1/sin 41 | M1 |
> |  | 1.5 | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** The answers to both parts of this question should have been straightforward. There were a significant number of answers that showed confusion between critical angle, refraction and total internal reflection. Candidates who did not use the terminology ‘total internal reflection’ needed to refer both to the idea of all the light being reflected and back (into the glass).
>
> **(b) (i)** Despite many candidates giving the correct answers in **(a)** relatively few gained both marks here. The most common answer was to draw a refracted ray at the first surface. As there were 2 marks for this question this should suggest to candidates that they would need to draw 2 rays. Apparently candidates did not realise that the angle marked was not the angle of incidence. Some candidates who correctly drew total internal reflection at the first surface did not draw a refracted ray at the second surface or showed refraction in the wrong direction.
>
> **(ii)** Candidates who realised that they needed to use the equation n = 1 / sin c were usually successful in obtaining 2 marks unless they gave a unit for refractive index. Those who tried to use n = sin i / sin r were usually unsuccessful unless they realised that i was 90°.
