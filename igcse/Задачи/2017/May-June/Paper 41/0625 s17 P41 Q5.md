# Question 5 (6 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2017 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 1 · вопрос 5  
Источник: [[_pdf/2017/0625_s17_qp_41.pdf#page=8|условие, стр. 8]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_s17_ms_41.pdf#page=6|схема оценивания, стр. 6]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_s17_er.pdf#page=23|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 s17 P41]]  
Темы: [[2.1.3 Gases and the absolute scale of temperature]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объяснить изменение давления нагретого газа и движение поршня до выравнивания внутреннего и внешнего давлений.

Fig. 5.1 shows some gas trapped in a metal cylinder by a piston.

![[0625-s17-41-p8-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 5.1**

**(a)** The position of the piston is fixed. The cylinder is moved from a cold room to a warm room.

Explain, in terms of molecules, what happens to the pressure of the gas in the cylinder. **[4]**

**(b)** The piston is now released. It moves to the right and finally stops.

Explain these observations in terms of the pressure and the volume of the gas in the cylinder. **[2]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | Pressure increases | B1 |
> |  | <u>Molecules (of gas) move faster/their kinetic energy increases/their momentum increases</u> | B1 |
> |  | (Molecules) collide with walls/piston more often/more frequently<br>OR greater (rate of) change of momentum | B1 |
> |  | <u>(Molecules) exert greater/more force (on wall)/hit (walls) harder</u> | B1 |
> | (b) | Pressure (of gas) falls **and** volume (of gas) increases | B1 |
> |  | Initially there is a larger pressure inside than outside/atmospheric pressure<br>OR (Piston stops when) pressure (of gas) = external/outside/atmospheric pressure | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Most candidates gained at least two marks on this question as they were able to state that the pressure increased and that the molecules moved faster or had greater KE. Some weaker candidates only referred to the energy of the molecules increasing and did not specify that it was their KE that increased. Many candidates were also awarded the mark for more frequent collisions of the molecules with the walls. There were more candidates correctly stating that the collisions were more frequent rather than just more of them. The most able candidates could also state that the force increased or that the molecules hit harder.
>
> **(b)** Good answers to this question showed that candidates had read the question carefully and answered both parts of it – stating what happened to the pressure and volume and explaining why the piston moved and eventually stopped. However there were relatively few answers that gained both marks. Some candidates were confused about the concept of volume and stated that the molecules had more room to move around and that the volume remained the same.
