# Question 11 (6 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2017 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 11  
Источник: [[_pdf/2017/0625_s17_qp_32.pdf#page=18|условие, стр. 18]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_s17_ms_32.pdf#page=11|схема оценивания, стр. 11]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_s17_er.pdf#page=16|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объяснить назначение предохранителя и рассчитать число витков вторичной обмотки трансформатора.

**(a)** The plug for a television contains a fuse.

Explain the purpose of the fuse. **[2]**

**(b)** The circuit of the television includes transformers.

**(i)** State the metal used for the two coils of each transformer. **[1]**

**(ii)** One transformer has an input voltage of 224 V and an output voltage of 16.0 V. The input coil contains 308 turns.

Calculate the number of turns on the output coil.

number of turns =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)(ii)** 22 (turns)

>
> **(b) (i)** Most candidates scored this mark. A common error was to state the metal was soft iron.
>
> **(ii)** The majority of candidates scored full marks. Most candidates who wrote down the correct equation
