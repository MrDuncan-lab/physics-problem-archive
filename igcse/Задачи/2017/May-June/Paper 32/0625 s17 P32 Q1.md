# Question 1 (5 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2017 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 1  
Источник: [[_pdf/2017/0625_s17_qp_32.pdf#page=2|условие, стр. 2]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_s17_ms_32.pdf#page=2|схема оценивания, стр. 2]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_s17_er.pdf#page=16|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбрать прибор для измерения бассейна, прочитать время заплыва и рассчитать среднюю скорость и разницу времён.

Fig. 1.1 shows students about to start a 50.0 m swimming race.

![[0625-s17-32-p2-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 1.1**

**(a)** The length of the pool is 50.0 m.

Name a suitable piece of equipment that could be used to measure the length of the pool. **[1]**

**(b)** The race starts and the students swim to the end of the 50.0 m pool.

Fig. 1.2 shows the times recorded on the stop watches for the winner and the swimmer in second place.

![[0625-s17-32-p2-f2.png]]

**Fig. 1.2**

**(i)** Determine the time taken by the winner to swim 50.0 m. Use information from Fig. 1.2.

winner’s time =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ s **[1]**

**(ii)** Calculate the average speed of the winner.

average speed =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ m/s **[2]**

**(iii)** Calculate the time difference between the winner and the swimmer in second place.

time difference =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ s **[1]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)(ii)** 0.85 (m / s)

> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | flexible rule/tape measure/measuring tape | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | 58.75 (s) | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | speed = distance ÷ time in any form | C1 |
> |  | 0.85 (m / s) | A1 |
> | (b)(iii) | 7.12 (s) | B1 |
