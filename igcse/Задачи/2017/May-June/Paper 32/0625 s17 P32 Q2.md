# Question 2 (5 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2017 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 2  
Источник: [[_pdf/2017/0625_s17_qp_32.pdf#page=3|условие, стр. 3]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_s17_ms_32.pdf#page=2|схема оценивания, стр. 2]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_s17_er.pdf#page=16|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 s17 P32]]  
Темы: [[1.4 Density]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать массу и плотность краски и объяснить плавание кисти.

**(a)** The walls of a room are to be painted.

A tin of paint has a total mass of 7000 g and a volume of 5000 cm$^{3}$.

The empty tin has a mass of 500 g.

**(i)** Determine the mass of the paint.

mass of paint =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ g **[1]**

**(ii)** Calculate the density of the paint. Include the unit.

density =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

**(b)** The painter drops a brush into the tin of paint. The brush floats.

Suggest why the brush floats. **[1]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)(ii)** 1.3

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | 6500 (g) | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | density = mass ÷ volume in any form | B1 |
> |  | 1.3 | A1 |
> |  | g / cm$^{3}$ | B1 |
> | (b) | density (of brush) is less (than) density of paint | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** The majority of candidates scored this mark. However, a surprising number tried to use density = mass ÷ volume rather than subtracting masses.
>
> **(ii)** With ecf from part **(i)** the majority of candidates scored two or three marks. Common errors were
>
> using density = volume × mass, and either omitting or inserting an incorrect unit.
>
> **(b)** The majority of candidates gave sensible answers relating to the density of the brush and the paint. Weaker candidates suggested that the brush was lighter than the paint.
