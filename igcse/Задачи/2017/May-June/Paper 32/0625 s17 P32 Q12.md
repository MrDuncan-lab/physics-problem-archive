# Question 12 (5 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2017 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 12  
Источник: [[_pdf/2017/0625_s17_qp_32.pdf#page=19|условие, стр. 19]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_s17_ms_32.pdf#page=12|схема оценивания, стр. 12]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_s17_er.pdf#page=16|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить частицы атома и изотоп лития, рассчитать оставшуюся массу после двух периодов полураспада.

Fig. 12.1 represents the particles in an atom of the element lithium.

![[0625-s17-32-p19-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 12.1** (not to scale)

**(a)** **(i)** State the name of particle X. **[1]**

**(ii)** State the charge of particle X. **[1]**

**(iii)** Tick **one** box in Fig. 12.2 that correctly represents an isotope of lithium.

![[0625-s17-32-p19-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 12.2**

**(b)** A sample of lithium contains 1.00 mg of a radioactive isotope of lithium.

Calculate the mass of the isotope that remains after 2 half-lives.

mass =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ mg **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** 0.25 (mg)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | proton | B1 |
> |  | positive or +1 | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | tick in third box | B1 |
> | (b) | idea of mass being halved, e.g. 0.5 | C1 |
> |  | 0.25 (mg) | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i),(ii)** The majority of candidates identified the name of and the charge on the particle. A common error
>
> was to call particle X an electron, and a few gave the name alpha particle.
>
> **(iii)** Many candidates correctly identified the isotope of lithium.
>
> **(b)** The vast majority of candidates scored either one or two marks for this question. Weaker candidates thought that the mass would either double or quadruple.
