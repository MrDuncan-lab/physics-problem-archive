> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить яркость ламп при изменении сопротивления другой ветви и неизменном напряжении питания.

The diagram shows a power supply with a constant electromotive force (e.m.f.). It is connected to

a voltmeter, a variable resistor and two identical lamps, P and Q. Both lamps are lit.

![[0625-s17-11-p15-f1.svg]]

The resistance of the variable resistor is increased. The voltmeter reading remains unchanged.

What happens to the brightness of the lamps?

|  | lamp P | lamp Q |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | brighter | brighter |
| **B** | dimmer | dimmer |
| **C** | dimmer | unchanged |
| **D** | unchanged | unchanged |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
