> Выбрать компонент, автоматически увеличивающий ток вентилятора при нагревании компьютера.

A computer engineer wants the speed of a fan to increase automatically when the temperature

inside a computer rises. The engineer knows that a larger current causes the fan to turn more quickly.

![[0625-s17-11-p14-f2.svg]]

Which component should be placed at X to make this happen?

**A** a relay  
**B** a thermistor  
**C** a transformer  
**D** a variable resistor

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
