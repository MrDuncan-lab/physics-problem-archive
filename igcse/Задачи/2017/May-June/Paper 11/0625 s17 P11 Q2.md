# Question 2 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2017 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 1 · вопрос 2  
Источник: [[_pdf/2017/0625_s17_qp_11.pdf#page=2|условие, стр. 2]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_s17_ms_11.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_s17_er.pdf#page=1|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить ускорение свободного падения на другой высоте в пределах лабораторной установки.

On Earth, a ball is dropped and falls 2.0 m in a vacuum.

The acceleration of the ball at 1.0 m is 10 m / s$^{2}$.

![[0625-s17-11-p2-f3.svg]]

What is the acceleration of the ball at 0.5 m?

**A** 5.0 m / s$^{2}$  
**B** 10 m / s$^{2}$  
**C** 15 m / s$^{2}$  
**D** 20 m / s$^{2}$

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The question was not answered well, with relatively few candidates recognising that the acceleration of free fall does not change as an object falls. The majority of candidates were under the impression that the acceleration would fall to half the value when the ball had fallen through half its initial height.
