> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбрать материалы магнитной удочки и рыбок с учётом постоянного и наведённого намагничивания.

In a child’s toy, a metal rod is used to lift metal fish out of a toy pond. The fish are magnetically

attracted to the end of the rod. There is no magnetic force between the fish.

![[0625-s17-11-p13-f1.png]]

metal fish

What are possible materials from which the fish and the rod are made?

|  | fish | rod |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | aluminium | soft iron |
| **B** | aluminium | steel |
| **C** | soft iron | soft iron |
| **D** | soft iron | steel |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
