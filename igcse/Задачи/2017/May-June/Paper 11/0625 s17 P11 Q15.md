The diagram shows a metal foot bridge located in the Sahara desert where the temperature is

much less at night than during the day. The ends of the bridge are firmly fixed to the sides of a narrow valley.

The solid line shows the bridge during the coldest part of the night.

Which dotted line shows the bridge at the hottest part of the day?
