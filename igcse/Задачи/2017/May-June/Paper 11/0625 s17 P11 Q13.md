# Question 13 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2017 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 1 · вопрос 13  
Источник: [[_pdf/2017/0625_s17_qp_11.pdf#page=7|условие, стр. 7]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_s17_ms_11.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_s17_er.pdf#page=1|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить содержимое закрытого шприца по возможности его сжатия.

A student has a syringe which has its open end sealed. The syringe is completely filled with a

certain substance, as shown in diagram 1. The student pushes the piston of the syringe in as far as possible, as shown in diagram 2.

syringe piston

![[0625-s17-11-p7-f2.svg]]

What is in the syringe?

**A** a gas and a liquid  
**B** a solid and a liquid  
**C** a liquid only  
**D** a solid only

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> A significant number of candidates thought the syringe contained a liquid only, not recognising that liquids are (virtually) incompressible.
