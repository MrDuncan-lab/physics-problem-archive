A block of ice at –20 °C is heated until it turns to steam. The graph of temperature against thermal

energy absorbed is shown.

The latent heat of fusion of ice is 340 kJ / kg.

![[0625-s17-23-p10-f1.svg]]

What is the mass of the ice?

**A** 1.0 kg  
**B** 2.0 kg  
**C** 3.0 kg  
**D** 4.0 kg

> [!answer]- Ответ
