> Определить изменения амплитуды и частоты при увеличении громкости и высоты звука.

A fire alarm is not loud enough and the pitch is too low. An engineer adjusts the alarm so that it

produces a louder note of a higher pitch.

What effect does this have on the amplitude and on the frequency of the sound?

|  | amplitude | frequency |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | larger | greater |
| **B** | larger | smaller |
| **C** | smaller | greater |
| **D** | smaller | smaller |
