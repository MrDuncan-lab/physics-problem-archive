> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить температуры плавления и кипения вещества по графику нагревания.

A solid substance is heated at a constant rate. The solid changes into a liquid and then into a

gas.

The graph shows how the temperature of the substance changes.

![[0625-s17-13-p8-f1.svg]]

What is the melting point and what is the boiling point of the substance?

|  | melting point / °C | boiling point / °C |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | 200 | 300 |
| **B** | 200 | 500 |
| **C** | 300 | 500 |
| **D** | 300 | 700 |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
