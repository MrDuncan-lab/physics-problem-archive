> Определить изменение движения молекул газа при нагревании закрытого сосуда.

The diagram represents molecules of a gas inside a closed container of constant volume.

![[0625-s17-13-p6-f1.svg]]

What happens to the molecules of the gas when the container is heated?

**A** They expand.  
**B** They get closer together.  
**C** They hit the container walls with less force.  
**D** They move faster.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
