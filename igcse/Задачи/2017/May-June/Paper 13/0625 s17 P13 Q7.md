> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить величину равнодействующей двух сил по рисунку.

The diagram shows an object being acted upon by two forces.

![[0625-s17-13-p4-f1.svg]]

3.0 N

What is the size of the resultant force on the object?

**A** 2.0 N  
**B** 3.0 N  
**C** 9.0 N  
**D** 18 N

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> There was a significant minority of candidates who simply added the magnitudes of the two forces, without recognising that they acted in opposite directions to each other.
