> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать давление куба на землю по массе и длине ребра.

A solid cube has sides 0.50 m long and a mass of 120 kg. It stands on the ground on one face.

What pressure does the cube exert on the ground?

**A** 480 kg / m$^{3}$  
**B** 960 kg / m$^{3}$  
**C** 4800 N / m$^{2}$  
**D** 9600 N / m$^{2}$

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question was not done well with the majority of candidates not recognising that pressure is force divided by area not mass divided by area.
