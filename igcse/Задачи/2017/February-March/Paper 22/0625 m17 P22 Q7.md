# Question 7 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · February/March 2017 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 2 · вопрос 7  
Источник: [[_pdf/2017/0625_m17_qp_22.pdf#page=4|условие, стр. 4]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_m17_ms_22.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_m17_er.pdf#page=5|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

Темы: [[1.5.1 Effects of forces]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить направление равнодействующей сил при движении автомобиля по окружности с постоянной скоростью.

A car moves in a circular path as it turns a corner on a horizontal road.

The car moves at constant speed.

![[0625-m17-22-p4-f1.png]]

Which description of the forces acting on the car is correct?

**A** All the forces are balanced as the car is moving at constant speed.  
**B** The forces are unbalanced and the resultant force acts away from the centre of the circle.  
**C** The forces are unbalanced and the resultant force acts towards the centre of the circle.  
**D** The forces are unbalanced and the resultant force is in the direction of travel of the car.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> There was some confusion here with candidates not showing an understanding that when an object moves in a circle at constant speed there is a resultant force towards the centre of the circle.
