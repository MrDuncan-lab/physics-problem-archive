> Определить массу тела и его вес на Земле по весу в более слабом гравитационном поле.

An object in a space probe above the Earth weighs 3.5 N. The gravitational field strength at the height of the space probe is 7.0 N / kg.

The gravitational field strength on the Earth’s surface is 10 N / kg.

What are the mass and the weight of the object on the Earth’s surface?

|  | mass / kg | weight / N |
|---|---|---|
| A | 0.50 | 3.5 |
| B | 0.50 | 5.0 |
| C | 2.0 | 3.5 |
| D | 2.0 | 20 |
