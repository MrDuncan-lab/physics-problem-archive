> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнить ускорения, времена падения и средние скорости шаров при свободном падении с разных высот.

Four balls with different masses are dropped from the heights shown.

P Q R S

![[0625-m17-22-p2-f2.png]]

Air resistance may be ignored.

Which statement about the balls is correct?

**A** Ball P has the greatest acceleration.  
**B** Balls Q and R take the same time to fall to the ground.  
**C** The acceleration of ball R is half the acceleration of ball P.  
**D** Ball S has the greatest average speed.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This was problematic for some candidates with the most common error being to think that the heavier the ball, the faster it would fall.
