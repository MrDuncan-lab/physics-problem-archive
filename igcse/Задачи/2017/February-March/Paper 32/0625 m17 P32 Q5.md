> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать давление столба на грунт и объяснить эффект заострения его нижнего конца.

The bucket at the front of a tractor is used to push fence posts down into the ground, as shown in Fig. 5.1.

![[0625-m17-32-p8-f1.png]]

**Fig. 5.1**

The area of each post in contact with the ground is 100 cm$^{2}$. When the bucket pushes a post, the downward force from the post on the ground is 6500 N.

**(a)** Calculate the pressure that the post exerts on the ground.

pressure =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ N / cm$^{2}$ **[3]**

**(b)** A farmer cuts the bottom of one of the posts to make it more pointed. The bucket applies the same force as before.

Explain the effect this has on the pressure exerted by the post on the ground. **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** 65 (N / cm$^{2}$)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | P = F ÷ A in any recognised form | C1 |
> |  | 6500 ÷ 100 | C1 |
> |  | 65 (N / cm$^{2}$) | A1 |
> | (b) | smaller area (at point) | B1 |
> |  | greater pressure | B1 |
