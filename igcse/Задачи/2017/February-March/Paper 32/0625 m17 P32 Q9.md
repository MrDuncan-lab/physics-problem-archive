> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объяснить конвективное нагревание воды через расширение, изменение плотности и движение жидкости.

Fig. 9.1 shows an electric heating element in a beaker containing water. The heating element is switched on.

![[0625-m17-32-p13-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 9.1**

Explain how thermal energy is transferred throughout the water in the beaker. Use ideas about density and expansion in your answer. **[5]**

> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |

> |  | water expands **OR** water volume increases | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question was generally not answered well and there were very few candidates who gained full credit. Common errors included responses in terms of the heater expanding, causing atoms to expand and then water evaporating resulting in water of a lower density.
