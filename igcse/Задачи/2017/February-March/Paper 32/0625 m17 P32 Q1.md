# Question 1 (8 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · February/March 2017 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 1  
Источник: [[_pdf/2017/0625_m17_qp_32.pdf#page=2|условие, стр. 2]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_m17_ms_32.pdf#page=4|схема оценивания, стр. 4]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_m17_er.pdf#page=9|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 m17 P32]]  
Темы: [[1.2 Motion]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Прочитать график скорости, определить расстояние при торможении и сравнить ускорения двух автомобилей по наклону графиков.

Fig. 1.1 shows how the speed of a car varies over a short time.

![[0625-m17-32-p2-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 1.1**

**(a)** Different parts of the journey are labelled A, B, C, D and E.

**(i)** State a part of the graph that shows the car at rest. **[1]**

**(ii)** State a part of the graph that shows the car moving with constant speed. **[1]**

**(iii)** During part of the journey the car decelerates.

Calculate the distance travelled by the car when it is decelerating.

distance travelled =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ m **[3]**

**(b)** Another car accelerates, from rest, starting at time 0 s. This car has a constant acceleration. Its speed at 20 s is 10 m / s.

On Fig. 1.1, draw a line to show this motion. **[2]**

**(c)** Describe, using Fig. 1.1, how you can decide which car has the greater acceleration. **[1]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)(iii)** 64 (m)
