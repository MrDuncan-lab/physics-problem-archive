> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить массу и объём камня по разностям показаний приборов и вычислить его плотность.

A student wants to find the density of a small stone. Fig. 2.1 shows a measuring cylinder, containing water, placed on a balance.

![[0625-m17-32-p3-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 2.1**

The stone is placed in the water as shown in Fig. 2.2.

![[0625-m17-32-p3-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 2.2**

**(a)** The student determines the mass of the stone and its volume.

Use the labels K, L, M and N from Fig. 2.1 and Fig. 2.2 to show how to calculate the mass and the volume of the stone.

mass of stone = reading  \_\_\_\_\_\_ minus reading  \_\_\_\_\_\_

volume of stone = reading  \_\_\_\_\_\_ minus reading  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(b)** The student obtained the following values.

78.0 g

mass of stone =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

30.0 cm$^{3}$

volume of stone =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

Calculate the density of the stone. Include the unit.

density =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ unit  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** 2.6

> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | N, L | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | M, K | B1 |
> | (b) | D = M ÷ V in any recognised form | C1 |
> |  | 2.6 | A1 |
> |  | g / cm$^{3}$ | B1 |
