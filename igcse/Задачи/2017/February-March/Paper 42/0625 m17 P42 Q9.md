# Question 9 (9 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · February/March 2017 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 2 · вопрос 9  
Источник: [[_pdf/2017/0625_m17_qp_42.pdf#page=12|условие, стр. 12–13]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_m17_ms_42.pdf#page=10|схема оценивания, стр. 10]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_m17_er.pdf#page=12|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 m17 P42]]  
Темы: [[4.2.4 Resistance]], [[4.2.5 Electrical energy and electrical power]], [[4.3.2 Series and parallel circuits]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> По вольт-амперной характеристике лампы определить сопротивление и мощность, рассчитать параметры питания пяти параллельных ламп.

Fig. 9.1 shows a graph of current against potential difference (p.d.) for a filament lamp.

![[0625-m17-42-p12-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 9.1**

**(a)** State what happens to the resistance of the filament of the lamp as the p.d. changes

**(i)** from 0 V to 1.0 V, **[1]**

**(ii)** from 1.0 V to 8.0 V. **[1]**

**(b)** At normal brightness, the p.d. across the lamp is 8.0 V.

Calculate, for normal brightness,

**(i)** the resistance of the lamp,

resistance =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

**(ii)** the power of the lamp.

power =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(c)** Five of these lamps, operating at normal brightness, are connected in parallel to a power supply.

![[0625-m17-42-p13-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 9.2**

Determine

**(i)** the electromotive force (e.m.f.) of the power supply,

e.m.f. =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(ii)** the current from the power supply.

current =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)(i)** 11 Ω
>
> **(b)(ii)** 5.8 W

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | Resistance constant | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | Resistance increases | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | I = V/R in any form OR (R=) V/I | C1 |
> |  | 8.0/0.72 | C1 |
> |  | 11 Ω | A1 |
> | (b)(ii) | (P = ) IV OR 0.72 × 8.0 | C1 |
> |  | 5.8 W | A1 |

> |  | 5.7 W or 5.8 W (dependent on exact data used) | (A1) |
> | (c)(i) | 8.0 V | B1 |
> | (c)(ii) | (5 × 0.72 =) 3.6 A | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Many candidates, in deciding what happens to the resistances, clearly looked at the shape of the graph rather than considering the values of p.d. / current. Common answers for **(i)** were increases rather than constant and for **(ii)**, decreases rather than increases.
>
> **(b)** Using correct formulae and correct data from the graph allowed many candidates to be awarded full credit in both **(i)** and **(ii)**. Mistakes were usually in reading data from the graph.
>
> **(c) (i)** The idea that in a parallel circuit, the e.m.f. of a supply is the same as the p.d. across each parallel circuit component, was not well known. Wrong answers of 40 V were often seen.
>
> **(ii)** The idea that the current from the supply in a parallel circuit is the sum of the currents in each parallel component was more firmly understood. Correct answers of 3.6 A were common.
