> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Найти равнодействующую силы тяги и сопротивления движению автомобиля.

A car engine causes a forward force of 100 kN to act on the car. The total resistive force on the car is 20 kN.

What is the resultant force on the car?

**A** 5.0 kN  
**B** 60 kN  
**C** 80 kN  
**D** 120 kN

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The question was answered well; there were some candidates who added the two forces together, not realising that when the forces are in opposite directions they will then subtract.
