> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объяснить повышение давления в автомобильной шине при нагревании учащением ударов молекул о стенки.

On a hot day, the pressure of the air in a car tyre is greater than on a cold day.

Why is the pressure greater on a hot day?

**A** The air molecules strike each other more frequently.  
**B** The air molecules strike each other with greater force.  
**C** The air molecules strike the tyre walls more frequently.  
**D** The number of air molecules in the tyre increases.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Better performing candidates recognised that it is the striking of the molecules against the tyre wall that exerts the pressure.
