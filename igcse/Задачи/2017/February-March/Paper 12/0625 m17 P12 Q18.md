> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить возможность теплопроводности и конвекции в различных средах и вакууме.

The diagram shows the gap between a hot surface and a cold surface. The gap can contain air,

solid iron, a vacuum or liquid water.

![[0625-m17-12-p8-f1.svg]]

Which row shows whether heat can be transferred between the surfaces by conduction and convection?

|  |  | conduction | convection |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | air (gas) | yes | no |
| B | iron (solid) | yes | no |
| C | vacuum | no | yes |
| D | water (liquid) | yes | no |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> A considerable number of candidates incorrectly believed that convection currents occur in a vacuum.
