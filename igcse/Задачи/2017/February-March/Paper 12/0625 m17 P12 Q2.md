> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнить ускорение свободно падающего тела на разных высотах в лабораторной вакуумной установке.

In a laboratory, a ball is dropped in a vacuum and falls 200 cm.

![[0625-m17-12-p2-f2.svg]]

Which statement describes the acceleration of the ball?

**A** It is greater at 10 cm than at 200 cm.  
**B** It is greatest at 200 cm.  
**C** It is smaller at 50 cm than at 100 cm.  
**D** It is the same value at 50 cm as at 150 cm.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Only better performing candidates understood that in the absence of air resistance, objects fall with a constant acceleration.
