What is the main type of radiation given out by a campfire?

**A** infra-red  
**B** microwave  
**C** ultraviolet  
**D** X-ray

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> A number of candidates thought that a campfire emits microwaves.
