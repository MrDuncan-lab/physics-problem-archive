> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить изменения скорости света и направления луча при переходе из воздуха в стекло.

A ray of light is incident on the surface of a glass block.

![[0625-m17-12-p11-f1.svg]]

Which row describes how the speed and the direction of the ray of light change when it enters the glass?

|  | speed in glass | direction in glass |
|---|---|---|
| A | decreases | closer to the normal |
| B | decreases | further from the normal |
| C | increases | closer to the normal |
| D | increases | further from the normal |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Many candidates knew that light slows down on moving from air to glass; fewer realised that the refracted ray moves towards the normal.
