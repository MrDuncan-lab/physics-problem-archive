> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить виды излучения источника по поглощению бумагой, алюминием и свинцом.

A radioactive source is placed near a detector.

The radiation arriving at the detector from the source is measured for 10 minutes with different materials placed between the source and the detector.

![[0625-m17-12-p19-f1.svg]]

radioactive source

| material between | radiation detected |
|---|---|
| source and detector | / counts |
| none | 5626 |
| sheet of paper | 5629 |
| thick sheet of aluminium | 2226 |
| thick sheet of lead | 255 |

Which types of radiation are emitted by the source?

**A** α-particles and γ-rays  
**B** α-particles only  
**C** β-particles and γ-rays  
**D** β-particles only

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
