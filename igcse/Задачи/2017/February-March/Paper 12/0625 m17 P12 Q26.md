# Question 26 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · February/March 2017 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 26  
Источник: [[_pdf/2017/0625_m17_qp_12.pdf#page=13|условие, стр. 13]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_m17_ms_12.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_m17_er.pdf#page=1|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 m17 P12]]  
Темы: [[3.4 Sound]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбрать верное утверждение об источнике и свойствах ультразвука.

Which statement about ultrasound is correct?

**A** It is produced by a rapidly vibrating source.  
**B** It is uncomfortable to human ears.  
**C** Its frequency must be greater than 300 kHz.  
**D** It travels the fastest in a vacuum.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Candidates found this question challenging as they did not recognise the idea that sound is produced by vibrating sources and that ultrasound is very high frequency (greater than 20 kHz).
