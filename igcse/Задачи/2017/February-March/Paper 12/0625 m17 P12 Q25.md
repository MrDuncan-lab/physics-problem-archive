> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбрать правильное описание звуковых волн, образующих слабое эхо.

A student stands a few hundred metres away from a wall and shouts. He hears a faint echo.

Which statement is correct?

**A** The sound waves returning are quiet because they have a reduced frequency.  
**B** The sound waves returning are quiet because they have a reduced wavelength.  
**C** The sound waves returning to the student are longitudinal.  
**D** The sound waves returning to the student are transverse.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The question was not answered particularly well, with candidates clearly not understanding that sound waves are longitudinal.
