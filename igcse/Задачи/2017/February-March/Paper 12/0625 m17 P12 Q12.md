> Выбрать тело, оказывающее наименьшее давление при одинаковом весе и разных площадях опоры.

The diagram shows four solid objects resting on a horizontal surface. The objects all have the

same weight, and are drawn to the same scale.

Which object exerts the least pressure on the surface?

![[0625-m17-12-p6-f2.png]]

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Many candidates found this question challenging. All the objects have the same mass but candidates did not realise that the maximum pressure will be exerted by the object with the smallest contact area with the surface.
