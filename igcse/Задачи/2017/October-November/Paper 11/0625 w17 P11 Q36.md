> Определить направление магнитного поля прямого провода и изменение его силы с расстоянием.

There is an electric current in a straight wire in the direction into the page. This produces a

magnetic field around the wire. All the field lines are circles but only one field line is shown.

![[0625-w17-11-p18-f1.svg]]

Which row describes the magnetic field?

|  | direction of the field lines | spacing of the field lines |
|---|---|---|
| **A** **B** **C** **D** | anti-clockwise anti-clockwise clockwise clockwise | evenly spaced over the whole field more widely spaced further from the wire evenly spaced over the whole field more widely spaced further from the wire |
