> Выбрать правильное включение предохранителя в сетевой цепи прибора.

An appliance is connected to a mains supply. Its circuit also contains a switch and a fuse.

Which circuit shows the fuse in the correct position?

![[0625-w17-11-q35-options.svg]]

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> In many cases candidates were not aware that the fuse in a circuit should be in the live line and that if a fuse in the neutral line blows then the potential difference between the live line and earth will remain high.
