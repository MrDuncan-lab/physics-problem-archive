> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить путь автомобиля по площади под графиком скорости от времени.

The graph shows how the speed of a car varies over a period of 10 s.

![[0625-w17-11-p2-f3.svg]]

How far does the car travel during the 10 s?

**A** 10 m  
**B** 50 m  
**C** 75 m  
**D** 100 m

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> A number of candidates did not recognise that in the first 5 s that the car was accelerating from rest. Consequently for those 5 s the average speed was half the maximum speed.
