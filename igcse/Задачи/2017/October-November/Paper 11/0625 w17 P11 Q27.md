> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Предсказать сохранение сцепления железных и стальных гвоздей после удаления внешнего магнита.

A train of steel nails and a train of iron nails hang from a strong magnet.

![[0625-w17-11-p13-f1.svg]]

The trains are then carefully removed from the magnet.

What happens to the trains?

**A** Both trains fall apart.  
**B** Both trains stay together.  
**C** Only the train of iron nails falls apart.  
**D** Only the train of steel nails falls apart.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Many candidates did not demonstrate a knowledge of the properties of magnetically hard and magnetically soft metals.
