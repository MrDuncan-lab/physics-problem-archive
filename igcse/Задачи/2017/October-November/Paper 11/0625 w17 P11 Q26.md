> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать скорость звука по расстоянию до скалы и задержке эха.

A student stands 180 m in front of a vertical, flat cliff and bangs together two pieces of wood to

make a short, loud sound.

A timer records the echo of the sound 1.5 seconds after the pieces of wood are banged together.

Based on this result, what is the speed of sound?

**A** 120 m / s  
**B** 240 m / s  
**C** 270 m / s  
**D** 540 m / s

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Most candidates failed to recognise that with an echo, the sound has to travel to the reflecting surface and back again.
