# Question 5 (4 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2017 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 1 · вопрос 5  
Источник: [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_qp_31.pdf#page=7|условие, стр. 7]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_ms_31.pdf#page=4|схема оценивания, стр. 4]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_er.pdf#page=17|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

Темы: [[2.1.1 States of matter]], [[2.1.2 Particle model]], [[2.2.3 Melting, boiling and evaporation]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Различить состояния вещества по расположению частиц, плотности, сжимаемости и испарению с поверхности.

Complete the sentences. Choose from the words in the box.

![[0625-w17-31-p7-f1.svg]]

The words may be used once, more than once or not at all.

**(a)** The atoms are usually arranged in regular patterns in a  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(b)** The state of matter with the lowest density is a  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(c)** Evaporation takes place when the most energetic molecules leave the surface of a **[1]**

**(d)** A small force can change the volume of a  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | solid | B1 |
> | (b) | gas | B1 |
> | (c) | liquid | B1 |
> | (d) | gas | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The majority of candidates gained full credit for this question. The most common error was to confuse lowest and highest, and so in **(b)** these candidates gave “solid” instead of “gas” as the answer.
