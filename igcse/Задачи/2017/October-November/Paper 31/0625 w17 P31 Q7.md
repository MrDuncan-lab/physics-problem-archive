# Question 7 (10 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2017 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 1 · вопрос 7  
Источник: [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_qp_31.pdf#page=10|условие, стр. 10–11]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_ms_31.pdf#page=5|схема оценивания, стр. 5]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_er.pdf#page=17|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w17 P31]]  
Темы: [[3.4 Sound]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Описать продольные звуковые колебания, диапазон слышимости и рассчитать скорость ультразвука и глубину моря по эху.

**(a)** Fig. 7.1 shows a man listening to a radio.

![[0625-w17-31-p10-f1.png]]

**Fig. 7.1**

**(i)** Sound from the radio makes an air particle at X vibrate.

On Fig. 7.1 draw two arrows on point X to show the directions of vibration of the air particle. **[2]**

**(ii)** Which of these terms correctly describes the sound wave?

![[0625-w17-31-p10-f2.svg]]

**[1]**

**(iii)** Suggest a value for the frequency of the sound that the man can hear. State the unit.

frequency =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(iv)** Explain why the man cannot hear ultrasound. **[1]**

**(b)** Fig. 7.2 shows a distance-time graph for ultrasound travelling in sea-water.

![[0625-w17-31-p11-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 7.2**

**(i)** Use the graph to calculate the speed of ultrasound in sea-water.

speed =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ m / s **[2]**

**(ii)** A scientist measures the depth of the sea by using ultrasound. She sends a pulse of ultrasound from the ship to the seabed. It reflects from the seabed as shown in Fig. 7.3.

![[0625-w17-31-p11-f2.png]]

**Fig. 7.3**

The time taken between sending a pulse and receiving the echo is 0.60 s. Use the graph to determine the depth of the sea.

depth =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ m **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)(i)** 1500 (m / s)
>
> **(b)(ii)** depth = 450 (m)

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** Many candidates failed to read the question carefully and merely drew one arrow on the diagram.
>
> **(ii)** The vast majority of candidates identified sound waves as being longitudinal.
>
> **(ii)** Most candidates answered this question well. The most common error was to give either a wrong unit or to omit the unit altogether.
>
> **(iv)** Most candidates identified ultrasound as a sound wave having a frequency above the range of
>
> normal human hearing. The most common error was to state that ultrasound is below the lowest frequency audible to the human ear.
>
> **(b) (i)** The majority of candidates correctly calculated the speed of ultrasound in sea water. Weaker candidates often multiplied their two values from the graph rather than dividing distance by time.
>
> **(ii)** The majority of candidates gained partial credit for this item. The most common error was failing to divide their distance by two.
