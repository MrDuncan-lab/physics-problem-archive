# Question 2 (5 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2017 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 1 · вопрос 2  
Источник: [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_qp_31.pdf#page=3|условие, стр. 3]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_ms_31.pdf#page=2|схема оценивания, стр. 2]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_er.pdf#page=17|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Описать измерение средней скорости плывущей палочки и определить соотношение сил при постоянной скорости.

Fig. 2.1 shows a river flowing through a village. There are two bridges across the river.

![[0625-w17-31-p3-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 2.1**

Two students plan to measure the speed of a stick as it floats on the river between bridge X and bridge Y.

**(a)** The students plan to drop a stick into the middle of the river from bridge X. The stick moves with the water between bridge X and bridge Y.

Describe how the students can determine the average speed of the stick. **[4]**

**(b)** The stick moves with constant speed. One statement correctly describes the horizontal forces acting on the stick.

Put a tick (✓) in the box next to the correct statement.

![[0625-w17-31-p3-f2.svg]]

The forward force and the backward force are equal.

The forward force is greater than the backward force.

The backward force is greater than the forward force. **[1]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | Any four from:<br>Measure the distance between the two bridges<br>Start stopwatch when stick hits water / starts moving (with river)<br>stop stopwatch when stick reaches bridge Y<br>Use speed = distance ÷ time<br>repeat procedure and find average | B4 |
> | (b) | 2nd box ticked<br>The forward force and the backward force are equal | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Many candidates answered fully correctly and gave clear and detailed descriptions of how to determine the average speed of the stick. However, weaker candidates gave vague and imprecise statements.
>
> **(b)** Many candidates answered correctly, but most thought that to move with constant speed the forward force is greater than the backward force or only a forward force acts.
