> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Вычислить равнодействующую сил на поплавок и описать измерение плотности металлического звена неправильной формы.

Fig 3.1 shows a warning marker floating on the surface of a lake.

![[0625-w17-31-p4-f1.png]]

**Fig. 3.1**

The marker is attached by a metal chain to a heavy object on the bottom of the lake.

**(a)** Fig. 3.2 shows the forces acting on the marker at one moment in time.

![[0625-w17-31-p4-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 3.2**

Calculate the resultant force on the marker.

resultant force =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ N

direction =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(b)** Fig. 3.3 shows part of the metal chain. It is made from small metal loops.

![[0625-w17-31-p5-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 3.3**

A damaged loop is removed from the chain. Describe a method to determine the density of the metal from which the loops are made. **[5]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | subtraction of forces to obtain resultant or 30 (N) | B1 |
> |  | up(wards) | B1 |
> | (b) | any five from:<br>measure mass (on top pan balance)<br>part fill measuring cylinder with water (and note volume)<br>submerge link in measuring cylinder<br>determine increase in volume<br>increase in volume = volume of link<br>use density = mass ÷ volume<br>Only award full marks for a viable method | B5 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** The majority of candidates correctly calculated the resultant force on the marker. A common error was to give the direction as north rather than up the page or in the direction of the 280 N force. Weaker candidates added or divided the forces.
>
> **(b)** Only the stronger candidates answered this question fully correctly. Many candidates gave answers which were too vague or imprecise. A significant number of candidates failed to read the question carefully, or just gave a description of a method to find the volume of one of the links.
