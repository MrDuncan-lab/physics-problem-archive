# Question 2 (7 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2017 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 2  
Источник: [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_qp_32.pdf#page=3|условие, стр. 3]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_ms_32.pdf#page=2|схема оценивания, стр. 2]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_er.pdf#page=21|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w17 P32]]  
Темы: [[1.2 Motion]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Описать движение корабля по графику скорости и определить пройденное расстояние.

Fig. 2.1 shows a speed-time graph for a ship starting to move.

![[0625-w17-32-p3-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 2.1**

**(a)** Describe the motion of the ship during each section of the graph.

A  \_\_\_\_\_\_

B  \_\_\_\_\_\_

C  \_\_\_\_\_\_

D  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[4]**

**(b)** Determine the distance travelled by the ship in section B of the graph.

distance =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ m **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** 240 (m)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | A – accelerates (from rest) | B1 |
> |  | B – constant speed (of 2 m / s) | B1 |
> |  | C – accelerates at faster rate / higher acceleration than previously | B1 |
> |  | D – faster constant speed (of 10 m / s) | B1 |
> | (b) | 2 minutes = 120 s | C1 |
> |  | area under the graph OR *d = s* × *t* OR 2 × 120 | C1 |
> |  | 240 (m) | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Sections A and B were answered well by nearly all candidates. A small proportion of candidates indicated that the vessel was stationary at Section B. Only the better prepared candidates gained full credit indicating faster rate and higher speeds for Sections C and D.
>
> **(b)** Many candidates gave an incorrect response of 4 m, failing to take account of the time in minutes. In many cases credit for any correct working could not be awarded as candidates had not given an equation or shown their calculations.
