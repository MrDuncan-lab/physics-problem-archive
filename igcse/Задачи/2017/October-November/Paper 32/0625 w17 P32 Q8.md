# Question 8 (8 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2017 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 8

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить расположение частиц атома, протонное и массовое числа, объяснить изотопы и записать обозначение нуклида.

**(a)** An atom of carbon contains protons, neutrons and electrons.

Indicate where each particle is found in the atom. Place a tick in the appropriate box.

![[0625-w17-32-p10-f1.svg]]

**[3]**

**(b)** An atom of carbon contains 6 protons, 7 neutrons and 6 electrons.

**(i)** State the proton number of the carbon. **[1]**

**(ii)** State the nucleon number of the carbon. **[1]**

**(c)** Carbon has many different isotopes.

**(i)** Explain the meaning of the term *isotope*. **[2]**

**(ii)** The nuclide notation for the carbon in **(b)** is $^{13}_{6}$C.

Suggest the nuclide notation for another possible isotope of carbon. **[1]**
