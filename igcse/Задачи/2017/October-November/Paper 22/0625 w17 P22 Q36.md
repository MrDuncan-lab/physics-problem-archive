> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить направление силы на проводник по направлению тока и магнитного поля.

The diagram shows a wire hanging freely between the poles of a magnet. There is a current in

the wire in the direction shown.

![[0625-w17-22-p16-f1.svg]]

The magnet and current cause a force to act on the wire.

In which direction does this force act?

**A** into the page (away from you)  
**B** out of the page (toward you)  
**C** to the left  
**D** to the right

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Most candidates showed an understanding of the three dimensional nature of motor effect and the majority chose the correct sense of the movement.
