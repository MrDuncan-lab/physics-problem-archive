> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбрать правильную связь массы, веса и напряжённости гравитационного поля.

A body of mass *m* has a weight *W* in a location where the gravitational field strength is *g*.

Which statement about these quantities is correct?

**A** *m* and *W* are both forces.  
**B** *m* and *W* are both vector quantities.  
**C** *m* and *W* are related by the equation $\dfrac{W}{m}$ = *g*.  
**D** *m* and *W* have the same unit.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
