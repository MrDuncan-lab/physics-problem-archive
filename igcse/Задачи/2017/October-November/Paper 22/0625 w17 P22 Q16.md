# Question 16 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2017 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 2 · вопрос 16  
Источник: [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_qp_22.pdf#page=8|условие, стр. 8]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_ms_22.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_er.pdf#page=12|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w17 P22]]  
Темы: [[2.2.1 Thermal expansion of solids, liquids and gases]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить расширение воздуха как причину подъёма ртути в трубке нагреваемой колбы.

The diagram shows a glass flask, sealed with a small volume of mercury in a glass tube. When

the flask is gently warmed the mercury rises up the tube.

![[0625-w17-22-p8-f2.svg]]

What is the main cause of the movement of the mercury?

**A** expansion of air in the flask  
**B** expansion of the glass flask  
**C** expansion of the glass tube  
**D** expansion of the mercury

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Most candidates correctly identified that the movement of the mercury in the tube was due to the air expanding but a number incorrectly thought it was due to the expansion of the mercury.
