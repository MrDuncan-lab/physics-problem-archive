# Question 24 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2017 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 2 · вопрос 24  
Источник: [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_qp_22.pdf#page=11|условие, стр. 11]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_ms_22.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_er.pdf#page=12|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w17 P22]]  
Темы: [[3.3 Electromagnetic spectrum]], [[1.2 Motion]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Вычислить время прохождения света от Земли до Луны и обратно.

The Moon is 380 000 km from the Earth. A laser light beam is directed from the Earth to the

Moon. The beam is reflected back to the Earth.

How long does it take for the light to travel to the Moon and back to the Earth?

**A** 1.27 ms  
**B** 2.53 ms  
**C** 1.27 s  
**D** 2.53 s

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question proved challenging for many candidates. Common errors were to fail to convert kilometres to metres and to not allow for the time for the light to travel both to and from the moon. Only the strongest candidates answered this correctly.
