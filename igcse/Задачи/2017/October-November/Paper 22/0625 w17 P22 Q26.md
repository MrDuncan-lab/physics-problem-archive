> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнить скорость звука во льду, воде и водяном паре.

The speed of sound is different in different states of matter.

The speed of sound in water is 1500 m / s.

Which row correctly compares the speed of sound in ice and the speed of sound in steam with the speed of sound in water?

|  | speed of sound in ice m / s | speed of sound in steam m / s |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | less than 1500 | less than 1500 |
| **B** | less than 1500 | more than 1500 |
| **C** | more than 1500 | less than 1500 |
| **D** | more than 1500 | more than 1500 |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
