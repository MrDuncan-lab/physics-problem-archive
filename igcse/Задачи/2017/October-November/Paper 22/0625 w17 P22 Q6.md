> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбрать выражение для момента силы через перпендикулярное расстояние до оси вращения.

An object is pivoted at point P. A student ties a length of string to a peg on the object. He pulls the string with a force *F*.

![[0625-w17-22-p4-f1.svg]]

What is the moment of the force *F* about the point P?

**A** *F* × *q*  
**B** *F* × *r*  
**C** *F* × *s*  
**D** *F* × *t*

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The most common error made on this question was to use the distance from the point of action of the force to the point **P**, rather than the perpendicular distance from the line of action of the force to **P**.
