# Question 1 (8 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2017 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 2 · вопрос 1  
Источник: [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_qp_42.pdf#page=2|условие, стр. 2–3]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_ms_42.pdf#page=2|схема оценивания, стр. 2]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_er.pdf#page=31|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

Темы: [[1.4 Density]], [[1.3 Mass and weight]], [[1.8 Pressure]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Вычислить массу и вес цилиндра, определить плотность жидкости по давлению и объяснить отсутствие плавания.

Fig. 1.1 shows a cylinder made from copper of density 9000 kg / m$^{3}$.

![[0625-w17-42-p2-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 1.1**

The volume of the cylinder is 75 cm$^{3}$.

**(a)** Calculate the mass of the cylinder.

mass =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(b)** The gravitational field strength is 10 N / kg.

**(i)** Calculate the weight of the cylinder.

weight =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(ii)** State **one** way in which weight differs from mass. **[1]**

**(c)** Fig. 1.2 shows the cylinder immersed in a liquid.

![[0625-w17-42-p3-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 1.2** (not to scale)

The upper face of the cylinder is at a depth of 2.7 cm below the surface of the liquid.

The pressure due to the liquid at the upper face of the cylinder is 560 Pa.

**(i)** Calculate the density of the liquid.

density =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(ii)** Explain why the cylinder does **not** float in this liquid. **[1]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** (*m* =) 0.68 kg **accept** 680 g
>
> **(b)(i)** (*W* =) 6.8 N
>
> **(c)(i)** (ρ =) 2.1 × 10$^{3}$ kg / m$^{3}$

> | (c)(i) | ρ = *h* ρ *g* in any form OR (ρ = ) ρ/ *h g*<br>OR (ρ =) 560 / (0.027 × 10) | C1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** The vast majority of candidates knew the density equation but many made errors in converting the units of some quantities.
>
> **(b) (i)** Candidates were able to gain full credit by correctly following on from their answer to **1(a)**, even if that was incorrect. Few incorrect units for weight were seen but a significant number of weaker candidates failed to give any unit.
>
> **(ii)** This was generally well answered. A minority of candidates just gave the units for mass and weight, which was insufficient.
>
> **(c) (i)** The majority of candidates knew the equation for the density of the liquid but failed to convert the height of the cylinder into metres, which led to an answer of 20.74 kg/m$^{3}$. A number of candidates attempted to use the equations p=ρ/V, p=hρ or p=mgh.
>
> **(ii)** This was generally well answered. A small number of candidates stated that the density of the cylinder was high, which was not sufficient for credit.
