# Question 10 (8 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2017 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 2 · вопрос 10  
Источник: [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_qp_42.pdf#page=14|условие, стр. 14–15]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_ms_42.pdf#page=9|схема оценивания, стр. 9]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_er.pdf#page=31|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Указать направление магнитного поля проводника и объяснить работу электромагнитного дверного замка.

**(a)** Fig. 10.1 shows a wire that carries a current into the page. The circles on Fig. 10.1 show the pattern of the magnetic field around the wire.

![[0625-w17-42-p14-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 10.1**

**(i)** On Fig. 10.1, draw an arrow on each circle to show the direction of the magnetic field. **[1]**

**(ii)** State why the spacing of the circles increases as the distance from the wire increases. **[1]**

**(b)** Fig. 10.2 shows an electric door lock.

The slot in the door contains an unmagnetised iron bolt attached to a spring. The slot in the door frame is empty. This slot is surrounded by the coils of a solenoid.

In Fig. 10.2 the door is unlocked. The spring is not stretched.

![[0625-w17-42-p15-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 10.2**

In Fig. 10.3 the door is locked. The spring is now stretched.

![[0625-w17-42-p15-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 10.3**

The bolt is initially in the position shown in Fig. 10.2.

Describe and explain what happens when

**(i)** the switch **S** is closed, **[4]**

**(ii)** the switch **S** is reopened. **[2]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | <u>clockwise arrows on at least 3 circles</u> | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | (magnetic) field becomes weaker / decreases (as distance from wire increases) | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | any **four** from these six:<br>• charge flows OR current in solenoid / wire / circuit<br>• solenoid becomes magnet / magnetised<br>• bolt becomes magnet / magnetised<br>• (such that) unlike poles (of solenoid and bolt are) facing o.w.t.t.e.<br>• bolt is attracted<br>• bolt moves / (door) locks / spring stretched | B4 |
> | (b)(ii) | solenoid OR bolt no longer magnetised OR bolt no longer attracted | B1 |
> |  | (spring contracts and pulls) bolt back / bolt returns (to original position) / (door) unlocked | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** The majority of candidates recognised that the magnetic field direction was clockwise.
>
> **(ii)** This question was usually answered well but a number of candidates incorrectly referred to an unqualified magnetic force.
>
> **(b) (i)** This was answered well and many candidates gained maximum credit giving clear, logical step-by step explanations of what happened. The weaker candidates confused the concepts of electromagnetism and electromagnetic induction and talked, for instance, about the bolt cutting field lines
>
> **(ii)** Most candidates gave partially correct answers. Weaker candidates failed to state either that the bolt was no longer magnetised or that the bolt now returned to its original position.
