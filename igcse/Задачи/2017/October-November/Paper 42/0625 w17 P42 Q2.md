# Question 2 (8 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2017 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 2 · вопрос 2  
Источник: [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_qp_42.pdf#page=4|условие, стр. 4]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_ms_42.pdf#page=3|схема оценивания, стр. 3]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_er.pdf#page=31|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

Темы: [[1.5.1 Effects of forces]], [[1.2 Motion]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Описать действие силы, рассчитать ускорение автомобиля и время разгона, назвать силу сопротивления движению.

**(a)** An object is moving in a straight line at constant speed. A resultant force begins to act upon the object.

State the ways in which the force may change the motion of the object. **[2]**

**(b)** State **one** other effect a force could have on the object. **[1]**

**(c)** The mass of a car is 1400 kg. The car, initially at rest, is moved along a level road by a resultant force of 3500 N. The car reaches a speed of 30 m / s.

**(i)** Calculate the average acceleration of the car.

acceleration =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(ii)** Calculate the time for which the force is applied.

time =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(iii)** State the name of a force which opposes the motion of the car. **[1]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(c)(i)** (*a* =) 2.5 m / s$^{2}$
>
> **(c)(ii)** (*t* =) 12 s

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | accelerate / increase speed OR decelerate / decrease speed OR stop | B1 |
> |  | change direction / move in a curve o.w.t.t.e. | B1 |
> | (b) | change of shape OR size | B1 |
> | (c)(i) | *F* = *m a* in any form OR (*a* =) *F* / *m*<br>OR (*a* =) 3500 / 1400 | C1 |
> |  | (*a* =) 2.5 m / s$^{2}$ | A1 |
> | (c)(ii) | *a* = (*v – u*) / *t* in any form OR (*t* =) (*v – u*) / *a*<br>OR (*t* =) (30 – 0) / 2.5 OR 30 / 2.5 | C1 |
> |  | (*t* =) 12 s | A1 |
> | (c)(iii) | friction / air resistance / drag | B1 |
