# Question 21 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2017 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 3 · вопрос 21  
Источник: [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_qp_13.pdf#page=11|условие, стр. 11]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_ms_13.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_er.pdf#page=6|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Распознать слабый отражённый луч при прохождении света через границу стекла и воздуха.

The diagram shows a ray of light in glass. The ray reaches a boundary with air.

One weak ray of light is missing from the diagram.

![[0625-w17-13-p11-f1.svg]]

Which statement is correct?

**A** At the boundary, the speed of the light will become less.  
**B** The critical angle for light at this boundary is 50°.  
**C** The diagram shows an example of diffraction of light.  
**D** The missing ray is a weak reflected ray.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Although nearly all candidates rejected option **A**, the remaining three options were roughly equally popular, suggesting that many candidates have never carried out this type of experiment.
