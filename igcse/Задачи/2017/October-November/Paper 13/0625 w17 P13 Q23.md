# Question 23 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2017 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 3 · вопрос 23  
Источник: [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_qp_13.pdf#page=12|условие, стр. 12]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_ms_13.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_er.pdf#page=6|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w17 P13]]  
Темы: [[3.3 Electromagnetic spectrum]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнить скорость радиоволн и ультрафиолетового излучения в вакууме.

Which statement about radio waves is correct?

**A** They are used in television remote controllers.  
**B** They can be detected by the human eye.  
**C** They travel as longitudinal waves.  
**D** They have the same speed in a vacuum as ultraviolet waves.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question proved challenging for many candidates with both responses **A** and **C** being frequently chosen.
