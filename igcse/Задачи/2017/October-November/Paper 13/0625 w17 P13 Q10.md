> Определить изменения кинетической и потенциальной энергии машинки на вертикальной петле.

A toy car without a motor is moving at high speed along a track. The toy car follows a vertical

loop as shown.

![[0625-w17-13-p6-f2.svg]]

Which row describes the changes that are taking place in the kinetic energy and in the gravitational potential energy of the car in the position shown?

|  | kinetic energy | gravitational potential energy |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | decreasing | decreasing |
| **B** | decreasing | increasing |
| **C** | increasing | decreasing |
| **D** | increasing | increasing |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
