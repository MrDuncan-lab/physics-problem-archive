> Выбрать положение опоры и нагрузки, обеспечивающие равновесие рычага.

The diagram shows a beam with a pivot, a load *P* at one end and a load *Q* at the other end.

The pivot can be moved left or right and the loads can be increased or decreased.

The weight of the beam can be ignored.

![[0625-w17-13-p5-f1.svg]]

Which combination of pivot position and loads causes the beam to be in equilibrium?

|  | pivot position / cm | load *P* / N | load *Q* / N |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | 30 | 6.0 | 4.0 |
| **B** | 40 | 6.0 | 4.0 |
| **C** | 50 | 5.0 | 9.0 |
| **D** | 60 | 3.0 | 9.0 |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
