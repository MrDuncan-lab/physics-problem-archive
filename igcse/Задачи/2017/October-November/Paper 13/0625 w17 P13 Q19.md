> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить распространение водяной волны после прохождения через узкую щель.

A plane wave in a shallow tank of water of uniform depth is incident normally on the small gap in

a barrier.

What happens after the wave passes through the gap?

**A** The frequency increases.  
**B** The speed decreases.  
**C** The wavelength decreases.  
**D** The wave spreads out.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
