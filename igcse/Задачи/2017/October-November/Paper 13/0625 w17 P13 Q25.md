# Question 25 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2017 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 3 · вопрос 25  
Источник: [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_qp_13.pdf#page=12|условие, стр. 12]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_ms_13.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_er.pdf#page=6|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w17 P13]]  
Темы: [[3.4 Sound]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбрать возможные частоту и скорость ультразвука в воздухе.

The speed of sound in air is 330 m / s.

The speed of ultraviolet waves in air is 300 000 000 m / s.

Which row gives a possible frequency and speed of an ultrasound wave in air?

|  | frequency / Hz | speed m / s |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | 4000 | 330 |
| **B** | 4000 | 300 000 000 |
| **C** | 40 000 | 330 |
| **D** | 40 000 | 300 000 000 |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Stronger candidates answered this question correctly and knew the range of frequencies that the human ear can detect and that ultrasound is sound of higher frequency than the sounds we can hear and will travel at the same speed as the sound we can hear.
