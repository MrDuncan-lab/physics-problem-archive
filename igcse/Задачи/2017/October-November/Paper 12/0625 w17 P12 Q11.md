# Question 11 (1 mark)

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбрать необходимые данные для расчёта мощности подъёма человека по лестнице через вес, высоту и время.

A student runs up a flight of stairs.

![[0625-w17-12-p7-f2.svg]]

Which information is **not** needed to calculate the rate at which the student is doing work against gravity?

**A** the height of the flight of stairs  
**B** the length of the flight of stairs  
**C** the time taken to run up the stairs  
**D** the weight of the student

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Candidates did not seem familiar with this activity. All options were selected and this indicated that many, including the better candidates, were unsure of the answer.
