# Question 24 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2017 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 24  
Источник: [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_qp_12.pdf#page=13|условие, стр. 13]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_ms_12.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_er.pdf#page=3|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w17 P12]]  
Темы: [[3.2.2 Refraction of light]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить угол преломления луча при переходе света из стекла в воздух.

The diagram shows light travelling from glass to air.

![[0625-w17-12-p13-f2.svg]]

What is the angle of refraction for this ray of light?

**A** 20°  
**B** 40°  
**C** 50°  
**D** 70°

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Candidates had difficulty in understanding that the angle of refraction is the angle between the normal to the surface and the ray, rather than the angle between the surface and the ray and the majority of candidates therefore incorrectly gave option **A** as their answer.
