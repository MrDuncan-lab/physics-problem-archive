> Определить массу и вес тела на другой планете по графикам зависимости веса от массы.

The graph shows how weight varies with mass on planet P and on planet Q.

![[0625-w17-12-p4-f1.svg]]

An object weighs 400 N on planet P. The object is taken to planet Q.

Which row is correct?

|  | mass of object on planet Q / kg | weight of object on planet Q / N |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | 40 | 200 |
| **B** | 40 | 400 |
| **C** | 80 | 200 |
| **D** | 80 | 400 |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The vast majority of candidates thought that both the mass of the object and its weight increased when taken to the second planet.
