> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить пройденное транспортным средством расстояние по графику движения.

The graph represents the motion of a vehicle.

![[0625-w17-12-p2-f2.svg]]

What is the distance travelled by the vehicle in 400 s?

**A** 20 m  
**B** 400 m  
**C** 4000 m  
**D** 8000 m

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Candidates generally just multiplied the maximum speed of the vehicle by the time taken. However, they should have recognised that the car was accelerating and that the distance travelled was equal to the area
>
> under the graph ( maximum speed × time).
