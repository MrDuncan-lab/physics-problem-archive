> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить изменение расстояния между железными стержнями при включении электромагнитов.

Two circuits are set up as shown. The iron rods are placed close together and are able to move.

![[0625-w17-12-p14-f2.svg]]

What happens to the size of the gap at X when switch S is closed?

**A** It decreases.  
**B** It decreases then increases.  
**C** It increases.  
**D** It does not change.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Only the strongest candidates answered correctly and worked out the poles induced on the ends of the magnets by the gap (both north-seeking poles) and then recognised that like poles repel each other.
