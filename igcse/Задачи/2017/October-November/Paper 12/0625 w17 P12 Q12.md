# Question 12 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2017 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 12  
Источник: [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_qp_12.pdf#page=8|условие, стр. 8]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_ms_12.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_er.pdf#page=3|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w17 P12]]  
Темы: [[1.8 Pressure]], [[1.3 Mass and weight]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать давление книги на стол по массе, силе тяжести и площади опоры.

A book has a mass of 400 g.

One of its surfaces measures 0.10 m × 0.20 m. This surface is in contact with a table.

The gravitational field strength *g* is10 N / kg.

What is the pressure exerted on the table due to the book?

**A** 0.08 N / m$^{2}$  
**B** 8.0 N / m$^{2}$  
**C** 20 N / m$^{2}$  
**D** 200 N / m$^{2}$

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Although a number of candidates correctly calculated the answer, an equal number simply multiplied the 400 by 0.02 not recognising that force is measured in newtons, not grams and that pressure is force divided by area.
