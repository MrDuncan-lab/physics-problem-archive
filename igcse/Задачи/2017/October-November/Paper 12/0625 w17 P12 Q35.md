> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбрать правильное положение предохранителя в цепи прибора, подключённого к сети.

An appliance is connected to a mains supply. Its circuit also contains a switch and a fuse.

Which circuit shows the fuse in the correct position?

![[0625-w17-12-q35-options.svg]]

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Candidates were not aware that the fuse in a circuit should be connected in series with the appliance that is to be protected and in the live line. If a fuse in the neutral line blows then the potential difference between the live line and earth will remain high.
