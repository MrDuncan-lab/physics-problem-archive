> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать объёмный расход масла по изменению объёма в цилиндре за минуту.

A student investigates the rate of flow of oil through a funnel.

The diagrams show the experiment and the volume of oil in the measuring cylinder at the start of the experiment, and one minute later.

![[0625-w17-12-p2-f1.svg]]

What is the rate of flow of oil through the funnel during the one minute?

**A** 0.73 cm$^{3}$ / s  
**B** 0.80 cm$^{3}$ / s  
**C** 44 cm$^{3}$ / s  
**D** 48 cm$^{3}$ / s

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The majority of candidates correctly calculated the change in height of the oil level but then failed to convert the minutes into seconds.
