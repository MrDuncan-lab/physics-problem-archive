# Question 33 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2017 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 33  
Источник: [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_qp_12.pdf#page=16|условие, стр. 16]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_ms_12.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_er.pdf#page=3|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w17 P12]]  
Темы: [[4.3.2 Series and parallel circuits]], [[4.2.4 Resistance]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбрать изменение цепи с фоторезистором, уменьшающее ток и скорость вращения двигателя.

The diagram shows a motor and a light-dependent resistor (LDR) connected in a circuit.

![[0625-w17-12-p16-f1.svg]]

Which change decreases the current in the motor so that it turns more slowly?

**A** connecting a resistor in parallel with the LDR  
**B** connecting a resistor in series with the LDR  
**C** exchanging the positions of the motor and the LDR in the circuit  
**D** increasing the brightness of the light falling on the LDR

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Candidates struggled with this question with many being distracted by the LDR and thinking it must be the change in light level which would decrease the speed of the motor. However an increase in the light level would reduce the resistance of the LDR, increasing the current and increasing the speed of the motor.
