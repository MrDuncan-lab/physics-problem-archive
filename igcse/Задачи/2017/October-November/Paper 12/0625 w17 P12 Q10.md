> Описать превращение упругой энергии пружины в тепловую при затухании колебаний груза.

A load is attached to the end of a spring. A student pulls the load down from its rest position and

releases it. It oscillates vertically and eventually comes to rest.

![[0625-w17-12-p7-f1.svg]]

Which row gives the type of energy transferred to the apparatus, and the type of energy to which this has then been transferred when the load comes to rest?

|  | type of energy transferred to the apparatus | type of energy to which this has then been transferred when the load comes to rest |
|---|---|---|
| **A** **B** **C** **D** | elastic energy in the spring elastic energy in the spring gravitational potential energy in the load gravitational potential energy in the load | chemical energy in the spring and in the air thermal energy in the spring and in the air chemical energy in the spring and in the air thermal energy in the spring and in the air |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
