# Question 5 (6 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2017 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 3 · вопрос 5  
Источник: [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_qp_33.pdf#page=7|условие, стр. 7]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_ms_33.pdf#page=4|схема оценивания, стр. 4]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_er.pdf#page=24|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w17 P33]]  
Темы: [[1.8 Pressure]], [[2.1.3 Gases and the absolute scale of temperature]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объяснить выбор шин для мягкого грунта и влияние нагревания воздуха на движение молекул и давление.

A vehicle may have tyres of type **A** or type **B**, as shown in Fig. 5.1.

![[0625-w17-33-p7-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 5.1**

**(a)** State and explain the type of tyre that is suitable for travelling over soft ground. **[3]**

**(b)** The temperature of the air in a tyre increases. This affects the motion of the air molecules in the tyre.

Describe and explain the changes. **[3]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | <u>Tyre B</u> | B1 |
> |  | <u>larger / bigger surface area</u> | B1 |
> |  | less pressure (on ground) / weight distributed | B1 |
> | (b) | molecules gain kinetic energy / move faster | B1 |
> |  | more (frequent) / harder collisions (with tyre) | B1 |
> |  | Increased / greater pressure (on tyre) | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Many correct responses were seen for this question. A frequent misconception was that tyres with smaller surface area would be less likely to get stuck in the soft ground.
>
> **(b)** Many candidates gained at least partial credit for their responses to this question recognising that molecules move faster or more frequently collide with the tyre.
