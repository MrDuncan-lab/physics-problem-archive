# Question 9 (6 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2017 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 3 · вопрос 9  
Источник: [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_qp_33.pdf#page=11|условие, стр. 11–12]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_ms_33.pdf#page=6|схема оценивания, стр. 6]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_er.pdf#page=24|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w17 P33]]  
Темы: [[3.4 Sound]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объяснить запаздывание звука и возникновение эха, вычислить задержку отражённого звука.

A machine pushes a metal post into the ground using a hammer, as shown in Fig. 9.1. The hammer makes a loud noise when it hits the post.

![[0625-w17-33-p11-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 9.1**

A man stands 160 m from the hammer.

**(a)** A man sees the hammer hit the post and a short time later hears the sound of the post being hit. Explain the delay in hearing the sound. **[1]**

**(b)** **(i)** The hammer is 320 m from a large building as shown in Fig. 9.2.

![[0625-w17-33-p11-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 9.2**

The man hears the hammer hit the post and then hears an echo. Explain why he hears the echo. **[1]**

**(ii)** The speed of sound in air is 320 m / s.

Calculate the time difference between the man hearing the first sound and the time at which he hears the echo.

time difference =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

**(c)** Suggest how the sound of the echo is different from the first sound. **[1]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(c)** 2.0 s

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | light travels faster than sound **OR** reverse argument | B1 |
> | (b) | reflection (from building) / bouncing back (from building) | B1 |
> | (c) | time taken for first sound = 0.5 s | C1 |
> |  | Time taken for echo = 2.5 s **OR** time for sound to travel from hammer and return = 2.0 s | C1 |
> |  | 2.0 s | A1 |
> | (d) | quieter / less amplitude / less energy | B1 |
