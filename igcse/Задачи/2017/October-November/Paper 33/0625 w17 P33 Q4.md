# Question 4 (6 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2017 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 3 · вопрос 4

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Описать превращения упругой, кинетической и тепловой энергии при выстреле из рогатки.

A catapult consists of a rubber band attached to a handle. A student uses the catapult to fire a ball towards a target. Fig. 4.1 shows the catapult just before the student releases the rubber band.

![[0625-w17-33-p5-f1.png]]

**Fig. 4.1**

**(a)** When the student releases the rubber band, the ball moves towards the target.

Complete the following sentences about energy transfers during this process. Use words from the box. You may use each word once, more than once or not at all.

![[0625-w17-33-p5-f2.svg]]

elastic force friction gravitational kinetic thermal

**(i)** As the rubber band is pulled back, the work done is transferred into  \_\_\_\_\_\_

potential energy. **[1]**

**(ii)** When the student releases the rubber band, the ball moves forward horizontally. Stored

\_\_\_\_\_\_ potential energy is transferred into  \_\_\_\_\_\_ energy. **[2]**

**(iii)** As the ball travels through the air there is friction with air molecules. This causes some of

the ball’s  \_\_\_\_\_\_ energy to be transferred into  \_\_\_\_\_\_ energy. **[2]**

**(b)** The ball does not reach the target, as shown in Fig. 4.2.

![[0625-w17-33-p6-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 4.2**

Describe how the student can increase the energy of the ball, using the same catapult. **[1]**

> | (a)(ii) | elastic | B1 |
> |  | kinetic | B1 |
> | (a)(iii) | kinetic | B1 |
> |  | thermal | B1 |
> | (b) | pull band further back / exert greater force on band / increase elastic potential energy | B1 |
