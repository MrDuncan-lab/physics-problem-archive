> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Построить лучи в собирающей линзе, определить фокусное расстояние и показать полное внутреннее отражение в стекле.

Fig 8.1 shows two parallel rays of light that pass through a thin converging lens. The diagram is incomplete. There is a principal focus at f$_{1}$ and at f$_{2}$.

![[0625-w17-33-p10-f1.svg]]

![[0625-w17-33-p10-t1.svg]]

**Fig. 8.1**

**(a)** **(i)** On Fig. 8.1, complete the ray diagram to show how the lens focuses the light. **[3]**

**(ii)** Which distance on Fig. 8.1 is a focal length of the lens? Tick (✓) one box.

![[0625-w17-33-p10-f2.svg]]

**[1]**

**(b)** **(i)** A ray of light travels through a semicircular glass block, as shown in Fig. 8.2.

![[0625-w17-33-p10-f3.svg]]

**Fig. 8.2**

State the term given to the angle of incidence labelled *z*. **[1]**

**(ii)** Fig. 8.3 shows another ray of light travelling in the semicircular glass block.

![[0625-w17-33-p10-f4.svg]]

**Fig. 8.3**

The angle of incidence is greater than *z*. Continue the path of the ray of light until it leaves the block. **[2]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | top ray passes through f$_{2}$ | B1 |
> |  | bottom ray passes through f$_{2}$ | B1 |
> |  | refraction correctly shown either at centre of lens **OR** at both edges of lens | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | C to f$_{2}$ | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | critical angle | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | ray internally reflected | B1 |
> |  | reflecting angle = incident angle | B1 |

>
