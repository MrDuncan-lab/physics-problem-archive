# Question 10 (6 marks)

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Изобразить магнитное поле стержневого магнита и описать проверку намагниченности металлического стержня по отталкиванию.

A student places a bar magnet onto a sheet of paper, as shown in Fig. 10.1.

![[0625-w17-33-p13-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 10.1**

**(a)** The student shows the pattern of the magnetic field lines around the magnet.

**(i)** On Fig. 10.1 carefully draw two magnetic field lines above the centre line XY and two magnetic field lines below XY. **[2]**

**(ii)** Indicate the direction of the magnetic field lines. Use an arrow. Mark one field line above XY and one field line below XY. **[1]**

**(iii)** State the name of a piece of equipment that can be used to determine the direction of the magnetic field. **[1]**

**(b)** Describe how the student can use a bar magnet to test whether a metal rod is a magnet. **[2]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | two curved field lines drawn above and below the magnet | B1 |
> |  | lines start and finish at the poles of the magnet | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | both arrows point left | B1 |
> | (a)(iii) | (plotting) compass | B1 |
> | (b) | place end on end / see if attraction / repulsion occurs | B1 |
> |  | repulsion at one end | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Relatively few fully correct responses were seen.
>
> **(b)** Only the very best candidates gained full credit recognising that the test for a magnet was repulsion at one end.
