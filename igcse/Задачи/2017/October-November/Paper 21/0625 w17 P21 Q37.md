The graph shows how the voltage induced across a coil changes with time as the coil spins in a

magnetic field.

Which graph shows what happens when the coil spins more quickly?

(All graphs are drawn to the same scale.)

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The most common error candidates made was not to recognise that when the coil is spun at a greater speed the frequency increases and the induced e.m.f. also increases.
