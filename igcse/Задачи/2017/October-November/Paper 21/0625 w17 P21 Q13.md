The diagram shows a simple mercury barometer.

![[0625-w17-21-p7-f2.svg]]

Which length is used to find the value of atmospheric pressure?

**A** 12 cm  
**B** 74 cm  
**C** 86 cm  
**D** 100 cm

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Many candidates thought that the distance from the bottom of the barometer tube and from the reservoir surface to the mercury level in the tube was the required distance.
