# Question 17 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2017 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 1 · вопрос 17  
Источник: [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_qp_21.pdf#page=9|условие, стр. 9]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_ms_21.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_er.pdf#page=9|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w17 P21]]  
Темы: [[2.2.1 Thermal expansion of solids, liquids and gases]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объяснить выпрямление охлаждаемой биметаллической полоски различным сокращением латуни и железа.

A strip of iron and a strip of brass are firmly attached to each other along their entire length. This

combination is a bimetallic strip.

![[0625-w17-21-p9-f2.svg]]

This bimetallic strip is heated and it bends as shown.

![[0625-w17-21-p9-f3.svg]]

The bimetallic strip is now cooled and becomes straight again.

What causes the bimetallic strip to become straight again?

**A** The brass contracts more than the iron.  
**B** The brass expands more than the iron.  
**C** The iron contracts more than the brass.  
**D** The iron expands more than the brass.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Many candidates answered this question correctly but a significant number thought the bending of the bimetal strip was caused by the iron contracting more than the brass. Contraction is mentioned in the stem of the question and it is possible that candidates were distracted by this. Candidates should be reminded to read the question carefully so that the context is fully understood.
