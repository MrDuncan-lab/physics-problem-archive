# Question 9 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2017 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 1 · вопрос 9  
Источник: [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_qp_21.pdf#page=5|условие, стр. 5]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_ms_21.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_er.pdf#page=9|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Вычислить среднюю силу удара ракетки по изменению импульса мяча и времени контакта.

A tennis ball of mass 0.060 kg travels horizontally at a speed of 25 m / s. The ball hits a tennis racket and rebounds horizontally at a speed of 40 m / s.

![[0625-w17-21-p5-f2.png]]

before hitting racket after hitting racket

The ball is in contact with the racket for 50 ms.

What force does the racket exert on the ball?

**A** 0.018 N  
**B** 0.078 N  
**C** 18 N  
**D** 78 N

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question proved very challenging for the majority of candidates. Only the very strongest candidates recognised that the change in velocity of the ball was 25–(–40) = 65 m/s, not (25–40) = 15 m/s.
