# Question 10 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2017 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 1 · вопрос 10  
Источник: [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_qp_21.pdf#page=6|условие, стр. 6]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_ms_21.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_er.pdf#page=9|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w17 P21]]  
Темы: [[1.7.1 Energy]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Найти начальную кинетическую энергию камня по её конечному значению и приросту потенциальной энергии.

The diagram shows the path of a stone that is thrown from X and reaches its maximum height

at Y.

![[0625-w17-21-p6-f1.svg]]

The stone gains 10 J of gravitational potential energy as it moves from X to Y.

The stone has 2.0 J of kinetic energy at Y.

Air resistance can be ignored.

How much kinetic energy did the stone have immediately after it was thrown at X?

**A** 2.0 J  
**B** 8.0 J  
**C** 10 J  
**D** 12 J

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Many candidates found this question difficult. Stronger candidates were able to use the simple law of conservation of energy (total energy at **X**=total energy at **Y)** to answer however.
