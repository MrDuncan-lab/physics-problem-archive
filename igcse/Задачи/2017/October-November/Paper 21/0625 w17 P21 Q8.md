> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить равнодействующую двух непараллельных сил с помощью масштабного векторного построения.

The diagram shows an incomplete scale drawing to find the resultant of two 10 N forces acting at a point in the directions shown.

![[0625-w17-21-p5-f1.svg]]

What is the magnitude of the resultant force?

**A** 7.5 N  
**B** 8.6 N  
**C** 18 N  
**D** 20 N

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The question was answered correctly by a significant number of candidates. However, some candidates simply added the two forces as though they were acting in the same direction.
