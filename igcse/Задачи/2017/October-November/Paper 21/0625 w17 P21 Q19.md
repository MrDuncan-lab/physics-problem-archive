Источник: [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_qp_21.pdf#page=10|условие, стр. 10]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_ms_21.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_er.pdf#page=9|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Связать возникновение конвекции с изменением плотности жидкости при охлаждении.

Which statement about convection currents is correct?

**A** Convection currents occur because, when cooled, liquids contract and become more dense.  
**B** Convection currents occur because, when warmed, liquids expand and become more dense.  
**C** Convection currents only occur in liquids.  
**D** Convection currents only occur in solids and liquids.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> There was little understanding of the cause of convection currents. Many candidates did not recognise that in this example it was cooling, as opposed to the more familiar heating which produced the current.
