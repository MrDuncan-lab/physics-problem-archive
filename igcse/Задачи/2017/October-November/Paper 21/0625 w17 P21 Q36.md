> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить направление движения проводника по направлению индуцированного тока и магнитному полю.

The diagram shows a copper wire XY connected to a resistor.

The wire is moved in the magnetic field between the poles of a magnet.

There is an induced current in the wire from X to Y.

In which labelled direction is the wire moving?

![[0625-w17-21-p18-f1.svg]]

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Most candidates showed an understanding of the three dimensional nature of electromagnetic induction. However, many then chose the movement upwards, rather than downwards.
