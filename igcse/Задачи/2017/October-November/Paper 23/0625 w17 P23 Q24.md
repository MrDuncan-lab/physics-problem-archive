# Question 24 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2017 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 3 · вопрос 24  
Источник: [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_qp_23.pdf#page=12|условие, стр. 12]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_ms_23.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_er.pdf#page=15|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w17 P23]]  
Темы: [[3.3 Electromagnetic spectrum]], [[1.2 Motion]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать время распространения лазерного сигнала до Луны и обратно на Землю.

The Moon is 380 000 km from the Earth. A laser light beam is directed from the Earth to the

Moon. The beam is reflected back to the Earth.

How long does it take for the light to travel to the Moon and back to the Earth?

**A** 1.27 ms  
**B** 2.53 ms  
**C** 1.27 s  
**D** 2.53 s

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question was only answered correctly by stronger candidates. Common errors were failing to convert kilometres to metres and not allowing for the time for the light to travel both to and from the moon.
