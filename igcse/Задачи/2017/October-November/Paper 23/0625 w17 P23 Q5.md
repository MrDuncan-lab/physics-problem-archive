# Question 5 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2017 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 3 · вопрос 5  
Источник: [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_qp_23.pdf#page=4|условие, стр. 4]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_ms_23.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_er.pdf#page=15|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

Темы: [[1.3 Mass and weight]], [[1.5.1 Effects of forces]], [[1.2 Motion]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объяснить одинаковое ускорение свободного падения пропорциональностью силы тяжести массе тела.

On the Moon, all objects fall with the same acceleration.

Which statement explains this?

**A** On the Moon, all objects have the same weight.  
**B** The Moon has a smaller gravitational field strength than the Earth.  
**C** The weight of an object is directly proportional to its mass.  
**D** The weight of an object is inversely proportional to its mass.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Although there were many candidates who answered correctly, option **B** was often chosen with these candidates knowing that this is a true statement (even though it does not explain why all objects fall with the same acceleration) without checking the remaining options carefully.
