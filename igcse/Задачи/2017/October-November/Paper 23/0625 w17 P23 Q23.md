> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить агрегатные состояния среды по изменению скорости звука при переходе между ними.

A sound wave travels from a medium in one state into the same medium but in another state.

This causes the speed of the wave to change from approximately 300 m / s to approximately 3000 m / s.

Between which two states is the sound wave travelling?

**A** gas to solid  
**B** liquid to gas  
**C** liquid to solid  
**D** solid to liquid

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**
