# Question 11 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2017 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 3 · вопрос 11  
Источник: [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_qp_23.pdf#page=6|условие, стр. 6]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_ms_23.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_er.pdf#page=15|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w17 P23]]  
Темы: [[1.7.3 Energy resources]], [[4.2.5 Electrical energy and electrical power]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнить полезную световую мощность и потери двух ламп по мощности потребления и КПД.

A 150 W filament lamp has an efficiency of 10%. A 40 W compact fluorescent lamp (CFL) has an

efficiency of 30%.

Each lamp is switched on for the same amount of time.

Which lamp produces more light and which lamp converts more energy into other forms of energy?

|  | produces more light | converts more energy into other forms |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | CFL lamp | CFL lamp |
| **B** | CFL lamp | filament lamp |
| **C** | filament lamp | CFL lamp |
| **D** | filament lamp | filament lamp |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The majority of candidates answered correctly but a significant minority made the error of thinking that the CFL lamp gave out the most light energy. To show which lamp gives out the most energy a simple calculation needed to be done (10% of 150 W = 15 W; 30% of 40 W = 12 W).
