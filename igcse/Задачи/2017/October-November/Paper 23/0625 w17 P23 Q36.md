# Question 36 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2017 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 3 · вопрос 36  
Источник: [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_qp_23.pdf#page=16|условие, стр. 16]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_ms_23.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_er.pdf#page=15|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w17 P23]]  
Темы: [[4.5.1 Electromagnetic induction]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить тормозящее действие индуцированного тока на движение замкнутой катушки в магнитном поле.

The diagram shows a short-circuited copper coil swinging about an axis at right-angles to a

strong magnetic field. The motion induces a current in the coil.

![[0625-w17-23-p16-f2.svg]]

What is the effect, if any, of this induced current in the coil?

**A** The induced current has no effect on the movement of the coil because copper is non-

magnetic.

**B** The induced current produces a magnetic field of constant magnitude in the coil.  
**C** The induced current produces forces that assist the change causing it.  
**D** The induced current produces forces that oppose the changes causing it.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Stronger candidates were able to recognise the application of the concept that an induced e.m.f. tends to oppose the change causing it.
