# Question 2 (6 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2017 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 1 · вопрос 2  
Источник: [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_qp_41.pdf#page=3|условие, стр. 3]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_ms_41.pdf#page=2|схема оценивания, стр. 2]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_er.pdf#page=27|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w17 P41]]  
Темы: [[1.5.1 Effects of forces]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сформулировать закон Гука, построить график нагрузки, вычислить жёсткость пружины и объяснить превышение предела пропорциональности.

**(a)** State Hooke’s Law. **[1]**

**(b)** For forces up to 120 N, a spring obeys Hooke’s Law.

A force of 120 N causes an extension of 64 mm.

**(i)** On Fig. 2.1, draw the force-extension graph for the spring for loads up to 120 N. **[1]**

![[0625-w17-41-p3-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 2.1**

**(ii)** Calculate the spring constant *k* of the spring.

*k* =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(c)** A student makes a spring balance using the spring in **(b)**. The maximum reading of this balance is 150 N.

The student tests his balance with a known weight of 140 N. He observes that the reading of the balance is not 140 N.

Suggest and explain why the reading is **not** 140 N. **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)(ii)** c.a.o. 1.9 N / mm OR 19 N / cm OR 1900 N / m

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | Extension of a spring is (directly) proportional to load / force / weight OR *F = ke* where *e* is extension | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | <u>Straight line drawn from origin to (64 mm, 120 N)</u> | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | *F* = *ke* in any form OR 120 / 64 OR 120 / 6.4 OR 120 / 0.064 | C1 |
> |  | c.a.o. 1.9 N / mm OR 19 N / cm OR 1900 N / m | A1 |
> | (c) | Above 120 N / at 140 N, the spring does not obey Hooke’s law<br>OR the extension is not proportional to the load / weight / force | B1 |
> |  | The elastic limit / limit of proportionality of the spring has been exceeded | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** The correct proportionality between force, load or weight and extension needed to be identified in answers to this question. Alternatively a reference to F = *kx* with the symbols explained was also accepted. It was insufficient to state ‘extension increases with load’.
>
> **(b) (i)** Almost all the candidates drew the correct graph.
>
> **(ii)** Most candidates correctly calculated the correct numerical number using F = *kx. Many* candidates, however, did not state the unit.
>
> **(c)** Only the strongest candidates gained full credit for stating that the elastic limit or limit of proportionality had been exceeded and that the spring no longer obeyed Hooke law.
