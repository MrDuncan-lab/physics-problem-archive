**(i)** On Fig. 5.1, draw the wavefronts of the wave in the shallow water where the wave travels more slowly. **[2]**

**(ii)** The depth of the shallow water is now changed so that the speed of the wave in the shallow water is 0.60 m / s. The speed of the wave in the deep water is 0.80 m / s. The distance between successive wavefronts in the deep water is 1.4 cm.

Calculate the wavelength of the wave in the shallow water.

wavelength =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[4]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)(ii)** Wavelength in shallow water = *v / f* OR 60 / 57.1 = 1.05 cm
