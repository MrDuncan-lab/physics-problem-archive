**(c)** Astronauts set up a mirror on the Moon’s surface. A laser beam is transmitted from the Earth’s surface to the mirror and is then reflected back to Earth.

On a certain day, the time between transmitting the beam from a point on the Earth’s surface and receiving the reflected signal at the same point is 2.56 s.

The speed of the laser beam is 3.00 × 10$^{8}$ m / s.

Calculate the distance between the Earth’s surface and the Moon’s surface.

distance =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(c)** 3.84 × 10$^{8}$ m OR 3.84 × 10$^{5}$ km

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | 1500 m / s liquid<br>5000 m / s solid<br>300 m / s gas | B2 |
> | (b)(i) | X and Y marked at centres of any two rarefactions | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | Area of low pressure or low density (of atoms) or where atoms / molecules far apart | B1 |
> | (c) | *v = = d / t* or 2 *d* / *t* in any form | C1 |
> |  | *d* = *v t* / 2 OR 3.0 × 10$^{8}$ × 2.56 / 2 | C1 |
> |  | 3.84 × 10$^{8}$ m OR 3.84 × 10$^{5}$ km | A1 |
