# Question 9 (8 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2017 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 1 · вопрос 9  
Источник: [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_qp_41.pdf#page=11|условие, стр. 11]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_ms_41.pdf#page=8|схема оценивания, стр. 8]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_er.pdf#page=27|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать общую ЭДС, сопротивление и ток в цепи, указать подключения амперметра и вольтметра.

Fig. 9.1 shows a circuit with three 1.5 V cells.

![[0625-w17-41-p11-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 9.1**

**(a)** Calculate

**(i)** the total electromotive force (e.m.f.) of the cells,

e.m.f. =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(ii)** the total resistance of the circuit,

resistance =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

**(iii)** the current in the 3.0 Ω resistor.

current =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(b)** State, using the letters in Fig. 9.1, how you would connect

**(i)** an ammeter to measure the total current in the circuit, **[1]**

**(ii)** a voltmeter to measure the potential difference (p.d.) across the 6.0 Ω resistor. **[1]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)(ii)** (*R* =) 2.0 Ω
>
> **(a)(iii)** 1.5 A

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | (3 × 1.5 =) 4.5 V | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | 1 / *R* = 1 / *R*$_{1}$ + 1 / *R*$_{2}$ OR *R* = 1 / (1 / *R*$_{1}$ + 1 / *R*$_{2}$) OR (*R* =) *R*$_{1}$*R*$_{2}$ / (*R*$_{1}$ + *R*$_{2}$) | C1 |
> |  | Correct substitution of 3 and 6 | C1 |
> |  | (*R* =) 2.0 Ω | A1 |
> | (a)(iii) | *V* = *IR* in any form OR (*I* =) *V* / *R* OR 4.5 / 3 | C1 |
> |  | 1.5 A | A1 |
> |  | OR |  |
> |  | *I*$_{\text{total}}$ = 4.5 / 2 = 2.25 A | (C1) |
> |  | For 3 Ω, *I* = 2.25 × 6 / 9 = 1.5 A | (A1) |
> | (b)(i) | Connect ammeter (in wire) from A to B OR from H to G | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | Connect voltmeter (terminals) to A and H OR B and G OR C and D OR E and F | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** A large number of candidates could not write down the total e.m.f, of the three cells in series.
>
> **(ii)** Many candidates did not know the formula for calculating the combined resistance of 2 resistors in parallel. Only the strongest candidates who did know the formula were able to use it correctly.
>
> **(iii)** With the benefit of carrying forward an error from **(i)**, full credit could be awarded to most answers.
>
> **(b)** Some of the suggestions for placing the ammeter and the voltmeter were not expressed very clearly. However, in **(i)** and **(ii),** most of the suggested positions for the meters were acceptable. The exception was for those who wrote down “at B” or “at G” for the ammeter.
