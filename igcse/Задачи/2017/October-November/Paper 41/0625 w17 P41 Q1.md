# Question 1 (7 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2017 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 1 · вопрос 1  
Источник: [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_qp_41.pdf#page=2|условие, стр. 2]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_ms_41.pdf#page=2|схема оценивания, стр. 2]] · [[_pdf/2017/0625_w17_er.pdf#page=27|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w17 P41]]  
Темы: [[1.2 Motion]], [[1.5.1 Effects of forces]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> По графику скорости автомобиля определить путь, ускорение, равнодействующую силу и характер движения.

Fig. 1.1 shows the speed-time graph for the motion of a car.

![[0625-w17-41-p2-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 1.1**

The mass of the car is 1200 kg.

**(a)** Calculate, for the first 20 s of the motion,

**(i)** the distance travelled by the car,

distance =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(ii)** the acceleration of the car,

acceleration =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(iii)** the resultant force acting on the car.

resultant force =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(b)** Describe the motion of the car in the period of time from 25 s to 40 s. **[1]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)(i)** 130 m
>
> **(a)(ii)** 0.65 m / s$^{2}$
>
> **(a)(iii)** = 780 N

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | Distance = area under graph OR 0.5 × 20 × 13 | C1 |
> |  | 130 m | A1 |
> | (a)(ii) | (*a* =) (*v* – *u*) / *t* OR (*a* =) *v* / *t* OR 13 / 20 | C1 |
> |  | 0.65 m / s$^{2}$ | A1 |
> | (a)(iii) | (*F* =) *ma* OR 1200 × 0.65 | C1 |
> |  | = 780 N | A1 |
> | (b) | Acceleration decreases OR rate of increase of speed decreases OR speed increases at a lower rate | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** A majority of candidates realised that the distance required was given by the triangular area under the graph and calculated the distance correctly. Many weaker candidates simply used distance = speed × time and failed to gain credit.
>
> **(ii)** Most candidates wrote down an applicable formula for calculating acceleration and gained partial credit. Many of these candidates then selected the correct data from the graph and calculated the required acceleration. However, others read the graph incorrectly.
>
> **(iii)** The formula F = ma was well known. In general, those failing to calculate the force correctly used the wrong acceleration, sometimes the acceleration of gravity, but other incorrect numbers were used. Wrong acceleration values carried forward from **(ii)** allowed some candidates to gain partial credit.
>
> **(b)** This question was challenging for many candidates and a variety of answers were seen. However stronger candidates identified decreasing acceleration or decreasing rate of increase in speed and could be credited.
