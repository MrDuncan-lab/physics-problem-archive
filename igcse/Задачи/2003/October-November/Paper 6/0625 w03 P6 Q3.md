> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> (a) Most candidates correctly showed the position of the pointer.
>
> (b) Most candidates correctly identified the variable resistor. To gain full marks in the table, candidates had to write in the correct units (most did but some apparently ignored the instruction), and then perform correct calculations giving the answers consistently to either 2 or 3 significant figures. Many correctly suggested using the variable resistor to vary V (the most obvious answer the Examiners expected to see) but credit was also given for other workable suggestions, e.g. changing the number of cells.
>
> (c) Here it was clear that some candidates are not familiar with setting up circuits and in particular do not realise that the ammeter must be in series with the component(s) carrying the current to be measured, whereas the voltmeter must be in parallel with the relevant component(s).
