> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Дифракция волн на щели и расчёт длины волны по скорости и частоте.

Fig. 6.1 shows the diffraction of waves by a narrow gap.

P is a wavefront that has passed through the gap.

![[0625-w03-3-p8-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 6.1**

**(a)** On Fig. 6.1, draw three more wavefronts to the right of the gap. **[3]**

**(b)** The waves travel towards the gap at a speed of 3 x 10$^{8}$ m/s and have a frequency of 5 x 10$^{14}$ Hz. Calculate the wavelength of these waves.

wavelength =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** answer 6 x 10 m

> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |

> | (a) | 3 more roughly circular | B1 |

> |  | wavelength constant throughout, both sides of barrier | B1 |
> | (b) | wavelength – speed/frequency in any form | C1 |
> |  | values substituted correctly | C1 |
> |  | answer 6 x 10 m | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> (a) A wide spread of answer quality was seen with a high proportion gaining full marks. Some gave two fronts when the question asked for three.
>
> (b) Almost all tried to use the correct wave equation, mostly completely successfully. Common mistakes were the use of the index 4 instead of 14, wrong transposition of the equation and an inability to deal with negative powers of 10.
>
> Answers: (b) 6 x 10$^{\text{–7}}$.
