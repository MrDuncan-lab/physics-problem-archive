> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Вычисление давления воды на глубине и силы на смотровое окно.

Fig. 2.1 shows a diver 50 m below the surface of the water.

![[0625-w03-3-p4-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 2.1**

**(a)** The density of water is 1000 kg/m$^{3}$ and the acceleration of free fall is 10 m/s$^{2}$. Calculate the pressure that the water exerts on the diver.

pressure =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

**(b)** The window in the diver’s helmet is 150 mm wide and 70 mm from top to bottom.

Calculate the force that the water exerts on this window.

force =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** so value is 500 000 Pa or N/m$^{2}$
>
> **(b)** force = 5250 N

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | pressure = depth x g x density of water | C1 |
> |  | pressure = 50 x 10 x 1000 | C1 |
> |  | so value is 500 000 Pa or N/m$^{2}$ | A1 |
> | (b) | force = pressure x area in any form | C1 |
> |  | force = 500 000 x 0.15 x 0.07 | C1 |
> |  | force = 5250 N | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> (a) A high proportion scored full marks. There were few incorrect units for pressure. The most common error was to try to use pressure = force/area, which led nowhere.
>
> (b) Again a high proportion of fully correct answers, although a number could not deal with the dimensions in mm, and the conversion to m.
>
> Answers: (a) 500 000 Pa; (b) 5250 N.
