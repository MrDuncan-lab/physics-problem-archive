> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнение времени прохождения равных участков при разгоне автомобиля.

Five telegraph poles are positioned at equal distances along the side of a road.

![[0625-w03-1-p2-f1.svg]]

A car accelerates until it is level with pole 4. The car then continues along the road at a steady speed. The times taken to travel between one pole and the next are measured.

Which time is the greatest?

The time between

**A** pole 1 and pole 2.  
**B** pole 2 and pole 3.  
**C** pole 3 and pole 4.  
**D** pole 4 and pole 5.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**
