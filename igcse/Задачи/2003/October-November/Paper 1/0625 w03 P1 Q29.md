> Определение знаков зарядов шариков по притяжению и отталкиванию.

Two very light, charged balls P and Q are hung, one above the other, from nylon threads. When a negatively charged plastic sheet is placed alongside them, P is repelled and Q is attracted.

![[0625-w03-1-p14-f2.svg]]

![[0625-w03-1-p14-f3.svg]]

What are the original charges on P and on Q?

|  | charge on P | charge on Q |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | negative | negative |
| **B** | negative | positive |
| **C** | positive | negative |
| **D** | positive | positive |

|  | charge on P | charge on Q |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | negative | negative |
| **B** | negative | positive |
| **C** | positive | negative |
| **D** | positive | positive |
