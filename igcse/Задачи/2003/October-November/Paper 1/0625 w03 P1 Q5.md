> Равновесие балки с грузами на одинаковых плечах.

A beam is pivoted at its centre. Two masses are suspended at equal distances from the pivot as shown in the diagram.

![[0625-w03-1-p3-f2.svg]]

Which statement is correct?

**A** If X has a mass of exactly 2 kg, it will rise.  
**B** If X has a mass of less than 2 kg, it will fall.  
**C** If X has a mass of more than 2 kg, it will fall.  
**D** If X has a mass of more than 2 kg, it will rise.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
