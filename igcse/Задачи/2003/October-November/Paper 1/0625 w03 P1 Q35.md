> Термоэлектронная эмиссия частиц катода.

In a cathode-ray tube, particles are given off from a hot cathode by thermionic emission.

Which particles are given off?

**A** atoms  
**B** electrons  
**C** ions  
**D** protons

> [!answer]- Ответ
