> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Отсутствие распространения звука в космическом вакууме.

Astronaut 1 uses a hammer to mend a satellite in space. Astronaut 2 is nearby. There is no atmosphere in space.

![[0625-w03-1-p12-f1.svg]]

Compared with the sound heard if they were working on Earth, what does astronaut 2 hear?

**A** no sound at all  
**B** a quieter sound  
**C** a sound of the same loudness  
**D** a louder sound

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**
