> Подавление теплопроводности и конвекции вакуумом в термосе.

There is a vacuum between the double walls of a vacuum flask.

Which types of heat transfer are reduced by the vacuum?

**A** conduction and convection  
**B** conduction and radiation  
**C** convection and radiation  
**D** conduction, convection and radiation

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**
