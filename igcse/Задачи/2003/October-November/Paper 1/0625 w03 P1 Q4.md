> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Время пути по средней скорости и расстоянию между городами.

A train travels along a track from Aytown to Beetown. The map shows the route.

![[0625-w03-1-p3-f1.svg]]

The distance travelled by the train between the towns is 210 km. It moves at an average speed of 70 km / h.

How long does the journey take?

70

**A** less than ––– hours, because the journey is not in a straight line

210

70

**B** exactly ––– hours

210

210

**C** exactly ––– hours

70

210

**D** more than ––– hours, because the journey is not in a straight line

70

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
