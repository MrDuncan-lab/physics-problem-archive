> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Превращение энергии приливной воды при работе электростанции.

A tidal power station is made by building a barrage across the mouth of a river. At high tide the sea water is trapped behind the barrage.

![[0625-w03-1-p6-f1.svg]]

At low tide the water is allowed to flow back into the sea through a turbine.

What is the useful energy change in a tidal power station?

**A** electrical energy → energy of position (potential)  
**B** electrical energy → energy of motion (kinetic)  
**C** energy of motion (kinetic) → energy of position (potential)  
**D** energy of position (potential) → electrical energy

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
