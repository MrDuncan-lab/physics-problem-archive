> Назначение предохранителя и автоматического выключателя.

Why is a circuit breaker or a fuse used in a mains electric circuit?

**A** It allows spare current to be returned to the mains.  
**B** It improves the insulation of the wiring.  
**C** It protects the mains wiring from current overload.  
**D** It saves energy by reducing the current.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
