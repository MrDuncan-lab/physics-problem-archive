> Сохранение нуклонного и зарядового числа при бета-распаде.

A radioactive nucleus R decays with the emission of a β-particle as shown.

${}^{\text{x}}_{\text{y}}\text{R} \longrightarrow {}^{\text{p}}_{\text{q}}\text{S} + \beta$

Which equation is correct?

**A** x = p  
**B** y = q  
**C** p = x – 1  
**D** q = y – 1

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**
