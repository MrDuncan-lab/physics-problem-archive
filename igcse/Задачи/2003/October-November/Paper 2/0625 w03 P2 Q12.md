> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Подбор числа витков трансформатора для последовательных и параллельных ламп.

In a country where the mains electricity supply is 240 V, the transformer in Fig. 12.1 is used to enable a 6 V lamp to be lit.

![[0625-w03-2-p14-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 12.1**

**(a)** Calculate the number of turns needed on the secondary coil if the lamp is to be lit at normal brightness.

number of turns =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

**(b)** Without further calculation, state the number of secondary turns needed to light, at normal brightness,

![[0625-w03-2-p14-f2.svg]]

**(i)** two identical 6 V lamps in parallel i.e.

number of turns =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

![[0625-w03-2-p14-f3.svg]]

**(ii)** two identical 6 V lamps in series i.e.

number of turns =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** 200 ALLOW B1 for 20 if 800 used instead of 8000 (working must be shown)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | N$_{1}$/N$_{2}$ = V$_{1}$/V$_{2}$ in any form | C1 |
> |  | 8000/N$_{2}$ = 240/6 or correct substitution into correct equation | C1 |
> |  | 200 ALLOW B1 for 20 if 800 used instead of<br>8000 (working must be shown) | A1 |
> | (b)(i) | 200 e.c.f. i.e. his (a) | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | 400 e.c.f. i.e. 2 x his (a), evaluated | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> As with other numerical questions, this question was usually very well done, with many candidates scoring all 5 marks. Surprisingly, lots of candidates misread 8000 turns as 800 turns. If their working was otherwise correct, this mistake only cost them 1 mark.
>
> Answer: 200, 200, 400.
