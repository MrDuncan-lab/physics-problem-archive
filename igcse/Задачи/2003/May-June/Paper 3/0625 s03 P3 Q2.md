# Question 2 (5 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2003 · Paper 3 — Extended theory · вопрос 2  
Источник: [[_pdf/2003/0625_s03_qp_3.pdf#page=3|условие, стр. 3]] · [[_pdf/2003/0625_s03_ms_1-2-3-5-6.pdf#page=12|схема оценивания, стр. 12]] · [[_pdf/2003/0625_s03_er.pdf#page=1|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Замедление велосипедиста и путь при равнозамедленном движении.

The speed of a cyclist reduces uniformly from 2.5 m/s to 1.0 m/s in 12 s.

**(a)** Calculate the deceleration of the cyclist.

deceleration =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

**(b)** Calculate the distance travelled by the cyclist in this time.

distance =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** a = (-/+) 0.125 m/s
>
> **(b)** distance = 21 m

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | change in speed is 1.5 m/s | C1 |
> |  | deceleration = decrease in speed/time or 1.5/12 | C1 |
> |  | a = (-/+) 0.125 m/s | A1 |
> | (b) | average speed = 1.75 m/s | C1 |
> |  | distance = 21 m | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> (a) Those who used deceleration = speed change/time almost always scored full marks. As expected the most common errors were to divide 2.5 or 1.0 by 12.
>
> (b) Surprisingly there were less correct answers than for part (a). Often complex formulae were employed with disastrous results. A number referred to a non-existent graph in an attempt to use the area under it. Those who used average speed x time generally scored full marks.
>
> Answers: (a) 0.13 m/s$^{2}$; (b) 21 m.
