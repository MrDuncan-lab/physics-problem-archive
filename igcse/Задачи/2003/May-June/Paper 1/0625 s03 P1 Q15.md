> Сравнение скорости молекул воздуха при разных температурах.

Two metal boxes containing air are standing in a room. Box X is on top of a heater. Box Y is on a bench. The boxes are left for a long time.

![[0625-s03-1-p7-f1.svg]]

Which line in the table best describes the average speed of the molecules in the containers?

|  | box X | box Y |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | fast | zero |
| **B** | fast | slow |
| **C** | slow | fast |
| **D** | zero | fast |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
