> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Расчёт времени возвращения звукового эха от скал.

A boy is stranded on an island 500 m from the shore.

![[0625-s03-1-p11-f1.svg]]

He shouts for help, but all he can hear in reply is the echo of his shout from some cliffs.

Sound travels at 340m/s through the air.

What is the time interval between the boy shouting and hearing the echo?

**A** $\dfrac{500}{340}$ s  
**B** $\dfrac{2 \times 500}{340}$ s  
**C** $\dfrac{340}{500}$ s  
**D** $\dfrac{2 \times 340}{500}$ s

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
