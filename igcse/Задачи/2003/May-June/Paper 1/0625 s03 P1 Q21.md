> Определение длины радиоволны из настройки приёмника.

A woman tunes her radio to a station broadcasting on 200 m.

What does the 200m tell her about the radio wave?

**A** its amplitude  
**B** its frequency  
**C** its speed  
**D** its wavelength

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
