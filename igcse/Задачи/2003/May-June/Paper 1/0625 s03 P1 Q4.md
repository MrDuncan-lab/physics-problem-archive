> Средняя скорость автомобиля при проезде тоннеля.

A tunnel has a length of 50 km. A car takes 20 min to travel between the two ends of the tunnel.

What is the average speed of the car?

**A** 2.5 km / h  
**B** 16.6 km / h  
**C** 150 km / h  
**D** 1000 km / h

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
