> Время прохождения тепла через стеклянный стакан.

A person holds a glass beaker in one hand and fills it quickly with hot water. It takes several seconds before his hand starts to feel the heat.

Why is there this delay?

**A** Glass is a poor conductor of heat.  
**B** Glass is a good conductor of heat.  
**C** Water is a poor conductor of heat.  
**D** Water is a good conductor of heat.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**
