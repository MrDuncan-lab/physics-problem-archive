> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question was competently answered by most, barring the usual arithmetical errors. Here 10.5 was a common incorrect answer for (b)(i). In (b)(iii), some candidates used D = MxV. Quite a few could not interpret their calculator displays, and many lost marks because they simply ignored the calculator’s attempt at showing powers of 10. This seems to be a silly way for candidates who know their Physics to lose marks. Lots tried to change the units for mass and/or volume, and got themselves in a tangle. The mark for the correct density unit was given if what was written was appropriate to the units the candidate was trying to use.
