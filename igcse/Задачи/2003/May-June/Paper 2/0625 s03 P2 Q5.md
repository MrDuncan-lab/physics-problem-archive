> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Температурные плато при плавлении льда и кипении воды.

An immersion heater is put into some crushed ice at 0 °C. The immersion heater is switched on.

![[0625-s03-2-p6-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 5.1**

**(a)** On Fig. 5.2, sketch the graph of temperature against time, up to the time when all the ice has melted. **[3]**

![[0625-s03-2-p6-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 5.2**

**(b)** The heater is left switched on after all the ice has melted, and the temperature rises. After some time, the temperature stops rising, even though the heater is still on.

**(i)** Suggest why the temperature stops rising, even though the heater is still on.

**(ii)** State what happens to the energy received by the water whilst this is happening. **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** horizontal from zero time as far as dotted line (ignore anything to R. of line) mark alongside graph

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | line starting at 0 °C | B1 |
> |  | reasonably horizontal line at any temp for ⩾ half the time | M1 |
> |  | horizontal from zero time as far as dotted line (ignore anything to R. of line)<br>mark alongside graph | A1 |
> | (b)(i) | water boils OR heat loss = heat supplied (NOT evaporates/turns to gas) | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | gives water/molecules energy to escape OR break bonds OR change state OR heat loss from sides/surface/to air<br>mark (i) and (ii) together | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Most candidates found this question difficult. Very few drew a horizontal line at “0”, to indicate that the temperature stayed constant at the ice point. A reasonable number spotted that in (b), the water had reached boiling. A few gave the alternative response that heat being lost = heat being supplied. Most answers were very vague, with many candidates being confused between melting and boiling.
