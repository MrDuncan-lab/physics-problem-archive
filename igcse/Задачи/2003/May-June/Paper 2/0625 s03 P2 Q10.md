> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Very few Centres had candidates who had a sound grasp of the workings of a cathode ray oscilloscope. Indeed, some answers were dismal, suggesting that this is part of the syllabus which had been glossed over. Clearly, it aids understanding if candidates have had hands-on experience of CROs, and it is understood that not all Centres may have the equipment to permit this. However, this is part of the core syllabus, and candidates are expected to be able to deal with such questions. Quite a lot of candidates only scored the marks on (a)(ii) and (b)(i), which might be inferred by a candidate prepared to think. Interestingly, a lot of candidates knew something about the inside workings of CROs, and made reasonable attempts at (a)(i) and
>
> (iv) even though they knew nothing about how to use the device.
