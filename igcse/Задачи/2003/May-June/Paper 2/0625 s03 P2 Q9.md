> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> A good number of candidates realised that the wires would be bent, most of these showing curves in opposite directions for the two parts. Very rarely were the curves smooth from A to B, although this was not penalised too harshly. Hardly any could explain satisfactorily why the wires behaved in this way. An acceptable answer had to involve the mention of a force. No penalty was imposed if the candidate showed the curving in the wrong direction.
