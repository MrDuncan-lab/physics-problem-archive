> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Candidates were very uncertain about time delay circuits, and there were very few good answers. It is worth noting that when a question asks for “one use of.....”, vague answers such as “In a television set” will not usually score marks. More detail than this is usually needed, and this is certainly the case here.
