> Качественно сравнить показания амперметров в цепи с одинаковыми резисторами.

A circuit includes a battery, two identical resistors and five ammeters, P, Q, R, S and T.

![[0625-m22-12-p14-f2.svg]]

Which statement about the readings on the ammeters is **not** correct?

**A** P has a greater reading than Q.  
**B** P has a greater reading than R.  
**C** P has a greater reading than S.  
**D** P has a greater reading than T.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**
