> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить знаки зарядов тел по их электростатическому отталкиванию.

When a plastic comb is placed next to a small piece of aluminium foil hanging from a nylon thread, the foil is repelled by the comb.

Why is this?

**A** The comb is charged and the foil is uncharged.  
**B** The comb is uncharged and the foil is charged.  
**C** The comb and the foil have charges of opposite sign.  
**D** The comb and the foil have charges of the same sign.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
