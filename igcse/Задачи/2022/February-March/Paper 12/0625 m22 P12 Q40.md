> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить интервал периода полураспада по таблице уменьшения скорости счёта.

When a radioactive isotope is set up close to a counter, a count rate of 38 000 counts / s is

obtained. The table shows the count rate from the isotope over a three-year period.

| time / years | count rate counts / s |
|---|---|
| 0 | 38 000 |
| 1 | 26 000 |
| 2 | 17 000 |
| 3 | 12 000 |

What is the half-life of the isotope?

**A** less than 1 year  
**B** more than 1 year but less than 2 years  
**C** more than 2 years but less than 3 years  
**D** more than 3 years

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
