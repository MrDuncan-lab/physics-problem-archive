# Question 20 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · February/March 2022 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 20

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объяснить различие скорости охлаждения сосудов свойствами их поверхностей.

Two similar metal containers, P and Q, each contain water at 90 °C. Both containers are the

same size and both are sealed.

The water in container P cools more quickly than the water in container Q.

Which statement is correct?

**A** P contains more water than Q.  
**B** P has a shinier surface than Q.  
**C** P is painted a darker colour than Q.  
**D** P is surrounded by a vacuum and Q is surrounded by air.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question tested the knowledge that darker colours are the best radiators of thermal energy and so the darker coloured container would cool more rapidly. More candidates chose option **B**, a shinier surface, than the correct option **C**.
