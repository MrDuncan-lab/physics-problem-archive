**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · February/March 2022 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 34  
Источник: [[_pdf/2022/0625_m22_qp_12.pdf#page=15|условие, стр. 15]] · [[_pdf/2022/0625_m22_ms_12.pdf|схема оценивания]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Различить постоянный и переменный ток по направлению движения зарядов.

Three statements about a.c. and d.c. currents are given.

1 A d.c. current is in one direction only whilst an a.c. current repeatedly changes

direction.

2 d.c. is the abbreviation for direct current and a.c. is the abbreviation for amplitude

current.

3 An a.c. current is in one direction only whilst a d.c. current repeatedly changes

direction.

Which statements are correct?

**A** 1 and 2  
**B** 1 only  
**C** 2 and 3  
**D** 3 only

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
