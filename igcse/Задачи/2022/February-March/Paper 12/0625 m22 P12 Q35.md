> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить взаимодействие магнитов с катушкой по заданному направлению тока.

Two magnets are placed near a current-carrying coil.

The diagram shows this experimental arrangement and the current direction in the coil.

![[0625-m22-12-p16-f1.svg]]

Which statement is correct?

**A** Both X and Y are attracted to the coil.  
**B** Both X and Y are repelled by the coil.  
**C** X is attracted to the coil and Y is repelled.  
**D** X is repelled by the coil and Y is attracted.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Few candidates answered this correctly. Candidates needed to be able to work out the polarity of each end of the coil. In this case, the left-hand side is an S pole, and the right-hand side is an N pole and so both X and Y are repelled by the coil. Most candidates thought both X and Y would be attracted.
