> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить изменение промежутка между железными сердечниками при замыкании цепи катушки.

Two circuits are set up as shown. The iron rods are placed close together and are able to move.

![[0625-m22-22-p14-f1.svg]]

What happens to the size of the gap at X when switch S is closed?

**A** It decreases.  
**B** It decreases then increases.  
**C** It increases.  
**D** It does not change.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Most candidates correctly rejected options **B** and **D**, but many candidates incorrectly chose option **A**. Candidates needed to determine the polarity of each coil at X. Both were N poles in this case so the coils repelled.
