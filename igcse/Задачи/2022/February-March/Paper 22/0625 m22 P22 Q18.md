> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Различить измерения для определения удельной теплоёмкости вещества и полной теплоёмкости тела.

The diagram shows the apparatus needed for an experiment to determine the specific heat

capacity of the material from which an object is made.

![[0625-m22-22-p7-f2.png]]

Which piece of apparatus could be omitted if the purpose of the experiment is to determine the thermal capacity of the object?

**A** ammeter  
**B** balance  
**C** stop-clock  
**D** thermometer

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Only stronger candidates answered this correctly. Many candidates did not understand the difference between the specific heat capacity of a material and the thermal capacity of an object. Most candidates chose either option **A**, **B** or **C**.
