> Выбрать логический элемент для блокировки двигателя и управления сигналом защитного ограждения.

The diagram shows an electric drill and safety guard.

![[0625-m22-22-p13-f1.svg]]

electric motor

The drill requires the logic circuit shown.

![[0625-m22-22-p13-f2.svg]]

The circuit must only allow the motor to start when the safety guard is closed. If the safety guard is not closed when the starting switch is on, the alarm must sound.

Which logic gate is missing from the circuit?

**A** NOT  
**B** NAND  
**C** AND  
**D** NOR

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
