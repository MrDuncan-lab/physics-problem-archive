> Определить результирующие силу и момент для уравновешенной линейки.

The diagram shows a uniform metre rule, MN, pivoted at its midpoint P.

![[0625-m22-22-p4-f1.svg]]

Two weights, *W*$_{1}$ and *W*$_{2}$, are hung either side of the pivot.

The rule remains balanced.

Which row is correct?

|  | direction of resultant moment about point M | direction of resultant force on the rule |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | clockwise | downwards |
| **B** | clockwise | zero |
| **C** | zero | downwards |
| **D** | zero | zero |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
