> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить изменение ускорения мяча при свободном падении без сопротивления воздуха.

A tennis ball falls from the upstairs window of a house.

![[0625-m22-22-p2-f1.svg]]

What can be said about the acceleration of the ball if air resistance is ignored?

**A** It depends on the density of the ball.  
**B** It depends on the mass of the ball.  
**C** It increases as the ball falls.  
**D** It stays the same as the ball falls.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> A significant number of candidates incorrectly thought that the acceleration of a ball falling freely in air increased as the ball fell.
