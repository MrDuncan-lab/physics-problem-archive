> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбрать выражение давления тела через вес и площадь контакта.

An object is at rest on a horizontal surface.

Which equation is used to calculate the pressure that the object exerts?

**A** $\dfrac{\text{mass of the object}}{\text{area of contact}}$  
**B** $\dfrac{\text{weight of the object}}{\text{area of contact}}$  
**C** mass of the object × area of contact  
**D** weight of the object × area of contact

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
