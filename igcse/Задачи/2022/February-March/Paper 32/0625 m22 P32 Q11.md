# Question 11 (6 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · February/March 2022 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 11  
Источник: [[_pdf/2022/0625_m22_qp_32.pdf#page=15|условие, стр. 15]] · [[_pdf/2022/0625_m22_ms_32.pdf#page=14|схема оценивания, стр. 14]] · [[_pdf/2022/0625_m22_er.pdf#page=5|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 m22 P32]]  
Темы: [[4.5.6 The transformer]], [[4.4 Electrical safety]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать напряжение зарядного трансформатора и описать действие плавкого предохранителя.

A battery charger for a laptop computer includes a transformer.

**(a)** The primary voltage *V*$_{\text{p}}$ to the transformer is 240 V.

The number of turns on the primary coil *N*$_{\text{p}}$ is 590 turns and the number of turns on the secondary coil *N*$_{\text{s}}$ of the transformer is 48 turns.

Calculate the secondary voltage *V*$_{\text{s}}$ of the transformer.

secondary voltage *V*$_{\text{s}}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ V **[3]**

**(b)** The plug connecting the transformer to the supply voltage contains a fuse.

Describe how a fuse works. **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** 20 (V)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | 20 (V) | A3 |
> |  | *V*s / 240 = 48 / 590 OR *V*s = (48 / 590) × 240 OR *V*s = 240 ÷ 12.3 OR 48 / 590 = ? / 240 OR $\dfrac{240 \times 48}{590}$ | (C2) |
> |  | *V*$_{\text{s}}$ / *V*$_{\text{p}}$ = *N*$_{\text{s}}$ / *N*$_{\text{p}}$ in any form | (C1) |
> | (b) | large current (in fuse) | B1 |
> |  | (causes) fuse to melt | B1 |
> |  | isolating appliance from supply OR prevents / stops current in appliance / circuit | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Most candidates gained at least partial credit. Weaker candidates used an incorrect form of the equation.
>
> **(b)** Most candidates scored at least partial credit. Weaker candidates thought that the fuse controlled the supply of current in the circuit.
