> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** Most candidates gained credit for knowing alpha-radiation has a high ionising ability. However, few candidates knew that a beta-particle is an electron, or that the relative charge on an alpha-particle is plus two.
>
> **(ii)** The vast majority of candidates answered this correctly.
>
> **(b)** The vast majority of candidates correctly identified the particles in the nucleus. The most common error was to have the answers interchanged.
