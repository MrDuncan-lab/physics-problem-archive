**(a)** Fig. 9.1 shows the regions of the electromagnetic spectrum. One region is not labelled.

**(i)** In Fig. 9.1, one region is unlabelled.

State the name of the unlabelled region. **[1]**

**(ii)** Complete the sentence.

The direction of the arrow in Fig. 9.1 shows the regions of the electromagnetic spectrum

in order of increasing  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(b)** State **two** uses for X-rays.

1 \_\_\_\_\_\_

2 \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(c)** State why X-rays can be harmful to people. **[2]**

> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | microwaves | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | frequency | B1 |
> | (b) | any **two** from:<br>imaging / scanning bones / internal organs owtte<br>treating cancer<br>bone density measurements<br>security scans (of baggage / parcels / freight at port / airport)<br>space telescopes | B2 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** Most candidates answered this well.
>
> **(ii)** Only stronger candidates recognised that the waves were increasing in frequency. The most common error was to state “wavelength”.
>
> **(b)** Most candidates gained full credit, but once again lack of precision resulted in many candidates only being awarded partial credit.
>
> **(c)** Many candidates answered this correctly, but others did not give sufficient detail in answers.
