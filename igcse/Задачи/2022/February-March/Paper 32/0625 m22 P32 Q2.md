# Question 2 (8 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · February/March 2022 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 2  
Источник: [[_pdf/2022/0625_m22_qp_32.pdf#page=4|условие, стр. 4–5]] · [[_pdf/2022/0625_m22_ms_32.pdf#page=9|схема оценивания, стр. 9]] · [[_pdf/2022/0625_m22_er.pdf#page=5|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 m22 P32]]  
Темы: [[1.5.2 Turning effect of forces]], [[1.2 Motion]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать момент веса и определить движение и путь спортсмена по графику скорости.

**(a)** A student is doing some physical exercise. Fig. 2.1 shows the student holding a 50 N weight.

![[0625-m22-32-p4-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 2.1**

The pivot in the shoulder is 0.90 m from the centre of mass of the weight.

Calculate the moment of the weight about this pivot.

moment of the weight =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ N m **[3]**

**(b)** The student does some running exercises. Fig. 2.2 shows the speed–time graph for one exercise.

![[0625-m22-32-p5-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 2.2**

**(i)** Describe the motion of the athlete in sections AB and DE.

section AB  \_\_\_\_\_\_

section DE  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(ii)** Calculate the distance moved by the athlete from time = 0 to time = 5.0 s.

distance =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ m **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** 45 (Nm)
>
> **(b)(ii)** 20 (m)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | 45 (Nm) | A3 |
> |  | (moment of force =) force × (perpendicular) distance (of force from pivot) | (C1) |
> |  | 50 × 0.9 | (C2) |
> | (b)(i) | (section AB) increasing speed OR acceleration | B1 |
> |  | (section DE) stationary OR stopped OR at rest | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | 20 (m) | A3 |
> |  | (distance =) ½ × 8(.0) × 5(.0) | (C2) |
> |  | distance travelled = area under graph OR (d = ) speed × time | (C1) |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Most candidates correctly calculated the moment as 45 N m. The most common error was to divide the force by the distance.
>
> **(b) (i)** Most candidates stated that section AB indicated acceleration, and in DE that the athlete was stationary. A common error was to state that the motion in DE was constant, or the athlete was moving at a constant speed.
>
> **(ii)** Most candidates realised that the distance travelled was the area under the speed–time graph, but a significant number failed to calculate this correctly. The most common error was 5 × 8 = 40 m.
