# Question 3 (8 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · February/March 2022 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 3  
Источник: [[_pdf/2022/0625_m22_qp_32.pdf#page=6|условие, стр. 6]] · [[_pdf/2022/0625_m22_ms_32.pdf#page=10|схема оценивания, стр. 10]] · [[_pdf/2022/0625_m22_er.pdf#page=5|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

Темы: [[1.4 Density]], [[E.1 Measurements, tables, graphs, precision and planning an experiment]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Вычислить объём и плотность блока, предсказать плавание и описать измерение плотности жидкости.

**(a)** Fig. 3.1 shows a metal block and its dimensions.

![[0625-m22-32-p6-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 3.1** (not to scale)

Calculate the volume of the metal block.

volume of the block =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm$^{3}$ **[2]**

**(b)** A different metal block has a mass of 86 g and a volume of 8.0 cm$^{3}$.

**(i)** Calculate the density ρ of the metal using the equation

ρ = $\dfrac{m}{V}$ .

density of metal =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ g / cm$^{3}$ **[2]**

**(ii)** The metal block is placed in some liquid. The metal block floats on the liquid.

Suggest a value for the density of the liquid.

\_\_\_\_\_\_ g / cm$^{3}$ **[1]**

**(c)** A student has a measuring cylinder, a beaker of liquid and a balance.

Describe how the student can use this equipment to determine the density of the liquid. **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** (volume =) 48 (cm$^{3}$)
>
> **(b)(i)** 11 (g / cm$^{3}$)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | (volume =) 48 (cm$^{3}$) | A2 |
> |  | (volume =) l × b × h | (C1) |
> | (b)(i) | 11 (g / cm$^{3}$) | A2 |
> |  | (density =) 86 ÷ 8.0 | (C1) |
> | (b)(ii) | any value greater than **(b)(i)** (g / cm$^{3}$) | B1 |
> | (c) | any **three** from:<br>measure mass of (empty) measuring cylinder on balance<br>add liquid to measuring cylinder **AND** read volume<br>measure mass of measuring cylinder **AND** liquid on balance<br>find difference in the 2 mass readings | B3 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Almost all candidates correctly calculated the volume of the block as 48 cm$^{3}$. A common error was to use 2 × *l* × b × h.
>
> **(b) (i) (ii)** Most candidates correctly calculated the density of the metal in **(i)**, and then gave a suitable density for the liquid.
>
> **(c)** Many candidates gave very precise descriptions on how to use the equipment to determine density. Weaker candidates failed to state that the mass was measured using the balance and the measuring cylinder was used to measure volume.
