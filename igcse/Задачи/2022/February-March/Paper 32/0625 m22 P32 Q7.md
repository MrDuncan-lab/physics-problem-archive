# Question 7 (6 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · February/March 2022 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 7

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Распознать теплопроводность, объяснить конвекцию воды и сравнить поглощение излучения чёрной и белой поверхностями.

**(a)** State the name for the transfer of thermal energy through a metal. **[1]**

**(b)** A metal can contains some water. The metal can is heated as shown in Fig. 7.1.

![[0625-m22-32-p10-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 7.1**

Describe how thermal energy causes the movement of water shown in Fig. 7.1. **[3]**

**(c)** A student places equal masses of water into two metal cans. The cans are identical apart from one having a white outer surface and the other having a black outer surface.

The student places the two cans in direct sunlight. The temperature of the water increases in both cans. After 10 minutes, the temperature increase is greater in one of the cans.

State and explain which can has the greater increase in temperature. **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(c)** (black) is a better absorber (of thermal radiation) OR white can is a better reflector

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | conduction | B1 |
> | (b) | any **three** from:<br>convection<br>(heated water) expands<br>(becomes) less dense<br><u>less dense water rises OR dense(r) water sinks</u> | B3 |
> | (c) | (dull) black (can) | M1 |
> |  | (black) is a better absorber (of thermal radiation) OR white can is a better reflector | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** The vast majority of candidates answered this correctly.
>
> **(b)** Many candidates gained partial credit, but only the strongest answered fully correctly. The most common error was to state that the water molecules expanded, rather than that the water molecules moved further apart, so decreasing the density of water when it is heated.
>
> **(c)** The majority of candidates gained full credit. The most common error was failing to state that the black surface is a better absorber of thermal radiation compared to the white surface.
