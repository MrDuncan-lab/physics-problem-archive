# Question 7 (9 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · February/March 2022 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 2 · вопрос 7  
Источник: [[_pdf/2022/0625_m22_qp_42.pdf#page=12|условие, стр. 12]] · [[_pdf/2022/0625_m22_ms_42.pdf#page=14|схема оценивания, стр. 14]] · [[_pdf/2022/0625_m22_er.pdf#page=8|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить число элементов батареи, сопротивления смешанной цепи и мощность одной лампы.

Fig. 7.1 shows a circuit including a 12 V battery and two identical lamps.

![[0625-m22-42-p12-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 7.1**

**(a)** The 12 V battery consists of cells connected in series. Each cell in the battery has an electromotive force (e.m.f.) of 1.5 V.

Determine how many cells are in the battery.

number of cells =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(b)** **(i)** When the switch is closed, the ammeter reading is 2.4 A.

Calculate the total resistance of the circuit.

resistance =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(ii)** Each lamp has a resistance of 3.0 Ω.

Calculate the resistance of Q.

resistance of Q =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(c)** **(i)** On Fig. 7.1, draw the symbol for a voltmeter that measures the potential difference (p.d.) across the two lamps. **[1]**

**(ii)** Calculate the power supplied to **one** lamp.

power =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)(i)** (12 / 2.4 =) 5.0 Ω
>
> **(b)(ii)** (Resistance of Q = 5–1.5) = 3.5 Ω
>
> **(c)(ii)** 4.3 W

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | 8 (cells) | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | (12 / 2.4 =) 5.0 Ω | A2 |
> |  | R = V / I in any form | (C1) |
> | (b)(ii) | (Resistance of Q = 5–1.5) = 3.5 Ω | A2 |
> |  | 1 / R = 1 / R$_{1}$ + 1 / R$_{2}$OR R= R$_{1}$R$_{2}$ / (R$_{1}$+R$_{2}$) | (C1) |
> | (c)(i) | correct voltmeter symbol connected correctly across both lamps | B1 |
> | (c)(ii) | 4.3 W | A3 |
> |  | (P = ) IV in any form OR 1.2 × 3.6 | (C1) |
> |  | 3.6 V **OR** 1.2 A | (C1) |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** There were many correct answers. A common mistake was to state 2 cells, probably arrived at by inspection of the diagram and misunderstanding the circuit symbol for a battery.
>
> **(b) (i)** This question was answered well by most candidates. Almost all candidates gained credit for recall of *V* = *IR* and it was only the weakest candidates who were unable to rearrange the formula correctly to arrive at the final answer.
>
> **(ii)** Stronger candidates correctly applied the formula for two resistors in parallel and then subtracted their answer from the total resistance calculated in **(i)**. Some weaker candidates did not attempt to use the equation for resistors in parallel and often just added resistances. Some candidates forgot to invert their answer for 1 / *R* and others did not attempt to subtract their answer from the answer to **(i)**.
>
> **(c) (i)** Most candidates used the correct symbol and placed the voltmeter appropriately across both lamps. A few weaker candidates put the voltmeter in series or showed a horizontal wire inside the voltmeter symbol.
>
> **(ii)** Most candidates correctly quoted *P* = *IV*. However, only stronger candidates were able to substitute a correct value for the current *I* and very few candidates identified that *V* = 3.6 V.
