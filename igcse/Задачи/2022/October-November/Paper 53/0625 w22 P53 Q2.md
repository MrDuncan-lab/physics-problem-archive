> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнить охлаждение сосудов с чёрной и блестящей поверхностями и исследовать влияние теплоизоляции покрытия.

In this experiment, you will investigate the effect of the colour of the outside surface of a beaker on the rate of cooling of water in the beaker.

Carry out the following instructions, referring to Fig. 2.1.

Beaker A is covered with thin black card. Beaker B is covered with shiny metal foil.

![[0625-w22-53-p8-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 2.1**

**(a)** Remove the lid from beaker A. Pour 150 cm$^{3}$ of hot water into beaker A and replace the lid. Place the thermometer in the water.

In the first row of Table 2.1, record the temperature θ of the water at time *t* = 0 and immediately start the stop-clock. Record the temperature θ of the water at *t* = 30 s, *t* = 60 s, *t* = 90 s, *t* = 120 s, *t* = 150 s and *t* = 180 s.

Remove the thermometer from the beaker. **[1]**

**(c)** Write a conclusion stating if the colour of the outside surface of the beaker affects the rate of cooling of the water in the beaker. Justify your answer by reference to values from your results. **[2]**

**(d)** **(i)** Calculate the average cooling rate *x*$_{\text{A}}$ for the water in beaker A during the experiment. Use your readings for beaker A from Table 2.1 and the equation

*x*$_{\text{A}}$ = $\dfrac{\theta _{0} – \theta _{180}}{T}$

where *T* = 180 s and θ$_{0}$ and θ$_{180}$ are the temperatures of the water in beaker A at times *t* = 0 and *t* = 180 s. Include the unit for the cooling rate.

*x*$_{\text{A}}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(ii)** Calculate the average cooling rate *x*$_{\text{B}}$ for the water in beaker B during the experiment. Use your readings for beaker B from Table 2.1 and the equation

*x*$_{\text{B}}$ = $\dfrac{\theta _{0} – \theta _{180}}{T}$

where *T* = 180 s and θ$_{0}$ and θ$_{180}$ are the temperatures of the water in beaker B at times *t* = 0 and *t* = 180 s. Include the unit.

*x*$_{\text{B}}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(e)** **(i)** A student states that the black card is a thermal insulator. He thinks this will affect the result.

Suggest an additional experiment to test this theory.

You are **not** required to carry out this additional experiment. **[1]**

**(ii)** Suggest **one** variable which should be kept the same for this additional experiment so that the comparison with your experiment is fair. Assume that the same type of beaker is used. **[1]**

**(iii)** State how the cooling rate of beaker A from the additional experiment is likely to compare with *x*$_{\text{A}}$ from **(d)(i)** if the student’s theory is correct. Explain your answer.
