> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Начертить схему измерения сопротивления проволоки и рассчитать сопротивление по напряжению и току.

A student uses the circuit in Fig. 8.1 to find the resistance of a piece of iron wire.

![[0625-w22-32-p12-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 8.1**

**(a)** Complete Fig. 8.2 to show the circuit diagram for the arrangement shown in Fig. 8.1.

The piece of iron wire is shown as the thicker line between the points A and B.

![[0625-w22-32-p12-f2.svg]]

**[3]**

**Fig. 8.2**

**(b)** The reading on the voltmeter is 1.56 V.

The reading on the ammeter is 0.112 A.

Calculate the resistance of the iron wire. Include the unit in your answer.

resistance =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ unit  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[4]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** 14

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | ammeter, battery and test wire in complete series circuit | B1 |
> |  | voltmeter in parallel with AB | B1 |
> |  | correct circuit symbols for ammeter AND voltmeter | B1 |
> | (b) | 14 | A3 |
> |  | 1.56 ÷ 0.112 | (C2) |
> |  | (R =) V ÷ I in any form OR V = I × R | (C1) |
> |  | Ω / ohm(s) | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** The majority of candidates answered this question well. A common error was placing the voltmeter in series with the ammeter. There were a noticeable number of no responses.
>
> **(b)** The majority of candidates gained credit for the correct numerical answer, writing the equation and showing full working. There were several incorrect answers based on an incorrect equation.
