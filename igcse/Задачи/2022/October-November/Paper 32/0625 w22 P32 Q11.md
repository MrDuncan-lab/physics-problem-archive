# Question 11 (6 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2022 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 11  
Источник: [[_pdf/2022/0625_w22_qp_32.pdf#page=15|условие, стр. 15]] · [[_pdf/2022/0625_w22_ms_32.pdf#page=12|схема оценивания, стр. 12]] · [[_pdf/2022/0625_w22_er.pdf#page=20|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

Темы: [[5.1.1 The atom]], [[5.1.2 The nucleus]], [[5.2.2 The three types of nuclear emission]], [[5.2.4 Half-life]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Описать строение углерода и природу бета-частиц, определить возраст деревянного предмета по распаду углерода.

Fig. 11.1 represents an atom of carbon-14.

![[0625-w22-32-p15-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 11.1**

**(a)** **(i)** State the name of the particle labelled X. **[1]**

**(ii)** State the name of the particle labelled Y. **[1]**

**(iii)** State the nucleon number of carbon-14. **[1]**

**(b)** Carbon-14 decays by emitting a β (beta)-particle.

State the nature of a β (beta)-particle. **[1]**

**(c)** Scientists find an ancient wooden spoon. They find that the spoon contains 2000 atoms of carbon-14.

When the spoon was made, it contained 16 000 atoms of carbon-14.

The half-life of carbon-14 is 5800 years.

Calculate the age of the ancient spoon.

age of spoon =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ years **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(c)** 17 400

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | neutron | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | electron | B1 |
> | (a)(iii) | 14 | B1 |
> | (b) | electron | B1 |
> | (c) | 17 400 | A2 |
> |  | 16000 – 8000 – 4000 – 2000 OR 3 half lives | (C1) |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** Most candidates answered this question well.
>
> **(ii)** There was some confusion between the ionising properties of alpha and beta particles.
>
> **(iii)** There was some confusion between the charge on alpha and beta particles.
>
> **(b)** Very few correct answers were seen. Candidates did not seem to recognise that beta particles are (high-speed) electrons.
>
> **(c)** A few candidates gained partial credit for understanding that the age of the spoon was equivalent to three half-lives. A noticeable number achieves full credit.
