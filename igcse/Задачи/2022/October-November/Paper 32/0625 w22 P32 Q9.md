# Question 9 (7 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2022 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 9  
Источник: [[_pdf/2022/0625_w22_qp_32.pdf#page=13|условие, стр. 13]] · [[_pdf/2022/0625_w22_ms_32.pdf#page=11|схема оценивания, стр. 11]] · [[_pdf/2022/0625_w22_er.pdf#page=20|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w22 P32]]  
Темы: [[4.5.6 The transformer]], [[4.2.2 Electric current]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить тип и выходное напряжение трансформатора, материал сердечника и результат замены переменного источника постоянным.

Fig. 9.1 shows a transformer. An a.c. voltmeter is connected to the output of the secondary coil.

![[0625-w22-32-p13-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 9.1**

**(a)** State the meaning of a.c. **[1]**

**(b)** State the name of the type of transformer shown. **[1]**

**(c)** State a suitable material for the core of the transformer in Fig. 9.1. **[1]**

**(d)** Using the information in Fig. 9.1, calculate the reading on the voltmeter.

reading on voltmeter =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ V **[3]**

**(e)** The 10 V a.c. power supply is replaced by a 10 V d.c. battery.

State the reading on the voltmeter.

reading on voltmeter =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ V **[1]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(d)** 40 (V)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | alternating (current) | B1 |
> | (b) | step-up | B1 |
> | (c) | (soft) iron | B1 |
> | (d) | 40 (V) | A3 |
> |  | (V$_{\text{S}}$ =) 800 / 200 × 10 | (C2) |
> |  | V$_{\text{P}}$/ V$_{\text{S =}}$N$_{\text{P}}$/ N$_{\text{S}}$ in any form | (C1) |
> | (e) | zero | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Candidates generally lacked the knowledge for this part and there were a significant number of no responses. A common mistake was “alternative current”.
>
> **(b)** Again a general lack of knowledge was evident and there were a substantial number of no responses.
>
> **(c)** Many correct answers were seen here. There was some confusion between the magnetic properties of iron and steel.
>
> **(d)** Most candidates answered this question well. Weaker candidates mainly gained credit for recalling the equation.
>
> **(e)** Very few correct answers were seen. Candidates did not seem to recognise that a transformer only works with an alternating current.
