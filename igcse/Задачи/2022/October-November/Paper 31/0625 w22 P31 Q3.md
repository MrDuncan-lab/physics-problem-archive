# Question 3 (6 marks)

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать момент силы на лебёдке и описать полезные и неполезные превращения энергии при подъёме паруса.

A sailor uses a winch to raise a sail on a boat. Fig. 3.1 shows the sailor turning the winch.

![[0625-w22-31-p5-f1.png]]

**Fig. 3.1**

**(a)** The sailor applies a force of 200 N at a distance of 30 cm from the pivot in the winch, as

![[0625-w22-31-p5-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 3.2**

Calculate the moment of this force about the pivot.

moment of force =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ N cm **[3]**

**(b)** **(i)** Describe **two** useful energy transfers when the sailor uses the winch to raise the sail.

1  \_\_\_\_\_\_

2  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(ii)** Describe **one** non-useful energy transfer when the sailor uses the winch to raise the sail. **[1]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** 6000 (N cm)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | 6000 (N cm) | A3 |
> |  | (moment of force =) 200 × 30 | C2 |
> |  | (moment of force =) force × (perpendicular) distance (of force from pivot) | C1 |
> | (b)(i) | any **two** from:<br>• chemical energy to (gravitational) potential energy (of sail)<br>• chemical energy to kinetic energy<br>• kinetic energy (of winch) to kinetic energy (of rope / sail)<br>• kinetic energy (of rope / sail) to (gravitational) potential energy (of sail). | B2 |
> | (b)(ii) | chemical energy **OR** kinetic energy to thermal **OR** sound (energy) | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** The vast majority of candidates gained full credit. The most common error was starting with an incorrect arrangement of the equation. This was usually moment = force ÷ distance.
>
> **(b) (i)** Only stronger candidates answered this fully correctly. Candidates should be encouraged to practice writing energy transfer equations for common situations or appliances.
>
> **(ii)** This item was slightly better answered than **(i)** but many candidates displayed a lack of understanding of useful and non-useful energy transfers.
