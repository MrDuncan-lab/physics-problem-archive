# Question 2 (10 marks)

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Найти равнодействующую сил, среднюю скорость и путь автомобиля по измерениям времени и графику скорости.

Fig. 2.1 shows the horizontal forces acting on a car.

![[0625-w22-31-p3-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 2.1** (not to scale)

**(a)** Calculate the resultant horizontal force on the car.

size of force =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ N

direction  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

**(b)** A student uses a digital stop-watch to measure the time for the car to travel 100 m.

Fig. 2.2 shows the time reading on the stop-watch.

![[0625-w22-31-p3-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 2.2**

**(i)** Using the information in Fig. 2.2, state the time taken to travel 100 m.

time to travel 100 m =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ s **[1]**

**(ii)** The car takes 12.8 s to travel the next 200 m.

Calculate the average speed of the car for this 200 m.

average speed =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ m / s **[3]**

**(c)** Fig. 2.3 shows the speed–time graph for another car.

![[0625-w22-31-p4-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 2.3**

Calculate the distance travelled by this car between time = 2.0 s and time = 6.0 s.

distance travelled =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ m **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** 300 (N)
>
> **(b)(ii)** 16 (m / s)
>
> **(c)** 48 (m)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | 300 (N) | A2 |
> |  | (resultant force =) force to right – force to left **OR** 1200 – 900 | C1 |
> |  | to the right **OR** in forward direction | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | 7.20 (s) | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | 16 (m / s) | A3 |
> |  | 200 / 12.8 | C2 |
> |  | (average speed =) (total) distance / (total) time in any form | C1 |
> | (c) | 48 (m) | A3 |
> |  | $\dfrac{1}{2}$ (6 + 18) × 4.0 **OR** 6 × 4 + $\dfrac{1}{2}$ × 12 × 4 | C2 |
> |  | distance = area under graph<br>**OR** area = $\dfrac{1}{2}$ (sum of parallel sides) × base | C1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Almost all candidates gained full credit on this question. Weaker candidates added the two forces instead of subtracting. A small number gave the direction as East with no indication on the diagram of what was meant by East.
>
> **(b) (i)** Many candidates simply wrote a copy of the stopwatch screen. Centres should encourage candidates to use stopwatches in practical exercises, such as determining the speed of sound, and to practise changing the display reading into a time in seconds.
>
> **(ii)** This calculation was answered well by most candidates. A small number started with the correct equation of speed = distance ÷ time but managed to rearrange this so that they divided 12.8 by 200 rather than 200 ÷ 12.8.
>
> **(c)** Only stronger candidates gained full credit for this question. Candidates should be encouraged to practise calculating the area below speed-time graphs when the area is a trapezium shape.
