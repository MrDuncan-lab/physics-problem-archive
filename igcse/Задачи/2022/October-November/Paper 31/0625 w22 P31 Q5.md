> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить давление газа по манометру и объяснить молекулярное происхождение давления и его уменьшение при расширении.

An engineer measures the pressure of the gas in a gas bottle. Fig. 5.1 shows the measuring device he uses, connected to the gas bottle.

![[0625-w22-31-p8-t1.svg]]

**(a)** **(i)** Atmospheric pressure is 756 mm of mercury.

Calculate the pressure of the gas in the gas bottle.

pressure of gas =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ mm of mercury **[3]**

**(ii)** State the name of the measuring device shown in Fig. 5.1. **[1]**

**(b)** Some gas is trapped in a cylinder fitted with a moveable piston. Fig. 5.2 shows the

![[0625-w22-31-p9-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 5.2**

**(i)** Describe how the gas exerts a pressure on the cylinder.

Use your ideas about molecules. **[2]**

**(ii)** The piston moves and increases the volume occupied by the gas. The temperature of the gas remains constant. Fig. 5.3 shows the new position of the piston.

![[0625-w22-31-p9-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 5.3**

State and explain what happens to the pressure of the gas on the cylinder. **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)(i)** 880 (mm Hg)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | 880 (mm Hg) | A3 |
> |  | (180 – 60 =) 120 (mm Hg) | C2 |
> |  | (left tube =) 60 (mm Hg) **AND** (right tube =) 180 (mm Hg) seen | C1 |
> | (a)(ii) | (U-tube) manometer | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | any **two** from:<br>• molecules in air moving at high speed / kinetic energy<br>• molecules collide with cylinder **OR** wall (of cylinder) **OR** piston<br>• force of collisions (per unit area) cause pressure. | B2 |
> | (b)(ii) | smaller / lower pressure (on cylinder) | B1 |
> |  | (because) reduced rate of collisions **OR** fewer collisions with cylinder **OR** wall (of cylinder) **OR** piston (per unit area) | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** The majority of candidates correctly calculated the difference in height between the two levels of mercury in the manometer. However, many did not know what to do with this value of 120 mm of Hg. Common errors included 760 ÷ 120, 760 × 120 and 760 – 120.
>
> **(ii)** There were many candidates who correctly identified the mercury manometer. Common errors included barometer and thermometer.
>
> **(b) (i)** Many candidates did not give a simple kinetic theory model of how a gas exerts a pressure on a surface. Few candidates mentioned molecules in air moving at high speed or molecules colliding with the cylinder.
>
> **(ii)** Many candidates recognised that the pressure decreased but far fewer went on to link this with the reduced rate of collisions with cylinder wall or even with fewer collisions with cylinder wall.
