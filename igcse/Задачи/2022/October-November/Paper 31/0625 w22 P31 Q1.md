# Question 1 (9 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2022 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 1 · вопрос 1  
Источник: [[_pdf/2022/0625_w22_qp_31.pdf#page=2|условие, стр. 2]] · [[_pdf/2022/0625_w22_ms_31.pdf#page=6|схема оценивания, стр. 6]] · [[_pdf/2022/0625_w22_er.pdf#page=16|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w22 P31]]  
Темы: [[1.1 Physical quantities and measurement techniques]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить объём капли и описать измерение объёма неправильного металлического тела вытеснением воды.

Fig. 1.1 shows a measuring cylinder containing some water.

![[0625-w22-31-p2-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 1.1**

**(a)** State the volume of the water in the measuring cylinder.

volume =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm$^{3}$ **[1]**

**(b)** A student adds 20 drops of water to the water that is in the measuring cylinder in Fig. 1.1. The new volume of water in the measuring cylinder is 25 cm$^{3}$.

Calculate the average volume of one drop of water.

average volume of one drop =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm$^{3}$ **[4]**

**(c)** A student has a measuring cylinder and a small, irregularly shaped piece of metal. The piece of metal can easily fit into the measuring cylinder.

Describe how the student can use the measuring cylinder and some water to find the volume of the metal. **[4]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** 0.2(0) (cm$^{3}$)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | 21 (cm$^{3}$) | B1 |
> | (b) | 0.2(0) (cm$^{3}$) | A4 |
> |  | (average volume of one drop) = 4(.0) / 20 | C3 |
> |  | (volume = 25 – 21 =) 4(.0) (cm$^{3}$) | C1 |
> |  | total volume = number of drops × (average) volume of one drop | C1 |
> | (c) | any **four** from:<br>• measure volume of water (in a measuring cylinder)<br>• add metal to water in the measuring cylinder<br>• so that metal is completely submerged<br>• measure (new) volume of water in a measuring cylinder (with metal)<br>• find the difference between the two volumes. | B4 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Most candidates gave the correct answer of 21 cm$^{3}$.
>
> **(b)** This calculation was done well by many candidates who gave the correct answer of 0.2 cm$^{3}$. With error carried forward from the previous question many candidates gained full credit. Weaker candidates did not read the question carefully and divided 25 cm$^{3}$ by 20.
>
> **(c)** Responses to this question were generally good. Some weaker candidates just stated “take the reading on the measuring cylinder” when they needed to say that they were measuring a volume.
