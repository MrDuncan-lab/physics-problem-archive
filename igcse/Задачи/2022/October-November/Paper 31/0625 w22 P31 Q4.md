# Question 4 (8 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2022 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 1 · вопрос 4  
Источник: [[_pdf/2022/0625_w22_qp_31.pdf#page=7|условие, стр. 7]] · [[_pdf/2022/0625_w22_ms_31.pdf#page=8|схема оценивания, стр. 8]] · [[_pdf/2022/0625_w22_er.pdf#page=16|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w22 P31]]  
Темы: [[1.3 Mass and weight]], [[1.7.1 Energy]], [[1.8 Pressure]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать вес тела и давление стола на пол, связать работу подъёма с приростом потенциальной энергии.

**(a)** A student has an object with a mass of 5.0 kg.

Calculate the weight of the object.

weight of object =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ N **[2]**

**(b)** The student lifts the 5.0 kg object from the floor onto a table. He does 75 J of work on the object in lifting it onto the table.

State the amount of gravitational potential energy gained by the object due to being lifted onto the table.

gravitational potential energy gained by object =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ J **[1]**

**(c)** The weight of a table is 280 N. The table has four legs. The area of each table leg in contact with the floor is 18 cm$^{2}$.

Calculate the pressure of the table on the floor. Give the correct unit.

pressure on the floor =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ unit  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[5]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** (weight =) 50 (N)
>
> **(c)** 3.9

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | (weight =) 50 (N) | A2 |
> |  | (weight =) mass × *g* **OR** 5 × 10 | C1 |
> | (b) | 75 (J) | B1 |
> | (c) | 3.9 | A4 |
> |  | 280 / 72 | C3 |
> |  | (*P* =) *F* / *A* **OR** (pressure =) force / area | C1 |
> |  | (area = 4 × 18 =) 72 (cm$^{2}$) | C1 |
> |  | N / cm$^{2}$ | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** The majority of candidates calculated the weight in newtons correctly, but a significant divided by 10 or multiplied by 1000.
>
> **(b)** Only stronger candidates answered this correctly. Most candidates did not realise that the work done on the mass was equal to its increase in gravitational potential energy.
>
> **(c)** This calculation was done well by the majority of candidates who evaluated P = F/A to give the correct answer. Only a few candidates used an incorrect rearrangement of the equation. Many candidates gave the correct unit but a significant number gave the unit as Pascals (Pa).
