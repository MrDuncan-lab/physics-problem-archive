> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить изменение капилляра, повышающее чувствительность жидкостного термометра.

The diagram shows a liquid-in-glass thermometer.

![[0625-w22-21-p6-f2.svg]]

Which change increases the sensitivity of the thermometer?

**A** a narrower capillary tube  
**B** a wider capillary tube  
**C** thicker glass around the bulb  
**D** thinner glass around the bulb

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The sensitivity of a liquid-in-glass thermometer is a measure of how far the thread moves along the capillary tube for a given change of temperature irrespective of how long it takes to do so. Many candidates thought that sensitivity was a question of rapidity of response.
