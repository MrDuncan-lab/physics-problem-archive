> Рассчитать превышение давления газа над атмосферным по водяному манометру.

A manometer containing water is used to measure the pressure of a gas supply, as shown.

![[0625-w22-21-p5-f1.svg]]

The density of water is 1000 kg / m$^{3}$.

What is the pressure of the gas supply?

**A** 300 Pa above atmospheric pressure  
**B** 400 Pa above atmospheric pressure  
**C** 3000 Pa above atmospheric pressure  
**D** 4000 Pa above atmospheric pressure

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
