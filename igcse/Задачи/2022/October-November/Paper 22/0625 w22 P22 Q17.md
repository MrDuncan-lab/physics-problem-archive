> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить изменение частоты и длины звуковой волны при переходе из воздуха в воду.

A sound wave travels from air into water.

Which row describes what happens to the frequency and the wavelength of the wave?

|  | frequency | wavelength |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | decreases | increases |
| **B** | decreases | stays the same |
| **C** | stays the same | decreases |
| **D** | stays the same | increases |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Candidates are expected to know that sound travels more quickly in water than in air, so that, given the constant frequency, the wavelength must increase. Only the top third were able to deal with this.
