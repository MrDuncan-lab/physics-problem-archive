> Связать расположение сгущений и разрежений воздуха с направлением распространения звуковой волны.

The diagram shows the air molecules in part of a sound wave at a particular moment in time.

![[0625-w22-22-p10-f3.svg]]

Which statement is **not** correct?

**A** Earlier, there was compression at X.  
**B** Later, there will be a rarefaction at X.  
**C** This part of the wave is travelling horizontally across the page.  
**D** This part of the wave is travelling towards the top of the page.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
