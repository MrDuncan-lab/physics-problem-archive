> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнить время прихода света и звука от стартового выстрела.

A 100 m race is started by firing a gun. The gun makes a bang and a puff of smoke at the same

time.

![[0625-w22-22-p11-f1.svg]]

When does the finishing judge see the smoke and when does he hear the bang?

|  | sees the smoke | hears the bang |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | almost immediately | almost immediately |
| **B** | almost immediately | after about 0.3 s |
| **C** | after about 0.3 s | almost immediately |
| **D** | after about 0.3 s | after about 0.3 s |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Nearly all candidates were clear that the two signals arrive at different times, but they were equally divided about which arrives first.
