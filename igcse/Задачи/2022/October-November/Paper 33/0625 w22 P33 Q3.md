**(i)** Complete the sentences about energy transfers.

**1.** When the player swings the tennis racket, his body converts  \_\_\_\_\_\_

energy to  \_\_\_\_\_\_ energy. **[1]**

**2.** When the tennis ball is moving upwards, the ball gains  \_\_\_\_\_\_ energy. **[1]**

**(ii)** Explain why the height gained by the ball decreases with each successive bounce. **[1]**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Many candidates gained some credit here. A common misconception was that wind is the input energy for a wind turbine instead of kinetic.
