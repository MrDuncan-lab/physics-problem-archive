> Определить верное утверждение о силах и моментах для уравновешенного стержня.

The diagram shows a uniform rod with its midpoint on a pivot. Two weights X and Y are hung from the rod. The rod is in equilibrium.

![[0625-w22-11-p4-f3.svg]]

Which statement is correct?

**A** The forces at X and Y have different values.  
**B** There is a resultant turning effect on the rod.  
**C** The resultant force on the rod is zero.  
**D** The rod does not have a centre of mass.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
