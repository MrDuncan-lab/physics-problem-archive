> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить изменение выходного напряжения делителя и яркости лампы при перемещении ползунка.

The circuit diagram shows a variable potential divider.

![[0625-w22-11-p12-f1.svg]]

The slider is moved from P towards Q.

What happens to the reading on the voltmeter and to the brightness of the lamp?

|  | reading on voltmeter | brightness of lamp |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | decreases | decreases |
| **B** | decreases | increases |
| **C** | increases | decreases |
| **D** | increases | increases |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Section 4.3.3 of the syllabus asks candidates to describe the action of a variable potential divider. However, many candidates thought that an increased p.d. across the lamp would result in less brightness or vice versa (options **B** and **C**).
