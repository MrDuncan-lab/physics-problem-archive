> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объяснить назначение предохранителя в электрической цепи.

Why is a fuse used in an electric circuit?

**A** to increase the circuit resistance  
**B** to prevent short circuits  
**C** to reduce the power loss  
**D** to stop the cables from overheating

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> There is a difference between preventing a short circuit and protecting against the consequences of a short circuit, so option **B** was incorrect. Fuses generally prevent too high a current in the next stretch of cable, so option **D** was the right answer.
