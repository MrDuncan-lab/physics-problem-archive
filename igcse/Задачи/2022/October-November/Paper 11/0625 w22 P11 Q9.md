> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Указать недостаток атомных электростанций как источника энергии.

What is a disadvantage of nuclear fission as a source of energy?

**A** Nuclear power stations are expensive to build.  
**B** Nuclear power stations are unreliable.  
**C** Nuclear power stations can only provide small quantities of energy.  
**D** Nuclear power stations release large quantities of carbon dioxide into the atmosphere.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The most popular response was **D**, that nuclear power stations release large quantities of carbon dioxide. Nuclear power may have many disadvantages, but the overwhelming point in its favour is precisely that it doesn’t release carbon dioxide.
