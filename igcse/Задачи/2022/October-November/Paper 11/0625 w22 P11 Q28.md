> Определить направления сил взаимодействия противоположно заряженных стержней.

A negatively charged plastic rod P is placed above a positively charged plastic rod Q.

![[0625-w22-11-p11-f2.svg]]

![[0625-w22-11-p11-f3.svg]]

What are the directions of the electrostatic forces on rod P and on rod Q?

|  | electrostatic force on rod P | electrostatic force on rod Q |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | downwards | downwards |
| **B** | downwards | upwards |
| **C** | upwards | downwards |
| **D** | upwards | upwards |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
