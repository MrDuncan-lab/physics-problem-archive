> Определить массу и вес камня по показанию весов.

A stone is placed on a balance as shown.

![[0625-w22-11-p3-f1.png]]

Which row gives the mass and weight of the stone?

|  | mass | weight |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | 120 g | 1.2 N |
| **B** | 120 g | 1200 N |
| **C** | 1.2 N | 120 g |
| **D** | 1200 N | 120 g |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Nearly all candidates recognised the units of mass and weight, but fewer candidates knew that the conversion factor is 10 N / kg rather than 10 N / g.
