> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Распознать углы падения и отражения луча от зеркала.

A ray of light is shone onto the surface of a mirror.

![[0625-w22-11-p9-f1.svg]]

Which two angles represent the angle of incidence and the angle of reflection?

**A** 1 and 2  
**B** 1 and 4  
**C** 2 and 3  
**D** 3 and 4

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Option **B** was commonly selected by the weakest candidates but was incorrect.
