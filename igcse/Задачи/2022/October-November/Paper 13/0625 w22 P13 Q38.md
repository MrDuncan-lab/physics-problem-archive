> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Распознать описание превращения элемента при альфа-распаде.

In the processes shown, X and Y are elements.

Which process describes α-decay?

**A** Atoms of X collide with atoms of Y.  
**B** Atoms of X emit atoms of Y.  
**C** Atoms of X change into ions of Y.  
**D** Atoms of X absorb α-particles.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Alpha decay involves the emission of two protons and two neutrons from the nucleus, leaving the previously neutral atom with two more electrons than it now needs. Therefore the atom has become a negative ion. **C** is the correct response.
