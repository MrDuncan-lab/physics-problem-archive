# Question 18 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2022 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 3 · вопрос 18  
Источник: [[_pdf/2022/0625_w22_qp_13.pdf#page=8|условие, стр. 8]] · [[_pdf/2022/0625_w22_ms_13.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2022/0625_w22_er.pdf#page=7|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w22 P13]]  
Темы: [[3.1 General properties of waves]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Указать изменение скорости волны при переходе между средами как условие преломления.

Wavefronts are incident on a boundary.

What is needed for the wave to refract at the boundary?

**A** a shiny surface at the boundary  
**B** a small gap in the boundary  
**C** different mediums either side of the boundary in which the frequency of the wave is different  
**D** different mediums either side of the boundary in which the speed of the wave is different

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Syllabus statement 3.1 specifies that candidates should be able to describe how waves can undergo refraction due to a change of speed. Some stronger candidates managed this, while other candidates did not recognise that there is no change of frequency involved. Weaker candidates were unable to display any understanding of the causes of refraction.
