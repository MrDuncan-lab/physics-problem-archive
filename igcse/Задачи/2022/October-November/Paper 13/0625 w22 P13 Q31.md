> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбрать освещённость фоторезистора и температуру термистора для максимальной яркости последовательной лампы.

The diagram shows a series circuit containing a fixed voltage power supply, a thermistor, a light-dependent resistor (LDR) and a lamp.

![[0625-w22-13-p14-f1.svg]]

Which set of conditions makes the lamp brightest?

|  | light intensity shining on LDR | temperature of thermistor |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | bright | high |
| **B** | bright | low |
| **C** | dim | high |
| **D** | dim | low |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> For the lamp to be bright, the resistances of the LDR and the thermistor should be as low as possible, which involves the LDR being in bright light and the thermistor being hot.
