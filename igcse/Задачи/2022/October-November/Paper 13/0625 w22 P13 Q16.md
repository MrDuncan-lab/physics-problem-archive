> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Распознать электрический нагреватель как источник теплового электромагнитного излучения.

Which piece of equipment is designed to produce a type of electromagnetic wave?

**A** electric fire  
**B** electric generator  
**C** electric motor  
**D** electromagnet

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Most candidates suggested that an electromagnet produces an electromagnetic wave whereas it actually produces a magnetic field, which is not a wave. Candidates needed to ask themselves just which part of the electromagnetic spectrum each device could produce before deciding on their response. It is the electric fire that produces infra-red radiation.
