# Question 10 (8 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2022 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 2 · вопрос 10  
Источник: [[_pdf/2022/0625_w22_qp_42.pdf#page=15|условие, стр. 15]] · [[_pdf/2022/0625_w22_ms_42.pdf#page=15|схема оценивания, стр. 15]] · [[_pdf/2022/0625_w22_er.pdf#page=31|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w22 P42]]  
Темы: [[5.2.2 The three types of nuclear emission]], [[5.2.3 Radioactive decay]], [[5.2.4 Half-life]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить состав и заряд альфа-частицы, записать бета-распад радия и рассчитать оставшуюся массу.

**(a)** The magnitude of the charge on a β (beta)-particle is 1.6 × 10$^{\text{–19}}$ C.

**(i)** State the proton number and nucleon number of an α (alpha)-particle.

proton number  \_\_\_\_\_\_

nucleon number  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(ii)** Determine the magnitude of the charge of an α (alpha)-particle.

charge  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(b)** A nucleus of radium-230 consists of 88 protons and 142 neutrons. Radium-230 is radioactive and decays by β (beta)-emission to an isotope of actinium. The symbol for radium is Ra and the symbol for actinium is Ac.

Write down the nuclide equation for this decay. **[3]**

**(c)** The half-life of radium-230 is 93 min. A sample contains 9.6 × 10$^{\text{–12}}$ g of radium-230.

Calculate the mass of radium in the sample after 279 min.

mass =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** $^{230}_{88}$Ra →$^{230}_{89}$Ac +$_{\text{–1}}^{0}$β
>
> **(c)** (mass =) 1.2 × 10$^{\text{–12}}$ g

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | (proton number) 2 | B1 |
> |  | (nucleon number) 4 | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | 3.2 × 10$^{\text{–19}}$ (C) | B1 |
> | (b) | $^{230}_{88}$Ra →$^{230}_{89}$Ac +$_{\text{–1}}^{0}$β | A3 |
> |  | *any two from:*<br>• nucleon numbers 230 on left **AND** 230 on right<br>• Ra and proton number 88 on left **AND** Ac and proton number 89 on right<br>• $_{\text{–1}}^{0}$β. | C2 |
> | (c) | (mass =) 1.2 × 10$^{\text{–12}}$ g | A2 |
> |  | 3 half-lives **OR** 9.6 × 10$^{\text{–12}}$ / 8 **OR** 9.6 × 10$^{\text{–12}}$ / 2$^{3}$ | C1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** Most candidates correctly stated the proton and nucleon number of an alpha particle. Weaker candidates gave a variety of incorrect responses with a few reversing the proton and nucleon numbers.
>
> **(ii)** The question provided the magnitude of the charge on a beta particle. Candidates needed to realise that the charge on an alpha particle is twice the magnitude of the charge on a beta particle. Most candidates ignored the information in the question and simply put that the magnitude of the charge on an alpha particle is 2.
>
> **(b)** Many candidates gave a fully correct response to this question with many more gaining partial credit. Common mistakes included using neutron, instead of nucleon numbers, or placing the beta particle on the wrong side of the nuclide equation. Some candidates made no attempt at this question.
>
> **(c)** Most candidates correctly deduced that 3 half-lives had elapsed and used this to calculate the correct value of mass of radium in the sample after 279 minutes. The unit of mass was missing in several answers. Weaker candidates divided by 3 rather than by 2$^{3}$ to find the mass remaining.
