> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбрать делитель с увеличением выходного напряжения при усилении освещения.

Each potential divider is placed in a circuit with a power supply.

Which potential divider makes the potential difference (p.d.) across component Y increase when the light intensity increases?

![[0625-w22-23-p12-f1.svg]]

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Most candidates correctly identified the circuit symbol for an LDR, but not all of them could work out that increasing the light intensity decreases the LDR’s resistance, so that the LDR has a lower proportion of the total resistance and therefore a lower proportion of the total p.d. across it.
