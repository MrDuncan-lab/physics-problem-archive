> Выбрать линейку и микрометр для точного измерения длины и диаметра проволоки.

A wire is approximately 48 cm long and has an approximate diameter of 0.3 mm.

Which measuring instruments can be used to obtain more precise values of the dimensions of the wire?

|  | length of the wire | diameter of the wire |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | 30 cm ruler | micrometer |
| **B** | half-metre rule | 30 cm rule |
| **C** | half-metre rule | micrometer |
| **D** | micrometer | half-metre rule |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
