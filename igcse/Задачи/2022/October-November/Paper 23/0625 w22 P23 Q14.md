> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбрать изменение конструкции жидкостного термометра для повышения чувствительности.

Which change in the design of a liquid-in-glass thermometer makes it more sensitive?

**A** a larger liquid reservoir  
**B** a longer tube  
**C** a smaller liquid reservoir  
**D** a wider tube

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Many candidates did not appear to realise that a sensitive liquid-in-glass thermometer is one that gives a large movement of the position of the liquid regardless of how long that takes or how much thermal energy is required to achieve it. Option **C** is incorrect because a small volume of liquid in the bulb will only be able to produce a small extra volume of liquid in the capillary tube when heated.
