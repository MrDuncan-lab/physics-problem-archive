> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбрать уравнение, связывающее импульс силы с изменением импульса тела.

A force *F* acts on a body of mass *m* for a time *t*. During this time, the velocity of the body increases from *u* to *v*.

Which equation relates *F*, *m*, *t*, *u* and *v* ?

**A** *Fm* = *t*(*v* – *u*)  
**B** *Fm* = *t*(*v* + *u*)  
**C** *Ft* = *m*(*v* – *u*)  
**D** *Ft* = *m*(*v* + *u*)

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Some candidates chose **A**, when at this level they might be expected to see that the (time × a speed) is a distance, which (force × mass) certainly is not.
