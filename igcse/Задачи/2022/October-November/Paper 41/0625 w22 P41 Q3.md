# Question 3 (8 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2022 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 1 · вопрос 3

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать вес и момент силы тяжести альпинистки, определить натяжение наклонной верёвки из условий равновесия.

A rock climber, of total mass 62 kg, holds herself in horizontal equilibrium against a vertical cliff. She pulls on a rope that is fixed at the top of the cliff and presses her feet against the cliff.

Fig. 3.1 shows her position.

![[0625-w22-41-p6-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 3.1** (not to scale)

**(a)** Calculate the total weight of the climber.

weight =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(b)** State the **two** conditions needed for equilibrium.

1 \_\_\_\_\_\_

2 \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(c)** The climber’s centre of mass is 0.90 m from the cliff.

**(i)** Calculate the moment about her feet due to her weight.

moment =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(ii)** The line of the rope meets the horizontal line through her centre of mass at a distance of 1.2 m from the cliff, as shown in Fig. 3.1. The rope is at an angle of 60° to the horizontal.

Determine the tension in the rope.

tension =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(c)(i)** 560 N m
>
> **(c)(ii)** 540 N

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | 620 N | B1 |
> | (b) |  | B2 |
> |  | no resultant force (on object in equilibrium) | B1 |
> |  | no resultant moment (on object in equilibrium) | B1 |
> | (c)(i) | 560 N m | A2 |
> |  | (Γ =) *Fx*$_{\text{┴r}}$ **or** 620 × 0.90 | C1 |
> | (c)(ii) | 540 N | A3 |
> |  | use of any moment | C1 |
> |  | *T* × 1.2 sin 60° (= 560) **or** (*T* =) 560 / (1.2 × sin 60°) | C1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** This was very often correct, but some candidates incorrectly gave 62 kg 620 J or another incorrect answer.
>
> **(b)** Many candidates were able to give at least one appropriate condition for equilibrium, but fewer gave two. Some incorrect answers referred to the centre of gravity being with the base area or made reference to some property of the climber.
>
> **(c) (i)** This calculation was often correct, but some candidates used an incorrect distance or unit.
>
> **(ii)** Many candidates found this question challenging and some made no attempt at it. Candidates who attempted to balance the moments often made some progress but only the strongest candidates supplied a correct final answer.
