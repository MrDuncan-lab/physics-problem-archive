> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить, на какой мяч действует ненулевая равнодействующая сила.

On which ball is a non-zero resultant force acting?

![[0625-w22-12-q6-options.svg]]

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> If the velocity of a body is changing, then it has a non-zero resultant force acting on it, and vice versa. Weaker candidates appear to find this very difficult to understand. Many candidates selected option **B** but the ball is at rest, so its velocity is not changing, and it must have no resultant force acting on it.
