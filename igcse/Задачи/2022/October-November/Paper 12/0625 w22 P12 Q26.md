> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать сопротивление лампы по напряжению и току.

The diagram shows a circuit used to control the potential difference (p.d.) across a lamp.

The variable resistor is adjusted until the p.d. across the lamp is 6.0 V.

The current in the lamp is 0.5 A.

![[0625-w22-12-p12-f1.svg]]

What is the resistance of the lamp?

**A** 0.083 Ω  
**B** 3.0 Ω  
**C** 6.5 Ω  
**D** 12.0 Ω

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> A significant proportion of candidates attempted to calculate the resistance by multiplying the current and pd. together.
