> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Распознать природные источники радиационного фона.

Everyone is exposed to background radiation.

What are sources of background radiation?

**A** food and drink only  
**B** rocks only  
**C** cosmic rays only  
**D** food and drink, rocks and cosmic rays

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The most popular response was to state that background radiation originates solely in outer space, instead of from many different sources in the surroundings.
