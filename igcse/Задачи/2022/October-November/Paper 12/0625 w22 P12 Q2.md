> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнить пройденные расстояния по площадям под графиками скорости.

The diagrams show speed–time graphs for four different bodies moving for 6.0 s.

Which body travelled the least distance?

![[0625-w22-12-p2-f1.svg]]

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question relied on knowing that the distance travelled is the (scaled) area under the graph. Some candidates chose **C.** One way of approaching the question was to evaluate all the areas, and then pick the lowest. Another was to cut triangles from the areas and then reconstruct the areas as rectangles. For example, in **B**, cutting off the triangle above the 4 m / s line gives a triangle which will fit neatly upside down between the 2 s and 4 s marks, leaving a 4 m / s × 4 s square plus a triangle, which is obviously smaller than the 4 m / s × 6 s rectangle obtained by carrying out the same procedure on **A**.
