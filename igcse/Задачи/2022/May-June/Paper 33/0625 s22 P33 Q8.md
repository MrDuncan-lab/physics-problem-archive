**(a)** The device in Fig. 8.1 is connected to a 240 V mains supply.

The device produces a potential difference (p.d.) of 12 V between A and B.

A 12 V lamp is connected between A and B.

**Fig. 8.1**

**(i)** Calculate the number of turns on the secondary coil. Use information from Fig. 8.1.

number of turns =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

**(ii)** State the name of the device shown in Fig. 8.1. **[1]**

**(iii)** The device includes two coils.

State the material used for the coils. **[1]**

**(b)** The 12 V lamp is disconnected.

Two 6.0 V lamps and a 12 V motor are connected between A and B. The p.d. across each lamp is 6.0 V and the p.d. across the motor is 12 V.

Draw on Fig. 8.2 to show how to connect the lamps and the motor between A and B.

![[0625-s22-33-p13-f2.svg]]

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)

>
> **(b)** 2 lamps in series connected between A and B
