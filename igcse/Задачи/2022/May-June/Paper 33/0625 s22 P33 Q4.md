**(a)** State and explain **one** application of thermal expansion.

You may draw a diagram to support your answer. **[2]**

**(b)** Fig. 4.1 shows a flask with air trapped inside it.

![[0625-s22-33-p7-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 4.1**

Initially, the flask and air are at room temperature. The flask is then cooled in a refrigerator.

**(i)** State the change in the pressure of the air in the flask as the air cools. **[1]**

**(ii)** Explain your answer to **(b)(i)**. Use your ideas about air molecules. **[3]**
