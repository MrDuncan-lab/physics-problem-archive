# Question 1 (9 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2022 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 3 · вопрос 1  
Источник: [[_pdf/2022/0625_s22_qp_33.pdf#page=2|условие, стр. 2–3]] · [[_pdf/2022/0625_s22_ms_33.pdf#page=8|схема оценивания, стр. 8]] · [[_pdf/2022/0625_s22_er.pdf#page=22|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

Темы: [[1.1 Physical quantities and measurement techniques]], [[1.2 Motion]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбор прибора для измерения дистанции, построение графика скорости бегуна и расчёт средней скорости.

Fig. 1.1 shows children about to run a race. They have to run 25 m, pick up a small plastic ring and run back to the base line. Each child finishes when they cross the base line holding the plastic ring.

![[0625-s22-33-p2-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 1.1**

**(a)** **(i)** Suggest what equipment the teacher uses to measure the length of 25 m. **[1]**

**(ii)** Determine the total distance for the race.

distance =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ m **[1]**

**(b)** The teacher records the following information for **one** of the children.

The child starts to run at time = 0. The child picks up the ring at time = 9.0 s. The child finishes the race at time = 17.0 s. The highest speed occurs as the child finishes the race.

Using this information, sketch a speed–time graph on Fig. 1.2, suggesting how the speed of this child varies during the race.

![[0625-s22-33-p3-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 1.2** **[3]**

**(c)** In a different race, a child runs 500 m in 4 minutes and 20 seconds.

**(i)** Determine how many seconds there are in 4 minutes and 20 seconds.

time =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ s **[1]**

**(ii)** Calculate the average speed of the child.

average speed =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ m / s **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(c)(ii)** 1.9 (m/s)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | metre rule | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | 50 (m) | B1 |
> | (b) | graph starts at origin | B1 |
> |  | speed = 0 at 9.0 s | B1 |
> |  | highest speed at 17 s | B1 |
> | (c)(i) | 260 (s) | B1 |
> | (c)(ii) | 1.9 (m/s) | A3 |
> |  | 500 ÷ 260 OR 500 ÷ **(c)(i)** | (C2) |
> |  | (speed = ) distance ÷ time in any form | (C1) |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** This question was answered well. A common incorrect answer was to state the unit, often metre, rather than the measuring equipment.
>
> **(ii)** The majority of candidates gave the correct answer to this question.
>
> **(b)** Most candidates were aware that the speed of the child was zero at the beginning and that the highest speed achieved was near the end of the race. It was not fully understood that the slowest speed (probably equalling zero) would be when the child picked up the ring.
>
> **(c) (i)** Most candidates converted the time correctly.
>
> **(ii)** The majority of candidates gave a correct answer, with many also showing their working.
