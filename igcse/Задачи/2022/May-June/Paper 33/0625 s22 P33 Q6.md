> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определение критического угла и построение преломлённых и полностью отражённых лучей на границе воды и воздуха.

A diver is swimming under water. She uses a torch emitting red light. Fig. 6.1 shows three rays of red light coming from the torch.

![[0625-s22-33-p10-f1.png]]

**Fig. 6.1**

**(a)** State the name of the dashed lines in Fig. 6.1. **[1]**

**(b)** The critical angle for red light travelling from water into air is 48°.

**(i)** State the meaning of the term critical angle. **[2]**

**(ii)** On Fig. 6.1, draw the path of each ray after it reaches the water–air boundary. **[3]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | normal(s) | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | angle of incidence | B1 |
> |  | that gives an angle of refraction of 90° | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | 20° – emergent ray is refracted in air | B1 |
> |  | 40° – emergent ray is refracted in air AND greater refraction | B1 |
> |  | 60° – ray is reflected internally | B1 |
