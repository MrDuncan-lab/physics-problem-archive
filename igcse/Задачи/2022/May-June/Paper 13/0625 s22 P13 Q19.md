> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбор поверхности, лучше всего испускающей тепловое излучение.

A metal container is the shape of a hollow cube.

The four sides of the container have different surface finishes.

Which side is the best emitter of radiation?

![[0625-s22-13-p9-f4.svg]]

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Familiarity with the basic facts was very limited. Dull surfaces are better absorbers and better emitters of radiation than shiny surfaces and black surfaces are better absorbers and emitters of radiation than light surfaces.
