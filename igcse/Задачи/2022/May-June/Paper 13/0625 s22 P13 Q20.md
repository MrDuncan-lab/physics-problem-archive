> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определение амплитуды и длины волны по рисунку.

The diagram shows a wave.

![[0625-s22-13-p10-f1.svg]]

Which row is correct?

|  | amplitude of the wave / cm | wavelength of the wave / cm |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | 1.0 | 4.0 |
| **B** | 1.0 | 8.0 |
| **C** | 2.0 | 4.0 |
| **D** | 2.0 | 8.0 |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Although most candidates correctly identified the amplitude of the wave as being 1.0 cm, very few then went on to realise that the wavelength was 8.0 cm, the distance between identical point on successive waves.
