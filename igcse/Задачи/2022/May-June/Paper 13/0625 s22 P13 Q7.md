bar 1 bar 2

![[0625-s22-13-p5-f1.svg]]

Which bars are in equilibrium?

**A** bar 1 and bar 2  
**B** bar 1 only  
**C** bar 2 only  
**D** neither bar 1 nor bar 2

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
