> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определение постоянства ускорения и пройденного пути по графику движения автомобиля.

The graph shows the motion of a car.

![[0625-s22-13-p2-f2.svg]]

Which row correctly describes this motion?

|  | acceleration | distance travelled / m |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | constant | 200 |
| **B** | constant | 300 |
| **C** | increasing at a constant rate | 200 |
| **D** | increasing at a constant rate | 300 |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Few candidates recognised that the speed was increasing at a constant rate and that therefore, the car was moving with constant acceleration.
