> Выбор условий получения постоянного магнита ударами по металлическому стержню в магнитном поле.

A student attempts to make a permanent magnet by hammering metal bars of the same size in

the same magnetic field.

In which case is the strongest permanent magnet produced?

![[0625-s22-13-p12-f1.svg]]

![[0625-s22-13-p12-f2.svg]]

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Candidates should be familiar with this method of making a magnet. They also needed to be aware that iron does not retain its magnetism but steel does. Thus, steel makes the stronger magnet.
