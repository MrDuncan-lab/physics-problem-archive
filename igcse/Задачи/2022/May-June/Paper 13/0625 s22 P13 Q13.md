> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Распознавание агрегатных состояний по расположению и движению молекул.

The properties of two states of matter are listed.

state 1 The molecules move quickly and randomly. There is a large distance between

the molecules.

state 2 The molecules vibrate about fixed positions. The molecules are closely packed

together.

What are states 1 and 2?

|  | state 1 | state 2 |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | gas | liquid |
| **B** | liquid | solid |
| **C** | solid | liquid |
| **D** | gas | solid |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Almost all candidates were familiar with the properties of different states of matter.
