> Определение магнитных свойств предмета и сравнение силы магнитов по показаниям весов.

A student places object X on a balance. The student first brings magnet Y and then magnet Z close to object X and observes the readings on the balance. The distance between Y and X is the same as the distance between Z and X.

The diagram shows the results of the experiment.

![[0625-s22-13-p12-f3.svg]]

![[0625-s22-13-p12-f4.png]]

Which statement explains the results?

**A** Object X is an iron block and magnet Y is stronger than magnet Z.  
**B** Object X is an iron block and magnet Y is weaker than magnet Z.  
**C** Object X is a permanent magnet and magnet Y is stronger than magnet Z.  
**D** Object X is a permanent magnet and magnet Y is weaker than magnet Z.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
