> Выбор правильных единиц измерения массы и веса.

Four students are given two different objects, P and Q.

Each student measures the mass of P and the weight of Q.

The results are shown in the table.

Which row gives a possible result?

|  | mass of object P | weight of object Q |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | 10 kg | 10 kg |
| **B** | 10 kg | 10 N |
| **C** | 10 N | 10 kg |
| **D** | 10 N | 10 N |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
