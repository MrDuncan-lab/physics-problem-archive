# Question 25 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2022 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 3 · вопрос 25

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Расчёт скорости света в прозрачном веществе по критическому углу.

The speed of light in air is 3.0 × 10$^{8}$ m / s.

The critical angle for light in a transparent plastic material placed in air is 37°.

What is the speed of light in the plastic material?

**A** 1.8 × 10$^{8}$ m / s  
**B** 2.4 × 10$^{8}$ m / s  
**C** 3.8 × 10$^{8}$ m / s  
**D** 5.0 × 10$^{8}$ m / s

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This was a challenging question which required candidates to complete a two-stage calculation. First, to calculate the refractive index of the plastic from 1 / sin 37° = *n*, then to divide the speed of light in air (3.0 × 10$^{8}$) by the calculated refractive index. Only stronger candidates completed this successfully.
