**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2022 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 3 · вопрос 16

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбор нагревания и охлаждения деталей для соединения с использованием теплового расширения.

The iron cylinder of an engine is to be fitted into a piece of aluminium.

The outside diameter *M* of the iron cylinder is slightly larger than the diameter *N* of the hole in the aluminium.

![[0625-s22-23-p7-f1.svg]]

![[0625-s22-23-p7-f2.svg]]

What is the best action to fit the cylinder into the aluminium?

**A** Cool the aluminium and cool the iron.  
**B** Cool the aluminium and heat the iron.  
**C** Heat the aluminium and cool the iron.  
**D** Heat the aluminium and heat the iron.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
