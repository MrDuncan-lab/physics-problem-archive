> Сравнение инфракрасных волн с микроволнами по длине волны и применению.

A student compares the wavelength and uses of infrared waves with microwaves.

Which row is correct?

|  | wavelength of infrared compared to microwaves | use of infrared waves |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | longer | intruder alarms |
| **B** | longer | satellite television |
| **C** | shorter | intruder alarms |
| **D** | shorter | satellite television |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
