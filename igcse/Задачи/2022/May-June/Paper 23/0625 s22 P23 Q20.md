> Определение зависимости мощности теплового излучения от площади поверхности и температуры.

An object emits infrared radiation.

Which two properties of the object determine the rate of radiation of thermal energy from the object?

**A** the density and the surface area of the object  
**B** the density and the surface temperature of the object  
**C** the mass and the surface area of the object  
**D** the surface area and the surface temperature of the object

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
