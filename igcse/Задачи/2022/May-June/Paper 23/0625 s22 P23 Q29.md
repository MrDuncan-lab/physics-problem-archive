> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определение возможного заряда металлической сферы с учётом электростатической индукции.

A metal sphere R is suspended on an insulating thread.

![[0625-s22-23-p12-f1.svg]]

Another sphere S is brought close to sphere R. Sphere S has a negative charge and is attached to a plastic stand. The diagram shows the spheres when they are close to each other.

![[0625-s22-23-p12-f2.svg]]

Which charged state of R accounts for the behaviour in the diagram?

**A** negative only  
**B** positive only  
**C** negative or uncharged  
**D** positive or uncharged

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Few candidates recognised that uncharged objects are attracted to charged objects.
