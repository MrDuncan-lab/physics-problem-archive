> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Расчёт плотности жидкости по разности масс и объёмов.

A measuring cylinder contains 30 cm$^{3}$ of a liquid.

![[0625-s22-12-p3-f1.svg]]

Some more of the liquid is added until the liquid level reaches the 50 cm$^{3}$ mark.

The reading on the balance increases by 30 g.

What is the density of the liquid?

**A** 0.60 g / cm$^{3}$  
**B** 0.67 g / cm$^{3}$  
**C** 1.5 g / cm$^{3}$  
**D** 1.7 g / cm$^{3}$

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Most candidates recognised and used the formula density = mass / volume. However, few had the depth of understanding to use the raw information correctly. Instead of using the increase in the mass and increase in volume of the liquid, they used increase in the mass and the total volume of the liquid.
