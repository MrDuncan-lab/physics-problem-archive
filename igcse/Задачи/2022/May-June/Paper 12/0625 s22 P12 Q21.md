> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Расчёт частоты волн по числу полных колебаний за заданное время.

A girl is sitting on a rock in the sea looking at passing waves. She notices that five complete

wavelengths pass her in 20 s.

What is the frequency of this wave?

**A** 0.25 Hz  
**B** 4.0 Hz  
**C** 15 Hz  
**D** 100 Hz

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Many candidates showed a lack of understanding of the term “frequency”. These candidates divided the time taken (5 s) for the total number of oscillations (20) to be completed, whereas frequency is defined as the number of oscillations per unit time.
