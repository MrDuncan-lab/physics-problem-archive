# Question 1 (1 mark)

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определение объёма пластилина по вытесненной воде в измерительных цилиндрах.

A lump of modelling clay is moved from a small measuring cylinder to a large measuring cylinder that has twice the diameter.

![[0625-s22-12-p2-f1.svg]]

The reading on the small measuring cylinder goes down by 20 cm$^{3}$.

By how much does the reading on the large cylinder go up?

**A** 10 cm$^{3}$  
**B** 20 cm$^{3}$  
**C** 40 cm$^{3}$  
**D** 80 cm$^{3}$

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Many candidates did not recognise that the volume of the object was 20 cm$^{3}$ and that the cross section of the measuring cylinder was irrelevant to the problem.
