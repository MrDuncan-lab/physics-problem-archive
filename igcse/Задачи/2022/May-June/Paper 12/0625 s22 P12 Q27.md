> Определение магнитных свойств предметов по изображению линий магнитного поля.

The diagram shows the magnetic fields around three objects, P, Q and R, placed close to each other.

![[0625-s22-12-p11-f1.svg]]

Which row shows the nature of each of the objects?

|  | P | Q | R |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** **B** **C** **D** | permanent magnet N S permanent magnet N S permanent magnet N S permanent magnet N S | copper rod copper rod iron rod iron rod | permanent magnet N S permanent magnet S N permanent magnet N S permanent magnet S N |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> It was apparent that candidates did not understand that copper is not a magnetic metal and will not affect the shape of the magnetic field.
