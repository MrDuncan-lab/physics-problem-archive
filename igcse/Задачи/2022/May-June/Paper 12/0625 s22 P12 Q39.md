> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Расчёт изменения скорости счёта источника за несколько периодов полураспада.

A radioactive source has a half-life of 0.5 hours.

A detector near the source shows a reading of 6000 counts per second.

Background radiation can be ignored.

What is the reading on the detector 1.5 hours later?

**A** 750 counts per second  
**B** 1500 counts per second  
**C** 2000 counts per second  
**D** 3000 counts per second

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The use of half-lives to predict the amount of radiation from a source in the future was challenging for candidates.
