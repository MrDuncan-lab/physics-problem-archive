> Определение направления отклонения бета-частиц в магнитном поле.

The diagram shows a stream of β-particles travelling in a line that passes between the poles of a

magnet.

![[0625-s22-21-p16-f1.svg]]

![[0625-s22-21-p16-f2.svg]]

![[0625-s22-21-p16-f3.svg]]

In which direction will the β-particles be deflected by the magnet?

**A** towards the N pole  
**B** towards the S pole  
**C** into the page  
**D** out of the page

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> A significant number of candidates thought that the β-particles would be attracted to one of the magnetic poles.
