> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определение полярности соленоида при падении магнита по правилу Ленца.

A magnet is dropped vertically through a solenoid. This induces magnetic poles at both ends of

the solenoid.

![[0625-s22-21-p15-f1.svg]]

diagram 1 diagram 2 diagram 3

Which magnetic poles are induced at position X in diagram 1 and diagram 3?

|  | diagram 1 | diagram 3 |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | N pole | N pole |
| **B** | N pole | S pole |
| **C** | S pole | N pole |
| **D** | S pole | S pole |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question proved challenging for many candidates. It relied on understanding that an induced e.m.f. will oppose (or tend to oppose) the change causing it. In this case, the pole induced at the top of the coil will be an S pole as it repels the approaching S pole. In diagram 3, an S pole is induced at the bottom of the coil, which attracts the disappearing N pole, therefore at point X an N pole will be induced.
