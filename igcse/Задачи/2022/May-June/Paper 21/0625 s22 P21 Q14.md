# Question 14 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2022 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 1 · вопрос 14

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определение изменения скорости и среднего расстояния между молекулами при охлаждении газа постоянного объёма.

A sealed rigid container has a fixed volume. The container is filled with air.

The container is placed in a freezer cabinet and the temperature of the air in the container decreases.

Which row correctly describes what happens to the air in the container?

|  | average distance between air particles | average speed of air particles |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | decreases | increases |
| **B** | decreases | decreases |
| **C** | no change | increases |
| **D** | no change | decreases |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Many candidates found this question challenging and chose option **B**, indicating that they thought the molecules get closer together as the gas cools. However, the question clearly stated that the volume of the container is fixed and, therefore, the molecules average separation remains unchanged.
