> Объяснение отталкивания пластмассовых предметов наличием одноимённых зарядов.

A plastic rod is brought near to a small plastic sphere suspended from a stand. The sphere is

repelled by the rod.

Why is this?

**A** The rod and the sphere have like charges.  
**B** The rod and the sphere have unlike charges.  
**C** The rod is charged and the sphere is uncharged.  
**D** The rod is uncharged and the sphere is charged.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**
