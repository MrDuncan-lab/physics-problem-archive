# Question 9 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2022 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 1 · вопрос 9  
Источник: [[_pdf/2022/0625_s22_qp_21.pdf#page=4|условие, стр. 4]] · [[_pdf/2022/0625_s22_ms_21.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2022/0625_s22_er.pdf#page=8|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Распознавание ситуаций, в которых изменяется импульс тела.

Three situations are listed.

1 An object has a resultant force acting on it.

2 A moving object experiences an impulse.

3 An object is decelerating.

In which situations is the momentum of the object changing?

**A** 1 and 2 only  
**B** 1 and 3 only  
**C** 2 and 3 only  
**D** 1, 2 and 3

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question was challenging for some candidates. Many did not realise that when there is a resultant force on an object, the speed of the object will either increase or decrease, thereby altering its momentum.
