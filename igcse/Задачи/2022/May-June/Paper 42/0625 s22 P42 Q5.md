# Question 5 (7 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2022 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 2 · вопрос 5  
Источник: [[_pdf/2022/0625_s22_qp_42.pdf#page=7|условие, стр. 7]] · [[_pdf/2022/0625_s22_ms_42.pdf#page=8|схема оценивания, стр. 8]] · [[_pdf/2022/0625_s22_er.pdf#page=29|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 s22 P42]]  
Темы: [[3.4 Sound]], [[3.1 General properties of waves]], [[3.3 Electromagnetic spectrum]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Распознавание ультразвука по частоте, расчёт длины волны и описание медицинского применения рентгеновского излучения.

Sound waves are longitudinal and electromagnetic waves are transverse.

**(a)** A sound wave used for a medical examination has a frequency of 1.5 MHz.

**(i)** State and explain what type of sound wave this is. **[2]**

**(ii)** The wave travels through soft human tissue at a speed of 1.3 km / s.

Calculate the wavelength of the wave in soft human tissue.

wavelength =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

**(b)** Describe **one** use of X-rays in medicine. **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)(ii)** 8.7 × 10$^{\text{–4}}$ m

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | ultrasound OR sound (frequency) above audible range | B1 |
> |  | frequency \> 20 kHz OR 20 000 Hz | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | 8.7 × 10$^{\text{–4}}$ m | A3 |
> |  | (λ =) *v* / *f* OR *v* = *f*λ in any form | C1 |
> |  | (λ =) 1.3 × 10$^{3}$/ 1.5 × 10$^{6}$ OR 8.7 × 10$^{\text{n}}$ | C1 |
> | (b) | basic description of use e.g. X-rays for detecting broken bones | B1 |
> |  | additional detail e.g. X-rays pass through soft tissue AND not through bone | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** Stronger answers stated that the sound was ultrasound and that its frequency was above 20 000 Hz. Weaker answers only stated that the frequency was outside the audible range or stated that it was infrasound. Some candidates read this question as if it was asking about the nature of sound waves and described longitudinal waves. Other candidates stated that they were X rays or other forms of electromagnetic radiation.
>
> **(ii)** Most candidates gained partial credit for identifying that they needed to use the equation v = fλ. Further credit was awarded if candidates rearranged the equation correctly to obtain the answer. As the velocity was in km/s and the frequency in MHz, two correct changes of prefix were needed to obtain the correct answer. There were errors in significant figures and units.
>
> **(b)** Stronger candidates gave a correct description of one use of X-rays in medicine but did not always give sufficient detail to be awarded full credit. Vague answers of “for scanning” or “to examine bones” were not precise enough for credit.
