> Определение расположения положительного заряда в атоме.

A model of an atom consists of small particles orbiting a central nucleus.

Where is the positive charge in an atom?

**A** on the orbiting particles  
**B** in the nucleus  
**C** in the space between the nucleus and the orbiting particles  
**D** spread throughout the atom

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
