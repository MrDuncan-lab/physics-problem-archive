> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определение случайного характера распада отдельного радиоактивного ядра.

Which statement about the radioactive decay of a substance is correct?

**A** It cannot be predicted when a particular nucleus will decay.  
**B** Placing a radioactive substance inside a lead-lined box prevents it from decaying.  
**C** The decay always produces poisonous gases.  
**D** The rate of decay increases if the substance is dissolved in water.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The concept that the spontaneity of radioactive decay is subtle and was challenging for some candidates. However, stronger candidates answered this correctly.
