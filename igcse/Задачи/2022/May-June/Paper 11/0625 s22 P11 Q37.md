> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбор изменений, увеличивающих вращающий момент катушки электродвигателя.

A current-carrying coil in a magnetic field experiences a turning effect.

Three suggestions for increasing the turning effect are given.

1 Increase the number of turns on the coil.

2 Increase the current in the coil.

3 Increase the strength of the magnetic field.

Which suggestions are correct?

**A** 1 and 2 only  
**B** 1 and 3 only  
**C** 2 and 3 only  
**D** 1, 2 and 3

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question required a basic knowledge of the motor effect, but few candidates had this and all options were chosen.
