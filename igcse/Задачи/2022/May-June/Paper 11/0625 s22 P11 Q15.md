> Определение изменения объёма твёрдого тела и жидкости при нагревании.

What happens to the volumes of liquid metal and of solid metal when heated at constant

pressure?

|  | volume of liquid metal | volume of solid metal |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | decreases | increases |
| **B** | decreases | no change |
| **C** | increases | increases |
| **D** | increases | no change |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
