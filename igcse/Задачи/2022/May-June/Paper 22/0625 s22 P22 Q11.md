> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определение преобразований энергии падающего теннисного мяча при ударе о поверхность.

A tennis ball is dropped from position 1. It falls vertically onto a hard surface at position 2.

![[0625-s22-22-p5-f1.svg]]

Which energy changes have taken place between position 1 and position 2?

**A** gravitational potential → kinetic → chemical  
**B** gravitational potential → kinetic → elastic (strain)  
**C** kinetic → gravitational potential → chemical  
**D** kinetic → gravitational potential → elastic (strain)

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Most candidates recognised the energy changes that take place as ball falls to the Earth and as it is deformed when it hits the ground.
