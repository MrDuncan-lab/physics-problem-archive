> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Различение ядерного деления и синтеза по уравнениям реакций.

A scientist was asked to separate the following equations into two categories: nuclear fission and

nuclear fusion.

1 $^{239}_{94}$Pu →$^{112}_{46}$Pd + $^{124}_{48}$ Cd + 3$_{0}^{1}$n

2 $^{3}_{2}$He + $^{3}_{2}$He →$^{4}_{2}$He + $^{1}_{1}$H + $^{1}_{1}$H

3 $^{14}_{7}$N + $^{1}_{1}$H →$^{15}_{8}$ O

4 $^{235}_{92}$U →$^{90}_{36}$Kr + $^{143}_{56}$Ba + 2$_{0}^{1}$ n

Which equations show nuclear fission?

**A** 1 and 2  
**B** 1 and 3  
**C** 1 and 4  
**D** 2 and 4

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Most candidates correctly identified the examples of nuclear fission and showed a good understanding of the difference between nuclear fission and fusion.
