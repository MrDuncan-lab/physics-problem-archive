> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определение движения магнита, воспроизводящего известное направление отклонения гальванометра.

A coil XY is wound around a cardboard tube.

When the S pole of a magnet is pushed into the coil XY, the galvanometer deflects to the left.

![[0625-s22-22-p16-f1.svg]]

What other movement of the magnet will produce a deflection to the left?

![[0625-s22-22-q35-options.svg]]

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question was only answered correctly by stronger candidates. The easiest way to identify the directions of the deflections is to determine induced polarity of each coil in each response and compare it with the initial example.
