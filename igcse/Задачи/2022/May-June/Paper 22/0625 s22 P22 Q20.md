> Сравнение мощности теплового излучения сосудов по площади поверхности и температуре.

Four cubic copper containers are filled with water. The surfaces of all the containers are painted

black.

Two of the containers have sides of length 20 cm and the other two containers have sides of length 40 cm.

Two of the containers contain water at 80 °C and the other two contain water at 30 °C.

Which container radiates energy at the lowest rate?
