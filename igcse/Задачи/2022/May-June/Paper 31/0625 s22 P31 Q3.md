# Question 3 (7 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2022 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 1 · вопрос 3  
Источник: [[_pdf/2022/0625_s22_qp_31.pdf#page=6|условие, стр. 6]] · [[_pdf/2022/0625_s22_ms_31.pdf#page=8|схема оценивания, стр. 8]] · [[_pdf/2022/0625_s22_er.pdf#page=14|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Расчёт веса блока и давления на опору по силе и площади контакта.

**(a)** Fig. 3.1 shows a metal block and its dimensions.

![[0625-s22-31-p6-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 3.1** (not to scale)

**(i)** Calculate the area of the metal block in contact with the ground.

area =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm$^{2}$ **[2]**

**(ii)** The mass of the metal block is 0.84 kg.

Calculate the weight of the metal block.

weight =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ N **[2]**

**(b)** A different metal block has a weight of 24 N. The area of this metal block in contact with the ground is 4.0 cm$^{2}$.

Calculate the pressure of this block on the ground.

pressure =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ N / cm$^{2}$ **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)(i)** 24 (cm$^{2}$)
>
> **(a)(ii)** (weight =) 8.4 (N)
>
> **(b)** (pressure =) 6(.0) (N/cm$^{2}$)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | 24 (cm$^{2}$) | A2 |
> |  | (area in contact with ground) =length × width OR 12 × 2(.0) | (C1) |
> | (a)(ii) | (weight =) 8.4 (N) | A2 |
> |  | (weight =) mass × g OR 0.84 × 10 | (C1) |
> | (b) | (pressure =) 6(.0) (N/cm$^{2}$) | A3 |
> |  | (pressure =) 24 ÷ 4(.0) | (C2) |
> |  | (pressure =) force ÷ area | (C1) |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** The majority of candidates correctly determined the area as 24 cm$^{2}$. A common error was to calculate the volume of the block to give an answer of 72.
>
> **(ii)** This was answered well by almost all candidates. The most common error was to divide the mass by 10 rather than multiplying.
>
> **(b)** The majority of candidates correctly determined the pressure as 6.0 N / m$^{2}$. The most common error was to use an incorrect arrangement of the equation *P* = *F* / *A*.
