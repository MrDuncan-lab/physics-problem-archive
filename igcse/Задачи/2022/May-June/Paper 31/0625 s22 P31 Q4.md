> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определение преобразований энергии при подъёме груза и расчёт момента его веса.

Fig. 4.1 shows an electric motor and pulley wheel being used to raise a load M. The electric motor uses a belt to turn the pulley wheel.

![[0625-s22-31-p7-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 4.1**

**(a)** When the electric motor lifts the load, it transfers energy. Fig. 4.2 shows the energy transfers.

Write on Fig. 4.2 to complete the label in each box. The first label is done for you.

![[0625-s22-31-p7-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 4.2** **[3]**

**(b)** Fig. 4.3 shows the force on the pulley from the load M.

![[0625-s22-31-p8-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 4.3**

The weight of load M is 2.5 N and the weight acts at a distance of 20 cm from the pivot of the pulley wheel.

Calculate the moment of the weight of load M about the pivot.

moment =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ N cm **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** 50 (Ncm)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | (useful energy transfers:) kinetic (energy) | B1 |
> |  | in either order gravitational potential (energy) | B1 |
> |  | (wasted energy transfer:) thermal (energy) | B1 |
> | (b) | 50 (Ncm) | A3 |
> |  | 2.5 × 20 | (C2) |
> |  | (moment of force =) force × (perpendicular) distance (of force from pivot) | (C1) |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** This question was answered well and candidates showed an understanding of this difficult concept.
>
> **(b)** The majority of candidates correctly determined the moment as 50 N cm. The most common error was to use an incorrect arrangement of the equation: moment = force × distance.
