# Question 1 (9 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2022 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 1  
Источник: [[_pdf/2022/0625_s22_qp_32.pdf#page=2|условие, стр. 2]] · [[_pdf/2022/0625_s22_ms_32.pdf#page=7|схема оценивания, стр. 7]] · [[_pdf/2022/0625_s22_er.pdf#page=18|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

Темы: [[1.2 Motion]], [[1.1 Physical quantities and measurement techniques]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбор измерений скорости тележки и определение её движения и пути по графику скорости.

A student investigates the motion of a trolley as it travels down a slope.

**(a)** The student makes **two** measurements to determine the average speed of the trolley as it travels down the slope.

State the **two** measurements. For each measurement, suggest the instrument used for making the measurement.

1. measurement  \_\_\_\_\_\_ instrument used  \_\_\_\_\_\_

2. measurement  \_\_\_\_\_\_ instrument used  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(b)** Fig. 1.1 shows the speed–time graph for a different trolley as it travels down a slope.

![[0625-s22-32-p2-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 1.1**

**(i)** Determine the speed of the trolley at time = 2.0 s.

speed =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm / s **[2]**

**(ii)** Determine the distance moved by the trolley from time = 0 to time = 4.0 s.

distance =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm **[3]**

**(iii)** Using the information in Fig. 1.1, describe the motion of the trolley from time = 0 to time = 10 s. **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)(i)** 12.5 (cm/s)
>
> **(b)(ii)** 50 (cm)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | (measurement) time (instrument used) stopwatch | B1 |
> |  | (measurement) distance (instrument used) metre rule(r) | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | 12.5 (cm/s) | A2 |
> |  | any indication on graph or in working of vertical line from 2.0 s | (C1) |
> | (b)(ii) | 50 (cm) | A3 |
> |  | ½ × 4 × 25 | (C2) |
> |  | ( distance = ) area under graph OR ( distance = ) speed × time | (C1) |
> | (b)(iii) | accelerating (for 4 seconds) | B1 |
> |  | (then) constant / steady speed (for 6 seconds) | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** A significant number of candidates were unsure of either the quantity to be measured or the instrument used to measure the quantity.
>
> **(b) (i)** The vast majority of candidates answered this correctly.
>
> **(ii)** The majority of candidates correctly determined the area below the speed–time graph to give the distance travelled. The most common error was simply multiplying the speed by the time to give an answer of 100 m.
>
> **(iii)** Most candidates gave a correct description of initially accelerating and then travelling at a steady speed of 25 m / s. The most common error was to state that the trolley stopped after 4 s.
