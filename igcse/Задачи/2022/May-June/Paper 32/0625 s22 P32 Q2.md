# Question 2 (6 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2022 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 2

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Расчёт средней толщины листа бумаги и веса учебника.

Fig. 2.1 shows a closed textbook.

![[0625-s22-32-p3-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 2.1**

**(a)** There are 270 sheets of paper in the textbook. The total thickness of the sheets is 29 mm.

Calculate the average thickness of **one** sheet of paper.

average thickness of one sheet =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ mm **[3]**

**(b)** The mass of the textbook is 1300 g.

Calculate the weight of the textbook.

weight =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ N **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** 0.11 (mm)
>
> **(b)** (weight =) 13(.0) N

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | 0.11 (mm) | A3 |
> |  | (average thickness =) 29 ÷ 270 | (C2) |
> |  | (average thickness =) total thickness ÷ number of sheets | (C1) |
> | (b) | (1300 g = ) 1.3 kg | (B1) |
> |  | (weight =) 13(.0) N | A3 |
> |  | (weight =) mass × g OR mass × 10 | (C1) |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** The majority of candidates correctly divided the total thickness of the sheets by the number of sheets to give an answer of 0.11 mm. The most common error was to divide 270 by 29. Candidates should be made aware that they should write their equation in words before substituting numbers,
>
> as failing to do this often means that no credit can be awarded if an incorrect calculation is carried out.
>
> **(b)** Stronger candidates gained full credit but the majority did not convert the mass in grams to a mass in kilograms.
