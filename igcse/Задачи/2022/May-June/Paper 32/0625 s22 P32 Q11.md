> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Запись обозначения изотопа америция и определение периода полураспада по графику.

**(a)** An isotope of americium has 95 protons and 146 neutrons in its nucleus.

Write the nuclide notation for the nucleus of this isotope. The chemical symbol for americium is Am. **[2]**

**(b)** Fig. 11.1 shows how the count rate of a sample of americium changes with time.

![[0625-s22-32-p16-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 11.1**

Determine the half-life of the americium in the sample. Use information from Fig. 11.1.

half-life =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ years **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** 430 (years)

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Many candidates found this question challenging. The most common error was to give a nucleon number of 146, or to have the proton and nucleon numbers reversed.
>
> **(b)** Only stronger candidates halved a count rate and used the graph to find the time taken for this halving. The most common error was simply to give 650 years, which is half the maximum time on the time axis.
