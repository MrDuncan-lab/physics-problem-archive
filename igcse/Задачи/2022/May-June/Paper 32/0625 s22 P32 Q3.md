# Question 3 (8 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2022 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 3  
Источник: [[_pdf/2022/0625_s22_qp_32.pdf#page=4|условие, стр. 4]] · [[_pdf/2022/0625_s22_ms_32.pdf#page=8|схема оценивания, стр. 8]] · [[_pdf/2022/0625_s22_er.pdf#page=18|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 s22 P32]]  
Темы: [[1.5.1 Effects of forces]], [[1.5.2 Turning effect of forces]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Расчёт результирующей силы на самолёт, описание движения при равновесии и определение момента силы дверной ручки.

**(a)** Fig. 3.1 shows an aeroplane flying. There are horizontal forces acting on the aeroplane, as shown in Fig. 3.1.

![[0625-s22-32-p4-f1.png]]

**Fig. 3.1** (not to scale)

**(i)** Calculate the resultant horizontal force on the aeroplane.

resultant force =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ N

direction of resultant force  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

**(ii)** State the name of the effect producing the 8000 N force on the aeroplane. **[1]**

**(iii)** At a later time in the flight, the resultant horizontal force on the aeroplane is zero.

Describe the horizontal motion of the aeroplane. **[1]**

**(b)** Fig. 3.2 shows the handle used to open and close a cupboard door on the aeroplane.

![[0625-s22-32-p4-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 3.2** (not to scale)

A force of 60 N acts at a distance of 20 cm from the pivot of the handle.

Calculate the moment of the 60 N force about the pivot.

moment =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ N cm **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)(i)** 4000 (N)
>
> **(b)** 1200 (Ncm)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | 4000 (N) | A2 |
> |  | (resultant force =) force to R – force to L OR 12000 – 8000 | (C1) |
> |  | (to the) left or forwards | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | air resistance | B1 |
> | (a)(iii) | constant/steady speed | B1 |
> | (b) | 1200 (Ncm) | A3 |
> |  | (moment of force =) 60 × 20 | (C2) |
> |  | (moment of force =) force × (perpendicular) distance of force from pivot | (C1) |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** The majority of candidates correctly calculated the resultant force on the aeroplane. The most common error was to add the two forces rather than subtracting.
>
> **(ii)** Most candidates recognised that the force was friction or air resistance.
>
> **(iii)** Candidates found this item challenging and many thought that when the forces on the aircraft were balanced whilst it is in flight, it would stop/become stationary.
>
> **(b)** The majority of candidates correctly calculated the moment as 1200 N cm. The most common error was an incorrect arrangement of the equation: moment = force × distance, usually written as moment = force ÷ distance.
