# Question 8 (6 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2022 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 8  
Источник: [[_pdf/2022/0625_s22_qp_32.pdf#page=12|условие, стр. 12–13]] · [[_pdf/2022/0625_s22_ms_32.pdf#page=10|схема оценивания, стр. 10]] · [[_pdf/2022/0625_s22_er.pdf#page=18|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 s22 P32]]  
Темы: [[4.2.1 Electric charge]], [[4.1 Simple phenomena of magnetism]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Распознавание изоляторов и магнитных материалов, определение полюсов и взаимодействия заряженных и незаряженных сфер.

**(a)** A student has a box containing objects made of different materials. The objects are:

**aluminium foil a silver ring an iron bar**

**a plastic strip a glass lens**

**(i)** State which objects are made of electrically insulating materials. **[1]**

**(ii)** State which object is made of a magnetic material. **[1]**

**(b)** Fig. 8.1 shows two magnets, X and Y. The magnets are attracting each other.

![[0625-s22-32-p12-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 8.1**

On magnet X, the N pole is labelled N.

On Fig. 8.1, complete the labelling for the magnetic poles of each magnet. **[1]**

**(c)** The student attaches a thin cotton thread to each of two light metal spheres, P and Q. She suspends the spheres as shown in Fig. 8.2.

![[0625-s22-32-p12-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 8.2**

**(i)** The student puts a positive charge on sphere P only.

Complete the diagram in Fig. 8.3 to show the positions of the spheres.

![[0625-s22-32-p13-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 8.3** **[1]**

**(ii)** The student puts a positive charge on sphere P **and** on sphere Q.

Complete the diagram in Fig. 8.4 to show the positions of the spheres.

![[0625-s22-32-p13-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 8.4** **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(c)(ii)** **both** strings at an angle to vertical

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | plastic strip **AND** glass lens | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | iron bar | B1 |
> | (b) | end of magnet X labelled S (pole) **AND** end of magnet Y nearest magnet X<br>labelled N (pole) **AND** other end is S (pole) | B1 |
> | (c)(i) | spheres drawn closer together | B1 |
> | (c)(ii) | spheres drawn further apart | M1 |
> |  | **both** strings at an angle to vertical | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** Only stronger candidates gave both insulating materials. The most common error was either a plastic strip or a glass lens, but not both.
>
> **(ii)** Most candidates identified an iron bar as a magnetic material, but then added another metallic object.
>
> **(b)** Most candidates correctly identified the poles on the magnets. The most common error was failing to write one of the poles on one of the magnets.
>
> **(c) (i)** Most candidates recognised that the two metal spheres would attract.
>
> **(ii)** Most candidates correctly drew a diagram showing repulsion between the two positively charged spheres.
