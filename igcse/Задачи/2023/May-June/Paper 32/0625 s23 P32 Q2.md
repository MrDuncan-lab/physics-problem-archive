# Question 2 (8 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2023 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 2  
Источник: [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_qp_32.pdf#page=3|условие, стр. 3–4]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_ms_32.pdf#page=6|схема оценивания, стр. 6]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_er.pdf#page=23|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить участки постоянного ускорения и замедления, путь и действие равнодействующей сил на велосипедиста.

Fig. 2.1 shows the speed–time graph for a cyclist.

![[0625-s23-32-p3-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 2.1**

**(a)** In Fig. 2.1, the sections ST, TW, WX, XY and YZ indicate stages of the cyclist’s journey.

State **one** section which shows the cyclist **moving** with:

**(i)** constant speed **[1]**

**(ii)** constant deceleration **[1]**

**(iii)** constant non-zero acceleration. **[1]**

**(b)** Calculate the distance travelled by the cyclist in section ST.

distance travelled =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ m **[3]**

**(c)** Fig. 2.2 shows the horizontal forces on a cyclist.

![[0625-s23-32-p4-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 2.2**

**(i)** Calculate the size of the resultant force on the cyclist.

resultant force =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ N **[1]**

**(ii)** State the effect, if any, of the resultant force on the motion of the cyclist. **[1]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** (distance travelled =) 100 (m)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | ST **OR** WX | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | XY | B1 |
> | (a)(iii) | TW **OR** XY | B1 |
> | (b) | (distance travelled =) 100 (m) | A3 |
> |  | (distance travelled =) 8 × 13 | (C2) |
> |  | (distance travelled =) area under graph **OR** *b* × *h* | (C1) |
> | (c)(i) | 60 (N) | B1 |
> | (c)(ii) | accelerates **OR** increases speed | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Most candidates identified regions ST or WX for constant speed, XY for constant deceleration, and TW for constant acceleration. Despite being asked for a constant speed when the cyclist was moving, a significant number chose region YZ.
>
> **(b)** Almost all candidates recalled that distance travelled was equal to the area under the speed–time graph and went on to calculate the distance travelled as 120 m. A common error was to halve this distance or to calculate the total distance travelled by the cyclist.
>
> **(c) (i)** The majority of candidates correctly calculated the resultant force as 60 N. The most common error was to add the two forces rather than subtract.
>
> **(ii)** Only stronger candidates recognised that the resultant force would cause the cyclist to accelerate. A common error was simply to state that the cyclist would move forward.
