# Question 7 (5 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2023 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 7  
Источник: [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_qp_32.pdf#page=9|условие, стр. 9]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_ms_32.pdf#page=9|схема оценивания, стр. 9]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_er.pdf#page=23|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 s23 P32]]  
Темы: [[4.1 Simple phenomena of magnetism]], [[4.5.1 Electromagnetic induction]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объяснить индуцированное намагничивание гвоздей и появление напряжения при движении провода в магнитном поле.

A student uses a permanent magnet to lift some unmagnetised nails. Some of the nails are made of iron and some are made of steel. Fig. 7.1 shows the magnet lifting the nails.

![[0625-s23-32-p9-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 7.1**

**(a)** **(i)** Each nail lifts the nail below it by induced magnetism.

Describe what is meant by induced magnetism. **[2]**

**(ii)** The student leaves the nails attached to the magnet for several hours, then removes the magnet.

State a difference between a magnetic property of the iron nails and of the steel nails. **[1]**

**(b)** A metal wire XY is connected to a voltmeter. The wire is placed between the poles of a permanent magnet. Fig. 7.2 shows the arrangement.

![[0625-s23-32-p9-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 7.2**

**(i)** State the reading on the voltmeter when the wire is stationary between the poles. **[1]**

**(ii)** Give a reason for the reading on the voltmeter when the wire is moving in the direction shown in Fig. 7.2. **[1]**

> | (a)(ii) | steel nails retain magnetism **OR** are magnetic | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | no **OR** zero reading on voltmeter | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | (as conductor / wire) is cutting / linking with magnetic field (of magnet) | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** The concept of induced magnetism was not well understood by most candidates. Only the strongest candidates stated that the magnetised nail would have the opposite pole in contact with the pole of the magnet.
>
> **(ii)** Many candidates were able to give a difference in the magnetic properties of iron and steel. However, many candidates interchanged the properties of iron and steel.
>
> **(b) (i)** Only stronger candidates stated that when the wire was stationary, the reading on the voltmeter was zero volts.
>
> **(ii)** The concept of electromagnetic induction was not well understood by most candidates. Very few stated that the wire would cut the magnetic field lines of the magnet when it moved.
