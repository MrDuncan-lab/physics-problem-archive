# Question 4 (8 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2023 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 4  
Источник: [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_qp_32.pdf#page=6|условие, стр. 6]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_ms_32.pdf#page=7|схема оценивания, стр. 7]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_er.pdf#page=23|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Описать частицы металла и охлаждение, указать абсолютный нуль и свойства хорошего излучателя тепла.

A student has a block of solid metal at room temperature.

**(a)** **(i)** Describe the arrangement, separation and motion of the particles in the solid metal. **[3]**

**(ii)** The student cools the block of metal in a freezer.

State the effect, if any, of cooling on the kinetic energy of the particles in the block of metal. **[1]**

**(b)** **(i)** State the name of the temperature at which particles have the least kinetic energy. **[1]**

**(ii)** State the value of temperature at which particles have the least kinetic energy. Include the unit. **[1]**

**(c)** The metal block emits thermal radiation from its surface.

State **two** features of a surface that is a good emitter of thermal radiation.

1  \_\_\_\_\_\_

2  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | any **three** from:<br>• (particles are) fixed in position / place<br>• regular arrangement<br>• vibrating<br>• close(r than in liquids or gases) | B3 |
> | (a)(ii) | (kinetic energy) decreases | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | absolute zero | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | –273 °C **OR** 0 K **OR** zero K / kelvin | B1 |
> | (c) | black **OR** dark (colour) | B1 |
> |  | dull **OR** rough (surface) | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** Many candidates gained full credit for this question.
>
> **(ii)** Only stronger candidates answered this correctly. Other candidates were unable to link the decrease in temperature to a decrease in kinetic energy of particles.
>
> **(b) (i)** This new section on the syllabus was only understood by stronger candidates. Very few recalled that the temperature at which particles have least kinetic energy is called absolute zero.
>
> **(ii)** The vast majority of candidates thought that absolute zero was 0 °C.
>
> **(c)** Candidates found this question challenging. Most thought that a good emitter would have a shiny white surface.
