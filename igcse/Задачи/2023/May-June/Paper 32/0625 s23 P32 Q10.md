# Question 10 (7 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2023 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 10  
Источник: [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_qp_32.pdf#page=12|условие, стр. 12]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_ms_32.pdf#page=10|схема оценивания, стр. 10]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_er.pdf#page=23|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 s23 P32]]  
Темы: [[5.1.2 The nucleus]], [[5.2.2 The three types of nuclear emission]], [[5.2.4 Half-life]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить состав ядра йода, природу бета- и гамма-излучения и оставшуюся массу после распада.

Iodine-131 is a radioactive isotope of the element iodine. Fig. 10.1 shows the nuclide notation for a nucleus of iodine-131.

131

I

53

**Fig. 10.1**

**(a)** **(i)** Determine the number of protons in one nucleus of iodine-131.

number of protons =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(ii)** Determine the number of neutrons in one nucleus of iodine-131.

number of neutrons =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(b)** When a nucleus of iodine-131 decays, it emits a beta (β)-particle and a gamma (γ) ray.

State the nature of a beta-particle and a gamma ray.

A beta-particle is  \_\_\_\_\_\_

A gamma ray is  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(c)** A sample contains 1.6 mg of iodine-131. The half-life of iodine-131 is 8.0 days.

Calculate the mass of iodine-131 remaining in the sample after 24.0 days.

mass of iodine-131 remaining =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ mg **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(c)** 0.2(0) (mg)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | 53 | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | (131 – 53 =) 78 | B1 |
> | (b) | (negatively charged) electron | B1 |
> |  | electromagnetic (wave / ray) | B1 |
> | (c) | 0.2(0) (mg) | A3 |
> |  | 1.6 × ½ × ½ × ½ **OR** 1.6 ÷ 8 **OR** 1.6, 0.8, 0.4 | (C2) |
> |  | 24(.0) ÷ 8(.0) **OR** idea of 3 half-lives | (C1) |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** The majority of candidates correctly stated that the number of protons was 53.
>
> **(ii)** Fewer candidates were able to determine the number of neutrons as 78.
>
> **(b)** Candidates found this question challenging. Very few recalled that a beta-particle was an electron and that a gamma ray is an electromagnetic wave.
>
> **(c)** Many candidates correctly calculated the mass of iodine. However, other candidates did not gain any credit for this question. Candidates should be encouraged to clearly state the number of half- lives involved in calculations such as this and then to clearly set out their working using this number of half-lives.
