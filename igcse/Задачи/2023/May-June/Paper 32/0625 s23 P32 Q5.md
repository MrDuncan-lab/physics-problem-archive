# Question 5 (8 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2023 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 5  
Источник: [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_qp_32.pdf#page=7|условие, стр. 7]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_ms_32.pdf#page=8|схема оценивания, стр. 8]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_er.pdf#page=23|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 s23 P32]]  
Темы: [[3.4 Sound]], [[3.1 General properties of waves]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать расстояние до взрыва и частоту сирены, объяснить более поздний звук отражением.

An observer stands at P and looks into a rock quarry. A small explosion takes place at X in the quarry.

Fig. 5.1 shows the situation.

![[0625-s23-32-p7-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 5.1** (not to scale)

**(a)** The observer first hears the sound from the explosion 1.8 s after the explosion occurs. The speed of the sound is 340 m / s.

**(i)** Calculate the distance XP from the explosion at X to the observer at P.

distance XP =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ m **[3]**

**(ii)** The observer then hears a quieter sound from the explosion.

Suggest how the quieter sound waves reach the observer. **[2]**

**(b)** Before the explosion, a warning siren produces a sound. The wavelength of the sound is 0.28 m.

The speed of the sound is 340 m / s.

Calculate the frequency of the sound.

frequency =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ Hz **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)(i)** 610 (m)
>
> **(b)** 1200 (Hz)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | 610 (m) | A3 |
> |  | 340 = distance ÷ 1.8 **OR** (distance =) 340 × 1.8 | (C2) |
> |  | speed = distance ÷ time in any form | (C1) |
> | (a)(ii) | an echo **OR** sound (waves) reflecting | B1 |
> |  | from rocks **OR** YZ **OR** Z **OR** bottom of quarry | B1 |
> | (b) | 1200 (Hz) | A3 |
> |  | 340 = *f* × 0.28 **OR** (*f* =) 340 ÷ 0.28 | (C2) |
> |  | *v* = *f* λ in any form **OR** (*f* =) *v* ÷ λ | (C1) |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** Most candidates correctly calculated the distance as 612 m. A common error was to divide 340 by 1.8 instead of multiplying.
>
> **(ii)** The majority of candidates recognised that the sound was caused by sound waves reflecting but did not state that they had reflected from a rocky surface. Weaker candidates thought that sound waves reflected off each other.
>
> **(b)** Most candidates correctly calculated the frequency as 1200 Hz. However, many candidates used an incorrect rearrangement of the equation, and failed to gain any credit. Candidates should be encouraged to write down the standard arrangement of the equation before they attempt any rearrangement.
