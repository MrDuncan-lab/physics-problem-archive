# Question 8 (8 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2023 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 8

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Обозначить измерительные приборы, рассчитать сопротивление нагревателя и переданную электрическую энергию.

A student uses the circuit in Fig. 8.1 to measure the resistance of the heater in the circuit.

![[0625-s23-32-p10-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 8.1**

**(a)** The symbols for the meters in Fig. 8.1 are incomplete.

Complete the symbols for the two meters by writing in the circles in Fig. 8.1. **[2]**

**(b)** The current in the heater is 1.4 A and the potential difference (p.d.) across the heater is 8.0 V.

Calculate the resistance of the heater.

resistance =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ Ω **[3]**

**(c)** The heater is switched on for 30 s. The current in the heater is 1.4 A and the p.d. across it is 8.0 V.

Calculate the electrical energy transferred by the heater during the 30 s.

energy transferred =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ J **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** (*R* =) 5.7 (Ω)
>
> **(c)** (*E* =) 340 (J)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | ammeter symbol correct | B1 |
> |  | voltmeter symbol correct | B1 |
> | (b) | (*R* =) 5.7 (Ω) | A3 |
> |  | (*R* =) 8(.0) ÷ 1.4 | (C2) |
> |  | *V* = *IR* in any form **OR** (*R* =) *V* ÷ *I* | (C1) |
> | (c) | (*E* =) 340 (J) | A3 |
> |  | (*E* =) 8(.0) × 1.4 × 30 | (C2) |
> |  | (*E* =) *V I t* | (C1) |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Candidates found this question challenging and a significant number did not give an answer. Many who attempted the question interchanged the positions of the ammeter and voltmeter.
>
> **(b)** Many candidates correctly calculated the resistance of the heater as 5.7 ohms. However, a very common error was to multiply 8 by 1.4 instead of dividing.
>
> **(c)** Many candidates correctly calculated the energy from the heater as 336 J, but the vast majority could not recall or use the equation *E* = *V*I*t*. Candidates should be encouraged to learn the new equations on in the syllabus and to practice using them in examples.
