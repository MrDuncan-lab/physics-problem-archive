> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** Many candidates recognised the process as refraction. Common errors included diffraction, reflection and even some rarefactions.
>
> **(ii)** The majority of candidates recognised that the speed, wavelength and direction of travel of the wave had changed. Weaker candidates just stated that the wavefronts were bent, which was insufficient for credit.
>
> **(b)** The topic of total internal reflection was not well understood by most candidates. Stronger candidates sometimes gained partial credit.
