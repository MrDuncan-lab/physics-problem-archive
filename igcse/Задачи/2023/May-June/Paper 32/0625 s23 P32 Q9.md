# Question 9 (8 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2023 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 9  
Источник: [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_qp_32.pdf#page=11|условие, стр. 11]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_ms_32.pdf#page=10|схема оценивания, стр. 10]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_er.pdf#page=23|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

Темы: [[4.4 Electrical safety]], [[4.5.6 The transformer]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объяснить безопасное подключение выключателя и описать конструкцию понижающего трансформатора с требуемым отношением витков.

A student has a desktop computer that connects to the 240 V a.c. mains electrical supply. Fig. 9.1 shows the desktop computer.

![[0625-s23-32-p11-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 9.1**

**(a)** The desktop computer has an on-off switch in one of the wires that connect it to the mains supply.

State and explain which wire includes the switch. **[3]**

**(b)** The desktop computer uses a transformer to change the 240 V a.c. voltage to a 12 V a.c. voltage.

**(i)** State the name of this type of transformer. **[1]**

**(ii)** Describe the construction of this transformer. You may include a labelled diagram. **[4]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | (switch is in) the live wire | B1 |
> |  | (so when switch is off, desktop is) disconnected from supply / mains(voltage) **OR** high voltage | B1 |
> |  | (so) no current (in computer / circuit) **OR** no risk of electrocution | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | step-down (transformer) | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | any **four** from:<br>• (soft) iron core<br>• two coils of copper wire<br>• primary coil **AND** secondary coil<br>• transformer equation stated<br>• <u>turns ratio of 20 : 1</u> **OR** 240 : 12 owtte **OR** use of transformer equation | B4 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Candidates found this question challenging. Very few recalled that the switch is fitted into the live wire and an even smaller number could explain the reasons for this.
>
> **(b) (i)** The majority of candidates recognised the transformer as a step-down transformer.
>
> **(ii)** Very few candidates recalled the structure of a step-down transformer. Only the strongest candidates gained full credit.
