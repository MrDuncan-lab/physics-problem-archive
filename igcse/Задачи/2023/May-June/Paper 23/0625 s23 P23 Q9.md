> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать импульс силы при отражении мяча от стены с изменением скорости.

A ball of mass 0.25 kg hits a wall at a speed of 16 m / s. It then rebounds back along its original path at a speed of 12 m / s.

What is the impulse experienced by the ball during its impact with the wall?

**A** 1.0 N s  
**B** 3.0 N s  
**C** 4.0 N s  
**D** 7.0 N s

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The strongest candidates were able to identify the correct option. Other candidates multiplied the change in speed by the mass instead of the change in velocity and chose option **A**.
