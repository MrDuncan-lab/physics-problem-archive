> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить физическую величину на оси графика зависимости сопротивления металлической проволоки.

A student does an experiment to investigate the resistance of a metal wire.

The graph shows the results from the experiment.

![[0625-s23-23-p10-f1.svg]]

What is plotted on the *x*-axis?

**A** diameter of the wire  
**B** length of the wire  
**C** temperature of the wire  
**D** current in the wire

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question assessed candidates’ knowledge of the relationship between the resistance of a wire, its length and its diameter. Only stronger candidates could identify the correct relationship shown by the graph.
