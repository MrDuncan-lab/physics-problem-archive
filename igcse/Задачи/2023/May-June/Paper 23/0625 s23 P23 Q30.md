> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить направление силы на провод с током между полюсами магнита.

The diagram shows a wire hanging freely between the poles of a magnet. There is a current in

the wire in the direction shown.

![[0625-s23-23-p12-f2.svg]]

The magnet and current cause a force to act on the wire.

In which direction does this force act?

**A** into the page (away from you)  
**B** out of the page (toward you)  
**C** to the left  
**D** to the right

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Only stronger candidates could use the left-hand rule successfully in order to determine the direction of the force on the wire.
