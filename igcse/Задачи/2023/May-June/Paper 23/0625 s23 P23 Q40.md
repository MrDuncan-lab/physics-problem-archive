# Question 40 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2023 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 3 · вопрос 40  
Источник: [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_qp_23.pdf#page=15|условие, стр. 15]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_ms_23.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_er.pdf#page=16|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 s23 P23]]  
Темы: [[6.2.3 The Universe]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить расстояние до галактики как величину, оцениваемую по яркости сверхновой.

Which quantity can be determined using the brightness of a supernova in a distant galaxy?

**A** the speed at which the galaxy is moving away from the Earth  
**B** the distance of the galaxy from the Earth  
**C** the Hubble constant  
**D** the age of the Universe

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Candidates had to recall which quantity can be determined using the brightness of a supernova in a distant galaxy (syllabus statement 6.2.3.8) and many candidates answered this correctly. However, others had the misconception that the speed at which the galaxy is moving away from the Earth can be determined and therefore chose incorrect option **A**.
