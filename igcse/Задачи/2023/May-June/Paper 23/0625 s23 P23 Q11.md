> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объяснить необходимость высокой температуры и трудность сближения положительных ядер для термоядерного синтеза.

Research is being carried out to produce electrical energy from the fusion of hydrogen nuclei.

Which row shows two of the problems in designing a fusion reactor?

|  | temperature needed | why obtaining a high density of hydrogen nuclei is difficult |
|---|---|---|
| **A** **B** **C** **D** | very low very low very high very high | the nuclei are negatively charged and repel each other the nuclei are positively charged and repel each other the nuclei are negatively charged and repel each other the nuclei are positively charged and repel each other |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Many weaker candidates had the misconception that nuclei are negatively charged and chose the incorrect option **C**.
