# Question 36 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2023 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 3 · вопрос 36  
Источник: [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_qp_23.pdf#page=14|условие, стр. 14]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_ms_23.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_er.pdf#page=16|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 s23 P23]]  
Темы: [[5.2.4 Half-life]], [[5.2.1 Detection of radioactivity]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить период полураспада по графику скорости счёта с учётом радиационного фона.

The graph shows how the count rate registered by a counter near to a sample of a radioactive

isotope changes over a period of a few days. The background count rate is 5 counts per minute.

![[0625-s23-23-p14-f1.svg]]

What is the half-life of the isotope?

**A** 2.0 days  
**B** 2.5 days  
**C** 3.0 days  
**D** 4.0 days

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> In this question, candidates had to work out the half-life of the radioactive isotope using the graph and the background count rate (syllabus statement 5.2.1.5). Stronger candidates answered this correctly. The most common error was not to take the background count rate into account, therefore choosing incorrect option **B**.
