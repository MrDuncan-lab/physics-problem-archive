> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбрать материал сердечника электромагнита и определить временный характер его намагничивания.

Which row gives the metal used to make the core of an electromagnet and one property of the

electromagnet?

|  | metal | property |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | iron | permanent magnet |
| **B** | iron | temporary magnet |
| **C** | steel | permanent magnet |
| **D** | steel | temporary magnet |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Candidates had to recall facts about electromagnets in this question. Many candidates answered this correctly, with incorrect answers spread over the other three options.
