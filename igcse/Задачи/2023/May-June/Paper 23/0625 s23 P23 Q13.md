> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать плотность жидкости по графику зависимости давления от глубины.

The graph shows how the pressure due to a liquid varies with the depth beneath the liquid

surface.

The gravitational field strength *g* is 9.8 N / kg.

![[0625-s23-23-p6-f1.svg]]

depth beneath the

liquid surface / m

What is the density of the liquid?

**A** 200 kg / m$^{3}$  
**B** 820 kg / m$^{3}$  
**C** 2000 kg / m$^{3}$  
**D** 8200 kg / m$^{3}$

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> For this question, candidates had to read values from the graph and then rearrange an equation for pressure in a liquid to calculate the density of the liquid. Most stronger candidates chose the correct option, but weaker candidates found this challenging.
