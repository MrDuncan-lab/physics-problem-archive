> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать модуль результирующей скорости самолёта при перпендикулярном боковом ветре.

An aircraft is moving at 60 m / s in a northerly direction when a cross-wind from the east starts to blow. The speed of the wind is 13 m / s.

What is the magnitude of the aircraft’s velocity when the wind is blowing?

**A** 47 m / s  
**B** 59 m / s  
**C** 61 m / s  
**D** 73 m / s

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Candidates found this question on determining the resultant velocity challenging. Many weaker candidates did not recognise that the direction of the aircraft was at right angles to the direction of the wind, and therefore just subtracted one speed from the other.
