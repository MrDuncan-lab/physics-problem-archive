> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбрать изменения рычага, уменьшающие необходимую силу подъёма бревна.

The diagram shows the minimum force *F*$_{1}$ acting vertically on a lever required to lift a heavy log of weight *W*.

![[0625-s23-23-p4-f1.svg]]

The log needs to be lifted by a smaller force than *F*$_{1}$. The diagrams show the changes tried. Each diagram has only **one** change from the original diagram. In each case, *F*$_{2}$ is the minimum force required to lift the log.

![[0625-s23-23-p4-f2.svg]]

increasing the length of the lever to the right of the pivot perpendicular to the lever

![[0625-s23-23-p4-f3.svg]]

In which situations will *F*$_{2}$ be smaller than *F*$_{1}$?

**A** P, Q and R  
**B** P and Q only  
**C** P only  
**D** Q and R only

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This was a challenging question on moments with only stronger candidates choosing the correct option. Weaker candidates chose option **C** as they did not recognise that the perpendicular distance in diagram Q was greater.
