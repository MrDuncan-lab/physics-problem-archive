# Question 6 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2023 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 3 · вопрос 6

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнить движущую силу и трение при равномерном прямолинейном движении велосипедиста.

A cyclist is travelling in a straight line along a horizontal road at a constant speed.

A constant driving force *F* acts on the cyclist in the forward direction shown.

![[0625-s23-23-p3-f1.svg]]

Which statement about the magnitude of the frictional forces acting on the cyclist is correct?

**A** The magnitude is equal to *F*.  
**B** The magnitude is smaller than *F*, but greater than zero.  
**C** The magnitude is greater than *F*.  
**D** The magnitude is zero.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Candidates had to recognise that when the cyclist travels at a constant speed, the forces acting on the cyclist must be balanced. Only stronger candidates answered correctly. Other candidates had the misconception that the driving force had to be larger than the frictional force.
