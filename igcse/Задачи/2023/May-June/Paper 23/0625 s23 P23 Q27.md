> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбрать проволоку с наименьшим сопротивлением по длине и диаметру.

The table describes four different resistance wires. They are all made from the same metal.

Which wire has the smallest resistance?

|  | length of wire / m | diameter of wire / mm |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | 2.0 | 1.0 |
| **B** | 2.0 | 1.5 |
| **C** | 3.0 | 1.0 |
| **D** | 3.0 | 1.5 |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Candidates were assessed on their knowledge of the effect of changing the length and diameter of a wire on its resistance. Many candidates chose the correct option, but weaker candidates’ responses were evenly distributed across the four options.
