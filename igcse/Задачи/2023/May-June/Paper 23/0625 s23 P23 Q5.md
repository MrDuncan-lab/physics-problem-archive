> Рассчитать массу и земной вес тела по весу в более слабом гравитационном поле.

An object in a space probe above the Earth weighs 3.5 N. The gravitational field strength at the height of the space probe is 7.0 N / kg.

The gravitational field strength on the Earth’s surface is 9.8 N / kg.

What are the mass and the weight of the object on the Earth’s surface?

|  | mass / kg | weight / N |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | 0.50 | 3.5 |
| **B** | 0.50 | 4.9 |
| **C** | 2.0 | 3.5 |
| **D** | 2.0 | 20 |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
