# Question 8 (6 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2023 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 3 · вопрос 8  
Источник: [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_qp_43.pdf#page=10|условие, стр. 10]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_ms_43.pdf#page=12|схема оценивания, стр. 12]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_er.pdf#page=40|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 s23 P43]]  
Темы: [[5.2.3 Radioactive decay]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Описать превращение нейтрона и сохранение заряда при бета-распаде, дополнить уравнение альфа-распада радона.

**(a)** During β-decay, one of the neutrons in the nucleus changes.

**(i)** State what happens to this neutron. **[1]**

**(ii)** Explain how charge is conserved during this change. **[2]**

**(b)** Complete the nuclide equation for the α-decay of radon-212 to form an isotope of polonium, symbol Po.

212

Rn

86 **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** ($^{212}_{86}$Rn →) $^{208}_{84}$Po + $^{4}_{2}$α

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | (neutron) becomes proton and electron **OR** (neutron) becomes proton and β particle | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | charge on neutron = 0 **OR** total charge on products = 0 | B1 |
> |  | charge on proton = +1 **AND** charge on electron = –1 | B1 |
> | (b) | ($^{212}_{86}$Rn →) $^{208}_{84}$Po + $^{4}_{2}$α | A3 |
> |  | any **two** from:<br>• proton numbers balance<br>• nucleon numbers balance<br>• $^{4}_{2}$α **OR** H$^{4}_{2}$ e seen | C2 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** Only stronger candidates gave the correct answer. Weaker candidates stated that the neutron is ejected from the nucleus or that it is changed into either a proton or an electron.
>
> **(ii)** Only stronger candidates answered this correctly. Some other responses discussed energy conservation instead of charge. Some weaker candidates gained partial credit for stating that the neutron has no charge.
>
> **(b)** The majority of candidates knew how to complete a fully correct decay equation. Weaker candidates could not recall the numerical notation for the alpha particle or had a fully correct equation except they had substituted Rn for Po.
