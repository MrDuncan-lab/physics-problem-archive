# Question 10 (7 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2023 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 3 · вопрос 10  
Источник: [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_qp_43.pdf#page=15|условие, стр. 15]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_ms_43.pdf#page=14|схема оценивания, стр. 14]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_er.pdf#page=40|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 s23 P43]]  
Темы: [[6.2.2 Stars]], [[6.2.3 The Universe]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Вычислить длину светового года, описать определение скорости удаления галактики и расстояния по закону Хаббла или сверхновой.

**(a)** Show that 1 light-year = 9.5 × 10$^{15}$ m. **[4]**

**(b)** **(i)** State **one** measurement that is taken when determining the speed *v* at which a galaxy is moving away from the Earth. **[1]**

**(ii)** Write down an equation relating *v* and the distance *d* of a far galaxy. **[1]**

**(iii)** State how the distance *d* of a far galaxy can be determined other than by using the equation in **(ii)**. **[1]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | *v* = *s* / *t* **OR** (*s* =) *vt* | B1 |
> |  | (*c* =) 3 × 10$^{8}$ (m / s) | B1 |
> |  | (1 year =) 365 × 24 × 3600 (s) **OR** 3.2 × 10$^{7}$ (s) **OR** 32 × 10$^{6}$ **OR** 8760 × 3600 | B1 |
> |  | (*s* =) candidate’s speed of light × candidate’s time (m) | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | change of wavelength (of galaxy’s starlight) **OR** red shift | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | *H*$_{0}$ = *v* / *d* | B1 |
> | (b)(iii) | brightness of a supernova | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** This question was generally very well answered, but some candidates did not recognise that for a ‘show that’ question the relevant equation needed to be included in addition to the necessary numerical steps.
>
> **(b) (i)** The majority of candidates answered this question incorrectly stating that direct measurements of either distance or time could be used to determine the speed of a galaxy.
>
> **(ii)** This question was only answered well by stronger candidates. Weaker candidates again assumed direct measurements were possible and often stated that speed = distance / time rather than recalling the equation involving the Hubble constant from the space physics section 6.2 (Stars and the Universe) of the syllabus.
>
> **(iii)** Although this was answered well by the strongest candidates, other candidates stated that direct measurements of speed and time were necessary.
