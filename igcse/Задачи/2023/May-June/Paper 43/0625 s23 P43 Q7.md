# Question 7 (5 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2023 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 3 · вопрос 7  
Источник: [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_qp_43.pdf#page=9|условие, стр. 9]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_ms_43.pdf#page=12|схема оценивания, стр. 12]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_er.pdf#page=40|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 s23 P43]]  
Темы: [[4.5.6 The transformer]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать первичный ток и отношение витков идеального трансформатора по напряжениям и вторичному току.

The voltage across the primary coil of a 100% efficient transformer is 220 V and the voltage across the secondary coil is 12 V.

**(a)** The current in the secondary coil is 2.5 A.

Calculate the current in the primary coil.

current =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

**(b)** Calculate the ratio of the number of turns on the primary coil to the number of turns on the secondary coil of the transformer.

ratio =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** (*I*$_{\text{p}}$ =) 0.14 A
>
> **(b)** (turns ratio =) 18 (: 1)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | (*I*$_{\text{p}}$ =) 0.14 A | A3 |
> |  | *V*$_{\text{p}}$*I*$_{\text{p}}$ = *V*$_{\text{s}}$*I*$_{\text{s}}$ **OR** (*I*$_{\text{p}}$ =) *V*$_{\text{s}}$*I*$_{\text{s}}$ / *V*$_{\text{p}}$**OR** (*I*$_{\text{p}}$ =) 12 × 2.5 / 220 | C1 |
> |  | (*I*$_{\text{p}}$ =) 12 × 2.5 / 220 | C1 |
> | (b) | (turns ratio =) 18 (: 1) | A2 |
> |  | *N*$_{\text{p}}$ / *N*$_{\text{s}}$ = *V*$_{\text{p}}$ / *V*$_{\text{s}}$ **OR** (*N*$_{\text{p}}$ / *N*$_{\text{s}}$ =) *V*$_{\text{p}}$ / *V*$_{\text{s}}$ **OR** (turns ratio =) 220 / 12 | C1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** This question was generally answered well. Weaker candidates arrived at a common incorrect answer of 46 A obtained by an incorrect recall of the relevant equation. Their correct ratio of voltages was equated to an incorrect and inverted ratio of the currents. Candidates should be reminded that since primary power = secondary power then *V*p *I*p = *V*s *I*s so equating ratios of voltages to currents does not follow the same pattern as the turns ratio equation.
>
> **(b)** This question was generally answered well. Weaker candidates sometimes gained partial credit by calculating the reciprocal of the correct answer.
