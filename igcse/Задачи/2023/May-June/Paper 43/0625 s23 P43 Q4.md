# Question 4 (9 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2023 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 3 · вопрос 4  
Источник: [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_qp_43.pdf#page=6|условие, стр. 6]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_ms_43.pdf#page=9|схема оценивания, стр. 9]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_er.pdf#page=40|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Построить мнимое изображение собирающей линзы и объяснить исправление дальнозоркости.

**(a)** Fig. 4.1 is an incomplete ray diagram showing an object O, a converging lens and the principal axis. The focal points of the lens are each labelled F.

![[0625-s23-43-p6-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 4.1**

**(i)** Complete the ray diagram to draw the image formed by the lens. Label your image I. **[3]**

**(ii)** Circle **three** descriptions in the list which describe the image formed in **(i)**.

**diminished enlarged inverted same size**

**real upright virtual** **[3]**

**(b)** **(i)** State the name for the defect of vision that can be corrected by a converging lens. **[1]**

**(ii)** Describe how a converging lens corrects the defect in **(i)**. You may find it helpful to sketch a ray diagram. **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)(i)** extended rays intersect

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | two correct rays extended back (towards the intersection) | M1 |
> |  | extended rays intersect | A1 |
> |  | image drawn from intersection to principal axis **AND** base of image lies in correct range | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | circles around:<br>• enlarged<br>• virtual<br>• upright | B3 |
> | (b)(i) | long-sightedness / long sight / far-sighted | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | converging lens reduces focal length of eye **OR** converging lens brings focal point forward **OR** without lens, rays converge<br>behind back of eye | B1 |
> |  | (so that) rays converge / focus on back of eye / retina | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** Candidates should be reminded that they should practice constructing the ray diagram using a sharp pencil and ruler for formation of a virtual image in the correct location. Weaker candidates displayed good knowledge of light-ray behaviour close to the principal axis but then tried to form a real image on the right-hand side of the lens.
>
> **(ii)** Candidates were often able to correctly identify the attributes of the image for an object located between the lens and the principal focus. Weaker candidates referred to their incorrect ray diagram to answer this question.
>
> **(b)** Many candidates correctly identified the defect of vision but were not confident about how this was corrected. Weaker candidates incorrectly mentioned use of a diverging lens or described the magnification properties of a converging lens as the means of correction.
