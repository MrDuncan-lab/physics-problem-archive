# Question 6 (8 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2023 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 3 · вопрос 6  
Источник: [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_qp_43.pdf#page=8|условие, стр. 8]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_ms_43.pdf#page=11|схема оценивания, стр. 11]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_er.pdf#page=40|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать ток нагревателя, сопротивление проволоки изменённых размеров и стоимость электроэнергии за два дня.

An electric heater uses a resistance wire of resistance 26 Ω. The power dissipated in the resistance wire is 2500 W.

**(a)** Calculate the current in the resistance wire.

current =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

**(b)** The resistance wire of the heater has a length of 1.2 m and a cross-sectional area of 7.9 × 10$^{\text{–7}}$ m$^{2}$.

A new heater is designed using wire of the same material with length 1.8 m and cross- sectional area 5.8 × 10$^{\text{–7}}$ m$^{2}$.

Calculate the resistance of this wire.

resistance =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

**(c)** The 2500 W heater is used in a country where electricity costs 0.30 dollars per kilowatt-hour.

Calculate the cost of using the heater continuously for two days.

cost =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ dollars **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** (*I* =) 9.8 A
>
> **(b)** (*R* =) 53 Ω
>
> **(c)** (cost =) \$36

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | (*I* =) 9.8 A | A3 |
> |  | *P* = *I*$^{2}$*R* **OR** *I*$^{2}$  $\dfrac{P}{R}$ **OR** *I*$^{2}$  $\dfrac{2500}{26}$ | C1 |
> |  | *I*$^{2}$ = 2500 / 26 **OR** (*I* =) √(*P* / *R*) **OR** $\dfrac{2500}{26}$ | C1 |
> | (b) | (*R* =) 53 Ω | A3 |
> |  | *R* ∝ *l* **OR** 1.8 / 1.2 **OR** 1.5 seen as multiplier | C1 |
> |  | R ∝ 1 / *A* **OR** 7.9 (× 10$^{\text{–7}}$) / 5.8 (× 10$^{\text{–7}}$) **OR** 1.36(2) seen as multiplier | C1 |
> | (c) | (cost =) \$36 | A2 |
> |  | *E* = *Pt* **AND** (cost =) *E* × 0.3(0) **OR** (cost =) 25 × 10$^{\text{N}}$ × 2 × 24 × 0.3(0)<br>**OR** 3.6 × 10$^{\text{N}}$ dollars | C1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Many candidates successfully selected and substituted into the correct electrical power equation from the Supplement column in the syllabus. Weaker candidates made an attempt to use *P* = *IV* by dividing the power by the resistance, believing this to be a voltage value. A very rare correct method was to use *P* = *V*$^{2}$/ *R* (beyond the scope of the syllabus) to find the voltage across the heater and then to calculate the current using *P* = *IV*.
>
> **(b)** This was a demanding question to which there was often no response, but stronger candidates gained full credit. Weaker candidates sometimes gained partial credit by evaluating the correct ratio of lengths and cross-sectional areas or by recalling the correct physics for the resistance of a conductor, e.g. resistance is directly proportional to length.
>
> **(c)** There were some good responses to this question. However, weaker candidates were unable to convert watts to kilowatts so consequently only partial credit was available.
