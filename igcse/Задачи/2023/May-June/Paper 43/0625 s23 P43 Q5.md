**(a)** Two types of electromagnetic radiation are used in glass optical fibres for high-speed broadband.

**(i)** State the type of electromagnetic radiation, other than visible light, which is used in glass optical fibres. **[1]**

**(b)** **(i)** The critical angle of the glass in an optical fibre is 45°.

Calculate the refractive index of the glass.

refractive index =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)(i)** (*n* =) 1.4

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** This question was only answered correctly by the strongest candidates.
>
> **(ii)** Generally, this question was not answered well. Candidates were not credited for statements such as ‘electromagnetic waves travel quickly’ or ‘undergo total internal reflection’. Correct answers needed to refer to the transparency to the radiation and also that high-speed broadband relies on the optical fibre carrying a high rate of data.
>
> **(b) (i)** This question was generally answered very well. Weaker candidates often scored partial credit for a correct equation but if *n* = sin*i* / sin*r* was used they did not realise that the sine of the critical angle should have been the denominator for light travelling from a denser to a rarer medium.
>
> **(ii)** This question demonstrated that total internal reflection was well understood but weaker candidates are advised to use a protractor in order to make the diagram look realistic with angles of incidence exceeding 45°.
