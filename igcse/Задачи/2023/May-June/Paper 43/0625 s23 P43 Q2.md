> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** The definition of pressure is in the Core column of the syllabus, but many students did not recall this correctly. Many said that force acted on an object but did not relate this to area. A common answer was to refer to “a certain area” rather than unit area. Simply quoting an equation even without the subject was sufficient for credit.
>
> **(ii)** Candidates who succeeded with this question had clearly learnt the equation for pressure at a depth. Weaker students often gained partial credit but thought that the pressure decreased with increasing density.
>
> **(b)** A considerable number of candidates thought that hydroelectric power and wind power were not derived from the Sun.
>
> **(c)** The majority of candidates answered both parts of this question well. They had many ways of explaining that supplies of nuclear fuel are finite and that power generation from waves in the sea is infinite.
