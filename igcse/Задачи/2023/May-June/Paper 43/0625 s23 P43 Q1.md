# Question 1 (7 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2023 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 3 · вопрос 1  
Источник: [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_qp_43.pdf#page=2|условие, стр. 2–3]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_ms_43.pdf#page=6|схема оценивания, стр. 6]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_er.pdf#page=40|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать вес и ускорение воздушного шара, скорость и путь при равноускоренном подъёме.

Fig. 1.1 shows a balloon filled with helium gas.

![[0625-s23-43-p2-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 1.1**

The mass of the balloon is 120 kg.

**(a)** Calculate the weight of the balloon. Show your working.

weight =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(b)** The resultant force on the balloon is 54 N.

Show that the acceleration of the balloon is 0.45 m / s$^{2}$. **[2]**

**(c)** The balloon accelerates upwards from rest at 0.45 m / s$^{2}$ for 8.0 s.

Calculate the velocity of the balloon after 8.0 s.

velocity =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(d)** Calculate the distance travelled by the balloon in the first 8.0 s.

distance =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(c)** (*v* =) 3.6 m / s
>
> **(d)** (*d* =) 14 m

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | 1200 N **AND**<br>*g* = *W* / *m* **OR** (*W* =) *mg* **OR** (*W* =) 120 × 9.8 | B1 |
> | (b) | *F* = *ma* **OR** (*a* =) *F* / *m* | B1 |
> |  | (*a* =) 54 / 120 | B1 |
> | (c) | (*v* =) 3.6 m / s | A2 |
> |  | *a* = (∆)*v* / *t* **OR** (∆*v* =) *at* **OR** (∆*v* =) 0.45 × 8(.0) | C1 |
> | (d) | (*d* =) 14 m | A2 |
> |  | <u>average</u> speed = (total) distance travelled / (total) time taken **OR** 1.8 × 8 **OR** $\left(\dfrac{3.6}{2}\right) \times 8$ | C1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** The majority of candidates correctly recalled the equation *W* = *mg* and calculated the weight of the balloon, stating the correct unit. Weaker candidates either omitted the units in the final answer or did not show their working as instructed or were unable to correctly recall the equation.
>
> **(b)** This ‘show that’ question required a correct equation to be quoted in any form and then the ratio 54/120 written down to gain credit. Weaker candidates correctly wrote down *F* = *ma* and then 54 = 120 × *a* without making acceleration the subject, so could not gain full credit.
>
> **(c)** Most candidates were able to recall the equation for acceleration and calculate the velocity by multiplying the acceleration by the time. Weaker candidates thought they needed to use velocity = distance / time, mistakenly dividing the acceleration value by the time.
>
> **(d)** The balloon did not have a constant velocity of 3.6 m / s so the majority of candidates did not realise they had to multiply the average velocity by 8.0s to calculate the correct answer. A few stronger
>
> candidates sketched a velocity–time graph and found the area. A small minority of candidates correctly used equations of motion beyond the scope of the syllabus.
