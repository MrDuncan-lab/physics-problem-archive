# Question 1 (7 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2023 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 3 · вопрос 1  
Источник: [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_qp_33.pdf#page=2|условие, стр. 2]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_ms_33.pdf#page=6|схема оценивания, стр. 6]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_er.pdf#page=26|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 s23 P33]]  
Темы: [[1.2 Motion]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить расстояния, длительность этапов и скорость поездки по графику пути от времени.

Fig. 1.1 shows the distance–time graph for an engineer’s journey. She drives from her home directly to her office and parks the car. She then drives from her office to her friend’s house and parks the car.

![[0625-s23-33-p2-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 1.1**

**(a)** Determine the distance between:

**(i)** the engineer’s home and her office  \_\_\_\_\_\_ km **[1]**

**(ii)** the engineer’s office and her friend’s house \_\_\_\_\_\_ km **[1]**

**(b)** Determine the time taken to travel between:

**(i)** the engineer’s home and her office  \_\_\_\_\_\_ h **[1]**

**(ii)** the engineer’s office and her friend’s house \_\_\_\_\_\_ h **[1]**

**(c)** Calculate the speed of the car between time = 7 h and time = 10 h.

speed =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ km / h **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(c)** 13 (km / h)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | 60 (km) | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | 40 (km) | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | 2 (h) | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | 3 (h) | B1 |
> | (c) | (speed =) distance / time in any form **OR** gradient of line | C1 |
> |  | 40 / 3 | C1 |
> |  | 13 (km / h) | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (b)** All four parts were answered very well with most candidates gaining full credit.
>
> **(c)** Many correct answers using the equation *speed* = *distance* / *time* and showing full working were seen. In almost every case, incorrect answers had no working, so no credit could be given.
