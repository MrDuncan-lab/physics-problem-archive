> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** A large number of correct answers were seen. A common error was interchanging the locations of the electrons and neutrons.
>
> **(ii)** Candidates generally lacked knowledge of term ‘isotope’. Some confusion between an ion and an isotope was seen.
>
> **(b)** A significant number of candidates gave sources that were too vague to credit, e.g. “industry”, “reactions”, “factories”, “hospitals” and often “radon gas”, which was given in the question.
