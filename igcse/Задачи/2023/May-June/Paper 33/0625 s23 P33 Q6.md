> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** A large number of correct answers were seen but a common error was γ-ray (i.e. the symbol γ not the word gamma). A noticeable number of candidates did not give an answer.
>
> **(ii)** Many candidates lacked the knowledge for this question. In addition, again a number of candidates did not give a response.
>
> **(b) (i)** Most candidates knew another use for X-rays, but some answers were too vague, e.g. “to see through things”, “security”. More detailed answers were required.
>
> **(ii)** Few candidates displayed sufficient knowledge about the required precautions. Most answers were at the level of “wear special glasses”, “protect their eyes”, “use special protection” and “have enough protection”.
