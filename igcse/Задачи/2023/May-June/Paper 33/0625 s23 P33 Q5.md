# Question 5 (10 marks)

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить длину волны, усреднить измерения времени колебаний, рассчитать частоту и скорость водяных волн.

A teacher demonstrates the behaviour of waves by using water waves in a ripple tank.

Fig. 5.1 shows a cross-section through part of the water waves.

![[0625-s23-33-p6-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 5.1** (not to scale)

**(a)** Calculate the wavelength of the water waves. Use the information in Fig. 5.1.

wavelength =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm **[2]**

**(b)** The teacher places a pointer above the water waves as shown in Fig. 5.2.

![[0625-s23-33-p6-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 5.2** (not to scale)

Three students use stop-watches to measure the time taken for 50 peaks to pass the pointer. Fig. 5.3 shows the measurements.

![[0625-s23-33-p6-f3.png]]

time =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ s time =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ s time =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ s

**Fig. 5.3**

**(i)** On the line below each stop-watch, state the time measurement, in seconds. **[1]**

**(ii)** Calculate the average of the three time measurements in **(b)(i)**.

average time =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ s **[2]**

**(iii)** Calculate the frequency of the water waves using your result in **(b)(ii)**.

frequency =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ Hz **[2]**

**(c)** The teacher repeats the demonstration using a different ripple tank and obtains these results for the waves.

wavelength = 0.025 m frequency = 2.4 Hz

Calculate the speed of the wave.

speed of wave =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ m / s **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** 2.6 (cm)
>
> **(b)(ii)** 17.18 (s)
>
> **(b)(iii)** 2.9 (Hz)
>
> **(c)** 0.06(0) (m/s)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | 18.2 ÷ 7 | C1 |
> |  | 2.6 (cm) | A1 |
> | (b)(i) | 17.24 **AND** 17.14 **AND** 17.16 (s) | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | (17.24 + 17.14 + 17.16) ÷ 3 **OR** 51.54 ÷ 3 | C1 |
> |  | 17.18 (s) | A1 |
> | (b)(iii) | 50 ÷ 17.18 | C1 |
> |  | 2.9 (Hz) | A1 |
> | (c) | (speed =) frequency × wavelength in any form | C1 |
> |  | 2.4 × 0.025 | C1 |
> |  | 0.06(0) (m/s) | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** The majority of candidates gave the correct answer but there was often a lack of working here.
>
> **(b) (i) (ii)** The majority of candidates gave the correct answers to these questions.
>
> **(iii)** Very few candidates recognised that the frequency was equal to the number of waves (peaks) passing the pointer divided by the time taken to pass the pointer.
>
> **(c)** Candidates who were able to recall the wave equation generally achieved full credit. A significant number of candidates did not give an answer.
