**(b)** Fig. 8.2 shows a circuit including a battery, a fixed resistor R and an ammeter.

![[0625-s23-33-p11-f6.svg]]

**Fig. 8.2**

The reading on the ammeter is 0.38 A. The potential difference across the fixed resistor R is 12 V.

**(i)** Calculate the resistance of the fixed resistor R.

resistance =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ Ω **[3]**

**(ii)** Calculate the electrical power transferred in the fixed resistor R. Include the unit.

power transferred =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ unit  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[4]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)(i)** 32 (Ω)
>
> **(b)(ii)** 4.6
