# Question 4 (9 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2023 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 3 · вопрос 4  
Источник: [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_qp_33.pdf#page=5|условие, стр. 5]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_ms_33.pdf#page=8|схема оценивания, стр. 8]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_er.pdf#page=26|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 s23 P33]]  
Темы: [[2.2.1 Thermal expansion of solids, liquids and gases]], [[2.1.3 Gases and the absolute scale of temperature]], [[1.8 Pressure]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Описать расширение жидкости термометра, перевести температуру в кельвины, объяснить давление газа и рассчитать силу на крышку.

A tight-fitting lid keeps air inside a metal can. An airtight rubber bung holds a liquid-in-glass thermometer that is inserted through a hole in the lid, as shown in Fig. 4.1.

![[0625-s23-33-p5-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 4.1**

**(a)** **(i)** State what happens to the liquid in the thermometer when the air temperature rises. **[1]**

**(ii)** The temperature of the air in the can is 18 °C.

Calculate the temperature of the air in kelvin.

temperature =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ K **[2]**

**(b)** The can is placed in a refrigerator. The temperature of the air inside the can decreases.

State and explain what happens to the pressure exerted by the air in the can. Use your ideas about gas particles. **[3]**

**(c)** The air in another can exerts a pressure of 102 000 N / m$^{2}$ on the lid. The area of the can lid is 0.0082 m$^{2}$.

Calculate the force on the lid due to the air in the can.

force =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ N **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)(ii)** 291 (K)
>
> **(c)** 840 (N)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | (liquid / it) expands | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | 273 + 18 | C1 |
> |  | 291 (K) | A1 |
> | (b) | pressure decreases | B1 |
> |  | any **two** from:<br>• particles slower / less kinetic energy<br>• collisions (with wall) less frequent<br>• collide (with wall) with less force | B2 |
> | (c) | (*F =*) *P* × *A* in any form | C1 |
> |  | 102 000 × 0.0082 | C1 |
> |  | 840 (N) | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** Very few candidates realised that the liquid would expand when the temperature increased.
>
> **(ii)** Most candidates gave the correct answer.
>
> **(b)** Most candidates realised that the pressure would decrease. Fewer stated that this was because the particles were moving more slowly. Only a few linked the decrease in speed to less frequent collisions with the wall or collisions with less force.
>
> **(c)** Many correct answers were seen here, generally with full working shown. Other answers usually gained partial credit for recall of the pressure equation which was subsequently rearranged incorrectly.
