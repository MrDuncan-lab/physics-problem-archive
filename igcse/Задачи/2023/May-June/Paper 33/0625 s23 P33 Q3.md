> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать момент и плечо силы для двух уравновешенных грузов, определить массу по весу.

A student balances a beam on a pivot. They then balance block A and block B on the beam, as shown in Fig. 3.1.

![[0625-s23-33-p4-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 3.1** (not to scale)

**(a)** **(i)** The weight of block A is 0.14 N.

Show that the moment of block A about the pivot is approximately 0.8 N cm. **[3]**

**(ii)** The weight of block B is 0.19 N.

Calculate the distance *d* between the pivot and the centre of block B.

distance *d* =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm **[3]**

**(b)** The weight of block B is 0.19 N.

Calculate the mass of block B.

mass of block B =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ kg **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)(i)** 0.77 (N cm)
>
> **(a)(ii)** 4.1 (cm) **OR** 4.2 (cm)
>
> **(b)** 0.019 (kg)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | (moment =) force × (perpendicular) distance in any form | C1 |
> |  | 0.14 × 5.5 | C1 |
> |  | 0.77 (N cm) | A1 |
> | (a)(ii) | (sum of) ACM = (sum of) CM | C1 |
> |  | 0.19 × *X* = 0.77 **OR** 0.19 × *X* = 0.8 **OR** 0.19 × *X* = 0.14 × 5.5 | C1 |
> |  | 4.1 (cm) **OR** 4.2 (cm) | A1 |
> | (b) | *(m =) W* ÷ *g* **OR** *W* ÷ 9.8 in any form | C1 |
> |  | 0.19 ÷ 9.8 | C1 |
> |  | 0.019 (kg) | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** Full working is required for a ‘show that’ question and many candidates achieved full credit. Other candidates just gained partial credit for the equation. There were a significant number of candidates who did not give any answer.
>
> **(ii)** Most of the candidates who answered **(i)** correctly also gave the correct answer here. Other candidates needed more practice with cross multiplication. Again, several candidates did not offer an answer.
>
> **(b)** The majority of candidates attempted this question with many getting the correct answer. A common error was to multiply the weight by 9.8 instead of dividing by 9.8.
