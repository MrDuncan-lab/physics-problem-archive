# Question 2 (4 marks)

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Указать происхождение ветровой энергии, устройство её преобразования и преимущества перед угольной энергетикой.

Fig. 2.1 shows an engineer working with wind turbines.

![[0625-s23-33-p3-f1.png]]

**Fig. 2.1**

**(a)** Complete the sentences describing how electrical power is generated by energy in the wind.

**(i)** The source of the wind energy is  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(ii)** When the blades turn, electrical power is generated in the  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(b)** Describe **two** advantages, apart from cost, of generating electrical power by using wind turbines compared with using a coal-fired power station.

1  \_\_\_\_\_\_

2  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | (the) Sun | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | generator | B1 |
> | (b) | any **two** from:<br>• no gaseous / SO$_{2}$emissions / air pollution<br>• does not contribute to global warming / CO$_{2}$<br>• do not use fossil fuels<br>• renewable | B2 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** Few correct answers were seen. Candidates did not seem to recognise that the Sun is the source of wind energy.
>
> **(ii)** There appeared to be confusion between the workings of a generator and a turbine, with turbine being a common incorrect answer.
>
> **(b)** A significant number of candidates gave advantages that were too vague, e.g. “a clean source of energy”, “environmentally friendly”, “less contamination”. More detailed specific answers were required.
