# Question 6 (7 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2023 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 1 · вопрос 6  
Источник: [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_qp_31.pdf#page=8|условие, стр. 8]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_ms_31.pdf#page=11|схема оценивания, стр. 11]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_er.pdf#page=19|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

Темы: [[3.1 General properties of waves]], [[E.1 Measurements, tables, graphs, precision and planning an experiment]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Предложить точное измерение длины волны, рассчитать скорость и описать преломление волн на мелководье.

A student observes waves on the surface of water in a tank. The waves all have the same wavelength.

**(a)** The student measures the wavelength of the waves by measuring the distance between one peak and the next peak.

Describe a more accurate method for determining the wavelength. **[2]**

**(b)** The wavelength of the waves is 4.0 cm and their frequency is 6.0 Hz.

Calculate the wave speed.

wave speed =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm / s **[3]**

**(c)** Fig. 6.1 shows water waves in the tank travelling from deep water to shallow water.

![[0625-s23-31-p8-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 6.1**

State and explain what happens to the waves as they move from deep water to shallow water.

name of effect  \_\_\_\_\_\_

explanation  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** (speed =) 24 (cm / s)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | idea of measure more than one wavelength | B1 |
> |  | idea of dividing measurement by number of wavelengths (measured) | B1 |
> | (b) | (speed =) 24 (cm / s) | A3 |
> |  | (speed =) 6(.0) × 4(.0) | (C2) |
> |  | (*v* =) *f* × λ | (C1) |
> | (c) | (name of effect) refraction | B1 |
> |  | change of speed | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Few candidates recognised that more than one wavelength should be measured. The most common errors were to either simply state “measure the distance between consecutive peaks or troughs” or to use *v* = *f*λ.
>
> **(b)** The majority of candidates were able to correctly recall the equation *v* = *f*λ and then to evaluate the wave speed as 24 cm / s. The most common error was to use an incorrect rearrangement of the equation, e.g. some candidates divided the frequency by the wavelength or the wavelength by the frequency.
>
> **(c)** Most candidates correctly identified the process as refraction. The most common incorrect answer was diffraction, but a few candidates gave rarefaction as their answer. Very few candidates recalled that refraction was caused by a change in speed of the wavefronts.
