> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Упорядочить преобразования солнечной энергии в электроэнергию ветротурбины и назвать недостатки ветроэнергетики.

Electricity is distributed from wind turbines to homes and industry.

**(a)** Statements A–F describe the main stages in the transfer of energy from the Sun to electrical energy in a wind turbine generator. The statements A–F are **not** in the correct order.

A  Air moves from regions of high pressure to regions of low pressure.  
B  The turbine blades turn a generator.  
C  Energy from the Sun heats the atmosphere unevenly.  
D  Uneven heating of the atmosphere produces regions of different atmospheric pressure.  
E  The generator produces electrical energy.  
F  Moving air turns the turbine blades.

Complete the flow chart to describe how a wind turbine uses energy from the Sun to generate electrical energy. Insert the missing letters in the empty boxes.

**(b)** State **two** disadvantages, apart from cost, of using wind turbines to produce electrical energy for homes and industry.

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | C (energy from the Sun heats the atmosphere unevenly)<br>D<br>A<br>F (moving air turns the turbine blades)<br>B (the turbine blades turn a generator)<br>E (the generator produces electrical energy)<br>4 correct – 3 marks<br>3 or 2 correct – 2 marks<br>1 correct – 1 mark | B3 |
> | (b) | any **two** from:<br>• idea of large(r) area of land needed<br>• intermittent supply **OR** cannot work if wind too strong / weak<br>• idea that energy output is small / not very large<br>• (possible) harm to (migrating) birds<br>• difficult to maintain (particularly if off-shore)<br>• <u>noise</u> **OR** <u>visual</u> pollution | B2 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** This question was answered well by almost all candidates. Most candidates were able to work out that C had to be first, E was last and F came before B. Only a small number got the order wrong but most of these gained partial credit.
>
> **(b)** The vast majority of candidates recalled a sensible disadvantage, but some were unable to express themselves well enough to gain credit. Some responses were too vague and answers such as being dependent on weather or causing pollution did not score. Weaker candidates gave responses not connected to wind turbines e.g. “will not work at night”.
