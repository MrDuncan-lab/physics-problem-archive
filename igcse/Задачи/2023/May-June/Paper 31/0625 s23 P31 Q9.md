> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Опознать элементы цепи, рассчитать мощность лампы и добавить параллельно вторую лампу.

Fig. 9.1 shows a series circuit. Two of the components in the circuit are labelled.

![[0625-s23-31-p11-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 9.1**

**(a)** State the name of **two** other components in the circuit.

1  \_\_\_\_\_\_

2  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(b)** The current in the lamp is 0.40 A. The potential difference (p.d.) across the lamp is 6.0 V.

Calculate the power dissipated in the lamp.

power =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ W **[3]**

**(c)** Draw on Fig. 9.1 to show a lamp connected in parallel with the lamp in the circuit. Use the correct symbol. **[1]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** (power =) 2.4 (W)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | any **two** from:<br>• switch<br>• ammeter<br>• <u>variable resistor</u> | B2 |
> | (b) | (power =) 2.4 (W) | A3 |
> |  | (power =) 0.4(0) × 6(.0) | (C2) |
> |  | (power =) *I* × *V* | (C1) |
> | (c) | <u>lamp symbol drawn in parallel with lamp in circuit</u> | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** This question was answered well by most candidates. The most common mistake was to omit ‘variable’ from the variable resistor.
>
> **(b)** The majority of candidates were able to correctly recall the equation power = *V* × *I* and then to evaluate the power as 2.4 W. The most common error was to calculate resistance, i.e. *V* ÷ *I*, and to give an answer of 15.
>
> **(c)** This question was usually answered well. Very few candidates showed the lamps in series. A significant number of candidates wasted time in redrawing the circuit diagram in **Fig. 9.1**. Candidates should be reminded to draw on the printed image when instructed to do this in the question.
