> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объяснить давление газа столкновениями частиц и влияние увеличения числа частиц при постоянной температуре.

Fig. 5.1 represents some particles of a gas in a metal box. The arrows represent the directions of movement of the particles.

![[0625-s23-31-p7-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 5.1** (not to scale)

**(a)** Describe how the particles in Fig. 5.1 exert a pressure on the walls of the box. **[3]**

**(b)** The number of gas particles in the box increases. The temperature of the gas does **not** change.

State and explain the effect, if any, on the pressure exerted by the gas particles on the walls of the box. **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** <u>(as) more (frequent) collisions (with walls of box)</u>

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | any **three** from:<br>• idea of (continuous) random movement (of gas particles)<br>• collisions / impacts (of particles)<br>• (collisions) with wall(s) of box<br>• idea that force is produced (by colliding particles)<br>• idea that pressure is force on an area | B3 |
> | (b) | pressure increases | M1 |
> |  | <u>(as) more (frequent) collisions (with walls of box)</u> | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** The simple kinetic molecular model of matter was well understood, and most candidates gained full credit.
>
> **(b)** This was generally answered well, with most candidates stating that the pressure increased as result of more collisions of particles with the walls. The most common error was to state that the pressure decreased.
