**(b)** A museum displays an item made of ancient wood. When the wood was new, the item contained 8.00 mg of the isotope shown in Fig. 11.1. The item now contains 2.00 mg of the isotope. The half-life of the isotope is 5700 years.

Calculate the age of the wood in the item.

age of wood =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ years **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** (2 × 5700 =) 11 400 (years)

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** The majority of candidates gained at least partial credit. They demonstrated a good understanding of atomic structure. The most common error was to state that the number of neutrons was 14 or to interchange the charges on the electron and the neutron.
>
> **(b)** Many candidates struggled with this calculation. The concept of half-life was not well understood by many candidates. Candidates should be encouraged to calculate and then clearly state the number of half-lives that are being used in this type of calculation.
