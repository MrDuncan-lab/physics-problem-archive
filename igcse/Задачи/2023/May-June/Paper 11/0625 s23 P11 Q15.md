Источник: [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_qp_11.pdf#page=6|условие, стр. 6]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_ms_11.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_er.pdf#page=1|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Описать испарение более энергичных молекул и охлаждение оставшейся жидкости.

Which statements about evaporation of water are correct?

1 Evaporation causes the remaining liquid to cool.

2 During evaporation, the more energetic particles escape from the surface of the

liquid.

3 Evaporation only happens at 100 °C.

**A** 1, 2 and 3  
**B** 1 and 2 only  
**C** 1 and 3 only  
**D** 2 and 3 only

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Many candidates chose the correct option. However, other candidates confused boiling and evaporation and incorrectly chose option **D**.
