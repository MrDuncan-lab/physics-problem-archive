> Определить перенос электронов и итоговые заряды стержня и ткани при электризации трением.

A plastic rod is rubbed with a cloth.

![[0625-s23-11-p10-f1.svg]]

The rod and the cloth both become charged as electrons move between them.

The rod becomes negatively charged.

Which diagram shows how the rod becomes negatively charged and shows the final charge on the cloth?

![[0625-s23-11-p10-f2.svg]]

![[0625-s23-11-p10-f3.svg]]

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Most candidates correctly identified that the final charge on the cloth would be positive and chose either option **A** or option **B**. However, some candidates had the misconception that the electrons would move from the negative rod to the positive cloth, possibly because they confused the charging of the rod with a situation where negative charges are attracted to positive charges.
