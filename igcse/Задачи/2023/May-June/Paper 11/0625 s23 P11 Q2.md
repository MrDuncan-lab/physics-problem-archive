> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить пройденное расстояние по графику движения.

The graph represents the motion of a vehicle.

![[0625-s23-11-p2-f1.svg]]

What is the distance travelled by the vehicle in 400 s?

**A** 20 m  
**B** 400 m  
**C** 4000 m  
**D** 8000 m

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Stronger candidates answered this correctly. However, a number of other candidates just tried to use distance = speed × time and therefore chose the incorrect option **D**.
