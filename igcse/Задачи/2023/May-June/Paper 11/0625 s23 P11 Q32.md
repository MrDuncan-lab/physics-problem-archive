> Определить направление силы на проводник относительно тока и магнитного поля.

The diagram shows a vertical wire carrying a current *I* placed between the poles of a magnet.

![[0625-s23-11-p14-f1.svg]]

What is the direction of the force on the wire exerted by the magnetic field?

**A** from N to S  
**B** from S to N  
**C** horizontal and at right angles to the direction from N to S  
**D** parallel to the wire

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
