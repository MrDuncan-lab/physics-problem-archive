# Question 19 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2023 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 1 · вопрос 19  
Источник: [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_qp_11.pdf#page=8|условие, стр. 8]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_ms_11.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_er.pdf#page=1|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 s23 P11]]  
Темы: [[3.2.3 Thin lenses]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить размер и ориентацию действительного изображения после перемещения предмета возле собирающей линзы.

An object O is placed at point P near to a thin converging lens. The diagram shows three rays

from the top of O passing through the lens. Each point F is one focal length from the centre of the lens. Each point 2F is two focal lengths from the centre of the lens.

![[0625-s23-11-p8-f1.svg]]

The object O is moved to point Q on the diagram.

Which type of image is produced when the object O is at point Q?

**A** inverted and the same size as the object  
**B** inverted and enlarged  
**C** upright and the same size as the object  
**D** upright and enlarged

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Many candidates correctly identified that as the object moves closer to the lens, but remains outside the principal focus, the image will increase in size.
