Which unit is a unit of weight?

**A** kilogram  
**B** kilojoule  
**C** kilometre  
**D** kilonewton

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Only stronger candidates answered this correctly, with the majority confusing weight and mass and therefore choosing option **A.**
