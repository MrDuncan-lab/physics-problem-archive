# Question 9 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2023 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 1 · вопрос 9  
Источник: [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_qp_11.pdf#page=5|условие, стр. 5]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_ms_11.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_er.pdf#page=1|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Оценить достаточность одного подвеса для определения центра тяжести неправильной пластины.

An irregularly shaped metal plate is freely suspended from a point P and is in equilibrium, as shown. Point Q is vertically below P.

![[0625-s23-11-p5-f1.svg]]

Which statement about the position of the centre of gravity of the metal plate is correct?

**A** It is at P.  
**B** It is at Q.  
**C** It is halfway between P and Q.  
**D** Further investigation is needed to determine its position.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Candidates should be familiar with an experiment to determine the position of the centre of gravity of an irregularly shaped object (syllabus statement 1.5.4). However, the majority of candidates did not recognise that suspending the object from just one point is not sufficient to determine the position of the centre of gravity, and instead chose option **C**.
