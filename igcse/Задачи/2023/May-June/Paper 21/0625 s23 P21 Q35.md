# Question 35 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2023 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 1 · вопрос 35  
Источник: [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_qp_21.pdf#page=14|условие, стр. 14]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_ms_21.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_er.pdf#page=10|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 s23 P21]]  
Темы: [[5.2.1 Detection of radioactivity]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать скорость счёта радиоактивного источника с поправкой на фон.

A radioactive source is placed near a detector connected to a counter.

210 counts are recorded by the counter in 3 minutes.

The background count rate is 20 counts per minute (cpm).

What is the corrected count rate for the radioactive source?

**A** 50 cpm  
**B** 70 cpm  
**C** 190 cpm  
**D** 270 cpm

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> In this question, candidates had to work out the corrected count rate for a radioactive source when given the number of counts in three minutes and the background count rate (syllabus statement 5.2.1.5). Many candidates either worked out the count rate but forgot to correct for background or worked out the count rate due to the source and forgot to subtract the background count from this.
