# Question 21 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2023 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 1 · вопрос 21  
Источник: [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_qp_21.pdf#page=8|условие, стр. 8]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_ms_21.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_er.pdf#page=10|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

Темы: [[3.1 General properties of waves]], [[3.3 Electromagnetic spectrum]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать диапазон частот видимого света по заданному диапазону длин волн.

Visible light has wavelengths in the range 4.0 × 10$^{\text{–7}}$ m to 7.0 × 10$^{\text{–7}}$ m.

What is the range of the frequencies of visible light?

**A** 0.12 Hz to 0.21 Hz  
**B** 120 Hz to 210 Hz  
**C** 4.3 × 10$^{11}$ Hz to 7.5 × 10$^{11}$ Hz  
**D** 4.3 × 10$^{14}$ Hz to 7.5 × 10$^{14}$ Hz

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question required candidates to recall the speed of light (syllabus statement 3.3.6) and rearrange the equation for wave speed (syllabus statement 3.1.4) to calculate frequency. Stronger candidates were able to do this correctly.
