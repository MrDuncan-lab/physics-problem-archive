> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить постоянство сопротивления металлической проволоки по её вольт-амперной характеристике.

The diagram shows the current–voltage graph for a metal wire.

![[0625-s23-21-p10-f1.svg]]

What can be deduced from the graph?

**A** As voltage increases, the temperature of the wire increases.  
**B** As voltage increases, the temperature of the wire decreases.  
**C** As voltage increases, the resistance of the wire increases.  
**D** As voltage increases, the resistance of the wire remains constant.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Some candidates could interpret the graph and deduced the correct relationship between the voltage across a wire and its resistance. However, other candidates had the misconception that as the voltage increases the resistance of the wire increases.
