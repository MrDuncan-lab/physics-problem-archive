# Question 7 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2023 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 1 · вопрос 7  
Источник: [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_qp_21.pdf#page=4|условие, стр. 4]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_ms_21.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_er.pdf#page=10|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 s23 P21]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить направление ускорения по силам, действующим на книгу в поезде.

A train is travelling horizontally in a straight line. A book is on a table in the train.

The diagram shows all the forces acting on the book.

![[0625-s23-21-p4-f1.svg]]

How is the train moving?

**A** accelerating to the left of the diagram  
**B** accelerating to the right of the diagram  
**C** moving at uniform speed to the left of the diagram  
**D** moving at uniform speed to the right of the diagram

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Candidates found this question challenging. The question required candidates to realise that the frictional force is preventing the book moving to the left relative to the table because the table is accelerating to the right. Many candidates had the misconception that the train must be moving to the left, possibly because they thought that the movement of the train would be opposite to the frictional force.
