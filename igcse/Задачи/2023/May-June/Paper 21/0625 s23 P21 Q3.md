> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить пройденное расстояние по графику движения автомобиля.

The graph represents the motion of a vehicle.

![[0625-s23-21-p2-f2.svg]]

What is the distance travelled by the vehicle in 400 s?

**A** 20 m  
**B** 400 m  
**C** 4000 m  
**D** 8000 m

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> While stronger candidates answered this correctly, others chose the incorrect option **D**, as they used distance = speed × time. Candidates should be aware that this equation can only be used when the speed is constant. To calculate the distance travelled from a speed–time graph, they should use the area under the graph.
