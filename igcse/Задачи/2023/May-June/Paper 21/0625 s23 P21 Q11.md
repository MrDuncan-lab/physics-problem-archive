> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать скорость человека по массе и кинетической энергии.

A woman of mass 50 kg has 81 J of kinetic energy.

What is her speed?

**A** 1.3 m / s  
**B** 1.6 m / s  
**C** 1.8 m / s  
**D** 3.2 m / s

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question required candidates to rearrange the equation for kinetic energy in order to determine the speed. Weaker candidates chose the incorrect option **B** as they just divided the kinetic energy by the mass of the woman. For numerical questions, candidates would benefit from writing down the relevant equation and their calculations to work out the answer before looking at the different options.
