> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать давление воды у основания плотины по глубине и плотности.

A dam holds water in a reservoir. The height of the water in the reservoir is 15 m.

![[0625-s23-21-p5-f1.svg]]

The density of water is 1000 kg / m$^{3}$.

What is the pressure due to the water at the bottom of the dam?

**A** 6.8 Pa  
**B** 1500 Pa  
**C** 15 000 Pa  
**D** 150 000 Pa

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> For this question, candidates had to use an equation to calculate the pressure at the bottom of a dam. Most stronger candidates chose the correct option. Weaker candidates chose the incorrect option **C**, as they used an incorrect equation and they did not multiply by the gravitational field strength.
