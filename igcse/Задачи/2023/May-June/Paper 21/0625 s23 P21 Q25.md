# Question 25 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2023 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 1 · вопрос 25  
Источник: [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_qp_21.pdf#page=10|условие, стр. 10]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_ms_21.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_er.pdf#page=10|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

Темы: [[4.2.4 Resistance]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить пропорциональную зависимость сопротивления проволоки от длины и площади сечения.

A wire has a uniform circular cross-sectional area.

Which statement is correct?

**A** The resistance of the wire is directly proportional to its cross-sectional area and inversely

proportional to its diameter.

**B** The resistance of the wire is directly proportional to its cross-sectional area and inversely

proportional to its length.

**C** The resistance of the wire is directly proportional to its length and inversely proportional to its

cross-sectional area.

**D** The resistance of the wire is directly proportional to its length and inversely proportional to its

diameter.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question assessed candidates’ knowledge of the relationship between the resistance of a wire, its length and cross-sectional area. Most candidates knew that resistance is directly proportional to the length of the wire, but a few candidates incorrectly thought that the resistance is inversely proportional to the diameter of the wire.
