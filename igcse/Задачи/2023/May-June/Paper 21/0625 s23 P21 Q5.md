# Question 5 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2023 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 1 · вопрос 5  
Источник: [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_qp_21.pdf#page=3|условие, стр. 3]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_ms_21.pdf|схема оценивания]]  
Работа: [[0625 s23 P21]]  
Темы: [[1.3 Mass and weight]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Связать одинаковые показания пружинных весов с одинаковым весом продуктов.

A shopkeeper pours rice into a dish that hangs from a spring balance. He records the reading.

![[0625-s23-21-p3-f1.svg]]

A customer buys some pasta. The shopkeeper notices that the reading on the spring balance, with just pasta in the dish, is the same as it was with just rice in the dish.

Which quantity **must** be the same for the rice and for the pasta?

**A** density  
**B** temperature  
**C** volume  
**D** weight

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
