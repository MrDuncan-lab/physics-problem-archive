> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнить массы, создающие одинаковое растяжение пружины на Земле и Луне.

On the Earth, a spring stretches by 5.0 cm when a mass of 3.0 kg is suspended from one end.

The gravitational field strength on the Moon is $\dfrac{1}{6}$ of that on the Earth.

Which mass, on the Moon, would stretch the spring by the same extension?

**A** 0.50 kg  
**B** 3.0 kg  
**C** 5.0 kg  
**D** 18 kg

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
