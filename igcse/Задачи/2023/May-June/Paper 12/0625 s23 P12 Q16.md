# Question 16 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2023 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 16

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объяснить сохранение холодной воды под нагреваемой поверхностью её низкой теплопроводностью.

A teacher puts some cold water in a test-tube.

She holds the bottom of the test-tube while heating the top.

![[0625-s23-12-p7-f1.svg]]

The water at the top boils but she continues to hold the test-tube as the bottom remains cold.

Which conclusion about water is made from this experiment?

**A** Water is a bad conductor.  
**B** Water is a bad convector.  
**C** Water is a good conductor.  
**D** Water is a good convector.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Although some candidates answered this correctly, others had the misconception that water is a bad convector and chose option **B**.
