# Question 21 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2023 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 21  
Источник: [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_qp_12.pdf#page=9|условие, стр. 9]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_ms_12.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_er.pdf#page=4|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 s23 P12]]  
Темы: [[3.3 Electromagnetic spectrum]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Соотнести электромагнитные волны с медицинским сканированием и дистанционным управлением.

The two devices shown use different types of electromagnetic waves.

medical scanning remote controller

![[0625-s23-12-p9-f3.svg]]

Which types of waves are used in these devices?

|  | medical scanning | remote controller |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | ultraviolet | infrared |
| **B** | ultraviolet | microwaves |
| **C** | X-rays | infrared |
| **D** | X-rays | microwaves |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Most stronger candidates correctly identified that X-rays are used in medical scanning. However, many other candidates had the misconception that microwaves are used in a remote controller and chose option **D.**
