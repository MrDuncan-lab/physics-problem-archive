# Question 12 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2023 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 12  
Источник: [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_qp_12.pdf#page=6|условие, стр. 6]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_ms_12.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_er.pdf#page=4|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

Темы: [[1.7.4 Power]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбрать работу и время как величины для расчёта мощности подъёма по лестнице.

Which two physical quantities must be used to calculate the power developed by a student

running up a flight of steps?

**A** force exerted and the vertical height of the steps only  
**B** force exerted and the time taken only  
**C** work done and the vertical height of the steps only  
**D** work done and the time taken only

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Stronger candidates recalled the two quantities from syllabus statement 1.7.4.1 used to calculate the power developed by the student. However, other candidates had the misconception that the vertical height of the steps was required in the calculation.
