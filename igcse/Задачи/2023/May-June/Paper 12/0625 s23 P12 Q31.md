Источник: [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_qp_12.pdf#page=12|условие, стр. 12]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_ms_12.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_er.pdf#page=4|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

Темы: [[4.5.1 Electromagnetic induction]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбрать движение магнита, создающее наибольшее индуцированное напряжение в катушке.

A student investigates the output voltage induced across a coil of wire by a bar magnet.

When will the induced voltage have the greatest value?

**A** The student slowly moves the bar magnet into the coil of wire.  
**B** The student leaves the bar magnet stationary in the coil of wire.  
**C** The student quickly removes the bar magnet from the coil of wire.  
**D** The student places the bar magnet at rest outside the coil of wire.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Few candidates answered this correctly and a large number of candidates had the misconception that moving a bar magnet slowly into a coil of wire will induce the greatest voltage.
