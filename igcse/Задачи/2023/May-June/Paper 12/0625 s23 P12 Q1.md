# Question 1 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2023 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 1  
Источник: [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_qp_12.pdf#page=2|условие, стр. 2]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_ms_12.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_er.pdf#page=4|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбрать приборы для определения объёма куба и жидкости.

Which single apparatus is used to find the volume of a solid cube and which single apparatus is used to find the volume of a quantity of liquid?

|  | volume of solid cube | volume of liquid |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | balance | balance |
| **B** | balance | measuring cylinder |
| **C** | ruler | balance |
| **D** | ruler | measuring cylinder |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Most candidates recognised that a measuring cylinder was needed to find the volume of a liquid. However, more candidates believed that a balance was needed to find the volume of a solid cube instead of a ruler and chose option **D**, as they were possibly thinking about determining the density instead.
