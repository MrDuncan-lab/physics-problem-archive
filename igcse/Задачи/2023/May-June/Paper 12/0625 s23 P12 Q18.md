> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Связать угол отражения с углом между падающим лучом и нормалью.

A light ray strikes a plane mirror and is reflected.

Which angle is always equal in size to the angle of reflection?

**A** the angle between the incident ray and the mirror  
**B** the angle between the incident ray and the normal to the mirror  
**C** the angle between the reflected ray and the mirror  
**D** the angle between the reflected ray and the incident ray

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Stronger candidates gave the correct response to this question on the reflection of a light ray. It was notable that the responses of other candidates were evenly distributed across the four possible responses indicating that some had guessed the answer.
