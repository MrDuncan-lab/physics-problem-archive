> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить пройденное автомобилем расстояние по графику движения.

The graph represents the motion of a car.

![[0625-s23-12-p3-f1.svg]]

How far has the car moved between 0 and 5 s?

**A** 2 m  
**B** 10 m  
**C** 25 m  
**D** 50 m

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Only stronger candidates knew that the area under the speed–time graph represented the distance travelled. Other candidates attempted to use distance = speed × time and chose the incorrect option **D**.
