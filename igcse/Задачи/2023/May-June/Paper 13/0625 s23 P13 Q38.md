Which row about the orbits of the Earth and the Moon is correct?

|  | approximate time for the Earth to orbit the Sun | approximate time for the Moon to orbit the Earth |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | 1 day | 30 days |
| **B** | 30 days | 1 day |

| **D** | 365 days | 30 days |

> [!answer]- Ответ
