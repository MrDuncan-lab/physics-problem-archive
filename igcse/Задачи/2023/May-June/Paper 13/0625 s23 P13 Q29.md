# Question 29 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2023 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 3 · вопрос 29  
Источник: [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_qp_13.pdf#page=11|условие, стр. 11]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_ms_13.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_er.pdf#page=7|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 s23 P13]]  
Темы: [[4.4 Electrical safety]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить правильное подключение предохранителя и выключателя сетевого нагревателя.

An electric heater has a metal frame.

The heating element is connected to the live and neutral wires of an a.c. supply. The metal frame is connected to the earth wire.

Which row gives the correct connections for the fuse and the switch?

|  | fuse | switch |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | in the earth | in the live |
| **B** | in the earth | in the neutral |
| **C** | in the live | in the live |
| **D** | in the live | in the neutral |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question about electrical safety was challenging for most candidates with only the strongest candidates answering correctly. Other candidates had the misconception that the switch was connected in the neutral wire and the fuse was connected in the earth wire.
