> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить путь при постоянной скорости по графику скорости от времени.

The speed–time graph shows the motion of an object.

![[0625-s23-13-p2-f1.svg]]

How far does the object travel at constant speed?

**A** 25 m  
**B** 50 m  
**C** 75 m  
**D** 125 m

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The majority of candidates were aware that the distance travelled had something to do with the area under the graph, but some candidates misinterpreted the first five seconds of the graph as where the object travelled at constant speed so found the distance travelled during that time (option **A**). Some other candidates attempted to use the equation distance travelled = speed × time for the whole graph and just multiplied 5 by 25 (option **D**).
