# Question 17 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2023 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 3 · вопрос 17  
Источник: [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_qp_13.pdf#page=7|условие, стр. 7]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_ms_13.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_er.pdf#page=7|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 s23 P13]]  
Темы: [[3.1 General properties of waves]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить тип поверхностных волн и одинаковую скорость распространения в воде постоянной глубины.

A drop of water from a tap falls onto the surface of some water of constant depth.

![[0625-s23-13-p7-f2.svg]]

Water waves spread out on the surface of the water.

Which statement is correct?

**A** The waves are longitudinal and travel at the same speed in all directions.  
**B** The waves are longitudinal and travel more quickly in one direction than in others.  
**C** The waves are transverse and travel at the same speed in all directions.  
**D** The waves are transverse and travel more quickly in one direction than in others.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question assessed candidates’ knowledge of the nature of water waves. Most candidates had the misconception that water waves are longitudinal.
