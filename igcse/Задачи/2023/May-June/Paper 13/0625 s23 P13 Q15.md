> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Описать расположение и движение частиц вещества после конденсации.

Which statement about the particles of a substance after condensation is correct?

**A** They are close to each other and slide over each other.  
**B** They are close to each other and vibrate about fixed points.  
**C** They are far apart from each other and vibrate about fixed points.  
**D** They are far apart from each other and move freely within the container.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Candidates found this question about what happens to the particles in a substance after condensation challenging. The majority of candidates were aware that the particles would be close together and therefore chose either option **A** or option **B**. Some candidates chose the correct option **A**, but others had the misconception that the particles in a condensed substance vibrate about fixed points.
