# Question 5 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2023 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 3 · вопрос 5

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать плотность тела по показаниям весов и вытесненному объёму воды.

A measuring cylinder containing 50 cm$^{3}$ of water is put on a balance.

![[0625-s23-13-p3-f1.png]]

A solid object is put in the cylinder and the water level rises to 75 cm$^{3}$.

What is the density of the object?

**A** 0.80 g / cm$^{3}$  
**B** 2.4 g / cm$^{3}$  
**C** 2.8 g / cm$^{3}$  
**D** 8.4 g / cm$^{3}$

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question required candidates to determine the mass and the volume of the object and use these values to calculate its density and some candidates answered it correctly. However, other candidates worked out the correct mass but used the wrong density of 75 cm$^{3}$ and chose option **A**. Some candidates used the reading for the mass on the second balance with the correct volume and chose option **D**.
