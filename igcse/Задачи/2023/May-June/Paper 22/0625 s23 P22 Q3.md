> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить путь автомобиля за заданное время по графику движения.

The graph represents the motion of a car.

![[0625-s23-22-p3-f1.svg]]

How far has the car moved between 0 and 5 s?

**A** 2 m  
**B** 10 m  
**C** 25 m  
**D** 50 m

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question was answered correctly by many candidates. A higher proportion of weaker candidates chose incorrect option **D** because instead of calculating the area under the graph, they attempted to use distance = speed × time.
