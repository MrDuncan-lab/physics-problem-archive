# Question 27 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2023 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 2 · вопрос 27  
Источник: [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_qp_22.pdf#page=10|условие, стр. 10]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_ms_22.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_er.pdf#page=13|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

Темы: [[4.2.5 Electrical energy and electrical power]], [[4.2.2 Electric current]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать заряд, проходящий через электронагреватель, по мощности, напряжению и времени.

An electric fire is connected to a 240 V supply and transfers energy at a rate of 1.0 kW.

How much charge passes through the fire in 1.0 h?

**A** 42 C  
**B** 250 C  
**C** 1.5 × 10$^{4}$ C  
**D** 2.4 × 10$^{5}$ C

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question required candidates to use an equation to firstly calculate the current in the fire and then use that value to calculate the charge passing in the fire in 1 hour. The most common error was to just multiply the voltage of the supply by the power in watts.
