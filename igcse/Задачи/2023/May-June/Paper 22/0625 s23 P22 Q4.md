> Различить массу и вес и способы их сравнения весами.

Which statement about mass or weight is **not** correct?

**A** Masses can be compared using a balance.  
**B** Mass is a force.  
**C** Weights can be compared using a balance.  
**D** Weight is a force.

> [!answer]- Ответ
