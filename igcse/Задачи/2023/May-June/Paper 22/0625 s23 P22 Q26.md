# Question 26 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2023 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 2 · вопрос 26  
Источник: [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_qp_22.pdf#page=9|условие, стр. 9]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_ms_22.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_er.pdf#page=13|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнить сопротивления проволок при изменении длины и площади поперечного сечения.

A piece of metal wire X with a uniform diameter has resistance *R*.

![[0625-s23-22-p9-f2.svg]]

A second piece of wire Y is made of the same metal and has a uniform diameter.

Y has double the cross-sectional area of X and half the length of X.

![[0625-s23-22-p9-f3.svg]]

What is the resistance of Y?

**A** $\dfrac{R}{4}$  
**B** $\dfrac{R}{2}$  
**C** *R*  
**D** 4*R*

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question assessed candidates’ knowledge of the relationship between the resistance of a wire, its length and its cross-sectional area. Many candidates answered this correctly. Some weaker candidates chose option **B**, as they only took the effect of one change to the wire into account.
