> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать длину радиоволны по частоте вещания радиостанции.

A radio station broadcasts a signal with a frequency of 89 MHz.

What is the wavelength of this signal?

**A** 3.7 µm  
**B** 3.4 m  
**C** 3.7 km  
**D** 3.4 Mm

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Candidates had to recall and rearrange the equation for wave speed as well as recall the speed of light. A large majority of stronger candidates chose the correct answer, but weaker responses were fairly evenly distributed across the four options, indicating errors with unit prefixes and using the speed of sound instead of the speed of light.
