> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить изменение окружающей среды, включающее лампу в управляющей электрической цепи.

The diagram shows a circuit that switches on a lamp when there is a change in the environment.

![[0625-s23-22-p10-f2.svg]]

Which change in the environment causes the lamp to be switched on?

**A** a decrease in light intensity  
**B** a decrease in temperature  
**C** an increase in light intensity  
**D** an increase in temperature

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Candidates were assessed on their knowledge of LDRs and relays in this question. Stronger candidates chose the correct option while the responses from weaker candidates were distributed across the four options.
