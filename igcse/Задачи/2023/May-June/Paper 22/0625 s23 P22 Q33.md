# Question 33 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2023 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 2 · вопрос 33  
Источник: [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_qp_22.pdf#page=12|условие, стр. 12]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_ms_22.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_s23_er.pdf#page=13|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнить суммарные массы исходных ядер и продуктов деления и синтеза.

Fission and fusion are two types of nuclear process.

How does the total mass of the nuclides produced compare with the total mass of the original nuclide or nuclides in these nuclear processes?

|  | total mass of fission products compared to original nuclide | total mass of fusion products compared to original nuclides |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | same | same |
| **B** | more | less |
| **C** | less | more |
| **D** | less | less |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Only stronger candidates were aware that the total mass of the fission products and the fusion products was less than the original nuclide (option **D**).
