> Описать изменение движения частиц воздуха при нагревании.

Which statement describes what happens to air particles when the air is heated?

**A** The particles move more slowly.  
**B** The particles move more quickly.  
**C** The particles move closer together.  
**D** The particles vibrate faster.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
