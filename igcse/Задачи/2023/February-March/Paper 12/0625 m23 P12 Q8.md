> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Найти неизвестный вес по равновесию двух моментов на линейке.

A metre rule is balanced at its midpoint.

It remains balanced when a 3.0 N load is hung from the 40 cm mark and a second load is hung from the 80 cm mark.

What is the weight of the second load?

**A** 1.0 N  
**B** 1.5 N  
**C** 3.0 N  
**D** 9.0 N

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Only stronger candidates recognised that the data given in the question gave the markings on the meter rule at which the loads were placed and then used these raw figures directly into the moment equation.
