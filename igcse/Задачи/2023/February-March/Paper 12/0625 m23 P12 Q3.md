> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить ускорение свободного падения на разных малых высотах над Марсом.

A ball is dropped in a vacuum from a height of 4.0 m above the surface of Mars. The acceleration of the ball at a height of 2.0 m is 3.8 m / s$^{2}$.

What is the acceleration of the ball at a height of 1.0 m above the surface of Mars?

**A** 1.9 m / s$^{2}$  
**B** 3.8 m / s$^{2}$  
**C** 5.7 m / s$^{2}$  
**D** 7.6 m / s$^{2}$

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Some candidates did not seem to be aware that when an object is close to the surface of a planet the acceleration due to gravity is constant. It is only when the object is at a distance of a similar order of magnitude as the diameter of the planet that the gravitational field strength becomes less, and consequently the acceleration is less.
