> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбрать соотношение веса, сопротивления и подъёмной силы при равномерном всплытии тела.

An object is rising vertically at constant speed through water. There are three vertical forces acting on it: the weight *W*, the drag force *D*, and the upward force *U*.

Which diagram shows the magnitude and direction of the vertical forces acting on the object?

![[0625-m23-12-q6-options.svg]]

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> . Many candidates found this question challenging. They needed to identify the correct direction that the three forces (weight, drag and upthrust) act in. They also needed to recognise that the resultant force on an object moving at constant velocity is zero.
