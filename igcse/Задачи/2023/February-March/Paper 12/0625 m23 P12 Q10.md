Источник: [[_pdf/2023/0625_m23_qp_12.pdf#page=5|условие, стр. 5]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_m23_ms_12.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_m23_er.pdf#page=1|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 m23 P12]]  
Темы: [[1.7.3 Energy resources]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнить загрязнение атмосферы различными способами производства электроэнергии.

Which power station produces the greatest atmospheric pollution for each unit of energy

generated?

**A** a gas fired power station  
**B** a hydroelectric power station  
**C** a nuclear power station  
**D** a wind farm

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The majority of candidates recognised that the gas fired power station produced the most atmospheric pollution per unit of energy generated, but a significant number opted for the nuclear power plant. The actual atmospheric pollution produced by a nuclear power station produces relatively small amounts of such pollution.
