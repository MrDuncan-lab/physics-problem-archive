> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать давление блока на опору по весу и площади основания.

The diagram shows a rectangular block of weight 16 N. It is resting on a flat surface.

![[0625-m23-12-p6-f1.svg]]

What is the pressure at the base of the block due to its weight?

**A** 0.089 N / cm$^{2}$  
**B** 0.36 N / cm$^{2}$  
**C** 0.80 N / cm$^{2}$  
**D** 320 N / cm$^{2}$

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Many candidates did not select the correct data to solve the problem and simply multiplied the 3 dimensions of the object together, finding the volume of the object rather than the two dimensions in contact with the surface to give area in contact.
