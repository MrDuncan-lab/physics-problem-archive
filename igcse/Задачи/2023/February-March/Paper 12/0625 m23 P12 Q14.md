> Определить изменения плотности и давления газа при увеличении объёма с постоянной температурой.

A piston traps a mass of gas inside a cylinder. Initially, the piston is halfway along the length of

the cylinder.

The piston is now moved towards the open end of the cylinder. The temperature of the gas remains constant.

![[0625-m23-12-p6-f2.svg]]

How are the density and the pressure of the gas affected by moving the piston?

|  | density | pressure |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | decreases | decreases |
| **B** | decreases | unchanged |
| **C** | increases | decreases |
| **D** | increases | unchanged |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**
