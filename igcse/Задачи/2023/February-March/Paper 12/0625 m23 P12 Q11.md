> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать работу подъёма по весу груза и высоте.

A 500 N weight is raised through a height of 5 cm.

How much work is done by the force?

**A** 25 J  
**B** 100 J  
**C** 2500 J  
**D** 10 000 J

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Most candidates simply multiplied the raw data figures together, not recognising that the distance moved by the point of application of the force was measured in centimetres, rather than the base unit, the metre.
