> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнить длины волн и частоты красного и синего света.

Blue light has a typical wavelength of 5.0 × 10$^{\text{–7}}$ m and frequency of 0.60 × 10$^{15}$ Hz.

Which row gives a typical wavelength and frequency for red light?

|  | wavelength | frequency |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | 2.4 × 10$^{\text{–7}}$ m | 0.40 × 10$^{15}$ Hz |
| **B** | 2.4 × 10$^{\text{–7}}$ m | 1.3 × 10$^{15}$ Hz |
| **C** | 6.9 × 10$^{\text{–7}}$ m | 0.40 × 10$^{15}$ Hz |
| **D** | 6.9 × 10$^{\text{–7}}$ m | 1.3 × 10$^{15}$ Hz |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> In this question, the powers of ten seemed to confuse some candidates but stronger candidates were able to identify the frequency and wavelength of typical red light.
