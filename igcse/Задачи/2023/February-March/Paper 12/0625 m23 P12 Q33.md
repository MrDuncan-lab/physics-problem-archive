> Компенсировать ослабление магнитов двигателя изменением тока и числа витков катушки.

Over time, the strength of the magnets in an electric motor decreases.

Which row describes **two** ways to keep the motor running at its original speed?

|  | current in the coil | number of turns on the coil |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | decrease | decrease |
| **B** | decrease | increase |
| **C** | increase | decrease |
| **D** | increase | increase |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
