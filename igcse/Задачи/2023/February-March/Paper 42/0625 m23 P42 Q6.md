# Question 6 (8 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · February/March 2023 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 2 · вопрос 6  
Источник: [[_pdf/2023/0625_m23_qp_42.pdf#page=10|условие, стр. 10–11]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_m23_ms_42.pdf#page=9|схема оценивания, стр. 9]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_m23_er.pdf#page=9|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 m23 P42]]  
Темы: [[3.4 Sound]], [[4.2.2 Electric current]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Описать сгущения и разрежения звука, определить расстояние до грозы и заряд молнии.

**(a)** Sound waves have compressions and rarefactions.

Explain what is meant by compression and rarefaction.

compression  \_\_\_\_\_\_

rarefaction  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(b)** We can see light from the Sun but we cannot hear any sound from it.

State the reason for this. **[1]**

**(c)** During a thunderstorm, an observer sees the lightning almost immediately but hears the sound of the thunder several seconds later. The thunder and lightning are produced at the same time.

The sound of the thunder is heard 9.0 s after the lightning is seen. The speed of sound in air is 340 m / s.

Calculate the distance from the thunderstorm to the observer.

distance =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(d)** In a lightning strike, there is a current of 3.0 × 10$^{4}$ A for 48 ms.

Calculate the charge that flows.

charge =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(c)** 3100 m **OR** 3.1 km
>
> **(d)** 1400 C

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | (region where) particles are close(r) together (than normal) **OR** (region where) there is a great(er) pressure (than normal) | B1 |
> |  | (region where) particles are further / far apart (than normal) **OR** (region where) there is a low(er) pressure (than normal) | B1 |
> | (b) | light does not need a medium to travel through **OR** sound needs a medium to travel through (and there is no medium<br>between Sun and Earth) | B1 |
> | (c) | 3100 m **OR** 3.1 km | A2 |
> |  | *v* = *s* / *t* **OR** (*s* =) *vt* **OR** 340 × 9 | (C1) |
> | (d) | 1400 C | A3 |
> |  | *I* = *Q* / *t* **OR** (*Q* = )*I t* **OR** 3.0 × 10$^{4}$ × 48 × 10$^{\text{–3}}$ | (C1) |
> |  | (*t* =) 48 × 10$^{\text{–3}}$ OR (*t* =) 4.8 × 10$^{\text{–2}}$ **OR** (*t* =) 0.048 SEEN | (C1) |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Stronger answers clearly referred to particle separation or pressure changes. Weaker answers that were incorrect or insufficient referred to wave or wavefront separation or repeated wording from the question e.g., “particles are compressed”.
>
> **(b)** Most candidates answered this question well, clearly stating that sound needed a medium to travel or that light does not need a medium. Incorrect answers referred to the difference in the speed of sound or light to suggested that sound from the Sun is ultrasound.
>
> **(c)** This was answered correctly by most candidates. A number of candidates applied ideas about echoes or use of sonar which did not apply in this question. Some were unsuccessful in rearranging the equation for speed in terms of distance and time.
>
> **(d)** Fully correct answers required the knowledge to convert ms to s and the ability to rearrange the equation relating current to charge and time correctly. Some candidates multiplied the time in ms by 1000 to get time in s or more often divided by 1000 or by 60. Most candidates knew which equation to use. The use of the symbol C is not an acceptable alternative to Q for charge.
