> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить вес одинаковых мальчиков на качелях по весу отца и вертикальным перемещениям всех участников.

Two boys of equal weight sit on one side of a see-saw, as shown. Their father, of weight 1000 N, sits on the other side. The see-saw is balanced and is being used so that it moves up and down.

![[0625-m23-22-p4-f1.svg]]

During one part of the cycle, the father descends through a distance of 40 cm. At the same time, the boy nearest the pivot rises through 20 cm, while the other boy rises through 80 cm.

What is the weight of each boy?

**A** 200 N  
**B** 400 N  
**C** 600 N  
**D** 800 N

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
