> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнить первичные и вторичные напряжения и токи идеального понижающего трансформатора.

A 100% efficient step-down transformer has primary voltage *V*$_{\text{p}}$ and primary current *I*$_{\text{p}}$.

Which row compares the secondary voltage with *V*$_{\text{p}}$ and the secondary current with *I*$_{\text{p}}$?

|  | secondary voltage | secondary current |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | greater than *V*$_{\text{p}}$ | greater than *I*$_{\text{p}}$ |
| **B** | greater than *V*$_{\text{p}}$ | less than *I*$_{\text{p}}$ |
| **C** | less than *V*$_{\text{p}}$ | greater than *I*$_{\text{p}}$ |
| **D** | less than *V*$_{\text{p}}$ | less than *I*$_{\text{p}}$ |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
