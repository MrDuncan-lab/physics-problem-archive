> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Указать величины в формуле изменения гравитационной потенциальной энергии.

The equation used to find the change in gravitational potential energy of an object can be written

as

∆*E*$_{\text{P}}$ = *Y* × *Z* × ∆*h*

where ∆*E*$_{\text{P}}$ is the change in gravitational energy and ∆*h* is the change in height.

Which row gives the quantities *Y* and *Z*?

|  | *Y* | *Z* |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | mass | gravitational field strength |
| **B** | gravitational field strength | density |
| **C** | density | speed |
| **D** | speed | mass |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**
