# Question 30 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · February/March 2023 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 2 · вопрос 30  
Источник: [[_pdf/2023/0625_m23_qp_22.pdf#page=11|условие, стр. 11]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_m23_ms_22.pdf|схема оценивания]]  
Работа: [[0625 m23 P22]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбрать изменение движения проводника, увеличивающее индуцированную электродвижущую силу.

A wire is moved down in a direction perpendicular to the magnetic field.

![[0625-m23-22-p11-f2.svg]]

Three changes are suggested.

1 The speed of the movement of the wire is increased.

2 The magnetic field strength is decreased.

3 The direction of the magnetic field is reversed.

Which changes increase the electromotive force (e.m.f.) induced in the wire?

**A** 1 and 3  
**B** 1 only  
**C** 2 and 3  
**D** 3 only

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
