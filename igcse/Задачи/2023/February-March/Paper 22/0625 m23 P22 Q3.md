> Распознать постоянное ненулевое ускорение по расстояниям, пройденным за последовательные секунды.

Four objects are moving in a straight line.

The table shows the distances moved by each object in each second of its motion.

Which object is moving with constant non-zero acceleration?

|  | distance moved in 1st second / m | distance moved in 2nd second / m | distance moved in 3rd second / m | distance moved in 4th second / m |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | 5 | 5 | 5 | 5 |
| **B** | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 |
| **C** | 5 | 7 | 10 | 14 |
| **D** | 5 | 8 | 14 | 26 |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
