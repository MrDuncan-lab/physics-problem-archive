> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать показатель преломления пластика по условию полного внутреннего отражения.

Light in transparent plastic meets a boundary with air. Light is transmitted into the air only if the

angle marked θ in the diagram is greater than 36°.

![[0625-m23-22-p7-f2.svg]]

What is the refractive index of the plastic?

**A** 0.59  
**B** 0.81  
**C** 1.2  
**D** 1.7

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question was challenging for many candidates. The strongest candidates were able to work with trigonometrical quantities and managed the fact that the given angle was the complement of the angle of incidence, rather than the angle of incidence itself.
