# Question 4 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · February/March 2023 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 2 · вопрос 4  
Источник: [[_pdf/2023/0625_m23_qp_22.pdf#page=3|условие, стр. 3]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_m23_ms_22.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_m23_er.pdf#page=4|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать ускорение автомобиля и объяснить его последующее уменьшение из-за растущего сопротивления.

The drag force on a car increases with speed. At 20 m / s, the total drag force is 400 N. The mass of the car is 1200 kg and the driving force is constant at 700 N.

Which statement about the acceleration of the car at 20 m / s is correct?

**A** The acceleration is 0.25 m / s$^{2}$ but will decrease as time passes.  
**B** The acceleration is 0.25 m / s$^{2}$ but will increase as time passes.  
**C** The acceleration is 0.58 m / s$^{2}$ but will decrease as time passes.  
**D** The acceleration is 0.58 m / s$^{2}$ but will increase as time passes.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> In this question, initially the acceleration of the car needed to be calculated (= resultant force divided by mass). Then candidates needed to recognise that the drag force increases and consequently the resultant force decreases as the car goes faster.
