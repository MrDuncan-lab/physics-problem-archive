# Question 7 (5 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · February/March 2023 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 7  
Источник: [[_pdf/2023/0625_m23_qp_32.pdf#page=11|условие, стр. 11]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_m23_ms_32.pdf#page=10|схема оценивания, стр. 10]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_m23_er.pdf#page=6|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 m23 P32]]  
Темы: [[3.2.1 Reflection of light]], [[3.3 Electromagnetic spectrum]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Описать изображение в плоском зеркале, применение ультрафиолета и его вредное воздействие.

**(a)** A student places a book in front of a plane mirror.

State **three** characteristics of the image of the book formed by the plane mirror.

1  \_\_\_\_\_\_

2  \_\_\_\_\_\_

3  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

**(b)** Visible light is one region of the electromagnetic spectrum. Another region is ultraviolet radiation.

**(i)** Give **one** use of ultraviolet radiation. **[1]**

**(ii)** Give **one** possible harmful effect of excessive exposure to ultraviolet radiation. **[1]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | same size (as object / book) | B1 |
> |  | <u>same distance from mirror (as book / object)</u> | B1 |
> |  | virtual | B1 |
> | (b) | (use:) security marking OR detecting fake bank notes OR sterilising water | B1 |
> |  | (harmful effect:) damage to surface cells / skin OR eyes OR damage to cells / genes / DNA OR skin cancer | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** The majority of candidates found this question challenging. A common error was to say that the image was the same shape rather than the same size, and to simply state that the image was the same distance, i.e. not stating that the image was the same distance from the mirror as the object.
>
> **(b) (i)** The majority of candidates gave a valid use of ultraviolet radiation.
>
> **(ii)** Most candidates gave a valid harmful effect of overexposure to ultraviolet radiation.
