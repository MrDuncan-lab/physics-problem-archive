# Question 6 (9 marks)

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить амплитуду и частоту волны, описать поперечные колебания и рассчитать скорость распространения.

Fig. 6.1 shows how the displacement of a transverse wave varies with time.

![[0625-m23-32-p10-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 6.1**

**(a)** **(i)** Determine the amplitude of the wave in Fig. 6.1.

amplitude =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm **[1]**

**(ii)** Determine the frequency of the wave in Fig. 6.1.

frequency =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ Hz **[2]**

**(b)** Describe the motion of particles in a transverse water wave. **[3]**

**(c)** A wave has a frequency of 400 Hz and a wavelength of 0.90 m.

Calculate the velocity of the wave.

velocity =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ m / s **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)(ii)** (so frequency =) 5.0 (Hz)
>
> **(c)** (v =) 360 (m / s)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | 2.0 (cm) | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | (so frequency =) 5.0 (Hz) | A2 |
> |  | (frequency =) number of waves (sent out) per s / unit time OR<br>time for 1 wave = 0.2 s OR 2 waves in 0.4 s | (C1) |
> | (b) | vibrations OR oscillations (of particles) | B1 |
> |  | at right angles OR perpendicular | B1 |
> |  | to direction of propogation | B1 |
> | (c) | (v =) 360 (m / s) | A3 |
> |  | (v =) 400 × 0.90 | (C2) |
> |  | (*v* =) $f\lambda$ | (C1) |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** The majority of candidates identified the amplitude as 2.0 cm. A common error was to give 4.0 cm.
>
> **(ii)** Many candidates found this item more challenging, with only stronger candidates able to reason from frequency = number of waves sent out per second to give a frequency of 5.0 Hz.
>
> **(b)** Only the strongest candidates were able to give a correct description of the movement of particles in a transverse wave.
>
> **(c)** Many candidates correctly calculated the wave speed as 360 m / s. However, a very large number thought that wave speed = frequency ÷ wavelength.
