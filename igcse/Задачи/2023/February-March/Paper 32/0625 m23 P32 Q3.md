# Question 3 (6 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · February/March 2023 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 3

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить скорость велосипедиста по графику пути и объяснить преимущество широких шин на мягком грунте.

Fig. 3.1 shows the distance-time graph for a cyclist. The journey has two sections, PQ and QR.

![[0625-m23-32-p6-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 3.1**

**(a)** **(i)** Calculate the speed of the cyclist in section PQ.

speed =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ m / s **[3]**

**(ii)** Describe the motion of the cyclist in section QR on the graph. **[1]**

**(b)** Fig. 3.2 shows a bicycle fitted with wide tyres and a bicycle fitted with narrow tyres. The two bicycles have the same weight. People use bicycles fitted with wide tyres to ride over soft ground.

![[0625-m23-32-p7-f1.svg]]

bicycle with wide tyres bicycle with narrow tyres

**Fig. 3.2**

Explain why people use bicycles fitted with wide tyres to ride over soft ground. Use your ideas about pressure. **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)(i)** (speed =) 25 (m / s)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | (speed =) 25 (m / s) | A3 |
> |  | (speed =) 250 ÷ 10 | (C2) |
> |  | (speed =) gradient of d-t graph OR d ÷ t in any form | (C1) |
> | (a)(ii) | (QR –) at rest or stationary | B1 |
> | (b) | any **two** from<br>(wide tyres have) large (contact) area<br>(so) less pressure (on ground)<br>so less likely to sink (into soft ground) | B2 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** Many candidates correctly calculated the speed as 25 m / s from the gradient of the line. The most common error was to state that the speed was equal to the area under the line.
>
> **(ii)** Most candidates identified the cyclist as being stationary. The most common errors were steady movement, steady speed and steady acceleration.
>
> **(b)** Almost all candidates scored at least partial credit. A common error was to state that increased surface area resulted in better grip on soft ground.
