# Question 1 (10 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · February/March 2023 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 1  
Источник: [[_pdf/2023/0625_m23_qp_32.pdf#page=2|условие, стр. 2–3]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_m23_ms_32.pdf#page=7|схема оценивания, стр. 7]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_m23_er.pdf#page=6|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить среднюю ширину скобы, описать измерение объёма вытеснением воды и рассчитать плотность металла.

Fig. 1.1 shows two strips of staples.

![[0625-m23-32-p2-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 1.1** NOT to scale

**(a)** The width of one strip is 56 mm. There are 40 staples in the strip.

Calculate the average width of **one** staple.

average width of **one** staple =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ mm **[2]**

**(b)** A student wants to find the volume of one strip of 40 staples. The student has a measuring cylinder and a beaker of water as shown in Fig. 1.2.

![[0625-m23-32-p2-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 1.2**

Describe how the student can determine the volume of one strip of staples by using the equipment shown in Fig. 1.2. **[4]**

**(c)** The staples are made from a block of metal. The mass of the block is 296 g. The volume of the block is 33.2 cm$^{3}$. Calculate the density of the metal. Include the unit.

density of the metal =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ unit  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[4]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** (average thickness =) 1.4 (mm)
>
> **(c)** (ρ =) 8.92

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | (average thickness =) 1.4 (mm) | A2 |
> |  | (average thickness =) 56 ÷ 40 | (C1) |
> | (b) | any **three** from:<br>measuring cylinder (partially) filled with water<br>(initial) volume measured / noted<br>strip submerged in water owtte<br>(new / 2nd) volume (of strip and water) measured | B3 |
> |  | volume of strip = difference in volumes | B1 |
> | (c) | (ρ =) 8.92 | A3 |
> |  | (ρ =) 296 ÷ 33.2 | (C2) |
> |  | (density =) mass ÷ volume OR (ρ *=) m / V* in any form | (C1) |
> |  | g / cm$^{3}$ | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** The vast majority of candidates correctly calculated the average thickness as 1.4 mm. The most common error was to divide 40 by 56 to give an answer of 0.71.
>
> **(b)** Most candidates gained full credit here.
