# Question 8 (5 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · February/March 2023 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 8  
Источник: [[_pdf/2023/0625_m23_qp_32.pdf#page=12|условие, стр. 12]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_m23_ms_32.pdf#page=10|схема оценивания, стр. 10]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_m23_er.pdf#page=6|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 m23 P32]]  
Темы: [[4.2.1 Electric charge]], [[4.2.2 Electric current]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объяснить электризацию трением, определить знаки взаимодействующих зарядов и назвать носители тока заземления.

**(a)** A student uses a dry cloth to rub a plastic rod.

State how the plastic rod gains a positive charge from friction between the cloth and the rod. **[2]**

**(b)** Three balls, P, Q and R, are electrically charged. The balls are suspended by threads of insulating material. Fig. 8.1 shows the arrangement.

![[0625-m23-32-p12-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 8.1**

The charge on ball P is positive.

State the charge on ball Q and the charge on ball R.

ball Q  \_\_\_\_\_\_

ball R  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(c)** The student connects ball P to earth with a copper wire. Charges from the earth flow in the copper wire to ball P.

State the name of the electrically charged particles moving in the copper wire. **[1]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | negative charges OR electrons | B1 |
> |  | move from rod OR move to cloth | B1 |
> | (b) | (ball Q is) positive | B1 |
> |  | (ball R is) negative | B1 |
> | (c) | (free) electrons | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Many candidates answered fully correctly, but a significant number of candidates incorrectly talked about protons or positive charges moving to the rod.
>
> **(b)** The majority of candidates answered this correctly.
>
> **(c)** The majority of candidates recognised that electrons were moving in the metal wire. The most common error was to state protons.
