# Question 2 (7 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · February/March 2023 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 2  
Источник: [[_pdf/2023/0625_m23_qp_32.pdf#page=4|условие, стр. 4–5]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_m23_ms_32.pdf#page=7|схема оценивания, стр. 7]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_m23_er.pdf#page=6|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 m23 P32]]  
Темы: [[1.5.1 Effects of forces]], [[1.3 Mass and weight]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Прочитать график растяжения пружины, описать действие силы и рассчитать массу штатива по весу.

**(a)** A student has a spring of length 14.0 cm. She stretches the spring by adding different loads to the spring. She measures the length of the spring for each load. She plots a graph of the results. Fig. 2.1 shows the graph of her results.

![[0625-m23-32-p4-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 2.1**

**(i)** Use the graph to determine the length of the spring when the student adds a load of 8.0 N to the spring.

length of spring =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm **[1]**

**(ii)** Use the graph to determine the load added to the spring when the **extension** of the spring is 7.0 cm.

load for an extension of 7.0 cm =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ N **[2]**

**(b)** Complete the sentence about effects of forces. Choose a word from the box.

![[0625-m23-32-p5-f1.svg]]

charge mass power shape velocity

A load stretching a spring is an example of a force changing the size and the

\_\_\_\_\_\_ of an object. **[1]**

**(c)** A clamp stand used in the experiment has a weight of 8.6 N. Calculate the mass of the clamp stand.

mass of clamp stand =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ kg **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)(ii)** (load for length of 21 cm =) 9.3 (N)
>
> **(c)** (m = ) 0.88 (kg)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | (length of spring with 8.0 N load =) 20 (cm) | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | (load for length of 21 cm =) 9.3 (N) | A2 |
> |  | (extension of 7 cm = length of) 21 cm | (C1) |
> | (b) | shape | B1 |
> | (c) | (m = ) 0.88 (kg) | A3 |
> |  | (m = ) 8.6 ÷ 9.8 | (C2) |
> |  | W = mg OR (m =) W ÷ g | (C1) |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** The majority of candidates correctly determined the length of the spring with an 8.0 N load as 20 cm.
>
> **(ii)** Many candidates found this item challenging. Only stronger candidates realised that an extension of 7 cm gave a spring length of 21 cm.
>
> **(b)** Many candidates answered this correctly, but almost every other option was chosen by some candidates.
>
> **(c)** Most candidates correctly calculated the mass as 0.88 kg. The most common error was using an incorrect rearrangement of the equation, e.g. most common error was m = W × g.
