# Question 9 (9 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · February/March 2023 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 9  
Источник: [[_pdf/2023/0625_m23_qp_32.pdf#page=14|условие, стр. 14–15]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_m23_ms_32.pdf#page=11|схема оценивания, стр. 11]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_m23_er.pdf#page=6|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать ток чайника и стоимость израсходованной энергии, объяснить опасность перегрузки удлинителя.

Fig. 9.1 shows an electric kettle.

![[0625-m23-32-p14-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 9.1**

**(a)** **(i)** The power input of the kettle is 1.5 kW. The potential difference of the mains electrical supply for the kettle is 220 V.

Calculate the current in the kettle when it is switched on.

current in kettle =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ A **[4]**

**(ii)** The 1.5 kW kettle is used for a total of 4.0 hours. The cost of 1.0 kW h of electrical energy is 14 pennies (p).

Calculate the cost of the energy used by the kettle in 4.0 hours.

cost of energy =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ p **[3]**

**(b)** Fig. 9.2 shows an overloaded extension lead.

![[0625-m23-32-p15-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 9.2**

Explain the danger of connecting too many plugs to an extension lead. **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)(i)** (I =) 6.8 (A)
>
> **(a)(ii)** (cost of energy =) 84 (p)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | (I =) 6.8 (A) | A4 |
> |  | (I =) 1500 ÷ 220 | (C2) |
> |  | P = I × V OR (I =) P ÷ V | (C1) |
> |  | 1.5 kW = 1500 W | (C1) |
> | (a)(ii) | (cost of energy =) 84 (p) | A3 |
> |  | (cost of energy =) 1.5 × 4(.0) × 14 | (C2) |
> |  | (cost of energy =) power × time × cost of 1 kW h<br>OR<br>number of kW h × cost of 1 kW h | (C1) |
> | (b) | large current (in extension lead / socket) | B1 |
> |  | (can cause) overheating / fire | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** Many candidates scored at least partial credit. However, a number did not recall the equation P = I × V and instead attempted to use V = I × R. Those with the correct method often failed to convert 1.5 kW to 1500 W.
>
> **(ii)** Many candidates gave an answer of 84 p. However, a significant number of candidates did not recall the equation (cost of energy) = power × time × cost of 1 kW h.
>
> **(b)** Lack of detail and poorly phrased responses resulted in many candidates not gaining full credit here.
