# Question 1 (8 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · Specimen 2023 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended) · вопрос 1  
Источник: [[_pdf/2023/0625_y23_qp_4.pdf#page=2|условие, стр. 2]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_y23_ms_4.pdf#page=6|схема оценивания, стр. 6]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать ускорение и путь по графику скорости, объяснить изменение ускорения действующими силами.

Fig. 1.1 shows the speed-time graph for a vehicle accelerating from rest.

![[0625-y23-4-p2-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 1.1**

**(a)** Calculate the acceleration of the vehicle at time *t* = 30 s.

acceleration =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

**(b)** Without further calculation, state how the acceleration at time *t* = 100 s compares to the acceleration at time *t* = 10 s. Using ideas about forces, explain why any change in the acceleration has occurred. **[2]**

**(c)** Determine the distance travelled by the vehicle between time *t* = 120 s and time *t* = 160 s.

distance =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** acceleration in range 0.30 to 0.45 m / s$^{2}$
>
> **(c)** 1000 m

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | mention of gradient of graph at *t* = 30 s **OR** tangent drawn at *t* = 30 s and triangle drawn **OR** values of *t* and *v* taken from graph | C1 |
> |  | $\alpha = \dfrac{\Delta v}{\Delta t}$ **OR** calculation shown | C1 |
> |  | acceleration in range 0.30 to 0.45 m / s$^{2}$ | A1 |
> | (b) | acceleration less / at a slower rate | B1 |
> |  | less driving force **OR** greater resistive force / friction / air resistance / drag **AND** resultant force less | B1 |
> | (c) | area under graph / area under line | C1 |
> |  | distance = (20 × 40) + ($\dfrac{1}{2}$ × 40 × 10) **OR** $\dfrac{1}{2}$ × (30 + 20) × 40 | C1 |
> |  | 1000 m | A1 |
