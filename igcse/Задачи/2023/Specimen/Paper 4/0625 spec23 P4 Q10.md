> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Записать альфа-распад америция, обосновать выбор излучения для дымового датчика и вычислить время распада.

**(a)** **(i)** An americium (Am) nucleus decays by the emission of an α-particle into a neptunium (Np) nucleus.

Complete the nuclear equation for this decay.

${}^{241}_{95}\text{Am} \rightarrow$ **[2]**

**(ii)** Americium is used in smoke detectors. Explain why beta (β) emitters or gamma (γ) emitters are **not** used in smoke detectors. **[1]**

**(b)** The half-life of this americium nuclide is 470 years. A sample of this nuclide contains 8.0 × 10$^{14}$ atoms. After some time, 6.0 × 10$^{14}$ americium atoms have decayed.

Calculate the time required for this decay.

time =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** (2 × $10^{14}$ Am atoms remain after) 940 yrs or 2 half-lives

> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | ${}^{237}_{93}\text{Np}$ | B1 |
> |  | ${}^{4}_{2}\alpha$ | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | gamma and beta will not produce enough ions / ion pairs per cm | B1 |
> | (b) | (No of Am atoms remaining = 8 × $10^{14}$ – 6 × $10^{14}$) = 2 × $10^{14}$ | C1 |
