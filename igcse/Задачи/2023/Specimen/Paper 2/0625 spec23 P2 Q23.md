A ray of light travels through transparent plastic to air.

![[0625-y23-2-p8-f2.svg]]

NOT TO SCALE

The ray of light enters the air travelling parallel to the surface of the plastic.

The refractive index of the plastic is 1.25.

What is angle *θ* ?

**A** 37°  
**B** 39°  
**C** 51°  
**D** 53°

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
