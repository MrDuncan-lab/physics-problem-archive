> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Вычислить скорость звука в воде по глубине и времени возвращения отражённого импульса.

A pulse of sound is produced at the bottom of a boat. The sound travels through the water and is reflected from the sea bed. The sound reaches the boat again after 1.3 s. The sea bed is 1000 m below the boat.

![[0625-y23-2-p9-f1.svg]]

Using this information, what is the speed of sound in the water?

**A** 770 m / s  
**B** 1300 m / s  
**C** 1500 m / s  
**D** 2600 m / s

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
