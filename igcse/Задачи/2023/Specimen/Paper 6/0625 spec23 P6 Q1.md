**(e)** Another student thinks that the cooling rate is directly proportional to the percentage of the surface area uncovered. He draws a graph of cooling rate against the percentage of uncovered area to investigate this.

Describe how his graph line shows whether the rate of cooling and the percentage of uncovered surface area are directly proportional. **[2]**

**(f)** Students in other countries are doing the same experiment.

State **one** factor they must keep the same to obtain similar readings. **[1]**
