**(b)** A radioactive isotope has a half-life of 6.0 years. A sample of this isotope has a mass of 12 mg.

Calculate the mass of this isotope that remains in the sample after 18 years.

mass remaining =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ mg **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** 1.5 (mg)
