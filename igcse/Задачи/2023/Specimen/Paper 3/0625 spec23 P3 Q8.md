![[0625-y23-3-p11-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 8.1**

**(b)** Fig. 8.2 represents some wavefronts approaching a barrier with a narrow gap.

![[0625-y23-3-p11-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 8.2**

On Fig. 8.2, draw **three** wavefronts that have passed through the gap. **[3]**
