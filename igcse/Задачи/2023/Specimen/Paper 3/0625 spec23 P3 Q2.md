# Question 2 (10 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · Specimen 2023 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core) · вопрос 2  
Источник: [[_pdf/2023/0625_y23_qp_3.pdf#page=4|условие, стр. 4]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_y23_ms_3.pdf#page=6|схема оценивания, стр. 6]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать массу металла, описать измерение объёма вытеснением воды и определить плотность.

**(a)** A piece of metal has an irregular shape. The weight of the metal is 3.0 N.

Calculate the mass of the metal.

mass =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ kg **[2]**

**(b)** Fig. 2.1 shows the piece of metal, a measuring cylinder and a beaker containing water.

![[0625-y23-3-p4-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 2.1**

**(i)** Describe how to determine the volume of the piece of metal, using the equipment in Fig. 2.1. **[4]**

**(ii)** Explain why the procedure in **(b)(i)** is not suitable for finding the volume of a piece of low-density wood that is a similar shape and size to the piece of metal. **[1]**

**(iii)** The mass of another piece of metal is 405 g and its volume is 150 cm$^{3}$. Calculate the density of this piece of metal.

density =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ g / cm$^{3}$ **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** 0.31 (kg)
>
> **(b)(iii)** 2.7 (g / cm$^{3}$)

> |  | 0.31 (kg) | A1 |
> | (b)(i) | any **four** from:<br>partly fill the measuring cylinder with water **o.w.t.t.e.**<br>determine/read volume of water in measuring cylinder<br>(submerge/sink) metal in water<br>determine/read new volume of water (and metal)<br>find difference between final and initial volumes | B4 |
> | (b)(ii) | wood floats | B1 |
> | (b)(iii) | *D* = $\dfrac{M}{V}$ | C1 |
> |  | $\dfrac{405}{150}$ | C1 |
> |  | 2.7 (g / cm$^{3}$) | A1 |
