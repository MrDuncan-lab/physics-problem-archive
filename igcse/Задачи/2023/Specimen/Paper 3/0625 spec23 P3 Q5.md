> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объяснить повышение давления нагретого газа и вычислить давление бутылки на стол.

Fig. 5.1 shows a glass bottle containing air. The bottle is sealed with a cap. The mass of the air in the bottle is constant.

![[0625-y23-3-p7-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 5.1**

**(a)** The temperature of the air in the bottle increases.

**(i)** State what happens to the pressure of the air in the bottle. **[1]**

**(ii)** Explain why the pressure of the air in the bottle changes. Use ideas about gas particles in your answer. **[4]**

**(b)** The bottle has a weight of 5.4 N and an area of 9.2 cm$^{2}$ in contact with the table.

Calculate the pressure produced by the bottle on the table.

pressure =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ N / cm$^{2}$ **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** 0.59 (N / cm$^{2}$)
