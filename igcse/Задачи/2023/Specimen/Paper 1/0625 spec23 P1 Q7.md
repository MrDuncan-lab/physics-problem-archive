A beam on a pivot supports a load *P* at one end and a load *Q* at the other end. The weight of the beam can be ignored.

![[0625-y23-1-p5-f1.svg]]

The beam is balanced.

|---|---|---|
| **A** | 21 | 7 |
| **B** | 21 | 9 |
| **C** | 30 | 10 |
| **D** | 30 | 70 |
