# Question 7 (5 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2023 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 1 · вопрос 7  
Источник: [[_pdf/2023/0625_w23_qp_31.pdf#page=12|условие, стр. 12]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_w23_ms_31.pdf#page=10|схема оценивания, стр. 10]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_w23_er.pdf#page=13|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w23 P31]]  
Темы: [[3.2.3 Thin lenses]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить фокусное расстояние собирающей линзы, построить изображение и описать его свойства.

Fig. 7.1 shows a ray diagram for an object positioned on the principal axis of a thin converging lens.

F$_{1}$ and F$_{2}$ are the focal points of the lens and C is the centre of the converging lens.

![[0625-w23-31-p12-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 7.1**

**(a)** On Fig. 7.1, each small square of the grid represents 1.0 cm.

Determine the focal length of the converging lens.

focal length =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm **[1]**

**(b)** On Fig. 7.1, draw an arrow to show the position of the image formed by the converging lens. **[1]**

**(c)** State **three** characteristics of the image formed by the converging lens.

1  \_\_\_\_\_\_

2  \_\_\_\_\_\_

3  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | 7 (cm) | B1 |
> | (b) | arrow drawn (perpendicularly) from principal axis to intersection of rays. | B1 |
> | (c) | (image is) real | B1 |
> |  | inverted | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Many candidates were unable to identify the distance from the centre of a lens to one of its principal foci as being the focal length of the lens.
>
> **(b)** A number of candidates were unable to identify the distance from the principal axis to the point where rays from an object converge to as being the image of the object formed by the lens.
>
> **(c)** Only stronger candidates gave three correct characteristics of the image formed by the converging lens. Generally, ray diagrams for thin lenses were not well understood by candidates.
