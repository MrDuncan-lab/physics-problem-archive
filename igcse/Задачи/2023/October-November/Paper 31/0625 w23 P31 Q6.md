# Question 6 (9 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2023 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 1 · вопрос 6

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Описать производство электричества ветротурбиной, рассчитать напряжение и назвать преимущества передачи энергии при высоком напряжении.

Fig. 6.1 shows four wind turbines.

![[0625-w23-31-p11-f1.png]]

**Fig. 6.1**

**(a)** Describe how a wind turbine generates electrical power. **[3]**

**(b)** The electrical power output of a wind turbine is 624 kW. The output current is 520 A.

Calculate the output voltage of the wind turbine.

output voltage =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ V **[4]**

**(c)** For transmission, the output voltage is increased to 132 kV.

State **two** advantages of transmitting electrical power at high voltage.

1  \_\_\_\_\_\_

2  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** <u>(output voltage =) 1200 (V) OR 1.2 kV</u>

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | KE of wind | B1 |
> |  | rotates / turns / spins turbine OR blades | B1 |
> |  | (turbine) turns / spins / rotates generator | B1 |
> | (b) | <u>(output voltage =) 1200 (V) OR 1.2 kV</u> | A4 |
> |  | (*V* =) 624 000 ÷ 520 | (C3) |
> |  | conversion: 624 kW = 624 000 (W) | (C1) |
> |  | power = *I* × *V* **OR** (*V* =) *P* ÷ *I* | (C1) |
> | (c) | any **two** from:<br>greater efficiency<br>as lower current (is used)<br>(so) reduces power / energy losses<br>thinner cables can be used (so reducing costs) OR pylons further apart<br>idea of increased distance of transmission (of electrical power) | B2 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** The majority of candidates stated that the wind turned the turbine, the turbine turned the generator and this generated electricity to score partial credit. Only stronger candidates identified the KE of the wind as the source of energy for the turbine. Weaker candidates believed that the generation of electricity always involves the heating of water to form steam which drives a turbine and consequently, a generator.
>
> **(b)** The majority of candidates were able to correctly recall the equation power = V × I and then to rearrange and evaluate the output voltage as 1200 (V). The most common error was to either multiply power and current or to divide the current by the power to give an answer of 0.83.
>
> **(c)** Only stronger candidates were able to give two advantages of transmitting electrical power at high voltages.
