# Question 1 (7 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2023 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 1 · вопрос 1  
Источник: [[_pdf/2023/0625_w23_qp_31.pdf#page=2|условие, стр. 2–3]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_w23_ms_31.pdf#page=7|схема оценивания, стр. 7]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_w23_er.pdf#page=13|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w23 P31]]  
Темы: [[1.2 Motion]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить путь и скорость велосипедиста по графику, описать движение и указать направление скорости.

Fig. 1.1 shows a distance–time graph for a cyclist.

![[0625-w23-31-p2-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 1.1**

**(a)** **(i)** Determine the distance travelled by the cyclist between time = 0 and time = 100 s.

distance travelled =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ m **[1]**

**(ii)** Calculate the speed of the cyclist between time = 0 and time = 100 s.

speed =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ m / s **[3]**

**(iii)** Describe the motion of the cyclist between time = 100 s and time = 250 s. **[2]**

**(b)** Fig. 1.2 shows the cyclist riding along a long straight road.

![[0625-w23-31-p3-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 1.2**

The speed of the cyclist is 15 m / s. Determine the velocity of the cyclist.

velocity =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ m / s

direction  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)(ii)** 4(0) (m / s)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | (distance =) 400 (m) | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | 4(0) (m / s) | A3 |
> |  | 400 ÷ 100 | (C2) |
> |  | (speed =) gradient of distance-time graph OR distance ÷ time | (C1) |
> | (a)(iii) | stationary OR stopped OR at rest (between 100 and 150 s) | B1 |
> |  | (then) constant / steady speed (between 150 and 250 s) | B1 |
> | (b) | 15 (m / s) (due) west / W | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** Most candidates correctly determined the distance travelled in the first 100 seconds.
>
> **(ii)** Most candidates correctly determined the speed during the first 100 seconds on the speed-time graph. The most common error was to substitute incorrect values from the graph.
>
> **(iii)** Candidates found this item challenging and only the strongest candidates gained full credit. The most common error was to confuse this distance-time graph with a speed-time graph.
>
> **(b)** The majority of candidates answered this correctly. The most common error was to give an incorrect or insufficient direction, e.g. forward.
