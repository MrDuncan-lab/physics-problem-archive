# Question 2 (10 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2023 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 1 · вопрос 2  
Источник: [[_pdf/2023/0625_w23_qp_31.pdf#page=4|условие, стр. 4–5]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_w23_ms_31.pdf#page=7|схема оценивания, стр. 7]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_w23_er.pdf#page=13|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w23 P31]]  
Темы: [[1.4 Density]], [[1.5.1 Effects of forces]], [[1.5.2 Turning effect of forces]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать плотность цилиндра, определить сопротивление воздуха при равномерном падении и найти вес блока по равновесию рычага.

The mass of a solid metal cylinder is 400 g and its volume is 52 cm$^{3}$.

**(a)** Calculate the density of the metal. Include the unit.

density =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[4]**

**(b)** The cylinder is falling at constant speed through the air. Fig. 2.1 shows the vertical forces acting on the cylinder.

![[0625-w23-31-p4-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 2.1** (not to scale)

On Fig. 2.1, write the name and the size of the upward force on the cylinder. **[2]**

**(c)** The student balances a beam on a pivot. On the beam, he positions the cylinder and a block so that the beam remains balanced. The arrangement is shown in Fig. 2.2.

![[0625-w23-31-p5-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 2.2** (not to scale)

Calculate the weight of the block.

weight of block =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ N **[4]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** 7.7
>
> **(c)** (weight = 97.5 ÷ 42) = 2.3 (N)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | 7.7 | A3 |
> |  | 400 ÷ 52 | (C2) |
> |  | (density =) mass ÷ volume **OR** *m* / *V* | (C1) |
> |  | g / cm$^{3}$ | B1 |
> | (b) | friction OR drag OR (air) resistance | B1 |
> |  | 3.9 (N) | B1 |
> | (c) | (weight = 97.5 ÷ 42) = 2.3 (N) | A4 |
> |  | *W* × 42 = 3.9 × 25 {OR 97.5} OR (*W* =) 3.9 × 25 / 42 | (C3) |
> |  | (moment of cylinder =) 3.9 × 25 OR 97.5 | (C1) |
> |  | clockwise moment = anticlockwise moment **OR** moment of cylinder = moment of block | (C1) |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** The vast majority of candidates gained full credit for an answer of 7.7 g / cm$^{3}$. The most common error was to give an incorrect or no unit. Another common error was to recall an incorrect rearrangement of the equation for density.
>
> **(b)** Candidates found this item challenging. Many candidates did not identify the correct name i.e. air resistance or drag, for the upward force. The most common errors were to call the force upward force or even gravity. Some candidates did not realise that at constant speed the upward force was equal in magnitude to the downward force or weight.
>
> **(c)** Weaker candidates struggled to recall and apply the principle of moments to the balanced beam. The most common error was to try to use some form of W = mg to find the weight of the block. Stronger candidates correctly evaluated the moments equation to give an answer of 2.3 (N).
