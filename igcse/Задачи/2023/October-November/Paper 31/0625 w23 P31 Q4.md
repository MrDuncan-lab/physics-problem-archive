> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить амплитуду и частоту волны, распознать дифракцию и преломление водных волн.

Fig. 4.1 represents a wave on the surface of water.

![[0625-w23-31-p8-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 4.1**

**(a)** **(i)** Determine the amplitude of the wave in Fig. 4.1.

amplitude =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm **[1]**

**(ii)** Determine the frequency of the wave in Fig. 4.1.

frequency =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ Hz **[2]**

**(b)** Fig. 4.2 shows wavefronts passing through a small gap in a barrier. The arrows on the diagram show the directions of propagation of the wavefronts.

![[0625-w23-31-p9-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 4.2**

State the name of the wave property shown in Fig. 4.2. **[1]**

**(c)** Fig. 4.3 shows wavefronts changing direction as they pass from shallow water to deep water. The arrows on the diagram show the directions of propagation of the wavefronts.

![[0625-w23-31-p9-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 4.3**

**(i)** State the name of the wave property shown in Fig. 4.3. **[1]**

**(ii)** State **one** property of the water wave, other than direction, that changes as it moves from shallow water to deep water. **[1]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)(ii)** (frequency =) 0.5 (Hz)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | (amplitude =) 15 (cm) | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | (frequency =) 0.5 (Hz) | A2 |
> |  | (frequency =) number of waves sent out / emitted in one second<br>OR 1 wave in 2.0 (s) OR frequency = 1 ÷ 2(0) | (C1) |
> | (b) | diffraction | B1 |
> | (c)(i) | refraction | B1 |
> | (c)(ii) | (change of) wavelength OR (wave) speed OR velocity | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** Not all candidates read the height of the wave as 15 (cm) from the displacement-time graph. The most common error was 30 cm but many candidates gave 5 as their answer, possibly the number of peaks and troughs on the diagram.
>
> **(ii)** Only stronger candidates could use their understanding of frequency to determine the number of complete waves sent out in one second as 0.5 from the displacement-time graph.
>
> **(b)** Only stronger candidates gained credit here by identifying diffraction.
>
> **(c) (i)** Only stronger candidates gained credit here by identifying refraction.
>
> **(ii)** Despite the wording of the question, many candidates gave an answer of ‘direction changes’.
