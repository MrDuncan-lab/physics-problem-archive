# Question 8 (7 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2023 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 1 · вопрос 8

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Описать исследование магнитного поля соленоида и объяснить кратковременную индукцию напряжения в соседней катушке.

Fig. 8.1 shows a solenoid (long coil of wire) connected in a circuit. When the switch is closed, there is a large current in the circuit.

![[0625-w23-31-p13-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 8.1**

**(a)** Describe an experiment to identify the pattern **and** direction of the magnetic field around the solenoid.

You may draw on Fig. 8.1 as part of your description. **[3]**

**(b)** A solenoid P is placed close to another solenoid Q. Solenoid Q is connected to a sensitive voltmeter. The arrangement is shown in Fig. 8.2.

![[0625-w23-31-p13-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 8.2**

Describe and explain what happens when the switch is closed. **[4]**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Only stronger candidates answered this correctly. Many other candidates thought the question was about electromagnetic induction and so gained no credit. Stronger candidates gave a clear description of a sensible method for plotting the shape and direction of the magnetic field, giving sufficient detail for someone to be able to follow.
>
> **(b)** Many candidates showed little understanding of electromagnetic induction and gained no credit here. Some gained partial credit for ‘magnetic field produced by coil P’ but did not link this to an induced current in coil Q.
