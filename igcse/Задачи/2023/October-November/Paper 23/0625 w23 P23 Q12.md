> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Описать увеличение скорости частиц при нагревании газа в закрытой бутылке неизменного объёма.

A sealed bottle of constant volume contains air.

The air in the bottle is heated by the Sun.

What is the effect on the average speed of the air particles in the bottle and the average distance between them?

|  | average speed of air particles | average distance between air particles |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | decreases | decreases |
| **B** | decreases | stays the same |
| **C** | increases | increases |
| **D** | increases | stays the same |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Many candidates thought that the average separation of the particles would increase, in spite of the volume of gas remaining unchanged.
