# Question 8 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2023 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 3 · вопрос 8  
Источник: [[_pdf/2023/0625_w23_qp_23.pdf#page=5|условие, стр. 5]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_w23_ms_23.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_w23_er.pdf#page=11|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить положения наименьшей гравитационной и наибольшей упругой энергии груза на пружине.

A mass bounces up and down on a steel spring. The diagram shows the mass and the spring at different points during the motion.

![[0625-w23-23-p5-f1.svg]]

At which point is the least energy in the gravitational potential store of the mass and at which point is the most energy in the elastic store of the spring?

|  | least energy in gravitational potential store of the mass | most energy in the elastic store of the spring |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | mass moving down | mass moving up |
| **B** | mass moving down | lowest point |
| **C** | lowest point | mass moving up |
| **D** | lowest point | lowest point |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Candidates who answered incorrectly were equally divided between options **B** and **C**, indicating that these candidates had not recognised that when the mass is at its lowest point, it is just turning round and so is neither moving up nor moving down.
