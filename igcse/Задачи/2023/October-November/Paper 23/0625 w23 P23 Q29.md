> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбрать соотношение сопротивлений и напряжений в резистивном делителе напряжения.

Two resistors, with resistances *R*$_{1}$ and *R*$_{2}$, are used as a potential divider.

![[0625-w23-23-p13-f1.svg]]

What is the relationship between *R*$_{1}$, *R*$_{2}$ and potential differences *V*$_{1}$ and *V*$_{2}$?

**A** *R*$_{1}$ × *R*$_{2}$ = *V*$_{1}$ × *V*$_{2}$  
**B** *R*$_{1}$ × *V*$_{1}$ = *R*$_{2}$ × *V*$_{2}$  
**C** $\dfrac{R_{1}}{R}$ = *V*$_{1}$ × *V*$_{2}$

2

= $\dfrac{V^{1}}{V}$

**D** $\dfrac{R_{1}}{R}$

2 2

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Most candidates recognised that options **A** and **C** have different units on each side of the equation, and so could not possibly be true.
