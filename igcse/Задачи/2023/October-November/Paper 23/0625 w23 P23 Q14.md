# Question 14 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2023 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 3 · вопрос 14

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объяснить охлаждение лица передачей энергии воде, испаряющейся с кожи.

A student splashes water on to her face. Here are three statements about the effects.

P The water uses energy to evaporate.

Q The water gains energy from the student.

R The face of the student cools.

Which statements are correct?

**A** P and Q only  
**B** P and R only  
**C** Q and R only  
**D** P, Q and R

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> P will always occur. If the water is cooler than the temperature of the student's face then Q and R will occur simultaneously as well. As ‘P only’ is not an option, the only possible answer is option **D**, in which all three occur.
