> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить изменения сопротивления фоторезистора и выходного напряжения делителя при усилении освещения.

The diagram shows a light-dependent resistor (LDR) connected in a potential divider circuit.

![[0625-w23-23-p14-f1.svg]]

The brightness of the light falling on the LDR is increased.

Which row shows what happens to the resistance of the LDR and what happens to the reading on the voltmeter?

|  | resistance of LDR | reading on voltmeter |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | decreases | decreases |
| **B** | decreases | increases |
| **C** | increases | decreases |
| **D** | increases | increases |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Many candidates found this question very challenging. When the brightness is increased, the resistance of the LDR goes down. That means that its share of the overall pd goes down too, so option **A** is the answer.
