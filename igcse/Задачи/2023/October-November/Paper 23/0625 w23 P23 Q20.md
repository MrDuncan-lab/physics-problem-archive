> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать угол отражения по углу между падающим лучом и поверхностью зеркала.

The diagram shows a ray of light incident on a plane mirror.

![[0625-w23-23-p10-f2.svg]]

The angle between the ray and the mirror is 35°.

The ray is reflected by the mirror.

What is the angle of reflection?

**A** 35°  
**B** 55°  
**C** 70°  
**D** 110°

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Most weaker candidates chose option **A**, forgetting that the angles of incidence have to be measured from the normal, and that the angle of incidence is not therefore shown on the diagram, and has to be calculated from the 35° angle that is shown.
