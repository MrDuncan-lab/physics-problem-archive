> Описать выделение ядерной энергии при делении тяжёлых ядер на более лёгкие.

Which statement describes how nuclear energy is released by fission in a nuclear power station?

**A** Atoms join together to make molecules.  
**B** Heavy nuclei split into lighter nuclei.  
**C** Light nuclei join together to form heavier nuclei.  
**D** Molecules break down into atoms.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
