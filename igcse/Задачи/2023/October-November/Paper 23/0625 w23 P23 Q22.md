> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать угол преломления света в стекле по углу падения и показателю преломления.

Light is travelling through air. It strikes a glass block at an angle of incidence of 45°. The glass

has a refractive index of 1.4.

What is the angle of refraction of the light as it enters the glass?

**A** 29°  
**B** 30°  
**C** 32°  
**D** 82°

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Many candidates simply divided the angle of incidence by the refractive index rather than working through the sines. Presumably these candidates were merely looking for a combination of the data given that would produce one of the responses.
