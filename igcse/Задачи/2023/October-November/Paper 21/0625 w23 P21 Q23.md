> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнить скорости звука в твёрдой, жидкой и газообразной ртути.

The element mercury exists as a solid, a liquid or a gas.

Which row gives a possible set of values of the speeds of sound through mercury?

|  | speed of sound in frozen mercury m / s | speed of sound in liquid mercury m / s | speed of sound in mercury vapour m / s |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | 250 | 1500 | 2500 |
| **B** | 250 | 2500 | 1500 |
| **C** | 1500 | 250 | 2500 |
| **D** | 2500 | 1500 | 250 |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Stronger candidates answered this correctly while weaker candidates often chose choose **A**.
