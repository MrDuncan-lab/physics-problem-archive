> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить период полураспада исходного изотопа по графику накопления стабильного продукта.

Radioisotope X decays to the stable isotope Y. The graph shows how the mass of Y present in a

sample varies with time.

![[0625-w23-21-p14-f1.svg]]

Which time interval gives the half-life of X?

**A** *t*$_{2}$ – *t*$_{1}$  
**B** *t*$_{3}$ – *t*$_{2}$  
**C** *t*$_{2}$  
**D** $\dfrac{1}{2}$ *t*$_{3}$

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> In this question, candidates needed to recognise that while they may be used to determining half-lives from a decay curve, here they had to realise that the atoms present are all either of nuclide X or of nuclide Y and the total number of atoms remains constant. Therefore, the graph for the mass of X against time is an upside-down version of the graph given. The result then follows.
