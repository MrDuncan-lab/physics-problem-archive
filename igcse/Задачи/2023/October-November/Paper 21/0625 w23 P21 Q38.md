> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объяснить лето в северном полушарии наклоном земной оси к Солнцу.

It is summer in the northern hemisphere of the Earth in June.

Which statement explains why?

**A** The Earth is closer to the Sun in June.  
**B** The Earth spins on its axis in the opposite direction to that in which it rotates around the Sun.  
**C** The Moon is full in June.  
**D** The north pole of the axis of the Earth’s rotation is tilted towards the Sun in June.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
