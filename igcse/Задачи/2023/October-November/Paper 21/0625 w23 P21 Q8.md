> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить последовательность превращений энергии при спуске велосипедиста и торможении до остановки.

A cyclist travels down a hill from rest at point X without pedalling.

The cyclist applies his brakes and the cycle stops at point Y.

![[0625-w23-21-p4-f1.svg]]

Which energy transfers have taken place between X and Y?

**A** gravitational potential → kinetic → internal (thermal)  
**B** gravitational potential → internal (thermal) → kinetic  
**C** kinetic → gravitational potential → internal (thermal)  
**D** kinetic → internal (thermal) → gravitational potential

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**
