> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать плотности несмешивающихся жидкостей и определить верхний и нижний слои.

The table shows the mass and volume of three different liquids, X, Y and Z.

| liquid | mass / g | volume / cm$^{3}$ |
|---|---|---|
| X | 120 | 200 |
| Y | 80 | 67 |
| Z | 100 | 120 |

The liquids are placed in the same container. The liquids do not mix.

Which liquid is at the top of the container and which liquid is at the bottom?

|  | liquid at top | liquid at bottom |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | X | Y |
| **B** | X | Z |
| **C** | Y | X |
| **D** | Y | Z |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**
