# Question 4 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2023 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 1 · вопрос 4  
Источник: [[_pdf/2023/0625_w23_qp_11.pdf#page=3|условие, стр. 3]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_w23_ms_11.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_w23_er.pdf#page=1|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объяснить изменение показаний пружинных весов на другой планете при неизменной массе тела.

An object is suspended from a spring balance on the Earth. The same object is suspended from the same spring balance on another planet.

![[0625-w23-11-p3-f1.svg]]

Which statement explains the difference between the two readings?

**A** Both the mass and the weight of the object are greater on the other planet.  
**B** The mass of the object is greater on the other planet than on the Earth, but the weight is

unchanged.

**C** The spring stretches more easily when on the other planet.  
**D** The weight of the object is greater on the other planet than on the Earth, but the mass is

unchanged.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> A majority of candidates answered correctly. The most popular incorrect option was **B**. This was possibly due to these candidates believing that a weighing device measures mass, whereas, in fact, a spring balance (properly called a newton-meter) measures force, even though it may have a scale expressed in kilograms.
