> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать мощность насоса по работе и времени наполнения резервуара.

A pump does 460 000 J of work to raise water to fill a tank. It takes 7 minutes to fill the tank.

What is the power of the pump?

**A** 1.1 kW  
**B** 66 kW  
**C** 3200 kW  
**D** 190 000 kW

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Many candidates forgot to convert the time into seconds, but all coped with ‘k’ as a multiplier. Few candidates knew that they needed to both divide energy by time and convert the units.
