# Question 31 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2023 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 1 · вопрос 31  
Источник: [[_pdf/2023/0625_w23_qp_11.pdf#page=12|условие, стр. 12]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_w23_ms_11.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_w23_er.pdf#page=1|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

Темы: [[4.5.1 Electromagnetic induction]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить опыты, демонстрирующие электромагнитную индукцию при движении магнита, провода или замыкании цепи.

A teacher sets up the equipment for three demonstrations, P, Q and R.

![[0625-w23-11-p12-f1.svg]]

In demonstration P, the magnet is moved to the right.

In demonstration Q, the switch is closed.

In demonstration R, the copper rod is moved vertically upwards.

Which demonstrations can be used to demonstrate electromagnetic induction?

**A** P and Q only  
**B** P and R only  
**C** Q and R only  
**D** P, Q and R

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The most popular option here was **B**, indicating that candidates thought of electromagnetic induction exclusively in terms of conductors and fields being in relative motion, and ignored the approach that focuses on a change in the magnetic flux linkage, as specified in section 4.5.1.1 of the syllabus, that is exemplified in diagram Q.
