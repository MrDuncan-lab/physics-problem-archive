> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить неизменность тока при подключении изолятора параллельно резистору.

A battery is connected to an ammeter and a resistor.

![[0625-w23-12-p11-f1.svg]]

The ammeter reading is 0.20 A.

An electrical insulator is connected in parallel with the resistor.

What is the ammeter reading?

**A** 0 A  
**B** between 0 A and 0.20 A  
**C** 0.20 A  
**D** greater than 0.20 A

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Option **B** was slightly more popular than option **C**, the correct answer. It was clear that Kirchhoff’s Laws were challenging for many candidates.
