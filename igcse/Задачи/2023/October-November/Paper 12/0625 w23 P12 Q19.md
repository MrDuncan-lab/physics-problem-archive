Источник: [[_pdf/2023/0625_w23_qp_12.pdf#page=9|условие, стр. 9]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_w23_ms_12.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_w23_er.pdf#page=3|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Упорядочить красный, зелёный и фиолетовый свет по возрастанию длины волны.

Red, green and violet lights are part of the visible spectrum of light.

What is the order of colours from shortest to longest wavelength?

**A** red → green → violet  
**B** red → violet → green  
**C** violet → red → green  
**D** violet → green → red

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> More candidates chose option **A** than the correct option (**D**), indicating that they had probably learned the ROYGBIV mnemonic by rote, without recognising that red light has a lower frequency and a higher wavelength than violet light (as specified in Syllabus Section 3.2.4.2).
