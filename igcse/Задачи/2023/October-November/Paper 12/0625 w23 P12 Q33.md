> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбрать соотношение напряжений и чисел витков обмоток трансформатора.

A transformer has *N*$_{\text{p}}$ turns on its primary coil and *N*$_{\text{s}}$ turns on its secondary coil. The voltage across the primary coil is *V*$_{\text{p}}$ and the voltage across the secondary coil is *V*$_{\text{s}}$.

What is the relationship between these four quantities?

**A** *V*$_{\text{p}}$ × *V*$_{\text{s}}$ = *N*$_{\text{p}}$ × *N*$_{\text{s}}$  
**B** $\dfrac{V_{p}}{V}$ = $\dfrac{N_{p}}{N}$

s s

**C** $\dfrac{V_{p}}{V}$ = $\dfrac{N_{s}}{N}$

s p

**D** $\dfrac{V_{p}}{V}$ = *N*$_{\text{p}}$ × *N*$_{\text{s}}$

s

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
