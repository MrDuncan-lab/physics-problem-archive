> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбрать наиболее эффективную меру снижения облучения при работе с гамма-источником.

What is the most effective precaution to reduce the risk when handling, storing or using a

radioactive source that emits γ-rays?

**A** Handle the source for the least possible time.  
**B** Have a fire extinguisher nearby when using the source.  
**C** Store the source at a low temperature.  
**D** Wear plastic safety goggles when handling the source.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Many candidates selected option **C** which was incorrect.
