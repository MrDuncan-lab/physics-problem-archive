Темы: [[1.3 Mass and weight]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбрать согласованные значения и единицы массы и веса человека.

A person steps onto a bathroom scale.

The bathroom scale records both mass and weight.

Which row shows the readings on the bathroom scale?

|  | mass | weight |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | 60 N | 590 kg |
| **B** | 60 kg | 590 N |
| **C** | 590 kg | 60 N |
| **D** | 590 N | 60 kg |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Many candidates knew the relationship between 590 N and 60 kg, but misidentified the former as the mass and the latter as the weight, and so chose option **D**.
