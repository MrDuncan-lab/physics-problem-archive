# Question 3 (1 mark)

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить путь спринтера за часть разгона по площади под графиком скорости.

The graph shows the motion of a sprinter.

![[0625-w23-12-p3-f1.svg]]

She accelerates steadily from rest to 8.0 m / s in 4.0 s.

How far does she travel in the last three seconds of her acceleration?

**A** 9.0 m  
**B** 15 m  
**C** 16 m  
**D** 24 m

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The most popular choice was **D**, which was the distance travelled between *t* = 4 s and *t* = 7 s. This was incorrect. The question asked for the distance travelled during the last three seconds of the athlete’s acceleration, i.e. between *t* = 1 s and *t* = 4 s. Of those candidates who recognised that the acceleration period was crucial, most calculated the acceleration during the whole of the 4 s period.
