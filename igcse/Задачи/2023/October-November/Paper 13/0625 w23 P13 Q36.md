> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить виды радиоактивного распада, изменяющие химический элемент.

Which type of radioactive decay causes the nucleus of one element to change into the nucleus of

another element?

|  | emission of an alpha-particle | emission of a beta-particle | emission of a gamma ray |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** **B** **C** **D** |  |  |  |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> A substantial number of candidates chose option **B** and were unaware that beta decay changes the substance into a different element, as specified in syllabus section 5.2.3.2.
