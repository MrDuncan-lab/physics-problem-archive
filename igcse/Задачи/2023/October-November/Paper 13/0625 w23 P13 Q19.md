> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить порядок цветов солнечного спектра после прохождения призмы.

A beam of light from the Sun strikes a prism.

![[0625-w23-13-p8-f3.svg]]

The dispersed beam is incident on a white wall between X and Y.

Which colours are seen at X, Z and Y?

|  | X | Z | Y |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | red | green | violet |
| **B** | red | violet | green |
| **C** | violet | green | red |
| **D** | violet | red | green |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Option **A** was the most popular choice, probably because candidates are used to seeing this diagram the other way up.
