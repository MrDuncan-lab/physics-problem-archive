> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать угол отражения по углу между падающим лучом и поверхностью зеркала.

The diagram shows a ray of light incident on a plane mirror.

![[0625-w23-13-p9-f1.svg]]

The angle between the ray and the mirror is 35°.

The ray is reflected by the mirror.

What is the angle of reflection?

**A** 35°  
**B** 55°  
**C** 70°  
**D** 110°

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Some candidates knew that the angle of reflection is measured between the ray and the normal. Most candidates selected option **A** rather than **B**.
