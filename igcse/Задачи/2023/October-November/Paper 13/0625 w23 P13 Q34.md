What are the relative charges on a proton, a neutron and an electron?

|  | proton | neutron | electron |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | 0 | –1 | +1 |
| **B** | 0 | –1 | –1 |
| **C** | +1 | 0 | –1 |
| **D** | +1 | 0 | +1 |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
