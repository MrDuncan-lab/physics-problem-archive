# Question 16 (1 mark)

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить температуру и направление движения воздуха у пола при конвекционном обогреве.

A cupboard is placed in front of a heater. Air can move through a gap under the cupboard.

![[0625-w23-13-p7-f1.svg]]

Which row describes the temperature and the direction of movement of the air in the gap?

|  | air temperature | air direction |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | cool | away from the heater |
| **B** | cool | towards the heater |
| **C** | warm | away from the heater |
| **D** | warm | towards the heater |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Option **C** was the most popular choice here, probably because candidates did not notice from the diagram that the gap is labelled as being below the cupboard.
