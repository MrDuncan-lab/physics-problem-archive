> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать скорость тела после спуска с наклонной плоскости с учётом работы против трения.

A box is initially at rest at the top of a rough slope.

The box slides down the slope.

The weight of the box is 20 N.

The slope is 4.0 m long and 2.0 m high.

![[0625-w23-22-p4-f2.svg]]

The box does 10 J of work against friction as it slides down the slope.

What is the speed of the box as it reaches the bottom of the slope?

**A** 5.4 m / s  
**B** 6.3 m / s  
**C** 7.1 m / s  
**D** 9.5 m / s

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The most common response was option **B**, which took no account of the work done against friction. The right approach was to calculate the loss in gravitational potential energy using the weight and the height, and then to argue that this appears as kinetic energy, but only after subtracting the 10 J lost to friction.
