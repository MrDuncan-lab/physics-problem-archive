> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбрать формулы импульса тела и импульса силы при разгоне автомобиля из состояния покоя.

A resultant force *F* accelerates a car of mass *m* along a straight horizontal road from rest to a speed *v* in time *t*, giving it momentum *p*.

Which pair of relationships for this situation is correct?

**A** *pt* = *mv* and *F* = *pt*  
**B** *p* = *mv* and *F* = *pt*  
**C** *p* = *mv* and *Ft* = *p*  
**D** *p* = *mvt* and *Ft* = *v*

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Many candidates were successful here, but weaker candidates found it difficult to understand the ideas of impulse and momentum.
