> Выбрать формулу удельной теплоёмкости через энергию, массу и изменение температуры.

Thermal energy Δ*E* is supplied to an object of mass *m* which does not change its state during the heating process. The temperature of the object rises by Δ*T*.

What is the specific heat capacity of the object?

**A** $\dfrac{\Delta E}{m \Delta T}$  
**B** $\dfrac{m \Delta T}{\Delta E}$  
**C** $\dfrac{\Delta E \Delta T}{m}$  
**D** $\dfrac{\Delta E m}{\Delta T}$

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**
