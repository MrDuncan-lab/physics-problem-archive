# Question 36 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2023 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 2 · вопрос 36  
Источник: [[_pdf/2023/0625_w23_qp_22.pdf#page=14|условие, стр. 14]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_w23_ms_22.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_w23_er.pdf#page=9|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать радиационный фон по изменениям скорости счёта источника с известным периодом полураспада.

A radioactive isotope of sodium has a half-life of 15 h.

The table gives data from an experiment to show how the rate of decay of the isotope varies with time.

The background count rate has not been subtracted from these data.

| time / h | 0 | 10 | 20 | 30 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| count rate counts/s | 400 | 260 | 170 | 115 |

What is the background radiation count rate?

**A** 12 counts / s  
**B** 15 counts / s  
**C** 20 counts / s  
**D** 30 counts / s

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Only stronger candidates answered this correctly. These candidates concentrated on the first and last columns in the table, which are 2 half-lives apart. One approach was trial and error. For example, if the background were 15 counts/s, the initial activity due to the source would have been 385 counts/s, so after two half-lives the count rate due to the source would be 96 count/s which, added to the presumed background, would result in a total count rate of 111 counts/s. This approach discounted this as an option. An alternative approach was algebraic, letting *s* be the final rate due to the source, and *b* be the background rate. Then we have 4*s* + *b* = 400 counts/s and *s* + *b* = 115 counts/s, from which simultaneous equations *b* may readily be found.
