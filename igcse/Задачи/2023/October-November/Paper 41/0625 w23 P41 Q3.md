# Question 3 (8 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2023 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 1 · вопрос 3  
Источник: [[_pdf/2023/0625_w23_qp_41.pdf#page=5|условие, стр. 5]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_w23_ms_41.pdf#page=10|схема оценивания, стр. 10]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_w23_er.pdf#page=23|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w23 P41]]  
Темы: [[2.1.2 Particle model]], [[1.8 Pressure]], [[1.3 Mass and weight]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объяснить малую сжимаемость жидкости, рассчитать гидростатическое давление, силу и массу плавающего блока.

Liquids are difficult to compress whereas gases can be compressed easily.

**(a)** Explain, in terms of particles, why it is difficult to compress liquids. **[2]**

**(b)** Fig. 3.1 shows a rectangular block floating in water. The density of the water is 1000 kg / m$^{3}$.

![[0625-w23-41-p5-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 3.1**

The area of the base of the block is 0.014 m$^{2}$. The base of the block is at a depth of 0.087 m below the surface of the water.

**(i)** Show that the pressure due to the water at the base of the block is approximately 850 Pa. **[2]**

**(ii)** Calculate the force *F* on the base of the block caused by the pressure given in **(b)(i)**.

*F* =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(iii)** Force *F* is equal to the weight of the block.

Calculate the mass of the block.

mass =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)(ii)** 12 N
>
> **(b)(iii)** 1.2 kg

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | particles (of liquid) are touching / close to each other | B1 |
> |  | forces (of repulsion) between particles (of liquid) are large | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | (Δ*p* =) ρ *g*(Δ)*h* | B1 |
> |  | 1000 × 9.8 × 0.087 **OR** (Δ*p* =) 852.6 (Pa) | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | 12 N | A2 |
> |  | *p* = *F* / *A* **OR** (*F* =) *pA* **OR** 850 × 0.014 | C1 |
> | (b)(iii) | 1.2 kg | A2 |
> |  | *g* = *W* / *m* **OR** (*m* =) *F* / *g* **OR** 12 / 9.8 | C1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Most candidates were able to state that the particles in liquids are close together or the equivalent. A completely correct answer required the additional statement that repulsive forces between particles in liquids are large. Some candidates wrote down general statements about the properties of particles in a liquid, such as that particles slide over each other, without applying the statement to the question asked.
>
> **(b) (i)** Showing that the pressure due to the water at the base of the block is approximately 850 Pa required more than manipulating the numbers given in the question to calculate 852. Most candidates were able to recall the formula for calculating the pressure in a liquid before substituting the values correctly to gain full credit. Candidates using symbols other than those given in the syllabus should define them clearly. A few candidates worked from first principles, using the equations p = F / A and 𝛒 = m / V with varying degrees of success.
>
> **(ii)** This was answered well with most candidates able to recall the equation p = F / A, and many able to rearrange it correctly and give the correct unit.
>
> **(iii)** There were many good answers here with candidates correctly applying the equation m = W / g. Weaker candidates recalled the equation incorrectly or were confused about whether they were
>
> finding the mass or weight. Some candidates calculated the value correctly but then gave the unit as g instead of kg.
