> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** A number of candidates did not give an answer. However. almost all candidates who answered were correct.
>
> **(ii)** The vast majority of candidates achieved full credit.
>
> **(b)** Many correct answers were seen. A common error was rays being refracted correctly inside the lens but emerging parallel.
>
> **(c)** A considerable number of candidates correctly named all seven colours but the knowledge that violet has the highest frequency was less secure. Weaker candidates included white as one of the colours.
