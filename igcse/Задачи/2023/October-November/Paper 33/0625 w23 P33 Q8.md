**(a)** Fig. 8.1 shows an arrangement for transmitting electricity generated by a power station.

Calculate the number of turns on the secondary coil of the step-up transformer. Use the information given in Fig. 8.1.

number of secondary turns =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

**(b)** State **two** benefits of using high voltages for transmitting electricity.

1  \_\_\_\_\_\_

2  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

> **(a)** 7200
