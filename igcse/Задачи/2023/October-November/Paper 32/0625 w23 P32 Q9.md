# Question 9 (7 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2023 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 9  
Источник: [[_pdf/2023/0625_w23_qp_32.pdf#page=14|условие, стр. 14–15]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_w23_ms_32.pdf#page=12|схема оценивания, стр. 12]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_w23_er.pdf#page=16|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w23 P32]]  
Темы: [[4.4 Electrical safety]], [[4.2.5 Electrical energy and electrical power]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объяснить безопасность обслуживания водонагревателя, вычислить ток и стоимость израсходованной электрической энергии.

Fig. 9.1 shows an electric water heater. The heater is connected to the mains electrical supply.

![[0625-w23-32-p14-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 9.1**

Fig. 9.2 shows the electrical safety label for the heater.

![[0625-w23-32-p14-f2.svg]]

Disconnect from the mains supply before removing the plastic cover.

**Fig. 9.2**

**(a)** **(i)** Explain why the safety label states, ‘Disconnect from the mains supply before removing the plastic cover.’ **[1]**

**(ii)** The heater is switched on.

Calculate the current in the heater. Use the information in Fig. 9.2.

current =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ A **[3]**

**(b)** Table 9.1 shows some electrical meter readings for the water heater.

**Table 9.1**

| date | meter reading / kW h |
|---|---|
| 1st October | 3771 |
| 31st October | 3797 |

Electrical energy costs 18 cents per kW h.

Calculate the cost of using the heater from 1st October until 31st October.

cost =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cents **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)(ii)** 3.1 (A)
>
> **(b)** 468 (cents)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | (prevent) risk of (electric) shock / electrocution | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | 3.1 (A) | A3 |
> |  | 720/230 | (C2) |
> |  | (current =) power / voltage **OR** (*I* =) *P* / *V* | (C1) |
> | (b) | 468 (cents) | A3 |
> |  | 26 × 18 | (C2) |
> |  | (cost =) number of kWh × cost per kWh | (C1) |
> |  | number of kWh = 3797 – 3771 OR 26 | (C1) |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** Many correct answers were seen. Other answers were too vague e.g. “the heater is still on” or “the current is still flowing” without making the safety link to the risk of someone getting an electric shock.
>
> **(ii)** Candidates who recalled that *power = current x potential difference* usually obtained full credit. Other candidates made incorrect attempts using the *potential difference = current x resistance* equation.
>
> **(b)** The majority of candidates gave the correct answer. A common error was to add the meter readings rather than subtracting them. This could still gain some credit if the incorrect number of kWh was then multiplied by the cost per kWh.
