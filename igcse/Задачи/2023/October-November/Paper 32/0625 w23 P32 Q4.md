# Question 4 (7 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2023 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 4  
Источник: [[_pdf/2023/0625_w23_qp_32.pdf#page=6|условие, стр. 6]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_w23_ms_32.pdf#page=8|схема оценивания, стр. 8]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_w23_er.pdf#page=16|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать вес книг и работу их подъёма, связать выполненную работу с приростом потенциальной энергии.

A student holds a pile of books. The mass of the books is 3.2 kg.

**(a)** Calculate the weight of the books.

weight =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ N **[2]**

**(b)** The student carries the books from the bottom to the top of the stairs shown in Fig. 4.1.

The vertical height of the stairs is 4.5 m.

![[0625-w23-32-p6-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 4.1**

**(i)** Show that the work done on the books when they are carried to the top of the stairs is approximately 140 J. **[3]**

**(ii)** Determine the gravitational potential energy gained by the books.

Give a reason for your answer.

gravitational potential energy =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ J

reason  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** 31 (N)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | 31 (N) | A2 |
> |  | (weight =) mass × gravitation field strength **OR** *m* × *g* **OR** *m* × 9.8 OR 3.2 × 9.8 | (C1) |
> | (b)(i) | 141(.12) (J) OR 139(.5) (J) | B1 |
> |  | 3.2 × 9.8 × 4.5 OR 31.36 × 4.5 OR 31 × 4.5 | B1 |
> |  | (work =) force × distance **OR** (*W* =) *F* × *d* | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | 141(.12) (J) OR 139(.5) (J) OR 140 (J) | B1 |
> |  | work done = gain in (g)PE | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** The vast majority of candidates gave the correct answer, writing the equation and showing full working.
>
> **(b) (i)** This was a ‘show that’ question. Answers needed to show each step in the calculation to achieve full credit. For such questions this will usually include writing the equation, substituting the correct values into the equation, and then working out the answer.
>
> **(ii)** Candidates needed to understand that mechanical work done is equal to the energy transferred. The work done on the books was equal to the gain in gravitational potential energy in this case. A number of candidates did not offer an answer.
