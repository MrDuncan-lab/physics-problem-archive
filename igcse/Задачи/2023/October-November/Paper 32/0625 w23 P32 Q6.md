> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Few correct answers were seen. Candidates needed to recognise that a medium is needed for thermal energy transfer by conduction and convection but not radiation.
>
> **(b)** Candidates needed a fuller understanding of how density changes explain the formation of convection currents in liquids. A number of candidates gained partial credit for recognising that the demonstration related to convection and/or that the heated water would rise.
