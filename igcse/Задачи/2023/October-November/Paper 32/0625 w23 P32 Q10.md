# Question 10 (7 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2023 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 10

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбрать материалы постоянного и временного магнитов и объяснить повторяющееся действие электрического звонка.

**(a)** Different materials have differing magnetic properties.

**(i)** State the name of a material that is suitable for a **temporary** magnet. **[1]**

**(ii)** State the name of a material that is suitable for a **permanent** magnet. **[1]**

**(iii)** State how a magnet can show that a material is non-magnetic. **[1]**

**(b)** A teacher uses the arrangement in Fig. 10.1 to demonstrate an electric bell. When the switch is closed, the hammer repeatedly hits the metal gong.

![[0625-w23-32-p16-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 10.1**

Using the information in Fig. 10.1, explain why the hammer repeatedly hits the metal gong when the switch is closed. **[4]**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)(ii)** Some confusion between the magnetic properties of iron and steel was evident.
>
> **(ii)** Answers tended to be too vague or incomplete, e.g. “place the non-magnetic material near the magnet”.
>
> **(b)** Few candidates were able to give a full detailed explanation. A common answer, scoring partial credit, included an understanding that there would be a current flowing which would have a magnetic effect.
