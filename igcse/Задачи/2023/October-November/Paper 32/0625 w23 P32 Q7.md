# Question 7 (7 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2023 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 7  
Источник: [[_pdf/2023/0625_w23_qp_32.pdf#page=10|условие, стр. 10]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_w23_ms_32.pdf#page=10|схема оценивания, стр. 10]] · [[_pdf/2023/0625_w23_er.pdf#page=16|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

Темы: [[3.4 Sound]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Описать колебания воздуха в звуке, определить расстояние по задержке свиста и указать диапазон слышимых частот.

A student can hear trains passing her house.

**(a)** Describe the motion that a sound wave gives to air particles. **[1]**

**(b)** When the student is at her house, she can hear and see the trains, as shown in Fig. 7.1.

![[0625-w23-32-p10-f1.png]]

**Fig. 7.1** (not to scale)

When a train whistle blows, steam comes out of the whistle.

The student measures the time interval between seeing the steam coming out of the whistle and hearing the whistle.

**(i)** Suggest a suitable device for measuring this time interval. **[1]**

**(ii)** The time interval is 1.6 s between the steam coming out of the whistle and the student hearing the whistle.

The speed of sound in air is 340 m / s.

Calculate the distance *d* from the whistle to the student.

distance *d* =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ m **[3]**

**(c)** State the range of audible frequencies for a healthy human ear. Include the unit. **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)(ii)** 540 (m)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | oscillating / vibrating/backwards and forwards | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | stopwatch / (stop)clock | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | 540 (m) | A3 |
> |  | 340 × 1.6 | (C1) |
> |  | (distance =) speed × time | (C1) |
> | (c) | 20 – 20 000 | B1 |
> |  | Hz / hertz | B1 |
