# Question 10 (6 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2004 · Paper 3 — Extended theory · вопрос 10  
Источник: [[_pdf/2004/0625_w04_qp_3.pdf#page=14|условие, стр. 14]] · [[_pdf/2004/0625_w04_ms_3.pdf#page=6|схема оценивания, стр. 6]] · [[_pdf/2004/0625_w04_er.pdf#page=1|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Функция транзистора и логический элемент ИЛИ.

**(a)** **(i)** What is the function of a transistor when placed in an electrical circuit?

**(ii)** Describe the action of a transistor. **[3]**

**(b)** **(i)** In the space below, draw the symbol for an OR gate. Label the inputs and the output. **[1]**

**(ii)** Describe the action of an OR gate that has two inputs. **[2]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | switch, relay or amplifier | 1 |
> | (a)(ii) | any one of the three versions below, each 2 marks<br>1. vary base current | 1 |
> |  | transistor switches on for V$_{\text{be}}$\>0.6 V | 1 |
> |  | 2. small change in base current | 1 |
> |  | produces a large change in collector/emitter current | 1 |
> |  | 3. vary potential divider connected to transistor base | 1 |
> |  | transistor switches on for V$_{\text{be}}$ \> 0.6 V | 1 |
> |  | (b) (i) standard symbol with 2 inputs and an output labelled | 1 |
> |  | one or both inputs 1, output 1 (accept on, high for 1) | 1 |
> |  | both inputs 0, output 0 (accept off, low for 0) | 1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Possibly the worst answered question on the paper.
>
> (a)(i) Very few gave one of the required answers such as switch, amplifier or relay.
>
> (ii) Most explanations were incorrect or inadequate. All ways of explaining the action were accepted.
>
> (b)(i) The correct symbol was known and properly drawn by about half of the entry.
>
> (ii) There were many correct answers. The most common mistake was not to consider two inputs of zero.
