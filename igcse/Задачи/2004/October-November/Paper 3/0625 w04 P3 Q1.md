> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Анализ движения капли воды по графику скорости и высоты.

Fig. 1.1 shows the path of one drop of water in the jet from a powerful hose.

![[0625-w04-3-p2-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 1.1**

Fig. 1.2 is a graph of speed against time for the water drop shown in Fig. 1.1.

![[0625-w04-3-p2-f2.svg]]

time after leaving the hose / s

**Fig. 1.2**

**(a)** Describe the movement of the water drop in the first 4 s after leaving the hose. **[2]**

**(b)** Use Fig. 1.2 to find

**(i)** the speed of the water leaving the hose,

speed =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

**(ii)** the time when the speed of the water is least.

time =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(c)** Use values from Fig. 1.2 to calculate the acceleration of the drop as it falls back towards the ground. Show your working.

acceleration =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

**(d)** Calculate the greatest distance above the ground reached by the drop.

distance =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | deceleration/slows down/speed reduces | 1 |
> |  | deceleration uniform/comes to rest at 4 s | 1 |
> | (b)(i) | 40 (m/s) | 1 |
> | (b)(ii) | 4 (s) | 1 |
> | (c) | speed falls from 0 to 40 m/s in 4 s | 1 |
> |  | acceleration = change in speed/time taken or 40(m/s)/4(s) | 1 |
> |  | acceleration = 10 m/s$^{2}$ | 1 |
> | (d) | distance = average speed x time or area of triangle under | 1 |
> |  | graph<br>= 20 x 4 or 2 x 40 | 1 |
> |  | = 80 m | 1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question was particularly well answered with full ten mark answers not uncommon.
>
> (a) A few candidates mixed up acceleration and deceleration.
>
> (b)(i) Most gave the correct answer.
>
> (ii) Again most gave the correct answer.
>
> (c) This part was well explained and correctly worked out by a high proportion of the entry.
>
> (d) This part was either fully correct or scored one or zero. A large number just used distance = speed x time and scored zero. Those using area under the graph scored at least one, providing that they attempted to use the correct area. Those using average speed x time usually scored all three marks.
>
> Answers: (b)(i) 40 (m/s), (ii) 4 (s); (c) 10 m/s$^{2}$; (d) 80 m.
