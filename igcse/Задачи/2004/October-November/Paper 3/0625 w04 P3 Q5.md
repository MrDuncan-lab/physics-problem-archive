# Question 5 (8 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2004 · Paper 3 — Extended theory · вопрос 5  
Источник: [[_pdf/2004/0625_w04_qp_3.pdf#page=8|условие, стр. 8]] · [[_pdf/2004/0625_w04_ms_3.pdf#page=5|схема оценивания, стр. 5]] · [[_pdf/2004/0625_w04_er.pdf#page=1|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Молекулярное объяснение давления и закон Бойля при сжатии газа.

**(a)** Fig. 5.1 shows a sealed box.

![[0625-w04-3-p8-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 5.1**

**(i)** The box contains a large number of air molecules. On Fig. 5.1, draw a possible path of **one** of the air molecules, as it moves inside the box.

**(ii)** Explain

**1** how air molecules in the box create a pressure on the inside walls,

**2** why this pressure rises as the temperature of the air in the box increases. **[5]**

**(b)** Air in a cylinder is compressed slowly, so that the temperature does not rise. The pressure changes from 2.0 x 10$^{5}$ Pa to 5.0 x 10$^{5}$ Pa. The original volume was 0.35 m$^{3}$. Calculate the new volume.

volume =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | any suitable random motion | 1 |
> |  | molecules hit walls | 1 |
> | (a)(ii) | 1.<br>rebound/bounce back or many hits per unit area or per unit<br>time or collisions create force | 1 |
> |  | 2.<br>(av) k.e./speed of molecules increases | 1 |
> |  | more hits(/sec) or harder hits | 1 |
> | (b) | p$_{1}$v$_{1}$= p$_{2}$v$_{2}$ quoted or any recognisable substitution | 1 |
> |  | 2 x 10$^{5}$x 0 . 3 5 = 5 x 1 0$^{5}$x v | 1 |
> |  | volume = 0.14 (m$^{3}$) | 1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The responses to this question were adequate with almost all candidates scoring some marks.
>
> (a)(i) The usual mistakes were drawing curved paths or where all the collisions were at the wall.
>
> (ii) 1. Almost all scored the mark for molecules/particles hitting the walls, but few developed this further to
>
> score the second mark e.g. hits cause a force.
>
> 2. Very well answered with most scoring the two marks.
>
> (b) As expected some had p/v as a constant instead of pv as a constant. A further number started with the correct formula but either substituted incorrectly or transposed the formula incorrectly. Considerable numbers did obtain the correct answer.
>
> Answer: (b) 0.14 (m$^{3}$).
