> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Гидростатическое давление и сила на клапан, энергия истекающей воды.

Fig. 2.1 shows a reservoir that stores water.

![[0625-w04-3-p4-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 2.1**

**(a)** The valve in the exit pipe is closed. The density of water is 1000 kg/m$^{3}$ and the acceleration of free fall is 10 m/s$^{2}$. Calculate the pressure of the water acting on the closed valve in the exit pipe.

pressure =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(b)** The cross-sectional area of the pipe is 0.5 m$^{2}$. Calculate the force exerted by the water on the closed valve.

force =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(c)** The valve is then opened and water, originally at the surface of the reservoir, finally flows out of the exit pipe. State the energy transformation of this water between the surface of the reservoir and the open end of the pipe. **[2]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | pressure = hdg or 20 x 1000 x 10 | 1 |
> |  | = 2 x 10$^{5}$Pa | 1 |
> | (b) | force = pressure x area or 2 x 10$^{5}$ x 0.5 e.c.f. | 1 |
> |  | = 1 x 10$^{5}$N | 1 |
> | (c) | potential energy (at water surface) | 1 |
> |  | changed to kinetic energy (at pipe exit) | 1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question was particularly well answered with many, scoring all six marks.
>
> (a) The equation pressure = hdg was widely known and correctly applied. Powers of 10 were handled well.
>
> (b) Quite a number wrote force = pressure/area and so scored no marks.
>
> (c) The majority found this easy and gave the straightforward answer of potential energy changed into kinetic energy that gained the marks. Less candidates than usual gave long energy chains that usually scored a maximum of one.
>
> Answers: (a) 2 x 10$^{5}$ Pa; (b) 1 x 10$^{5}$ N.
