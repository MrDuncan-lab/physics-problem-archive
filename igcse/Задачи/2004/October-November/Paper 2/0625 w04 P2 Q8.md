> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> As is often the case with electrostatics, this question usually scored poorly. By far the most common set of answers was top two lines correct, bottom three lines incorrect. Like charges are clearly known to repel, but surprisingly line 3 did not often lead to the answer “attraction”. Virtually no candidate realised that a charged body and an uncharged body attract, so the last 2 marks were rarely scored. Most said “nothing” to these two.
