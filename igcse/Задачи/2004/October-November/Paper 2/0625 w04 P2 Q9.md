# Question 9 (6 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2004 · Paper 2 — Core theory · вопрос 9  
Источник: [[_pdf/2004/0625_w04_qp_2.pdf#page=10|условие, стр. 10–11]] · [[_pdf/2004/0625_w04_ms_2.pdf#page=6|схема оценивания, стр. 6]] · [[_pdf/2004/0625_w04_er.pdf#page=1|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Распределение тока и общее сопротивление последовательно и параллельно соединённых резисторов.

**(a)** Two 10 Ω resistors are connected in series in a circuit, as shown in Fig. 9.1.

![[0625-w04-2-p10-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 9.1**

The current at point **X** is 3.0 A.

**(i)** State the current at

**1.** point **Y**,  \_\_\_\_\_\_ A

**2.** point **Z** \_\_\_\_\_\_ A

**(ii)** Calculate the combined resistance of the two 10 Ω resistors.

combined resistance =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ Ω **[3]**

**(b)** The 10 Ω resistors are now arranged in parallel, as shown in Fig. 9.2.

![[0625-w04-2-p11-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 9.2**

**(i)** The current at **X** is adjusted to 3.0 A. The current through one resistor is 1.5 A.

**1.** What is the current at **Y**? Tick one box.

![[0625-w04-2-p11-f2.svg]]

0 A 1.5 A 3.0 A 4.5 A

**2.** State the value of the current at **Z** \_\_\_\_\_\_ A

**(ii)** What is the combined resistance of the two 10 Ω resistors? Tick one box.

0 Ω 5 Ω 10 Ω 20 Ω **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)(ii)** 20

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | 3<br>3<br>both | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | 10 + 10 | C1 |
> |  | 20 | A1 |
> | (b)(i) | 1 1.5A ticked | B1 |
> |  | 2 3A OR 3.0A | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | 5 Ω ticked | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> There are still a large number of candidates who do not know that the current is the same at all points in a series circuit. For some reason there were a lot of 0.3A and 0.03A answers. Most, but not all could find the combined resistance in (a)(ii). There were a mixed selection of answers to (b), but probably no worse than those to (a). The positive side was that good candidates were scoring all the marks on this question.
>
> Answer: 3, 3, 20, 1.5, 3, 5.
