> Выявление гамма-излучения по проникновению через свинец.

During a fire in a laboratory storeroom, some radioactive material was spilled. A firefighter

detected radiation through the lead-lined walls of the storeroom. The radiation was emitted by the radioactive material.

![[0625-w04-1-p14-f1.svg]]

Which type of radiation was being detected?

**A** α-particles  
**B** β-particles  
**C** γ-rays  
**D** X-rays

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
