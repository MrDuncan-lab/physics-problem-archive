> Изменение уровня воды при нагревании колбы.

A glass flask full of cool water is placed in a container of hot water.

![[0625-w04-1-p7-f1.svg]]

What will happen to the level of water at X as the cool water becomes warmer?

**A** It will fall.  
**B** It will rise.  
**C** It will rise then fall.  
**D** It will stay the same.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
