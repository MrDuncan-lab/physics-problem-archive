> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определение магнитов, железа и алюминия по притяжению и отталкиванию.

A, B, C and D are an aluminium bar, an unmagnetised iron bar and two bar magnets. Tests are performed to find out which bar is which. Each row of Fig. 8.1 shows what happens when two of the bars are placed end to end.

Which bar is which? Complete the lines below.

Bar A is  \_\_\_\_\_\_

Bar B is  \_\_\_\_\_\_

Bar C is \_\_\_\_\_\_

Bar D is \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[4]**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question is basically very simple. Those candidates, who were able and prepared to think carefully through the situations in this question, scored well. Those who did not fit into this group generally wrote nonsense, sometimes even writing things like “Positive” or “North pole”. It was common to find that bars A and B had been correctly identified, but that C and D were interchanged.
