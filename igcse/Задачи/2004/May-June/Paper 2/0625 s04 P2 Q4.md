> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> (a) It was disappointing to see how many did not know how to calculate work done. Answers frequently included speed, time, power, weight or rope length.
>
> (b) A good proportion realised that the second person’s barrow would move faster, but only the best candidates spotted that because of the unbalanced force, there would be acceleration.
>
> (c) Those who realised that B had the greater power, generally could give at least one valid reason for their answer (often two). Those who got (i) wrong struggled to give convincing reasons.
