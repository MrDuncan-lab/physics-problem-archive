> Число нестабильных ядер после трёх периодов полураспада.

A small amount of a radioactive isotope contains 72 billion unstable nuclei. The half-life of the

isotope is 4 hours.

How many unstable nuclei would remain after 12 hours?

**A** 6 billion  
**B** 9 billion  
**C** 18 billion  
**D** 24 billion

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
