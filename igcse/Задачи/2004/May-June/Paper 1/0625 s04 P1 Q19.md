> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Опускание холодного воздуха вследствие конвекции.

Thermometer X is held above an ice cube and thermometer Y is held the same distance below

the ice cube. After several minutes, the reading on one thermometer changes. The ice cube does not melt.

![[0625-s04-1-p9-f2.svg]]

Which thermometer reading changes and why?

|  | thermometer | reason |
|---|---|---|
| A | X | cool air rises from the ice cube |
| B | X | warm air rises from the ice cube |
| C | Y | cool air falls from the ice cube |
| D | Y | warm air falls from the ice cube |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
