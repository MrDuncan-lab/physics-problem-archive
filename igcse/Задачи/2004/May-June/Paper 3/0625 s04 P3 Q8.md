# Question 8 (11 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2004 · Paper 3 — Extended theory · вопрос 8  
Источник: [[_pdf/2004/0625_s04_qp_3.pdf#page=10|условие, стр. 10]] · [[_pdf/2004/0625_s04_ms.pdf#page=14|схема оценивания, стр. 14]] · [[_pdf/2004/0625_s04_er.pdf#page=1|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Параллельная бытовая электросеть, электрическая мощность и потребление энергии.

Fig. 8.1 shows a 240 V a.c. mains circuit to which a number of appliances are connected and switched on.

![[0625-s04-3-p10-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 8.1**

**(a)** Calculate the power supplied to the circuit.

power =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(b)** The appliances are connected in parallel.

**(i)** Explain what connected *in parallel* means.

**(ii)** State two advantages of connecting the appliances in parallel rather than in series.

advantage 1 \_\_\_\_\_\_

advantage 2 \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

**(c)** Calculate

**(i)** the current in the refrigerator,

current =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

**(ii)** the energy used by the fan in 3 hours,

energy =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

**(iii)** the resistance of the filament of one lamp.

resistance =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[7]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** 1.52 kW
>
> **(c)(i)** Current = 0.83 A
>
> **(c)(ii)** Energy = 3.6 kWh or 1.3 x 10$^{7}$ J
>
> **(c)(iii)** R =960Ω

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | 1.52 kW | A1 |
> | (b)(i) | Each appliance is connected across 240 V supply or<br>equivalent | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | Any 2: all work on same voltage or on 240 V or mains<br>OR all have full/stated power OR each can be on or off<br>OR one goes off/breaks others stay on | B2 |
> | (c)(i) | Current = power/voltage or 200/240 | C1 |
> |  | Current = 0.83 A | A1 |
> | (c)(ii) | Energy = power x time or 1.2 x 3 | C1 |
> |  | Energy = 3.6 kWh or 1.3 x 10$^{7}$ J | A1 |
> | (c)(iii) | Current = 60/240 | C1 |
> |  | R= V/I or 240/0.25 | C1 |
> |  | R =960Ω | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> (a) It was surprising how many wrong answers were seen. All the powers in the diagram were quoted and many only added in one 60 W.
>
> (b)(i) Answers were poor, vague and confused, when all that was required was an indication that each and every appliance was connected across the same power supply.
>
> (ii) The majority of candidates scored the marks for independent switching and continued operation of the other appliances when one ceased to work.
>
> (c)(i) Generally well done, but many used the wrong power or gave a wrong unit.
>
> (ii) Many wrong answers because a wrong power or a wrong formula, for example, energy = power/time were used. The unit was often given as watts instead of joules.
>
> (iii) This part was well answered, being often correct when parts (i) and (ii) were not. Again some wrong powers were used but not nearly so many as in the previous parts. Wrong or missing units were rare.
>
> Answers: (a) 1.52 kW; (c)(i) 0.83 A, (ii) 1.3 x 107 J, (iii) 960 Ω.
