# Question 9 (7 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2024 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 3 · вопрос 9  
Источник: [[_pdf/2024/0625_s24_qp_43.pdf#page=13|условие, стр. 13]] · [[_pdf/2024/0625_s24_ms_43.pdf#page=10|схема оценивания, стр. 10]] · [[_pdf/2024/0625_s24_er.pdf#page=35|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 s24 P43]]  
Темы: [[4.5.1 Electromagnetic induction]], [[4.2.5 Electrical energy and electrical power]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объяснить индукцию в беспроводном зарядном устройстве, перевести запас энергии аккумулятора в джоули и проверить время зарядки.

Fig. 9.1 shows a mobile phone (cell phone) being charged on a wireless charging plate.

![[0625-s24-43-p13-f1.png]]

**Fig. 9.1**

**(a)** When the charging plate is switched on, there is an alternating current (a.c.) in the primary coil. A secondary coil is in the mobile phone.

Explain how a current is produced in the secondary coil. **[3]**

**(b)** The maximum energy stored in the battery of the mobile phone is 0.012 kW h.

**(i)** Show that this maximum energy is 4.3 × 10$^{4}$ J. **[1]**

**(ii)** The charging plate in Fig. 9.1 has a useful output power of 15 W. The phone manufacturer claims that the battery can be charged to 50% capacity in less than 30 minutes.

Show that this claim is true. **[3]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | (alternating current / a.c. in primary coil / plate produces) changing magnetic field (in primary coil) | B1 |
> |  | secondary / phone coil cuts (this) magnetic field **OR** secondary / phone coil is in this / changing magnetic field | B1 |
> |  | <u>(changing magnetic field) causes induced current (in secondary coil)</u> | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | 1 kW h = 1000 × 60 × 60 J **AND** 0.012 × 3.6 × 10$^{6}$ (= 4.3 × 10$^{4}$ J) | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | 50% charged = (4.3 × 10$^{4}$ / 2 =) 2.15 × 10$^{4}$ (J) **OR** 63% charged in 30 min<br>**OR** (50% charged in *t* =) 24 min | B1 |
> |  | *P* = *E* / *t* **OR** (*t* =) *E* / *P* **OR** 2.15 × 10$^{4}$ / 15 (s)<br>**OR** energy provided in 30 min = (15 × 30 x 60=) 2.7 × 10$^{4}$(J) | B1 |
> |  | 2.7 × 10$^{4}$  2.15 × 10$^{4}$ **OR** 63%  50% **OR** 30 min  24 min | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** The strongest candidates recognised that this question, although set in the context of charging a mobile phone, was about how a current is produced in the secondary coil of any transformer. These answers contained three elements; the a.c. current produces a changing magnetic field, the secondary coil is in (or cuts) this magnetic field and this causes an induced current in the secondary coil. Weaker candidates often stated that the current from the primary coil travels into the secondary coil when they are in contact. A number of candidates made no attempt at this question.
>
> **(b) (i)** Few candidates converted kW to W and h to s to show that 0.012 kW h = 4.3 × 10$^{4}$ J. Many candidates made no attempt at this question.
>
> **(ii)** Candidates needed to use the formula *P* = *E* ÷ *t* to answer this question. One method was to calculate the energy supplied by the charger in 30 minutes, the value of 50% of the maximum energy stored in the battery and then compare these values to show that more energy is supplied in 30 minutes than is needed to charge the battery to 50%. Another method was to calculate how much energy is needed for 50% charge and then calculate the time taken for the charger to produce this amount of energy and show that it is less than 30 minutes. Many candidates gained some credit in this question with the strongest candidates gaining full credit. Many candidates made no attempt at this question.
