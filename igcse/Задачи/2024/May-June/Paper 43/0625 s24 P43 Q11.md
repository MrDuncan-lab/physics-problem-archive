# Question 11 (6 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2024 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 3 · вопрос 11  
Источник: [[_pdf/2024/0625_s24_qp_43.pdf#page=16|условие, стр. 16]] · [[_pdf/2024/0625_s24_ms_43.pdf#page=12|схема оценивания, стр. 12]] · [[_pdf/2024/0625_s24_er.pdf#page=35|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 s24 P43]]  
Темы: [[6.2.3 The Universe]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить красное смещение, рассчитать скорость удаления галактики по закону Хаббла и связать расширение с происхождением Вселенной.

A galaxy is approximately 1.2 × 10$^{26}$ m from the Earth.

**(a)** Scientists observe light from the distant galaxy.

The wavelength of the observed light is longer than the wavelength of the light emitted from the galaxy.

State the name of this effect. **[1]**

**(b)** **(i)** State the current estimate for the Hubble constant *H*$_{0}$.

*H*$_{0}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(ii)** Calculate the speed at which the galaxy is moving away from the Earth.

speed =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(iii)** Scientists have measured the speeds at which distant galaxies are moving away from the Earth and their distances from the Earth.

These measurements suggest that all the Universe was once at a single point.

Explain why. **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)(ii)** 2.6 × 10$^{8}$ m / s

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | redshift | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | (*H*$_{0}$ =) 2.2 × 10$^{\text{–18}}$ per second | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | 2.6 × 10$^{8}$ m / s | A2 |
> |  | *H*$_{0}$ = *v* / *d* **OR** (*v* =) *H*$_{0}$*d* **OR** (*v* =) 1.2 × 10$^{26}$ × 2.2 × 10$^{\text{–18}}$ | C1 |
> | (b)(iii) | any **two** from:<br>• more distant galaxies are moving away faster<br>• universe is expanding<br>• (if galaxies are moving away from each other now, then) in the past galaxies were closer together | B2 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** The majority of candidates who attempted this question stated that redshift is the effect that scientists observe in light from distant galaxies. The most frequent incorrect response was to state “Doppler effect”. Many candidates made no attempt at this question.
>
> **(b) (i)** Stronger candidates correctly recalled the numerical value of the Hubble constant. However, many did not include the unit in their answer. A wide variety of incorrect values were seen. Many candidates made no attempt at this question.
>
> **(ii)** Many candidates gained at least partial credit here by recalling the formula *H*0 = *v*/*d*. Some candidates rearranged the equation and calculated the speed of the galaxy, including the unit with their numerical value to gain full credit. Many candidates made no attempt at this question.
>
> **(iii)** The strongest answers to this question stated that scientists’ measurements show that the more distant galaxies are moving away faster and that this is evidence of the Universe expanding. Weaker candidates often repeated the question by stating that if distant galaxies are moving away from the Earth now, then in the past they must all have been at a single point. Many candidates made no attempt at this question.
