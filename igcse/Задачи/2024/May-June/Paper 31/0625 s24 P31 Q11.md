# Question 11 (6 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2024 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 1 · вопрос 11  
Источник: [[_pdf/2024/0625_s24_qp_31.pdf#page=15|условие, стр. 15]] · [[_pdf/2024/0625_s24_ms_31.pdf#page=13|схема оценивания, стр. 13]] · [[_pdf/2024/0625_s24_er.pdf#page=13|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 s24 P31]]  
Темы: [[6.1.2 The Solar System]], [[6.2.2 Stars]], [[6.2.3 The Universe]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Назвать тела Солнечной системы и нашу галактику, объяснить связь красного смещения с теорией Большого взрыва.

**(a)** The Sun is the star in our Solar System. Eight planets orbit the Sun.

State the names of **two** other categories of bodies in the Solar System.

1  \_\_\_\_\_\_

2  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(b)** State the name of the galaxy that includes our Solar System. **[1]**

**(c)** Describe how the light from distant galaxies gives evidence to support the Big Bang Theory. **[3]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | any **two** from:<br>• minor / dwarf planet(s)<br>• asteroid(s) **OR** asteroid belt<br>• comet(s)<br>• (planetary) moon(s) | B2 |
> | (b) | Milky Way (galaxy) | B1 |
> | (c) | any **three** from:<br>• (light from distant galaxies or it) is redshifted<br>• compared to light on Earth<br>• (redshift is an) increase in (the observed) wavelength (of light)<br>• (because) galaxies are moving away / receding (from Earth) **OR** moving apart<br>• (Big Bang theory predicts / has) an expanding Universe | B3 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Most candidates managed to give at least one other category of bodies in the Solar System apart from the Sun and planets. However, the most common error was planets (including named planets) and even stars or galaxies.
>
> **(b)** Almost all candidates recalled that the galaxy containing our Solar system was the Milky Way.
>
> **(c)** Only stronger candidates gave detailed and accurate descriptions about how the light from distant galaxies gives evidence to support the Big Bang Theory. Many candidates scored partial credit for describing the redshift of light from distant galaxies.
