# Question 8 (8 marks)

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить сердечник и полюс электромагнита, число последовательных элементов питания и напряжение на соленоиде.

Fig. 8.1 shows an arrangement for making an electromagnet. The electromagnet consists of a

![[0625-s24-31-p12-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 8.1**

**(a)** **(i)** The electromagnet is a temporary magnet.

State **one** material which is suitable for the core of the electromagnet. **[1]**

**(ii)** The battery is made from cells connected in series. Each cell in the battery has an electromotive force (e.m.f.) of 1.5 V.

Calculate the number of cells in the battery in Fig. 8.1.

number of cells =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(b)** The switch is closed. The direction of the current in the solenoid is shown in Fig. 8.1.

**(i)** There is a magnetic field around the electromagnet.

State the name of a piece of equipment that can show the direction of a magnetic field. **[1]**

**(ii)** State the name of the pole labelled X on the core of the electromagnet. **[1]**

**(c)** The resistance of the solenoid is 8.0 Ω. The current in the solenoid is 1.2 A.

Calculate the potential difference (p.d.) across the solenoid.

p.d. =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ V **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)(ii)** 8 (cells)
>
> **(c)** (*V* =) 9.6 (V)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | (soft) iron | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | 8 (cells) | A2 |
> |  | (number of cells =) 12 ÷ 1.5 **OR** battery e.m.f ÷ e.m.f. of 1 cell | (C1) |
> | (b)(i) | (plotting) compass | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | north **OR** N (pole) | B1 |
> | (c) | (*V* =) 9.6 (V) | A3 |
> |  | (*V* =) 8(.0) × 1.2 | (C2) |
> |  | *R* = *V* ÷ *I* in any form **OR** (*V* =) *I* × *R* | (C1) |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** Most candidates knew that iron was a suitable material for the core of the electromagnet. The most common error was steel or iron and steel.
>
> **(ii)** This question was answered well with most candidates evaluating the correct number of cells from 12 ÷ 1.5 = 8 cells. The most common error was to multiply the two values i.e. 12 × 1.5 = 18 cells.
>
> **(b) (i)** Most candidates knew that a compass was needed to determine the direction of a magnetic field. Some common errors were iron filings or galvanometer or magnet.
>
> **(ii)** Many candidates found this question challenging. Most gave an incorrect answer of south pole. Only stronger candidates correctly identified X as a north pole.
>
> **(c)** Many candidates correctly evaluated their substitution into *V* = *I* × *R* to give the correct answer of 9.6 V. The most common errors involved using incorrect rearrangements of the equation such as *V* = *I* ÷ *R* or *V* = *R* ÷ *I*. Candidates would benefit from practicing the rearrangement of common equations in the syllabus.
