# Question 7 (8 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2024 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 1 · вопрос 7  
Источник: [[_pdf/2024/0625_s24_qp_31.pdf#page=10|условие, стр. 10–11]] · [[_pdf/2024/0625_s24_ms_31.pdf#page=11|схема оценивания, стр. 11]] · [[_pdf/2024/0625_s24_er.pdf#page=13|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

Темы: [[3.2.1 Reflection of light]], [[3.2.3 Thin lenses]], [[3.2.4 Dispersion of light]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить угол отражения, построить изображение собирающей линзы и описать дисперсию белого света.

**(a)** Fig. 7.1 shows a ray of light striking a plane mirror. The ray is reflected as shown.

![[0625-s24-31-p10-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 7.1**

The angle of incidence for the ray of light is 40°.

**(i)** Indicate the angle of reflection by drawing a letter R on Fig. 7.1. **[1]**

**(ii)** State the size of the angle of reflection in Fig. 7.1.

angle of reflection =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ ° **[1]**

**(b)** An object O is placed to the left of a thin converging lens. F$_{1}$ is the principal focus on one side of the lens and F$_{2}$ is the principal focus on the other side of the lens.

Two rays from the top of the object are incident on the lens, as shown in Fig. 7.2.

![[0625-s24-31-p10-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 7.2**

**(i)** On Fig. 7.2, locate the image of O by continuing the path of each ray. **[2]**

**(ii)** Draw an arrow to represent the image of O. **[1]**

**(c)** Fig. 7.3 shows a prism producing a spectrum of colours from a narrow beam of white light.

![[0625-s24-31-p11-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 7.3**

**(i)** The prism refracts the white light.

State the name of the effect that produces a spectrum. **[1]**

**(ii)** In the spectrum shown in Fig. 7.3, there are seven colours.

List the seven colours in the order they appear between X and Z.

X  \_\_\_\_\_\_

Z  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(c)(ii)** all 7 colours **AND** in correct order

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | <u>angle of reflection identified</u> | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | 40 (°) | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | horizontal ray drawn to continue through F$_{2}$ | B1 |
> |  | ray to centre drawn to continue undeviated | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | (image drawn / identified) where rays cross | B1 |
> | (c)(i) | dispersion | B1 |
> | (c)(ii) | all 7 colours **AND** in correct order | A2 |
> |  | 6 of the seven colours given | (C1) |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** This question was answered well by most candidates. The most common error was to identify a ray rather than an angle and occasionally to indicate the angle of incidence.
>
> **(ii)** Most candidates gave the correct answer of 40°. The most common error was 50° but 80°, 90°, 140° and various values close to but not equal to 40 were regularly seen.
>
> **(b) (i) (ii)** This question was well answered by most candidates with correct rays often drawn, and a sensible answer given for showing the location of the image. The most common error was to draw the ray through the centre of the lens deviated along the principal axis.
>
> **(c) (i)** Only a minority of candidates could recall that the production of a spectrum for visible light by a prism was known as dispersion. The most common errors were refraction and diffraction.
>
> **(ii)** The colours of the visible light spectrum were well known by most candidates. Only a few candidates had the order reversed.
