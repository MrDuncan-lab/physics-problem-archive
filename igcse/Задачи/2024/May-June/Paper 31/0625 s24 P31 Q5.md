# Question 5 (8 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2024 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 1 · вопрос 5  
Источник: [[_pdf/2024/0625_s24_qp_31.pdf#page=8|условие, стр. 8]] · [[_pdf/2024/0625_s24_ms_31.pdf#page=9|схема оценивания, стр. 9]] · [[_pdf/2024/0625_s24_er.pdf#page=13|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Описать частицы газа, рассчитать работу поршня и объяснить рост давления при изотермическом сжатии.

**(a)** Describe the arrangement, separation and motion of gas particles. **[3]**

**(b)** Fig. 5.1 shows some gas in a container with a piston. The piston can move into the container.

![[0625-s24-31-p8-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 5.1**

**(i)** A force of 30 N pushes the piston into the container for a distance of 0.18 m.

Calculate the work done by the 30 N force.

work done =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ J **[3]**

**(ii)** When the piston moves into the container, the temperature of the gas does **not** change.

Describe and explain any change in the pressure on the walls of the container. **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)(i)** 5.4 (J)
>
> **(b)(ii)** (because) collisions are more frequent / often (of gas particles with walls)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | any **three** from (particles / molecules of gas are / have):<br>• random arrangement<br>• widely spaced (compared to solid / liquid)<br>• random motion<br>• colliding (with each other / walls)<br>• high speed / KE | B3 |
> | (b)(i) | 5.4 (J) | A3 |
> |  | (work done =) 30 × 0.18 | (C2) |
> |  | (work done =) force × distance (moved in direction of force) | (C1) |
> | (b)(ii) | (pressure) increases | M1 |
> |  | (because) collisions are more frequent / often (of gas particles with walls) | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Many candidates were able to give good answers to this question and there was a good understanding of the topic by most candidates. The only common error was for candidates to give several answers that were essentially the same property. Those candidates who used the structure of the question to formulate their answers by giving one answer about arrangement, one about separation and one about the motion, usually gave three different, correct answers.
>
> **(b) (i)** This question was answered well with most candidates knowing that work done is equal to force × distance moved, and giving a correct answer of 5.4 J. Those who did not answer correctly usually used an incorrect rearrangement of the equation i.e. force ÷ distance or 30/0.18.
>
> **(ii)** Most candidates knew that the pressure increased but several were unable to give a reasoned explanation. The key idea that there were more frequent collisions (with the wall of the container) was rarely seen. Most simply said “more collisions”. Others thought that kinetic energy or speed increased even though a constant temperature was specified in question.
