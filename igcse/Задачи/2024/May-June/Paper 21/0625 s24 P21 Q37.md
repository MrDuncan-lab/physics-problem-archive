> Сравнить скорость и гравитационную энергию планеты в ближайшей к Солнцу точке орбиты.

A planet in the Solar System is at the point in its orbit where it is closest to the Sun.

Which row is correct?

|  | orbital speed | energy in gravitational potential store |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | at its maximum | at its maximum |
| **B** | at its maximum | at its minimum |
| **C** | at its minimum | at its maximum |
| **D** | at its minimum | at its minimum |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
