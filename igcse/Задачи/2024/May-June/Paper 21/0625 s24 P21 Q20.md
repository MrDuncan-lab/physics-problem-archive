# Question 20 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2024 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 1 · вопрос 20  
Источник: [[_pdf/2024/0625_s24_qp_21.pdf#page=7|условие, стр. 7]] · [[_pdf/2024/0625_s24_ms_21.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2024/0625_s24_er.pdf#page=7|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Связать замедление света в оптоволокне с критическим углом и эффективностью передачи света.

The diagram shows a ray of light in an optical fibre.

![[0625-s24-21-p7-f2.svg]]

Which statement correctly explains the condition for the maximum transmission of light by the optical fibre?

**A** The glass must slow the light as little as possible to make the critical angle for the fibre as

large as possible.

**B** The glass must slow the light as little as possible to make the critical angle for the fibre as

small as possible.

**C** The glass must slow the light as much as possible to make the critical angle for the fibre as

large as possible.

**D** The glass must slow the light as much as possible to make the critical angle for the fibre as

small as possible.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Most candidates found this question very challenging and showed poor understanding of how the amount of light transmitted by the optical fibre depended on the relationship between the speed of light in the glass and the critical angle. Responses were evenly distributed across the four options.
