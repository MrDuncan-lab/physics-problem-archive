> Определить график тока через диод при заданном изменении напряжения источника.

A diode and a resistor are connected across a variable d.c. supply. Terminal X is initially positive

and at a maximum value. The potential difference (p.d.) across XY is adjusted so that it changes, as shown by the graph.

![[0625-s24-21-p9-f2.svg]]

Which graph shows how the current in the circuit (from X to Y) varies during the same time interval?

![[0625-s24-21-p9-f4.svg]]

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Candidates demonstrated poor knowledge of the behaviour of a diode in a circuit and the correct graph was the least chosen answer overall. There was evidence that some stronger candidates recalled that a diode only lets current pass in one direction as most of these candidates chose option **C** or **D**. However, more stronger candidates chose option **C** as they did not take into account the initial polarities of terminal X and terminal Y.
