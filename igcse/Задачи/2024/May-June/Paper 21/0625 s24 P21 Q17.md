# Question 17 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2024 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 1 · вопрос 17  
Источник: [[_pdf/2024/0625_s24_qp_21.pdf#page=6|условие, стр. 6]] · [[_pdf/2024/0625_s24_ms_21.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2024/0625_s24_er.pdf#page=7|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 s24 P21]]  
Темы: [[3.2.1 Reflection of light]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить максимально возможный угол между падающим и отражённым от плоского зеркала лучами.

The angle of incidence of a ray of light incident on a plane mirror is gradually increased.

To the nearest degree, what is the maximum possible angle between the incident and reflected rays?

**A** 0°  
**B** 45°  
**C** 90°  
**D** 180°

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Some candidates answered this question about reflection correctly, but it was challenging for weaker candidates with most of these candidates choosing incorrect options **B** or **C**. Many stronger candidates also thought that the maximum angle could only be 90° and so chose option **C**.
