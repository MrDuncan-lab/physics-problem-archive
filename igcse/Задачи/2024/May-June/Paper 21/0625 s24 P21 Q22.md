# Question 22 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2024 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 1 · вопрос 22  
Источник: [[_pdf/2024/0625_s24_qp_21.pdf#page=7|условие, стр. 7]] · [[_pdf/2024/0625_s24_ms_21.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2024/0625_s24_er.pdf#page=7|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 s24 P21]]  
Темы: [[3.4 Sound]], [[3.1 General properties of waves]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Вычислить частоту звука по скорости и расстоянию между ближайшими сжатием и разрежением.

A sound wave travels at 330 m / s. The distance between the centre of a compression and the

centre of the nearest rarefaction in the sound wave is 2.5 cm.

What is the frequency of the sound wave?

**A** 66 Hz  
**B** 130 Hz  
**C** 6600 Hz  
**D** 13 000 Hz

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question required candidates to determine the wavelength from the information provided, convert centimetres into metres, and then recall and rearrange the equation for wave speed to calculate frequency. Many stronger candidates were able to do this correctly. The most common error made by stronger candidates was misinterpreting the information so that they used the wrong value for the wavelength. The most common errors made by weaker candidates included using the wrong value for the wavelength and forgetting to convert the units into metres.
