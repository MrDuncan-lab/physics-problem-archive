# Question 6 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2024 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 1 · вопрос 6

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить изменение импульса мяча при вертикальном отскоке от поверхности.

A ball of mass *m* falls vertically and hits a hard surface.

Its speed on hitting the surface is *v*$_{1}$.

It rebounds vertically upwards with speed *v*$_{2}$.

What is the change in momentum of the ball?

**A** *mv*$_{1}$  
**B** *mv*$_{2}$  
**C** *m*(*v*$_{1}$ + *v*$_{2}$)  
**D** *m*(*v*$_{2}$ – *v*$_{1}$)

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question assessed candidates’ knowledge of change in momentum. Few candidates answered it correctly. Candidates did not note that *v*1 and *v*2 were in opposite directions and therefore so were the momentums, so the majority of candidates chose option **D**.
