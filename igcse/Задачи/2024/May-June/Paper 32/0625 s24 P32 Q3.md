# Question 3 (7 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2024 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 3  
Источник: [[_pdf/2024/0625_s24_qp_32.pdf#page=4|условие, стр. 4–5]] · [[_pdf/2024/0625_s24_ms_32.pdf#page=7|схема оценивания, стр. 7]] · [[_pdf/2024/0625_s24_er.pdf#page=17|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 s24 P32]]  
Темы: [[1.5.3 Centre of gravity]], [[1.3 Mass and weight]], [[1.5.2 Turning effect of forces]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнить устойчивость конуса и цилиндра, вычислить вес и применить принцип моментов к наклонённому конусу.

Fig. 3.1 shows two solid shapes, a cylinder and a cone, which are made from the same material.

![[0625-s24-32-p4-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 3.1**

**(a)** State and explain which shape is the more stable.

the more stable shape is  \_\_\_\_\_\_

explanation  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(b)** The mass of the cylinder is 0.25 kg.

Calculate the weight of the cylinder.

weight =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ N **[2]**

**(c)** A horizontal force of 3.0 N tilts the cone. The cone balances on one edge, as shown in Fig. 3.2.

![[0625-s24-32-p4-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 3.2**

**(i)** Calculate the moment of the 3.0 N force about the pivot in Fig. 3.2.

moment =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ N cm **[3]**

**(ii)** Determine the moment of the weight of the cone about the pivot. Use ideas about the principle of moments.

moment of weight about pivot =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ N cm **[1]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** (because it has) lower centre of mass/gravity
>
> **(b)** (weight =) 2.5 (N)
>
> **(c)(i)** (moment =) 66 (Ncm)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | cone | M0 |
> |  | (because it has) lower centre of mass/gravity | A1 |
> | (b) | (weight =) 2.5 (N) | A2 |
> |  | (weight =) mass × *g* **OR** 0.25 × 9.8 | (C1) |
> | (c)(i) | (moment =) 66 (Ncm) | A3 |
> |  | (moment =) 3(.0) × 22 | (C2) |
> |  | moment = force × (perpendicular) distance (from pivot) | (C1) |
> | (c)(ii) | (moment of weight =) answer to **(c)(i) OR** 66 (Ncm) | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Candidates found this question challenging with many giving inadequate descriptions, for example “the cone has more surface area”. Very few candidates recognised that the centre of mass of the cone would be lower.
>
> **(b)** Most candidates correctly calculated the weight of the cylinder as 2.5 N. Those who did not usually divided the mass by *g*.
>
> **(c) (i)** Most candidates correctly calculated the moment as 66 N cm. The most common error was using an incorrect rearrangement of the equation. Candidates should be encouraged to write down the equation before attempting to rearrange it.
>
> **(ii)** Only stronger candidates realised that the moment of the weight of the cone was the same as the answer to **(i)**.
