> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать сопротивление лампы и объяснить изменение её яркости при замыкании выключателя.

A battery, a lamp L, a fixed resistor R and a switch S are connected as shown in Fig. 7.1.

6.0 V

![[0625-s24-32-p10-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 7.1**

**(a)** The potential difference (p.d.) across lamp L is 4.8 V and the current in lamp L is 0.40 A.

Calculate the resistance of lamp L.

resistance =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ Ω **[3]**

**(b)** State and explain how closing switch S affects the brightness of lamp L. **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** 12 (Ω)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | 12 (Ω) | A3 |
> |  | 4.8 / 0.4 | (C2) |
> |  | *V* = *IR* **OR** (*R* = ) *V* / *I* | (C1) |
> | (b) | (lamp is) brighter **OR** (brightness) increases | B1 |
> |  | resistance of wire and resistor in parallel is less than resistance of wire owtte<br>**OR** voltage across lamp / L increases | B1 |
> |  | (so) current in lamp increases | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** The majority of candidates correctly calculated the resistance of the lamp as 12 Ω. The most common error was using an incorrectly rearranged form of the equation *V* = *I* × *R*. The most common of these was *R* = *V* × *I* = 4.8 × 0.4 = 1.92.
>
> **(b)** Some candidates recognised that the brightness of the lamp would increase, but many could not explain how the resistance of the circuit had been decreased by closing switch S.
