# Question 9 (5 marks)

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить неисправность сетевого кабеля, опасность повреждения и правильное подключение выключателя.

**(a)** Fig. 9.1 shows an electricity cable that has a fault.

![[0625-s24-32-p12-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 9.1**

The cable is used for supplying electricity at a high voltage.

State the fault and describe the hazard shown in Fig. 9.1.

fault  \_\_\_\_\_\_

hazard  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(b)** Fig. 9.2 shows a piece of cable used in a mains circuit.

![[0625-s24-32-p12-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 9.2**

**(i)** State the name of wire X in Fig. 9.2. **[1]**

**(ii)** An electrical appliance is connected in the mains circuit. One of the wires in the cable is connected to the switch for the appliance.

State and explain which wire is connected to the switch. **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)(ii)** (so appliance is) disconnected from main / supply **OR** disconnected from high voltage (when switch is open / off)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | <u>fault: insulation damaged owtte</u> | B1 |
> |  | hazard: electrocution **OR** electric shock | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | earth (wire) | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | (switch is connected in) live (wire) | M1 |
> |  | (so appliance is) disconnected from main / supply **OR** disconnected from high voltage (when switch is open / off) | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** The majority of candidates correctly identified the fault as damaged insulation and the hazard as electrocution. Some answers lacked detail, with answers such as “the wire is damaged”.
>
> **(b) (i)** Many candidates identified wire X as the earth wire. Common errors included copper wire, negative wire and positive wire.
>
> **(ii)** The majority of candidates identified the live wire, but only a few were able to give a correct explanation for positioning the fuse in the live wire.
