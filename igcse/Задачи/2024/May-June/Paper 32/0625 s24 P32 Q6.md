# Question 6 (9 marks)

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать скорость водяных волн, объяснить преломление на мелководье и сравнить свойства электромагнитных волн.

A student studies different types of wave.

**(a)** She studies waves on the surface of water in a ripple tank. The frequency of the waves is 4.0 Hz. The wavelength of the waves is 5.0 cm.

Calculate the speed of the waves.

speed =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm / s **[3]**

**(b)** The student puts a block into the ripple tank, as shown in Fig. 6.1. The block sinks. The waves travel towards the block and then over the block.

![[0625-s24-32-p9-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 6.1**

State and explain what happens to the waves as they travel over the edge of the block. **[3]**

**(c)** The chart in Fig. 6.2 shows the main regions of the electromagnetic spectrum.

![[0625-s24-32-p9-f2.svg]]

radio waves microwaves visible light ultraviolet

**Fig. 6.2**

**(i)** State the name of **one** region in Fig. 6.2 that has longer wavelengths than visible light. **[1]**

**(ii)** Describe **one** use of ultraviolet radiation. **[1]**

**(iii)** Compare the speed of radio waves with the speed of gamma rays as they both travel through a vacuum. **[1]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** (*v* =) 20 (cm / s)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | (*v* =) 20 (cm / s) | A3 |
> |  | (*v* =) 4(.0) × 5(.0) | (C2) |
> |  | (*v* =) *f* × λ | (C1) |
> | (b) | any **three** from:<br>• refraction<br>• direction of waves / wavefronts changes<br>• (due to) change in speed<br>• wavelength changes<br>• as depth of water changes | B3 |
> | (c)(i) | radio waves **OR** microwaves **OR** infrared | B1 |
> | (c)(ii) | security marking **OR** detecting forged bank notes **OR** sterilising food / water | B1 |
> | (c)(iii) | (both have) same speed owtte | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Many candidates scored full credit for an answer of 20 cm / s. The most common errors were using an incorrect rearrangement of *v* = *f* ×λ and attempting to use speed = distance ÷ time, with frequency substituted for the time.
>
> **(b)** Most candidates correctly identified refraction, but few were able to explain this in terms of changes of speed or wavelength due to a change in depth.
>
> **(c) (i)** Most candidates correctly identified a region of the electromagnetic spectrum with wavelengths longer than visible light.
>
> **(ii)** Some candidates gave one use of ultraviolet radiation but many did not gain credit here. Common errors included vague responses such as “used in scans” or incorrect uses such as “used in X-rays or mobile phones”.
>
> **(iii)** Some candidates did not know that in a vacuum, radio waves and gamma rays travel at the same speed.
