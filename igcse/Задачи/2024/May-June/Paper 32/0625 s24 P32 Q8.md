# Question 8 (8 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2024 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 8  
Источник: [[_pdf/2024/0625_s24_qp_32.pdf#page=11|условие, стр. 11]] · [[_pdf/2024/0625_s24_ms_32.pdf#page=11|схема оценивания, стр. 11]] · [[_pdf/2024/0625_s24_er.pdf#page=17|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 s24 P32]]  
Темы: [[4.2.5 Electrical energy and electrical power]], [[4.5.6 The transformer]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать энергию зарядки аккумулятора и число витков вторичной обмотки трансформатора зарядного устройства.

**(a)** The battery in a laptop computer is connected to a battery charger for 20 minutes. The potential difference (p.d.) across the battery is 14 V. The current in the battery is 1.8  A.

Calculate the energy transferred to the battery in 20 minutes.

energy transferred =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ J **[4]**

**(b)** The battery charger includes a transformer. Fig. 8.1 shows the transformer.

![[0625-s24-32-p11-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 8.1** (not to scale)

**(i)** State the name of the material used for the core of the transformer. **[1]**

**(ii)** The transformer has 4800 turns on the primary (input) coil.

Calculate the number of turns on the secondary (output) coil.

Use information from Fig. 8.1.

number of turns =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** (*E* =) 30 000 (J)
>
> **(b)(ii)** 320 (turns)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | (*E* =) 30 000 (J) | A4 |
> |  | (*E* =) 1.8 × 1200 × 14 **OR** 2160 × 14 | (C3) |
> |  | (*E* =) 1.8 × 20 × 14 **OR** 36 × 14 | (C2) |
> |  | (*E* =) *I* × *t* × *V* **OR** *E* = *P* × *t* **AND** *P* = *I* × *V* | (C1) |
> |  | conversion 20 (minutes) = 1200 (s) | (C1) |
> | (b)(i) | (soft) iron | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | 320 (turns) | A3 |
> |  | 16 / 240 = *N*$_{\text{s}}$ / 4800 **OR** 240 / 16 = 4800 / *N*$_{\text{s}}$**OR** *N*$_{\text{s}}$ = 4800 × {16 / 240} | (C2) |
> |  | (*V*$_{\text{s}}$ / *V*$_{\text{p}}$) = (*N*$_{\text{s}}$ / *N*$_{\text{p}}$) in any form | (C1) |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Many candidates did not recall the equation for energy transferred, *E* = *I* × *t* × *V*. The most common error was the use of the equation for electrical power. Those who did recall the correct equation often did not convert the time to seconds. Centres should encourage candidates to practise writing out the standard forms of all the equations in the core subject content and then to practise rearranging the equations.
>
> **(b) (i)** Many candidates recalled that the material used for the core of the transformer was iron. Common errors included copper, aluminium and steel.
>
> **(ii)** This question was answered well by most candidates, with many scoring full credit for evaluating the number of turns on the output coil as 320.
