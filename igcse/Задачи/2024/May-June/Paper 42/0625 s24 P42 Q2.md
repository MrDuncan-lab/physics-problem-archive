# Question 2 (6 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2024 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 2 · вопрос 2  
Источник: [[_pdf/2024/0625_s24_qp_42.pdf#page=4|условие, стр. 4]] · [[_pdf/2024/0625_s24_ms_42.pdf#page=7|схема оценивания, стр. 7]] · [[_pdf/2024/0625_s24_er.pdf#page=30|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 s24 P42]]  
Темы: [[1.2 Motion]], [[1.5.1 Effects of forces]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить ускорение, рассчитать время разгона и равнодействующую сил поезда с учётом сопротивления движению.

**(a)** Define acceleration. **[1]**

**(b)** A train has a total mass of 520 000 kg. The train accelerates at 1.1 m / s$^{2}$.

**(i)** Calculate the time taken for the train to increase its speed from 15 m / s to 28 m / s.

time =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(ii)** Calculate the force required to produce an acceleration of 1.1 m / s$^{2}$ for this train.

force =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(iii)** The train uses electric motors.

Explain why the force on the train due to the motors is greater than the value calculated in **(ii)**. **[1]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)(i)** 12 s
>
> **(b)(ii)** 570 000 N

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | change in velocity per unit time **OR** rate of change of velocity **OR** (*a* =) Δ*v* / Δ*t* | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | 12 s | A2 |
> |  | (Δ*t* =) Δ*v* / *a* **OR** 13 / 1.1 | C1 |
> | (b)(ii) | 570 000 N | A2 |
> |  | *F* = *ma* **OR** (*F* =) *ma* **OR** (*F* =) 520 000 × 1.1 | C1 |
> | (b)(iii) | (additional force is needed to overcome) friction **OR** air resistance **OR** drag | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Many candidates defined acceleration as rate of change of velocity or change of velocity per unit time. The use of both the word ‘rate’ and ‘per unit time’ was incorrect.
>
> **(b) (i)** Candidates needed to use the equation which defined acceleration and rearrange it correctly to give a value for time. Most candidates did this and gave the correct unit. Weaker candidates made mistakes in rearranging the equation or omitted the unit. Some multiplied by the mass, attempting to use all the information given in the question for this part.
>
> **(ii)** Most candidates correctly recalled the equation *F* = *ma*. The correct unit for force is N, the derived unit of kg m / s$^{2}$ was not an accepted unit.
>
> **(iii)** Only stronger candidates correctly stated that the force required was greater than the force provided by the train’s motors due to the need to overcome friction or air resistance. The question asked about force, so answers referring only to energy changes, heat losses, efficiency did not answer the question.
