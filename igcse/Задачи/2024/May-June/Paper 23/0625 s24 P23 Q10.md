# Question 10 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2024 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 3 · вопрос 10  
Источник: [[_pdf/2024/0625_s24_qp_23.pdf#page=4|условие, стр. 4]] · [[_pdf/2024/0625_s24_ms_23.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2024/0625_s24_er.pdf#page=11|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить случайный характер движения частиц при броуновском движении.

Brownian motion of particles is observed.

Which statements describe the movement of the particles?

1 The particles all travel along a curved path.

2 The particles move randomly.

3 The particles all travel in the same direction.

**A** 1 and 3  
**B** 1 only  
**C** 2 and 3  
**D** 2 only

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Candidates demonstrated a good understanding of Brownian motion of particles with all stronger candidates and many weaker candidates choosing the correct answer. However, a significant number of weaker candidates believed that all the particles travel in the same direction and along a curved path and so chose option **A**.
