A cell has an electromotive force (e.m.f.) of 1.5 V.

How much energy is transferred by the cell when it drives 6.0 C of charge round a complete circuit?

**A** 4.0 J  
**B** 4.0 W  
**C** 9.0 J  
**D** 9.0 W

> [!answer]- Ответ
