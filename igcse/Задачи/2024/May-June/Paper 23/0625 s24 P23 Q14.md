> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить направление передачи энергии и постоянство температуры при плавлении льда.

Which statement describes what happens as ice at 0 °C melts to become water?

**A** Energy is absorbed and the temperature remains constant.  
**B** Energy is absorbed and the temperature rises.  
**C** Energy is released and the temperature remains constant.  
**D** Energy is released and the temperature rises.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question about what happens to energy and temperature as ice at 0 °C melts to become water was answered correctly by most stronger candidates, who knew that energy is absorbed while the temperature remains constant. Most weaker candidates incorrectly thought that the temperature rises during this change in state and therefore chose option **B** or **D**.
