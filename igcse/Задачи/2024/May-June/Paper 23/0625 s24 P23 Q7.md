# Question 7 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2024 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 3 · вопрос 7

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать общую скорость шарика и деревянного блока после неупругого столкновения.

A small ball of mass 0.10 kg travels horizontally at a speed of 600 m / s. It strikes a stationary wooden block of mass 1.9 kg resting on a frictionless horizontal surface.

The ball stays in the block.

What is the speed of the ball and the block immediately after the impact?

**A** 30 m / s  
**B** 32 m / s  
**C** 60 m / s  
**D** 130 m / s

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question assessed candidate’s abilities to apply the principle of the conservation of momentum. Most stronger candidates applied the principle and rearranged the equation correctly. However, weaker candidates found this question very challenging, with very few correct answers, and most choosing option **B** or **C**.
