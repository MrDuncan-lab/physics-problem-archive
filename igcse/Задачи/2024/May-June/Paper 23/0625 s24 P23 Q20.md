What is the speed of light in the glass?

**A** 2.0 × 10$^{8}$ m / s  
**B** 2.2 × 10$^{8}$ m / s  
**C** 2.3 × 10$^{8}$ m / s  
**D** 2.7 × 10$^{8}$ m / s

> **A**
