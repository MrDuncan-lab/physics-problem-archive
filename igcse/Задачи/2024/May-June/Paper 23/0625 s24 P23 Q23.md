> Сравнить частоты и скорости различных электромагнитных волн в вакууме.

The diagram shows three types of electromagnetic radiation listed in a particular order. The

electromagnetic radiation is travelling in a vacuum.

![[0625-s24-23-p9-f1.svg]]

Which quantities increase in magnitude going from left to right across the list?

**A** both speed and frequency  
**B** frequency only  
**C** speed only  
**D** neither speed nor frequency

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
