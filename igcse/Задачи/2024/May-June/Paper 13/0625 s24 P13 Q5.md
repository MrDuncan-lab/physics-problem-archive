> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Вычислить плотность жидкости по массе и объёму, измеренному цилиндром.

The diagram shows some liquid in a measuring cylinder.

The mass of the liquid is 16 g.

![[0625-s24-13-p4-f1.svg]]

What is the density of the liquid?

**A** 0.80 g / cm$^{3}$  
**B** 1.3 g / cm$^{3}$  
**C** 36 g / cm$^{3}$  
**D** 320 g / cm$^{3}$

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question required candidates to read the volume of the liquid in the measuring cylinder and use this value to determine the density of the liquid. Most candidates were able to do this correctly.
