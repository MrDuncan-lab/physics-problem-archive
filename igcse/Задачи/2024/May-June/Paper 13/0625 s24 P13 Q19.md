# Question 19 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2024 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 3 · вопрос 19  
Источник: [[_pdf/2024/0625_s24_qp_13.pdf#page=8|условие, стр. 8]] · [[_pdf/2024/0625_s24_ms_13.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2024/0625_s24_er.pdf#page=5|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 s24 P13]]  
Темы: [[3.2.1 Reflection of light]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Узнать закон отражения света по равенству углов падения и отражения.

A student reads the following relationship in his physics book.

*i* = *r*

What is the student reading about?

**A** diffraction due to a gap  
**B** dispersion of light by a prism  
**C** reflection in a plane mirror  
**D** refraction as light enters glass

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Most stronger candidates chose the correct option. However, many weaker candidates incorrectly believed that the relationship *i = r* was related to the refraction of light as it enters glass.
