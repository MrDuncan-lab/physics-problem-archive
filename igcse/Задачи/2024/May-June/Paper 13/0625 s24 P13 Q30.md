> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать напряжения на двух вторичных обмотках трансформатора по числу витков.

A transformer has 400 turns on the primary coil connected to the 240 V mains supply.

There are two secondary coils to serve different parts of a television. Secondary coil 1 has 2500 turns and secondary coil 2 has 20 turns.

Which row is correct?

|  | p.d. across secondary coil 1 / V | p.d. across secondary coil 2 / V |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | 12 | 3.2 |
| **B** | 1500 | 12 |
| **C** | 4200 | 12 |
| **D** | 4200 | 33 |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
