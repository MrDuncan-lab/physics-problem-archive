> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить физический смысл произведения тока, напряжения и времени.

There is a current *I* in a resistor for a time *t*. The potential difference (p.d) across the resistor is *V*.

What does the product *IVt* represent?

**A** the charge flowing through the resistor  
**B** the energy transferred by the resistor  
**C** the power dissipated by the resistor  
**D** the resistance of the resistor

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
