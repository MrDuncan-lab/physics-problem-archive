# Question 21 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2024 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 3 · вопрос 21  
Источник: [[_pdf/2024/0625_s24_qp_13.pdf#page=9|условие, стр. 9]] · [[_pdf/2024/0625_s24_ms_13.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2024/0625_s24_er.pdf#page=5|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить виды электромагнитных волн по их длинам.

The table gives the wavelengths of three electromagnetic waves.

| electromagnetic wave | wavelength / m |
|---|---|
| P | 3.2 × 10$^{\text{–3}}$ |
| Q | 5.0 × 10$^{\text{–9}}$ |
| R | 2.6 × 10$^{3}$ |

Which row correctly identifies P, Q and R?

|  | P | Q | R |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | microwave | radio wave | X-ray |
| **B** | microwave | X-ray | radio wave |
| **C** | radio wave | microwave | X-ray |
| **D** | radio wave | X-ray | microwave |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question about electromagnetic waves proved very challenging for all candidates, with responses evenly distributed across the four possible responses indicating that many had guessed the answer. Some candidates incorrectly thought that X-rays have the longest wavelength and radio waves or microwaves have the shortest wavelength.
