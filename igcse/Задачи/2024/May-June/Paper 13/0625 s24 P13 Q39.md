> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Указать основные области спектра солнечного излучения.

In which regions of the electromagnetic spectrum does the Sun radiate most of its energy?

**A** microwave to infrared  
**B** ultraviolet to X-ray  
**C** infrared to ultraviolet  
**D** radio to infrared

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Most candidates demonstrated good knowledge about the radiation emitted by the Sun.
