> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Candidates found this question on a.c. voltage challenging. Stronger candidates tended to confuse d.c. voltage with a.c. voltage and chose option **A**. Weaker candidates appeared to think that because the graph line was going up and down, it meant that the voltage was changing direction so chose option **C**.
