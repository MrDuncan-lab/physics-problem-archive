> Выбрать полезное применение теплового расширения твёрдых тел.

Solids expand when heated and contract when cooled.

In which example is there a useful application of expansion and **not** an unwanted effect?

**A** bridges  
**B** railway lines  
**C** telephone cables  
**D** thermostats

> [!answer]- Ответ
