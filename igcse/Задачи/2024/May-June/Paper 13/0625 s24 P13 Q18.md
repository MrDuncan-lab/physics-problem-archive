# Question 18 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2024 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 3 · вопрос 18  
Источник: [[_pdf/2024/0625_s24_qp_13.pdf#page=8|условие, стр. 8]] · [[_pdf/2024/0625_s24_ms_13.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2024/0625_s24_er.pdf#page=5|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 s24 P13]]  
Темы: [[3.2.3 Thin lenses]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить ход лучей после собирающей линзы, если источник расположен в её фокусе.

The diagram shows a thin converging lens with principal focuses at F$_{1}$ and F$_{2}$.

![[0625-s24-13-p8-f1.svg]]

A small light source is placed at F$_{1}$. A beam of light from the source passes through the lens.

Which statement correctly describes the beam of light emerging from the lens?

**A** The beam converges to F$_{2}$.  
**B** The beam converges to a point to the right of F$_{2}$.  
**C** The beam diverges from a point to the left of F$_{1}$.  
**D** The beam travels parallel to the principal axis.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The majority of candidates across the ability levels found this question challenging and demonstrated poor knowledge about converging lenses. Most candidates thought that the beam of light converged to F2 or to the right of F2.
