What is the relationship between temperatures in kelvin (*T* ) and temperatures in degrees Celsius

(θ)?

**A** θ = *T* + 273  
**B** θ = *T* – 273  
**C** θ = *T* + 373  
**D** θ = *T* – 373

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
