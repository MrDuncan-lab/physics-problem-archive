> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Измерить размеры изображения и расстояния в опыте с линзой, рассчитать фокусное расстояние и улучшить фокусировку.

In this experiment, you will investigate the image produced by a lens.

Refer to Fig. 3.1 and Fig. 3.2.

![[0625-s24-51-p6-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 3.1**

![[0625-s24-51-p6-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 3.2**

**(a)** Measure the height *h*$_{\text{O}}$ of the illuminated object. Fig. 3.2 shows the height to measure on the illuminated object provided.

*h*$_{\text{O}}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

- Place the lens a distance *u* = 20.0 cm from the illuminated object.
- Adjust the position of the screen until a focused image is formed on the screen.

Measure the distance *v* between the centre of the lens and the screen.

*v* =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm **[1]**

**(c)** Calculate the focal length *f* of the lens using the equation *f* = $\dfrac{uv}{( u + v )}$ .

*f* =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm **[3]**

- Place the lens at a distance *d* = 2*f* from the illuminated object.
- Place the screen at a distance *s* = 4*f* from the illuminated object.
- Adjust the position of the screen until a focused image is formed on the screen.

Record the distance *d*.

*d* =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

Measure the distance *x* between the lens and the screen.

*x* =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(e)** Measure the height *h*$_{\text{I}}$ of the image.

*h*$_{\text{I}}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(f)** In this type of experiment, it can be difficult to judge the screen position that produces the clearest image.

Suggest **one** precaution or technique to overcome this difficulty. **[1]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | 1.4 – 1.6 (or 14 – 16) | 1 |
> |  | matching unit (cm or mm) | 1 |
> | (b) | realistic value for *v* | 1 |
> | (c) | correct calculation of *f* | 1 |
> |  | 2 or 3 significant figures | 1 |
> |  | 14 – 16 (cm) | 1 |
> | (d) | *d* = 2*f* (± 1 cm) | 1 |
> |  | *x* = 2*f* (± 1 cm) | 1 |
> | (e) | value given for *h*$_{\text{I}}$with unit | 1 |
> |  | *h*$_{\text{I}}$ = *h*$_{\text{O}}$to within 10% | 1 |
> | (f) | darkened room / bright object **OR** move screen slowly / back and forth / approach clearest position from both directions (and<br>average) | 1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Most candidates obtained a value for *h*O within the acceptable range and included the appropriate unit (cm or mm).
>
> **(b)** Most candidates obtained a realistic value for the image distance.
>
> **(c)** The calculation of focal length was completed successfully by many candidates. An answer to two or three significant figures was expected.
>
> **(d)** In this question, many candidates needed to read the instruction more carefully to produce values of *d* and *x* that were both close to 2*f*. Many candidates gave answers that were very far from the accepted range.
>
> **(e)** The image height *h*$_{\text{I}}$ should have been equal to *h*O within 10%. A significant number of candidates did not achieve this and this was probably due to not moving the screen to the position which gave the clearest focus of the image.
>
> **(f)** Candidates were expected to suggest a suitable technique such as using a darkened room or a bright object lamp. Moving the screen slowly back and forth to find the clearest image was another sensible suggestion.
