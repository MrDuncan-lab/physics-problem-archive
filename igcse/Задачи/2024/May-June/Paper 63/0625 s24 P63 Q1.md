> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить плотность пластилина геометрическим методом и вытеснением воды, сравнить точность результатов.

A student determines the density of modelling clay by two methods.

**Method 1**

He uses the block of modelling clay shown in Fig. 1.1.

![[0625-s24-63-p2-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 1.1**

**(a)** **(i)** Measure the dimensions of the block of modelling clay shown in Fig. 1.1. Record the values in centimetres to the nearest millimetre.

*l* =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm

*w* =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm

*h* =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm **[1]**

**(ii)** Calculate the volume *V*$_{\text{A}}$ of the block. Use your measurements from **(a)(i)** and the equation *V*$_{\text{A}}$ = *l* × *w* × *h*.

*V*$_{\text{A}}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm$^{3}$ **[1]**

**(b)** Suggest why the value of *V*$_{\text{A}}$ is only an approximation of the volume of the block.

Describe how the accuracy of *V*$_{\text{A}}$ can be improved.

suggestion  \_\_\_\_\_\_

improvement  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(c)** Record the mass *m*$_{\text{A}}$ of the block shown on the top-pan balance in Fig. 1.2.

*m*$_{\text{A}}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ g

![[0625-s24-63-p3-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 1.2**

Calculate a value ρ$_{\text{A}}$ for the density of the modelling clay. Use your results from **(a)(ii)** and **(c)** and the equation $\rho_{\text{A}} = \dfrac{m_{\text{A}}}{V_{\text{A}}}$. Include the unit for the density.

ρ$_{\text{A}}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**Method 2**

**(d)** The student then uses a smaller block of modelling clay.

![[0625-s24-63-p4-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 1.3**

Record the weight *W*$_{\text{B}}$ of the block of modelling clay shown in Fig. 1.3.

*W*$_{\text{B}}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ N **[1]**

**(e)** **(i)** The student pours water into a measuring cylinder.

Record the volume *V*$_{1}$ of the water in the measuring cylinder shown in Fig. 1.4.

*V*$_{1}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm$^{3}$ **[1]**

![[0625-s24-63-p5-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 1.4**

**(ii)** Describe briefly how a measuring cylinder is read to obtain a value for the volume of water. **[1]**

**(f)** **(i)** The student lowers the modelling clay into the water, as shown in Fig. 1.5.

![[0625-s24-63-p5-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 1.5**

Record the new reading *V*$_{2}$ of the measuring cylinder.

*V*$_{2}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm$^{3}$

Calculate another value ρ$_{\text{B}}$ for the density of modelling clay. Use your value for *V*$_{2}$, your

readings from **(d)** and **(e)(i)** and the equation $\rho_{\text{B}} = \dfrac{W_{\text{B}} \times k}{(V_{2} - V_{1})}$, where *k* = 100 g / N.

ρ$_{\text{B}}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(ii)** Suggest which of ρ$_{\text{A}}$ and ρ$_{\text{B}}$ is likely to be the more accurate value.

Justify your answer by referring to method 1 and method 2. **[1]**
