> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить плотность пластилина двумя способами по геометрическим размерам и вытеснению воды, улучшая точность измерений.

In this experiment, you will determine the density of modelling clay by two methods.

Refer to Fig. 1.1.

**Method 1**

![[0625-s24-53-p2-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 1.1**

**(a)** **(i)** Measure the length *l*, width *w* and height *h* of block A. Record your values in centimetres to the nearest millimetre.

*l* =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm

*w* =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm

*h* =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm **[1]**

**(ii)** Calculate the volume *V*$_{\text{A}}$ of block A. Use your measurements from **(a)(i)** and the equation *V*$_{\text{A}}$ = *l* × *w* × *h*.

*V*$_{\text{A}}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm$^{3}$ **[1]**

**(b)** Suggest a possible source of inaccuracy in measuring the dimensions of the block.

Describe how the accuracy of these measurements can be improved.

You are **not** required to do this improved investigation.

suggestion  \_\_\_\_\_\_

improvement  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(c)** Measure the mass *m*$_{\text{A}}$ of block A. Use the top-pan balance.

*m*$_{\text{A}}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ g **[1]**

**(d)** Calculate a value ρ$_{\text{A}}$ for the density of the modelling clay. Use your results from **(a)(ii)** and **(c)** and the equation $\rho_{\text{A}} = \dfrac{m_{\text{A}}}{V_{\text{A}}}$. Include the unit for the density.

ρ$_{\text{A}}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**Method 2**

**(e)** Measure the weight *W*$_{\text{B}}$ of block B, as shown in Fig. 1.2.

*W*$_{\text{B}}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ N **[1]**

![[0625-s24-53-p3-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 1.2**

**(f)** **(i)** Pour approximately 60 cm$^{3}$ of water into the measuring cylinder.

Measure and record the actual volume *V*$_{1}$ of the water in the measuring cylinder.

*V*$_{1}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm$^{3}$

Remove the loop of thread from the force meter and lower block B carefully into the water in the measuring cylinder, as shown in Fig. 1.3.

![[0625-s24-53-p4-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 1.3**

Measure and record the new reading *V*$_{2}$ of the measuring cylinder.

*V*$_{2}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm$^{3}$ **[1]**

**(ii)** Draw an arrow on Fig. 1.4 to show the correct line of sight to obtain the value for the volume of water in the measuring cylinder.

![[0625-s24-53-p4-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 1.4** **[1]**

**(g)** Calculate another value ρ$_{\text{B}}$ for the density of modelling clay. Use your readings from **(e)** and **(f)** and the equation $\rho_{\text{B}} = \dfrac{W_{\text{B}} \times k}{(V_{2} - V_{1})}$, where *k* = 100 g / N.

ρ$_{\text{B}}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**
