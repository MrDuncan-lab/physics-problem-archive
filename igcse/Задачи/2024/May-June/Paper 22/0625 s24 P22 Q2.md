Источник: [[_pdf/2024/0625_s24_qp_22.pdf#page=2|условие, стр. 2]] · [[_pdf/2024/0625_s24_ms_22.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2024/0625_s24_er.pdf#page=9|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить физический смысл площади под графиком скорости от времени.

The motion of an object is represented by the speed–time graph shown.

![[0625-s24-22-p2-f2.svg]]

Which quantity is equal to the area under the graph?

**A** acceleration  
**B** average speed  
**C** distance travelled  
**D** kinetic energy

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Candidates demonstrated excellent knowledge of speed–time graphs and nearly all stronger candidates chose the correct response **C**, but some weaker candidates thought that the area under the graph represented acceleration.
