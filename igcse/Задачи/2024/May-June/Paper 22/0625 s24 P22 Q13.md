> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Вычислить нагрев воды в водопаде при превращении гравитационной энергии во внутреннюю.

The temperature of the water at the bottom of a waterfall is greater than the temperature of the

water at the top.

The energy in the gravitational potential store of the water at the top is transferred to the thermal store at the bottom.

The specific heat capacity of water is 4200 J / (kg °C).

What is the temperature difference for a waterfall of height 21 m?

**A** 0.0050 °C  
**B** 0.049 °C  
**C** 20 °C  
**D** 200 °C

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
