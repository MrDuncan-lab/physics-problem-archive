> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Вычислить вес астронавта по массе и напряжённости гравитационного поля планеты.

An astronaut of mass 80 kg is standing on a planet with gravitational field strength *g* = 3.8 N / kg.

What is the weight of the astronaut on this planet?

**A** 780 N  
**B** 300 N  
**C** 210 N  
**D** 21 N

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question was answered correctly by all stronger candidates. Some weaker candidates could not recall the correct equation to calculate weight and instead divided the mass by the gravitational field strength.
