> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Различить графики переменного и постоянного напряжений.

A student draws graphs of an a.c. supply and a d.c. supply.

![[0625-s24-22-p10-f1.svg]]

Which row shows the correct labels for W, X and Y?

|  | label of W | a.c. supply | d.c. supply |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | time | X | Y |
| **B** | time | Y | X |
| **C** | voltage | X | Y |
| **D** | voltage | Y | X |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question about a.c. supply and d.c. supply was answered correctly by all stronger candidates. However, although weaker candidates correctly identified that the *y*-axis of the graph represented voltage, they mixed up the graphs for a.c. supply and d.c. supply and so chose option **C**.
