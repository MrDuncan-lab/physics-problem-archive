> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Вычислить минимальную задержку обмена радиосообщениями с планетоходом по расстоянию до пункта управления.

A remote-controlled vehicle is travelling on the surface of a planet. The vehicle senses an

obstacle ahead. It sends a radio message to the control room from where it is being controlled. The control room is 2.4 × 10$^{6}$ km away from the vehicle. The control room sends a message back to the vehicle telling it to stop.

What is the minimum time that elapses between the vehicle sensing the obstacle and receiving the message back from the control room?

**A** 8.0 ms  
**B** 16 ms  
**C** 8.0 s  
**D** 16 s

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
