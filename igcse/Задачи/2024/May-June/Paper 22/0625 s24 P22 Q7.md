> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить энергетический ресурс, первичным источником энергии которого является Солнце.

The table gives four energy resources and states whether the main source of energy for the resource is the Sun.

Which row is correct?

|  | energy resource | main source of energy is the Sun |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | geothermal | yes |
| **B** | oil | no |
| **C** | water held behind a dam | yes |
| **D** | wind | no |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question assessed candidates’ knowledge of energy resources. Many stronger candidates chose the correct option but overall the responses from candidates were distributed across the four options, with weaker candidates incorrectly believing that the Sun is the main source of energy for a geothermal energy resource.
