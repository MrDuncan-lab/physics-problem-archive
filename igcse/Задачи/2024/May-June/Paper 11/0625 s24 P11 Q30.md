> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбрать фактор, не влияющий на величину ЭДС индукции при движении магнита в катушку.

A student moves a magnet into a coil, as shown. The voltmeter measures the magnitude of the

electromotive force (e.m.f.) induced in the coil.

![[0625-s24-11-p13-f1.svg]]

Which quantity does **not** affect the magnitude of the induced e.m.f.?

**A** the number of turns per unit length in the coil  
**B** the polarity of the magnet  
**C** the speed of the magnet  
**D** the strength of the magnet

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Candidates found this question on induced electromotive force challenging. Many stronger candidates answered it correctly, but a common misconception of both stronger and weaker candidates was that the speed of the magnet did not affect the magnitude of the induced e.m.f.
