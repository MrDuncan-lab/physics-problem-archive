> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбрать материал защитной облицовки контейнера для радиоактивных источников.

A box is used for storing radioactive sources.

What is the best material to use for lining the box to prevent radiation from escaping?

**A** aluminium  
**B** lead  
**C** paper  
**D** plastic

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Although most stronger candidates knew that lead was the best material to use for lining the box, a significant number of candidates incorrectly thought that aluminium was the best material and therefore chose option **A**.
