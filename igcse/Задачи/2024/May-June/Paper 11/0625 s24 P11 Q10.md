Источник: [[_pdf/2024/0625_s24_qp_11.pdf#page=6|условие, стр. 6]] · [[_pdf/2024/0625_s24_ms_11.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2024/0625_s24_er.pdf#page=1|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

Темы: [[2.1.2 Particle model]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объяснить случайное движение дымовых частиц столкновениями с молекулами воздуха.

Diagram 1 shows apparatus being used to observe smoke particles.

Diagram 2 shows how a smoke particle moves randomly.

![[0625-s24-11-p6-f1.svg]]

Why do the smoke particles move randomly?

**A** They are hit by air particles.  
**B** They are less dense than air.  
**C** They are moved by convection currents.  
**D** They gain energy from the light.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Many weaker candidates incorrectly believed that the smoke particles moved randomly because they are less dense than air, and therefore chose option **B**.
