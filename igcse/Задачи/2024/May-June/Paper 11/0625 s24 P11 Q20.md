**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2024 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 1 · вопрос 20

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнить частоту и длину волны синего света с красным.

Red light of frequency 430 × 10$^{12}$ Hz has a wavelength of 700 × 10$^{\text{–9}}$ m.

What is possible for blue light?

**A** a frequency of 190 × 10$^{12}$ Hz and a wavelength of 450 × 10$^{\text{–9}}$ m  
**B** a frequency of 190 × 10$^{12}$ Hz and a wavelength of 950 × 10$^{\text{–9}}$ m  
**C** a frequency of 670 × 10$^{12}$ Hz and a wavelength of 450 × 10$^{\text{–9}}$ m  
**D** a frequency of 670 × 10$^{12}$ Hz and a wavelength of 950 × 10$^{\text{–9}}$ m

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
