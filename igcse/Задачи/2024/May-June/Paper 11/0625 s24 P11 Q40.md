What is a light-year?

**A** the time for light to travel 1 km in space  
**B** the time for light to travel from the Sun to the Earth  
**C** the distance travelled in space by light in one year  
**D** the distance travelled by light from the Sun to the Earth

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
