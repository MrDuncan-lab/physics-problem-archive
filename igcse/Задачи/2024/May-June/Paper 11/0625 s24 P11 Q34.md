> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить изменение элемента и протонного числа при альфа-распаде.

An isotope of radon is radioactive. It decays by emitting an α-particle.

What happens to the nucleus of a radon atom during the emission of the α-particle?

**A** It becomes the nucleus of a different isotope of radon with fewer neutrons.  
**B** It becomes the nucleus of a different isotope of radon with more neutrons.  
**C** It becomes the nucleus of an element with a higher proton number.  
**D** It becomes the nucleus of an element with a lower proton number.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Candidates struggled to recall the necessary facts about radioactive decay by the emission of an alpha particle. The majority of candidates demonstrated poor knowledge with strong evidence of guesswork as answers were spread across all four options almost equally.
