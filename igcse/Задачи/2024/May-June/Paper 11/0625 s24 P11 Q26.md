> Определить электрический ток и способ подключения амперметра.

What is an electric current and how is an ammeter positioned in a circuit to measure the current

in a resistor?

|  | what is an electric current? | positioning of an ammeter |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | a flow of charge | in parallel with the resistor |
| **B** | a flow of charge | in series with the resistor |
| **C** | a flow of voltage | in parallel with the resistor |
| **D** | a flow of voltage | in series with the resistor |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
