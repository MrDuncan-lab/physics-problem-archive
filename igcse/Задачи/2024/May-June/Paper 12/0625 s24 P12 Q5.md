# Question 5 (1 mark)

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить нагрузку по графику удлинения пружины и её начальной и конечной длинам.

The diagram shows a load–extension graph for a steel spring.

The length of the spring with no load attached is 2.0 cm.

![[0625-s24-12-p3-f1.svg]]

Which load increases the length of the spring to 6.0 cm?

**A** 0.5 N  
**B** 1.0 N  
**C** 1.5 N  
**D** 2.0 N

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The majority of candidates found this question challenging and there was evidence that they did not read all of the information carefully enough. Most candidates misinterpreted the question, confusing the extension with the length of the spring and therefore read the value for the load on the graph when the extension was 6 cm instead of when the extension was 4 cm.
