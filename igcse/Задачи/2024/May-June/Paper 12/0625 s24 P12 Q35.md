> Определить вид проникающего через свинец излучения и действие на него электрического поля.

The emissions from a radioactive source pass through a sheet of lead that is 10 mm thick.

Which type of radiation is emitted from the source and how is the radiation affected by an electric field?

|  | type of radiation | effect of electric field on radiation |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | α | radiation deflected |
| **B** | α | radiation **not** deflected |
| **C** | γ | radiation deflected |
| **D** | γ | radiation **not** deflected |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
