> Объяснить безопасное хранение радиоактивных отходов в затвердевшем стекле.

Radioactive waste is often mixed with liquid glass. This mixture solidifies and is stored in steel

drums.

Why is this process used to store radioactive waste?

**A** It reduces the decay rate.  
**B** It reduces the half-life of the waste.  
**C** The waste is contained in a solid block so it cannot leak out.  
**D** The glass is transparent so the waste can be seen.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
