> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбрать выражение для работы против силы тяжести при подъёме ящика по лестнице.

A box of mass 2.0 kg and weight 20 N is carried to the top of some stairs.

![[0625-s24-12-p4-f1.svg]]

How is the work done against gravity on the box calculated?

**A** 2.0 × 1.8  
**B** 20 × 1.2  
**C** 2.0 × 1.2  
**D** 20 × 1.2 × 1.8

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Many candidates struggled to recall the quantities needed to calculate the work done on the box. Most candidates incorrectly believed that the width of the stairs was required in the calculation.
