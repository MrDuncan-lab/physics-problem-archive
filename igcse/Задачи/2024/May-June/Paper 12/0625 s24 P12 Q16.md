> Вычислить длину волны и частоту по числу волн, радиусу пруда и времени.

The radius of a circular pond is 3.0 m.

Circular waves are made at the centre of the pond.

There are 10 whole waves between the centre and the edge of the pond and it takes 5.0 s for all 10 waves to reach the edge.

What is the wavelength and the frequency of the waves?

|  | wavelength / m | frequency / Hz |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | 0.30 | 0.50 |
| **B** | 0.30 | 2.0 |
| **C** | 0.60 | 0.50 |
| **D** | 0.60 | 2.0 |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
