> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбрать материал ложки с низкой теплопроводностью для защиты руки от ожога.

A student stirs a hot liquid in a pan with a spoon.

Which row explains which material the spoon should be made from so that the student does **not** burn their hand?

|  | material of spoon | explanation |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | metal | it is a good conductor |
| **B** | metal | it is a good insulator |
| **C** | wood | it is a good conductor |
| **D** | wood | it is a good insulator |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
