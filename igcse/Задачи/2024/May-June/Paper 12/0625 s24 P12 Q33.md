> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить обязательное общее свойство изотопов одного элемента.

Four statements about isotopes of a certain element are listed.

Which statement about the isotopes **must** be correct?

**A** They are radioactive.  
**B** They are unstable.  
**C** They have the same number of neutrons.  
**D** They have the same number of protons.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
