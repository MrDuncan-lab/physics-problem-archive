> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить виды электромагнитного излучения, вызывающие мутации, ожоги кожи и внутренний нагрев тканей.

Excessive exposure to some regions of the electromagnetic spectrum can cause harmful effects

to people.

Which regions cause mutation of cells in the body, skin burns and internal heating of body cells?

|  | mutation of cells | skin burns | internal heating of body cells |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | radio waves | infrared | visible light |
| **B** | radio waves | ultraviolet | microwaves |
| **C** | X-rays | infrared | microwaves |
| **D** | X-rays | ultraviolet | visible light |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
