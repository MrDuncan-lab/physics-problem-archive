> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить самого быстрого бегуна на втором круге по накопленным временам прохождения дистанции.

Four athletes run twice around a track. The table shows their times at the end of each lap.

Which athlete runs the second lap the fastest?

|  | time at end of first lap / s | time at end of second lap / s |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | 22.99 | 47.04 |
| **B** | 23.04 | 47.00 |
| **C** | 23.16 | 47.18 |
| **D** | 23.39 | 47.24 |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question required candidates to subtract the two times in the table in order to determine the shortest time for the second lap. Most stronger candidates did this correctly. However, weaker candidates struggled to interpret the table and were more likely to choose option **B** as it was the smallest time.
