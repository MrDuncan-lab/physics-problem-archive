> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i) (ii)** Most candidates had a good understanding of the basic ideas of the simple kinetic theory model and answered well here.
>
> **(iii)** Some candidates only described one aspect of the motion, usually that the motion was random. Two aspects were required for full credit.
>
> **(b) (i)** The vast majority of candidates gave the correct answer.
>
> **(ii)** There were a wide variety of answers but only the strongest candidates gained full credit. Most candidates correctly stated that the liquid molecules became gas or vapour molecules. A number of candidates focused their answers on the cooling effect of evaporation, but this was not required here.
