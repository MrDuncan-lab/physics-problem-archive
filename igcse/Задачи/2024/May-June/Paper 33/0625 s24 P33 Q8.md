# Question 8 (9 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2024 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 3 · вопрос 8

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Подключить вольтметр и рассчитать сопротивление и мощность лампы по току и напряжению.

Fig. 8.1 shows part of a circuit for measuring the resistance of a lamp.

![[0625-s24-33-p11-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 8.1**

**(a)** Draw on Fig. 8.1 to show how to connect a voltmeter to measure the potential difference across the lamp. Use the electrical symbol for a voltmeter. **[2]**

**(b)** The current in the lamp is 0.41 A and the potential difference across the lamp is 12 V.

Calculate the resistance of the lamp.

resistance =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ Ω **[3]**

**(c)** Calculate the electrical power transferred in the lamp. Include the unit.

power transferred =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

unit  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[4]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** 29 (Ω)
>
> **(c)** 4.9

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | correct symbol | B1 |
> |  | voltmeter in parallel with lamp | B1 |
> | (b) | 29 (Ω) | A3 |
> |  | 12 ÷ 0.41 | (C2) |
> |  | (*R* =) *V* ÷ *I* **OR** *V = I* × *R* in any form | (C1) |
> | (c) | 4.9 | A3 |
> |  | 0.41 × 12 **OR** 0.41$^{2}$ × 29 | (C2) |
> |  | (*P* =) *I* × *V* in any form<br>**OR** (*P* =) *I*$^{2}$ × *R* | (C1) |
> |  | W | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Many candidates answered this question well. A common error was placing the voltmeter in series with the ammeter. Some candidates did not give a response.
>
> **(b)** The majority of candidates correctly calculated the resistance of the lamp to be 29 Ω. A common error was using an incorrectly rearranged form of the equation *V* = *I* × *R* with the most common of these being *R = V × I*.
>
> **(c)** Many candidates did not recall the equation for power transferred as *P = I × V.* Incorrect attempts to find the power using power = energy ÷ time and *V* = *I* × *R* were seen. Some candidates did not give the correct unit. Some candidates did not give a response.
