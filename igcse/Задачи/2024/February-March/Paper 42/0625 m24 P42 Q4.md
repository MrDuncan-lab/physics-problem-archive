# Question 4 (8 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · February/March 2024 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 2 · вопрос 4  
Источник: [[_pdf/2024/0625_m24_qp_42.pdf#page=6|условие, стр. 6]] · [[_pdf/2024/0625_m24_ms_42.pdf#page=7|схема оценивания, стр. 7]] · [[_pdf/2024/0625_m24_er.pdf#page=9|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 m24 P42]]  
Темы: [[2.2.2 Specific heat capacity]], [[1.4 Density]], [[1.7.4 Power]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить удельную теплоёмкость, рассчитать массу масла, энергию его нагревания и необходимую мощность.

**(a)** Define specific heat capacity. **[2]**

**(b)** A volume of 0.0024 m$^{3}$ of oil is heated in a pan for 7.0 min. The temperature of the oil increases from 20 °C to 180 °C.

The density of the oil is 910 kg / m$^{3}$. The specific heat capacity of the oil is 2000 J / (kg °C).

**(i)** Calculate the mass of oil in the pan.

mass =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(ii)** Calculate the energy required to increase the temperature of the oil.

energy =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(iii)** Calculate the power required to supply the energy calculated in **(b)(ii)**.

power =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** energy transferred per unit mass per unit temperature change
>
> **(b)(i)** (*m* =) 2.2 kg
>
> **(b)(ii)** 7.0 × 10$^{5}$ J **OR** 700 000 J
>
> **(b)(iii)** 1700 W

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | energy transferred per unit mass per unit temperature change | A2 |
> |  | (thermal) energy (transferred) per unit temperature change | (C1) |
> | (b)(i) | (*m* =) 2.2 kg | A2 |
> |  | *m* = ρ*V* in any form **OR** 910 × 0.0024 | (C1) |
> | (b)(ii) | 7.0 × 10$^{5}$ J **OR** 700 000 J | A2 |
> |  | *c* = ∆*E* / { *m*∆θ } **OR** (∆*E* =) *mc*∆θ**OR** 2.2 × 2000 × 160 **OR** 2.184 × 2000 × 160 | (C1) |
> | (b)(iii) | 1700 W | A2 |
> |  | (*P* =) *E* / *t* **OR** 700 000 / 7 × 60 **OR** 704 000 / 7 × 60 **OR** 698 880 / 7 × 60 | (C1) |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Stronger candidates clearly stated that the specific heat capacity is the energy transferred per unit mass per unit temperature. Candidates most often expressed this as the energy needed to raise the temperature of 1 kg mass by 1 °C. Some candidates confused a rise of 1 °C with a rise to 1 °C. A definition in symbols is clearer when candidates use symbols given in the syllabus, e.g. *T* or *θ* for temperature and *t* for time. Where candidates use other symbols, they must be defined. Some candidates confused temperature and time.
>
> **(b) (i)** Almost all candidates recalled the equation *m* = *ρV* to obtain the mass of oil. Occasionally candidates made a transcription error when substituting the volume of oil. Some weaker candidates tried to use an equation with specific heat capacity in this question.
>
> **(ii)** Candidates usually knew the equation ∆*E* = *mc*∆*θ* to use for this question. There were a few transcription or arithmetic errors seen. In a symbol equation, ∆*T* is accepted for ∆*θ*. Some weaker candidates used time instead of temperature change in their calculation. ∆*t* is not accepted for ∆*θ*. Also, *Q* is not accepted for *E*.
>
> **(iii)** The formula *P* = *E* / *t* was well known. A common mistake was to substitute time in minutes rather than seconds.
