# Question 11 (5 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · February/March 2024 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 2 · вопрос 11  
Источник: [[_pdf/2024/0625_m24_qp_42.pdf#page=18|условие, стр. 18]] · [[_pdf/2024/0625_m24_ms_42.pdf#page=11|схема оценивания, стр. 11]] · [[_pdf/2024/0625_m24_er.pdf#page=9|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 m24 P42]]  
Темы: [[6.2.2 Stars]], [[6.2.3 The Universe]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Указать условие устойчивости звезды, определить постоянную Хаббла и оценить возраст Вселенной.

**(a)** State the condition required for a protostar to become a stable star. **[1]**

**(b)** **(i)** Define the Hubble constant. **[2]**

**(ii)** The current estimate for the Hubble constant is 2.2 × 10$^{\text{–18}}$ per second.

State the equation which gives an estimate for the age of the Universe. **[1]**

**(iii)** Calculate an estimate for the age of the Universe.

estimate of age =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ s **[1]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)(i)** ratio of the speed at which the galaxy is moving away from the Earth/observer to its distance (from the Earth/observer)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | inward force / force of gravitational attraction, is balanced by an outward force / force due to fusion reactions | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | ratio of the speed at which the galaxy is moving away from the Earth/observer to its distance (from the Earth/observer) | A2 |
> |  | ratio of speed (of a galaxy) to distance (away from observer) | (C1) |
> | (b)(ii) | (age of universe =) *d* / *v* = 1 / *H*0 **OR** age (of universe) = 1 / *H*0 | B1 |
> | (b)(iii) | 4.5 × 10$^{17}$ (s) | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Stronger answers stated that in a stable star the inward gravitational force is balanced by an equal and opposite outward force due to the fusion reactions in the star. Some candidates expressed this equilibrium in terms of two pressures balancing. Answers that suggested a force is balanced by a pressure or an energy did not gain credit. A few candidates made contradictory statements such as defining gravitational force as outwards.
>
> **(b) (i)** This question required a precise answer stating that the Hubble constant is the ratio of recessional speed of a galaxy (or star) to the distance of the galaxy (or star) from the Earth (or observer). Candidates who defined *H*0 as the subject of the equation *H*0 = *v* / *d* without defining *v* and *d* gained some credit. Common misconceptions here included reference to the distance travelled by a galaxy or reference to the speed that the Universe is moving away from the Earth. Some candidates gave a use of the Hubble constant, e.g., to estimate the age of the Universe, rather than a definition.
>
> **(ii)** This question required either an equation with age of the Universe as the subject or the equation 1 / *H*0 = *d* / *v*. Common insufficient answers here included writing an equation with *H*0 as the subject or writing the term 1 / *H*0 on its own.
>
> **(iii)** Many candidates correctly evaluated 1 / *H*0 in s. The command word was ‘calculate’ and the answer line had the unit s so there was no credit for recalling a value for the age of the Universe in e.g., years.
