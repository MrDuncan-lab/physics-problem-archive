# Question 6 (6 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · February/March 2024 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 2 · вопрос 6  
Источник: [[_pdf/2024/0625_m24_qp_42.pdf#page=10|условие, стр. 10]] · [[_pdf/2024/0625_m24_ms_42.pdf#page=9|схема оценивания, стр. 9]] · [[_pdf/2024/0625_m24_er.pdf#page=9|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 m24 P42]]  
Темы: [[3.2.3 Thin lenses]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Восстановить положение и фокус линзы по предмету и изображению, определить изменение изображения при перемещении предмета.

Fig. 6.1 shows a full-scale diagram of an object O and its image I produced by a converging lens. The lens and its position on the principal axis are not shown.

![[0625-m24-42-p10-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 6.1**

**(a)** On Fig. 6.1, draw:

- a single ray to locate the position of the centre of the converging lens
- a line to represent the position of the lens and label the line L. **[2]**

**(b)** Determine the focal length of the lens by drawing another ray on Fig. 6.1.

focal length =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(c)** The object is moved 2.0 cm closer to the lens.

State **two** changes to the characteristics of the image.

1  \_\_\_\_\_\_

2  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** line labelled L drawn perpendicular to principal axis at its intersection with previous ray

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | ray from top of object to tip of image | M1 |
> |  | line labelled L drawn perpendicular to principal axis at its intersection with previous ray | A1 |
> | (b) | ray from top of O parallel to principal axis to lens **AND** ray from lens to tip of I<br>**OR** ray from tip of I parallel to principal axis to lens **AND** ray from lens to top of O | B1 |
> |  | 2.1 cm | B1 |
> | (c) | virtual | B1 |
> |  | upright | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Most candidates used a sharp pencil and ruler to draw the ray accurately and to find the position of the lens. Few candidates were unable to place the lens correctly at the intersection of the ray with the principal axis.
>
> **(b)** Most candidates drew correct rays from O to their lens position and from the lens to the image. Stronger candidates measured the focal length correctly. Some candidates only gave an answer to 1 significant figure, omitted the unit or did not draw the ray sufficiently parallel to the principal axis to obtain an accurate value for the focal length. Weaker candidates often did not know how to measure the focal length. Several alternative measurements were given, including distances along the principal axis to O or I.
>
> **(c)** Stronger candidates clearly stated that the image would be virtual and upright. Answers that were not characteristics of an image, e.g. “the image is on the same side of the lens as the object”, were ignored. The command word showed that candidates were not expected to determine from **Fig. 6.1** how the magnification of the image changed. A few candidates attempted to compare magnification and often gave contradictory statements, e.g., “image is larger than original image”.
