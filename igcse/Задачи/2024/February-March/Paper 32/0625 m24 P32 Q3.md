# Question 3 (6 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · February/March 2024 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 3  
Источник: [[_pdf/2024/0625_m24_qp_32.pdf#page=4|условие, стр. 4]] · [[_pdf/2024/0625_m24_ms_32.pdf#page=7|схема оценивания, стр. 7]] · [[_pdf/2024/0625_m24_er.pdf#page=5|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 m24 P32]]  
Темы: [[1.8 Pressure]], [[1.5.2 Turning effect of forces]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать давление компьютера на стол и момент силы, наклоняющей его.

Fig. 3.1 shows a computer on the surface of a desk.

![[0625-m24-32-p4-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 3.1**

**(a)** The weight of the computer is 48 N. The area of the computer in contact with the surface of the desk is 20 cm$^{2}$.

Calculate the pressure due to the computer on the surface of the desk.

pressure =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ N / cm$^{2}$ **[3]**

**(b)** A student uses a force of 12 N to tilt the computer as shown in Fig. 3.2.

![[0625-m24-32-p4-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 3.2**

Calculate the moment of the 12 N force about the pivot.

moment =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ N cm **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** (*P* =) 2.4 (N / cm$^{2}$)
>
> **(b)** (moment = ) 380 (N cm)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | (*P* =) 2.4 (N / cm$^{2}$) | A3 |
> |  | (*P* =) 48 ÷ 20 | (C2) |
> |  | (*P* =) *F* ÷ *A* | (C1) |
> | (b) | (moment = ) 380 (N cm) | A3 |
> |  | (moment = ) 12 × 32 | (C2) |
> |  | moment = force × (perp.) distance from pivot | (C1) |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** The vast majority of candidates gave the correct answer. The most common errors were to either invert the equation for pressure, or to simply multiply the force and the area.
>
> **(b)** The vast majority of candidates answered correctly. The calculation of the moment of a force was well understood by most candidates.
