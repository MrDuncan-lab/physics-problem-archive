# Question 8 (6 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · February/March 2024 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 8  
Источник: [[_pdf/2024/0625_m24_qp_32.pdf#page=9|условие, стр. 9]] · [[_pdf/2024/0625_m24_ms_32.pdf#page=9|схема оценивания, стр. 9]] · [[_pdf/2024/0625_m24_er.pdf#page=5|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 m24 P32]]  
Темы: [[4.1 Simple phenomena of magnetism]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Описать исследование поля полосового магнита, назвать материал и применение постоянного магнита.

**(a)** Fig. 8.1 shows a permanent bar magnet.

![[0625-m24-32-p9-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 8.1**

Describe an experiment to identify the **pattern** and **directions** of the magnetic field lines around the bar magnet. You may draw on Fig. 8.1 as part of your answer. **[4]**

**(b)** State the name of a material that is suitable for a permanent magnet. **[1]**

**(c)** State **one** use of a permanent magnet. **[1]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | use of compass to give direction of field lines<br>first method<br>(use of plotting) compass(es)<br>idea of mark arrow position **OR** move compass in direction of<br>arrow | B1 |
> |  | start from different position(s) **OR** join up marks / draw lines<br>(to show pattern)<br>**OR** alternative method<br>(use of plotting) compass(es)<br>place number of compasses around magnet<br>idea that arrows line up to show pattern | B3 |
> | (b) | steel | B1 |
> | (c) | electric motors **OR** loudspeakers **OR** burglar alarms | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Many candidates answered well, with some very clear descriptions of how to identify both the pattern and direction of the magnetic field using either one or more plotting compasses or a combination of iron filings and a plotting compass. However, despite this experiment being on the syllabus for many years, a large number of candidates did not know how to do this.
>
> **(b)** The majority of candidates answered correctly. The most common error was to give iron as a suitable material.
>
> **(c)** The majority of candidates identified a valid use for a permanent magnet. However, there were many answers that were too vague for credit, including “in a kitchen appliance” or “in a door” or “picking up metals in a scrapyard”.
