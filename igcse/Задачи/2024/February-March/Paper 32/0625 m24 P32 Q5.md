# Question 5 (7 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · February/March 2024 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 5  
Источник: [[_pdf/2024/0625_m24_qp_32.pdf#page=6|условие, стр. 6]] · [[_pdf/2024/0625_m24_ms_32.pdf#page=8|схема оценивания, стр. 8]] · [[_pdf/2024/0625_m24_er.pdf#page=5|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Описать частицы жидкого бензина, объяснить давление нагретого газа и перенос солнечного тепла внутрь металлической ёмкости.

Fig. 5.1 shows a metal container used for storing petrol. There is some petrol gas above the liquid petrol inside the metal container.

![[0625-m24-32-p6-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 5.1** (not to scale)

**(a)** Describe the arrangement and motion of the particles in the **liquid** petrol stored in the container. Use your ideas from the kinetic particle model of matter. **[2]**

**(b)** The temperature of the petrol **gas** inside the metal container increases.

State and explain any change in the pressure of the petrol gas on the metal container. **[3]**

**(c)** Describe how thermal energy travels from the Sun to the petrol inside the metal container. **[2]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | any **two** from:<br>• (particles are;) random arrangement / pattern<br>• close together **OR** idea slightly further apart than in solid<br>• move randomly **OR** move around / about (freely)<br>• colliding with each other / walls<br>• have some vibrational energy / motion | B2 |
> | (b) | pressure increases | B1 |
> |  | any **two** from: (because)<br>• particles move faster **OR** have increased kinetic energy<br>• more (frequent) collisions (with walls of container)<br>• harder collisions (with walls of container) | B2 |
> | (c) | infrared **OR** radiation (through space and atmosphere) | B1 |
> |  | conduction (through the metal) | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Many candidates correctly described the arrangement and movement of particles in a liquid. Some descriptions lacked clarity and did not differentiate clearly between solids and liquids.
>
> **(b)** Candidates found this challenging but stronger candidates gained full credit. The most common error was to simply state that the pressure increased but then not to explain in terms of particles why the pressure increased. Others simply gave a description about how the temperature of the gas changed.
>
> **(c)** Very few candidates gained full credit for a description that included the ideas that the thermal energy is first transferred by infrared radiation through space and the atmosphere and then by conduction through the metal.
