> Описать выход частиц с поверхности жидкости при испарении.

Which statement describes what happens to particles during the evaporation of a liquid?

**A** Particles become fixed in position.  
**B** Particles escape from the surface.  
**C** Particles move much closer together.  
**D** Particles start to move around at random.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
