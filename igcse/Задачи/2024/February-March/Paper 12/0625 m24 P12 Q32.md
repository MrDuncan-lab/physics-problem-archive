# Question 32 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · February/March 2024 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 32  
Источник: [[_pdf/2024/0625_m24_qp_12.pdf#page=11|условие, стр. 11]] · [[_pdf/2024/0625_m24_ms_12.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2024/0625_m24_er.pdf#page=1|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 m24 P12]]  
Темы: [[5.2.1 Detection of radioactivity]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбрать оборудование для измерения фоновой радиации.

Which apparatus is used to measure background radiation?

**A** a counter, a detector and a source  
**B** a counter and a detector only  
**C** a counter and a source only  
**D** a detector and a source only

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> As many candidates chose **A** as chose the correct answer (**B**). The most important point of the concept of background radiation is that it is detected even when there is no source present.
