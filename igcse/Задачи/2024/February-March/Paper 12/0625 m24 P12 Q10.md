Источник: [[_pdf/2024/0625_m24_qp_12.pdf#page=4|условие, стр. 4]] · [[_pdf/2024/0625_m24_ms_12.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2024/0625_m24_er.pdf#page=1|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

Темы: [[2.2.3 Melting, boiling and evaporation]], [[2.1.3 Gases and the absolute scale of temperature]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Указать температуру кипения воды в кельвинах при стандартном атмосферном давлении.

What is the boiling temperature of water at standard atmospheric pressure?

**A** –173 K  
**B** 100 K  
**C** 273 K  
**D** 373 K

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Stronger candidates answered this correctly. However, the most popular option was **B**, from those who knew that the boiling temperature of water is 100 degrees of some sort. The next most popular option was **C**, from candidates who recognised that ‘K’ indicated the kelvin scale, and that the number 273 was somehow involved.
