> Распознать изотопы по протонным и нуклонным числам.

The table gives information about four nuclei.

| nucleus | proton number | nucleon number |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 26 | 59 |
| 2 | 27 | 59 |
| 3 | 27 | 60 |
| 4 | 28 | 60 |

Which nuclei are isotopes of the same element?

**A** 1 and 2  
**B** 1 and 4  
**C** 2 and 3  
**D** 3 and 4

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
