# Question 26 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · February/March 2024 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 26  
Источник: [[_pdf/2024/0625_m24_qp_12.pdf#page=10|условие, стр. 10]] · [[_pdf/2024/0625_m24_ms_12.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2024/0625_m24_er.pdf#page=1|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить токи в параллельных ветвях с одинаковыми лампами и в общей части цепи.

The diagram shows a circuit containing two identical lamps and three ammeters.

![[0625-m24-12-p10-f1.svg]]

The current in ammeter 1 is 0.30 A.

Which row gives possible values for the currents in ammeters 2 and 3?

|  | current in ammeter 2 / A | current in ammeter 3 / A |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | 0.15 | 0.00 |
| **B** | 0.15 | 0.30 |
| **C** | 0.30 | 0.00 |
| **D** | 0.30 | 0.30 |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Some candidates thought that there would be no current at the site of ammeter 3. Current does not get ‘used up’. Current is a flow of charge, and charge is conserved. The current splits at a junction and, because the lamps are identical, half goes through each lamp, with the result that ammeter 2 records 0.15 A. Even some stronger candidates appeared to misunderstand this point.
