> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объяснить электрическую нейтральность атома равенством положительного и отрицательного зарядов.

Which statement about an atom is correct?

**A** An atom has positive charge because there are only positive charges in the nucleus.  
**B** An atom has negative charge because there are only negative charges in the nucleus.  
**C** An atom has neutral charge because the positive charge in the nucleus equals the total

negative charge of the electrons.

**D** An atom has neutral charge because the negative charge in the nucleus equals the total

positive charge of the electrons.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
