> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбрать каменистые планеты по их близости к Солнцу.

The table gives information about four of the planets in our Solar System.

Two of these planets are gaseous and two are rocky.

| planet | average distance from the Sun / km |
|---|---|
| 1 | 1.4 × 10$^{9}$ |
| 2 | 2.3 × 10$^{8}$ |
| 3 | 5.8 × 10$^{7}$ |
| 4 | 7.8 × 10$^{8}$ |

Which two planets are rocky?

**A** 1 and 2  
**B** 1 and 4  
**C** 2 and 3  
**D** 3 and 4

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Option **A** was a popular choice, presumably from candidates who knew that the two rocky planets are the two closest to the sun, and who therefore chose the two with the lowest mantissas, ignoring the exponents.
