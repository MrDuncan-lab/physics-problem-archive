> Сравнить преломление разных цветов света стеклянной призмой.

A ray of green light passes through a glass prism, as shown.

![[0625-m24-12-p8-f1.svg]]

Which colours of light refract as shown in the table?

|  | refracts more than green | refracts less than green |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | red | blue |
| **B** | red | yellow |
| **C** | violet | blue |
| **D** | violet | yellow |
