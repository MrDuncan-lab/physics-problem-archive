> Назвать основные элементы Солнца и главные области его излучения.

Which row shows the most common elements in the Sun and the regions of the electromagnetic

spectrum in which most of the Sun’s energy is radiated?

|  | elements | regions |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | carbon and oxygen | gamma and radio waves |
| **B** | carbon and oxygen | infrared and visible |
| **C** | hydrogen and helium | gamma and radio waves |
| **D** | hydrogen and helium | infrared and visible |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
