The Sun radiates most of its energy in three regions of the electromagnetic spectrum.

In the pie chart, region 1 is infrared radiation and region 2 is visible radiation.

![[0625-m24-22-p15-f1.svg]]

**B** radio  
**C** ultraviolet  
**D** X-ray

> [!answer]- Ответ
