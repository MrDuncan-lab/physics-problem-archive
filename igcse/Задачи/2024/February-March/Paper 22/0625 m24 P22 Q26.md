> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить области наиболее слабого и сильного поля пары полосовых магнитов.

The diagrams show two arrangements of a pair of identical bar magnets.

![[0625-m24-22-p12-f1.svg]]

diagram 1

![[0625-m24-22-p12-f2.svg]]

diagram 2

Three points, P, Q and R, are shown.

At which point is the magnetic field due to the magnets weakest and at which point is it strongest?

|  | weakest magnetic field | strongest magnetic field |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | P | Q |
| **B** | P | R |
| **C** | Q | P |
| **D** | Q | R |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Nearly all candidates realised that P is a neutral point. When the field lines emerge from the left-hand N pole in diagram 2 they splay out as they pass Q and then reconverge before entering the S pole of the right-hand magnet. Therefore, they are closer together at R than at Q. Weaker candidates did not realise this.
