> Рассчитать число нейтронов дочернего ядра после альфа-распада радона.

The radioactive isotope radon, $^{222}_{86}$Rn, is an alpha (α) emitter.

During this radioactive decay, an isotope of polonium, Po, is produced.

How many neutrons does a nucleus of this isotope of polonium contain?

**A** 130  
**B** 132  
**C** 134  
**D** 136

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
