> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить эквивалентное сопротивление двух сильно различающихся резисторов при параллельном соединении.

A student has two resistors.

One resistor has a resistance of 2.0 Ω and the other has a resistance of 40 kΩ.

Which statement is correct?

**A** When connected in parallel, the combined resistance is 1.9 Ω.  
**B** When connected in parallel, the combined resistance is 2.0 Ω.  
**C** When connected in series, the combined resistance is 1.9 Ω.  
**D** When connected in series, the combined resistance is 42 Ω.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
