> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить знаки зарядов стержней по изменению показания весов при их взаимодействии.

A charged rod X is placed on a balance and another rod Y is brought close to it, as shown.

![[0625-m24-22-p11-f1.svg]]

Which combination of charges would cause the change in the balance reading shown?

|  | X | Y |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | negative charge | negative charge |
| **B** | negative charge | positive charge |
| **C** | negative charge | no charge |
| **D** | positive charge | no charge |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question required careful thought. In the right-hand diagram, X appears heavier because it is being pushed downwards so the charges on X and Y must be the same.
