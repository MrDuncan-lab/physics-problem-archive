> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать энергию нагревателя и число витков трансформатора, назвать провода сетевого кабеля.

In an experiment, a student uses an electrical heater connected to a power supply.

**(a)** The current in the electrical heater is 2.2 A. The voltage (p.d.) across the heater is 12 V.

Calculate the energy transferred to the heater in 90 s.

energy transferred =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ J **[3]**

**(b)** The power supply is connected to the electrical mains by a cable that consists of three wires.

State the name for each of the three wires in the cable.

1  \_\_\_\_\_\_ 2  \_\_\_\_\_\_ 3  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(c)** The power supply includes a transformer.

The voltage (V$_{\text{p}}$) across the primary coil of the transformer is 228 V. The voltage (V$_{\text{s}}$) across the secondary coil of the transformer is 12 V. The number of turns on the primary coil (N$_{\text{p}}$) is 760.

Calculate the number of turns (N$_{\text{s}}$) on the secondary coil.

number of turns on secondary coil =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** (energy =) 2400 (J)
>
> **(c)** 40

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | (energy =) 2400 (J) | A3 |
> |  | (energy =) 2.2 × 90 × 12 | (C2) |
> |  | (energy =) current × time × voltage OR(*E*) = *V* × *I* × *t* OR (energy =) power × time OR *P* × *t* | (C1) |
> | (b) | live OR line earth OR ground neutral | B2 |
> | (c) | 40 | A3 |
> |  | (*N*$_{\text{s}}$) = (12 ÷ 228) × 760 OR 228 / 12 = 760 / *N*$_{\text{s}}$ | (C2) |
> |  | *N*$_{\text{s}}$/ *N*$_{\text{p}}$ = *V*$_{\text{s}}$/ *V*$_{\text{p}}$ | (C1) |
