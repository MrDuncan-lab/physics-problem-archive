**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2024 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 1 · вопрос 5

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Описать расположение и движение частиц воздуха, объяснить изменение их движения при охлаждении.

Fig. 5.1 shows a metal box. The air in the box is at room temperature, 20 °C. Air cannot leave or enter the box.

![[0625-w24-31-p8-f1.svg]]

Fig. 5.1

**(a)** Describe the motion, separation and arrangement of the air particles in the metal box. **[3]**

**(b)** A student puts the box in a freezer. The temperature of the air in the box decreases.

Describe the changes in the motion of the air particles in the box when the temperature decreases. **[2]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | any **three** from:<br>• random motion (of particles)<br>• high speed<br>• widely separated (compared to particles in liquid or solid)<br>• random arrangement<br>• constantly colliding (with each other / walls) | B3 |
> | (b) | any **two** from:<br>• speed decreases<br>• (because) kinetic energy OR (internal) energy decreases<br>• (and so) collision rate decreases | B2 |
