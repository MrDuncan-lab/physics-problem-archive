> Измерить ток при разных длинах проволоки, определить сопротивление по графику и заданной формуле, предложить уменьшение перегрева.

In this experiment, you will determine the resistance of a resistor.

The circuit has been set up for you.

![[0625-w24-53-p5-f1.svg]]

Fig. 2.1

**(a)** Attach the crocodile clip to a length l = 100.0 cm of the resistance wire. Close the switch.

Measure the potential difference (p.d.) V$_{0}$ across the resistor for l = 100.0 cm.

V$_{0}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ V **[1]**

Open the switch.

**(b)** Adjust the position of the crocodile clip on the resistance wire to a length l = 20.0 cm.

Close the switch.

Measure and record, in Table 2.1, the value of the current I in the circuit.

Measure and record, in Table 2.1, values of the current I for l = 30.0 cm, l = 40.0 cm, l = 50.0 cm and l = 60.0 cm.

Open the switch.

**(c)** Plot a graph of l / cm (y-axis) against I / A (x-axis).

Draw a best-fit straight line. You do not need to start the axes at the origin (0,0).

**(d)** **(i)** Determine the gradient G of the graph. Show clearly on the graph how you obtained the

necessary information.

G =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(ii)** Calculate the resistance R of the resistor.

Use your value of V$_{0}$ from (a) and the equation

R = $\dfrac{G \times V_{0}}{100k}$ where k = 1.0 A

R =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ Ω **[1]**

**(e)** Suggest one practical reason why other students might not obtain the same result as you, even if they do the experiment carefully. **[1]**

**(f)** The resistance wire can become very hot during this type of experiment.

Suggest one change to the apparatus that prevents this. **[1]**
