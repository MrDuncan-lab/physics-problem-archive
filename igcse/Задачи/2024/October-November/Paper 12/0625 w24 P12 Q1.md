> Проверить правильность считывания объёмов жидкости с измерительных цилиндров.

A student measures the volumes of three liquids using three different measuring cylinders.

![[0625-w24-12-p2-f1.svg]]

The table shows the volumes recorded by the student.

| measuring | volume |
|---|---|
| cylinder | / cm$^{3}$ |
| 1 | 1.2 |
| 2 | 2.2 |
| 3 | 25 |

Which readings are correctly recorded?

**A** 1, 2 and 3  
**B** 1 and 2 only  
**C** 1 and 3 only  
**D** 1 only

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
