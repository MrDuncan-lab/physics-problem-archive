> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Описать выход наиболее энергичных молекул и охлаждение жидкости при испарении.

Which statement about the evaporation of a liquid is correct?

**A** The least energetic particles escape from the surface and the temperature of the liquid

decreases.

**B** The least energetic particles escape from the surface and the temperature of the liquid

increases.

**C** The most energetic particles escape from the surface and the temperature of the liquid

decreases.

**D** The most energetic particles escape from the surface and the temperature of the liquid

increases.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
