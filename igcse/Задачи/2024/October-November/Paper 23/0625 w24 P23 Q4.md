> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить жидкости, всплывающие на поверхность воды, по их плотностям.

An engineer researched the density of different liquids being used as coolants.

| liquid | density kg/m$^{3}$ |
|---|---|
| X | 785 |
| Y | 1020 |
| Z | 1250 |

None of the liquids mix with water.

Each liquid is poured, in turn, into a container of water. The density of water is 1000 kg / m$^{3}$.

Which liquids rise to the surface?

**A** X only  
**B** Y and Z  
**C** Y only  
**D** Z only

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**
