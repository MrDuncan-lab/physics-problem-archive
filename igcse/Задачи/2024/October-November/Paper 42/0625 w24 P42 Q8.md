# Question 8 (6 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2024 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 2 · вопрос 8  
Источник: [[_pdf/2024/0625_w24_qp_42.pdf#page=10|условие, стр. 10–11]] · [[_pdf/2024/0625_w24_ms_42.pdf#page=13|схема оценивания, стр. 13]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить ультразвук, рассчитать задержку отражённого сигнала и сравнить эхо от рыбы и морского дна.

A fisherman uses high frequency sound waves to locate fish in the sea. Fig. 8.1 shows the sound waves emitted from the boat.

![[0625-w24-42-p10-f1.svg]]

![[0625-w24-42-p10-f2.svg]]

Fig. 8.1 (not to scale)

**(a)** State the name of sound waves which have a frequency greater than 20 kHz. **[1]**

**(b)** High frequency sound waves travel from the boat through the sea water. The speed of sound in water is 1500 m / s. The seabed is 22 m below the boat.

Calculate the time taken for the boat to receive the reflected wave from the seabed after the sound is emitted.

time =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

**(c)** Fig. 8.2 shows a fish below the boat.

![[0625-w24-42-p11-f1.svg]]

![[0625-w24-42-p11-f2.svg]]

![[0625-w24-42-p11-f3.svg]]

Fig. 8.2 (not to scale)

Describe and explain how the reflected sound wave received by the boat from the fish differs from the reflected sound wave received from the seabed. **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** 0.029 s
>
> **(c)** fish is small(er) **OR** only small part of wave reflects off fish OR most of sound goes to seabed

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | ultrasound | B1 |
> | (b) | 0.029 s | A3 |
> |  | (distance travelled =) 22 × 2 **OR** 44 SEEN | C1 |
> |  | *v* = *s* / *t* OR (*t* =) *s / v* OR 44 / 1500 | C1 |
> | (c) | reflected wave is weaker / has smaller amplitude | M1 |
> |  | fish is small(er) **OR** only small part of wave reflects off fish OR most of sound goes to seabed | A1 |
