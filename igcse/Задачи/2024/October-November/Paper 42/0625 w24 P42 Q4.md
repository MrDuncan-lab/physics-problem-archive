> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнить испарение и кипение, объяснить увеличение давления нагреваемого газа в скороварке через движение и число частиц.

Fig. 4.1 shows a pressure cooker on an electric heating element. The cooker has a tight-fitting lid.

![[0625-w24-42-p6-f1.svg]]

Fig. 4.1

**(a)** The pressure cooker is half-full of water. As the water is heated some water evaporates before the water boils. Describe two differences between evaporation and boiling of the water in the cooker. **[2]**

**(b)** As the water is heated, the pressure of the gas inside the cooker increases. Explain this increase in pressure in terms of particles. **[4]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | boiling happens at a specific temperature **OR** evaporation happens at a range of temperatures **OR** evaporation happens at<br>any temperature (below the boiling point) | B1 |
> |  | evaporation happens at the surface of the water OR boiling happens throughout the water | B1 |
> | (b) | *any* ***four*** *from:*<br>1 (as the water is heated) the number of gas particles increases<br>2 (particles) gain internal / kinetic energy<br>3 (there are) more frequent collisions between particles and surface / wall / lid of the cooker<br>4 (each) collision (of particles) is harder / exerts more force (on cooker surface) **OR** greater change of momentum when<br>particles collide (on cooker surface)<br>5 pressure ∝ (total) force (of collisions) **OR** pressure = force / area | B4 |
