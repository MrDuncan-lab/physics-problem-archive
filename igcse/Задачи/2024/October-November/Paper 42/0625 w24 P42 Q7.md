A washing machine has an electric motor and an electric heater. Fig. 7.1 shows a simplified circuit diagram for the washing machine.

![[0625-w24-42-p9-f1.svg]]

Fig. 7.1

The heater has a resistance of 25 Ω and the power supply has an electromotive force (e.m.f.) of 230 V.

**(a)** State the meaning of electromotive force. **[2]**

**(b)** State the potential difference (p.d.) across the heater.

p.d. =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(c)** Calculate the current in the heater.

Current =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(d)** The current in the motor is 1.6 A. Determine the reading on the ammeter in Fig. 7.1. Explain your answer.

Ammeter reading  \_\_\_\_\_\_

Explanation  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** (e.m.f. is the electrical) work done (by a source in) moving a unit charge around a (complete) circuit
>
> **(c)** 9.2 A
