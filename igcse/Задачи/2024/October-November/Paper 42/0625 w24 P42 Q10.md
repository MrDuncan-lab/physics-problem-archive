# Question 10 (9 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2024 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 2 · вопрос 10  
Источник: [[_pdf/2024/0625_w24_qp_42.pdf#page=14|условие, стр. 14]] · [[_pdf/2024/0625_w24_ms_42.pdf#page=15|схема оценивания, стр. 15]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнить изотопы углерода, определить возраст древесины по радиоактивному распаду и обосновать применение короткоживущего изотопа.

Carbon-14 ($^{14}_{6}$C) is a radioactive isotope of carbon. Carbon-12 ($^{12}_{6}$C) is not radioactive.

**(a)** Explain how an atom of carbon-14 ($^{14}_{6}$C) differs from an atom of carbon-12 ($^{12}_{6}$C). **[2]**

**(b)** All living organisms contain both carbon-12 atoms and carbon-14 atoms. The ratio of carbon-14 to carbon-12 is 1 : 1 × 10$^{12}$.

Carbon-14 has a half-life of 5700 years.

**(i)** When an organism dies no new carbon is absorbed. The amount of carbon-12 in the dead organism remains fixed.

Describe how the amount of carbon-14 in the dead organism decreases with time. **[2]**

**(ii)** A sample of wood contains carbon-14 to carbon-12 atoms in the ratio 1 : 4 × 10$^{12}$.

Calculate how many years ago the tree died.

\_\_\_\_\_\_ years ago **[3]**

**(c)** Other radioactive isotopes have different half-lives.

Suggest a use of a radioactive isotope with a half-life of one hour.

Explain why a short half-life is suitable for this use.

use  \_\_\_\_\_\_

explanation  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)(i)** Every 5700 years the (remaining) carbon-14 decreases by half **OR** amount of C-14 halved every half life
>
> **(b)(ii)** 11 000 (years ago)
