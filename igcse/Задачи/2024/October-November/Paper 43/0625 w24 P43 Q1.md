> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Различить скаляры и векторы, определить импульс, рассчитать начальную скорость автомобиля и построить график столкновения.

**(a)** **(i)** State the difference between a scalar quantity and a vector quantity. **[1]**

**(ii)** Define momentum. **[1]**

**(b)** A test car crashes into a barrier to test the safety features. The test car has a total mass of 950 kg. It is moving with constant velocity from time t = 0 for 4.0 s. At t = 4.0 s, the car hits the barrier.

Fig. 1.1 shows the car as it hits the barrier.

![[0625-w24-43-p2-f1.png]]

Fig. 1.1

**(i)** During the test crash, the resultant force acting on the car is 27 000 N. The car takes 1.5 s to come to rest. The deceleration is uniform.

Calculate the initial velocity of the car.

initial velocity =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

**(ii)** On Fig. 1.2, sketch a speed–time graph to show the motion of the car from time t = 0 until the car becomes stationary.

![[0625-w24-43-p3-f1.svg]]

Fig. 1.2 **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)(i)** 43 m / s

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | (scalar) does not have direction ***or*** vector has direction | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | (momentum =) mass × velocity | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | 43 m / s | A3 |
> |  | *F*Δ*t =* Δ*(mv)* ***OR*** *F =* Δ(*mv*) / Δ*t* ***OR*** *F = ma* ***OR*** *a = ∆v / ∆t* | C1 |
> |  | *(u=) Ft* / *m* ***OR*** *(u* =) (27 000 × 1.5) / 950 | C1 |
> | (b)(ii) | straight horizontal line from *y* axis extending to *t* = 4.0 s | B1 |
> |  | straight line from *t* = 4.0 s with negative gradient meeting the x axis (less than half-way between 4.0 and the end of the time<br>axis) | B1 |
