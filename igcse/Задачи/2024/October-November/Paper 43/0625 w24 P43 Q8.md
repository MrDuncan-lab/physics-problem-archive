**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2024 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 3 · вопрос 8

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнить деление и синтез, записать ядерную реакцию бора, обосновать применение альфа-излучения и меры радиационной безопасности.

An isotope of boron is used in the treatment of cancer in the brain.

Boron sticks to cancer cells in the brain.

**(a)** The isotope of boron is bombarded with neutrons then undergoes fission to form lithium and alpha-particles.

**(i)** Describe **one** difference between fission and fusion. **[1]**

**(ii)** A nucleus of boron (B) contains 5 protons and 5 neutrons. Complete the nuclide equation for this fission reaction.

${}^{\dots}_{\dots}\text{B} + {}^{1}_{0}\text{n} \rightarrow {}^{\dots}_{\dots}\text{Li} + {}^{\dots}_{\dots}\alpha$ **[3]**

**(b)** The alpha-particles destroy the cancer cells. Suggest and explain **one** reason why alpha particles are more suitable than gamma radiation for use in this treatment of brain cancer. **[2]**

**(c)** Other cancers are treated with gamma radiation. Describe **one** safety precaution a nurse or radiologist takes during this treatment. **[1]**
