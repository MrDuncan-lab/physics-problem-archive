> Рассчитать КПД подъёмника по массе, высоте, времени подъёма и мощности электродвигателя.

An electrician uses a lifting machine, as shown. The lifting machine takes 4.5 s to lift the

electrician a vertical height of 3.2 m.

![[0625-w24-22-p5-f1.svg]]

The mass of the electrician is 72 kg.

The energy for the lifting machine is supplied by a 2.0 kW electric motor.

What is the efficiency of the lifting machine?

**A** 0.026  
**B** 0.25  
**C** 0.50  
**D** 0.75

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
