# Question 30 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2024 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 2 · вопрос 30  
Источник: [[_pdf/2024/0625_w24_qp_22.pdf#page=13|условие, стр. 13]] · [[_pdf/2024/0625_w24_ms_22.pdf|схема оценивания]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить постоянство суммы напряжений последовательных компонентов при неизменных общем сопротивлении и токе.

An electrical engineer has made a pair of components P and Q.

The resistance of P decreases as the temperature rises. The resistance of Q increases as the temperature rises.

The total resistance of P and Q in series remains constant when the temperature changes. The two components are connected in series with a power source supplying a constant current.

Which statement is correct?

**A** If a temperature change causes the potential difference (p.d.) across P to double, the p.d.

across Q will always halve.

**B** The difference between the p.d. across P and the p.d. across Q does not change with

temperature.

**C** The p.d. across component P increases as the temperature rises.  
**D** The sum of the p.d. across P and the p.d. across Q does not change with temperature.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
