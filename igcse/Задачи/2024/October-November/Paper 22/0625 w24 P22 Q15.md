> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбрать формулу удельной теплоёмкости по энергии нагревания, массе и изменению температуры.

In an experiment to measure specific heat capacity, a block of aluminium is heated and its rise in

temperature is measured.

The internal energy gained by the block is Δ*E*. The mass of the block is *m*. The rise in temperature of the block is Δ*T*.

Which expression gives the specific heat capacity of aluminium?

**A** $\dfrac{m}{\Delta E \Delta T}$  
**B** $\dfrac{m \Delta T}{\Delta E}$  
**C** $\dfrac{\Delta E}{m \Delta T}$  
**D** $\dfrac{\Delta E \Delta T}{m}$

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
