> Определить период полураспада по изменению скорости счёта с учётом постоянного фона.

The count rate measured near a radioactive source drops from 542 counts per minute to

94 counts per minute in 12 hours. The background count remains constant at 30 counts per minute.

What is the half-life of the source?

**A** 2 hours  
**B** 3 hours  
**C** 4 hours  
**D** 8 hours

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
