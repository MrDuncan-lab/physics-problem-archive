> Выбрать выражения моментов двух сил с правильными перпендикулярными плечами.

The diagram shows a uniform beam pivoted at its centre.

A student applies two forces, *F*$_{1}$ and *F*$_{2}$, as shown.

![[0625-w24-22-p3-f2.svg]]

Which row is correct?

|  | clockwise moment about the pivot | anticlockwise moment about the pivot |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | *F*$_{2}$*d*$_{2}$ | *F*$_{1}$*d*$_{1}$ |
| **B** | *F*$_{2}$*d*$_{2}$ | *F*$_{1}$*d*$_{3}$ |
| **C** | *F*$_{2}$*d*$_{4}$ | *F*$_{1}$*d*$_{1}$ |
| **D** | *F*$_{2}$*d*$_{4}$ | *F*$_{1}$*d*$_{3}$ |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
