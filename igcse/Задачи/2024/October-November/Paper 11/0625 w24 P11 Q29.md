> Определить последовательное соединение резисторов и сравнить токи в разных точках цепи.

The diagram shows a circuit containing two identical resistors connected to a battery.

The current is measured at X and at Y.

![[0625-w24-11-p12-f1.svg]]

Which row is correct?

|  | arrangement of resistors | current at Y compared with current at X |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | parallel | less than X |
| **B** | parallel | equal to X |
| **C** | series | less than X |
| **D** | series | equal to X |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
