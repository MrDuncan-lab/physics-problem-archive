> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Измерить расстояния до изображения линзы, вычислить фокусное расстояние по заданной формуле и сравнить результаты с учётом точности.

In this experiment, you will investigate the image produced by a lens.

Refer to Fig. 3.1 and Fig. 3.2.

![[0625-w24-51-p8-f1.svg]]

Fig. 3.1 (not to scale)

![[0625-w24-51-p8-f2.svg]]

Fig. 3.2 (not to scale)

**(a)** Measure the height h$_{\text{O}}$ of the illuminated object in your experiment. (Fig. 3.2 shows the height to be measured on your illuminated object.)

h$_{\text{O}}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

- Place the lens a distance u = 25.0 cm from the illuminated object.
- Move the screen slowly until a clearly focused image is formed on the screen.
- Measure the distance v between the centre of the lens and the screen.

v =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm **[1]**

**(c)** Calculate the focal length f of the lens using the equation f = $\dfrac{\text{uv}}{(u+v)}$

Give your answer to three significant figures and include the unit.

f =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

**(d)** The object is brighter than the image. Complete the sentences to describe two other ways in which the image seen on the screen is different from the object.

1. The image is  \_\_\_\_\_\_

2. The image is  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(e)** Repeat the steps in (b) and (c) using a distance u = 30.0 cm.

v =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm

f =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(f)** State and explain whether your results in (c) and (e) for the focal length f are equal within the limits of experimental accuracy. **[1]**
