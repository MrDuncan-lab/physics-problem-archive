**(a)** Fig. 9.1 shows a magnet spinning steadily near to a coil. There is a reading on the voltmeter.

![[0625-w24-33-p13-f1.svg]]

Fig. 9.1

Explain why there is a reading on the voltmeter. **[2]**

**(b)** A step-down transformer reduces the mains voltage of 220 V a.c. to 12 V a.c. The transformer has 5000 turns on the primary coil.

Calculate the number of turns on the secondary coil.

number of turns =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** (number of turns =) 270
