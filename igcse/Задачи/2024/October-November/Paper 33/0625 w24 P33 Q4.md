> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сформулировать закон сохранения энергии и описать её превращения в электронном секундомере.

**(a)** State the principle of conservation of energy. **[1]**

**(b)** Fig. 4.1 shows a stop-watch that is powered by a battery. The stop-watch measures a time period in minutes and seconds.

![[0625-w24-33-p7-f1.png]]

Fig. 4.1

Complete the sentences about energy transfers in the stop-watch that is measuring a time period.

**(i)** The type of energy stored in the battery is  \_\_\_\_\_\_ energy. **[1]**

**(ii)** The type of energy stored in the stop-watch’s second hand is  \_\_\_\_\_\_

energy. **[1]**

**(iii)** The stop-watch has a bell.

When the bell rings, energy is transferred to the surroundings as  \_\_\_\_\_\_

energy. **[1]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | **energy can neither be created nor be destroyed** OR<br>energy only transferred / transformed from one form / store to another. | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | chemical | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | kinetic | B1 |
> | (b)(iii) | sound | B1 |
