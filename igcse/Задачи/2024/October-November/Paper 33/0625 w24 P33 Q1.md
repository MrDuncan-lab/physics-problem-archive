> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить путь и среднюю скорость велосипедиста, рассчитать равнодействующую сил и описать её влияние на движение.

**(a)** Fig. 1.1 shows the speed–time graph for a cyclist at the beginning of a race.

![[0625-w24-33-p2-f1.svg]]

Fig. 1.1

**(i)** Describe the motion of the cyclist from time = 6.0 s to time = 10.0 s. **[1]**

**(ii)** Determine the distance moved by the cyclist from time = 6.0 s to time = 10.0 s.

distance =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ m **[3]**

**(b)** Another cyclist travels 1000 m in 1 minute and 29 seconds.

**(i)** Determine how many seconds there are in 1 minute and 29 seconds.

number of seconds =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ s **[1]**

**(ii)** Calculate the average speed of this cyclist.

average speed =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ m / s **[3]**

**(c)** The horizontal forces acting on a cyclist vary. Fig. 1.2 shows the horizontal forces at one moment during a race.

![[0625-w24-33-p3-f1.svg]]

backward force = 50 N forward force = 90 N

Fig. 1.2 (not to scale)

**(i)** Calculate the resultant horizontal force acting on the cyclist.

resultant horizontal force =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ N

direction =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(ii)** Describe the effect of the resultant horizontal force in (c)(i) on the motion of the cyclist. **[1]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)(ii)** 18 (m)
>
> **(b)(ii)** 11 (m / s)
