> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Описать точное измерение диаметра проволоки и определить удлинение и длину пружины по графику нагрузки.

A teacher uses a spring in a demonstration.

The spring is shown in Fig. 2.1.

![[0625-w24-33-p4-f1.svg]]

Fig. 2.1

**(a)** The spring is made from wire. Describe how to determine the diameter of the wire accurately. You may include a diagram as part of your answer. **[3]**

**(b)** A student adds loads to the spring. She measures the extension for each load. Fig. 2.2 shows the graph of extension against load for the spring.

![[0625-w24-33-p5-f1.svg]]

Fig. 2.2

**(i)** Using Fig. 2.2, determine the extension of the spring with a load of 4.0 N.

extension =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm **[2]**

**(ii)** The length of the spring without a load is 8.0 cm.

Calculate the length of the spring with a load of 4.0 N.

length =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm **[1]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)(i)** 1.6 (cm)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | measure the diameter of n loops / turns with rule(r) | B1 |
> |  | n = 5 or more loops / loops | B1 |
> |  | (diameter of wire =) their measurement ÷ <u>n if n  1</u> | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | 1.6 (cm) | A2 |
> |  | any indication of correct attempt on graph | (C1) |
> | (b)(ii) | 9.6 (cm) | B1 |
