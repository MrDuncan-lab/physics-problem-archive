> Определение сопротивления светодиода по измерениям тока и напряжения и выбор переменного резистора.

A student measures the resistance of a light-emitting diode (LED) when the current in it changes.

The student sets up the circuit shown in Fig. 2.1.

![[0625-w24-62-p4-f1.svg]]

Fig. 2.1

**(a)** The student:

- connects a voltmeter to measure the potential difference across the 220 Ω resistor
- closes the switch
- records the voltmeter reading V$_{\text{PQ}}$ in the top row of Table 2.1
- opens the switch.

**(i)** On Fig. 2.1, draw the symbol for a voltmeter connected to measure the potential difference across the 220 Ω resistor. **[2]**

**(ii)** The voltmeter reading is shown in Fig. 2.2.

![[0625-w24-62-p4-f2.svg]]

Fig. 2.2

Write down the reading in the top row of Table 2.1 under the heading V$_{\text{PQ}}$. **[1]**

Table 2.1

| resistance between P and Q / Ω | V$_{\text{PQ}}$ / V | V$_{\text{QR}}$ / V | I / A | R$_{\text{LED}}$ / Ω |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 220 |  | 2.1 |  |  |
| 330 |  | 2.0 | 0.0076 | 263 |

**(b)** The student:

- disconnects the voltmeter from points P and Q
- reconnects the voltmeter across the LED between points Q and R
- closes the switch.
- records the voltmeter reading V$_{\text{QR}}$ in the top row of Table 2.1
- opens the switch.

Calculate the current I in the circuit in part (a) using the equation

V

I = $\dfrac{\text{PQ}}{R}$

where R = 220 Ω.

Record your answer in the top row of Table 2.1. **[1]**

**(c)** Calculate the resistance R$_{\text{LED}}$ of the LED using the equation

V

R$_{\text{LED}}$ = $\dfrac{\text{QR}}{I}$

Record your answer to a suitable number of significant figures for this experiment in the top row of Table 2.1. **[2]**

**(d)** The student:

- removes the 220 Ω resistor from the circuit and replaces it with a 330 Ω resistor
- reconnects the voltmeter across the resistor between points P and Q
- repeats parts (a) to (c) for the 330 Ω resistor.

Use the information in Table 2.1 to calculate the value of the potential difference V$_{\text{PQ}}$across the 330 Ω resistor.

Show your working and record your answer in Table 2.1. **[1]**

**(e)** As the resistance between points P and Q changes, the current in the circuit changes.

Use your results in Table 2.1 to write a conclusion, stating how the change in current affects the potential difference V$_{\text{QR}}$ across the LED and the resistance R$_{\text{LED}}$ of the LED. **[2]**

**(f)** **(i)** Another student uses a variable resistor to control the current in the circuit.

Draw the symbol for a variable resistor. **[1]**

**(ii)** Explain one advantage of using a variable resistor for this purpose rather than the procedure carried out in (a) and (b). **[1]**
