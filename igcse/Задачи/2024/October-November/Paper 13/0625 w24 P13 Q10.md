> Выразить мощность двигателя через силу, пройденное расстояние и время.

What is the relationship between the power of a motor, the force exerted by the motor, the distance moved by the force and the time taken?

distance moved × time taken

**A** power =

force

**B** power = force × distance moved × time taken  
**C** power = $\dfrac{\text{force} \times \text{time} \text{taken}}{\text{distance} \text{moved}}$  
**D** power = $\dfrac{\text{force} \times \text{distance} \text{moved}}{\text{time} \text{taken}}$

> [!answer]- Ответ
