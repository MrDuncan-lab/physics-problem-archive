> Рассчитать работу силы, перемещающей ящик вдоль наклонной плоскости.

The diagram shows a force of 12 N being used to push a box up a slope. The box is moved 8.0 m along the slope. This lifts the box through a vertical height of 2.5 m.

![[0625-w24-13-p4-f2.svg]]

How much work is done pushing the box from the bottom to the top of the slope?

**A** 1.5 J  
**B** 4.8 J  
**C** 30 J  
**D** 96 J

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
