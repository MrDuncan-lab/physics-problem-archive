**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2024 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 1

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить расстояния, время ходьбы и среднюю скорость по графику движения ученика.

Fig. 1.1 shows the distance–time graph for a student’s journey.

![[0625-w24-32-p2-f1.svg]]

Fig. 1.1

The student walks from his home to a shop. He stops at the shop. Then he walks to his friend’s house and stops there for 50 minutes. Then he walks back to his home without stopping.

**(a)** **(i)** Determine the distance between the student’s home and his friend’s house.

distance =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ m **[1]**

**(ii)** Calculate the distance between the shop and the friend’s house.

distance =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ m **[1]**

**(b)** Calculate the total time for which the student is walking.

time =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ min **[1]**

**(c)** Calculate the average speed of the student when he walks back to his home.

speed =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ m / s **[4]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(c)** 1.2 (m / s)
