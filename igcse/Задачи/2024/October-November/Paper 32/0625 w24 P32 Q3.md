> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать момент силы на рукоятке и работу при подъёме автомобиля, предложить увеличение момента силы.

A car has a fault. A mechanic uses a machine to pull the car onto a recovery vehicle as shown in Fig. 3.1.

![[0625-w24-32-p6-f1.svg]]

Fig. 3.1

**(a)** Fig. 3.2 shows how the mechanic applies a force to the handle of the machine.

![[0625-w24-32-p6-f2.svg]]

Fig. 3.2

**(i)** Calculate the moment of the 26 N force about the pivot. Use the information in Fig. 3.2.

moment =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ N m **[3]**

**(ii)** Describe one way the mechanic can increase the moment of the 26 N force about the pivot. **[1]**

**(b)** The car is lifted vertically 0.78 m onto the recovery vehicle, as shown in Fig. 3.3.

![[0625-w24-32-p7-f1.svg]]

Fig. 3.3

The weight of the car is 14 000 N. Calculate the minimum work done on the car in lifting it onto the recovery vehicle from the ground. Include the unit.

work done =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ unit  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[4]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)(i)** 24 (N m)
>
> **(b)** 11 000
