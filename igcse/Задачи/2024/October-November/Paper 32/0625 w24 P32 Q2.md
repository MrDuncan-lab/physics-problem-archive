> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Описать точное измерение диаметра проволоки, определить её массу и объём и рассчитать плотность металла.

A student wants to measure the diameter of a wire. The wire is thinner than a single gradation on her ruler. She coils the wire carefully and makes 12 loops as shown in Fig. 2.1.

![[0625-w24-32-p4-f1.png]]

Fig. 2.1

**(a)** Describe how she can use her ruler to determine the diameter of the wire accurately. You may draw on Fig. 2.1 as part of your answer. **[3]**

**(b)** The student determines the density of the metal of the wire. She folds some of the wire into a small shape as shown in Fig. 2.2.

![[0625-w24-32-p4-f2.svg]]

Fig. 2.2

She then puts this small shape of wire into a measuring cylinder containing water. The measuring cylinder is on an electric balance.

This procedure is shown in Fig. 2.3.

![[0625-w24-32-p5-f1.svg]]

Fig. 2.3

Using the information in Fig. 2.3, calculate:

(i) the mass of the wire

mass of the wire =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ g **[1]**

(ii) the volume of the wire.

volume of the wire =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm$^{3}$ **[2]**

**(c)** The mass of a different wire is 64 g. The volume of this wire is 7.2 cm$^{3}$. Using this information, calculate the density of this wire.

density =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ g / cm$^{3}$ **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)(ii)** 5(.0) (cm$^{3}$)
>
> **(c)** 8.9 (g / cm$^{3}$)
