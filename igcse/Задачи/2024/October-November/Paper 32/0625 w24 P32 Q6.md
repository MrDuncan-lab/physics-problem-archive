> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объяснить обогрев помещения конвекцией и влияние чёрной поверхности и отражающей фольги на тепловое излучение.

Fig. 6.1 shows a wood burner in a cabin. The wood burner keeps the inside of the cabin warm when it is cold outside.

![[0625-w24-32-p10-f1.svg]]

Fig. 6.1

**(a)** Explain how thermal energy from the wood burner warms the cabin by convection. Use your ideas about the density of air. You may draw on Fig. 6.1 as part of your answer. **[3]**

**(b)** **(i)** The outer surface of the chimney is dull and black.

Explain how the dull black surface helps to warm the cabin. **[2]**

**(ii)** There is shiny foil on the wall.

Explain how the shiny foil helps to warm the cabin. **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)(ii)** of radiation / infrared
