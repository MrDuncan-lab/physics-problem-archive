> Исследование устойчивости деревянного блока методом наклона.

The IGCSE class is investigating the stability of a block of wood.

Figs. 1.1 and 1.2 show the dimensions of the block.

![[0625-s13-61-p2-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 1.1 Fig. 1.2**

**(a)** **(i)** On Figs. 1.1 and 1.2, measure the height *h*, width *w* and depth *d* of the block.

*h* =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

*w* =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

*d* =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(ii)** On Fig. 1.2, draw the line **AC**. **[1]**

**(iii)** Measure and record the angle α between lines **AD** and **AC**.

α =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(b)** A student places the block on the edge of the bench, as shown in Fig. 1.3.

![[0625-s13-61-p3-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 1.3**

He holds the protractor next to face **ABCD** of the block, as shown in Fig. 1.3. He gently pushes the top of the block (as indicated in Fig. 1.3) so that the block tips over.

He records the angle θ between side **BC** of the block and the vertical line on the protractor. The angle θ is when the block just tips over. He repeats this procedure a suitable number of times.

Suggest the number of measurements of θ that you think would be suitable for this experiment.

number =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(c)** The student calculates the average value θ$_{\text{av}}$ of all his values for θ.

20°

θ$_{\text{av}}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

He suggests that θ$_{\text{av}}$ should be equal to α. State whether the results support this suggestion. Justify your statement by reference to the results.

statement  \_\_\_\_\_\_

justification  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Most candidates successfully measured the dimensions of the block and recorded all of them with the correct unit. The line AC was usually accurately drawn. A minority of candidates measured the wrong angle and some recorded 90° for *α*.
>
> **(b)** Answers between a minimum of 3 and a maximum of 20 were accepted as being practical. Candidates who added a unit did not gain credit.
>
> **(c)** Here candidates were asked to think carefully about the experiment, relating it to their own experience of using a protractor, and make a judgement on the results. The Examiners awarded marks in relation to the candidates’ own value for the angle. Successful candidates were able to make a clear statement, e.g. ‘The results support the suggestion’, and justify their statement with reference to the results, e.g. ‘There is only 1° difference between *α* and *θ’*.
