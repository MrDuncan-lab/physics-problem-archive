# Question 8 (6 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2013 · Paper 2 — Core theory, вариант 1 · вопрос 8  
Источник: [[_pdf/2013/0625_s13_qp_21.pdf#page=14|условие, стр. 14]] · [[_pdf/2013/0625_s13_ms_21.pdf#page=5|схема оценивания, стр. 5]] · [[_pdf/2013/0625_s13_er.pdf#page=7|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 s13 P21]]  
Темы: [[3.2.3 Thin lenses]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Фокус собирающей линзы и построение изображения предмета.

**(a)** Fig. 8.1 is a ray diagram of parallel rays passing through a lens.

![[0625-s13-21-p14-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 8.1**

**(i)** State the name given to point F.

**(ii)** Which distance is the focal length of the lens? **[2]**

**(b)** Fig. 8.2 is another drawing of the same lens as in Fig. 8.1, with an object AX placed in front of it.

![[0625-s13-21-p14-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 8.2**

**(i)** On Fig. 8.2, draw two rays to locate the image of point X. Label this point Z.

**(ii)** On Fig. 8.2, draw in the image of AX, and label it “image”. **[4]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)(ii)** <u>correct inverted image drawn (condone no labelling) between candidate’s Z</u> and the axis and perpendicular to axis (if no label, must be very clear what is image)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | principal focus / focal point / focus / focus point | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | PF | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | rays from top of object<br>ray parallel to axis, to lens centre and through F<br>ray to P and then straight on any 2 | B1×2 |
> |  | ray through other f.p. and then parallel<br><u>Z labelled at intersection of rays (even if rays wrong)</u> | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | <u>correct inverted image drawn (condone no labelling) between candidate’s Z</u><br>and the axis and perpendicular to axis<br>(if no label, must be very clear what is image) | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Middle and lower ability candidates would benefit from greater experience of working with lenses and drawing ray diagrams.
>
> **(a)** There were many correct responses but a common misconception was to describe the ‘focal point’ as the ‘focal length’.
>
> **(b)** A surprising number of candidates did not follow the instructions and thus failed to gain credit which they might have obtained. For example, some candidates only drew one ray, or having drawn two rays failed to label the intersection, or having drawn the rays and labelled the intersection failed to include an acceptable image.
