# Question 2 (7 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2013 · Paper 2 — Core theory, вариант 2 · вопрос 2  
Источник: [[_pdf/2013/0625_s13_qp_22.pdf#page=4|условие, стр. 4–5]] · [[_pdf/2013/0625_s13_ms_22.pdf#page=3|схема оценивания, стр. 3]] · [[_pdf/2013/0625_s13_er.pdf#page=11|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Момент силы на шарнирах и устойчивость коробки на наклонной плоскости.

A piece of stiff cardboard is attached to a plank of wood by two sticky-tape “hinges”, as shown in Fig. 2.1.

![[0625-s13-22-p4-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 2.1**

**(a)** The cardboard in Fig. 2.1 is to be lifted by a vertical force *F*, so that it turns about the hinges.

**(i)** What name do we give to the turning effect of a force? **[1]**

**(ii)** Force *F* is to be as small as possible.

On Fig. 2.1, show force *F*, positioned so that it fulfils this requirement. **[1]**

**(b)** A box of matches is balanced on the cardboard, as shown in Fig. 2.2.

![[0625-s13-22-p4-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 2.2**

**(i)** The left-hand edge of the cardboard is gradually raised. The box does not slide.

What will eventually happen to the box of matches as the edge is raised? **[1]**

**(ii)** State where the centre of mass of the box is positioned when this happens. **[2]**

**(c)** Filing cabinets often have a mechanism that prevents more than one drawer being opened at a time. Fig. 2.3 shows a filing cabinet standing on the floor with the middle drawer open.

![[0625-s13-22-p5-f1.png]]

**Fig. 2.3**

State and explain why it might be dangerous to open the top drawer at the same time as the middle drawer. **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)(ii)** <u>vertically above edge of box OR above R.H. edge of box</u>
>
> **(c)** idea of (vertical through) Centre of Mass being outside base OR clockwise moment becomes too great special case: accept for 1 mark might jam/catch hand between drawers

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | moment<br>accept torque | B1 |

> | (b)(i) | idea of toppling<br>accept falls (over/onto its side)<br>ignore slides | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | above or just beyond edge of box OR outside base of box | C1 |
> |  | <u>vertically above edge of box OR above R.H. edge of box</u> | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Only the better candidates recalled that the turning effect of a force is called a moment, and very few were able to correctly indicate force *F* as being upwards and at the left hand edge of the cardboard.
>
> **(b)** Most candidates correctly indicated that the matchbox would topple over onto its side, but only the very best candidates were able to explain that the centre of mass would be vertically above the right hand edge of the box as it toppled.
>
> **(c)** The majority of candidates realised that the filing cabinet would topple, but again the explanations were very weak and often not worthy of any credit.
