> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Фокусное расстояние и изображение через собирающую линзу.

Fig. 6.1 shows a converging lens with an object placed to one side of it.

Points F$_{1}$ and F$_{2}$ are the principal foci of the lens.

![[0625-s13-22-p10-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 6.1**

**(a)** On Fig. 6.1, mark the focal length of the lens, showing clearly where it starts and finishes. **[2]**

**(b)** One ray has been drawn through the lens from the top of the object.

**(i)** On Fig. 6.1, draw another ray to locate the image of the top of the object. Draw and label the image of the whole object. **[2]**

**(ii)** State two ways in which this image differs from the object.

1 \_\_\_\_\_\_

2 \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** focal length accurately shown ± 1 mm
>
> **(b)(i)** ray from top of object, straight through centre of lens (NOTE: ray need not intersect printed one to score M1) image drawn perpendicularly between intersection of candidate’s rays and axis

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | correct idea of focal length | C1 |
> |  | focal length accurately shown ± 1 mm | A1 |
> | (b)(i) | ray from top of object parallel to axis as far as lens, then down through F$_{1}$<br>(ignore point of refraction, as long as somewhere on lens)<br>OR | M1 |
> |  | ray from top of object, straight through centre of lens<br>(NOTE: ray need not intersect printed one to score M1)<br>image drawn perpendicularly between intersection of candidate’s rays and axis | A1 |
> | (b)(ii) | diminished o.w.t.t.e. | B1 |
> |  | inverted (ignore laterally) OR upside down<br>ignore brightness, ignore direction is changed, accept direction is reversed | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Only the best candidates were able to indicate the focal length of a lens on the diagram.
>
> **(b) (i)** The majority of candidates gained partial credit for drawing a correct ray, but then failed to draw and label the image at the correct position.
>
> **(ii)** This relatively straightforward item was very poorly answered, with some candidates’ descriptions having no relationship to the image they had just drawn.
