> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Энергетический баланс и изменение КПД изношенного механизма.

Fig. 10.1 illustrates the energy into and out of a machine.

![[0625-s13-23-p14-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 10.1**

**(a)** Write an equation that links *I*, *U* and *W*. **[1]**

**(b)** Which of these three quantities needs to be as low as possible in order to keep the efficiency of the machine as high as possible? **[1]**

**(c)** The machine gets older and parts of it become worn.

Suggest what is likely to happen, for the same input energy *I*, to

**(i)** the useful energy output *U*,  \_\_\_\_\_\_

**(ii)** the wasted energy *W*,  \_\_\_\_\_\_

**(iii)** the efficiency of the machine \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | I = U + W accept correct re-arrangements | B1 |
> | (b) | W OR wasted energy | B1 |
> | (c)(i) | decrease | B1 |
> | (c)(ii) | increase | B1 |
> | (c)(iii) | decrease | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Most candidates were successful, but many tried to read too much into a straightforward item and attempted to write an equation based on the efficiency equation.
>
> **(b)** Despite their problems with part **(a)**, the vast majority recognised that for a high efficiency the wasted energy needed to be kept as low as possible.
>
> **(c)** This was probably one of the best scoring questions on the paper, with the majority of candidates gaining most or all of the available credit.
