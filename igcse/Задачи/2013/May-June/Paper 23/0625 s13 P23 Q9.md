# Question 9 (9 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2013 · Paper 2 — Core theory, вариант 3 · вопрос 9  
Источник: [[_pdf/2013/0625_s13_qp_23.pdf#page=12|условие, стр. 12–13]] · [[_pdf/2013/0625_s13_ms_23.pdf#page=5|схема оценивания, стр. 5]] · [[_pdf/2013/0625_s13_er.pdf#page=15|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Преобразование напряжения трансформатором и передача энергии электросетью.

**(a)** The transformer in Fig. 9.1 is being used in an attempt to light a lamp using a 120 V a.c. mains supply. The lamp is designed for use in a country where the mains supply is 240 V a.c.

![[0625-s13-23-p12-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 9.1**

**(i)** **1.** Calculate the voltage across the lamp in this arrangement.

voltage =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ V **[3]**

**2.** Comment on the brightness of the lamp in this arrangement. **[2]**

**(ii)** The transformer is reversed, so that the 300 turn coil is connected to the 120 V a.c. supply and the 150 turn coil is connected to the lamp.

Comment on the brightness of the lamp in this arrangement. Explain your answer. **[2]**

**(b)** In the National Grid system of electrical energy transmission, a transformer links the power station to the transmission cables, as shown in Fig. 9.2.

![[0625-s13-23-p13-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 9.2**

**(i)** Why is a transformer used here? **[1]**

**(ii)** What has to be done at the other end of the transmission cables, before connection is made to a factory in a town? **[1]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)(i)** 240 (V)
>
> **(a)(i)** full/normal brightness OR as designed, e.c.f. from 1.

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | 1. V$_{1}$/V$_{2}$ = N$_{1}$/N$_{2}$ in any form | C1 |
> |  | correct substitution e.g. 120/ V$_{2}$ = 150/300 | C1 |
> |  | 240 (V) | A1 |
> |  | 2. lamp lights | C1 |
> |  | full/normal brightness OR as designed, e.c.f. from 1. | A1 |
> | (a)(ii) | lamp dim/does not light | B1 |
> |  | voltage low(ered)/stepped down | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | to step up voltage/increase voltage<br>OR to save energy OR to reduce energy losses | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | <u>step-down transformer OR reduce voltage</u><br>OR make voltage safe/mains voltage | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** Many candidates found the calculation involving the transformer equation difficult but could possibly have gained some more credit if they had clearly set out their method of working.
>
> **(ii)** Many candidates recognised that the lamp would be dimmer, but failed to state that the voltage would be lower.
>
> **(b) (i)** Very few candidates stated that the transformer was used to increase voltage.
>
> **(ii)** Many candidates did not realise that a **step-down** transformer was needed.
