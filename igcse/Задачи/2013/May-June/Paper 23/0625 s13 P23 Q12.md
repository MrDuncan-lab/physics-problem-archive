> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Нуклонный состав урана и превращение в торий при альфа распаде.

The nucleus of uranium-238 is represented in nuclide notation as$^{238}_{92}$U.

**(a)** **(i)** State the meaning of the *nucleon number* of a nuclide.

**(ii)** State the value of the nucleon number of$^{238}_{92}$U. **[2]**

**(b)** A nucleus of $^{238}_{92}$U decays by emitting an α-particle. It becomes a nucleus of thorium (Th).

**(i)** State

**1.** the nucleon number of an α-particle,  \_\_\_\_\_\_

**2.** the proton number of an α-particle \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(ii)** In nuclide notation, the thorium nucleus formed is written as$^{\text{X}}_{\text{Y}}$Th.

State the values of

**1.** X  \_\_\_\_\_\_ ,

**2.** Y  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(c)** **(i)** How many electrons are to be found in a neutral atom of$^{238}_{92}$U?  \_\_\_\_\_\_

**(ii)** Where in the atom are these electrons to be found? **[2]**

> | (a)(i) | number of protons plus neutrons<br>accept “and” accept (total) number of particles in the nucleus | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | 238 | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | 1. 4 | B1 |
> |  | 2. 2 | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | 234 e.c.f. (a)(ii) and (b)(i) | B1 |
> |  | 90 e.c.f. (b)(i) | B1 |

> | (c)(ii) | orbit(s) OR shell(s) OR outside nucleus<br>accept surround the nucleus | B1 |
