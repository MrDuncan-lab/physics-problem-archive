# Question 4 (6 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2013 · Paper 2 — Core theory, вариант 3 · вопрос 4  
Источник: [[_pdf/2013/0625_s13_qp_23.pdf#page=6|условие, стр. 6]] · [[_pdf/2013/0625_s13_ms_23.pdf#page=4|схема оценивания, стр. 4]] · [[_pdf/2013/0625_s13_er.pdf#page=15|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Проводимость металлов и электризация пластиковой палочки.

**(a)** Explain why metals are able to conduct electricity well, whereas insulators, like plastic, are very poor conductors. **[4]**

**(b)** A plastic rod is rubbed with a dry cloth, as shown in Fig. 4.1.

![[0625-s13-23-p6-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 4.1**

After this, the rod is held close to a girl’s long hair. The hair is attracted to the rod.

Suggest why this happens. **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** free electrons/charge or equiv.

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | mention of electrons | C1 |
> |  | free electrons/charge or equiv. | A1 |
> |  | idea of very many (free) charges in metals/conductors | B1 |
> |  | idea of very few/no (free) charges in plastic/insulators<br>(note: “more (free) charges in metal than plastic” gets B1,B1) | B1 |
> | (b) | rod charged, condone (gains)static charge/electricity | B1 |
> |  | charges attract (light objects)<br>(note: “charges on the rod attract (hair)” gets B1, B1) | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Only the better candidates seemed able to explain why metals were good conductors. A large number of weaker candidates failed to score on this part.
>
> **(b)** Most candidates gained credit for this section, as the majority recognised that the rod would become charged.
