> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Масса воды и гидростатическое давление и сила на смотровое окно.

Fig. 1.1 shows a side view of a large tank in a marine visitor attraction.

![[0625-s13-32-p2-f1.png]]

**Fig. 1.1** (not to scale)

The tank is 51 m long and 20 m wide. The sea-water in the tank is 11 m deep and has a density of 1030 kg / m$^{3}$.

**(a)** Calculate the mass of water in the tank.

mass =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

**(b)** The pressure at point M, halfway down the large viewing panel, is 60 kPa more than atmospheric pressure.

Calculate the depth of M below the surface of the water.

depth =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(c)** The viewing panel is 32.8 m wide and 8.3 m high.

Calculate the outward force of the water on the panel. Assume that the pressure at M is the average pressure on the whole panel.

force =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** (M = 51 × 20 × 11 × 1030 = 11 556 600 =) 1.2 × 10$^{7}$ kg
>
> **(b)** (∆h = 60 000 / (1030 × 10) =) 5.8(25) m
>
> **(c)** (F = 60 000 × 32.8 × 8.3 = 60 000 × 272.2 =) 1.6(33) × 10$^{7}$ N e.c.f. from (b)

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The majority of candidates scored well on this question. Weaker candidates could often remember the required equation but did not then apply it correctly.
>
> **(a)** The most frequent error was to use an area instead of the volume of water.
>
> **(b)** Many candidates scored gained full credit. Common errors were using *p* = *F*/*A* or *p* = *mgh*.
>
> **(c)** Again many candidates gained full credit. Common errors were using the wrong pressure or failing to transpose the equation *p* = *F*/*A* correctly.
