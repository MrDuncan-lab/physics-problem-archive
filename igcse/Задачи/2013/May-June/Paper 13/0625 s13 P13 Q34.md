# Question 34 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2013 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice, вариант 3 · вопрос 34  
Источник: [[_pdf/2013/0625_s13_qp_13.pdf#page=17|условие, стр. 17]] · [[_pdf/2013/0625_s13_ms_13.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2013/0625_s13_er.pdf#page=5|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Ошибка выбора номинала предохранителя настольной лампы.

A desk lamp should have a 3 A fuse fitted, but a 13 A fuse has been fitted by mistake.

The lamp is not faulty.

The lamp is switched on. What happens?

**A** The fuse blows.  
**B** The fuse does not blow but the lamp does not light.  
**C** The lamp draws too much current and the supply cables could melt.  
**D** The lamp works normally.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> All distractors were popular in this question on fuses, with option **C** attracting more candidates than the key (**D**). It was explained that the lamp was not faulty, so the wrong choice of fuse would not be apparent and the lamp would work normally. The point is that a fuse that is rated too highly for a particular purpose is a hidden danger.
