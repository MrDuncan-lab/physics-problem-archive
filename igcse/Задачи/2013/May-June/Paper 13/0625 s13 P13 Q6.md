> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Равнодействующая горизонтальных сил на движущийся автомобиль.

A car moves along a level road.

The diagram shows all of the horizontal forces acting on the car.

![[0625-s13-13-p4-f1.png]]

Which statement is correct?

**A** The car is slowing down.  
**B** The car is speeding up.  
**C** The car is moving at a constant speed.  
**D** The car is moving backwards.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question on resultant force and acceleration was generally well answered, although distractor **C** was chosen quite commonly. These candidates failed to add the two resistive forces and then realise that their total was less than the force from the engine.
