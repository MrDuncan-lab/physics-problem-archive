> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Измерение атмосферного давления ртутным барометром.

The diagram shows a simple mercury barometer.

![[0625-s13-13-p7-f1.svg]]

Which length is used to find the value of atmospheric pressure?

**A** 12 cm  
**B** 74 cm  
**C** 86 cm  
**D** 100 cm

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question concerned the simple barometer, and candidates needed to be aware that the *difference* in liquid levels could be used to find the atmospheric pressure. A significant number opted for **C**, failing to appreciate that the lower level was not zero.
