> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Общая характеристика образцов железа и алюминия одинаковой массы.

A 1 kg sample of aluminium is stored in a laboratory. In a different laboratory, in the same town, there is a 1 kg sample of iron.

Which quantity must these two samples always have in common?

**A** the same density  
**B** the same temperature  
**C** the same volume  
**D** the same weight

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
