> Плотность жидкости по массе и объёму в мерном цилиндре.

A measuring cylinder has a mass of 120 g when empty.

When it contains 50 cm$^{3}$ of a liquid, the total mass of the measuring cylinder and the liquid is 160 g.

What is the density of the liquid?

**A** $\dfrac{40}{50}$ g / cm$^{3}$  
**B** $\dfrac{50}{40}$ g / cm$^{3}$  
**C** $\dfrac{120}{50}$ g / cm$^{3}$  
**D** $\dfrac{160}{50}$ g / cm$^{3}$

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**
