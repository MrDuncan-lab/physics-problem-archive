> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Поперечность световых и гамма волн.

Visible light and γ-rays are both waves.

How may they correctly be described?

|  | visible light | γ-rays |
|---|---|---|
| A | longitudinal | longitudinal |
| B | longitudinal | transverse |
| C | transverse | longitudinal |
| D | transverse | transverse |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This was a simple recall question about the nature of visible light and γ-rays; although the majority of responses were correct, others were apparently guessing. Possibly some candidates thought that both types of wave could not be transverse.
