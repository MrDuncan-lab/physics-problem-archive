> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Калибровка ртутного термометра по двум реперным температурам.

A mercury thermometer with no scale is taped to a ruler as shown.

When the thermometer is placed in steam, the mercury level rises to 22.0 cm.

When the thermometer is placed in pure melting ice, the mercury level falls to 2.0 cm.

![[0625-s13-11-p9-f1.svg]]

![[0625-s13-11-p9-t1.svg]]

Which temperature is shown by the mercury level in the diagram?

**A** 6 °C  
**B** 8 °C  
**C** 30 °C  
**D** 40 °C

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Although most responses to this question on thermometers were correct, many candidates chose option B, not attempting any calculation and simply reading the number from the scale.
