> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Изменение показаний весов при стоянии на одной ноге.

A student stands with both feet on some scales in order to measure his weight.

The reading on the scales is 500 N. He lifts one foot off the scales and keeps it lifted.

What is the new reading on the scales?

**A** 0  
**B** 250 N  
**C** 500 N  
**D** 1000 N

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
