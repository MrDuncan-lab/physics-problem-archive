> Определение знаков зарядов по отталкиванию и притяжению шариков.

Three charged balls, P, Q and R are suspended by insulating threads. Ball P is negatively charged.

Ball Q is brought close to ball P.

![[0625-s13-12-p14-f1.svg]]

Ball Q is now brought close to ball R.

![[0625-s13-12-p14-f2.svg]]

What are the charges on ball Q and on ball R?

|  | ball Q | ball R |
|---|---|---|
| A | positive | positive |
| B | positive | negative |
| C | negative | positive |
| D | negative | negative |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
