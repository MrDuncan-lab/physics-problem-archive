> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Электромагнитное излучение для спутникового телевидения.

An electronic engineer makes devices which can receive television pictures from satellites.

Which type of electromagnetic radiation must these devices be able to receive?

**A** infra-red waves  
**B** microwaves  
**C** radio waves  
**D** ultra-violet waves

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Few candidates were aware that television pictures are transmitted from satellites to Earth using microwaves; the syllabus gives this as one of several specific examples of uses of electromagnetic waves.
