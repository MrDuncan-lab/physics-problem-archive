> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Разность времени прохождения прямого и отражённого звука.

Some sound from a loudspeaker at P travels directly to Q. Sound also reaches Q after being

reflected from a wall at R.

![[0625-s13-12-p13-f2.svg]]

The speed of sound is 330 m / s.

What is the difference in time for a sound to travel from P to Q by the two routes?

   

**A**  $\dfrac{6}{330}$  s  
**B**  $\dfrac{16}{330}$  s  
**C** (6 × 330) s  
**D** (16 × 330) s

   

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question on sound was usually well answered, with the most common error being not to calculate the time *difference*; candidates should read the question carefully, especially words in bold type.
