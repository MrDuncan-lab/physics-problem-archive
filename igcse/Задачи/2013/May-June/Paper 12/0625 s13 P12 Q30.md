> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Правильные приборы измерения тока в параллельной цепи.

A student investigates a circuit that contains two parallel resistors. The circuit includes meters R,

S and T which are all connected correctly.

![[0625-s13-12-p15-f1.svg]]

Which types of meter are R, S and T?

|  | meter R | meter S | meter T |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | ammeter | ammeter | ammeter |
| B | ammeter | voltmeter | voltmeter |
| C | voltmeter | ammeter | ammeter |
| D | voltmeter | voltmeter | voltmeter |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Many candidates did not take account of the fact that all three meters were in series with a component, so could not be voltmeters. It should be remembered in this type of question that it is possible for the correct response in all three columns to be the same.
