> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Частота водных волн по числу гребней за время.

A boy throws a small stone into a pond. Waves spread out from where the stone hits the water

and travel to the side of the pond.

The boy notices that eight waves reach the side of the pond in a time of 5.0 s.

What is the frequency of the waves?

**A** 0.20 Hz  
**B** 0.63 Hz  
**C** 1.6 Hz  
**D** 40 Hz

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The most common mistake in this question about the frequency of waves was to divide the time by the number of waves produced, rather than the other way round; this is a common error.
