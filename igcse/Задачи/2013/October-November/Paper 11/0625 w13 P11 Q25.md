> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Скорость звука в воде по времени возвращения эха.

A loudspeaker on a boat produces a pulse of sound in the sea. The echo of the pulse is received

back at the boat after 3.0 s. The depth of the sea under the boat is 2250 m.

![[0625-w13-11-p11-f1.svg]]

From this information, what is the speed of sound in the sea water?

**A** 330 m / s  
**B** 750 m / s  
**C** 1500 m / s  
**D** 6750 m / s

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Most of the errors here involved failing to double the distance from the boat to the sea bed when considering how far the sound had travelled in the 3.0s. This is often a problem in any question on echoes.
