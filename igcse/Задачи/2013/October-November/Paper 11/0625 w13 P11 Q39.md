> Альфа распад с испусканием двух протонов и двух нейтронов.

A radioactive substance emits a particle from the nucleus of one of its atoms. The particle

consists of two protons and two neutrons.

What is the name of this process?

**A** α-emission  
**B** β-emission  
**C** γ-emission  
**D** nuclear fission

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**
