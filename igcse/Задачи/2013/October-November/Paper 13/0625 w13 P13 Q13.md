> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Скорость движения молекул газа при охлаждении.

A closed flask of gas is placed in a cold-water bath.

![[0625-w13-13-p6-f2.svg]]

As the flask cools, the temperature of the gas decreases.

What happens to the molecules of the gas?

**A** They contract.  
**B** They expand.  
**C** They move more quickly.  
**D** They move more slowly.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
