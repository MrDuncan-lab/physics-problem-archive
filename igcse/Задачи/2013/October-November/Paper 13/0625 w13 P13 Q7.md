> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Удлинение пружины при заданной нагрузке по графику.

The diagrams show a steel spring and a graph of its length against the load applied to it.

![[0625-w13-13-p4-f2.svg]]

What is the extension of the spring when a load of 20 N is applied to it?

**A** 3.0 cm  
**B** 4.5 cm  
**C** 5.0 cm  
**D** 8.0 cm

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question concerned stretching a spring, and a significant number of candidates forgot to subtract the original length when calculating the extension; this is a commonly seen error in questions about springs.
