> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Оценка ширины долины по времени отражённого звука.

To estimate the width of a valley, a climber starts a stopwatch as he shouts. He hears an echo

from the opposite side of the valley after 1.0 s.

![[0625-w13-13-p12-f1.svg]]

The sound travels at 330 m / s.

What is the width of the valley?

**A** 82.5 m  
**B** 165 m  
**C** 330 m  
**D** 660 m

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
