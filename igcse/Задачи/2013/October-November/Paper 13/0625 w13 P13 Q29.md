> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Измерение ЭДС батареи и напряжения на резисторе.

A student wishes to measure first the electromotive force (e.m.f.) of a battery, and then the

potential difference (p.d.) across a resistor.

She has the resistor, the battery and some connecting wires.

What else does she need?

**A** a force meter (newton meter) and a voltmeter  
**B** an ammeter and a voltmeter  
**C** an ammeter only  
**D** a voltmeter only

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Many candidates thought that an ammeter and a voltmeter would be needed to measure e.m.f. and p.d.
