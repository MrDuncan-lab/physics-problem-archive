> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Поглощение разных видов излучения и опасность радона.

**(a)** Radon-220 is a radioactive gas. It decays by emitting α-particles. An α-particle is a

![[0625-w13-22-p15-f1.svg]]

**(i)** What four particles together make up an α-particle?

**(ii)** Suggest one reason why the fact that radon-220 is a gas makes it potentially more dangerous than an α-emitting solid source of similar activity.

**(b)** In the situations illustrated in Fig. 11.1, the radioactive source is emitting α-particles, β-particles and γ-rays. The detector is sensitive to all three types of radiation. The

![[0625-w13-22-p15-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 11.1**

State one of the situations, **A**, **B**, **C** or **D** where

**(i)** α-particles are detected,  \_\_\_\_\_\_

**(ii)** only β-particles and γ-rays are detected,  \_\_\_\_\_\_

**(iii)** only γ-rays are detected,  \_\_\_\_\_\_

**(iv)** α-particles, β-particles and γ-rays are all detected \_\_\_\_\_\_

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)(i)** two of each
