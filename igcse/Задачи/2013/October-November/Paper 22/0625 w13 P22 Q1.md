> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Усреднение времени спуска тележки и измерение её скорости.

A slope is made by resting one end of a plank of wood on a block, as shown in Fig. 1.1.

![[0625-w13-22-p2-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 1.1**

Three students each use a digital stopwatch to time a small trolley rolling down the full length of the slope.

The times on their stopwatches as the trolley reached the bottom of the slope are shown in Fig. 1.2.

![[0625-w13-22-p2-f2.png]]

student 1 time =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ s

![[0625-w13-22-p2-f3.png]]

student 2 time =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ s

![[0625-w13-22-p2-f4.png]]

student 3 time =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ s

**Fig. 1.2**

**(a)** On the line next to each stopwatch, write the time measured by each student. **[1]**

**(b)** Calculate the average time measured for the trolley to roll down the slope. Show your working.

average time =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ s **[2]**

**(c)** What other measurement must be taken in order to be able to calculate the average speed of the trolley? **[1]**

**(d)** Suggest one change that might be made to the arrangement in Fig. 1.1 so that the same trolley takes less time to roll down the full length of the slope. **[1]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** 7.04 e.c.f. (a)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)

> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | 7.02 7.13 6.97 | B1 |
> | (b) | evidence of adding three times | C1 |
> |  | 7.04 e.c.f. (a) | A1 |
> | (c) | distance / length of slope | B1 |
> | (d) | oil axles (accept oil wheels)<br>steeper slope / raise plank any 1<br>push trolley | B1 |
