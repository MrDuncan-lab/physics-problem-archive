> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Управление отклонением электронов катодно-лучевой трубки.

The diagram shows a cathode-ray tube used to produce cathode rays.

![[0625-w13-12-p16-f1.svg]]

Which change would increase the deflection of the cathode rays?

**A** decreasing the current I in the filament  
**B** increasing the current I in the filament  
**C** increasing the potential difference between the filament and the anode  
**D** increasing the potential difference between the metal plates

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The most common error here was a belief that increasing the filament current would increase the beam deflection.
