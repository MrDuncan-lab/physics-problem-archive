> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Излучения для спутникового телевидения и пульта управления.

The table gives common uses for three types of electromagnetic wave.

Which row correctly identifies the waves?

|  | satellite television | terrestrial television (not satellite) | television remote controllers |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | infra-red waves | microwaves | radio waves |
| B | microwaves | radio waves | infra-red waves |
| C | radio waves | infra-red waves | microwaves |
| D | radio waves | microwaves | infra-red waves |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Distractor D was chosen by a large proportion of the candidates in this question about the uses of electromagnetic waves; careful reference to the syllabus is recommended.
