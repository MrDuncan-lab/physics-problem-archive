> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Правильное расположение предохранителей в цепи обогревателя.

The diagram shows the connections to an electric heater. Three fuses have been added to the

circuit.

![[0625-w13-12-p14-f5.svg]]

Which of the fuses are correctly placed?

**A** fuse 1, fuse 2 and fuse 3  
**B** fuse 1 and fuse 2 only  
**C** fuse 1 only  
**D** fuse 2 only

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> A significant number of candidates did not realise that fuse 2 was wrongly (and dangerously) placed.
