Fig. 8.2 is the trace seen on the c.r.o. screen.

**Fig. 8.3 Fig. 8.4**

**(a)** **(i)** On Fig. 8.4, draw the shape of the trace now seen on the screen. **[2]**

**(ii)** Suggest a device that uses a diode in this way. **[1]**

**(b)** State and explain the effect on the lamp of inserting the diode into the circuit. **[2]**
