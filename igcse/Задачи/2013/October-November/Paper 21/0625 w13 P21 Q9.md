# Question 9 (11 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2013 · Paper 2 — Core theory, вариант 1 · вопрос 9  
Источник: [[_pdf/2013/0625_w13_qp_21.pdf#page=12|условие, стр. 12–13]] · [[_pdf/2013/0625_w13_ms_21.pdf#page=5|схема оценивания, стр. 5]] · [[_pdf/2013/0625_w13_er.pdf#page=7|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Напряжение ток и сопротивление в цепи с параллельной ветвью.

Fig. 9.1 shows a series circuit. The resistances of the ammeter and of the battery may be ignored.

![[0625-w13-21-p12-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 9.1**

**(a)** What is component X? Tick one box.

![[0625-w13-21-p12-f2.svg]]

**[1]**

**(b)** State the value of

**(i)** the e.m.f. of the battery,  \_\_\_\_\_\_

**(ii)** the potential difference across component X,  \_\_\_\_\_\_

**(iii)** the current in the circuit \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

**(c)** Use values from Fig. 9.1 to calculate the resistance of component X. Give the unit.

resistance =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[4]**

**(d)** A resistor R is connected in parallel with component X.

State what effect, if any, this has on

**(i)** the total resistance of the circuit,

**(ii)** the reading on the ammeter,

**(iii)** the current in component X. **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(c)** 24 OR 0.024

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | resistor | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | 6.0 V OR 6 V, unity penalty applies | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | 6.0 V OR 6 V, unity penalty applies unless penalised in (i), no e.c.f. from (i) | B1 |
> | (b)(iii) | 250 mA OR 0.25 A, unit penalty applies unless penalised in (i) or (ii) | B1 |
> | (c) | (R =) V/I | C1 |
> |  | 6/0.25 OR 6/250 | C1 |
> |  | 24 OR 0.024 | A1 |
> |  | Ω OR ohm(s) OR kΩ (note: if value calculated, unit must agree with value) | B1 |
> | (d)(i) | decreases | B1 |
> | (d)(ii) | increases | B1 |
> | (d)(iii) | unchanged<br>accept no effect/none | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** The correct answer was usually indicated.
>
> **(b)** Many candidates scored full credit, although some did not give the correct unit and a significant number tried some convoluted calculation to find the current in the circuit.
>
> **(c)** The majority of candidates were able to calculate the resistance of the resistor. The most common errors were in rearranging the equation *V*= I*R* and failing to correctly convert mA to A.
>
> **(d)** The effect of adding another resistor in parallel was not well understood. Only the more able candidates scored full credit.
