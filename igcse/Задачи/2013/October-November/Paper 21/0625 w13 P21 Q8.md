> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Only the most able candidates scored well on this relatively straightforward question. Most candidates thought that the magnetic field near the centre of the bar magnet was vertical rather than horizontal or that the direction was towards the N pole.
>
> **(b)** Many candidates displayed a poor understanding of the magnetic properties of these common metals. Many would benefit from carrying out similar straightforward practical experiments involving magnets and magnetic/non-magnetic materials.
