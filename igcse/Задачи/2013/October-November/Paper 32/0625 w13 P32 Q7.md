> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** The boxes in part **(i)** for virtual and magnified were generally ticked although a small minority of candidates made wild guesses. The correct distance for the focal length was less often identified.
>
> **(b)** Many candidates answered very well and gained full credit for what was designed to be a test of using known principles in an unfamiliar situation. In part **(iii)**, many weaker candidates did not think through that the incident ray OP was on the same line as the normal, so the reflected ray at P was drawn with an angle from the normal greater than 0. This made it almost impossible to gain credit for the second marking point in part **(iv)** for accuracy.
