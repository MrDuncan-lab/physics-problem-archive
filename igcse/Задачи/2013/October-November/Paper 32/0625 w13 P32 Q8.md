# Question 8 (10 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2013 · Paper 3 — Extended theory, вариант 2 · вопрос 8

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сопротивление провода при изменении размеров и расчёт разветвлённой цепи.

**(a)** A piece of wire has a resistance of 0.45 Ω.

Calculate the resistance of another piece of wire of the same material with a third of the length and half the cross-sectional area.

resistance =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

**(b)** Fig. 8.1 shows a circuit with three resistors, a power supply and four voltmeters.

![[0625-w13-32-p14-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 8.1**

**(i)** Calculate the combined resistance of the three resistors.

resistance =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

**(ii)** Write down **two** relationships for the currents in the circuit. **[2]**

**(iii)** Write down **two** relationships for the voltmeter readings in the circuit. **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** (resistance = 0.45 × 2/3) = 0.3(0) Ω accept 1 sig. fig.
>
> **(b)(i)** 2.7 or 2.8 or 2.75 Ω
