# Question 9 (7 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2013 · Paper 3 — Extended theory, вариант 1 · вопрос 9  
Источник: [[_pdf/2013/0625_w13_qp_31.pdf#page=16|условие, стр. 16–17]] · [[_pdf/2013/0625_w13_ms_31.pdf#page=6|схема оценивания, стр. 6]] · [[_pdf/2013/0625_w13_er.pdf#page=18|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w13 P31]]  
Темы: [[4.2.4 Resistance]], [[4.2.5 Electrical energy and electrical power]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Зависимость сопротивления провода от длины и сечения и ток ламп.

**(a)** State the relationship between

**(i)** the resistance *R* and the length *L* of a wire of constant cross-sectional area,

**(ii)** the resistance *R* and the cross-sectional area *A* of a wire of constant length. **[1]**

**(b)** A 60 W filament lamp X is connected to a 230 V supply, as shown in Fig. 9.1.

![[0625-w13-31-p16-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 9.1**

Calculate the current in the filament.

current =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(c)** Lamp Y has a filament made of the same metal as the filament of lamp X in **(b)**.

This filament has half the length and one-third of the cross-sectional area of the filament of X.

Lamp Y is also connected to a 230 V supply.

Calculate the ratio $\dfrac{\text{current in filament of Y}}{\text{current in filament of X}}$ . Show your working.

ratio =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[4]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** 0.26 A
>
> **(c)** current in Y / Current in X = 2/3

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(ii) | **(a)(i)** R ∝ L in words or symbols<br>AND R ∝ 1 / A in words or symbols | B1 |
> | (b) | P = IV OR (I =) P / V OR 60 / 230 | C1 |
> |  | 0.26 A | A1 |
> | (c) | length change divides resistance by 2 / multiplies current by 2 | C1 |
> |  | cross-section change multiplies resistance by 3 / divides current by 3 | C1 |
> |  | (overall) resistance of Y is 3/2 times bigger / 3/2 × 885 Ω / 1327 Ω<br>OR current in Y 2/3 of 0.26 A = 0.17 A | C1 |
> |  | current in Y / Current in X = 2/3 | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** A good proportion of the candidates could recall the relationships between resistance and length and between resistance and area of cross-section.
>
> **(b)** Many candidates failed to recall the correct formula, Some of those who could recall *P* = I*V* transposed it wrongly.
>
> **(c)** Few candidates achieved the final correct answer, but many gained compensation marks for correct steps towards that answer. Correct fractional changes to the resistance or the current were rewarded, taking into account use of the wrong current carried forward from **(b)**.
