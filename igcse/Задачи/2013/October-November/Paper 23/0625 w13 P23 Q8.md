> Частоты и применения электромагнитного излучения и защита от микроволн.

In alphabetical order, some major regions of the electromagnetic spectrum are

gamma, infra-red, radio, ultra-violet, visible, X-rays.

**(a)** List these regions in order of decreasing wavelength. The first one has been filled in for you.

radio

longest wavelength  \_\_\_\_\_\_

shortest wavelength  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(b)** Where in this list would you insert microwaves? **[1]**

**(c)** A microwave oven is one device that makes use of microwaves.

Explain why the design of microwave ovens must include effective screening, so that microwaves do not escape. **[1]**

**(d)** Suggest one other use for microwaves. **[1]**
