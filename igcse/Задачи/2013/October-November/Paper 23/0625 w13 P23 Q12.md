> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Расчёт понижающего трансформатора и подключение трёх ламп.

Fig. 12.1 shows a simple transformer.

![[0625-w13-23-p16-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 12.1**

Coil A has 5000 turns and coil B has 250 turns.

**(a)** Name a suitable material for

**(i)** the core,  \_\_\_\_\_\_

**(ii)** the coils \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(b)** Coil A is connected to a 240 V alternating voltage.

Calculate the output voltage across coil B.

output voltage =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ V **[3]**

**(c)** Three lamps each require a voltage equal to the output voltage of coil B in order to operate at full brightness.

On Fig. 12.1, show these three lamps, suitably connected to run at full brightness. Use the standard circuit symbol for each lamp. **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** 12 (V)
