> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определение объёма пробирки разностью показаний воды.

The IGCSE class is determining the internal volume of a test-tube using two displacement methods.

The apparatus used is shown in Figs. 5.1, 5.2 and 5.3.

![[0625-s12-61-p10-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 5.1 Fig. 5.2**

**(a)** **(i)** Fig. 5.1 shows water in a measuring cylinder. Record the volume *V*$_{1}$of the water.

*V*$_{1}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(ii)** On Fig. 5.1, show clearly the line of sight that you would use to obtain an accurate volume reading. **[2]**

**(b)** **(i)** A student lowers a test-tube, closed end first, into the water in the measuring cylinder and pushes the tube down until it is filled with water. From Fig. 5.2, record the new water level *V*$_{2}$.

*V*$_{2}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

**(ii)** Calculate the volume *V*$_{\text{G}}$ of the glass of the test-tube using the equation *V*$_{\text{G}}$ = (*V*$_{2}$ – *V*$_{1}$).

*V*$_{\text{G}}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(c)** The student removes the test-tube from the measuring cylinder and empties the water back into the measuring cylinder. He then puts the test-tube, open end first, into the water in the measuring cylinder and carefully pushes it down with his finger until it is covered with water as shown in Fig. 5.3.

![[0625-s12-61-p11-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 5.3**

**(i)** Record the new water level *V*$_{3}$.

*V*$_{3}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

**(ii)** Calculate the increase in water level (*V*$_{3}$ – *V*$_{1}$).

(*V*$_{3}$ – *V*$_{1}$) =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

**(iii)** Calculate the volume *V*$_{\text{A}}$of air in the test-tube using the equation *V*$_{\text{A}}$ = (*V*$_{3}$ – *V*$_{1}$) – *V*$_{\text{G}}$.

*V*$_{\text{A}}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(d)** The student removes the test-tube from the measuring cylinder and fills the test-tube with water from a beaker. He pours the water from the test-tube into an empty measuring cylinder and records the volume *V*$_{\text{W}}$ of water:

18 cm$^{3}$

*V*$_{\text{W}}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

The student has attempted to determine the internal volume of the test-tube by two methods. His two values for the internal volume are *V*$_{\text{A}}$ and *V*$_{\text{W}}$.

Assuming that the experiments have been carried out correctly and carefully and that the measuring cylinder scale is accurate, suggest two reasons why the value *V*$_{\text{A}}$ may be inaccurate and two reasons why the value *V*$_{\text{W}}$ may be inaccurate.

*V*$_{\text{A}}$:

reason 1  \_\_\_\_\_\_

reason 2  \_\_\_\_\_\_

*V*$_{\text{W}}$:

reason 1  \_\_\_\_\_\_

reason 2  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | V$_{1}$ = 74 | 1 |
> |  | Line of sight perpendicular to scale | 1 |
> |  | Perpendicular line continues to measuring cylinder at surface level | 1 |
> | (b) | V$_{2}$ = 81, V$_{\text{G}}$ = 7 (ecf allowed) | 1 |
> |  | All volumes in cm$^{3}$, unit given at least once, not contradicted | 1 |
> | (c) | (V$_{3}$ – V$_{1}$) = 24, V$_{\text{A}}$ = 17 (ecf allowed) | 1 |
> | (d) | Any three from:<br>V$_{\text{A}}$: Finger increases V$_{3}$ / tube not pushed in far enough<br>Some water in test-tube/air is compressed<br>V$_{\text{W}}$: Water remaining in tube<br>Water remaining in measuring cylinder<br>Tube overfilled, wtte (surface tension effect)<br>Either V$_{\text{A}}$ or V$_{\text{W}}$ (accept only once):<br>Measuring cylinder readings not very sensitive<br>Subtraction produces large percentage uncertainty | 3 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Most candidates correctly showed a line of sight perpendicular to the measuring cylinder and directed towards the bottom of the meniscus.
>
> **(b)(c)** Most candidates gained full credit by successfully recording the volumes with the unit cm$^{3}$.
>
> **(d)** Many candidates found it difficult to focus on the requirements of the question and made suggestions involving careless technique, for example spilling water. The most confident candidates were able to study the diagrams and descriptions carefully and imagine themselves carrying out the procedure, based on personal experience. In this way they were able to make relevant suggestions.
