> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Частицы атома и запись нуклидной формулы.

Fig. 12.1 represents a neutral atom.

![[0625-s12-23-p15-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 12.1**

**(a)** What name do we give to

**(i)** the central mass,  \_\_\_\_\_\_

**(ii)** the two orbiting particles?  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(b)** The central mass contains two neutrons.

**(i)** What other type of particle does it contain?  \_\_\_\_\_\_

**(ii)** How many of these other particles are there?  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(iii)** Use nuclide notation,$^{\text{A}}_{\text{Z}}$ X, to describe the nuclide in Fig. 12.1, writing appropriate numbers in place of *A* and *Z*.

\_\_\_\_\_\_ X **[2]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | nucleus | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | electron(s) | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | proton(s) | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | 2 | B1 |
> | (b)(iii) | 4 at top | B1 |
> |  | 2 at bottom | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> A good proportion of candidates were able to score well on this question. The most common mistakes were the confusion between nucleus and nucleon, nuclide and neutron in (a), and the inversion of the 4 and 2 in
>
> (b)(iii).
