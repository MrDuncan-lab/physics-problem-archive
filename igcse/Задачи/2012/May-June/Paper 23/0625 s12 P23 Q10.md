**(b)** Calculate the reading on the ammeter when the length *l* is zero.

**(i)** the total resistance of the circuit,

ammeter reading =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ A **[2]**

Calculate the resistance of 25 cm of the resistance wire.

resistance =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ Ω **[2]**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This problem was perhaps slightly more demanding than others on electric circuits. However, no physics was involved that was beyond the syllabus. Many candidates did not correctly recall the relationship between resistance and current; many times I = VR or I = R/V was quoted. Similarly, very few added 5 Ω and 15 Ω to get the answer to (c). Generally, this question was poor answered.
