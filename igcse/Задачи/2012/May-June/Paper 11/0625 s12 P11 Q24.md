> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Построение лучей при прохождении собирающей линзы.

Each point labelled F is a principal focus of the lens.

![[0625-s12-11-p10-f2.svg]]

Which of the rays are drawn correctly?

**A** ray Y only  
**B** ray Z only  
**C** ray X and ray Y  
**D** ray X and ray Z

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This lens question was well answered, the main misconception being that all refracted rays must pass through the principal focus.
