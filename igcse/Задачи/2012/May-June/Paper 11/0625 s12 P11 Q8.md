> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Результирующее действие трёх сил на рычаг.

The diagram shows a handle with three forces, each 100 N, applied to it. The handle is free to move.

![[0625-s12-11-p4-f2.svg]]

What is the effect of the forces on the handle?

**A** The handle will move downwards.  
**B** The handle will not move.  
**C** The handle will turn anticlockwise (to the left).  
**D** The handle will turn clockwise (to the right).

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question concerned equilibrium, and many of the candidates opted for **B**. Although the two horizontal forces were equal, they acted at different distances from the pivot, so the handle was not in equilibrium.
