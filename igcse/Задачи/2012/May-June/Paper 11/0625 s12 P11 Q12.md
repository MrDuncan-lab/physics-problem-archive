> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Давление газа по показаниям ртутного манометра.

The diagram shows a mercury manometer used to measure the pressure of gas in a container.

Atmospheric pressure is 76 cm of mercury.

![[0625-s12-11-p6-f1.svg]]

What is the pressure of the gas?

**A** 56 cm of mercury  
**B** 68 cm of mercury  
**C** 84 cm of mercury  
**D** 96 cm of mercury

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question concerned a manometer; almost a quarter of the candidates added the 8 cm difference between the two levels to the atmospheric pressure, instead of subtracting it.
