> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Дифракция водных волн через отверстие в стенке.

The diagram shows water waves passing through a gap in a harbour wall. The waves curve

round the wall and reach a small boat in the harbour.

![[0625-s12-11-p9-f1.svg]]

What is the name of this curving effect, and how can the gap be changed so that the waves are less likely to reach the boat?

|  | name of effect | change to the gap |
|---|---|---|
| A | diffraction | make the gap slightly bigger |
| B | diffraction | make the gap slightly smaller |
| C | refraction | make the gap slightly bigger |
| D | refraction | make the gap slightly smaller |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The name of the effect shown (diffraction) was very well known, but there was considerable uncertainty about the effect of gap size on the amount of diffraction which would occur.
