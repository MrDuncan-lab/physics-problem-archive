> Направление силы Ампера при изменениях тока и магнитного поля.

A wire passes between the poles of a horseshoe magnet. There is a current in the wire in the

direction shown, and this causes a force to act on the wire.

![[0625-s12-11-p16-f1.svg]]

Three other arrangements, P, Q and R, of the wire and magnet are set up as shown.

![[0625-s12-11-p16-f2.svg]]

current direction reversed current direction reversed

and magnet turned around

Which arrangement or arrangements will cause a force in the same direction as the original arrangement?

**A** P, Q and R  
**B** P and Q only C P only  
**D** R only

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
