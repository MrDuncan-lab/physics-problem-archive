# Question 7 (6 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2012 · Paper 3 — Extended theory, вариант 1 · вопрос 7  
Источник: [[_pdf/2012/0625_s12_qp_31.pdf#page=8|условие, стр. 8]] · [[_pdf/2012/0625_s12_ms_31.pdf#page=7|схема оценивания, стр. 7]] · [[_pdf/2012/0625_s12_er.pdf#page=19|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Дифракция водных волн и их частота.

**(a)** A wave passes along the surface of the water in a ripple tank. Describe the motion of a molecule on the surface as the wave passes. **[1]**

**(b)** Fig. 7.1 shows a view from above of water waves approaching a narrow gap in a barrier. The water on both sides of the barrier has the same depth.

![[0625-s12-31-p8-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 7.1**

**(i)** On Fig. 7.1, sketch the pattern of waves in the region to the right of the barrier. **[2]**

**(ii)** State the process by which waves arrive at point P to the right of the barrier. **[1]**

**(c)** The waves approaching the barrier in Fig. 7.1 have a wavelength of 1.4 cm and travel at a speed of 12 cm / s.

Calculate the frequency of the waves.

frequency =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(c)** f = 8.57 Hz / per s / waves or vibrations per s at least 2 s.f.

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Only answers referring to up and down motion of a molecule, or its vibration perpendicular to the wave’s direction were rewarded. Some candidates described longitudinal motion.
>
> **(b) (i)** Some excellent compass-drawn and free-hand attempts were seen. Full credit was awarded for circular arcs with approximately the same spacing as the incident waves and of sufficient angular spread. Many other less accurate sketches gained partial credit.
>
> **(ii)** The term *diffraction* is generally well known.
>
> **(c)** A very large majority of candidates calculated the frequency correctly.
