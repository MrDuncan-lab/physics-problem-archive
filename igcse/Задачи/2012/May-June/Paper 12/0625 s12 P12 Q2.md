> Определение числа капель воды по объёму в мензурке.

Drops of water are dripping steadily from a tap (faucet). The diagram shows a measuring cylinder which has collected 120 drops of water.

![[0625-s12-12-p2-f2.svg]]

![[0625-s12-12-p2-f3.svg]]

How many drops in total will have been collected when the measuring cylinder reads 10 cm$^{3}$?

**A** 48  
**B** 60  
**C** 180  
**D** 300

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
