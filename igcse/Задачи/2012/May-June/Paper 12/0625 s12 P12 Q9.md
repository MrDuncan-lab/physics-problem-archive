> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Источник электричества без предварительного кипячения воды.

Electricity can be obtained from different energy resources.

Which energy resource is used to obtain electricity without producing heat to boil water?

**A** coal  
**B** gas  
**C** hydroelectric  
**D** nuclear

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Many candidates knew which of the energy sources were used to boil water to obtain electricity, but several thought that nuclear energy was not in this group.
