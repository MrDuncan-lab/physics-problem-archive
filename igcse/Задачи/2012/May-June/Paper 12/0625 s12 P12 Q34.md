> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбор предохранителя для двух параллельных приборов.

An air conditioner and a television are both connected to the same electrical circuit.

![[0625-s12-12-p15-f1.svg]]

The current in the air conditioner is 4.0 A and the current in the television is 6.0 A.

Several different fuses are available.

Which fuse should be connected at X?

**A** 3 A  
**B** 5 A  
**C** 10 A  
**D** 13 A

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question required candidates to choose the correct fuse for a circuit, and many of the weaker candidates opted for **C** (10 A). The normal operating current in this circuit is 10 A, and the fuse ought to be selected with a rating slightly higher than this, hence the key **D** (13 A).
