Источник: [[_pdf/2012/0625_s12_qp_13.pdf#page=11|условие, стр. 11]] · [[_pdf/2012/0625_s12_ms_13.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2012/0625_s12_er.pdf#page=7|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Поперечные световые и радиоволны.

Which row correctly describes light waves and radio waves?

|  | light waves | radio waves |
|---|---|---|
| A | longitudinal | longitudinal |
| B | longitudinal | transverse |
| C | transverse | longitudinal |
| D | transverse | transverse |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Knowledge of the nature of light waves and radio waves was shown to be lacking here, with over half of the candidates choosing incorrectly. It would be worth reminding them that the two columns in a question do not necessarily have to have different contents for a row to be correct.
