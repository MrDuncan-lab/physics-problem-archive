> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Превращения механической энергии колеблющегося груза.

An object on a thread is swinging between X and Z, as shown in the diagram. It is momentarily at rest at X and at Z.

![[0625-s12-13-p5-f1.svg]]

An incomplete word equation about the energy of the object is shown below.

gravitational potential energy = kinetic energy +  \_\_\_\_\_\_  energy + energy losses

at X at Y at Y

Which form of energy is needed to complete the word equation?

**A** chemical  
**B** gravitational potential  
**C** internal  
**D** strain

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Options **C** and **D** were both quite popular distractors. Candidates needed to realise that the gravitational potential energy of the object will not be zero at Y.
