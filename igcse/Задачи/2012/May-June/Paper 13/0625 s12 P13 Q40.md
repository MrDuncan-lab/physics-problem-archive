> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Изменение зарядового числа ядра при бета-распаде.

A radioactive nucleus emits a β-particle.

![[0625-s12-13-p19-f1.svg]]

![[0625-s12-13-p19-f2.svg]]

What happens to the proton number (atomic number) of the nucleus?

**A** It stays the same.  
**B** It increases by 1.  
**C** It decreases by 2.  
**D** It decreases by 4.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question was about the change in a nucleus following emission of a β-particle. Almost half the candidates chose options **A** or **C**, showing confusion over this part of the syllabus.
