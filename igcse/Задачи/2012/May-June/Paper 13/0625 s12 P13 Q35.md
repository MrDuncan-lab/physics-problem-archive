> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Размагничивание стального стержня.

Which procedure may be used to demagnetise a steel bar?

**A** cooling it in a freezer  
**B** earthing it with a copper wire  
**C** placing it in a solenoid carrying a large direct current (d.c.)  
**D** striking it repeatedly with a hammer

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> A significant proportion of candidates could not recognise a method by which a steel bar could be demagnetised.
