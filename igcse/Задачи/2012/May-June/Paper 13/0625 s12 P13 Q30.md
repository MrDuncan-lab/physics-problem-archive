> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определение электрического компонента по вольт-амперной характеристике.

The circuit shown in the diagram contains an unknown component X, hidden in a box.

The voltage-current graph for X is as shown.

![[0625-s12-13-p14-f1.svg]]

What is the component X?

**A** a capacitor  
**B** a closed switch  
**C** an open switch  
**D** a resistor of constant resistance

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> A significant number of candidates could not identify a resistor of constant resistance from its V – I characteristic.
