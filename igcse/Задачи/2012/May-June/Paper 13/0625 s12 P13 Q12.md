> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Возникновение конвекции при нагревании жидкости.

Why does convection take place in a liquid when it is heated?

**A** Liquids expand when they are heated.  
**B** Liquids start to bubble when they get close to boiling point.  
**C** Molecules in the liquid expand when they are heated.  
**D** Molecules near to the surface of the liquid escape into the air.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Candidates found this the most difficult question on the paper. Option **D** was very popular, with candidates apparently confusing evaporation with convection. A significant number chose option **C**, believing that liquid molecules expand when heated.
