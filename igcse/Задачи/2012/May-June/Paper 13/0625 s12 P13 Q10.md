**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2012 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice, вариант 3 · вопрос 10  
Источник: [[_pdf/2012/0625_s12_qp_13.pdf#page=5|условие, стр. 5]] · [[_pdf/2012/0625_s12_ms_13.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2012/0625_s12_er.pdf#page=7|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 s12 P13]]  
Темы: [[2.2.1 Thermal expansion of solids, liquids and gases]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбор термометрических величин, изменяющихся с температурой.

Which pair contains only physical quantities that vary with temperature and so could be used in

making a thermometer?

**A** activity of a radioactive source, volume of a gas  
**B** mass of a liquid, volume of a liquid  
**C** activity of a radioactive source, mass of a solid  
**D** volume of a gas, volume of a liquid

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The most common error was to select option **B**, these candidates believing that mass could be affected by temperature.
