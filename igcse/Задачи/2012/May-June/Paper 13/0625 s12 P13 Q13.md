> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Распознавание двух возобновляемых источников энергии.

In which pair of energy sources are both sources renewable?

**A** oil and coal  
**B** oil and tidal  
**C** tidal and geothermal  
**D** tidal and nuclear fission

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Most candidates knew which of the energy sources were renewable, the main misconception being that nuclear fuels were in this group; it should be stressed that nuclear fuel, although not a fossil fuel, is non- renewable.
