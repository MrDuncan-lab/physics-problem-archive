**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2012 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice, вариант 3 · вопрос 21  
Источник: [[_pdf/2012/0625_s12_qp_13.pdf#page=10|условие, стр. 10]] · [[_pdf/2012/0625_s12_ms_13.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2012/0625_s12_er.pdf#page=7|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Правильность построения трёх лучей собирающей линзы.

A student draws three rays of light from point P through a converging lens.

Each point labelled F is a principal focus of the lens.

![[0625-s12-13-p10-f1.svg]]

Which of the rays are drawn correctly?

**A** ray Y only  
**B** ray Z only  
**C** ray X and ray Y  
**D** ray X and ray Z

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This lens question was generally well answered, the main misconception being that all refracted rays must pass through the principal focus.
