> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Questions on waves often cause candidates to struggle, but many, on this occasion, were able to score reasonable credit. Almost all candidates knew where the stones were hitting the water, and most made a reasonable attempt at the shape of the wave in **(b)**. It is quite difficult to draw shapes such as sine waves, so quality of drawing was dealt with generously. Many candidates drew the wave very well indeed. Many stated that the speed of the wave increased or decreased, but a good proportion of candidates were able to spot that the wave speed was constant. A few even correctly explained their reasoning, which was good but not needed. Very few, however, realised that the speed was the same in all directions (circular waves).
