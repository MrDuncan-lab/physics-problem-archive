> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Энергия и мощность подъёма камней на скалу.

Two geologists are collecting rocks from the bottom of a cliff. The rocks are loaded into a basket and then pulled up the cliff on the end of a rope, as shown in Fig. 4.1. The basket of rocks is brought to rest at the top of the cliff.

![[0625-s12-22-p5-f1.svg]]

**(a)** **(i)** Which form of energy that the basket possesses is significantly greater at the top of the cliff than when it is at the bottom of the cliff? **[1]**

**(ii)** Which two measurements must be made in order to calculate the increase in energy in **(i)**?

1 \_\_\_\_\_\_

2 \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(b)** Which form of energy in his body has the man at the top of the cliff used in order to raise the basket of rocks? **[1]**

**Fig. 4.1**

**(c)** State the measurement needed, in addition to those in **(a)(ii)**, in order to calculate the useful power developed by the man at the top of the cliff. **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)(ii)** <u>force/mass/weight of (basket) of rocks AND height/distance of cliff</u>
>
> **(c)** to raise basket up cliff
