# Question 1 (10 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2012 · Paper 2 — Core theory, вариант 1 · вопрос 1  
Источник: [[_pdf/2012/0625_s12_qp_21.pdf#page=2|условие, стр. 2–3]] · [[_pdf/2012/0625_s12_ms_21.pdf#page=3|схема оценивания, стр. 3]] · [[_pdf/2012/0625_s12_er.pdf#page=10|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Скорость и путь автомобиля при движении по горам.

A car is travelling along a level road at a steady speed. Fig. 1.1 shows the speedometer in the car. A speedometer registers how fast the car is going.

![[0625-s12-21-p2-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 1.1**

**(a)** How far, in km, does the car travel in ½ hour at the speed shown in Fig. 1.1?

distance =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ km **[3]**

**(b)** **(i)** On the axes shown in Fig. 1.2, draw a line representing the motion of the car for the ½ hour mentioned in **(a)**. Do not go beyond ½ hour. **[3]**

![[0625-s12-21-p3-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 1.2**

**(ii)** At the end of the ½ hour, the car reaches a region where the road begins to rise up into some mountains. The car climbs the mountains for a further ½ hour.

During the climb, its speed steadily decreases to 30 km / hour. The driver then stops the car so that he can admire the view.

On Fig. 1.2, draw a line representing the climb and the stopping of the car. **[4]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** 40 (km)
>
> **(b)(i)** at 80 km/hour
>
> **(b)(i)** from 0 to 0.5 hour, no further

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | speed = distance ÷ time in any form OR (distance =) speed × time | C1 |
> |  | 80 × ½ OR 80 × 0.5 | C1 |
> |  | 40 (km) | A1 |
> | (b)(i) | <u>First section of line:</u><br>horizontal line starting at zero time, any speed | M1 |
> |  | at 80 km/hour | A1 |
> |  | from 0 to 0.5 hour, no further | A1 |
> | (b)(ii) | <u>Second section of line:</u><br><u>straight line sloping down</u> | B1 |
> |  | line starting at end of previous section and ending at 1 hour<br>(condone not straight) | B1 |
> |  | line ending at 30 km/hour | B1 |
> |  | <u>Third section of line:</u><br>vertical/near vertical line down to 0 at 1 hour<br>ignore further sections of graph \[Total: 10\] | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Most candidates were able to take the necessary reading and make the appropriate calculation. Some thought that distance = speed/time, and a few needlessly converted the time into minutes and/or distance into metres and usually got mixed up with units.
>
> **(b)** Many candidates were confused by the idea that the car was travelling at 80 km / hour for the whole of the first ½ hour. Such candidates invariably showed a line sloping up from the origin to 80 km / hour, usually at the ½ hour mark. Thereafter, graphs were often well drawn, although many failed to show the line vertically down to the axis at the 1 hour mark.
