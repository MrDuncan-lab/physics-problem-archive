> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Первое превращение энергии ядерной реакции в электростанции.

A power station uses nuclear fission to obtain energy.

In this process, nuclear energy is first changed into

**A** chemical energy.  
**B** electrical energy.  
**C** gravitational energy.  
**D** thermal (heat) energy.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Almost a fifth of candidates believed that nuclear energy in a power station is first converted into chemical energy, rather than into thermal energy.
