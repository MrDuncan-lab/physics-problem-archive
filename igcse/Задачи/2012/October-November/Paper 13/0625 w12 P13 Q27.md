Which row shows whether iron and steel are ferrous or non-ferrous?

|  | iron | steel |
|---|---|---|
| A | ferrous | ferrous |
| B | ferrous | non-ferrous |
| C | non-ferrous | ferrous |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**
