> Термоэлектронная эмиссия катода трубки.

The metal filament shown is heated by passing a current through it. This makes it hot and it

releases electrons.

![[0625-w12-13-p15-f2.svg]]

What is the name of this process of releasing electrons?

**A** convection  
**B** evaporation  
**C** radioactive emission  
**D** thermionic emission

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
