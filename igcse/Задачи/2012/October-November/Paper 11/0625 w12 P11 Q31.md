> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Управление звонком и лампой несколькими выключателями.

A student connects the circuit shown.

![[0625-w12-11-p13-f1.svg]]

Which switches must be closed for the bell to ring without lighting the lamp?

**A** 1 and 2 only  
**B** 1 and 3 only  
**C** 1, 3 and 4 only  
**D** 2, 3 and 4 only

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Just over a quarter of candidates opted for C, failing to realise that closing switch 4 would short-circuit the battery.
