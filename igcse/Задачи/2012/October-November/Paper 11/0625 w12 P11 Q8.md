**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2012 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice, вариант 1 · вопрос 8  
Источник: [[_pdf/2012/0625_w12_qp_11.pdf#page=4|условие, стр. 4]] · [[_pdf/2012/0625_w12_ms_11.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2012/0625_w12_er.pdf#page=1|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Равновесие качелей и моменты весов двух детей.

A see-saw is made by resting a long plank of wood with its centre of mass on a barrel.

A boy sits on one side of the barrel and a girl sits on the other side so that the see-saw is balanced.

![[0625-w12-11-p4-f2.svg]]

Which statement must be true?

**A** boy’s weight = girl’s weight  
**B** distance x = distance y  
**C** total downward force = total moment about the barrel  
**D** resultant force and resultant moment are both zero

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The main error here was in candidates confusing forces and moments, thus choosing C.
