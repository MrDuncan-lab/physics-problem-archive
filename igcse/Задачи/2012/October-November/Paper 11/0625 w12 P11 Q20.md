> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Передача тепла от костра к руке через металл и излучение.

A boy sits near a campfire. He pokes the fire with an iron bar. His hand becomes hot.

![[0625-w12-11-p9-f1.png]]

In which ways does thermal energy (heat) from the fire reach his hand?

**A** conduction and convection only  
**B** conduction and radiation only  
**C** convection and radiation only  
**D** conduction, convection and radiation

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Almost a third of candidates chose distractor A, failing to realise that a convection current would not transfer thermal energy sideways to the boy in the open air.
