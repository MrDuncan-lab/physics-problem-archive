> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассеяние механической энергии лыжницы при спуске.

A skier walks from the bottom of a ski slope to the top and gains 10 000 J of gravitational potential

energy.

She skis down the slope. At the bottom of the slope, her kinetic energy is 2000 J.

![[0625-w12-11-p5-f2.svg]]

How much energy was converted into thermal energy and sound energy as the skier moved down the slope?

**A** 2000 J  
**B** 8000J  
**C** 10 000J  
**D** 12 000J

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
