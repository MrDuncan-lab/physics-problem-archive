> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Most candidates correctly read the temperature. Some recorded 20.4$^{\text{o}}$C.
>
> **(b) (i)** Most candidates correctly completed the column headings with s,°C,°C.
>
> **(ii)** Candidates familiar with this type of experiment realised that the rates of cooling were about the same. Not all of these candidates, however, were able to express the idea of the rates being the same within the limits of experimental accuracy.
>
> **(c)** Most candidates could offer at least one relevant condition, usually room temperature. The initial temperature of the hot water is another important condition to control, but vague references to the temperature of water did not gain the mark.
