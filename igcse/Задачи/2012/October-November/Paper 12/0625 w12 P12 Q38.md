> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Состав ядра углерода-14.

Which statement about a carbon nucleus represented by$^{14}_{6}$ C is correct?

**A** It contains 6 neutrons.  
**B** It contains 6 electrons.  
**C** It contains 8 protons.  
**D** It contains 14 nucleons.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> More candidates in this question chose option B than the correct response D, suggesting that either they were unaware of the meaning of the term ‘nucleons’, or that they misread the question and answered as if it concerned an atom rather than a nucleus.
