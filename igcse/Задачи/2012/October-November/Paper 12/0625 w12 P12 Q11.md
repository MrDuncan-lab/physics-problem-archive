# Question 11 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2012 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice, вариант 2 · вопрос 11  
Источник: [[_pdf/2012/0625_w12_qp_12.pdf#page=6|условие, стр. 6]] · [[_pdf/2012/0625_w12_ms_12.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2012/0625_w12_er.pdf#page=4|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w12 P12]]  
Темы: [[1.5.2 Turning effect of forces]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Равновесие качелей и моменты весов двух детей.

A see-saw is made by resting a long plank of wood with its centre of mass on a barrel.

A boy sits on one side of the barrel and a girl sits on the other side so that the see-saw is balanced.

![[0625-w12-12-p6-f1.svg]]

Which statement must be true?

**A** boy’s weight = girl’s weight  
**B** distance x = distance y  
**C** total downward force = total moment about the barrel  
**D** resultant force and resultant moment are both zero

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> A sizeable number confused force and moment in this question, thus choosing distractor C.
