> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Запасённая энергия в ночном теплоаккумулирующем нагревателе.

A night storage heater contains a large block of material that is heated electrically during the night.

During the day the block cools down, releasing thermal energy into the room.

![[0625-w12-12-p9-f1.svg]]

Which thermal capacity and which night-time temperature increase will cause the most energy to be stored by the block?

![[0625-w12-12-p9-f2.svg]]

|  | thermal capacity of block | night-time temperature increase |
|---|---|---|
| A | large | large |
| B | large | small |
| C | small | large |
| D | small | small |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> As many candidates opted for B or C rather than the correct response, showing confusion over the concept of thermal capacity.
