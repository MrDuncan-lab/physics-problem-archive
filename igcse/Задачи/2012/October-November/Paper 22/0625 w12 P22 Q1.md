# Question 1 (9 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2012 · Paper 2 — Core theory, вариант 2 · вопрос 1

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Расстояние время и скорость автобуса по графику движения.

Fig. 1.1 shows the distance / time graph for a bus that goes to collect supporters from a football match and take them home to their villages.

![[0625-w12-22-p2-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 1.1**

**(a)** The bus drives from its depot, without stopping, along the straight road to the football ground.

From Fig. 1.1, find

**(i)** the distance from the bus depot to the football ground,

distance =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ km **[1]**

**(ii)** the time taken, in hours, to travel from the bus depot to the football ground,

time =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ hours **[2]**

**(iii)** the speed, in km / hour, of the bus on its journey to the football ground.

speed =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ km / hour **[3]**

**(b)** State how you can tell from Fig. 1.1 that the bus travelled at a constant speed to the football ground. **[1]**

**(c)** The bus returned to the depot by the same route, stopping a number of times to let supporters get off near to their home villages.

**(i)** At how many villages did the bus stop?  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(ii)** What was the smallest distance from a village to the football ground?

distance =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ km **[1]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)(ii)** 0.13 (hours) OR 2/15 (hours)
>
> **(a)(iii)** answer in range 45.0 – 46.2 (km/hr) NO e.c.f.

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | 6 (km) | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | 8 minutes OR 8/60 | C1 |
> |  | 0.13 (hours) OR 2/15 (hours) | A1 |
> | (a)(iii) | Speed = distance/time in any form | C1 |
> |  | distance/time correctly calculated and rounded | C1 |
> |  | answer in range 45.0 – 46.2 (km/hr) NO e.c.f. | A1 |
> | (b) | straight line graph | B1 |
> | (c)(i) | 3 or 4 | B1 |
> | (c)(ii) | 1 (km) | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Many candidates had a good understanding of speed. A significant number were unable to convert the time in minutes into a time in hours. A number of candidates failed to recognise that the straight line on the graph was an indication of the constant speed of the bus travelling to the football ground.
