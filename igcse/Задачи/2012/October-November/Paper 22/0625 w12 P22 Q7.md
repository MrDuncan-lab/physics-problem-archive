> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Области спектра и свойства электромагнитных волн.

Fig. 7.1 shows the regions of the electromagnetic spectrum. Two regions have not been named, and there is an enlargement of the visible region.

![[0625-w12-22-p10-f1.svg]]

visible ultra- X-rays

**Fig. 7.1**

**(a)** In the two boxes on Fig. 7.1, write the names of the regions they are indicating. **[2]**

**(b)** State the name of the colour of the light that is found

**(i)** at the end L of the visible spectrum,  \_\_\_\_\_\_

**(ii)** at the end M of the visible spectrum \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(c)** **(i)** A man stands in the beam of light from a spot-lamp and finds that it makes him feel warm.

Which radiation, other than visible, does this observation show that the spotlight is emitting?

**(ii)** Suggest one use to which X-rays are put. **[2]**

**(d)** State one property, apart from their electromagnetic nature, that is the same for the radiations in all the regions of the electromagnetic spectrum. **[1]**
