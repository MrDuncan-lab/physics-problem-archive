> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> (a) Most candidates were able to draw a correct symbol for a voltmeter connected in parallel with the lamp.
>
> (b) Suitable values of V, to at least one decimal place, and I, to two decimal places, were recorded by most.
>
> (c) The correct units were usually given, although it was clear that some candidates were unused to the conventions employed in tables, responding with the quantity rather than the unit, e.g. ‘voltage’ rather than ‘V’.
>
> There were few errors in the calculation of the R values, with the most common reason for loss of credit being inconsistent or excessive significant figures.
