> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Скорость автомобиля по измеренным расстоянию и времени с учётом ускорения.

Two boys, X and Y, decide to measure the speed of some of the vehicles travelling along a road. The two boys stand 405 m apart beside the road, as shown in Fig. 1.1.

![[0625-w12-23-p2-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 1.1** (not to scale)

Boy X has a stopwatch which he sets to zero. As a vehicle passes boy Y, boy Y drops his hand as a signal to boy X to start his stopwatch. Boy X then stops the stopwatch as the vehicle goes past him.

The appearance of the stopwatch is then as shown in Fig. 1.2.

![[0625-w12-23-p2-f2.png]]

**Fig. 1.2**

**(a)** How long did it take for the vehicle to travel from Y to X?

time =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ s **[1]**

**(b)** Calculate the average speed of the vehicle as it travels from Y to X.

average speed =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[4]**

**(c)** The vehicle in **(a)** and **(b)** is accelerating as it travels from Y to X.

**(i)** How does its speed at X compare with that at Y?

Tick one box.

![[0625-w12-23-p3-f1.svg]]

greater than at Y

same as that at Y

less than that at Y

**(ii)** How does its speed at X compare with the average speed calculated in **(b)**?

Tick one box.

![[0625-w12-23-p3-f2.svg]]

greater than average speed

same as average speed

less than average speed **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** 7.5

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The majority of candidates gave good responses to this question and demonstrated a good understanding of calculating speed using the equation ‘distance ÷ time’. A small number of candidates lost a mark for not including a correct unit for speed. Part **(c)** of the question was not as well answered, indicating that candidates were unsure of how acceleration altered the speed of the vehicle.
