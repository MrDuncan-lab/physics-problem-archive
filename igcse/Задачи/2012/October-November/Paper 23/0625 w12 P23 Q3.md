> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Разделение источников энергии на возобновляемые и невозобновляемые.

Here is a list of energy resources available to the world. Some of these are renewable and some are non-renewable.

In the first blank column, put a tick by any **two** resources that are renewable.

In the second blank column, put a tick by any **two** resources that are non-renewable.
