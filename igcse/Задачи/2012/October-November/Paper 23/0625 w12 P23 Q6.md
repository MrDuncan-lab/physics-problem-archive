> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Теплопроводность стекла конвекция воды и установление температуры кипения.

Some water in a glass beaker is heated from below, as shown in Fig. 6.1.

![[0625-w12-23-p10-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 6.1**

**(a)** Name the process by which thermal energy is transferred

**(i)** through the glass,  \_\_\_\_\_\_

**(ii)** throughout the water \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(b)** As thermal energy is supplied, the temperature of the water begins to rise. Although the supply of energy remains constant, eventually the temperature becomes steady at about 80°C.

Suggest why this happens. **[1]**

**(c)** The rate of energy supply is increased. The temperature of the water begins to rise again, but eventually becomes steady at a higher temperature. This time many bubbles are seen throughout the water.

**(i)** State what is now happening to the water. **[1]**

**(ii)** What gas do the bubbles contain? Tick **one** box.

![[0625-w12-23-p10-f2.svg]]

air

hydrogen

oxygen

steam **[1]**

>
> boiling at 80$^{\text{o}}$C.

> **(c)** There were many correct responses to the first part of this question, but a common misconception was that the water was evaporating. About half of the candidates responded correctly to the second part of the question with incorrect responses being chosen in equal measure.
