> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Зарядка конденсатора через резистор в цепи с задержкой.

Fig. 9.1 shows a time-delay circuit that includes a capacitor C and a resistor of very high resistance.

![[0625-w12-21-p13-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 9.1**

**(a)** On Fig. 9.1, use the letter S to label the switch. **[1]**

**(b)** When the switch is open, the voltmeter in the circuit registers zero.

After the switch has been closed, what happens, if anything, to

**(i)** the charge in the circuit, **[1]**

**(ii)** the reading on the voltmeter? **[2]**

**(c)** The switch is now opened again.

State what happens, if anything, to the reading on the voltmeter. **[1]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)(ii)** condone greater than zero any indication of gradual increase

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | switch correctly identified | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | moves/flows condone (current) flows OR stays the same<br>ignore nothing (happens) | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | increases/higher/greater | M1 |
> |  | condone greater than zero<br>any indication of gradual increase | A1 |
> | (c) | <u>remains the same OR decreases/goes back to zero (very) slowly i.e. ignore</u><br>decreases/getting smaller on their own. | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** The majority of candidates scored this mark, but a significant number of candidates failed to label the diagram at all.
>
> **(b) (i)** Many candidates scored this mark, but a significant number thought that the charge in the circuit would increase.
>
> **(ii)** Most candidates scored one mark, but only the best candidates realised that the voltage would increase slowly.
>
> **(c)** Only better candidates recalled that a capacitor stores charge, and that its voltage would only decrease slowly.
