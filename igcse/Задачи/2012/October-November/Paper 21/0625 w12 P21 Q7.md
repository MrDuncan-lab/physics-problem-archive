> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Компас в магнитном поле Земли, магнита и проводника с током.

Fig. 7.1 shows a compass needle that has come to rest in the Earth’s magnetic field.

![[0625-w12-21-p10-f1.png]]

**Fig. 7.1**

**(a)** On Fig. 7.1, draw an arrow pointing towards the north pole of the Earth. **[1]**

**(b)** The S pole of a bar magnet is brought towards the S pole of the compass needle, as shown in Fig. 7.2.

![[0625-w12-21-p10-f2.svg]]

![[0625-w12-21-p10-f3.png]]

**Fig. 7.2**

Describe what is seen happening to the compass needle as this is done. **[2]**

**(c)** The magnet in **(b)** is removed and a horizontal wire is positioned above the compass needle, as shown in Fig. 7.3.

wire

![[0625-w12-21-p11-f1.png]]

**Fig. 7.3**

When there is a current in the wire, the compass needle rotates through a small angle.

Suggest why this rotation occurs. **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(c)** caused by current

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | arrow pointing to left | B1 |
> | (b) | rotates/turns/S pole goes away from magnet/repelled/ changes direction | B1 |
> |  | N pole points to magnet/S Pole points to N Pole (of Earth)/turns through 180°<br>S Pole/N Pole points in opposite direction | B1 |
> | (c) | magnetic field/electromagnet(ism)/(ic) | M1 |
> |  | caused by current | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Most candidates scored the mark for this question. However, many weaker candidates misread the question and drew an arrow pointing to the north-seeking pole of the compass.
>
> **(b)** Most candidates scored both marks, but a significant number only scored one mark by merely stating that the two poles would repel each other.
>
> **(c)** Only better candidates recalled that a current flowing in a wire would produce a magnetic field.
