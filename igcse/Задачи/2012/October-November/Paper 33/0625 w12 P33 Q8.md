> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Токи и мощность нагревателя при переключении параллельных резисторов.

An electric heater is connected to a 230 V mains supply. The heater circuit includes two resistors R$_{1}$ and R$_{2}$, and two switches S$_{1}$ and S$_{2}$. Fig. 8.1 is the circuit diagram.

![[0625-w12-33-p9-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 8.1**

The resistance of R$_{1}$ is 46 Ω and the resistance of R$_{2}$ is also 46 Ω.

Switch S$_{1}$ is closed and switch S$_{2}$ remains open.

**(a)** Calculate

**(i)** the current from the mains supply,

current =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(ii)** the power dissipated in the heater.

power =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(b)** Switch S$_{2}$ is now closed.

State the current in R$_{2}$.

current =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)(i)** 5.0 A \*Unit penalty applies
>
> **(a)(ii)** 1100/1150/1200 W \*Unit penalty applies ecf from 8(a)(i)
