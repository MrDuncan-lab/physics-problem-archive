The isotope thorium-234 is radioactive. It emits β-particles as it decays.

**(a)** The incomplete nuclide equation represents the decay of thorium-234 to an isotope of protactinium (Pa).

Complete the equation.

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Although some candidates scored full marks here, there were two common reasons for not doing so. Some candidates used the numbers 4 and 2 for the β-particle and gave the numbers that the correctly balanced equation for α-emission would have produced. More commonly, candidates who correctly used the numbers 0 and –1 for the β-particle, gave the proton number 89 for the protactinium. This suggests that the subtraction of a negative number presents an arithmetical difficulty for some candidates.
>
> **(b) (i)** Most candidates drew an acceptable curve that showed the correct direction of deviation. Only a minority drew the curve downwards or drew a straight line. A small number of candidates drew in the electric field lines instead.
>
> **(ii)** Most candidates stated that the negative β-particle was attracted to the positive plate or repelled by the negative one in some way.
