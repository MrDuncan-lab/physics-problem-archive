> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Измерение токов и напряжений двух ламп в цепи.

In this experiment, you will investigate the potential differences across circuit components. The circuit is set up for you.

**(a)** Draw a circuit diagram of the circuit set up for you, using standard symbols. **[3]**

**(b)** **(i)** Switch on. Measure and record the current *I*$_{\text{A}}$, and the potential difference *V*$_{\text{L}}$ across lamp **L**. Switch off.

*I*$_{\text{A}}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

*V*$_{\text{L}}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

**(ii)** Disconnect the voltmeter and reconnect it across lamp **M**. Switch on.

Measure and record the potential difference *V*$_{\text{M}}$ across lamp **M**. Switch off.

*V*$_{\text{M}}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

**(iii)** Calculate the potential difference across both lamps using the equation *V*$_{\text{A}}$ = *V*$_{\text{L}}$ + *V*$_{\text{M}}$.

*V*$_{\text{A}}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

*V*

**(iv)** Calculate the combined resistance *R*$_{\text{A}}$ of the three lamps using the equation *R*$_{\text{A}}$ = $\dfrac{A}{I}$ .

A

*R*$_{\text{A}}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

**(c)** Rearrange the circuit so that the three lamps are in **series** with each other.

**(i)** Switch on. By connecting the voltmeter suitably each time, measure and record the potential difference across each lamp in turn. Switch off.

*V*$_{\text{L}}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

*V*$_{\text{M}}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

*V*$_{\text{N}}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

**(ii)** Calculate the potential difference *V*$_{\text{B}}$ across the three lamps using the equation *V*$_{\text{B}}$ = *V*$_{\text{L}}$ + *V*$_{\text{M}}$ + *V*$_{\text{N}}$.

*V*$_{\text{B}}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(d)** A student suggests that *V*$_{\text{A}}$ should be equal to *V*$_{\text{B}}$.

State whether your results support this suggestion and justify your answer with reference to the results.

statement  \_\_\_\_\_\_

justification  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Most candidates knew the correct circuit symbols. Only a few drew a picture of the circuit rather than a circuit diagram. Many candidates correctly positioned the voltmeter, but a significant number lost a mark by drawing an ammeter with three terminals.
>
> **(b) (c)** Many candidates successfully recorded all the values to appropriate numbers of decimal places and with the correct units.
>
> **(d)** Here the statement had to match the candidate’s values of *V*$_{\text{A}}$and *V*$_{\text{B}}$ and the justification follow logically, expressing the idea that the values were the same, within the limits of experimental accuracy, or that they were not because they were beyond the limits of experimental accuracy. Many candidates lost this mark because they attempted a theoretical explanation of series and parallel circuits.
