> Сравнить числа альфа-частиц, рассеянных металлической фольгой под разными углами.

The diagram shows α-particles being scattered by a very thin metal foil.

foil

![[1524-j21-22-p16-f2.svg]]

![[1524-j21-22-p16-f3.svg]]

P, Q and R represent three directions in which α-particles are scattered. The number of α-particles scattered in each direction is *N*$_{\text{P}}$, *N*$_{\text{Q}}$ and *N*$_{\text{R}}$.

Which sequence correctly shows the number of α-particles from lowest to highest being scattered in each direction?

**A** *N*$_{\text{P}}$, *N*$_{\text{Q}}$, *N*$_{\text{R}}$  
**B** *N*$_{\text{P}}$, *N*$_{\text{R}}$, *N*$_{\text{Q}}$  
**C** *N*$_{\text{Q}}$, *N*$_{\text{R}}$, *N*$_{\text{P}}$  
**D** *N*$_{\text{R}}$, *N*$_{\text{P}}$, *N*$_{\text{Q}}$

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
