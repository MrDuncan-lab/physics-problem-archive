> Определить радиус проволоки по показанию микрометра с поправкой на нулевую погрешность.

A micrometer screw gauge is used to determine the radius of a copper wire.

The reading on the screw gauge when measuring the diameter of the wire is 3.12 mm.

The reading on the screw gauge with no wire (zero error) is 0.04 mm.

What is the radius of the wire?

**A** 1.52 mm  
**B** 1.54 mm  
**C** 1.56 mm  
**D** 1.58 mm

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
