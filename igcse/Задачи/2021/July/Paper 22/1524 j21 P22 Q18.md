> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Предсказать тепловое расширение закреплённого металлического моста.

The diagram shows a metal bridge. The ends of the bridge are fixed to the sides of a valley. The

air temperature is much less at night than it is during the day.

The solid line shows the bridge during the coldest part of the night.

Which dashed line shows the bridge at the hottest part of the day?

![[1524-j21-22-p9-f2.svg]]

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The solid line shows the position of the bridge at the coldest part of the night, so as the temperature rose the bridge would expand. At no point would it contract and therefore it could not go through positions **B** or **C** to be at position **D**, leaving position **A** as the only alternative.
