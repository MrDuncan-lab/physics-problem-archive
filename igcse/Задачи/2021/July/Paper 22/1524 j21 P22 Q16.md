> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить, при каких температурах и в какой части жидкости происходит испарение.

A student writes four statements about evaporation from a liquid.

1 Evaporation happens at any temperature.

2 Evaporation happens at one fixed temperature only.

3 Evaporation happens at the surface of the liquid only.

4 Evaporation happens throughout the liquid.

Which statements are correct?

**A** 1 and 3  
**B** 1 and 4  
**C** 2 and 3  
**D** 2 and 4

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**
