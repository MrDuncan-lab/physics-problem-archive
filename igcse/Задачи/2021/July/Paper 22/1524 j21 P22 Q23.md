> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать критический угол по скорости света в материале призмы.

A prism is made from transparent plastic. In this plastic, light travels at 0.80 *c*, where *c* is the speed of light in air. Light enters one face of the prism at right angles, as shown.

![[1524-j21-22-p11-f2.svg]]

NOT TO SCALE

The light just escapes from the sloping face of the prism.

What is angle θ?

**A** 37°  
**B** 40°  
**C** 50°  
**D** 53°

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Stronger candidates managed the two steps in the calculation. First, they used the speed of light in a vacuum and the speed in the plastic prism to calculate the refractive index (*n*) of the material of the prism. They then used *n* = 1 / sin *c* to find *c*, the critical angle.
