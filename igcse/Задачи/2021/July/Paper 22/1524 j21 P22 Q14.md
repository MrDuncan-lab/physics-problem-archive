> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить реакцию ртутного барометра на снижение атмосферного давления.

The diagram shows a simple mercury barometer used to measure atmospheric pressure.

![[1524-j21-22-p8-f1.svg]]

Atmospheric pressure decreases.

Which row states what happens to the pressure at point P and what happens to the level L of mercury?

|  | pressure at P | level L |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | decreases | falls |
| **B** | decreases | rises |
| **C** | stays the same | falls |
| **D** | stays the same | rises |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Significantly more candidates thought that the length of section P was a measure of atmospheric pressure than the length of the mercury column.
