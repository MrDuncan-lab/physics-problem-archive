> Применить сохранение импульса для определения скорости вагона до неупругого столкновения.

Two railway wagons are on the same straight horizontal track. One wagon is moving with initial

velocity *u* and the other wagon is stationary.

The moving wagon collides with the stationary wagon.

The two wagons connect to each other and move with a velocity of 5.80 m / s.

before after

![[1524-j21-22-p6-f2.svg]]

mass = 8000 kg mass = 12 000 kg

What is the initial velocity *u* of the moving wagon before they collide?

**A** 3.87 m / s  
**B** 8.70 m / s  
**C** 9.67 m / s  
**D** 14.5 m / s

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
