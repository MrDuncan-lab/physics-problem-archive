> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбрать размеры проволоки, обеспечивающие заданное изменение сопротивления.

A piece of wire is 40 cm long and has a diameter of 2.0 mm.

The resistance of the piece of wire is 0.30 Ω.

Which wire of the same material has a resistance of 0.15 Ω?

|  | length / cm | diameter / mm |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | 20 | 1.0 |
| **B** | 20 | 4.0 |
| **C** | 80 | 1.0 |
| **D** | 80 | 4.0 |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Only stronger candidates recognised that the resistance of a length of wire is inversely proportional to its cross-sectional area and therefore inversely proportional to it diameter squared. Thus, doubling the diameter reduces the resistance by a factor of four.
