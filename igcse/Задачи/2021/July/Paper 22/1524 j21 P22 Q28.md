> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить возможный заряд стержня по притяжению отрицательно заряженного изолятора.

A negatively charged insulating rod P is suspended from an insulating thread.

A second insulating rod Q is brought close to rod P.

Rod P swings towards rod Q.

What can be deduced from this experiment?

**A** Rod Q is either negatively charged or is uncharged.  
**B** Rod Q is either positively charged or is uncharged.  
**C** Rod Q is negatively charged.  
**D** Rod Q is positively charged.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Only stronger candidates recognised that an uncharged object is attracted to both positively and negatively charged objects.
