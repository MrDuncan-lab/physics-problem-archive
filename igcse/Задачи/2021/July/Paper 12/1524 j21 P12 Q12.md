> Сравнить давление воды на дно сосудов по глубине жидкости.

The diagram shows three vessels, P, Q and R. Each vessel contains water. The water exerts a

pressure at the bottom of each vessel.

The three vessels are shown to the same scale.

![[1524-j21-12-p6-f2.svg]]

Which statement about the pressure at the bottom of the vessels is correct?

**A** The pressure is greatest at the bottom of P.  
**B** The pressure is greatest at the bottom of Q.  
**C** The pressure is greatest at the bottom of R.  
**D** The pressure is the same at the bottom of each vessel.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
