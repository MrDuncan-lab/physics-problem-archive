> Выбрать теплопроводный материал дна кастрюли и теплоизолирующий материал ручки.

A chef tests different types of saucepans. She wants the material that is the best conductor for

the bottom of the pan and the best insulator for the handle.

Which materials should she use?

|  | bottom of the pan | handle |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | metal | metal |
| **B** | metal | wood |
| **C** | plastic | wood |
| **D** | wood | wood |
