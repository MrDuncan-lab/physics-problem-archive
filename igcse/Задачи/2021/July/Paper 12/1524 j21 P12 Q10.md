> Определить исходный запас энергии, используемый гидроэлектростанцией.

What is the source of the electrical energy produced by a hydroelectric power station?

**A** chemical energy stored in fossil fuel  
**B** gravitational potential energy stored behind a dam  
**C** geothermal energy from the Earth’s crust  
**D** nuclear energy stored in uranium

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
