> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить общую характеристику изотопов одного элемента.

Which statement about different isotopes of a certain element **must** be correct?

**A** They have a different number of electrons.  
**B** They have the same number of neutrons.  
**C** They have the same number of nucleons.  
**D** They have the same number of protons.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
