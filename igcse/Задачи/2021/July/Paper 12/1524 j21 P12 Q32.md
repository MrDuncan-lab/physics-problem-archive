![[1524-j21-12-p14-f1.svg]]

A light-dependent resistor (LDR) and a thermistor are each connected across XY in turn and changes to the environment are made.

Which row shows the changes to the environment that cause the total resistance in the circuit to decrease?

|  | thermistor connected across XY | light-dependent resistor connected across XY |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | increase light level | increase the temperature |
| **B** | increase the temperature | increase the light level |
| **C** | decrease the light level | decrease the temperature |
| **D** | decrease the temperature | decrease the light level |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
