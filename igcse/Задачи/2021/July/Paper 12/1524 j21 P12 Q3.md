> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать среднюю скорость самолёта в воздухе, исключив время остановки.

An aeroplane flies from town X to town Z, stopping for 1 hour at town Y to pick up more passengers. The distances between the towns are shown.

![[1524-j21-12-p2-f2.svg]]

The total time taken between leaving X and arriving at Z is 3 hours.

What is the average speed of the aeroplane **in the air**?

**A** $\dfrac{500}{4}$ km / h  
**B** $\dfrac{500}{3}$ km / h  
**C** $\dfrac{500}{2}$ km / h  
**D** $\dfrac{500}{1}$ km / h

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Some candidates did not read the question carefully enough and simply took the total time and divided that into the total distance travelled. Candidates should be careful to pay close attention to words in bold in the instructions. In this question, the emboldening told them to calculate the average speed when the aeroplane was in the air.
