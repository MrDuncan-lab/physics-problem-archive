Темы: [[4.3.2 Series and parallel circuits]], [[4.2.4 Resistance]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать ток в последовательной цепи из двух резисторов.

A 4.0 Ω resistor and a 2.0 Ω resistor are connected to a 12 V battery in a circuit.

![[1524-j21-12-p13-f2.svg]]

What is the current in the 2.0 Ω resistor?

**A** 0.50 A  
**B** 2.0 A  
**C** 3.0 A  
**D** 6.0 A

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Most candidates simply divided the e.m.f. of the cell by the quoted value of the 2.0 Ω resistor, not understanding that the current in a series circuit is determined by the total resistance in the circuit (in this case 4.0 + 2.0 = 6.0 Ω).
