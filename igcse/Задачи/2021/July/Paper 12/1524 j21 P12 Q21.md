> Сравнить амплитуды и частоты волн по изображениям при одинаковой скорости распространения.

The diagrams show two water waves, P and Q, that are travelling at the same speed on the

surface of a pond. The diagrams are drawn to the same scale.

![[1524-j21-12-p9-f2.svg]]

Which wave has the greater amplitude and which wave has the greater frequency?

|  | greater amplitude | greater frequency |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | P | P |
| **B** | P | Q |
| **C** | Q | P |
| **D** | Q | Q |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
