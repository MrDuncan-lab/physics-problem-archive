> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объяснить назначение предохранителя как защиты проводки от перегрева.

Why is the electricity supply in a house fitted with a fuse?

**A** to increase the power supplied to the circuits  
**B** to increase the resistance in the circuits  
**C** to prevent overheating of cables in the circuits  
**D** to prevent short circuits

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> There was a lot of misunderstanding of the use of a fuse in a household circuit with only stronger candidates answering this correctly.
