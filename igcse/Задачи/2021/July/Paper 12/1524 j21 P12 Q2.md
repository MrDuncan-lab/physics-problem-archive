> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить пройденное расстояние по площади под графиком скорости.

A student draws a speed–time graph for a car.

![[1524-j21-12-p2-f1.svg]]

What is the total distance travelled by the car?

**A** 200 m  
**B** 400 m  
**C** 600 m  
**D** 800 m

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Most candidates found this question challenging. Many candidates did not understand that the distance travelled is equal to the area under the graph. Some candidates either multiplied the initial or the final speed by the time elapsed.
