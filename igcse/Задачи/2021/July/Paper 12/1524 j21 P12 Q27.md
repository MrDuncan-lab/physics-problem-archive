> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить знаки зарядов и характер взаимодействия стержня и ткани после трения.

A plastic rod is rubbed with a cotton cloth. This causes the rod and the cloth to become charged.

These charges cause a force between the rod and the cloth.

Which row compares the charges on the rod and the cloth, and describes the effect of the force between the rod and the cloth?

|  | charges on rod and cloth | effect of the force on rod and cloth |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | opposite | they attract |
| **B** | opposite | they repel |
| **C** | the same | they attract |
| **D** | the same | they repel |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**
