> Качественно сравнить токи в ветвях и общей части цепи.

The diagram shows a circuit containing a power supply, a lamp and a motor. Three ammeters

measure the current at different points in the circuit. The readings on the ammeters are *I*$_{1}$, *I*$_{2}$ and *I*$_{3}$.

![[0625-m21-12-p13-f1.svg]]

Which statement about the ammeter readings is correct?

**A** All three readings are the same.  
**B** *I*$_{1}$ is greater than *I*$_{2}$ and is greater than *I*$_{3}$.  
**C** *I*$_{2}$ is greater than *I*$_{1}$ and is greater than *I*$_{3}$.  
**D** *I*$_{3}$ is greater than *I*$_{1}$ and is greater than *I*$_{2}$.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
