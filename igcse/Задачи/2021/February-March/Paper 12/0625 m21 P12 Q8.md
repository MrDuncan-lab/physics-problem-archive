> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить источник энергии для получения пара на электростанции.

Which energy source boils water to make steam in power stations?

**A** energy from tides  
**B** energy from waves  
**C** hydroelectric energy  
**D** nuclear energy

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Some candidates believed that hydroelectric power involves the heating of water to produce high pressure steam. This fundamental error showed a lack of understanding of the generation of electricity.
