# Question 29 (1 mark)

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить знаки зарядов по изменению показаний весов при взаимодействии палочек.

A charged rod X is placed on a balance and another rod Y is brought close to it, as shown.

![[0625-m21-12-p11-f1.svg]]

Which combination of charges would cause the change in the balance reading shown?

|  | X | Y |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | negative charge | negative charge |
| **B** | negative charge | positive charge |
| **C** | negative charge | no charge |
| **D** | positive charge | no charge |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Only stronger candidates answered this correctly. The increase in the reading on the balance indicated that the upper rod pushes down on the lower rod, meaning they repel, which in turn means that either they both carry positive charge or they both carry negative charge.
