> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнить движение и расстояния двух ракет по графикам скорости.

Two rockets are launched at the same time from the surface of the Earth. The graph shows how the speeds of the rockets change with time.

![[0625-m21-12-p2-f1.svg]]

Which statement about the rockets is correct?

**A** Both rockets travel the same distance.  
**B** Rocket P accelerates and then decelerates.  
**C** Rocket P travels further than rocket Q.  
**D** Rocket Q has zero acceleration.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Many candidates had difficulty in interpreting the graphs and worked as though the vertical axis showed the distance that each rocket had travelled.
