# Question 18 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · February/March 2021 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 18  
Источник: [[_pdf/2021/0625_m21_qp_12.pdf#page=8|условие, стр. 8]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_m21_ms_12.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_m21_er.pdf#page=1|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

Темы: [[2.2.1 Thermal expansion of solids, liquids and gases]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить изменение длины стального стержня, используемое для измерения температуры.

The diagram shows a simple type of thermometer.

![[0625-m21-12-p8-f1.svg]]

The roller rests on a horizontal surface and the steel bar rests on top of the roller.

When the temperature changes, the pointer rotates.

Which physical property is being used to measure temperature?

**A** the length of the pointer  
**B** the length of the roller  
**C** the length of the steel bar  
**D** the thickness of the roller

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Candidates did not seem to be familiar with this type of experiment. Many thought that the variable being used to measure the temperature was the length of the pointer.
