> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить период полураспада по таблице скорости счёта с поправкой на фон.

A laboratory worker measures the count rate from a radioactive source. He records his results in

a table.

| time | count rate |
|---|---|
| minutes | counts/s |
| 0 | 100 |
| 1.0 | 73 |
| 2.0 | 54 |
| 3.0 | 41 |
| 4.0 | 31 |

The average background radiation in the laboratory is 8 counts per second.

What is the half-life of the source?

**A** 1.5 minutes  
**B** 2.0 minutes  
**C** 3.0 minutes  
**D** 4.0 minutes

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
