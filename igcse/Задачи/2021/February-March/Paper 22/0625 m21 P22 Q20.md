# Question 20 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · February/March 2021 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 2 · вопрос 20  
Источник: [[_pdf/2021/0625_m21_qp_22.pdf#page=8|условие, стр. 8]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_m21_ms_22.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_m21_er.pdf#page=3|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 m21 P22]]  
Темы: [[3.4 Sound]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Оценить длину звуковой волны на верхней границе человеческого слуха.

What is the approximate wavelength in air of the highest frequency sound that can be heard by a

normal healthy person?

**A** 0.02 m  
**B** 60 m  
**C** 20 000 m  
**D** 7 000 000 m

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Although the question was usually answered well with the majority of candidates identifying the correct wavelength, a significant number of candidates did not recognise that the wavelength was being asked for, not the more usual frequency.
