> Выбрать изменения длины и сечения провода, уменьшающие его сопротивление.

Which two changes to a metal wire both decrease its resistance?

|  | length of wire | cross-sectional area of wire |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | decrease | decrease |
| **B** | decrease | increase |
| **C** | increase | decrease |
| **D** | increase | increase |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
