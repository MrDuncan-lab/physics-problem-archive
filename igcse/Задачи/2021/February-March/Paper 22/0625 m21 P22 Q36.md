> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить изменение показаний вольтметра при быстром намагничивании соседнего электромагнита.

The diagram shows an electromagnet near a coil of wire connected to a voltmeter. The reading

on the voltmeter is zero.

![[0625-m21-22-p14-f2.svg]]

The switch is closed. The electromagnet magnetises quickly.

What happens to the reading on the voltmeter?

**A** It keeps increasing.  
**B** It quickly increases and stays at maximum.  
**C** It quickly increases and then decreases.  
**D** It stays on zero.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Electromagnetic induction is a very challenging topic but stronger candidates were able to answer the question correctly. Other candidates found it very difficult to understand that induction of an e.m.f. occurs when a magnetic field is actually changing.
