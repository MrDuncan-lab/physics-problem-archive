> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить направление ускорения при равномерном движении по окружности.

An object moves at constant speed in the circular path shown.

![[0625-m21-22-p3-f1.svg]]

Which statement about the acceleration of the object when it is at point P is correct?

**A** The acceleration is in the direction of arrow X.  
**B** The acceleration is in the direction of arrow Y.  
**C** The acceleration is in the direction of arrow Z.  
**D** The object is not accelerating.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Although almost all candidates ruled out the centrifugal direction, the fact that the force on the rotating object is towards the centre of the circle was often not recognised.
