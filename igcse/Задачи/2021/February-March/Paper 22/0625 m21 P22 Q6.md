> Рассчитать силу по массе тела и изменению скорости за заданное время.

An object of mass 0.80 kg is moving in a straight line at a velocity of 2.0 m / s. A force is exerted on the object, in the direction of motion, for a period of 1.0 minute and the velocity of the object increases to 6.0 m / s.

What force is exerted on the object?

**A** 0.053 N  
**B** 0.080 N  
**C** 3.2 N  
**D** 4.8 N

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**
