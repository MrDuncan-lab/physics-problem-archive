# Question 3 (7 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · February/March 2021 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 3  
Источник: [[_pdf/2021/0625_m21_qp_32.pdf#page=5|условие, стр. 5]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_m21_ms_32.pdf#page=6|схема оценивания, стр. 6]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_m21_er.pdf#page=5|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать момент заданной силы и неизвестную силу из равновесия балки.

Fig. 3.1 shows the forces acting on a uniform balanced beam. The beam is pivoted at its centre.

![[0625-m21-32-p5-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 3.1**

**(a)** Calculate the moment of the 5.2 N force about the pivot and show that its value is close to 30 Ncm. **[3]**

**(b)** The beam is balanced. Calculate force *P*.

force *P* =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ N **[4]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** 7.5 (N)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | (moment of force =) force × (perpendicular) distance of force from pivot | B1 |
> |  | 5.2 × 6.0 | B1 |
> |  | 31.2 | B1 |
> | (b) | (sum of) clockwise moment(s) = (sum of) anticlockwise moment(s) | C1 |
> |  | P × 2.0 + 8.1 × 2.0 = 5.2 × 6.0 OR 31.2 OR answer from (a) | C1 |
> |  | P = (31.2 – 16.2) ÷ 2.0 OR 15 ÷ 2.0 | C1 |
> |  | 7.5 (N) | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** The vast majority of candidates were able to calculate the moment of the 5.2N force as 31.2N m. A common error was to multiply the force by 30 instead of the correct distance of 6.0cm.
>
> **(b)** Only stronger candidates gained full credit for this question. Weaker candidates failed to use the principle of moments and unsuccessfully attempted to balance vertical forces. A common error was to identify the clockwise and anti-clockwise moments incorrectly.
