> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Most candidates gained at least partial credit for this question. The most common errors were in identifying the turbine as a fan and/or the generator as a motor.
>
> **(b)** The vast majority of candidates were able to identify two other sources of renewable energy. Weaker candidates only gave one example or gave examples of fossil fuels.
>
> **(c)** Most candidates gave answers of environmental advantages as “eco-friendly”, “environmentally friendly”, “non-polluting”, etc. These answers were insufficient as they did not identify advantages of the geothermal power station. Candidates needed to be more precise and stronger candidates gave examples such as “reducing acid rain” or “emitting less carbon dioxide”.
