# Question 7 (8 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · February/March 2021 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 7  
Источник: [[_pdf/2021/0625_m21_qp_32.pdf#page=9|условие, стр. 9]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_m21_ms_32.pdf#page=8|схема оценивания, стр. 8]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_m21_er.pdf#page=5|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 m21 P32]]  
Темы: [[3.1 General properties of waves]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить характеристики волны, рассчитать частоту и сравнить поперечные и продольные колебания.

Fig. 7.1 shows a transverse wave.

![[0625-m21-32-p9-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 7.1**

**(a)** Give the label letter **P**, **Q**, **R**, **S or T** for the arrow which represents:

1. the amplitude of the wave  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

2. the wavelength of the wave \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(b)** A student stands next to a pond and observes water waves on its surface. She counts 12 complete waves passing a point in the pond in a time of 8.0 s.

Calculate the frequency of the water waves.

frequency =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ Hz **[3]**

**(c)** Fig. 7.1 shows a transverse wave. Describe the difference between transverse and longitudinal waves.

You may draw a labelled diagram. **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** 1.5 (Hz)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | 1 (amplitude of wave = arrow) **R** | B1 |
> |  | 2 ( wavelength of wave = arrow) **S** | B1 |
> | (b) | (frequency =) number of (complete) waves per second | C1 |
> |  | (frequency =) 12 ÷ 8 | C1 |
> |  | 1.5 (Hz) | A1 |
> | (c) | vibration(s) OR oscillation(s) | B1 |
> |  | in transverse waves is / are perpendicular / at right angles to the direction of energy transfer / wave travel | B1 |
> |  | in longitudinal waves is / are in same direction OR parallel to the direction of energy transfer / wave travel | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Most candidates answered this well. The most common errors were to give the amplitude of the wave as P and the wavelength as T.
>
> **(b)** This question was answered well by the vast majority of candidates.
>
> **(c)** Only the strongest candidates scored full credit for this question. The most common error was not stating that the oscillations of the transverse wave were at right angles to the direction of propagation of the wave. Many candidates gave vague statements such as “the wave is at right angles to the wave”.
