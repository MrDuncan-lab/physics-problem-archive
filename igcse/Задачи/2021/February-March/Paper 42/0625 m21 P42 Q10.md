# Question 10 (8 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · February/March 2021 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 2 · вопрос 10  
Источник: [[_pdf/2021/0625_m21_qp_42.pdf#page=14|условие, стр. 14]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_m21_ms_42.pdf#page=10|схема оценивания, стр. 10]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_m21_er.pdf#page=9|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

Темы: [[5.2.2 The three types of nuclear emission]], [[5.2.3 Radioactive decay]], [[5.2.4 Half-life]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Указать свойства альфа-частицы, записать уравнение бета-распада и восстановить исходную массу радиоактивного образца.

**(a)** State the proton number, nucleon number and the value of the charge on an α-particle.

proton number  \_\_\_\_\_\_

nucleon number  \_\_\_\_\_\_

charge  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

**(b)** A nucleus of strontium-90 consists of 38 protons and 52 neutrons. Strontium-90 is radioactive and decays by β-emission to an isotope of yttrium. The symbol for strontium is Sr and the symbol for yttrium is Y. Write down the nuclide equation of this decay. **[3]**

**(c)** The half-life of radon-220 is 56 s. A sample of radon-220 is in a container. After 112 s the mass of radon-220 is 9.2 mg.

Calculate the mass of the original sample.

mass =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(c)** (original mass = 4 / 9.2 =) 37 mg

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | 2 | B1 |
> |  | 4 | B1 |
> |  | +2 | B1 |
> | (b) | ${}^{90}_{38}\text{Sr} \rightarrow {}^{90}_{39}\text{Y} + {}^{0}_{-1}\beta$ |  |
> |  | nucleon numbers 90 on both sides of equation | B1 |
> |  | Sr and proton number 38 on left AND Y and proton number 39 on right | B1 |
> |  | ${}^{0}_{-1}\beta$ (to right of arrow) | B1 |
> | (c) | (original mass = 4 / 9.2 =) 37 mg | A2 |
> |  | 2 half-lives | C1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** There were many fully correct answers to this question. The most common mistake was to omit the magnitude for the charge on an alpha-particle, recording just the + sign for the charge.
>
> **(b)** Stronger candidates successfully worked out the nucleon numbers for Sr, Y and the beta-particle. Common mistakes included using the neutron number as the nucleon number, omitting the nucleon number for beta and repetition of the Sr symbol on both sides of the equation. A few weaker candidates wrote an equation for alpha-particle decay.
>
> **(c)** Stronger candidates identified that two half-lives had elapsed in 112s and retained the unit of mass given in the question to give a final answer of 37mg. There was no requirement to convert to g or kg and candidates who did a conversion often ended up with an error in the final answer. Weaker candidates sometimes correctly stated that 112 was twice 56 but did not realise that as two half-lives had passed, the original mass of the sample would have been four times that of the mass after 112s. A common incorrect answer was 18.4mg.
