# Question 8 (8 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · February/March 2021 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 2 · вопрос 8  
Источник: [[_pdf/2021/0625_m21_qp_42.pdf#page=12|условие, стр. 12]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_m21_ms_42.pdf#page=9|схема оценивания, стр. 9]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_m21_er.pdf#page=9|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

Темы: [[4.2.3 Electromotive force and potential difference]], [[4.2.5 Electrical energy and electrical power]], [[4.3.2 Series and parallel circuits]], [[4.2.4 Resistance]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить ЭДС и рассчитать мощность нагревателя, распределение напряжений и токи цепи.

**(a)** Define electromotive force (e.m.f.). **[1]**

**(b)** Fig. 8.1 shows a source E of e.m.f. 60 V in a circuit.

![[0625-m21-42-p12-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 8.1**

The heater H has a resistance of 22.5 Ω and the potential difference (p.d.) across it is 45 V.

Calculate:

**(i)** the power of the heater

power =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

**(ii)** the p.d. across resistor X

p.d. =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(iii)** the current in the 10 Ω resistor.

current =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)(i)** (*P*=) 90 W
>
> **(b)(ii)** (p.d. =) 15 V
>
> **(b)(iii)** (*I* = 15 / 10 =) 1.5 A

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | <u>energy supplied by a source in driving charge around a complete circuit / energy needed to drive unit charge / 1 coulomb</u><br>round circuit | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | (*P*=) 90 W | A3 |
> |  | (*P*=) *V I* in any form | C1 |
> |  | (*V* / *R* OR *I* =) 2 | C1 |
> | (b)(ii) | (p.d. =) 15 V | A2 |
> |  | (p.d. =) 60 – 45 | C1 |
> | (b)(iii) | (*I* = 15 / 10 =) 1.5 A | A2 |
> |  | (*I* =) *V* / *R* OR *V* = *IR* in any form | C1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** A full answer to this question required candidates to refer to the energy needed to move unit charge around a circuit. Common misconceptions included the idea that e.m.f. is a force or that e.m.f. is the open circuit reading of p.d.
>
> **(b) (i)** This question was generally answered well. A common mistake was to use an incorrect equation, e.g. P = V / R.
>
> **(ii)** In this question candidates needed to realise that the e.m.f. is the sum of the values of p.d. across components in series and that the p.d. across components in parallel is the same. The correct value for the p.d. across X is 15 V.
>
> **(iii)** This question was well answered with most candidates correctly using V = IR and supplying the
>
> correct unit to gain full credit.
