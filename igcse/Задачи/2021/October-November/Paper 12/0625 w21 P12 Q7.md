> Сравнить работу перемещения ящиков по силе и расстоянию.

A student carries out an investigation by pulling four different boxes across the floor.

The results are shown in the table.

On which box is the most work done?

|  | frictional force needed to pull the box / N | distance moved across the floor / m |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | 5 | 4 |
| **B** | 10 | 2 |
| **C** | 15 | 2 |
| **D** | 20 | 4 |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
