> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнить сопротивление параллельной комбинации с сопротивлениями её резисторов.

Two resistors, with resistances *R*$_{1}$ and *R*$_{2}$, are connected in parallel.

The resistance *R*$_{1}$ is greater than the resistance *R*$_{2}$.

![[0625-w21-12-p13-f1.svg]]

What is the resistance of the parallel combination?

**A** less than either *R*$_{1}$ or *R*$_{2}$  
**B** equal to *R*$_{1}$  
**C** equal to *R*$_{2}$  
**D** the average of *R*$_{1}$ and *R*$_{2}$

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Most candidates thought that the total resistance of two resistors in parallel is equal to the average of the two resistances rather than it being smaller than either resistor. The logic is that the charge carriers now have a choice of paths to take, meaning that more charge carriers can pass per unit time.
