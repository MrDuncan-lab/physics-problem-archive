# Question 18 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2021 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 18  
Источник: [[_pdf/2021/0625_w21_qp_12.pdf#page=8|условие, стр. 8]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_w21_ms_12.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_w21_er.pdf#page=3|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

Темы: [[3.1 General properties of waves]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Найти неверно указанную единицу характеристики волны.

Which row is **not** correct for a wave on the surface of water?

|  | quantity | usual unit |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | amplitude | m |
| **B** | frequency | Hz |
| **C** | wavelength | λ |
| **D** | speed | m / s |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Some candidates did not read the question carefully enough. The question asked which row in the table did **not** show a correct unit for the quantity in the left-hand column. The symbol λ does not represent a unit, it is merely used to label an unknown wavelength.
