> Распознать природный источник фонового излучения.

Some sources of background radiation are natural and others are due to human activity.

Which source is natural?

**A** medical X-rays  
**B** nuclear weapons testing  
**C** radioactive waste from power stations  
**D** radon gas from rocks

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
