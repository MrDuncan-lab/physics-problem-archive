> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить причину отклонения альфа-частиц в опыте с золотой фольгой.

A very important experiment improved scientists’ understanding of the structure of matter.

The experiment involved α-particles being fired at a thin, gold foil.

What happened?

**A** All the α-particles were absorbed by the nuclei of the gold atoms.  
**B** All the α-particles were unaffected by the gold atoms.  
**C** Some of the α-particles were attracted by the neutrons in the nuclei of the gold atoms.  
**D** Some of the α-particles were repelled by the protons in the nuclei of the gold atoms.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Only stronger candidates demonstrated their understanding of the α-scattering experiment. To show full understanding of the experiment, candidates needed to know that a small proportion of the α-particles are repelled by the nucleus and that the electric charge on the nucleus is produced by the protons in the nucleus.
