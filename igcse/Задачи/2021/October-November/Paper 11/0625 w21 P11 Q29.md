> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить вид энергии, теряемой трансформатором в окружающую среду.

The step-down transformer shown reduces mains voltage to 12 V.

![[0625-w21-11-p13-f2.svg]]

When the transformer is used, some energy is transferred to the surroundings.

Which type of energy is transferred to the surroundings?

**A** chemical energy  
**B** light energy  
**C** thermal energy  
**D** elastic energy

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Most candidates recognised that the energy lost is in the form of low-grade thermal energy.
