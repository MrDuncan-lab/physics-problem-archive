# Question 13 (1 mark)

Источник: [[_pdf/2021/0625_w21_qp_11.pdf#page=6|условие, стр. 6]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_w21_ms_11.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_w21_er.pdf#page=1|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнить движение частиц в твёрдом теле и жидкости.

Which row correctly describes the movement of particles in solids and liquids?

|  | solids | liquids |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | no movement | move around each other |
| **B** | no movement | vibration only |
| **C** | vibration only | move around each other |
| **D** | vibration only | vibration only |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Although most candidates correctly identified the movement of molecules in liquids and solids, there were a few who thought that there was no movement of the molecules in a solid.
