> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить количество элементов, ламп и выключателей на схеме цепи.

The diagram shows an electric circuit.

![[0625-w21-11-p14-f2.svg]]

Which row correctly shows the numbers of different components in the circuit?

|  | cells | lamps | switches |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | 1 | 1 | 4 |
| **B** | 1 | 4 | 1 |
| **C** | 3 | 1 | 4 |
| **D** | 3 | 4 | 1 |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The majority of candidates correctly identified the components in the circuit. Those who got it wrong, almost always confused cells with batteries.
