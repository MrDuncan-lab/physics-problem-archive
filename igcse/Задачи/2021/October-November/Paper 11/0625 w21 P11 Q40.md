# Question 40 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2021 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 1 · вопрос 40

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбрать расчёт уменьшения активности за несколько периодов полураспада.

A radioactive isotope has a half-life of 3 years.

A sample gives a count rate of 100 counts / min on a detector.

Which calculation is used to predict the count rate after 12 years?

**A** 100 × $\dfrac{1}{2}$ × $\dfrac{1}{2}$ × $\dfrac{1}{2}$ × $\dfrac{1}{2}$  
**B** 100 × $\dfrac{1}{2}$ × $\dfrac{1}{2}$ × $\dfrac{1}{2}$  
**C** 100 × $\dfrac{3}{12}$  
**D** 100 × $\dfrac{12}{3}$ × $\dfrac{1}{2}$

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Only stronger candidates answered this correctly. Candidates needed to be familiar with, and to fully understand the meaning of half-life in order to answer this type of question.
