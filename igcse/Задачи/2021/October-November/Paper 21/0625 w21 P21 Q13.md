> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать давление газа после расширения в дополнительный объём при постоянной температуре.

The diagram shows two cylinders connected by a narrow tube fitted with a tap.

![[0625-w21-21-p5-f2.svg]]

One cylinder contains 80 cm$^{3}$ of gas at a pressure of 2.0 × 10$^{5}$ Pa. The other cylinder contains a vacuum.

The volume of the evacuated cylinder is 20 cm$^{3}$. The tap is opened so that the gas can flow to fill both cylinders.

The temperature of the gas remains constant.

What is the new pressure of the gas?

**A** 0.50 × 10$^{5}$ Pa  
**B** 1.6 × 10$^{5}$ Pa  
**C** 2.5 × 10$^{5}$ Pa  
**D** 8.0 × 10$^{5}$ Pa

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Many candidates were under the misapprehension that all the gas was pushed into the 20 cm$^{3}$ cylinder, rather than the gas now being contained in both cylinders giving an increased volume of 100 cm$^{3}$.
