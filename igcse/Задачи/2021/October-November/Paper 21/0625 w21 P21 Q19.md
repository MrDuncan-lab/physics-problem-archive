> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить общую частоту света одного цвета при распространении в разных средах.

Two beams of light are both the same colour of red. One beam is travelling through air. The other

beam is travelling through water. Each beam has a different brightness.

Which quantity is the same for both sets of waves?

**A** amplitude  
**B** frequency  
**C** speed  
**D** wavelength

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
