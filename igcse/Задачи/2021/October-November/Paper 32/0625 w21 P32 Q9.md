# Question 9 (4 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2021 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 9  
Источник: [[_pdf/2021/0625_w21_qp_32.pdf#page=15|условие, стр. 15]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_w21_ms_32.pdf#page=13|схема оценивания, стр. 13]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_w21_er.pdf#page=16|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

Темы: [[3.2.1 Reflection of light]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить углы падения и отражения и построить луч между автомобилями через плоское зеркало.

**(a)** Fig. 9.1 shows a ray of light reflected by a plane mirror.

![[0625-w21-32-p15-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 9.1**

**(i)** State which angle, *a*, *b*, *c* or *d*, is the angle of incidence \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(ii)** State which angle, *a*, *b*, *c* or *d*, is the angle of reflection \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(b)** Fig. 9.2 shows a road junction viewed from above. A plane mirror allows the drivers of the two cars A and B to see each other.

![[0625-w21-32-p15-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 9.2**

Fig. 9.2 shows a ray of light from car A travelling towards the plane mirror.

On Fig. 9.2, carefully continue this ray to show how the driver of car B can see car A. **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** ray reflected to car B

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | b | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | c | B1 |
> | (b) | ray from lamp extended to mirror as straight line (by eye) | M1 |
> |  | ray reflected to car B | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Many correct answers were seen here. A common error was selecting the angles between the mirror and the rays rather than the angles between the rays and the normal.
>
> **(b)** Many correct answers were seen. Other answers lacked accuracy, e.g. the reflected ray missing car B or rays drawn as curves.
