> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объяснить вращение вентилятора конвекцией и действие биметаллического температурного выключателя.

**(a)** Fig. 7.1 shows a candle underneath a thin, metal fan.

![[0625-w21-32-p11-f1.svg]]

![[0625-w21-32-p11-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 7.1**

When the candle starts to burn, the fan starts to rotate around its support.

Explain what causes the fan to rotate. **[3]**

**(b)** Fig. 7.2 shows a bimetallic strip. It is made of two metals, steel and copper, fastened together.

The bimetallic strip is straight when the temperature is 25 °C.

![[0625-w21-32-p12-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 7.2**

Fig. 7.3 shows the bimetallic strip when the temperature is 40 °C.

![[0625-w21-32-p12-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 7.3**

**(i)** Draw the bimetallic strip when the temperature is 10 °C. **[1]**

**(ii)** The bimetallic strip is used in a circuit, as shown in Fig. 7.4. The circuit is in a room.

![[0625-w21-32-p13-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 7.4**

The room temperature is 25 °C.

State and explain any changes in the circuit as the temperature of the room rises above 40 °C. **[2]**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Only stronger candidates answered this correctly. A thorough description of the thermal process involved, convection, was required. Most answers were vague or concerned with the candle, or its smoke, heating the metal fan.
>
> **(b) (i)** Candidates needed to recognise that the bimetallic strip would only be straight at 25°C. Many candidates applied this knowledge correctly, but weaker candidates found the situation more challenging.
>
> **(ii)** The majority of candidates answered this question well.
