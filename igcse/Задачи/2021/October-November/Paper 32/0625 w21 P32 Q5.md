> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Описать определение центра масс пластинки и объяснить устойчивость тела положением центра масс.

**(a)** A student determines the centre of mass of a piece of wood. The wood is an irregular shape of constant thickness.

He suspends the piece of wood from a nail as shown in Fig. 5.1. The wood is able to swing freely. The student suspends a weight on a thin string from the nail.

![[0625-w21-32-p8-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 5.1**

Describe how to determine the centre of mass of the piece of wood in Fig. 5.1. You may draw a diagram to help your answer. **[3]**

**(b)** Fig. 5.2 shows a flat, symmetrical object. Indicate its centre of mass by drawing X in the correct position.

![[0625-w21-32-p9-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 5.2** **[1]**

**(c)** Fig. 5.3 shows a side view of a drinking-glass in two different positions, A and B.

![[0625-w21-32-p9-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 5.3**

State which position, A or B, is more stable. Explain your answer. **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(c)** <u>lower centre of mass owtte</u>

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | any **three** from:<br>mark 2 x’s behind string AND join x’s with a line<br>suspend from different hole<br>repeat step 1 owtte<br>centre of mass is where lines cross | B3 |
> | (b) | marked on line of symmetry approximately where navel located | B1 |
> | (c) | A | M1 |
> |  | <u>lower centre of mass owtte</u> | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Only stronger candidates answered this question well and many others did not give an answer at all. Candidates familiar with the experiment gave fully correct answers, usually with a clear labelled diagram supporting the written answer. Other incorrect answers focused on swinging the wood on the nail or merely paraphrased the question.
>
> **(b)** The majority of answers were correct, but some candidates drew the X too high on the line of symmetry.
>
> **(c)** Most candidates gave the correct answer. Many other explanations were either too vague or concentrated on a difference in surface area.
