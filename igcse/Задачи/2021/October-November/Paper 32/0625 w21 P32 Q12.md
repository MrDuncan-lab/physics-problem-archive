# Question 12 (6 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2021 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 12  
Источник: [[_pdf/2021/0625_w21_qp_32.pdf#page=20|условие, стр. 20]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_w21_ms_32.pdf#page=14|схема оценивания, стр. 14]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_w21_er.pdf#page=16|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w21 P32]]  
Темы: [[5.2.2 The three types of nuclear emission]], [[5.1.2 The nucleus]], [[5.2.4 Half-life]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнить свойства излучений, определить изотопы и вычислить оставшуюся долю радиоактивного йода.

**(a)** State which radioactive emission is:

**(i)** the most penetrating  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(ii)** the most ionising \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(b)** Explain the meaning of the term *isotope*. **[2]**

**(c)** The isotope iodine-131 is used in hospitals. A sample of iodine-131 is prepared for use.

The half-life of iodine-131 is 8 days.

Determine the fraction of iodine-131 remaining in the sample after 16 days.

fraction remaining =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(c)** 1 / 4

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | gamma OR | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | alpha OR | B1 |
> | (b) | same atomic number / Z / number of protons | B1 |
> |  | different nucleon number / A / number of neutrons | B1 |
> | (c) | idea of 2 half-lives | C1 |
> |  | 1 / 4 | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> There were a number of nil responses for all parts of this question.
>
> **(a)** Some confusion between the characteristics of the three kinds of radioactive emission was evident.
>
> **(b)** Very few correct answers were seen. Candidates did not seem to know the meaning of the term isotope.
>
> **(c)** Some candidates gained at least partial credit for the idea of dividing by 2 twice, but often incorrectly divided the mass number by 4. However, a number of candidates achieved full credit.
