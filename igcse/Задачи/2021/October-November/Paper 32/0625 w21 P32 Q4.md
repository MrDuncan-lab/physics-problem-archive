# Question 4 (9 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2021 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 4  
Источник: [[_pdf/2021/0625_w21_qp_32.pdf#page=6|условие, стр. 6–7]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_w21_ms_32.pdf#page=10|схема оценивания, стр. 10]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_w21_er.pdf#page=16|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

Темы: [[1.1 Physical quantities and measurement techniques]], [[1.3 Mass and weight]], [[1.8 Pressure]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбрать измерительные приборы, вычислить вес блока и давление на поверхность.

**(a)** A teacher wants to measure the mass of a block of metal. She also wants to measure the length, width and height of the block.

Fig. 4.1 shows the block of metal.

![[0625-w21-32-p6-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 4.1**

Complete each sentence using a word from the list.

**balance barometer protractor ruler voltmeter**

**(i)** To find the mass of the metal block, the teacher uses a  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(ii)** To measure the length, width and height of the metal block, she uses a  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(b)** The mass of the block is 5000 g.

Calculate the weight of the block.

weight =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ N **[3]**

**(c)** Fig. 4.2 shows another block of metal on a solid surface.

![[0625-w21-32-p7-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 4.2** (not to scale)

**(i)** Calculate the area of the block of metal in contact with the solid surface.

area =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm$^{2}$ **[1]**

**(ii)** The weight of the block of metal in Fig. 4.2 is 60 N.

Calculate the pressure of the block of metal on the solid surface.

pressure =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ N / cm$^{2}$ **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** 50 (N)
>
> **(c)(ii)** 0.25 (N / cm$^{2}$)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | balance | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | ruler | B1 |
> | (b) | mass = 5(.0) kg | B1 |
> |  | (W =) m × g OR 5(.0) × 10 | C1 |
> |  | 50 (N) | A1 |
> | (c)(i) | 240 (cm$^{2}$) | B1 |
> | (c)(ii) | (P =) F ÷ A in any form | C1 |
> |  | 60 ÷ (20 × 12) OR 60 ÷ 240 | C1 |
> |  | 0.25 (N / cm$^{2}$) | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)(ii)** The vast majority of candidates gave the correct answers.
>
> **(b)** Many candidates gave the correct answer, writing the equation and showing full working. For full credit, an understanding that the mass of 5 000 g needed to be converted to kg before being substituted into W = mg was required. However, some candidates performed an incorrect conversion. A common error was to divide the mass by 100. Weaker candidates often used an incorrect equation i.e. W = m/g.
>
> **(c) (i)** Many correct answers were seen and a significant number of candidates calculated the volume.
>
> **(ii)** The vast majority of candidates gave the correct answer, writing the equation and showing full working. There were several incorrect answers based on the incorrect equation pressure = force/volume.
