# Question 8 (6 marks)

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Описать источник и преобразование энергии геотермальной станции и сравнить её с угольной.

Fig. 8.1 shows a simplified diagram of a geothermal power station.

![[0625-w21-32-p14-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 8.1**

**(a)** **(i)** State the energy source for a geothermal power station \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(ii)** Complete the sentence about useful energy transfer in the power station.

The generator converts  \_\_\_\_\_\_ energy into  \_\_\_\_\_\_ energy. **[2]**

**(b)** **(i)** State **two** advantages of a geothermal power station compared to a coal-fired power station.

1  \_\_\_\_\_\_

2  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(ii)** State **one** disadvantage of using geothermal energy rather than coal in a power station. **[1]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | hot / molten rocks | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | kinetic<br>(to) | B1 |
> |  | electrical | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | Any **two** from:<br>no sulphur dioxide OR acid rain produced<br>no carbon dioxide / greenhouse gases produced OR no (contribution to) global warming<br>no need to transport coal<br>renewable (energy source)<br>no fuel costs | B2 |
> | (b)(ii) | locations limited / land instability / distribution costs high / water pollution / smaller energy output / rocks may cool (over<br>time) | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** Only the strongest candidates answered this correctly and “steam” was a common incorrect answer.
>
> **(ii)** The question was about the energy changes in the generator rather than the whole power station. “Thermal” was therefore an incorrect answer for the input energy. Many correct answers were seen for the output energy.
>
> **(b) (i)** Many answers were not developed sufficiently i.e. simply stating “pollution” or “environmental friend” were too vague. Comprehensive ideas based on using geothermal as “not contributing to global warming” or “not producing acid rain” were frequently seen.
>
> **(ii)** Only stronger candidates answered this correctly.
