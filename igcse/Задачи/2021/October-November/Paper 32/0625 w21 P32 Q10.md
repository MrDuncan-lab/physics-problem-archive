> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** The physical quantity was required here, not the unit.
>
> **(ii)** Many correct answers were seen but the most common error was A1 = A2 + A3.
>
> **(b) (i)** Most candidates gained some credit here. The most common errors were drawing incorrect symbols for resistors or short circuiting the two resistors in parallel.
>
> **(ii)** Many candidates knew an advantage of connecting lamps in parallel. Several answers were too vague for credit, e.g. “same amount of electricity in each lamp”.
>
> **(c)** Only the strongest candidates answered these parts correctly and many candidates did not give responses. Many answers focused on the use of a resistor to prevent too large a current that would damage the lamp.
