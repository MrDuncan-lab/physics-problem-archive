# Question 11 (7 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2021 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 11  
Источник: [[_pdf/2021/0625_w21_qp_32.pdf#page=18|условие, стр. 18–19]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_w21_ms_32.pdf#page=14|схема оценивания, стр. 14]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_w21_er.pdf#page=16|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Назвать способы усиления индукции и рассчитать выходное напряжение трансформатора.

**(a)** Fig. 11.1 shows a magnet and a coil of wire connected to a galvanometer.

![[0625-w21-32-p18-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 11.1**

A student slowly moves the magnet into the coil. The pointer on the galvanometer moves to the left. This deflection shows that an electromotive force (e.m.f.) is induced in the coil.

State **three** ways of increasing the size of the e.m.f. in the coil.

1  \_\_\_\_\_\_

2  \_\_\_\_\_\_

3  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

**(b)** Fig. 11.2 shows a transformer.

![[0625-w21-32-p19-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 11.2**

**(i)** Name **one** material that is suitable for the core of the transformer. **[1]**

**(ii)** The primary coil has 1000 turns and its input is 240 V a.c. The secondary coil has 50 turns.

Calculate the output voltage across the secondary coil.

output voltage =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ V **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)(ii)** 12 (V)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | strong(er) magnet | B1 |
> |  | move (magnet) more quickly / faster movement | B1 |
> |  | more turns / coils (per unit length) | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | (soft) iron | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | V$_{\text{p}}$ / V$_{\text{s}}$ = N$_{\text{p}}$ / N$_{\text{s}}$ in any form | C1 |
> |  | (V$_{\text{s}}$=) 240 × 50 / 1000 | C1 |
> |  | 12 (V) | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Some candidates gained at least partial credit with many achieving full credit. A common error was to state “bigger” magnets instead of “stronger” or “more powerful” magnets. A common incorrect answer was “to increase the current” or voltage, showing some confusion with the d.c. motor.
>
> **(b) (i)** Many correct answers were seen but common incorrect answers were “steel” or “copper”.
>
> **(ii)** Most candidates answered this question well. Weaker candidates mainly gained credit for recalling the equation.
