> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить состав ядра америция после альфа-распада.

The nucleus of an americium atom contains 146 neutrons and 95 protons. It decays by emitting

an α-particle.

How many neutrons and how many protons remain in the nucleus when this form of americium decays?

|  | number of neutrons remaining | number of protons remaining |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | 142 | 93 |
| **B** | 142 | 95 |
| **C** | 144 | 93 |
| **D** | 144 | 95 |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Many candidates thought that the neutron number fell by 4. This may have been because candidates did not read the question carefully enough and were confused between neutron number and nucleon number.
