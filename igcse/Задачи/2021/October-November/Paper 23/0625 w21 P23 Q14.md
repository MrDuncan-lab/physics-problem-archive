# Question 14 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2021 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 3 · вопрос 14  
Источник: [[_pdf/2021/0625_w21_qp_23.pdf#page=7|условие, стр. 7]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_w21_ms_23.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_w21_er.pdf#page=11|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w21 P23]]  
Темы: [[2.2.2 Specific heat capacity]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Вычислить энергию повышения температуры алюминиевого блока.

An aluminium block has a mass of 200 g.

The specific heat capacity of aluminium is 900 J / (kg °C).

How much energy is needed to increase the temperature of the block from 20 °C to 110 °C?

**A** 2.0 J  
**B** 2000 J  
**C** 16 200 J  
**D** 16 200 000 J

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This was a well answered question with relatively few candidates failing to notice that the mass was given in grams whereas the specific heat capacity was given in J / kg°C.
