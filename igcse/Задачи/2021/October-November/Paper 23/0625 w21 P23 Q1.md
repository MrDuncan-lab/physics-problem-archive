> Выбрать измерение, для которого подходит микрометр.

For which purpose is a micrometer screw gauge suitable?

**A** measuring the current in a coil that is known to be about 3 × 10$^{\text{–6}}$ A  
**B** measuring the diameter of a ball bearing that is known to be about 3 × 10$^{\text{–3}}$ m  
**C** measuring the mass of a grain of sand that is known to be about 3 × 10$^{\text{–3}}$ g  
**D** measuring the moment used to turn a screw that is known to be about 3 × 10$^{\text{–6}}$ N m

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
