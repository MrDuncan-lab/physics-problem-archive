Источник: [[_pdf/2021/0625_w21_qp_23.pdf#page=11|условие, стр. 11]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_w21_ms_23.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_w21_er.pdf#page=11|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить поле, действующее на неподвижную частицу силой вследствие её заряда.

A stationary, charged particle is in a field.

Which fields can cause there to be a force on this particle due to its charge?

1 gravitational field

2 magnetic field

3 electric field

**A** 1 and 2  
**B** 1 only  
**C** 2 and 3  
**D** 3 only

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Only stronger candidates answered this correctly and recognised that the only field which produces a force on a stationary charge is an electric field. Other candidates incorrectly thought that a stationary charged object in a magnetic field experienced a charge.
