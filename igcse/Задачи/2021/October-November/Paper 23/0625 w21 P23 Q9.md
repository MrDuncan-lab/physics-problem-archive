> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить энергетический ресурс, исходная энергия которого связана с Солнцем.

Which energy resource has the Sun as its only source of energy?

**A** geothermal  
**B** nuclear  
**C** oil  
**D** tidal

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> There was a wide spread of responses to this question. Many candidates chose option **A** (geothermal energy) but the centre of the Earth is hot due to the residual energy during the formation of the planet and also radioactive decay of heavy elements also contributes.
