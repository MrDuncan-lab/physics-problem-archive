> Назвать носители электрического тока в медном проводе.

An electric current in a copper wire is due to the flow of charge.

Which particles are moving along the wire?

**A** α-particles  
**B** copper nuclei  
**C** electrons  
**D** protons

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
