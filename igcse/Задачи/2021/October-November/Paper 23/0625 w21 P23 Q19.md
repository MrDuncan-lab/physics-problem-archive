# Question 19 (1 mark)

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выразить показатель преломления через скорости и длины волн в двух средах.

A ray of light travels from air into a glass block.

|  | in air | in glass |
|---|---|---|
| speed of ray | *v*$_{\text{a}}$ | *v*$_{\text{g}}$ |
| wavelength of ray | λ$_{\text{a}}$ | λ$_{\text{g}}$ |
| frequency of ray | *f*$_{\text{a}}$ | *f*$_{\text{g}}$ |

Three suggestions as to how the refractive index of glass *n* may be calculated are listed.

1 *n* = $\dfrac{v_{a}}{v}$

g

2 *n* = $\dfrac{\lambda _{a}}{\lambda}$

g

3 *n* = $\dfrac{f_{a}}{f}$

g

Which suggestions are correct?

**A** 1, 2 and 3  
**B** 1 and 2 only  
**C** 1 and 3 only  
**D** 2 and 3 only

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> A relatively large number of candidates were under the misapprehension that there is a change in frequency when a light travels from one medium to another. It is the wavelength that changes and this produces a change in speed.
