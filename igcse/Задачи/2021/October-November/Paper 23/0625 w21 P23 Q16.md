> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить требуемую теплопроводность материалов кастрюли.

The diagram shows a pan used for cooking food.

![[0625-w21-23-p8-f1.png]]

Which row is correct for the materials used to make the base and the handle of the pan?

|  | base of pan | handle of pan |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | good thermal conductor | good thermal conductor |
| **B** | good thermal conductor | poor thermal conductor |
| **C** | poor thermal conductor | good thermal conductor |
| **D** | poor thermal conductor | poor thermal conductor |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Most candidates were able to identify the materials from which the pan was made, showing that there was a good practical understanding of conduction.
