The diagram shows a ray of light in air incident on a glass block. Some of the light is refracted

and some of the light is reflected. Two angles, *p* and *q*, are marked on the diagram.

![[0625-w21-23-p10-f1.svg]]

Which row gives the angle of incidence and states whether total internal reflection occurs?

|  | angle of incidence | total internal reflection |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | *p* | no |
| **B** | *p* | yes |
| **C** | *q* | no |
| **D** | *q* | yes |

> [!answer]- Ответ
