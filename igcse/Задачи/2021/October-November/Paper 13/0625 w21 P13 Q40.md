> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Связать необходимость толстой свинцовой защиты с проникающей способностью гамма-излучения.

Why is a thick shield made of lead needed to protect people from a source of γ-rays?

**A** Gamma radiation is strongly ionising and so is not very penetrating.  
**B** Gamma radiation is strongly ionising and so is very penetrating.  
**C** Gamma radiation is weakly ionising and so is not very penetrating.  
**D** Gamma radiation is weakly ionising and so is very penetrating.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Only stronger candidates answered this question correctly. Other candidates incorrectly thought that γ-rays are not very penetrating.
