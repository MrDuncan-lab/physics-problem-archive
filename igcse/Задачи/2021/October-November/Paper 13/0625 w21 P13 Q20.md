# Question 20 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2021 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 3 · вопрос 20  
Источник: [[_pdf/2021/0625_w21_qp_13.pdf#page=9|условие, стр. 9]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_w21_ms_13.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_w21_er.pdf#page=5|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w21 P13]]  
Темы: [[3.2.2 Refraction of light]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Распознать угол падения и определить возможность полного внутреннего отражения на границе воздуха и стекла.

The diagram shows a ray of light in air incident on a glass block. Some of the light is refracted

and some of the light is reflected. Two angles, *p* and *q*, are marked on the diagram.

![[0625-w21-13-p9-f2.svg]]

Which row gives the angle of incidence and states whether total internal reflection occurs?

|  | angle of incidence | total internal reflection |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | *p* | no |
| **B** | *p* | yes |
| **C** | *q* | no |
| **D** | *q* | yes |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> A large majority of candidates were able to identify the angle of incidence of a light ray striking the surface of a glass block. However, few of those correctly stated that total internal reflection did not occur.
