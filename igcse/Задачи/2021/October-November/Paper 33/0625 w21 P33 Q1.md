# Question 1 (4 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2021 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 3 · вопрос 1  
Источник: [[_pdf/2021/0625_w21_qp_33.pdf#page=3|условие, стр. 3]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_w21_ms_33.pdf#page=8|схема оценивания, стр. 8]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_w21_er.pdf#page=20|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Назвать причину свободного падения и определить высоту по площади под графиком скорости.

Fig. 1.1 shows a plant pot falling from an upstairs balcony. The plant pot has a constant acceleration as it falls.

![[0625-w21-33-p3-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 1.1**

**(a)** State the cause of the acceleration. **[1]**

**(b)** Fig. 1.2 shows the speed–time graph for the falling plant pot. The plant pot hits the ground at

![[0625-w21-33-p3-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 1.2**

Determine the height of the balcony above the ground using the information shown in Fig. 1.2.

height =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ m **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** 14 (m)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | weight / gravitational force / attraction (acting downwards) | B1 |
> | (b) | area under the graph / line | C1 |
> |  | ½ × 1.8 × 16 | C1 |
> |  | 14 (m) | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** This was answered very well with most candidates gaining full credit.
>
> **(b)** It was not fully understood that the area under a speed–time graph determines the distance travelled. There were many incorrect answers based on the equation speed = total distance/total time, but these gained credit for recall of the equation.
