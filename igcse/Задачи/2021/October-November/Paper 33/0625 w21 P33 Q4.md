> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** The majority of candidates gave a fully correct answer.
>
> **(ii)** The majority of candidates gave a correct answer, and many showed the working.
>
> **(iii)** Many correct answers were seen here. A common error was giving answers relating to the light bulb getting hot rather than the surrounding air.
>
> **(b)** There was confusion between the types of energy stored by objects or situations and the usual form of energy that this stored emery is transferred into. For example, a mobile phone battery stores chemical energy, not electrical energy which was a common answer, a piece of coal stores chemical energy, not thermal energy which was another common answer.
