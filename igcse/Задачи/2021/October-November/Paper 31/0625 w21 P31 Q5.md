# Question 5 (7 marks)

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать давление бутылки на стол и объяснить её сжатие при откачке воздуха.

Fig. 5.1 shows a plastic bottle on a bench. The plastic bottle contains a liquid.

![[0625-w21-31-p9-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 5.1**

**(a)** The weight of the bottle and liquid is 12 N. The area of the bottle in contact with the bench is 25 cm$^{2}$.

Calculate the pressure of the bottle on the bench.

pressure on bench =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ N / cm$^{2}$ **[3]**

**(b)** A student pours out all the liquid from the bottle. She then connects the bottle to a vacuum pump which removes most of the air from the bottle. Fig. 5.2 shows the bottle after most of the air is removed.

![[0625-w21-31-p9-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 5.2**

Explain why the bottle is crushed. Use your ideas about molecules. **[4]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** 0.48 (N / cm$^{2}$)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | (P =) F ÷ A OR (pressure = ) force ÷ area in any form | C1 |
> |  | 12 ÷ 25 | C1 |
> |  | 0.48 (N / cm$^{2}$) | A1 |
> | (b) | Any **four** from:<br>molecules in air moving at high speed / kinetic energy<br>molecules collide with (plastic) bottle OR wall (of bottle)<br>force of collisions (per unit area) cause pressure<br>fewer air molecules or collisions on inside(compared to outside)<br>(and so) greater force OR pressure on outside (than inside) | B4 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Most candidates correctly calculated the pressure of the bottle on the bench as 0.48 N/cm$^{2}$. Weaker candidates used an incorrect arrangement of the equation, often multiplying 25 and 12.
>
> **(b)** Only stronger candidates answered this correctly. Weaker candidates did not link the collisions of high-speed molecules on the walls of the bottle with the internal pressure of the bottle. Very few compared the internal gas pressure and the external atmospheric pressure.
