# Question 8 (7 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2021 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 1 · вопрос 8  
Источник: [[_pdf/2021/0625_w21_qp_31.pdf#page=13|условие, стр. 13]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_w21_ms_31.pdf#page=12|схема оценивания, стр. 12]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_w21_er.pdf#page=13|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объяснить притяжение железа магнитом, электризацию трением и различие проводящих и изолирующих материалов.

**(a)** Fig. 8.1 shows the magnetic field pattern around a bar magnet.

![[0625-w21-31-p13-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 8.1**

**(i)** On Fig. 8.1, label the north and south poles of the magnet, using the letters N and S. **[1]**

**(ii)** A soft-iron bar is positioned as shown in Fig. 8.2.

![[0625-w21-31-p13-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 8.2**

State and explain what happens to the soft-iron bar. **[3]**

**(b)** **(i)** A plastic rod is rubbed with a cloth. The plastic rod becomes positively charged.

Explain how the friction between the cloth and the rod causes the rod to become positively charged.

Use your ideas about the movement of charge. **[2]**

**(ii)** Plastic is an electrical insulator. Iron is an electrical conductor.

State **two** other materials that are electrical conductors.

1  \_\_\_\_\_\_

2  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)(i)** from the rod OR to the cloth

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | N marked on left AND S on the right of magnet | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | (soft-iron / bar / it) is attracted to the (N pole of the) magnet | B1 |
> |  | (soft-iron / bar / it) becomes induced magnet | B1 |
> |  | with opposite pole nearest to magnet | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | electrons move | M1 |
> |  | from the rod OR to the cloth | A1 |
> | (b)(ii) | **TWO** electrical conductors named | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** The vast majority of candidates answered this correctly.
>
> **(ii)** Stronger candidates gained full credit for this question. Very few candidates linked the induced magnetism in the iron bar to two opposite poles being next to each other and therefore attracting.
>
> **(b) (i)** The majority of candidates gave good descriptions of the movement of electrons. Weaker candidates did not identify the direction in which the electrons moved, or stated that positive charges were moving.
>
> **(ii)** The majority of candidates answered this correctly. A common error was to state two insulators.
