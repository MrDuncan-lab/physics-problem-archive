# Question 2 (10 marks)

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Обработать измерения времени движения тележки, вычислить среднюю скорость и расстояние по графику.

A slope is made by resting one end of a plank of wood on a block, as shown in Fig. 2.1.

![[0625-w21-31-p4-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 2.1**

Two students each use a digital stop-watch to measure the time for a small trolley to roll down the full length of the slope.

Fig. 2.2 shows the times on the stop-watches.

![[0625-w21-31-p4-f2.svg]]

$_{\text{student 1}}$ 00$^{\text{min}}$ :$^{\text{sec}}$06 14$^{100}$ time =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ s

![[0625-w21-31-p4-f3.svg]]

$_{\text{student 2}}$ 00$^{\text{min}}$ :$^{\text{sec}}$06 28$^{100}$ time =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ s

**Fig. 2.2**

**(a)** **(i)** On the line next to each stop-watch, write the time it shows. **[1]**

**(ii)** Calculate the average time for the trolley to roll down the slope.

average time =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ s **[2]**

**(iii)** The students want the same trolley to take more time to roll down the plank.

Suggest how the students alter the arrangement in Fig. 2.1. **[1]**

**(b)** A different trolley travels 1.2 m down the slope in a time of 7.8 s.

Calculate the average speed of the trolley.

average speed =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ m / s **[3]**

**(c)** The trolley travels down a different slope. Fig. 2.3 shows the speed–time graph.

![[0625-w21-31-p5-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 2.3**

Calculate the distance travelled by the trolley between time = 0 and time = 4.0 s.

distance travelled =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ m **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)(ii)** 6.21 (s)
>
> **(b)** 0.15 (m / s)
>
> **(c)** 3.2 (m)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | 6.14 (s) AND 6.28 (s) | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | (6.14 + 6.28) ÷ 2 OR 12.42 ÷ 2 | C1 |
> |  | 6.21 (s) | A1 |
> | (a)(iii) | idea of decreasing (angle of) slope OR less steep OR smaller gradient | B1 |
> | (b) | (average speed =)( total) distance ÷ (total) time in any form | C1 |
> |  | 1.2 ÷ 7.8 | C1 |
> |  | 0.15 (m / s) | A1 |
> | (c) | distance = area under graph OR ½ × base × height | C1 |
> |  | 4.0 × 1.6 × 0.5 | C1 |
> |  | 3.2 (m) | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** Almost all candidates correctly stated the times indicated on the stopwatches. A common error was to simply write down the numbers shown on the stopwatches, e.g., 0614, instead of placing a decimal point after the 6 to read 6.14 s.
>
> **(ii)** The majority of candidates correctly determined the average of the two readings. A common error was to subtract the two readings instead of adding them before dividing by two.
>
> **(iii)** Most candidates correctly stated that decreasing the angle of the slope would decrease the speed of the trolley. The most common error was to make statements that were too vague, e.g., “move the block backwards”.
>
> **(b)** Almost all candidates correctly determined the average speed as 0.15 m/s. The most common errors were using an incorrect arrangement of the equation, or rounding their answer to one significant figure, i.e., 0.2 m/s.
>
> **(c)** The majority of candidates correctly determined the distance travelled by evaluating the area under the graph as 3.2 m. The most common error was to state distance = speed x time, and to evaluate 1.6 × 4.0. Weaker candidates divided the time by the speed.
