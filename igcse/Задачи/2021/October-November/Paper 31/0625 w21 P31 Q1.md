# Question 1 (8 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2021 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 1 · вопрос 1  
Источник: [[_pdf/2021/0625_w21_qp_31.pdf#page=2|условие, стр. 2–3]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_w21_ms_31.pdf#page=9|схема оценивания, стр. 9]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_w21_er.pdf#page=13|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w21 P31]]  
Темы: [[1.1 Physical quantities and measurement techniques]], [[1.4 Density]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Измерить длину и объём проволоки, выбрать прибор для массы и вычислить плотность металла.

A student uses a ruler to measure the length of a piece of wire, as shown in Fig. 1.1.

![[0625-w21-31-p2-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 1.1** (not to scale)

**(a)** Use the ruler in Fig. 1.1 to determine the length of the piece of wire.

length of wire =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm **[2]**

**(b)** The student folds the piece of wire and measures its mass.

**(i)** State the name of an instrument the student can use to measure mass. **[1]**

**(ii)** The student determines the volume of the wire.

He uses a measuring cylinder part-filled with water and places the wire in it, as shown in Fig. 1.2.

![[0625-w21-31-p2-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 1.2**

Determine the volume of the wire by using information in Fig. 1.2.

volume of wire =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm$^{3}$ **[2]**

**(c)** The student measures the mass and the volume of a piece of metal. The mass of the piece of metal is 93.6 g and its volume is 12 cm$^{3}$.

Calculate the density of the metal.

density of metal =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ g / cm$^{3}$ **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** 13.2 (cm)
>
> **(b)(ii)** 4(.0) (cm$^{3}$)
>
> **(c)** 7.8 (g / cm$^{3}$)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | 1.6 (cm) OR 14.8 (cm) seen OR used | C1 |
> |  | 13.2 (cm) | A1 |
> | (b)(i) | (top pan / chemical / beam) balance | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | 22 (cm$^{3}$) OR 18 (cm$^{3}$) seen OR used | C1 |
> |  | 4(.0) (cm$^{3}$) | A1 |
> | (c) | (density = ) mass ÷ volume OR ( d =) m ÷ v in any form | C1 |
> |  | 93.6 ÷ 12 | C1 |
> |  | 7.8 (g / cm$^{3}$) | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** The majority of candidates gained full credit but errors in using the millimetre scale on the ruler were common. A small number of candidates used their own rulers rather than the ruler in the figure.
>
> **(b) (i)** The vast majority of candidates correctly identified some form of balance as a suitable device for measuring mass.
>
> **(ii)** The majority of candidates correctly determined the volume as 4 cm$^{3}$ using the scale on the measuring cylinders. A common error was to simply state the volume with the wire submerged, i.e., 22 cm$^{3}$.
>
> **(c)** Most candidates correctly evaluated the density as being 7.8 g/cm$^{3}$. The most common error was to use an incorrect arrangement of the equation density = mass/volume.
