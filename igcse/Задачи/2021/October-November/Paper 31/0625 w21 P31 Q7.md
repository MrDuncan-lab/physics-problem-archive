# Question 7 (8 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2021 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 1 · вопрос 7  
Источник: [[_pdf/2021/0625_w21_qp_31.pdf#page=12|условие, стр. 12]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_w21_ms_31.pdf#page=12|схема оценивания, стр. 12]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_w21_er.pdf#page=13|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w21 P31]]  
Темы: [[3.2.2 Refraction of light]], [[3.3 Electromagnetic spectrum]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Построить преломлённый луч, объяснить полное внутреннее отражение и сравнить видимый свет с рентгеновским излучением.

Fig. 7.1 shows a ray of red light entering a semicircular glass block. The ray strikes the flat surface of the block at X and emerges into the air. Fig. 7.1 does not show the path of the refracted ray in the air.

![[0625-w21-31-p12-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 7.1**

**(a)** On Fig. 7.1:

**(i)** draw the path of the refracted ray in the air **[1]**

**(ii)** mark, and label with the letter *i*, the angle of incidence **[1]**

**(iii)** mark, and label with the letter *r*, the angle of refraction. **[1]**

**(b)** When the angle of incidence at X is 70°, the ray does **not** emerge from the glass into the air.

State what happens to the ray at X and explain why this happens. **[2]**

**(c)** Visible light is one part of the electromagnetic spectrum. X-rays are also part of the electromagnetic spectrum.

**(i)** Visible light and X-rays are travelling through a vacuum.

Compare their speed and frequency by completing the sentences.

The speed of visible light is  \_\_\_\_\_\_ the speed of X-rays.

The frequency of visible light is  \_\_\_\_\_\_ the frequency of X-rays. **[2]**

**(ii)** Describe **one** use of X-rays. **[1]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | ray drawn refracted away from normal | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | angle of incidence correctly identified | B1 |
> | (a)(iii) | angle of refraction correctly identified | B1 |
> | (b) | total internal reflection (at flat surface) | B1 |
> |  | angle (of incidence) is greater than the critical angle | B1 |
> | (c)(i) | (The speed of visible light is) same (as) (the speed of X-rays.) | B1 |
> |  | (The frequency of visible light is) lower OR smaller (than the frequency of X-rays) | B1 |
> | (c)(ii) | example of medical / security imaging or treatment of cancer | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Many candidates found this item challenging, with many drawing a reflected rather than a refracted ray. Only stronger candidates correctly identified the angle of incidence and the angle of refraction.
>
> **(b)** Only stronger candidates were able to link the angle of incidence being greater than the critical angle causing the ray to have total internal reflection.
>
> **(c) (i)** Only stronger candidates stated that the speed of light was the same as the speed of the X-rays. Candidates seemed evenly split on whether the frequency of visible light was lower or higher than the frequency of X-rays.
>
> **(ii)** The vast majority of candidates were able to give a suitable use of X-rays.
