# Question 1 (5 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2021 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 2 · вопрос 1  
Источник: [[_pdf/2021/0625_w21_qp_42.pdf#page=2|условие, стр. 2–3]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_w21_ms_42.pdf#page=10|схема оценивания, стр. 10]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_w21_er.pdf#page=27|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w21 P42]]  
Темы: [[1.2 Motion]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить изменение ускорения ракеты, найти ускорение по касательной и расстояние по графику скорости.

Fig. 1.1 shows a space rocket accelerating away from a launch pad.

![[0625-w21-42-p2-f1.png]]

**Fig. 1.1**

Fig. 1.2 is a speed–time graph for the first 30 s of the rocket’s flight.

![[0625-w21-42-p2-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 1.2**

**(a)** Describe how the acceleration of the rocket changes between time = 10 s and time = 30 s. **[1]**

**(b)** By drawing a tangent to the graph, determine the acceleration of the rocket at time = 25 s.

acceleration =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(c)** Determine the distance travelled by the rocket between time = 0 and time = 10 s.

distance =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** 78 to 82 m / s$^{2}$
>
> **(c)** 2000 m

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | (acceleration) increases | B1 |
> | (b) | tangent drawn at 25 s | M1 |
> |  | 78 to 82 m / s$^{2}$ | A1 |
> | (c) | (distance =) area under graph (stated or correct area clearly shown on graph) OR (400 x 10) / 2 OR (b x h) ÷ 2 | C1 |
> |  | 2000 m | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Most candidates correctly stated that the acceleration increases between 10 s and 30 s. Weaker candidates did gain credit as they gave incomplete or ambiguous answers or answers which did not answer the question, e.g. “the speed increases” or “it accelerates”.
>
> **(b)** Stronger candidates drew a correct tangent at 25 s and correctly calculated the acceleration from the gradient of the tangent. Weaker candidates made no attempt to draw a tangent and attempted to calculate an acceleration from acceleration = change in velocity / time. This indicated a lack of understanding that the acceleration at 25 s was not constant. A few candidates did not place the tangent at 25 s. Candidates should be encouraged to practice drawing tangents. They should know that the gradient of speed-time graphs gives a value of the acceleration at that point and the gradient of distance-time graphs gives a value of the velocity at that point.
>
> **(c)** The distance travelled by the rocket is determined by calculating the area under the graph or, since the acceleration is constant during the first 10 s, using distance = average speed × time.
