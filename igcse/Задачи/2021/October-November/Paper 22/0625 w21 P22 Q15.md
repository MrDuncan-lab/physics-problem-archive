The diagram shows the apparatus used to measure the specific latent heat of vaporisation of water.

Using these results, what is the value of the specific latent heat of vaporisation of water?

**B** 1800 J / kg  
**C** 2200 J / kg  
**D** 2 200 000 J / kg
