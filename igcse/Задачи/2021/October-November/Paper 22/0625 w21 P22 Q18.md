> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить неверное обозначение единицы волновой величины.

Which row is **not** correct for a wave on the surface of water?

|  | quantity | usual unit |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | amplitude | m |
| **B** | frequency | Hz |
| **C** | wavelength | λ |
| **D** | speed | m / s |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Candidates needed to read the question carefully. The question asked which row in the table does **not** show a correct unit for the quantity in the left-hand column. The symbol λ does not represent a unit and is merely used to label an unknown wavelength.
