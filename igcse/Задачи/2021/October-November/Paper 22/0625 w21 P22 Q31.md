# Question 31 (1 mark)

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить общую электродвижущую силу последовательно соединённых элементов.

Several cells are connected in series, as shown.

![[0625-w21-22-p13-f2.svg]]

What is the combined electromotive force (e.m.f.) of the cells?

**A** the average of the e.m.f.s of the separate cells  
**B** the e.m.f. of one of the cells  
**C** the product of the e.m.f.s of the cells  
**D** the sum of the e.m.f.s of the cells

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> A large majority of candidates recognised that the e.m.f. of the battery was the sum of the e.mf.s of the individual cells in series.
