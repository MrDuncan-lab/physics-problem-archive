> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Связать углы преломления с критическим углом через закон Снеллиуса.

The diagram shows how a ray of light refracts when going from air to Perspex.

![[0625-w21-22-p9-f1.svg]]

The critical angle of Perspex is *c*.

Which expression is correct?

**A** $\dfrac{\text{sin} x}{\text{sin} z}$ = sin *c*  
**B** $\dfrac{\text{sin} z}{\text{sin} x}$ = sin *c*  
**C** $\dfrac{\text{sin} w}{\text{sin} y}$ = sin *c*  
**D** $\dfrac{\text{sin} y}{\text{sin} w}$ = sin *c*

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Only stronger candidates answered this correctly. Candidates needed to link the two formulae *n* = 1 / sin *c* and *n* = sin *i* / sin *r.* Although many candidates clearly attempted to do this, many forgot the inversion in the latter formula.
