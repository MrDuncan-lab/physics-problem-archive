> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Only stronger candidates used the information about the half-life of the isotope that was given. If the term half-life was understood then candidates would have known that the count rate would have fallen to half the initial rate after six hours; after another six hours it would have fallen to half again, i.e., a quarter of the initial count. Graph **C** should have been identifiable as the only possible graph.
