# Question 22 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2021 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 2 · вопрос 22  
Источник: [[_pdf/2021/0625_w21_qp_22.pdf#page=11|условие, стр. 11]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_w21_ms_22.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_w21_er.pdf#page=9|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w21 P22]]  
Темы: [[3.3 Electromagnetic spectrum]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнить инфракрасное и видимое излучение по длине волны и времени распространения.

The Sun emits infrared radiation and light.

Light from the Sun reaches the Earth in 8 minutes.

Which row gives correct information about the infrared radiation?

|  | wavelength of infrared radiation | time taken for infrared radiation to reach the Earth |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | longer than wavelength of light | 8 minutes |
| **B** | longer than wavelength of light | much less than 8 minutes |
| **C** | shorter than wavelength of light | 8 minutes |
| **D** | shorter than wavelength of light | much more than 8 minutes |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Although many candidates got this correct, there was a significant minority who did not recognise that it takes light from the Sun approximately 8 minutes to reach the Earth.
