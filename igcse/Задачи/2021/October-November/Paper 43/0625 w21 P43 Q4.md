> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объяснить рост давления нагреваемого газа через изменение импульса молекул при столкновениях.

Explain what happens to the pressure of a constant volume of air when the temperature of the air increases. Use ideas of momentum of molecules in your explanation. **[4]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> |  |  | B4 |
> |  | <u>(temperature of air increases) so molecules move faster / their KE increases</u> | B1 |
> |  | <u>molecules collide with walls of container and change momentum</u> | B1 |
> |  | greater change of momentum when temperature is higher OR collisions more frequent OR harder collisions OR force =<br>rate of change of momentum | B1 |
> |  | (higher force and hence) higher pressure | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> There were many good answers that were awarded at least partial credit. Many candidates understood that increasing the temperature would make the molecules move faster or the momentum would increase. If candidates referred to an increase in energy, they needed to stipulate this as kinetic. Other answers went on to say that there would be more frequent collisions. Only the strongest candidates were able to use ideas of momentum to link to an increase in the pressure of the gas, which was asked for in the question. Candidates needed to remember that collisions with the walls of the container would lead to a change in momentum and that a higher temperature would produce a greater change in momentum.
