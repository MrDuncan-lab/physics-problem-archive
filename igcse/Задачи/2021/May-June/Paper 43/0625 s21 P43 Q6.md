# Question 6 (8 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2021 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 3 · вопрос 6  
Источник: [[_pdf/2021/0625_s21_qp_43.pdf#page=9|условие, стр. 9]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_s21_ms_43.pdf#page=8|схема оценивания, стр. 8]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_s21_er.pdf#page=31|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Построить изображение в собирающей линзе и определить условия её использования как лупы.

Fig. 6.1 is a full-scale diagram of a lens and an object O.

![[0625-s21-43-p9-f1.svg]]

![[0625-s21-43-p9-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 6.1**

**(a)** The focal length of the lens is 3.5 cm.

On Fig. 6.1, mark and label with the letter F the positions of the **two** principal focuses. **[1]**

**(b)** On Fig. 6.1, draw **three** rays to locate the image. Draw an arrow to represent the image and label the image I. **[3]**

**(c)** State **three** properties of the image I. **[2]**

**(d)** A student incorrectly states that this lens is being used as a magnifying glass.

**(i)** State how the image produced by a magnifying glass is different from the image I. **[1]**

**(ii)** The student moves the object O to a position P so that the lens shown in Fig. 6.1 acts as a magnifying glass.

On Fig. 6.1, mark a possible position for P. **[1]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | principal focuses marked in correct position | B1 |
> | (b) | 1 mark for each of:<br>• 1 correct ray<br>• 2nd correct ray<br>• 3rd correct ray and image, labelled I, in correct position with arrow at bottom | B3 |
> | (c) | real | B1 |
> |  | <u>inverted and enlarged</u> | B1 |
> | (d)(i) | (image produced by a magnifying glass is) upright<br>OR NOT inverted OR virtual | B1 |
> | (d)(ii) | position marked between principal focus and lens | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** The positions of both focal points were usually marked accurately. It was important that candidates marked both points clearly.
>
> **(b)** Most candidates drew the ray that passes through the centre of the lens correctly. Stronger candidates drew all three rays and the image carefully, using a ruler.
>
> **(c)** Candidates who did not draw an accurate diagram in **(b)** often produced unclear answers about the properties of the image formed.
>
> **(d) (i)** Stronger candidates correctly identified that the image formed by a magnifying glass was a virtual image or that it was not inverted.
>
> **(ii)** Candidates who had been unable to answer other parts of this question correctly were able to mark a suitable position for the object to be placed so that the lens was used as a magnifying glass.
