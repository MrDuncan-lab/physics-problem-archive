# Question 6 (6 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2021 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 2 · вопрос 6  
Источник: [[_pdf/2021/0625_s21_qp_42.pdf#page=8|условие, стр. 8]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_s21_ms_42.pdf#page=8|схема оценивания, стр. 8]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_s21_er.pdf#page=27|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

Темы: [[3.2.4 Dispersion of light]], [[3.1 General properties of waves]], [[3.3 Electromagnetic spectrum]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнить отклонение цветов призмой и вычислить частоту света, используя скорость электромагнитных волн.

**(a)** Fig. 6.1 shows a ray of green light passing through a prism.

![[0625-s21-42-p8-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 6.1**

A ray of blue light is directed towards the prism on the same path as the ray of green light.

On Fig. 6.1, draw the path of the blue light through and out of the prism. **[3]**

**(b)** The wavelength of the blue light in air is 4.8 × 10$^{\text{–7}}$ m.

Calculate the frequency of the blue light.

frequency =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** (f =) 6.3 × 10$^{14}$ Hz

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | blue ray refracted MORE towards normal at first surface | B1 |
> |  | refraction away from normal at second surface | B1 |
> |  | ray of blue light below ray of green light and diverging throughout path (after entering prism) | B1 |
> | (b) | v = fλ in any form OR (f=) v / λ | C1 |
> |  | (f =) 3 × 10$^{8}$ ÷ 4.8 × 10$^{\text{–7}}$ | C1 |
> |  | (f =) 6.3 × 10$^{14}$ Hz | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Many candidates gained full credit. The most common errors included drawing the blue ray as being refracted less than the green ray at the first face or using a different path to the incident ray of green light for their ray of blue light. These candidates had not read the question carefully as it stated that the ray of blue light is on the same path as the ray of green light.
>
> **(b)** The majority of candidates gained full credit. The most common errors were using an incorrect arrangement of the equation or not being able to recall the velocity of light. Some candidates omitted the unit or gave an incorrect unit. It may be dimensionally equivalent but s$^{\text{–1}}$ is not the same as Hz and it is not the unit of frequency to be used. (see Syllabus Appendix).
