# Question 3 (7 marks)

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Найти скорость падающей воды из сохранения энергии и силу удара по изменению импульса.

Fig. 3.1 shows water flowing at very slow speed over a cliff edge.

![[0625-s21-42-p5-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 3.1**

The water falls 15 m onto the rocks below.

**(a)** Show that the velocity of the water when it strikes the rocks is 17 m / s. **[4]**

**(b)** 30 kg of water flows over the cliff edge every second.

Calculate the force exerted by the rocks on the falling water. Ignore any splashing.

force =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** (F =) 520 N

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | (PE loss =) mgh AND (KE gain =) ½ mv$^{2}$ | B1 |
> |  | PE (loss) = KE (gain) | B1 |
> |  | alternative route 1 for 1$^{\text{st}}$ two m.p.s |  |
> |  | v$^{2}$ = u$^{2}$ + 2as | (B1) |
> |  | u = 0 | (B1) |
> |  | alternative route 2 for 1$^{\text{st}}$ two m.p.s |  |
> |  | s = ut + 0.5at$^{2}$ OR h = 0.5gt$^{2}$ | (B1) |
> |  | u = 0 AND t = √3 OR 1.73 | (B1) |
> |  | v$^{2}$ (= 2gh) = 2 × 10 × 15 OR v$^{2}$ = 300 OR v = 10√3 OR v = 10 × 1.73 | B1 |
> |  | {v = 17 m / s AND v$^{2}$ = 300 or v = 10√3} OR v = 17.3(2) m / s | B1 |
> | (b) | (F =) change of *p* / (change of) time OR rate of change of momentum | C1 |
> |  | (F =) 30 × 17.32 | C1 |
> |  | (F =) 520 N | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** This was generally well answered with many candidates gaining full credit. Candidates should be aware that in a “show that” question it is essential that every step in the working is written down to show the full argument and not doing this will limit the credit that can be awarded. A common error was the circular argument in which 15 was divided by 17 to get the time and this was then used in the equation speed = distance ÷ time.
>
> **(b)** The correct route through this question, with force equal to rate of change of momentum, was used by most stronger candidates who gained full credit. The route through *F* = *ma* was not correct in this instance.
