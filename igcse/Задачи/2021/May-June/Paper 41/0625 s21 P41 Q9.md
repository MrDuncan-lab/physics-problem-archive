> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить состав изотопов водорода, дополнить реакцию синтеза и связать энергетические ресурсы с энергией Солнца.

There are three naturally occurring isotopes of hydrogen: hydrogen-1, hydrogen-2 and hydrogen-3.

$_{1}$H

1

The nuclide notation for hydrogen-1 is .

**(a)** Write down the symbol, using nuclide notation, for:

hydrogen-2  \_\_\_\_\_\_

hydrogen-3 \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(b)** In a fusion reactor, a nucleus of hydrogen-2 and a nucleus of hydrogen-3 undergo fusion.

**(i)** State what is meant by *nuclear fusion*. **[2]**

**(ii)** The fusion reaction produces a free neutron and **one** other particle.

Write down, using nuclide notation, the equation that represents this reaction. **[3]**

**(c)** Nuclear fusion in the Sun is the source of most but not all of the resources that are used to generate electrical energy on Earth.

State **two** resources for which nuclear fusion in the Sun is **not** the source.

1 \_\_\_\_\_\_

2 \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** The nuclide notation symbols were very frequently correct with a small minority of candidates writing the numbers on the right of the Hs or reversing the nucleon and proton numbers.
>
> **(b) (i)** The two main causes of error were candidates who explained nuclear fusion by using a word such as “fuse” or “fusing” or who made no reference to the involvement of nuclei. A small number of candidates stated what is meant by nuclear fission.
>
> **(ii)** Only a few candidates were awarded full credit in this part. Many candidates gave the nuclide notations for hydrogen-2 and hydrogen-3 as the products as well as the nuclear reactants.
>
> **(c)** There were many candidates who obtained at least partial credit here but few gained full credit. A common incorrect answer was that wave energy does not have a solar origin. The term “tidal wave” is a common term for a tsunami rather than an energy source.
