# Question 2 (7 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2021 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 1 · вопрос 2  
Источник: [[_pdf/2021/0625_s21_qp_41.pdf#page=3|условие, стр. 3]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_s21_ms_41.pdf#page=6|схема оценивания, стр. 6]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_s21_er.pdf#page=23|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Использовать сохранение импульса при столкновении и определить работу трения и изменение кинетической энергии.

Fig. 2.1 shows a wooden trolley of mass 1.2 kg at rest on the rough surface of a bench.

![[0625-s21-41-p3-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 2.1**

A ball of mass 0.52 g travels horizontally towards the trolley. The ball embeds itself in the wood of the trolley. The trolley moves with an initial speed of 0.065 m / s.

**(a)** Calculate:

**(i)** the impulse exerted on the trolley

impulse =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(ii)** the speed of the ball as it hits the trolley.

speed =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(b)** As the trolley moves across the rough surface, it slows down and stops.

Explain, in terms of the work done, the energy change that takes place as the trolley slows down. **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)(i)** 0.078 N s **or** 0.078 kg m / s
>
> **(a)(ii)** 150 m / s

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | 0.078 N s **or** 0.078 kg m / s | A2 |
> |  | (*I* =) *m*$_{\text{t}}$(Δ)*v*$_{\text{t}}$ in any form **or** 1.2 × 0.065 | C1 |
> | (a)(ii) | 150 m / s | A2 |
> |  | *v*$_{\text{b}}$ = (*m*$_{\text{t}}$ + *v*$_{\text{t}}$) / *m*$_{\text{b}}$ in any form **or** initial momentum = final momentum<br>**or** 1.2(0052) × 0.065 / 0.00052 **or** 0.078(0338) / 0.00052 | C1 |
> | (b) | <u>work done against / due to / because of friction</u> **or** <u>kinetic energy (of trolley) used to do work</u> | B1 |
> |  | kinetic energy decreases (to zero) | B1 |
> |  | thermal energy produced | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** Many answers related to the momentum change experienced by the trolley and the correct answer was often given. A common error was the use of the mass of the ball rather than that of the trolley and candidates who tried to use the combined mass of the ball and trolley did not always convert the mass of the ball in grams to a mass in kilograms correctly. The unit given for impulse was
>
> sometimes inaccurate. In addition to the acceptable units, kg / m / s, kg m / s$^{\text{–1}}$ and N / s were sometimes given.
>
> **(ii)** Although the final answer was often given correctly, the use of either the mass of the trolley or a mass in units that did not match those of the number in the answer often led to incorrect answers.
>
> **(b)** Many candidates obtained credit for an accurate description of the energy transfer that occurs as the trolley slows. However, fewer candidates were able to describe what was happening in terms of work done. Some candidates simply omitted any reference to work done whilst others just defined work done and made no further reference to it in the response.
