> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Проверить расчёты веса космонавта на спутниках с разной напряжённостью гравитационного поля.

An astronaut wants to know how much she would weigh on different moons.

She measures her mass on Earth to be 82 kg. She researches the values of *g* on different moons and uses these values to calculate her weight.

The results are shown.

Which weight is calculated correctly?

|  | name of moon | *g* N/kg | calculated weight / N |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | Callisto | 1.2 | 68 |
| **B** | Charon | 0.28 | 23 |
| **C** | Dione | 0.23 | 2.3 |
| **D** | Umbriel | 0.23 | 0.0028 |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
