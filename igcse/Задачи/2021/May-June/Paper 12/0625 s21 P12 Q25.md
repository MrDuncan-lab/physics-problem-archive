# Question 25 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2021 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 25  
Источник: [[_pdf/2021/0625_s21_qp_12.pdf#page=13|условие, стр. 13]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_s21_ms_12.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_s21_er.pdf#page=4|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 s21 P12]]  
Темы: [[3.4 Sound]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Описать продольную передачу звука и направление колебаний частиц.

Sound is a transfer of energy from an oscillating source.

Which statement describes how sound energy is transferred?

**A** a longitudinal wave with the oscillation parallel to the direction in which energy is transferred  
**B** a longitudinal wave with the oscillation perpendicular to the direction in which energy is

transferred

**C** a transverse wave with the oscillation parallel to the direction in which energy is transferred  
**D** a transverse wave with the oscillation perpendicular to the direction in which energy is

transferred

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The most popular option was **B**. Candidates needed to be clear that when a wavy line drawn to represent a longitudinal wave, it is not a picture of the wave but is a graph showing how the pressure or the longitudinal displacement is behaving.
