> Выбрать правильное объяснение конвекции через изменение плотности воздуха.

The table gives some examples of convection and an explanation of why the convection occurs.

Which row is correct?

|  | example | explanation |
|---|---|---|
| **A** **B** **C** **D** | air conditioner unit convection oven hot-air balloon land and sea breezes | The unit is placed in a high position to circulate the cold air rising from the floor level and so keeping the room cold. The air in the oven becomes more dense when heated so it falls to the bottom heating the food faster. Air inside the balloon becomes less dense which causes the balloon to rise. A breeze forms due to the warm air above the land moving down towards the sea. |

> [!answer]- Ответ
