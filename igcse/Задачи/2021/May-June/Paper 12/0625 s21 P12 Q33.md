> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбрать предохранитель по характеристикам телевизора.

The information on the back of a television is shown.

![[0625-s21-12-p18-f1.svg]]

Which fuse provides the best protection for the circuit?

**A** 0.5 A  
**B** 1 A  
**C** 5 A  
**D** 13 A

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Candidates needed to realise that choosing a fuse is not a matter of selecting the nearest value. It has to be the nearest value above the expected current.
