> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Проверить численные показания амперметра при включении разных сочетаний параллельных ламп.

The diagram shows a circuit containing a battery, an ammeter, three switches, S$_{1}$, S$_{2}$ and S$_{3}$, and three identical lamps.

![[0625-s21-12-p17-f1.svg]]

With only switch S$_{1}$ closed, the reading on the ammeter is 0.04 A.

Which row states the **incorrect** ammeter reading for the switch conditions given?

|  | switch S$_{1}$ | switch S$_{2}$ | switch S$_{3}$ | ammeter reading / A |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | open | open | open | 0.00 |
| **B** | open | closed | open | 0.04 |
| **C** | open | open | closed | 0.08 |
| **D** | closed | closed | closed | 0.12 |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
