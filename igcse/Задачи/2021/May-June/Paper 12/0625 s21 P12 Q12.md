# Question 12 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2021 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 12

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать давление книги по массе и площади контакта со столом.

A book has a mass of 400 g.

The surface of the book in contact with a table has dimensions 0.10 m × 0.20 m.

The gravitational field strength *g* is 10 N / kg.

What is the pressure exerted on the table due to the book?

**A** 0.08 N / m$^{2}$  
**B** 8.0 N / m$^{2}$  
**C** 20 N / m$^{2}$  
**D** 200 N / m$^{2}$

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Although the correct option was frequently chosen, the other options were all popular choices. To answer correctly, candidates needed to know the formula for pressure, and to be careful about unit conversions.
