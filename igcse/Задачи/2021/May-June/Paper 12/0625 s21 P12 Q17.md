> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Указать свойство спирта, используемое жидкостным термометром.

A liquid-in-glass thermometer uses alcohol as its liquid.

Which property of alcohol is used to measure the temperature?

**A** boiling point  
**B** colour  
**C** thermal capacity  
**D** volume

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> While the strongest candidates answered this correctly, the most popular choice was option **C**, followed fairly closely by option **A**.
