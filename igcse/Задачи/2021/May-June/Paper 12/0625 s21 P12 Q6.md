> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать плотность камня по массе и вытесненному объёму воды.

A measuring cylinder contains 40 cm$^{3}$ of water.

A stone of mass 94 g is lowered into the water so that it is fully submerged as shown.

![[0625-s21-12-p4-f1.svg]]

What is the density of the stone?

**A** 1.1 g / cm$^{3}$  
**B** 1.2 g / cm$^{3}$  
**C** 2.1 g / cm$^{3}$  
**D** 2.6 g / cm$^{3}$

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Many candidates were successful in answering this question. The most common error was to fail to subtract the initial 40 cm$^{3}$ before dividing the volume into the mass.
