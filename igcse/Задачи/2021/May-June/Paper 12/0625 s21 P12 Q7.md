> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Указать равнодействующую сил для автомобиля, равномерно движущегося по наклонной плоскости.

A toy car travels down a sloping ramp at constant speed.

![[0625-s21-12-p5-f1.svg]]

Which statement about the forces acting on the car is correct?

**A** There are no forces acting on the car vertically.  
**B** There is no resultant force acting on the car.  
**C** There is no gravitational force acting on the car.  
**D** There is no frictional force acting on the car.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> A number of candidates decided that there are no vertical forces on the car in spite of having presumably rejected as false the statement that there is no gravitational force acting on the car.
