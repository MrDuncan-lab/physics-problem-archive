# Question 26 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2021 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 2 · вопрос 26  
Источник: [[_pdf/2021/0625_s21_qp_22.pdf#page=11|условие, стр. 11]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_s21_ms_22.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_s21_er.pdf#page=11|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнить скорость распространения звука в газах, жидкостях и металлах.

The sound from a loudspeaker must pass through two materials to reach a microphone.

![[0625-s21-22-p11-f1.svg]]

Which combination of materials gives the shortest time for the sound to reach the microphone?

|  | material 1 | material 2 |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | air | hydrogen |
| **B** | air | water |
| **C** | copper | aluminium |
| **D** | water | oil |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Candidates were not expected to know any of the relative velocities, but they should know that sound travels considerably faster in solids than in gases or liquids.
