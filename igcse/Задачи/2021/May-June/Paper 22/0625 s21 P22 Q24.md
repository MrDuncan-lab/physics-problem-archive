# Question 24 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2021 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 2 · вопрос 24  
Источник: [[_pdf/2021/0625_s21_qp_22.pdf#page=10|условие, стр. 10]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_s21_ms_22.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_s21_er.pdf#page=11|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 s21 P22]]  
Темы: [[3.2.3 Thin lenses]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Описать размер и ориентацию действительного изображения, образованного собирающей линзой.

An object is placed in front of a thin converging lens.

The diagram shows the paths of two rays from the top of the object.

![[0625-s21-22-p10-f1.svg]]

An image of the object is formed on a screen to the right of the lens.

How does this image compare with the object?

**A** It is larger and inverted.  
**B** It is larger and the same way up.  
**C** It is smaller and inverted.  
**D** It is smaller and the same way up.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> A significant number of candidates incorrectly chose option **C**. They only needed to continue the two rays on the question paper to discover their error.
