> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбрать верные утверждения о скорости электромагнитных волн в вакууме и воздухе.

Here are three statements about the speed of electromagnetic waves.

1 The speed of an electromagnetic wave in a vacuum is 340 m / s.

2 The speed of an electromagnetic wave in a vacuum is 3.0 × 10$^{8}$ m / s.

3 The speed of an electromagnetic wave in a vacuum is approximately the same as in

air.

Which statements are correct?

**A** 1 and 3  
**B** 1 only  
**C** 2 and 3  
**D** 2 only

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
