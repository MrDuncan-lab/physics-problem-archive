# Question 5 (7 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2021 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 1 · вопрос 5  
Источник: [[_pdf/2021/0625_s21_qp_31.pdf#page=7|условие, стр. 7]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_s21_ms_31.pdf#page=7|схема оценивания, стр. 7]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_s21_er.pdf#page=15|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать равнодействующую сил на чемодан, определить условия равномерного движения и давление колёс на пол.

**(a)** A man starts pulling his suitcase across the floor.

![[0625-s21-31-p7-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 5.1** (not to scale)

**(i)** Fig. 5.1 shows the horizontal forces acting on the suitcase.

Calculate the resultant horizontal force on the suitcase.

size of force =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ N

direction  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(ii)** After a short time, the suitcase is moving at a constant speed.

Suggest values for the sizes of the two horizontal forces on the suitcase when it is moving at a constant speed.

pulling force =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ (N)

friction force =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ (N) **[1]**

**(b)** The total downward force of the suitcase on the ground is 150 N. The suitcase has two wheels. Each wheel has an area of 0.60 cm$^{2}$ touching the ground.

Calculate the pressure of the suitcase on the ground.

pressure on the ground =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ N / cm$^{2}$ **[4]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** 125 (N / cm$^{2}$)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | 8 (N) | B1 |
> |  | forwards | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | same non-zero values for pulling and friction force | B1 |
> | (b) | (area = 2 × 0.60) = 1.2 (cm$^{2}$) | B1 |
> |  | (P =) F ÷ A | C1 |
> |  | 150 ÷ 1.2 OR 150 ÷ 0.60 | C1 |
> |  | 125 (N / cm$^{2}$) | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** The vast majority of candidates gained full credit. Common errors included giving 32 or 240 for the force and a very small number of candidates stated “east” without indicating which direction they thought was east.
>
> **(ii)** The majority of candidates answered this correctly, giving two equal values of the force. A common error was to put two zeroes.
>
> **(b)** This was another question that was very well answered and most candidates gained full credit by calculating the pressure as 125 (N / cm$^{2}$). A common error was to forget that the suitcase had two wheels and so these candidates gave 250 (N / cm$^{2}$) as the answer. Weaker candidates multiplied the force by the area.
