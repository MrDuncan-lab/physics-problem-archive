# Question 3 (6 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2021 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 1 · вопрос 3  
Источник: [[_pdf/2021/0625_s21_qp_31.pdf#page=5|условие, стр. 5]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_s21_ms_31.pdf#page=6|схема оценивания, стр. 6]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_s21_er.pdf#page=15|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 s21 P31]]  
Темы: [[1.5.2 Turning effect of forces]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать момент веса девочки и силу мальчика, уравновешивающую доску.

A plank balances horizontally on a log of wood, which acts as a pivot.

**(a)** A girl sits on one end of the plank, and her brother pushes down on the other end to make the plank balance horizontally. Fig. 3.1 shows the arrangement.

![[0625-s21-31-p5-f1.png]]

**Fig. 3.1** (not to scale)

Calculate the moment of the girl’s weight about the pivot and show that it is close to 480 N m. **[3]**

**(b)** The plank balances horizontally when the boy pushes down with a force *F* at a distance of 1.6 m from the pivot.

Calculate the size of force *F*.

force *F* =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ N **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** 484.8 (N m) (which is about 480 N m)
>
> **(b)** (F = ) 300 (N)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | moment = force × distance (of direction of force from pivot) | C1 |
> |  | 404 × 1.2 | C1 |
> |  | 484.8 (N m) (which is about 480 N m) | A1 |
> | (b) | c.w. moment = a.c.w moment **OR** moment of weight = moment of force/F | C1 |
> |  | 404 × 1.2 = F × 1.6 **OR** (F =) 484.8 ÷ 1.6 | C1 |
> |  | (F = ) 300 (N) | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** The vast majority of candidates calculated the correct moment. The most common errors were to multiply 404 by 1.6 or 404 × (1.2 + 1.6) or to divide the force by distance.
>
> **(b)** Again, most candidates correctly equated moments and calculated the force to be about 300 (N). The most common error was to either divide 404 by 1.6 or to multiply 404 by 1.6.
