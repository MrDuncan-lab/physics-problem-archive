# Question 6 (4 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2021 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 1 · вопрос 6

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Назвать броуновское движение и объяснить случайную траекторию дымовой частицы столкновениями с молекулами воздуха.

Fig. 6.1 shows a smoke cell. The smoke cell contains air molecules and smoke particles. A student views the motion of the smoke particles in the smoke cell by using a microscope.

![[0625-s21-31-p8-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 6.1**

Fig. 6.2 shows the path of one of the smoke particles.

![[0625-s21-31-p8-f2.png]]

**Fig. 6.2**

**(a)** State the term used for the motion of the smoke particle. **[1]**

**(b)** Explain the motion of the smoke particle in Fig. 6.2. **[3]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | Brownian (motion) | B1 |
> | (b) | any **three** from:<br>air molecules<br>(air molecules) collide with (smoke particle)<br>(air molecules) moving randomly<br>(air molecules/collisions) cause change in direction (of smoke particle)<br>(air molecules moving with) high speed<br>idea of more collisions on one side (of particle at a given time) | B3 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Most candidates knew that the motion was Brownian motion. Most of those who did not give the name realised that the motion was random. The most common error was to state that the term used was diffusion.
>
> **(b)** Stronger candidates realised that the haphazard motion of the pollen grains was a result of (invisible) air molecules bombarding the pollen grains. Weaker candidates thought that the motion was a result of pollen grains colliding with other pollen grains.
