# Question 11 (3 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2021 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 1 · вопрос 11  
Источник: [[_pdf/2021/0625_s21_qp_31.pdf#page=14|условие, стр. 14]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_s21_ms_31.pdf#page=10|схема оценивания, стр. 10]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_s21_er.pdf#page=15|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 s21 P31]]  
Темы: [[4.5.1 Electromagnetic induction]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить тип напряжения от колеблющегося магнита и назвать способы увеличения индуцированной ЭДС.

A student uses a coil and a magnet on a spring to generate an electromotive force (e.m.f.) that varies. He suspends the magnet above a coil as shown in Fig. 11.1.

![[0625-s21-31-p14-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 11.1**

**(a)** The student pulls the magnet through the coil to X and then releases it. The magnet moves up and down through the coil.

State the type of voltage induced in the coil. Tick (3) **one** box.

![[0625-s21-31-p14-f2.svg]]

**[1]**

**(b)** State **two** ways of increasing the voltage induced in the coil.

1 \_\_\_\_\_\_

2 \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | top box ticked (alternating) | B1 |
> | (b) | any **two** from:<br>increase strength of magnet/magnetic field<br>increase speed of magnet<br>increase number of turns (of wire in coil) | B2 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** The majority of candidates correctly identified the type of voltage in the coil as alternating.
>
> **(b)** The majority of candidates correctly identified two ways of increasing the induced voltage. The most common correct answers included “increase turns”, “use a stronger magnet” or “increase speed of magnet”. The most common error was to suggest increasing the current.
