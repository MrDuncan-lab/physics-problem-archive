# Question 12 (6 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2021 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 1 · вопрос 12  
Источник: [[_pdf/2021/0625_s21_qp_31.pdf#page=15|условие, стр. 15]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_s21_ms_31.pdf#page=11|схема оценивания, стр. 11]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_s21_er.pdf#page=15|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 s21 P31]]  
Темы: [[5.1.2 The nucleus]], [[5.2.4 Half-life]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнить состав атомов и ядер по обозначениям нуклидов и рассчитать оставшуюся массу радиоактивного тория.

**(a)** Table 12.1 describes four nuclides.

**Table 12.1**

| name of nuclide | plutonium-238 | thorium-234 | uranium-235 | uranium-238 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| nuclide notation | 238 Pu 94 | 234 Th 90 | 235 U 92 | 238 U 92 |

**(i)** State which **two** nuclides have the same number of protons. **[1]**

**(ii)** State which **two** nuclides have the same number of nucleons. **[1]**

**(iii)** State which **one** of the four nuclides has the most electrons orbiting when it is in a neutral atom. **[1]**

**(b)** Thorium-234 has a half-life of 24 days. A sample of radioactive material contains 40 mg of thorium-234.

Calculate the mass of thorium-234 remaining after 72 days.

mass of thorium-234 remaining =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ mg **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** 5(.0) (mg)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | uranium-235 AND uranium-238 | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | plutonium(-238) AND uranium-238 | B1 |
> | (a)(iii) | plutonium(-238) | B1 |
> | (b) | idea of 3 half-lives OR 72 ÷ 24 | B1 |
> |  | 40 ÷ 8 | C1 |
> |  | 5(.0) (mg) | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** The majority of candidates correctly identified the two uranium isotopes. The most common error was to state Pu-238 and U-238.
>
> **(ii)** The majority of candidates correctly identified Pu-238 and U-238. The most common error was to state Pu-238 and Th-34. This was probably due to confusion between nucleon number and neutron number.
>
> **(iii)** The majority of candidates correctly identified Pu-238. The most common error was to state U-238.
>
> **(b)** The majority of candidates correctly worked out that there are 3 half-lives and many of these used the 3 to work out the correct answer of 5.0 mg. The most common errors were to multiply 40 by 3 or to divide 40 by 3. A few considered only 2 half-lives to get the answer 10 mg.
