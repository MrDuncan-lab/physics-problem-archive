# Question 10 (8 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2021 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 1 · вопрос 10  
Источник: [[_pdf/2021/0625_s21_qp_31.pdf#page=12|условие, стр. 12–13]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_s21_ms_31.pdf#page=10|схема оценивания, стр. 10]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_s21_er.pdf#page=15|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 s21 P31]]  
Темы: [[4.3.2 Series and parallel circuits]], [[4.2.4 Resistance]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнить последовательное и параллельное соединения лампы с резистором и рассчитать сопротивление по напряжению и току.

**(a)** Fig. 10.1 shows a lamp and a resistor connected in a circuit.

![[0625-s21-31-p12-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 10.1**

**(i)** Determine the combined resistance of the 3.0 Ω resistor and the 5.0 Ω lamp.

combined resistance =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ Ω **[1]**

**(ii)** The reading on ammeter X is 0.50 A.

State the reading on ammeter Y.

reading on ammeter Y =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ A **[1]**

**(b)** In another circuit, the 3.0 Ω resistor and the 5.0 Ω lamp are connected in parallel, as shown in Fig. 10.2.

![[0625-s21-31-p12-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 10.2**

The lamp and resistor have changed from a series to a parallel combination.

State and explain the effect of this change on the current in ammeter X. **[3]**

**(c)** The current in a different lamp is 0.40 A when the potential difference (p.d.) across the lamp is 6.0 V.

Calculate the resistance of the lamp.

resistance of lamp =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ Ω **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(c)** 15 (Ω)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | (3.0 + 5.0 =) 8.0 (Ω) | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | 0.5(0) (A) | B1 |
> | (b) | (current/reading) increases | B1 |
> |  | (because circuit) resistance decreases | B1 |
> |  | (circuit)resistance becomes less than 3(.0) Ω OR less than smallest resistor (value)<br>OR current/reading more than doubles | B1 |
> | (c) | V= IR or (R =) V/I | C1 |
> |  | 6.0 ÷ 0.4 | C1 |
> |  | 15 (Ω) | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)(i)(ii)** The vast majority of candidates correctly evaluated the combined resistance in **(i)** and slightly fewer
>
> evaluated the current in ammeter Y in **(ii)**.
>
> **(b)** Most candidates gained partial credit for stating that the current in ammeter X would increase. Only a minority stated that the resistance decreases and the current increases. Only stronger candidates scored full credit.
>
> **(c)** This was often answered well with a correct evaluation giving the resistance of the lamp as 15 (Ω). Weaker candidates inverted the equation and so divided the current by the voltage or simply multiplied the two values.
