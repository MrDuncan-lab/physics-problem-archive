# Question 3 (10 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2021 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 3 · вопрос 3

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Применить правило моментов к качелям и найти массу по весу.

**(a)** A girl and her brother sit on opposite sides of a see-saw as shown in Fig. 3.1.

![[0625-s21-33-p4-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 3.1**

**(i)** Calculate the girl’s moment about the pivot and show that it is close to 460 N m. **[3]**

**(ii)** The see-saw is balanced horizontally.

Calculate the weight *W* of the brother.

*W* =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ N **[3]**

**(b)** The weight of the girl in Fig. 3.1 is 240 N.

Calculate the mass of the girl. Include the unit in your answer.

mass of girl =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ unit  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[4]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)(i)** 456 (Nm) (which is close to 460 Nm)
>
> **(a)(ii)** 380 (N)
>
> **(b)** 24

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | force × (perp) distance | C1 |
> |  | 240 × 1.9 | C1 |
> |  | 456 (Nm) (which is close to 460 Nm) | A1 |
> | (a)(ii) | c.w. moment = a.c.w moment **OR** girl’s moment = brother’s moment | C1 |
> |  | 240 × 1.9 = W × 1.2 **OR** 456 ÷ 1.2 **OR** 460 ÷ 1.2 | C1 |
> |  | 380 (N) | A1 |
> | (b) | W = mg in any form OR (m =) W ÷ g | C1 |
> |  | 240 ÷ 10 | C1 |
> |  | 24 | A1 |
> |  | kg | B1 |
