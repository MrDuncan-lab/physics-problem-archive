**(a)** A power station burns fossil fuel to produce electrical energy.

**(i)** State the form of the energy stored in the fuel. **[1]**

**(ii)** Some of the energy stored in the fuel is **not** converted to electrical energy.

Suggest how energy is wasted in the generator. **[2]**

**(b)** Solar panels produce electrical energy.

State **two** advantages and **two** disadvantages of using solar panels, rather than fossil fuels, to produce electrical energy.
