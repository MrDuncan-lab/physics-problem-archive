> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Построить отражённый луч и объяснить полное внутреннее отражение в прозрачном блоке.

**(a)** Fig. 8.1 shows a ray of light incident on a plane mirror.

![[0625-s21-33-p10-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 8.1**

**(i)** State which angle, a, b or c, is the angle of incidence.

angle of incidence =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(ii)** State the name of the line labelled X. **[1]**

**(iii)** The mirror reflects the ray of light.

On Fig. 8.1, draw the reflected ray. **[2]**

**(b)** Fig. 8.2 shows a ray of red light travelling through a semicircular glass block.

![[0625-s21-33-p11-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 8.2**

**(i)** Fig. 8.3 shows another ray of red light entering the semicircular glass block at 60°.

Continue the path of this ray through the glass block and into the air.

![[0625-s21-33-p11-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 8.3** **[2]**

**(ii)** Fig. 8.4 shows another ray of red light entering the semicircular glass block at 20°.

Continue the path of this ray through the glass block and into the air.

![[0625-s21-33-p11-f3.svg]]

**Fig. 8.4** **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)(iii)** angle of reflection equals angle of incidence
>
> **(b)(i)** angle of reflection equals angle of incidence
>
> **(b)(ii)** angle of refraction larger than angle of incidence
