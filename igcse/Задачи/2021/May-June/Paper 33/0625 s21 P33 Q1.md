> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить путь и среднюю скорость автомобиля и описать участки графика скорости от времени.

Fig. 1.1 shows the speed–time graph for a car travelling along a road.

![[0625-s21-33-p2-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 1.1**

**(a)** Determine the distance travelled by the car between 30 s and 60 s.

distance travelled =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ m **[3]**

**(b)** The distance travelled by the car between 60 s and 110 s is 460 m.

Calculate the average speed of the car between 60 s and 110 s.

average speed =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ m / s **[4]**

**(c)** Describe the motion of the car between 30 s and 60 s. **[1]**

**(d)** Describe the motion of the car between 60 s and 80 s. **[1]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** 480 (m)
>
> **(b)** 9.2 (m / s)
