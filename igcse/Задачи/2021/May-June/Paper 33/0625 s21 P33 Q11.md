7

Determine:

**(i)** the number of protons in one nucleus of nitrogen-13  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(ii)** the number of neutrons in one nucleus of nitrogen-13  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(iii)** the number of electrons in one neutral atom of nitrogen-13 \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(b)** Fig. 11.1 shows a counter measuring the radioactivity of a sample of nitrogen-13.

**Fig. 11.1**

**Table 11.1**

| time / min | count rate due to nitrogen-13 counts / min |
|---|---|
| 0 | 300 |
| 5 | 212 |
| 10 | 150 |
| 15 | 106 |

Calculate the half-life of nitrogen-13 using information from Table 11.1.

half-life of nitrogen-13 =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ min **[2]**
