> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнить работу и изменение энергии при попытке сдвинуть и при подъёме ящика.

Two men, X and Y, try to move identical heavy boxes, P and Q.

Man X tries to push box P along the floor. The box does not move because an object is in the way.

Man Y lifts box Q from the floor onto a shelf.

![[0625-s21-11-p5-f1.svg]]

box Q

direction of force

Which man does the most work on their box, and which box gains the most energy?

|  | man doing most work | box gaining most energy |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | X | P |
| **B** | X | Q |
| **C** | Y | P |
| **D** | Y | Q |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Weaker candidates did not realise that for the box to gain energy, work must be done on it, and that for work to be done, movement in the direction of the applied force is needed.
