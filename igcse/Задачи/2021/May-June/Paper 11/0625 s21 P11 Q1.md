> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить свойство камня, измеряемое погружением в цилиндр с водой.

The diagram shows a stone of irregular shape.

![[0625-s21-11-p2-f1.svg]]

Which property of the stone can be found by lowering it into a measuring cylinder half-filled with water?

**A** length  
**B** mass  
**C** volume  
**D** weight

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> A number of weaker candidates thought that mass could be determined by lowering an irregular object into a measuring cylinder half-full of water.
