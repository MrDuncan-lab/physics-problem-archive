> Выбрать верное утверждение о свойствах альфа-излучения и изменении элемента при альфа-распаде.

Which statement about α-particles and γ-rays is correct?

**A** α-particles are a form of electromagnetic radiation.  
**B** α-particles penetrate materials more easily than γ-rays.  
**C** The emission of an α-particle produces a nucleus of a different element.  
**D** γ-rays are more ionising than α-particles.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
