# Question 18 (1 mark)

Источник: [[_pdf/2021/0625_s21_qp_11.pdf#page=8|условие, стр. 8]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_s21_ms_11.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_s21_er.pdf#page=1|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объяснить смысл большей теплоёмкости тела через энергию повышения температуры.

The thermal capacity of object Y is greater than that of object Z.

What is a consequence of this?

**A** Object Y needs less thermal energy to melt it than object Z.  
**B** Object Y needs less thermal energy to raise its temperature by 1 °C than object Z.  
**C** Object Y needs more thermal energy to melt it than object Z.  
**D** Object Y needs more thermal energy to raise its temperature by 1 °C than object Z.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Many candidates were able to spot the connection between a greater thermal capacity and a greater quantity of energy being needed, but then some confused thermal capacity with latent heat.
