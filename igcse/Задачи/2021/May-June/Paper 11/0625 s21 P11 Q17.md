> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбрать физическое свойство, непригодное для измерения температуры термометром.

All thermometers require a physical property that changes with temperature.

Which property would **not** be suitable for use in a thermometer?

**A** pressure  
**B** volume  
**C** electrical resistance  
**D** mass

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Only stronger candidates identified mass as the non-thermometric property. Other candidates were unable to demonstrate a secure knowledge of this area.
