> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбрать способы увеличения вращающего действия катушки электродвигателя.

A class is designing a d.c. motor. To achieve a greater turning effect, three suggestions are

made.

1 Have a larger current in the coil of the motor.

2 Have a stronger magnet in the motor.

3 Put a larger number of turns on the coil.

Which suggestions will help to increase the turning effect?

**A** 1, 2 and 3  
**B** 1 and 2 only  
**C** 1 and 3 only  
**D** 2 and 3 only

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> A significant number of weaker candidates thought that one or other of the suggestions would have no effect.
