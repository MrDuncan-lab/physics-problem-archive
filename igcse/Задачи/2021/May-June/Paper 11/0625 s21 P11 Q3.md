> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить среднюю скорость поездки по графику расстояния.

The distance–time graph for a motorway journey is shown.

![[0625-s21-11-p2-f2.svg]]

What is the average speed for the journey?

**A** 50 km / h  
**B** 67 km / h  
**C** 70 km / h  
**D** 83 km / h

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Only stronger candidates answered this question well, with other responses evenly spread over the options. Candidates had to translate minutes into hours and to recognise that average speed is total distance divided by total time.
