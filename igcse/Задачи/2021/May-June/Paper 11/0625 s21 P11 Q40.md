> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить период полураспада по графику активности источника.

The graph shows the activity of a radioactive source over a period of time.

![[0625-s21-11-p16-f1.svg]]

What is the half-life of the source?

**A** 1.0 minute  
**B** 2.0 minutes  
**C** 2.5 minutes  
**D** 4.0 minutes

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> A significant number of weaker candidates thought that the half-life was half of the maximum time shown on the horizontal axis.
