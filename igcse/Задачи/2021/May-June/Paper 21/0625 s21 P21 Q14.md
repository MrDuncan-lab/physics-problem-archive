> Описать газ через расстояния и силы взаимодействия между молекулами.

The table gives information about molecules.

Which row describes a gas?

|  | force between molecules | distance between molecules |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | strong | close together |
| **B** | strong | far apart |
| **C** | negligible | far apart |
| **D** | negligible | close together |
