# Question 12 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2021 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 1 · вопрос 12  
Источник: [[_pdf/2021/0625_s21_qp_21.pdf#page=5|условие, стр. 5]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_s21_ms_21.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_s21_er.pdf#page=8|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

Темы: [[1.8 Pressure]], [[1.3 Mass and weight]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать давление книги на стол.

A book has a mass of 400 g.

The surface of the book in contact with a table has dimensions 0.10 m × 0.20 m.

The gravitational field strength *g* is 10 N / kg.

What is the pressure exerted on the table due to the book?

**A** 0.08 N / m$^{2}$  
**B** 8.0 N / m$^{2}$  
**C** 20 N / m$^{2}$  
**D** 200 N / m$^{2}$

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Although stronger candidates answered this question correctly, many others did not. Candidates needed to know the formula for pressure, and to be careful about the conversion of units.
