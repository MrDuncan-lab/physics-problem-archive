> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Распознать критический угол и отличить его от полного внутреннего отражения.

The diagram shows a narrow beam of light incident on a glass-air boundary. Some of the light

emerges along the surface of the glass and some is reflected back into the glass.

![[0625-s21-21-p9-f2.svg]]

Which row is correct?

|  | this is an example of total internal reflection | angle θ is the critical angle |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | no | yes |
| **B** | no | no |
| **C** | yes | no |
| **D** | yes | yes |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Only stronger candidates answered this correctly. At angles *infinitesimally* larger than θ, no light will emerge, so this is indeed the critical angle. And since the question declares that there is *some* light not travelling along the reflection path, it cannot be *total* internal reflection.
