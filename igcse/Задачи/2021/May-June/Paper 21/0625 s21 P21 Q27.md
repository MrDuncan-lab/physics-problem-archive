# Question 27 (1 mark)

Источник: [[_pdf/2021/0625_s21_qp_21.pdf#page=11|условие, стр. 11]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_s21_ms_21.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_s21_er.pdf#page=8|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбрать материалы, обеспечивающие наименьшее время распространения звука.

The sound from a loudspeaker must pass through two materials to reach a microphone.

![[0625-s21-21-p11-f1.svg]]

Which combination of materials gives the shortest time for the sound to reach the microphone?

|  | material 1 | material 2 |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | air | hydrogen |
| **B** | air | water |
| **C** | copper | aluminium |
| **D** | water | oil |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Candidates were not expected to know any of the relative velocities, but they should know that sound travels considerably faster in solids than in gases or liquids.
