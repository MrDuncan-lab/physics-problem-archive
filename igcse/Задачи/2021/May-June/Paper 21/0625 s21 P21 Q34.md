> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Распознать магнитный стержень по возникновению индукционного тока при его движении.

A solenoid is connected to a very sensitive ammeter. A rod is inserted into one end of the

solenoid. The ammeter shows that there is a small electric current in the solenoid while the rod is moving.

![[0625-s21-21-p14-f1.svg]]

Which rod is being inserted?

**A** a heated copper rod  
**B** a magnetised steel rod  
**C** an uncharged nylon rod  
**D** a radioactive uranium rod

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
