Источник: [[_pdf/2021/0625_s21_qp_21.pdf#page=4|условие, стр. 4]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_s21_ms_21.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_s21_er.pdf#page=8|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Указать способы передачи энергии неподвижному металлическому блоку.

A student suggests that there are several ways of transferring energy to a small, stationary block

of iron on a smooth table. He makes the following suggestions.

1 Heat it.

2 Shine light on it.

3 Pass a current through it.

Which suggestions are correct?

**A** 1 and 2 only  
**B** 1 and 3 only  
**C** 2 and 3 only  
**D** 1, 2 and 3

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> A significant number of candidates believed that shining a light onto a block of iron does not transfer energy. For that to be true, the block would have to be reflecting the light perfectly, which is impossible.
