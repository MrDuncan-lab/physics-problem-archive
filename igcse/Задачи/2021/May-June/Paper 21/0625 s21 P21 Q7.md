> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать силу трения по приложенной силе, массе и ускорению тележки.

A cart has a mass of 10 kg. A boy pushes on the cart horizontally with a force of 50 N. The cart accelerates at 0.50 m / s$^{2}$.

What is the frictional force acting on the cart?

**A** 5.0 N  
**B** 20 N  
**C** 30 N  
**D** 45 N

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The most popular option was **A**, implying that candidates had multiplied force by mass to obtain the resultant force, and had then not performed the necessary subtraction.
