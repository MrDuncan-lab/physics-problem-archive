# Question 32 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2021 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 1 · вопрос 32  
Источник: [[_pdf/2021/0625_s21_qp_21.pdf#page=13|условие, стр. 13]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_s21_ms_21.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_s21_er.pdf#page=8|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Связать освещённость фоторезистора с его сопротивлением и напряжением делителя.

The circuit diagram shows a light-dependent resistor (LDR) in a potential divider.

A voltmeter is connected across the LDR.

![[0625-s21-21-p13-f1.svg]]

Which row shows the resistance of the LDR and the potential difference (p.d.) shown on the voltmeter at a specific light level?

|  | light level | resistance of LDR | p.d. shown on the voltmeter |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | bright | low | high |
| **B** | bright | high | low |
| **C** | dim | high | high |
| **D** | dim | low | low |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Many candidates found this question challenging. When the resistance of the LDR is low, the p.d. across it is low and vice versa, so that eliminates **A** and **B**. Candidates then needed to be clear that, in dim lighting, the LDR’s resistance is high.
