# Question 26 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2021 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 1 · вопрос 26  
Источник: [[_pdf/2021/0625_s21_qp_21.pdf#page=10|условие, стр. 10]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_s21_ms_21.pdf|схема оценивания]]  
Работа: [[0625 s21 P21]]  
Темы: [[3.4 Sound]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнить давление, плотность и расстояния между сжатиями и разрежениями звуковой волны.

A sound wave is travelling outwards from a loudspeaker into the surrounding air.

Here are three statements.

1 The air pressure is lower at a rarefaction compared with undisturbed air.

2 The density of the air is less at a compression compared with undisturbed air.

3 The distance from a compression to a rarefaction equals half a wavelength.

Which statements about the sound wave are correct?

**A** 1 and 2 only  
**B** 1 and 3 only  
**C** 2 and 3 only  
**D** 1, 2 and 3

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
