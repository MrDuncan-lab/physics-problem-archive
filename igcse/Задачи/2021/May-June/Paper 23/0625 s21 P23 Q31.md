> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить ЭДС элемента по показаниям вольтметра при разомкнутом и замкнутом выключателе.

The diagram shows a circuit containing a cell, two resistors, a switch and a voltmeter.

![[0625-s21-23-p13-f2.svg]]

When the switch is open the voltmeter reads 1.5 V.

When the switch is closed the voltmeter reads 2.0 V.

What is the electromotive force (e.m.f.) of the cell?

**A** 0.5 V  
**B** 1.5 V  
**C** 2.0 V  
**D** 3.5 V

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> With the switch closed, the voltmeter is simultaneously measuring the p.d. across the resistor and the e.m.f. of the cell.
