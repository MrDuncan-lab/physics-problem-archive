# Question 30 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2021 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 3 · вопрос 30  
Источник: [[_pdf/2021/0625_s21_qp_23.pdf#page=13|условие, стр. 13]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_s21_ms_23.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_s21_er.pdf#page=13|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

Темы: [[4.2.1 Electric charge]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить перераспределение зарядов нейтральной металлической сферы вблизи положительно заряженной сферы.

An isolated metal sphere is positively charged.

It is then brought near to another isolated metal sphere that is neutral.

![[0625-s21-23-p13-f1.svg]]

positively charged

metal sphere

What happens to the charges on the neutral sphere as the positively charged sphere is brought close to it?

**A** Some positive charges move to the left and some negative charges move to the right.  
**B** Some positive charges move to the right and some negative charges move to the left.  
**C** Some positive charges move to the right, but the negative charges do not move.  
**D** The positive charges do not move, but some negative charges move to the left.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> A large number of candidates believed that positive ions can move around in a metal. It is only the electrons that do the moving.
