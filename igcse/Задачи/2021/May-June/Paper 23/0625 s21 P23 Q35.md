> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать число витков вторичной обмотки трансформатора и ток питания лампы со стороны сети.

A 12 V, 36 W lamp shines at normal brightness when connected to a mains transformer.

Assume the transformer is 100% efficient.

![[0625-s21-23-p15-f2.svg]]

Which row shows the number of turns in the secondary coil and the current in the mains wires?

|  | number of turns in secondary coil | current in the mains wire / A |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | 60 | 0.15 |
| **B** | 60 | 3.0 |
| **C** | 180 | 0.15 |
| **D** | 180 | 3.0 |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Stronger candidates were able to apply *N*p / *N*s = *V*p / *V*s, but not, apparently, *I*p*V*p = *I*s*V*s.
