> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Применить закон сохранения импульса при столкновении тел на поверхности без трения.

Object X moves to the right along a frictionless surface towards a stationary object Y, as shown.

![[0625-s21-23-p4-f2.svg]]

They make a noise as they collide and then both objects move to the right.

Which equation is correct?

**A** change in momentum of X = change in momentum of Y  
**B** impulse of force acting on X = impulse of force acting on Y  
**C** kinetic energy of X before collision = (kinetic energy of X + kinetic energy of Y) after collision  
**D** momentum of X before collision = (momentum of X + momentum of Y) after collision

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Although stronger candidates answered this correctly, all of the other options were chosen by a significant number of candidates.
