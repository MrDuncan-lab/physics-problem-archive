> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбрать формулу давления воды на дне резервуара через плотность и высоту столба жидкости.

The diagram shows a tank full of water.

![[0625-s21-23-p5-f1.svg]]

The mass of water in the tank is *m*.

The density of the water is ρ.

The height of water in the tank is *h*.

Which equation gives the pressure *p* due to the water at the bottom of the tank?

**A** *p* = *h*ρ*g*  
**B** *p* = *mgh*  
**C** *p* = *m*ρ*g*  
**D** *p* = *m*ρ*h*

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> A number of candidates were unaware of the equation *p* = ρ*gh*, even though it is explicitly required by the syllabus.
