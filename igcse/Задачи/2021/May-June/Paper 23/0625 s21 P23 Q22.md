> Рассчитать скорость света в стекле по преломлению луча из воздуха.

Light travelling at a speed of 3.0 × 10$^{8}$ m / s strikes the surface of a glass block and undergoes

refraction as it enters the block.

The diagram shows a ray of this light before and after it enters the block.

![[0625-s21-23-p9-f1.svg]]

What is the speed of light in the glass?

**A** 1.8 × 10$^{8}$ m / s  
**B** 2.0 × 10$^{8}$ m / s  
**C** 4.5 × 10$^{8}$ m / s  
**D** 5.0 × 10$^{8}$ m / s

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
