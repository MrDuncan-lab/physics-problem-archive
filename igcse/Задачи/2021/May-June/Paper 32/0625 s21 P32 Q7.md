# Question 7 (9 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2021 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 7  
Источник: [[_pdf/2021/0625_s21_qp_32.pdf#page=10|условие, стр. 10–11]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_s21_ms_32.pdf#page=8|схема оценивания, стр. 8]] · [[_pdf/2021/0625_s21_er.pdf#page=19|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 s21 P32]]  
Темы: [[3.3 Electromagnetic spectrum]], [[3.1 General properties of waves]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Дополнить электромагнитный спектр, сравнить скорости излучений и определить частоту периодических импульсов.

**(a)** Both radio waves and γ-rays (gamma) are radiations in the electromagnetic spectrum. Fig. 7.1 shows the main regions of the electromagnetic spectrum. Most regions are labelled.

![[0625-s21-32-p10-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 7.1**

**(i)** On Fig. 7.1, write the names of the radiations in the other **two** parts of the electromagnetic spectrum. **[2]**

**(ii)** State **one** use of γ-rays. **[1]**

**(iii)** A star emits radio waves and γ-rays at the same time. They all travel across the vacuum of space to the Earth’s atmosphere.

State whether the radio waves or the γ-rays, if either, arrive first at the Earth’s atmosphere. Give a reason for your answer.

statement  \_\_\_\_\_\_

reason  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(b)** Fig. 7.2 shows pulses of a signal from a star.

![[0625-s21-32-p11-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 7.2**

**(i)** Use the graph in Fig. 7.2 to determine the time between pulses.

time between pulses =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ s **[2]**

**(ii)** Determine the frequency of the pulses in Fig. 7.2.

frequency =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ Hz **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)(iii)** reason: (because they have) same speed (in a vacuum)
>
> **(b)(i)** 0.2 (s)
>
> **(b)(ii)** 5 (Hz)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |

> | (a)(i) | (1st box/next to visible) ultraviolet (rays) | B1 |

> | (a)(ii) | detection and treatment of cancer OR imaging/gamma photography OR (space) telescopes OR sterilisation of<br>food/medical equipment | B1 |

> |  | 0.2 (s) | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** The majority of candidates correctly identified X-rays, but a number could not identify ultraviolet as the other missing region.
>
> **(ii)** Most candidates identified a suitable use of gamma-rays. The most common errors were either to be too vague, e.g. “medical uses”, or to describe the use of X-rays in hospitals.
>
> **(iii)** Very few candidates gained full credit. The majority of candidates thought that gamma-rays would travel faster than radio waves in a vacuum, with explanations such as “they have a shorter wavelength”.
>
> **(b) (i)** The vast majority of candidates answered this well but some could only be awarded partial credit as answers were not precise enough.
>
> **(ii)** Very few candidates stated that frequency was equal to the number of waves/pulses per second. Only stronger candidates gained full credit here.
