> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Построить изображение и ход отражённого луча в плоском зеркале, обозначить углы.

A man looks at his reflection in a vertical mirror. This is shown from the side in Fig. 8.1.

![[0625-y20-3-p12-f1.png]]

**Fig. 8.1**

**(a)** On Fig. 8.1, accurately mark with a **clear cross** where the image of the tip **A** of the man’s beard will be. Label the cross **B**. **[2]**

**(b)** On Fig. 8.1, accurately draw a ray from the tip of the man’s beard that reflects from the mirror and goes into his eye. Use arrows to show the direction of the ray. **[2]**

**(c)** On Fig. 8.1, mark the angles of incidence and reflection at the mirror using the letters *i* and *r*. **[1]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | beard tip to cross perpendicular to mirror | 1 |
> |  | distance beard tip to mirror = distance mirror to cross B | 1 |
> | (b) | incident ray from beard tip to mirror and reflected ray along line from eye to cross B or<br>angles of incidence and reflection are approximately the same | 1 |
> |  | arrows from beard to eye | 1 |
> | (c) | angles *i* and *r* correctly labelled | 1 |
