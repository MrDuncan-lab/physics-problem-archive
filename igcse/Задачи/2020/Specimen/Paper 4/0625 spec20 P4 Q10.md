> Определить состав радиоактивного излучения по отклонению в магнитном поле с учётом фоновых показаний.

Emissions from a radioactive source pass through a hole in a lead screen and into a magnetic

field, as shown in Fig. 10.1. The experiment is carried out in a vacuum.

**Fig. 10.1**

Radiation detectors are placed at A, B and C. They give the following readings:

| A | B | C |
|---|---|---|
| 32 counts / min | 543 counts / min | 396 counts / min |

The radioactive source is then completely removed, and the readings become:

| A | B | C |
|---|---|---|
| 33 counts / min | 30 counts / min | 31 counts / min |

From the data given for positions A, B and C, deduce the type of emissions coming from the radioactive source. Explain your reasoning. **[7]**
