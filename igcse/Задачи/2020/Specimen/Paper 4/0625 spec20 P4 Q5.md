At first, the length of cylinder containing the gas is 100 cm. The pressure of the gas, shown by the pressure gauge, is 300 kPa. The area of cross-section of the cylinder is 0.12 m$^{2}$.

**(a)** **(i)** Describe the motion of the molecules of the gas. **[1]**

**(ii)** Use the idea of momentum to explain how the molecules exert a force on the walls of the cylinder. **[2]**

**(b)** The piston is moved so that the new length of cylinder occupied by the gas is 40 cm. The temperature of the gas is unchanged.

**(i)** Calculate the new pressure of the gas.

**(ii)** Explain, in terms of the behaviour of the molecules, why the pressure has changed. **[2]**
