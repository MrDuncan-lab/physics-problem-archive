# Question 34 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · February/March 2020 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 2 · вопрос 34  
Источник: [[_pdf/2020/0625_m20_qp_22.pdf#page=17|условие, стр. 17]] · [[_pdf/2020/0625_m20_ms_22.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2020/0625_m20_er.pdf#page=3|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 m20 P22]]  
Темы: [[4.3.2 Series and parallel circuits]], [[4.2.4 Resistance]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитывают общий ток источника по току одной ветви и сопротивлениям параллельных резисторов.

A cell is connected to a parallel combination of a 2.0 Ω resistor and a 4.0 Ω resistor. The current

in the 4.0 Ω resistor is 1.0 A.

![[0625-m20-22-p17-f2.svg]]

What is the current in the cell?

**A** 1.0 A  
**B** 1.5 A  
**C** 2.0 A  
**D** 3.0 A

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question proved challenging for some candidates. Many candidates thought that the total current in the cell was double the current in the lower arm of the parallel section even though the resistances in the two arms were different. This showed a failure to really understand parallel circuits.
