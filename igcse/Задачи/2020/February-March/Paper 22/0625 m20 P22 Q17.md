> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определяют испарение как причину уменьшения массы воды при постоянной температуре.

A student measures the mass of warm water in an open container over two minutes. The

container is kept at a constant temperature. The results are in the table.

| time / minutes | mass / g |
|---|---|
| 0.0 | 33.9 |
| 0.5 | 30.6 |
| 1.0 | 27.6 |
| 1.5 | 24.9 |
| 2.0 | 22.5 |

Why does the mass of the water change?

**A** The water evaporates.  
**B** The water freezes.  
**C** The water condenses.  
**D** The water boils.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**
