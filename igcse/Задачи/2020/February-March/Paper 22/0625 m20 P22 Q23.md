> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбирают сочетание размера щели и длины волны, обеспечивающее наибольшую дифракцию.

Four students **A**, **B**, **C** and **D**, investigate the diffraction of water waves through a gap.

![[0625-m20-22-p11-f1.svg]]

Each student uses a different gap size and a different wavelength for the water waves.

Which student produces the waves which have the most diffraction?

|  | gap size / cm | wavelength / cm |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | 2.0 | 1.8 |
| **B** | 3.0 | 2.1 |
| **C** | 4.0 | 2.0 |
| **D** | 5.0 | 0.9 |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**
