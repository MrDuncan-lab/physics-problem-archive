> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определяют изменение концентрации частиц в сжатиях и разрежениях при уменьшении громкости.

The diagram shows compressions and rarefactions in air as a sound wave moves from left to

![[0625-m20-22-p14-f1.svg]]

A quieter sound of the same frequency is made.

What will happen to the number of particles in a region of rarefaction and in a region of compression?

|  | number of particles in region of rarefaction | number of particles in region of compression |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | decrease | decrease |
| **B** | decrease | increase |
| **C** | increase | decrease |
| **D** | increase | increase |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Most candidates thought that the number of molecules in both the compressions and in the rarefactions would decrease when a quieter sound is played.
