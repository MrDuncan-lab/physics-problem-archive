# Question 3 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · February/March 2020 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 2 · вопрос 3  
Источник: [[_pdf/2020/0625_m20_qp_22.pdf#page=3|условие, стр. 3]] · [[_pdf/2020/0625_m20_ms_22.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2020/0625_m20_er.pdf#page=3|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнивают скорость горизонтального запуска и дальность полёта шарика на Земле и Луне.

A compressed spring projects a ball horizontally in a vacuum chamber.

On the Earth, the ball reaches the chamber floor 4.0 m in front of the spring.

An identical experiment is done on the Moon. The gravitational field strength is lower on the Moon than on the Earth.

The experimental results on the Moon are compared with those on the Earth.

Which statement is correct?

**A** The horizontal speed is greater on the Moon and the ball hits the floor 4.0 m in front of the

spring.

**B** The horizontal speed is greater on the Moon and the ball hits the floor more than 4.0 m in

front of the spring.

**C** The horizontal speed is the same on the Moon and the ball hits the floor 4.0 m in front of the

spring.

**D** The horizontal speed is the same on the Moon and the ball hits the floor more than 4.0 m in

front of the spring.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question required candidates to consider a novel situation. Most candidates were able to rule out option **A**, but candidates chose the remaining options in almost equal numbers, indicating possible guessing.
