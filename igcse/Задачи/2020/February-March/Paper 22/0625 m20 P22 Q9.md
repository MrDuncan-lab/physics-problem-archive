# Question 9 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · February/March 2020 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 2 · вопрос 9  
Источник: [[_pdf/2020/0625_m20_qp_22.pdf#page=6|условие, стр. 6]] · [[_pdf/2020/0625_m20_ms_22.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2020/0625_m20_er.pdf#page=3|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 m20 P22]]  
Темы: [[1.5.2 Turning effect of forces]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитывают силу опоры на руки спортсмена с дополнительным грузом из равновесия нескольких моментов.

An athlete with mass 70 kg trains by performing press-ups with a load on his back. The diagram shows the perpendicular distances involved.

The centre of mass of the athlete is C$_{\text{M}}$ and the centre of mass of the load he is carrying is C$_{\text{L}}$.

![[0625-m20-22-p6-f1.svg]]

The mass of the load is 6.0 kg.

What is the upward force exerted by his two arms?

**A** 54 N  
**B** 76 N  
**C** 540 N  
**D** 760 N

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The most common error in answering this question was to incorrectly think that the total weight of the athlete and his pack was placed on his arms, whereas his feet carried some of the weight.
