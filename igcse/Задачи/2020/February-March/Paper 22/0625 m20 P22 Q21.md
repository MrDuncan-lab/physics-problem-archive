# Question 21 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · February/March 2020 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 2 · вопрос 21  
Источник: [[_pdf/2020/0625_m20_qp_22.pdf#page=11|условие, стр. 11]] · [[_pdf/2020/0625_m20_ms_22.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2020/0625_m20_er.pdf#page=3|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 m20 P22]]  
Темы: [[2.3.1 Conduction]], [[2.3.2 Convection]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объясняют теплоизоляцию пенопластового стакана низкой теплопроводностью и подавлением конвекции в пузырьках воздуха.

A teacher shows his class a polystyrene cup. The cup is made from thick plastic with lots of tiny

air bubbles in it.

He asks the class why the cup is so good at keeping a hot drink warm. Three suggestions are made.

1 It contains air which is a poor thermal conductor.

2 The air is trapped in tiny bubbles so very little convection is possible.

3 The plastic is a poor thermal conductor.

Which suggestions are correct?

**A** 1 and 2 only  
**B** 1 and 3 only  
**C** 2 and 3 only  
**D** 1, 2 and 3

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Only the strongest candidates answered this question correctly. All three observations were correct, but many candidates thought that the reduction in energy loss by convection, due to the small size of the air bubbles, was incorrect.
