> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определяют одинаковые показания амперметров в последовательной цепи.

The diagram shows a battery connected to two resistors. Three ammeters M$_{1}$, M$_{2}$ and M$_{3}$ are

connected in the circuit.

![[0625-m20-12-p16-f1.svg]]

Ammeter M$_{1}$ reads 1.0 A.

What are the readings on M$_{2}$ and on M$_{3}$?

|  | reading on M$_{2}$ / A | reading on M$_{3}$ / A |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | 0.0 | 0.0 |
| **B** | 0.5 | 0.5 |
| **C** | 0.5 | 1.0 |
| **D** | 1.0 | 1.0 |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Many candidates confused the circuit with a parallel circuit. Only stronger candidates recognised that in this series circuit, the current is the same all the way round the circuit.
