> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определяют характер ускорения стального шарика при свободном падении без сопротивления воздуха.

A steel ball is dropped from the top floor of a building. Air resistance can be ignored.

Which statement describes the motion of the ball?

**A** The ball falls with constant acceleration.  
**B** The ball falls with constant speed.  
**C** The ball falls with decreasing speed.  
**D** The ball falls with increasing acceleration.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Many candidates did not realise that the acceleration of free fall is constant if friction can be ignored. Many candidates thought that the acceleration would increase.
