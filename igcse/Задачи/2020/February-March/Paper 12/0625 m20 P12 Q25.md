# Question 25 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · February/March 2020 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 25  
Источник: [[_pdf/2020/0625_m20_qp_12.pdf#page=13|условие, стр. 13]] · [[_pdf/2020/0625_m20_ms_12.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2020/0625_m20_er.pdf#page=1|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 m20 P12]]  
Темы: [[3.4 Sound]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитывают расстояние до скалы по времени возвращения эха судового гудка.

The horn on a ship makes a sound. The captain on the ship hears an echo from a cliff 4.0 s later.

The speed of sound is 340 m / s.

How far away is the cliff from the ship?

**A** 170 m  
**B** 340 m  
**C** 680 m  
**D** 1360 m

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Only stronger candidates recognised that the sound travelled to the cliff, and then reflected from the cliff and travelled back to the ship. Thus, the distance from the ship to the cliff is half the total distance travelled by the sound and is equal to the speed of the sound multiplied by half the time taken, not the total time.
