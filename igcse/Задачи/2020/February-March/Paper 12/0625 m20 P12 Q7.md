> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Находят неизвестную горизонтальную силу из условия равномерного движения автомобиля.

A car travels along a horizontal road at constant speed. Three horizontal forces act on the car. The diagram shows two of these forces.

![[0625-m20-12-p4-f1.png]]

What is the size and the direction of the third horizontal force acting on the car?

**A** 1200 N backwards  
**B** 1200 N forwards  
**C** 1800 N backwards  
**D** 1800 N forwards

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Many candidates showed little understanding of the concept of the principle of equilibrium. They did not understand that if an object is moving in the straight line at constant speed the resultant force on the object is zero. Most candidates thought that there must be a force in the direction of the movement.
