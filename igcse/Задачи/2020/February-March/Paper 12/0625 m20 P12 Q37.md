# Question 37 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · February/March 2020 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 37  
Источник: [[_pdf/2020/0625_m20_qp_12.pdf#page=18|условие, стр. 18]] · [[_pdf/2020/0625_m20_ms_12.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2020/0625_m20_er.pdf#page=1|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 m20 P12]]  
Темы: [[4.5.4 Force on a current-carrying conductor]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определяют изменения направления тока и поля, обращающие силу на проводник.

A current-carrying conductor is placed between two magnetic poles. The current causes a force

to act on the wire.

![[0625-m20-12-p18-f1.svg]]

Three other arrangements P, Q and R, of the wire and magnetic poles are set up as shown.

![[0625-m20-12-p18-f2.svg]]

Which arrangements will cause a force in the opposite direction to that shown in the top diagram?

**A** P, Q and R  
**B** P and Q only  
**C** P only  
**D** R only

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Only the strongest candidates answered this question correctly and the most common response was **D** (circuit R only). Many candidates did not read the question with sufficient care and thought it was asking for the circuits which produced a force on the wire in the same direction as in the original example, not the opposite direction.
