# Question 39 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · February/March 2020 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 39  
Источник: [[_pdf/2020/0625_m20_qp_12.pdf#page=19|условие, стр. 19]] · [[_pdf/2020/0625_m20_ms_12.pdf|схема оценивания]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определяют тип излучения по уменьшению счёта с расстоянием от источника.

A student measures the rate at which ionising radiation is emitted from a radioactive substance.

He places a detector at different distances from the radioactive source.

![[0625-m20-12-p19-f1.svg]]

The table shows how the reading on the counter varies with distance *d*.

The readings on the counter are corrected for background radiation.

| distance *d* / cm | 0 | 2 | 4 | 6 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| counter reading / counts per minute | 1250 | 115 | 0 | 0 |

Which type of ionising radiation is being emitted by the substance?

**A** α-particles  
**B** β-particles  
**C** γ-rays  
**D** X-rays

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**
