# Question 10 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · February/March 2020 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 10  
Источник: [[_pdf/2020/0625_m20_qp_12.pdf#page=5|условие, стр. 5]] · [[_pdf/2020/0625_m20_ms_12.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2020/0625_m20_er.pdf#page=1|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнивают преимущества и недостатки ветровой энергетики.

Which row describes an advantage and a disadvantage of wind turbines?

|  | advantage | disadvantage |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | no fuel needed | harmful gases released |
| **B** | variable supply | fuel needed |
| **C** | no harmful gases released | variable supply |
| **D** | constant supply | noisy |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Although many candidates were able to identify the advantages and disadvantages of using wind turbines to generate electricity, a significant number incorrectly thought that they produced a constant output irrespective of weather conditions.
