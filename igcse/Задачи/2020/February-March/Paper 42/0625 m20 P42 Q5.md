# Question 5 (7 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · February/March 2020 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 2 · вопрос 5  
Источник: [[_pdf/2020/0625_m20_qp_42.pdf#page=7|условие, стр. 7]] · [[_pdf/2020/0625_m20_ms_42.pdf#page=12|схема оценивания, стр. 12]] · [[_pdf/2020/0625_m20_er.pdf#page=9|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 m20 P42]]  
Темы: [[2.3.1 Conduction]], [[2.3.3 Radiation]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Распознают способы теплопередачи, объясняют проводимость металла частицами и выбирают отражающую поверхность автомобильного экрана.

**(a)** Complete the sentences with words that describe the main process of thermal energy transfer in each case.

A man goes for a walk on a cold day. He touches a metal gate, which removes thermal

energy from his hands by  \_\_\_\_\_\_ He holds the sides of a cup containing a

hot drink. His hands gain thermal energy by  \_\_\_\_\_\_ Some farm workers have

lit a fire. The man warms his hands by the side of the fire. His hands gain thermal energy by **[3]**

**(b)** Describe in terms of particles the transfer of thermal energy through the metal of the gate after transfer from the man’s hands. **[2]**

**(c)** Fig. 5.1 shows a car on a sunny day in a hot country.

![[0625-m20-42-p7-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 5.1**

The object labelled A is placed inside the windscreen. It is used by the owner of the car to reduce the temperature rise of the air in the car.

Ring the most suitable material for the outer surface of object A. Explain your choice.

**dull black dull white shiny black shiny white**

explanation  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(c)** best reflector

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** It was quite common for candidates to incorrectly quote convection as one of the processes of thermal energy transfer.
>
> **(b)** Most answers referred to vibration of particles and overlooked the fact that the question was about thermal energy transfer in a metal. Very few candidates gave complete answers mentioning the movement of delocalised electrons.
>
> **(c)** Most candidates correctly selected ‘shiny white’ and gave an acceptable explanation. Some explanations were too vague, e.g. ‘shiny white reflects heat’. A valid comparison needed to be made for full credit to be awarded.
