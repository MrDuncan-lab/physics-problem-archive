# Question 1 (8 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · February/March 2020 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 2 · вопрос 1  
Источник: [[_pdf/2020/0625_m20_qp_42.pdf#page=2|условие, стр. 2]] · [[_pdf/2020/0625_m20_ms_42.pdf#page=10|схема оценивания, стр. 10]] · [[_pdf/2020/0625_m20_er.pdf#page=9|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 m20 P42]]  
Темы: [[1.2 Motion]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитывают ускорение ракеты, строят график скорости и определяют высоту подъёма по площади под графиком.

A rocket is launched vertically upwards from the ground. The rocket travels with uniform acceleration from rest. After 8.0 s, the speed of the rocket is 120 m / s.

**(a)** Calculate the acceleration of the rocket.

acceleration =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(b)** **(i)** On Fig. 1.1, draw the graph for the motion of the rocket in the first 8.0 s.

![[0625-m20-42-p2-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 1.1** **[1]**

**(ii)** Use the graph to determine the height of the rocket at 8.0 s.

height =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(iii)** From time = 8.0 s to time = 20.0 s, the rocket rises with increasing speed but with decreasing acceleration.

From time = 20.0 s to time = 25.0 s, the rocket has a constant speed of less than 200 m / s.

On Fig. 1.1, draw the graph for this motion. **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** (a) = 15 m / s$^{2}$
>
> **(b)(ii)** (h=) 480 m

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | (a=)Δv / Δt in any form OR (a=)Δv / Δt OR (a)=120 / 8 | C1 |
> |  | (a) = 15 m / s$^{2}$ | A1 |
> | (b)(i) | straight line from (0,0) to (8,120) | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | (h = A =) ½ × 120 × 8 | C1 |
> |  | (h=) 480 m | A1 |
> | (b)(iii) | <u>rising curve from 8 s to 20 s</u> | B1 |
> |  | decreasing gradient from 8 s to 20 s | B1 |
> |  | horizontal from 20 s to 25 s AND below 200 m / s, AND above 120 m / s | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** This was correctly answered by nearly all candidates. A few candidates made an error in the unit in their answer.
>
> **(b) (i)** Mistakes in drawing a straight line to the correct point were rare. A few candidates did not interpret the scale correctly and plotted (9, 120) or (8, 110) or were completely wrong.
>
> **(ii)** Most candidates calculated the correct height having recognised the need to use the area under the graph. Others incorrectly used distance = speed x time or Pythagoras theorem to calculate the length of the line.
>
> **(iii)** A number of candidates incorrectly assumed that a straight line with a positive gradient was required. However, in most cases these candidates gained some credit for the correct constant speed completion of the graph. A small number of candidates drew the horizontal section at 200 m/s instead of below 200 m/s.
