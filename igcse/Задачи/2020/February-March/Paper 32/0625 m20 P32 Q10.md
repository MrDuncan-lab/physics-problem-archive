> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объясняют регулирование силы электромагнита реостатом и рассчитывают сопротивление его цепи.

Fig. 10.1 shows an arrangement for making an electromagnet.

![[0625-m20-32-p14-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 10.1**

**(a)** **(i)** State a material which is suitable for the core of the electromagnet. **[1]**

**(ii)** State the name for component X in Fig. 10.1. **[1]**

**(iii)** Describe and explain how component X varies the strength of the electromagnet. **[2]**

**(b)** The switch is closed. The reading on the ammeter is 1.5 A.

Calculate the resistance of the circuit.

resistance =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ Ω **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** (R =) 8.0 (Ω)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | (soft) iron (bar) | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | <u>variable resistor</u> | B1 |
> | (a)(iii) | any **two** from:<br>increasing the resistance<br>(will) decrease the current<br>(decreasing the current will) decrease the strength (of the electromagnet) | B2 |
> | (b) | (R =) V ÷ I in any form | C1 |
> |  | (R =) 12 ÷ 1.5 | C1 |
> |  | (R =) 8.0 (Ω) | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** The majority of candidates answered this question correctly. The most common error was to give copper instead of iron, or to add steel to a correct answer of iron.
>
> **(ii)** Most candidates identified the variable resistor.
>
> **(iii)** Many candidates found this question challenging, with only stronger candidates able to explain that by varying the resistance, the current in the coil and therefore the strength of the electromagnet was varied.
>
> **(b)** Many candidates answered this well. The most common error was to recall an incorrect arrangement of V = IR.
