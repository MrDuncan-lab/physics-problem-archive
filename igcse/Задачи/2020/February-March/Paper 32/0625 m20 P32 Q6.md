> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнивают частицы твёрдого тела и газа и объясняют, что броуновское движение показывает о молекулах воздуха.

**(a)** Table 6.1 gives a list of statements about molecules in gases and solids.

**Table 6.1**

![[0625-m20-32-p8-f1.svg]]

molecules are free to move around from place to place

molecules are far apart compared to their size

molecules can only vibrate about a fixed position

molecules change position randomly

Put **one** tick in every row to indicate whether each statement refers to a gas or a solid. **[4]**

![[0625-m20-32-p8-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 6.1**

![[0625-m20-32-p8-f3.svg]]

**(i)** State the term given to the movement of the smoke particle. **[1]**

**(ii)** State what the motion of the smoke particle shows about air molecules. **[3]**
