> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объясняют производство электричества ветряной турбиной и сравнивают её с угольной электростанцией.

Fig. 5.1 shows a wind turbine.

![[0625-m20-32-p7-f1.png]]

**Fig. 5.1**

**(a)** Describe how the wind turbine produces electrical energy. **[3]**

**(b)** Wind turbines are used in many countries to replace coal-fired power stations.

**(i)** State **one** disadvantage of using wind turbines compared to coal-fired power stations. **[1]**

**(ii)** State **two** advantages of using wind turbines instead of coal-fired power stations.

1 \_\_\_\_\_\_

2 \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | Any **three** from:<br>kinetic energy (of wind / air)<br>turns / drives turbine (blades)<br>(turbine blades) turn generator<br>coil turns in magnetic field | B3 |
> | (b)(i) | any **one** from:<br>a dilute source of energy<br>dependent on weather / intermittent supply | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | any **two** from:<br>renewable source of energy<br>no atmospheric pollution<br>conserves fossil fuels owtte<br>do not contribute to global warming | B2 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Only the strongest candidates answered this question fully correctly. A number of candidates thought it sufficient to state that the wind turned the turbine blades and this then produced electrical energy.
>
> **(b) (i)** Most candidates identified a disadvantage of using wind turbines compared to coal fired power stations.
>
> **(ii)** Many candidates gave vague or insufficient responses, such as “it is more eco-friendly” or “it has less pollution”.
