# Question 1 (6 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · February/March 2020 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 1

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определяют среднюю толщину монет и описывают измерение объёма одной монеты методом вытеснения воды.

**(a)** A student places 8 similar coins in a pile, as shown in Fig. 1.1.

![[0625-m20-32-p2-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 1.1** (not to scale)

The height of the pile of coins is 2.4 cm.

Calculate the average thickness of one coin.

average thickness =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm **[2]**

**(b)** Fig. 1.2 shows the pile of coins, a measuring cylinder and a beaker containing some water.

![[0625-m20-32-p2-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 1.2** (not to scale)

Describe how the student can measure the volume of **one** of the coins using the set-up shown in Fig. 1.2. **[4]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** (average thickness =) 0.3 (cm)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | (average thickness =) 2.4 ÷ 8 | C1 |
> |  | (average thickness =) 0.3 (cm) | A1 |
> | (b) | any **four** from:<br>measuring cylinder partially filled with water / displacement can filled with water<br>volume of water recorded / empty measuring cylinder under spout<br>coin(s) in water OR water covers all coin(s)<br>new volume noted / displaced water collected in measuring cylinder<br>( average) volume of a coin = increase in volume OR increase in volume ÷ number of coins | B4 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Most candidates gave a correct answer of 0.3 cm.
>
> **(b)** Most candidates were well prepared for questions involving the displacement method of determining volume and gave clear concise descriptions.
