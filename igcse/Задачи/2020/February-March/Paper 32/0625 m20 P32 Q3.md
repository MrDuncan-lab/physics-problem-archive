> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Находят вес мяча, характеризуют участки графика скорости и определяют расстояния падения.

A student drops a ball from a high window.

**(a)** The mass of the ball is 0.12 kg.

Calculate the weight of the ball.

weight =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ N **[3]**

**(b)** Fig. 3.1 shows the speed of the ball while it is falling. The points **S**, **T**, **U**, **V** and **W** are shown

![[0625-m20-32-p4-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 3.1**

Draw **one** line from each section of the graph to the correct description of the motion.

One has been drawn for you.

![[0625-m20-32-p4-f2.svg]]

moving with constant speed

**U – V**

slowing down **[2]**

**(c)** Determine the distance fallen by the ball in section **U – V** of the graph.

distance =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ m **[3]**

**(d)** State the distance fallen by the ball in section **V – W** of the graph.

distance =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ m **[1]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** (weight =) 1.2 (N)
>
> **(c)** 40 (m)

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Most candidates correctly calculated the weight of the ball. Weaker candidates transformed the mass in kilograms to a mass in grams. Another common error was to divide by 10 instead of multiplying.
>
> **(b)** Only stronger candidates recognised **T – U** as decreasing acceleration. The most common error was to link to slowing down. Most candidates recognised **U – V** as moving with constant speed, but many linked **U – V** to at rest.
