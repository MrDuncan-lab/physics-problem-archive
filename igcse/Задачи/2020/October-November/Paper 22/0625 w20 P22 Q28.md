> Определить притяжение между магнитами и металлическими предметами.

A student sets up four experiments using bar magnets and other metal objects. The N and S

poles of the bar magnets are labelled N and S.

1 2

![[0625-w20-22-p11-f1.svg]]

3 4

![[0625-w20-22-p11-f2.svg]]

Which pairs attract each other?

**A** 1 and 2  
**B** 1 and 3  
**C** 2 and 4  
**D** 3 and 4

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This was answered well, and most candidates recognised that an unmagnetised iron bar is attracted to both N-poles and S-poles.
