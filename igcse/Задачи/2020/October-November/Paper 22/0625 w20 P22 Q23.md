> Определить поворот отражённого луча и условия повышения чувствительности оптического рычага.

An optical lever is a very sensitive device for detecting small rotations. A lamp sends a narrow

beam of light on to a small plane mirror attached to a shaft whose rotation is to be measured. The operation of the device is shown in plan view.

![[0625-w20-22-p9-f1.svg]]

The beam from the lamp reflects from the mirror to give a small spot of light on a scale placed just above the lamp. The shaft and mirror rotate through 1°. The spot of light moves along the scale.

The table shows the angle θ through which the reflected beam rotates and the conditions required for high sensitivity.

Which row is correct?

|  | angle θ | to achieve high sensitivity |
|---|---|---|
| **A** **B** **C** **D** | 1° 1° 2° 2° | the lamp and scale need to be as close to the mirror as possible the lamp and scale need to be as far from the mirror as possible the lamp and scale need to be as close to the mirror as possible the lamp and scale need to be as far from the mirror as possible |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
