> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнить гидростатическое давление на глубине с атмосферным давлением.

A research submarine is at a depth of 10 000 m below the surface of the sea.

The average density of the water above the submarine is 1030 kg / m$^{3}$.

The atmospheric pressure at the surface of the sea is 103 000 Pa.

How many times greater is the pressure due to the sea water than the atmospheric pressure?

**A** 10  
**B** 100  
**C** 1000  
**D** 100 000

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
