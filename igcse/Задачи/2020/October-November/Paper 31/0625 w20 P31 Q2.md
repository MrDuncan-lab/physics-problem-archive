# Question 2 (8 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2020 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 1 · вопрос 2

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить массу и плотность жидкости, рассчитать вес пустого стакана.

Fig. 2.1 shows a beaker containing liquid on a top pan balance.

![[0625-w20-31-p4-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 2.1**

The mass of the empty beaker is 400 g.

**(a)** Using the information in Fig. 2.1, determine the mass of the liquid in the beaker.

mass =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ g **[1]**

**(b)** The beaker contains 750 cm$^{3}$ of liquid.

Calculate the density of the liquid.

density =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ g / cm$^{3}$ **[3]**

**(c)** Calculate the weight of the empty beaker.

weight =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ N **[4]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** (ρ =) 0.93 (g / cm$^{3}$)
>
> **(c)** (weight =) 4(.0) (N)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | (1100 – 400 =) 700 (g) | B1 |
> | (b) | density = mass ÷ volume OR ρ *= m ÷ V* in any form | C1 |
> |  | (ρ =) 700 ÷ 750 | C1 |
> |  | (ρ =) 0.93 (g / cm$^{3}$) | A1 |
> | (c) | 400 (g) = 0.4 (kg) | B1 |
> |  | *w* = *m* × *g* in any form | C1 |
> |  | 0.4 × 10 | C1 |
> |  | (weight =) 4(.0) (N) | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** The vast majority of candidates answered correctly. The most common error was failing to read the question carefully enough and simply writing the mass reading on the top pan balance.
>
> **(b)** With allowance for an error carried forward, the vast majority of candidates scored full credit here. The most common error was to recall an inverted form of the equation for density.
>
> **(c)** Most candidates answered this well. The most common error which resulted in candidates not gaining full credit, was failing to convert the mass from grams to kilograms.
