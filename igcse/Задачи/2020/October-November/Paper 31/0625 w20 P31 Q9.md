> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Различить магнит и ненамагниченный магнитный материал по притяжению и отталкиванию концов стержней.

A student has a permanent bar magnet and two metal bars, as shown in Fig. 9.1.

![[0625-w20-31-p16-f1.svg]]

![[0625-w20-31-p16-f2.svg]]

metal bar PQ metal bar RS

**Fig. 9.1**

The student tests bar PQ and bar RS separately. He holds the N pole of the permanent bar magnet close to each end of each metal bar. Table 9.1 shows the results of the tests.

**Table 9.1**

| end of metal bar | result of test with N pole |
|---|---|
| P | attracted |
| Q | repelled |
| R | attracted |
| S | attracted |

Deduce whether each metal bar is a magnet, an unmagnetised magnetic material or a non-magnetic material.

Give a reason for each of your answers.

1. metal bar PQ  \_\_\_\_\_\_

2. metal bar RS  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[4]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> (because magnet) repels end Q
>
> (because magnet) attracts both ends (of R–S)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> |  | 1 (bar P–Q is a) (permanent) magnet | M1 |
> |  | (because magnet) repels end Q | A1 |
> |  | 2 (bar R–S is an) unmagnetised magnetic material | M1 |
> |  | (because magnet) attracts both ends (of R–S) | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The majority of candidates scored full credit. The most common error was to state that metal bar RS was made from a non-magnetic material.
