# Question 10 (7 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2020 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 1 · вопрос 10  
Источник: [[_pdf/2020/0625_w20_qp_31.pdf#page=17|условие, стр. 17–18]] · [[_pdf/2020/0625_w20_ms_31.pdf#page=13|схема оценивания, стр. 13]] · [[_pdf/2020/0625_w20_er.pdf#page=16|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w20 P31]]  
Темы: [[4.2.4 Resistance]], [[4.3.2 Series and parallel circuits]], [[4.3.1 Circuit diagrams and circuit components]], [[4.2.2 Electric current]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Подключить вольтметр, указать носители тока и сравнить сопротивление и ток в проводах.

A student determines the resistance of a piece of metal wire XY. Fig. 10.1 shows the wire connected in the circuit.

![[0625-w20-31-p17-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 10.1**

**(a)** **(i)** The student measures the potential difference (p.d.) across the wire XY. On Fig. 10.1, show the voltmeter correctly connected. Use the correct symbol. **[1]**

**(ii)** There is a current in the wire. State the name of the particles that flow through the wire. **[1]**

**(iii)** The student removes wire XY and replaces it with another wire CD. Wire CD is the same length and is made of the same material as wire XY, but thinner.

State any difference between the current in wire CD and the current in wire XY. Explain your answer. **[2]**

**(iv)** State the unit used for the electromotive force (e.m.f.) of the battery. **[1]**

**(b)** **(i)** The resistance of wire CD is 8.7 Ω and the resistance of the variable resistor is 7.4 Ω.

Determine the combined resistance of the wire CD and the variable resistor.

resistance =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ Ω **[1]**

**(ii)** The current in the variable resistor is 0.40 A.

State the current in wire CD.

current =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ A **[1]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | correct symbol for voltmeter correctly connected | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | electrons | B1 |
> | (a)(iii) | (current in CD) decreases | B1 |
> |  | (because thinner wire / CD has) increased / bigger resistance | B1 |
> | (a)(iv) | volt(s) | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | 16.1 (Ω) | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | 0.40 (A) | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** Very few candidates answer this question correctly. The most common error was to draw the voltmeter in series with the wire, whilst those candidates who remembered it should be connected in parallel drew the voltmeter in parallel with the variable resistor.
>
> **(ii)** The majority of candidates answered this well. The most common errors were to state beta particles or just charged particles.
>
> **(iii)** The majority of candidates stated that the size of the current would decrease, but only the strongest linked this to an increase in the resistance of wire CD compared to XY.
>
> **(iv)** This was usually answered correctly. The most common error was to simply write voltage.
>
> **(b) (i)** The majority of candidates correctly calculated the combined resistance as 16.1 ohms.
>
> **(ii)** The vast majority of candidates did not realise that the current in the variable resistor was the same as the current in wire CD.
