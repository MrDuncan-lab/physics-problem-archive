# Question 8 (6 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2020 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 1 · вопрос 8  
Источник: [[_pdf/2020/0625_w20_qp_31.pdf#page=15|условие, стр. 15]] · [[_pdf/2020/0625_w20_ms_31.pdf#page=12|схема оценивания, стр. 12]] · [[_pdf/2020/0625_w20_er.pdf#page=16|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w20 P31]]  
Темы: [[3.3 Electromagnetic spectrum]], [[3.4 Sound]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Указать излучение телефона, диапазон слуха и различия волн двух звуковых нот.

Fig. 8.1 shows a mobile (cell) phone.

![[0625-w20-31-p15-f1.png]]

**Fig. 8.1**

**(a)** **(i)** State the type of electromagnetic wave used for the mobile phone signal. **[1]**

**(ii)** The screen of the mobile phone emits visible light. State **one** type of electromagnetic wave with a shorter wavelength than visible light. **[1]**

**(b)** The mobile phone produces sound waves.

**(i)** State the range of audible frequencies for a healthy human ear. Include the unit. **[2]**

**(ii)** The ring tone of the mobile phone consists of two musical notes, note A and note B.

Note A is louder and is higher in pitch than note B.

Fig. 8.2 shows note A displayed on an oscilloscope screen.

![[0625-w20-31-p15-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 8.2**

Note B is displayed on the same oscilloscope screen as note A. Describe the differences between the wave for note B and the wave for note A. You may draw on Fig. 8.2 to show the differences. **[2]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | microwave | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | ultraviolet / UV **OR** X-ray **OR** γ- / gamma (rays) | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | 20 to 20 000 | B1 |
> |  | Hz or hertz | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | (note B has) smaller amplitude<br>OR<br><u>note A has larger amplitude</u> | B1 |
> |  | (note B has) lower frequency OR longer wavelength<br>OR<br><u>note A has higher frequency OR shorter wavelength</u> | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** Very few candidates correctly identified microwaves as the electromagnetic wave used for a mobile phone signal.
>
> **(ii)** The vast majority of candidates answered this correctly.
>
> **(b) (i)** Most candidates answered this question well. A common error was to give the upper limit of the range as 20 000 kHz.
>
> **(ii)** The majority of candidates gained at least partial credit here. The most common error was a contradiction such as “*the wave for note B has a lower frequency and a shorter wavelength*”.
