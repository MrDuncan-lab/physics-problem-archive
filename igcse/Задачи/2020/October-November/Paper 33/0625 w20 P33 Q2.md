> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать массу ребёнка и плечо силы на качелях, назвать сопротивление движению.

**(a)** Fig. 2.1 shows two children sitting on a see-saw.

![[0625-w20-33-p4-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 2.1** (not to scale)

**(i)** The weight of child A is 125 N.

Calculate the mass of child A. Include the unit in your answer.

mass of child A =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ unit  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

**(ii)** Fig. 2.1 shows child A and child B sitting in positions which balance the see-saw horizontally.

Using the information in Fig. 2.1, determine the distance *X*.

distance *X* =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ m **[3]**

**(b)** The person in Fig. 2.2 is pushing a child on a swing.

![[0625-w20-33-p5-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 2.2**

State the name of the force that acts against the motion of the swing. **[1]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)(i)** 12.5
>
> **(a)(ii)** (X=) 1.0 (m)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | *W* = *mg* OR *W* = 10 × *m* | C1 |
> |  | 12.5 | A1 |
> |  | kg | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | 250 × 0.5 OR X × 125 | C1 |
> |  | clockwise moment = anticlockwise moment | C1 |
> |  | (X=) 1.0 (m) | A1 |
> | (b) | air resistance / drag | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** Most candidates attempted this part with many getting the correct answer and unit. A common error was to multiply the weight by 10 instead of dividing by 10.
>
> **(ii)** Many candidates answered this question well and showed full working. Weaker candidates often gained partial credit for the moments equation, gave an incorrect answer with no working or gave no response.
>
> **(b)** Many correct answers were seen for the force. A common incorrect answer was kinetic energy.
