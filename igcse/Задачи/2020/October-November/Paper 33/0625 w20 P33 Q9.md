# Question 9 (8 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2020 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 3 · вопрос 9

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объяснить притяжение железа электромагнитом и работу реле звонка при изменении питания.

**(a)** A clamp and stand hold a soft-iron bar above a bench. A coil of wire is wrapped round the soft-iron bar. The coil of wire is part of an electric circuit. Fig. 9.1 shows the arrangement.

![[0625-w20-33-p16-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 9.1**

Switch S is closed. A student holds a soft-iron disc close to the bar and releases the disc. The disc becomes attached to the bar as shown in Fig. 9.1.

Explain why the soft-iron disc is attracted to the soft-iron bar. **[3]**

**(b)** The circuit in Fig. 9.1 is used to operate a bell in a different circuit, as shown in Fig. 9.2.

![[0625-w20-33-p17-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 9.2**

**(i)** Switch S is closed. The soft-iron arm is attracted to the soft-iron bar.

Explain why the bell operates when the switch S is opened. **[4]**

**(ii)** The switch S is closed. The soft-iron arm is attracted to the soft-iron bar.

The battery in the circuit containing the soft-iron bar becomes fully discharged.

State and explain whether the bell operates. **[1]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** coil OR soft iron rod / bar (becomes a electro)magnet

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | current (in coil / circuit) | B1 |
> |  | magnetic (effect of current) | C1 |
> |  | coil OR soft iron rod / bar (becomes a electro)magnet | A1 |
> | (b)(i) | any **four** from:<br>residual magnetism<br>circuit broken OR no current in electromagnet / circuit<br>electromagnet switched off / no longer attracts the (pivoted) arm<br>(pivoted) arm falls<br>current in bell OR alarm / bell circuit complete | B4 |
> | (b)(ii) | bell rings | M0 |
> |  | (because) electromagnet switched off / no longer attracts the (pivoted) arm / (pivoted) arm falls | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Many candidates knew that magnetism was involved. A fuller explanation was required to gain further credit.
>
> **(b) (i)** Few correct answers were seen with many candidates offering brief statements or no response. The magnetic properties of iron and electromagnets did not seem to be fully understood.
>
> **(ii)** Very few candidates recognised that the bell had its own battery.
