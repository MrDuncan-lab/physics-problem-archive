total stopping distance =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ m **[1]**

**(ii)** Suggest **one** factor that could increase the total stopping distance. **[1]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)(i)** 0 / zero

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** This was answered very well, with most candidates gaining full credit. A common error was to add all three forces.
>
> **(ii)** Not all candidates recognised that the lorry would continue at the same speed if there was no resultant force acting.
>
> **(b) (i)** The majority of candidates gave the correct answer.
>
> **(ii)** Often more detailed answers were needed. For example, answers simply stating “friction” or “speed” were too vague. The question asked for a factor that could increase the total stopping distance, so “reduced friction” or “higher speed” would have been sufficient.
