> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Измерить плотность пластилина двумя способами, сравнить результаты и выбрать правильное считывание мениска.

In this experiment, you will determine the density of modelling clay by two methods.

**Method 1**

**(a)** **(i)** Measure the length *l*, width *w* and height *h* of the sample A of modelling clay. Do **not** change the shape of the sample.

*l* =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm

*w* =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm

*h* =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm **[1]**

**(ii)** Calculate the volume *V*$_{\text{A}}$ of sample A using the equation *V*$_{\text{A}}$ = *l* × *w* × *h*.

*V*$_{\text{A}}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm$^{3}$ **[1]**

**(iii)** Measure and record the mass *m*$_{\text{A}}$ of sample A of modelling clay using the balance provided.

*m*$_{\text{A}}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ g **[1]**

**(iv)** Calculate the density ρ$_{\text{A}}$ of sample A using the equation ρ$_{\text{A}}$ = $\dfrac{m_{A}}{V}_{\text{A}}$ .

Give your answer to a suitable number of significant figures for this method and include the unit.

ρ$_{\text{A}}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**Method 2**

**(b)** Pour approximately 50 cm$^{3}$ of the water provided into the measuring cylinder.

**(i)** Record the volume *V*$_{1}$ of water in the measuring cylinder.

*V*$_{1}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm$^{3}$

Carefully lower sample B of the same modelling clay into the measuring cylinder until it is completely covered with water.

Record the new reading *V*$_{2}$ of the water level in the measuring cylinder.

*V*$_{2}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm$^{3}$

Calculate the volume *V*$_{\text{B}}$ of sample B using the equation *V*$_{\text{B}}$ = *V*$_{2}$ – *V*$_{1}$.

*V*$_{\text{B}}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm$^{3}$ **[1]**

**(ii)** Measure and record the mass *m*$_{\text{B}}$ of sample B of measuring clay using the balance provided.

*m*$_{\text{B}}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ g

Calculate the density ρ$_{\text{B}}$ of sample B using the equation ρ$_{\text{B}}$ = $\dfrac{m_{B}}{V}_{\text{B}}$ .

Give your answer to a suitable number of significant figures for this experiment and include the unit.

ρ$_{\text{B}}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

State whether your results agree with the suggestion. Justify your answer by reference to your results.

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)–(iv)** The majority of candidates successfully recorded the *l*, *w* and *h* values in cm. The volume
>
> calculation was usually correct, and the mass value was within the expected range. The density needed to be given to two or three significant figures and with the unit g/cm$^{3}$. A few candidates tried to convert to kg/m$^{3}$ which would have been acceptable but most who attempted the conversion did so incorrectly.
>
> **(b) (i)** Most candidates recorded realistic volumes and completed the calculation correctly.
>
> **(ii)** Candidates were expected to have carried out the experiment with care to obtain a second value of density within 10 per cent of the first value of density.
>
> **(c)** Candidates were required to make a judgement based on their own results. The justification needed to be clear and consistent with the results to gain credit. Most candidates gained partial credit but many made a vague comment without showing any understanding of the concept of results being beyond the limits of experimental inaccuracy.
>
> **(d)** Candidates were expected to know the best way to read a value from a measuring cylinder but significant numbers of candidates did not tick the appropriate boxes “perpendicular to the scale” and “in line with the bottom of the meniscus”.
