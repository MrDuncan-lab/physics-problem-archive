# Question 13 (1 mark)

Источник: [[_pdf/2020/0625_w20_qp_12.pdf#page=6|условие, стр. 6]] · [[_pdf/2020/0625_w20_ms_12.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2020/0625_w20_er.pdf#page=4|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать давление человека на опору по весу и площади ступней.

A man who weighs 540 N stands with both feet on the ground. The area of contact between one

foot and the ground is 0.015 m$^{2}$.

What is the pressure exerted on the floor by the man?

**A** 1800 N / m$^{2}$  
**B** 3600 N / m$^{2}$  
**C** 18 000 N / m$^{2}$  
**D** 36 000 N / m$^{2}$

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Many candidates did not read this question carefully enough. The question stated the area of one foot of the man and that he was standing with two feet on the ground. Nevertheless, a majority of candidates found the pressure exerted when the man was standing on one foot.
