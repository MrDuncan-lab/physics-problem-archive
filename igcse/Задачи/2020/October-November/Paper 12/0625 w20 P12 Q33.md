# Question 33 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2020 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 33  
Источник: [[_pdf/2020/0625_w20_qp_12.pdf#page=12|условие, стр. 12]] · [[_pdf/2020/0625_w20_ms_12.pdf|схема оценивания]]  
Работа: [[0625 w20 P12]]  
Темы: [[4.4 Electrical safety]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбрать предохранитель с учётом рабочего и пускового токов прибора.

An electrical appliance is powered from a mains supply.

The appliance normally uses a current of 3 A, but the current briefly rises to 4 A at the instant the appliance is switched on. The cable to the appliance is designed for currents up to 6 A.

A fuse is used to protect the circuit.

What should be the rating of the fuse?

**A** 1 A  
**B** 3 A  
**C** 5 A  
**D** 13 A

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
