# Question 9 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2020 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 9  
Источник: [[_pdf/2020/0625_w20_qp_12.pdf#page=4|условие, стр. 4]] · [[_pdf/2020/0625_w20_ms_12.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2020/0625_w20_er.pdf#page=4|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

Темы: [[1.7.3 Energy resources]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнить эффективность и надёжность двух способов использования энергии ветра.

Two wind farms supply electrical energy to consumers in different ways.

Wind farm 1 supplies energy directly to consumers and for every 1000 J of energy extracted from the wind, 360 J is transferred to consumers as electrical energy.

Wind farm 2 stores electrical energy in batteries and then supplies energy from the batteries to the consumer. For every 1000 J of energy extracted from the wind, 270 J is transferred to consumers as electrical energy.

Which statement about the two wind farms is correct?

**A** Wind farm 1 is less reliable and less efficient than wind farm 2.  
**B** Wind farm 1 is less reliable but more efficient than wind farm 2.  
**C** Wind farm 1 is more reliable but less efficient than wind farm 2.  
**D** Wind farm 1 is more reliable and more efficient than wind farm 2.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question was challenging for some candidates. They needed to recognise that a wind powered generation of electric energy is dependent on the wind strength while batteries can continuously supply power. Stronger candidates were able to deduce the more efficient supply when given the output per 1000 W input.
