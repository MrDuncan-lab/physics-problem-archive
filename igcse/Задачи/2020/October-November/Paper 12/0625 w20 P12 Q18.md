# Question 18 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2020 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 18

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнить теплопроводность материалов по разности температур концов стержней.

Thermal energy is supplied at the same rate to four bars made from different materials.

![[0625-w20-12-p7-f2.svg]]

After several minutes, there is a temperature difference between X and Y for each bar.

Which statement identifies the bar with the greatest temperature difference between points X and Y, and gives the correct reason?

**A** copper because it is a good conductor of thermal energy  
**B** lead because it is the densest metal  
**C** plastic because it is a poor conductor of thermal energy  
**D** wood because it is a good conductor of thermal energy

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question was challenging for many candidates, with most thinking that the greatest temperature difference between the ends of the bar would be the copper bar. In practice, this would have the smallest temperature rise because the energy is passed along the bar, warming the far end. Consequently, the largest temperature difference will be the bar which conducts least energy, where the far end of the bar is at the lowest temperature.
