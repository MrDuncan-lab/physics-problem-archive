> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить изменение направления силы на проводник при перестановке магнита и изменении тока.

The diagram shows a wire carrying a current in the direction shown. The wire is between the poles of a magnet.

A force is produced on the wire out of the page.

![[0625-w20-12-p14-f1.svg]]

The wire and magnet are now put into different arrangements X, Y and Z. The arrow shows the direction of the current in each case.

X Y Z

![[0625-w20-12-p14-f2.svg]]

In which arrangements is the force on the wire out of the page?

**A** X only  
**B** X and Y  
**C** X and Z  
**D** Z only

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The three-dimensional nature of the simple motor effect and the a.c. generator make them conceptually difficult. Nevertheless, the logic of the changes in this question allowed stronger candidates to identify the correct response.
