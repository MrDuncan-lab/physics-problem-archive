Источник: [[_pdf/2020/0625_w20_qp_13.pdf#page=11|условие, стр. 11]] · [[_pdf/2020/0625_w20_ms_13.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2020/0625_w20_er.pdf#page=7|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w20 P13]]  
Темы: [[3.3 Electromagnetic spectrum]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнить частоты и скорости разных видов электромагнитного излучения в вакууме.

The diagram shows three types of electromagnetic radiation listed in a particular order. The

electromagnetic radiation is travelling in a vacuum.

![[0625-w20-13-p11-f1.svg]]

Which quantities increase in magnitude going from left to right across the list?

**A** frequency only  
**B** neither speed nor frequency  
**C** speed and frequency  
**D** speed only

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The majority of candidates did not recognise that the speed in a vacuum for all electromagnetic waves is the same. It is the frequency which determines the properties of a particular portion of a section of the spectrum.
