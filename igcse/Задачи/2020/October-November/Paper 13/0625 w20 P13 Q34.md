**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2020 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 3 · вопрос 34  
Источник: [[_pdf/2020/0625_w20_qp_13.pdf#page=14|условие, стр. 14]] · [[_pdf/2020/0625_w20_ms_13.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2020/0625_w20_er.pdf#page=7|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить фактор, увеличивающий индукционную ЭДС движущегося проводника.

When a metal wire moves up, cutting a magnetic field, an electromotive force (e.m.f.) is induced

across the wire.

Which change affects the magnitude of the induced e.m.f.?

**A** moving the wire down at the same speed  
**B** moving the wire up at a faster speed  
**C** using a thicker wire  
**D** using a wire made from a different metal

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The concept of electromagnetic induction was challenging for many candidates. Many candidates had difficulty with the meanings of potential difference, current and e.m.f.
