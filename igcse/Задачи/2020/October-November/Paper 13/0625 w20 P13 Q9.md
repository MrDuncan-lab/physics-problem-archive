> Сравнить полную энергию маятника в разных положениях при отсутствии трения.

The diagram shows a frictionless pendulum.

![[0625-w20-13-p5-f1.svg]]

The pendulum bob swings from point P through point Q to point R then back to P.

At which point is the energy of the pendulum bob greatest?

**A** at point P  
**B** at point Q  
**C** at point R  
**D** it is the same at points P, Q and R

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
