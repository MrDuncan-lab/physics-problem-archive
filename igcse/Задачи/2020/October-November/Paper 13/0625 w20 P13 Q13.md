> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать давление ящика на пол по весу и площади опоры.

A large box has a weight of 700 N. The box is placed on the floor.

![[0625-w20-13-p6-f2.svg]]

What is the pressure on the floor due to the box?

**A** 0.20 N / m$^{2}$  
**B** 10 N / m$^{2}$  
**C** 245 N / m$^{2}$  
**D** 2000 N / m$^{2}$

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The dimensions of the block are given in centimetres, and the pressure is given in newtons per metre squared. A number of candidates did not convert units into a consistent set and so gave an incorrect answer.
