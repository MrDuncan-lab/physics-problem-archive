> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить изменение направления силы на проводник при обращении тока.

The diagram shows a wire carrying a current in the direction shown. There is a magnetic field acting from left to right. The wire experiences a force acting out of the page.

![[0625-w20-13-p15-f1.svg]]

The current is now reversed.

In which direction does the force on the wire now act?

**A** into the page  
**B** out of the page  
**C** to the left  
**D** to the right

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The three-dimensional nature of the simple motor effect and the a.c. generator make them conceptually difficult. Nevertheless, the logic of the changes in this question allowed stronger candidates to identify the correct response.
