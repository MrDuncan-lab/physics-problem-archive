> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнить токи в разных частях разветвлённой цепи.

A student uses four ammeters P, Q, R and S to measure the current in different parts of the circuit shown.

![[0625-w20-13-p13-f2.svg]]

Which two ammeters read the largest current?

**A** P and Q  
**B** P and R  
**C** R and Q  
**D** R and S

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question was done well with most candidates recognising that the current through the power supply is greater than the current through each arm of the resistors in parallel.
