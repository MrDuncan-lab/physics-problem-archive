> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать расстояние от водителя до изображения автобуса в плоском зеркале.

A driver sits in a car. She has a rear-view plane mirror 0.5 m in front of her. A bus is 7.5 m behind

the driver.

![[0625-w20-23-p8-f1.svg]]

![[0625-w20-23-p8-f2.svg]]

The driver looks at the image of the bus in her mirror.

How far is the image away from her?

**A** 1.0 m  
**B** 7.5 m  
**C** 8.0 m  
**D** 8.5 m

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The majority of candidates incorrectly chose option **B**. This suggests that they were able to calculate the frequency of the wave (4.0 / 10 = 0.40 Hz), but then multiplied that by the speed (2.0 m/s) rather than using the formula *c* = *f*λ, which when rearranged gives λ *= c* / *f =* 2.0 / 0.40.
