> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать диаметр проволоки с поправкой на нулевую ошибку микрометра.

A micrometer screw gauge reads 0.02 mm when the jaws are fully closed. It reads 0.56 mm when measuring the diameter of a metal wire.

What is the diameter of the wire?

**A** 0.36 mm  
**B** 0.54 mm  
**C** 0.56 mm  
**D** 0.58 mm

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Almost all candidates recognised that the zero-error meant that the actual diameter of the wire was 0.02 mm less than the reading on the micrometer.
