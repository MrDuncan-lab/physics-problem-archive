> Сравнить вес и ускорение одинаковых тел при разной напряжённости гравитационного поля.

An object of mass 1.0 kg is at rest on Earth. An identical object is at rest on a planet with a gravitational field strength of twice that on Earth.

Which row correctly compares the object on the planet to the object on Earth?

|  | its weight | its acceleration when the same horizontal force is applied |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | double | equal to that on Earth |
| **B** | double | half that on Earth |
| **C** | half | equal to that on Earth |
| **D** | half | half that on Earth |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**
