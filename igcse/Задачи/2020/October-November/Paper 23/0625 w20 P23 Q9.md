> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить изменение импульса мяча при отскоке от поверхности.

A ball of mass *m* falls vertically and hits a hard surface.

Its speed on hitting the surface is *v*$_{1}$.

It rebounds vertically upwards with speed *v*$_{2}$.

What is the change in momentum of the ball?

**A** *mv*$_{1}$  
**B** *mv*$_{2}$  
**C** *m*(*v*$_{1}$ + *v*$_{2}$)  
**D** *m*(*v*$_{2}$ – *v*$_{1}$)

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> There was a lack of understanding in some answers that a vector is direction dependent. The two speeds of the ball were given, and it was clear that the velocities must be in opposite directions. Therefore the change in velocity of the ball was *v*1 – (–*v*2) = (*v*1 + *v*2) and the change in momentum = *m*(*v*1 + *v*2).
