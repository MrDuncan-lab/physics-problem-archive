# Question 2 (7 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2020 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 2

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать и увеличить момент ключа, сравнить работу и мощность при откручивании гаек.

A car driver needs to remove one of the wheels on his car. He puts a spanner on a wheel nut.

![[0625-w20-32-p4-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 2.1**

**(a)** The driver applies a force of 200 N, as shown in Fig. 2.1.

Calculate the moment of the 200 N force about the centre of the wheel nut.

moment of force =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ N cm **[3]**

**(b)** The moment in **(a)** does not release the wheel nut. The driver **cannot** increase the force but can increase its moment.

State and explain how the driver can increase the moment of the force.

statement  \_\_\_\_\_\_

explanation  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(c)** The driver releases a second wheel nut in a shorter time than the first wheel nut. The driver uses the same amount of energy in releasing both wheel nuts.

![[0625-w20-32-p4-f2.svg]]

less than the same as greater than

Complete the sentences using the phrases in the box. Each phrase may be used once, more than once or not at all.

The work done in releasing the second wheel nut is  \_\_\_\_\_\_ the work

done in releasing the first wheel nut.

The power produced in releasing the second wheel nut is  \_\_\_\_\_\_ the

power produced in releasing the first wheel nut. **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** 10 000 (Ncm)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | (Moment) = *F* × *d* | C1 |
> |  | 200 × 50 | C1 |
> |  | 10 000 (Ncm) | A1 |
> | (b) | use a longer spanner / move force to end of spanner owtte | B1 |
> |  | (to) increase the distance (from force to wheel nut or pivot) OR distance (from force to wheel nut or pivot) is greater than<br>50 cm | B1 |
> | (c) | (work done is the) same (as) | B1 |
> |  | (power produced is) greater (than) | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** The majority of candidates calculated the moment correctly as 10 000 Nm. The most common error was to divide the force by the distance instead of multiplying.
>
> **(b)** The majority of candidates gained full credited here. Weaker candidates suggested increasing the force despite being told in the question that this could not happen.
>
> **(c)** Many candidates found this item difficult. There was considerable confusion between the concepts of work done and power produced.
