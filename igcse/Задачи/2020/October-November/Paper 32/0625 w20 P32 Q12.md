# Question 12 (5 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2020 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 12

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Назвать источник радиоактивного фона, определить период полураспада и описать безопасное хранение изотопа.

A teacher is investigating radioactivity.

The teacher measures the background radiation in the laboratory.

**(a)** State **one** source of background radiation. **[1]**

**(b)** A teacher measures the count rate of a radioactive isotope.

Fig. 12.1 shows the graph of her results.

![[0625-w20-32-p16-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 12.1**

**(i)** Determine the half-life of the radioactive isotope. Use information from Fig. 12.1.

Show on Fig. 12.1 how you obtained your value.

half-life =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ minutes **[3]**

**(ii)** The radioactive isotope emits γ-radiation.

Describe **one** method of safely storing the radioactive isotope. **[1]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)(i)** 7.5 (min)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | rocks, buildings, (natural) radon, air, cosmic rays, sun, food, drink | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | evidence of using graph | C1 |
> |  | TWO pairs of coordinates seen | C1 |
> |  | 7.5 (min) | A1 |
> | (b)(ii) | Use a lead(-lined) box / container | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** The majority of candidates answered correctly. Weaker candidates gave answers such as radios or mobile phones.
>
> **(b) (i)** The majority of candidates gained at least partial credit for a correct use of the half-life graph. Weaker candidates simply gave an answer of 20, i.e. half the maximum time on the *x*-axis.
>
> **(ii)** The majority of candidates identified a suitable method of storage.
