# Question 8 (6 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2020 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 8  
Источник: [[_pdf/2020/0625_w20_qp_32.pdf#page=10|условие, стр. 10]] · [[_pdf/2020/0625_w20_ms_32.pdf#page=13|схема оценивания, стр. 13]] · [[_pdf/2020/0625_w20_er.pdf#page=19|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w20 P32]]  
Темы: [[3.2.3 Thin lenses]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить расстояние до предмета, обозначить фокусное расстояние и описать изображение линзы.

Fig. 8.1 shows a converging lens and an object. The side of each square represents 0.5 cm.

![[0625-w20-32-p10-f1.svg]]

![[0625-w20-32-p10-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 8.1**

**(a)** Using Fig. 8.1, determine the distance of the object from the centre of the lens.

distance =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm **[2]**

**(b)** Fig. 8.2 shows another lens forming the image IY of object OP.

![[0625-w20-32-p10-f3.svg]]

**Fig. 8.2**

**(i)** On Fig. 8.2, draw an arrow to represent the focal length of the lens. Label this arrow f. **[2]**

**(ii)** Circle **two** words or phrases from those shown to describe the image formed in Fig. 8.2.

**enlarged upright inverted same size diminished** **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** 4.5 (cm)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | 8.5 to 9.1 squares counted | C1 |
> |  | 4.5 (cm) | A1 |
> | (b)(i) | focal length marked from centre of lens | B1 |
> |  | to point where ray parallel to axis is refracted by lens to cross axis | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | inverted (third word circled) | B1 |
> |  | diminished (5$^{\text{th}}$ / last word circled) | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Most candidates correctly determined the object distance as 4.5 cm. The most common error was failing to use the scale and giving an answer of 9 cm.
>
> **(b) (i)** Few candidates correctly identified the focal length of the lens. This topic was not well understood by most candidates.
>
> **(ii)** Only the strongest candidates answered this question correctly. The description of the image formed by a converging lens was not well understood.
