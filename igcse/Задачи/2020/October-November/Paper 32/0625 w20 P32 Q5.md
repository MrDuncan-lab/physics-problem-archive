# Question 5 (5 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2020 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 5  
Источник: [[_pdf/2020/0625_w20_qp_32.pdf#page=7|условие, стр. 7]] · [[_pdf/2020/0625_w20_ms_32.pdf#page=11|схема оценивания, стр. 11]] · [[_pdf/2020/0625_w20_er.pdf#page=19|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w20 P32]]  
Темы: [[2.2.3 Melting, boiling and evaporation]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объяснить испарение и охлаждение оставшейся воды движением её молекул.

A beaker contains water. Some of the water evaporates.

**(a)** Describe and explain how the water evaporates. Use your ideas about molecules. **[2]**

**(b)** Evaporation changes the temperature of the water that remains in the beaker.

State and explain the change in temperature of the water due to evaporation. **[3]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | high(er / est) energy molecules | B1 |
> |  | (near the surface) escape (from surface) | B1 |
> | (b) | (temperature) decreases | B1 |
> |  | AND any **two** from:<br>higher energy molecules have escaped<br>(leaving) lower energy particles behind<br>(so)<br>average energy of remaining molecules is lower | B2 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Many candidates gained full credit here, but weaker candidates failed to link the escape from the water surface to the most energetic molecules.
>
> **(b)** Candidates found this item quite challenging. Only the strongest linked the decrease in temperature to the reduction in average kinetic energy of molecules remaining in the water.
