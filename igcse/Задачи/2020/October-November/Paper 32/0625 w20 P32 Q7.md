# Question 7 (8 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2020 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 7  
Источник: [[_pdf/2020/0625_w20_qp_32.pdf#page=9|условие, стр. 9]] · [[_pdf/2020/0625_w20_ms_32.pdf#page=12|схема оценивания, стр. 12]] · [[_pdf/2020/0625_w20_er.pdf#page=19|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w20 P32]]  
Темы: [[3.1 General properties of waves]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Описать продольную волну, определить длину и частоту, рассчитать скорость распространения.

A teacher uses a long spring to demonstrate wave motion. She makes a wave move along the coils of the spring. Fig. 7.1 shows the wave on the spring.

![[0625-w20-32-p9-f1.svg]]

![[0625-w20-32-p9-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 7.1**

**(a)** Explain why the type of wave in Fig. 7.1 is a longitudinal wave. **[2]**

**(b)** Measure the wavelength of the wave shown in Fig. 7.1.

wavelength =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm **[1]**

**(c)** State what is meant by the frequency of a wave. **[2]**

**(d)** The wave in Fig. 7.1 travels 25 cm in 0.20 s.

Calculate the speed of the wave.

speed =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm / s **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(d)** 125 (cm / s)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | movement (of coils / spring) parallel | B1 |
> |  | to the direction wave / it / disturbance travels | B1 |
> | (b) | 5.2 (cm) | B1 |
> | (c) | number of waves (passing a point OR sent out) | B1 |
> |  | (in) one second / unit time. | B1 |
> | (d) | speed = distance ÷ time | C1 |
> |  | 25 ÷ 0.2(0) | C1 |
> |  | 125 (cm / s) | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Only the strongest candidates were able to correctly link the movement of the coils to the direction of wave travel.
>
> **(b)** Very few candidates gave the correct wavelength. The most common error was simply to write down the total length of the spring.
>
> **(c)** Many candidates gained full credit here, but weaker candidates gave descriptions of wavelength and wave speed.
>
> **(d)** The majority of candidates correctly calculated the speed of the wave as 125 cm / s. Weaker candidates inverted the equation for speed, or even multiplied the distance and the time.
