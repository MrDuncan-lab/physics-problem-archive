> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Описать молекулы газа и объяснить рост давления при нагревании в жёстком сосуде.

A rigid container is filled with a gas.

**(a)** Describe the movement and arrangement of the gas molecules in the container. **[3]**

**(b)** The gas in the container is heated. The volume of the gas does **not** change.

State and explain the change in pressure of the gas as the temperature of the gas increases. Use your ideas about molecules in your answer. **[3]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | any **three** from:<br>high speed<br>random movement<br>random arrangement<br>large spaces / gaps between molecules<br>colliding (with each other / walls of container) | B3 |
> | (b) | (pressure) increases | B1 |
> |  | AND any **two** from:<br>(because) molecules move faster<br>collide more frequently (with walls of container)<br>collide with greater force (with walls of container) | B2 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** The vast majority of candidates answered this question well. The simple kinetic molecular model of matter seemed to be well understood by many candidates.
>
> **(b)** This question was answered accurately by many candidates. Weaker candidates failed to link an increase in speed to an increased rate of collisions with the container walls.
