> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить период маятника по числу смен направления и времени наблюдения.

The diagram shows a pendulum. The pendulum bob swings repeatedly between points M and N.

![[0625-w20-11-p2-f1.svg]]

A student starts a stop-watch when the bob reaches point M.

He counts each time the bob changes direction and stops the watch on the tenth change in direction.

The watch shows a time of 12.0 seconds.

What is the period of the pendulum?

**A** 0.60 s  
**B** 1.2 s  
**C** 2.4 s  
**D** 12.0 s

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The question was challenging for many candidates and only the strongest candidates recognised that there are two changes of direction in each complete oscillation of the pendulum.
