> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Указать частицы, движение которых создаёт ток в металле.

What is an electric current in a metal wire?

**A** a flow of electrons  
**B** a flow of neutrons  
**C** a flow of nucleons  
**D** a flow of protons

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This was a well-answered question with almost all candidates recognising that a current in a metal wire is due to electron flow.
