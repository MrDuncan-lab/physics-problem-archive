> Определить влияние перестановки измерительных приборов на их показания в цепи ламп.

A circuit X is set up with two identical lamps. Circuit Y is then set up, changing the positions of the

meters.

![[0625-w20-11-p13-f2.svg]]

On which meters do the readings change?

**A** both the ammeter and the voltmeter  
**B** the ammeter only  
**C** the voltmeter only  
**D** neither the ammeter nor the voltmeter

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
