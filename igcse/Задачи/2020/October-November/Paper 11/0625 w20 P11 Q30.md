> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнить сопротивления проводов разной длины и диаметра.

Four wires are made of the same metal.

Which wire has the greatest resistance?

**A** a 100 cm long wire with a diameter of 3.0 mm  
**B** a 100 cm long wire with a diameter of 6.0 mm  
**C** a 10 cm long wire with a diameter of 3.0 mm  
**D** a 10 cm long wire with a diameter of 6.0 mm

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Although most candidates recognised that the longer wires would have more resistance than the shorter wires, few recognised the inverse relationship between the resistance and the diameter of the wire.
