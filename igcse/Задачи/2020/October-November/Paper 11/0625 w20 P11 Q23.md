# Question 23 (1 mark)

Источник: [[_pdf/2020/0625_w20_qp_11.pdf#page=10|условие, стр. 10]] · [[_pdf/2020/0625_w20_ms_11.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2020/0625_w20_er.pdf#page=1|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбрать верное утверждение о фокусировке параллельных лучей собирающей линзой.

Which statement about a thin converging lens is correct?

**A** All rays of light refracted by the lens pass through the principal focus.  
**B** All rays initially parallel to the principal axis of the lens are refracted through the principal

focus.

**C** The focal length of the lens is the distance between the image and the principal focus.  
**D** The focal length of the lens is the distance between the object and the image.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Many candidates did not read the question carefully enough and thought that option **A** was correct. However, candidates who read the question more carefully selected the correct option **B.**
