> Выбрать цвет чашки и наличие крышки для уменьшения охлаждения кофе.

Four cups **A**, **B**, **C** and **D** contain hot coffee.

Which cup keeps the coffee warm the longest?

|  | the outside surface of the cup | the top of the cup |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | black | covered with a lid |
| **B** | black | no lid |
| **C** | white | covered with a lid |
| **D** | white | no lid |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
