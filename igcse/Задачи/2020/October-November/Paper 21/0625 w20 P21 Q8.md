> Определить равнодействующую трёх сил, показанных на рисунке.

The diagram shows three forces acting on an object.

![[0625-w20-21-p4-f2.svg]]

What is the value of the resultant force acting on the object?

**A** 2 N  
**B** 10 N  
**C** 14 N  
**D** 16 N

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This was not well understood by many candidates. Most candidates simply added the three forces, unaware that when adding vectors direction must also be considered.
