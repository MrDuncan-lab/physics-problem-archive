> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить условия действия магнитной силы на движущиеся частицы.

A beam of particles moves through a magnetic field.

In which situation do the particles experience a magnetic force?

**A** a beam of α-particles moving parallel to the magnetic field lines  
**B** a beam of electrons moving parallel to the magnetic field lines  
**C** a beam of β-particles moving perpendicularly across the magnetic field lines  
**D** a beam of neutrons moving perpendicularly across the magnetic field lines

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
