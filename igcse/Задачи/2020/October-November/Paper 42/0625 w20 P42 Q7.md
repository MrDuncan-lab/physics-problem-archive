# Question 7 (8 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2020 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 2 · вопрос 7

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать критический угол оптического волокна, объяснить медицинское применение и определить монохроматический свет.

Fig. 7.1 shows a ray of light passing through an optical fibre.

![[0625-w20-42-p10-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 7.1**

The optical fibre is made of glass that has a refractive index of 1.4.

**(a)** **(i)** No light refracts from the fibre at points P and Q.

State the name of the process that occurs at P and Q. **[1]**

**(ii)** Calculate the minimum value of angle *i* for there to be no refraction at point P.

angle =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(b)** State and explain the use of optical fibres in medicine. **[3]**

**(c)** The ray of light shown in Fig. 7.1 is monochromatic light from a laser.

State what is meant by monochromatic light. Use **one** of the following quantities in your answer.

**amplitude brightness frequency refractive index speed** **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)(ii)** (*C* = sin$^{\text{–1}}$ {1 ÷ 1.4} = sin$^{\text{–1}}$ 0.714 =) 46°

> |  | (*C* = sin$^{\text{–1}}$ {1 ÷ 1.4} = sin$^{\text{–1}}$ 0.714 =) 46° | A1 |
> | (b) | description of fibre passing to site to be examined / treated | B1 |

> | (c) | any mention of frequency | B1 |
> |  | (all of light) same / single / one frequency | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** This was generally answered well. Reflection on its own was an insufficient answer. Weaker candidates mentioned refraction or even reflaction. Reflaction will never be acceptable to gain credit.
>
> **(ii)** Most stronger candidates gained full credit but there was a wide range of errors made by others. Some candidates rounded their final answer up to 47°, perhaps incorrectly thinking of grazing refraction.
>
> **(b)** There were many good responses gaining full credit but many candidates exclusively described total internal reflection in optical fibres, which was not the focus of this question.
>
> **(c)** This question tested the syllabus definition of monochromatic as light of a single frequency. Many candidates did not gain credit because they only referred to light of one colour. This is true but did not answer the question as colour was not one of the quantities given.
