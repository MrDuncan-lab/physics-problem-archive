# Question 3 (5 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2020 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 2 · вопрос 3  
Источник: [[_pdf/2020/0625_w20_qp_42.pdf#page=5|условие, стр. 5]] · [[_pdf/2020/0625_w20_ms_42.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2020/0625_w20_er.pdf#page=29|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать КПД ветрогенератора по энергии ветра и электрической мощности.

The kinetic energy of air passing through a wind turbine every minute is 720 000 J. The electrical output of the turbine is 9.0 A at a potential difference (p.d.) of 240 V.

Calculate the efficiency (%) of the wind turbine.

efficiency =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ % **[5]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> (efficiency =) 18(%)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> |  | (output) *P* = *VI* OR *E* = *VIt* OR *E* = *Pt* in any form words, symbols or numbers<br>OR (*P* =) *VI* OR (*P* =) 240 × 9 OR (*P* =) 2160 (W)<br>OR (*E* =) 240 × 9 × 60 = 129 600 (J) | C1 |
> |  | (rate of energy input = 720 000 / 60 =) 12 000 (J / s)<br>OR energy input = 720 000 (J) | C1 |
> |  | (efficiency =) (100 ×) output power / input power<br>OR (100 ×) output energy / input energy<br>words, symbols or numbers | C1 |
> |  | (efficiency =) 100 × {2160 / 12 000} | C1 |
> |  | (efficiency =) 18(%) | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Many candidates successfully answered this complex multi-stage calculation. Typical errors from other candidates were confusion about power and energy and about input and output. Weaker candidates, who could have gained partial credit by correctly quoting the efficiency equation, often did not do so because they merely quoted output/input and did not mention energy or power.
