# Question 8 (4 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2020 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 2 · вопрос 8

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объяснить требования к наружным розеткам и защиту цепей предохранителями и автоматическими выключателями.

**(a)** State and explain why electrical sockets and plugs used outside in a garden need to be different from those that can be used safely in a room inside a house. **[2]**

**(b)** State and explain why fuses and circuit breakers are installed in electrical circuits connected to the mains supply. **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** water conducts (electricity) OR clear statement of need for waterproof / outdoor specification (components)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | conditions (outdoors) may be damp / wet | C1 |
> |  | water conducts (electricity) OR clear statement of need for waterproof / outdoor specification (components) | A1 |
> | (b) | protects components / appliances / circuit / wires / user / mains supply<br>prevents electrical supply overheating / fires / electrocution / shocks | B1 |
> |  | excess current / power in circuit / wires OR fuse melts / blows OR circuit breaker opens | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) and (b)** Some credit was gained by most candidates in both parts but often answers were too vague. A
>
> significant minority of candidates talked about fuses as their answer to **(a)**.
>
> In **(b)** a common misconception amongst candidates was that somehow fuses or circuit breakers were there as current control device allowing the required current to flow.
