> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** The question asked candidates to draw the paths of α-particles, β-particles and γ-rays between the plates and after leaving the plates. However, many candidates did not read the question carefully enough. Very few answers showed paths after leaving the region between the plates and some candidates showed γ-rays stopping when they reached the end of the region instead of continuing undeflected. There was some confusion about the direction of deflection of α-particles and β- particles. Most candidates knew that α-particles and β-particle were deflected in opposite directions to each other. Few showed a greater deflection for β-particles than for α-particles. Most showed γ- rays undeflected.
>
> **(b)** There were some good answers to this part of the question. Those who gave the answer of sterilisation of medical equipment or treatment of cancer found their explanation easier than those who tried to describe the use of γ-rays in detecting leaks in underground pipes. Some weaker candidates confused X-rays with γ-rays or stated that γ-rays were used in chemotherapy.
