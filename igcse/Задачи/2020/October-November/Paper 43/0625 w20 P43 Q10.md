# Question 10 (5 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2020 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 3 · вопрос 10  
Источник: [[_pdf/2020/0625_w20_qp_43.pdf#page=16|условие, стр. 16]] · [[_pdf/2020/0625_w20_ms_43.pdf#page=15|схема оценивания, стр. 15]] · [[_pdf/2020/0625_w20_er.pdf#page=33|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объяснить работу реле и последствия замены железного якоря стальным.

Fig. 10.1 shows a relay.

![[0625-w20-43-p16-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 10.1**

**(a)** The switch in circuit A is closed. Describe how this operates the motor in circuit B. **[3]**

**(b)** The switch in circuit A is opened. The soft-iron armature is replaced with a steel armature. The switch in circuit A is closed.

Explain what happens when the switch in circuit A is then opened. **[2]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | iron core / coil / solenoid becomes a magnet | B1 |
> |  | <u>iron core / coil / solenoid attracts iron armature</u> | B1 |
> |  | armature pivots/moves **and** contacts touch / there is a current in circuit B | B1 |
> | (b) | current in circuit B does not stop when switch in circuit A is opened | B1 |
> |  | steel remains magnetised when there is no current in the coil | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Many candidates clearly understood the operation of a relay and gave a good explanation for their answer. Some candidates misunderstood how the current flowed in circuit B and stated that the current flowed from circuit A via the iron armature to circuit B. Other weaker answers suggested that the current in B was produced by induction.
>
> **(b)** Stronger candidates correctly identified that the motor or circuit B would continue working if the soft iron armature was replaced with a steel one as the steel would remain magnetised. Common incorrect answers included circuit B being switched off, either immediately or after a short time interval.
