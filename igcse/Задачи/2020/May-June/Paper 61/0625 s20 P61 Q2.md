**(c)** Calculate *R*$_{1}$ + *R*$_{2}$ + *R*$_{3}$. Give your answer to a suitable number of significant figures for this experiment.

*R*$_{1}$ + *R*$_{2}$ + *R*$_{3}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

Suggestion **C**: *R*$_{1}$ + *R*$_{2}$ + *R*$_{3}$ should be greater than 3 × *R*$_{1}$.
