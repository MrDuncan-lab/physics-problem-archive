> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Указать постоянство температуры и массы газа как условия закона Бойля.

The relationship between pressure *p* and volume *V* of a gas is given as *pV* = constant.

Under which conditions for the mass of a gas and for its temperature does the equation hold?

|  | mass | temperature |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | changing | changing |
| **B** | changing | constant |
| **C** | constant | changing |
| **D** | constant | constant |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
