**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2020 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 2 · вопрос 23  
Источник: [[_pdf/2020/0625_s20_qp_22.pdf#page=9|условие, стр. 9]] · [[_pdf/2020/0625_s20_ms_22.pdf|схема оценивания]]

Темы: [[3.2.2 Refraction of light]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбрать верную связь предельного угла полного внутреннего отражения с показателем преломления.

Which statement is correct?

**A** The speed of light in glass is equal to the speed of light in a vacuum multiplied by the refractive index of glass.

**B** The incident angle of a light ray at an air-glass surface is the angle between the ray and the glass surface.

**C** The sine of the critical angle at an air-glass surface is equal to $\dfrac{1}{\text{refractive index of glass}}$.

**D** The angle of refraction for light passing through an air-glass surface is proportional to the angle of incidence at that surface.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
