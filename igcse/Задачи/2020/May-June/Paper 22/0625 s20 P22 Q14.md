> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Описать силы между молекулами и характер их движения в твёрдом теле.

Which row describes the forces between the molecules and the motion of the molecules in a

solid?

|  | forces between molecules | motion of molecules |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | strong | move freely |
| **B** | strong | vibrate only |
| **C** | weak | move freely |
| **D** | weak | vibrate only |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
