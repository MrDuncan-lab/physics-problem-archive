has a door opening at the top.

![[0625-s20-11-p11-f1.svg]]

|  | freezer gaining the least thermal energy | reason |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | X | cold air falls |
| **B** | X | warm air falls |
| **C** | Y | cold air falls |
| **D** | Y | warm air falls |
