# Question 19 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2020 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 1 · вопрос 19  
Источник: [[_pdf/2020/0625_s20_qp_11.pdf#page=10|условие, стр. 10]] · [[_pdf/2020/0625_s20_ms_11.pdf|схема оценивания]]  
Работа: [[0625 s20 P11]]  
Темы: [[2.3.1 Conduction]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объясняют разное время таяния льда на металле и пластике различием теплопроводности.

One ice cube is placed on a metal block. An identical ice cube is placed on a plastic block. The

blocks are left next to each other on a table in a laboratory.

![[0625-s20-11-p10-f2.svg]]

Which ice cube melts first and why?

**A** The ice cube on the plastic block melts first because plastic is a good insulator of thermal

energy.

**B** The ice cube on the plastic block melts first because plastic is a good conductor of thermal

energy.

**C** The ice cube on the metal block melts first because metal is a good conductor of thermal

energy.

**D** The ice cube on the metal block melts first because metal is a good insulator of thermal

energy.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
