> Определяют результирующую тяги, трения и сопротивления воздуха, действующих на сани.

A sledge is pulled in a straight line by dogs, as shown.

![[0625-s20-11-p4-f1.svg]]

The dogs produce a total horizontal driving force of 600 N.

The frictional force between the sledge and the surface is 150 N and the air resistance on the sledge is 450 N.

What is the resultant force acting on the sledge?

**A** 0 N  
**B** 300 N  
**C** 900 N  
**D** 1200 N

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**
