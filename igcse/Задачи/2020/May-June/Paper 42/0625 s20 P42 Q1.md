**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2020 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 2 · вопрос 1

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Расчёт пути и средней скорости по графику путешествия и объяснение связи ускорения с наклоном графика.

Fig. 1.1 shows the speed–time graph of a person on a journey.

On the journey, he walks and then waits for a bus. He then travels by bus. He gets off the bus and waits for two minutes. He then walks again. His journey takes 74 minutes.

![[0625-s20-42-p2-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 1.1**

**(a)** For the whole journey calculate:

**(i)** the distance travelled

distance =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

**(ii)** the average speed.

average speed =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(b)** State and explain which feature of a speed–time graph shows acceleration. **[2]**

**(c)** State and explain the acceleration of the person at time = 40 minutes. **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)(i)** (s = 1.667 + 13.333 + 2.2 =) 17 km
>
> **(a)(ii)** (average speed = 17 × 60 / 74 =) 14 km / h
