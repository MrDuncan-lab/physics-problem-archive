Fig. 4.1 shows a liquid-in-glass thermometer without a temperature scale. The liquid inside the thermometer has a melting point of –39 °C.

![[0625-s20-42-p6-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 4.1**

**(a)** Describe simple experiments to mark the positions of the fixed points on this liquid-in-glass thermometer. **[4]**

**(b)** A scientist is measuring temperatures at the South Pole. These temperatures have a minimum value of –90 °C.

State why the liquid used in the thermometer in Fig. 4.1 would **not** be suitable for this scientist. **[1]**

**(c)** State a design change that:

**(i)** increases the sensitivity of a liquid-in-glass thermometer **[1]**

**(ii)** increases the range of a liquid-in-glass thermometer. **[1]**

**(d)** State the property of the liquid which ensures that the scale on a liquid-in-glass thermometer is linear. **[1]**
