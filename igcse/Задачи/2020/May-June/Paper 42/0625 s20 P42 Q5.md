> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Построение отражённых волновых фронтов, расчёт частоты и описание эха и сгущений продольной волны.

Fig. 5.1 shows crests of a wave approaching a barrier where the wave is reflected.

![[0625-s20-42-p7-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 5.1**

**(a)** On Fig. 5.1, draw **three** crests of the reflected wave. **[3]**

**(b)** The wave has a wavelength of 36 cm and a speed of 1.2 m / s.

Calculate the frequency of the wave.

frequency =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

**(c)** Complete the following sentences.

An echo is the name for a reflected  \_\_\_\_\_\_ wave.

The waves that form an echo are a type of longitudinal wave. Longitudinal waves are made

up of  \_\_\_\_\_\_ and rarefactions. **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** (f =) 3.3 Hz
