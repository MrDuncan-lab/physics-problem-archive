> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнение изотопов, определение продукта бета-распада и объяснение различий ионизирующей способности альфа- и бета-частиц.

Fig. 10.1 represents a neutral atom of an isotope of element X.

![[0625-s20-41-p15-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 10.1**

**(a)** State **one** similarity between this atom and a neutral atom of a different isotope of element X. **[1]**

**(b)** The isotope of element X is radioactive. It decays to form an isotope of element Y by emitting a β-particle.

**(i)** Using Fig. 10.1 deduce the nuclide notation for the isotope of Y produced by this decay.

nuclide notation: ${}^{\dots}_{\dots}\text{Y}$ **[3]**

**(ii)** β-particles ionise the air they pass through less strongly than the same number of α-particles.

Suggest why this is so. **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)(i)** ${}^{13}_{6}\text{Y}$
