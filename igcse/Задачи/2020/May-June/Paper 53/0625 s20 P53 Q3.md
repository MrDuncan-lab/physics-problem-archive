In this experiment you will determine the focal length of a converging lens. Carry out the following instructions, referring to Fig. 3.1.

**(i)** Measure the distance *v* between the lens and the screen as indicated in Fig. 3.1.

*v* =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(ii)** Calculate a value *f*$_{1}$ for the focal length of the lens. Use the equation

*f*$_{1}$ = $\dfrac{uv}{u v}$ .

( + )
