Источник: [[_pdf/2020/0625_s20_qp_12.pdf#page=7|условие, стр. 7]] · [[_pdf/2020/0625_s20_ms_12.pdf|схема оценивания]]

Темы: [[2.2.3 Melting, boiling and evaporation]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объясняют испарение уходом наиболее энергичных молекул с поверхности воды.

Evaporation occurs from the surface of a pool of water.

Which statement describes this change of state?

**A** Electrons move from the liquid and become a gas.  
**B** Molecules that move from the liquid have the same energy as those that stay in the liquid.  
**C** The more energetic molecules escape the liquid.  
**D** The more energetic molecules remain in the liquid.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
