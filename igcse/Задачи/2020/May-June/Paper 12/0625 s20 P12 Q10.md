# Question 10 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2020 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 10  
Источник: [[_pdf/2020/0625_s20_qp_12.pdf#page=5|условие, стр. 5]] · [[_pdf/2020/0625_s20_ms_12.pdf|схема оценивания]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнивают затраты энергии двигателей при одинаковой полезной работе и разной эффективности.

Two motors X and Y lift loads of the same weight through the same vertical distance.

Motor X is more efficient than motor Y.

Which statement about the motors is correct?

**A** The useful energy output of motor X is larger than that of motor Y.  
**B** The useful energy output of motor X is smaller than that of motor Y.  
**C** The energy input of motor X is larger than that of motor Y.  
**D** The energy input of motor X is smaller than that of motor Y.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
