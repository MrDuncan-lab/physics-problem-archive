> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Расчёт средней скорости автомобиля, построение графика скорости и определение пути мотоцикла по графику.

**(a)** Some students determine the speed of a car on a road. The students measure the time for the car to travel 30 m along the road. The time is 5.4 s.

Calculate the average speed of the car.

average speed =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ m / s **[3]**

**(b)** Another car moves at a constant speed of 16 m / s for 4.0 seconds. During the next 2.0 seconds, the car decelerates from a speed of 16 m / s to a speed of 13 m / s. It then continues at a constant speed of 13 m / s for 3.0 seconds.

On Fig. 2.1, plot the speed–time graph for the motion of the car during these 9.0 s.

![[0625-s20-32-p3-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 2.1** **[3]**

**(c)** A motorcycle accelerates as shown in Fig. 2.2. Calculate the distance the motorcycle travels while it is accelerating. Use information from Fig. 2.2.

![[0625-s20-32-p4-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 2.2**

distance travelled =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ m **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** 5.6 (m / s)
>
> **(b)(ii)** 67.5 (m)
