# Question 1 (6 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2020 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 1  
Источник: [[_pdf/2020/0625_s20_qp_32.pdf#page=2|условие, стр. 2]] · [[_pdf/2020/0625_s20_ms_32.pdf#page=5|схема оценивания, стр. 5]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определение времени между каплями, выбор прибора для измерения объёма и расчёт веса собранной воды.

Some students observe drops of water falling from a tap that leaks, as shown in Fig. 1.1.

![[0625-s20-32-p2-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 1.1**

**(a)** The students measure the time for 50 drops to fall from the tap. The time for 50 drops to fall is 20 s.

Calculate the average time between two drops falling.

average time =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ s **[2]**

**(b)** The students collect some drops of water.

**(i)** The students measure the volume of the water they collect.

State the term for the equipment that is suitable for measuring the volume accurately. **[1]**

**(ii)** In a similar experiment, another student collects 0.21 kg of water.

Calculate the weight of this water.

weight of water =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ N **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** 0.4 (s)
>
> **(b)(ii)** 2.1 (N)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | (time =) 20 ÷ 50 | C1 |
> |  | 0.4 (s) | A1 |
> | (b)(i) | measuring cylinder | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | W = m x g | C1 |
> |  | (W =) 0.21 × 10 | C1 |
> |  | 2.1 (N) | A1 |
