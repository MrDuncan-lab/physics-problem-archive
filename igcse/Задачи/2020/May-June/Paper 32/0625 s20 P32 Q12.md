> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Состав изотопов углерода, изменение ядра при бета-распаде и меры радиационной защиты.

**(a)** Carbon-14 is a radioactive isotope of carbon. An atom of carbon-14 has 6 protons in its nucleus.

Another isotope of carbon is carbon-12.

**(i)** Determine the number of protons in a carbon-12 nucleus. **[1]**

**(ii)** Determine the number of neutrons in a carbon-14 nucleus. **[1]**

**(iii)** Determine the number of electrons orbiting the nucleus of a single carbon-14 atom. **[1]**

**(b)** Carbon-14 decays by emitting a β-particle.

State what happens to a nucleus of carbon-14 when it emits a β-particle. **[1]**

**(c)** People working with radioactive sources need to take safety precautions.

**(i)** A shielding material can absorb ionising radiation and reduce the damage to living tissue.

State a suitable material that will absorb all types of naturally occurring nuclear radiation. **[1]**

**(ii)** Apart from using shielding, state how a person can reduce the amount of ionising radiation they absorb when they handle samples of radioactive substances. **[1]**
