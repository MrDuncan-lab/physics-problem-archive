> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Способы увеличения вращающего действия на катушку двигателя и расчёт числа витков трансформатора.

A model train uses an electric motor. The motor has a coil of wire in a magnetic field.

**(a)** Fig. 11.1 shows a coil of wire in a magnetic field.

![[0625-s20-32-p15-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 11.1**

Describe **two** ways of increasing the turning effect on the coil. **[2]**

**(b)** The motor for the model train uses an alternating voltage of 12 V. This is supplied by the secondary coil of a transformer.

The primary coil of the transformer is connected to a mains voltage of 240 V.

The primary coil has 900 turns.

Calculate the number of turns on the secondary coil.

number of turns on the secondary coil =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** 45

> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |

> |  | N$_{\text{s}}$ = (12 ÷ 240) × 900 | C1 |
