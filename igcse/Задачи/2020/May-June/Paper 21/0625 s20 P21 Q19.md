> Объяснить различие потерь холодного воздуха при открывании морозильников разных конструкций.

Two freezers X and Y are identical except that one has a door opening at the front and the other

has a door opening at the top.

![[0625-s20-21-p7-f1.svg]]

Both doors are the same size and are opened for the same amount of time.

Which freezer gains the least amount of thermal energy in this time and why?

|  | freezer gaining the least thermal energy | reason |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | X | cold air falls |
| **B** | X | warm air falls |
| **C** | Y | cold air falls |
| **D** | Y | warm air falls |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
