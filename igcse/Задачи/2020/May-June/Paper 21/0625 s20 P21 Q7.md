> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Найти положение человека на невесомой балке по моментам его веса и натяжения вертикальной верёвки.

The diagram shows a wooden beam PQ, of negligible weight, which is attached to a wall by a hinge at P and kept in a horizontal position by a vertical rope attached at Q.

The beam is 3.0 m in length.

A man of weight 800 N walks along the beam from P to Q.

![[0625-s20-21-p3-f1.png]]

What is the distance of the man from P when the tension in the rope at Q becomes equal to 500 N?

**A** 0.53 m  
**B** 1.1 m  
**C** 1.9 m  
**D** 2.5 m

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
