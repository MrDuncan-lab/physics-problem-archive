> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определение толщины проволоки, плотности металла и объёма катушки с оценкой точности измерений.

Fig. 1.1 shows a coil of wire.

![[0625-s20-31-p2-f1.png]]

**Fig. 1.1** (not to scale)

**(a)** A student measures the length of the coil using a ruler. His measurement is 3.8 cm.

There are 20 turns of wire in the coil. The student uses his measurement to calculate the average thickness of the wire.

**(i)** Show that the average thickness of the wire is about 0.2 cm.

average thickness of wire =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm **[2]**

**(ii)** The student’s measurement of 3.8 cm is inaccurate.

Suggest **one** reason why the measurement is inaccurate. **[1]**

**(b)** The volume of the wire in the coil is 16.6 cm$^{3}$ and its mass is 148 g.

Calculate the density of the metal used for the wire in the coil.

density =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ g / cm$^{3}$ **[3]**

**(c)** The student has a measuring cylinder and a beaker of water, as shown in Fig. 1.2.

![[0625-s20-31-p3-f1.png]]

**Fig. 1.2**

Describe how the student can determine the volume of the coil by using the equipment shown in Fig. 1.2. **[4]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)(i)** (average thickness =) 0.19 (cm) (which is about 0.2 cm)
>
> **(b)** (*ρ* =) 8.9 (g / cm$^{3}$)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | (average thickness =) 3.8 ÷ 20 | C1 |
> |  | (average thickness =) 0.19 (cm) (which is about 0.2 cm) | A1 |
> | (a)(ii) | any **one** from:<br>wire(s) not touching OR wire stretched (in places) OR ruler not at zero (owtte) OR wire(s) overlapping OR eye not<br>directly above ruler (owtte) | B1 |
> | (b) | density = mass ÷ volume OR ρ= $\dfrac{m}{V}$ in any form. | C1 |
> |  | (*ρ* =) 148 ÷ 16.6 | C1 |
> |  | (*ρ* =) 8.9 (g / cm$^{3}$) | A1 |
> | (c) | measuring cylinder partially filled with water<br>coil submerged in water (owtte)<br>new volume noted<br>volume of wire = difference or increase in volume(s) | B4 |
