> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объяснение защиты заземлением и предохранителем и расчёт выходного напряжения трансформатора.

A teacher uses a power supply in a metal case. The circuit for the power supply includes a fuse.

**(a)** **(i)** Draw the electrical symbol for a fuse. **[1]**

**(ii)** The metal case of the power supply is earthed. A fault occurs and a live wire touches the metal case.

Explain how earthing the metal case protects the teacher. **[3]**

**(b)** The power supply circuit includes a transformer. Its input voltage is 240 V. There are 960 turns on the input coil and 64 turns on the output coil.

Calculate the output voltage of the transformer.

output voltage =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ V **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** 16 (V)

> |  | any **two** from:<br>current heats (fuse) wire<br>fuse melts / blows<br>(power supply circuit / metal case) is disconnected from mains (supply) | B2 |

> |  | (V$_{\text{s}}$ =) (64 × 240) ÷ 960 | C1 |
