Some gas molecules are in a box at room temperature. Fig. 3.1 shows the position of some of the molecules and the direction of movement of each molecule.

![[0625-s20-31-p5-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 3.1**

**(a)** **(i)** Describe the movement of the gas molecules. **[2]**

**(ii)** Describe how the molecules exert a pressure on the walls of the box. **[2]**

**(b)** The gas in Fig. 3.1 is cooled. The gas turns into a liquid then into a solid.

State how the average separation of molecules in the gas is different from the average separation of molecules in the solid. **[1]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
