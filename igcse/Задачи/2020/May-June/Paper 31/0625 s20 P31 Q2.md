> Определение длины и удлинения пружины по графику и описание деформирующего действия силы.

**(a)** A student stretches a spring by adding different loads to it. She measures the length of the spring for each load. She plots a graph of the results.

Fig. 2.1 shows the graph of her results.

![[0625-s20-31-p4-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 2.1**

Use the graph to determine:

**(i)** the length of the spring without a load

length =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm **[1]**

**(ii)** the length of the spring with a load of 4.0 N

length =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm **[1]**

**(iii)** the extension due to a 4.0 N load.

extension =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm **[1]**

**(b)** Complete the sentence about effects of forces. Choose words from the box.

![[0625-s20-31-p4-f2.svg]]

colour friction pressure shape size speed

Stretching a spring with a load is an example of how a force can change the

\_\_\_\_\_\_ and the  \_\_\_\_\_\_ of an object. **[2]**
