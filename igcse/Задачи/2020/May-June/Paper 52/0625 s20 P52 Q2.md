In this experiment, you will investigate how the potential difference across a resistance wire varies with the length of the wire.

Carry out the following instructions, referring to Fig. 2.1.

![[0625-s20-52-p6-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 2.1**

**(a)**

- Switch on.
- Place the sliding contact C on the resistance wire at a distance *l* = 10.0 cm from B.
- Measure, and record in Table 2.1, the potential difference *V* across the length *l* of the resistance wire.
- Measure, and record in Table 2.1, the current *I* in the circuit.
- Repeat the procedure using *l* = 30.0 cm, 50.0 cm, 70.0 cm and 90.0 cm.
- Switch off.

**(b)** Plot a graph of *V* / V (*y*-axis) against *l* / cm (*x*-axis). Start both axes at the origin (0,0).

**(c)** **(i)** Write a conclusion about the value of the current *I* in the circuit as the position of the sliding contact C is changed. **[1]**
