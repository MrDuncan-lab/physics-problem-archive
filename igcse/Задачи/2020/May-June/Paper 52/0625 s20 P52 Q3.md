Carry out the following instructions referring to Fig. 3.1.

**(a)** Use the thermometer to measure room temperature θ$_{\text{S}}$.

θ$_{\text{S}}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

Use the thermometer to measure the temperature θ$_{\text{H}}$ of the hot water in beaker B.

θ$_{\text{H}}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(b)** Beaker A contains sand. Pour 100 cm$^{3}$ of hot water from beaker B into beaker A. Carefully stir the water and sand mixture.

Record the highest temperature θ$_{\text{M}}$ of the mixture.

θ$_{\text{M}}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(c)** **(i)** Calculate the rise in temperature θ$_{\text{R}}$ of the sand using the equation θ$_{\text{R}}$ = (θ$_{\text{M}}$ – θ$_{\text{S}}$).

θ$_{\text{R}}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

Calculate the fall in temperature θ$_{\text{F}}$ of the hot water using the equation θ$_{\text{F}}$ = (θ$_{\text{H}}$ – θ$_{\text{M}}$).

θ$_{\text{F}}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(ii)** Calculate the ratio *S* using the equation *S* = $\dfrac{\theta _{R}}{\theta}_{\text{F}}$ .

*S* =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(d)** Measure the new temperature θ$_{\text{H}}$ of the hot water supplied.

θ$_{\text{H}}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(e)** Beaker C contains water at room temperature. Pour 100 cm$^{3}$ of the hot water into beaker C. Carefully stir the water.

Record the highest temperature θ$_{\text{M}}$ of the mixture.

θ$_{\text{M}}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(f)** Calculate the rise in temperature θ$_{\text{R}}$ of the cold water using the equation θ$_{\text{R}}$ = (θ$_{\text{M}}$ – θ$_{\text{S}}$). Use the value of room temperature θ$_{\text{S}}$ recorded in **(a)** and the value of θ$_{\text{M}}$ recorded in **(e)**.

θ$_{\text{R}}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

Calculate the fall in temperature θ$_{\text{F}}$ of the hot water using the equation θ$_{\text{F}}$ = (θ$_{\text{H}}$ – θ$_{\text{M}}$). Use the value of θ$_{\text{H}}$from **(d)** and θ$_{\text{M}}$ from **(e)**.

θ$_{\text{F}}$ =  \_\_\_\_\_\_

Calculate the ratio *W* using the equation *W* = $\dfrac{\theta _{R}}{\theta}_{\text{F}}$ .

*W* =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**
