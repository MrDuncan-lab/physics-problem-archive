> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определяют положения минимальной потенциальной и максимальной упругой энергии груза на пружине.

A mass bounces up and down on a steel spring. The diagram shows the mass and the spring at different points during the motion.

![[0625-s20-13-p5-f1.svg]]

At which point does the mass have the least gravitational potential energy and at which point is the most elastic energy stored in the spring?

|  | least amount of gravitational potential energy | most elastic energy stored in spring |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | mass moving down | mass moving up |
| **B** | mass moving down | lowest point |
| **C** | lowest point | mass moving up |
| **D** | lowest point | lowest point |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
