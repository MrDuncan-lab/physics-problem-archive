> Перенос тепловой энергии излучением через вакуум.

How does thermal energy (heat energy) travel through the vacuum between the Earth and the Sun?

**A** by conduction  
**B** by convection  
**C** by radiation  
**D** by radioactive decay

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
