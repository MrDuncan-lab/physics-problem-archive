> Изменения кинетической и потенциальной энергии автомобиля на подъёме.

A car accelerates along a road as it rises uphill.

![[0625-w02-1-p5-f2.svg]]

Which energy changes are taking place?

|  | energy of motion (kinetic energy) | energy of position (gravitational potential energy) |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | decreasing | decreasing |
| **B** | decreasing | increasing |
| **C** | increasing | decreasing |
| **D** | increasing | increasing |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
