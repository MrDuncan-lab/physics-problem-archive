> Разложение белого света призмой на цвета.

One of the effects of passing a ray of white light through a prism is to split the light into colours.

![[0625-w02-1-p10-f1.svg]]

What is the name given to this effect?

**A** deviation  
**B** dispersion  
**C** reflection  
**D** refraction

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
