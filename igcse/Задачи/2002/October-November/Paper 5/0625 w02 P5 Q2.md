> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> (a) – (e) Full marks here were awarded to candidates who recorded all the temperatures with correct trends
>
> (showing the appropriate decreases or increases in temperature) and who gave all temperatures to better than 1°C. Many failed to show this level of accuracy.
>
> (f) The graphs were generally well plotted with a suitable temperature scale using at least half of the available grid but some candidates lost marks since their line was too thick or a poorly judged best fit curve.
>
> (g) Most candidates correctly concluded that the rate of cooling is slower, but few responded correctly to the question since they did not justify their conclusion by reference to the table of readings. Rather they attempted a theoretical answer. They should be advised that theoretical answers are not required in the practical examination.
