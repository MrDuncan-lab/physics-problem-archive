> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> (a) – (c) The majority of candidates recorded currents and voltages correctly with values given to at least 1
>
> dp and the units present. Full marks for the ratio were awarded if it was arithmetically correct, to 2 or 3 sf and was given no unit.
>
> (d) – (f) Some candidates performed the calculation of resistance incorrectly in spite of being given the
>
> equation to use. Most however were correct, enabling them to answer part (f) sensibly. Here candidates should realise that within the limits of experimental error they can conclude that the two ratios are equal.
>
> (g) Most candidates drew a correct circuit with the voltmeter in parallel with the resistor. The most common error was in the variable resistor symbol. Some candidates drew a thermistor symbol and others a symbol which was a cross between a thermistor and a variable resistor.
