> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> (a) Accuracy was expected in measuring x. Most candidates were then able to calculate the average diameter correctly and go on to calculate V giving the answer to 2 or 3 sf and with the correct unit.
>
> (b) Some candidates were apparently unused to a measuring cylinder and a range of incorrect readings was seen although many gave the correct response. Most could continue to calculate the volume of one bead.
>
> (c) A variety of answers was accepted here. Candidates had to show an appreciation of limits of accuracy. For example the following were given credit.

> � Method 2 is better; method 1 does not take into account the irregular shapes of the beads.
