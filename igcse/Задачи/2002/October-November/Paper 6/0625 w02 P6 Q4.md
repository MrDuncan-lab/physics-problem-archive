> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The majority of candidates scored well on the question, correctly stating that heat loss was the most likely cause. Precautions involving lids and insulation were commonly suggested and these were given credit, as were sensible suggestions about stirring or repeat experiments. However suggestions of an assistant to check results and similar points were not credited. Most gave the correct thermometer reading of 38°C but some wrote 30.8°C. The majority of candidates calculated the power (66 W) but some lost a mark by giving an incorrect unit (e.g. N or kg).
