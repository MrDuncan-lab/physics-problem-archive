# Question 10 (6 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2002 · Paper 3 — Extended theory · вопрос 10  
Источник: [[_pdf/2002/0625_w02_qp_3.pdf#page=14|условие, стр. 14]] · [[_pdf/2002/0625_w02_ms_3.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2002/0625_w02_er.pdf#page=1|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Построение графика полураспада бета-источника и запись ядерной реакции.

Fig. 10.1 is part of the decay curve for a sample of a β-emitting isotope.

![[0625-w02-3-p14-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 10.1**

**(a)** Use Fig. 10.1 to find the half-life of the isotope.

half-life =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(b)** Complete Fig. 10.1 as far as time = 20 days, by working out the values of a number of points and plotting them. Show your working. **[2]**

**(c)** The decay product of the β-emitting isotope is not radioactive. Explain why the sample of the radioactive isotope will be safer after 20 days than after 1 day. Support your answer by reference to the graph. **[1]**

**(d)** The isotope used for this decay curve may be represented by the symbol$^{\text{A}}_{\text{Z}}$X. Write down an equation, by filling in the gaps below, to show the β-decay of this isotope to a decay product that has the symbol Y.

$^{\text{A}}_{\text{Z}}$X → + Y **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** half-life 4 days*
>
> **(b)** suitable curve completed
>
> **(d)** ${}^{A}_{Z}\text{X} \rightarrow {}^{0}_{-1}\text{e} + {}^{A}_{Z+1}\text{Y}$ top line, A1/ bottom line A1 or ${}^{0}_{-1}\beta$ (not e or β alone) ${}^{A}_{Z}\text{X} \rightarrow \text{e}/\beta + {}^{A}_{Z+1}\text{Y}$ (C1)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | half-life 4 days* | A1 |
> | (b) | at least two points worked out | M1 |
> |  | suitable curve completed | A1 |
> | (c) | by 20 days little radioactivity left, after 1 day about 85% left | B1 |
> | (d) | ${}^{A}_{Z}\text{X} \rightarrow {}^{0}_{-1}\text{e} + {}^{A}_{Z+1}\text{Y}$ top line, A1/ bottom line A1<br>or ${}^{0}_{-1}\beta$ (not e or β alone)<br>${}^{A}_{Z}\text{X} \rightarrow \text{e}/\beta + {}^{A}_{Z+1}\text{Y}$ (C1) | A2 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> (a) The value 4 was usually given wrong units included time/days, min and even s.
>
> (b) Most managed to do this correctly, with a significant number wrongly halving the percentage every 5 days even when they gave 4 days in (a).
>
> (c) Many good clear answers, but some refused to quote any evidence from the graph.
>
> (d) Most candidates seemed to find balancing decay equations very easy and scored 2 marks. The others made every possible mistake one could think of, with some making no attempt at all.
>
> Answer: (a) 4 days.
