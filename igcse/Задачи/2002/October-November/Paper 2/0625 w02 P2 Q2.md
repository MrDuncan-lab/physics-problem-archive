> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Теплопроводники, теплоизоляторы, конвекция и излучение на бытовых примерах.

Here are five words relating to the transfer of thermal energy.

**conductor**, **convection**, **evaporation**, **insulator**, **radiation**

Complete each of the following sentences by writing in the appropriate word from the list.

**(a)** Cooking pots often have a wooden handle, because wood is a good thermal

**(b)** Thermal energy reaches Earth from the Sun by means of  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(c)** Copper is a good  \_\_\_\_\_\_ of thermal energy.

**(d)** The heating element is put at the bottom of an electric kettle, so that

\_\_\_\_\_\_ can rapidly transfer thermal energy throughout the water. **[1]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | insulator | B1 |
> | (b) | radiation | B1 |
> | (c) | conductor | B1 |
> | (d) | convection | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This was a question which was well answered by most candidates. The most common mistake was to interchange insulator and conductor in (a) and (c).
