# Question 5 (3 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2002 · Paper 2 — Core theory · вопрос 5  
Источник: [[_pdf/2002/0625_w02_qp_2.pdf#page=6|условие, стр. 6]] · [[_pdf/2002/0625_w02_ms_2.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2002/0625_w02_er.pdf#page=1|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w02 P2]]  
Темы: [[5.2.3 Radioactive decay]], [[5.2.4 Half-life]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Изменения ядра при распаде и расчёт нескольких периодов полураспада.

**(a)** What happens to the nucleus of an atom that undergoes radioactive decay? **[1]**

**(b)** Strontium-90 has a half-life of 28 years. How much time must pass before its activity falls to of its original value?

time =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ years **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** 56 (years)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | changes into a different nucleus/substance/isotope/nuclide<br>OR loses/emits part of itself/particles<br>OR loses/emits an alpha/beta particle/gamma ray<br>OR mass decreases OR different mass no. | B1 |
> | (b) | evidence of 2 half-lives | C1 |
> |  | 56 (years) | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Answers to this question were almost always dismally poor. Vagueness ruled! Most candidates do not seem to know precisely what happens to a nucleus during radioactive decay. A lot of answers confused decay with fission. In (b), it was rare to find a candidate who even realised that two half-lives were involved, let alone obtain the correct answer.
>
> Answer: 56 yrs.
