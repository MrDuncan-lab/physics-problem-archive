> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Экспериментальное измерение плотности масла и сравнение объёмов жидкостей.

In an experiment to find the density of some oil, a student takes the following readings.

![[0625-w02-2-p5-f1.svg]]

mass of empty measuring jug = 610g

**Fig. 4.1**

![[0625-w02-2-p5-f2.svg]]

mass of jug containing 500cm$^{3}$ of oil = 1020g

**Fig. 4.2**

**(a)** **(i)** Calculate the mass of oil in the jug.

mass of oil = \_\_\_\_\_\_ g

**(ii)** Calculate the density of the oil.

density of oil = \_\_\_\_\_\_

**(iii)** How could the volume of the oil be more accurately measured than with the measuring jug? **[7]**

**(b)** Water is more dense than oil.

On Fig. 4.3, mark approximately where the surface of the same mass of water would be if it replaced the oil. **[1]**

![[0625-w02-2-p5-f3.svg]]

**Fig. 4.3**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)(i)** 410 (g)
>
> **(a)(ii)** 0.82 e.c.f.

> |  | g/cm$^{3}$ | B1 |
> | (a)(iii) | use measuring cylinder/pipette/narrower jug/burette | B1 |
> | (b) | level shown below oil level | B1 |
