> Предложить опыт с поглотителями и радиационным детектором для подтверждения чистого бета-излучения источника.

Fig. 12.1 shows a radioactive source placed close to a radiation detector and counter. The detector can detect α, β and γ radiation.

**Fig. 12.1**

The radioactive source emits β-particles only.

Describe how you could show that the source emits β-particles only. As part of your answer, you may draw on Fig. 12.1 and add any other apparatus you may need. **[4]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> |  | For full marks the method described must work.<br>any **four** from:<br>take background reading / reading without source<br>use piece of paper between source and detector<br>beta particles can pass through paper or alpha particles stopped / absorbed by paper?)<br>reading on detector similar / unchanged<br>so no alpha particles emitted<br>use few mm of aluminium between source and detector<br>reading on detector now similar to background reading<br>(because) beta particles stopped by a few mm of aluminium | B4 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Only the most able candidates answered fully correctly. Many candidates gave good descriptions of using different absorbers between the source and the detector, but a lack of detail or accuracy often resulted in only partial credit being awarded.
