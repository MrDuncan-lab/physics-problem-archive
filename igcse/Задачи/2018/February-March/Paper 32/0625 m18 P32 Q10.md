# Question 10 (6 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · February/March 2018 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 10  
Источник: [[_pdf/2018/0625_m18_qp_32.pdf#page=17|условие, стр. 17–18]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_m18_ms_32.pdf#page=8|схема оценивания, стр. 8]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_m18_er.pdf#page=7|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 m18 P32]]  
Темы: [[4.5.1 Electromagnetic induction]], [[4.5.6 The transformer]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Описать индукцию при колебаниях магнита в катушке и распознать трансформаторы линии электропередачи.

**(a)** A teacher demonstrates electromagnetic induction using the apparatus shown in Fig. 10.1.

![[0625-m18-32-p17-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 10.1**

The teacher pulls the magnet down until one end is inside the coil. The teacher then releases the magnet. The magnet moves up and down repeatedly. As it moves, one end of the magnet enters and leaves the coil.

Describe and explain the readings on the centre-zero millivoltmeter as the magnet enters and leaves the coil. **[4]**

**(b)** Fig. 10.2 shows a system for transmitting electricity from a power station.

![[0625-m18-32-p18-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 10.2**

State the name of the device used in X and the name of the device used in Y.

X  \_\_\_\_\_\_

Y  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | any **four** from:<br>needle moves (one way) as N pole / magnet moves downward / into coil<br>as coil cuts across magnetic field (of magnet)<br>needle moves in opposite direction as N pole / magnet moves upward / out of coil<br>field is cut in opposite direction<br>needle at zero when spring at max extension<br>magnet is stationary so no cutting of field lines | B4 |
> | (b) | (device) X is a step-up transformer | B1 |
> |  | (device) Y is a step-down transformer | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Very few candidates gained full credit for this question. The majority of candidates failed to link the relative movement of the magnet and coil to the cutting of magnetic field lines, and hence the direction of the e.m.f.
>
> **(b)** Many candidates answered this question well. A common error was to fail to identify **X** as a step-up transformer and **Y** as a step-down transformer.
