# Question 1 (6 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · February/March 2018 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 1  
Источник: [[_pdf/2018/0625_m18_qp_32.pdf#page=2|условие, стр. 2–3]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_m18_ms_32.pdf#page=4|схема оценивания, стр. 4]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_m18_er.pdf#page=7|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 m18 P32]]  
Темы: [[1.1 Physical quantities and measurement techniques]], [[1.4 Density]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить толщину листа, объём стопки бумаги, прибор для измерения массы и плотность бумаги.

A student has a pile of A4 paper for his computer printer.

Fig. 1.1 shows the dimensions of the pile of paper.

![[0625-m18-32-p2-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 1.1** (not to scale)

**(a)** The pile contains 500 sheets of paper.

Calculate the average thickness of one sheet of paper.

average thickness =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ cm **[1]**

**(b)** Show that the pile of paper has a volume of 3000 cm$^{3}$. Use the information shown in Fig. 1.1. **[1]**

**(c)** The student measures the total mass of the paper in the pile.

State the name of a device used to measure mass. **[1]**

**(d)** The mass of the paper in the pile is 2400 g.

Calculate the density of the paper.

density =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ g / cm$^{3}$ **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(d)** 0.80 (g / cm$^{3}$) or 0.8 (g / cm$^{3}$)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | 5.0 ÷ 500 or 0.01 (cm) | B1 |
> | (b) | 30 × 20 × 5 or length × width × height or cross-sectional area × length | B1 |
> | (c) | balance | B1 |
> | (d) | D = M / V in any acceptable form | C1 |
> |  | 2400 ÷ 3000 | C1 |
> |  | 0.80 (g / cm$^{3}$) or 0.8 (g / cm$^{3}$) | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** The majority of candidates correctly calculated the thickness of one sheet of paper. Weaker candidates divided one of the dimensions of the pile paper by another dimension.
>
> **(b)** Most candidates correctly used length × breadth × height to show that the volume of the pile of paper was 3000 cm$^{3}$.
>
> **(c)** Most candidates gave a suitable device for measuring mass. Weaker candidates gave answers such as vernier callipers.
>
> **(d)** Most candidates correctly calculated the density as 0.8 g/cm$^{3}$. Weaker candidates used an inverted form of the equation for density.
