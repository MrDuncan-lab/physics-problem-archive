> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Дополнить схему измерительными приборами и реостатом и рассчитать сопротивление лампы.

A student sets up an electrical circuit. She draws part of the circuit diagram, as shown in Fig. 8.1.

![[0625-m18-32-p15-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 8.1**

**(a)** On Fig. 8.1, draw the circuit symbols for three devices so that the student can:

**(i)** measure the total current in the circuit **[1]**

**(ii)** vary the current in the lamp B only **[1]**

**(iii)** measure the potential difference (p.d.) across lamp B. **[2]**

**(b)** The current in lamp A is 0.20 A. The potential difference (p.d.) across lamp A is 6.0 V.

Calculate the resistance of lamp A.

resistance =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ Ω **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** 30 (Ω)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | ammeter in series with power supply | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | variable resistor symbol in series with lamp B | B1 |
> | (a)(iii) | voltmeter symbol seen | B1 |
> |  | voltmeter symbol in parallel with lamp B | B1 |
> | (b) | V = I R in any form | C1 |
> |  | 6.0 ÷ 0.2 accept 6 ÷ 0.2 | C1 |
> |  | 30 (Ω) | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Many candidates gained full credit for this question. The most common errors were incorrect placement of the voltmeter, or not identifying a variable resistor as a device for varying the current in lamp **B**.
>
> **(b)** The majority of candidates gained full credit for this calculation. Weaker candidates used an inverted form of the equation for resistance.
