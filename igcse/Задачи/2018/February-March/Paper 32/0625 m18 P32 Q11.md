# Question 11 (5 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · February/March 2018 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 11  
Источник: [[_pdf/2018/0625_m18_qp_32.pdf#page=19|условие, стр. 19]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_m18_ms_32.pdf#page=9|схема оценивания, стр. 9]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_m18_er.pdf#page=7|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

Темы: [[4.2.1 Electric charge]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объяснить электризацию полимерного стержня трением и взаимодействие одноимённо заряженных стержней.

**(a)** A student rubs a polythene rod with a dry cloth. The polythene rod becomes negatively charged.

Describe and explain how the rod becomes negatively charged. **[3]**

**(b)** The negatively charged polythene rod hangs from a nylon thread so that it is free to turn.

The student charges a second polythene rod and brings it close to the first rod, as shown in Fig. 11.1.

![[0625-m18-32-p19-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 11.1**

Describe and explain what happens when the negatively charged rods are close to each other. **[2]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | friction (between cloth and rod) | B1 |
> |  | (causes) electrons to move | B1 |
> |  | from cloth / to rod | B1 |
> | (b) | rod {moves or rotates} away | B1 |
> |  | like charges repel | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Many candidates gained partial credit, with only the strongest gaining full credit. A common error was omitting that friction caused electrons to transfer.
>
> **(b)** The majority of candidates recognised that the rods would move apart because they both had the same type of electric charge.
