**(b)** A tennis player is practising by hitting a ball many times against a wall.

The ball hits the wall 20 times in 60 s. The average change in momentum for each collision with the wall is 4.2 kg m / s.

Calculate the average force that the ball exerts on the wall.

average force =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** 1.4 N
