# Question 2 (7 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · February/March 2018 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 2 · вопрос 2  
Источник: [[_pdf/2018/0625_m18_qp_42.pdf#page=3|условие, стр. 3]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_m18_ms_42.pdf#page=5|схема оценивания, стр. 5]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_m18_er.pdf#page=11|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 m18 P42]]  
Темы: [[1.7.1 Energy]], [[1.7.3 Energy resources]], [[1.7.4 Power]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать увеличение потенциальной энергии пассажиров горного поезда и эффективность их подъёма по мощности и времени работы двигателя.

**(a)** A force is used to move an object from the Earth’s surface to a greater height.

Explain why the gravitational potential energy (g.p.e.) of the object increases. **[1]**

**(b)** Fig. 2.1 shows a train moving up towards the top of a mountain.

![[0625-m18-42-p3-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 2.1**

The train transports 80 passengers, each of average mass 65 kg, through a vertical height of 1600 m.

Calculate the increase in the total gravitational potential energy (g.p.e.) of the passengers.

increase in g.p.e. =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(c)** The engine of the train has a power of 1500 kW. The time taken to reach the top of the mountain is 30 minutes.

Calculate the efficiency of the engine in raising the 80 passengers 1600 m to the top of the mountain.

efficiency =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[4]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** 8.3 × 10$^{7}$ J
>
> **(c)** 0.031 OR 3.1 %

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | (Because g.p.e. is)<br>the work done by the force<br>OR the force × the distance that the object rises<br><u>OR mgh and height is greater</u> | B1 |
> | (b) | mgh OR 80 × 65 × 10 × 1600 | C1 |
> |  | 8.3 × 10$^{7}$ J | A1 |
> | (c) | <u>Method 1</u> |  |
> |  | W = Pt OR E = Pt in any form | C1 |
> |  | Work input =1500 × 10$^{3}$ × 30 × 60 OR 2.7 × 10$^{9}$ J | C1 |
> |  | Efficiency = work output / work input (x 100) | C1 |
> |  | 0.031 OR 3.1 % | A1 |
> |  | <u>Method 2</u> |  |
> |  | P = E/t in any form | (C1) |
> |  | Power output = 8.3 × 10$^{7}$ / 30 × 60 | (C1) |
> |  | Efficiency = power output / power input (× 100) | (C1) |
> |  | 0.031 OR 3.1% | (A1) |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** A number of candidates repeated the question in their answers. A small minority of candidates grasped the idea that the potential energy of the object increases because work is done by the force used to raise it. Alternatively, some candidates gained credit by referring to mgh and the increase in the value of h.
>
> **(b)** The increase in g.p.e. was usually calculated correctly. However a small minority of candidates failed to calculate the total mass of all the passengers.
>
> **(c)** The efficiency could be calculated using either work or power, but some candidates confused the two. Others wrote down a formula for efficiency with input and output interchanged from their correct positions. Some candidates had the correct working but gave the final fraction or percentage with one significant figure only and so could not be awarded credit.
