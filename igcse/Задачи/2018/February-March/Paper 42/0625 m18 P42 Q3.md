# Question 3 (7 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · February/March 2018 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 2 · вопрос 3  
Источник: [[_pdf/2018/0625_m18_qp_42.pdf#page=4|условие, стр. 4]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_m18_ms_42.pdf#page=6|схема оценивания, стр. 6]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_m18_er.pdf#page=11|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 m18 P42]]  
Темы: [[1.5.2 Turning effect of forces]], [[1.5.3 Centre of gravity]], [[1.3 Mass and weight]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить момент силы, центр тяжести и массу стержня и объяснить равновесие сил и моментов.

**(a)** Complete the statement by writing in the blank spaces.

The moment of a force about a pivot is equal to  \_\_\_\_\_\_

multiplied by  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(b)** Fig. 3.1 shows a horizontal rod of length 2.4 m and weight 160 N. The weight of the rod acts at its centre. The rod is suspended by two vertical ropes X and Y. The tension in each rope is 80 N.

![[0625-m18-42-p4-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 3.1**

**(i)** State the name given to the point at which the weight of the rod acts. **[1]**

**(ii)** Calculate the mass of the rod.

mass =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(iii)** The rod is in equilibrium.

Using data from Fig. 3.1, explain why. **[4]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)(iii)** 80 N + 80 N = 160 N
>
> **(b)(iii)** clockwise moment (about centre) = 80 × 1.2 anticlockwise moment (about centre) = 80 × 1.2

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | ...(the) force × <u>its perpendicular distance from pivot / a point</u> | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | centre of mass | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | (mass =) 160 / 10 = 16 kg | B1 |
> | (b)(iii) | (Not moving up or down because) no resultant (vertical) force<br>OR upward force = downward force | C1 |
> |  | 80 N + 80 N = 160 N | A1 |
> |  | (Not rotating because) no resultant moment (about any point)<br>OR (sum of) clockwise moments = (sum of) anticlockwise moments | C1 |
> |  | clockwise moment (about centre) = 80 × 1.2<br>anticlockwise moment (about centre) = 80 × 1.2 | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** This was only answered well by the strongest candidates. The answer “force multiplied by distance” was a common answer. Others candidates gave “perpendicular distance” but failed to add ‘”from a point” or “from a pivot”.
>
> **(b) (i)** A significant number of candidates wrote down “pivot” rather than centre of mass. Centre of gravity, though not the normal term, was also accepted.
>
> **(ii)** Most candidates successfully worked out the correct answer of 16 kg. Some gave the answer as 16 g however.
>
> **(iii)** Some candidates wrote only about the equilibrium of the forces. A smaller number of candidates only considered the equilibrium of the moments of the forces. However, many candidates covered both aspects. More candidates were successful in using the data from the figure about the forces correctly rather than about the moments.
