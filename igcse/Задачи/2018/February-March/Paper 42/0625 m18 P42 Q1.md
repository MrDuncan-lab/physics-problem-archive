# Question 1 (8 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · February/March 2018 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 2 · вопрос 1

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить ускорение, построить график движения автомобиля и описать силу и скорость при движении по окружности.

**(a)** Define *acceleration*. **[1]**

**(b)** Fig. 1.1 shows the speed-time axes for the graph of the motion of a car.

![[0625-m18-42-p2-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 1.1**

**(i)** The car starts from rest.

From time = 0 to time = 15 s, the car has a constant acceleration to a speed of 28 m / s.

From time = 15 s to time = 32 s, the car has a constant speed of 28 m / s.

From time = 32 s, the car has a constant deceleration of 2.0 m / s$^{2}$ until it comes to rest.

On Fig. 1.1, draw the graph, using the space below for any calculations. **[5]**

**(ii)** From time = 15 s to time = 32 s, the path of the car is part of a circle.

For this motion, state

**1.** the direction of the resultant force on the car,

**2.** what happens to the velocity of the car. **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)(i)** Straight line from (32, 28) to (46, 0)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | Rate of change of velocity OR change of velocity / time<br>OR change of velocity over time OR (v – u)/t | B1 |

> |  | Horizontal line {from (15, 28)} to (32, 28) | B1 |
> |  | a = (v – u) / t<br>OR (t =) (v – u) / a OR (0 – 28) / 2.0 | C1 |
> |  | = 14 (s) | C1 |
> |  | Straight line from (32, 28) to (46, 0) | A1 |
> | (b)(ii) | **1** Towards the centre of the circle / inwards | B1 |
> |  | **2** Velocity is (continually) changing its direction | B1 |
