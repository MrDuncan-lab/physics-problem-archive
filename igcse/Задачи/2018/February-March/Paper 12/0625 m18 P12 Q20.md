> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнить тепловое излучение окрашенных пластин; в присоединённом вопросе различить поперечные и продольные волны.

A hot metal plate is placed near to a cold metal plate. Infra-red radiation transfers thermal energy

between the two plates.

![[0625-m18-12-p10-f1.svg]]

Which choice of colour causes the temperature of the cold plate to increase most slowly?

|  | hot plate | cold plate |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | matt black | matt black |
| **B** | matt black | shiny white |
| **C** | shiny white | matt black |
| **D** | shiny white | shiny white |

|  |  |  | Which row shows an example of a transverse wave and an example of a longitudinal wave? |
|---|---|---|---|
|  | transverse | longitudinal |  |
| **A** | light | radio |  |
| **B** | radio | sound |  |
| **C** | sound | water |  |
| **D** | water | light |  |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Only stronger candidates were able to answer this question correctly. The logic is that the rise in temperature of the cooler plate will be slowest when there is least energy emitted from the hotter plate (shiny white) and when it absorbs the smallest proportion of that energy (once more, the shiny white plate).
