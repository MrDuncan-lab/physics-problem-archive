> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнить ток, яркость и длительность питания ламп при последовательном и параллельном соединениях.

Diagram 1 shows two identical lamps connected in series with a cell. Diagram 2 shows the same

two lamps connected in parallel with an identical cell.

![[0625-m18-12-p14-f2.svg]]

Which statement is correct?

**A** The cell in diagram 1 can supply current to the lamps for longer than the cell in diagram 2.  
**B** The current in the cell in diagram 1 is greater than the current in the cell in diagram 2.  
**C** The lamps in diagram 1 are brighter than the lamps in diagram 2.  
**D** The voltage across each lamp in diagram 1 is greater than the voltage in diagram 2.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Understanding that a parallel circuit has less resistance than a series circuit (and therefore more current) was not well understood. Candidates chose each of the four options in almost equal numbers.
