# Question 6 (1 mark)

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Найти плотность жидкости по увеличению массы и объёма при доливании.

A measuring cylinder contains 30 cm$^{3}$ of a liquid.

![[0625-m18-12-p4-f1.svg]]

Some more of the liquid is added until the liquid level reaches the 50 cm$^{3}$ mark.

The reading on the balance increases by 30 g.

What is the density of the liquid?

**A** 0.60 g / cm$^{3}$  
**B** 0.67 g / cm$^{3}$  
**C** 1.5 g / cm$^{3}$  
**D** 1.7 g / cm$^{3}$

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Only stronger candidates answered this question correctly. Many candidates thought that response **A** was correct as they applied the formula for density without thinking the problem through.
