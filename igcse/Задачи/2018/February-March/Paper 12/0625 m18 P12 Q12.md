> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить процесс деления тяжёлых ядер как источник энергии атомной электростанции.

Which source of energy is used in a nuclear power station to generate electrical energy?

**A** different types of atom regrouping  
**B** heavy nuclei splitting  
**C** radioactive isotopes decaying  
**D** radioactive atoms emitting β-particles

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question proved challenging for many candidates. Although most candidates ruled out option **A,** (chemical change) all other options were selected by equal numbers of candidates.
