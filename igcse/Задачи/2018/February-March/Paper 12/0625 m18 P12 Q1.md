> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбрать прибор для определения объёма камня неправильной формы методом вытеснения воды.

A student is asked to find the volume of a small irregularly-shaped piece of rock.

He has the following apparatus available.

![[0625-m18-12-p2-f1.svg]]

containing water

Which apparatus must the student use to find the volume of the small piece of rock?

**A** balance and rule  
**B** rule only  
**C** balance and measuring cylinder  
**D** measuring cylinder only

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Some candidates did not read this question carefully enough. The question required candidates to identify the instruments necessary to measure the volume of an irregularly shaped rock – it did not require the mass of the rock as well.
