# Question 40 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · February/March 2018 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 40  
Источник: [[_pdf/2018/0625_m18_qp_12.pdf#page=18|условие, стр. 18]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_m18_ms_12.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_m18_er.pdf#page=1|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 m18 P12]]  
Темы: [[5.2.4 Half-life]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить время уменьшения активности радиоактивного образца по графику доли оставшегося изотопа.

The graph shows the percentage of a radioactive isotope that remains as time passes.

![[0625-m18-12-p18-f1.svg]]

A student measures the initial rate of emission of this isotope as 40 000 emissions per second.

How long does it take for the rate of emission to fall to 5000 emissions per second?

**A** 5 minutes  
**B** 15 minutes  
**C** 20 minutes  
**D** 40 minutes

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question proved challenging to many candidates. The most common error was to simply find the half-life of the isotope and not to go on to use that to find the remaining mass of the isotope.
