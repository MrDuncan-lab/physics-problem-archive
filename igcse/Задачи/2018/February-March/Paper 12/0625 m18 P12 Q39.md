# Question 39 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · February/March 2018 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 39  
Источник: [[_pdf/2018/0625_m18_qp_12.pdf#page=17|условие, стр. 17]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_m18_ms_12.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_m18_er.pdf#page=1|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 m18 P12]]  
Темы: [[5.2.1 Detection of radioactivity]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить понятие фонового ионизирующего излучения при измерении активности образца.

When measuring the emissions from a radioactive rock brought into the laboratory, a teacher

mentions that background radiation must be taken into account.

What is this background radiation?

**A** infra-red radiation from warm objects in the laboratory  
**B** infra-red radiation from the Sun  
**C** ionising radiation from the radioactive rock brought into the laboratory  
**D** ionising radiation in the laboratory when the radioactive rock is not present

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Many candidates lacked an understanding of background radiation and some thought it was due to the rock itself while other candidates thought that it is infra-red radiation from the Sun.
