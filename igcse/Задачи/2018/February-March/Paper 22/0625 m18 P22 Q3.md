> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить мгновенное ускорение автомобиля по наклону графика скорости.

The curved line on the graph shows the motion of a car.

![[0625-m18-22-p2-f1.svg]]

What is the acceleration of the car at the time of 4.0 s?

**A** 0.33 m / s$^{2}$  
**B** 0.44 m / s$^{2}$  
**C** 2.3 m / s$^{2}$  
**D** 3.0 m / s$^{2}$

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Although most candidates were able to answer this question correctly, there was a sizeable minority who divided the change in speed by the total time elapsed (4.0 s).
