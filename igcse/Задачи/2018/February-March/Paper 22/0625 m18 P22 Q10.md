# Question 10 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · February/March 2018 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 2 · вопрос 10  
Источник: [[_pdf/2018/0625_m18_qp_22.pdf#page=5|условие, стр. 5]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_m18_ms_22.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_m18_er.pdf#page=4|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 m18 P22]]  
Темы: [[1.6 Momentum]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Найти скорость первого шара после столкновения с другим шаром по сохранению импульса.

A ball of mass 0.16 kg is moving forwards at a speed of 0.50 m / s. A second ball of mass 0.10 kg

is stationary. The first ball strikes the second ball. The second ball moves forwards at a speed of 0.50 m / s.

What is the speed of the first ball after the collision?

**A** 0.0 m / s  
**B** 0.19 m / s  
**C** 0.31 m / s  
**D** 0.50 m / s

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question proved challenging and many candidates thought that the incoming ball stopped after the collision. Even more candidates appeared to equate the momentum of the second ball and the momentum of the first ball.
