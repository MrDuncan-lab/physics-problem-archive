> Сравнить пары логических схем и найти неэквивалентную пару.

The diagrams show pairs of circuits containing logic gates.

In which diagram does the lower circuit of the pair **not** behave in the same way as the upper circuit?

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Only stronger candidates were able to answer this question correctly. The way forward here was to recognise that when the two inputs to a NAND-gate are joined, then it acts as a NOT-gate. The logic then becomes much more accessible.
