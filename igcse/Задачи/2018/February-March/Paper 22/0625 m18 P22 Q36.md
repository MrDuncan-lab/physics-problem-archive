# Question 36 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · February/March 2018 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 2 · вопрос 36  
Источник: [[_pdf/2018/0625_m18_qp_22.pdf#page=13|условие, стр. 13]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_m18_ms_22.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_m18_er.pdf#page=4|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

Темы: [[4.5.3 Magnetic effect of a current]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить изменение силы и направления магнитного поля катушки при увеличении и смене полярности напряжения.

The current in a coil produces a magnetic field around it, as shown.

![[0625-m18-22-p13-f2.svg]]

The magnitude of the potential difference across the coil is increased and its direction is reversed.

What happens to the magnetic field?

**A** The lines become closer together and the right-hand end becomes a south pole.  
**B** The lines become closer together and the right-hand end remains a north pole.  
**C** The lines become further apart and the right-hand end becomes a south pole.  
**D** The lines become further apart and the right-hand end remains a north pole.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Although the majority got the correct answer, a significant number of candidates did not understand that the increase in the field strength caused by increasing the current would increase the density of the field lines.
