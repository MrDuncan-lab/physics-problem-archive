> Определить направление изменения уровней жидкости манометра при утечке газа.

A water manometer is connected to a gas supply.

![[0625-m18-22-p6-f1.svg]]

There is a gas leak and the pressure of the gas supply falls.

What happens to the water level at P and what happens to the water level at Q?

|  | water level at P | water level at Q |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | falls | falls |
| **B** | falls | rises |
| **C** | rises | falls |
| **D** | rises | rises |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
