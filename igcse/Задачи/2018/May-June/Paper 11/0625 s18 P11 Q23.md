> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбрать процесс, при котором меняется длина волны.

In which situation is the wavelength of the wave changed?

**A** light from the Sun passing from air into water  
**B** radio waves travelling from an Earth satellite to the Moon  
**C** sound reflecting from a wall  
**D** water waves passing through a narrow gap

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Stronger candidates understood that refraction, which is caused by the change of speed of radiation as it moves from one medium to another, leads to a change in wavelength of the radiation.
