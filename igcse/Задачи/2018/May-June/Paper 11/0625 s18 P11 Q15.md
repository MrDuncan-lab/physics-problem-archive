> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объяснить охлаждение молока влажной тканью за счёт испарения воды.

On a warm day, a carton of fresh milk is covered with a wet cloth.

Why does this help to reduce the temperature of the milk?

**A** Some water evaporates from the cloth so the remaining water becomes cooler.  
**B** The water has a very high thermal capacity.  
**C** The water insulates the milk from the warm air around it.  
**D** Water is always colder than the air around it.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Very few candidates were aware that evaporation causes cooling. There are some dramatic experiments which can be done which emphasise this, for example the freezing of water by the forced evaporation of ethanol.
