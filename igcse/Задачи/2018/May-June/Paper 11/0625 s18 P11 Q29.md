# Question 29 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2018 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 1 · вопрос 29  
Источник: [[_pdf/2018/0625_s18_qp_11.pdf#page=11|условие, стр. 11]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_s18_ms_11.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_s18_er.pdf#page=1|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

Темы: [[4.3.2 Series and parallel circuits]], [[4.2.2 Electric current]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить положения амперметра, в которых измеряется ток заданного резистора.

The diagram shows a cell connected to three resistors R$_{1}$, R$_{2}$ and R$_{3}$.

![[0625-s18-11-p11-f1.svg]]

A student connects an ammeter first in position 1, then in position 2, 3 and 4 in turn.

In which positions does the ammeter show the current in R$_{1}$?

**A** 1, 2 and 4  
**B** 1 and 2 only  
**C** 3 only  
**D** 4 only

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Most candidates thought that the ammeter read the current in the resistor R$_{1}$ when it was connected at points 1 and 2 only (option **B**) failing to understand that it also gives it at point 4, where the current has recombined after passing through the two arms of the parallel circuit.
