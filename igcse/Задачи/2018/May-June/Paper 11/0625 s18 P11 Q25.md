Источник: [[_pdf/2018/0625_s18_qp_11.pdf#page=10|условие, стр. 10]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_s18_ms_11.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_s18_er.pdf#page=1|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сопоставить устройства связи и зрения с областями электромагнитного спектра и длинами волн.

The diagram shows the electromagnetic spectrum. The numbers indicate the approximate

wavelength at the boundaries between the various regions of the spectrum.

For a device to be able to make use of electromagnetic radiation, it needs an aerial of approximately the same size as the radiation it is designed to work with.

![[0625-s18-11-p10-f1.svg]]

Which statement is correct?

**A** A mobile phone uses radiation from region P.  
**B** A television satellite dish uses radiation from region Q.  
**C** The receptor cells in an eye use radiation from region R.  
**D** The remote controller for a television uses radiation from region U.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
