# Question 5 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2018 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 1 · вопрос 5  
Источник: [[_pdf/2018/0625_s18_qp_11.pdf#page=3|условие, стр. 3]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_s18_ms_11.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_s18_er.pdf#page=1|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 s18 P11]]  
Темы: [[1.3 Mass and weight]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить вес воды по разности показаний весов для пустого и наполненного сосуда.

An empty beaker is placed on a top-pan balance. Some water is now poured into the beaker.

![[0625-s18-11-p3-f2.png]]

empty beaker beaker + water

What is the weight of the water?

**A** 0.044 kg  
**B** 0.168 kg  
**C** 0.0044 N  
**D** 0.44 N

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The most common answer given was option **A**, 0.044 kg, showing that the majority of candidates failed to recognise that the question asked for the weight of water, not the mass. This emphasised that careful reading of the question is required.
