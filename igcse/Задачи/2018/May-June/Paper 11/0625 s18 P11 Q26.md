# Question 26 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2018 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 1 · вопрос 26  
Источник: [[_pdf/2018/0625_s18_qp_11.pdf#page=10|условие, стр. 10]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_s18_ms_11.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_s18_er.pdf#page=1|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 s18 P11]]  
Темы: [[3.4 Sound]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить пересечение диапазонов слышимых частот человека и дельфина.

A dolphin has a range of audible frequencies of 150 Hz–150 kHz.

Which range of frequencies can be heard both by humans with good hearing and by dolphins?

**A** 20 Hz–150 Hz  
**B** 20 Hz–150 kHz  
**C** 20 kHz–150 kHz  
**D** 150 Hz–20 kHz

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Candidates were expected to know that the range of audible frequencies for humans is 20 Hz to 20 kHz. It seemed that difficulties arose in this question in combining this knowledge with the information given regarding the audible frequencies of dolphins.
