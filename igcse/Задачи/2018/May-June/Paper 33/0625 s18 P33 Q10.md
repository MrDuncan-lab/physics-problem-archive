**(ii)** Describe how the circuit shown in Fig. 10.1 could be used to find the resistance of the fixed resistor R. **[3]**

**(b)** An electrical heater has a resistance of 21.8 Ω when connected to a 240 V mains supply.

**(i)** Calculate the current in the heater.

current =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ A **[3]**

**(ii)** Suggest a rating for the fuse to be fitted to the mains plug for the heater.

fuse rating =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ A **[1]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | <u>variable resistor</u> | 1 |
> |  | change the current | 1 |
> | (a)(ii) | Any **three** from:<br>use low value of current<br>measure (and record) current<br>measure (and record) voltage<br>repeat other values (of I and V)<br>plot graph of pd against current (and find gradient) OR use V = I × R | 3 |
> | (b)(i) | V = I × R in any form **OR** (I =) V ÷ R | 1 |
> |  | 240 ÷ 21.8 | 1 |
> |  | 11.0 (A) | 1 |
> | (b)(ii) | Answer in range 12 to 15 (A) | 1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** Very few candidates knew this was a variable resistor. Many thought it was a thermistor and that the circuit was used for temperature control.
>
> **(ii)** Answers seen to this question were often very brief and a number of candidates did not give an answer at all. Very few scored more than partial credit, usually for use of V = IR.
>
> **(b) (i)** This part was answered well with most candidates scoring full credit.
>
> **(ii)** A common error was rounding down the answer from **(i)**, giving 11 A for the value of fuse to be used.
