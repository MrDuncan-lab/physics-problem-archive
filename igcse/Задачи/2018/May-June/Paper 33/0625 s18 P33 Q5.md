# Question 5 (6 marks)

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Вычислить силу давления газа на крышку и объяснить рост давления при нагревании движением молекул.

**(a)** Fig. 5.1 shows a metal can containing air. The can is sealed with a lid.

![[0625-s18-33-p7-f1.png]]

**Fig. 5.1**

The air in the can exerts a pressure of 20 000 N / m$^{2}$ on the lid. The area of the can lid is 0.09 m$^{2}$.

Calculate the force on the lid due to the air in the can.

force =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ N **[3]**

**(b)** The air in the can becomes warmer.

State and explain what happens to the pressure of the air in the can. Use your ideas about gas molecules. **[3]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | P = F ÷ A **OR** (F =) P × A in any form | 1 |
> |  | 20 000 × 0.009 | 1 |
> |  | 1800 (N) | 1 |
> | (b) | pressure increases | 1 |
> |  | any **two** from:<br>molecules move faster/have more ke<br>collide harder/more often (with walls of can)<br>(change in momentum due to) collisions impart(s) force on can walls | 2 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** A large number of candidates answered this question well. Weaker candidates often used an incorrect equation, i.e. force = pressure ÷ area or gave no response.
>
> **(b)** The majority of candidates gained partial credit by recognising that the pressure would increase. Several also went on to attribute this to faster moving molecules and harder or more frequent collisions with the can walls.
