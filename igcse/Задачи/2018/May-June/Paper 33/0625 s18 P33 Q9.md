# Question 9 (5 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2018 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 3 · вопрос 9  
Источник: [[_pdf/2018/0625_s18_qp_33.pdf#page=12|условие, стр. 12]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_s18_ms_33.pdf#page=8|схема оценивания, стр. 8]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_s18_er.pdf#page=23|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

Темы: [[4.1 Simple phenomena of magnetism]], [[X.3 Other topics not in the current syllabus]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Описать намагничивание стального стержня постоянным магнитом, проверить полученный магнит и сравнить сталь с мягким железом.

A student magnetises a steel rod by using a permanent magnet.

**(a)** Describe a method that the student could use. You may draw a diagram to help you. **[2]**

**(b)** Explain how the student could test that the steel rod has been magnetised. **[2]**

**(c)** Magnets can be made from soft iron or from steel.

State **one** difference between the magnetic properties of soft iron and steel. **[1]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | Any **two** from:<br>pole of magnet placed on/near steel rod<br>magnet stroked along rod owtte<br>repeat strokes<br>same direction | 2 |
> | (b) | place pole of rod next to a known magnet | 1 |
> |  | like poles repel | 1 |
> | (c) | accept iron loses magnetism easily owtte or reverse argument | 1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Most candidates had sufficient understanding of magnetism to know that the magnet needed to be stroked along the rod. This mark was often awarded on the diagram drawn by the candidate. A considerable number of candidates described and drew electromagnets which did not answer the question.
>
> **(b)** Many candidates knew to place the rod near a known magnet to see if the rod attracted or repelled without explicitly stating that repulsion was required.
>
> **(c)** Most candidates who gave an answer to this question were correct, but there were a significant number of candidates who did not give a response.
