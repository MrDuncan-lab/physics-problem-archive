# Question 2 (7 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2018 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 2  
Источник: [[_pdf/2018/0625_s18_qp_32.pdf#page=3|условие, стр. 3]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_s18_ms_32.pdf#page=5|схема оценивания, стр. 5]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_s18_er.pdf#page=19|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Вычислить вес деревянного плота и плотность бревна, объяснить плавание дерева в воде.

Fig. 2.1 shows a wooden raft. The raft is made from 8 logs. The logs are all of the same type of wood.

![[0625-s18-32-p3-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 2.1**

**(a)** The average mass of each log is 65.0 kg.

Calculate the total weight of the raft.

total weight of the raft =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ N **[3]**

**(b)** **(i)** The mass of one of the logs is 66.0 kg. It is 3.0 m long and has a cross sectional area of 0.040 m$^{2}$.

Calculate the density of the wood in the log.

density =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ kg / m$^{3}$ **[3]**

**(ii)** Explain why the log in **(b)(i)** floats on water. **[1]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | (W =) m × g | 1 |
> |  | 650 × 8 | 1 |
> |  | 5200 (N) | 1 |
> | (b)(i) | (volume of log =) 3 × 0.04 = 0.12 (m$^{3}$) | 1 |
> |  | D = M / V OR (D =) M / V | 1 |
> |  | 550 (kg / m$^{3}$) | 1 |
> | (b)(ii) | The density of logs is less than density of water owtte. | 1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Most candidates correctly calculated the total weight. Weaker candidates neglected to convert mass to weight or overlooked the fact that there were 8 logs.
>
> **(b) (i)** The majority of candidates correctly used length × area to calculate volume. Weaker candidates used length or area for volume. Stronger candidates then went on to calculate the correct value for density of 550 (kg / m$^{3}$)
>
> **(ii)** Most candidates correctly identified the log as having lower density than water, but many stated it had a higher density or commented on surface area.
