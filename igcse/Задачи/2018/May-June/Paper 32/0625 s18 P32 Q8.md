**(b)** Humans, elephants, mice and dolphins have different hearing ranges. Fig. 8.1 shows the hearing range for each type of animal.

![[0625-s18-32-p10-f4.svg]]

**Fig. 8.1**

**(i)** State the lowest frequency of sound that can be heard by mice.

\_\_\_\_\_\_ Hz **[1]**

**(ii)** State the highest frequency of sound that can be heard by elephants.

\_\_\_\_\_\_ Hz **[1]**

**(iii)** Explain how the chart shows that elephants can hear some sounds that humans **cannot** hear. **[2]**

**(iv)** State the term given to the high frequencies that dolphins can hear but humans **cannot** hear. **[1]**
