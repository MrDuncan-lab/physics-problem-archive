# Question 10 (4 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2018 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 10  
Источник: [[_pdf/2018/0625_s18_qp_32.pdf#page=13|условие, стр. 13–14]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_s18_ms_32.pdf#page=9|схема оценивания, стр. 9]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_s18_er.pdf#page=19|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объяснить положительный заряд шарика, определить заряд взаимодействующего стержня и выбрать электрические проводники.

**(a)** Fig. 10.1 shows a balloon hanging from an insulating thread.

![[0625-s18-32-p13-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 10.1**

**(i)** A student gives the balloon a positive charge.

Which statement explains why the balloon becomes positively charged? Tick **one** box.

![[0625-s18-32-p13-f2.svg]]

The balloon loses protons **[1]**

**(ii)** The student brings a charged rod close to the balloon as shown in Fig. 10.2.

![[0625-s18-32-p14-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 10.2**

State the type of charge on the rod.

Explain your answer. **[2]**

**(b)** Electrical charges can move easily through some materials.

Draw a circle around each material that charges can move through easily.

copper plastic rubber silver wood **[1]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | second box (The balloon loses electrons) ticked | 1 |
> | (a)(ii) | positive (charge) | 1 |
> |  | like charge(s) repel | 1 |
> | (b) | circle around copper **AND** silver | 1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** The stronger candidates identified that the balloon loses electrons.
>
> **(ii)** Most candidates identified the charge on the rod as positive. Fewer explained that like charges repel.
>
> **(b)** A few candidates identified both copper and silver as materials which allow charges to move easily.
