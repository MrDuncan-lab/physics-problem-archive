# Question 20 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2018 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 3 · вопрос 20  
Источник: [[_pdf/2018/0625_s18_qp_13.pdf#page=9|условие, стр. 9]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_s18_ms_13.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_s18_er.pdf#page=6|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 s18 P13]]  
Темы: [[3.2.2 Refraction of light]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить углы падения и преломления при критическом угле на границе стекла и воздуха.

The diagram shows a ray of light passing from glass to air, at the critical angle.

![[0625-s18-13-p9-f1.svg]]

Which angles are the angle of incidence and the angle of refraction?

|  | angle of incidence | angle of refraction |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | W | Y |
| **B** | W | Z |
| **C** | X | Y |
| **D** | X | Z |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Only the strongest candidates answered this question correctly. Most candidates were not confident identifying angles of incidence. There was evidence of guessing and the most popular choice was option **C** (angles X and Y) rather than the key, **D** (angles X and Z).
