> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбрать способ сушки белья с наименьшим воздействием на окружающую среду.

Which method of drying clothes has the least impact on the environment?

**A** Evaporate the water in them in an electrically heated tumble dryer.  
**B** Hang them on a washing line in direct sunlight.  
**C** Remove the water from them in an electric spin dryer.  
**D** Suspend them close to a coal fire.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Most candidates answered this question correctly and showed a clear understanding of the idea of the effects on the environment of using powered dryers.
