# Question 22 (1 mark)

Темы: [[3.3 Electromagnetic spectrum]], [[1.2 Motion]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать расстояние до спутника по времени прохождения микроволнового сигнала туда и обратно.

It takes 0.24 seconds for a microwave signal to travel from a transmitting station on Earth to a

satellite and then back to the station on Earth. Microwaves travel at 3.0 × 10$^{8}$ m / s.

How far is the transmitting station from the satellite?

**A** 36 000 km  
**B** 72 000 km  
**C** 36 000 000 km  
**D** 72 000 000 km

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question proved challenging for many candidates. Even where candidates were aware of the physics of the situation there were two issues which candidates needed to recognise: the signal travels to the satellite and back, and the answers were given in kilometres rather than metres.
