> Сравнить периоды полураспада источников по уменьшению активности за одинаковые интервалы времени.

The rates of emission from four radioactive sources are measured at 20 minute intervals.

Each row in the table shows the results for one of the radioactive sources.

Which source has the longest half-life?

|  | rate of emission / emissions per minute |  |  |
|---|---|---|---|
|  | time 0 | time 20 min | time 40 min |
| **A** | 120 | 60 | 30 |
| **B** | 120 | 110 | 101 |
| **C** | 240 | 60 | 15 |
| **D** | 240 | 170 | 122 |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
