> Выбрать естественные источники фонового ионизирующего излучения.

Three sources of background radiation are listed.

1 cosmic rays

2 medical X-rays

3 radioactive emissions from radon gas from the ground

Which of these sources are naturally occurring?

**A** 1 and 3 only  
**B** 1 only  
**C** 2 and 3 only  
**D** 2 only

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**
