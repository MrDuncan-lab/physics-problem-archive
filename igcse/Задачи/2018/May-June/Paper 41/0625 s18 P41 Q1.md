# Question 1 (8 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2018 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 1 · вопрос 1  
Источник: [[_pdf/2018/0625_s18_qp_41.pdf#page=2|условие, стр. 2]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_s18_ms_41.pdf#page=4|схема оценивания, стр. 4]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_s18_er.pdf#page=26|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 s18 P41]]  
Темы: [[1.2 Motion]], [[1.5.1 Effects of forces]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Вычислить ускорение и путь по графику скорости, объяснить изменение ускорения действующими силами.

Fig. 1.1 shows the speed-time graph for a vehicle accelerating from rest.

![[0625-s18-41-p2-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 1.1**

**(a)** Calculate the acceleration of the vehicle at time = 30 s.

acceleration =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(b)** Without further calculation, state how the acceleration at time = 100 s compares to the acceleration at time = 10 s. Suggest, in terms of force, a reason why any change has taken place. **[3]**

**(c)** Determine the distance travelled by the vehicle between time = 120 s and time = 160 s.

distance =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | Mention of gradient of graph at t = 30 s **OR** tangent drawn at t = 30 s and triangle drawn | 1 |
> |  | Acceleration in range 0.30 to 0.45 m / s$^{2}$ | 1 |
> | (b) | Acceleration less/at a slower rate | 1 |
> |  | Less driving force **OR** greater resistive force/friction/air resistance/drag | 1 |
> |  | Resultant force less | 1 |
> | (c) | Area under graph | 1 |
> |  | Distance = (20 × 40) + (½ × 40 × 10) **OR** ½ × (30 + 20) × 40 | 1 |
> |  | 1000 m | 1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** An application of the syllabus item ‘Calculate acceleration from the gradient of a speed-time graph’ was required. Very few candidates recognised that this involved drawing a tangent to the graph at time = 30 s and calculating its gradient. Most candidates merely divided the speed at 30 s by 30 and failed to gain any credit.
>
> **(b)** Many candidates could not interpret the lower gradient of the graph at time 100 s as showing that the acceleration had become less than the acceleration at time 10 s. Suggestions that this was caused by less driving force or greater resistive force, and thus a lower resultant force, were often not clearly stated.
>
> **(c)** A clear statement or implication that the area under a graph is distance travelled gained credit. Many candidates went on to correctly calculate the area of the trapezium or the sum of the areas of a rectangle and a triangle.
