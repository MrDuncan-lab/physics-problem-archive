# Question 11 (7 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2018 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 1 · вопрос 11  
Источник: [[_pdf/2018/0625_s18_qp_41.pdf#page=15|условие, стр. 15]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_s18_ms_41.pdf#page=9|схема оценивания, стр. 9]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_s18_er.pdf#page=26|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 s18 P41]]  
Темы: [[5.1.2 The nucleus]], [[5.2.3 Radioactive decay]], [[5.2.4 Half-life]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить состав атома радона, записать альфа-распад и вычислить число испущенных частиц за два периода полураспада.

Radon-222 is radioactive. It can be represented as ${}^{222}_{86}\text{Rn}$.

**(a)** For a neutral atom of radon-222, state

**1.** the number of protons,  \_\_\_\_\_\_

**2.** the number of neutrons,  \_\_\_\_\_\_

**3.** the number of electrons. \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(b)** A radon-222 nucleus decays by α-particle emission to a polonium (Po) nucleus.

Complete the equation for the decay of radon-222.

${}^{222}_{86}\text{Rn} \rightarrow$ **[2]**

**(c)** Radon-222 has a half-life of 3.8 days.

At a certain time, a sample contains 6.4 × 10$^{6}$ radon nuclei.

Calculate the number of α-particles emitted by the radon nuclei in the following 7.6 days.

number =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | Number of protons = 86 and number of electrons = 86 | 1 |
> |  | Number of neutrons = 136 | 1 |
> | (b) | ${}^{218}_{84}\text{Po}$ | 1 |
> |  | $+\ {}^{4}_{2}\alpha$ | 1 |
> | (c) | 7.6 days = 2 half-lives or evidence of two halvings | 1 |
> |  | (number of Rn atoms left = 6.4 × 10$^{6}$ ÷ 4 =) 1.6 × 10$^{6}$ | 1 |
> |  | number of α-particles emitted = (6.4 × 10$^{6}$ – 1.6 × 10$^{6}$ =) 4.8 × 10$^{6}$ | 1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** A large majority of candidates stated the correct particle numbers.
>
> **(b)** Many candidates gained full credit, but some wrote down the product nucleus as Rn instead of Po, and less frequently, a wrong alpha symbol.
>
> **(c)** Most candidates correctly recognised that 7.6 days equalled 2 half-lives, requiring 2 successive halving of the initial number of Rn nuclei. The more challenging calculation of the number of alpha particles emitted was achieved by quite a small number of candidates.
