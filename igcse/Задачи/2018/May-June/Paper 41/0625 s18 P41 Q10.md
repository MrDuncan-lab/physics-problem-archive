# Question 10 (7 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2018 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 1 · вопрос 10  
Источник: [[_pdf/2018/0625_s18_qp_41.pdf#page=14|условие, стр. 14]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_s18_ms_41.pdf#page=9|схема оценивания, стр. 9]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_s18_er.pdf#page=26|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

Темы: [[4.5.6 The transformer]], [[4.3.2 Series and parallel circuits]], [[4.4 Electrical safety]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объяснить необходимость переменного напряжения трансформатора, вычислить число витков, первичный ток и допустимое число ламп.

**(a)** Explain why the voltage of the supply to the primary coil of a transformer must be alternating. **[2]**

**(b)** Fig. 10.1 shows a transformer.

![[0625-s18-41-p14-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 10.1**

There are 8000 turns in the primary coil of the transformer. The primary coil is connected to a 240 V mains supply. A 6.0 V lamp connected to the secondary coil operates at full brightness.

**(i)** Calculate the number of turns in the secondary coil,

number of turns =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(ii)** The current in the lamp is 2.0 A. The transformer operates with 100% efficiency.

Calculate the current in the primary circuit.

current =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(iii)** The primary circuit contains a 2 A fuse.

Calculate the maximum number of lamps, identical to the lamp in **(ii)**, that can be connected in parallel in the secondary circuit without blowing the fuse.

number of lamps =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | To produce an alternating/changing magnetic field | 1 |
> |  | <u>so that current/voltage is induced (continuously) in the secondary coil</u> **OR** <u>secondary circuit</u> | 1 |
> | (b)(i) | N$_{\text{s}}$ ÷ N$_{\text{p}}$ = V$_{\text{s}}$ ÷ V$_{\text{p}}$ in any form **OR** (N$_{\text{s}}$ =) N$_{\text{p}}$ × V$_{\text{s}}$ ÷ V$_{\text{p}}$ **OR** 8000 × 6 ÷ 240 | 1 |
> |  | 200 | 1 |
> | (b)(ii) | *I*$_{\text{p}}$V$_{\text{p}}$ = *I*$_{\text{s}}$V$_{\text{s}}$ in any form **OR** (*I*$_{\text{p}}$ =) *I*$_{\text{s}}$× V$_{\text{s}}$ ÷ V$_{\text{p}}$ **OR** 2.0 × 6 ÷ 240 | 1 |
> |  | 0.050 A | 1 |
> | (b)(iii) | (Number of lamps =) 2 ÷ 0.05 = 40 | 1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** This question proved challenging for many candidates. The strongest candidates suggested that an alternating current produces a changing magnetic field for credit. References to voltage or current being induced in the secondary coil were more common.
>
> **(b) (i)** A large majority of candidates wrote down the correct formula and used it correctly.
>
> **(ii)** Rather fewer candidates than in **(i)** were familiar with the formula relating to equal power in the coils of a transformer operating with notional 100 per cent efficiency. Even with the correct formula quoted, wrong manipulation of the numbers sometimes produced the wrong current.
>
> **(iii)** With the correct current, or the wrong current in **(ii)** carried forward, many correct maximum numbers of lamps could be credited. However, particularly for those who had calculated the wrong current, many candidates divided that current by the fuse rating and gave an incorrect answer.
