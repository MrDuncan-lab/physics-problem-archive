# Question 27 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2018 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 1 · вопрос 27  
Источник: [[_pdf/2018/0625_s18_qp_21.pdf#page=10|условие, стр. 10]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_s18_ms_21.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_s18_er.pdf#page=8|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

Темы: [[4.2.3 Electromotive force and potential difference]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объяснить ЭДС батареи как энергию, передаваемую единичному заряду.

The electromotive force (e.m.f.) of a rechargeable battery is 6.0 V.

What does this mean?

**A** 6.0 J is the maximum energy the battery can provide in 1.0 s.  
**B** 6.0 J is the total energy the battery can provide before it has to be recharged.  
**C** 6.0 J of energy is provided by the battery to drive a charge of 1.0 C around a complete circuit.  
**D** 6.0 J of energy is provided by the battery to drive a current of 1.0 A around a complete circuit.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Although nearly half the candidates were able to identify the correct response, there were many who showed little understanding of the electromotive force of a rechargeable battery and were under the impression that it referred directly to the energy stored in the battery.
