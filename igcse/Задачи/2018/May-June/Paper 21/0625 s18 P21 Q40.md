> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить период полураспада по графику счётной скорости с учётом радиационного фона.

The graph shows how the count rate registered by a counter near to a sample of a radioactive

isotope changes over a period of a few days. The background count rate is 5 counts per minute.

![[0625-s18-21-p15-f2.svg]]

What is the half-life of the isotope?

**A** 2.0 days  
**B** 2.5 days  
**C** 3.0 days  
**D** 4.0 days

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Only the strongest candidates answered this question correctly. A significant minority of candidates showed a lack of understanding of half-life. Amongst those who did show an understanding, over half failed to reduce the raw count (shown on the graph) to take account of background radiation.
