> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать угол призмы по скорости света в материале и условию критического преломления.

A prism is made from transparent plastic. In this plastic, light travels at 0.80 *c*, where *c* is its speed in air. Light enters one face of the prism at right-angles as shown.

![[0625-s18-21-p9-f1.svg]]

NOT TO SCALE

The light just escapes from the sloping face of the prism.

What is angle θ?

**A** 37°  
**B** 39°  
**C** 51°  
**D** 53°

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question was challenging for many candidates. There was some evidence of guessing. Candidates needed to be aware that the diagram showed light incident on the glass-air boundary at the critical angle and then had to use the formula *n =* 1 / sin *c.*
