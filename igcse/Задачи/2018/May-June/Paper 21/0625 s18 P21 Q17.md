# Question 17 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2018 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 1 · вопрос 17  
Источник: [[_pdf/2018/0625_s18_qp_21.pdf#page=7|условие, стр. 7]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_s18_ms_21.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_s18_er.pdf#page=8|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнить области жидкости, в которых происходят кипение и испарение.

Which statements about boiling and about evaporation are both correct?

|  | boiling | evaporation |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | takes place only at the surface | takes place only at the surface |
| **B** | takes place only at the surface | takes place throughout the liquid |
| **C** | takes place throughout the liquid | takes place only at the surface |
| **D** | takes place throughout the liquid | takes place throughout the liquid |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Candidates had a clear understanding of the differences between boiling and evaporation.
