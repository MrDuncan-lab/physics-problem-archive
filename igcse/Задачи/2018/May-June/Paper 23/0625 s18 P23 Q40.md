> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать период полураспада по графику счётной скорости с вычитанием фонового излучения.

The graph shows how the count rate registered by a counter near to a sample of a radioactive

isotope changes over a period of a few days. The background count rate is 5 counts per minute.

![[0625-s18-23-p16-f1.svg]]

What is the half-life of the isotope?

**A** 2.0 days  
**B** 2.5 days  
**C** 3.0 days  
**D** 4.0 days

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question proved challenging and although most candidates showed an understanding of half-life, the majority failed to reduce the raw count (shown on the graph) to take account of background radiation.
