# Question 12 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2018 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 3 · вопрос 12

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить скорость увеличения потенциальной энергии по графику вертикального перемещения тела.

The vertical displacement of a mass of 0.20 kg changes with time. The graph shows how this

displacement changes.

![[0625-s18-23-p5-f1.svg]]

At which rate does it gain gravitational potential energy as it moves upwards?

**A** 0.025 W  
**B** 0.050 W  
**C** 0.20 W  
**D** 0.40 W

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The question was challenging for many candidates as it had stages. Stage 1 was to accurately calculate the gradient of a relevant section of the graph. Stage 2 was to convert the units of this to m / s. Stage 3 was to insert this into the potential energy formula. Stronger candidates were able to work their way through the problem to gain the correct answer.
