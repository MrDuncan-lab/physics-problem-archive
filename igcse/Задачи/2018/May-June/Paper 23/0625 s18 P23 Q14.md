# Question 14 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2018 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 3 · вопрос 14  
Источник: [[_pdf/2018/0625_s18_qp_23.pdf#page=5|условие, стр. 5]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_s18_ms_23.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_s18_er.pdf#page=13|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 s18 P23]]  
Темы: [[1.8 Pressure]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать силу гидростатического давления масла на дно резервуара.

An oil tank has a base of area 2.5 m$^{2}$ and is filled with oil to a depth of 1.2 m.

The density of the oil is 800 kg / m$^{3}$.

What is the force exerted on the base of the tank due to the oil?

**A** 960 N  
**B** 2400 N  
**C** 9600 N  
**D** 24 000 N

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> In answering this question many candidates found the volume of the tank and so the total mass of oil in the tank, with some going on to find the weight of oil but then incorrectly assuming this was the pressure on the base.
