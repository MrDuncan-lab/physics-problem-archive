> Сравнить диапазон и чувствительность ртутных термометров с разными диаметрами капилляров.

Two liquid-in-glass thermometers P and Q contain the same volume of mercury and have

capillary tubes of the same length.

Thermometer P has a capillary tube with a smaller diameter than thermometer Q.

Which thermometer has the greater range and which has the greater sensitivity?

|  | greater range | greater sensitivity |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | P | P |
| **B** | P | Q |
| **C** | Q | P |
| **D** | Q | Q |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
