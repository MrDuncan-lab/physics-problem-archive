> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить собственный вес линейки из равновесия моментов двух подвешенных грузов.

The diagram shows a uniform metre rule pivoted at the 30 cm mark.

![[0625-s18-23-p3-f1.svg]]

The rule balances when a weight of 6.0 N is hanging from the zero mark and a weight of 2.0 N is hanging from the 70 cm mark.

What is the weight of the rule?

**A** 2.0 N  
**B** 5.0 N  
**C** 6.0 N  
**D** 13.0 N

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Only the strongest candidates answered this question correctly. Many candidates did not realise that the centre of mass of a uniform beam is at its centre and that the weight may be considered to act at this point.
