> Определить изотопы одного элемента по числам протонов и нейтронов.

The table shows the composition of three different nuclei.

| nucleus | number of protons | number of neutrons |
|---|---|---|
| X | 3 | 3 |
| Y | 3 | 4 |
| Z | 4 | 3 |

Which nuclei are isotopes of the same element?

**A** X, Y and Z  
**B** X and Y only  
**C** X and Z only  
**D** Y and Z only

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**
