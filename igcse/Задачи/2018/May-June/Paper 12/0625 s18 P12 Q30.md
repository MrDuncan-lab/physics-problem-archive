# Question 30 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2018 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 30  
Источник: [[_pdf/2018/0625_s18_qp_12.pdf#page=12|условие, стр. 12]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_s18_ms_12.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_s18_er.pdf#page=4|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 s18 P12]]  
Темы: [[4.2.5 Electrical energy and electrical power]], [[1.7.4 Power]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить энергию нагревателя за заданное время по графику передачи энергии с постоянной мощностью.

An electrical heater transfers thermal energy to the surroundings.

The graph shows how the amount of thermal energy transferred varies with time.

![[0625-s18-12-p12-f1.svg]]

The heater continues to transfer energy at the same rate.

How much thermal energy is transferred by the heater in 5.0 minutes?

**A** 200 J  
**B** 400 J  
**C** 2000 J  
**D** 12 000 J

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Obtaining information from graphs is an important skill which some candidates found challenging. In this case, many candidates did not recognise that the graph showed the total energy transferred by the heater, not the energy transferred per unit time. Care needed be taken in establishing what the variables were.
