> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить частоту и скорость водяной волны по последовательным изображениям её распространения.

The diagrams show a wave on the surface of the water in a tank at times 1.0 s apart. The wave is

produced at P and travels to the right.

![[0625-s18-12-p8-f1.svg]]

Which row gives the frequency and the speed of this water wave?

|  | frequency / Hz | speed cm / s |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | 2.0 | 4.0 |
| **B** | 2.0 | 8.0 |
| **C** | 4.0 | 4.0 |
| **D** | 4.0 | 8.0 |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Candidates appeared to make two errors. The first was that they failed to spot that the second diagram showed that two more waves had been produced in one second. The second error was to apply the formula for wave speed, incorrectly.
