# Question 8 (6 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2018 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 1 · вопрос 8  
Источник: [[_pdf/2018/0625_s18_qp_31.pdf#page=13|условие, стр. 13]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_s18_ms_31.pdf#page=7|схема оценивания, стр. 7]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_s18_er.pdf#page=15|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 s18 P31]]  
Темы: [[3.4 Sound]], [[1.1 Physical quantities and measurement techniques]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбрать прибор для измерения расстояния, объяснить эхо и определить скорость звука по повторным ударам барабана.

This question is about measuring the speed of sound in air.

A student stands in front of a large wall. She hits a drum and hears an echo. Fig. 8.1 shows the position of the student and the wall.

![[0625-s18-31-p13-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 8.1**

**(a)** **(i)** State the name of a piece of equipment for measuring the distance from the student to the wall. **[1]**

**(ii)** Explain how sound forms an echo. **[1]**

**(b)** The student hits her drum repeatedly once per second. She walks away from the wall and listens for the echo. When the student is 170 m from the wall she hears the echo from one beat of the drum at the same time as the next beat of the drum.

Use this information to determine the speed of sound. State the unit.

speed =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[4]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | tape measure | 1 |
> | (a)(ii) | reflection (of sound) | 1 |
> | (b) | time for sound to travel to wall and back = 1.0 s | 1 |
> |  | 340 m in 1.0 s | 1 |
> |  | (speed =) 340 | 1 |
> |  | m / s | 1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)(i)** Most candidates gave a sensible measuring instrument with “metre rule” the most common. Weaker candidates gave answers such as “meter” or “meter tape”. Trundle wheels were rarely mentioned and were more often described than named.
>
> **(ii)** Most candidates had the idea of echoes formed by reflection of sound but often expressed this in very vague terms such as “hits the wall and comes back”. A small number of weaker candidates thought that echoes occur in large open spaces.
>
> **(b)** The majority of candidates scored full credit with answers of 340 m / s. However many others gave answers of 170 m / s and 85 m / s often without any working shown.
