> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Восстановить последовательность работы геотермальной электростанции и сравнить её преимущества и недостатки с ветроэнергетикой.

Fig. 5.1 shows a geothermal power station. It generates electricity.

![[0625-s18-31-p8-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 5.1**

**(a)** In a geothermal power station, the process of generating electricity includes seven stages. Four of the stages are shown below.

P steam turns a turbine

Q hot underground rocks heat the cold water

R the turbine spins a generator

S hot water rises to the surface

The flow chart in Fig. 5.2 shows the seven stages, but it is incomplete. Complete the flow chart by adding the letters P, Q, R and S in the correct sequence.

![[0625-s18-31-p8-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 5.2** **[3]**

**(b)** The cost of electrical energy obtained from a geothermal power station is similar to the cost of electrical energy obtained from wind turbines.

Describe one advantage and one disadvantage of using a geothermal power station to generate electricity compared with using wind turbines.

advantage  \_\_\_\_\_\_

disadvantage  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | Q<br>S<br>P<br>R | 3 |
> | (b) | any **one** advantage from:<br>continuous supply/steady supply or reverse argument<br>any **one** disadvantage from:<br>only available in certain areas/thin crust/near geysers or can damage water table OR limited lifespan/rocks can cool | 2 |
