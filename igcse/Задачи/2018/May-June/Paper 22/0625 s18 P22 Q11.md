> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбрать формулу механической работы силы.

The work done *W* by a force is related to the magnitude *F* of the force and the distance *d* moved

in the direction of the force.

Which equation for *W* is correct?

**A** *W* = *d* ÷ *F*  
**B** *W* = *d* + *F*  
**C** *W* = *F* ÷ *d*  
**D** *W* = *F* × *d*

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
