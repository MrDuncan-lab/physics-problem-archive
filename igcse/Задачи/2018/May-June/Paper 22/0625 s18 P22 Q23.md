# Question 23 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · May/June 2018 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 2 · вопрос 23  
Источник: [[_pdf/2018/0625_s18_qp_22.pdf#page=8|условие, стр. 8]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_s18_ms_22.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_s18_er.pdf#page=11|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

Темы: [[3.3 Electromagnetic spectrum]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сравнить скорости разных видов электромагнитного излучения.

Which statement is **not** correct?

**A** The speed of long-wavelength infra-red radiation in a vacuum is greater than that of short-

wavelength ultraviolet light.

**B** The speed of microwaves in air is approximately 3.0 × 10$^{8}$ m / s.  
**C** The speed of γ-rays emitted from a sample of cobalt-60 is 3.0 × 10$^{5}$ km / s.  
**D** The X-rays emitted in a supernova explosion reach the Earth at the same time as the visible

light emitted.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question was challenging for many candidates. Some candidates did not appear to recognise that all electromagnetic radiation travels at the same speed in a vacuum.
