> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать равнодействующую силу по изменению скорости, массе и времени торможения.

An object decelerates from 25.0 m / s to 5.0 m / s in a time of 4.0 s.

It has a mass of 50 kg.

What is the resultant force on the object?

**A** 0.63 N  
**B** 10 N  
**C** 250 N  
**D** 4000 N

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question was answered extremely well and the vast majority of candidates were able to cope with a two- stage calculation, i.e. Stage 1: calculation of the acceleration of the object. Stage 2: use of the formula for force, mass and acceleration.
