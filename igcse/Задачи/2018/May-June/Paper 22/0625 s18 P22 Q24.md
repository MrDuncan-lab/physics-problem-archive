> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Связать увеличение громкости и высоты звука с амплитудой и частотой.

A siren is emitting a sound. As time passes, the sound becomes louder and higher pitched.

What is happening to the amplitude and to the frequency of the emitted sound wave?

|  | amplitude | frequency |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | decreasing | decreasing |
| **B** | decreasing | increasing |
| **C** | increasing | decreasing |
| **D** | increasing | increasing |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Candidates were able to apply the formula for wave speed and, generally, managed the use of powers of ten.
>
> The vast majority of candidates were able to link the loudness and pitch of sound to amplitude and frequency respectively.
