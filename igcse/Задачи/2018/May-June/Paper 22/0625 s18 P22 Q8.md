> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить состояние тела при нулевых равнодействующей силе и суммарном моменте.

A spacecraft is travelling in space with no resultant force and no resultant moment acting on it.

Which statement about the spacecraft is correct?

**A** Its direction is changing.  
**B** It is in equilibrium.  
**C** Its speed is decreasing.  
**D** Its speed is increasing.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Candidates were familiar with the concept that if there is no resultant force on an object and no resultant couple then the object is in equilibrium.
