> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Назвать факторы скорости испарения и объяснить охлаждение человека при испарении воды с кожи.

**(a)** State **three** factors that determine the rate of evaporation of water.

1 \_\_\_\_\_\_

2 \_\_\_\_\_\_

3 \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

**(b)** A person climbs out of a swimming pool and stands in the open air.

Explain why evaporation of water from the surface of the person’s body causes the person to feel cold. **[2]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | Any **three** from:<br>Temperature (of liquid / water)<br>Surface area (of liquid / water)<br>Draught / wind / movement of air (over surface)<br><u>Temperature of surroundings</u><br>Humidity (of surrounding air) | B3 |
> | (b) | **Any two** from:<br>More energetic / faster molecules escape<br>Less energetic / slower molecules remain OR remaining<br>water is colder<br>Thermal energy / heat flows from body / skin to colder water<br>(and person feels colder)<br>OR (for one mark each)<br>(Evaporation requires) latent heat of vaporisation<br>Thermal energy / heat flows from body / skin | B2 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Most candidates gave at least two factors that determine the rate of evaporate.
>
> **(b)** The most common approach required the ideas of higher energy or faster molecules evaporating, leaving behind the lower energy or slower molecules. References to hotter or colder molecules were not acceptable. There was sometimes confusion in answers, with sweating mentioned or with convection being involved.
