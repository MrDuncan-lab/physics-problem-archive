# Question 10 (8 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2018 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 1 · вопрос 10  
Источник: [[_pdf/2018/0625_w18_qp_41.pdf#page=13|условие, стр. 13]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_w18_ms_41.pdf#page=13|схема оценивания, стр. 13]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_w18_er.pdf#page=27|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w18 P41]]  
Темы: [[4.5.6 The transformer]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объяснить возникновение вторичного напряжения трансформатора, рассчитать первичное напряжение и объяснить снижение потерь высоковольтной передачи.

A transformer consists of two coils of wire wound on a metal core. Fig. 10.1 represents the transformer.

![[0625-w18-41-p13-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 10.1**

**(a)** State the name of the metal from which the core is made. **[1]**

**(b)** The primary coil of the transformer is connected to the output voltage of an a.c. generator which supplies an alternating current.

**(i)** Explain why there is a voltage between the two terminals of the secondary coil. **[3]**

**(ii)** There are 560 turns on the primary coil and 910 turns on the secondary coil of the transformer. The voltage between the two terminals of the secondary coil is 78 V.

Calculate the voltage supplied by the a.c. generator.

generator voltage =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(c)** Transformers are used to increase the voltage when electrical energy is transmitted in cables across long distances.

Explain why power losses in the cables are lower when the voltage is high. **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)(ii)** 48 V

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | (soft) iron | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | Alternating / changing magnetic field in primary (coil) | B1 |
> |  | Alternating / changing (magnetic) field in core (and in secondary coil) OR (magnetic) field lines / flux link secondary | B1 |
> |  | <u>e.m.f / voltage induced (in secondary coil)</u> | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | *V*$_{\text{P}}$ / *V*$_{\text{S}}$= *N*$_{\text{P}}$ / *N*$_{\text{S}}$in any form OR (*V*$_{\text{P}}$=) *V*$_{\text{S}}$ × *N*$_{\text{P}}$ / *N*$_{\text{S}}$<br>OR 78 × 560 / 910 | C1 |
> |  | 48 V | A1 |
> | (c) | Lower current | B1 |
> |  | (Power loss from cables =) *I*$^{2}$*R* so lower current means less power loss OR less heat loss | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Most candidates correctly suggested iron or soft iron for the material of the core.
>
> **(b) (i)** The magnetic field set up by the primary coil was sometimes not described as alternating or changing. Few candidates stated that this field was cut by or linked with the secondary coil. Candidates also needed to state that a voltage was induced in the secondary coil. The required word “induced” was sometimes absent.
>
> **(ii)** Most candidates calculated the generator voltage correctly. However, some candidates failed to state a formula and so used the numbers incorrectly.
>
> **(c)** Candidates who suggested that current would be lower gained partial credit. Following this, many went on to the conclusion that this leads to less heat production. Those who suggested changes in resistance were unsuccessful.
