# Question 3 (7 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2018 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 1 · вопрос 3

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Сформулировать сохранение энергии, описать превращения энергии броска и вычислить импульс мяча и среднюю силу.

**(a)** State what is meant by *the principle of conservation of energy*. **[1]**

**(b)** Fig. 3.1 shows a girl throwing a heavy ball.

![[0625-w18-41-p5-f1.png]]

**Fig. 3.1**

**(i)** State the energy changes that take place from when the girl begins to exert a force on the ball until the ball hits the ground and stops moving. **[2]**

**(ii)** The mass of the ball is 4.0 kg. The girl exerts a force on the ball for 0.60 s. The speed of the ball increases from 0 m / s to 12 m / s before it leaves the girl’s hand.

Calculate:

**1.** the momentum of the ball on leaving the girl’s hand

momentum =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**2.** the average resultant force exerted on the ball.

average resultant force =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)(ii)** 48 kg m/ s or N s
>
> **(b)(ii)** 80 N

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | Energy cannot be created or destroyed<br>OR energy can only be transferred from one form to another<br>OR total energy remains constant | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | Chemical (energy) to kinetic (energy) AND / OR potential (energy) | B1 |
> |  | Any **one** of:<br>Kinetic (energy) to potential (energy) OR gravitational (energy)<br>Potential (energy) OR gravitational (energy) to kinetic (energy)<br>Kinetic (energy) to thermal (energy) OR heat (energy) | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | (momentum =*) mv* OR 4.0 × 12 | C1 |
> |  | 48 kg m/ s or N s | A1 |
> |  | (average force =) momentum change / time OR *m(v – u) / t*<br>OR *(mv – mu) / t* OR *F = ma* AND *a = (v – u) / t* OR 48 / 0.60 | C1 |
> |  | 80 N | A1 |
