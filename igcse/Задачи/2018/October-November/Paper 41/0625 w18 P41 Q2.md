# Question 2 (6 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2018 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 1 · вопрос 2  
Источник: [[_pdf/2018/0625_w18_qp_41.pdf#page=4|условие, стр. 4]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_w18_ms_41.pdf#page=6|схема оценивания, стр. 6]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_w18_er.pdf#page=27|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Вычислить моменты сил однородной доски и натяжения двух удерживающих верёвок из условий равновесия.

Fig. 2.1 shows a uniform plank AB of length 2.0 m suspended from two ropes X and Y.

![[0625-w18-41-p4-t1.svg]]

*W* = 210 N

**Fig. 2.1**

The weight *W* of the plank is 210 N. The force in rope X is *P*. The force in rope Y is *Q*.

**(a)** State, in terms of *P*, the moment of force *P* about B. **[1]**

**(b)** Calculate:

**(i)** the moment of *W* about B

moment =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[1]**

**(ii)** the force *P*

force *P* =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(iii)** the force *Q*.

force *Q* =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)(ii)** 140 N
>
> **(b)(iii)** Q = 70 N

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | *P* × 1.5 | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | (*W* × 1.0 OR 210 × 1.0 =) 210 N m | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | *P* × 1.5 = 210 OR *P* = 210 / 1.5 | C1 |
> |  | 140 N | A1 |
> | (b)(iii) | *P* + *Q* = 210 OR 140 + *Q* = 210 OR Q × 1.5 = 210 × 0.5 OR Q = 210 × 0.5 / 1.5 OR P × 0.5 = Q | C1 |
> |  | Q = 70 N | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Only the strongest candidates answered this question correctly and stated the moment of *P* about B as *P* × 1.5 or 1.5*P.*
>
> **(b) (i)** Some candidates stated the moment of *W* about B as 210 Nm but some answers did not included the unit.
>
> **(ii)** Only the strongest candidates answered this question correctly.
>
> **(iii)** Two approaches were possible and the easier one required the idea that total upward force = total downward force. The other method, using moments, was less successful.
