# Question 5 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2018 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 5  
Источник: [[_pdf/2018/0625_w18_qp_12.pdf#page=3|условие, стр. 3]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_w18_ms_12.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_w18_er.pdf#page=3|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w18 P12]]  
Темы: [[1.3 Mass and weight]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Объяснить изменение показаний пружинных весов на другой планете при неизменной массе тела.

An object is suspended from a spring balance on the Earth. The same object is suspended from the same spring balance on another planet.

![[0625-w18-12-p3-f1.svg]]

Which statement explains the difference between the two readings?

**A** Both the mass and the weight of the object are greater on the other planet.  
**B** The mass of the object is greater on the other planet than on Earth, but the weight is

unchanged.

**C** The spring stretches more easily when on the other planet.  
**D** The weight of the object is greater on the other planet than on Earth, but the mass is

unchanged.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Candidates showed a good understanding of the concept that mass is independent of the gravitational field, but weight is dependent on it.
