> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить смещение центра масс треугольной пластины после удаления её верхней части.

Point X is the centre of mass of a lamina in the shape of a triangle with sides of equal length. The top of the triangle is cut off along the dotted line shown.

![[0625-w18-12-p4-f2.svg]]

What happens to the centre of mass, X?

**A** moves towards the bottom of the page  
**B** moves to the left  
**C** moves to the right  
**D** moves towards the top of the page

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question was answered well and candidates thought their way through the problem clearly and recognised that the centre of mass of the lamina moved towards the bottom of the page.
