> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбрать изменение манометра, увеличивающее разность уровней жидкости при заданном давлении газа.

A manometer is used to measure the pressure of a gas supply.

![[0625-w18-12-p6-f1.svg]]

Which change gives a greater value of height *h* ?

**A** using a less dense liquid  
**B** using a more dense liquid  
**C** using a narrower tube  
**D** using a wider tube

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The majority of candidates incorrectly thought that the width of the tube would alter the height of the liquid column and showed little understanding of this method of measuring pressure.
