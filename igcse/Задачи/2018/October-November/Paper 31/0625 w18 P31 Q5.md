# Question 5 (4 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2018 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 1 · вопрос 5  
Источник: [[_pdf/2018/0625_w18_qp_31.pdf#page=6|условие, стр. 6]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_w18_ms_31.pdf#page=8|схема оценивания, стр. 8]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_w18_er.pdf#page=17|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить запасённую энергию воды и описать получение электричества приливной электростанцией.

A tidal barrage (dam) produces electricity using tides. Fig. 5.1 shows a diagram of a tidal barrage (simplified).

tide coming in tide going out

![[0625-w18-31-p6-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 5.1**

**(a)** The water behind the barrage (dam) is a store of energy. State the name of this stored energy. **[1]**

**(b)** Explain how the tidal barrage (dam) produces electricity. **[3]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | (gravitational) potential energy | B1 |
> | (b) | **Any 3 from:**<br>water flows through tunnel / has kinetic energy<br>when tide coming in / going out<br>(moving) water causes turbines / (component) X to rotate / turn<br>(the turbine)turns a generator | B3 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Most candidates correctly stated that the stored energy was gravitational potential energy, but many others gave answers such as electric energy or hydroelectric energy.
>
> **(b)** Most candidates gained at least partial credit on this question. The most common error was to not mention that the turbine turned a generator which produced electrical energy. Most candidates seemed to think that the turbine generated electricity.
