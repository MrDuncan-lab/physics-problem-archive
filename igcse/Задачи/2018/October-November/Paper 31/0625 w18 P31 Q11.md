# Question 11 (7 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2018 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 1 · вопрос 11  
Источник: [[_pdf/2018/0625_w18_qp_31.pdf#page=14|условие, стр. 14]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_w18_ms_31.pdf#page=12|схема оценивания, стр. 12]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_w18_er.pdf#page=17|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w18 P31]]  
Темы: [[4.5.6 The transformer]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать выходное напряжение трансформатора, сравнить обмотки повышающего и понижающего типов и объяснить преимущества высоковольтной передачи.

**(a)** A student has a model electric railway. The model railway uses a step-down transformer.

The input voltage is 230 V. The transformer has 1710 turns on the input coil and 90 turns on the output coil.

Calculate the output voltage of the transformer.

output voltage =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ V **[3]**

**(b)** A step-up transformer is used to increase voltage.

Step-up transformers and step-down transformers have different coil arrangements.

Describe the differences in the coil arrangement for the two types of transformer. **[2]**

**(c)** Explain the advantage of transmitting electricity at high voltages, rather than at low voltages. **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** 12 (V)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | (Vp / Vs) = (Np / Ns) in any form | C1 |
> |  | 230 / Vs = 1710 / 90 or Vs = (230 × 90) / 1710 OR Vs = 230 / 19 | C1 |
> |  | 12 (V) | A1 |
> | (b) | <u>In a step-down transformer there are fewer turns on secondary / output coil (than on primary / input coil)</u><br><u>In a step-up transformer there are more turns on secondary / output coil (than on primary / input coil)</u> | B2 |
> | (c) | less energy / power wasted (in cables ) / more efficient (transmission) | B1 |
> |  | <u>**And any one from:**</u><br>(because) smaller current (in transmission cables)<br>(and so) smaller heating effect (in transmission cables)<br>(and so) thinner cables can be used (which are cheaper) | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Candidates who set out a correct equation and substituted values often gave correct answers. Candidates who attempted to use some form of ratios often failed to score any credit.
>
> **(b)** The majority of candidates gave correct descriptions of the coil arrangements in step-up and step- down transformers. Weaker candidates merely stated that they increased or decreased the voltage.
>
> **(c)** The majority of candidates gained at least partial credit, although weaker candidates thought that the higher voltage helped to prevent the loss of electricity.
