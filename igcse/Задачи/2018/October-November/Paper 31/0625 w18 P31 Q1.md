# Question 1 (8 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2018 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 1 · вопрос 1  
Источник: [[_pdf/2018/0625_w18_qp_31.pdf#page=2|условие, стр. 2]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_w18_ms_31.pdf#page=6|схема оценивания, стр. 6]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_w18_er.pdf#page=17|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w18 P31]]  
Темы: [[1.2 Motion]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Описать движение по графику скорости, определить путь и вычислить среднюю скорость на двух участках.

Fig. 1.1 shows a speed-time graph for a student who is running.

![[0625-w18-31-p2-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 1.1**

**(a)** **(i)** Describe the movement of the student, as shown in Fig. 1.1. **[2]**

**(ii)** Calculate the distance travelled by the student between 80 s and 100 s.

distance travelled =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ m **[3]**

**(b)** An athlete runs 630 m in 130 s on a flat section of a road and then 254 m in 40 s on a downhill slope.

Calculate the average speed for the total distance run by the athlete.

average speed =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ m / s **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)(ii)** 40 (m)
>
> **(b)** 5.2 (m / s)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | constant speed OR speed of 4 m / s (for 80 s) | B1 |
> |  | (constant) deceleration OR speed decreases OR slows (down after 80 s) OR stops after 100 s | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | distance = area under graph | C1 |
> |  | 20 × 4 × 0.5 or area = ½ × base × height | C1 |
> |  | 40 (m) | A1 |
> | (b) | (average speed =) total distance ÷ total time | C1 |
> |  | (630 + 254) ÷ (130 +40) OR 884 ÷ 170 | C1 |
> |  | 5.2 (m / s) | A1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** Most candidates gave descriptions detailed enough to gain full credit.
>
> **(ii)** Most candidates gained full credit, but weaker candidates sometimes made errors, such as using the wrong times.
>
> **(b)** The majority of candidates gained at least partial credit for calculating an average speed, but only the strongest candidates correctly added the two distances and divided by the total time.
