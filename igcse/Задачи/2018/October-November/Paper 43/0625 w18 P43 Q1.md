# Question 1 (8 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2018 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 3 · вопрос 1  
Источник: [[_pdf/2018/0625_w18_qp_43.pdf#page=2|условие, стр. 2]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_w18_ms_43.pdf#page=6|схема оценивания, стр. 6]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_w18_er.pdf#page=34|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w18 P43]]  
Темы: [[1.2 Motion]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить скорость и среднюю скорость по графику пути, отметить нулевое ускорение и продолжить график замедлением и остановкой.

Fig. 1.1 is the distance-time graph for a moving car.

![[0625-w18-43-p2-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 1.1**

**(a)** On Fig. 1.1, mark a point P where the acceleration of the car is zero. **[1]**

**(b)** Determine:

**(i)** the speed of the car at time *t* = 15 s

speed =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(ii)** the average speed of the car between time *t* = 30 s and time *t* = 45 s.

average speed =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[2]**

**(c)** At time *t* = 45 s, the car starts to decelerate. At time *t* = 55 s and at a distance of 400 m from the starting point, the car stops. It then remains stationary for 5.0 s.

On Fig. 1.1, draw a possible continuation of the distance-time graph. **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)(i)** 5.0 m/ s
>
> **(b)(ii)** 10 m / s

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | P marked on line between *t* = 0 s and *t* = 30 s | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | (*v* =) gradient **or** 150 / 30 **or** appropriate division using other points | C1 |
> |  | 5.0 m/ s | A1 |
> | (b)(ii) | (*v* =) *x* / *t* **or** (300 – 150) / (45 – 30) **or** 150 / 15 | C1 |
> |  | 10 m / s | A1 |
> | (c) | gradient decreasing | B1 |
> |  | smooth transition to horizontal **and** line not too thick | B1 |
> |  | horizontal to (60 s, 400 m) | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** This was usually correct with P marked at an appropriate position. Some candidates marked other points on the line when answering subsequent parts of the question and if the label P was omitted, this led to a lack of clarity.
>
> **(b) (i)** This was often correctly answered although often no working was shown.
>
> **(ii)** A common incorrect approach was to calculate a value for a speed using the coordinates for the car at t = 30 s and a second value at t = 45 s and then to average the two values.
>
> **(c)** There were many good answers here and most candidates obtained at least partial credit. However, there were also answers that were drawn with very little care and some of the lines were very thick.
