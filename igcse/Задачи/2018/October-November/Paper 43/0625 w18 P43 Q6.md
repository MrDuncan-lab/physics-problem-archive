# Question 6 (9 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2018 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 3 · вопрос 6  
Источник: [[_pdf/2018/0625_w18_qp_43.pdf#page=8|условие, стр. 8–9]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_w18_ms_43.pdf#page=9|схема оценивания, стр. 9]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_w18_er.pdf#page=34|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w18 P43]]  
Темы: [[3.2.4 Dispersion of light]], [[3.2.2 Refraction of light]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Построить дисперсию света, определить монохроматичность и объяснить отражение или преломление лучей в призмах по критическому углу.

**(a)** Fig. 6.1 shows white light incident at point X on a glass prism.

![[0625-w18-43-p8-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 6.1**

**(i)** From point X on Fig. 6.1, draw a ray of red light, labelled R and a ray of violet light, labelled V, to show how a spectrum is formed on the screen. **[2]**

**(ii)** State the colour of light in the visible spectrum with the shortest wavelength. **[1]**

**(b)** The critical angle for a type of glass is 42°.

Fig. 6.2 and Fig. 6.3 show two prisms ABC and PQR made of this type of glass. A ray of monochromatic red light passes into each of the prisms.

![[0625-w18-43-p8-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 6.2 Fig. 6.3**

**(i)** State what is meant by *monochromatic* light. **[1]**

**(ii)** Describe and explain what happens to the ray of light in Fig. 6.2 as it strikes side AC of the prism. **[2]**

**(iii)** Describe and explain what happens to the ray of light in Fig. 6.3 as it strikes the glass at point Y. **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)(i)** correct **and** more refractions of both rays at second surface **and** (more) divergence **and** labels

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | correct refractions **and** dispersion at first surface | M1 |
> |  | correct **and** more refractions of both rays at second surface **and** (more) divergence **and** labels | A1 |
> | (a)(ii) | violet | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | <u>(light of) a single frequency</u> | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | total internal reflection (at side AC) **or** internal reflection **and** no refraction | B1 |
> |  | angle of incidence greater than critical angle / 42°<br>(and refractive index of glass greater than that of air than air) | B1 |
> | (b)(iii) | <u>light refracts (at Y)</u> | B1 |
> |  | <u>angle of incidence less than critical angle / 42°</u> | B1 |
> |  | (some) light reflects | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** This was often correctly answered and the light passing through the prism was very commonly shown correctly. Where full credit was not awarded, it was the light emerging from the prism into the air that was drawn incorrectly.
>
> **(ii) “**Red” and “violet” were given with a similar frequency.
>
> **(b) (i)** Most answers referred to a single wavelength, frequency or colour. Only answers that referred to the two simply measurable quantities were awarded credit.
>
> **(ii)** The expression “Total Internal Reflection” was seen quite often. Few candidates explained the cause and answers such as “The ray is less than the critical angle” were considered too vague for credit.
>
> **(iii)** Many candidates referred to partial reflection and refraction but fewer made any comment about the angle of incidence. There were several references to the occurrence of both Total Internal Reflection and refraction.
