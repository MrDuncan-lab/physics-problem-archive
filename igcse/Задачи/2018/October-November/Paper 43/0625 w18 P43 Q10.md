# Question 10 (8 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2018 · Paper 4 — Theory (Extended), вариант 3 · вопрос 10  
Источник: [[_pdf/2018/0625_w18_qp_43.pdf#page=14|условие, стр. 14]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_w18_ms_43.pdf#page=13|схема оценивания, стр. 13]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_w18_er.pdf#page=34|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w18 P43]]  
Темы: [[5.2.3 Radioactive decay]], [[5.2.4 Half-life]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Записать бета-распад тория, вычислить активность после нескольких периодов полураспада и учесть излучение дочернего изотопа.

Thorium-234 (${}^{234}_{90}\text{Th}$) is radioactive. It decays by β-emission to form an isotope of protactinium (Pa).

**(a)** Complete the nuclide equation for this decay.

![[0625-w18-43-p14-f1.svg]]

Pa + β **[2]**

**(b)** A pure sample of thorium-234 emits β-particles at a count rate of 2480 counts / second. The half-life of thorium-234 is 24 days.

Calculate the count rate for the emission of β-particles from the thorium in the sample after 72 days have passed.

count rate  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

**(c)** The isotope of protactinium in **(a)** is also radioactive. It decays by β-emission and has a half-life of 70 seconds.

State and explain how this would affect the **observed** count rate for the sample in **(b)** after 72 days. **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(b)** 310 counts / second

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | ${}^{234}_{91}$(Pa) | B1 |
> |  | ${}^{0}_{-1}$(β) | B1 |
> | (b) | 72 / 24 **or** 3.0 (half-lives) | C1 |
> |  | 2$^{3}$ **or** 1 / 8 **or** 2480 / 8 | C1 |
> |  | 310 counts / second | A1 |
> | (c) | count rate larger (than 310 counts / second) | B1 |
> |  | protactinium is also emitting (β-)particles / (nuclear) radiation | B1 |
> |  | count rate (approximately) double **or** product of protactinium decay also radioactive **or** amount of protactinium small **or** protactinium is <u>highly</u> radioactive **or** half-life of protactinium much shorter (than half-life of thorium) / very short | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** The decay equation was often correctly completed and full credit was awarded. The most common incorrect answers were to give the atomic number (proton number) of protactinium as 89 or the equivalent number for the beta-particle as 1 rather than –1.
>
> **(b)** This was often correct and awarded full credit. Even the answer 620 counts / s (which comes from miscounting the number of half-lives) was rare.
>
> **(c)** Most candidates gained at least partial credit, but full credit was quite rare for this question.
