Источник: [[_pdf/2018/0625_w18_qp_11.pdf#page=3|условие, стр. 3]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_w18_ms_11.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_w18_er.pdf#page=1|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

Темы: [[1.3 Mass and weight]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Найти массу одного из четырёх одинаковых блоков по их общему весу.

Four identical steel blocks are placed on a balance. The reading on the balance is 220 N.

What is the mass of one steel block?

**A** 5.5 kg  
**B** 22 kg  
**C** 55 kg  
**D** 88 kg

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Many candidates did not calculate the mass of the blocks from the weight. Of those who correctly did this, many did not divide their answer by four in order to calculate the mass of a single block, which demonstrated their inexperience in tackling two stage problems.
