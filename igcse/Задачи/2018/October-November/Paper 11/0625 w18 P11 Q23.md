> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить размер светового пятна параллельного пучка после собирающей линзы на расстоянии двух фокусов.

The diagram shows a parallel, cylindrical light beam of diameter *d* incident on a thin converging

lens. A screen is placed a distance equal to two focal lengths 2*f* from the lens.

![[0625-w18-11-p10-f1.svg]]

Which diagram shows the size of the spot of light seen on the screen?

![[0625-w18-11-p10-f2.svg]]

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This was a challenging question for many candidates. Candidates needed to identify the principal focus, then trace the two rays from the lens to the screen in order to determine the size of the image.
