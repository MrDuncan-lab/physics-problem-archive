> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать среднюю скорость бегуньи на двух равных участках с разными скоростями.

A runner runs 300 m at an average speed of 3.0 m / s. She then runs another 300 m at an average speed of 6.0 m / s.

What is her average speed for the total distance of 600 m?

**A** 2.0 m / s  
**B** 4.0 m / s  
**C** 4.5 m / s  
**D** 8.0 m / s

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Many candidates failed to work through the problem logically. Most candidates, incorrectly took the total time taken to be the average of the times for the first 300 m and the second 300 m as the average time. They then used this figure to calculate the average speed.
