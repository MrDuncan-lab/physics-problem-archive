> Определить изменение направления движения проводника в магнитном поле при обращении тока.

A wire is placed in a strong magnetic field. When a current is passed through the wire it moves

upwards, as shown.

![[0625-w18-11-p15-f1.svg]]

The current is reversed.

In which direction does the wire move?

**A** downwards  
**B** towards the north pole  
**C** towards the south pole  
**D** upwards

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**
