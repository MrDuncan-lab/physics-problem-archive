> Вычислить увеличение объёма воздушного пузырька при всплытии по закону Бойля.

A bubble of air has a volume of 2.0 cm$^{3}$ at the bottom of a lake where the total pressure is

4.0 × 10$^{5}$ Pa. The temperature of the water in the lake is constant. The atmospheric pressure at the surface is 1.0 × 10$^{5}$ Pa.

What is the volume of the bubble when it rises to the surface?

**A** 0.13 cm$^{3}$  
**B** 0.17 cm$^{3}$  
**C** 6.0 cm$^{3}$  
**D** 8.0 cm$^{3}$

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
