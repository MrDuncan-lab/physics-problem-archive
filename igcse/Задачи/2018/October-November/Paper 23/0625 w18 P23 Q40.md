> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить период полураспада радиоактивного образца по таблице скорости счёта.

The count rate due to a sample of a radioactive isotope is measured for 80 minutes.

| time | count rate |
|---|---|
| / minutes | counts / second |
| 0 | 480 |
| 20 | 380 |
| 40 | 300 |
| 60 | 240 |
| 80 | 190 |

What is the half-life of the isotope?

**A** 20 minutes  
**B** 40 minutes  
**C** 60 minutes  
**D** 80 minutes

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Candidates showed an understanding of the meaning of half-life, and calculated the half-life of this isotope correctly.
