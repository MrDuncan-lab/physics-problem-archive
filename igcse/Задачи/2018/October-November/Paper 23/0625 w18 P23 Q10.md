# Question 10 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2018 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 3 · вопрос 10  
Источник: [[_pdf/2018/0625_w18_qp_23.pdf#page=5|условие, стр. 5]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_w18_ms_23.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_w18_er.pdf#page=15|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Вычислить прирост потенциальной энергии бочки и силу подъёма по наклонной плоскости без трения.

A barrel of mass 40 kg is raised to a height of 1.5 m by rolling it up a ramp. The length of the ramp is 8.0 m. The force *F* applied to the barrel acts parallel to the ramp. The frictional force is negligible.

![[0625-w18-23-p5-f1.svg]]

Which row is correct?

|  | gain in gravitational potential energy / J | force *F* / N |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | 60 | 7.5 |
| **B** | 60 | 40 |
| **C** | 600 | 75 |
| **D** | 600 | 400 |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The majority of candidates incorrectly thought that the force up the slope was equal to the weight of the barrel, rather than calculating it by equating the work done to the gravitational energy gained and then using the formula, work done = force × distance.
