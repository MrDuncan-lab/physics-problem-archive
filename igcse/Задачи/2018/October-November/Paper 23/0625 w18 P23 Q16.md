> Упорядочить твёрдые тела, жидкости и газы по величине теплового расширения.

Which row shows the relative order of thermal expansion of solids, liquids and gases?

|  | most expansion least expansion |  |  |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | solids | liquids | gases |
| **B** | solids | gases | liquids |
| **C** | gases | solids | liquids |
| **D** | gases | liquids | solids |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
