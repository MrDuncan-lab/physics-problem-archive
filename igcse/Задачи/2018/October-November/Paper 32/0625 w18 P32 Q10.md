# Question 10 (7 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2018 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 10  
Источник: [[_pdf/2018/0625_w18_qp_32.pdf#page=12|условие, стр. 12]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_w18_ms_32.pdf#page=10|схема оценивания, стр. 10]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_w18_er.pdf#page=20|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Распознать компонент цепи, определить последовательный ток, вычислить сопротивление проволоки и объяснить влияние толщины на ток.

Fig. 10.1 shows a circuit used by a student to test a metal wire made of nichrome.

![[0625-w18-32-p12-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 10.1**

**(a)** State the name of component Z. **[1]**

**(b)** The current reading on ammeter X is 0.8 A. State the reading on ammeter Y. **[1]**

**(c)** The current in the nichrome wire is 0.8 A. The potential difference (p.d.) across the nichrome wire is 4.5 V.

Calculate the resistance of the nichrome wire.

resistance =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ Ω **[3]**

**(d)** The student tests a different nichrome wire, which is thicker than the wire in **(c)**, but of the same length. When testing this wire, the current in the wire is different from the value given in **(c)**.

State and explain the difference in current. **[2]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(c)** 5.6(25)

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | variable resistor or rheostat | B1 |
> | (b) | 0.8 (A) | B1 |
> | (c) | V = IR **OR** (R =) V ÷ I | C1 |
> |  | 4.5 ÷ 0.8 | C1 |
> |  | 5.6(25) | A1 |
> | (d) | (current) increases **OR** larger | B1 |
> |  | (as new/thicker wire has) less resistance | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Most candidates correctly named the variable resistor.
>
> **(b)** Most candidates gave the correct current.
>
> **(c)** The vast majority of candidates correctly calculated the resistance.
>
> **(d)** Although many candidates stated the correct relationship between resistance and current, many stated that a thicker wire would have less current. Stronger candidates correctly stated that there would be more current through the thicker wire.
