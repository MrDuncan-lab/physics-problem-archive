# Question 11 (7 marks)

Темы: [[4.5.3 Magnetic effect of a current]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Построить магнитное поле прямого проводника и объяснить повторяющиеся удары электрического звонка.

**(a)** A long straight wire passes through a piece of card. There is a current in the wire, as shown in Fig. 11.1.

![[0625-w18-32-p13-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 11.1**

Fig. 11.2 shows the view of the card from above. The current is into the page.

![[0625-w18-32-p13-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 11.2**

The current in the wire produces a magnetic field around the wire. One magnetic field line is drawn.

On Fig. 11.2, draw **two** more magnetic field lines around the wire. Show the direction of the magnetic field by drawing an arrow on each field line. **[2]**

**(b)** Fig. 11.3 shows the circuit for an electric bell.

![[0625-w18-32-p14-f1.png]]

**Fig. 11.3**

Explain how the circuit causes the hammer to hit the bell repeatedly when the switch is closed.

Use your ideas about circuits and electromagnets. **[5]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | two roughly circular lines drawn | B1 |
> |  | arrow showing clockwise direction | B1 |
> | (b) | **any 5 from:**<br>current in circuit / coil(s)<br>coil becomes electromagnet owtte<br>(coil / electromagnet) attracts (soft iron) armature<br>(so) hammer hits gong<br>circuit is broken (at A / contact screw)<br>electromagnet loses magnetic field / stops working<br>armature springs back<br>cycle repeats | B5 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Most candidates correctly drew two circles with a clockwise arrow.
>
> **(b)** Most of the stronger candidates gave a good description of how the bell worked. Weaker candidates just described the flow of current round the circuit.
