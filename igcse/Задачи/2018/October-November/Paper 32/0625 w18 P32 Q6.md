# Question 6 (5 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2018 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 2 · вопрос 6  
Источник: [[_pdf/2018/0625_w18_qp_32.pdf#page=7|условие, стр. 7]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_w18_ms_32.pdf#page=8|схема оценивания, стр. 8]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_w18_er.pdf#page=20|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Продолжить преломлённый луч через линзу и построить схождение параллельных лучей.

**(a)** Fig. 6.1 shows a ray of red light incident on part of a lens.

![[0625-w18-32-p7-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 6.1**

**(i)** On Fig. 6.1, continue the path of the ray as it passes through the lens and emerges from it. **[2]**

**(ii)** State the term used to describe the process as the ray enters and leaves the lens. **[1]**

**(b)** Fig. 6.2 shows two parallel rays of light travelling towards another lens.

![[0625-w18-32-p7-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 6.2**

The two rays of light pass through the lens to form an image.

On Fig. 6.2, continue the path of the rays. Extend the rays for at least 5 cm beyond the lens. **[2]**

> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | refracted at first boundary correctly | B1 |
> |  | refracted at second boundary correctly | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | refraction | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | converging / bent inwards / meet at a point e.g. focus | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | two straight rays drawn converging | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a) (i)** Many candidates found it challenging to determine the correct direction of the refracted ray at each surface. Normals were often drawn at angles other than at 90° to the surface.
>
> **(ii)** Stronger candidates answered this well but weaker candidates found it more of a challenge with diffraction and reflection amongst the more frequently seen incorrect answers.
>
> **(b)** Most candidates correctly showed the rays converging and intersecting.
