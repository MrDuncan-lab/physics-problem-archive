> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить перераспределение напряжений резистора и термистора в делителе при снижении температуры.

The circuit diagram shows a fixed resistor R and a thermistor T used in a potential divider circuit.

![[0625-w18-21-p12-f1.svg]]

*V*$_{\text{R}}$ and *V*$_{\text{T}}$ are the potential differences across R and T respectively.

What happens to *V*$_{\text{R}}$ and to *V*$_{\text{T}}$ as the temperature of the thermistor decreases?

|  | *V*$_{\text{R}}$ | *V*$_{\text{T}}$ |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | decreases | increases |
| **B** | increases | decreases |
| **C** | stays the same | decreases |
| **D** | stays the same | increases |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> This question proved challenging for many candidates. Candidates needed to recognise that the thermistor’s resistance increases as the temperature decreases, thus the current in the circuit decreases, the p.d. across the resistor decreases and therefore the p.d. across the thermistor increases.
