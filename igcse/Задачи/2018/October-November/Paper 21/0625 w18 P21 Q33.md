> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить логическую функцию цифровой схемы с двумя входами.

The diagram shows a digital circuit with two inputs and one output.

![[0625-w18-21-p13-f1.svg]]

What describes the action of the circuit?

**A** AND  
**B** NAND  
**C** NOR  
**D** OR

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> Stronger candidates answered this question correctly. Weaker candidates did not recognise the logic gates that were being used and failed to work their way through the truth table.
