> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать силу гидростатического давления воды на сливную пробку по глубине и площади.

A washbasin has an exit pipe covered with a plug of area 12 cm$^{2}$. A chain is attached to the

centre of the plug to assist in pulling the plug away from the exit hole. The washbasin contains water to a depth of 0.080 m.

The density of the water is 1000 kg / m$^{3}$.

![[0625-w18-21-p5-f2.png]]

What is the force acting on the plug due to the water?

**A** 0.96 N  
**B** 800 N  
**C** 9600 N  
**D** 80 000 N

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Many candidates did not take sufficient care when considering consistency of units. Only a relatively small percentage recognised that the area of the plug was in cm$^{2}$.
