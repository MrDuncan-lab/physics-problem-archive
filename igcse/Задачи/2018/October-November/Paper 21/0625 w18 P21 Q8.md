# Question 8 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2018 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 1 · вопрос 8  
Источник: [[_pdf/2018/0625_w18_qp_21.pdf#page=4|условие, стр. 4]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_w18_ms_21.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_w18_er.pdf#page=9|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w18 P21]]  
Темы: [[1.6 Momentum]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Вычислить среднюю силу удара мяча о стену по изменению импульса и длительности столкновения.

A moving ball with a momentum of 25 kg m / s collides head-on with a wall.

![[0625-w18-21-p4-f1.svg]]

It rebounds from the wall with the same speed but in the opposite direction. The time of collision is 50 ms.

What is the average force exerted on the wall by the ball during the collision?

**A** 0.50 N  
**B** 1.00 N  
**C** 500 N  
**D** 1000 N

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Only the strongest candidates answered this question correctly. The most common error was to fail to recognise that the change in momentum was 50 kg m s$^{\text{–1}}$ (25 – (–25)). In addition, a lot of candidates did not notice that the time was given in milliseconds.
