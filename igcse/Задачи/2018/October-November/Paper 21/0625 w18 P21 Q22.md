> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Указать верное свойство изображения в плоском зеркале.

Which statement about the image formed by a plane mirror is correct?

**A** The image is larger than the object.  
**B** The image is smaller than the object.  
**C** The image is twice as far from the mirror as the object.  
**D** The image is virtual.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Most candidates were aware that the image in a plane mirror is virtual.
