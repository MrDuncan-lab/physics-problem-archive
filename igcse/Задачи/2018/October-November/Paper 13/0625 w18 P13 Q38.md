> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить протонный и нейтронный состав ядра углерода по записи нуклида.

What is a carbon nucleus, $^{12}_{6}$ C , composed of?

**A** 6 neutrons and 6 electrons  
**B** 6 neutrons, 6 protons and 6 electrons  
**C** 6 protons and 6 electrons  
**D** 6 protons and 6 neutrons

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Another question where detailed and careful reading was requires. The question referred to the nucleus of carbon, not the atom itself. Many candidates chose the response which described the complete atom.
