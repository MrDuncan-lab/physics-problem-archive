# Question 35 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2018 · Paper 1 — Multiple Choice (Core), вариант 3 · вопрос 35  
Источник: [[_pdf/2018/0625_w18_qp_13.pdf#page=14|условие, стр. 14]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_w18_ms_13.pdf|схема оценивания]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_w18_er.pdf#page=6|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w18 P13]]  
Темы: [[4.5.4 Force on a current-carrying conductor]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить влияние одновременного обращения тока и магнитного поля на направление силы проводника.

There is an electric current in a wire. The wire is placed in a magnetic field. A force acts on the

wire due to the current.

Which statement is correct?

**A** The magnetic field must be produced by a permanent magnet and not by an electromagnet.  
**B** The wire must be made from a magnetic material.  
**C** When both the current and the magnetic field are reversed, the direction of the force is

unchanged.

**D** When the current is reversed, but not the magnetic field, there will be no force on the wire.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**
