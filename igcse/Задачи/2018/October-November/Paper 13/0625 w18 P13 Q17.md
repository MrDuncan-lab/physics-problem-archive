> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить изменения расстояния и скорости молекул при конденсации пара без изменения температуры.

Steam at 100 °C condenses to form water at 100 °C.

What happens to the average separation and what happens to the average speed of the water molecules?

|  | separation | average speed |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | decreases | decreases |
| **B** | decreases | remains the same |
| **C** | increases | decreases |
| **D** | increases | remains the same |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **B**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Many candidates were not aware that there is no change in the average speed of the particles as a gas condenses to form a liquid. The only change is a reduction in their potential energy as their separation decreases.
