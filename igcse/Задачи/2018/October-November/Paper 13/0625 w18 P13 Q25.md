> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить продольную природу звука и отражение как причину эха.

Which row gives the nature of sound waves and the name of the effect that causes an echo of a

sound?

|  | nature of sound waves | effect causing an echo |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | longitudinal | reflection |
| **B** | longitudinal | refraction |
| **C** | transverse | reflection |
| **D** | transverse | refraction |

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Few candidates knew the basic fact that sound is transmitted by longitudinal waves and many were unaware that an echo is a reflection of a sound wave.
