# Question 8 (7 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2018 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 3 · вопрос 8  
Источник: [[_pdf/2018/0625_w18_qp_33.pdf#page=10|условие, стр. 10–11]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_w18_ms_33.pdf#page=10|схема оценивания, стр. 10]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_w18_er.pdf#page=23|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

Темы: [[4.1 Simple phenomena of magnetism]], [[4.5.3 Magnetic effect of a current]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Построить поле магнита и сравнить удержание железных опилок электромагнитами с железным и стальным сердечниками.

Fig. 8.1 shows a sheet of paper. A bar magnet is underneath the paper. A student sprinkles iron filings onto the paper.

![[0625-w18-33-p10-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 8.1**

**(a)** On Fig. 8.1

- Label the position of each pole of the magnet. Use the label N for the north pole and S for the south pole.
- Draw one magnetic field line above the magnet and draw one magnetic field line below the magnet.
- Add **one** arrow to show the direction of the magnetic field. **[4]**

**(b)** A student places a soft iron rod inside a coil of insulated wire. The coil is connected to a battery, as shown in Fig. 8.2.

![[0625-w18-33-p10-f2.svg]]

**Fig. 8.2**

**(i)** State the name given to the device shown in Fig. 8.2. **[1]**

**(ii)** The student puts one end of the device in Fig. 8.2 just above a pile of iron filings.

He closes the switch for a short time and then opens it again.

Describe the effect this has on the iron filings. **[1]**

**(iii)** The student removes the soft iron rod and replaces it with a steel rod.

He puts one end of the steel rod just above the pile of iron filings. He closes the switch for a short time and then opens it again.

Describe the effect this has on the iron fillings. **[1]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | N and S labelled at either end of magnet<br>correct field line shown above magnet<br>correct field line shown below magnet<br>field points in direction N to S | B4 |
> | (b)(i) | electromagnet | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | (soft iron) filings attracted, but (most) fall off when switch opened | B1 |
> | (b)(iii) | (steel) (most / more / they) remain attached when switch opened | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** This was well answered by the majority of candidates. The only recurring errors were an incorrect or contradictory field direction.
>
> **(b) (i)** Few candidates knew this was an electromagnet. Many thought it was an a.c. generator or gave no response.
>
> **(ii)(iii)** Very few correct answers were seen to either parts and many candidates gave no response. The different magnetic properties of iron and steel did not seem to be fully understood.
