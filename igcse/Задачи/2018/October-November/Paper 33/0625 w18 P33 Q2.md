# Question 2 (10 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2018 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 3 · вопрос 2  
Источник: [[_pdf/2018/0625_w18_qp_33.pdf#page=4|условие, стр. 4]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_w18_ms_33.pdf#page=7|схема оценивания, стр. 7]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_w18_er.pdf#page=23|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w18 P33]]  
Темы: [[1.3 Mass and weight]], [[1.4 Density]], [[E.1 Measurements, tables, graphs, precision and planning an experiment]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Вычислить массу металла, описать измерение объёма вытеснением воды, объяснить ограничение для плавающего дерева и определить плотность.

**(a)** A student has a piece of metal that has an irregular shape. The weight of the metal is 3.0 N.

Calculate the mass of the metal.

mass =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ kg **[2]**

**(b)** Fig. 2.1 shows the piece of metal, a measuring cylinder and a beaker containing water.

![[0625-w18-33-p4-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 2.1**

**(i)** Describe how to determine the volume of the metal, using the equipment in Fig. 2.1. **[4]**

**(ii)** Explain why the procedure in **(b)(i)** is not suitable for finding the volume of a piece of low-density wood that is of similar shape and size to the piece of metal in **(a)**. **[1]**

**(iii)** The mass of another piece of metal is 405 g and its volume is 150 cm$^{3}$. Calculate the density of the metal. State the unit.

density =  \_\_\_\_\_\_ **[3]**

> [!answer]- Ответ (итоговые ответы из схемы оценивания)
> **(a)** 0.3 (kg)
>
> **(b)(iii)** 2.7

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | W = m g OR (m =) W / g **OR** 3.0 ÷ 10 | C1 |
> |  | 0.3 (kg) | A1 |
> | (b)(i) | determine / read volume of water in measuring cylinder | B1 |
> |  | (submerge / sink) metal in water / measuring cylinder | B1 |
> |  | determine / read new volume of water (and metal) | B1 |
> |  | find difference between final and initial volumes | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | wood floats OR does not sink | B1 |
> | (b)(iii) | D = M / V **OR** 405 ÷ 150 | C1 |
> |  | 2.7 | A1 |
> |  | g / cm$^{3}$ | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Many candidates answered correctly. A common error was to multiply the weight by 10 instead of dividing by 10.
>
> **(b) (i)** This was answered very well, with many candidates gaining full credit. However some candidates forgot to subtract the original volume of water from the volume of water and metal.
>
> **(ii)** Many candidates realised that the wood would float/not sink and so would not displace any water.
>
> **(iii)** The calculation was well answered by the majority of candidates. However, few candidates gave the appropriate unit in their answer.
