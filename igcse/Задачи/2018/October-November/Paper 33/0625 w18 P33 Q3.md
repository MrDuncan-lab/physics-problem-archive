> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Описать получение электричества изображёнными устройствами и сравнить их с угольной электростанцией.

Fig. 3.1 shows devices that generate electricity.

![[0625-w18-33-p5-f1.png]]

**Fig. 3.1**

**(a)** Describe how the devices shown in Fig. 3.1 generate electrical energy. **[3]**

**(b)** Describe the advantages and disadvantages of generating electrical energy by using a coal-fired power station compared with the devices shown in Fig. 3.1. **[4]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | Kinetic **OR** movement energy from wind **OR** moving air | B1 |
> |  | <u>turns turbine</u> | B1 |
> |  | turbine turns generator (to generate electricity) | B1 |
> | (b) | **Any two advantages from:**<br>reliable supply of electricity owtte<br>large amount of electrical energy produced / power output<br>plentiful supply of fuel | B2 |
> |  | **Any two disadvantages from:**<br>non-renewable (energy source)<br>greenhouse gases / carbon dioxide produced / increases global warming<br>contributes to atmospheric / air pollution / acid rain | B2 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** Stronger candidates were able to give a thorough description. However, many candidates just stated “the wind moves the turbine”. When more detail was given, candidates often failed to realise that it is the generator that generates electricity not the turbine.
>
> **(b)** Many candidates simply stated “pollution” or “environmental damage” which was too vague. Fuller answers based on using coal as it is more reliable/not wind dependent and/or non-renewable were frequently seen.
