# Question 7 (4 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2018 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 3 · вопрос 7  
Источник: [[_pdf/2018/0625_w18_qp_33.pdf#page=9|условие, стр. 9]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_w18_ms_33.pdf#page=9|схема оценивания, стр. 9]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_w18_er.pdf#page=23|отчёт экзаменаторов]]  
Работа: [[0625 w18 P33]]  
Темы: [[3.4 Sound]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбрать верные утверждения о скорости звука, слуховом диапазоне, громкости и образовании эха.

Some students make statements about sound.

In each part of the question, only **one** statement is correct. Place a tick in the box next to each correct statement.

![[0625-w18-33-p9-f1.svg]]

Sound travels at the same speed as light.

Sound travels faster than the speed of light.

Sound travels slower than the speed of light. **[1]**

![[0625-w18-33-p9-f2.svg]]

Healthy human ears can hear sounds in the range 2.0 Hz to 2000 Hz.

Healthy human ears can hear sounds in the range 20 Hz to 20 000 Hz.

Healthy human ears can hear sounds in the range 200 Hz to 200 000 Hz. **[1]**

![[0625-w18-33-p9-f3.svg]]

Increasing the amplitude of a sound wave increases its speed.

Increasing the amplitude of a sound wave increases its frequency.

Increasing the amplitude of a sound wave increases its loudness. **[1]**

![[0625-w18-33-p9-f4.svg]]

An echo is produced when sound is diffracted.

An echo is produced when sound is refracted.

An echo is produced when sound is reflected. **[1]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a) | 3rd box – sound travels slower than the speed of light | B1 |
> | (b) | 2nd box – 20 to 20000 Hz | B1 |
> | (c) | 3rd box – Increasing the amplitude of a sound wave increases its loudness | B1 |
> | (d) | 3rd box – an echo is produced when sound is reflected | B1 |

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> **(a)** The majority of candidates gave the correct answer.
>
> **(b)** Most candidates gave the correct answer but a noticeable number thought that healthy human ears could hear sounds in the range 2.0 Hz to 2000 Hz.
>
> **(c)** Most candidates gave the correct answer. However, some thought that increasing the amplitude of a sound wave increased its frequency.
>
> **(d)** Many candidates answered correctly but a significant number thought that an echo was produced when sound was refracted.
