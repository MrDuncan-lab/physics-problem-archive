# Question 6 (5 marks)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2018 · Paper 3 — Theory (Core), вариант 3 · вопрос 6  
Источник: [[_pdf/2018/0625_w18_qp_33.pdf#page=8|условие, стр. 8]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_w18_ms_33.pdf#page=9|схема оценивания, стр. 9]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_w18_er.pdf#page=23|отчёт экзаменаторов]]

Темы: [[3.2.2 Refraction of light]], [[3.2.4 Dispersion of light]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Построить преломление луча в призме, отметить угол падения и описать дисперсию белого света.

**(a)** Fig. 6.1 shows a ray of green light hitting a glass prism.

![[0625-w18-33-p8-f1.svg]]

**Fig. 6.1**

**(i)** On Fig. 6.1, label the angle of incidence for the ray, using the letter, *i.* **[1]**

**(ii)** On Fig. 6.1, complete the path of the ray of green light until it hits the screen. **[2]**

**(b)** The ray of green light is replaced with a ray of white light.

**(i)** The white light splits into a spectrum of colours. State the term used to describe this effect. **[1]**

**(ii)** State the colour that is refracted most by the prism. **[1]**

> [!grading]- Схема оценивания (mark scheme)
> | Пункт | Ответ | Баллы |
> |---|---|---|
> | (a)(i) | *i* placed between incident ray and normal | B1 |
> | (a)(ii) | ray refracted towards normal at 1st surface | B1 |
> |  | ray refracted down at 2nd surface | B1 |
> | (b)(i) | <u>dispersion</u> | B1 |
> | (b)(ii) | violet | B1 |

>

>
> **(b) (i)** The correct answer of “dispersion” was only given by the strongest candidates.
>
