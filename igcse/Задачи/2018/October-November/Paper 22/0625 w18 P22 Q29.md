> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить зависимость сопротивления медной проволоки от квадрата её диаметра при неизменной длине.

Copper wire is available in fixed lengths but in various diameters *d*. Each diameter has a different

resistance *R*.

Which relationship between *R* and *d* is correct?

**A** *R* is directly proportional to *d*.  
**B** *R* is directly proportional to *d*$^{2}$.  
**C** *R* is inversely proportional to *d*.  
**D** *R* is inversely proportional to *d*$^{2}$.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Although most candidates recognised that the relationship between the diameter and the resistance of a conductor is an inverse relationship, many did not recognise that the resistance is proportional to the cross sectional area of the conductor, hence it is inversely proportional to the diameter squared.
