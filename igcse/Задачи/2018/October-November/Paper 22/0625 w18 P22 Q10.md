> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Вычислить скорость маятника в нижней точке по изменению высоты груза.

The diagram shows a pendulum.

![[0625-w18-22-p5-f5.svg]]

The pendulum swings from P to Q to R and back to P.

P is 10 cm higher than Q.

At which speed does the pendulum bob pass through Q?

**A** 0.44 m / s  
**B** 1.0 m / s  
**C** 1.4 m / s  
**D** 2.0 m / s

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> The question required candidates to equate the loss of gravitational potential energy with the gain in kinetic
>
> energy. Relatively few successfully completed the task. The most common error was to ignore the in the
>
> formula E$_{\text{k}}$ = mv$^{2}$ .
