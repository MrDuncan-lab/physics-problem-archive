> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить работу крана как увеличение потенциальной и кинетической энергии ускоренно поднимаемых кирпичей.

A crane on a building site lifts bricks of total mass 200 kg, initially at rest on the ground, with

uniform acceleration.

When the bricks are 5.0 m from the ground, they have a speed of 5.0 m / s.

How much work is done during this process?

**A** 2.5 kJ  
**B** 10.0 kJ  
**C** 12.5 kJ  
**D** 35 kJ

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **C**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Only the strongest candidates answered this question correctly. The majority of candidates simply calculated the work done against gravity (the gravitational potential energy) ignoring the work done in accelerating the object (the kinetic energy).
