# Question 35 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2018 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 2 · вопрос 35  
Источник: [[_pdf/2018/0625_w18_qp_22.pdf#page=14|условие, стр. 14]] · [[_pdf/2018/0625_w18_ms_22.pdf|схема оценивания]]  
Работа: [[0625 w18 P22]]

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбрать преимущество высоковольтной передачи энергии по уменьшению нагрева окружающей среды.

Electrical power is transmitted at 400 kV and transformed down to 240 V before being delivered to

consumers.

Which statement is correct?

**A** A 400 kV cable requires less insulation than a 240 V cable.  
**B** Birds are subject to a high-voltage shock when resting on a high-voltage cable.  
**C** The current in the transmission wires is equal to the current delivered to the consumers.  
**D** With a 400 kV transmission system, a smaller proportion of the energy is used to heat the

atmosphere than with a 240 V system.

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
