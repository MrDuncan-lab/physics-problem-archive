# Question 8 (1 mark)

**IGCSE 0625 Physics** · October/November 2018 · Paper 2 — Multiple Choice (Extended), вариант 2 · вопрос 8

> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Рассчитать скорость и направление совместного движения тел после неупругого встречного столкновения.

An object of mass 3.0 kg, travelling at a speed of 6.0 m / s, collides with an object of mass 2.0 kg, travelling in the opposite direction at a speed of 2.0 m / s.

![[0625-w18-22-p5-f1.svg]]

![[0625-w18-22-p5-f2.svg]]

![[0625-w18-22-p5-f3.svg]]

![[0625-w18-22-p5-f4.svg]]

The objects stick together during the collision.

What is the speed and direction of the combined mass after the collision?

**A** 4.4 m / s to the left  
**B** 4.4 m / s to the right  
**C** 2.8 m / s to the left  
**D** 2.8 m / s to the right

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**
