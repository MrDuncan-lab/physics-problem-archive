> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Определить конструктивные параметры, влияющие на чувствительность жидкостного термометра.

Which factors affect the sensitivity of a thermometer?

1 the diameter of the bore of the tube

2 the length of the capillary tube

3 the thickness of the bulb wall

**A** 1 only  
**B** 1 and 2 only  
**C** 1 and 3 only  
**D** 1, 2 and 3

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **A**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Most candidates thought that the thickness of the wall of the bulb would affect the sensitivity of a thermometer. The effect is that the thermometer reacts more quickly to a change in temperature, but this does not make it more sensitive.
