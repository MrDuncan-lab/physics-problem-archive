> [!abstract] Кратко (составлено моделью)
> Выбрать логические элементы, дающие единицу при двух нулевых входных сигналах.

A logic gate has inputs 0,0 and an output 1.

Which two types of logic gate behave in this way?

**A** OR, AND  
**B** OR, NAND  
**C** NOR, AND  
**D** NOR, NAND

> [!answer]- Ответ
> **D**

> [!report]- Отчёт экзаменаторов
> Stronger candidates answered this question correctly. Weaker candidates did not recognise the logic gates that were being used and failed to work their way through the truth table.
