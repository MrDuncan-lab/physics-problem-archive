## Общие замечания экзаменаторов

**General comments**

The aim of the examination is to enable candidates to display their knowledge and understanding of practical physics techniques. These include

- graph plotting

- tabulation of readings

- manipulation of data to obtain results

- drawing conclusions

- dealing with possible sources or error

- control of variables

- accurate measurements

- choice of the most effective way to use the equipment provided

The general level of competence shown by the candidates was sound. Very few candidates failed to attempt all sections of each of the questions. There was no evidence of candidates suffering from lack of time. Many candidates dealt well with the range of practical skills tested. Each question differentiated in its own way. The majority of candidates showed evidence of preparation for all the different types of question in the examination. However **Question 2** proved to be straightforward for the majority of candidates, whereas **Question 3** caused many more problems.
