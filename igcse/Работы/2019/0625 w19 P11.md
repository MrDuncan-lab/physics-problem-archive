## Общие замечания экзаменаторов

**General comments**

Many candidates answered the questions well. Although there were areas of the syllabus which caused major problems, such as electromagnetism and radioactivity, there was evidence that many candidates had a good understanding of the majority of the syllabus. **Questions 1**, **4**, **5**, **8**, **11**, **20**, **26** and **37** were answered well, whereas **Questions 17**, **19**, **28**, **35**, **39** and **40** proved more challenging.
