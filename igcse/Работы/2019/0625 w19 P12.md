## Общие замечания экзаменаторов

**General comments**

Many candidates answered the questions well. Although there were areas of the syllabus which caused major problems, such as electromagnetism and radioactivity, there was evidence that many candidates had a good understanding of the majority of the syllabus. **Questions 8**, **11**, **14**, **15**, **17**, **20**, **23** and **26** were answered well, whereas **Questions 21**, **24**, **28**, **36**, and **40** proved more challenging.
