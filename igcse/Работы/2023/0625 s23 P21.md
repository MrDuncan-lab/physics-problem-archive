**General comments**

Questions on apparatus used to determine density and evaporation were answered well. However, there were some misconceptions about transformers and the life cycle of stars. It was evident that questions involving a.c. generators, transformers and the direction of the force on an electron in a magnetic field were not well understood. The questions on the new syllabus topic, space physics, showed mixed levels of understanding.
