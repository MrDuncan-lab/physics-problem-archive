**General comments**

While large numbers of candidates were able to provide adequate answers to all questions, outstanding answers were comparatively rare.

To end the ‘General comments’ on a more positive note, Question 5, a style of question which candidates often find difficult, and Question 6 which could be regarded as an unfamiliar application were both well done.
