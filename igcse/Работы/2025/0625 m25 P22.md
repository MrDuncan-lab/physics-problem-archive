## Общие замечания экзаменаторов

**General comments**

Candidates demonstrated very good knowledge of simple momentum calculations, gas pressure and the process of evaporation. However, there were some misconceptions about energy resources and total internal reflection.

It was evident that most candidates struggled with change in momentum calculations and weaker candidates struggled with calculations involving the combined resistance of resistors in parallel.
